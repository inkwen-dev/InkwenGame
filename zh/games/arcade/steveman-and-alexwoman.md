# Steveman 和 Alexwoman

<a href="https://inkwengame.com/zh/games/steveman-and-alexwoman" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">在 InkwenGame 玩</a>

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/steveman-and-alexwoman/cover.jpg)

你必须陪伴 Steveman 和 AlexWoman 展开危险的冒险，帮助他们避开屋内的怪物，抵达传送门。赶快前往传送门，注意躲避怪物，注意避开荆棘。跳过怪物，小心不要掉进有毒的水中。
