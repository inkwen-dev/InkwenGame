## How to play

Ride your motorcycle from start to finish while overcoming difficult obstacles along the track. Use the controls to accelerate, brake, and balance your bike carefully to avoid falling. Try to finish each level as fast as possible and collect as many stars as you can during the run. The more stars you earn, the more new tracks you unlock. To win, you need both quick reflexes and strong control skills.

## About

Trial Xtreme is a thrilling motorcycle racing game that puts your balance, precision, and endurance to the test. Ride through challenging obstacle-filled tracks packed with ramps, pipes, rocks, and tricky barriers where every move can lead to victory or a crash. This is not just a race to the finish — it is a skill-based challenge where speed and control matter equally. Complete tracks, improve your time, and collect stars to unlock new levels.
