## About

Deliver All Puzzle is a very addicted online puzzle game made for boys for free. Collect all the packages on the blocks and deliver them to the receivers. Sounds easy but in fact, it is a tricky game. The packages are placed at many places on the blocks, and you are only allowed to turn your direction in case of you hit the wall. There are many levels in this game. Good luck!
