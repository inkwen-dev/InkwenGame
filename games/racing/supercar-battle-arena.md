## How to play

Your main objective is to be the last car standing in the arena by destroying your opponents. You can fire rockets at your enemies or ram into them at high speed to push them off the map. As you eliminate enemies, you earn coins which can be used to buy much stronger battle cars from the garage menu.

Controls (PC)

Steer / Move: W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys
Jump: W or Up Arrow
Shoot (Rocket): Spacebar
Dash / Fly: Double tap the jump key quickly (W, W)
Controls (Mobile)

Steer / Move: Use the virtual joystick (◉) on the screen to steer your car.
Jump: Single tap (1x Tap) on the screen.
Shoot (Rocket): Tap the fire (🔥) icon on the screen.
Dash / Fly: Double tap (2x Tap) quickly on the screen.

## About

Supercar Battle Arena is an action-packed 3D bumper car game where you fight to survive in a ruthless arena with modified battle cars. Use your driving skills to push your opponents out of the arena or blow them up with your rockets. Earn coins in every match to unlock new, more powerful vehicles from your garage. What makes this game unique is the perfect blend of smooth vehicle physics with explosive combat mechanics and special abilities (jumping, dashing in mid-air). Can you become the true champion of the arena?
