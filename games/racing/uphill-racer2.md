## How to play

Use the left and right arrow keys (or on-screen buttons) to control your car.

Right to accelerate
Left to brake or reverse

Keep your balance while driving uphill and downhill, avoid crashing, and collect coins along the way. Try to reach the longest distance possible and beat your best score!

## About

Uphill Race2 is a fun and challenging physics-based driving game where you race across hilly terrain and rough roads. Control your car carefully, balance your speed, and climb as far as possible without flipping over. Collect coins, unlock new vehicles, and upgrade your ride to go even further. The farther you go, the harder it gets!
