## About

In this vehicle parking game, you will learn about different traffic signs like no parking signs, turn left sign, turn right, one way and many others that will also help you in the real life. The player should also avoid hitting obstacles and pass driving school test, by hitting an obstacle the game will be over and you have to restart the whole level again. Hit the finish line and unlock new exotic cars and new levels to enhance your car parking experience.
