## About

One descent down a freshly dealt mountain &mdash; gravity is the engine, and your air rotation is the only resource you cant take back. Follow the speed signs, land on the back of every hill, hop the boulders, ride the logs like rails. One wipeout ends the run: finish for a star, ring every cone for two, beat par time for three. How fast can you get the duck down?
