## About

Hit the road in Last Garage, a fast-paced 3D zombie survival driving game. Race through shifting districts&mdash;from city streets and suburbs to a forest highway&mdash;while dodging wrecks, fallen trees, barriers, and evacuation traffic. Fight walkers, armored brutes, and the towering Colossus with machine guns and rockets. Collect scrap, fuel, and ammo during each run, then use your haul to repair and upgrade your vehicle between runs. Choose from four vehicles and improve your armor, engine, weapons, and nitro.
