## About

Paint Drive IO is a fast-paced .io style arcade racer where you drive a car around an open arena, leaving a colored trail that claims territory as you go. Close your loop to paint the ground your color and grow your zone, while rival cars try to cut you off first. Race against opponents, dodge collisions, climb the leaderboard, and paint the biggest area to win! Web Dev h<a href='https://www.poki.com.es/'>https://www.poki.com.es/</a>
