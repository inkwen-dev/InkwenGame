## About

Nitro Car Racing is a simple yet exciting top down arcade racing game with a smooth game play. Enjoy driving a retro styled F1 nitro car and relive the fun and excitement of the arcade race game. Drive and collect all the power ups such as N for nitro boost, D to repair car damages and M for maximum speed. These power ups can be run over on the track as you drive. Avoid crashing into the side or corners. Surpass all the other cars to win and continue to unlock all the other track levels.
