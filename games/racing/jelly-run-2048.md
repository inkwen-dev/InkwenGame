## About

Jelly Run 2048 is a challenging casual puzzle game that combines the classic 2048 number merging with exciting parkour gameplay. Guide your jelly block by swiping left or right to connect and disconnect cubes. Merge cubes to get double the number, reaching 2048 or greater. When the jelly block collides with tiles of the same number, they merge into a larger number.
