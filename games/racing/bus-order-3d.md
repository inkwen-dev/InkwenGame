## About

Bus Order 3D is a matching puzzle game. There are three game modes to choose from. The first is to help passengers find their bus, sort them by color and send them on vacation! The second is to place coins of the same color in the space. The third is to turn the screw and remove the wood block from the board. Its cute design and variety of challenges make it a must-play for you. Come and challenge yourself and see if you can complete all levels!
