## About

In this simple game you will try to control the trafic lights to avoid crash between cars. You have to switch lights properly to manage the trafic. Feel like a traffic controler police officer standing in the middle of a dangerous crossroad. Try to finish all levels with 3 stars. Watch out for other cars and avoid crash and try to make the best score. Try to survive as long as you can in the multitude of levels
