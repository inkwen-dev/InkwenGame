## How to play

Use the arrow keys to ride and balance your bike. Press the left arrow to lean backward and the right arrow to lean forward. Use the up arrow to move forward and the down arrow to reverse when needed. Collect coins during your ride to unlock new and more powerful bikes as you progress!
