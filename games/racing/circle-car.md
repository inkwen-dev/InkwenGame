## How to play

Become the Ultimate Traffic Master!
Step into the high-stakes world of traffic control in Circle Traffic Car! Your main objective is to guide a queue of vehicles into a busy 3D circular intersection without causing a crash. Time your moves with precision to merge every car seamlessly into the moving traffic loop. Pay close attention to vehicle speeds, spacing, and tight timing gaps. Safely merge all queued cars onto the track to clear the level and advance to higher challenges!

How to Win:
- Analyze the Traffic Loop: Observe the speed and rhythm of vehicles already circling the roundabout.
- Time Your Move: Launch the next vehicle at the perfect moment to merge safely into an open gap.
- Avoid Crashes: A single collision will cause a traffic pile-up and fail the level!
- Clear the Queue: Successfully send every car in your queue onto the track to win the level.


Desktop Controls:
- Left Mouse Click / Spacebar / Up Arrow (↑) / W Key: Launch the next car into the traffic loop / Start or retry level.
- Mouse Drag (Click & Drag): Rotate and orbit the 3D camera around the intersection for the best angle.
- Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out of the track.

Mobile Controls:
- Tap Screen: Tap anywhere on the screen to launch the next car / Start or retry level.
- One-Finger Drag / Swipe: Drag across the screen to rotate and orbit the 3D camera view.
- Pinch (Two Fingers): Pinch in or out to adjust camera zoom.

# Traffic Car Circle

Traffic Car Circle is a fast-paced traffic management puzzle game that puts your timing, focus, and decision-making skills to the test. Your mission is simple: guide a continuous stream of cars safely into a busy roundabout without causing collisions. While the controls are easy to learn, mastering the traffic becomes increasingly challenging as more vehicles enter the road.

Tap at the perfect moment to send each car into the circle, carefully watching the flow of traffic. Every successful move keeps the vehicles moving smoothly, but one mistimed tap can create a chain reaction of crashes. Stay alert, react quickly, and keep the roads clear as the pace continues to increase.

Each level introduces faster traffic and greater challenges that require precise timing and smart planning. Race against the clock, improve your high score, and discover how long you can keep the traffic flowing without an accident. Whether you're looking for a quick casual game or a satisfying skill challenge, Traffic Car Circle delivers addictive gameplay that is easy to pick up and difficult to master.
