## How to play

💻 Desktop (Keyboard):

W / Up Arrow: Accelerate / Gas

S / Down Arrow: Reverse / Brake

A / Left Arrow: Tilt truck backwards (balance in mid-air / pull wheelies)

D / Right Arrow: Tilt truck forwards (balance in mid-air / stick nose landings)

Spacebar / Shift: Activate Nitro Boost 🚀

📱 Mobile / Touchscreen:

Tap the on-screen Pedals to accelerate and brake.

Use the Left / Right Tilt Buttons while airborne to balance the chassis and land cleanly on all four wheels.

Tap the Nitro Icon for an explosive speed burst.
