## How to play

Keyboard:
WASD / Arrow Keys = Movement
Space = Handbrake
N = Nitro / Boost
B = Start / Stop Engine
Q / E = Left / Right Turn Signal
H = Toggle Headlights
G = Hazard Lights (both blinkers)
V = Change Camera View
R = Reset Car
F = Car Fix
Shift = Shift Up
Ctrl = Shift Down
Mouse = Camera Movement
Gamepad:
Left Stick = Steer
RT / R2 = Accelerate
LT / L2 = Brake
A / X = Nitro / Boost
X / Square = Start / Stop Engine
Y / Triangle = Reset Car
B / Circle = Handbrake
D-Pad ← / → = Left / Right Turn Signals
D-Pad ↑ = Toggle Headlights
D-Pad ↓ = Hazard Lights (both blinkers)
RB / R1 = Shift Up
LB / L1 = Shift Down
Right Stick = Camera Movement
R3 (Stick Press) = Change Camera View

## About

Crash Skill Racing is a high-speed street racing game that throws you onto the busy highways of Japan. Weave through dense traffic, perform daring stunts, and push your car to its limits as you chase speed, style, and mastery behind the wheel.

Feel the adrenaline of threading through traffic at incredible speeds, where every second demands precision. Upgrade your vehicle, unlock new cars, and take on challenging skill-based events as you progress through an action-packed stunt journey.

With intense highway driving, deep vehicle progression, and a thrilling racing atmosphere, Crash Skill Racing delivers nonstop excitement from your first run to your fastest record.

Features:
High-speed racing on Japanese highways

Fast-paced gameplay focused on precision and speed
