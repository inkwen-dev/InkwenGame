## About

Spooky Racing is a Halloween kart race in full 3D! Pick one of 8 classic monsters like Dracula, the Mummy, the Witch or Jack Pumpkin, each with a kart of their own. Race through pumpkin patches, haunted swamps, a neon ghost town and a vampire castle. Grab item boxes, throw slime potions and homing ghosts, drift to charge blue, orange and purple boosts, and win gold trophies in the Grand Prix from 50cc to Mirror. Gamepad supported!
