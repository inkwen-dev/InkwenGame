## About

Quite a lot of young people attend various car clubs where they learn to ride such vehicles as go-karts. In the game Karting, you too can get behind the wheel of this car and drive along the ring road. Your car will gradually speed up. The road will have several sharp turns, which your car will have to go through without slowing down. For this moment you will need to click on the screen with the mouse. Then your car will make a maneuver on the road and fit into the turn.Heave fun!
