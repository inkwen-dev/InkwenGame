## How to play

Drive carefully and keep your balance while climbing hills, landing jumps, and crossing dangerous track sections.
W / S — accelerate / brake
A / D — tilt forward / backward
N — nitro
P — pause

Use tilt controls to land more smoothly after jumps, avoid flipping over on steep terrain, and stay stable on rough sections. Nitro helps you gain speed quickly, but using it at the right moment is key. The better your control, the farther you can drive and the more coins you can collect.

## About

A bright arcade racing game with 3D physics where speed alone is not enough. Keep your balance, control your landings, and overcome dangerous sections filled with hills, ramps, bridges, steep drops, and tricky obstacles. Perform stunts, collect coins, and unlock new opportunities as you progress.
The game features different characters, vehicles, and maps with unique atmospheres: green hills, desert roads, snowy tracks, and night routes. Earn coins to upgrade your engine, suspension, grip, and fuel tank, and buy useful bonuses to handle harder challenges.
The farther you go, the faster and more dangerous the ride becomes. Master the vehicle physics, improve your timing, and beat your own distance records in every run.

这是一款画面明亮的街机竞速游戏，采用3D物理引擎，速度固然重要，但绝非取胜之道。你需要保持平衡，精准控制落地，克服遍布山丘、坡道、桥梁、陡坡和各种棘手障碍的危险路段。随着游戏的进行，你可以表演特技、收集金币，解锁更多精彩内容。
