## How to play

Controls:
WASD - steer the car
Space - brake hard
Shift - enable nitro
C - switch camera
N - change car
R - put car back in service
K - repair car
B - slow down time
Tab - open pause

On mobile: use the buttons on the screen.
Goal: drive, crash, tune - do whatever you want!
