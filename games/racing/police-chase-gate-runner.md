## How to play

Outrun the police for as long as you can. Drive through BLUE gates to gain speed and avoid RED ones - if your speed drops too low, the police catch you. Use nitro to break away, and spend your cash on upgrades between runs.

Desktop: A / D or Left / Right arrows to change lane, Space for nitro, Esc to pause. In mouse mode: point at a lane to move there, click for nitro.
Mobile: tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane, tap the bolt button for nitro.

## About

Hit the road and push your limits in a high-speed pursuit where every second counts. Accelerate through power gates to build speed, gain temporary boosts, and create the momentum you need to stay ahead. Weave through traffic, avoid crashes, and react quickly as the road becomes more dangerous the longer you survive. The police are always closing in, arriving in increasingly aggressive waves with faster vehicles, tighter pursuit tactics, and less room for error. Each new wave raises the pressure, forcing you to take go faster, make sharper decisions, and squeeze every bit of performance out of your vehicle.

Every run gives you a chance to come back stronger. Earn rewards, upgrade your vehicle between attempts, and unlock permanent upgrades. Build your ideal setup, chase longer runs, beat your previous records, and see how far you can make it before the pursuit finally catches up.

尽可能长时间地摆脱警察的追捕。穿过蓝色大门加速，避开红色大门——如果速度过低，警察就会抓住你。使用氮气加速摆脱追捕，并在两次追逐之间用现金升级装备。

电脑端：A/D 或左右方向键切换车道，空格键使用氮气加速，Esc 键暂停。鼠标模式下：指向车道即可移动到该车道，点击即可使用氮气加速。

移动端：点击屏幕左侧或右侧切换车道，点击箭头按钮使用氮气加速。
