## About

When winter comes the yard is constantly snowing, which sleeps the whole area. Because of this, the roads come to a dangerous state and quite often various accidents occur on them. Today in the game Snow Plow Jeep Driving you will work with the municipal service of a small town located in a mountainous area. Your duties include cleaning the roads from snow.
