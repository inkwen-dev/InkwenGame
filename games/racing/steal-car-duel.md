## About

Step into a high-energy world of engines, rivalry, and fast reflexes. Steal Car Duel is a competitive action game that evolves the idea of Steal Brainrot Duel into a car-based experience filled with duels, rare collectibles, and endless upgrades! Race, collide, and steal your way to the top. Build your own garage, earn coins and tickets, and outsmart rivals to prove who rules the arena. Each match combines skill, timing, and strategy as you fight for dominance in Solo or 2 Player mode.

Core Features:
40+ unlockable cars across 8 rarity tiers.
Real-time stealing, dueling, and defense mechanics.
Functional Weapons Shop and Market systems.
Cosmetic customization with outfits, wings, and pets.
Solo and local 2 Player support.
Progressive income and rarity-based rewards.

Game Modes: 
Solo Mode: Hunt for rare cars, raid rival garages, and defend your own collection.
2 Player Local Mode: Face a friend on the same device, steal their cars, block their progress, and see who builds the best garage.

Rarity System:
Standard – Basic models, perfect for starting your journey...
Custom – Personalized versions with balanced performance and earnings!
Street – Sportier cars built for strong garage.
Track – Designed for tighter handling and speed for extra income.
Premium – Exclusive visuals with solid performance.
Hyper – Advanced power and huge income for your garage.
Prototype – Experimental vehicles with rare features and extra huge income!
Legend – Ultra-rare vehicles including the F117 jet, Formula car, and skull-themed retro car. These are hard to obtain but possible through persistence and long-term play!

### 介绍

踏入一个充满引擎轰鸣、竞争激烈、反应敏捷的高速世界。《偷车决斗》是一款竞技动作游戏，它将《偷车决斗》的理念升级为以汽车为主题的体验，充满了决斗、稀有收藏品和无尽的升级！竞速、碰撞、偷车，一路攀升至巅峰。打造你的专属车库，赢取金币和门票，智胜对手，证明谁才是竞技场的王者。每场比赛都融合了技巧、时机和策略，让你在单人或双人模式下争夺霸主地位。

核心特色：

超过 40 辆可解锁车辆，分为 8 个稀有度等级。

实时偷车、决斗和防御机制。

功能完善的武器商店和市场系统。

丰富的外观定制选项，包括服装、翅膀和宠物。

支持单人和本地双人游戏。

基于收入和稀有度的奖励机制。

游戏模式：

单人模式：寻找稀有车辆，突袭对手车库，捍卫你的收藏。

双人本地模式：与好友在同一设备上对战，抢夺他们的车辆，阻碍他们的游戏进度，看看谁能打造最棒的车库。

稀有度系统：

标准版 – 基础车型，完美开启你的游戏之旅……

定制版 – 个性化版本，性能与收益均衡！

街头版 – 更具运动感的车型，打造强大的车库。

赛道版 – 专为更精准的操控和更快的速度而设计，带来额外收益。

高级版 – 专属视觉效果，性能卓越。

超级版 – 动力强劲，为你的车库带来巨额收益。

原型版 – 拥有稀有特性的实验性车辆，带来超高收益！

传奇版 – 极其稀有的车辆，包括 F117 喷气式战斗机、方程式赛车和骷髅主题复古车。这些车辆难以获得，但只要坚持不懈，长期游玩，就有机会获得！
