🎯 Objective: Overcome obstacles and outrun your opponent to become the champion! Control the ragdoll stickman and avoid traps to reach the finish line first!

🕹️ Controls:

On PC: Use the left mouse button (LMB) to start running. Move the mouse left and right to steer the character and avoid obstacles.

On mobile devices: Tap the screen to run and swipe left or right to control the stickman and dodge traps.
