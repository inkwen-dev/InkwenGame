## How to play

W / Up Arrow: Accelerate

S / Down Arrow: Brake

A / D or Left / Right Arrows: Steer

Space / Shift: Nitro in race and drift modes; shift gears in drag mode

N: Nitro

Esc / P: Pause

Touch controls are available on mobile. Play in landscape mode for the best experience.

## About

Hit the road in Nitro Racing Gear, a high-speed arcade racing game by BREYMANTECH! Take on 40 stages across eight worlds, from coastal highways and neon cities to snowy mountains and volcanic roads.
Compete in races, sprints, drift challenges, and drag duels. Unlock cars, upgrade their performance in the garage, and unleash nitro boosts to chase victory. Enjoy realistic 3D-rendered artwork, distinctive tracks, and an energetic soundtrack as you work toward the final championship.
