## About

Welcome to Haunted Heroes! Experience the combination of ghost parkour and evolution in this colorful platformer. Take on the role of a ghost and traverse a ghostly platform full of obstacles. For example, big pendulum, cutting machine, wall and so on. You can choose from a wide variety of superheroes and go through this hero door.
