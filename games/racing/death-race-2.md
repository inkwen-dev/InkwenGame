## About

The road is dangerous, can you survive? This is an action shooter game where you get to shoot down the enemy's air and ground attacks. Survive as long as you can and earn points. Spend those points to upgrade and make additions to your convoy until you can survive to the end of the city. Unlock new cars as you last longer in battle. Get ready to take on the Battle On Road.
