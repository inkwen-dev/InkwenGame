## How to play

Main Objectives & How to Win
Collect the Wealth: Drive along the curving walls of the tunnel to collect as many glowing gold coins as possible.

Survive the Obstacles: Avoid or maneuver around hazardous blockades, including giant red spinning blades, rocks, and barricades.

Reach the Light: Successfully navigate the twists and turns of the loop to reach the blinding light exit at the end of the tunnel to complete the level.

Primary Controls
* A: Steer Left
* D: Steer Right

## About

Car Runner is an exhilarating low-poly arcade racing game with puzzle-platformer elements set in surreal, gravity-defying environments. Players navigate a compact delivery van through twisted, winding pipes and looping checkerboard tunnels, dodging hazardous obstacles like spinning giant red windmills, wooden barriers, and scattered boulders. The core unique selling point is the combination of momentum-based tunnel racing with high-stakes physics, where mastering the curves of the track is just as important as maintaining high speed to scoop up valuable gold coins.

### 介绍

《Car Runner》是一款令人兴奋的低多边形街机竞速游戏，融合了益智平台跳跃元素，背景设定在超现实、反重力的环境中。玩家驾驶一辆小型货车穿梭于蜿蜒曲折的管道和棋盘格隧道，躲避旋转的巨型红色风车、木制路障和散落的巨石等危险障碍。游戏的核心卖点在于将基于动量的隧道竞速与高风险的物理引擎相结合，玩家不仅需要保持高速以收集珍贵的金币，还需要掌握赛道的弯道技巧。
