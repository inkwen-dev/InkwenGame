## How to play

Control the car with on-screen buttons or the keyboard. Press A or the left arrow to move left. Press D or the right arrow to move right. Use the up and down arrows to accelerate and brake when available in the current mode.

Dodge traffic, avoid crashes, collect coins, and try to drive as far as possible. After each run, upgrade your car and unlock new vehicles.

## About

A single-player arcade racing game where you drive along a busy road, overtake cars, and try to survive as long as possible. The farther you go and the riskier your maneuvers are, the higher your final score becomes.

Collect coins, unlock new cars, and upgrade speed, handling, and other stats. Choose a mode, watch the road carefully, and set new records in each run.
