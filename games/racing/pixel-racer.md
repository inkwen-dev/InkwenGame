## About

Get in a nostalgic journey with Pixel Racer, a thrilling 2D pixel art racing game where you take control of a vibrant red car, weaving through a bustling highway filled with unpredictable traffic. Your objective is simple yet challenging: dodge all the other vehicles and slippery oil spills that come your way. Precision and quick reflexes are your best allies as you strive to survive for as long as possible. The longer you last, the higher your score climbs. Relive the golden age of arcade gaming with Pixel Racer and see how long you can keep your car on the road!
