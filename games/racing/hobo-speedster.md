## About

Hobo Speedster is an exciting motorcycle platform BMX game featuring 2D cartoon graphics. Your mission? Keep the motorcycle balanced until you reach the finish line. As you earn coins and diamonds, upgrade your current bike at the motorcycle settlement, purchase 3 more motorcycles from the Shop, and unlock 4 additional riders in the Decorate section. Ready for a personal challenge? Explore various maps and aim to complete all 7 tasks for extra rewards!
