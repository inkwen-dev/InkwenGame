## About

Construct routes and transport the taxi to the destination platform! This one-touch casual game is easy, addictive, and will keep you occupied for several hours. There are over 100 levels of addictive gameplay to enjoy! Then tap and hold your screen to construct bridges for the cab to travel on. The importance of tap timing cannot be overstated. Characteristics -More than 100 addictive levels to go through -Colours that are beautiful and relaxing -Interesting and addicting gameplay -Compete with your pals to see who can get the furthest in the most time!
