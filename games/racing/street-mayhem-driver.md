## About

Street Mayhem Driver is a fast-paced arcade driving survival game where chaos rules the streets. Choose your difficulty and race through endless neon-lit city roads filled with traffic, hazards and relentless police chases. The longer you survive, the faster and more dangerous the streets become. One mistake ends the run. No story, no limits &mdash; just pure speed, reflex and nonstop action designed for quick, addictive gameplay sessions.
