## About

Get ready to rev up your engines and flex your mental muscles in the thrilling online game, Kart Stroop Challenge! This unique racing game puts a cognitive twist on traditional kart racing, combining high-speed action with brain-bending challenges.In Kart Stroop Challenge, players navigate colorful tracks in customizable karts while facing the famous Stroop effect at every turn. As you speed through vibrant courses, youll encounter words displayed in various colors. Your task? Respond correctly to either the words meaning or its color, depending on the current rule.
