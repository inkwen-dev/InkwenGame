STUNSTEVAL SIDEROLLERS is an arcade race with a side view. Try to outrun a helicopter, a biplane or a fighter jet. Drive in the rain or on a dark highway.

## How to play

PC control:
D - forward
Double-tap D to lower the gear
A - back
W - gear up
S - gear low
The control on the phone is carried out using the arrows.
