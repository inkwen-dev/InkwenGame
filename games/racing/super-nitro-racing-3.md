## About

Super Nitro Racing 3 is a top down racer with 21 tracks and very little room to relax. The car accelerates on its own, so your job is steering, braking and finding the right line. Charge the nitro bar to full and it fires in one long burst, then you have to earn it again. Rain, snow and fog change how much grip you get, and the icy sections will spit you into the barriers if you get greedy. Damage never repairs by itself, grab the D pods on track to fix the car. Five modes to play: Time Attack, Race against three rivals, Ghost, Survival and Championship.

### 介绍

《超级氮气竞速3》是一款俯视视角的赛车游戏，拥有21条赛道，几乎没有放松的空间。车辆会自动加速，因此你的任务是控制方向、刹车并找到最佳路线。将氮气条充至满格后，它会一次性爆发，之后你必须重新积累。雨、雪和雾会影响抓地力，如果过于贪心，在结冰路段可能会被甩向护栏。车辆损伤不会自动修复，需在赛道上拾取D方块来修理汽车。五种游戏模式可供选择：计时挑战、与三位对手竞速、幽灵模式、生存模式和锦标赛。
