## About

According to the plot, a bunch of bandits attacked a laboratory for the production of biological weapons in order to steal a new development. Your task is to prevent them from doing this. To do this, you can use both Spider-Man himself and his abilities, as well as traps that can be placed in several places at once. In addition to ordinary bandits, you will have to defeat all their leaders.
