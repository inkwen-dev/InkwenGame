## How to play

Use the arrow keys on your keyboard to move press Ctrl to stop and Shift to switch modules On mobile swipe to move tap once to stop and double-tap to switch modules

## About

Embark on an exciting journey with Foxy! Our cute character rushes to the aid of his friends who are waiting for help in different climate conditions with his powerful tractor. He saves penguins by breaking ice in the poles, provides water to thirsty foxes by collecting cacti in the deserts, and collects carrots for hungry rabbits by breaking rocks in the forests. A different adventure, different tasks and colorful environments await you in each section. Choose the right module, steer your tractor and successfully complete Foxys tasks!
