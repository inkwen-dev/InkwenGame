## About

A Duo Race is beginning on the Flying Way by controlling casual-style cars in two player game mode! The main goal at this race is to get to the longest distance with your car. During your journey, you can collect the diamonds on your way. The diamonds will let you buy new cars on the shop menu. Jump over the ramps, fly as long as you can, and avoid obstacles on your way. Let the race begin!
