## About

Hill Climber is a simple yet challenging physics-based casual driving game where the goal is to drive as far as possible without having an accident or running out of fuel! Controls are minimal, but finding the right balance between braking and accelerating is crucial, especially when navigating hills. Buckle up and get ready for the (hopefully not) bumpy ride.
