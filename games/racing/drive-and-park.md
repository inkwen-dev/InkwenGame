## About

Drive and Park - A game that tests your parking skills, for a change from the usual driving skill-testing games. This game lets you earn points every time you’re able to smoothly park your car. Every time you park your car without any subsequent crash, you earn brownie points which you can use further in-game. You can redeem these coins for various updates and enhancements as you level up in the game.
