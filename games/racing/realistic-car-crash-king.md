## About

Realistic Car Crash King is an extreme crash-test simulator where every collision, rollover, and high-speed impact turns into spectacular destruction. Test different vehicles, accelerate to insane speeds, smash into obstacles, jump from massive ramps, and watch each car deform realistically on impact.

Choose from sports cars, SUVs, trucks, off-road vehicles, and more, then explore a variety of maps including mountain roads, mega ramps, crash-test arenas, obstacle tracks, and open-world sandbox locations. Drop cars from cliffs, crash into traffic, destroy AI vehicles, and find out how much damage each machine can survive.

With realistic physics, detachable parts, dynamic damage, and real-time metal deformation, every crash feels unique and satisfying. Create your own destruction scenarios, perform massive crash tests, and enjoy total freedom to wreck different cars across diverse maps.
