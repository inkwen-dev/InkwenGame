## How to play

Controls:
TAB - pause menu (or Escape)

Driving - WASD.
Spacebar - handbrake.
Shift - nitro.
C - change camera.
R - reset car.
K - restore car.
N - switch car.
B - slow motion.

For mobile devices, use the game interface.
