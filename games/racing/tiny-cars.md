## How to play

Controls
Acceleration: W/S or the Up/Down arrow keys
Steering: A/D or Left/Right arrow keys
Restart: R or Backspace
Hide ghost: E

How to Play
Avoid obstacles and collect all checkpoints to finish the track.

## About

Tiny Cars is a fast, low-poly racing game built around speed, precision, and perfect timing. Drift through tight turns, launch over jumps, and chase perfect runs across miniature tracks built from the everyday objects of home. Race the clock, learn from ghost replays, climb the leaderboards, and push your time lower with every attempt. With 25 official tracks to master, the road to perfection is always just one more run away.
