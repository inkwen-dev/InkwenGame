## About

Using a forklift, your objective in this new and entertaining Real City Forklift Style game is to relocate illegally parked automobiles to their proper lots. Please use caution! Forklift the automobiles carefully you can adjust your camera angle to get better look, then transport them to a designated drop off area all across town.
