## About

Blacktop Police Chase is a low-poly 3D getaway driving game set in a big open city with alleys, docks, parks, bridges and a monorail. You are the wheelman. Pick up the bank robber, shake the cops and get him back to the hideout before your car falls apart. Just follow the green arrow. The police escalate through six levels of pursuit, so the longer you stay out, the harder they hit - and your car takes damage with every crash. Grab coins along the way and spend them in the garage: 12 cars to unlock, each with its own speed, grip, weight and health stats, plus 12 paint colors.
