## About

My Twin Dolphin Baby Care is a caring game. In this game, you will be a keeper in the oceanarium to care for two cute little dolphins. As a keeper, you not only have to play with the little dolphins, but you also have to feed them, help them bathe, and treat their illnesses. Not only can you take care of the little dolphins emotions, but you can also heal your own heart through this heartwarming game. This game also has smooth animations and bright colors, which are very suitable for little girls to play. Come and try it!
