# Bouncy Motors

<a href="https://inkwengame.com/games/bouncy-motors" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Play on InkwenGame</a>

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/bouncy-motors/cover.jpg)

Driving a car that bounces like jelly, you need to get to the finish line. Beware of red zones - wheels fall off on impact! Also be careful when driving on ice!

## How to play

Drive forward: W or ↑ or left stick (mobile)
Drive backward: S or ↓ or left stick (mobile)

Rotate counterclockwise: A or ← or right stick (on mobile)
Rotate clockwise: D or → or right stick (mobile)
