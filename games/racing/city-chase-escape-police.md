## How to play

Dodge the police cars and survive as long as you can to achieve the highest score. Collect coins to fill the garage with cars.

Mobile Controls:
Hold Left Side of Screen: Steer left.
Hold Right Side of Screen: Steer right.
Hold Both Sides: Reverse.
Release: Straighten up — the car keeps accelerating on its own.
Button Mode: Switch to on-screen arrows with GO and REVERSE pedals at any time; your choice is saved.
Camera Button: Tap to drop the view in behind the car.
Reset Button: Tap to free your car if it gets stuck.

Desktop Controls:
← (Left Arrow) or A: Steer left.
→ (Right Arrow) or D: Steer right.
↓ (Down Arrow) or S: Reverse.
↑ (Up Arrow), W or Spacebar: Accelerate (in button mode — by default the car accelerates on its own).
Mouse: Hold the left button on the left or right side of the screen to steer that way.
R: Reset your car if it gets stuck, without ending the run.
Esc or the on-screen pause button: Pause.

### 怎么玩

躲避警车，尽可能长时间地生存下去，以获得最高分。收集金币，把车库填满。

手机操控：

按住屏幕左侧：向左转向。

按住屏幕右侧：向右转向。

同时按住屏幕两侧：倒车。

松开：恢复车辆直线行驶——车辆会自动加速。

按键模式：随时切换到屏幕方向键，配合前进和倒车踏板；您的选择将被保存。

视角按钮：点击可将视角切换到车后。

重置按钮：如果车辆卡住，点击可将其释放。

电脑操控：

←（左箭头）或 A：向左转向。

→（右箭头）或 D：向右转向。

↓（下箭头）或 S：倒车。

↑（上箭头）、W 或空格键：加速（在按键模式下——默认情况下车辆会自动加速）。

鼠标：按住屏幕左侧或右侧的鼠标左键即可向该方向转向。

R：如果车辆卡住，可重置车辆，而不会结束游戏。

Esc 键或屏幕上的暂停按钮：暂停游戏。
