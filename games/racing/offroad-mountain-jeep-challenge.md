## How to play

Desktop W or Up Arrow to accelerate S or Down Arrow to brake or reverse A and D or Left and Right Arrow to steer Space for handbrake Mobile use the on-screen driving buttons

## About

Drive a powerful offroad jeep across steep mountain roads, narrow bridges, impossible tracks, and dangerous obstacles. Complete 15 challenging levels, keep your vehicle balanced, avoid falling from the course, and reach each finish point. Choose your route carefully and master acceleration, braking, and steering on rough terrain. The game supports desktop and mobile play.
