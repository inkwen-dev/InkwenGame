## About

In this intense game, navigate a car through a zombie-infested apocalypse, skillfully avoiding the undead on treacherous roads. Your survival hinges on adeptly maneuvering up and down challenging terrains while collecting vital items: time-extensions, speed-reducing potions, and life-replenishing hearts. Each element is crucial for endurance and scoring, blending strategy with quick reflexes. Mastering the art of evasion and collection is key to thriving in this post-apocalyptic world.
