To start paving the way click on the car and lead the way to the parking lot of the same color and let go as soon as you pave the way and the direction of movement will correspond to the direction of parking

## About

Funny Cars Route is an exciting game where your task is to draw a path for cars so that they can park in places. The game has various levels, from easy to very difficult, which require you to be smart and draw, and you will need to park several different cars at the same time. When passing the levels, the paths will become more complicated, so you will have to put more effort and creativity to make all the cars pass.
