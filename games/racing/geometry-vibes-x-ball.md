## How to play

The aim of the game is to reach the portal at the end without hitting anything.
Click "MOUSE LEFT-CLICK" or "SPACE" to accelerate upwards. Release the button to go down.
In 2,3,4 Player Modes, other players are controlled with "H", "L" and "UP ARROW KEY".
You can use touch controls on mobile devices such as tablets and phones.

Classic Mode: Try to reach the portals at the end of a total of 10 levels that get harder as the levels pass. The last 3 levels are quite challenging!
Endless Mode: Try to go as far as you can without hitting obstacles and traps.

Race Mode: You can compete with your friends locally by playing levels with 2-3-4 player.

Is Geometry Vibes X-Ball a Difficult Game?
Geometry Vibes X-Ball is definitely not a difficult game and you can complete 10 levels in the classic section if you practice enough.


Can Geometry Vibes X-Ball be played on mobile devices?
