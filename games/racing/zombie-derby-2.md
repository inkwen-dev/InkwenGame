## How to play

Accelerate - W/Arrow up
Shoot gun - Spacebar
Use nitro - Left Shift/N
Tilt forward/backward - Left/Right arrow or A/D

## About

Zombie Derby 2 is a fast and fun driving game with zombies everywhere. Get in your car, run over the undead, and try to survive as long as you can. Use your fuel wisely - if you run out, the zombies will catch you. Collect upgrades, unlock new cars, and use nitro boosts to jump over hills and smash through big groups of zombies.
