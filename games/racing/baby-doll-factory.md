## About

Baby Doll Factory is another collecting-running arcade game. With 3D cute dolls, you can make baby dolls on the conveyor in this factory. About the whole production process, you can collect more basic dolls,shape them, choose their haircuts, decorate them with clothes, and put them into the boxes. The more dolls you make, the more money you will claim. I hope you can unlock your favorite skins.
