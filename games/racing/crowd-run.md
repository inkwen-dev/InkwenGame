## About

You are ready to start this fun and addictive puzzle game called Crowd Run 3D. Here you will need to control a large crowd of playable characters on an obstacle course. It will contain a huge variety of different traps and holes. You have to be very careful not to lose characters. In the course of this race, you will not only lose allies, but also replenish them. The blue portals will help you with this, they tend to increase your army or add a certain number of people.
