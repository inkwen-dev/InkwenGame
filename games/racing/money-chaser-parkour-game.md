## About

Chasing/Running endlessly for collecting coins(Money). Try to avoid traps &amp; *kick out all the enemy/competitors on your way. *You can kick out enemy infront of you with slide skill* After you have enough money, you can challenge the Boss fight. The amount of coins(Money Bullets) is the only weapon to defeat Boss, the more the better. Remember, no money no life...just keep running :)
