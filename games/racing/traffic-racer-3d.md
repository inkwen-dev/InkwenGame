## How to play

Drag left or right to steer your car between lanes. Brush past oncoming traffic without crashing to trigger near-miss bonuses and build your combo for extra coins. The longer you survive, the higher your score and the bigger your reward at the end of the run. Spend coins in the garage to unlock faster cars and level up your stats. Complete challenge levels to earn stars, then try the endurance mode to see how long you can last. Crash and you're back to the menu — but tap "Watch Ad · 2× Coins" on the results screen to double your reward and gear up faster. Quick reflexes win the race

## About

Traffic Racer 3D drops you behind the wheel of a high-speed sports car on an endless highway packed with traffic. Weave through cars at full throttle, rack up near-miss bonuses, and chase the longest distance you can survive. Earn coins on every run to unlock a garage of new vehicles — from agile hatchbacks to heavyweight muscle cars — and upgrade your fuel, top speed, and armour to push deeper into the chaos. With multiple challenge levels, an endurance mode, and a star-rating system that rewards skilful driving, every run pushes you to beat your best.
