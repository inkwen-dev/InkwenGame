## About

The Aquapark adventure is beginning with the rolling balls this time. Pass through the math gates and increase or decrease the ball number inside your group. Fly between the ramps and try to get your ball group to the finish pool before your opponents. Join this race in 2 player mode and double your fun by competing against a friend. Dont forget to visit the shop from time to time. New ball skins will be waiting for you. Let the roll begin!
