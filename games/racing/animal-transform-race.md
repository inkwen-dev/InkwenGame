## How to play

You can use "MOUSE" or touch controls to change animals. For 2 player mode, you can use "A,S,D" and "J,K,L" keys for shortcuts to change animals.

## About

Animal Transform Race is a fun game based on reaction where different types of animals compete. Each animal is fast in some areas and very slow in some tracks according to their abilities. Try to lead the race by making the right choices. The faster you make animal choices, the more advantages you gain. You can unlock new animals from the market section with the coins you earn from the races.
