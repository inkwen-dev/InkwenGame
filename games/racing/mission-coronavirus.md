## How to play

You can direct the ambulance with the arrow keys or W A S D You can change the camera angles with the C key You can turn the lights on and off with the L button

## About

Are you ready to fight the Corona virus that emerged in Wuhan, China? As an ambulance driver, you need to take the patients to the hospital before they die. The virus spreads rapidly in the human body. You need to find and bring the patient to the hospital without blocking the airways. In Mission Coronavirus, an action-packed ambulance driving game, a unique city and flowing traffic await you. Advanced graphics, realistic physics and fluid gaming pleasure await you in Mission Coronavirus. Come on behind the wheel to fight Coronavirus! Stop the virus from spreading in Mission Coronavirus with +50 different missions.
