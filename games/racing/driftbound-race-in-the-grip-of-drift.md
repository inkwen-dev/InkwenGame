## How to play

Your car accelerates automatically. Stay on the road, avoid crashes, collect cash, and master every turn.
In Solo mode, travel as far as possible, complete increasingly difficult levels, and beat your distance record. In Rivals mode, overtake the AI drivers and cross the finish line first.

Hook Controls:
Hold the hook control near a turn to connect to the glowing magnet.
Keep holding to swing the car around the corner.
Release at the right moment to continue along the road.

Steering Controls:
Turn left or right while keeping the car on the track.
Choose between an on-screen steering wheel and directional controls in Settings.

Desktop Controls:
Spacebar or hold the mouse button: Activate the magnetic hook.
Release Spacebar or the mouse button: Release the hook.
Left Arrow or A: Steer left.
Right Arrow or D: Steer right.
Mouse drag: Turn the on-screen steering wheel.
Esc: Pause the game.

Mobile Controls:
Touch and hold the screen: Activate the magnetic hook.
Release your finger: Release the hook.
Drag the on-screen wheel: Steer the car.
Directional mode: Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer.

## About

Driftbound: Race in the Grip of Drift! is a fast-paced arcade racing game built around a unique magnetic drifting mechanic. Attach your hook to magnets near the road, swing through sharp turns, and release at the perfect moment to stay on the track. Race through procedurally generated environments filled with bridges, intersections, changing weather, and challenging turns. Set new distance records in Solo mode or compete against AI rivals, collect cash, upgrade your car, and unlock new vehicles and control modes.

### 怎么玩

你的车会自动加速。保持在道路上，避免碰撞，收集金币，并熟练掌握每一个弯道。

在单人模式中，尽可能行驶更远的距离，完成难度递增的关卡，并打破你的行驶距离记录。在对手模式中，超越AI车手，率先冲过终点线。

钩爪控制：

在弯道附近按住钩爪控制键，即可与发光的磁铁连接。

持续按住，即可让车辆转弯。

在合适的时机松开，即可继续沿道路行驶。

方向盘控制：

在保持车辆行驶在赛道上的同时，向左或向右转弯。

在设置中选择使用屏幕方向盘或方向控制。

桌面控制：

空格键或按住鼠标按钮：激活磁力钩爪。

松开空格键或鼠标按钮：释放钩爪。

左箭头或A键：向左转弯。

右箭头或D键：向右转弯。

拖动鼠标：转动屏幕方向盘。

Esc键：暂停游戏。

手机操控：

触摸并按住屏幕：激活磁吸功能。

松开手指：释放磁吸功能。

拖动屏幕上的方向盘：控制车辆转向。

方向模式：按住屏幕左侧或右侧进行转向。
