## How to play

Up or W Down or S - Drive forward backward Left or A Right or D - Tilt left right P - pause

## About

Overcome all the obstacles as fast as you can. This game is a sequel to Moto Maniac set in a different environment. This time it's a bridge in the night. It's under construction so you have to be careful not to fall into the abyss and not to hit your head. On top of the obstacles on the way, it's dark! Fortunately you have a drone that will light your way.
