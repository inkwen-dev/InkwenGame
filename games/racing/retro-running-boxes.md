## About

Retro Running Boxes features an exciting one-player mode and a unique two-player mode where two players can play simultaneously on one device. This adds a competitive edge and makes it perfect for playing with friends. In Retro Running Boxes, your goal is simple: run as far as possible while jumping over and avoiding dangers along the way. The game captures the essence of classic arcade games with its super retro feel, specially made for arcade lovers around the world. Enjoy the vibrant 8-bit graphics and nostalgic sound effects that will take you back to the golden age of gaming.
