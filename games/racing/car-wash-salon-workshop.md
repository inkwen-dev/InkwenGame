## About

If you love cleaning then Kids Car wash Service Garage is for you! Have fun cleaning and fixing dirty old cars. Make them look all clean and new by washing the dirty cars in your workshop garage. With this car wash service game you can make your children learn how to clean and wash your car in a fun way! They would love to help wash your dirty car in real life too! Repairing and washing a dirty car in your own wash salon workshop garage will make you feel satisfied. You can choose any vehicle you like and fix it in a fun way! Kids car wash service is a great car washing game. Being a car mechanic in your own auto workshop will make you feel happy and satisfied. You will be doing all the work but will not get tired. You will be having tons of fun, isn’t it great? This will soon become your favorite kids car washing game. You will be able to choose a car out of three unlocked cars. Then you will have to wash the dirty car in your auto workshop garage. You can wash it with a water pipe and then scrub it with soap to get rid of all the stains on your dirty car. Fix and clean your tyres by scrubbing them filling air into them by an air pump! Also, you can fill your oil tanks in your workshop service station. After you are done with washing and fixing your dirty car, you can take it for a test drive. Have fun in this great car wash driving. You can then unlock other cars too, after completing your tasks!
