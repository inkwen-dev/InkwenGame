## How to play

Use W / Up Arrow to accelerate and S / Down Arrow to brake. Steer with A / D or the Left / Right Arrow keys.
Use ERS/Nitro on straight sections or when overtaking rivals for a temporary speed boost. Avoid leaving the track because grass and off-road areas will slow your car down significantly.
Complete every lap as quickly as possible, overtake the AI racers, and cross the finish line in the best position you can. Earn credits from races and use them in the Garage to improve your car for tougher events.
On mobile, use the on-screen steering, accelerator, brake, and boost controls.

## About

Formula Apex Rush is a high-speed open-wheel racing game where every second counts. Compete against aggressive AI rivals across a variety of circuits, including technical Grand Prix tracks, fast sprint races, challenging street events, and dramatic night races.
Master precise steering, control your speed through difficult corners, use ERS at the right moment, and fight your way through the field to reach the podium. Upgrade your racing machine in the garage, improve performance, earn credits, and unlock stronger setups as the championship becomes increasingly competitive.
With responsive controls, dynamic race positions, cinematic racing visuals, multiple event types, and fast arcade-style gameplay, Formula Apex Rush delivers an exciting motorsport experience for both desktop and mobile players.
