## How to play

WASD Arrow-Keys to Drive -- Spacebar for Handbrake -- F for Launch Control -- L to Turn on lights -- R to Reset Car -- Q for Left signal -- E for Right signal -- X for all signal lights -- Z to turn off all signal lights

## About

Across many browser car games, you've driving various supercars from around the world. Like Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren and Porsche to name a few. But in fact there's one country, or more specifically one brand sometimes left out of the scene. Which is the powerful Ford GT from America! With their modern version of this amazing supercar you're now able to take it for a test run. No limits and just pure driving simulation, ready to go! Let's see how you drive this supercar and have fun!
