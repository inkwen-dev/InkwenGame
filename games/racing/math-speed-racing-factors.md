## About

A fun math number factoring race game. Keep on racing by keeping your racecar fueled up. Get fuel by getting the gas cans that contain a factor of your racecar's number. Avoid other cars and get coins to see how high of a score you can get. It is game over if you run out of gas or run into another car!
