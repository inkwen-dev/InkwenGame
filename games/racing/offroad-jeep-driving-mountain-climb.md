## How to play

Desktop:
W / Up Arrow = Race
A / Left Arrow = Move Left
S / Down Arrow / Space = Break
D / Right Arrow = Move Right
P = Pause
E = Setting
N: Skip Level

Mobile:
Left Button: Move Left
Right Button: Move Right
Race: Jeep Race
Break: Jeep Break

## About

Get behind the wheel and take on the ultimate off-road driving adventure in Mountain Climb Jeep Driving! Drive powerful 4x4 jeeps across dangerous mountain roads, steep hills, muddy tracks, snowy terrain, and challenging mega ramps. Test your driving skills as you navigate sharp turns, uneven paths, obstacles, and impossible tracks without losing control.
Choose your favorite off-road vehicle and customize or upgrade it to handle tougher challenges. Complete exciting missions, pass checkpoints, collect coins, and unlock new vehicles and challenging tracks as you progress. Every level puts your driving skills to the test with difficult climbs, narrow roads, ramps, and tricky obstacles.
