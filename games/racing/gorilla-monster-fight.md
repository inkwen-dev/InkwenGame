Gorilla Monster Fight is a fun and exciting endless runner game where you control a powerful gorilla on a jungle adventure. Move your gorilla using the mouse, collect bananas, and avoid dangerous obstacles along the way. Stay focused as the speed increases and challenges become harder. Test your reflexes, survive as long as possible, and beat your high score. With smooth gameplay, colorful visuals, and simple controls, Gorilla Monster Fight offers an addictive experience for players of all ages.
