## About

Hit the streets and experience the thrill of high-speed street racing! Drift through sharp corners, escape intense police chases, and push your car to its limits. Earn rewards by completing races and dangerous challenges, then upgrade your vehicle to become faster and more powerful. Explore the city, master your driving skills, outrun the police, and prove that you are the ultimate street racer. Are you ready to burn rubber and dominate the streets?
