## About

Welcome to Bracelet Rush! This is a challenging and fun parkour adventure game. You will control a crystal bracelet through different platforms and run fast. Collect more beads for a beautiful bracelet. Every step is thrilling and fun. The track is full of obstacles like ducks and milk cartons.
