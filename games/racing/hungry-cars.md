# Hungry Cars

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/hungry-cars/cover.jpg)

You are a car that gets stronger by eating other cars! Get stronger and unlock customization options every run!

## How to play

Go to cars with a lower power level to eat them and get stronger. Avoid stronger cars or get damaged.
Desktop Controls:
↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward.
↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward.
← (Left Arrow): Move left.
→ (Right Arrow): Move right.
Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.

### 怎么玩

攻击低等级车辆来吞噬它们，从而提升自身实力。避开高等级车辆，否则会被损坏。

桌面控制：

↑（上箭头）：向上移动。

↓（下箭头）：向下移动。

←（左箭头）：向左移动。

→（右箭头）：向右移动。

移动控制：

屏幕摇杆：拖动摇杆即可向各个方向移动。
