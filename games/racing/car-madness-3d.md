## About

You can race with your friend with mini cars in Car Madness 3D game. Race on a random track with "Quick Race" mode, or play a 10-race tournament in "Season" mode. Watch out for obstacles and black holes! You can be the winner of the tournament by leading the races. For slow computers, you can adjust the quality on the gear button.
