## About

Are you good at remembering short words with four to six letters? Letter Boom Blast is actually not a pure baseball arcade game but a letter puzzle game. Your objective is to help the red stickman baseball player run to the finish area by means of blasting all wrong letters out from the obstacle word cube walls on the platform track at each level on time. Glad you will always find the wrong letter fast and complete all levels!
