## About

Kick is a single player game in which player assumes the character of a soccer player keen on sharpening his skills. Awareness, speed and shot accuracy will come handy as player takes his practice into alleys which are full of obstacles; ranging from dumpsters to fire hydrants. Don’t smash windows! Points are subtracted when ball breaks a window but are added when the ball consequently hits a wall. The aim is to keep the points total above zero for as long as possible
