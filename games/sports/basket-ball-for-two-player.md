## How to play

Player 1 to jump press W or tap the screen Player 2 to jump press UP or tap the screen

## About

1v1 basketball showdown for two players on the same device. Jump, clash, and score in a minimalist arena where every second counts. Control your sphere&mdash;blue on the left, purple on the right&mdash;using a single button: tap to jump. Time your leap perfectly to send your ball into the central hoop before your rival does.
