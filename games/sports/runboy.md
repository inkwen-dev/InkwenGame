## About

Step into the boots of a brave little farmer and outsmart the mischievous raccoons invading your fields! Runboy is a charming grid-based puzzle game that challenges your strategic thinking across dozens of hand-crafted levels. Navigate your farmer across an 8&times;10 tile board, collect scattered seeds, and avoid &mdash; or confront &mdash; the pesky raccoons lurking around every corner. Every move counts, so think carefully before you step!
