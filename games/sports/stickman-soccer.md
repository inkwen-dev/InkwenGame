## How to play

Aim Hold Left Click Take a shot Left Relase

## About

You will play a football match with stickman on a big football field. During this soccer match you will aim and kick the ball. After that, the soccer ball will fly quickly and go to the opponent's goal. You will hear a loud "GOAL" sound. The side that attacks the most goals in 90 seconds wins. If you want to play football match with your friend, you can use "2 PLAYER" mode.
