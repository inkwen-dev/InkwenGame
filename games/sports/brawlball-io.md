## About

BrawlBall.io is a fast-paced multiplayer football (soccer) game where you must be a team player and have the skill to climb the trophy leaderboards! You must log in with Discord to save your weekly trophies. Join a random team based on your trophies, or CREATE A TEAM and invite your friends to play against others!
