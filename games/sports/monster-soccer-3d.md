## About

Monster Soccer 3D is a funny soccer game for 1 and 2 players. Before starting the game, choose the monster character you like. You can include bots as a co-player in the game if you want. Accelerate the ball by hitting it with the hands of the Monsters and try to win the match by scoring the most goals before the time runs out!
