## About

Neon Hockey 2: Championship Edition is a fast-paced arcade air hockey game styled in a neon retro aesthetic. Enjoy smooth, physics-based gameplay with vibrant visual effects. The game offers two modes: 1 Player vs AI (featuring significantly improved Artificial Intelligence with three difficulty levels) and 2 Players (local multiplayer on a single device). Customize mallet size and maximum puck speed in the settings. Compete in a Best of 3 Periods format, needing 7 goals to win a period. Perfect for quick, competitive fun!
