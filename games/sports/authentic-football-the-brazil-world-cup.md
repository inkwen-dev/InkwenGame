## About

3D modeling technology is employed to construct a lifelike football field for the Brazil World Cup, encompassing elements such as the green pitch, goalposts, and stands, thereby creating an immersive match atmosphere. The player and football models also boast a high level of intricacy: the players movements are smooth and natural, and the flight path of the football adheres to the laws of physics, enabling players to feel as if they are in an actual Brazil World Cup match.
