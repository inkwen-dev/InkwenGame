## How to play

Tap or click the playfield, or press Space, to stop the moving timing marker and swing. Aim for the green zone for a powerful hit. The small red and orange zones provide rare distance multipliers. Earn one coin per metre, then use your coins to unlock and equip new bats and scenery.

## About

Time your swing and launch the ball as far as possible in an endless arcade baseball challenge. Earn coins from every hit, unlock 25 increasingly powerful bats, discover 10 distinctive fields, and compete for the longest hit on the Playgama leaderboard.
