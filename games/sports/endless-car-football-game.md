Take control of your vehicles using the arrow keys and compete head-to-head on a never-ending football field. The objective is simple: score as many goals as you can against your opponent. Maneuver your car with precision and speed, strategically positioning yourself for the perfect shot. Use your driving skills to outwit your rival and defend your goal with expert timing.
