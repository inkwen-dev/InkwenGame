## About

Baby Learns Transportation is an educational learning game for kids. Kids will learn to recognize different modes of transport such as cars, trains, and planes through various interesting carts and vehicles in the game. In the game, children must complete various colorful tasks, such as helping animals get on planes, repairing broken bikes, helping to put out fires, and so on.
