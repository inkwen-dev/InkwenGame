## About

Color Ball Rush is a fun and addictive arcade ball game where you merge numbered balls to grow bigger and reach the ultimate goal of 2048. Swipe and control the ball with smooth one-touch controls, combine balls of the same number, and watch them increase in value while changing vibrant colors. Avoid obstacles, stay on the track, and test your reflex and timing skills in this fast-paced endless rush. Easy to play for kids, yet challenging to master, Color Ball Rush rewards skill, speed, and smart merges. Can you create the rainbow 2048 ball?
