**Drag to move** — your bot shoots on its own

**Controls**: drag anywhere (or A/D / arrow keys) · Special: tap DRIVE or press Space

## About

BBlaster — Shoot. Hit. Win!

BBlaster is a fast-paced 3D arcade battler set on a neon court. You pilot a customizable mecha — the B-Bot — that fires marbles automatically. Victory comes down to smart positioning, quick reflexes, and well-timed specials.

When your marbles clash with enemy fire, both shatter and charge your Drive gauge. Meanwhile, the chaos puck ricochets across the court — knock it past the enemy goal line for a PUCK GOAL and heavy damage. When your Drive is ready, unleash an anime-style Special with a full-screen cut-in that can turn the entire battle around.

A boss guards every 5th stage. Defeat them to earn gold and rare parts, then upgrade and fuse equipment in the Workshop to build your strongest loadout. The stage ladder is endless — and your progress, gold, and loot are saved automatically.

### 介绍

BBlaster — 射击、击中、胜利！

BBlaster 是一款快节奏的 3D 街机格斗游戏，背景设定在一个霓虹闪烁的球场上。你将驾驶可自定义的机甲——B-Bot——自动发射弹珠。胜利的关键在于巧妙的走位、敏捷的反应和精准的特殊技能。

当你的弹珠与敌方的弹珠碰撞时，双方都会破碎并积攒你的驱动槽。与此同时，混乱的冰球会在球场上弹跳——将其击过敌方球门线即可得分，并造成大量伤害。当你的驱动槽蓄满时，即可释放带有全屏动画的动画风格特殊技能，扭转战局。

每 5 个关卡都会出现一个 Boss。击败 Boss 可获得金币和稀有零件，然后在工坊中升级和融合装备，打造你的最强阵容。关卡难度无限——你的游戏进度、金币和战利品都会自动保存。
