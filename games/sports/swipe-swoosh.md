## How to play

Aim carefully and shoot the basketball through the hoop to complete each level. Adjust the direction and strength of your shot, score the required baskets, and avoid running out of attempts. Use available power-ups to overcome harder challenges.

Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Click and drag from the basketball to aim and set power.
Release Mouse Button: Shoot.
Left Click: Select buttons and power-ups.

Mobile Controls:
Swipe: Drag from the basketball to aim and set power.
Release Touch: Shoot.
Tap: Select buttons and activate power-ups.

## About
