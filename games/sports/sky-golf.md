## About

Sky Golf is a physics-based puzzle game where your objective is to guide a golf ball from a starting point to the hole. The golf ball rests on a complex structure made entirely of flat, interconnected platforms. Your primary mechanism for moving the ball is to rotate the entire platform structure either clockwise or anti-clockwise by set increments. When you rotate the platforms, gravity acts on the ball, causing it to roll across the flat surfaces. You must strategically time and execute these rotations to change the angle of the platforms, build momentum, and perfectly direct the ball across gaps and around obstacles until it safely drops into the goal. Each level challenges your spatial reasoning to find the precise sequence of rotations required for success.
