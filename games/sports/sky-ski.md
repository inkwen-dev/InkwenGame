## About

What is the best thing to do in Sky Ski snowy winter days? Come on, get your ski suit, gloves and come to slip with us. There are two things to consider when sliding: one is trees, the other is rocks, if you hit them, the game is over and you have to start again. Do not forget to collect gold while sliding with pleasure, gold allows you to unlock other characters.
