## About

Battle of the Minds is a fast-paced chess puzzle game that puts your strategic thinking and decision-making skills to the test under intense time pressure! Choose between 3-minute or 6-minute challenges and solve as many chess puzzles as possible before time runs out. Each puzzle presents a critical game situation where you must find the best move to checkmate, gain material, or secure a winning position. The faster you solve, the higher you score! Compete against players worldwide, climb the leaderboard, and prove you have the sharpest mind in the ultimate chess showdown. Do you have what it takes to dominate the Battle of the Minds?
