## About

In this awesome basketball game you need to draw the right way to get the ball into the basket! Use the spray cans of the color you prefer, red, blue and yellow as soon as you have drawn the right path after press the button that activates the gravity and if you have drawn the right path the ball will end up in the basket! Try to complete all 24 different levels!
