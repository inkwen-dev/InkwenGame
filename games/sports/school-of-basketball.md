## How to play

Touch and drug anywhere on screen to throw a ball!
You can play either arcade, time attack, and distance mode. Arcade mode gives you 10 balls to use; you must continue to score baskets although it becomes harder each time you score.
Time attack mode requires you to score as many baskets within the time limit as possible; you have unlimited balls but limited time. 
Distance mode allows you to increase the distance of your shot each time that you score. The distance decreases every time you miss a shot, don’t let the distance reach zero; otherwise, it is game over.

## About

It is the basketball arcade game in which you must try to score baskets in all the different game modes available. A basketball school opens its doors for enthusiasts like you. Do you know where to aim the ball so that you always hit the basket? Do you know any secret strategies? You have a limited number of balls and every time you miss, you also miss one ball. However, if you never miss, on the contrary, sometimes the ball will be added. How many points will you score?

您可以选择街机模式、限时挑战模式和距离模式。街机模式提供 10 个球供您使用；您需要不断投篮得分，但每次得分后难度都会增加。

限时挑战模式要求您在限定时间内尽可能多地投篮得分；球的数量不限，但时间有限。

距离模式允许您每次得分后增加投篮距离。每次投篮不中，距离都会减少，不要让距离降至零；否则，游戏结束。
