## About

Billiard Diamond Challenge is a fun and free online billiard game for both kids and adults. Your goal is to sink all the balls into the pockets except for the white ball. While playing, you can collect blue diamonds that allow you to unlock a brand new cue. The game offers both level mode and time mode, so you can choose the challenge that suits you best. Get ready to test your skills and have fun at the billiard table!
