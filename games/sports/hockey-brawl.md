## How to play

Player 1: WASD. Player 2 (local): Arrow Keys. Mobile: drag the touch joystick. Movement is your only input — everything else is emergent physics.

## About

Hockey Brawl is chaos hockey — move your player and let physics do the rest. Body checks send helmets flying, sticks shatter glass, and a random modifier flips the rules every match. Climb three leagues to unlock arenas and cosmetics, or grab a friend for local 2-player mayhem.
