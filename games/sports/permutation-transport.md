Mouse click or tap to play

## About

In this game, there are 9 card slots and each slot can hold 12 fruits. Various fruits are randomly generated. When a card slot is filled with the same kind of fruit, it can be transported away by a truck. The goal is to reach the target number of transportation trips to win the game. It challenges your strategic planning and management skills as you arrange and transport fruits efficiently to achieve victory.
