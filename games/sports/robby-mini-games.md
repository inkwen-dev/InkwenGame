## How to play

Control (computer):
● WASD - Movement
● Mouse - Camera Overview
● Space - Jump
● Tab or Esc - Pause
● E - Tutorial

Control (phone):
● Using the game interface
● Move in any place - Camera view.

## About

In this game you can spend time in different fun modes. We have a cool lobby where you can play soccer, go through a light round and have fun on the swing.

There are more than 20 different mini-games in the game, in which you have to play with rivals or by yourself.

Earn coins, collect game gifts and buy accessories for your character.
