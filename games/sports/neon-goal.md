## How to play

Touch and drag to aim your throw
Release your finger to shoot/throw the ball
The ball will bounce and move based on physics
Throw the ball into the net/goal
Complete the level using limited hits/throws

## About

Step into the Neon Arena and test your aim in this fun physics puzzle game!
Your mission is simple: throw the ball and reach the net but the challenge is real because you have limited hits and obstacles are everywhere. 
Each level is a new puzzle where you must think, aim, and throw perfectly to win.

If you love short, satisfying puzzle levels with neon style, Neon Goal is a perfect quick-play game for you.
