## How to play

Objective: score more points than the opposing team before the match ends. Take possession of the ball, move toward the highlighted basket, avoid or bump past defenders, and reach the rim to perform a dunk. Win matches to earn coins, unlock new players, basketballs, and courts, and advance through tougher opponents.

Controls:
- Mouse: hold the left mouse button and drag in the direction you want your player to move. Release to stop changing direction. Click interface buttons to choose modes and upgrades.
- Touch: press and drag anywhere on the play area to move. Release your finger to stop changing direction. Tap interface buttons to choose modes and upgrades.
