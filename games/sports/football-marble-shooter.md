## About

Lets Play Football Marble Shooter Online! Football Marble Shooter is a fun and addictive marble shooter game with a football twist! A chain of colorful footballs keeps rolling toward the goal, and its your job to stop them. Aim carefully and shoot matching footballs into the chain. Match 3 or more balls of the same color to make them disappear and earn points. Keep the chain under control and chase the highest score! How Do You Play Football Marble Shooter? Use your mouse to aim the launcher and click to shoot a football into the moving chain. Match 3 or more identical footballs to remove them from the line. Every successful match earns points and helps keep the chain from reaching the end. Stay focused, because the balls never stop rolling!
