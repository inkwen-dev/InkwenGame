## How to play

Drag back from the ball and release to putt — the farther you pull, the harder the hit. Reach the hole in as few strokes as you can to earn up to three stars, and mind the walls, water and sand along the way. Two tools help when you're stuck: Ball Back rewinds your last shot, and Aim Line shows a longer trajectory preview.

Controls: drag & release (mouse or touch).

## About

Putt Party is a laid-back mini-golf puzzler with a bold retro-poster look. Line up your shot, pull back, and let it fly — then watch the ball bank off walls, skirt the water, and drop into the cup. No two holes feel the same: the greens twist into doglegs, funnels and crosses, with boosters and portals to figure out along the way. Sink it in as few strokes as you can, earn your stars, unlock new balls, and see how you stack up on the leaderboard.

### 怎么玩

从球后方拖动并松开即可推杆——拖动距离越远，击球力度越大。尽可能用最少的杆数将球送入洞中，即可获得最高三颗星的评价。注意沿途的墙壁、水障碍和沙坑。遇到难题时，可以使用两个工具：回放功能可以回放上一次击球，瞄准线功能则可以显示更长的弹道预览。

操作方式：拖动并松开（鼠标或触摸）。
