## About

Hop on your bike, start riding it, and make big jumps. Execute stunts perfectly, avoid obstacles and reach the end safely. Extreme Bikers gives you the possibility to prove your motorbike driving skills on a very difficult levels. You have a lot of moving obstacles, and some spikes which will destroy your bike if you touch them. Be ready for this tough challenge.
