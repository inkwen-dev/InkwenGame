## About

Use only your mouse (or gamepad) to control your climbers hands to pull yourself up an enormous, treacherous mountain full of challenges and surprises. Watch out for slippery surfaces as you make your way towards the peak. Eternal glory and a wonderful gift awaits all climbers who manage to reach the top! Enjoy: Punishing but not impossible gameplay. Practice makes perfect. No filler, every obstacle is designed with intention. Focused gameplay with no distracting elements. No violence.
