## How to play

🎮 How to play "Gym Boss"

Start with simple exercises:
You begin with basic equipment (Push-ups, sit-ups).
Tap the screen (or click) to perform sets and earn strength points 💪.

Level up and unlock new gear:
The harder the equipment – the faster your strength grows!

Compete with players:
Other gym-goers are on the map – overtake them in level to get bonuses!
The higher your level, the better the rewards.

Defeat all the bosses in the ring 🥊
Come to the ring and fight bosses of different difficulty levels.
Each boss requires a certain strength level.
Defeating all bosses earns you the title of Ultimate Gym Boss!

Use boosters and upgrades:
Speed up your progress.
Unlock cool outfits for your character.

Tip: Don’t stay on light weights – only constant growth will lead you to victory!
