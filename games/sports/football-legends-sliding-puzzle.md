## About

In this game, you will find a set of sixteen puzzle pieces. Judging by the name, the theme of the puzzles is legendary football players. After the game starts, a picture is shown that has been broken into nine fragments. To assemble the picture, you must move square fragments, relative to each other, until the picture is formed. After the picture is formed, the name of the player is finally revealed. The assembly process is simple, so the time is limited to a certain number of seconds.
