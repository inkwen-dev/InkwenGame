## How to play

Score as many points as you can before the two minutes run out.

Mouse or touch: press anywhere on the screen and drag. The circle is the highest point the ball will reach, and the dotted line shows how it gets there. Let go to shoot. If you let go without dragging, the throw is cancelled.

Keyboard: the arrow keys move the circle, Space shoots.

Longer shots score more. Clean swishes, bank shots and makes in a row raise your multiplier, and a miss resets the streak. P or Esc pauses the game. On a phone, play in landscape.

## About

You have two minutes. A ball appears somewhere on the court, you drag to set the top of its arc and let go. A new ball is waiting as soon as the last one leaves your hand.

A make is worth the length of the shot, measured to the centimetre, times your multiplier. A clean swish adds 1 to it, a bank shot adds 0.5, and every make in a row adds another 1, up to ×10. One miss and the streak is gone. A shot released before 0:00 that drops after the buzzer gets 5 extra points.

The ball flies in 3D and spins. The rim gives a little when it's hit, so a shot that clips the iron can still roll in, or rattle out.

Ranked games go on the global leaderboard and count toward your league, and you can be relegated from a league too. Practice has no clock and counts for nothing, so that's the place to learn the long ones.

### 介绍

你有两分钟时间。球会出现在球场的某个位置，你拖动鼠标调整球的飞行轨迹，然后松手。上一个球离开你的手后，一个新的球就会立即出现。

进球的得分取决于投篮距离（精确到厘米）乘以你的倍率。空心入网加1分，擦板球加0.5分，连续进球再加1分，最高可达10倍。一次失误，连胜纪录就会结束。在0:00之前出手，如果球在蜂鸣器响起后落地，则额外获得5分。

球以3D形式飞行并旋转。篮筐在被击中时会略微变形，因此即使球擦到篮筐，也可能滚进或弹出。

排位赛的成绩会进入全球排行榜，并计入你的联赛排名，你也有可能被降级。练习赛没有时间限制，成绩不计入联赛排名，所以练习赛是练习远距离投篮的好地方。
