## How to play

Use the virtual joystick to drive your car.
Press the blue button to activate Boost.
Press the yellow button to perform a Hard Hit.
Use W, A, S, and D to drive your car.
Press Shift to use Boost.
Press Q to perform a Hard Hit.
Use the Arrow Keys to drive
