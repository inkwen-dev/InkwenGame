## How to play

Accuracy is essential in Soccer Training—focus on the corners of the goal to improve your scoring opportunities. Be mindful of moving targets—they increase your score but can be challenging! Don’t feel disheartened by shots that get blocked; instead, use them as a learning experience and modify your next attempt accordingly.

## About

In Soccer Training, your aspirations in soccer become a reality as you enter the prestigious realm of the world’s top team. This sports game turns your device into a football pitch where your free-kick skills are challenged to the maximum. With 15 levels, each progressively tougher than the one before, you'll require all of your accuracy and strength to prevail.
