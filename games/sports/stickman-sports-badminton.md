## About

Different Stickman characters meet in Stickman Sports Badminton game! The game will give you a good badminton experience with its realistic ball physics. At the same time, the boosters for the game (big racket, fireball, faster) and some negative bonuses  (slowly) increase the fun. You can play the game for 1 Player or 2 Players. There are "Normal" and "Hard" game modes when playing against the CPU. You can choose the number of sets in the game as 5,7 or 9. Show who is the better badminton player now!
