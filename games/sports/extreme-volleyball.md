## About

The game Extreme Volleyball is a variation on the subject of volleyball. In this game, players will be robots. And unlike normal volleyball instead of the ball will be a bomb. A bomb is known to have a tendency to explode ... The purpose of game to blow up a bomb on the territory of the opponent. The bomb blows up, if it concerns a floor, there were more than three contacts of a bomb or time of burning of a fuse ended. In the game settings menu you can select the number of bombs, bomb fuse burning time, game difficulty level and location of your robot.
