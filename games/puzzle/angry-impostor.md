## About

The battle of the humanoid team against Impostors continues. This time your task is to get from the slingshot to the enemy. They are hidden behind large columns. It will not be as easy as it may seem. Try to break the structures so that Impostors is in an accessible place. There are several different levels in the game, each time their complexity will increase. Good luck and good mood. Website Developer: ulyagames.com
