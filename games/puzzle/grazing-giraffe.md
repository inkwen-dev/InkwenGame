## About

Grazing Giraffe is a cozy sliding puzzle game about one very hungry, very long giraffe. Swipe to send your giraffe gliding across the savanna. It keeps sliding until it hits something, stretching its neck behind it as it goes. Fill every tile of the trail to eat all the leaves and finish the level.

It sounds simple, but you can only stop where a wall, tree or pond stops you, and your own neck gets in the way. Plan each move, find the one path that fills the whole board, and use rewind whenever you paint yourself into a corner.

Features

64 handcrafted levels across 8 savanna chapters, from Grassland to Starlit Savanna
New mechanics as you go: snacks that stop you mid-slide, rope bridges, leafy tunnels and plank overpasses your neck can pass under and over
Earn up to 3 stars per level by solving it on your first attempt
Spend stars on 13 outfits, including hats, glasses, a swinging tie and a golden crown, and wear up to three at once
Unlimited rewinds, so you're never stuck for long
A cute, expressive giraffe that munches, worries and dances when you win
Your progress is saved automatically

### 介绍

《长颈鹿吃草》是一款轻松有趣的滑动益智游戏，主角是一只饥肠辘辘、身形修长的长颈鹿。滑动屏幕，让你的长颈鹿在稀树草原上滑行。它会一直滑行，直到碰到障碍物，同时伸长脖子前进。填满路径上的每一块方格，吃掉所有的树叶，即可通关。

听起来很简单，但你只能在墙壁、树木或池塘阻挡的地方停下来，而且你的脖子也会妨碍你的前进。所以，你需要规划好每一步，找到填满整个棋盘的唯一路径，并在陷入困境时使用回溯功能。
