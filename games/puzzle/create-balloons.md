Use mouse on desktop or touch buttons on your mobile device

## About

In the game Create Balloons, you will go behind a factory where balls are produced and for this a new means of production was recently invented - a special machine. It is easy to operate and consists of an ordinary small bucket. Click on it and balls will start to appear in infinite numbers. Your task is to fill the existing container without pouring it beyond its limits. The dotted line at the top should turn from white to green.
