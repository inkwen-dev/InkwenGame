## About

Enjoy a relaxing logic puzzle built around colorful shapes. Drag pieces onto empty cells and create groups of the same color. The more cells you clear in one move, the more points you earn. Several shapes are always available, so planning ahead matters. Save room for larger pieces, build combinations, and try to beat your best score. The round continues while at least one available shape can still fit on the board. Play for a quick break or settle in for a longer thoughtful session. There is no time limit, so you can study the board and choose the most rewarding move at your own pace.
