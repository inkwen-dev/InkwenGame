## How to play

Choose a difficulty and start a board. Each color region hides exactly one capybara, and each row and column can also contain only one capybara. Capybaras cannot touch each other, even diagonally. Tap once to mark with an X the places where you think there are no capybaras, and double tap a square when you think a capybara is hidden there. Find them all before you run out of lives.

## About

Capybas is a cozy logic puzzle game that blends the deduction of Minesweeper with the rule-based thinking of Sudoku. Find the hidden capybaras by using clues from colors, rows, columns, and adjacency rules. Complete levels, earn coins, and unlock new capybaras and themes as you progress through Easy, Medium, and Hard modes.

水豚

《水豚》（Capybas）是一款轻松有趣的逻辑益智游戏，它融合了扫雷的推理元素和数独的规则思维。玩家需要根据颜色、行、列和邻接规则等线索，找到隐藏的水豚。完成关卡，赢取金币，并在简单、中等和困难三种模式下解锁新的水豚和主题。

### 怎么玩

选择难度并开始游戏。每个颜色区域都隐藏着一只水豚，每行和每列也只能包含一只水豚。水豚之间不能互相接触，即使是斜向也不行。单击一次用“X”标记你认为没有水豚的地方，双击你认为藏有水豚的方格。在生命值耗尽之前找到所有水豚。
