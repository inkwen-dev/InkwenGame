## How to play

Three blocks appear on the left of the playing field which must be placed on the playing field in order to assemble a horizontal or rotating line

## About

There are several coins on the 9 by 9 playing field. It is necessary to place the blocks on the playing field in such a way as to completely assemble a vertical or horizontal line. Gold coins can be used to reset unnecessary blocks and continue the game if you lose.
