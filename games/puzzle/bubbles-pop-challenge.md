## About

Pop, pop, pop! Welcome to Bubbles Pop! Throw your bubbles and challenge yourself to achieve the highest score possible in this satisfying and relaxing game. Collect coins to buy new weapons from the shop. Eaxh weapon has its own unique ability. Drag and release according to the direction you want to throw your weapons, watch out for the energy bar; if it depletes, its game over.
