## About

DuckysColorfulLife is a colorful puzzle adventure where you guide a cute duck on a journey to find a precious egg. Switch colors to remove matching walls, open new paths, and avoid dangerous traps along the way. Each level challenges your logic, observation, and timing skills. With bright graphics, creative color-switching mechanics, simple controls, and addictive puzzle gameplay, help the little duck reach its goal safely!
