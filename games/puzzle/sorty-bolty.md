## How to play

1 step: Click/Tap on a bolt to select a nut
2 step: Click/Tap on another bolt with the same color (or empty) to collect nuts
Use the hints if you get stuck:
Cancel move - undoes the last step you made
Extra bolt - opens extra space for nuts

## About

SORTY BOLTY revolves around the theme of organization and color coordination. Players are tasked with sorting vibrant nuts onto corresponding bolts, creating a visually satisfying and mentally stimulating experience. The game's appeal lies in its straightforward mechanics that gradually increase in complexity, challenging players to think several moves ahead.

步骤一：点击/轻触螺栓以选择螺母

步骤二：点击/轻触另一个相同颜色（或空白）的螺栓以收集螺母

如果遇到困难，请使用提示：

取消操作 - 撤销上一步操作

额外螺栓 - 打开额外的螺母放置空间
