## About

Pin Pull 3D is a classic puzzle game with a 3D art style. The goal of the game is to fill a pool. You need to remove the obstacles that block the flow of liquid to allow the water to flow into the corresponding pool. At some point, you may also need to move obstacles back and forth to separate the liquid.
