## About

Hamster Kombat Pairs is an entertaining brain memory puzzle game where the objective is to match pairs of identical hamsters by flipping over the cards. Youll be racing against the clock, so focus on flipping the right cards to find matching hamsters. Each level presents an increasing difficulty, requiring quick thinking and fast actions to win.
