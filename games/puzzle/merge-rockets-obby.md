## About

“Merge Rockets: Obby” is a fast-paced 3D game inspired by 2048, where the rockets you merge can be launched straight at your opponents!

Merge two identical rockets to create a more powerful rocket of the next level. Expand your arsenal, choose a target, and attack other players bases.

Do not let your rivals destroy your rockets: activate your shield at the right moment, block incoming attacks, and strike back. Earn in-game currency by dealing damage, buy upgrades, and become even stronger.

Destroy buildings, sink ships, and turn the entire city into a massive rocket battlefield!
