## About

In today's game, you can put together several puzzles with the image of Cinderella and her friends. The highlight of the game is that here you can put not one puzzle, but several and choose the most beautiful one. If you are ready, you can proceed. First, you need to choose the most beautiful puzzle, the difficulty of folding. When everything is ready, you can start folding the most beautiful puzzle with Cinderella. If you are confused and do not know where to insert the desired piece of the puzzle, you will have a hint that will show you the whole picture for a few seconds. You need to remember everything carefully so that you can put this picture together from the puzzle. When the first puzzle is ready, you can proceed to the next one, so that as a result you have a whole set of folded puzzles
