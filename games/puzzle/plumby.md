## How to play

Tap or click a pipe to rotate it a quarter turn. Connect the tank to every flask so each flask gets the state drawn on it: a water drop or a steam cloud. Steam spins fans that move motor pipes and open valves of the same color; water spins generators that power furnaces and freezers of the same color. Tap a bolted pipe to use a screwdriver, tap a rock to use a pickaxe. Fewer moves give more stars; use a hint if you are stuck. Keyboard: R restart, H hint, Enter next level, Esc close a window.

## About

PLUMBY is a pipe-rotation puzzle where water changes state on its way. Rotate the pipes to lead the water from the tank to every flask in the form it asks for: water or steam. A boiler turns water into steam, steam melts ice blocks back into water, a fan spun by steam drives motor pipes and opens valves, and a water generator powers furnaces and freezers. Unscrew bolted pipes with a screwdriver and smash rocks with a pickaxe. An endless mode brings in new elements one by one, five worlds of 100 levels each focus on one element, eight difficulty tiers go from Super easy to Impossible, and a daily challenge of 10 levels mixes everything. Chests, mascots and pipe skins to collect.

### 怎么玩

点击或轻触管道即可将其旋转四分之一圈。将水箱连接到每个烧瓶，使每个烧瓶都呈现其上所示的状态：水滴或蒸汽云。蒸汽会带动风扇旋转，风扇带动电机管道运转并打开同色阀门；水会带动发电机旋转，发电机为同色熔炉和冷冻机供电。点击带螺栓的管道即可使用螺丝刀，点击岩石即可使用镐。步数越少，获得的星星越多；如果遇到困难，可以使用提示。键盘快捷键：R 键重新开始，H 键提示，Enter 键进入下一关，Esc 键关闭窗口。
