## About

Get ready to become a pizza master in this fun and fast-paced cooking game! Follow the order shown at the top and create the perfect pizza by selecting the correct ingredients in the right sequence. All items are available at the bottom, and you must choose wisely to match the recipe. Keep an eye on the timer on the left&mdash;complete each pizza before time runs out! As you progress, levels become more challenging with new ingredients and complex orders. Test your speed, memory, and accuracy as you move to the next levels. Can you make every pizza perfectly?
