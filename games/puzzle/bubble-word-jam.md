## How to play

Tap or click two bubbles with related words (e.g. DOG + CAT = ANIMALS) to merge them. Merge bubbles to reach the level goal before you run out of moves. Use boosters to break blockers like locks, ice and screws. Clear the goal to win the level and progress on the map.
