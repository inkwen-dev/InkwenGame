## About

Animal: Find the Difference is a fun and engaging puzzle game where players test their observation skills by spotting differences between pairs of animal-themed images. Each level presents two seemingly identical pictures with subtle variations, challenging players to identify and tap on the differences before time runs out. Perfect for all ages, this game combines entertainment with cognitive exercise, making it a delightful and educational experience.
