## About

Slip Blocks is interesting puzzle game for kids.Your are cube of a certain color and you can move only on route along which dots a certain color as your cube. With the help of the control keys, you will have to indicate on which route it will have to move. Having reached the end point, your hero will enter the portal and be transported to the next level of the game.
