## About

Introducing Paint Circle Shooter, a thrilling mobile arcade game that will test your reflexes and precision. In this fast-paced adventure, you control a paint cannon tasked with eliminating colorful circles that fill the screen. The catch? You must match the color of your shots with the targets, creating satisfying splashes of vibrant paint as you go. With a variety of challenging levels and mesmerizing visuals, Paint Circle Shooter offers an addictive experience for players seeking excitement and accuracy.
