## About

Find &amp; Match is an addictive pyramid puzzle game that tests your observation skills and quick thinking! Dive into beautifully stacked layers of colorful items - fruits, flowers, animals, foods, and more. Only surface items can be clicked, so plan your moves wisely. Collect three matching items to clear them from your basket. Race against the clock through 6 unique themed levels: Meadow, Garden, Farm, Ocean, Market, and Festival. Each level unlocks after completing the previous one. Can you conquer all pyramids?
