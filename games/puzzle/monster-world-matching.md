## About

Monster World Matching is a matching game with funny little monsters. Connect three or more monsters to destroy. Try to play as many levels as you can before your time runs out. On the left side of the screen is the timer to which play time is added each time you connect a larger string of monsters. So you need to be fast and connect as many monsters as you can. In the upper level of the game time flows fast so you need to be fast because your playing time will run out faster.
