## About

Samurai VS Zombies is a math game where you need to click on a correct answer and samurai will throw an arrow at the zombie. Do not think long because the zombie will kill you. You have offered several answers and you need to click on the right one. That makes this math game easier. Play long as you can, be fast, learn and practice math and enjoy in this zombie mathematical game.
