## How to play

Tap a ship type to buy a squadron. It flies into your dock.
Drag a ship onto an identical ship (same type, same tier) to merge it into a higher tier.
Drag ships to arrange your formation. The top row is the front line.
Counter triangle:
Line ships beat Lancers.
Lancers beat Artillery.
Artillery beats Line ships.
Keep Artillery in the back. Lancers hit harder from the side (×1.25) and from behind (×1.5).
Press FIGHT. Battles are automatic, and you can tap to change the speed.
Spend credits and cores on upgrades, deploy heroes, and come back for patrol rewards and daily missions.

## About

Command a fleet of starships in this merge auto-battler. Buy Line ships, Artillery and Lancers, then merge identical ships to build stronger ones. Your dock is your battle formation: every ship deploys exactly where you place it.

Read the enemy fleet on the radar and put the right counter in front of each target. Then watch the battle play out on its own. Push through the Star Map and take on bosses and giant Arks. Earn three stars in every sector.

Recruit heroes such as Nova, Valkyrie and Aegis, and learn their tricks in the Tactics Drill. Hold the line in Defense, hunt convoys in Cargo Raid, and challenge other commanders' fleets in Fleet Clash. Rise from Cadet to Grand Admiral and top the power ranking.

### 怎么玩

点击舰船类型即可购买中队。中队会飞入您的船坞。

将舰船拖到另一艘相同类型、相同等级的舰船上，即可将其合并升级。

拖动舰船来排列您的阵型。顶行是前排。

克制三角：

线列舰克制长矛舰。

长矛舰克制炮兵。

炮兵克制线列舰。

将炮兵放在后方。长矛舰从侧面（×1.25）和后方（×1.5）攻击时伤害更高。

点击“战斗”。战斗会自动进行，您可以点击屏幕调整速度。

花费银币和核心进行升级，部署英雄，并返回领取巡逻奖励和每日任务。
