Players are tasked with matching colorful bears to their corresponding holes, allowing them to jump into a refreshing aqua lake. With the game’s increasing complexity, players must employ strategic thinking to navigate through the challenging levels.

## About

Cube Drop Puzzle: Match Color is a vibrant brain teaser that blends block placement, color matching, and a playful water park vibe. You guide falling cubes to help cheerful bears enjoy their splash time. You solve increasingly clever puzzles, plan every drop carefully, and enjoy lively water visuals that make each challenge feel refreshing and satisfying.
