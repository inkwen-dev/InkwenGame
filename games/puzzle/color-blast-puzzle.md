## How to play

Tap matching blocks: Tap groups of two or more blocks with the same color to clear them from the board.
Complete the objectives: Each level has specific goals, such as clearing a certain number of colored blocks.
Plan your moves: You have a limited number of moves, so choose each tap carefully.
Create bigger matches: Clear larger groups of matching blocks to earn higher scores and create powerful effects.
Use power-ups: Use available boosters and special items to clear difficult areas and complete challenging levels.
Win the level: Complete all required objectives before running out of moves.
Controls: Tap or click on matching blocks to make your move.

Get ready for a colorful and satisfying puzzle adventure with Color Blast Puzzle! Tap and clear groups of matching colored blocks, create powerful chain reactions, and complete challenging objectives across exciting levels.
