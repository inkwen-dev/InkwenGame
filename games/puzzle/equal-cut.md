## How to play

Press and drag across the food to aim a straight cut, then release to lock it in. Tap Cut to drop both halves on the scale — the closer to a 50/50 split, the more stars you earn. Didn't land it? Redraw the cut and try again.

## About

Equal Cut is a one-swipe balancing puzzle: cut each piece of food into two exactly equal halves, then watch the scale prove how close you got. There's no timer and no live weight readout while you aim — just the shape in front of you and your own eye for where it balances.

25 foods, each an odd or irregular shape that hides its true balance point: a lopsided pretzel, a corn cob, a wedge of cheese, a whole pumpkin slice. Land a clean, even cut for up to three stars; miss and you get another attempt — there's always a free way to keep playing, never a wall.

### 介绍

《等切》是一款单指滑动平衡益智游戏：将每块食物切成完全相等的两半，然后看着秤显示你切得有多准。游戏没有计时器，也没有实时重量显示——你只需观察眼前的食物形状，用你的眼睛判断它的平衡点即可。
