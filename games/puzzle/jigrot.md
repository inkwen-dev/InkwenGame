## How to play

Desktop (mouse & keyboard)
Move pieces: Click and hold a piece or cluster, drag it, then release to drop. Matching edges snap together when they line up.

Mobile (touch)
Move pieces: Touch and drag a piece or cluster, then lift your finger to place. Edges merge when they align.

## About

JigRot is a chill puzzler: drag scattered pieces, snap them together, and merge clusters until the full crazy brainrot picture locks in. Easy to learn, smooth to play, and built for a relaxing flow — one oddball pack after another when you want to unwind without pressure.
