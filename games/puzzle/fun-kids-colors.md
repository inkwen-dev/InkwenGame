## About

Fun Kids Colors is puzzle educational game designed to teach kids about colors. It will also test the young one's reflexes. A color pencil will show up and the kids will just select the answer yes or no. You need to answer as many as you can before your time runs out. It's a fun and enjoying game that little ones with love. Heave fun.
