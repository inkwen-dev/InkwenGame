## How to play

In Toytopia, you simply need to fix the broken parts of a toy by merging art items to produce the required art materials. Use the mouse cursor (desktop) or finger (mobile) to drag and drop items on the board.

## About

Toytopia is a cozy merge game where you are tasked to combine and produce art materials which you can use to fix broken toys. Each toy presents a unique challenge and difficulty.
