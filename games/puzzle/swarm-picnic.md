## How to play

Tap the front box in any queue. It takes an open slot, and its ants collect every matching cube they can reach. Watch which colors will be uncovered next: if all work slots hold boxes that cannot move, you run out of moves. Clear the whole picture to finish the level. Play with a mouse or touch. Boosters can shuffle the queues, pick a deeper box, or open one extra slot.

## About

Swarm Picnic is a relaxing puzzle about color and order. Choose the front box from a queue and place it in an open work slot. Ants of that color remove matching cubes from the picture and carry them home. Plan the order carefully, keep slots free for colors that will appear next, and use boosters when the box you need is deeper in a queue. Explore 30 garden levels, tougher challenges, and a random level order after the campaign.
