## How to play

Tap / Click the Buy button — purchase a box
Tap / Click a box — open it (may require multiple taps)
Drag & Drop an item — move tools between slots
Drag an item onto an identical one — merge and upgrade
Drag a tool to one of the 4 builders — they start building the house automatically
Tap boost buttons (Efficiency x2, Auto Open) — activate temporary power-ups

## About

Open. Merge. Build! Dive into a bright and cheerful merge arcade where your goal is simple: assemble the ultimate toolkit and construct beautiful houses step by step.
Buy mystery boxes, open them, and merge identical tools—like screwdrivers, paint rollers, and hammers—to upgrade their efficiency. Strategically hand the best tools to your four hardworking builders stationed around the foundation. They’ll work automatically while you focus on optimizing your board. Watch in satisfaction as the house realistically takes shape and grows right before your eyes!
Take on increasingly ambitious construction projects, from a Cozy Cottage to a Grand Mansion. Each new building brings escalating requirements that will push your merging tactics to the limit.
Grab limited-time gift events, boost your progress with Efficiency x2, and activate Auto-Open to speed up your workflow. Every decision matters as your building empire scales endlessly. Finish projects, earn rewards, and see how far your crew can go in an infinite challenge of fun and strategy.
Complete the tutorial, master the coin economy, and prove that your team can construct any dream home!
