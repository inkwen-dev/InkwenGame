## About

Pull the Pins is a strategic puzzle game where you must carefully pull pins in the correct order to rescue a young boy and make him reach his brother. Each decision matters, as a wrong move could lead to failure. Plan your actions wisely and use problem-solving skills to ensure a successful rescue.
