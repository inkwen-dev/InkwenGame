## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

A small neon ball went on a journey through his world. You are in the game Neon Path will help him in this exciting adventure. Your character will move along a certain route. The road will have many turns and various projections. You will have to control his actions to do so that he would not face obstacles. To do this, clicking on the screen will have to change the trajectory of its movement. If you come across luminous points on the way, you will have to touch your hero with them and thus get extra points.
