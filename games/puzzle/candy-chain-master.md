Use a mouse or touchpad to play this game

## About

Welcome to Candy Chain Master! The board has blue tiles with candies on them. Start by selecting a candy and move across nearby tiles with the same candy to make a chain of three or more. Each tile must turn golden by matching at least one candy on it. Think carefully and move fast to turn all tiles golden before time runs out.
