## About

Puzzle Dash is a puzzle bubble shoot game but in this case this is a game where the wizard shoots candy instead of bubbles. Move the wizard left to right to fired the cookie where you want it to be. Hit two or more identical cookies to destroy them. Destroy all the cookies before they reach the goal.
