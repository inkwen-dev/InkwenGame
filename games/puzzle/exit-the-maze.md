## How to play

Use left and right arrow keys or touch the screen

## About

A three-dimensional image of the maze will be seen on the screen in front of you. Your character will be in a certain place. You will also see the exit from the maze. You will have to move the ball to it. To do this, you need to be using the keys to turn the whole maze in space in the desired direction. As soon as the ball gets to the exit from the maze you will be given points and you will go to the next level of the game.
