## How to play

The core mechanic is simple but strategic:

Place numbers next to values they can divide.
When a valid division happens, the tile either disappears or is reduced based on the result.
Keep clearing space to prevent the board from filling up.
Your run ends when no more placements are possible.
Survival depends on smart positioning and planning.

### 怎么玩

核心机制简单却极具策略性：

将数字放置在可以整除的数值旁边。

当发生有效的整除运算时，该数字要么消失，要么根据结果减少。

不断清理空间，防止棋盘被填满。

当棋盘上无法再放置任何数字时，游戏结束。

生存取决于巧妙的布局和周密的计划。
