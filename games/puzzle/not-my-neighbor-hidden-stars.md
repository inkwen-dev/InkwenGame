## About

This exciting object-search game, Not My Neighbor Hidden Stars, is a great way to pass the time. Each level has a different amount of stars, and the complexity of each level increases. There are 10 levels. The timer is set to limit the amount of time you have. Be quick and find the hidden objects before time runs out. Be careful, as the wrong clicks will shorten your time by 5 additional seconds. Are you ready to begin? Have fun searching for stars! Website Developer: ulyagames.com
