## How to play

Control your character and make your way along the game path, unlocking one area after another. The pickaxes automatically rotate around your character, so your task is to position yourself correctly next to the right blocks and start mining.

With each hit, the pickaxe deals damage, gradually destroying the target and granting experience. Keep an eye on the durability indicator to see how many hits are left before the block is completely destroyed. Once destroyed, the loot is sent to your character.

Gain experience, level up, and improve your character’s stats. Unlock additional pickaxes, increase your mining speed, and become stronger.

For your victories, you’ll receive rewards that can be spent on new pickaxes and upgrades. More powerful gear will help you break through sturdy blocks faster and advance to the next zones.

Explore new territories, upgrade your gear, and try to reach the farthest zone!
