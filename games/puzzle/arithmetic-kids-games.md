## About

Arithmetic Kids Game is a game where you can show your mathematical knowledge. In this math game you have to add, subtract, multiply and divide to get the right result. Try to achieve the best result in the given time with minimally wrong answers. Tap the symbol that you think corresponds to the answer offered on the screen to play this game.
