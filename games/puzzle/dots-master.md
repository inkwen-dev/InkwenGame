## About

Dots Master is a fun and strategic puzzle game where you connect matching dots to solve colorful challenges. Connect dots vertically or horizontally, and form squares to clear all dots of the same color in a single move! Each level has a special goal &mdash; clear a certain number of colored dots, pop stars, make loops, or conquer the tiles &mdash; all within a limited number of moves. With 5 exciting chapters packed with creative puzzles, theres always something new to explore. Use helpful power-ups like extra moves, bombs, shuffle, and auto-loop** to get through tricky levels. Dots Master keeps your mind sharp and your fingers busy!
