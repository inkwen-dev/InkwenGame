## How to play

Tap a dot and drag a line to another dot to connect them.

The number inside a dot shows how many lines must meet there — when it is satisfied, the dot turns green. A wrong link turns red: tap the red line to remove it and try another pair.

Swipe on empty space to rotate the camera — the puzzle is three-dimensional, and some dots are hidden behind others until you turn the model.

Pinch or scroll to zoom.

Connect every dot correctly and the model is revealed and added to your gallery; larger models are assembled part by part. 

Stuck? Use the Hint button to reveal one correct connection. Complete as many models as you can to climb the leaderboard.

## About

3D Dots is a relaxing connect-the-dots puzzle that happens in real 3D space. Every level starts as a cloud of numbered dots floating in the air — link them with the right lines and a hidden object comes to life.

The number on each dot tells you how many lines it needs, so there is no guessing, only clean logic. Unlike flat dot-to-dot games, the puzzle has depth: orbit the camera to look at the figure from any side and find connections you could not see before.

### 怎么玩

点击一个点，然后拖动一条线到另一个点，将它们连接起来。

点内的数字表示需要连接多少条线——当连接满足要求时，点会变成绿色。错误的连接会变成红色：点击红色的线将其移除，然后尝试另一对连接。

在空白处滑动即可旋转视角——拼图是三维的，有些点会被其他点遮挡，直到你旋转模型才能看到。

捏合或滚动即可缩放。

正确连接所有点后，模型就会显示出来并添加到你的图库中；较大的模型需要逐个部分组装。

遇到困难？使用提示按钮来显示一个正确的连接。尽可能多地完成模型，提升你的排行榜排名。
