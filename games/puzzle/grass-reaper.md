## About

Grass Reaper is a casual mini game that only requires you to control the reaper and harvest a set amount of grass for each level. The initial state of your reaper is not enough to collect all the grass at once, so you need to collect and sell it, and then use the gold to upgrade the reaper to achieve the effect of passing the level faster!
