## About

Christmas N Tiles is a fun and simple elimination mahjong-inspired game, your goal is to move tiles onto the bottom of the screen (there is limited space for that) and match 3 tiles to clear them. If you like playing Match 3 puzzles or mahjong game, you will love to play this game.
