## How to play

Designed for quick sessions but challenging to master, Flow Blast Shooter blends satisfying shooter mechanics with smart flow-control puzzles. The gameplay is simple to learn, yet increasingly intense as new block patterns, faster flows, and tricky color combinations appear.

## About

Flow Blast Shooter is a fast-paced puzzle shooter where timing and color control are the key to victory. Blocks flow continuously along a conveyor system, and it’s your job to place shooters, aim carefully, and fire at the perfect moment to keep the flow moving.
