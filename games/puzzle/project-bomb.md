## About

Project Bomb is a super new puzzle game that has 80 explosively cool and challenging levels! The levels are split across 4 distinct worlds that will provide fun for the whole family. Main goal is to blow some stars! How to Play: 1. Drop the bomb close to the stars while avoiding the black squares. 2. Click on the bomb to activate it. 3. Stand back... and enjoy the fire works!. Key features: -80 levels across four different, colorful worlds! -Graphics that really Cute! -Progressively challenging gameplay! -Power up and game-changing elements like laser cannons and warps! Have fun with this great explosive puzzle-game!
