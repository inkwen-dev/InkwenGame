## About

Fruity Craft Merge is a fun and relaxing merge game where you grow fruits from tiny seeds into delicious dishes. Merge matching items to evolve them through multiple stages, including seeds, sprouts, saplings, trees, fruits, and tasty recipes. Create unique fruit-based dishes, serve or deliver them to customers, earn rewards, and expand your fruity collection as you progress through exciting merge adventures.
