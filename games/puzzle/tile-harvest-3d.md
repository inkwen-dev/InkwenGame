## How to play

Your goal: Clear every tile from the 3D board to win the level.

1. Pick matching tiles
Tap any accessible tile on the 3D stack. When you tap 3 tiles of the same type, they are collected and sent to your Factory Belt — a row of slots at the bottom of the screen.

2. Watch your Factory Belt
The Factory Belt holds the tiles you've collected. When 3 identical items sit in the belt at the same time they automatically merge into a brand-new, higher-tier item — clearing those slots and making room for more.

3. Rotate & zoom the board
The tiles are stacked in 3D. Swipe to rotate the board and expose hidden tiles underneath. Pinch to zoom in or out. Uncovering the right tiles at the right time is the key to a clean chain of merges.

4. Don't run out of space
If your Factory Belt fills up with tiles that can't merge, you're stuck — and the level is lost. Think ahead, plan your picks, and keep the belt flowing.

5. Use boosters wisely
Four boosters help you out of tight spots:

🔄 Throwback — Send the last tile in the belt back to the board.
🌀 Whirlwind — Shuffle the tiles on the board for a fresh look.
🔍 Magnifier — Highlights a set of 3 matching tiles for you.
🔨 Hammer — Smash and remove a single tile from the board.

### 怎么玩

你的目标：清除 3D 棋盘上的所有方块，即可赢得关卡。

1. 选择匹配的方块

点击 3D 方块堆上任意一个可点击的方块。当你点击 3 个相同类型的方块时，它们会被收集并送入你的传送带——屏幕底部的一排槽位。

2. 注意你的传送带

传送带存放着你收集的方块。当传送带上同时出现 3 个相同的方块时，它们会自动合并成一个全新的、更高等级的方块——清空这些槽位，腾出空间放置更多方块。

3. 旋转和缩放棋盘

方块以 3D 形式堆叠。滑动屏幕旋转棋盘，即可显示下方隐藏的方块。捏合屏幕即可放大或缩小。在正确的时间显示正确的方块是顺利合并的关键。

4. 注意空间

如果你的传送带被无法合并的方块填满，你就无法继续游戏——关卡失败。提前思考，规划你的选择，让传送带上的牌源源不断地涌出。

5. 明智地使用道具

四种道具助你摆脱困境：

🔄 回旋镖 — 将传送带上的最后一张牌送回牌桌。

🌀 旋风 — 重新洗牌，让牌桌焕然一新。

🔍 放大镜 — 高亮显示一组三个相同的牌。

🔨 锤子 — 击碎并移除牌桌上的一张牌。
