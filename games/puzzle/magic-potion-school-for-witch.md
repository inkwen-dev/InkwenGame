## How to play

Brew Magical Potions! Enroll in Magic Potion School for Witch, the enchanting puzzle adventure where you play as a young witch wielding a magic wand to cast spells as bouncing balls that strike and reduce numbered magical bubbles and shapes in a bubbling cauldron. Clear all enchantments before time runs out to brew potions successfully and conquer challenging levels in wizarding school!

Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Aim with the wand using the mouse, click to cast spell and launch magic ball.

Mobile Controls:
Touch: Drag to aim the wand, tap to cast spell and launch magic ball.

## About

Magic Potion School for Witch is an enchanting match-3 puzzle game where young witches cast spells with a wand to connect and pop numbered magical bubbles and shapes like triangles and squares in a bubbling cauldron, mastering potion brewing through increasingly challenging levels in a whimsical wizarding academy.

### 怎么玩

熬制魔法药水！加入魔法药水学院，开启一段奇妙的解谜冒险之旅！你将扮演一位年轻的女巫，挥舞魔杖，施放魔法，让魔法球化作弹跳的魔法球，击碎并消除沸腾坩埚中带有数字的魔法泡泡和各种形状。在时间耗尽前清除所有魔法，成功熬制药水，征服魔法学院的重重关卡！

桌面端操作：

鼠标：使用鼠标瞄准魔杖，点击施法并发射魔法球。

移动端操作：

触控：拖动魔杖瞄准，点击施法并发射魔法球。
