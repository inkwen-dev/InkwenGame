## About

Play as your favorite Barbie characters in this exciting match-3 puzzle game, Barbie Match Master! Remove tiles from play by making sets of three or more of the same kind. Youll get access to more playable characters and levels as you make your way through the game. Moreover, you may compete with your friends to see who can achieve the highest score.
