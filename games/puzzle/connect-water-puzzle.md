## How to play

Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Rotate the pipes to connect them and guide water to all the trees in this challenging puzzle game. Match the color of the water sources to the corresponding trees to ensure each one is properly watered. Tap to adjust the direction of the pipes and strategize your moves carefully to complete each level. Save all the trees by providing them with their correct colored water to win the game!
