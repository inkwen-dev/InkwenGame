## How to play

Tap or click a front box to collect reachable blocks of its color. On desktop, press the queue number instead. Clear the portrait without filling every slot. Undo, shuffle, hints and extra slots are free. Open Settings to pause or change sound.

## About

Sort colorful blocks into matching boxes and uncover 49 original pixel-art reactions. Plan the front-box order, manage your slots, and clear each portrait. Recommended age: 13+. AI tools assisted code, procedural art and sound, localization, and promotional artwork; no generative AI runs during gameplay.
