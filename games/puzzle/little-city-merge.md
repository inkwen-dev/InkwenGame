## How to play

Goal: grow your city and build a Public building of the level the city asks for, then travel to the next city.

- Tap or click an empty lot to build. Building costs energy, which refills over time.
- Drag a building onto another one of the same type and level to merge them into the next level.
- Drag a building onto an empty lot to move it, or onto another building to swap them. Drop it on the trash bin to remove it.
- Shops earn coins. Homes bring residents.
- Public buildings open new land and raise the max level of homes and shops. Merging them needs enough residents.
- Spend coins in Upgrades to earn more, build faster and get more energy.
- When the city goal is done, open World and move to a new city.

Controls: mouse or touch. Tap / click to build, drag and drop to merge, move or remove.

## About

Little City Merge is a cozy merge-and-build idle game drawn in a hand-sketched pencil style. Tap empty lots to build homes, shops and public buildings, then drag two identical buildings together to merge them into a bigger one: a hut grows into a skyscraper, a market stall into a megamall. Shops earn coins, homes bring residents, and public buildings unlock new land and let your whole city grow higher. Spend coins on upgrades, reach each city's goal and move on to new cities around the world, each with its own architecture and twists. Your city keeps earning while you're away.

### 怎么玩

目标：发展你的城市，建造符合城市要求等级的公共建筑，然后前往下一个城市。

- 点击或轻触空地即可建造。建造需要消耗能量，能量会随时间恢复。

- 将建筑拖拽到同类型同等级的建筑上，即可合并到更高等级。

- 将建筑拖拽到空地上即可移动，拖拽到其他建筑上即可交换位置。将其扔到垃圾桶上即可移除。

- 商店赚取金币。住宅吸引居民。

- 公共建筑可以解锁新的土地，并提升住宅和商店的最高等级。合并需要足够的居民。

- 使用金币升级可以获得更多金币、更快的建造速度和更多的能量。

- 当城市目标完成后，打开“世界”并前往下一个城市。

操作方式：鼠标或触摸。点击/轻触即可建造，拖拽即可合并、移动或移除。
