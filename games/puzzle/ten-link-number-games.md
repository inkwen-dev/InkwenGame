## How to play

Tap (or click) two numbers to link them. A pair is valid when the two numbers
are equal or add up to 10, and the path between them is clear — horizontally,
vertically, diagonally, or wrapping from the end of a line to the start of the
next one.

Clear every number on the board to win the stage. Fill a whole row and it
disappears. If no moves are left, press ADD to copy the remaining numbers to
the end of the board, or use a HINT to reveal a pair.

Controls: mouse click or touch only — no keyboard required.

## About

Ten Link is a relaxing yet brain-teasing number puzzle. Clear the board by
linking pairs of numbers that are either the same or add up to 10 — connect
them across rows, columns, diagonals, or even from the end of one line to the
start of the next.

Colors do the thinking with you: every matching group shares one color, so you
can spot valid pairs at a glance. Out of moves? Add a new wave of numbers and
keep the chain going. Four difficulty modes, a fresh Daily Challenge every day
with monthly trophies, and a level system that grows your Calculation,
Observation and Focus as you play.
