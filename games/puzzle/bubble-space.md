## How to play

Use the mouse to aim your bubble shooter and click the left mouse button to shoot a bubble If you hit 2 or more bubbles of the same color as the one you shoot they will pop

## About

The bubble shooter game that is very easy to play. Once you start playing you wont stop! The goal of the game is to beat all enemies, and help a captive spaceship. Special Items: [Heart] Your health is restored. [Helmet] Stamina is increased. [Gun] Attack force is increased. [Pair of Bubbles] The capacity of spare bubbles is increased.
