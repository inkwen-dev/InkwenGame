## How to play

Tap on targets to play this game

## About

There will be three targets on the bottom of the game. They will be yellow, pink and blue. A bomb will move in front of them, which you must not hit. In the main part of the game there will be very small colored balls with same colors as the targets. When some of the targets is free and the bomb is away from there click on the target to go all colored balls into that target. Try to collect more balls before bomb hit some of the balls and then the game will over.
