## How to play

Drag or tap a piece from the tray onto any open cell — a piece only fits where its rings have room. Match three of the same color across any row, column, or diagonal to clear that color and score points; clearing more than one line at once multiplies your score. Out of good moves? Use the discard or eraser tool to make space. Keep placing pieces for as long as you can — the run ends when the board fills up — and try to beat your best score.

## About

A hypercasual color-matching puzzle on tic-tac-toe's 3×3 board. Instead of X's and O's, you stack concentric colored rings in each cell. Line up three of the same color in a row, column, or diagonal — big ring, small ring, or the center dot, size never matters — and that line clears for points. Chain several clears in one move for escalating combo multipliers. There's no win state, just an endless run and a new high score to chase.

从托盘中拖动或点击棋子到任意空位——棋子只能放置在其圆环有空间的地方。在任意行、列或对角线上匹配三个相同颜色的棋子即可消除该颜色并获得分数；一次消除多行可获得倍数分数。没有好走的棋子了？使用弃牌或橡皮擦工具腾出空间。尽可能长时间地放置棋子——当棋盘填满时，游戏结束——并努力打破你的最高分。
