## About

Halloween Match Trio is a classic match-3 puzzle game with fun challenges. Swap items to make a line of three or more matching items, horizontally or vertically. Keep matching to unlock all tiles and reach the final stage. Use power-ups like bombs, arrows, magnets, and more to clear levels faster. Complete all 75 levels to win the game!
