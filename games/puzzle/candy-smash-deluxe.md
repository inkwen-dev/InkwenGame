Use mouse to play this game

## About

Slide the blocks towards another identical block to eliminate both. Use as least moves as possible to do this. Elimination of a block gives you 100 points, but if you slide the block more than one times, 10 points are reduced for each move. Eliminate all the blocks to complete a level. There are 24 challenging levels in this game.
