## How to play

Tap SUMMON to call a fighter onto the grid - it costs gold, which you earn by defeating monsters. Drag a fighter onto another fighter of the same level to merge them into a stronger hero. Your army attacks automatically; keep monsters from breaking your wall. Between waves pick one of three blessings. Tap spell buttons to cast meteor, freeze, wall repair or rage. Survive all waves to win the mission and earn up to 3 stars. Controls: mouse or touch - click/tap and drag.

## About

Merge Wall: Last Stand is a bright merge tower-defense game. Hordes of goblins, wolves, skeletons, ogres and fire-breathing dragons march on your castle - only your wall and your army stand in the way! Summon fighters, drag two identical heroes together to merge them into a stronger one, and grow a squad of 10 hero tiers: knights, archers, wizards and more, each with its own special attack. Every monster fights differently: wolves pounce, bats dodge, shamans heal, trolls roar and dragons rain fireballs. Choose blessings between waves, cast meteor, freeze, repair and rage spells, and defeat epic bosses. Clear a 60-mission campaign across meadows, snowfields and lava lands, then test yourself in Endless mode. Upgrade heroes and your castle, open treasure chests, spin the wheel of fortune, complete daily quests and achievements.

### 怎么玩

点击“召唤”按钮即可召唤一名战士上场——召唤需要消耗金币，金币可通过击败怪物获得。将一名战士拖拽到另一名同等级的战士身上，即可合并成更强大的英雄。你的军队会自动攻击；阻止怪物突破你的城墙。每波敌人之间，你可以选择三种祝福之一。点击法术按钮即可施放陨石术、冰冻术、城墙修复术或狂暴术。成功抵御所有敌人的进攻即可赢得任务，并获得最多3颗星。操作方式：鼠标或触摸——点击/轻触并拖动。

### 介绍

《合并之墙：最后的防线》是一款画面精美的合并塔防游戏。成群的哥布林、狼群、骷髅、食人魔和喷火巨龙正向你的城堡进发——只有你的城墙和军队才能阻挡他们！召唤战士，将两个相同的英雄合并成更强大的英雄，并组建一支拥有10个英雄等级的队伍：骑士、弓箭手、法师等等，每个英雄都有自己独特的特殊攻击。每个怪物的战斗方式都各不相同：狼群会猛扑，蝙蝠会躲避，萨满会治疗，巨魔会咆哮，巨龙会降下火球。在每一波敌人之间选择祝福，施放陨石术、冰冻术、修复术和狂暴术，并击败史诗级首领。完成包含60个任务的战役，穿越草地、雪原和熔岩地带，然后在无尽模式中挑战自我。升级英雄和城堡，开启宝箱，转动幸运轮盘，完成每日任务和成就。
