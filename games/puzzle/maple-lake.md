## How to play

🏗️ Tap BUILD to create a new building.

🔄 Drag two identical buildings together to merge them into a higher-level building.

🔁 Drop a building onto a different one to swap their positions.

🗑️ Drag unwanted buildings to the bin to remove them.

⬆️ Use UPGRADE to increase the level of buildings created by BUILD.

🗺️ Use EXPAND to unlock more land for your resort.

💰 Buildings from level 5 onward generate passive income automatically.

🌲 Place decorations like trees, fountains, lanterns, and wells to increase your income.

📋 Complete contracts to earn cash and unlock free land.

🎡 Spin the Lucky Wheel for extra rewards.

🏆 Keep merging and expanding until you build the ultimate Maple Lake resort.

Merge, build, expand, and turn Maple Lake into the resort of your dreams! 🏡🌲✨

## About

Welcome to Maple Lake, a relaxing merge adventure where you turn a small lakeside camp into a beautiful luxury resort! 🌲🏞️✨

Help Theo rebuild the old camp he inherited from Grandma Hazel. Create new buildings, merge two identical ones, and upgrade them step by step — from simple wood planks and cabins to chalets, hotels, and impressive palaces.

As your resort grows, buildings begin generating passive income, allowing you to expand faster, unlock more land, and decorate the area with trees, fountains, lanterns, flower beds, and more. 💰🌳

Follow a story across 7 chapters and 24 contracts, meet different characters, complete their requests, and slowly transform Maple Lake into a thriving vacation destination.

Spin the Lucky Wheel for extra rewards, expand your plot, improve your buildings, and enjoy a cozy merge experience with no energy system and no waiting timers. 🎡✨
