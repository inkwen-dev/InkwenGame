## About

It is a spooky bubble shooter game; collect the Golden 'Jack-o'-lanterns' hidden among the zombie's eyes. There will be 72 challenging levels. In each level you will find a different challenge with 'different type of items and power ups'. To complete a level you must collect all the 'Jack-o'-lanterns'. Shoot the eye on the board to make groups of 3 or more eyes of the same color. You must complete a level within allowed time. You will get better score if you complete the level early.
