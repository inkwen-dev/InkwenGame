## How to play

Tap and slide on the screen to control the ball

## About

Control the ball during the gameplay. There will be horizontal bricks with different colors. The ball will be bouncing on them. The ball will change the color when you pass the beam, and the ball will get the same color as the beam. Later the ball needs to bounce only at the brick that has the same color as the ball. Try to reach great distances and earn more points as you can.
