## About

3D isometric Puzzle Game is a fun and addictive puzzle game with isometric tiles. Find secret path to finish all levels. - move the player to the next isometric block. - Once you leave a yellow block, it will fall to the ground. - To finish a level, all yellow blocks have to be down on the ground and the player has to be on the Purple block, to solve the puzzle
