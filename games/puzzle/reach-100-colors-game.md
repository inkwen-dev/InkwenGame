## About

Reach 100 is a game about puzzle and reflexion in which you must regroup numbers to create sums of 100. You have the possibility to slide the different forms and numbers, horizontally and vertically, and add numbers to each other. If you reach the sum of 100, this form freezes and cannot be used anymore. Regroup all forms to reach the end of the level. Put your neurons to the trial and find the solutions on sixty different levels. Levels are becoming more and more complexes.
