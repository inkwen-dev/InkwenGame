## How to play

Tap and drag a bubble to aim.
Release to launch the bubble.
Merge two identical bubbles to create a higher-level item.
Create the items displayed at the top of the screen to complete the level.
Finish all objectives before running out of moves.
Use power-ups when you need extra help.

## About

Bubble Merge is a relaxing physics-based puzzle game where you combine matching bubbles to create new items and complete each level’s objectives. Plan your moves carefully, use helpful power-ups, and discover new combinations as the challenges become more difficult.
