## About

New addictive puzzle. Use your imagination and arrange the puzzle pipes and elbows to create a perfect piping connection between two points. The hero began his journey for treasure and to save the princess, but the puzzles in front of the hunter trapped him. What you need to do is to connect the pipes in the right order, so that the hero can take the hidden treasure and save the princess away from the dragons, wolves and monsters safely. Solve puzzles and rescue the hero, collect all the treasures, kill monsters and save the princess. Connect the pipes in order to destroy your enemies by pulling lava and poisonous gas into their puzzle units.
