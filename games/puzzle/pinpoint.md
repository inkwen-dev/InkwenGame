## About

PinPoint is a fast-paced precision arcade game where timing is everything. With every turn, you&rsquo;re given a number &mdash; and that&rsquo;s exactly how many arrows you must launch at a rotating circle. The challenge? Each arrow must land cleanly without colliding into the others already in place. As the circle speeds up, slows down, or even changes direction, your reflexes and focus will be put to the test. Every perfect shot pushes your score higher, while a single wrong move ends the round. Key Features: Addictive one-tap gameplay that&rsquo;s easy to learn, hard to master. Dynamic rotating circles with unpredictable patterns.
