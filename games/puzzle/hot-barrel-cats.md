## How to play

Drag a cat onto a matching cat to merge them into the next tier. The merged cat starts completely cool.

Drag a hot cat down to the spawn zone to cool it, and drag a cool one up to the wall to fire. You can also tap a cat to select it, then tap where it should go.

Only cats on the wall shoot. The bar under each cat is its burn time — when it runs out the cat jams, stops firing and has to cool before it works again.

Call a cat with the button at the bottom; gold comes from every Mildew you kill.

Don't let them reach the gate. When the gate falls, the level is over.

Esc pauses, M mutes.

## About

Only the cats standing on the wall can shoot — and shooting makes them overheat.

A cat that fires long enough jams, goes silent, and needs time to cool. So you are always deciding who holds the wall: drag a hot cat down to the spawn zone to cool, send a fresh one up, and drop two matching cats together to merge them into a stronger tier. Merging resets the heat, which makes it a rescue as well as an upgrade.

The Mildews — a damp fungus horde — walk the road toward your gate, and heat is what dries them out: a cat firing above 80 % scorches spore-carriers before they can splash heat back at your wall.

16 hand-tuned levels, five kinds of Mildew and three bosses, each of which attacks your heat rather than your health: one bursts and heats every pad at once, one shuts a shield and makes you waste fire, one keeps summoning while you rotate. Fifteen tiers of cat to merge toward, four perks to buy, a café shelf to fill, and a new daily challenge every day.

Hold the gate. Watch the barrels.

### 怎么玩

把一只猫拖到另一只匹配的猫身上，即可将它们合并到下一层。合并后的猫初始状态是完全冷却的。

把一只发热的猫拖到生成区域冷却，把一只冷却的猫拖到墙上射击。你也可以点击猫来选中它，然后点击它应该移动到的位置。

只有墙上的猫才能射击。每只猫下方的进度条显示的是它的冷却时间——冷却时间结束后，猫会卡住，停止射击，需要冷却后才能再次使用。

点击底部的按钮召唤猫；每杀死一只霉菌怪，你都会获得金币。

不要让它们到达大门。大门倒下时，关卡结束。

Esc 键暂停，M 键静音。

### 介绍

只有站在墙上的猫才能射击——而射击会使它们过热。

猫如果长时间射击，就会卡壳、失去动力，需要时间冷却。因此，你必须时刻决定谁守住墙：把一只过热的猫拖到刷新点冷却，派一只新的猫上去，或者把两只属性相同的猫合并成更强大的猫。合并会重置温度，这既是一种救援，也是一种升级。

霉菌——一群潮湿的真菌——会沿着道路向你的大门走来，而热量正是让它们干燥的关键：一只火力超过 80% 的猫会烧焦携带孢子的霉菌，阻止它们将热量溅射到你的墙上。

16 个精心设计的关卡，五种霉菌和三个 Boss，每个 Boss 都会攻击你的温度而不是生命值：一个 Boss 会爆裂并同时加热所有平台，一个 Boss 会关闭护盾并让你浪费火力，一个 Boss 会在你旋转时不断召唤。十五层猫咪等你来融合，四种特权等你来买，咖啡馆货架等你来填满，还有每天都有的全新挑战。

守住大门。注意木桶。
