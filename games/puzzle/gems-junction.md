## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Stop the gem troops to come forward by shooting a similar gem towards them. There are 2 modes. In beginner mode a group of two gems can be eliminated while in expert mode you need at least 3 gems. Collect all the stars to win this game. Fall down of a gem will take one out of four lives. Enjoy this game with great power ups.
