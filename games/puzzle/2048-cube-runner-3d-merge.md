Desktop: Click to start. Hold the left mouse button and drag left or right to steer.

Mobile: Tap to start. Swipe or drag left and right to steer.

## About

2048 Cube Runner is a fast-paced 3D runner that combines obstacle-course action with satisfying 2048-style merging. Guide a growing chain of numbered cubes, collect matching cubes to create higher values, pass through helpful gates, and avoid saws, spikes, and moving obstacles. Reach the finish with as many powerful cubes as possible and keep building your ultimate 2048 collection!
