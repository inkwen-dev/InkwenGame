## About

Jail Drop is a physics puzzle where youve got to get the little prisoner down to the grass in one piece. The catch: hes buried under a pile of boxes. Tap a block to make it disappear, and everything resting on it comes crashing down. So the order matters. Clear every box except the grass, use the ramps and planks to break the fall, and dont squash the poor guy on the way. Messed up? One tap resets the level. 50+ levels, and they get nasty fast.
