## About

Dunk Jumps is another ball game where you need jump with the ball on the wall full with spikes. The ball will jump on the left side of the wall and on right side of the wall. There are spikes on both sides and you need to be precise and jump either above or below the spikes. If you hit them the ball will explode and the game will be over. There is a stars who you need to collect for extra points. But be careful sometimes stars are under the spike and that will destroy your ball
