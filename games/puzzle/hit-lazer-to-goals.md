## About

Hit Laser To Goals is an engaging puzzle game where you rotate and adjust mirrors to reflect laser beams to the correct targets. Calculate reflection angles, overcome obstacles, and find the optimal path for each laser beam. Each level presents a new challenge with increasing difficulty, helping to develop logical thinking and observation skills. The gameplay is simple but extremely captivating!
