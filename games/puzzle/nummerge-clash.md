## How to play

Become a Master of Number Combinations!
Your main goal is to achieve the highest score and survive as long as possible. Connect adjacent cards with matching values, increase their worth, and clear space for new cards. The game ends when there are no available moves left and you lack energy for tactical actions. Plan your strategy, watch the upcoming card queue, and use tactical moves to avoid deadlocks!
Controls:
Desktop Controls:
Left Click on Card: Merge the card with all neighbors of the same value.
Long Press on Card: View card description and properties.
Swap Button: Activate mode to swap two adjacent cards.
Destroy Button: Remove selected card using energy.
“+” Button near Card Queue: Open shop and buy cards with coins.
Mobile Controls:
Tap on Card: Merge the card with all neighbors of the same value.
Long Press on Card: View card description and properties.
Energy Panel: Select swap or destroy mode.
Destroy Button: Remove selected card using energy.
“+” Button near Card Queue: Open shop and buy cards with coins.

## About

A dynamic number puzzle where you connect adjacent cards with matching values. Tap a card to merge it with all neighbors of the same value, increase its worth, and clear space for new cards falling from above. Use energy for tactical moves: swap adjacent cards or remove obstacles. Simple at first glance, this mechanic hides deep strategy: plan your moves, adapt to the shifting board, and chase high scores under limited conditions.

### 怎么玩

成为数字组合大师！

你的主要目标是获得最高分并尽可能长时间地生存下去。连接相邻的同值卡牌，提升它们的数值，并为新卡牌腾出空间。当没有可移动的卡牌且能量不足以进行战术操作时，游戏结束。制定你的策略，关注即将出现的卡牌队列，并运用战术操作来避免陷入僵局！

操作方式：

桌面端操作：

左键点击卡牌：将卡牌与所有相邻的同值卡牌合并。

长按卡牌：查看卡牌描述和属性。

交换按钮：激活交换模式，交换两张相邻卡牌。

销毁按钮：消耗能量移除选定的卡牌。

卡牌队列旁的“+”按钮：打开商店，使用金币购买卡牌。

移动端操作：

点击卡牌：将卡牌与所有相邻的同值卡牌合并。

长按卡牌：查看卡牌描述和属性。

能量面板：选择交换或销毁模式。

销毁按钮：消耗能量移除选定的卡牌。卡片队列附近的“+”按钮：打开商店，用金币购买卡片。
