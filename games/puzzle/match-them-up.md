## How to play

Your goal is to find and match pairs of identical objects scattered around the room. Select an item with the right mouse button. Move the camera by dragging with the right mouse button, rotate it by dragging with the left mouse button, and zoom using the mouse wheel. Match all pairs to clear the level and beat your best time!
