## About

The online game "Minecraft Puzzles" invites boys and girls to have an interesting time for developing block dimension puzzles. In the game you will open 12 pictures, where you will see various stories and the main characters of Minecraft. For example, Steve and Alex are fighting enemies. Or Steve in the image of a knight on a horse is eager to fight. Or a brave traveler explores the caves and worlds of Minecraft. Only one puzzle is available at once in the game. The rest are opened in turn after passing the previous ones. Puzzles are offered to be folded in different modes — from easy to difficult. Connect the fragments of 25 or 100.
