Help a young mother take care of her restless baby. Relax in this colorful game! Connect tiles and decorate the nursery.

## How to play

Connect identical tiles.

Tiles can be connected as long as there are no obstacles in their path.
This mental path is laid out cell by cell.
Each subsequent cell in the path must be in the same row but in an adjacent column, or in the same column but in an adjacent row.
The path can be laid outside the playing field.
Cells containing tiles block the path.

Click one tile, then the second.

### 怎么玩

连接相同的方块。

只要路径上没有障碍物，方块就可以连接。

这条路径需要逐格铺设。

路径上的每个后续方块必须位于同一行但相邻列，或者位于同一列但相邻行。

路径可以铺设在游戏区域之外。

包含方块的方块会阻挡路径。

点击一个方块，然后再点击第二个。
