## About

This is funny jigsaw game with Santa Claus. There is 12 images with funny Santa's and when the image pieces are shuffled you need to put them in to right position to get image with Santa Claus. When you click on the image you can choose the mode that you can play. Play with 25 piece, 49 or 100 pieces. Solve the first image to go to the next one. Try to unlock all levels and have fun with this Christmas Santa game.
