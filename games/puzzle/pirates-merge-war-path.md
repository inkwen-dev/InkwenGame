## How to play

Train units and merge them to get stronger units, go the battlefield and destroy all defenses to loot the coins, these coins will help you train more units. 
Flying units can reach to the defense directly  and take less while ground units can take more damage so plan accordingly and take them both in battle field.

Desktop Controls:
Use mouse click to interact with game ui to train & deploy units.
Drag and drop units of same level to merge them 
␛ (ESCAPE KEY) : To pause and resume game.


Mobile Controls:
Use screen touch to interact with game ui to train & deploy units.
Drag and drop units of same level to merge them

## About

Pirates Merge: War Path is a strategy adventure where you combine swashbucklers to form a powerful army of ground and flying units. Complete 50 challenging missions across three regions, gathering coins and unlocking new upgrades as you go. Train, merge, and expand your forces to create an unstoppable pirate legion ready to conquer the seas.

海盗合并：战争之路

《海盗合并：战争之路》是一款策略冒险游戏，玩家需要将海盗们合并成一支强大的地面和飞行部队。完成横跨三大区域的 50 个挑战任务，一路收集金币并解锁新的升级。训练、合并并扩充你的部队，打造一支所向披靡的海盗军团，征服海洋。

### 怎么玩

训练部队并合并它们以获得更强大的部队，前往战场摧毁所有防御工事以掠夺金币，这些金币将用于训练更多部队。

飞行部队可以直接攻击防御工事并承受较少伤害，而地面部队则会承受更多伤害，因此请合理规划，并在战场上同时使用飞行部队和地面部队。

桌面版操作：

使用鼠标点击与游戏界面互动，训练和部署部队。

拖放相同等级的部队即可合并它们。

␛（Esc 键）：暂停和继续游戏。

移动版操作：

使用屏幕触摸与游戏界面互动，训练和部署部队。

拖放相同等级的部队即可合并它们。
