## About

This is a pixel slide puzzle where you build scenes similar to the classic 15-puzzle &mdash; but with one major twist: every piece can go in any position. Inside, youll find nearly 70 unique levels, each representing a handcrafted pixel illustration. Use the hint feature to preview the completed scene and figure out the correct layout. Perfect for fans of pixel art and relaxing puzzles!
