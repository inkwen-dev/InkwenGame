Use the mouse to play this game or tap the screen to slide the pieces

## About

Happy Kids Jigsaw is a fun game where you can enjoy piecing together different pictures of happy kids. With 12 different pictures to choose from, you can select your preferred level and mode - easy, medium, or hard, depending on the number of pieces the image will be cut into. Drag and drop the pieces into the right position to complete the image and have a great time! Have fun playing Happy Kids Jigsaw and enjoy the adorable pictures of happy kids!
