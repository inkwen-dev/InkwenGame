## About

This is an engaging dart and balloon game played within a two-dimensional grid. Gameplay:In this game, you are presented with a two-dimensional grid filled with balloons of various colors. Your task is to use darts of the same color to pop balloons of the same color. By doing so, you aim to eliminate all the balloons in each row and column. As you successfully pop balloons with matching darts, the grid gradually clears. The goal is to completely eliminate all the balloons and achieve victory. This game requires strategic thinking and precision as you plan your moves to efficiently clear the grid.
