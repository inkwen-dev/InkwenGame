## How to play

Mouse or touch: swap two neighbouring fruits to line up three or more of the same kind (drag, or tap one fruit and then its neighbour).
The level goal is shown at the top: collect fruits, reach a score, clear the board, dig up turf (match right on it), bring baskets down (match below a basket), catch fireflies (clear the tile under a firefly) or, on the Sky Islands, push the pinwheel to the weathervane (a match next to the pinwheel moves it along the arrows).
Special pieces: four in a row — rake (clears a row or column); 2x2 square — beehive; L or T shape — barrel; five in a row — magic watering can (removes every fruit of one kind). Swap two special pieces and both go off.
Fog clears when you match on it or next to it. A sleepy cloud wakes up when you match next to it — then its rain hits obstacles or brings goal fruits.
Moves left at the end turn into points — finish fast to earn three stars.

## About

Sunny Farm is a cozy match-3 puzzle with 400 levels across four big worlds: your home farm, a trip around the farms of 10 countries, the Magic Forest and the brand-new Sky Islands.

Swap neighbouring fruits, line up three or more and set off explosive combos. Every level has its own goal: harvest crops, clear hay, crates, weeds, ice and chains, dig up turf, bring baskets down, catch fireflies — and above the clouds, blow a pinwheel all the way to the weathervane.

What's inside:
• 400 levels and 40 chapters on a living map: the farm through the seasons, 10 countries from Holland to Lapland, the Magic Forest and the Sky Islands with a rainbow finale
• New in the sky: pinwheels that ride the wind along the arrows, sleepy clouds that wake up and rain on obstacles or drop the fruits you need
• Sky folk: Keeper Lumen, Breezy the Marmot, Queen Aurora and their friends send postcards to your album and bring gifts
• Special pieces: rake, beehive, barrel and the magic watering can. Swap two special pieces and both go off!
• The Manor: repair and decorate plots with your stars — and up in the sky, the Cloud Pier, the Rainbow Garden and the Castle in the Clouds
• Pumpkin Festival: catch jack-o'-lanterns on the board and open festive chests
• Farm friends with bonuses, your own garden with golden harvests, a weekly fair, daily orders, a wheel of fortune and a frog crossing
• Boosters: hammer, shovel, dynamite, pitchfork, basket, bee swarm and lantern
• Every land has its own music

Free to play, no sign-up. Works on phone, tablet and computer.
