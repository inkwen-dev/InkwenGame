## How to play

Merge two identical items on the board to create a higher-level item. Keep combining items to produce the objects needed for your current orders.

1. Find two identical items and merge them.
2. Create higher-level items.
3. Complete the orders shown at the top of the screen.
4. Earn stars for completed tasks.
5. Return to the room and spend stars on renovations and upgrades.
6. If you run out of energy, wait for it to recover or refill it using an available option.

Try not to fill the entire board: free cells are needed for new items and future merges.

## About

Dream Home: Merge Story is a cozy game about merging items and renovating a home. Combine identical items on the board, unlock higher-level items, and complete orders.

Completing tasks rewards you with stars. Spend them on renovations, furniture, and new interior elements as you gradually turn an old house into a beautiful and cozy home.

Keep an eye on your energy, plan your moves, and make sure there is enough free space on the board. Crystals can help you restore energy faster, while additional rewards let you keep playing.

Complete tasks, discover new items, and watch your new home transform room by room.
