## About

This game demands from you good shooting skills to running animals. You have to camouflage yourself against them. Fast running, massive figure, thick skin and strong body make the boar a difficult target to challenge. When the boar feels threatened, these wild animals can be really dangerous and attack on you instantly and fight intensively.
