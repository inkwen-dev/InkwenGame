## How to play

Select a die (or a pair) from your tray and drag it onto the grid where it fits.
Tap the dice in the tray to rotate them before placing, especially helpful for pairs
When three or more dice of the same face touch, whether horizontally or vertically (even diagonally adjacently), they automatically merge into one higher-value die

## About

Dice Puzzle is a puzzle game where your task is to strategically place numbered cubes on the board, aiming to merge three adjacent cubes with the same number. As they combine, their value increases. Carefully select cubes with different points, considering their placement to match with others effectively. Be mindful, as cubes may appear individually or in pairs. Merge three or more dice horizontally or vertically to create higher-valued cubes. Utilize the storage space wisely to save cubes for later use. Test your puzzle-solving skills in this engaging dice-merging game!

### 怎么玩

从骰盅中选择一个骰子（或一对），然后将其拖到合适的网格上。

点击骰盅中的骰子旋转它们，然后再放置，这对于放置一对骰子尤其有用。

当三个或更多个相同点数的骰子接触时，无论是水平、垂直还是对角相邻，它们都会自动合并成一个点数更高的骰子。
