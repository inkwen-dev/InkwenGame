## About

The hero of our colorful puzzle game for the little ones is a beautiful panda bear. The body of this funny animal is covered with fluffy white fur, and the paws, ears and the area around the eyes are black. The panda lives in the forests of China and feeds mainly on the stems and roots of bamboo. So, get to the game soon! Move the scattered squares of the puzzle to the right places on the yellow field, and as a result you will get a finished picture with the image of a bamboo bear.
