## How to play

"Mem Brawl: Merge Simulator" - Purchase — click on the store and buy new meme fighters.
🚀 Buy characters and match two identical characters to get a stronger one. The higher the level, the higher the income!

🚀 Farm memes bring passive income, and quick clicks on the screen give coins manually.

Upgrade — go to the upgrade menu and download multipliers and passive earnings.

🚀 Arena — choose the 3 most powerful memes and send them into battle! Autoboys bring cups, gold and raise the ranking.

The stronger your team is, the higher the rewards and crazier the memes! 🚀
