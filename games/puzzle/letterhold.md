LetterHold is a word-building board game: draw letters, spell them across a 15x15 board, and take the premium squares before your opponent does.

## How to play

Goal: score more points than your opponent by building words on the board. The game ends when the bag is empty and one player uses up their rack; the highest score wins.

Mouse or touch - everything is drag and tap, no keyboard required:
- Drag a tile from your rack onto the board to place it; drag it again to move it
- Undo lifts the last tile, Undo-all takes the whole turn back
- Play commits your word and scores it
- Pass gives up the turn; Exchange swaps chosen tiles back into the bag
- Shuffle re-orders your rack, Hint suggests a move

Words must connect to what is already on the board, and every word you form is checked against the dictionary of the chosen language. Premium squares multiply a letter or the whole word, and using all your tiles in one turn earns a bonus.

## About

LetterHold is a word-building board game: draw letters, spell them across a 15x15 board, and take the premium squares before your opponent does.

Every tile you drag shows what the word is worth before you commit it, so a turn is a decision and not a guess - swap a letter for a bigger one, stretch a word through a triple, or hook onto what is already on the board and score both words at once. Blanks can stand for any letter, and a hint will find you a move when you run dry.

Play it however you like: solo against the bot on easy, medium or hard; pass-and-play on one screen for two to four seats; online with a friend by invite or room code, or a quick match with a stranger. You can also watch a live room instead of playing.

Three letter sets and three languages - English, Ukrainian and Polish - each with its own tile distribution and its own word list, checked on the spot.

### 介绍

LetterHold 是一款单词构建棋盘游戏：抽取字母，在 15x15 的棋盘上拼写单词，并在对手之前抢占高分方格。

你拖动的每个字母牌都会显示该单词的分值，因此每次行动都是一次决策，而非猜测——你可以用一个更大的字母替换一个较小的字母，用三个字母组成一个单词，或者连接到棋盘上已有的单词，同时获得两个单词的分数。空格可以代表任何字母，当你词穷时，提示功能会为你提供下一步行动。

游戏方式多种多样：你可以选择单人挑战电脑，难度可选简单、中等或困难；也可以在同一屏幕上进行多人游戏，支持 2 到 4 人同时游戏；你还可以通过邀请或房间代码与朋友在线对战，或者与陌生人进行快速比赛。你也可以选择观看其他玩家的实时游戏。

游戏提供三种字母组合和三种语言——英语、乌克兰语和波兰语——每种语言都有其独特的字母牌分布和单词列表，并会实时进行验证。
