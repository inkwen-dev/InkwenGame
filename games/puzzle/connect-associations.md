## How to play

1. Find two words that belong to the same theme.
2. Tap the first word and then the second, or drag one onto the other.
3. Keep adding related words until the group contains all four.
4. Every correct merge damages the ice. Free frozen words before using them.
5. Reveal every category to complete the level.

Incorrect pairs cost one attempt. If you get stuck, shuffle the words or use a hint.

## About

A set of words hides several meaningful connections. Find four words that share a theme and merge them step by step into one group. Correct matches reveal categories, while later levels introduce fresh word sets and ice obstacles.

The relaxed pace gives you time to consider every move. Hints can keep the solution moving when a connection is especially tricky. Complete levels, earn coins, and watch your overall progress grow.

### 怎么玩

1. 找出两个主题相同的单词。

2. 点击第一个单词，然后点击第二个单词，或者将一个单词拖到另一个单词上。

3. 继续添加相关单词，直到四个单词全部匹配。

4. 每次正确合并单词都会破坏冰块。使用单词前，请先解冻冰块。

5. 揭开所有类别即可完成关卡。

错误的单词组合会消耗一次尝试机会。如果遇到困难，可以重新排列单词或使用提示。
