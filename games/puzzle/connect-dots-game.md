## About

Connect Dots is a free and interesting online puzzle game for boys. This game is very addictive to play. You need to connect all the dots into lines, but you can't cross other lines, otherwise, you won't be able to go on. The game will test your logical ability and make the challenge more and more difficult. Start the challenge with your own hands to ensure that you can successfully complete the levels!
