## About

Family Guy is a big fan of various puzzles, which they are happy to solve to improve their intellectual abilities. If you want to develop logic, then join an entertaining game where you need to look for the same images. To collect a set of drawings, you need to move them, as soon as there are three or more identical images in a row, they will disappear and open a new row of drawings. Collect them until they all disappear, then you will go to the next level.
