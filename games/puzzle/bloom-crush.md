## About

Bloom Crush is a classic match-3 puzzle game filled with fun challenges. Swap items to make lines of three or more matching pieces, horizontally or vertically. Keep matching to clear tiles and move through each stage. Use power-ups like bombs, arrows, magnets, and more to finish levels faster. Beat all 75 levels to win the game!
