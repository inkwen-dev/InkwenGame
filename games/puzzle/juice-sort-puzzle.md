## About

Juice Sort Puzzle is a colorful and relaxing sorting game set in a lively jungle juice bar. Pour vibrant juices into the correct cups, organize each order, and keep the serving area under control. Every level introduces new bottle layouts, colors, and challenges that test your observation and planning skills. Complete customer orders, unlock new cups and decorations, collect rewards, and use helpful boosters when a puzzle becomes tricky. With cheerful cartoon graphics, simple controls, and satisfying pouring effects, Juice Sort Puzzle is easy to learn and fun to master.
