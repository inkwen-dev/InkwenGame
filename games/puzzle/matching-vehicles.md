## About

This is matching game with kids vehicles. Your job is to connect three or more same vehicles. When you do that the vehicle will disappear and you will earn points. If you connect five or more vehicles you will add play time. Increase your playing time and try to spend as many levels and time as you can playing this game. As you progress in the game, the time left for the game will accelerate. Tap on screen to play this game. Move the finger on same images to connect them.
