## About

This is jigsaw game with Christmas Cartoon cars. The game have 12 images with different Christmas cars. Start with first one, choose the mode you will play and start the game. You can play with different number of pieces, play with 25,49 or 100 pieces. Drag the pieces into right place to get the image with Christmas Car. When you will complete first image you can go to the next one. So you will have fun for the holidays, start to play and enjoy the game.
