## How to play

- Drop Brainrots into the puzzle box.
- Merge two of the same Brainrot to evolve them.
- Unlock new Brainrot creatures and climb the evolution ladder.
- Keep dropping, keep merging, and discover the funniest meme ever.

## About

Brainrot Merge: Drop Puzzle is a wildly absurd merge puzzle game where quirky creatures evolve into chaotic meme-powered icons. Drop and combine animals to unlock hilarious AI-generated characters, each more bizarre than the last.
