## How to play

Safari Merge is an endless food-chain merge puzzle. Drag two matching animals together to combine them into the next creature up the food chain, starting from simple grass and rabbits, all the way up to elephants and beyond. Each new tier you discover comes with a real animal fact, so you're learning as you play.

The board fills up over time as new creatures spawn in. Merge strategically to keep space open. If the board fills completely, the run ends. There's no fixed "win," so the goal is to reach the highest tier and score you can before the board fills up. Watch for combos: chain several higher-tier merges together in quick succession for a multiplied score and reward bonus.

Controls: Click and drag (or tap and drag on touch devices) an animal onto a matching one to merge them. No keyboard required — the game is fully playable with mouse or touch.
