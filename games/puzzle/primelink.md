## About

Enter the world of numbers and logic with PrimeLink &mdash; a brainy and addictive match-3+ puzzle game built entirely around prime numbers! Your goal is simple: link three or more of the same prime numbers in a chain to clear them from the board. The longer the chain, the higher your score &mdash; and the greater your combo bonus! But be quick and strategic &mdash; the board fills fast, and every move counts. With every level, the challenge grows: new primes appear, patterns get trickier, and your number skills are put to the ultimate test.
