## How to play

Tap or click an empty cell to place a husky. Tap again to remove it. Use the hint button whenever you need a little help. That's all — quick to pick up, deeply satisfying to solve!

## About

Huskeydoku is a cozy logic puzzle that gives the classic Sudoku a cute husky twist. Fill each grid so no symbol repeats in any row, column, or colored region — simple to learn, wonderfully tricky to master. With calm visuals and handy boosters to save you when you're stuck, it's the perfect brain-teaser for a quick break or a long, relaxing session.
