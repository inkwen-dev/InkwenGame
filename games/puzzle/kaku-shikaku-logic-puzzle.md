## How to play

Drag on the board (hold the left mouse button or swipe with your finger) to draw a rectangle. Each rectangle must contain exactly one number, and the number must match the count of cells it covers. Tap a placed rectangle to remove it. Fill the entire board with no gaps or overlaps to clear the stage. Stuck? Use a hint to reveal one correct rectangle. Your IQ score grows with every clear — climb the leaderboard!

## About

Kaku is a relaxing logic puzzle based on the classic Shikaku (rectangle division). Split the grid into rectangles so that each rectangle contains exactly one number — and that number equals its size. 2,000 handcrafted levels across 4 difficulties, daily challenges with monthly trophies, and an IQ score that grows with every puzzle you solve. Easy to learn, but Expert 13×13 boards will truly test your logic.

在棋盘上拖动（按住鼠标左键或用手指滑动）绘制矩形。每个矩形必须包含一个数字，且该数字必须与其覆盖的单元格数量相符。点击已放置的矩形即可将其移除。填满整个棋盘，确保没有空隙或重叠，即可通关。遇到难题？使用提示即可找到一个正确的矩形。每次通关都会提升你的智商分数——努力登上排行榜吧！
