## About

Get that golden coin into the bucket; sounds easy, until physics gets involved. Tap the wooden planks and crates to knock them away at just the right moment and watch the coin roll, bounce and tumble its way down. One wrong move and it rolls right past the target. 24 levels packed with wobbly towers, tilted ramps and tricky setups that get harder as you go. Simple to understand, surprisingly satisfying to master. Think before you tap!
