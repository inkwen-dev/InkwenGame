## How to play

Drag one boxer onto another boxer of the same level to merge them into a stronger fighter. Buy and open new fighters, upgrade speed and income, and build a team strong enough to win each battle. On desktop, use the mouse to click buttons and drag fighters. On mobile, tap buttons and drag fighters with one finger. Win battles, unlock stronger boxers, and keep improving your gym to progress.

## About

Merge MMA is a portrait-friendly casual merge and idle fighting game. Combine matching boxers to discover stronger fighters, improve your gym, grow your earnings, and challenge increasingly powerful opponents. Its blend of quick merging, steady progression, and animated MMA battles makes every upgrade feel rewarding.
