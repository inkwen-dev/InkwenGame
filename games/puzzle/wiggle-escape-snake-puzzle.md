## How to play

At the start of each level, snakes are tangled into a single shape. Your goal is to untangle it completely. Tap snakes one by one — only snakes that are not blocked can move. Choosing the wrong order will stop your progress, so think ahead. In sandbox mode, you can select preset shapes or draw your own levels and try to solve them.
