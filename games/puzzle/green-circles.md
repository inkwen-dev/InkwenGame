## About

Our ball in the game Green Circles has found itself in a very dangerous trap, moreover, it consists of thirty difficult levels that must be overcome in order to get out. Each level is two rotating circles with an empty space. There are sharp thorns inside the circle. If the ball hits them, the game is over. The circles are constantly rotating, you just need to have time to press the ball for the jump, and preferably twice, so that the jump is of the desired height and the ball does not touch the tip of the spike.
