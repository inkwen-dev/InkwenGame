Use mouse to play this game

## About

In this game you can choose one of nine pictures with children and vehicles on them. Once you have selected your favorite image it will be divided into 12 parts and your job will be to compose it. With help it is easy to do this because you will have a transparent image on the background. If you want a challenge, remove the help and arrange the pieces yourself to get the picture you have chosen for the game.
