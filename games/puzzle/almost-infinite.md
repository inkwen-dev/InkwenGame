## How to play

Drag elements from your inventory onto the table, then drop one card onto another to combine them. If the pair makes something, you get a new card; anything you haven't found before goes into your inventory. Some pairs make nothing, so try another. Use search to find a card, and sort your inventory by when it was found or by depth. The Relics filter shows the final forms you've collected. Undo takes back your last move, and Clear Table tidies up. Stuck? Tap Hint and watch a short ad to see a pair you haven't tried yet that makes something new. Controls: mouse or touch, drag and drop.

## About

Almost Infinite is a calm crafting and discovery game. You start with four elements (Fire, Water, Earth and Wind) and combine them into new ones. Fire and Water make Steam, and a few steps later you're making volcanoes, dragons, galaxies and microwaves. There are more than 23,000 things to discover. Every combination is fixed, so the same pair always gives the same result and there's always something new to find. Some discoveries are Relics: final forms that can't be combined any further. Collect them all, if you can. Works offline, saves your progress, and plays in any browser on desktop or mobile.

### 怎么玩

将物品栏中的卡牌拖放到桌面上，然后将一张卡牌放到另一张卡牌上进行组合。如果组合成功，你将获得一张新卡牌；任何你之前未找到的卡牌都会进入你的物品栏。有些组合无法组合，所以请尝试其他组合。使用搜索功能查找卡牌，并按发现时间或深度对物品栏进行排序。“遗物”筛选器会显示你收集到的最终形态。“撤销”功能可以撤销你上一步操作，“清空桌面”功能可以整理桌面。遇到难题？点击“提示”并观看一段简短的广告，即可看到一个你尚未尝试过的组合，它可以组合出新的卡牌。操作方式：鼠标或触摸，拖放。
