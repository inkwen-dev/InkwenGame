## About

Christmas Winnie Pooh Jigsaw is a fantastic jigsaw puzzle game with the theme of the Christmas Winnie Pooh. Drag and drop the pieces to their exact locations. In this game you have a total of 12 christmas Winnie Pooh themed images. You need to start from the first image and to unlock the next one. You have three modes for each image: Easy mode with 25(5x5) pieces, medium mode with 49(7x7) pieces, and hard mode with 100(10x10) pieces.
