## How to play

- Drag and place blocks onto the 8x8 board. Complete full rows or columns to clear blocks and earn points.
- Classic Mode: Play endlessly and beat your high score.
- Diamond Quest: Clear blocks strategically to collect all diamonds and finish the level.
- Clear the Board: Use limited moves wisely to remove every block on the board.
Plan ahead, think carefully, and enjoy the satisfying puzzle challenge!
