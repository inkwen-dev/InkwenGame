## About

Dice Helix Drop is a fun and addictive one-touch 3D arcade game where you guide a falling ball through spinning helix stacks. Hold to smash through platforms, avoid obstacles, and build powerful combos to break black blocks. The longer you hold, the faster and stronger your drop becomes! Bounce, crash, and blast your way down through over 300 exciting levels filled with colorful visuals and smooth, optimized performance for all devices. Easy to play but challenging to master, Dice Helix Drop will keep you hooked for hours.
