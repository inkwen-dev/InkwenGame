## How to play

Controls:
Desktop: Click and drag to move puzzle pieces.
Mobile: Touch and drag to move pieces.
How to Play:
Drag pieces to arrange them. Adjacent pieces that belong together will snap and lock.
Complete the full image before the timer runs out.
Power-ups:
Hint (Lightbulb): Automatically moves a piece to its correct spot.
Peek (Eye): Shows the completed image for a few seconds.
Freeze (Snowflake): Pauses the timer for 10 seconds.

## About

Jixora is a relaxing jigsaw solitaire puzzle game where you piece together beautiful artwork. Journey through 100 unique levels with increasing difficulty, or unlock Free Play mode to replay your favorites with custom grid sizes. Race against the clock to earn stars, use strategic power-ups like Hints and Time Freezes, and immerse yourself in a cinematic atmosphere with soothing music.
