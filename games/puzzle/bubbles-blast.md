## About

Bubbles Blast is a fast-paced neon bubble shooter set in deep space. Aim and fire colorful glowing bubbles to match 3 or more of the same color and pop them for points. Chain combos for massive scores, trigger chain reactions that send floating bubbles crashing down, and master powerful power-ups: the explosive Bomb, the row-wiping Lightning, and the color-copying Rainbow. With 40 handcrafted levels of increasing difficulty, earn up to 3 stars per stage, unlock 18 achievements, and climb the leaderboard. The grid descends &mdash; stay sharp or its game over!
