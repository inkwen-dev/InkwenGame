## About

Bubble Warriors is a bubble shooter game where player needs to collect key for entering into next mission. Army of bubbles is moving towards you constantly and you have to eliminate them by shooting bubbles in order to make a match of three or more before reaching to your attack base. Some Iron bubble warriors can be removed only through blasting bombs or running spikes. Try to finish your mission in given time. Saved time will give you extra reward points.
