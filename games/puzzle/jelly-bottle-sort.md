## How to play

Tap a bottle to select it, then tap an empty bottle or one with the same color on top to pour. Fill each bottle with one color. Use Undo or Restart to try another route. Hints show a useful move; an extra bottle helps but limits that attempt to two stars. Open the pause menu to skip an uncompleted level for 100 coins, or watch a rewarded video when available if you have fewer coins. Skipping grants one star and no coins. On desktop, use arrow keys to focus controls and Space to select or pour.
