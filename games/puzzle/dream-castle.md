## How to play

1. Match three or more identical items (apples, bells, crystals) in a row to collect them.
2. Each successful combo earns you coins and magical energy.
3. Spend coins on restoring the castle: clear debris, repair walls, buy furniture.
4. Talk to characters, complete tasks, and unlock new rooms.
5. Use bonuses and boosters to pass difficult levels.
