## About

Roll, match, and merge your way to the highest score in Dice Merge! Combine three or more dice with the same number in any direction to create higher values and keep the board alive. Plan your moves carefully as every merge opens new opportunities - but once no combinations remain, the game ends. Simple to learn yet deeply strategic, Dice Merge challenges your logic, foresight, and decision-making with every placement. How long can you keep merging before the board fills up?
