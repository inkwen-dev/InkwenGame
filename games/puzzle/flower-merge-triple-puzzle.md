## How to play

Tap to collect flowers and place them into the merge tray. Match three identical flowers to make them disappear and free up space. Clear all flowers to complete each level. Plan your moves carefully—if the tray fills up, the game is over. Use boosters when you need help overcoming tricky puzzles.
