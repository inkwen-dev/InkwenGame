## How to play

Tap – Mark a cell with an X.
Double Tap – Place a dog in a cell.
Drag – Mark multiple cells quickly.
Swipe – Move across cells to mark them.
Undo – Reverse your last move.
Hint – Get help with the next move.

## About

Get ready for an exciting challenge with a thrilling twist on two classic games! Dive into a captivating puzzle that merges the strategic thinking of Minesweeper with the number-crunching fun of Sudoku, all while uncovering the hidden map of your dog's favorite territory.
Can you solve it and reveal where your furry friend's adventures lie?
