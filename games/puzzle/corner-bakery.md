## How to play

Click or tap a kitchen producer to create an item. Drag two identical items together to merge them into the next recipe. You can also select one item, then click or tap its matching partner. Complete orders with the requested items to earn coins and experience. Use coins to improve your kitchen and restore neighborhood locations. Follow story tasks, talk to neighbors, and choose your replies. Store items to free up the board. Energy refills over time; an optional rewarded video can give you 20 extra energy. Play with a mouse or touch controls.

## About

Welcome to Corner Bakery, a cozy merge game about good food and the people next door. Combine matching pastries and drinks, fill your neighbors’ orders, and bring a neglected bakery and its neighborhood back to life. Meet seven characters, discover hidden keepsakes, and make dialogue choices across eight story chapters. Improve your kitchen, unlock recipes and explore six illustrated locations at your own pace.
