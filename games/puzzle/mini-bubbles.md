## About

Mini Bubbles is a platform game where you have to pop bubbles to jump higher and reach the goal. It was inspired by some classics of the NES era with retro-style graphics and mechanics, with a flexible difficulty aiming to be an enjoyable and relaxing experience, although some skill is needed to complete the game.
