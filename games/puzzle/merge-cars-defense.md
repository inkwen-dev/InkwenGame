## About

Expand your garage, purchase upgrades, complete missions, and unlock new opportunities. Earn resources while you are away and use them to strengthen your defenses. Plan your vehicle placement, upgrade your cars at the right time, and prepare to face powerful opponents. Simple controls make it easy to begin, while steady progression provides new goals and rewards.
