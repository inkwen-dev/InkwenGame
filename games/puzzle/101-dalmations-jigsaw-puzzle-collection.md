## About

Who but little mischievous spotted puppies is so fond of all sorts of puzzles? Obviously, many of the regulars on our gaming portal. It is for them that the clever developers have created a new game from the series "101 Dalmatians". There are three unusual puzzles in it. Their elements do not need to be rearranged, just twist the pieces of the mosaic to assemble the whole picture. The main difficulty of puzzles with cute puppies is to collect them as quickly as possible. Under the incendiary music, it's easy. Who of the gamers will be able to become the first in our standings, will decide a fair competition.
