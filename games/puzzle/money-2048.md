1 Swipe to move all items on the board 2 Merge two matching items to create a higher-value item 3 Keep merging to unlock new money treasures 4 Dont let the board fill up 5 Reach the highest score possible

## About

Money 2048 is a fun and relaxing puzzle game where you merge matching money items to create bigger and more valuable ones. Start with small coins and keep combining them to unlock wallets, cash bundles, money bags, diamonds, and more. Think ahead, make smart moves, and see how far you can go. Easy to play but very challenging to master.
