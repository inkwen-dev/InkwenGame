## How to play

Save the Jungle Paradise! Step into the role of a pirate parrot shooter and blast colorful bubbles from your cannon to match 3+ of the same color—reds, blues, yellows, and specials—triggering chain reactions to pop clusters and clear encroaching bubble walls before they reach the bottom, collecting gems, coins, and power-ups to hit star and trophy goals in each tropical level!

**Desktop Controls:**  
Mouse: Drag to aim (dotted line shows trajectory), release to shoot.

**Mobile Controls:**  
Touch: Drag to aim, release/tap to shoot; tap barrels for power-ups.

## About

Traffic Puzzle Game Linky is a captivating logic puzzle game set in a lush forest meadow, where players connect pairs of numbered construction blocks with precisely the exact count of road segments—no more, no less—to link all cities and clear the board within time limits, earning stars and trophies in increasingly challenging levels tracked by a dynamic timer and handy undo feature.

《交通益智游戏 Linky》是一款引人入胜的逻辑益智游戏，背景设定在郁郁葱葱的森林草地上。玩家需要连接成对的编号建筑方块，并精确计算道路段的数量——不多不少——以连接所有城市，并在限定时间内清除棋盘，在动态计时器和便捷的撤销功能跟踪的、难度不断增加的关卡中赢得星星和奖杯。

### 怎么玩

拯救丛林天堂！化身海盗鹦鹉射手，用你的大炮发射五彩缤纷的泡泡，匹配三个或三个以上相同颜色的泡泡——红色、蓝色、黄色以及特殊颜色——即可触发连锁反应，消除成簇的泡泡，并在泡泡墙到达底部之前将其清除。收集宝石、金币和能量道具，在每个热带关卡中达成星星和奖杯目标！

**电脑端操作：** 鼠标：拖动瞄准（虚线显示弹道），松开发射。

**移动端操作：** 触摸：拖动瞄准，松开/点击发射；点击木桶可获得能量道具。
