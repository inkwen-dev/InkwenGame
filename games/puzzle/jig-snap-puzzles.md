## How to play

Getting Started:
Drag and drop puzzle pieces to their correct positions on the board. When two correct pieces are next to each other, they'll automatically merge together!
Controls:

Drag: Move individual pieces or groups
Swap: Drop a piece on another to swap positions
Hint Button: Use hints to automatically place pieces (costs coins or limited per level)

Progression:

Complete puzzles to earn ⭐ stars and 💰 coins
Collect enough stars to unlock new themed packs
Difficulty increases as you progress (3×3 → 4×4 → 5×5)
First completion of each level earns stars - replay anytime for more coins!

Tips:

Start by finding corner and edge pieces
Group similar colors or patterns together
Use hints wisely for tricky puzzles
Complete earlier packs to unlock harder challenges
