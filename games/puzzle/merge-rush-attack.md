## How to play

Build a strong enough crowd to break the wall at the end of each run. If your squad’s power is equal to or higher than the wall’s HP, you win the level and earn coins. If not, the run fails and you can retry (or watch an ad for a soft boost).

The crowd runs forward automatically.
Tap/click the screen to spawn more units.
Merge 3 identical units into 1 stronger tier (unlock merge and upgrades in the Shop with coins).
Reach the wall and compare your power vs its HP.
Spend coins between runs to unlock higher merges, auto-merge, and power upgrades. From level 5, manage overheat — tapping too fast fills the heat bar and can weaken your squad.

Controls:

Tap / Left click — spawn units
Merge button — merge 3 matching units (when available)
Shop button — open upgrades
Overheat Shop — buy heat defenses or claim crystal rewards
No keyboard movement — the squad runs on its own; the game is designed for touch and mouse click

组建一支足够强大的队伍，在每次挑战结束时突破墙壁。如果你的队伍战力等于或高于墙壁的生命值，你将赢得关卡并获得金币。否则，挑战失败，你可以重试（或观看广告获得少量增益）。

队伍会自动向前冲锋。
