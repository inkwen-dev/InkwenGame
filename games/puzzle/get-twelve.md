## About

Get Twelve is a puzzle game where you need to pair blocks with same numbers. If you combine two identical numbers they will increase by one. You can move the number plates across the board to merge two identical numbers. The goal is to match the numbers until you get the number 12. Try to do that. Game play time is unlimited. The game will end if you do not have more moves and you have not achieved the goal to get number 12.
