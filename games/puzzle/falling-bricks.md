## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

In the new exciting game Falling Bricks, you can test your reaction speed and agility. A black block will be located on the playing field below. You can move it right or left using the control keys. From above, blocks between which you will see passages will fall at different speeds. You will need to do so that your block would slip between objects through these passages. The longer you last doing these actions, the more points you get.
