Welcome to Color Path Game, a relaxing yet challenging puzzle adventure where colors, paths, and smart thinking come together. In Color Path Game, your mission is to draw the correct color paths without crossing or overlapping. Each level presents a new puzzle with multiple colors and tricky layouts that require careful planning and strategic moves. As you progress, puzzles become more complex and rewarding.
