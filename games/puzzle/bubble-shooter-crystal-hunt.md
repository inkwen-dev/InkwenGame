## About

Bubble Shooter Crystal Hunt is an exciting endless bubble-shooting puzzle game where players must collect specific target bubbles along with rare magical crystals that appear among the bubbles. Aim carefully and match three or more bubbles of the same color to clear space while the bubble wall slowly moves toward the border line, increasing tension with every shot. Crystals are hidden within bubble clusters and must also be collected to progress and boost scores.
