## How to play

Become the Maze Master! Take control of a fearless fighter and conquer the entire arena. Move across the field to paint every tile in your color while taking down all enemies standing in your way. Plan your route, clear the battlefield, and leave no enemy - or unpainted tile - behind!

Desktop Controls:
↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward.
↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward.
← (Left Arrow): Move left.
→ (Right Arrow): Move right.  

Mobile Controls:
Swipe Up: Move upward.
Swipe Down: Move downward.
Swipe Left: Move left.
Swipe Right: Move right.  

Objective:
Paint the entire playing field and defeat all enemies to complete the level.
