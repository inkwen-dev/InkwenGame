## About

Semi Trucks Jigsaw is a game where you need to drag the pieces of image with semi trucks to the right position to get the full image with Semi Truck. This game has 12 images and you need to play and to solve all images to win in this jigsaw game. Use a mouse to drag the pieces and to play this game. Use your finger if you play on mobile devices.
