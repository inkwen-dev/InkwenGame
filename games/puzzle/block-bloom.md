## How to play

Watch the conveyor. Colored blocks ride the belt across the top of the board.
Tap a shooter in the grid below. A shooter is only tappable when nothing is blocking it.
It slides into a free slot and starts firing automatically at every block on the belt that matches its color.
Mind your shot counter. Each shooter has limited ammo. When it runs dry, it disappears and frees its slot.
Keep your slots healthy. Slots are limited — a shooter whose color isn't on the belt just sits there taking up space.
Clear every block on the conveyor to win the level.

## About

Block Bloom is a colorful puzzle-shooter about smart timing and sharper planning.

A conveyor belt carries a stream of colored blocks across the board. Below it sits your grid of shooters — each one loaded with a color and a limited number of shots. Tap a shooter to send it into a firing slot, and it will automatically blast every matching block that rolls past.

The catch: you only have a handful of slots. Send in the wrong shooter and it sits there, clogging a slot with nothing to shoot. Fill every slot with dead weight and the board jams — game over. Clear the whole conveyor and the level is yours.

### 介绍

《方块绽放》是一款色彩缤纷的益智射击游戏，考验玩家的精准时机把握和周密策略。

传送带运送着源源不断的彩色方块穿过游戏区域。传送带下方是你的射击器阵列——每个射击器都装载着一种颜色和有限的射击次数。点击射击器即可将其送入射击槽，它会自动击碎所有滚动经过的同色方块。

但问题在于：射击槽数量有限。如果送错了射击器，它就会卡在那里，无法射击。如果所有射击槽都被无效方块填满，游戏就会卡住——游戏结束。清除传送带上的所有方块，即可通关。
