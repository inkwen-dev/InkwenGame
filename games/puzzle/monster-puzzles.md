## About

Monster Puzzles is a cute puzzle game with all funny and scary monsters. Here is the season of Halloween where you see lots of similar items like pumpkins, hats. eyeballs, skeletons, spiders, and more scary stuff. Here in this game select any scary item and try to solve the jigsaw puzzle by matching all the pieces. Complete all the items and enjoy the game.
