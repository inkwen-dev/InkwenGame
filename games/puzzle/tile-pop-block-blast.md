## How to play

🖥️ Desktop (Mouse)
Select & Drag: Click and hold Left Mouse Button on any of the 3 available blocks at the bottom of the screen, then drag it onto the grid.
Place Block: Release the mouse button over valid highlighted grid cells to drop the block.
📱 Mobile (Touch Screen)
Select & Drag: Touch and hold your finger on any block shape at the bottom, then slide your finger to position it on the grid.
Place Block: Lift your finger when the block aligns over open grid cells.
