## How to play

Collect every red diamond in a level to unlock the exit, then step onto the exit to finish. Watch your energy, each move costs one, and running out restarts the level, so plan a route that grabs every diamond without wasting a step.

Move Bloop with the Arrow Keys or WASD.

## About

Bloop is a brainy isometric maze puzzler. Each level is a small grid, roll Bloop around, collect every diamond to open the exit, then reach it before your energy runs out. Every step costs energy, so the clever route always wins.

Across four themed worlds: Splash Garden, Frankenstein Lab, Wonderland and Lost Pyramid and 130+ levels, paths that look obvious rarely are.
