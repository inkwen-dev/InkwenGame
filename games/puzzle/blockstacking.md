## About

Block Stacking is a fun and addictive block puzzle game where your goal is to arrange blocks to complete full rows, clear the board, and earn high scores. Plan every move carefully, as one wrong placement can quickly fill the grid and end the game. Challenge your logic, improve your strategy, and see how long you can survive in this classic block-stacking puzzle adventure!
