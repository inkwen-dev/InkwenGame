## About

In this game you have to control the ball that moves along the horizontal path. There are spikes at both ends of the track that you should not touch or you will lose the game. The main goal is to avoid the shapes that come to the ball that you control. There will be blue balls in them, which if you touch them will make all the shapes disappear, or they will slow down depending on which ball you collect. So collect blue balls and avoid other shapes. Do that more time as you can.
