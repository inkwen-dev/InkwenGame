## About

Block Puzzle Tropical Story is a vibrant puzzle game that blends relaxing gameplay with a sense of island adventure. You’ll connect blocks, solve clever challenges, and unlock stunning tropical locations. As you explore lush jungles and serene beaches, each level rewards you with stars and new areas, turning every puzzle into a step deeper into paradise.

The booster system will help you in difficult situations, and the well-thought-out mechanics allow both beginners and experienced players to enjoy the game.
