## About

Send another picture collected from the puzzles to your personal collection. Here, before you start, you will be able to choose the difficulty. Each one has its own picture and the number of individual fragments. Having decided, proceed to the assembly. The main task is to combine the parts by controlling the computer mouse until you get a complete illustration. Once you have done it successfully, proceed to the next one and so on until you finish everything.
