## About

In &ldquo;Hungry Crab&rdquo; your objective is to guide the candy to the crab&rsquo;s mouth by strategically cutting ropes while collecting the stars. Simply swipe your finger across the screen to cut the ropes to play. The candy will then be released and begin its descent toward the crab. However, you must be careful not to let the candy flow away.
