## About

Press to Push, you are the block machine master, with total control over block pushers, block creators, and conveyor belts! The goal is to push the blocks into their designated craters using the different machines, but in this tricky puzzle game, nothing is as simple as it seems. Even though the game starts off simple, the more levels you complete, the more complex the game gets.
