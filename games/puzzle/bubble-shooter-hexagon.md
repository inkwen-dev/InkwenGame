## About

Bubble Shooter Hexagon is a classical bubble shooter game with hexa bubbles. Aim and release the bubble in order to group it with other similar bubbles. Your goal is to collect all the bubbles within the allowed time. Don’t let the bubbles reach the border. Complete all 48 levels to win this game.
