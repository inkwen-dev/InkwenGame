## About

Dive into a delightful puzzle experience with Candy Puzzle Match, where matching candies is all about tapping! Simply tap on groups of two or more adjacent candies of the same color to clear them from the board. The more candies you clear at once, the higher your score! Watch the board shift and cascade with every move as you complete deliciously fun objectives. With colorful visuals and satisfying chain reactions, this game is perfect for fans of casual and relaxing puzzle games.
