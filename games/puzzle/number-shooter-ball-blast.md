## How to play

Goal: survive as long as you can and score as high as you can. There is no finish line — waves keep coming
and the numbers keep growing. Your run ends the moment a ball touches your cannon.

Controls
- Hold the mouse button (or keep your finger on the screen) to play. Release to pause.
- Move the cannon: drag left and right, or use the Arrow keys (← →) or A / D.
- The cannon fires by itself, so aiming is all about where you stand.

Tips
- Score = the damage you deal. Chipping a big number down earns you every point of it, so you do not have to
  land the final hit to be rewarded.
- Catch the drops that fall from broken balls: ⚡ raises fire rate, 🔥 raises damage, and $ is coins.
- Special items come from the first ball of a wave — multi shot, shield, slow and magnet.
- Every wave showers bonus coins, and every 5th wave pays out a lot more.
- A shield blocks one hit. Without it, one touch ends the run.
- Spend coins in the Shop on permanent upgrades (fire rate, attack, item duration) — those stay forever.

## About

Number Shooter is an endless arcade shooter built around one idea: the number on the ball IS your score.

Numbered balls slide in from the walls and bounce around while your cannon fires nonstop. Every hit chips
their number down, and when it reaches zero the ball bursts into two smaller ones — so a single big ball
cascades into a screen full of targets. You never clear a level; the waves just keep coming and getting harder.

Between shots you grab drops that raise your fire rate and damage mid-run, and the coins you collect buy
permanent upgrades, 15 ball themes and 10 cannons that carry over to your next run.
