## About

Air Slip is a game where you need to control the beam. There is a green ball on the beam that moves along the beam as it rotates. From the top of the game come cubes in green and purple color. The object of the game is to catch the green cubes with the green ball on the beam and avoid the purple ones. The result counts only if you catch a green cube. With every purple cube you touch, you lose a life. You have the right to three errors, after that game ends.
