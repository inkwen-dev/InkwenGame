## How to play

Goal: Guide the worm to the heart in every level.

Controls: Arrow keys / WASD to move, Z to undo, P to switch worms after cutting. On mobile, drag/swipe to move or tap beside the head. Use on-screen buttons for Undo, Restart, Hint, and Switch.

Eat apples to grow, open number gates, push rocks, hold buttons to open doors, and use scissors to split the worm. Complete levels in fewer moves for 3 stars.

Get 10 free undos per level. Watch a short video for 10 more undos, 2 hints, or to skip a level.

## About

Wiggly Worm is a cozy snake puzzle game set in colorful underwater reefs. Guide a hungry worm through handcrafted mazes, eating apples, opening gates, pushing rocks, activating buttons, and splitting into two worms to solve tricky puzzles. Earn up to three stars, collect pearls, customize your worm with fun outfits, meet cute pets, and claim daily gifts. Use undo, hints, or optional videos to get extra help or skip a level.

### 怎么玩

目标：引导蠕虫到达每个关卡的心脏。

操作：方向键/WASD 移动，Z 撤销，P 剪切后切换蠕虫。在移动设备上，拖动/滑动移动或点击头部旁边。使用屏幕按钮进行撤销、重新开始、提示和切换。

吃苹果来成长，打开数字门，推动石头，按住按钮打开门，并使用剪刀将蠕虫剪开。用更少的步数完成关卡即可获得 3 颗星。
