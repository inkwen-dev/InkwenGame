Tap to move → Use one-tap controls to guide your pet’s head/character through the maze.
Avoid traps → Watch for dangerous obstacles and time your moves so you don’t get hit.
Collect treasures/items → Pick up the hidden treasures along the path while moving to the goal
Finish the maze → Reach the end to clear the level. Each level adds new twists and challenges.
