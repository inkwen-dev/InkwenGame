## About

Enjoy a fun and relaxing puzzle experience in Liquid Color Sort! Your objective is to sort colorful liquids into separate tubes until each tube contains only one color. Use logic, planning, and problem-solving skills to complete each level. While the rules are simple, the puzzles become more challenging as new colors and tube arrangements are introduced. Think carefully before each move and find the most efficient way to organize the liquids. With satisfying animations and calming gameplay, Liquid Color Sort is perfect for players who enjoy brain-training puzzles and relaxing challenges.
