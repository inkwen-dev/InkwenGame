## About

Bubble Shooter Vale is a fun and relaxing bubble shooting puzzle game where you match colorful bubbles and clear the board before time runs out. Use strategy, quick thinking, and precise shots to create huge combos. If the board gets filled, watch a reward ad to remove the last 3 lines of bubbles, or stop the timer and continue playing in relaxing mode.
