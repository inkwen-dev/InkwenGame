## About

In this pinball game you will play only with one ball. If you drop the ball the game will over. But is not that simple. You need to shoot in yellow stars before time is out or the star will explode and game will over. When black star is shown on the screen you need to avoid that star. If you hit the black star the pinball will explode and the game will over. Try to get more stars with one ball and have fun.
