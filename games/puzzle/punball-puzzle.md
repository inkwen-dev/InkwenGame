## About

Its time for the sleeping mages to awaken! The magic continent is at stake, so as a member of the Mages Guild, you have the important task of resisting evil and saving the Mages continent. Here, you are going to fight against the evil army to the end, resisting their wave after wave of invasion. You need to attack the enemies, but it is important to note that you should upgrade your power. A little inattention and you could be all but wiped out and have to start all over again!
