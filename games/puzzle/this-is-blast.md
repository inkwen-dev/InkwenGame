## How to play

Aim Carefully
Drag or move your aim direction and choose the best place to shoot the balls.
Shoot the Balls
Release the balls toward the puzzle area. Try to hit the correct blocks, balls, or targets.
Create Big Blasts
Match and merge similar items to create powerful blast effects. Bigger matches can clear more space.
Use Chain Reactions
One good shot can create multiple blasts. Plan your shot so it clears many objects at once.
Clear the Level Goal
Every level has a puzzle layout. Your main goal is to clear the required targets and complete the level.
Use Power-Ups When Needed
If the level becomes hard, use boosters or power-ups to clear difficult parts and save your game.
Think Before Every Move
The game is easy to play, but later levels need better strategy, timing, and smart aiming.
