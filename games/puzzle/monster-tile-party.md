## How to play

Select an uncovered tile to move it into the tray. When three identical tiles enter the tray, they are removed automatically. Clear all tiles from the board to win the level. If the tray fills before the board is cleared, the level ends. Use Undo, Shuffle, and Extra Slot boosters when you need help.

- Desktop: move the pointer and use the left mouse button to select tiles and UI buttons.
- Mobile: tap tiles and UI buttons with one finger.
- No keyboard controls are required.

## About

Monster Tile Party is a bright, family-friendly triple-tile puzzle game. Pick tiles from a layered board, collect three matching monster tiles, and clear every level before your tray fills up. Cheerful original characters, satisfying match effects, helpful boosters, daily rewards, and a growing level map make every quick round feel like a tiny party.
