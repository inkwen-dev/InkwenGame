## About

Route Digger is a 2D logic game. Dig tunnels and build a path for colorful balls to the pipes of the right color. Carefully! On some levels there are thorns that will prevent you from doing so. The game is quite difficult! Play for free in your browser. Fun game with colors and balls, that is Route Digger. You can be creative and dig your own routes. There is no just one right answer. However, beware of obstacle! Do not get stuck. The second challenging task is later with more balls. You have to match pipe and ball of the same color. The more balls is in the game the bigger challenge it is. Are you ready for digging? Do not hesitate and play right now! Have fun.
