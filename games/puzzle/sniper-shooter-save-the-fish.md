## How to play

Aim and launch bubbles toward the hexagonal structure. Impacts may trigger rotation, changing the direction and behavior of bubbles in real time. Use timing, angles, and structural movement to clear bubbles and complete level objectives.

## About

This game is a physics-based puzzle experience that reimagines the bubble shooter genre. Built around a rotating hexagonal structure, each level challenges players to adapt to changing angles, motion, and gravity. With an ocean-themed visual style and a light narrative about a crab rescuing a clownfish, the game delivers a fresh combination of strategy, physics, and immersive design.
