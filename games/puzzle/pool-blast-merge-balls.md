## How to play

Aim: Click/tap and drag to direct the aiming line.
Shoot: Release to launch the cue ball across the table.
Merge: Collide two balls of the same number/tier to merge them into the next bigger ball.
Survive & Score: Keep making successful merges before you run out of moves!
