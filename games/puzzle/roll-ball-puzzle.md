## About

The Roll Ball Puzzle is a challenging and entertaining game that requires strategic thinking and problem-solving skills. The objective is to maneuver a small ball through a maze or series of obstacles by tilting the game board or using controls to guide the ball towards its goal. Players must carefully plan each move to avoid falling into traps or dead ends while aiming to reach the destination in the least number of moves possible. With its addictive nature and varying levels of difficulty, the Roll Ball Puzzle offers a fun and engaging experience for players of all ages.
