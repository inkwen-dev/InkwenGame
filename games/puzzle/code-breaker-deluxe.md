## How to play

Crack the Code! Step into the role of a master codebreaker in Code Breaker Deluxe, the digital adaptation of the classic Mastermind puzzle developed by PLAYTOUCH. Your goal is to deduce the hidden sequence of 4 colored pegs (from 6 colors: yellow, green, red, white, blue, pink) using logic and iterative guesses under timed pressure with limited attempts (3 diamonds remaining)—win by cracking the code in as few guesses as possible to earn high scores on leaderboards!

**Desktop Controls:**  
Mouse: Click and drag colored pegs from the bottom row into the guess slots (row 1 shown in screenshot).  
Green Check Button: Click to submit guess and receive black/white key peg feedback.

**Mobile Controls:**  
Tap: Select and place colored pegs from the bottom row into the 4 guess slots.  
Green Check Button: Tap to confirm and submit your guess for feedback (as shown with first guess and one white peg).  
Lightbulb: Tap for hints.  
Restart (Loop Icon): Tap to reset the puzzle.

## About

Code Breaker Deluxe, developed by PLAYTOUCH, is a digital adaptation of the classic Mastermind code-breaking logic puzzle game, featuring a luxurious wooden aesthetic and intuitive mobile interface. Players must deduce a hidden sequence of 4 colored pegs (from 6 colors: yellow, green, red, white, blue, pink) through iterative guesses, receiving feedback via key pegs—black/white indicators for correct position/color but wrong position (as shown in the screenshot: first guess with one white peg confirmed by green checkmark)—under timed pressure with limited attempts, blending nostalgic deduction with modern single-player challenges, hints (lightbulb), shop, restart, and leaderboards for endless replayability.

### 怎么玩

破解密码！在 PLAYTOUCH 开发的经典益智游戏“Mastermind”的数字版《密码破解豪华版》中，化身密码破解大师。你的目标是在有限的尝试次数（剩余 3 个钻石）内，运用逻辑推理和反复尝试，推断出 4 个彩色木钉（6 种颜色：黄、绿、红、白、蓝、粉）的隐藏顺序——用尽可能少的尝试次数破解密码，在排行榜上赢得高分！

**桌面版操作：**

鼠标：点击并拖动底部一行的彩色木钉到猜测槽中（截图中显示第一行）。

绿色勾选按钮：点击提交猜测，并获得黑白钥匙木钉的反馈。

**移动版操作：**

点击：选择底部一行的彩色木钉，并将其放置到 4 个猜测槽中。

绿色勾选按钮：点击确认并提交你的猜测以获取反馈（如图所示，第一个猜测和一个白色木钉）。灯泡：点击获取提示。

重新开始（循环图标）：点击重置谜题。

### 介绍

由 PLAYTOUCH 开发的《Code Breaker Deluxe》是一款经典解谜逻辑游戏《Mastermind》的数字版，拥有奢华的木质外观和直观的移动界面。玩家需要通过反复猜测，推断出隐藏的 4 个彩色木钉（共有 6 种颜色：黄、绿、红、白、蓝、粉）的序列。关键木钉会提供反馈——黑色/白色指示器表示位置/颜色正确但位置错误（如截图所示：第一次猜测用一个白色木钉，绿色对勾确认）。游戏在有限的时间和尝试次数下进行，将怀旧的推理玩法与现代单人挑战、提示（灯泡）、商店、重新开始和排行榜等功能相结合，带来无限的重玩价值。
