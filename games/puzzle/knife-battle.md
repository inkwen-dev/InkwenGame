Knife Battle is an addictive game in which you need to upgrade knives and defeat enemies. Get knives in battles, create new ones and defeat all bosses!

## How to play

To create a new knife, simply combine two knives of the same type by dragging one onto the other.
Tap the screen to throw the knife at the enemy. Try to avoid hitting the knife you threw earlier.
