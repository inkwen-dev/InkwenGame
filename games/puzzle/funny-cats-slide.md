## About

Everyone loves kittens, but in this toy you do not need to play with them, but collect puzzles with them piece by piece! You will find 16 unique images with kittens, which are located in a variety of places. Try to collect at least one puzzle with them – on the one hand it is very exciting, but on the other hand, not everyone can cope with such a test. The fact is that the collection of puzzles is accompanied by a countdown. If you take too long to pick up the puzzles and put them into the original picture, then you will be left without a single star.
