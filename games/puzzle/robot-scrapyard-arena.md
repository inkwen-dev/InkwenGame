## How to play

Survive every wave and defeat the two bosses. Collect the parts that enemies and crates drop, then open the Workshop and drag one part onto an identical one to merge them into a higher tier. Equip your best parts and keep fighting!
Desktop:
WASD or arrow keys: Move
Mouse: Aim
Left / right mouse button: Fire left / right arm
Space or Shift: Dash
Q: Overdrive
Tab: Workshop
Enter: Start the next wave early
Esc: Pause

Mobile:
Left thumb: Move (drag anywhere on the left side)
Your arms aim and fire automatically
DASH and OVERDRIVE buttons on the right

## About

Robot Scrapyard Arena is a 3D top-down robot shooter. Salvage parts from the scrapyard, merge them into stronger ones, bolt them onto your robot, and survive 10 waves and two giant bosses. Every wave drops parts. Two identical parts merge into one a tier higher, from Mk I up to legendary Mk V and beyond. Mix 15 part types across five slots (two arms, core, head and legs) to build your robot.
