## About

Hide Or Seek is a puzzle action online game for boys for free. In this game, you will start the competition with others in a small maze. When seeking other people, you need to carefully observe some dust and footprints around the character as clues to find others. As a hider, you need to avoid the seeker's vision as much as possible, so you should immediately choose an appropriate route to avoid being found within the specified time. Have fun!
