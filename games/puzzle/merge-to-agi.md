## How to play

Tap a generator to make items. Drag two identical items together to merge them into the next tier. Hand in the items customers ask for to earn coins and XP, then spend coins in the Empire to build projects and unlock new districts. Works with mouse or touch.

## About

A cozy merge-2 puzzle about a garage AI startup. Merge hardware, code, data and coffee into better gear, fill orders for a cast of parody customers, and grow your lab into an AGI empire across ten districts while eight rival labs race you on the leaderboard.
