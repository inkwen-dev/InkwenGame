## How to play

Drag a hole to move it around the board and line it up under the marbles you want.
A hole only takes marbles that match its colour. Clear the board to finish a level.

Controls: click and drag with the mouse on desktop, tap and drag on touch.
Tap a power-up to use it — Magnet pulls loose balls into a hole, Claw drops any
single marble you tap, Shrinker cuts an oversized hole down to size.

Obstacles: fill the hole under ice to shatter it, collect hammers to break crates,
find a matching-colour key to open a lock, and peel layered marbles starting from
the inner ring.

## About

Color Ball Escape is a colour-matching puzzle. Marbles pour from towers onto a
crowded board, and you slide holes into position to catch them — but a hole only
swallows marbles of its own colour, so the order you clear them in decides whether
the board opens up or jams solid. Ice, crates, colour locks, one-way tiles and
layered marbles keep rewriting what the right order is.

彩球逃脱

《彩色弹珠逃脱》是一款颜色匹配益智游戏。弹珠从塔楼倾泻而下，落入拥挤的棋盘上，你需要滑动孔洞来接住它们——但孔洞只能吞噬同色弹珠，因此你清除弹珠的顺序决定了棋盘是会打开还是会卡住。冰块、箱子、颜色锁、单向方块和层叠的弹珠会不断改变正确的顺序。

### 怎么玩

拖动洞口即可在棋盘上移动，并将其对准你想要放置的弹珠下方。

洞口只能容纳与其颜色相同的弹珠。清空棋盘即可完成关卡。

操作方式：在电脑上使用鼠标点击并拖动，在触摸屏上点击并拖动。

点击道具即可使用——磁铁可以将散落的弹珠吸入洞口，爪子可以放下你点击的任意一颗弹珠，缩小器可以将过大的洞口缩小到合适的大小。

障碍物：填满冰块下的洞口即可打破冰块，收集锤子可以砸碎箱子，找到与洞口颜色相同的钥匙可以打开锁，以及从内环开始剥离多层弹珠。
