Category Match is a fun, relaxing, and brain-teasing puzzle game where you match cute memoji cards by their correct categories.

## How to play

Look at the memoji cards on the board.
Find cards that belong to the same category.
Tap and move each card to the correct group.
Match all memojis with the right category to clear the level.
Use your empty space carefully because some levels have limited space.
Think before placing a card, because the wrong move can block your next match.
Complete all category groups and win the level.
