## About

Dive into the colorful world of Easter Match 3 Saga, a fun and relaxing puzzle game filled with bright, falling Easter eggs! Tap on groups of two or more connected eggs to clear them from the board and create satisfying chain reactions. Keep an eye on the top of the screen to see your goal&mdash;destroy the required number of eggs to unlock the next level. With simple controls, vibrant visuals, and addictive gameplay, every level brings a fresh challenge and more egg-smashing fun!
