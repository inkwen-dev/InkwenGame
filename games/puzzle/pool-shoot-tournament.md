## How to play

The objective of  is to earn the highest possible score by shooting the bubbles. Ball Shooting Mechanics: In order to earn maximum points, you need to shoot down a large group of balls in one shot, taking into account the rules of ricochet and direct hit. The minimum number of balls to group is three balls of the same color. Loss occurs as soon as at least one ball from the impending wall touches the bottom.
