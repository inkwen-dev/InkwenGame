## About

The poor little bear is trapped, and he needs your help! Are you willing to help the little bear out of danger? The operation of the game is very simple, you only need to slide to cut the rope, and the bear will run to the exit by himself. But there are many dangerous traps in the room, and the little bear is so scared that he ignores the danger. Help Little Bear plan his escape route!
