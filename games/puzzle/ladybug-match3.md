## About

Move the ladybugs from left to right and change their colors by moving up and down. This fun game can be made from any device: smartphone, tablet, and PC. Don't be afraid and tap on this addictive game by ordering colored ladybugs, lines of three or more of the same color to get them off the screen as fast as you can.
