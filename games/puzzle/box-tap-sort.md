## How to play

How to play
Tap exposed cubes that match one of the three color orders at the bottom.
Each order shows how many cubes of that color you still need. Clear the whole structure to finish the puzzle.
Wrong colors go to your holding bin. Fill all 7 slots and the current puzzle resets.
Drag to rotate the stack. Pinch or scroll to zoom in and out.
Clear more puzzles than your rival before the 90-second timer ends.

## About

A head-to-head 3D color-sort puzzle. Dismantle candy-colored cube stacks by tapping cubes that match your active color orders. Wrong taps fill a limited holding bin — overflow resets the puzzle. Clear more structures than your rival before time runs out.

### 怎么玩

玩法介绍

点击底部三种颜色顺序之一对应的方块。

每种顺序会显示你还需要多少个该颜色的方块。消除整个方块即可完成拼图。

错误的颜色会进入你的收纳盒。填满所有 7 个格子后，当前拼图将重置。

拖动即可旋转方块堆。捏合或滚动即可放大或缩小。

在 90 秒倒计时结束前，消除的方块数量要比你的对手多。
