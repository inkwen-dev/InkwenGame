## About

It is a classical bubble shooter game with 48 challenging levels. In each level you will find a different challenge. To complete a level you must eliminate all the MARBLES from the screen. Eliminate marbles by shooting marbles to make groups of 3 or more marbles of the same color.. You must complete a level within allowed time. You will get better score if you complete the level early.
