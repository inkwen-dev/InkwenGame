## About

Candy Match Puzzle is an exciting and free-to-play online match-4 style game designed to bring endless fun and challenge to players of all ages! Your goal is simple but addictive &mdash; match as many colorful candies as possible to achieve the highest score. Connect four or more identical candies to clear them from the board and watch as sweet combos and chain reactions fill your screen with rewarding points.
