## How to play

Walk to the CAR PARTS stand and buy an engine, then open the WORKSHOP, pick a part and press PLACE. Add wheels, a fuel tank and a weapon, then press DRIVE. Desktop: WASD to walk, A/D or arrow keys to steer, W to speed up and S to brake. Mobile: the on-screen stick walks, the big pads steer, brake and speed up. Weapons fire automatically. Dodge wreckage, save durability and fuel, then spend the coins on new parts and drive farther.

## About

Zombies have taken over the world, and only your car can break through. Build it yourself in the workshop from engines, wheels, fuel tanks, armour and weapons: saws, spikes, machine guns, crossbows, shotguns and rockets. Weapons fire on their own while you drive down a road full of zombies, dodge wreckage and watch your fuel. The farther you go, the more money you earn and the more dangerous the zones ahead: a ruined city, a highway, a swamp, snowy mountains, a volcano and a cyber zone. Roll new parts at the stands, merge duplicates into rarer ones, unlock skills, fight a giant boss in raids and complete the whole collection. Progress is saved automatically.

### 怎么玩

走到汽车零件摊位购买引擎，然后打开维修站，选择一个零件并按下“安装”键。装上车轮、油箱和武器，然后按下“驾驶”键。电脑端：WASD 键行走，A/D 键或方向键转向，W 键加速，S 键刹车。移动端：屏幕上的摇杆控制行走，大按键控制转向、刹车和加速。武器自动开火。躲避障碍物，节省耐久度和燃料，然后用金币购买新零件，开得更远。
