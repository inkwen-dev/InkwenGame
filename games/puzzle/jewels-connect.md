## How to play

Tap on each pair of colorful jewels which can be connected by maximum 3 line segments without touching the other jewels

## About

A fun and addictive matching game to train your eyes and brain. You need to find all the pairs of jewels (by tapping them) within a certain amount of time. Features: - 3 difficulties (corresponding to the board sizes): Easy, Medium, Hard - Showing hints option - Shuffle option - Language support: English and Russian PLAY NOW!
