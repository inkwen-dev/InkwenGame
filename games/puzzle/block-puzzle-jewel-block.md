## How to play

Drag a jewel piece from the tray and drop it onto an empty area of the board. Fill a complete horizontal row or vertical column to clear it and earn points. Clear multiple lines and make consecutive clears to build bigger combos. The game ends when none of the available pieces can fit, so keep space open and plan ahead. Use the available boosters when needed and try to beat your saved high score.

Controls:
- Mouse: hold the left mouse button to drag a piece, then release to place it.
- Touchscreen: touch and drag a piece, then lift your finger to place it.
- Menu buttons: click or tap to pause, continue, restart, return home, or mute music and sound.
