## How to play

Click and drag with a mouse, or drag with your finger, across neighboring matching animals. Release to collect the chain and let new animals drop in. Reach the goals above the board within the move limit. Once introduced, chains of four or more launch extra paws. Clear animals next to numbered ice to break it; metal blocks stay in place. Use Undo to rethink a move or Hint when you need help.

## About

Link matching cats, bears, and rabbits to collect your goals in 50 colorful puzzle levels. Build long chains to launch helpful paws, break through ice, and find paths around metal blocks before your moves run out.
