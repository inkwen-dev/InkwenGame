## How to play

**Blast the Bubbles Away!**  
**Embark on a tropical adventure in Bubble Shooter Island Quest and clear every bubble cluster from the jungle canopy before they reach the bottom!**  
**In this enchanting bubble shooter puzzle, aim and launch colorful bubbles—reds, blues, yellows, plus specials like eggs and coins—from your pirate parrot's cannon to match 3+ of the same color, triggering chain reactions to pop massive groups. Collect gems/coins for upgrades, hit star/trophy goals (e.g., 0/5 eggs), and conquer levels amid vines and barrels!**

**Desktop Controls:**  
**Mouse Drag/Aim:** Drag to aim the bubble from the cannon.  
**Mouse Click/Release:** Fire the bubble to match and pop.  
**Barrel Buttons:** Click power-ups (🌈 Rainbow, ⚡ Lightning, ❄️ Ice) when available.  
**|| Button:** Pause/Resume.  
**35 Shots:** Manage your limited ammo wisely.

**Mobile Controls:**  
**Drag to Aim:** Swipe/drag on screen to aim the next bubble.  
**Tap/Release:** Tap or release to shoot and match 3+.  
**On-Screen Barrels:** Tap power-ups (🌈, ⚡, ❄️) for special effects.  
**|| Button:** Pause/Resume gameplay.

## About

Bubble Shooter Island Quest is an enchanting bubble shooter puzzle game set in a lush jungle island paradise. Its core concept involves launching colorful bubbles from a pirate bird's cannon to match 3+ of the same color—reds, blues, yellows, and specials like eggs and coins—causing chain reactions to pop clusters and clear encroaching bubble walls before they reach the bottom. Unique elements include a charming red parrot pirate shooter, power-up barrels (rainbow, lightning, ice), gem/coin collection for upgrades, star/trophy goals per level, and tropical vibes with vines and barrels for immersive, relaxing yet addictive play.

### 介绍

《泡泡射击岛探险》是一款迷人的泡泡射击益智游戏，背景设定在一个郁郁葱葱的丛林岛屿天堂。游戏的核心玩法是操控海盗鸟发射五彩缤纷的泡泡，匹配三个或三个以上相同颜色的泡泡——红色、蓝色、黄色以及蛋和金币等特殊泡泡——引发连锁反应，消除成簇的泡泡，并在泡泡墙到达底部之前将其清除。游戏特色包括：可爱的红色鹦鹉海盗射击手、强化道具桶（彩虹、闪电、冰冻）、宝石/金币收集系统（用于升级）、每个关卡的星星/奖杯目标，以及藤蔓和木桶营造的热带风情，带来沉浸式、轻松又令人上瘾的游戏体验。
