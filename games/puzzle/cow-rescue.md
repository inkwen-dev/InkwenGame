## How to play

1. Tap screws of the same color to match and remove them.
2. Remove the wooden boards carefully to clear the path.
3. Plan your moves to avoid getting stuck.
4. Use helpful tools when you need assistance.
5. Complete levels and rescue the cow from every tricky puzzle!

## About

A cute cow is trapped behind a pile of wooden boards, and only you can rescue her! Match screws of the same color to remove them and carefully clear the wooden boards blocking the way. Plan your moves, solve tricky puzzles, and create a path for the cow to escape. With colorful screws, challenging board layouts, helpful tools, and a funny bird companion, Cow Rescue offers a fun and relaxing puzzle experience for players of all ages. Can you solve every puzzle and rescue the cow?
