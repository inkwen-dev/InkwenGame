## How to play

Goal of the Game:
Collect all the stars on the level by digging through the sand and guiding streams of water, lava, and steam along the correct path.

Controls:
🖱️ Left Mouse Button — hold and drag the cursor to dig through the sand
📱 Touch / Swipe — drag your finger across the screen to create paths for the elements

## About

Dig through the sand and guide flowing elements through level labyrinths to collect all the stars. Use element physics, plan the route carefully, and control the streams to activate mechanisms and open new paths. Simple controls make the game easy to understand from the first seconds, but each level requires more logic and precision.

Different elements appear throughout the levels — water, lava, and hot steam. Each behaves differently and opens new ways to solve puzzles.
