## How to play

Remove hearts by clicking on hearts with at least one heart of the same color next to it horizontally or vertically

## About

Click on blocks to remove them in this fun heart filled game. Remove hearts of the same color that are horizontally or vertically connected to each other. Plan ahead to clear out as many hearts as you can from each level. Use the information in the Projection box to see how much each removal is worth. Reach or pass each level's Target amount to go on to the next level. That's okay if you don't reach the target amount as you can always retry the level.
