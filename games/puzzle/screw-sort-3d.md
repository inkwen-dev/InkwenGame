## About

Welcome to Screw Sort 3D – a thrilling puzzle game for mechanics enthusiasts who enjoy unscrewing screws, bolts, and nuts! You’ll face complex mechanisms filled with screws and bolts that need to be sorted correctly. Carefully examine the structures, unscrew the screws, disassemble the elements, and sort them into the boxes using logic and attention. Each new challenge tests your spatial thinking and problem-solving skills.
