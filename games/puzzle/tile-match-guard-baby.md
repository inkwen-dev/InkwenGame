Protect the sleeping baby from noisy monsters! In Tile Match Guard Baby, solve fun tile-matching puzzles to keep the room quiet.

## How to play

1. Tap tiles to move them into the tray.
2. Match 3 identical tiles to clear them.
3. Clear all tiles before the tray fills up to win and guard the baby's sleep!
