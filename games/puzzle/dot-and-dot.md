## How to play

Use your mouse or touchscreen to drag and connect dots of the same colour. There should be no empty spaces on the map.

## About

Dot and Dot is a vibrant puzzle game where players connect matching dots without crossing paths.Challenge yourself across three exciting game modes:

Normal Mode: 5000 classic puzzles to master
Loops Mode: 1500 puzzles to connect dots back to their starting point
Bridges Mode: 1500 puzzles to cross paths strategically on special bridge tiles

Features designed to enhance your experience:

Daily Login Rewards: Earn hints for consecutive daily logins
Smart Auto-Complete: Double-tap to automatically complete the last remaining connection
U-Turn Detection: Visual warnings prevent invalid patterns
Colorblind Support: Toggle color labels (A-Z) for better accessibility
Hint System: Get help when you're stuck
Quick Reset: Restart any stage instantly

Test your logic and spatial skills with intuitive controls, right-click line removal, and progressively challenging puzzles!
