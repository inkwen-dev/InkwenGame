## How to play

Arrow keys to move player or use on-screen arrow if you are on a mobile device

## About

Find the 15 Christmas items and collect them by walking over them. Then go and see the Yeti who will let you through the igloo to the other side. Then you'll have only 60 seconds to find the little lost reindeer before you freeze in the winter snow. The reindeer will be in a different location every game. Sometimes he is easy to find and sometimes not. Good luck and Merry Christmas.
