## About

Overcome tricky obstacles, solve clever puzzles, and plan the perfect route to make sure everyone gets a treat. Each level challenges your logic, timing, and problem-solving skills. With simple controls and addictive puzzle gameplay, Go Find the Cake offers a sweet and entertaining brain-training adventure!
