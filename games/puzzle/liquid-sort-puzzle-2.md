## How to play

Objective: Sort all the mixed colored liquids so that each test tube holds exactly one solid color.

Controls:
Click or Tap on any tube to lift the top layer of colored liquid.
Click or Tap on another tube to pour the liquid into it.

Rules:
You can only pour water on top of another liquid if they share the exact same color AND if the destination tube has enough empty space.
You can always pour any colored liquid into an entirely empty tube.
If you get stuck, use the 'Undo' button to reverse your last move, or the 'Restart' button to reset the current level. Earn more of these power-ups by playing the Lucky Spin!

## About

Liquid Sort Puzzle is a highly relaxing yet mentally stimulating color-sorting puzzle game set in a vibrant jungle environment. The main idea is to sort mixed colored liquids across various test tubes until each tube contains only one uniform color. What makes this game unique is its soothing ASMR-like pouring animations combined with a rewarding "Lucky Spin" system, allowing players to earn 'Undo' and 'Restart' power-ups to conquer increasingly complex levels. It's the perfect blend of casual brain-training and satisfying visual feedback!

目标：将所有混合颜色的液体分类，使每个试管中只盛放一种颜色的液体。

操作：

您可以将任何颜色的液体倒入完全空的试管中。
