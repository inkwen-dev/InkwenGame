## About

Crush and match the monsters till you make enough points to decorate your Halloween Party dream. Here you will need to be very skilled and fast, one time you need to crush the head of monsters and the other time you will need to match them. Be fast, and earn more points! After that, use your points to buy different decorations for the party.
