## About

A thoughtful and serious online game in the form of a puzzle, where you need to put each piece in the designated place. The task is complicated by the fact that the base - the picture - is barely visible, and with each mouse click, the details begin to rotate, so before you stop the selection, once again carefully look and study everything, and only then click with an unwavering hand. There is no time limit, in case of difficulties, the game for children itself will prompt the move by placing the object in its cell. To assess whether your actions are correct, you can fix the part-it does not move, and the piece of the picture has become colored, so you can move on to the next part of the puzzle.

### 介绍

一款富有思考性和严肃性的在线拼图游戏，你需要将每一块拼图放置在指定的位置。任务的难度在于底图几乎不可见，且每次鼠标点击后，拼图块都会开始旋转，因此在确定选择之前，必须再次仔细观察和研究所有细节，然后才可坚定地点击。游戏中没有时间限制，如果遇到困难，游戏会自动提示下一步，通过将物体放置在对应格子中来引导你。为了判断你的操作是否正确，你可以固定某一部分——它将不再移动，而拼图的这一部分也会着色，这样你就可以继续进行下一部分的拼图了。
