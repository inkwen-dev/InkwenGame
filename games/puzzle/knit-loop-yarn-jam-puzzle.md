## How to play

Tap yarn pieces and move them to the right places. Your goal is to sort the threads, untangle wool loops and free all pieces on the level.

Look at the color, order and direction of each thread. If a yarn piece is blocked, move the pieces that stand in the way first. Use logic and plan a few moves ahead to open the correct path.

Complete levels one by one, build the right combinations and take your time. This is a relaxing 3D puzzle: think, sort, untangle and enjoy the cozy knitting mood.

The further you play, the more interesting the tasks become. More yarn, loops and tangled pieces appear, so plan every move carefully.
