## About

Welcome to Fruits Crush Saga! This is an amazing Match3 Fruits puzzle game with tons of challenging levels. before the level start, you will see the mission pop-up! Connect 3 or more identical fruits to feed the little baby panda! When you connect 6 or more fruits you will get some power-ups. Let's feed the panda!
