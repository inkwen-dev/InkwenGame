## How to play

Reorganize the chaotic board so that every column (stack) contains blocks of only one matching color.

## About

Dive into the colorful and satisfying world of Stack Sort! Welcome to Stack Sort: Build Color Stacks, the ultimate brain-teasing puzzle game designed to relax your mind and test your logic. Your mission is simple but highly addictive: bring order to the colorful chaos! You will start with a grid of mixed-up blocks, and it is up to you to carefully shift and sort them until every single color has its own dedicated stack.

With vibrant 3D graphics, smooth animations, and incredibly satisfying gameplay, Stack Sort is the perfect way to unwind after a long day or give your brain a quick workout. As you progress, the puzzles get trickier, requiring clever strategies and sharp spatial awareness. Can you master the art of the perfect sort?
