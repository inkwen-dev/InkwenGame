## About

Dogs Connect Deluxe is a classic puzzle game, inspired by popular Chinese Block removal game, Mahjong. In this you need to eliminate all the tiles appearing on the board. If 2 identical tiles can be connected using 3 or less straight lines, both will be eliminated. To connect them, simply tap on those tiles. There are 15 challenging levels in this game. Complete a level before time bounds to get extra bonus.
