## About

So the holidays have come, now only memories remain. Fortunately, various cameras have been invented now and the brightest moments can be captured and left in memory forever. The three holidays that all Pou's friends gathered for were very bright and there are just a lot of memories in the memory. Choose any of these memorable moments in this game and try to lay it out with the help of small puzzles.
