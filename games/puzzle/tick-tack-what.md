## How to play

Simply click on any active cell to place your X, and try to form a line of three—but watch out as the cheating AI uses unexpected tricks and twists to disrupt your strategy!

## About

Think you have mastered Tic-Tac-Toe? Think again! In this mind-bending twist on the classic game, you are playing against an AI opponent that absolutely refuses to play by the rules.
As you progress through increasingly complex and irregular board shapes, the bot will use an arsenal of dirty tricks to stop you from winning. From erasing your precious X's and locking cells, to literally dropping bombs on the board and faking its own victories! You'll need to stay one step ahead of its gravity-shifting, glitching, and rule-breaking mechanics.
Can you outsmart a machine that constantly cheats? Test your logic, adapt to unpredictable board mutations, and prove that human ingenuity can beat an unfair AI!
