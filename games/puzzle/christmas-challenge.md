## About

WOW Santa have a lot of challenges this Christmas. This time the gifts are frozen, so he need to broke the ice with snowball and to collect the presents that belong to kids. But the presents move around and they are not static, so it is more difficult to hit them. Use mouse to aim and shoot and have fun with this interesting Christmas game.
