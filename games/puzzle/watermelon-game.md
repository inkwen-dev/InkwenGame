## How to play

Embark on an exciting adventure of fruit collection, where you'll connect identical fruits, fostering their evolution and preventing them from falling out of the box. There's also an interesting aspect: when identical fruits collide, they transform into a new kind, larger in size. Can you develop a strategy leading to the appearance of a massive watermelon?
