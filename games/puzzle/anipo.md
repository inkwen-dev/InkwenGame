## About

Anipo is a light yet challenging puzzle game where you control a character with a unique color to move across land pieces on the map. Each step leaves a color on the land tile you pass through, and your task is to calculate a logical route to color the entire area and complete the level. The levels are designed with variety, requiring logical thinking and dexterity in every move. Anipo offers a relaxing experience suitable for all ages.
