## How to play

- Drag anywhere to rotate the cube and see all six faces.
- Tap an arrow to make it slide off in the direction it points.
- An arrow escapes only if nothing blocks its path — remove blockers first.
- Clear every arrow to finish the level. Watch your hearts — a blocked tap costs one!

## About

Arrow Escape 3D is a calm 3D puzzle game played on the surface of a cube. Tap a colorful arrow to send it sliding off the cube — but it can only escape if its path is clear. Rotate the cube to spot every arrow, clear the blockers in the right order, and empty the whole cube. 1000 levels of bite‑sized, satisfying puzzles await.
