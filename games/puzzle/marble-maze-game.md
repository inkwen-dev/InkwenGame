## About

Players rotate a circular maze packed with small moving balls, guiding a single ball toward the center. The goal is to carefully tilt and rotate the maze so the main ball drops perfectly into the vertical cylinder in the middle. Each level increases difficulty with tighter paths, faster movement, and more obstacles to avoid. With smooth controls, clean visuals, and satisfying physics, this rotation maze challenge offers a fun mix of skill, timing, and strategy. Ideal for fans of puzzle, balance.
