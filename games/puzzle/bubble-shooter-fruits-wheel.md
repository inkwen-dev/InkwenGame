## About

This is a special bubble shooter game in which you will find a rotating wheel of fruits. To complete the game you need to collect all the fruits on the wheel. Shoot the fruits towards the wheel to make the group of at least three bubble of the same color. Your points are reducing with the time, So finish the game early to save more points.
