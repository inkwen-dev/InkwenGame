## How to play

Train units and merge them to get stronger units, start the mission and clear the wave to get extra coins, these coins will help you train more units. 
Make you place most elite units on defense positions as they the ones that will attack at the enemies. Use power ups wisely and timely as they have a cool down.

Desktop Controls:
Use mouse click to interact with game ui to train units.
Drag and drop units of same level to merge them
Drag and drop units to move them on different slots

Mobile Controls:
Use screen touch to interact with game ui to train units.
Drag and drop units of same level to merge them       
Drag and drop units to move them on different slots

### 怎么玩

训练并合并部队以获得更强大的部队。开始任务并清除一波敌人即可获得额外金币，这些金币可用于训练更多部队。

将最精锐的部队部署在防御位置，因为它们将负责攻击敌人。谨慎且适时地使用强化道具，因为它们有冷却时间。

桌面版操作：

使用鼠标点击与游戏界面互动以训练部队。

拖放相同等级的部队即可合并它们。

拖放部队即可将它们移动到不同的位置。
