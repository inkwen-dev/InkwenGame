## About

In this game you have to make a tower of gifts. Touch the gift held by the pliers to release it and drop it on the other gifts. Try to keep the gifts from falling on top of each other without falling to the floor. Center and choose the right moment to release the gift. Earn maximum points and make the biggest tower of gifts. Tap on the screen to play this game called release the gift boxes.
