## How to play

Tap or click any uncovered animal tile to move it into the tray above the board. As soon as three identical animals meet in the tray, they merge and free up space. Tiles covered by other tiles stay locked until you clear the ones above them. Clear the whole pyramid to win the level. If the tray fills up with animals that do not match, the run ends, but you can rescue it with coins. Undo and shuffle boosters help when you get stuck; when you run out, watch a short ad to get more. Mouse or touch only, no keyboard required.

## About

Happy Zoo: Mahjong Triple Match is a cozy mahjong-style puzzle about matching three identical animals. Clear layered tile pyramids by sending animals into your tray - every three of a kind merge and vanish. 130 handcrafted levels across four zoo themes, 30 animal species to discover, daily quests and a collection album that fills up as you play. Easy to start, hard to put down.
