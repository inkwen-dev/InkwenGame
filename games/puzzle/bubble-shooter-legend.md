Tap and drag to change direction Release to shoot

## About

Aim, match and smash all the colorful bubbles in this relaxing color matching adventure. To break the bubbles, you need to connect three or more bubbles of the same color together and then launch the bubble you are given into a bunch of magic bubbles stacked in the play area. Try to break all the bubbles with as few shots as possible. Unlimited levels to play. Enjoy!
