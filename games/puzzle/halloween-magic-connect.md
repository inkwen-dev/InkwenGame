## About

Aim, match and smash all the colorful monster bubbles in this relaxing color matching adventure. To break the bubbles, you need to connect three or more bubbles of the same color together and then shoot the bubble you are given into a bunch of monster bubbles stacked in the play area. Try to break all the bubbles with as few shots as possible. Enjoy! Features: - Unlimited levels to play - Languages support: English and Russian
