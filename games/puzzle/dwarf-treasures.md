## About

Dwarf Treasures is a deep and captivating puzzle game that transports you to the heart of mysterious mountain caves. Your task is to navigate hundreds of unique levels, clearing the path to treasure by smashing ice blocks and overcoming other treacherous obstacles hidden deep within the earth. But simply matching stones and gems isnt enough! Discover new locations, uncover the secrets of the dwarven clan, and amass untold treasures in this epic adventure puzzle game, where strategy, luck, and fun combine perfectly!
