## How to play

Slide on the screen to move the big ball and tap to rotate

## About

Ball Catcher is a puzzle game for kids. At this game you need to have good reflex. The goal is to catch the small balls with big ball. You can move the big ball horizontal on left or right.The ball have two part with different colors and the ball can rotate. The small balls are with different colors too. So you need to catch the brown balls with the brown part of the big ball and the green balls with green part. It is little bit complicated but it is funny and interesting.
