## About

Roll and Balance your ball in the sky. Reach the destination without falling. Select your Rolling Ball and start the Sky Race. Collect all the coins on your way and you can unlock the balls. Do not fall and try to balance between the path. Always try not to fall and reach the end point. This is an adventure based game and you have to use your balancing skills and reach the target. Choose your path and be sure to roll properly without falling.
