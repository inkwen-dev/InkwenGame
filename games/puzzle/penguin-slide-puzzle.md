## About

We offer you a classic puzzle game. In this game, to add a picture, you need to arrange the parts of the picture in the correct sequence. The difference from puzzles is that all the parts of the picture are in a limited space and you can not take them out. You will have to move the parts of the picture to build them in the correct sequence.
