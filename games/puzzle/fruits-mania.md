## About

Fruit Mania is a colorful match &amp; tap puzzle game for all ages. Your mission is to help the cute little lemming collect the right fruits! On the board, you&rsquo;ll see plenty of smiling fruit blocks (red strawberries, blue blueberries, yellow lemons, green watermelons, and more). The player must tap groups of same-colored fruits to clear them and earn points. The larger the group of fruits you tap, the higher your score and combo bonus! At the bottom right, the game shows the fruit needed (for example, a red strawberry) that you must collect to complete the level&rsquo;s goal.
