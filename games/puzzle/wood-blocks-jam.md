## How to play

Wood Blocks Jam is easy to learn and fun to master:

📱 On mobile: Drag blocks with your finger and drop them into the matching color gates.

🖱️ On desktop: Click and hold the left mouse button to grab a block, then drag it to the correct gate.

Your goal is to clear the board by matching each colored block with its corresponding gate. As you progress, the puzzles get trickier — introducing new mechanics, obstacles, and challenges. Think ahead, plan your moves carefully, and don’t forget to use boosters if you’re stuck!

## About

Wood Blocks Jam is a relaxing logic puzzle game with colorful wooden blocks. Your goal is to drag each block into a gate of the same color. No time limits — just calm, thoughtful gameplay.

Enjoy thousands of handcrafted levels with increasing difficulty and new mechanics to discover. Use powerful boosters to help you solve tough puzzles:
🔧 Saw – cuts through annoying blocks,
🔨 Hammer – smashes any single block,
✨ Magic – removes 3 random blocks.

Perfect for players of all ages, Wood Blocks Jam combines strategy, color-matching, and satisfying visuals in one cozy package.
