Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

You are a little (but fat!) cute flightless chicken that is hungry and needs to eat as much as it can! But because you can’t fly, you have to walk on the ground and collect the foods that drop from the sky. No one knows who is dropping these food, just eat them and don’t ask any questions! But pay attention because there also other objects among these foods and if they hit you, you will lose. So do your best and eat as much as you can.
