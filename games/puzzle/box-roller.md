## About

Box Roller is an exciting puzzle game where you control a wooden box rolling through terrain to land precisely in the target hole. Each level requires you to calculate the direction, time your movements accurately, and avoid falling off course. The gameplay is simple yet challenging, increasingly addictive, providing a satisfying sense of achievement with each completed level.
