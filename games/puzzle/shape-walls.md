## About

Shape Walls is a fun and challenging puzzle game where you arrange shape pieces to reveal hidden objects. Tap to rotate the pieces and fit them into the correct outline. With increasing difficulty, each level tests your logic, observation, and spatial skills. Enjoy a relaxing yet brain-teasing experience as you complete unique puzzles. No time limits&mdash;just pure problem-solving fun!
