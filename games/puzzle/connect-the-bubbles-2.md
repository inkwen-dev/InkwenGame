## About

Connect the Bubbles is a captivating puzzle game where you strategically link colorful bubbles to clear the board and score big. With limited moves, plan your connections wisely to unleash powerful power-ups like Bombs and Rockets. Engage in endless fun, plotting moves and popping bubbles in this vibrant, brain-teasing adventure.
