## About

This little kitty got stuck on top of a wobbly tower and its up to you to get her down safely. Click the boxes to clear them away, one block at a time, but watch out: red blocks, hammers and other tricky obstacles will mess up your plan if you touch them. Only the grass block stays. Plan your moves, clear the stack, and help Kitty land safe and sound. Simple to play, surprisingly tricky to master!
