## How to play

Draw One Line to Save the Farm!
Become a pipe-connecting master in Happy Farm 1 Line Puzzle! In this charming one-line puzzle game, your only goal is to draw a single continuous line that connects all the water pipes on the farm and makes the water flow. With clever spatial thinking and just one stroke, you’ll solve every level and bring life back to the vibrant cartoon farm filled with blooming flowers, cute animals, and relaxing scenery.
Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Click and drag to draw one continuous line.
Left Mouse Button: Hold to draw, release to finish the line.
Mobile Controls:
Finger: Tap and drag across the screen to draw one continuous line.
No extra buttons — just one smooth stroke to win!

## About

Happy Farm 1 Line Puzzle is a charming and addictive one-line puzzle game where players draw a single continuous line to connect and activate all the water pipes on the farm. Set in a vibrant, colorful cartoon farm filled with blooming flowers, cute animals, and relaxing scenery, the core gameplay revolves around clever pipe-connecting logic that challenges your spatial thinking. With its relaxing atmosphere, satisfying one-stroke mechanics, and progressively tricky levels, it brings the joy of farming and puzzle-solving together in one delightful package.
