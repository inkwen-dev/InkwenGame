## How to play

The board is covered in tiles, and some of them carry a number. Every number is
the size of the rectangle it belongs to.

1. Find a number and work out where its rectangle can fit. A 6 needs six
   squares: 1x6, 2x3, 3x2 or 6x1.
2. Drag across the grid to draw that rectangle, and release to place it.
3. The tiles it covered turn into part of the hidden picture.
4. Repeat until every square on the board is inside a rectangle.

Rules:
Each rectangle covers exactly one number.
That number is how many squares the rectangle covers.
Rectangles never overlap.
Every square ends up inside one.

A rectangle that does not belong is refused. Nothing is lost and nothing is
counted against you - draw another one.

Stuck? The hint button opens one rectangle for you. You have three, and after
that a short video buys another.

Desktop Controls:
Mouse drag: Hold the left button and drag across the grid to draw a rectangle.
Release: Place the rectangle.
Hint button: Opens one rectangle for you.
Restart button: Clears the board and starts the same puzzle again.
Settings button: Sound, music, and ten colour themes.

Mobile Controls:
Touch drag: Press and drag across the grid to draw a rectangle.
Lift your finger: Place the rectangle.
Hint button: Tap to open one rectangle.

## About

Cut the grid into rectangles and uncover the picture hiding underneath.

Uncover the Picture! Every number on the board is the size of the rectangle it
belongs to. A 6 sits inside a rectangle of six squares. Find where that
rectangle goes, and the tiles it covers turn into part of a picture you have
not seen yet.

Draw them one at a time and the picture arrives a piece at a time - a cactus, a
teapot, a small orange cat - built out of the moves you made rather than shown
to you at the end.

There is no timer, there are no lives, and there is nothing to lose. A
rectangle that does not belong is simply refused, and you try another. The game
waits for as long as you want to look at it.

757 puzzles, every one checked by machine to have exactly one solution, so a
board is never a guess. They start small enough to learn on and grow with you.
When you reach the end they come round again as new puzzles on the same
pictures.

Ten colour themes, light and dark. A hint for when a board will not open. And a
cat called Tuna, who turns up when you finish one.

### 怎么玩

1. 找到一个数字，并计算出它对应的矩形可以放在哪里。例如，数字 6 需要六个方格：

3. 它覆盖的方格会变成隐藏图案的一部分。

4. 重复以上步骤，直到棋盘上的每个方格都被一个矩形覆盖。

规则：

每个矩形覆盖的数字恰好是一个。

这个数字就是矩形覆盖的方格数量。

矩形之间不会重叠。

每个方格最终都会被一个矩形覆盖。

如果绘制的矩形不属于某个方格，则会被拒绝。不会有任何损失，也不会
