## About

Unblock Metro is a fascinating puzzle game. You need to move all the vehicles in front of the metro so that the metro drives smoothly along the track. You have to be careful that the head of the vehicle is facing that way and you cant change the direction of the vehicle. There are cars,trucks, ambulances, and so on. Lovely pictures and a wide variety of cars give you a refreshing gaming experience. The intricate vehicles placed in front of the metro will dazzle you. Waiting for your challenge
