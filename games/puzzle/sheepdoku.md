## How to play

Look at the puzzle board carefully.
The board is divided into different colored areas.
Place exactly one sheep in each colored area.
Sheep cannot be in the same row.
Sheep cannot be in the same column.
Sheep cannot touch each other, even diagonally.
Think before placing a sheep.
Complete the full board with the correct sheep positions.
Avoid wrong moves and solve each level with logic.

Your mission is to give every sheep its own perfect space. Use your mind, follow the rules, and complete every Sheepdoku puzzle.

## About

Welcome to Sheepdoku, a calm and clever puzzle game made for players who love logic, strategy, and relaxing brain challenges.

In Sheepdoku, your goal is simple: place one sheep in every colored area without breaking the rules. It looks easy at first, but every level needs smart thinking, careful moves, and perfect placement.

Each puzzle board is divided into different colored fields. Every field needs exactly one sheep. But sheep need their own space, so they cannot be placed in the same row, same column, or next to each other, even diagonally.

Think carefully before every move. Use your brain, find the safe spots, and complete the board without mistakes. The more you play, the better your logic skills become.

Sheepdoku is perfect for quick relaxing sessions, daily brain training, and puzzle lovers who enjoy simple rules with deep strategy.
