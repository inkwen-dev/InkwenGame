## About

The BUS ROUTE is a colourful and fun puzzlesorting game created by gameNply.com In this game: You have a queue of passengers waiting for their bus, each passenger having different colours, Your task is to sort them by colour, match them up in the right way, and organize the flow so that VIP passengers or special ones can board on time, The game features smooth animations, lively backgrounds, and vibrant, bus-themed visuals. As you progress, it gets more challenging: more colours, more passengers, more pressure to sort quickly and efficiently, It&rsquo;s simple to get into but clever in design&mdash; Thanks for Play
