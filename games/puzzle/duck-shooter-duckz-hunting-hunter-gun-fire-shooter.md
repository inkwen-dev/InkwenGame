## About

Duck Shooter is a simple endless carnival style shooting gallery game, that progressively speeds up the higher your score. As well as shooting the yellow duck to gain points you have to avoid the red ducks. When shooting the red ducks the game penalizes your score. If you spend too long before shooting another duck the game ends! Enjoy the game!
