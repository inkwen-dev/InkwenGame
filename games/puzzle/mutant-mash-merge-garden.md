## How to play

Move your finger or mouse to aim, release to drop. Touch two matching fruits to merge them. Don't let the pile reach the top line! Tap Swap to switch to the next fruit once per round. Keyboard (optional): arrow keys to move, Space to drop, S to swap, P to pause.

## About

Drop, bump and merge goofy fruit-animal hybrids to create bigger and weirder ones! Every merge can mutate into a shiny Golden, Rainbow or Glitch version. Collect them all in your Album, grab rare seeds at the Seed Stand, and grow your very own Mega Mutant plant a little more every day. Play a quick 2-3 minute round, come back tomorrow, and see what new part your plant grows!
