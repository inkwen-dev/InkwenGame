## About

MERGE SHOOT RUN is a one-touch runner where your whole squad fires while you steer. You drag left and right to guide a crowd of little shooters down a lane, and they shoot on their own, constantly. Break the towers ahead and the riders on top drop down and run to join you. Steer onto the people scattered along the ground and they fall in behind. Your shooters carry numbers and merge into heavier ones as the crowd grows, so a big crowd is worth far more than the sum of its people.

The decision the game is built around is what your firepower is worth spending on. Gates stand in your path with a number on them, and shooting a gate makes that number climb — run through and you collect whatever you charged it to. But every bullet you put into a gate is a bullet that never reached the tower behind it, and a gate you ignore stays in the red and takes people off you instead. There is no right answer, only the one you can afford.

Crossing the finish line is not the end of it. The horde comes at you, and every attacker that reaches your crowd kills a shooter and cuts your damage with them, so a run you scraped through can still fall apart at the last. Win and you land in a new world — Lagoon, Sunset, Deep, Jade and on — with your winnings and a clean slate, each one its own build-up rather than one endless ramp. Every run pays whether you win or wipe, that cash buys fire rate and start power, and the first wipe of any run is free.

### 介绍

《MERGE SHOOT RUN》是一款一键式跑酷游戏，玩家操控角色时，整个小队都会自动开火。左右拖动即可引导一群小射手沿着跑道前进，他们会持续不断地自主射击。摧毁前方的塔楼，塔楼上的骑手就会跳下来加入你的队伍。将角色转向地面上散落的人群，他们也会跟在你身后。你的射手身上带有数字，随着人群的增加，他们会合并成更强大的个体，因此，人数越多，价值就越高。

游戏的核心在于如何合理分配火力。跑道上会设有带有数字的门，射击门会使数字增加——穿过门即可获得你之前增加的奖励。但是，你射向门的每一颗子弹都意味着少了一颗子弹击中门后的塔楼；而你忽略的门则会一直处于红色状态，并会消耗你的人手。没有绝对正确的答案，只有你能负担得起的答案。

冲过终点线并不意味着游戏结束。敌人蜂拥而至，每个接近你阵营的攻击者都会杀死一名射手，从而降低你的伤害输出，所以即使你侥幸过关，也可能在最后关头功亏一篑。获胜后，你将带着奖金和全新的起点进入一个全新的世界——泻湖、日落、深海、翡翠等等——每个世界都有其独特的成长路线，而非一条无尽的阶梯。无论胜负，每次游戏都会获得奖励，这些奖励可以购买射速和初始火力，而且任何一次游戏的第一次团灭都是免费的。
