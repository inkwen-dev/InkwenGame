## About

Muddy Trucks Jigsaw is a game where you can play with some of twelve images with muddy trucks. Start with first image click on it and choose which mode you want to play. You can play with easy, medium or hard mode. When you solve the image you will unlock the next one. Try to unlock all levels and to solve all images. To play this game just drag the pieces in right side to get the image with mud truck.
