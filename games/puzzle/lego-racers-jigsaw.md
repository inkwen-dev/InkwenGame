## About

Lego Racers Jigsaw is a jigsaw game with Lego Racers. In this jigsaw game you will put together pictures of Lego Racers. Choose your picture with your favorite toy on it and start playing. Choose which mode you want to play with. You can assemble the image with 25 pieces, 49 or 100 pieces. If you are already an expert in assembling puzzles, start with 49 or 100 pieces. If you are a beginner, play with 25 pieces of the puzzle. Whatever you choose you have guaranteed fun with this game.
