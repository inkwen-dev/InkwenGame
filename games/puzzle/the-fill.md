## How to play

Swipe or draw one continuous line that passes through every block without lifting your finger or mouse.
You must fill every block in one go; backtracking isn’t allowed.
If a puzzle feels too complex, tap the hint icon to reveal part of the solution and it’s free.
s you complete puzzles, higher difficulty levels unlock more challenging puzzles.
