## How to play

Use the WASD and Arrow keys to move The player holding the Lucky Block when the time runs out wins the game Playable on both mobile and PC

## About

Steve and Alex need to race against each other. The one who holds the Lucky Block wins the game. Hold onto the Lucky Block until the time runs out to win the game. You can earn money by collecting socks and you can also earn money by defeating your friend. You must be very careful; steal the Lucky Block from your friend and keep it until the time is up.
