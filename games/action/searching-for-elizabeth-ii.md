## About

Angelo is on a quest to find his long lost love Elizabeth. He has to battle a variety of odd creatures on his way to find her. Collect coins along the way. Each time you collect 30 coins you gain extra health. You may have to find certain objects in order to progress through the game. You can also go back the way you came to find more items/objects.
