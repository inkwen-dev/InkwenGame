## About

Rescue Escape is an action puzzle game where you control stick figures trying to escape from a prison filled with traps. Your mission: guide all the characters past dangerous obstacles and gather them safely at the escape point to open the way out. Each level presents a new challenge&mdash;spike traps, moving obstacles, narrow paths, and countless other dangers force you to move skillfully and calculate each step. If just one stick figure gets stuck, the whole group wont be able to escape!
