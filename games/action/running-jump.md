## About

Running Jump is a game where you need to jump on platforms. Your enemies are patrolling on the platforms. You can only destroy them if you jump over them. You have to be fast and careful. From the bottom comes a platform with spikes that will hurt you and the game will end. Try to jump on more platforms and destroy more enemies before the spikes reach you.
