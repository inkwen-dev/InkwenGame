## How to play

Break an egg in a zone, grab the pet that jumps out and carry it back to your base. Place pets in your pen to earn money, then buy stronger pickaxes and more speed to reach farther zones.

💻 PC Controls:

WASD – Move
Hold Right Mouse Button – Rotate camera
Space – Jump
Left Mouse Button / F – Swing the pickaxe or the bat
1 / 2 – Pickaxe / bat
Hold E – Grab a pet
R – Drop the pet

📱 Mobile Controls:

Joystick – Move
Swipe on the right side of the screen – Rotate camera
On-screen buttons – Swing, jump, grab and other actions
