## About

Start the crazy fight with your crash battle cars. Enter the battle with your opponents by preparing your tiny collision vehicles. Put your sharp sword, which is your first weapon, at the most accurate point of your collision vehicle. When designing your vehicles, move forward by thinking about your steps. Buy wheels. And here is the war! When the game starts, you make forward moves at the same time as your opponent. Whoever makes the most moves becomes the winner of the game. When you smash the opponent vehicle, you enter the fight with the next opponent. The battle continues until you lose. If you lose, the game goes back to the beginning. When the coins you have collected are enough, you can buy new weapons in your vehicle. The stronger you become, the more battle challenges you will win. To spend time in the fun game, come on, click!
