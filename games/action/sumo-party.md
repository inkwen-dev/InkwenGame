## About

How about some entertaining sumo wrestles with Huge Sumos game? You can play single or 2 Player modes in this game with amazing 3D graphics and traditional Sumo wrestle sound effects. You will control the sumo wrestlers by using "W, A, S, D" and "Arrow" button. If you press buttons once your wrestler will move in that direction; if you press it twice quickly, your wrestler will take action in that specific direction.
