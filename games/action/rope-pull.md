## How to play

## About

Rope Pull game series continues with Rope Pull War in its new episode. The aim of the game is to pull the rope as fast as possible and pull your opponent to your side. The green radioactive area in the middle eliminates players. Each time you touch this area, your health bar will drop. Press the keys as fast as you can and pull the opponent. Rope Pull War has 1P and 2P modes. The first to reach a score of 5 in the game wins the match!
