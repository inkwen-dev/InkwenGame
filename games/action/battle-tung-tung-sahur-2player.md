## How to play

Use the WASD keys to play Press the S key to hit with the stick Use the Arrow keys to play Press the Down Arrow key to hit with the stick Stay near the chest for 20 seconds to win the game

## About

Are you ready for the battle of the Tung Tung Sahurs? Two teams are fighting each other, and the one who reaches the chest wins the game. Get ready to fight alongside your friend. Hit your opponent with the sticks and win the game. The player who stays at the chest the longest will be the winner. To win the game, you must defeat your friend.
