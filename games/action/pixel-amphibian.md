## About

Pixel Amphibian is a fast-paced endless runner where quick reflexes and perfect timing keep a pixelated toad hopping forward. Guide your toad through a vibrant pixel world filled with ever-changing obstacles. Jump, dodge, and react in split seconds as the speed steadily increases and the path becomes more unpredictable. One mistimed hop can end the run, so staying focused is the key to survival. As you last longer, the challenge ramps up with tighter gaps, faster obstacles, and trickier patterns. Simple controls make it easy to start, but mastering the rhythm of movement and timing every leap takes skill and practice.
