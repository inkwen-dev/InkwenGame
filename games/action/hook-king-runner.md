## How to play

Tap and hold to launch your hook and swing forward. Release at the right timing to jump from the rope and pass through obstacles. Avoid traps and enemies, collect coins, and use your momentum to reach the finish line. Timing and control are the key to becoming the Hook King!

## About

Hook King Runner is a physics-based runner where you tap, swing, and time your jumps to master wild obstacle courses. Use ropes and hooks with precision to dodge traps, collect coins, and outmaneuver enemies. With ragdoll physics and varied levels, your agility and timing are the keys to conquering every thrilling challenge that awaits.
