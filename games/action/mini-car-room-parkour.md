## How to play

Control the car using the WASD keys or the arrow keys on your keyboard. On mobile devices, use the virtual controls.

## About

An ordinary home becomes a giant obstacle course when you drive a tiny car! Explore different rooms, race across the floor, climb chairs and tables using ramps, and discover creative routes through the furniture.

Maneuver carefully, conquer steep climbs, keep your balance, and see how far you can go. Every room brings a new adventure where everyday objects become giant challenges!
