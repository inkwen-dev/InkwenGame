## About

Stickman Jump is a fast-paced platformer in which you have to jump and run across platforms with a little stickman. You control a small character who sprints at full speed and needs precise timing to jump over obstacles and complete each level. The further you get, the greater the challenges that will test your reflexes and concentration.
