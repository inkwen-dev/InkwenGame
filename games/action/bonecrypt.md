## How to play

Clear each room, find keys and open the way down to the chapter boss. Defeat all three bosses to win. WASD or Arrows: move. Left mouse or J: attack. Right mouse or K: parry yellow attacks. Space or Shift: dodge red attacks. R: special ability. Q: drink flask. E: interact. P: pause. M or the speaker icon: mute.

## About

Bonecrypt is a dungeon action roguelite with a hand-drawn ink look. Fight through branching crypt floors full of skeleton warriors, archers, mages, beetles and wraiths. Yellow attacks can be parried for a big counter, red attacks must be dodged. Every run is different, with keys, locked vaults, secret walls, cursed chests and timed trial rooms. Play as a Knight, Ranger or Mage, unlock 9 weapons and 37 blessings, beat 3 bosses across 3 chapters, buy permanent upgrades at camp and try 5 Nightmare difficulty levels.
