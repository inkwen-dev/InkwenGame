## How to play

Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Etano is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the historic golden ornaments while avoiding all of the enemies, spikes, fire and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
