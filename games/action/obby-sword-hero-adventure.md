## How to play

PC Controls:
WASD - Move your character
Space - Jump
Right Mouse Button - Camera control
Left Mouse Button - Attack

Mobile Controls:
Joystick - Move your character
Hold your finger on the screen - Camera control
Tap the screen - Attack

## About

Obby: Sword Hero Adventure is an exciting game where you’ll master your sword skills. Increase your attack power, obtain and use rarer swords. Build a collection of the best companions, defeat enemies, fight bosses, and explore all the game worlds in search of adventure!

• Increase your attack power
• Collect rare swords!
• Earn trophies and skill points!
• Recruit companions!
• Upgrade your hero’s abilities!
• Unlock all game worlds!
