## About

MOMENTUM is a high-speed endless runner that challenges your reflexes! Control a unique doodle character dashing through evolving worlds&mdash;from the Animal Kingdom to futuristic Alien Realms. Features: - Dynamic Worlds: Unlock new environments as you speed up (Animal, Human, Alien). - Custom Art Style: Cool doodle aesthetics with smooth animations. - Global Leaderboard: Compete for the top rank and earn titles like Reflex King or God. - Day/Night Cycle: Experience the game in different atmospheric modes. Dodge obstacles, collect crowns, and survive the increasing speed. How far can you run before the void consumes you?
