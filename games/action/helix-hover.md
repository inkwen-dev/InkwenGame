## How to play

Desktop

← / → or A / D — Steer left and right
↑ / W / Space — Jump
P — Pause

Mobile

Hold the left side of the screen — Steer left
Hold the right side of the screen — Steer right
Press both sides — Jump

## About

Helix Hover is a fast-paced 3D arcade game where you must ride through a twisting tube without touching the walls!

Steer your hover ship around the tube, dodge dangerous pink walls, and jump over yellow barriers. Collect shards along the way, build your score, and see how far you can go. The tube gets increasingly challenging as your run continues, so quick reactions and precise movement are essential.

### 介绍

《螺旋悬浮》是一款快节奏的3D街机游戏，你需要驾驶悬浮飞船穿过蜿蜒曲折的管道，并且不能碰到任何墙壁！

操控你的悬浮飞船在管道中穿梭，躲避危险的粉色墙壁，跳过黄色障碍物。沿途收集碎片，提升你的分数，看看你能走多远。随着游戏的进行，管道的难度会越来越高，因此快速的反应和精准的操作至关重要。
