## About

Play Legendary Warrior Goblin Rush and have fun, in this game you must make your way past the goblins, undead skeleton warriors, and other ghouls. Try to shoot them in their big head with your arrows. Sometimes you will have to take down an enemy so that your character can move forward to gain a better vantage point. The monsters will jump and move about, so try to analyze their movements and perfect your timing!
