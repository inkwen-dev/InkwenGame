## About

Guide Willie the mouse through a whimsical platforming adventure back to his steamboat. Traverse slow-paced, peril-filled levels, collecting coins and strategically jumping on enemies. With only one life per level, deliberate movement and precise timing ensure success. Help Willie rediscover his riverbound joy, one careful step at a time.
