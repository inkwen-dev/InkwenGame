## About

Disc Battle Ultimate is an exciting and fast-paced 3D hyper-casual action game! Take control of your stickman player, aim carefully with precision target lines, and throw your disc to knock out defenders or clear the field. Avoid the opponents, time your throws perfectly, and conquer every level in this ultimate battle of skills. Can you beat the defense and score the winning points? Play now and find out!
