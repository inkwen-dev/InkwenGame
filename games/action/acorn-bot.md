## About

Acorn Bot is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the ice cream cones while avoiding enemy bots, spikes, ground saws, and rotating lasers and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
