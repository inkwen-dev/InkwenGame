## About

Wobble Run is a 13+ arcade balance runner. You auto-run a leaning stack of wet-ink cans across Sky Docks, Slice Street, and Hive Yard. Catch more to grow taller. Tip is the only fail.

• Three worlds, 16 levels each
• Touch or mouse to steer into the lean
• Cosmetics unlock from play — no accounts, no IAP
• Stack wet. Don’t tip.

Height is easy. Balance is the game.
