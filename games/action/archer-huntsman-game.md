## About

Archer Huntsman is a fun confrontation arcade game. You can either choose the parkour hunting mode or the endless breakout mode. In parkour hunting mode, you need to survive to the end with your skills within the time limit. In the endless mode, you need to protect the king, if the king dies you will also fail. You can use the reward for passing to unlock new characters and skills. Have fun!
