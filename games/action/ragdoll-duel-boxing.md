## About

Welcome to closed fight club. Ragdoll Duel: Boxing is a fun and full of effects action game. Stickman boxer characters in different shapes fight in this game. Send quick punches to your opponent with your stickman fighter and don't waste your Super Punch when it's ready! Get 2 rounds and be victorious! You can play against AI opponent or vs your friend.
