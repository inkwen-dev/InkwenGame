## About

Forest Adventure is an immersive escape game set in a magical forest. As a round ball character, you must explore the forest to find lost keys, unlock mysterious doors, and reach new areas. Navigate through obstacles, solve mysterious mechanisms, and discover the secrets within the forest. There are no scores or time limits&mdash;simply get lost in the tranquil atmosphere of nature and savor the adventure. Each level leads to an even more exciting journey. Get lost in the depths of the forest, collect the keys, and fall under the enchantment of Forest Adventure!
