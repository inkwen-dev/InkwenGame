## How to play

The red player moves using the WASD keys The blue player moves using the arrow keys The one who gets hit 20 times loses

## About

The Tung Tung Sahur brothers are very angry. Both of them are furious with each other now. You must help them in their battle together with your friend. Grab a weapon and let the fastest one win. You need to collect the drops falling from the sky. The one who gets hit the most loses the battle. Fight your friend and win this war. Take the drop to get a more powerful weapon.
