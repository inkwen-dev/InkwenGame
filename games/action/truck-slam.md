## How to play

Drive your truck into the enemy and swing your mace to crush them — but watch out, they're swinging too.
Desktop — Player 1: A/D move, Q jump, S or Space swing. Player 2 (2-player mode): ←/→ move, ↑ jump, ↓ or Enter swing.
Mobile: on-screen buttons for left, right, jump and swing.
Single-player: clear levels, collect cogs, choose an upgrade after each victory. 2-player: best of the round wins.

### 怎么玩

驾驶卡车冲向敌人，挥舞狼牙棒粉碎他们——但要小心，他们也会反击。

桌面版——玩家 1：A/D 移动，Q 跳跃，S 或空格键挥舞狼牙棒。玩家 2（双人模式）：←/→ 移动，↑ 跳跃，↓ 或 Enter 键挥舞狼牙棒。

移动版：屏幕上有左右移动、跳跃和挥舞狼牙棒的按钮。

单人模式：通关、收集齿轮，每次胜利后选择升级。双人模式：每回合最佳者获胜。
