## How to play

Player 1: WASD to move, SPACE to jump.
Player 2: Arrow Keys to move, L to jump.
Double-tap jump button for Double Jump.
Hold forward key for Sprint.

## About

Step into the thrilling world of Ninja Obby Speed Run, where every level is a test of speed, agility, and strategy. Packed with hundreds of unique obstacle courses, this game combines competitive racing with ninja-style combat and dazzling customization options.

From smashing glass doors at checkpoints with your ninja power to unlocking powerful bonuses like speed boost, giant mode, and flight, every run feels fresh. During flight, gather golden rings in the sky to earn coins and unlock even more items in the shop.
