## About

No One Survives 1 minute is a fast-paced endless survival runner. Dodge incoming barriers, keep distance from the dragon, and stay alive as long as possible. Collect stars and chests to increase score, level up your core, and gain stronger shield regeneration. Every 60 seconds triggers a victory pulse. Practice mode unlocks after 3 defeats to help players improve before full-speed runs.
