## About

Rexo 2 is the Second installment of the original game, Rexo, which is a simple 2D Platformer where you play as the Cube and your aim is to collect all of the coins while avoiding spikes, and enemies. You have to get to the Red flag which will lead you to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
