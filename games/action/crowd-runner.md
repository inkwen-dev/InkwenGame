## How to play

- Hold and drag (or click and drag) anywhere to steer the crowd left and right.
  There is nothing else to press.
- Run your crowd through the better gate: green gates add or multiply your
  runners, red ones subtract or divide. Read both before you commit.
- Hazards knock runners out - saws, hammers, spinners, crushers, flame jets,
  lasers, boulders, TNT. None of them can end a run by itself, but together
  they will.
- Reach the finish and your crowd climbs the victory stairs; the more runners
  you have left, the higher you get and the more coins you earn.
- On a boss level, your crowd charges the boss: bring enough runners and you win.
- Spend coins on permanent upgrades, on one-run boosts before you set off, or on
  new jelly skins. Boosts and skins can also be earned by watching a short ad.
- Win three levels to fill the treasure chest, and come back each day for the
  daily reward.

Controls: touch drag / mouse drag only. No keyboard needed. Works on phone,
tablet and desktop.

## About

Crowd Runner is a one-finger running game about turning a handful of little jelly
people into an army. Drag left and right to steer your crowd down the track and
pick your gate: x3 and +20 make the crowd swell, -15 and /2 cut it down. Get it
right and a dozen runners become hundreds by the time you reach the end.

The track fights back. Saws slide across it, hammers swing, crushers drop, flame
jets and lasers sweep the lanes, boulders roll at you and TNT waits to be
triggered - each one taking a bite out of your crowd but never ending the run on
its own. Get to the finish and your runners pile up the victory stairs: the more
of them you brought, the higher you climb and the bigger the payout. Some levels
end at a boss instead, and there it is simply your numbers against his.

Levels are generated, so they never run out, and they roll through eight themes -
forest, snow, desert, night, autumn, candy, beach, volcano. Coins buy permanent
upgrades (a bigger starting crowd, better income, richer coins), one-run boosts
(extra runners, a shield, double coins) and fifteen jelly skins, from plain blue
to cowboy, viking, angel and king. There is a seven-day login reward and a
treasure chest that opens every three wins.

### 介绍

《人群奔跑者》是一款单指跑酷游戏，目标是将一小群果冻人

变成一支军队。左右拖动即可控制你的人群沿着赛道前进，

并选择你的进场门：x3 和 +20 会让人群数量激增，-15 和 /2 则会减少人群。

如果操作得当，到达终点时，十几个跑者就会变成数百个。

赛道会反击。锯子会滑过赛道，锤子会挥舞，粉碎机会落下，火焰喷射器和激光会扫过赛道，巨石会向你滚来，TNT 也随时准备引爆——

每一个都会减少你的人群，但不会自行结束你的奔跑。

到达终点后，你的跑者会堆积在胜利阶梯上：

你带的人越多，
你爬得越高，
奖励也越丰厚。有些关卡的终点是 Boss，在那里，你将与 Boss 展开一场人数上的较量。

关卡是随机生成的，所以永远不会用完，并且会轮换八个主题——

森林、雪地、沙漠、夜晚、秋季、糖果、海滩、火山。金币可以购买永久的

升级（更多初始玩家、更高的收入、更丰厚的金币）、单次游戏增益

（额外玩家、护盾、双倍金币）以及十五种果冻皮肤，从纯蓝色

到牛仔、维京人、天使和国王。此外，还有七天登录奖励和一个

每赢三场即可开启的宝箱。
