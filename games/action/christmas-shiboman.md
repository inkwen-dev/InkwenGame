Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Christmas Shiboman is a 2D Christmas themed cute little platformer where you have to collect blue gift boxes while avoiding the enemies, giant blades, spikes and reach the orange flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
