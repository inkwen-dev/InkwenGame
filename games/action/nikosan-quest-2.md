## About

Nikosan Quest 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as an anime girl called Nikosan who have to collect all of the yellow cubes while avoiding the monsters, flying creatures, spikes, rotating blades and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Nikosan Quest" series.
