## About

The game begins with Sprunki or Cameramen standing on the ground, surrounded by structures inside an old, rusty neighborhood. Looking up, he can see various objects resembling pipelines in the sky, and he is determined to find out what they are. And so, Sprunki begins his quest of jumping to higher surfaces in this journey full of surreal objects, only to find out that his quest is far from complete. He may trip and fall along the way, but miraculously, nothing ever happens to him when falling from unfathomable heights. It is up to you to guide him and find out what&rsquo;s waiting at the end of this vertical maze!
