## How to play

Get down, take the crown, get back out alive.

Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys to move, left-click or Space to attack, Shift to roll, P to pause, M for music.
Touch: left thumb stick to move, attack and roll buttons on the right.

Smash crates and barrels for coins and potions, watch for spike traps on a timer, and roll through anything you cannot out-trade. Kill enough to level up and pick an upgrade. Each new floor heals you and hands you a free one. When the Underking falls, run - the way out is the real fight.

## About

Undercrown is a top-down 3D dungeon brawler with a nasty little twist: getting to the treasure is only half the run.

Pick one of 8 heroes and cut your way down three floors - 18 rooms, each with its own colours - past the Gravewarden and the Maw Mother to the Underking himself. Every hero fights differently: the Vanguard swings a greatsword, the Paladin holds the line with sword and shield, Kachujin uses nothing but fists, the Ninja works twin daggers, Erika picks enemies off with a crossbow, Medea casts from a staff, the Captain brawls with a cutlass, and Heraklios brings a greataxe to every argument.

Then the twist. Beat the Underking, take his crown, and the whole mountain starts coming down - because that crown was the only thing holding it up. Now you have to run all the way back to the mine lift through collapsing rooms, falling rock, doors on timers and monsters flooding back in. You are not done until you are standing in daylight with the crown still on your head.

- 8 heroes, each unlocked with coins you bank between runs
- Level up mid-run and pick one of three upgrades - no two runs build the same
- Normal, Hard and Nightmare
- A full run is 6-8 minutes, if you survive it

### 介绍

《地下王冠》（Undercrown）是一款俯视角3D地牢格斗游戏，但它有一个令人意想不到的设定：找到宝藏仅仅是游戏的一半。

从八位英雄中任选一位，一路披荆斩棘，深入三层——共十八个房间，每个房间都拥有独特的颜色——击败墓地守卫和噬魂之母，最终挑战地下王。每位英雄的战斗方式都截然不同：先锋挥舞巨剑，圣骑士以剑盾坚守阵线，卡丘金只用拳头作战，忍者使用双匕首，艾丽卡用弩箭精准射杀敌人，美狄亚使用法杖施法，队长挥舞弯刀近身搏斗，而赫拉克利奥斯则挥舞着巨斧横扫一切。

接下来才是真正的转折点。击败地下王，夺取他的王冠，整座山峰便开始崩塌——因为那顶王冠是支撑山体的唯一支柱。现在你必须一路狂奔，穿过坍塌的房间、落石、定时关闭的大门，以及不断涌入的怪物，才能回到矿井升降机。只有当你头戴王冠，沐浴在阳光下时，你的旅程才算结束。

- 8 位英雄，每位英雄都需要用你在游戏间隙积累的金币解锁

- 游戏过程中可以升级，并从三种升级选项中选择一种 - 每次游戏体验都独一无二

- 普通、困难和噩梦三种难度

- 一次完整的游戏需要 6-8 分钟，前提是你能活下来
