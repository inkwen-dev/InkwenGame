## About

It's time for adventure, but Fin will have to deal with the difficulties on his own, because Jake was kidnapped by a lich - an evil king. He locked his friend in the dark labyrinths of the dungeon, teeming with bone monsters of various kinds. The hero is not afraid of monsters, he is ready to go through 22 rooms with weapons at the ready. Can you show miracles of valor and keep him company?
