## How to play

Welcome to the chaotic world of slaps 🖐️ — a fast-paced multiplayer slap battle inspired by Slap Battles!

Your main goal is to collect as many slaps as possible. Slaps are the main currency in the game — the more players you hit, the more slaps you earn.

Use your slaps to unlock stronger gloves and become more powerful in battle.

Game modes:
⚔️ Arena — free-for-all chaos
🥊 Ring — stay on the platform and don’t fall
🏃 67 — reach the end while avoiding dangerous heads

Every match is full of action, chaos, and competition 😂
Can you become the player with the most slaps?
