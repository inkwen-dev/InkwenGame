## About

Long ago there was a toy factory far from the city. Slave robots were produced in this factory. However, not every robot produced was perfect. Some had scratches on the screen, some did not match the color. Robots with faulty production were brought to sector 01 for destruction. But one day, one of the robots that were brought to sector 01, gained consciousness.
