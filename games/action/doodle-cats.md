## About

Guide your doodle cat ever upward through an endless world of platforms, power-ups, and enemies! Jump, double-jump, and dodge bats, bees, and ghosts while collecting coins to unlock new cat skins, trails, and upgrades in the shop. Trigger the bonus level by collecting 3 bonus items in a single run and rack up coins in a frantic flying sequence. Beat your high score!
