## About

Enter the chaotic world of Life of a Happy Block, where you become a fast-moving Lucky Block trying to survive an endless chase. Bosses are always behind you, but not all of them bring the same outcome. Your goal is to avoid weaker bosses while staying in the run long enough to meet stronger ones.
You control a rapidly moving Happy Block, making quick decisions to dodge danger and extend your run. Getting caught too early ends everything, but being caught by the right boss can transform your Lucky Block into a rare and powerful secret form.
Each run is a balance between risk and timing. The longer your Happy Block survives, the better your chances of unlocking unique transformations. Simple controls make it easy to start, but mastering the flow takes skill and focus.
How far can your Lucky Block go, and what will it become?
