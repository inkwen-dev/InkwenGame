## How to play

Win the duel by reading your opponent: after every attack, guard. Player 1: A / D walk, W jump, S guard (tap S just before a hit lands to parry, then strike back at once), F light slash, G heavy slash, R kick (hold a direction for a sweep, spinning or disarming kick), T shuriken, Left Shift dash, E ki technique. Player 2: arrow keys, K / L / O / I / Right Shift / U. P or gamepad Start pauses. On phones and tablets: a d-pad on the left (▼ guards) and attack buttons on the right; turn the device sideways.

## About

A one-on-one ninja duel drawn in ink and moonlight. Read your rival, guard, then strike back: every defence opens a counter, and a run of good defence triggers your ninja's own counter cinematic (一 二 三) that ends in a finishing strike.

- 13 ninjas with their own weapons: katana, nodachi, twin tanto, naginata, kusarigama, bo staff, war fans and a bow, each with a signature kick and ki technique
- Knock the sword out of your rival's hands and fight on with fists and kicks, or flick your blade back up from the floor
- 7 arenas that hit back: waves, gales, falling beams, runaway carts, snow slides, lightning and leaf storms, with slippery floors, walls and props you can use
- An eight-fight journey for every ninja with its own ending, mastery stars and Legacy colors
- A monthly tournament, a Dan rank trial from Kyu 10 to Dan 10 and the Shadow Pass
- Japanese announcer and voiced fighters
- Training, combo trials and a short tutorial
- Two players on one keyboard or two gamepads, or fight the AI on three difficulties

### 怎么玩

赢得决斗的关键在于预判对手：每次攻击后都要进行防御。玩家 1：A/D 移动，W 跳跃，S 防御（在攻击即将命中时轻按 S 进行格挡，然后立即反击），F 轻斩，G 重斩，R 踢（按住方向键可使出扫腿、旋踢或缴械踢），T 手里剑，左 Shift 冲刺，E 气技。玩家 2：方向键，K/L/O/I/右 Shift/U。P 或游戏手柄开始游戏暂停。在手机和平板电脑上：左侧是方向键（▼ 防御），右侧是攻击键；将设备横屏放置。

### 介绍

一场以墨色和月光描绘的忍者一对一决斗。洞察对手，防御，然后反击：每一次防御都蕴藏着反击的机会，而连续的成功防御将触发忍者专属的反击动画（一二三），最终以致命一击收尾。

- 13 位忍者，各自拥有专属武器：武士刀、野太刀、双短刀、薙刀、锁镰、棍棒、战扇和弓箭，每位忍者都拥有独特的踢技和气功技巧。

- 击落对手手中的剑，继续用拳脚战斗，或者将掉落在地上的刀刃弹回。

- 7 个充满挑战的竞技场：巨浪、狂风、坠落的横梁、失控的马车、雪崩、闪电和落叶风暴，以及湿滑的地面、墙壁和可供利用的道具。

- 每位忍者都有八场战斗的专属旅程，每个旅程都有其独特的结局、精通之星和传承颜色。

- 每月举办锦标赛，从十级到十段的段位挑战赛以及暗影通道。

- 日语解说和配音角色。

- 训练模式、连招练习和简短教程。

- 支持双人使用同一键盘或两个游戏手柄进行游戏，或与电脑AI进行三种难度的对战。
