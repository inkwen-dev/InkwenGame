## About

A game in Roblox style with the Obby mode! Complete exciting and varied obstacle courses for a while, using checkpoints to save your progress and not start all over again if you accidentally fall.

Incredible challenges and unique mechanics await you here! The main feature of the game is the ability to change the size of the character to overcome obstacles and find a way to the finish.

The game has a large selection of characters and deep customization: choose the appearance, clothes and accessories to create a unique hero. Each level is a new challenge, where it is important not only to complete the track, but also to do it faster than everyone else. Watch the timer and improve your results to become the best in this game.

Nice graphics with bright colors and detailed levels make the game visually attractive, and smooth animation adds pleasure from the passage. Compete with friends and players from all over the world to take first place in the leaderboard!
