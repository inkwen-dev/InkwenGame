## How to play

1. Press "Play". You start on a rooftop above a crowd of infected — jump down into them. The first mission teaches every move on the way: fighting with borrowed mauls, absorbing, throwing a car, running up a wall and gliding.
2. Move with WASD or the arrow keys (on a touch screen — the stick on the left half of the screen). Hold Shift or push the stick to the edge to sprint.
3. Sprint into a wall to run up it. Tap Space for a short jump, hold it for a high one; hold it in the air to glide. Ctrl (or the Dash button in the air) dashes forward.
4. Left click (the Attack button) strikes with claws — tap three times for a combo. The camera turns with the mouse after a click on the game; without mouse capture drag with the right button (on a touch screen — drag on the right half).
5. A weakened enemy gets a marker: press E (the context button) to absorb it.
6. Q (the Echo button) highlights mission targets through walls. Esc or the pause button in the top-left corner pauses the game.
7. Terminals give missions: walk up to one and press E. The arrow at the screen edge points to the goal.
8. Hold the attack button for a maul smash (after mission 4); F (the Harpoon button) hooks and pulls enemies in (after mission 7); Tab (the Form button) switches arm forms.
9. E next to a car lifts it; E or attack throws it — the best answer to drones, turrets and the helicopter.
10. Yellow pillars start medal challenges outside missions and alerts: press E (the context button) next to one. R (the circle-arrow button) restarts a challenge at once; the "Daily challenge" card in the menu takes you straight to today's one.
11. The "Achievements" button in the menu lists all 10 achievements: each with its icon, name, what to do to unlock it and your progress.

## About

Block Zero is a third-person action game in an open low-poly city. You are a blocky mutant born of the outbreak: every wall is a road to the roof, every roof is a launch pad. Sprint into a building to run straight up its wall, hold jump to glide across the streets, dash in mid-air and land wherever you like. Your very first move is a leap from a rooftop into a crowd of infected.

The city has three zones — the quiet residential blocks, the quarantine full of infected and the corporate base in the north. Resistance terminals give short missions: thin out the infected, hunt down a witness, burn out a nest that fights back in three waves. Weaken an enemy and absorb it to heal, gain biomass and even take its appearance: security will not shoot at one of their own.

Between missions, yellow pillars start medal challenges: rooftop runs through a chain of gates and 60-second Smash arenas full of cars, drones and turrets. Go for bronze, silver or gold — a restart is one press away. The daily challenge is the same for everyone, comes with a twist — low gravity or explosive cars — and keeps a streak that makes your claw trail grow.

Features:
• An open low-poly city of 64 blocks — no loading screens, no invisible walls between the roofs.
• Wall running, high jumps, gliding and air dashes.
• Claw combos with auto-aim, knock-ups and a score multiplier — every hit lands with hit-stop, a camera kick and a sound of its own.
• Absorb weakened enemies to heal, grow biomass and disguise yourself.
• Mauls, a harpoon and car throwing; a corporate alert that sends squads, drones and a helicopter.
• A tutorial that starts with a leap into a crowd, then 12 missions with nest bosses, a courier chase, a convoy and the final Source.
• 6 rooftop runs and 3 Smash arenas for bronze, silver and gold, plus a daily challenge with a twist and a streak.
• 12 mutations, 18 forms, 20 hidden data capsules, 10 achievements and a daily reward.
• A living menu: your mutant waits on a rooftop at golden hour, wearing the form you picked; mission results count up to a fanfare, and a best run earns a "New record".
• Play in landscape or portrait — with a mouse and keyboard or a touch screen; camera shake and motion can be toned down in the settings.
• Interface in 7 languages.

### 怎么玩

1. 按下“开始游戏”。你将从屋顶跳下去，下方是一群感染者。第一个任务会教你各种动作：使用借来的巨锤战斗、吸收敌人、投掷汽车、跑墙和滑翔。

2. 使用 WASD 键或方向键移动（在触摸屏上，使用屏幕左侧的摇杆）。按住 Shift 键或将摇杆推到边缘即可冲刺。

3. 冲刺撞墙即可跑上墙。轻按空格键进行短跳，按住空格键进行高跳；在空中按住空格键可滑翔。按住 Ctrl 键（或在空中按冲刺键）向前冲刺。

4. 左键（攻击键）使用利爪攻击——连续点击三次可使出连击。点击鼠标即可转动视角；不使用鼠标时，用右键拖动（在触摸屏上，拖动屏幕右侧）即可转动视角。

5. 虚弱的敌人会出现标记：按下 E 键（上下文键）即可吸收敌人。

6. Q（回声按钮）可高亮显示穿墙任务目标。按 Esc 键或左上角的暂停按钮可暂停游戏。

7. 终端会发布任务：走到终端前并按 E 键。屏幕边缘的箭头会指向目标。

8. 按住攻击键可使出重击（完成任务 4 后）；F（鱼叉按钮）可钩住并拉近敌人（完成任务 7 后）；Tab（形态按钮）可切换手臂形态。

9. 在汽车旁边按 E 键可将其举起；按 E 键或攻击键可将其投掷出去——这是对付无人机、炮塔和直升机的最佳方法。

10. 黄色柱子会在任务和警报之外开启奖牌挑战：在柱子旁边按 E 键（上下文按钮）。R（圆圈箭头按钮）可立即重新开始挑战；菜单中的“每日挑战”卡片可直接跳转到今天的挑战。

11. 菜单中的“成就”按钮列出了全部 10 项成就：每项成就都有其图标、名称、解锁方法和进度。

### 介绍

《零号街区》（Block Zero）是一款第三人称动作游戏，背景设定在一个开放的低多边形城市中。你扮演的是一个在疫情爆发后诞生的方块状变异体：每一面墙都是通往屋顶的道路，每一个屋顶都是一个跳板。冲刺进入建筑物即可沿着墙壁直冲而上，按住跳跃键即可滑翔穿过街道，在空中冲刺并随意降落。你的第一个动作就是从屋顶跃入感染者的人群中。

这座城市分为三个区域——安静的住宅区、充满感染者的隔离区以及位于北部的企业基地。抵抗军终端会发布一些短任务：消灭感染者、追捕目击者、摧毁一个会分三波反击的巢穴。削弱敌人并吸收其生命值来恢复生命值、获取生物质，甚至还能改变敌人的外貌：安保人员不会向自己人开枪。

任务间隙，黄色柱子会触发奖牌挑战：在屋顶上奔跑穿过一系列大门，以及在充满汽车、无人机和炮塔的60秒速通竞技场中展开激战。目标是获得铜牌、银牌或金牌——只需按下按钮即可重新开始。每日挑战对所有玩家都一样，但略有不同——例如低重力或爆炸汽车——并且会记录你的连胜纪录，让你的爪痕不断增长。

游戏特色：

• 一个由 64 个方块组成的开放式低多边形城市——没有加载画面，屋顶之间也没有隐形墙。

• 跑墙、高跳、滑翔和空中冲刺。

• 爪击连击，带有自动瞄准、击飞和得分倍增功能——每次命中都会触发击中停止、镜头抖动和专属音效。

• 吸收虚弱的敌人来治疗、增长生物质并伪装自己。

• 巨锤、鱼叉和汽车投掷；企业警报会派出小队、无人机和直升机。

• 新手教程从跳入人群开始，然后是 12 个任务，包括巢穴首领、快递追逐、车队护送和最终的源头。

• 6 个屋顶跑酷关卡和 3 个乱斗竞技场，分别对应青铜、白银和黄金段位，外加每日挑战（包含趣味挑战和连胜奖励）。

• 12 种变异体、18 种形态、20 个隐藏数据胶囊、10 项成就以及每日奖励。

• 动态菜单：你的变异体会在日落时分出现在屋顶，身着你选择的形态；任务完成时会响起欢呼声，最佳成绩将获得“新纪录”。

• 支持横屏或竖屏模式——可使用鼠标键盘或触摸屏操作；可在设置中调整镜头抖动和运动效果。

• 支持 7 种语言界面。
