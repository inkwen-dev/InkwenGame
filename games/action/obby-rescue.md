## About

Obby Rescue is a casual and very funny puzzle game in which the main objective of the game is to protect your adorable little dog from the attack of a swarm of killer bees! The goal of the game is to use the objects that will serve as a barrier against the attack, taking into account at all times your environment and the laws of physics.
