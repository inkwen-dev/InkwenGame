## About

Forest Dash is a fast, colorful 3D endless runner. Sprint down a sunlit forest trail, switch lanes to dodge rocks and logs, and leap over obstacles while scooping up coins. The longer you run, the faster it gets &mdash; how far can you go? Spend the coins you collect on fun character skins, and use a revive to push your best distance even further. Simple one-tap-and-swipe controls make it instant to play, on desktop or mobile.
