## About

Get ready for an adrenaline-pumping adventure in Cube Runner Adventure! Maneuver a cube character through an endless maze of obstacles using intuitive touch-based controls. The realistic 3D camera and physics behavior make the cubes movements feel dynamic as you swipe left or correct to change direction. Beware of four types of shapes: white boxes that protect you, red boxes that are enemies, and spiked boxes that are deadly hazards. Can you survive the challenging maze, collect coins, and achieve the highest score? Its time to put your skills to the test in this thrilling 3D mobile game!
