## About

Iron Pink is a fast-paced, hilarious fighting game where you play as a fearless pig defending against an endless onslaught of hungry wolves! Use lightning-fast punches, kicks, and combos to smash, kick, and throw your enemies before they reach you. The action never stops as wave after wave of wolves attack from both sides, armed with everything from chainsaws to rockets. Features: - Addictive, never-ending fighting action - Dozens of unique enemies and crazy weapons to grab and use against them - Multiple characters to unlock, each with their own style - Different game modes including Classic and Survival
