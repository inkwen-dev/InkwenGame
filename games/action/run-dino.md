## About

Take your dino buddy T-Rex for a run along the North American plains in this super fun, endless running game. Jump over rocks, steer clear of shrubs, duck under palm trees, and dodge flying Pterodactyls. Collect eggs that can be used for respawning if you crash, or buy new powerups.
