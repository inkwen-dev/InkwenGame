## How to play

Drag your finger (or mouse) left and right to steer. That is the entire control scheme. The game handles your speed, you handle the carving.

Ski between the paired gates to score points and grow your combo. Missing a gate resets the combo. Hitting a tree, rock, or pylon ends the run. Tap once to instantly go again.

Stay ahead of the yeti. If he catches you, you get knocked off the trail into slow deep snow and lose your combo.

Grab snowmen for currency, knock out missions for bonus snowmen, and unlock skins in the shop. The real objective is simple: ski past the flag from your last best run and plant it a little further down the mountain.

## About

Yeti Run is an endless skiing game where the mountain never runs out but your luck does. You carve down the slope with one finger, ski between gates to build combos, and dodge trees, rocks, and lift pylons at increasingly stupid speeds. There is also a yeti. He will not end your run, but if he catches you he shoves you into the deep snow and wrecks your combo, which honestly feels worse. Collect snowmen as you go and spend them on skins in the shop. Every run ends the same way, but the flag marking your best distance is always sitting there, daring you to pass it.

### 怎么玩

左右拖动手指（或鼠标）即可控制方向。这就是全部的操作方式。游戏会自动控制速度，你只需掌控滑行轨迹。

在成对的旗门之间滑行即可得分并增加连击数。错过旗门会重置连击数。撞到树木、岩石或路标杆会结束滑行。点击一下即可立即重新开始。

保持领先于雪人。如果被雪人追上，你会被撞出雪道，陷入缓慢滑行的深雪中，并失去连击数。

收集雪人可以获得游戏货币，完成任务可以获得额外雪人奖励，并在商店解锁皮肤。真正的目标很简单：滑过你上次最佳滑行的旗帜，并将其插在更下方的山坡上。
