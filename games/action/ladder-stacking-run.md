## About

In Ladder Stacking Run, race through vibrant, obstacle-filled courses while stacking ladders to climb higher and reach new heights. Collect ladder pieces scattered along the way and use them to scale walls, cross gaps, and overcome challenges. The faster you stack, the higher you climb&mdash; but beware of falling behind!
