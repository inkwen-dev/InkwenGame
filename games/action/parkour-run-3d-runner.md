## How to play

Overcome obstacles and reach the finish line as fast as possible. Level 1 features a tutorial, and you will discover some gameplay mechanics as you progress. 

Maximum speed is achieved when moving straight forward. 

🖥️ PC Controls:
• A / 🡸 — Move left / Wall-run (hold against the wall)
• D / 🡺 — Move right / Wall-run (hold against the wall)
• Space — Jump / Push off the wall
• Shift — Slide (you can still steer left/right while sliding)
• R — Restart 

📱 Mobile Controls:
Tap anywhere on the screen to bring up the virtual joystick. Move and hold your finger within the joystick area to perform actions:
• Center — Idle (no action)
• Top area — Jump
• Left area — Move left / Wall-run (hold against the wall)
• Right area — Move right / Wall-run (hold against the wall)
• Bottom center — Slide
• Bottom left — Slide + Move left
• Bottom right — Slide + Move right 

You can adjust the joystick size in the Options menu.
