## About

Etano 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the historic golden ornaments while avoiding all of the enemies, flying creatures, spikes, fire and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Etano" series.
