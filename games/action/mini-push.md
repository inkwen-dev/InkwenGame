## About

Mini Push is a retro-style arcade game where you have to help a blob to reach his objective by enabling or disabling blocks in his way and making him avoid enemies. It is inspired in some NES style games but with simple controls and an accessible difficulty for everyone. It also has a speedrun mode for more demanding players.
