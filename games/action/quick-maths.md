## How to play

Solve each math problem before the timer runs out. Tap or click an answer, or press 1, 2, or 3. A correct answer clears the obstacle. Mistakes cost stars but never end a level: one star for finishing, two for two or fewer mistakes, three for no mistakes and enough fast PERFECT answers. Clear level 20 to unlock Endless, where one crash ends the run. Enter or Space starts and continues.

## About

Quick Maths is an arcade runner where your brain is the controller. Solve math problems to clear obstacles across 20 levels of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Finish all 20 to unlock Endless mode and chase a high score.

在计时器归零前解决每个数学题。点击或轻触答案，或按 1、2 或 3。答对即可清除障碍。错误会扣除星星，但不会导致关卡结束：完成关卡可得一颗星，错误不超过两次可得两颗星，没有错误且快速答对足够多的题目可得三颗星。通关第 20 关即可解锁“无尽模式”，在该模式下，一次碰撞即结束游戏。按 Enter 键或空格键开始和继续游戏。
