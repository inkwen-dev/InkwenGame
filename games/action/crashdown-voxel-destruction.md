Choose any available location, take everything possible from the arsenal and start destroying everything you see, it is possible to destroy literally anything, have fun.

Control of the game:

WASD - Movement.
Space - Jump.
Esc - Pause.
Mouse - Overview.
Left Shift - Running.
Left mouse button - Shot, throw.
I - Inventory.
M - Map.

## About

Crash Down - Smash Everything is a voxel-based sandbox game. The player is given complete freedom of action. There are various locations at your disposal, filled to capacity with structures. The key feature is the possibility of their complete destruction. Using various methods, you can destroy everything you see. Have you ever dreamed of leveling a multi-storey house with your own hands? Now you have such an opportunity! The physics of destruction is close to reality. Use a variety of arsenal to destroy objects, from a hammer to a powerful bazooka.
