## About

Guide two rope-linked heroes across tricky platforms in Double Boy Jump Press and hold to jump then release at the right moment to land safely and switch between the partners. Keep both characters out of danger as you cross gaps collect coins and aim for a higher score. Unlock character skins and explore themed modes such as Snow Dash Face to Face Cloud Ride Monster Escape and Escort Quest. With simple one-touch controls and physics-based teamwork every jump is a fresh test of timing. Play with a mouse on desktop or touch controls on mobile.
