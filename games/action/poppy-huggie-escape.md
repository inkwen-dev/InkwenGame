## About

Poppy Huggie Escape is an exciting escape adventure game. The abominable blue monster Huggy is chasing you and you need to escape the monster by being nimble, avoiding obstacles that block your way, running, jumping, and sliding to escape the house. You need to collect all the keys to open the door, otherwise, you will be caught by the monster. Take the challenge when you are ready! Good luck!
