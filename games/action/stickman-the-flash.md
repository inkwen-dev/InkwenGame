## About

Stickman The Flash is a fighting RPG arcade game with 2D stickman warriors. In the Survival Mode, you need to survive as long as you can by dodging red areas and killing incoming enemies. You can collect different weapons when you kill the corresponding enemies. When you earn enough gems, you can unlock and equip them in the Equipment Store, and they will increase your full abilities. Keep calm and slaughter them all!
