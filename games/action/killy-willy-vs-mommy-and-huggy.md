## About

Welcome to Killy Willy vs Mommy and Huggy! Choose one of the characters Huggy Wuggy or Mommy long legs and start to Run and jump. Tap or click to jump higher to avoid Killy Willy and other creatures and obstacles. Click the bonus button to get a full energy. Dont collapse with enemies when your energy if empty. First you will have only mommy long legs as a character, complete the level to unlock Poppy huggy wuggy. Good Luck
