## How to play

Use A/D to move left and right. Use Left Click to throw your orb, Space to recall it, 1/2/3 to switch orb colors, and F to activate a power-up. Master your orb abilities, clear obstacles, and smash targets to make it as far as possible!

## About

Retro Run is a free to play pc game. Players must use A and D to navigate side to side to avoid some obstacles while using orbs to smash through others. Collect power-ups along the journey to get yourself out of sticky situations. But be careful! If you lose all your orbs, you will be forced to restart. Pressing space to recall orbs or smashing special crystals throughout the progression will restore balls. Can you make it to the end?
