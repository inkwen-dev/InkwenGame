## About

Hana Bot is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you play as a bot who have to collect all of the test tubes while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots, giant blades, and spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
