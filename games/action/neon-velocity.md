## About

Neon Velocity is a fast-paced, hardcore precision platformer set in a vibrant, pulse-pounding cyberpunk universe. You are a high-speed operative designed to run, wall-jump, and dash through 50 handcrafted, increasingly difficult levels. Can you master the momentum? Dash through enemy drones to destroy them, navigate crumbling platforms, and survive the deadly laser grids. With tight controls, instant respawns, and a neon aesthetic, Neon Velocity is the ultimate test of your reflexes.
