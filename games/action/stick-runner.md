## How to play

Use the space button to jump and use the arrow key to slide

## About

Stick Runner is a fun online game. There is only one track in this game, and obstacles of different sizes and lengths appear randomly. The stickman needs to use the two buttons on the bottom to jump or squat down to avoid obstacles! Of course, there is a time limit for squatting. When the red progress bar disappears, the stickman will stand up. You need to pay attention to this!
