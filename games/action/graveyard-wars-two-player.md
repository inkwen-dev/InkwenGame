## How to play

*Use 'WASD' keys and 'Arrow Keys' to move.
*Playable on both mobile and PC.
*Four different hats are available in the shops.
*The player who collects 10 pumpkins the fastest wins.

## About

Are you ready for a competitive game between the Mummy and the Pumpkin King? Yes, someone has to win! Team up with a friend and choose either the Mummy or the Pumpkin King.Collect as many flying pumpkins as you can! You can earn coins by eating candy and win the game by collecting all the pumpkins. Catch the balloons and bring them to the treasure chest. The fastest collector wins! Different hats await you as you win. So, gather your friends, race, and collect all the pumpkins!
