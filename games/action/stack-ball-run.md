## About

Stack Ball Run is a fast-paced arcade runner game. Drag left and right to collect coins and build your ball stack. Avoid dangerous obstacles that reduce your stack. The game speed increases over time, making it more challenging. Try to survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score.
