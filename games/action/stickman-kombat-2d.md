## How to play

CONTROLS FOR 1 PLAYER:

WASD — move
J — attack
K — special move
BACK + K — second special move
S + J — uppercut
HOLD S — block

CONTROLS FOR 2 PLAYERS ON ONE PC:

Player 1: WASD + F (attack), G (special moves)
Player 2: IJKL + : (colon) (attack), " (quote) (special moves)

COMBO:
Press attack at the right moment to continue the combo. Jump behind the enemy and hit — this triggers aerial strikes.

TACTICS:
Manage stamina, block timely, and use breakers to escape combos. Earn coins for victories and unlock new fighters!

WASD — 移动

J — 攻击

K — 特殊技

后 + K — 第二特殊技

S + J — 上勾拳

双人游戏（同一台电脑）操作：

玩家 1：WASD + F（攻击），G（特殊技）

玩家 2：IJKL + :（冒号）（攻击），"（引号）（特殊技）

连击：

在合适的时机按下攻击键即可继续连击。跳到敌人身后攻击——这将触发空中攻击。
