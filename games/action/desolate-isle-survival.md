## How to play

Universal:
Interaction with objects happens automatically when you get close enough. However, some objects require you to press the button that appears above them to interact.

Keyboard :
Move - WASD or Left/Right/Up/Down arrow keys  

Mobile 
Move and turn - use joystick

## About

Survive and escape from a mysterious island in this open-world survival adventure game. Gather resources, craft tools, build your base, and explore new areas with unique materials and challenges. Complete quests to unlock new territories and uncover the secrets of the island. Perfect for fans of survival, exploration, and crafting games.
