## About

Heart Star is undoubtedly one of the most interesting new arcade games this year. It has addictive gameplay and retro pixel art style. It is worth noting that you can control two game characters and use your wisdom to think carefully to make boys reach the same position as the girl, there are many obstacles on the platform. When you can't get over these obstacles, you can try to use another game character to increase the height of the jump. Are you ready?
