## How to play

Use WASD or Arrow keys for the players movement and Use W or up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Quevi is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect all of the metal balls while avoiding the enemy robots, flying robots and their bullets, rotating lasers, giant blades and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels with increasing difficulty.
