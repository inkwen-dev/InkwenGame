## About

Cuckoo vs Crow Monster 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a Cuckoo who have to collect the eggs while avoiding Crow monsters, flying crow monsters, spikes and reach the Crow nest in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Cuckoo vs Crow Monster" series.
