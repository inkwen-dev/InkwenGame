## About

Jake Black Cat is a 2D platformer where you play as a famous black cat who have to collect the bowls of the cat food while avoiding the enemy cats, spikes, and saws and reach your cat house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
