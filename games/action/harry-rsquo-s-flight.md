## About

Harry&rsquo;s Flight invites players to embark on an enchanting journey through the magical world. In this hypercasual game, players take control of the iconic bespectacled wizard as he navigates through a series of obstacles and challenges while soaring through the skies on his broomstick. With simple one-touch controls, players must tap the screen to help Harry ascend and descend, avoiding obstacles. The game features vibrant and whimsical graphics inspired. Players also can unlock new characters as they progress through the game.
