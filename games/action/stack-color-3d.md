## About

In the "Stacking Color 3D" game, transport as many bricks as possible. Each level will show you a new scene, and there is a person with a different color. Pay attention to transporting bricks of the same color, avoiding bricks of different colors. You only need to touch the bricks and it will stack on its own, enjoy the Stacking Color 3D.
