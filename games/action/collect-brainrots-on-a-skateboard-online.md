## How to play

Goal: Steal Brainrot from the arena, bring him home across the finish line, and place him on the podium—he will start generating money.

Computer:
• WASD or arrow keys – movement
• Space – jump
• Right mouse button – rotate camera
• Mouse wheel – zoom in/out
• E – interact (sometimes requires holding the key)
• Keys 1-9 – switch backpack slots; left mouse button – use held item

Smartphone:
• Left joystick – movement
• Right arrow button – jump
• Hold finger – rotate camera
• Tap – use/activate
• Pinch – zoom in/out
• Right button with item icon – interact with item

## About

The guard looks away - that Brainrot is yours. Grab it and ride before he turns back.

A couple of evenings from now you will have a base where brainrots stand in rows and print money while you hunt for the next one.

🛹 Collect them from the arenas, carry them past your finish line and put them on podiums - the gold piles up on its own. Get caught, and you tumble across the ground while your loot scatters.

🏝️ The islands hang higher and higher: the further you fly off a ramp, the fatter the catch - common, rare, secret, celestial. Open lucky blocks on the way and hunt gold, diamond and radioactive variants for your collection.

🛒 Money buys speed, jump power and faster boards, and those get you to islands you could only stare up at before.

How much can you carry off before they catch you? 💰
