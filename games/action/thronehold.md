## About

The throne is lost. The castle is cursed. Take it back. In Thronehold, you step into the boots of a lone warrior trapped in a crumbling castle filled with danger, darkness, and long-forgotten secrets. This intense platformer combines fast-paced action, precision jumps, and clever traps set across a labyrinthine stronghold once ruled by kings &mdash; now haunted by creatures of shadow. Leap over deadly spikes, scale ancient towers, unlock hidden paths, and fight your way through cursed knights, spectral beasts, and twisted machines guarding the throne room. Only the strongest can reclaim the Thronehold.
