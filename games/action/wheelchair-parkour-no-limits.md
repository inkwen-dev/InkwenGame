## How to play

Desktop Controls:
← / → (Arrow Keys) or A / D: Move backward and forward.
Spacebar: Jump.
Hold Spacebar while in the air: Perform a frontflip.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Move backward and forward by dragging the joystick.
Jump Button: Tap to jump.
Hold the Jump Button while in the air: Perform a frontflip.

## About

Race across the city rooftops in a wheelchair, build up speed, jump across huge gaps, and overcome dangerous obstacles. Perform tricks in the air, collect coins, and try to travel as far as possible to set a new record.

The farther you go, the more challenging the obstacles become. One wrong move — and the run is over.

Collect coins, unlock new looks for your character, and head back out to conquer the rooftops again.

### 怎么玩

桌面版操作：

← / →（方向键）或 A / D：前后移动。

空格键：跳跃。

在空中按住空格键：进行前空翻。

移动版操作：

屏幕摇杆：拖动摇杆前后移动。

跳跃按钮：点击跳跃。

在空中按住跳跃按钮：进行前空翻。
