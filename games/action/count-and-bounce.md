## About

Count And Bounce is a ball puzzle game. It guides the ball forward. Release your hand and the ball will keep moving forward. You need to turn the tiles to prevent the ball from falling. There will be calculation questions on some tiles and you need to choose the best tile to bounce based on the number of balls you have. Have fun!
