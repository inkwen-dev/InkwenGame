## About

Brokun 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you play as a human being called Brokun who have to collect all of the magical stones while avoiding the little devils, flying monsters, their bullets, spikes, fire of hell and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play with increasing difficulty. This is the second installment of the original "Brokun" series.
