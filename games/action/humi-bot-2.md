## About

Humi Bot 2 is a 2D Sci-Fi themed platformer where you have to collect yellow orbs while avoiding spikes, saws, enemy robots, flying bots and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Humi Bot" series.
