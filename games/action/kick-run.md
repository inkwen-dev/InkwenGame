## How to play

Become the ultimate runner! 🥊 Race through action-packed levels, dodge traps, outmaneuver enemies, and kick your way to the finish line. Keep moving, choose a safe path, and knock opponents aside before they stop your run.

Desktop Controls:
Arrow Keys or WASD: Move your character.
Mouse and Arrow key "Right": Click and drag to attack.

Mobile Controls:
Swipe on the screen: Move your character.
Touch on the screen: Attack

Goal:
Reach the finish line and complete each level.
