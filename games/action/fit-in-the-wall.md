## How to play

Score as much as you can by adjusting the yellow cube to fit in the pattern.

Desktop:
Use arrow keys to move the yellow cube

Mobile:
Tp on left and right side of screen to move the yellow cube in that direction.

## About

Fit in the Wall challenges you to guide a floating block into the matching slot on moving walls. Using quick reflexes and perfect timing is essential, as each wall offers just one correct fit. Success means survival, but a miss ends the game. Stay alert and keep fitting!
