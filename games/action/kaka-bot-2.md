## About

Kaka Bot 2 is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect money while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots and their bullets, spikes and rotating blades and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Kaka Bot" series.
