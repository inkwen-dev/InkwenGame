## About

Ichikas Adventure is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as Anime girl called Ichika who have to collect all of the necklaces while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures, spikes, and saws and reach the door of your house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
