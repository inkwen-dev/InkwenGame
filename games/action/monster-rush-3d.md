## About

As a funny running arcade game, there is a boss chimpanzee standing on the finish point of the track at each level. It is managed for you to collect as many yellow hearts as you can to punch it farther and crash down buildings behind the chimpanzee. There are also another eight different animals for you to unlock in the shop. Have a good time!
