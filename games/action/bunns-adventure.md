## About

Bunn's Adventure is a fun classic adventure platformer game in which you collect coins and jump over obstacles, traps and enemy's. Your goal is to collect as much coins as you can and beat all the levels. The game gets progressively harder with each level. Take your time and calculate your jumps and movement precisely and have fun playing!
