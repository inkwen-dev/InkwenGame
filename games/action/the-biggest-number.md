## How to play

🎯 Goal
Reach the finish with the biggest number possible and smash through as many walls as you can!

🎮 Controls
On desktop, use A/D or the Left/Right arrow keys. You can also hold the left mouse button and drag.
On mobile, swipe left or right. 📱

🔵 Grow your number
Collect numbers equal to or smaller than yours. Avoid larger red numbers—they will stop your run.

➕ Choose the best gates
Addition and multiplication make you stronger. Subtraction and division reduce your number, so pick your route carefully! ✖️

🛡️ Use boosters
A shield protects you from danger, while a magnet pulls useful numbers toward you.

🪙 Upgrade
Earn coins and improve your starting number and income to progress faster.
