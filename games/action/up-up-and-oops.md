## How to play

Climb the floating path by choosing the correct action for the next tile. If the path continues straight ahead, jump forward. If it turns, press the direction in which the path continues. Make your choice before the gauge runs out.

In Classic mode, clear every step to unlock the next level. Each level becomes longer and more difficult, and your next run starts after the latest level you cleared. You can earn rewards for reaching new steps even if you fail, and you may watch an advertisement to double the reward earned from that run.

In Endless mode, climb as high as possible and aim for a new high score. Classic and Endless give you three lives. After losing all three, you may use one available revive.

In Online Race, compete against 2–6 players for two minutes. Reach a higher step than the other racers to finish ahead of them. Falling returns you to your latest checkpoint, placed every 20 steps.

Desktop Controls:
W or ↑: Move upward.
S or ↓: Move downward.
A or ←: Move left.
D or →: Move right.
Spacebar: Jump forward.

Mobile Controls:
UP, DOWN, LEFT, and RIGHT Buttons: Move in the selected screen direction.
JUMP Button: Jump forward in the direction your character is facing.

### 怎么玩

沿着浮空路径攀爬，选择下一个方块对应的正确操作。如果路径笔直向前，则向前跳跃。如果路径转弯，则按下路径转弯方向的按钮。在能量槽耗尽前做出选择。

在经典模式中，清除所有台阶即可解锁下一关。每个关卡都会变得更长、更难，你的下一轮挑战将从你上次通关的关卡之后开始。即使失败，到达新的台阶也能获得奖励，观看广告还能使本次挑战获得的奖励翻倍。

在无尽模式中，尽可能攀爬到更高处，冲击新的最高分。经典模式和无尽模式都提供三条命。三条命用完后，你可以使用一次复活机会。

在在线竞速模式中，与 2-6 名玩家进行两分钟的比赛。到达比其他玩家更高的台阶即可领先他们到达终点。坠落会将你送回最近的检查点，检查点每 20 步设置一个。

桌面控制：

W 或 ↑：向上移动。

S 或 ↓：向下移动。

A 或 ←：向左移动。

D 或 →：向右移动。

空格键：向前跳跃。

移动端操作：

上、下、左、右方向键：朝所选屏幕方向移动。

跳跃键：朝角色面向的方向向前跳跃。
