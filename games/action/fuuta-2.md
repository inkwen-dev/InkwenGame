## About

Fuuta 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the lipsticks while avoiding all of the single-eyed enemy monsters and their bullets, flying monsters, pins and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Fuuta" series.
