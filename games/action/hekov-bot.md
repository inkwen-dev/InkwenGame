## About

Hekov Bot is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect all of the battery cells while avoiding the enemy bots, the flying bots and their bullets, spikes, and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
