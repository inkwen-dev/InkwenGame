## How to play

Your task is to destroy as many monsters as possible😎! Save your points and get to the top of the leaderboard🏆! Have fun playing😉!

On PC:

Left mouse button: shooting.
Tab: opening the monster spawn panel.
WASD: moving the character.
Mouse movement: controlling the camera.
Spacebar: flying and jumping.

On phone:

Left joystick: moving.
The jump button on the right side of the screen: flight and jump.
The right side of the screen: camera control.
The box icon: opening the monster spawn panel.
The gun icon: shooting.

## About

SMod: Sandbox in Backrooms is a mod-style game based on the sandbox and the Backrooms. It is a real-time simulator with enemies spawning and levels to complete. In this game, you can spawn enemies using a portal gun, complete quests, and search for weapons.
