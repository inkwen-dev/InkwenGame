## How to play

Fight your way through four powerful bosses to become the ultimate Brainrot.
After each battle, you earn gold based on your performance. Spend your gold to upgrade your character’s skills and abilities and grow stronger with every fight.

Unlock new characters as you progress through the game, each bringing unique abilities and playstyles to master.

Desktop: 
Hotkeys for the skills are 1 2 3 4 5 6 by default but you can change it to Q W E R T Y or A Z E R T Y in the settings.
