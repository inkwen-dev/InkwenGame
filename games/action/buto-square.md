Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Buto Square is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the special red hats while avoiding the square enemies, spikes, ground saws, flying square enemies and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
