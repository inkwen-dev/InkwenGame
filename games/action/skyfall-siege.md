## How to play

Play as a brave defender and defeat the threats falling from the sky. In this exciting tower defense game, you will use your turrets and fearless defensive structures to help you defeat countless enemies and ultimately protect your base.

⌨️ Desktop Controls

*← Left Arrow: Move left
*→ Right Arrow: Move right
*Spacebar: Fire weapon
*TAB:Open upgrade UI

📱 Mobile Controls

*Tap left or right of the character**: Move left/right
*Fire button**: Auto-fire

🛠 Building & Upgrading

*Mouse / Tap the Build button**: Spend money to construct or upgrade defensive facilities

## About

In this fast paced aerial battleground, I wanted to create a pure, adrenaline-driven experience where every second counts. You’ll face relentless waves of enemies descending from the skies, and survival depends on your reflexes, precision, and smart movement.
