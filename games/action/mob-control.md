## About

How to invade a castle? Mob Control will show you the correct way to win a war with the number of people's advantages. With 3D arcade play rules and stickman models, you are allowed to launch blue stickmen to mob castles at each level. It will be necessary for you to send them past the number area. Good luck and enjoy the victory!
