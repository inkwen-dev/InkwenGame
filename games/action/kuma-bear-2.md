## About

Kuma Bear 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a bear who have to collect all of the chocolate bars while avoiding the enemy dogs and foxes, flying birds, spikes and pins and reach your house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Kuma Bear" series.
