## About

The newspapers tell of a small savage boy living in the Berries Forest… And they are right ! He has a peculiar look. He jumps from berries to berries. You must carefully guide his jumps from platform to platform. But you have to take care that he never fall in empty spaces... any fall means "the death"! "Berries Jumper" is a reflex and anticipation based game. Try to make the kid progress as fast as you can without making a single mistake. And the colorful atmosphere is sure to enchant any and all players. How far can you go?
