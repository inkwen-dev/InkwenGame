## How to play

Your goal is to reach the end of the level while overcoming obstacles and defeating enemies.

Desktop Controls:

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

Jump: Spacebar

Shoot: Press Spacebar more than once

Mobile Controls:

Move, Jump: On-screen buttons.

Shoot: Tap the jump button more than once

Time your jumps carefully, break through obstacles, and take down enemies with precision. Good luck!

## About

How fast can you react in a world full of obstacles and enemies? In Jiffy, a retro-style, fast-paced action platformer, every jump counts—literally! Leap over obstacles, break through destructible blocks, and unleash powerful shots by double-tapping jump to take down foes. With over 25 levels, challenging bosses, and endless and battle modes, the action never stops. Ready to put your skills to the test?
