## How to play

Goal: climb as high as you can. Reach the flag at the top of each level to unlock the next one. There are 30 levels; finish them all to complete the tower. If you fall, you lose height, so land every jump.

How to play
- Move across platforms and avoid traps: spinning blades, crushers, pistons, vanishing and moving platforms, conveyors and wind.
- Use springs and rings to launch yourself up. Timing is everything.
- You can jump twice in the air (double jump) to fix a short jump.
- Collect coins and diamonds: buy skins in the shop, or skip a level that is too hard.
- Set your best height and compete in the online leaderboard.
- Start in the Training Hall to try every obstacle safely.

Controls (desktop)
- WASD / Arrow keys: move
- Mouse: look around
- Space: jump (press again in the air for a double jump)
- Shift: run
- Pause button (top left): pause and settings

Controls (mobile)
- Left side of the screen: floating joystick (push further to run)
- Right side of the screen: swipe to rotate the camera
- JUMP button: jump (tap twice for a double jump)
- RUN button: auto-run on/off

## About

Only Up Obby: Tower Climb is a 3D obby parkour platformer: run, jump and climb a huge tower made of 30 hand-built levels. Every level brings new traps: moving and vanishing platforms, spinning blades, crushers, conveyors, springs, wind zones and swinging hammers. Fall and you lose height, so every jump counts.

Collect coins and diamonds on the way, unlock 30+ skins (frog, spider, cat, dog, capybara, stickman and their color variants), compete for the best height in the online leaderboard and skip a level if it gets too hard. A training hall teaches every obstacle before the real climb.

Works on desktop and mobile: keyboard and mouse, or a floating joystick with auto-run and one big jump button on touch screens. English and Russian.
