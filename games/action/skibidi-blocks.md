## About

Skibidi Blocks Game is a fast-paced and challenging building block runner game that will test your reflexes and strategic thinking skills. A stack of blocks through a series of obstacles, collecting Poo along the way. The blocks will automatically move forward, so you must carefully plan your jumps and movements to avoid falling off the screen.
