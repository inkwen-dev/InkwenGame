## About

TNTcraft is a 2 player TNT game in a minecraft world where your mission is to explode the zombie spawner using TNT. Make tactics with your friend and explode zombies but beware, some of the zombies can explode and may hurt you. With 4 different maps fight against zombies with your friend, make tactics and explode your TNTs.
