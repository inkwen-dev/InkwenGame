## About

Aroka 2 is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as a cute anime girl who have to collect all of the bottles containing medicine while avoiding the enemy monsters, super fast monsters, flying creatures, rotating blades, spikes, fire and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Aroka"series.
