## How to play

Desktop: Use WASD / Arrow keys / Mouse to move. Shoot with Space / Click.

Mobile: Swipe your finger on the screen to move. Swipe to aim and release to shoot.

## About

Man Runner 2048 is a fun casual game where you run, grow big and fight against monsters. Your goal is to run and merge to reach 2048 or the biggest number that you can in every level. Grow big by merging with the collectible numbers, or by running through green multiplier gates. Avoid all obstacles and running through red gates. Activate the cannon at the end of every level and shoot the monsters so they can't reach you. Collect gems to buy more rockets, fire power, and hats. Get the highest scores, defeat the monsters and conquer all the islands!
