## About

Little Big Fighters is an action survival game where you'll embark on a thrilling journey to show the world who truly reigns supreme in the arena of combat. Defeat your opponents, grow stronger with each victory, and collect valuable resources to bolster your power and dominance. Are you ready to prove yourself as the ultimate fighter in an epic battle for survival?
