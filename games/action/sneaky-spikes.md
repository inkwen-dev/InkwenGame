## About

Sneaky Spikes is a troll platformer drawn on notebook paper. Floors drop, spikes pop and doors bite, and a sarcastic narrator comments on every death. Finish a page and every one of your deaths replays at once in the Death Parade. 60 levels, stars to collect and a speedrun timer. How to play: Run and jump to the door at the end of each page. Expect the unexpected: watch for traps, learn from each death and try again.
