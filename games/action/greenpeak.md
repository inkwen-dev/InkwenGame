## How to play

🎮 PC Controls
• Move — WASD or arrow keys ⌨️
• Jump — standard jump key
• Sprint — Shift ⚡

📱 Mobile Controls
• Move — virtual joystick on the left
• Jump and sprint — buttons on the right

⚡ Stamina
Sprinting uses stamina. If you run for too long, stamina will run out. Slow down or stop to recover it.

🎯 Game Goal
Climb to the top of the green mountain ⛰️, explore the world 🌿, and collect all meme collections 😄 along the way.

🔥 Checkpoints
Checkpoints are areas surrounded by torches 🔥.
Stand inside a checkpoint to save your progress. After falling or dying, you will respawn there.

🤝 Online Play
Explore the mountain together with friends, walk, jump, and enjoy the journey at your own pace.
