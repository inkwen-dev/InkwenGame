## How to play

Survive the island by completing obstacle stages, collecting resources, crafting useful items, and helping others along the way. Reach the end of each level without falling or getting eliminated to win.

PC: Move using Arrow Keys or W A S D.
Mobile: Move using the on-screen joystick.

Collect. Craft. Help. Survive.
