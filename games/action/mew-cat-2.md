## About

Mew Cat 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect cat food bowls while avoiding the enemy cats, flying robots,spikes and saws and reach the green flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Mew Cat" game series.
