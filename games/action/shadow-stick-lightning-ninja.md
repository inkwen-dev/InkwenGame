## How to play

Controls for PC/Keyboard:

1. arrow keys "left" and "right" for movement;
2. arrow key "up" - jump;
3. key "V" - normal attack;
4. key "B" - Special skill, allowing a 30% increase in attack and defense for 15 seconds;
5. key "F" - Summon dragon;
6. keys "Z," "X," "C" - skill buttons providing powerful attacks (effect depends on the skill branches chosen).


Controls for mobile devices/touchscreen:

1. slider in the bottom left corner - for movement "left" and "right";
2. other control buttons are displayed on the screen.
