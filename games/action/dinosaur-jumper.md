## About

Welcome to our new game Dinosaur Jumper, which takes you back to prehistoric times. The little dinosaur has to jump, but not very high because if it jumps too high it will touch the bones and explode. The dinosaur needs to jump and stand on the platform. Each time the dinosaur stop on the platform, you get points. Enjoy and have fun!!!
