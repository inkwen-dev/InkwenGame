## About

Two brothers need to escape from prison help them! The brothers are very bored in prison. There are many puzzle-style buttons in the prison that need to be solved. Each button activates a different platform. Move the platforms and find the three golden keys you need. After finding the golden keys, escape from the prison together with your brother. Remember, one of you can break doors while the other can pass through narrow tunnels and snow.
