## How to play

Take control of a rolling ball and race through a track filled with platforms, obstacles, gaps, and dangerous traps. Stay on course, react quickly, and keep your momentum as you push your run as far as possible.

Collect coins along the way, discover new ball skins, and chase your highest score. The further you go, the faster and more challenging the course becomes.

Keep rolling. Don't fall. How far can you go?

Controls

Mobile:
Drag left or right to steer the ball.

Desktop:
Move the mouse left or right to control the ball.

Goal:
Stay on the course, avoid obstacles, collect coins, and survive for as long as possible.
