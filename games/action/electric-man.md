## About

Electric Man is an awesome stickman fighter Flash game in which you must fight against a range of opponents using a variety of different punch and kick combos. Before you enter the game, you can choose the name and color of your stickman, and also the difficulty level (easy, normal or pro). Once you have chosen your character you can then complete a tutorial that shows you the basic game mechanics and move sets.
