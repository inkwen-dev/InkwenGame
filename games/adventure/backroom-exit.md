## About

Are you ready to face the eerie emptiness of the Backrooms? In Backroom Exit, a tense first-person escape game, you find yourself trapped in a disturbing world of yellow walls and endless corridors. Your only objective is to find the exit, but it wont be easy. Explore the chilling environment, search for hidden objects, and gather clues to unravel the mysteries that surround you. Youll need to use your wits to solve clever puzzles, combine objects, and discover how to unlock the door to freedom before the atmosphere completely engulfs you.
