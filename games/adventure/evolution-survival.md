## How to play

Controls:

- Mouse / finger — steer your creature's movement
- WASD / arrow keys — alternative controls on PC
- Ability buttons — displayed at the bottom of the screen (or press keys 1–5)
- Spacebar — dash (if unlocked)

Tips:

- Don't rush strong enemies early on — build up your mutations first
- Pick up enemy drops: healing, magnet, shield, explosion
- Collect rare golden stars — they grant currency for permanent upgrades

## About

A survival game where you begin as a tiny cell in the primordial soup. Collect DNA by consuming food and defeating enemies to trigger evolution and choose your mutation: speed, attack, defense, poison, regeneration, or intelligence.

With each evolution, the world transforms — from open ocean to the deep sea, from shallow waters to dry land. Enemies grow stronger, elite opponents emerge, and fearsome bosses stand in your way. Combine mutations to unlock secret forms: Dragon, Leviathan, Phantom, Hive Mind, and more.

Collect rare stars scattered across the map and invest them into permanent upgrades that carry over between runs. Use active abilities — dash, acid spit, death cloud, traps, and cover — to survive as long as possible.

How many evolutions will you conquer?

### 介绍

一款生存游戏，你将从原始汤中的一个微小细胞开始。通过摄取食物和击败敌人来收集DNA，触发进化，并选择你的变异：速度、攻击、防御、毒素、再生或智力。

随着每一次进化，世界都会发生变化——从广阔的海洋到深海，从浅滩到陆地。敌人会变得更加强大，精英对手会不断涌现，可怕的Boss也会挡在你的面前。组合不同的变异，解锁隐藏形态：巨龙、利维坦、幻影、蜂巢思维等等。

收集散落在地图各处的稀有星星，并将它们投资到永久升级中，这些升级会在下次游戏时保留。利用主动技能——冲刺、酸液喷吐、死亡之云、陷阱和掩体——尽可能地生存下去。

你能征服多少次进化？
