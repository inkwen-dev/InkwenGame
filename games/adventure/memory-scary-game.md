## How to play

Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

Before you on the screen you will see the playing field on which the cards will lie. You can flip two of them in one move. Carefully consider the drawings that are drawn on them and remember their location. As soon as you find two identical pictures, click on the card data and open them simultaneously. Thus, you remove them from the playing field and get points for it.
