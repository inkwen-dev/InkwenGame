## About

Clown Escape is a point and click escape game developed by 8BGames. Imagine that you are about to pick up a Boy to school for fancy dress competition. When he went to wear Clown costume; he got stuck in his room. Find some hidden object to solve some interesting clues to escape the Clown. Good luck…Have fun!
