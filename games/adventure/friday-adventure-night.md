## About

This is a mod for Friday night funkin music game, enjoy racing with all the characters : Boyfriend. Girlfriend, Daddy Dearest, Skid and Pump. Drive and listen to friday night funkin soundtrack at the same time, boyfriend’s mission is to drive without loosing in order to prove himself worthy to be Girlfriend's partner. You will be able to drive in the city with in friday night funkin at week 4, this mod has also a wide selection of cars that you can buy after collecting coins.
