## About

Chicken Escape is a 3D stealth puzzle on a colorful farm. Play as a clever chicken planning a daring escape through yards, barns and fences guarded by patrolling dogs. Your main trick is the cardboard box disguise: hide under it right in front of a guard and the dog sees just a harmless crate. But stay still while you are in its sight, or your cover is blown! Watch the glowing vision cones of the dogs, learn their patrol routes, hide behind fences and haystacks and time your dashes. Collect all golden corn grains to unlock the gate and run to the green beacon of freedom. Many levels with 3-star ratings, a tutorial level, and a wardrobe with fun skins for the chicken and the box.
