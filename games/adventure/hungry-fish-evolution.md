## About

Hungry Fish Evolution is a free online game for kids. In the beginning, you are an ordinary fish who needs to eat small fish to evolve into a big one. At the same time, you need to show superb dodging skills, avoid dangerous giant fish, and avoid your own death. Keep winning in the game, devour all kinds of opponents in front of you, and become the ultimate winner!
