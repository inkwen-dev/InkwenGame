## How to play

Controls
PC: WASD — move, mouse — look, E — interact / admit, R — refuse, F — patient chart, G — records log, Q — flashlight, C — coffee to go, RMB (hold) — zoom in, 1–6 — quick phrases in co-op, Esc — pause. Cameras: walk up to the surveillance tablet and press E.

Smartphone: left stick — move, swipe on the right — look, round buttons on the right — flashlight, magnifier, and coffee; decisions and all other actions — tap the buttons and prompts on screen.

## About

There's a queue of patients outside the reception window — but after midnight, it isn't only sick animals that come to town.

Some of them are only pretending to be a cat, a dog, or a rabbit: an extra eye, the wrong coat color, a smile that's a little too human, a shadow that shouldn't be there, a voice that's subtly wrong.

Your job is to tell a real patient from an anomaly using nothing but a photo and their paperwork. Let your own kind in and heal them; keep the others off the threshold. Every animal you admit has to be led to a ward and treated through mini-games — and in between, you'll need to run the generator, check the cages, and keep an eye on the security cameras. All of it without losing your mind, and without looking where you shouldn't.

Every shift gets darker, the anomalies grow more numerous, and something that was only pretending to be a patient begins to roam the clinic's corridors. Play solo or in co-op with up to 4 players. Will you live to see the dawn?

### 介绍

接待窗口外排起了长队——但午夜过后，来到镇上的可不只有生病的动物。

有些动物只是伪装成猫、狗或兔子：多长了一只眼睛，毛色不对，笑容过于人性化，身后出现了不该出现的影子，声音也略显怪异。

你的任务是仅凭照片和病历来区分真正的病人和异常生物。允许同类进入并治愈它们；阻止其他生物靠近。你接收的每一只动物都必须被带到病房，并通过一系列小游戏进行治疗——除此之外，你还需要运行发电机、检查笼子，并监控监控摄像头。所有这一切都必须在保持理智的前提下完成，并且不能去看那些不该看的地方。

每个班次都变得更加黑暗，异常生物的数量也越来越多，而那些原本只是伪装成病人的生物开始在诊所的走廊里游荡。可单人游玩，也可与最多 4 名玩家合作游玩。你能活到黎明吗？
