## How to play

Use the 4 arrow keys on the keyboard or the touch button on the screen

## About

In the game you will play the role of a squirrel who will go to strange lands and fight monsters, overcome obstacles and collect all the fruits to complete the level. Each level you will be given three lives and try to use your dexterity and quick hands to complete the game. The game is designed with 20 levels and is optimized for mobile and PC.
