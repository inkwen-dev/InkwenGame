## How to play

- Walk through a fully explorable 1892 cabin: hallway, living room, bedroom, kitchen, fireplace nook and the L-leg evidence room.
- Find and photograph 8 haunted objects - Lily's rag doll, the grimoire, the bone blade, the pentagram, the demon-child and more.
- Every photograph you take is a piece of evidence. Open your journal (TAB) to read the cabin's history and pin each proof to the evidence board.
- Real first-person controls with mouse look, sprint, crouch, a flashlight, an old film camera with a working viewfinder (battery, shots, date/time), flickering ceiling lights and dust-filled air.
- Dynamic audio: footsteps on wood, camera shutter, an atmospheric ambience track and a whisper that gets closer the longer you stay.
- Survive the finale: the demon-child is waiting at the foot of the bed. Photograph her before she learns your name - then get out.

## About

Winter, 1892. In a remote cabin, a grieving mother named Elara lost her daughter, Lily, to a fever. Consumed by grief, she performed a forbidden ritual hidden inside the cabin's walls to bring her daughter back. The girl returned - but her eyes were black, her voice a whisper of static, and she spoke in tongues. The thing that came back has worn Lily's face for eight years... and it is still in the cabin. Waiting.
You are a hunter of places with dark histories. Enter the house. Look. Prove what happened.

### 怎么玩

- 漫步于一座可完全探索的1892年小屋：走廊、客厅、卧室、厨房、壁炉角落以及L形物证室。

- 寻找并拍摄8件闹鬼物品——莉莉的布娃娃、魔法书、骨刃、五芒星、恶魔之子等等。

- 你拍摄的每张照片都是一份证据。打开你的日记（TAB键）阅读小屋的历史，并将每份证据钉在证据板上。

- 真实的第一人称视角操控，包括鼠标视角、冲刺、蹲伏、手电筒、一台带有可用取景器的老式胶片相机（电池、拍摄张数、日期/时间显示）、闪烁的天花板灯光以及弥漫着灰尘的空气。

- 动态音效：木地板上的脚步声、相机快门声、氛围音乐以及随着你停留时间的延长而越来越近的低语声。

- 活过最终关卡：恶魔之子正守候在床脚。在她知道你的名字之前拍下她的照片——然后逃出去。
