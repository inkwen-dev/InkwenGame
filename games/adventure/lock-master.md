## How to play

Click and hold the left mouse button to move the lock release to stop You have 3 lives in each room if you run out you go back to level 1 Complete each level by reaching the orange dot with the lock There are a total of 16 levels

## About

Lock Master is an exciting adventure game where players try to solve challenging puzzles to unlock locks in a mysterious maze world. You need to use the mouse to bring the lock to an orange dot. Each level becomes increasingly difficult, and there are a total of 16 levels. You must overcome obstacles, use your logical skills, and react quickly. If you successfully unlock the lock, you can proceed to the next level. Lock Master is waiting to test your skills!
