Use Mouse To Play and Keyboard

## About

Collect gifts with moving the snack without collides it's body You have three types of control. The default one is relative. If the snake is moving horizontally, you need to touch the screen above or below the snake to make him go up or down. If he moves virtically, you need to touch the screen on the snake's right or left to make him go in the adequate direction.
