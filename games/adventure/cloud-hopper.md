## How to play

An emoji continuously falls through clouds Tap the screen to tilt the clouds in one direction tap again to tilt them the opposite way Collect coins along the way Use or avoid red obstacles to control your speed Go as far as possible to achieve a high

## About

Cloud Hopper is a fun, fast-paced arcade game where you guide a falling emoji through a sky full of shifting clouds. Tap to tilt the clouds and control your descent, bouncing your way downward while collecting coins. Red obstacles don&rsquo;t just slow you down they can be used strategically to reduce speed if you gain too much momentum. Don&rsquo;t drift too far off the edges, or you&rsquo;ll fall. Stay balanced, react quickly, and see how far you can go.
