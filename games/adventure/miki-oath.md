## How to play

Desktop: use A/D or the Left/Right Arrow keys to move and Space, W, or Up Arrow to jump. Mobile: use the on-screen touch controls. Reach the flag to clear each level, avoid hazards, and keep trying after every fall. Use the sound control to mute audio. Rewarded ads are optional and can be used to skip a difficult level.

## About

Miki Oath is a story-driven precision platformer with 106 handcrafted levels across ten magical worlds. Guide Miki through spikes, saws, moving platforms, springs, and teleporters as she keeps her promise to save Luna. Fast restarts, responsive controls, and full localization in nine languages make every attempt easy to jump back into.
