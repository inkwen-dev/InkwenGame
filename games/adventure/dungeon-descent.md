## How to play

Dungeon Descent is a fast-paced voxel dungeon crawler. Clear rooms of monsters, crack open chests, and stack weapons, armor, upgrades, and artifacts as you push deeper. Combat is quick and deadly—dodge, swap weapons on the fly, and unleash artifact powers when the fight turns ugly. Each run gets harder, with tougher biomes, brutal bosses, and Ascension modes for players who want more heat. Loot. Survive. Descend deeper.
