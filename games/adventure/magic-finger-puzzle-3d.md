## About

Magic Finger Puzzle 3D is an interesting casual puzzle online game. You have an amazing pair of hands that can fire the laser. It can help you smash the cages that are holding you. It can also launch boxes onto barricades so that you can escape from the cage without stepping on spikes and dying. But dont worry, you need to use your ingenuity to make good use of those amazing hands. All difficulties will be solved. Join the game if you want to try a journey full of adventure.
