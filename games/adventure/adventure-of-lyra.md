## About

Find butterflies to save Lyra from a bad magician Story begins with a collision with a dragon and Lyra, all the butterflies flew away during this collision. Bad magician prisoned Lyra and gave her a condition to collect those butterflies to free herself. Now the player has to help to collect those butterflies. Guide the way to get the butterflies and free Lyra. Be Careful! There were full of mysterious enemies waiting so don&rsquo;t let fall of Lyra either the game will be over.
