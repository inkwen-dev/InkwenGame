## How to play

WASD Management
Space - Jump
E - Start growth
RMB - Rotate camera

Pets give income multipliers
Potions give powerups
The higher the world, the more difficult it is to level up

Joystick on the left - Movement
The buttons on the right are Jump/Purchase

## About

An exciting adventure in which you have to boost your growth with a variety of food, pets, potions and other methods.

Conquer the peaks of each location, discover new worlds and take the lead among the players!
