## About

Lady Gold Miner is a sweet game version of classic gold miner game. In this game your will help our Cute Lady to collect gold to help her to create a good life. She is a strong woman but she still needs your help. Sometimes she catch useless things to collect and she waste her precious time. So she can finish her job if you help her to collect only good things. Avoid bombs, especially TNT! Or else you may lose some of golds. Have fun!
