## About

This is a brain-burning and challenging escape room online game. Interesting gameplay so you wont get bored. You need to open 100 doors to escape the room. Interact with objects and solve puzzles to complete the tasks in the escape game. Each level is unlocked to give you a thrilling adventure experience. Each room requires a collection of clues and you may explore different places with unexpected rewards. Solve the puzzles, and take inventory of the hidden items. You may use them to open the room, move on to the next level, use your skills, and do whatever it takes to escape.
