## About

Flappy Sprunki Adventure is a super fun flappy game for kids and grown ups. Your goal is to keep Sprunki flying in the air for as long as you can. Watch out though: if you grab a sandwich, Sprunki gets bigger and heavier. That makes flying harder! Eat a salad instead to shrink back down and fly easier. Stay up, flap fast, and have a great time!
