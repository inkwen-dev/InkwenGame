## About

Superhero in the City is an online game in the style of GTA, but with Spider-Man in the title role. At your disposal is a whole city in which you can do whatever you want. You can steal cars, you can fight with residents and policemen, and using both firearms and heroic abilities, you can complete tasks, or you can find a tank and smash everything to the ground, in general, you decide how to entertain yourself, as in other sandboxes.
