## How to play

The controls are simple and intuitive:

Movement: Keys W, A, S, D

Jump: Space bar (use it together with trampolines for super jumps!)

The goal of the game is to explore the map, find and collect all the hidden capybaras.

Look around! Capybaras can hide in the most unexpected places. Use trampolines and power-ups to reach the secret corners.

## About

Welcome to a huge and crazy world where your main goal is to find and collect all the hidden capybaras!

Explore a giant, diverse map with dizzying trampolines that will take you to the highest platforms. But be careful — the territories are guarded by dangerous monsters! Don't let yourself get caught.

To fight back, you have a whole arsenal of funny but effective "slaps": a giant hand, a roll of toilet paper, or even a venerable bream fish! Stun the enemy and run on.

Use special power-ups: a magic carpet for flying, a speed spring for a lightning-fast dash and a jump spring to conquer inaccessible places.

For the capybaras you find, you will receive three unique skins for your character. Become the most stylish seeker!

Ready for a big adventure? Launch the game, explore every corner and become a legendary capybara hunter!

### 介绍

欢迎来到一个巨大而疯狂的世界！你的主要目标是找到并收集所有隐藏的水豚！

探索一张巨大而多样的地图，这里有令人眼花缭乱的蹦床，可以带你登上最高的平台。但要小心——领地由危险的怪物守护！别让自己被抓住。

为了反击，你拥有各种滑稽但有效的“招式”攻击：巨手、卷筒卫生纸，甚至是威风凛凛的鲷鱼！击晕敌人，然后继续前进。

使用特殊道具：魔毯可以让你飞翔，加速弹簧可以让你闪电般冲刺，跳跃弹簧可以让你征服难以到达的地方。

找到水豚后，你将获得三款独特的角色皮肤。成为最时尚的搜寻者！
