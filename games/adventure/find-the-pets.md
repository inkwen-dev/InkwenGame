## How to play

PC Controls
WASD / arrow keys = move
Space = jump
Move mouse = camera rotation
Tab = show / hide mouse cursor
Left-click = interact with the in-game buttons
M = open map
Q, E, F = get the power up (by watching a rewarded ad)
P = pets menu
L = location menu
N = Settings menu

Mobile Controls
Left joystick = move
Jump button = jump
Move your finger on the right area = camera rotation

## About

Find The Pets is an exciting 3D adventure game where your mission is to track down 150 adorable, hilarious, and sometimes elusive pets hiding across imaginative maps. From meme-famous cats like Floppa to other quirky and delightful creatures, every discovery adds fun and charm to your journey. Explore diverse worlds, overcome tricky obstacles, and make the most of powerful boosts to complete your collection. Are you ready for the ultimate pet-finding challenge?
Features
150 unique pets to discover, including popular meme animals and whimsical creatures
6 distinct maps set in different themed worlds, each offering unique designs and challenges
Special boosts such as fast running, super jump, and radar to help you locate pets more efficiently
Engaging Obby gameplay with tricky routes and hidden secrets to uncover
Beautiful 3D graphics that bring each map and character to life
Exciting achievements and tasks to complete as you progress
