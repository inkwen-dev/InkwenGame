## About

Maze Escape Challenge is a fast-paced maze game where your goal is to reach the exit as quickly as possible. Each level features a unique labyrinth with increasing difficulty and new surprises. Some mazes include Dark Mode, where visibility is reduced and you must navigate carefully using only the small light around your player. To progress to higher levels, you need to collect a required number of stars. Stars are earned by completing mazes fast &mdash; the quicker you finish, the more stars you receive (1 to 3 stars). Unlock new levels, improve your best times, and try to perfect every maze. Can you master all labyrinths and collect every star?
