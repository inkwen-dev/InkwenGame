## About

Relive the classic fun of Lode Retro Adventure, a 2D pixel game that will make you dig for gold. You will play as a gold miner who must collect a certain amount of gold coins in each level. You will use your shovel to break the ground tiles and trap the purple monsters, but be careful, they can escape and chase you. You will also find other treasures such as golden bananas that will boost your score. Lode Retro Adventure is a game that will test your strategy. Can you collect all the gold coins?
