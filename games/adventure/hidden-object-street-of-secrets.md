## About

Hidden Objects: Street of Secrets is an atmospheric hidden object game where you become the lead detective. A mysterious case awaits, and your insightful assistant is there to help, offering hints and guiding you to crucial details.

Explore a series of picturesque and mysterious locations. Each scene is a new challenge for your keen eye, from evening-lit streets and cozy cafés to enigmatic rooms where every detail matters. Find all the hidden objects to progress.

Immerse yourself in the perfect relaxing experience. Enjoy stylish graphics, a calm, detective-themed soundtrack, and leisurely yet engaging gameplay. It's the ideal way to unwind and sharpen your powers of observation.
