PLAYING TIPS If you can't find all the chicks in a level it's likely they are hiding in a secret area. Friendly butterflies indicate that a secret area is nearby. If you see a butterfly, search nearby walls and floors for a secret area. Remember, swipe down on the screen to drop through platforms. Secret areas could be below Cluckles.
