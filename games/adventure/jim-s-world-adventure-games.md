Jim's World gives you the chance to step back in time to your childhood with the legendary challenge in the adventure game: Princess Rescue. Your task is to help Jim run through the mysterious jungle, jump over the obstacles and super evil monsters, and save the beautiful Princess at the adventure's final destination
