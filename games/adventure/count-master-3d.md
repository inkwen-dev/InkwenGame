## About

Counting Master 3D is a fun and engaging game that helps improve your counting and decision-making skills. In this game, players collect numbers while navigating through exciting obstacles and challenges. As you progress, the levels become more difficult, making the gameplay even more thrilling. With smooth controls and colorful 3D graphics, it is enjoyable for players of all ages. You need to think quickly and use smart strategies to collect the maximum number of units and grow your crowd. The goal is to reach the finish line with the highest count possible. Counting Master 3D is not only entertaining but also keeps your mind active and sharp, making it a perfect game for passing time.
