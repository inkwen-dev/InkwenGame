## About

It's Garbin' time! Billionaire polluters are wrecking the planet! This is a job for Garby the Garbage Can! Guide Garby through colorful worlds as you run and jump collecting all the trash you see. Learn about the affects of e-waste, oil spills, and space debris on our ecosystem. Take on big corporate polluters in exciting boss battles.
