## About

Match To Paint is an interesting game that mixed match elements and painting elements. In a limited number of steps, you only need to click two squares of the same color to eliminate them directly, and the beautiful 3D game graphics bring you the best game experience. This is a big competition of brainpower, will you accept the challenge? Come on, join us and show your game talent in the game Match To Paint!
