## How to play

Use the hook to capture heroes underground to bring them to the battlefield.
Improve your hook's features to get more heroes underground.
Remember, each hero has its own unique features.
Also, as you complete each arena, the next one becomes much more difficult. Don't forget to strengthen your heroes.

## About
