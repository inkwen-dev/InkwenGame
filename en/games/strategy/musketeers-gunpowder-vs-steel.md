## How to play

Left Mouse Button or Joystick: Control the commander’s movement
Q: Turn Squad Left
W: Move Squad Forward
E: Turn Squad Right
A: Squad Follows the Player
S: Stop Squad Movement
D: Indicate Target for Squad
X: Move Squad Backwards
F: Fire Pistol
V: Mount Horse
Space: Switch Camera View

## About

Musketeers: Gunpowder vs Steel is an exciting free-to-play strategy game set in the 17th century. Command a team of diverse soldiers, issuing orders and making vital choices during intense battles. Available on iOS, Android, and desktop, it’s ideal for fans of historical tactics.
