## How to play

Swipe to aim the Claw. Release your finger to drop it onto items.
Picked-up items are applied automatically.

Upgrade your Claw's grip strength and item storage width to collect items more efficiently.
Choose abilities between enemy waves to improve your item-grabbing power even further!

## About

Immerse yourself in the thrilling blend of addictive gameplay of Clawler fight! In this action-packed game, you step into the shoes of an ordinary guy on a mission to survive the zombie apocalypse while tackling challenging levels. Maneuver a crane equipped with a claw to pick up objects and launch them at zombies! As you advance, you will collect valuable resources and currency, unlocking powerful upgrades for your character through a card-based skill progression system.

Prepare for fierce battles against a variety of terrifying zombies, each possessing unique abilities and strengths. Your ultimate objective is to survive.

Are you ready to face the ultimate challenge of skill and strategy? Gather your courage, sharpen your weapons, and embark on an unforgettable adventure in this ARPG masterpiece!
