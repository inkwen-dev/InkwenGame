## How to play

On PC Use the arrow keys Up Down Left Right to move the tiles On mobile devices Swipe your finger in the desired direction Up Down Left Right to move the tiles

## About

Merge tiles with identical numbers by sliding them across the board. When two tiles with the same number combine, they create a new tile with double the value. The goal is to reach the 2048 tile to win, but you can keep playing for an even higher score. Be careful not to run out of moves!
