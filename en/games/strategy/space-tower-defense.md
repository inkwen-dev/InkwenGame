Mouse or keyboard or touch screen

## About

Tower Defense is a strategy game where the player's goal is to protect their territories and the main tower from enemy attacks. To do this, the player builds defensive structures along the attack trajectory, the strength of which depends on the cost of energy that the player receives for defeating enemy equipment.
