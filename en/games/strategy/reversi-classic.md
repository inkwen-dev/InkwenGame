## How to play

Goal: have more discs of your color on the board when the game ends. Players take turns placing one disc. A move is only allowed where it sandwiches at least one opponent disc in a straight or diagonal line between the new disc and one of yours — all sandwiched discs flip to your color. Dots show legal moves. If you have no legal move, your turn passes; the game ends when nobody can move. Tip: corners can never be flipped back, so grab them. Controls: click or tap a cell to place a disc (mouse, touch). Hover or hold to preview what will flip (training mode). Esc opens the game menu.
