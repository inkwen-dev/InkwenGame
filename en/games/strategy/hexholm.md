## How to play

1. Tap one of your territories, then pick a unit from the bottom bar and place it on an empty hex inside your land.
2. Tap a unit to see where it can go — highlighted hexes show valid moves. Tap one to move, or tap an enemy to attack.
3. Capturing neutral and enemy hexes expands your territory and raises your income.
4. Each turn you earn gold based on the land you hold. Spend it on stronger units — and merge matching units to upgrade them.
5. Tap END TURN when you're finished; the AI factions then take their turns.
6. Eliminate every rival faction to win the map. Slipped up? Use UNDO to roll back a turn.
