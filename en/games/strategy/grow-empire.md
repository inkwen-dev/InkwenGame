## How to play

Your task is either to defend the fortress or to attack! Release troops to attack or defend - they will fight automatically. To defeat the enemy, use various abilities and upgrade your warriors.

## About

Strategically upgrade your walls, towers, and warriors. Defend your city from the attacks of elephants, siege guns and waves of enemy troops, covering the sky with arrows and fire.

Defeat your enemies by clashing with Egyptian, Italian, Gallic, Iberian and Carthaginian warriors, and expand your empire across Europe and Africa. Conquer their cities and get chests with gems, gold, potions and maps! Defend your colonies and replenish your bank with gold. Become the leader that Rome claims to be.
