## How to play

Mouse click or tap: Select cards, buttons, and menus
Drag and drop: Place cat cards on the battlefield
Click or tap deployed cats: View their stats
Click or tap the language button: Switch between Chinese and English

## About

Cat Snack Defense is a casual tower-defense strategy game where you deploy unique cats to protect precious fish crackers from waves of hungry animals. Each cat has different abilities, attack styles, and roles, so choosing the right team and placement is essential.
Your objective is to survive every enemy wave and keep the goldfish cracker alive. You win by defeating all attackers before they destroy the cracker. Collect rewards, unlock new cat cards, and build a stronger defense.
