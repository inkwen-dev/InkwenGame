## About

TRZ Battleship is a free strategy and classic online game for boys played on a puzzle game. Each player takes turns trying and hitting each other's battleships. You must guess the positions of all enemy ships. If you hit all parts of a ship then it will become visible to you. Do it before the opponent annihilates all of yours! Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop are playable. Enjoy TRZ Battleship!
