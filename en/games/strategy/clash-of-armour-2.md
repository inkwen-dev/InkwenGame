## About

Tanks are always an effective weapon in wartime. In the fun and addictive game Clash of Tanks, you get to challenge your opponent in a fierce tank battle. Just deploy your tanks by placing them in strategic positions on your side of the map. Wait until the power bar below each tank type is sufficient and grab your new unit to place it wherever you like. Earn cash and spend it on cool upgrades for your tanks to make them more durable and deadly! Eliminate your enemy's tank fleet and lead your machines of war to victory.
