## About

Hold the line. Build deadly defenses. Survive the zombie rush. In this fast-paced tower defense game, waves of zombies never stop coming&mdash;and they get smarter, tougher, and faster every round. Place and upgrade your towers, combine different damage types, and adapt your layout to counter brute tanks, sprinting runners, and armored horrors. Earn resources from every victory, unlock new towers and power-ups, and create the ultimate kill zone with slows, splash damage, and high DPS combos. One wrong placement can break your defense&hellip; but a perfect strategy turns the battlefield into a zombie grinder.
