## How to play

Your survivor walks and fights automatically. When gear drops, swipe the card right to equip it or left to sell it for gold (on desktop, drag it with the mouse). When the ability bar appears, tap it to land a power strike. Spend gold in the Tavern to train your hero, summon bosses from the Bosses tab, and open PvP Raid to attack rival shelters for trophies and new maps.

## About

Walk the last highway through a zombie wasteland. Highway of the Dead is an auto-battler RPG: your survivor fights on his own while you make the calls that matter. Every fallen zombie can drop new gear - swipe it right to equip or left to sell it for gold. Train your hero in the tavern, fill your slots with weapons, armor and trinkets, and push from section to section as day turns to night on the road. Meet lost souls and choose good or evil to call faction bosses, survive elite special encounters, and raid rival survivor shelters to win trophies that unlock new maps with stronger loot. Short sessions, steady progress, and a road that never ends.
