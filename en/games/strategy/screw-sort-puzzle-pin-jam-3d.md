## About

Want a fun 3D puzzle that&rsquo;s strangely relaxing? In ScrewSortPuzzle:PinJam3D, you&rsquo;ll remove every screw, sort colorful pins, and match them to the right slots without causing a jam! You can experience remove screw mode.
