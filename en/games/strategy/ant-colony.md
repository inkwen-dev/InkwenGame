## About

ANT COLONY is a relaxing puzzle where a tiny ant crew hauls a pixel picture away, block by block. Tap a coloured box to drop it into a slot. Ants march out, pick up bricks of that colour and carry them down the hole. Clear the whole picture before your five slots jam up. FEATURES - Hand-made pixel pictures to dig away, from a small apple to a 36x44 mural - Colour-matching logic that gets tighter as the pictures grow - Three power-ups: Speed x3, Pick Any and Shuffle - Extra slots you can unlock when things get tight - One tap to play, no download, works right in your browser Simple to start, tricky to finish. How far can you dig?
