## About

Stickboy Defender is an action merge-and-shoot game. Recruit stickman units, drag them to the wall and merge identical units to create stronger fighters. Survive waves of enemies, upgrade your damage, income and prices, spin the lucky wheel for bonuses and collect offline earnings. Buy new troops in the shop, activate boosts like the delivery drone, auto-merge and coin multipliers, and clear every wave to claim victory.
