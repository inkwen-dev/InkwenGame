## About

Welcome to Brain Twister! The aim of the game, is to find the exact positions of the colors in the sequence. Drag the balls in the empty spaces. Every ball can be used only once in a sequence. Once filled the line, you’ll get the Brain Twister response. A Grey ball stands for a correct color in a wrong position. A Black ball stands for a correct color in a correct position.
