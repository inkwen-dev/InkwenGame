1. Build Arrow Towers at strategic locations to stop incoming monsters.
2. Defeat enemies to earn coins and valuable resources.

4. Create different defense formations to handle various enemy types.

## About

Archer Madness: Crystal Defense is an exciting casual tower defense game packed with endless waves of monsters, powerful archers, and addictive strategic gameplay!

The Crystal Core is under attack, and only you can protect it from the invading monster army. Start as a rookie archer and gradually unlock powerful abilities, upgrade your weapons, and build the ultimate defense lineup. Place Arrow Towers wisely, combine different tower skills, and create unique strategies to stop increasingly dangerous enemies.

Explore colorful worlds filled with forests, castles, and mysterious battlefields while enjoying smooth controls and satisfying combat. Collect resources, strengthen your archers, upgrade your towers, and discover powerful combinations to survive every wave.

Game Features:

• Fast-paced tower defense battles with endless monster waves
• Multiple Arrow Towers with unique skills and attack styles
• Train your archer and unlock powerful abilities
• Build different defensive formations and strategies
• Upgrade weapons, towers, and talents to defeat stronger enemies
• Bright cartoon graphics with beautiful maps and animations
• Easy to learn but challenging to master

Be brave and defend the Crystal Core! If it falls, the entire continent will become the monsters' kingdom!
