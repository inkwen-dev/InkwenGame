## About

This Tower Defence game "Kingdom Defence: Mercenary" is the combination of War Games, Magic Weapons, Castle and Guardians with mission of protect The Kingdom and against the aggression of Monster. Your Kingdom is under attack by fearful Monster. In this Tower Defense, you need to react fast and use the right weapons in order to win one of War Games. Like Strategy Games, this TD challenges you, makes you to be the best king to protect all the kingdom.
