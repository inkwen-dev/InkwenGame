## How to play

Protect your base and defeat every snake wave to complete a level. Choose a level, follow the short tutorial, then tap or click an available deployment position and choose a soldier to place there. Units attack automatically when enemies enter their range. Spend your battle gold carefully and combine attacking soldiers with support units.

Between battles, use coins to unlock soldiers and improve their stats. Optional rewarded videos provide extra coins. Keep your base standing until the final wave is defeated to win.

Desktop: use the left mouse button to select menus, units, and deployment positions. Hold and drag the mouse to move the battlefield camera or browse the level map. Use the on-screen Pause/Resume button to pause or continue.

Mobile: tap to select and deploy; drag to move the camera or browse the map. Both portrait and landscape orientations are supported. Sound and music can be adjusted in Settings.

## About

Snake Army Defense is a colorful 3D tower defense game where squads of soldiers face giant, winding snakes. Place fighters around the battlefield, combine different weapons and support units, and protect your base through successive enemy waves. Earn coins to unlock and upgrade your army, then tackle tougher levels across varied environments. Tactical placement, a growing roster, and segmented snake enemies give every defense a different challenge.
