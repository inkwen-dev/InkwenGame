## How to play

Move with WASD, the arrow keys, or by holding and dragging your mouse or finger. Stop moving to shoot automatically. Collect dropped gold, then stand still on marked building plots to construct or upgrade defenses. Press Space or tap the strike button to hit nearby enemies. Protect your castle through five waves to unlock the next map. Choose a reward after each wave and spend crowns on permanent upgrades in the pause menu.

## About

Crown Guard is an action tower defense game where you control a king defending his castle from enemy hordes. Collect gold, build and upgrade towers, recruit knights, and unleash a powerful royal strike. Conquer 100 maps across 10 biomes, unlock new defenses, and strengthen your king with permanent upgrades.
