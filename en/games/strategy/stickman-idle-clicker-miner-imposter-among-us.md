## About

This is a unique relaxing idle miner game. It is based on the stickman universe. Build units, make upgrades, and try to reach a final goal! Stickmen want freedom - help them to get out. But an imposter is among us so be aware. - Create more Stickman's; - Make Upgrades; - Earn more ore; - Dig deeper until You earn enough money; - Buy all train parts, so Stickman can move on; - Be careful - an imposter is among us!
