## How to play

Move the queen with WASD or the arrow keys on desktop, or drag the on screen joystick on mobile. Press Space or J, or tap Gather near a flower, to collect nectar. Tap or click the upgrade tabs and buttons to grow your hive. Recruit guards to defend against invaders. Open Settings with Esc or the gear button. Optional rewarded ads grant boosts only after the video is completed.

## About

Bee Rise is an idle colony building game where you guide a queen bee and grow a lively hive. Gather nectar and pollen, hatch workers to bring resources home, and train guards to protect the hive from wasps and hornets. Spend honey on upgrades, finish quests, claim daily gifts, and use prestige to make each new colony stronger.
