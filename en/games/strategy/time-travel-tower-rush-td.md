## How to play

🎮 Core Gameplay:
✔ Kill monsters – The more enemies you defeat, the more gold you earn.
✔ Collect gold – Dropped by defeated enemies.
✔ Upgrade your tower – Increase durability, firing speed, and damage.
✔ Buy weapons – Different gun types offer unique advantages.
✔ Combine weapons – Find the most powerful setups for maximum damage!

LMB (Left Mouse Button) – Manual target selection for priority attacks (especially useful against elite enemies and bosses).
