## How to play

PC Controls:
Mouse: Click to deploy soldiers, place defenses, upgrade weapons, and activate special attacks.
Arrows/WASD: Scroll the battlefield view.
Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap to deploy units, place sandbags/mines/turrets, upgrade, and fire abilities.

## About

Command elite SWAT soldiers armed with machine guns, bazookas, flamethrowers, sniper rifles, and more to defend your position against relentless waves of terrorists. The core idea is strategic fortress building and troop deployment: upgrade weapons, place defenses like sandbags, barbed wire, mines, and turrets, and unleash powerful attacks to stop the enemy horde.
