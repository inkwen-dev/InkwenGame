## How to play

On mobile, use your finger to grab and throw enemies. On desktop, use the mouse to pick up and launch them into the air.

## About

You are a necromancer living in a tower, but the local kingdom sees you as a threat. They continuously attack, trying to destroy your tower. Defend against relentless waves of enemies, master dark magic to summon skeletons and zombies, upgrade and expand your tower, and develop strategies to counter different enemy types.
