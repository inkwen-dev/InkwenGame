## How to play

Use the mouse or touch controls to select map nodes, build and upgrade barracks, manage squads, and cast spells during battle. Click or tap buttons to buy upgrades and start each encounter.

## About

Lead your clan through a tactical samurai tower defense campaign. Build and upgrade barracks, send Hatamoto, Yumi archers, cavalry, and other warriors into battle, and use powerful spells to turn the tide when enemy waves break through your defenses.
Between battles, choose your path across the campaign map, earn Honor, unlock new provisions, strengthen your wall, and prepare for tougher encounters. Each run pushes your army deeper into hostile territory, with stronger enemies, boss fights, and permanent upgrades waiting along the way.
