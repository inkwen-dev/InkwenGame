## About

Welcome to Kingdom Wars TD, an exciting tower-defense strategy game set in a captivating 2D kingdom. Assume the role of a skilled commander entrusted with the defense of a mighty castle. In your admirable quest, assemble a formidable army comprising up to five soldiers, including archers, warriors, and priests. Brace yourself for the challenge of surviving two perilous waves across various meticulously designed levels scattered throughout the map. To aid you in your noble endeavor, utilize a diverse array of equipment items and unlock the incredible powers of not one, but two legendary heroes. Immerse yourself in this thrilling adventure!
