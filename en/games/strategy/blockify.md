## About

Welcome to blockify, the ultimate brain-teasing block puzzle experience! Strategically place vibrant shapes onto the grid to clear lines and rack up massive combos. With 100 unique levels that increase in difficulty, youll need to use your logic and quick thinking to reach the target scores before time runs out. Stuck on a tricky level? Use the Hammer to smash single blocks or the Refresh to get a whole new set of shapes. Explore the beautiful level map, unlock new challenges, and see if you can master all 100 levels. Its simple to learn but hard to put down!
