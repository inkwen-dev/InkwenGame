## About

Penguin Love Puzzle is a fun pixel puzzle game. A couple of penguins are forced to separate, and you need to move and exchange blocks to reunite them. On the way, be careful of spikes and traps, if you accidentally hit them you will fail. Use your brain, help them and have fun!
