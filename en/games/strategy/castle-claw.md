## How to play

Move the claw left and right, then drop it into the capsule field. Unit capsules summon troops, while empty capsules do nothing. Your troops and enemy troops fight automatically. Destroy the enemy castle to clear the level. Use coins to upgrade the claw and army between attempts.
Keyboard: Left / Right or A / D to move, Down / Space to drop, P / Esc to pause, M to mute.
Touch: drag in the capsule field to move the claw, release or tap DOWN to drop. On-screen LEFT / DOWN / RIGHT buttons are available during gameplay.

## About

Castle Claw is a fast arcade strategy game where every capsule pull changes the battle. Move the claw, drop it into the capsule field, summon units, and push your army toward the enemy castle. Between runs, upgrade claw size, claw speed, army health, army damage, and support claws. The game includes a 30-level campaign, endless mode, daily quests, weekly contracts, skins, command talents,  storage, lifecycle messages, and leaderboard submission.
