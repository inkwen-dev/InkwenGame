## How to play

Game Objective:

Defend the village from alien invaders using tactics, magic and the power of warriors. Don't let the enemies break through to the heart of the settlement!

Main Mechanics:

Build towers - place archers, spearmen, stone throwers and shamans at strategic points on the map.
Improve defense - spend resources to strengthen towers and unlock new abilities.
Use magic - activate shaman spells to slow down, stun or cause massive damage to enemies.
Control the waves - watch the enemy routes and adapt your defense to different types of threats.

Tips for victory:

Combine towers - some warriors are better against light enemies, others against armored ones.
Save resources - do not build everything in a row, first assess the strength of the next wave.
Study the enemies - each type of invader has weak points!

Are you ready for battle? Become a legend of the tribe and save the jungle from destruction!

## About

A tower defense strategy action game where you become the last hope of a tribe living at the foot of an ancient volcano. When an asteroid with an alien threat falls to the planet, you must combine magic, tactics and the strength of warriors to stop the invader who is devouring all life.

The village is a place where magic and nature merge. Its inhabitants, divided into four factions (archers, spearmen, stone throwers and shamans), have been defending their home from the dangers of the jungle for centuries. But everything changes when an alien organism falls from the sky - capable of taking over the minds of creatures. Now the tribe must prepare for war, enchant the jungle, build defensive towers and fight back the invader before it is too late.
