## How to play

Tap a tube to pick the top color then tap another tube to pour it You can only pour onto the same color or into an empty tube if there is enough space

## About

Jungle Tube Sort is a fun and relaxing color sorting puzzle game that challenges your logic and strategy. Pour colorful liquids between tubes until each tube contains only one color. Enjoy hundreds of satisfying puzzles with simple controls and a beautiful jungle atmosphere.
