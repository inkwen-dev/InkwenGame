## How to play

Start by selecting equipment in the store. Choose weapons to suit your style. After landing on the island, study the map – it shows enemy bases to capture.

To capture a base, eliminate all enemies within its perimeter. After capture, establish defense: the enemy will try to retake positions. Use turrets, fortifications, and tactical terrain advantages.

Controls:
WASD – movement
Shift – run/swim down
Space – jump/swim up
1,2,3,4 – weapon switch
Left mouse button – fire
Right mouse button – aim
R – reload
T – deploy turret
G – throw grenade
V – switch grenade type
E – interact
Q – roll
Tab – camera switch
H – holster/draw weapon
I – inventory/settings
P – pause
M – map

Earn resources for successful captures and base defense. Spend them in the store on new weapons, upgrades, and equipment. The more difficult the mission – the more valuable the reward.

Tips: study the map, change tactics, use elevation, upgrade equipment, and combine weapon types.

## About

A tropical island awaits where every piece of land is a battlefield. This is a third-person tactical action game with an open world. Your goal is to capture enemy bases and hold them under enemy pressure.

Master a diverse arsenal: assault rifles, sniper rifles, machine guns, grenades, and special equipment. Each weapon affects your combat style. In the store, you can purchase and upgrade equipment using resources earned in battles.

The island impresses with its variety: tropical beaches, palm groves, and fortified bases with unique architecture. Each base requires an individual approach. Act strategically – combine attacks and use the terrain.

A dynamic capture and defense system, thoughtful weapon balance, and large-scale battles await you. For fans of the Far Cry series and tactical shooters, this is the perfect project combining strategy and action.
