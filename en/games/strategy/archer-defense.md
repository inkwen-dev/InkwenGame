## How to play

🎯 Objective: Defend your castle from endless waves of monsters. Survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score!

🎮 Controls:
• MOUSE/TAP: Aim and shoot arrows
• CLICK/TAP: Select upgrades in the shop
• AUTOMATIC: Archers auto-attack nearby enemies

🏆 How to Win:
1. Eliminate all enemies in each wave
2. Earn gold from defeated monsters
3. Upgrade your archer's damage, speed, and abilities
4. Survive endless waves - the longer you last, the higher your score!

Perfect for both casual players and tower defense fans!
