## About

Connection elimination is a classic casual puzzle gameplay, which can be completed by connecting 3 or more elements of the same color/pattern (balls, candies, fruits, etc.). The more elements connected at a time, the higher the score and special effects bonus obtained. Most levels have step or time limits, and players need to achieve specified goals to complete them.
