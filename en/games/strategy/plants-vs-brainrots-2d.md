## About

🌱 Plants vs Brainrots 2D is a fast-paced action game inspired by the classic “Plants vs Zombies”, but with a completely different and chaotic twist.

Brainrots appear in waves and fly across the screen. They don’t attack plants directly - their main threat is their huge amount of health. Your goal is to place plants wisely, upgrade them, and deal maximum damage to take down the toughest enemies.

🧠 The game features common, rare, and legendary Brainrots.
🎯 Your main objective is to defeat 3 legendary Brainrots with massive health pools.
