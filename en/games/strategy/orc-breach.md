## How to play

Build turrets near enemy routes and aim them toward the approaching horde. Use different weapons and support abilities to slow, burn, blast and destroy the orcs before they reach your base. On PC, use the mouse to place and aim turrets, the mouse wheel to zoom, WASD or arrow keys to move the camera, and Space to start the battle. On mobile, use touch controls to place, aim and activate abilities.

## About

Orc Breach is a fast-paced tower defense game where you must stop enormous orc hordes before they break through your defenses. Build and aim different turrets, combine their strengths, use powerful support abilities, upgrade your arsenal, and survive increasingly massive enemy assaults across a full campaign.
