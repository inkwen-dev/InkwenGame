## About

Evolution Era is a tower defense strategy game where you battle through waves of enemies and conquer their lands. Start from a primitive society and evolve through the ages by upgrading your skills and warriors. Lead a squad of brave fighters, summon units to defend your base, and launch attacks on enemy strongholds. Evolve through eras and achieve victory!
