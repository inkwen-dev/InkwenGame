## About

Get ready for adventure with Tactical Knight, a fun and gripping knight game. You have to be careful to defeat the enemies in the right order. You must kill all the enemies before they fall into the sea. In addition, the powers of the enemies are indicated by asterisks. You should get the swords on the map to strengthen your character. You must kill a boss monster every 5 levels. Get ready for a fun puzzle game with 30 different levels and 12 different characters. Visit meyagames.com for more adventures.
