## How to play

Either with the onscreen controle for mobile, or the keyboard on PC, you controle your ship to decide where to stack blocks.  You need to get to a sertain hight to escape the planet. 

Get stars as rewards. Then use those stars to buy skins or temporary updates like see what the next block will be or more ship speed.

## About

Build a tower, reach the stars! In this challenging and addictive block-stacking game, you play as an alien trying to escape a mysterious planet. Strategic stacking is key: each block has unique properties, and one mistake can make your tower collapse. Unlock new levels, blocks and customizations as you play. Choose between a challange mode to unlock levels Or compete for the largest tower in endless mode. Wana chillout there is even a sandbox mode to build into the stars.
