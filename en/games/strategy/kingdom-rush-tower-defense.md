## About

The critically acclaimed medieval fantasy defense game is now available for Android phones and tablets! Prepare to embark on an epic journey to defend your kingdom against hordes of orcs, trolls, evil wizards and other vile demons with an arsenal of tricks and spells! Fight in forests, mountains and deserts by adapting your defensive strategy thanks to different upgrades and tower specializations! Rain fire on your enemies, summon reinforcements, command your troops, recruit elven warriors and face legendary monsters on your quest to save the kingdom from the forces of darkness! GAME FEATURES ★ Epic defense battles that will keep you going for hours! ★ Command your soldiers and watch them deliver cartoon melee combat! ★ 8 specialist tower upgrades to tailor your strategy, including Barbarians, Arcane Wizards and Forest Rangers. ★ Over 18 tower abilities! Command your wizards to summon earth elementals and transfiguration your enemies! ★ Over 50 different enemies, from goblins to demons, each with their own skills! (Watch out for mountain trolls!) ★ 9 heroes: choose the champion who will lead your troops to victory! ★ Intense boss battles never before seen in a defense game! ★ hours of play with over 60 achievements! ★ Additional game modes that will test your tactical skills. ★ Beautiful high definition cartoon graphics! ★ An in-game encyclopedia with information about towers and enemies.
