Join Little Waldy, the determined wild boar, on his acorn-stocking quest! Navigate through 12 increasingly challenging levels, strategically pushing acorns onto target spots. With no time pressure, enjoy delightful puzzles that test your wits and planning skills in this charming, brain-teasing adventure. Help Waldy prepare for winter now!
