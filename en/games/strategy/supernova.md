## About

Supernova is a skill game where you fly a ship and have to dodge objects along the way. The game has great visuals, and contains multiple vehicles for you to unlock. Your speed increases as you proceed in the procedurally generated colorful stages of Supernova. Try to beat your streak by carefully passing all the obstacles!
