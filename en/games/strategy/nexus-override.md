## How to play

Player 1: WASD — Move

Player 2: Arrow Keys — Move

Space — Dash (unlock via upgrade)

1 — Build Wall

2 — Deploy Turret

Shooting is automatic

## About

Nexus Override is a fast-paced 3D horde survival game set in a cyberpunk arena. You are a rogue AI fighting to escape the mainframe — survive waves of hostile programs, build walls and turrets, collect XP, and level up your abilities. Face increasingly dangerous enemies across 6 unique sectors, each with its own difficulty and boss. Can you outlast the system?

Features:
• Solo or 2-player local co-op
• 6 unlockable sectors with boss fights
• Build walls and deploy turrets mid-battle
• Level up and choose powerful upgrades every round
• Multiple characters and skins to unlock
• Upgrade your stats with earned credits between runs
