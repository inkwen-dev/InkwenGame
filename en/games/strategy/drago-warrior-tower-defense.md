## How to play

Your main goal is to protect the kingdom by placing towers, summoning heroes, and using skills to stop enemy waves (including giant dragons) from reaching your base—survive all waves for victory, earn upgrades, and achieve high scores with efficient strategies!
PC Controls:
Mouse: Click to place towers, select heroes/skills, tap enemies for targeting, and use fast-forward button.
Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap hero portraits/skill icons to activate spells, tap enemies for tower focus, tap fast-forward to speed up combat.

## About

Defend your kingdom in Drago Warrior Tower Defense, an epic strategy tower defense game where ferocious dragons threaten a trapped warrior hero! As the last guardian, summon legendary heroes, command powerful dragons, and deploy diverse towers to build an impenetrable defense against waves of dark monsters. The core idea is tactical placement and skill activation—unique for its dragon-commanding mechanics, hero spells, manual targeting, and fiery boss battles that blend classic TD with mythical fantasy flair!
