## About

Welcome to Pin Gems Rescue in this game you will need to Pull the Pin and help the monster to loot the treasure or gold & free him from the blockage. Be careful when you pull the pins and use your brain to not drop treasure into lava or water. and don't let other monsters destroy your hero. Good luck!
