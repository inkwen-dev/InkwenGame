## About

Zombie Parade Defense is a tower defense game that can be played by 1 Player or 2 Player. You must protect the building from the zombies that come in waves. With each wave, the zombies are getting stronger. That's why you should take advantage of Power-ups and improve your weapons. Whether you play 1 Player or 2 Player, the goal is to keep the zombies away from the building. If you can survive a total of 10 waves, you win the game!
