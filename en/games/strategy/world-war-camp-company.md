## How to play

To send troops into battle, you need to press the "BATTLE" button next to the gate. Allied troops will automatically move out to search for enemies.

Upgrade your army and weapons at the base in special places.

To win, you must destroy all enemies on the level and capture the enemy flag.

Control:

For PC.
Character control - "WASD", "arrows" or hold the left mouse button and drag the mouse in the desired direction.
Shooting - the hero shoots automatically.

For Mobile devices.
Character control - press your finger on the screen and drag your finger in the desired direction.
Shooting - the hero shoots automatically.
