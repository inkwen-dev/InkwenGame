## About

Conquer The City is a simple and fun puzzle game that allows you to Conquer cities of other colors while defending your own City. When all cities turn blue, you win. Slide your finger to connect two cities, and when you're about to fail, try the next skill! Join the game and exercise your brain to take over the whole city!
