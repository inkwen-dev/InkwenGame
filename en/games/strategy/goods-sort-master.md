## About

Goods Sort Master is a fun sorting game. Have you ever fantasized about spending money in the supermarket? How much can you take away in a limited time? In this game you can sort snacks, drinks and fruits and have fun exploring triple matches in 3D cabinets to unlock more of your favorite products. The game is simple, just drag the same 3D products onto several shelves to match or triple match them. If you are a fan of matching games, why not give it a try?
