## How to play

Objective: Defend your crystal-powered signal tower from waves of enemies. Upgrade your stats, collect resources, and survive as long as possible in a hostile red wasteland.

Gameplay Overview:

Enemies approach your tower in waves. Your goal is to stop them before they destroy it.

Use earned currency (₮) to upgrade your character’s Damage, Max Health, Speed, Bounce, and Diamond Yield.

The longer you survive, the stronger the enemies become—so keep upgrading!
