## About

Defence Wall is an exciting tower defense strategy game where you build and upgrade fortresses to withstand waves of enemies. Strategically place defensive towers, utilize resources and skills to eliminate enemies before they break through the walls. The gameplay is simple yet rich in tactics, with increasing difficulty providing a thrilling experience. Build, defend, and win!
