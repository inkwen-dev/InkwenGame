## About

Mech Monster Arena is a robot-fighting turn-based strategy game. Differently in this fighting game, you dont need to learn how to operate the robot and release each skill. You only need to learn the basic skill counter rules and select the skills flexibly. By earning EXPs and coins from battles in Mech Battle, you can fight with AI opponents and develop your robot. When you have some sources to upgrade your robot in the Mech Warehouse, you can challenge other players in the Arena. Have fun!
