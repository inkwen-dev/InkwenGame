## About

Defend your position against relentless waves of the undead. Aim and shoot, then spend your earnings between rounds to upgrade your weapons and defenses. Every wave hits harder than the last, so plan your build carefully and see how long you can survive. Zombi Defense is a fast-paced browser survival shooter with procedural action, a between-wave upgrade shop, and endless replayability. Playable on desktop and mobile.
