## About

Stickboy War is a strategic battle game where you command an army of stick warriors against the Imperial Berserkers. Mine gold, hire miners, shielders, archers and explosive units, then upgrade your forces to break through enemy gates and capture castles across the island. Each level introduces new units, tactics and reinforcements. Defend your camp, manage resources wisely, and unlock powerful abilities to turn the tide of battle. Features 10+ campaign levels, multiple unit types, upgrade system and reward chests.
