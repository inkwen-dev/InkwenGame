## How to play

1. On the squad screen, recruit heroes and drag them onto empty slots. Place defenders in front and keep archers and mages behind them.
2. Open the mission map and select an available battle.
3. Heroes attack automatically. Tap a ready hero ability and select a highlighted target when required. You can pause the battle.
4. Enable Auto mode to cast abilities automatically. Each automatic cast consumes one AutoCoin.
5. Defeat enemy waves, earn gold, recruit new fighters, and upgrade hero classes. If your squad is defeated, adjust the formation or strengthen the team, then retry the mission.

Controls: click or tap buttons and characters; drag heroes between squad slots.

## About

Adventure Squad is a tactical RPG where you assemble a party from 60 unique classes and lead it through dangerous campaigns. Recruit heroes of different classes, arrange them on the battlefield, and combine tanks, fighters, archers, and mages. Your heroes battle waves of monsters while you control their active abilities and choose the right moment to turn the fight around.

Earn gold from victories, strengthen your roster, and unlock new class branches. Each path changes a hero’s stats, role, and abilities. Enemies have distinct traits: they can attack the back line, inflict poison, summon reinforcements, heal allies, or rely on heavy armor. Adapt your formation and tactics, defeat powerful bosses, and advance across the adventure map.
