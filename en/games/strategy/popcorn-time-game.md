## About

Popcorn Time is an interesting puzzle game. Whether you are watching any kind of movie, you could not miss popcorn. How lucky, you have got a popcorn-making machine. It will make popcorn constantly when you press the mouse. You need to complete all levels to get them. Pop the popcorn to the target line. Dont let the popcorn fall to the ground. There are so many challenges waiting for you.
