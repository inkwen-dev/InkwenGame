## About

Fighting Penguin is a game where you have to control the penguin and defend your house or base from the snowmen enemies who want to destroy you. Some of the snowmen have shields and helmets on their heads, so it will be harder for you to destroy them. You will earn points and money, which you can use to upgrade your weapons and shields for you and your base.
