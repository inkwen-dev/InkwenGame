## How to play

Protect the kingdom. You will save the inhabitants and the peace of the kingdom. Upgrade your towers and spells, and unleash their full power on the battlefield.

Features:
* 12 levels.
* Orcs, goblins, witches, spirits, trolls, zombies, giants, and a multitude of enemies.
* Spells that can be summoned at any time.
* Bosses at the end of levels.
* 4 super bosses at the end of each location.
* Stunning locations – forests, swamps, deserts, winter north, and volcanoes.
* Upgrading towers and spells
