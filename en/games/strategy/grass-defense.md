## About

Grass Defense is a strategic tower defense where you must protect the wilderness from waves of fierce creatures. Build and upgrade powerful towers, manage resources, and adapt your tactics as enemies grow stronger. With dynamic environments, diverse foes, and challenging levels, you’ll test your strategy and skill to survive the untamed wilds and uncover hidden mysteries.
