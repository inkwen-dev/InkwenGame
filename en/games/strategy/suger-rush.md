## About

Explore the candy quest game Sugar Rush Game story begins with a monster of the chocoland and a rabbit. The rabbit wants to eat candies but he has to win before eating a candy. Player has to cut the candy rope in the perfect position to feed the rabbit. Be careful! While cutting the rope follow the path either candy will fall down and game will be over. If you like genres such as physics based games, quests or just want to have a good time then this game is for you.
