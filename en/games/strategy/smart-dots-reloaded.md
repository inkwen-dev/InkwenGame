## About

Smart Dots Reloaded is a fresh remake of the classic Dots and Boxes puzzle from Windows 3.1. Players take turns drawing lines between dots. Completing a square earns a point, and the game ends when the board is full. Featuring modern visuals, smooth animations, and both AI and two-player modes, this is a strategy game that&rsquo;s easy to learn but hard to master.
