These dragons from hell, under the witch's spell, are going to destroy the village you dwell! The only way to repel, is to show them the power of your shell!

## How to play

1.Build turrets to destroy waves of incoming dragons.
2.Combine two identical turrets to create a stronger unit (turret fusion).
3.Drag and position turrets anywhere on the grid.
4.Each dragon eliminated rewards gold to reinvest in defences.
