## About

Zombie Parade Defense continues with its second game in the series. Although the zombies are stronger in the new game, the game can be played up to the 3rd player. Like before, the zombies are getting stronger with each wave. You have to use weapons, turret towers,mines,shield and bonuses to stop them. Whether you play 1 Player or 2-3 Player, the goal is to keep the zombies away from the building. If you can survive a total of 10 waves, you win the game!
