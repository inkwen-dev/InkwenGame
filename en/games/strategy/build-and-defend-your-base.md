## How to play

Build your base in peaceful mode, then press "Battle" to start the waves. Enemies try to destroy your base block — stop them with walls, turrets and your weapon. Survive as many waves as you can to set a new record, and spend your rewards on new blocks, weapons and upgrades.

Controls (PC):
WASD — move
Space — jump
Hold right mouse button — rotate the camera
Mouse wheel — zoom
Left mouse button — attack / place a block
1 / 2 / 3 — build / remove blocks / weapon
R — reload
U — upgrades
Esc — settings

Mobile: joystick to move, on-screen buttons to jump and attack, swipe to rotate the camera.

## About

Build your own base block by block and defend it against endless waves of enemies! Place walls, turrets and traps, then grab your weapon and fight alongside your defenses. Zombies, skeletons, robots, pirates, aliens and demons attack in 200+ waves with bosses — each wave harder than the last. Earn coins and diamonds, unlock rare and mythic blocks, upgrade your weapons and collect skins. How long can your base hold out?
