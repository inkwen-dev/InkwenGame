## About

Defense Base 2 is an action-packed base defense game! Protect your stronghold from endless waves of enemies by placing and upgrading powerful weapons - cannons, gunners, lasers, freezers, poison towers, rocket launchers and heavy melee blades. Every weapon has its own damage type, range and reload speed, so build the perfect loadout to survive. Position your weapons wisely: top slots reach farther, melee hits hard up close, and special weapons pierce armor or deal damage over time. Earn coins in battle to unlock new weapons, upgrade your arsenal and reinforce your base health
