## About

Defend your precious sugar with furious blasts of ant-smashing action! Ants flood in on all sides. Your mission is to tap and squash them before they get your sweet stash. Bonus for quick reflexes in crushing ants while defending your sugar. Improve your defenses because the waves of ants will get tougher and tougher. For how long?
