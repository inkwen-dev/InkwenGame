## About

Cockroach Tower Defense is a top-down survival game where you defend your base against relentless roach swarms using auto-firing weapons. Exterminate bugs to buy mid-run upgrades, and spend your earned "Public Support" between runs on permanent stat boosts to survive increasingly difficult enemy variants and boss waves.
