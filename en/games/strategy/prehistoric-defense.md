## About

Prehistoric Defense is a funny game which takes you back to prehistoric times where you had to defend your home from dinosaurs and other enemies. To do this, you need to control your small army, which is equipped mainly with stones. Defend yourself from the dinosaurs and attack their home and destroy it to advance to the next level. As you unlock more levels, you will have the opportunity to get new warriors with which you will be able to defend yourself more easily from the enemies. Click on the warriors to play this game.
