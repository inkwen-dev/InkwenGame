## How to play

Your primary goal is to prevent waves of zombies and mythical enemies from breaching your defense line on the left. Keep your ammo loaded by grabbing bananas falling from the sky, then command your gorillas to fire banana projectiles at approaching enemies in each lane. Clear all enemy waves and take down the final boss to win the level! Collect gold coins to purchase permanent shop upgrades and recruit powerful allies like Bora, Mira, and Kaya to boost your defense.

Desktop Controls:

Left Mouse Click:
Click on falling Bananas and Coins to collect ammo and gold.
Click on a Gorilla to throw a banana along that lane.
Click the (+) Button above a gorilla to upgrade its attack power.
Click UI buttons to pause, toggle game speed (1x / 2x), and navigate menus.
Mobile Controls:

Tap (Touch Screen):
Tap falling Bananas and Coins to collect ammo and currency.
Tap any Gorilla to shoot bananas down that lane.
Tap the (+) Icon above a gorilla to upgrade its level during battle.
Tap on-screen buttons to pause or speed up gameplay.
