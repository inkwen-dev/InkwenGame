## How to play

- Select runes to build and improve your spells.
  - Your wizard automatically attacks nearby enemies.
  - Click dropped gold and Arcane Dust before they disappear.
  - Use gold between waves to buy health, runes, and upgrades.
  - Combine elemental runes with modifiers to discover new spells.
  - Defeat bosses and survive as many waves as possible.
  - Spend Arcane Dust on permanent upgrades after a run.
  - Use the bonus wheel when it becomes available.
