## About

Dead Frequency is a unique tower defense game where survival depends on a single central tower. Waves of relentless enemies close in from all directions, and your lone tower must hold the line. Upgrade its abilities, unleash powerful attacks, and adapt your strategy to withstand the growing chaos. With only one tower to rely on, every decision counts &mdash; can you endure the frequency of death?
