## How to play

Pick a robot card from the bottom panel and drag it onto your half of the field. Each card costs energy, and energy refills over time. Your robots walk forward on their own and attack the nearest enemy; destroy the enemy towers before your own fall. Win battles to earn chests with new cards and coins, then upgrade your robots between fights. Controls: drag cards with the mouse or finger; tap a card first to see its energy cost.

## About

A real-time robot card duel. Build a deck of 8 robots, drop them on the field to destroy the enemy towers, open chests of cards and upgrade your squad. Over 40 unique robots, 6 arenas, a 30-mission campaign and a reboot system that lets you start again with powerful bonuses. Easy to learn and quick to play on phone or desktop.
