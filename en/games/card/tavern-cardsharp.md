## How to play

Drag a creature from your hand to an empty slot on your side of the table. Tap your creature, then tap a target to attack: an enemy creature or the rival's portrait when the way is clear. Tap the seal to end your turn.
Your bet is your life: every hit on a portrait takes coins from that pile. Empty the rival's pile to win the duel and his deck.
Neighbours matter: some cards grow stronger in formation, others fight best alone.
Buy packs from the innkeeper, pick a table that matches your deck and climb the ladder to the champions upstairs.

## About

Tavern Cardsharp is a pixel card game. Take a seat across from a rival, bet your coins and duel. Win and you take the bet and the rival's whole deck. Lose and you hand over yours.

Inside:
- 15 classes, from militia and archer to thief and giant;
- 15 affixes: thorns, lifesteal, echo, charge, vengeance and more;
- innkeeper's packs with rare, legendary and foil cards;
- rivals with character and living faces: a smirk gives away a strong card;
- a ladder of tables on three floors: beat the champions and climb higher;
- a tutorial at the table with the innkeeper.
