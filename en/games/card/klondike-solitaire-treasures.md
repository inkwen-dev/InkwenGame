## About

Klondike Solitaire Treasures is classic Klondike solitaire with a collection to build. HOW TO PLAY Move all 52 cards onto the four foundations, Ace to King. Build the tableau downwards in alternating colours; empty columns start with a King. The stock draws one card at a time. MODES Play freely, or switch on Winnable games only and every deal is guaranteed solvable. Undo any move. Highscores keep your best score, time and number of moves. COLLECT Wins, daily quests and a 7-day login streak earn coins.
