## How to play

Mouse / touch: tap the PEEK, FLIP and CASH buttons. Keyboard: Space or F to flip, Enter or C to cash in, P to peek.

## About

Flip 8 is a press-your-luck card game about knowing when to stop.
Every card you flip joins your hand and adds its value to your round score. Flip a number you already hold and you BUST, the entire round is wiped. A live bust meter shows you the exact odds before every flip, so every card is your call not the game's. Cash in to bank your points safely, or keep going for the Flip 8: eight different cards in a single round banks automatically with a +20 bonus. First player to the target score wins.

Play solo against three AI opponents, or take it online — quick match with random players, or create a private room and send friends the code. Rooms hold 2 to 7 players. Pick your target (100, 150 or 200), switch on Blitz mode for fast timed turns, chase the daily goal, unlock extra themes, and climb the global leaderboards for best score, wins and Flip 8s.

One Peek Token per match lets you look at the next card before you commit. Use it well.
