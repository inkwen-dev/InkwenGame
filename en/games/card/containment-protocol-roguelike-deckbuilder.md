## How to play

🎓 Complete the tutorial and launch your first Campaign mission. After your first run, choose your next mission on the Atlas.

🃏 Before a new run, open DECK to customize your starting cards. Chips unlock permanent Protocol choices or rent cards for one run.

⚔️ In battle, inspect enemy intents and click or tap enemies to read their dossiers. Select a card, then its target, or drag it onto an enemy or the battlefield. Cards cost energy; use attacks, Block and effects to survive. Press “End Turn” when ready. Energy refills each turn.

🗺️ Follow the map connections. Choose battles, events, shops and repairs. Take cards that fit your strategy and defeat the boss to clear the area or act.

🦾 Equip earned Modules between runs to strengthen your Operator in Campaign and Open Breach. Classic and Certified become available as you progress.

💾 Use “Save and quit” in the run menu, then “Continue” to resume. Weekly Breach saves expire at the weekly reset.

## About

🎮 Command a combat android in a sci-fi roguelike deckbuilder. Combine cards, anticipate enemy attacks and outsmart dangerous bosses.

🦾 Three Operators, three distinct playstyles. Build a deck around your tactics and discover new card combinations with every run.

🗺️ Campaign: explore 10 areas on the Atlas, uncover the story behind the containment breach, raise Operator Rank and collect permanent Modules.

⚔️ Classic: take on three-act runs with 10 difficulty levels. Earn Chips, refine your starting deck and test your tactics without permanent Rank or Module bonuses.

💥 Breach: explore branching weekly maps with changing anomalies. Push personal records with your own equipment in Open, or qualify for Certified and compete for Season Rating using standardized Kits.

🎯 Complete daily Operations and work toward monthly milestones.

📱 Play with mouse or touch, in portrait or landscape.
