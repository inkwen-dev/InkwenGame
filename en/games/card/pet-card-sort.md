## How to play

Tap cards at the bottom of the screen to select them
Tap empty slots on the game board to place your selected cards
Match 3 identical pet cards anywhere on the board to clear them
Clear all cards from both sides of the board to complete the level
Plan your moves carefully - limited slots mean strategy matters!

## About

Pet Card Sort is an adorable matching puzzle game where you organize cute animal cards across a double-sided game board! Match three identical pet cards to clear them from the board and collect dozens of charming pets including owls, bunnies, rainbow sloths, and more. With its satisfying card-sorting mechanics and colorful cast of collectible creatures, Pet Card Sort offers relaxing puzzle gameplay that's easy to learn but challenging to master. Can you sort all the pets and clear every level?
