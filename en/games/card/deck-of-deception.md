## How to play

Goal: finish the match with the most coins and the fewest cards. Your score is the coins (PC) you have left.

Controls: mouse click or screen tap only. No keyboard needed.

1. On your turn, tap a card in your hand that matches the number the previous player called, then tap a number on the pad to set what the next player must match. Playing a card earns you +1 coin.
2. No matching card? Tap the Market Deck to buy one for 2 coins. You can draw up to 3 cards per turn, or tap "Stop here".
3. Action cards: Wild Swap matches any number (2 coins), Power drains a player's coins, Steal takes coins for yourself, Insurance lets you skip safely, and Reverse flips the turn direction. Tap a card, then tap a player when asked.
4. You have 30 seconds per turn. If time runs out you lose 2 coins and your turn is skipped.
5. Deception Showdown: when a player is broke with no matching card for two turns in a row, a showdown starts. Players with coins secretly pick a real number from 1 to 9 and announce a claim, which can be an honest claim or a bluff. Broke players tap the claim they believe. A right guess earns +15 coins, and a wrong guess gets you eliminated.
6. When the 20-minute limit is reached, the player with more coins and fewer cards wins. Win matches to rank up, earn coins and unlock cosmetics.

## About

Deck of Deception is an online multiplayer bluffing card game where every coin counts. Play number cards to match the call, use action cards to drain, steal or protect coins, and survive the Deception Showdown, where a wrong guess means instant elimination. Climb from Apprentice to Legend through ranked matches, and play against real players or bots in fast matches of up to 20 minutes.
