## About

Stacklands is a card game with innovative gameplay. It is a mixture of city management and stacked card game, where various entities are represented by cards, making it a novelty game. For example, by placing a Villager card on a Berry Shrub card, the villager will automatically collect berries and then drag the Berry card onto the Villager card to restore the villagers satiety. Dozens of quests and novelty gameplay await your challenge and digging, are you ready?
