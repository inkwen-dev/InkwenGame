## How to play

Dive into the world of word solitaire, where traditional playing cards are replaced with word cards and category cards. Just like in classic solitaire, each level begins with a partially filled board. Draw one card at a time from the deck and place it strategically. To build a category stack, you must first place a category card, then add all related word cards to complete the set.

## About

Word Solitaire is not just another word game - it’s a unique and addictive word puzzle that blends the challenge of a card game with clever word associations. Match words by meaning, discover hidden connections, and test your brain like never before. Start playing today and see how many associations you can master!

This is a fresh take on solitaire card games, combining classic mechanics with modern word puzzles. It’s a new kind of cards game that challenges your vocabulary, logic, and strategy in equal measure.
