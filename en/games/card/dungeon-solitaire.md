## About

Dungeon Solitaire is a dark fantasy card roguelike inspired by the classic game Scoundrel. Plan your strategy carefully, manage your health, and escape deadly situations in this fast and addictive solitaire-inspired adventure. Can you survive until the end of the dungeon?
