## About

Best your score or just have a relaxing game of Free cell Solitaire! Jump right in, no additional settings needed. Look for the Aces in the four suits, move them to the foundation row as soon as possible and create stacks of cards in ascending order. You can place four cards in the free cells to get access to cards in the columns. And remember, Freecell solitaire can always be won!
