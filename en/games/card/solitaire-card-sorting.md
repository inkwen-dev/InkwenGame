## About

An intellectual card puzzle that combines the mechanics of classic solitaire with the logic of thematic sorting. At each level, you need to organize cards into categories: plants, jewelry, toys, tools, flowers, and many more. Use observation and strategy to clear the field and complete the level within a limited number of moves.
