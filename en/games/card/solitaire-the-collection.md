## How to play

How to play

- Pick a solitaire from the collection and a difficulty level.
- Lay out cards by the rules of the mode: build sequences, pairs or tableaus as required.
-In Klondike-style games, build foundations by suit from ace up; on the tableau, build down by alternating colors when the rules say so.
-In Spider, assemble full same-suit sequences and remove them from the board.
-In Pyramid, clear pairs that match the mode’s sum rule.
-In Memory, flip cards and find matching pairs.

Use hints, undo and auto-collect when stuck. Choose random or solvable deals where supported. Games save automatically so you can return later. Resize cards, drag with mouse or finger, or move with a single tap.

## About

33 card game variants in one collection. Play familiar solitaires, discover new rules and pick the difficulty — from a calm deal to a real puzzle.

Modes include Klondike with draw one or three; Spider with one, two or four suits; FreeCell; Pyramid; Vegas; Yukon, Russian Solitaire and Alaska; Scorpion; Forty Thieves and Josephine; Tri Peaks; Golf; Eight Off; Double Klondike and Easthaven; Spiderette and Simple Simon; Memory on a normal or large board.

Take daily challenges, journeys with quests, collect keys, postcards and achievements. Customize card faces, backs and backgrounds, enable dark mode and left-handed layout. Hints, undo and auto-collect help mid-game; progress saves automatically. Winning deals greet you with sounds and a card dance.
