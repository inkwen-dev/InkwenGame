## About

Spider Solitaire card game with 2 suits. The objective of this Spider Solitaire game is to create eight sequences of the same suit from King to Ace ( 13 cards ). When a sequence is completed, it will be removed from tableau. Empty tableau columns may be filled by any card or sequence. This version of the card game has an helpful hint function.
