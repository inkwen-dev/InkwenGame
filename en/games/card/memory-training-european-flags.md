## About

MEMORY TRAINING EUROPEAN FLAGS has 3 different categories with 4 levels each. To play, click on the cards to reveal the flag of an European country. Memorize it so you can relate it to another equal flag. Find the pair of each flag on the board to complete the level before time runs out.
