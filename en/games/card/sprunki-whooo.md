## About

Face the funniest card puzzle ever in Sprunki Whooo. Together with the adorable Sprunki characters, youll figure out the right answer in a deck full of surprises! Choose a character as your target. Each round presents a weird or funny question. Constantly ask each other about the characteristics of the target and eliminate all the wrong ones. Finally, identify the target and arrest him. Win rounds to unlock more Sprunki cards and characters!
