## How to play

You can get a joker or a bonus card directly on the playing field or by making several moves in a row

## About

Play Classic TriPeaks or 100 different autumn levels. Remove all cards from the tableau, you can remove top cards that are 1 higher or 1 lower in value than the open card on the bottom. Click on the closed stack to get a new open card.
