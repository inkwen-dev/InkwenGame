## About

Battle Cards is a card roguelike where you move your hero card around the playing field. When moved, you collide with adjacent cards. For example, monster cards and spike cards will reduce your card&rsquo;s health, healing cards will heal you, and gold cards will increase your game score. Each movement of your card creates a unique and challenging situation, a mini-puzzle that requires strategic thinking to solve.
