## How to play

Use spaces to arrange all cards in suit and in sequence from 2 to King You can move a card to a space or empty spot if it is the same color and 1 higher in value than the card to the left of the space

## About

The full pack of cards is dealt to the card table, into four rows of cards. Each row has a single space. Your task is to rearrange the cards so they form a correctly ascending sequence of cards, one suit in each row. The catch, you can only move cards into the empty spaces if the card to the left of the empty space is of the same suit and of lower face value.
