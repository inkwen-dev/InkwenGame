## How to play

Move all 52 cards to the four foundation piles, building each suit upward from Ace to King. In the seven tableau columns, build downward while alternating red and black cards. You may move a correctly ordered face-up sequence as a group, and only a King may fill an empty column. Select the stock to turn over one or three cards - the draw mode is available from the Game menu. Only the top card of the waste is playable. When the stock is empty, recycle the waste and keep looking for moves. Drag cards or select them for a quick move. Auto move sends safe cards to the foundations, while Undo restores the previous position. Every starting deal is verified and has a solution, but an individual move can still lead to a dead end; undo it or request a Hint if needed. You win when all 52 cards are on the foundations.

## About

Ola! Solitaire brings classic Klondike gameplay together with clear rules, large cards, and comfortable controls for seniors. All 999,999 available deals have been checked by a solver and have a verified path to victory in both Draw 1 and Draw 3. Start a new or numbered deal, then drag cards or move them with a single tap. Undo a move when your plan changes, use Auto move to send safe cards to the foundations, or ask for a Hint when you need help from the current position. Complete Daily Challenges, travel through the Journey, unlock collectible cards, and earn monthly trophies. Victories award experience and coins, increase your level and win streak, while Statistics tracks your time, moves, score, and personal bests. Set up the table for your right or left hand. Enjoy a timeless card game on your phone, tablet, or computer - and turn every deal into a well-earned win.
