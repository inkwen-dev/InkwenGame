## About

Immerse yourself in the captivating world of solitaire and test your strategic planning and logical thinking abilities. This version offers two popular variations: Spider and Klondike. Spider has three modes (1, 2, and 4 suits). In Spider solitaire, your goal is to arrange cards in ascending order by suit. Untangle the complex web of cards using the fewest moves possible. Klondike offers the standard game with a single deck of cards. Your task is to clear the playing field by building sequences from King to Ace, alternating colors.
