## How to play

The goal
Battle league rivals, earn coins, open packs, and build your prehistoric card collection. Climb the league ladder, grow a stronger battle deck, and fill your binder with rare finds.

Core loop
Battle a league opponent in a 3v3 fight using your cursor or your finger
Win to earn coins and progress toward promotion.
Buy packs in the Shop and tap to open them.
Swipe each pulled card: left to sell for coins, right to sleeve into your binder.
Repeat — climb the league and complete your collection.

## About

A prehistoric trading-card shop game. Battle league rivals in 3v3 fights where a card’s power is both its attack and its health. Win to earn coins and climb the league ladder, then spend coins on packs and tap to open them. After each pull, swipe left to sell for coins or right to sleeve the card into your binder. Repeat the loop to grow your deck, rise through the leagues, and complete your collection.
