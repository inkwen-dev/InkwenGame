## About

Planet Pair is a memory game with pairs of planets. Each round has 3 phases of increasing difficulty: 3 pairs in the first, 6 pairs in the second, and 9 pairs in the final phase. The pairs and their positions are randomized in each phase, ensuring unique gameplay and high replay value. A timer challenges you to complete each round faster.
