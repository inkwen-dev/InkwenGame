## About

Welcome to the quiet hunt, mushroom picker! Embark on an exciting journey through a virtual forest where mushrooms change shape depending on their habitat and the time of the year! In this puzzle game, you have to be careful and have a good memory to find three mushrooms of the same species. Keep in mind that even mushrooms of the same species can look different! Dont forget that there may be a lot of mushrooms in the forest, but your time is always limited. Think fast, but accurately!
