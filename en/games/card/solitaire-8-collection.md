## How to play

The goal — Each solitaire has its own, and the game explains it the first time you open one. In Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Yukon and Scorpion you build the four foundations from Ace to King. In TriPeaks and Golf you clear the table by chaining cards one rank up or down. In Pyramid you remove pairs that add up to thirteen.

Mouse — Click a card and the game sends it where it belongs. Or drag it exactly where you want it. Click the stock pile to deal.

Touch — Tap a card to play it, or drag to place it yourself. Tap the stock to deal.

Keyboard — The whole game is playable without a mouse:
Arrow keys move between cards and piles · Enter or Space picks up, drops or plays the focused card · Z undo · P hint · N new deal · M game menu · Esc closes any panel.
