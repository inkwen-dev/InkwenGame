Black Hole is an addictive solitaire card game with similar rules as Tri Peaks or golf solitaire.

## How to play

Your goal is to move all cards to the black hole by either counting up or counting down in value If you play in easy mode you have the ability to move cards to empty spaces if you re up for a challenge you play in hard mode and really have to plan ahead
