## How to play

Goal: Sort every card into its matching themed stack to complete the level before you run out of moves.

How to play:
- On desktop, use the left mouse button to select and move cards.
- On mobile and tablet, tap or drag cards with one finger.
- Match cards with the correct category and clear all required cards from the board.
- Use boosters when you need help with a difficult layout.
- Complete the level to unlock the next challenge. If you run out of moves, retry the level and plan a different sequence.
