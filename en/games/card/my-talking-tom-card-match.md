## About

This simple but elegant memory card matching game was designed for anyone that wants brain improvement. Our pairs card game offers Easy, Medium, and Hard modes so you can choose your level of difficulty. Take turns against the computer. You can turn over 2 cards at a time. When it is your turn again, use your memory to turn over two cards that match. each pair of matching numbers counts towards your score. The final winning score is determined by the player with the most pairs accumulated.
