## How to play

In the game of "Cards a Fool", the classic rules are that the one with the smaller trump card goes first. There are 36 cards in the deck. At the beginning, one trump card is selected, which determines the highest suit over all. The principle of the game is the same, First one goes with any card, the other must fight off with the highest of the same suit or a trump card. If the one who strayed then he walks and so on in turn. The winner is the one who has no cards left and the deck is empty. In the case when no one has any cards left, a draw is recognized, in this case no points are awarded or taken away. Track your score on the leaderboard.
