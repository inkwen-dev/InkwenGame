## About

Flip cards, remember their positions, and clear the board before time runs out, or challenge a friend in local two-player mode. Match pairs or triples across hundreds of levels with increasing difficulty, and unlock new levels. Use coins to get helpful boosts like Hints and Time Refills, collect free coins, or grab coin bonuses. In multiplayer, keep your turn by finding matches before the 10-second timer ends, while surprise Shuffle Time keeps everyone guessing!
