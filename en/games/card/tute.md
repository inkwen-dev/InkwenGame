## About

Tute is a captivating card game blending strategy and chance using a 40-card deck. Aim to win tricks and declare high-scoring combinations, but beware: holding all four Kings lets you claim victory instantly. Each round is filled with unpredictable excitement, demanding tactical card play and savvy declarations.
