## About

Mermaids inhabit the wonderful world of underwater. Mermaid girls have gorgeous hair, fancy bathing suits and amazing fishtails. In this new dressup games for girls you can choose new outfits, hairstyles and fishtails for 6 different mermaids. Help these little girls to become the most beautiful princesses in the whole underwater world! Make sure that their bikinis fit well with the fishtails. And don't forget about accessories, necklaces, handbags and tridents!
