## About

Meet the young sorceresses! On this wonderful holiday - World Day of Knowledge - they rush to classes at the academy of magic. But, as in any school, you need to follow the dress-code. School uniforms can be stylish too. Let's help the girls choose the right outfits for their first day of school: blouses, jackets, skirts or pants. Use different combinations to find your unique style. Cute accessories - handbags, necklaces and earrings - accentuate the look. And of course the wings. What are fairies without wings?
