## About

Little Panda Summer Travels is a beautiful travel game. This game has four parts, they are, Hula Dance Party, Thanksgiving Party, Pyramid&nbsp;&nbsp;Archaeology, and Ancient Egyptian Masquerade Ball. It is a combination of crafting, food making, dress-up games, and adventure games. You can experience multiple fun in one game. In the Hula Dance Party, follow Little Pandas lead in making grass skirts and dancing to the music. At Thanksgiving Party, decorate the living room and prepare a delicious turkey for Thanksgiving. At Pyramid Archaeology, look for pieces of the cat statue in the wreckage and carefully piece it back together.
