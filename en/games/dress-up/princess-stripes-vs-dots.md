## About

The Two little princess of Cutedressup simply adore modern world and fashion. They are trendsetters and best friends. But sometimes their taste in fashion is not the same. As example, one is convinced that this summer's big hit are stripes but other contradicts her, she being convinced that the ultimate trend this summer are dots. Well, they want to prove their point so they want to create a statement outfit. Help them out and explore both styles!
