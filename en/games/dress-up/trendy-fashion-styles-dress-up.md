## About

Trendy Fashion Styles Dress Up is a cute and fashionable dress up game. There are 2 characters in this game and you can choose any of them to start the game. Dress them in beautiful clothes and smart accessories! We've prepared 4 dressing styles for you to enjoy. They are Kawaii Look, Punk Look, Girly Look and Tomboy Look. And each dressing style includes plenty of beautiful outfits. You can always find your favorite dressing style in this game. If you find you like this game, why not try all the looks one by one? Maybe you will find something new and fun. Enjoy!
