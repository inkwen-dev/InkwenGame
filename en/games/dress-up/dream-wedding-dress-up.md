## About

Step into the world of romance and fashion in Dream Wedding Dress Up! Explore 4 beautiful wedding levels, each with its own unique theme and atmosphere. Choose the perfect bridal look by mixing and matching stunning dresses, elegant hairstyles, sparkling necklaces, beautiful bouquets, stylish shoes, and glamorous accessories. Create the dream wedding outfit for every special moment! Once your bride is ready, take a memorable photo with her handsome husband and capture the magic of the day. Complete all 4 wedding scenes, collect your favorite pictures, and place them inside your beautiful wedding photo album to create unforgettable memories.
