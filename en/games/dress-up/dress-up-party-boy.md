Step into a world of fun and fashion with Dress Up Match 3! This game combines the excitement and strategy of classic Match 3 games with the process of creating your style. In the game, make matches to complete levels filled with different outfits, accessories, and makeup items. Each match helps your character add new items to their wardrobe and update their style.
