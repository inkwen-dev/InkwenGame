## About

Diy Joystick is a very popular game. In this game you are a talented artist that design fashionable joysticks. Today lots of people come to your place and ask you to save their ugly joysticks. Come and pick up the sprayer and create your masterpieces. You can design the joysticks according to the styles of customers. You can also design the joysticks following your heart. Anyway, in this game, with so many beautiful colors, smart stickers and amazing production methods, we are sure that you will have lots of fun!
