## About

Welcome to a cute pet care salon!
In this casual simulation game, you run your own animal care salon. Wash your pets, take care of them, decorate them, and train them so they become happier and healthier.

Complete simple tasks, upgrade your salon, and unlock new features. Every pet needs attention and care. The game offers relaxing gameplay, a cozy atmosphere, and lots of adorable moments with lovely pets.

Perfect for players who love animals and calm casual games.
