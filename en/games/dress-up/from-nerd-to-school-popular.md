## How to play

Playing is super easy!
1. Drag and drop tools to complete beauty treatments.
2. Follow the tutorial in early levels, then explore freely as you progress.
3. Watch for glowing hints on the character’s face, they show where she needs a makeover.
As you play, unlock new cosmetics, hairstyles, and accessories to make your character truly stand out.
