## About

It's time to turn our beloved princesses into dazzling goddesses! In this game you will find a great selection of divine outfits and accessories. You can choose the image of the formidable ancient goddess or pretty nymph. Or do you like Egyptian mythology? Then choose sparkling gold outfits in the style of Queen Cleopatra. We can go to the East and to India. For local goddesses there are gold-embroidered sarees and airy skirts. Lay the hair of the princesses in a divine hairstyle. Pick up wings and accessories for your goddess. Create your own unique goddess!
