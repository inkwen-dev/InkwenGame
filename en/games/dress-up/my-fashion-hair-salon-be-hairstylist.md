## About

For all girls, fashion is a very important thing! Fashionable hairstyles will make girls sparkle! Every girl deserves the most beautiful and fashionable hairstyle! In your salon, first, give the girls a comfortable hair treatment, wash their hair, and then make a homemade hair mask for them. When your girl has finished her hair care, you can design a delicate hairstyle for her. In order to make her beautiful and fashionable, don't forget to choose fashionable headwear, necklaces, and earrings for them. Have fun!
