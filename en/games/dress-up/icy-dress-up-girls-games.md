## About

It’s a new sisters' dressup & makeover game! Icy girls are going to princess fashion salon. They are always together and their favorite thing is – fashion! Changing clothes, doing hairstyle and makeup are the most exciting things for them! Since they are ice princesses, they trust only their own fashion designer and it is you! Although they are twins, they still like different types of clothes, so it’s your goal to dress twins the way that they’ll look harmoniously or the way you want.
