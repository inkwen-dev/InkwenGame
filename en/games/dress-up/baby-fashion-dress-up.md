## About

Baby Fashion Dress Up is a very enjoyable design game. Think your design talents are going nowhere? Try this game! In this game, you own a very famous tailor shop. Many customers are attracted by your shop and come to your shop. They may want fancy shoes. They may want cute dresses, hats or scarfs etc. As a talented tailor, come and design clothes for them according to their styles! Many carefully selected decorations and dyes are here for you to pick. We are sure you'll like it!
