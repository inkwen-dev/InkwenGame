## About

The annual Paris Fashion Week event is about to begin. As a well-known fashion designer, you need to design more and more fashionable clothes and cut out basic styles. You can display the clothes you just cut in your closet, or you can sell them to customers in need, if you want to become a qualified tailor, don't miss this game!
