## About

Lovely Fox is a 2D dress-up game where you can customize the looks of your cute fox character. Select from nearly a dozen fur patterns, different eye colors, various types of ears, faces, tails, snouts, clothes, accessories, and much more! You can even change the background to winter lands, farm view or gorgeous nature.
