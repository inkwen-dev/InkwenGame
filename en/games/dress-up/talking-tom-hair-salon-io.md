Making someone look great is awesome so in this baby talking Tom hair salon game you will get to do just that and more because he is really young and just a kitten at this time. You will surely have a great time making his hair curly or straight, coloring it different ways and styling it with plenty of products. Make him a new hairstyle in this Talking Tom makeover game and he will definitely be ready for a new photo shoot for some big shot magazine that will have him on their cover.
