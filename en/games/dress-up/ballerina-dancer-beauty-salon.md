## About

This girl game provides multiple challenges and will allow you to enter into the world of a ballerina dancer to see her ritual before any show. Take care of her face with different treatments and special masks that you are going to prepare with natural ingredients. Have a nail makeover and choose a pretty manicure for each nail. Add cool accessories and nail polish, then move to the clothing step where you have to dress her up.
