## How to play

Help the girls create a cosplay based on a computer game. Apply bright makeup. Choose the appropriate items of clothing, shoes, accessories and jewelry that match the theme of this cosplay. A very important point is the choice of hairstyle or wig. The result can be saved as a PNG image. Depending on the gaming device, a computer mouse click or a simple touch on touchscreens is used to control it.

## About

Two BFF are going to a summer cosplay festival. They love computer games. Help the girls choose costumes for their favorite video game heroes and princesses. It is very important for a cosplayer to create a complete look: from makeup and hair to dress. Lots of original and gorgeous outfits are waiting for you!
