## About

A young bestie goes to school to learn magic and make new friends. She has her own special style. The girl prefers black colors in clothes and a gothic look. Her roommate likes bright colors in her clothes. It will not be easy for new acquaintances to find a common language. However, this bright girl is not as simple as it seems at first glance. She has her secrets. Try to choose for each student an image that suits her perfectly. And one day these besties will be BFF.
