## About

Two BFF, Lily and Roxana, are striking opposites. Lily is the personification of tenderness and romance, dressed in airy pink dresses decorated with sequins and floral patterns. Players will be able to experiment with outfits, creating their own styles, and choose which one is best suited for Valentines Day celebrations!
