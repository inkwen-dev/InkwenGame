## About

Princesses Eliza and Milana are going on winter vacation, but for each of the girls, winter vacation will be special. Eliza lives in snowy regions and loves fluffy fur coats, warm scarves and winter dresses. Milana celebrates her holidays under palm trees and chooses swimsuits, light capes and bright accessories. Players will have to create stylish images, combining the cold shine of snow and the warm glow of the tropics. Help Eliza and Milana become the most fashionable princesses of winter!
