## How to play

1. Click or tap to select sweater colors for each girl
2. Choose from a variety of Christmas decorations and place them on the sweaters
3. Mix and match patterns, icons, and details for a truly ugly look
4. Style each BFF with festive hairstyles and accessories
5. Enjoy your finished sweaters and admire your holiday creations
Simple controls make it easy to play on both desktop and mobile devices.
