## How to play

1. Choose Your Character: Start your Halloween adventure by picking one of the three K-pop idols. Each idol has her own fierce personality and magical charm.
2. Select a Halloween Costume: Every girl has two exclusive Halloween looks to try. Dress Rumi as a Witch or Pumpkin Queen, style Mira as a Vampire Idol or Demon Diva, and transform Zoey into a Ghost Princess or Corpse Bride.
3. Apply Spooky Makeup: Add the perfect finishing touch with themed face paint to match each costume’s vibe.
4. Customize Accessories: Choose the best jewelry and props to complete your K-pop Halloween look.
5. Preview and Save Your Creations: Once your idol’s transformation is complete, take a screenshot of your stylish masterpiece and share it with friends or on social media.
