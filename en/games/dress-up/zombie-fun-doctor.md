## About

Halloween is coming and it's time to get all the Zombies ready to terrorize the population! Enjoy with Zombie Fun Doctor a funny doctor game where you will have to test your skills as a doctor and fix all the ailments of this badly wounded zombie. Tear out nails, heal broken bones, remove pus-filled wounds, provide electric shocks and staple open wounds. Once you've worked through all the damage, get ready to select the most daring and terrifying look so as not to leave a single soul free from your scares. Have fun!
