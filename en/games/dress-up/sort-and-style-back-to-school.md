1. Choose a Unit – Start with cluttered spaces like study desks, lockers, or wardrobes.
2. Sort Items – Drag and drop objects into the right place, category, or design.
3. Unlock Levels – Complete organizing tasks to move to the next challenge.
4. Dress Up Fun – In the final unit, pick school uniforms, shoes, bags, hairstyles, and accessories to create your perfect back-to-school style.
