## About

Sofia and Zoe come to your nail salon, they want to get manicures. They are tired of the same old manicure and nail art, they want to try out something new. Now, you have two new nail art ideas, flower style and animal style, choose one of them for Sofia, so another is for Zoe. After doing their nails, please pick cute rings and necklaces to complete the nail design. Have fun!
