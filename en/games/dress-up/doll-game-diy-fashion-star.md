## About

Welcome to DIY Fashion Star, a casual DIY simulator game for girls who love fashion clothes and dressing. You need to meet customers needs and offer them your tailored clothes! Use your sense of fashion and beauty to create the best and suitable garments for every client. From selecting cloth formats to cutting and sewing, you witness how a piece of cloth transforms into a beautiful suit.
