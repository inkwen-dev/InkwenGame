## About

Hey girls, time to dust off your hairstyle skills because in this new fun girls game you are about to do a real-life makeover to these lovely princesses girls. Maybe you are used to their feminine and delicate look, but this game has a twist and you get to see them like the egirl who enjoy a bad girl makeup look. Start by dying their hair in crazy bold colors and then choose the right makeup to go with the whole e-girl/emo look. Last but not least, let's choose some fashion outfits for them. This change of look will clearly be a shock for their other friends. Have fun!
