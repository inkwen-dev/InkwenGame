## About

Do you like kittens? Do you know how to take care of kittens? Come take a look at this game. These are some injured kittens and need your care. Please choose the one you like, tend to its wounds, give it some medicine and clean its dirty fur. Thanks to your good care, it's looking much better already! Afterwards, feed it so the little pet can recover its strength fully. As soon as the fluffy cat is completely healthy, you can get creative and style it with amazing costumes!
