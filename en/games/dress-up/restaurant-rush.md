## About

Step into the kitchen and build your culinary empire in Restaurant Rush! Start with a small burger joint and take customer orders to earn money. Invest your earnings wisely buy new cooking stations and hire skilled workers to handle more orders. Your strategy is key: decide when to expand and how to allocate your resources to maximize profits. Easy to pick up but deeply strategic, Restaurant Rush challenges your planning and management skills as you grow from a humble eatery into a thriving food empire.
