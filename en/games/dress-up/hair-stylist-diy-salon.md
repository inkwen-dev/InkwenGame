## About

Hair Stylist DIY Salon is a fun educational game. In this game, children will manage their own beauty salon for the same young beauties. The salon will provide you with a large selection of tools that can be used for curling and washing curls, and combing your hair the way you like. You can cut your hair, dye your hair, curl your hair, dress up your beauty the way you want, and create the most colorful and fashionable image that even the most fashionable stylist envy, because your hairstyle will Become a masterpiece. Choose your model in our salon and make it the star of the fashion show!
