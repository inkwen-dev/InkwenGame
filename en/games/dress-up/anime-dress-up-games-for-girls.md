## About

Welcome to the fashion & beauty salon in the theme of Japan and Asia, with hundreds of apparel items, fancy hairstyles and adorable pets - all there for you to create amazing, moe looks for your virtual animatronic characters. Rummage through a huge wardrobe of dresses, tops, skirts, pants, shorts, suits, with style ranging from the traditional kimonos to modern sailor high-school uniforms. Put together a cute outfit of a cartoonish Tokio schoolgirl, with high heels, short-skirted and wearing a school uniform. Or instead create a Kioto princess in luxury kimono, jewelry and modest hairstyle.
