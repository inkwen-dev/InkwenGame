## About

The Oh My Goth dress-up game shows that there are many ways to express your gothic style. Get inspired by this new trend that emerged from the collision of gothic and whimsical styles. It fuses codes from the 80s gothic style with surrealist evasions inspired by fairy tales and cinematic darkness. Gothic, grunge and gothfied fashion trends dont have to be restricted to the streets, thats why we are here to teach you all about their aesthetics and how to integrate them into your wardrobe.
