## About

Bag Art DIY 3D is a design game. Almost everyone on the street carries a bag. As people pursue fashion, the styles, colors, and patterns of bags have become very different. In this game, you can customize a bag that is exclusive to you. First, choose the style of the bag, and then customize the color of your exclusive bag, which can be a dreamy pink or a starry blue. Then, customize the exclusive pattern and hang cute accessories to decorate it. Finally, a stylish bag with a great sense of design is completed. Come and try this game!
