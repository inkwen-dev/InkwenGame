## About

Fashion Superstar Dress Up is an unparalleled adventure in the world of fashion. Dress up your favorite superstars. You get to dress them up for red carpet, coolest parties, movie awards, dates and photo shoots. Create your look-a-like avatar and be your own personal stylist with mixing and matching your favorite look. Huge collection of clothes and accessories to choose for every occasion. Billions of combinations for avatar and outfits. Play Fashion Superstar Dress Up: • Choose one of the superstar characters to dress up, or design your own dolls with the Avatar generator. • Choose a category by clicking on it, and then clothes and accessories from that category to put on your character.
