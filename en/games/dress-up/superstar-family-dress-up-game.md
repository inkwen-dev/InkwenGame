## About

Superstar Family Dress Up Game is a casual fashion dress-up game where you create looks for a superstar family. Each character has their own wardrobe with 200+ items of apparel, makeup, and accessories. Add cute pets, fireworks, and various cool stickers to get the final picture ready for a photo album. There are 8 backgrounds you can choose, which are: a shopping run across a boutique street downtown, a red carpet walk, a photo session in a studio, a mansion (interior and exterior), a living room of a castle, a picnic in the park, and an oriental palace. You can customize everything from eye shape, iris color, dresses, accessories, etc.
