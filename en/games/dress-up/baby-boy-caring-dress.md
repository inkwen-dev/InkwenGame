## About

Start his day by taking him to the bathroom where youll give him a nice bubble bath. Before he gets out of the tub, make sure to clean him too. Your baby needs feeding. Prepare him some delicious meals and serve them. Now that your boy has a full tummy, you can move on to the dressing part. The most exciting thing is that you can choose cute animal costumes for him. You can choose to dress him up as a panda, rabbit or any other animal. Choose fun accessories to match your babys look. Let him refuel you for another day of quality time that you can spend together.
