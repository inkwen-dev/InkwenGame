## About

Funny Haircut is a hairstyling game where you give your customers unique haircuts in your salon. Wash your customer's hair and clean it first, then use your professional hairdressing equipment such as curlers, straighteners, dryers, dyes, and hairspray to give them a fresh hairdo. The hair physics in this game is so realistic that you will feel like a natural hairstylist. We trust you to make your customers look unrecognizable. Let your hair down and enjoy the hilarious Funny Haircut!
