## About

Hippo Hair Salon is a very unique and fun makeover game. In this game, the adorable animals are going to attend a party tonight. They need to have their hairstyles done. But the barber in the shop got sick. She cant work. So, Hippo decided to help her cut hair for animals. As Hippos close friend, come and help her design cool hairstyles for the animals. You are going to try lots of interesting tools and outfits. Have fun!
