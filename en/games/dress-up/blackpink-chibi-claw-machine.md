## About

Try your luck in the new game BlackPink Chibi Claw Machine. Use the right, left and claw buttons to pull out the prize egg. Inside you may find candy or one of the collectible figurines with members of the BlackPink band. You can also get four special golden figurines with fairy singers. Collect a collection of 20 dolls. Youll be able to take a closer look at your collection on the achievements screen - just click on the figurine image to see all the details.
