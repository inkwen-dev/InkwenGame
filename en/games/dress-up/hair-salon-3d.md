## How to play

- Check what hairstyle the client wants — tap the card on the right for a 3D preview
- Choose your tool: scissors for precise cutting, clippers for even trimming, a razor for detailing
- Swipe across the head to cut hair — pinch and rotate the camera with two fingers
- If coloring is needed — select the brush and pick a color
- Tap "Done" when the hairstyle is complete and receive your rating
- Earn in-game currency and purchase new tools and dyes in the shop

## About

Hair Salon 3D — a vibrant 3D hair salon simulator! Clients come to you with all kinds of requests: bob cuts, mohawks, undercuts, long layers, and much more. Pick up scissors, clippers, a razor, and a color brush — cut, style, and dye hair, trying to recreate the requested hairstyle as accurately as possible.

The better the result — the more stars and tips you earn! Collect in-game currency, buy new tools and dyes, unlock complex haircuts, and serve VIP clients. 23 unique hairstyles, 5 tools, a rating system, and daily challenges — you'll never get bored!
