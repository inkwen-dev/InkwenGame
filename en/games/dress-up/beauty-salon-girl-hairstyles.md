## About

Beauty Salon Girl Hairstyles is a hair-cutting game with an innovative style. The gameplay of this game is very simple and this game has 6 characters which are three kids and three kittens. Click on the little kids and the kids will walk into the elevator from their homes and then sit in the elevator to the 1st floor and then walk into the barber store. But the kittens wont walk into the barber store, and clicking on the kittens will take you directly to the barber store. This is very much like real life.
