## About

This is a make-up game designed for girls. There are 3 princesses with very different styles in the castle and you need to make up and dress them up to give them a makeover. Princess Emma is a princess with fair skin, big eyes, and an outgoing personality. Princess Isabella is a lovely princess. Princess Ava is an oriental princess with a shy and quiet personality.
