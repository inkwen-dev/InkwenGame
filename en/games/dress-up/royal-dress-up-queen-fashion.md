## About

In this game, you can use more than 150 items of clothing in an aristocratic, Royal style and 6 dolls of different races. Many of the items are red, because red is the traditional color of royalty in many cultures, although there are other colors. Among the accessories there are typical attributes of a European Queen: crowns, scepters, powers (a Golden ball with a cross) and mantles.
