## About

Makeover Studio is a popular makeover game for girls. We all have difficulties in life. Sometimes we overcome them on our own. And sometimes we are in need of others help. In this game, two ladies need your help to get over their hard times by doing makeovers to them. Lots of cute makeups, smart accessories and fancy outfits are prepared for you. Come and make them stand out of the crowd!
