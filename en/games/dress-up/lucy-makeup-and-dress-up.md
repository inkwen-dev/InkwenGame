## About

Lucy: Makeup and Dress up is a gentle beauty and fashion game for kids featuring Lucy from Wolfoo! Play two fun modes in one game. In Makeup mode, give Lucy a colorful makeover, makeup, hairstyles and sparkly nails. In Dress Up mode, style beautiful princesses with pretty dresses, skirts and accessories, then create your own party look. Mix and match hundreds of cute items, unlock new outfits, and design your favorite style. Easy one-click or tap controls, bright kid-friendly visuals and no scary content perfect for young players who love makeover and dress up fun!
