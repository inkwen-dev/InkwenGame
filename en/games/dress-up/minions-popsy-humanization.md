## About

Minions have settled on the planet since the beginning of time. They have one goal - to serve the most Ugly Personality available. Dinosaurs, pharaohs, Dracula, Napoleon - whoever did not become their boss. Let's mix the style of popular dolls and minions and see what comes out of it! Each era needs its own outfit, hairstyle and accessories. In the days of the dinosaurs, there are not so many materials at hand, so you have to show your imagination. The more technology advances, the more choice we have!
