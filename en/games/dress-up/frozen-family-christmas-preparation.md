## About

Elsa and Jack are about to usher in a special Christmas! Because they have a baby this year, this is their baby's first Christmas! So, Elsa and Jack thought that Christmas this year should be celebrated. But preparing for Christmas is hard work. They need to clean up the house before decorating it. Please help Elsa clean the room when Jack goes out to buy a Christmas tree. Then let their room be decorated. Finally, don't forget to choose beautiful Christmas costumes for Elsa and Jack! Have fun!
