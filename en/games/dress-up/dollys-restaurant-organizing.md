## About

This exciting game is designed to help you organize better and release your stress. You will draw inspiration and creativity of storage through [Storage Master], and pack your life comfortably and cleanly. You can organize and clean objects by category, storage location and cleaning order. This game will bring you the creative inspiration of storage, allowing you to easily apply it in real life. Similar to a jigsaw puzzle, you have to pick the right object and put it in the right place
