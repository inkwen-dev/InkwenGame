## About

From charming and sexy clothes to fashion outfits and holiday costumes, all kinds of outfits are waiting for you in Catwalk Battle - Dress Up! Dress up the cute girl with your own creations and defeat your opponent! Each level has a different theme, such as Christmas, wedding, maid, skate park, etc. If you want to get high scores and gain levels, you need to choose clothes that not only look good, but also match a specific theme. In each level you can come across some of your favorite clothes and you really want to own them. You know what? You can collect them and put them in your collection closet! And you can go to the fitting room at any time to put on your clothing collection!
