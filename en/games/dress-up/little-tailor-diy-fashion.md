## About

Little Tailor DIY Fashion is a fun dress-up game. Do you want to be the best fashion designer in the town? Play this amazing game. You can design gorgeous outfits for a cute boy and a beautiful girl without knowing any sewing skills in this game. You just need to follow the instructions and you will make the cutest outfits ever. After designing beautiful clothes for your children, you can also add many fashionable stickers and patterns to the clothes. After that, you can also let the children pose, take pictures of them, and then choose the right frames for the photos. Play this game if you are interested.
