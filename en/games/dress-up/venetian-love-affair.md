## About

Venetian Love Affair is an exciting dress-up game for girls that combines two magical worlds: Valentines Day and the Venetian masquerade festival. Youll become a stylist for two princesses, each dreaming of an unforgettable masquerade outfit. Create unique masks with stunning designs and choose luxurious dresses, jewelry, and accessories. Immerse yourself in the atmosphere of mystery and romance of Venice, unleash your fashion designer talent, and create a unique look for each princess. Get ready for a magical journey into the world of love and enigma!
