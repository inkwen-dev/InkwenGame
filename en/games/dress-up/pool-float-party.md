## How to play

On hot summer days, two best friends want to have fun drinking refreshing cocktails in a small but private pool. But to do this, you need to clean and repair the pool, choose outfits and accessories for relaxing on the water and, of course, make cocktails. Help your friends in carrying out their plans! The result can be saved as a PNG image. Depending on the gaming device, a computer mouse click or a simple touch on touchscreens is used to control it.

## About

The best friends Blondie and Marie decided to have a pool party. What kind of party is there without cocktails? Let's make a refreshing mojito from soda, lemon, lime and mint leaves together. While Blondie is making cocktails, Marie is cleaning up the area next to the pool. Help the girls with creating a unique design for the party. Now that everything is ready, you can take care of yourself. Make up for each of the girls and choose the right outfit. Choose from a variety of swimsuits and pareos. Blondie prefers pink shades, while Marie loves the combination of red and black colors and the polka dot pattern. Don't forget to add stylish accessories to your look and choose an original-shaped inflatable raft for girls.
