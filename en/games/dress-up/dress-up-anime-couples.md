## About

Choose one of 4 couples with different skin colors and dress them up for a date! You can dress a couple at once: both a girl and a guy - all on the same screen. The game has more than 250 items for two: 150 for a girl and 100 for a guy. All items are free, everything is open, no locks! Collect the best and unique outfits for each couple, take a screenshot and share it with your friends!
