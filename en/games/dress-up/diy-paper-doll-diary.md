## About

Welcome to DIY Paper Doll Diary, a casual DIY game where you design your own house with different themes. You are free to unleash your creativity to decorate the room, build coffee shops, and design play yards. Challenge your imagination, logic, and spatial ability because you have to furnish the room from scratch. Yet, do not worry. Here are plenty of items for you to choose from. Just enjoy the transformation of an empty room into a lively and cozy place!
