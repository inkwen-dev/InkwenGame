## About

Fashion Doll House Cleaning is a very careful and patient game. Your task is to clean every room in the house and take out all the clutter. Your goal is to make the house a new look. First, you need to clean up all the rooms from the kitchen to the bedroom. Then, use the sweeping robot to clean the floor. Finally, throw away all the garbage. It is worth noting that you can't stain anything. Are you ready?
