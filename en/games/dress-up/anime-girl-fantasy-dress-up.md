## About

Do you like manga or anime girl dress up games? Sure you do! And what's you've really missed is fantasy anime dress up in a Role Playing Games style! 6 cute anime girls are looking for a good RPG fan to dress them up! Dress up six anime warrior girls, each with her own custom look. A unique touch in this game, the music changes with the background, helping to set the mood. Each girl comes with a beautiful selection of anime-style clothing, as well as many hairstyles in different colors. You can then arm her with fierce weapons and accessorize with jewelry. But no anime warrior's look would be complete without a weird and kawaii pet companion! What kind of anime girl squad will you design?
