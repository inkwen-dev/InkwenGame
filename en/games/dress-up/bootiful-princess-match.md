## About

Welcome to an exciting puzzle-game, created in a Halloween atmosphere. There are three princesses in the game and each one is ready for her own eerily beautiful transformation. The player will have to look for paired cards to discover new elements of the girls future image. Each successful match brings you closer to creating a unique costume, makeup or hairstyle. Only the most attentive players will be able to make their princesses truly scary-beautiful!
