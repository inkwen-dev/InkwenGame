## About

Favorite cartoon characters Barkers go to treat their teeth to a new dentist in a children's hospital. Except this new pediatrician is an intern Rosa! And Rosa really wanted to know how a dentist doctor works, how he treats teeth, removes caries and performs complex operations. To do this, she signed up for an internship at a dental clinic. But what fearless patients would go to the hospital to see an inexperienced newcomer? Of course, it will be the whole Barboskin family! Rosa gathered the whole family and told them this pleasant news. Someone took it joyfully, and someone with great fear for their teeth. What will be next? How will this venture end?
