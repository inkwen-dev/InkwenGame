## About

Left or Right: Women Fashions is a fashion dress-up game. If there were two clothes in front of you, which one would you choose? This seems like a very difficult choice. In this game, you need to make a total of 6 choices. You need to choose your own hair, top, skirt, hat, shoes and makeup. Each choice can affect your final style appeal. The game uses a PK mechanism. After you finish dressing up, the judges will score you and your opponents, and the one with the higher score will win. Come and play! Genius stylist!
