## About

Princess Elsa is getting ready for Halloween. She decided to turn into a charming witch. This Halloween must be special. Elsa wants to work out her image to the smallest detail. Let us help her with this. First, the princess needs a festive make-up - use the traditional Halloween colors - black, orange and red. Then, you need to prepare Elsa's hands for manicure. Treat your nails with a nail file, get rid of defects and apply cream from irritation. It is time to turn the princess nails into a work of art. Use nail polish, texture and decoration. Create your own unique composition. And finally, choose Elsa the appropriate witch costume. Just look at these gorgeous hats and belts. Friends will be delighted.
