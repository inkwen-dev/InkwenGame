## About

Hair Salon and Dress Up Girl is a casual simulation girl game where you can fully explore your creativity and design skills. From hair washing, hair drying, and hair ironing, to hair dyeing, you complete each step with the guide in the game to give your model proper makeup. In this hair salon, you are the master, and you are the one to decide on which color or style is to be applied. Dont be afraid if you know nothing about fashion.
