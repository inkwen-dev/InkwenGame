## About

Taylor's mom's best friend is about to get married, and she invites the Taylors to the wedding. But this perfect wedding still lacks a cute little flower girl, Taylor is invited to be the flower girl for the wedding. Please help Taylor and her mother prepare what the flower girl needs. Please accompany them to shop first, then dress up Taylor, and finally, decorate a beautiful flower basket. Please make Taylor the cutest wedding flower girl!
