## About

Meet the cutest pet friends the Cat and the Dog! Feed, bath, style, and dress up your new virtual pets in this super fun kids game! Play and take care of two sweet virtual pets: cat Kiki and dog Fifi. Give a bubble bath, shower, clean up, brush fur, polish nails, and design the perfect outfits for your virtual pets. Build a house and design the sweetest home for them. A perfect game for kids who want to have a cat or a dog and love taking care of cute virtual pets!
