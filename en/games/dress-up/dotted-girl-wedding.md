## About

Louis has long and hopelessly in love with Marie. What if we were in a parallel universe, where they are destined to be together? Let's fantasize about this and arrange a magnificent wedding for Marie and Louis. Choose a cute wedding dress for the bride in pink, white or lilac. Don't forget about a veil and a festive bouquet of flowers. Apply light makeup and get a wedding hairstyle for Marie. Now let's take care of the groom. Choose a stylish man's hairstyle, as well as a suit in Louis favorite colors - turquoise-blue. Finish the groom's outfit with a cute boutonniere in the color of the bride's dress. Our new couple is ready to get married!
