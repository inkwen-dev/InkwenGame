## About

Diy Makeup is an unique makeup game for girls. In this game, you run a DIY makeups store. You can not only do makeups for your customers, but also DIY makeups according to your fashion taste. With the raw ingredients that your customers give you, you can DIY Lipsticks, face masks, eye shadows etc. Ready for a try? Have a good time!
