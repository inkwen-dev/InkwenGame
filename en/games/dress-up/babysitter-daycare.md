## About

Babysitter Daycare is a very cute caring game. Do you like playing with little babies? In this game, you will help babysitter care the babies.There are 4 parts for you to enjoy: bath time, play ground, sleep time and room sorting. Each part has great surprises for you. In these parts, we prepare lots of mini games for you such as, cleaning and decorating the house, DIY clothes, building sand castles, feeding babies and coloring games etc. There are so much fun in this game!
