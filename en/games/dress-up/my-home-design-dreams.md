## About

Want to own your own dream house in a bustling metropolis? Dream Home allows you to realize your design wishes, from the landlord's apartment to your own mansion, to create your own dream home! Challenge a variety of match-3 levels to help you design and decorate your dream home! Various decoration style is decided by you! In spare time, you can drink tea and chat with the kind landlord grandma, and talk about dreams with the best best friend! Now come to dream home, design your own house!
