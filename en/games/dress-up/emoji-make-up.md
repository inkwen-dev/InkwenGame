## About

Emoji Make Up is a very stylish makeup game. In the game, you need to wear makeup. The make-up needs to be according to a random emoji theme. The themes are Cookie, Angel, and Evil. You will also be matched with a random opponent. She will also wear the same makeup. After the makeup, you will be graded by the judges based on how well the makeup matches the subject. Also based on how beautiful the makeup is. I hope you like it. Come and call your good friends to try it.
