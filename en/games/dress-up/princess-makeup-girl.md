## About

Princess Makeup Girl is a very fun dress up game. Princesses and princes are going to a costume party tonight. As a famous stylist, using your sense of fashion, put on fancy makeups and gorgeous outfits for them. We carefully select lots of pretty eye shadows, lipsticks, blushers and outfits for you. Come and try your best to make them stand out of the crowd. They will appreciate your kindness and advanced skills. Instead of playing this alone at your home, invite your friends to enjoy together! Let's see who the best stylist is!
