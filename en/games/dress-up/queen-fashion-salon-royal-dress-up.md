## About

This app allows you to try over 150 apparel items in the aristocratic style on 6 dolls of different races. Many of the items are red - the traditional color of royalty in many cultures, but there are other colors, too. Among the accessories the game has those typical for a European monarch: the crown, the sceptre, the orb, and the mantle.
