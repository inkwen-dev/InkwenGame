## About

My Newborn Baby Twins Care is a caring game. Full-time mother Lucy finds it very hard to take care of her children. She has a pair of twins. Today, she not only has to dress up the children, but also play with them, and then clean up the messy house. These are just the tip of the iceberg of her complicated life. As a caring and helpful child, are you willing to help Lucy take care of the cute twins?
