## How to play

To play Darling Doll, players just choose their favorite clothing items, hairstyles, and accessories from the game's large wardrobe and apply them to their princess figure using the game's touch and drag controls. Once their princess is properly dressed and accessorized, they can save and share their masterpiece on social media with friends and family.
