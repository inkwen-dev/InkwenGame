## About

You are a tailor that makes gorgeous wedding dresses for people. Today, a young couple comes to your shop and asks you to make a wedding dress for them. You need to measure their sizes, choose your favorite color, cut the cloth into suitable shapes, and then sew the pieces of cloth together to finish the production of the dresses. Don't forget to attach some accessories to the bride and the bridegroom.
