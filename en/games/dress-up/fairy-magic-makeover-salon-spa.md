## About

Have you ever wondered what it would be like to be a magic fairy in an enchanted land? Play a beautiful fairy in this wonderfully magnificent design game. Head to the secret spa and have a relaxing facial with special potions and treatments. Cleanse, exfoliate and tone your skin with glowing concoctions made from all natural ingredients like flowers and sea salt! Moisturize your skin with pearlescent lotions and embrace your magical nature! Then it’s time for a total makeover at the mystical salon. Mix and match lots of different colors of makeup and apply them all carefully. Add eye shadow, eye liner and lipstick. Complement the look with sparkly face decals, glitter and stickers!
