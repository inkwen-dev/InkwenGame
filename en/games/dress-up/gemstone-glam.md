## About

Welcome to the fabulous world filled with sparkling gems! Here you will meet two charming princess girlfriends, Eliza and Tiara, who adore fashionable outfits and the glitter of colorful stones in fashionable outfits. Try on dresses inspired by the radiance of a diamond, the passion of a ruby, the freshness of an emerald and the mystery of an amethyst. Create shining combinations of hairstyles, dresses and accessories to transform our young princesses into real fashion queens. Be inspired by the natural beauty of gems and come up with images worthy of a fairy tale. Feel like a real stylist of the jewelry kingdom!
