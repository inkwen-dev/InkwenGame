## About

In this game, you will meet Laura and Frankie, two charming monsters who study at a school for young monsters. Every morning, the girls carefully think over their images, mixing elements of a classic princess and a creepy monster. Each of the girls has their own special fashion preferences, but when going to class, Laura and Frankie always attract the admiring glances of classmates. After all, they are not just princesses, but real monsters whose terrifying style makes even the bravest tremble!
