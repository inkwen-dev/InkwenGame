## About

This is a dessert-making game for girls. If you like unicorns and baking simultaneously, this game is just for you. Our game will briefly describe the girls favorite food recipes to give you a real cake game feel. The food tools used in cooking will be controlled smoothly and realistically so that you can experience a novel gaming experience. Here you can become a food unicorn chef and decorate it as you wish.
