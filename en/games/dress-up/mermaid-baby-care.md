## About

Mermaid Baby Care is a very popular caring game. Princess Mermaid has been so busy recently. She is really in need of a helper caring her baby. Do you want to take care of little baby? Firstly take a shower for the mermaid baby and dress up for him. Mermaid baby loves coloring. There are 3 cute animals for you to color. Will you give the mermaid baby a big surprise by coloring the most beautiful artworks for him? It's been a while since the baby ate food, come and feed him some fruits. After the wonderful activities, the mermaid baby is tired. Come and coax him to sleep. Thank you for helping Princess caring her baby. You are such a nice kid!
