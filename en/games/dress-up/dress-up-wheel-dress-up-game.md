Which fashion style do you hope to unlock first? Spin the dress up wheel and let it guide you through 4 different styles: retro, classy, rockstar and school style. Mira can't wait to see what clothes you'll get. You will have a great time creating stylish outfits for our sweet fashionista! Get this fabulous dress up game and you will be enchanted by all the stunning clothes available. All kinds of dress up items are waiting for you - dresses, pants, skirts, tops and more. Spin the wheel until you discover all the fashion options each style offers you, or if you want to take matters into your own hands, choose not to spin. This way, you can try everything!
