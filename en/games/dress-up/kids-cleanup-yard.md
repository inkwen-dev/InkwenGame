## About

Kids Cleanup House is a very cute cleaning game. If you are interested in doing housework and want to learn how to do it, then this is the perfect game for you. This game consists of a total of 4 parts. You can clean the house of the puppy, the cats cat carrier, the playground, and the swimming pool. Not only do you get to experience cleaning, but you also get to experience decorating. You can get cool and fashionable decorations by watching reward ads. Then decorate the house with your fashion taste by just clicking on the mouse. Want to know more about this game? Come and play it!
