## About

Welcome to a world where fashion meets mystery. The city is falling asleep, but for the noir ladies, life is just beginning. Two friends live in this gloomy place. And tonight they will have a magnificent Gothic ball in an old mansion. Help create the perfect holiday images, which will be dominated by deep black, shades of graphite, blood red and dazzling silver. In the world of noir, there is no place for gray everyday life - there is only fatal beauty and style!
