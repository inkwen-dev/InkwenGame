## About

Help the boy and girl find style and charm in this beautiful dress game! Get creative and find your favorite style. There are over two hundred products to choose from in eighteen categories, such as haircuts, color, hairstyle, eye color, dresses, shirts, jeans, clothes, shoes, body color, lip color, nail color and much more! Once you’ve created a style, you can head to a cool photo studio and you can take a picture of a girl and a guy separately or together!
