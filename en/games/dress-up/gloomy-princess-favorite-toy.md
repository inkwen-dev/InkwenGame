## About

Immerse yourself in the mysterious world of the Gloomy Princess and her strange but touching teddy friend. Create unique images for the heroine of the game: choose makeup, outfits and accessories - from witch hats to lace corsets in the Gothic spirit. Add personality to her favorite toy: change the expression of the muzzle, color, add ears, bows, scars, button eyes and other cute or creepy details. Assemble the perfect duo - a princess hiding her feelings behind a flawless image, and her beloved companion reflecting the inner world of the hostess!
