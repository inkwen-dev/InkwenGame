## About

Pony creator game where you can create your magic pony. The design is all yours, choose head shape, tail, body, wings and a nice garment. Color your pony as you wish, and add some stickers for final touch. At the end of the game you can take a photo of your pony and download it. Show your creation to your friends and family.
