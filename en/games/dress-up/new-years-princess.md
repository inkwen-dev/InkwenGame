## About

New Year is on the threshold! Anna and Elsa decorate the palace for the upcoming holiday. It's time to dress up a Christmas tree! Help the princesses decorate the tree with toys, Christmas balls, garlands and tinsel. On New Year's Eve, a miracle will happen - and the beautiful Christmas tree will turn into a beautiful princess. Christmas decorations will turn into a wonderful dress! Choose different decorations to change the design of the Christmas tree princess dress! Happy New Year!
