## About

Define your fashion style with a fun game! It's time to spin the wheel, which will decide for you which style to explore. These princesses are ready to do the same. Each girl will spin the wheel and dress up according to the style they get! What's going to be Rebel, Girly, E-girl, Geeky, Artsy, VSCO, or Preppy! There are seven princesses and seven styles, so you get to try them all! Find the trendiest dresses, loveliest skirts, edgiest jeans and tops and most cools jackets to combine and make these princesses rock their style!
