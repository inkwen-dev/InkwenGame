## About

Kitty Bedtime Activities is a caring game. Wondering what kittens do before bed? Enter this game to find out the answers. You can bathe the kitten, change the kitten's pajamas and play lullabies for it. Don't forget to brush kitten's teeth before it goes to bed. If the kitten can't sleep, you can count sheep to help it sleep. Have a good time!
