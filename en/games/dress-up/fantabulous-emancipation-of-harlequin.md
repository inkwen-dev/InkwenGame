## About

Princess Harlequin has always been unlike others. Cheeky hairstyles, bright multi-colored makeup and crazy outfits - all this is about Princess Harlequin. Abandon the usual fashion framework with her. Create your own image for the princess, use gold, black and red tones, as well as a pattern of rhombuses typical for harlequin. Forget about homemade accessories - necklaces and earrings. Write your Fantabulous fashion story about Princess Harlequin.
