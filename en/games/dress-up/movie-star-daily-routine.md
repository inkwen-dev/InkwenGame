## About

Let's join the a day in the life of a movie star stream. We will spend the whole day with a famous actress from the series. The morning begins with cosmetic procedures and skin care. See how the skin of a movie star shines! Time to go in for sports to maintain excellent physical shape. Preparing for going to the gym begins with packing a bag - you need to take water, a yoga mat and other accessories. A star needs to look stylish even in the gym, so you need to get serious about choosing a tracksuit. It's time to put on your makeup and go to the screen test. The movie star will have to go through a casting and try on the image of the heroines of films and series. And finally, it's time to enjoy the glory. Having collected everything you need in a small clutch, the girl goes to the red carpet to amaze everyone with brilliant and original outfits!
