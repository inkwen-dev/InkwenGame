## About

Fashion Foot Shop is a very popular make over game. In this game, you own a foot shop and deal with many customers. They have different problems with their foot. Such as, long nails, dirty nails and wounds etc. Remember! You are not just a manicurist. You are also a nail artist who can design amazing nail styles. Come and use your fashion potential. Design nails for customers according to their characteristics!
