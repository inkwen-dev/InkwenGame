## About

Welcome to a small humorous adventure in which you will try on the role of a stylist for the most mysterious lady of all time - Mona Lisa Gioconda. Connect the past and the present by mocking trends and memes. Each of your changes is a small work of art, and each outfit immediately causes fireworks of pleasant emotions!
