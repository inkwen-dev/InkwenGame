## About

Princess Eliza, Tiara and Milana want to stay stylish and beautiful despite the circumstances. If you need to wear a mask - let's make it a fashion accessory. Choose a color and pattern for your mask, lots of funny stickers and rhinestones. During this difficult time, decorating the mask will help to raise your mood. Now you can choose an outfit for the new princess mask. Do not forget about stylish accessories and makeup, a real princess always remains a lady.
