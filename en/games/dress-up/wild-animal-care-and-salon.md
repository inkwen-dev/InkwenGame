## About

&ldquo;Wild Animal Care And Salon&rdquo; is an interactive simulation game where players take on the role of caretaker and stylist for a variety of wild animals. Players will be responsible for giving treatment, feeding, grooming, and playing with the animals to keep them healthy and happy. As they progress through the game, players will unlock new animals to care for, each with their own unique needs and personalities.
