## About

Cookie Master is a simulation game in which you create and decorate different types of cookies and sell them to your customers. Try to offer creations that match the wishes of customers, and you will quickly become an outstanding craftsman! As you progress through the game, you will gain access to more advanced tools to further personalize your desserts. Put on your chef's hat and grab all your kitchen tools, because Cookie Master is the best way to make your dream cookies without messing up the real kitchen. Have fun and don't forget to show your friends your amazing creations!
