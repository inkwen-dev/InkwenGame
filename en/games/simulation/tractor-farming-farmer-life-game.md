## How to play

Desktop:
AWSD: Player Movement
Space: Break
Mouse: Camera Movement
P = Pause
Ctrl = Setting
I= Info
Shift = Sprint
F = Actions
N: Skip Level

Mobile:
Joystick: Move Player
Screen Drag : Move Camera
Left Button: Move Tractor Left
Right Button: Move Tractor Right
Race: Tractor Race
Break: Tractor Break

## About

Experience the life of a real farmer in Tractor Farming Simulator! Drive powerful tractors, cultivate fields, grow crops, and complete realistic farming missions across beautiful countryside environments. Operate modern agricultural machinery, transport your harvest, and expand your farming business as you become the ultimate farming expert.

Choose from four unique farming modes, each focused on a different crop and farming experience. Grow Corn, Cotton, Wheat, and Pumpkins, with each mode featuring its own farming missions and harvesting challenges. Plough the fields, sow seeds, irrigate crops, apply fertilizer, harvest at the perfect time, and transport your produce to earn rewards.

Unlock new tractors, trailers, and farming equipment as you progress through increasingly challenging levels. Explore large open farms, improve your skills, and enjoy an authentic farming adventure with smooth controls and realistic gameplay.
