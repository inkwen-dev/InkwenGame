## How to play

Move the mouse over the edge of the forest to chop trees (no click needed), or tap a tree on mobile. Hire villagers at the town hall: they chop on their own and carry the wood home. Spend wood, food, stone and gold on the building cards at the bottom; buildings next to the right neighbours get bonuses. Click a building to upgrade it. Drag to move the camera, right-drag or Q/E to turn, mouse wheel or two fingers to zoom. Clear every tree, build the ship on the beach and set sail to the next island. Finish the island's three goals for three stars.

## About

A cozy 3D village builder on a hexagon island covered in forest. Move your pointer over the trees to chop them, hire villagers who chop, farm and mine for you, and grow your hamlet from a settlers' camp into a castle. Place buildings next to the right neighbours for bonuses, find relics hidden in the woods, fill the orders of passing merchant ships and build your own ship on the beach. Clear the whole island, earn up to three stars and sail away to a new one. Every island plays differently: lightning storms in autumn, a freezing winter, sacred cherry trees, a treasure island and an erupting volcano. Later islands mix two of them, each one has an Expert mode, and every Monday there is a new Island of the Week that is the same for everyone.
