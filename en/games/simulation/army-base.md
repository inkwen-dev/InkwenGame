## How to play

Take your recruits to training stations. Recruits develop gradually. To pick up a recruit, simply approach them.

## About

This is a simulation game where you become a commander for training new recruits. Your task is to lead them through various training facilities and transform ordinary privates into true fighters. Lead recruits through physical training, improve their endurance, teach them shooting, medicine, and tactical skills, improving the overall quality of your squad.

Each zone of the base is responsible for its own type of training: a shooting range for accuracy, a sports area for strength, a medical center for skill recovery and healing. After training, send your trained soldiers into battle against the enemy, where they can demonstrate their acquired skills. But be prepared for the consequences—some soldiers may end up in the infirmary, and you'll have to ensure their recovery.

You will constantly accept new recruits and improve the base's infrastructure to speed up and strengthen the training process. Develop buildings, open new training zones, and create the perfect army training system.
