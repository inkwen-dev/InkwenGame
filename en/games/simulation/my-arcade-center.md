## About

My Arcade Center is an addictive idle arcade tycoon and management simulator where you build, expand, and run your very own arcade business. Start with a small game room and turn it into a thriving arcade empire by placing machines, upgrading equipment, and attracting more customers.

Manage your arcade efficiently by collecting earnings, unlocking new areas, and optimizing your layout to maximize profit. Keep your machines running smoothly, serve customers quickly, and maintain a clean environment to boost satisfaction and income.

As you progress, unlock a wide variety of arcade machines, expand your space, and discover new strategies to grow faster. Each upgrade increases your revenue and helps you become the ultimate arcade tycoon.

Whether you enjoy idle games, simulation games, or business management experiences, My Arcade Center offers a satisfying progression system, simple controls, and relaxing gameplay that keeps you coming back for more.

Play now and build the best arcade center in this fun and rewarding idle management game!

• Day–Night loop: earn by day, upgrade by night.
• 9 maps to progress.
• Unlock and place machines anytime.
• Hire staff and use coin bots.
• Handle freeloaders, vandals, and misbehaving customers.
• Optimize layouts and expand your arcade.
