## How to play

Drag anywhere (or use WASD / arrow keys) to move. Leaves near you are sucked into your bag. When the bag is full, walk to the crate to sell. Spend coins on upgrades and clean the whole yard.

## About

Autumn is here and every yard is buried in colorful leaves! Drag to walk your leaf blower around and watch thousands of leaves swirl into your bag. Empty the bag at the compost crate to earn coins, then upgrade your suction size, bag, speed and buy little robot helpers that clean on their own. Find hidden treasures under the leaf piles, clean spooky Halloween yards at night, and collect funny hats like a pumpkin head, witch hat and crown. Relaxing, satisfying and endless.
