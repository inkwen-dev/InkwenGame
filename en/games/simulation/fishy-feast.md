## About

Fishy Feast is a fast-paced underwater survival game where you control a cartoon fish in a colorful ocean world. The rules are simple but addictive &mdash; eat fish smaller than you to grow, and dodge the bigger ones or its game over! Starting as a tiny little fish, your goal is to climb the food chain, grow as large as possible, and rack up the highest score you can before something bigger swallows you whole.
