## About

Do you like to eat Pancakes in breakfast? Do you like to cook it by yourself? So be ready for cooking your own delicious pancakes in Sweet Pancake Maker cooking game. In this game you'll cook delicious pancake which is always favorites for all age people. Make attractive colorful and sweet pancake to satisfy your hunger. To start cooking pancake, first you need some ingredients like flour, sugar, butter, baking soda, milk and egg. Mix all ingredients and bake it in oven, make sure it does not burn while baking! Once it's ready then decorate is with huge collection of sprinkles, frostings, toppings and many other decorative items. There is lots of fun by making pancakes and it's all free to play.
