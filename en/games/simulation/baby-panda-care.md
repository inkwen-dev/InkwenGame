## About

Children love to engage in all sorts of pretend play and imitating parents is one of the most enjoyed. Caring for a baby is an experience they are familiar with happening to themselves. It’s fun for children to switch roles and take on some caring responsibilities. Baby Panda Care gives children the chance of caring for another baby, a cute baby panda. You might be surprised to find how much your children will enjoy caring for this new baby!
