## About

Another day, another recipe to cook. Join this baking game where you get to see how a homemade cheesecake is made and what ingredients you should use. Purchase the required items from the list, pay for them and go to the kitchen to prepare the space. You will have to clean it first and use some of the bought products, then you will get to bake and in the end, you can even decorate with yummy fruits and details.
