## About

First, grow your plants and feed your chickens with Frenzy Farming Simulator, a fun farming game. If you run out of water for the plants, you can get coin water from the water well. Collect and sell the eggs of the chickens you raise. Get milk by including cows in your farm in the next levels. You can make cakes with the milk and eggs you obtain and sell them for money.
