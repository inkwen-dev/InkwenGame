## How to play

Objective 🎯
Take perfect care of your pet by managing their Needs meters (Hunger, Hygiene, Sleep, and Happiness). Keep all the bars full so your pet stays healthy and joyful! ❤️

Controls 🕹️
💻 Desktop (Mouse): Click on the action icons (Food, Bath, Toys) at the bottom of the screen. Click and hold to drag items like soap or food directly onto your pet to use them.

📱 Mobile / Touchscreen: Tap the category buttons to open your items, then drag and drop the items onto your pet using your finger.

## About

Welcome to My Cute Virtual Pet, a heartwarming casual game where you get to adopt, care for, and bond with an adorable digital companion! 🥰 Your new pet relies on you for everything. It's up to you to keep their belly full, their fur clean, and their spirits high!

Keep a close eye on your pet's status meters. Serve up delicious snacks when they are hungry 🍎, scrub them down with bubbly soap when they get messy 🫧, and dive into exciting mini-games to keep them entertained! 🎮

🌟 Key Features:

🐾 Interactive Care: Feed, clean, and heal your pet to keep all their health and happiness meters completely full!
