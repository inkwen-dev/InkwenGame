## How to play

How to Play
1. Buy an operation and click its Run button to begin producing Scrap.
2. Spend Scrap to level up operations, unlock more businesses, and improve your income.
3. Hire Crew Chiefs to automate production cycles.
4. Purchase Retrofits and collect Lucky Finds to accelerate your growth.
5. Use Reclamation when you are ready to earn Reactor Cores and rebuild faster.

Mouse
- Left-click buttons to run operations, collect rewards, make purchases, and navigate menus.

Touchscreen
- Tap to use the same controls.

## About

Ashfall Industries is a comic atompunk idle management game about turning a battered wasteland into a thriving industrial frontier.
Start with a humble Rustwater Pump, collect Scrap, and build a network of increasingly ambitious operations: greenhouses, salvage yards, radio stations, caravan routes, drone workshops, refineries, and more.
Every operation can be upgraded for bigger payouts and faster cycles. Hire distinctive Crew Chiefs to automate production, then invest in Retrofits that improve output, speed, costs, and Lucky Find rewards. When your industry is ready for a fresh start, trigger Reclamation to earn Reactor Cores and unlock permanent Recovery Protocols that make every rebuild stronger.

Core Features
- Build 15 original wasteland operations, from clean-water pumps to the Horizon Reactor.
- Collect Scrap, level up production, and hit new Benchmarks for bigger rewards.
- Hire Crew Chiefs to automate your best operations while you expand the business.
- Buy Retrofits to improve output, cycle speed, salvage efficiency, and Lucky Finds.
- Catch Lucky Finds for boosted payouts and satisfying bursts of progress.
- Use Reclamation to reset strategically, earn Reactor Cores, and purchase permanent upgrades.
- Grow a colorful rust-and-turquoise industrial empire at your own pace.
