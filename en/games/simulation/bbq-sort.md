## How to play

- Sort & Match: Tap and move 3 identical BBQ skewers onto the same grill to complete an order and clear the grill for the next match!
- Strategic Moves: Think ahead! Each level presents unique sorting challenges and a limited time and space. 
   Plan your placements carefully to achieve your goals.
- Sizzling Power-Ups: Stuck on a tricky arrangement? Unleash awesome boosters to clear obstacles and heat up the action!
