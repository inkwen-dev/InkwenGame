## How to play

🛡 Protect the barrier: Your main goal is to prevent the zombie horde from breaking into the camp.
🏗 Place traps: Simply drag defensive devices from the bottom panel to the highlighted zones on the battlefield.
💰 Complete quests: Follow the quest list to earn resources and grow your camp's economy.
🚀 Upgrade everything: Click on buildings and objects to increase their durability, damage, or income.
🖱 Interact: Click directly on zombies and objects on the map to actively assist your survivors.
Controls:

    Mouse: Click and drag (to install traps), left-click (for menus and actions).

## About

Survival Tycoon: City of Zombie - Your Camp, Your Rules! 

You are the leader of a survivor group in the heart of a zombie apocalypse. This is a thrilling idle tycoon strategy where your goal is to protect the barrier, gather resources, and survive! A true survival simulator for those who love camp management and crushing hordes of enemies.

Strengthen the barrier, gear up your scouts, and hit the gas. Become a survival legend right now!
