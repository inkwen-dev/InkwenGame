## About

Bounce, rebound, hop and spring! The Alien Bounce game is a very fun game. Offers a very original way to control the main character. Go through dozens of fun levels and also try the chalange mod, which offers a randomly generated level. Alien finds himself on an unknown planet and aims to collect planets wealth that takes the form of gems. But suddenly he finds out that the flora of this planet is not very friendly and tries to prevent him in his intentions in every possible way. Be fast and accurate and try to complete all the levels. When you complete every individual level you can try higher difficulty and time challenges.
