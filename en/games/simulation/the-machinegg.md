## About

Welcome to The MachinEGG, a relaxing idle clicker game where eggs are your greatest treasure. Grow your farm, automate production, and see if you can earn enough to retire.

Buy chickens, feed them, collect their eggs, and take them to market. As your farm grows, unlock automation systems to streamline every step of the process and maximize your profits.

There's a normal mode and a speedrun mode to help you reach the top. Two replayable handicap challenges will change everything, and finally, there's an endless mode if you're still craving more chickens.

And please… treat the chickens well.
