## How to play

PC Controls
Move mouse = look around
WASD / Arrow keys = move
Keys 1-4 = equip tools
Left-click = hit / use tool
Tab = open inventory;
E = interact with an object
P = pause menu
B = unlock Mouse
Mobile Controls
Left joytick = move
Swipe on the screen = look around
Right side buttons = jump, use tools, interact with an object

## About

Island Expander is a cozy crafting game where you transform a tiny island into a paradise. Collect resources, unlock recipes, craft items from simple tools to complex machines. Watch your island grow with every step.
Features
Relaxing, meditative gameplay
30+ recipes to discover and craft
Expand your island tile by tile
Calm atmosphere and sense of progress
