## About

Battle Warship Arena is a thrilling 2D naval combat game that lets you command your own warship and blast away enemy ships. You can choose from five different ships and five unique captains, each with their own skills and abilities. You can also upgrade your warship&rsquo;s firepower, armor, speed, and more. To win each level, you need to destroy all enemy ships while staying within the red circle. If you go outside the circle, your warship will take constant damage until it sinks. Battle Warship Arena is a fun and challenging game that will test your strategic skills and reflexes. Are you ready to rule the seas?
