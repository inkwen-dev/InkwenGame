## About

“Sniper Simulator” is a first-person shooting simulation game, in the game, players will play the role of a sniper and control a variety of classic guns for sniper training. In addition, a popular assembly gameplay has been added, allowing players to experience the excitement of assembling while shooting. The game is easy to operate, the screen is beautiful, the sound effects and gun textures are realistic, restoring the most realistic shooting experience.
