Upgrade your bee's flight speed, cargo capacity. Hire hard-working helper bees to automate pollen collection, expand your swarm.

Desktop: WASD / Arrow Keys / Hold Left Mouse Button — Fly / Move

Mobile / Touch: Virtual Joystick

## About
