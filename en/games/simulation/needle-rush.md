## How to play

Dig the haystack until you find the needle hidden inside. Hay you dig falls into the bins and turns into coins. Spend coins in the shop on better tools, farmhands and machines that dig for you. Mouse: hold the left button on the haystack to dig, click a card in the shop to buy it. Touch: hold a finger on the haystack, or on the strip under the barn when the phone is upright, and tap a card to buy it. Tap the needle when you uncover it to win.

## About

An idle clicker about finding one needle in a haystack of ten million straws. Dig the stack down by hand, sell the hay and spend it on better tools: a rake, a pitchfork, a shovel, a vacuum and dynamite. Hire farmhands and build claw arms, fans, winches, hay robots and drones that dig for you. The hay behaves like a real pile: it slides, caves in and spills into the bins. Claim achievements, and find the needle faster each time. Hand-made pixel art.
