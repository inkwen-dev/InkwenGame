## How to play

In game, your task is to skillfully control various vehicles and carefully park them in the designated spots. Victory is achieved when you successfully park in the assigned space and earn at least one star. The game demands precision and focus, as any collision with an obstacle results in failure. A guiding arrow helps you locate the parking spot. The levels vary in difficulty and require different skills depending on the class of the vehicle, adding variety and excitement to the gameplay.

## About

Car Parking Simulator is a unique blend of driving games and puzzle challenges. The game resembles classic driving simulators but with a distinctive focus on the art of parking. It is perfectly suited for both car game enthusiasts and those who want to improve their parking skills in a safe and enjoyable environment. The game features a wide variety of vehicles, from city sedans to trucks with trailers, each offering its own unique challenge. With 39 levels—each of which can be replayed to improve your results — Car Parking Simulator  promises hours of engaging gameplay.
