## How to play

Mouse: Click and drag in the direction you want the cat to move.
Touch: Touch and drag in any direction to move the cat.
Stand near an interactive object to fill the progress bar and perform the action.

## About

Run a cozy hot bathhouse with an adorable cat! Collect hot and cold water, prepare the bathtubs, welcome customers, and keep every visitor happy.
Earn money from your guests and invest it in new bathtubs, useful upgrades, worker cats, a tea shop, and restroom facilities. Expand your bathhouse step by step and turn it into a thriving spa business!
