## About

Swing a wrecking ball through six colorful districts and grow your demolition business. Hit the green zone for a super strike with extra damage, slow motion, zoom and comic impact bursts. Smash supports, send bricks and sparks flying, then spend the cash on bigger balls, faster swings and five cranes. Collect ball skins, complete daily contracts and renew your license for permanent bonuses.
