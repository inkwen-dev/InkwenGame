## How to play

Controls:
TAB - pause menu (or Escape)

Moving W (forward), S (backward), A (left), D (right).
Spacebar - handbrake.
Shift - nitro.
C - change camera.
U - free camera.
R - reset car.
K - restore car.
B - slow down time.
N - switch car.
