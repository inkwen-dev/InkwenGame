## About

Have you ever thought about being a veterinarian by profession? You will play this role in the Paw Care game to help cute little animals cope with physical issues that they can not handle on their own, including nail cleaning, wound healing, skin repair, etc. This game is soothing and enjoyable. There are all sorts of cute pets you might see. Be alert and polite. You will become a pet doctor with a practical simulation of a claw care game, helping cute animals fix nails, clean claws, treat wounds, etc. Have fun at Paw Care!
