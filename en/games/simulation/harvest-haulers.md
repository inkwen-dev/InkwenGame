## How to play

- Tap / click the hopper — releases a burst of fruit into the funnel below it.
- Tap a truck on the belt to dispatch it early if you don't want to wait for a full load.
- Tap an upgrade card in the shop rail to spend gold and boost your production.

## About

Harvest is an idle tap game about running a fruit farm. Tap the hopper to shake loose fruit, dumping it onto trucks on the belt. Full trucks drive off and pay gold. Spend gold on upgrades: more fruit per tap, bigger trucks, a faster belt, extra lanes.
