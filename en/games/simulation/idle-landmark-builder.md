## About

Enjoy a casual idle simulation management game in the Idle Landmark - Builder. Here, youll play the role of a construction tycoon and build world-famous landmarks from scratch. Manage workers to increase speed or your income, hire more workers and build your own construction empire. Build world-famous landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and the Pyramids, and witness the entire process of their construction.
