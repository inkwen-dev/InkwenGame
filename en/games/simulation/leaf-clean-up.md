## About

Start from scratch with your bare hands, collecting fallen leaves and dropping them off at the sell zone for cash. Step onto glowing upgrade pads to boost your bag capacity, suction radius, and movement speed. As you expand through multiple themed worlds, unlock powerful tools including the Leaf Blower for quick sweeping and the heavy-duty Lawnmower for instant mulching.
