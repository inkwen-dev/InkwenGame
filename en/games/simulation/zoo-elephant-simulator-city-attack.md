Desktop:

Mouse: Camera Movement / UI Actions
P = Pause

E = Attack

F = Actions

## About

Step into the world of a mighty wild elephant and experience an exciting wildlife adventure across forests and cities. Explore vast environments, raise your elephant family, battle dangerous predators, and complete action-packed missions in this realistic animal simulator. With Jungle, City, and Free Mode, there's always a new adventure waiting.

In Jungle Mode, survive in the wilderness by finding food, protecting your mate and calves, and fighting fierce predators like lions, tigers, wolves, and cheetahs. Build your elephant family, complete survival missions, and explore beautiful forests, rivers, and open landscapes.

In City Mode, unleash your elephant's strength as you roam through busy streets, smash vehicles, destroy objects, collect coins, and complete exciting city rampage challenges. Test your power and cause chaos while earning rewards to unlock new content.

In Free Mode, explore the world at your own pace without mission restrictions. Discover hidden locations, enjoy peaceful exploration, interact with wildlife, and experience the beauty of nature however you like.

Features
-3 exciting game modes: Jungle, City, and Free Mode.
-Raise and protect your elephant family.
-Fight dangerous wild animals in thrilling survival battles.
-Large 3D open-world environments with forests and cities.
-Customize your elephant with different skins and appearances.
-Complete exciting missions, challenges, and city rampage objectives.
-Collect coins and unlock new rewards.
-Smooth controls, realistic animations, and immersive gameplay.

Become the leader of your elephant herd, conquer the jungle, dominate the city, or simply explore the open world in Zoo Elephant Simulator City Attacck. Every mode offers a unique adventure for animal simulation fans.
