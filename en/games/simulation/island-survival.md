## How to play

🎯 Goal — complete 6 stages, build a raft, and escape the deserted island.

🎮 Core mechanic — an 8×8 board with 6 types of tiles: stone, wood, food, water, leaves, fire. Match 3+ tiles in a row to collect the corresponding resource into your inventory. Combos of 4 give a bonus resource. Combos of 5 give a "wild" tile (replaces any material).

🔨 Crafting — open the menu to see recipes. To craft an item, you need the required amount of resources. All crafted items (ropes, planks, ingots, nails, tools, raft parts) are permanent and carry over between stages.

🔄 Resource reset — raw resources (stone, wood, food, water, leaves, fire, ore) reset at the end of each stage.

## About

🏝️ "Island Survival" is a unique blend of match-3, survival, and strategic crafting in a vibrant pixel art style.

🎯 Your goal — complete 6 stages, build a raft, and escape the deserted island. Start with primitive tools, build a house, furnish the interior, smelt ore, create workbenches, and prepare for your sea getaway.

🔄 The key twist: raw resources (stone, wood, food, water, leaves, fire, ore) reset between stages — you can't hoard indefinitely. But everything you craft (ropes, planks, ingots, nails, tools, raft parts) carries over to later stages. This forces you to plan every move: what to craft now, and what to save for later? Your foresight is the key to success!

⚙️ Progression stages: from a campfire and stone knife — to a furnace, workbench, iron tools, and finally raft assembly. Each stage brings new challenges and new recipes.
