## How to play

Desktop:
WASD or arrow keys = move
E = interact or take
F = drop
Left mouse button = use tools
P = pause

Try on the role of a nightclub security guard, using visitor screening and complete freedom of action to achieve maximum entertainment and control over the situation.

## About

Immerse yourself in the unique atmosphere of a security guard simulator inspired by the cult game I'm Security.

Check visitors for prohibited items, decide who is worthy of entry and who is not. Your power is absolute, because you are an unlimited security guard! Use your imagination to have fun and unusual "entertainment" with visitors, leaving them without things or even with a couple of bruises. Everything that happens is in your hands.

Game features:

🔎 Check people for prohibited items.
🕶 Decide who to allow entry and who to refuse.
🤪 Give free rein to your imagination - everything is allowed!
🤝 Interact with visitors in any way.
🎭 Feel like a real "owner" of the club.
