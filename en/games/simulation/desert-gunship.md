## How to play

- Free-flight helicopter handling with chase or free-look camera

- Four weapons, from a rapid-fire gun to guided missiles

- Kill, rescue, destroy, survive and reach-zone objectives

- Knockout tournament with permanent elimination

- Upgrades that carry across the whole campaign

WASD - fly (W/S forward and back, A/D turn)

Q / E - turn left and right

Space - climb

C - descend

Left mouse - fire

Mouse - aim the camera

1-3 - select weapon

X - cycle weapon

N / M - next / previous target (mouse wheel also works)

V - switch between chase and free-look camera

F - pick up or drop a bomb

L - release the mouse cursor

H - show or hide the control tips

## About

Desert Gunship puts you in the cockpit of an attack helicopter over a hostile

desert airbase. Fly low between the hangars, pick your weapon, and clear the

camp before it clears you.



Seven operations, each with a different job: wipe out a camp, raid a supply

depot, pull survivors out under fire, hold a line against an assault, run a

bomb to its target, and fight for air superiority against enemy gunships.



Then enter the Tournament. Twelve countries seeded by strength, three

battlefields, three difficulty levels, and one rule: a single loss ends

the run.



Coins and XP from every sortie go into the hangar. Heavier armour, harder

hitting guns, more speed, then back out to the mission that beat you.
