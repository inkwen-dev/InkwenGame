Collect Earnings: Walk over to the Money Piles to gather your cash stacks.
Clean & Maintain: Sweep up trash, fix broken machines, and refill locker room supplies.

## About

🏋️‍♂️ Build the Ultimate Fitness Empire! 🏆
Are you ready to run the most successful gym in the world? In My Gym Manager: Idle Tycoon, you will guide members on their path to gains and fitness, unlock state-of-the-art exercise equipment, and manage a team of expert personal trainers and cleaning staff!
Step onto the gym floor and take charge: sign up new members at the reception desk, guide them to specialized workout zones, and collect piles of cash as they complete their training sessions. Keep the gym floor spotless, restock protein drinks and supplies, and unlock massive new rooms!
🎮 GAME FEATURES
🏋️‍♂️ Unlock Specialized Workout Zones: Start with a basic reception desk and expand into cardio treadmill zones, exercise mats, heavy weightlifting beds, sauna spaces, and clean locker rooms!
💰 Satisfying Cash Pile System: Collect physical cash stacks that pile up dynamically as members finish their workouts. Walk over to siphon your earnings in a satisfying flow of bills!
🧹 Keep the Gym Clean: Sweaty workouts leave trash! Grab your broom to sweep up litter and keep the gym hygiene high, or hire AI Cleaning Staff to keep the floors sparkling.
💪 Hire Professional Trainers: Recruit personal trainers to automatically assist gym members, repair broken machines, and refill locker room supplies so the workouts never stop.
😀 Real-Time Member Emotions: Watch customer heads! Impatient gym-goers will show anger emojis if they wait too long for a station, while happy members cheer and pay extra cash. Look out for high-paying VIP Members!
⚡ Juicy Squash-and-Stretch Upgrades: Unlock new treadmills, workout racks, and zones using elastic purchase pads that react dynamically to cash deposits.
📈 Strategic Tycoon Progression: Invest your earnings wisely to speed up workout times, increase locker room capacities, and unlock advanced fitness equipment.
