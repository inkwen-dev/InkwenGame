## How to play

Tap wandering villagers to earn coins. Visit the shop to buy lottery tickets, then scratch each ticket to reveal prizes — magical scrolls or even brand-new buildings. Use scrolls in the upgrade menu to strengthen your kingdom and unlock new perks. Place buildings on your realm to automate progress: the Tower collects villagers for you, the Bell Tower attracts more residents to tap and the Laboratory generates coins over time. Keep tapping, buy more tickets and reinvest your rewards wisely. Your goal is to unlock every upgrade and expand the kingdom to its maximum size — so click, scratch and build your way to the ultimate Clickdom!
