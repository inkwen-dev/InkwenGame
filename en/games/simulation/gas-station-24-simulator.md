## How to play

Run your own gas station and serve as many customers as possible! Drive up to each vehicle, refuel it with the correct type of fuel, collect payments, and make sure no one waits too long.

Earn money, upgrade your equipment, increase your service speed, and expand your gas station. The faster and better your service, the more profit you'll make and the happier your customers will be!

## About

Welcome to the world of your very own gas station! In this gas station simulator, you'll need to refuel cars quickly, serve a steady flow of customers, earn profits, and grow your business.

Purchase new equipment, improve service speed, unlock upgrades, and transform a small fuel station into a modern gas station. Keep queues moving, don't let drivers wait too long, and prove you have what it takes to build the best gas station around.

Simple controls, appealing visuals, and addictive gameplay make this game a perfect choice for fans of management and casual simulation games.

Can you become the ultimate gas station tycoon?
