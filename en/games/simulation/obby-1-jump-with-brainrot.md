## How to play

PC controls:
Move: WASD or arrows
Camera: mouse
Jump: Space
Interact: hold E (stands, portals)
Camera zoom: mouse wheel

Phone controls:
Move: left joystick
Camera: swipe on the right side
Jump: jump button
Interact: hold the action button
Inventory, shop and rewards: on-screen buttons

How to play:
1. Jump around the arena — every jump gives agility and new levels.
2. Reach the top of a wall, step on the coin plate and collect coins.
3. Spend them on your first brainrot at a stand, a skin at the shop or a pet from an egg.
4. Repeat: higher level — taller walls and richer plates.
5. Rebirth for a new level cap and unlock the frost, then the fire world.

## About

Welcome to jump obby with brainrots! You run around the arena, and every jump earns agility: the higher your level, the higher you jump — from a meter to the skies.

Ahead stand five tall walls like a staircase. Reach the top — a coin plate waits there: step on it, collect your coins and return to start for the next one.

Spend coins wisely: buy 15 brainrots (from Tralalero Tralala to Bobrito Bandito), cool skins and pets that boost your coins. The cooler the brainrot, the faster you progress.

Rebirth to raise your level cap and jump even higher — all the way to level 300. Open portals to the frost and fire worlds, climb ever taller walls and collect plates worth hundreds of thousands.

Compete on the agility, coins and time leaderboards, come back every day for rewards and grab playtime gifts. Jumped — reach the top!
