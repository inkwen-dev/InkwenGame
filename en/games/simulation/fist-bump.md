## About

Give me a Fist Bump is a great game where you can have fun with your friends. Shaking hands is forbidden on Corona days. So when you come together, do not shake hands and give them a Fist Bump while greeting. Show them who has the strongest punch. This game is also a game where you can test your reflexes. It throws the strongest punch that can stop the indicator hand in green. Have fun.
