## How to play

Desktop:
F = action
Tab = Skip Cutscene
Shift = Run
Q = Attack
R = Special attack 1
V = Special attack 2
C = Special attack 3
X = Special attack 4
Z = Special attack 5
p = Pause
N = Skip level
I = Info

Mobile:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
Use the screen drag to move the camera

## About

Enter the wilderness and experience life as a powerful wolf in Wild Wolf Simulator Animal Games! Explore forests, mountains, snowy areas, plains, and deserts while hunting animals, protecting your territory, and completing exciting survival missions.

Build your own wolf pack and become the Alpha. Hunt together, fight rival wolf packs, defend your den, and survive encounters with dangerous wild animals. Complete level-based challenges to improve your wolf's abilities and unlock stronger powers.

Choose from different wolves, including the Gray Wolf, Black Wolf, Timber Wolf, and Arctic Wolf, each offering a unique appearance and powerful abilities.
