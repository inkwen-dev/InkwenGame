## How to play

Use the joystick or WASD/arrow keys to move. Approach animals to hunt them, collect the dropped meat, and carry it to the cooking and selling areas.

## About

Become the ultimate hunter and build a growing hunting business in Capitalist Hunter!

Explore the wilderness, hunt animals, collect meat, and bring your haul back to town. Cook food for villagers, earn cash, upgrade your equipment, and unlock new areas as your business grows.

Hunt, collect, cook, sell, and upgrade in a satisfying arcade idle adventure.
