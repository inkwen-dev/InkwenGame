## How to play

Prepare and serve customer orders accurately and quickly. Customers will request different food items such as:

Shawarma ,Fries,Pasta & Cold drinks

Complete each order correctly to keep your customers happy and earn rewards.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Desktop Controls (Mouse)

Fry Chips:
Hold Right Mouse Button to fry the fries.

Cut the Meat:
Hold Right Mouse Button and drag to slice the meat.

Fill the Shawarma:
Click the Right Mouse Button multiple times on ingredients to add them to the shawarma.

Serve the Order:
Hold Right Mouse Button, then drag the completed item to the customer.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mobile Controls (Touch)

Fry Chips:
Tap and hold to fry the fries.

Cut the Meat:
Tap and drag to slice the meat.

Fill the Shawarma:
Tap multiple times on ingredients to add them.

Serve the Order:
Tap and hold, then drag the completed item to the customer.
