## About

Puppy Pet Vet Care is a cute caring game. In this game, you will take care of stray cats and dogs. First, bathe them, because they must not have the opportunity to take care of their hair and appearance when they are wandering. Then give the kittens and puppies a full body check, because they will inevitably encounter ferocious animals and bad people in the wild, which will cause them to have some diseases. After checking their bodies, you can choose suitable clothes for them to dress them up. Want to know more about this game? Come and enjoy it.
