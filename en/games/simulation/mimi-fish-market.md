## About

Mimi the cat just took over a tiny fish stall on a sunny wooden pier - and she has big plans. Catch fish, stock the counters and ring up happy customers before they lose patience. Every coin goes back into your market: unlock new counters and products, hire helper cats and cashiers, and grow your little stall into a seafood empire across five islands - from the glowing Night Market to the busy Harbor, the Airfield and Sunny Cove. Features: - Build and upgrade your market on 5 islands: shrimp, sushi, crab, lobster, oysters, tuna, caviar and more - Hire helpers and cashiers who keep the market running for you - Go fishing yourself......
