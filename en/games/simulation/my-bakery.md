## How to play

To control, you need to press the WASD keys or use a mouse if you're playing on a computer.
If you're playing on a phone, you need to use your finger.

## About

Build your confectionery empire in Family Bakery. Bake delicious treats, serve guests, and decorate your café in this cozy simulator designed for everyone.
Have you ever dreamed of running your own café? This bakery game gives you the opportunity. Your task is to bake pastries and satisfy customers to grow your business.
Why You Should Play
Simple and Engaging Mechanics: Customers arrive, you bake pastries, and serve them quickly.
Pleasant Atmosphere: A vibrant and warm design creates a relaxing mood.
Constant Progression: Unlock new recipes and expand your dining area.
Perfect for Relaxation: Easy-to-learn gameplay makes it a great game to unwind and relieve stress.
