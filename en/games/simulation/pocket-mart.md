## How to play

Goal: stock your shelves, serve customers, collect the cash and grow your shop until every room and all ten products are unlocked.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag anywhere on the screen on a phone. Stand in a marked circle to work automatically: collect goods in the warehouse, stock shelves, run the register and pick up cash. Tap the button that appears at pads and desks to buy shelves, rooms, upgrades and staff.

Tap Order to phone in more stock, or walk to your car, press Drive, and visit the Farm market and Mega Mall for cheaper boxes, then park in the delivery bay to unload. While driving, use W/S or drag up and down for gas and brake, A/D or drag sideways to steer, hold Space or the DRIFT button to slide, and press C or View to change camera. Esc or the pause button opens the menu.

## About

Pocket Mart is a 3D shop-management game set in a small open town. You start with one shelf of tomatoes and a tiny corner shop: carry stock from your warehouse to the shelves, ring customers up at the register, collect your cash, and reinvest it in upgrades, new products and staff. Grow from a single produce corner into a three-room market selling ten products, from fruit and dairy to bread and household goods.

When the warehouse runs low, order stock by phone or jump in your car and drive across town to the farm market and the mall for cheaper boxes. The town is fully drivable, with traffic, pedestrians, a petrol station, a car showroom with four cars to buy, a bank that pays interest on your savings, and your home, where you can sleep through the night. Hold the handbrake to drift around corners and chase a high score.

A full day-and-night cycle, impatient customers who comment on empty shelves and slow queues, hireable cashiers, stockers and cleaners, achievements and ever-growing goals keep the shop busy.
