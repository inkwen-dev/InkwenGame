## About

Welcome to Coffee Shop Idle, the ultimate coffee game for barista life enthusiasts! In this exciting cafe simulator, youll be running your very own coffee shop, serving up perfect coffee stacks to a growing number of customers. As you start out, youll need to master the art of the coffee stack. With each perfect coffee pour, youll earn money to grow your business, upgrade equipment and hire skilled baristas to help you keep up with the coffee rush. Keep practicing and perfecting your coffee stack technique to impress your customers and earn more money to say &ldquo;my cafe is the best!&rdquo;
