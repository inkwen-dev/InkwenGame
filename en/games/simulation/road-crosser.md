## About

Road Crosser is a high-quality 3D HTML5 game built with Three.js, featuring endless city-crossing game play like Crossy Road — but with a modern twist. Players must navigate through busy roads, forests, and city lanes while dodging vehicles and obstacles. With realistic lighting, animated environments, touch and keyboard support, and clean responsive design, this game delivers an immersive experience across desktop and mobile browsers.
