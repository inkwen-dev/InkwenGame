## How to play

Steal an egg from a biome nest and bring it back to your base before the guards catch you. Plant eggs in your base to hatch pets that earn coins every second. Use coins in the shops, train speed on the treadmill and rebirth for multipliers. Desktop: WASD to move, Space to jump, E/F to interact, hold right mouse to turn the camera. Mobile: joystick to move, drag to look, Jump and Hand buttons to act.

## About

Egg Escape Tycoon is a colorful blocky tycoon game where you sneak into guarded biomes, steal eggs from the animal guards and race them back to your own base. Plant eggs, hatch cute pets of different sizes and rarities, earn passive income, train your speed on the treadmill, unlock trails and rebirth for bigger multipliers. Play with up to 4 players in the same world, but watch the clock: when night falls the biomes lock.
