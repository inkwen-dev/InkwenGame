## How to play

Desktop: WASD to walk, click or hold left click to dig blocks. Space to jump. Press E or click UI buttons to shop, manage pets and rebirth.
Mobile: Move with the virtual stick, tap or hold blocks to mine. Tap buttons to upgrade tools, hatch eggs and sell.

## About

Dig, sell, upgrade! Mining Master is a blocky 3D mining simulator set in a sunny voxel world by the sea. Dig blocks and ores, sell your bag for coins, upgrade your gear and dig through six layers, from Topsoil to the Frozen Abyss. Hatch pets from eggs, forge treasures at the Forge, play Plinko, and rebirth for permanent bonuses!
