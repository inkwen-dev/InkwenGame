## How to play

On desktop, move your character with virtual joystick. On mobile, use the virtual joystick.

Walk up to trees, construction zones, upgrades, and income collection points — the required actions happen automatically. Earn money from visitors, unlock new plots, and reinvest your profits in the park. Your goal is to build every attraction and create a dream park filled with happy guests.

## About

Turn an empty plot into a lively amusement park! Clear new areas, build exciting rides, welcome visitors, and grow your dream park into a successful business.

Start with a small plot and build your own amusement park step by step. Control your character, clear the land, unlock new areas, and invest the money you earn in new rides and upgrades.

Visitors will enter the park, enjoy the attractions, buy treats, and generate income. The better your park becomes, the more guests it attracts and the faster you unlock new opportunities.

Build swings, carousels, and other attractions, improve the park infrastructure, collect profits, and turn a modest park into a popular entertainment destination!
