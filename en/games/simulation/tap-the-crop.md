## About

Tap the Crop is a simple, colorful idle clicker where you grow your farm one harvest at a time.
Start with wheat and work your way up through corn, carrots, tomatoes, and more, all the way to the rare Golden Crop. Hire farmers and machines to earn crops while you’re away, stack combos for bigger clicks, and catch events for big boosts.
