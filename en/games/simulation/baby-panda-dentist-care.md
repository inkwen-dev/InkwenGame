## About

Teeth are so important! Want to show a beautiful smile? Want to have white and healthy teeth? Come to Baby Panda Dentist Care to see what problems the baby animals have with their teeth and give them the appropriate treatment. This is a casual doctor simulation game. You will play as a dentist and accept every cute animal with dental problems.
