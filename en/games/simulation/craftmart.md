## How to play

Control Noob with the arrows on your keyboard (or by tapping on the screen of your mobile device). Build a building, pick up products and take them to the counter. When the customer has bought everything, help him pay at the cash register.

## About

Noob decided to go into business and open his own shop. And what do all the people of Minecraft want? That's right - to eat!

In Noob's shop you can always buy beet soup and bread! And also a lot of other food.

And now all the residents of CubeWorld rushed to the best store! Help Noob to serve all the customers! No one should go hungry! :)

Build new fields for wheat and beets, get a cow and a chicken and expand the shop to the maximum assortment!

Simulate a grocery shop in the world of Minecraft:

- plant turnips and wheat, raise chickens and a cow

- make turnip soup as well as warm bread

- serve your customers

- improve your means of production and your abilities

- hire workers to help you

Show your business skill!
