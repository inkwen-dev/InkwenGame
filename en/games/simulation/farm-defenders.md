## How to play

Touch or mouse — press and drag ANYWHERE on the screen. The joystick appears right under your finger, so there is no fixed pad to hunt for.
Keyboard — WASD, arrow keys or ZQSD.
Both work at once. Use whatever feels right.

## About

By day you farm. By night you fight for it.

Farm Defenders is a farming sim wrapped around a last-stand survival shooter, and the two
halves need each other. Your crops pay for your firepower. Your firepower protects your
crops. Every wave you clear is bigger than the one before, and every coin you spend decides
whether you see the next sunrise.

🌱 GROW YOUR FARM
Plant, water and harvest your fields, then stock the storage to turn produce into coins.
Hire farmhands to work the land while you plan, upgrade your storage, and unlock new plots
as your profits grow. The farm never stops earning.

🧟 HOLD THE LINE
Start the wave when you are ready — nothing moves until you say so. Then the horde comes
through the gates and throws itself at your fence. When the fence falls, the run is over.
Your character aims and fires automatically, so the real skill is positioning: read the
pressure, cut off the gate that is breaking, never let them settle.

🔫 GEAR UP
Four weapons, each with its own upgrade path — Rifle, Shotgun, RPG and Flamethrower. Add a
combat drone, hire soldiers to fight beside you, and raise defense towers that hold the
gates you cannot reach. Unlock new character skins along the way.

⚡ PICK YOUR EDGE
Every wave opens with a free boost of your choice: heavier damage, a tougher fence, or a
faster trigger. Read the wave ahead and choose well.

💀 ONE LAST CHANCE
Fence nearly gone? Take the revive and stay in the fight.

✨ FEATURES
• Two connected loops — farming funds your firepower
• Endless waves that escalate with every clear
• 4 upgradable weapons: Rifle, Shotgun, RPG, Flamethrower
• Soldiers, defense towers and a combat drone fighting alongside you
• Farmhands, storage upgrades and land expansion
• Character skins to unlock
• Auto-aim combat — pick up and play, no aiming required
• Full touch, mouse and keyboard support
• Cloud saves — leave on your laptop, pick up on your phone
