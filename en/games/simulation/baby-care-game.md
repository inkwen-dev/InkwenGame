## About

Baby Care Game is a very cute caring game. In this game, you will learn how to take care of a baby. There are four babies waiting for you to take care. Choose your favorite one. Come and feed the baby with delicious biscuits and nice tea...After all the interesting activities, you can take a shower for the baby. Have fun!
