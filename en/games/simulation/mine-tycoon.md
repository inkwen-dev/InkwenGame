## How to play

Your goal is to dig the mine, sell the loot and get richer.

PC controls:
• WASD — move, Space — jump, mouse — look around
• hold the left mouse button on a block — dig
• E — inventory, T — blow up TNT, H — climb out of the mine
• Ctrl — free the cursor to press buttons

Mobile controls:
• left stick — move, arrow button — jump
• swipe the screen — look around, hold your finger on a block — dig

How to progress:

1) Go down into the mine in the middle of the village and dig blocks — they go into your bag.
2) Bring the loot to the market: villagers buy it for emeralds and give profitable orders.
3) Buy better tools at the forge, a new mine and upgrades at the office.
4) Dig out the whole mine to get a reward and a fresh mine with new ores.
5) Hire golems and collect their income when you come back.
6) Rebirth for stars and unlock new worlds.

Tip: keep digging without pauses — combos give you more loot!

## About

A blocky world, a pickaxe in your hand and a mine that grows with you! Dig dirt, stone and ore — every block really breaks, and the right tool digs faster. Sell your loot to villagers at the market, complete their orders and buy new pickaxes, shovels and axes at the forge.

Expand your mine from a small pit to a huge quarry 96 blocks deep: iron, gold, diamonds, emeralds, geodes and hidden stashes wait below. Open lucky blocks, blow up TNT, build combos and hire iron golems — they keep digging even while you are away. Rebirth for stars and permanent bonuses, and descend into new fiery and dark worlds.

• three worlds with their own mines and blocks
• pickaxes, shovels, axes, shears and enchantments
• lucky blocks, TNT, boosts and combos
• daily quests, login rewards and playtime gifts
• block book, leaderboards and tool skins
• offline income: golems work while you rest
• play on PC and mobile, your progress is saved
