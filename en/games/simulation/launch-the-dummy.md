## How to play

Goal: launch the dummy as far as you can and cause as much damage as possible to earn cash, then buy upgrades and reach the lake at 1500 m. Controls: tap or click to push off and jump in, tap or click again at the ramp to launch. Hold the screen or mouse button to glide with the wingsuit. Fly through boost rings and smash objects to chain combos.

## About

Push a crash test dummy down the hill, jump in, tap at the ramp to launch him and watch him fly, bounce and smash through bowling pins, dominoes, crates and balloons. Earn cash for distance and damage, then upgrade the hill, your ride (shopping cart to rocket sled), rockets, eject spring and wingsuit to fly farther. Glide by holding, hit boost rings, chain combos, unlock characters and reach the lake at 1500 m!
