## How to play

Goal: follow the arrow to each character, complete their delivery and collect-quests, and earn coins to upgrade your tractor.

Desktop: W/A/S/D or arrow keys — drive and steer; Space — brake. Pause button in the corner.
Mobile: drag the on-screen joystick to drive and steer.

Tips: keep an eye on the fuel bar — buy fuel in the shop (if the tank runs empty, the farmers refill it for you after a short stop). Repair broken wheels in the shop, or wait and the farm mechanic will fix them. Avoid deep water. The pause menu shows your longest ride and longest time on the farm — try to beat the leaderboard!

## About

A cheerful 3D driving adventure for kids. Hop into the blue tractor, explore a big sunny countryside and help the villagers: bring cheese to chef Peppino, deliver hay, eggs and parcels, collect lost animals and find hidden treasures. Earn coins for every job and spend them in the garage on a faster engine, a bigger fuel tank, mud tyres, paint and fun cosmetics. Simple controls, no violence and no time pressure — a relaxing open world with music, friendly characters and dozens of quests.
