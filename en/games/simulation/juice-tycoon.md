## How to play

Objective: Harvest fruits, process them into juice, and sell them to earn money. Reinvest your profits to unlock new
  fruit types, faster machines, and helpful staff!
  Controls:

  • On Desktop: Use WASD, Arrow Keys, or click and drag the mouse to move.
  • On Mobile: Touch and drag anywhere on the screen to use the virtual joystick.
  • To interact with objects (harvesting, blending, cashing out), simply walk into their glowing green areas!

## About

A relaxing and addictive arcade-idle game where you build your own juice empire! Harvest fresh fruits, blend them
