## About

Become a skilled heart surgeon and take on challenging medical emergencies! Diagnose patients, use realistic surgical tools, perform heart operations, treat critical conditions, and help patients recover. Manage different hospital departments, complete exciting missions, and make important decisions under pressure. From heart surgery and emergency care to dental, eye, ENT, and orthopedic treatments, every case brings a new challenge. Save lives, complete surgeries, earn rewards, and build your career as a professional doctor. Are you ready to enter the operating room and become a life-saving surgeon?
