## About

Good Night Baby Taylor is a marvelous casual game. Who stole sleep from baby's eyes? This verse by Tagore may sound incredibly beautiful, yet when the kid is too excited to fall asleep, things might not turn out to be that poetic. This game might help the kids appreciate the wonderfulness of the dreamland. Once they realize that there is also a lot of fun in sleeping, they will be more willing to go to bed when it is time to sleep.
