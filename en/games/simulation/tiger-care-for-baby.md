## About

What flavor of candy cane do you like to eat? Come and join Fruit Sort Puzzle to make your own candy cane to suit your taste! Move the fruits, three or more of them can be combined and upgraded, and finally, you have to target the hawthorn. If the fruit exceeds the maximum limit of the fork, you will fail. Can you successfully make hawthorn candy canes? Believe in yourself! Have fun!
