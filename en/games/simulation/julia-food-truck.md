## About

Working in fast food requires increased responsibility, coordination and high professionalism! Don't believe? Then welcome to the ranks of sellers who sell delicious burgers. You are guaranteed to enjoy the gameplay, but you should not relax, because customers will be seriously outraged if you mess up something in the recipe. If you combine all three of the above qualities together, you will become a real salesman who is not afraid to work under tight deadlines!
