## How to play

Controls:
Tab – Pause
WASD – Drive
Space – Handbrake
Shift – Nitro
N – Next vehicle
C – Change camera
R – Repair car
V – Slow motion

On mobile devices, use the on-screen controls.

Game Objective:
Just drive around and smash cars however you like!
