## About

Enter Junkyard Keeper, where you become the owner of a junkyard. Create amazing new machinery with recycled items. This is a creative simulation game where you will assemble new mechanical equipment by collecting abandoned vehicles and parts. Drive vehicles around the junkyard to collect old parts and transport them back to your base.
