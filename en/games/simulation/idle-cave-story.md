## About

Idle Cave Story is an idle simulation and strategy game set in a primitive caveman village. Build and expand your settlement, assign workers, and manage your resources as your tribe grows stronger. Hire new villagers with different roles, upgrade your buildings, and unlock new areas to explore. Send your warriors into battle to clear dangerous caves, defeat enemies, and bring back valuable loot for the village. Balance your economy and army to keep your tribe safe and thriving. Whether you&rsquo;re actively managing every click or letting your village progress on its own, Idle Cave Story offers a relaxing yet strategic idle experience.
