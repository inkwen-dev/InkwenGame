## How to play

Use Arrow Keys to control and steer the truck through the city roads. Drive carefully, follow the route to the broken-down vehicle, and load it onto your flatbed. Once secured, transport it safely to the repair shop to complete your delivery mission. Avoid crashing into traffic — each collision costs you a life, so drive smart and stay alert. Complete tasks to earn rewards and unlock more powerful trucks. Keep towing, keep hauling, and become the best truck rescuer on the road

## About

Get behind the wheel of powerful trucks in this realistic grand truck simulator! Your mission is simple yet challenging — drive to the location of a broken-down car, load it onto your flatbed trailer, and transport it safely to the repair shop. Navigate busy roads, sharp turns, and unexpected obstacles on your route. Precision matters — secure the vehicle properly before hitting the highway!

Unlock and choose from a variety of trucks, each with unique handling and performance. Complete delivery jobs, earn rewards, and upgrade your fleet to become the ultimate roadside hero.

Feel the thrill of heavy vehicle transport, test your driving skills, and prove you’re the best recovery truck driver in town! Ready to tow, haul, and deliver? Start the engine and roll out!
