## How to play

Goal: build the biggest ice cream empire and unlock all 5 worlds.

Move with WASD / arrow keys, or drag on the screen (mobile).
1. Pick up ice cream from a machine and drop it on the counter.
2. Stand at the register to serve customers, then collect your cash.
3. Stand on a price tile to unlock machines, tables, rooms and staff.
4. Clean dirty tables and throw trash or extra items in the bin.
5. Walk into floating power-ups for timed boosts, and visit the Trader by the door and the Supplier by the drive-thru for special deals.
6. Unlock "Next Location" to travel to a new world.

Mouse wheel / pinch to zoom. Press the trophy button to see the leaderboard.

## About

Run your own ice cream shop and grow it into an empire! Serve customers, collect cash, unlock new rooms, hire staff, research new flavors and serve cars at the drive-thru. Open a sidewalk café, grab floating power-ups like Speed Shoes, Cash Magnet and Turbo Machines, and meet the travelling Trader and Supplier for surprise deals. Travel across 5 worlds, from Sunny Town to Fun Park, each with its own menu, and climb the global leaderboard!
