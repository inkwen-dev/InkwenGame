## How to play

To control, you need to press the WASD keys or use a mouse if you're playing on a computer.
If you're playing on a phone, you need to use your finger.

## About

You are the director of a waste recycling plant. There is no room for accidents here. Every bottle, every tire, every roll of toilet paper is the result of your control and skill. Trash Master is a recycling simulator for those who enjoy setting up mechanisms, not just money.

Gameplay:
🏭 Full-fledged factory: Conveyors, presses, shredders, sorting lines. It's just like in a real industry.
⚙️ The focus on efficiency: Your task is not just to process, but to do it with maximum speed, minimal losses, and without stopping the conveyor.
