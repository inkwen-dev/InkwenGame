## How to play

Use WASD or the arrow keys to move the keeper. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick or tap to move. Walk to highlighted areas to build and upgrade habitats, collect income, care for animals, and complete park projects. Finish all upgrades to reach 100% park completion.
