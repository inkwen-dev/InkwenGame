## About

Agent Sniper City is a high-stakes sniper game where you step into the role of an elite sharpshooter. Perched atop towering buildings, your mission is to eliminate dangerous criminals with precision shots. Use your skills to aim, scope, and execute targets while scanning the city for new threats. Stay focused—the fate of the city rests in your hands!
