## How to play

Goal: survive 99 nights and rescue the four lost kids. Keep the campfire burning, upgrade it to clear the fog, and defend the camp at night.
PC: WASD - move, mouse - look, left click - attack / use item, Space - jump, Shift - sprint, E - interact, F - upgrade the fire (near it), 1-6 - hotbar, Tab - inventory, Q - drop, R - reload, M - map, J - quests, N - notes, K - shop, Esc - pause. Keys can be rebound in Settings.
Mobile: joystick on the left to move, swipe on the right to look, on-screen buttons to attack, jump, sprint and interact. Gamepads are supported.

## About

Survive 99 nights in a foggy forest. By day you chop trees, gather food and craft gear at your camp; by night monsters come for your campfire. Feed and upgrade the fire to push back the fog, explore new zones, find the four lost kids and bring them home, read the forest notes and face the boss in the final nights. Play solo or with up to three AI companions, try hardcore mode with a single heart, and unlock skins with the coins you earn.
