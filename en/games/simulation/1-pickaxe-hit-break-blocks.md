## How to play

Mobile:
- Use the on-screen buttons
- Left joystick = Move the character
- Swipe on the right side of the screen = Rotate the camera

Desktop:
- WASD / Arrow keys = Move the character
- Right Mouse Button (hold and drag) = Rotate the camera
- P = Settings menu
- R = Rebirth menu
- T = Trails shop
- Shift = Toggle cursor lock

## About

Break blocks, collect weapons, and grow stronger in a colorful block world!

Pickaxes spin around your character — approach walls, smash blocks, and earn XP. Level up to unlock more pickaxes, then rebirth to boost your progress and take on tougher obstacles.

Open cases to expand your collection of pickaxes, axes, and swords. Unlock auras and buy trails with useful bonuses. Destroy enough blocks to spin the reward roulette and win temporary boosts!

Train your character, complete the map, and enter new worlds with increasing difficulty. Climb the leaderboard by breaking more blocks and build your own weapon collection!
