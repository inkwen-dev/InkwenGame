## About

Chef Right Mix is a great cooking challenge in which you can test your cooking skills. Help our chef prepare the most delicious dishes. For that purpose, you need to get the ingredients for your recipe, mix them well, cook and serve. Click on the food or flavoring that you want to use for your dish and then click on the Add button to put it in the basket. When you have no more space in your basket you can start cooking. Finally, when you finish cooking, then you can serve your wonderful creation. Cross your fingers to make the best meal ever and earn a high score. If you mix up the ingredients, you will get a pile of crap that nobody will like to eat. Have fun and good luck with Chef Right Mix!
