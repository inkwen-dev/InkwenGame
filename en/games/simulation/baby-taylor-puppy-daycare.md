## About

Baby Taylor Puppy Daycare is a simulation game. Taylor has a very cute puppy. One month ago, her puppy got lost. She missed her puppy very much. Maybe the cute puppy felt Taylors missing. Today her puppy finally came back. Puppy was covered with dust. Taylor was going to help Puppy clean his body and disinfect his wounds. After waiting for Puppy to recover, put beautiful clothes on Puppy, feed him delicious food, and build a warm doghouse for him. If you want to dress him up beautifully, give him a puppy manicure. Come and try it!
