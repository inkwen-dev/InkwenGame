## About

High School Girls Classroom Life is a fun and exciting high school adventure where you experience the daily life of a school girl. Start your first day at school, attend classes, make new friends, complete quizzes, and participate in exciting school activities. Drive your sports car to school, collect your student card, meet teachers, and explore a detailed campus filled with classrooms, a cafeteria, playgrounds, and a library. Take part in sports day competitions, dance classes, drawing lessons, and group study sessions to become the top student in school.
