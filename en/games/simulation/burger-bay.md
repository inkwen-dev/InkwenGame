## How to play

Walk to stations to cook and carry food, then serve the customers. Spend cash on new stations, upgrades and helpers. Serve the sea-monster orders before the timer runs out.

Controls: Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to walk, T to toss the top item, P to pause. Mobile: drag to walk.

## About

Burger Bay is an idle burger shop on the beach. Cook patties, stack burgers, serve customers and collect cash to unlock new stations, hire helpers and grow your boardwalk along the coast to new beaches.

Every few minutes a sea-creature boss shows up: a Kraken, a giant crab or a whale with a mega order and a countdown. Serve it in time for a huge payout.
