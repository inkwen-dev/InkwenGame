Rob the Robot, you have been given a crucial mission: to prepare Mars for human settlement! Mine resources. Build factories. Recycle resources. Prepare Mars…

## How to play

Use the joystick to move.

If you have a keyboard, you can use:
WASD or arrow keys to move.

Move Rob to a resource and he will begin mining it.
Move Rob to a production facility and he will begin processing the resource, and a production acceleration panel will appear.
To speed up resource processing, quickly click the panel that appears.
Move Rob to a construction zone and he will invest resources in the building.
Move Rob to a warehouse and the resource movement panel will open.
Move Rob to the transport assistant control zone and the assistant control panel will open.
