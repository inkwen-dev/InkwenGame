## How to play

- Click on the ants to destroy them - Protect the cookie - Collect coins - Upgrade your skills - Get the most points

## About

Your mission is to fight with ants that attack your cookies. The basic mechanics of the game is that you need to click on the ants to destroy them. In the game, you can collect money that will help you pump up your health, defense, attack, and resource gathering radius. Improving your parameters allows you to survive longer and more efficiently in battle. Your main goal is to last as long as possible by repelling ant attacks and protecting your cookies.
