## How to play

Guide your rocket to the target before running out of fuel. Avoid obstacles, collect fuel cans, and improve your completion time to earn more stars. Spend earned credits on rockets and equipment.
Keyboard & mouse: Press Enter to launch. Steer with WASD or ZQSD, move the mouse to look around, hold Space to boost, and hold E to use an equipped parachute.
Controller: Steer with the left stick, look around with the right stick, hold RT/R2 to boost, and hold B/Circle to use an equipped parachute. Use A/Cross to confirm menu selections.
Touch: Tap to launch, steer with the on-screen joystick, drag to look around, and hold the boost or parachute buttons.

## About

Take control of a rocket in a 3D arcade flying challenge! Navigate 20 handcrafted levels, dodge obstacles, manage your fuel, and hit your target. Fly through forests, buildings, and industrial tunnels, earn stars, and unlock different rockets and equipment to tackle increasingly demanding routes.
