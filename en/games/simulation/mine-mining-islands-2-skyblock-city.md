## How to play

🖥 PC:
W, A, S, D or ↑ ← ↓ → — movement
Button on the right — break blocks / attack

📱 Mobile devices:
Joystick on the left — movement
Button on the right — break blocks / attack

## About

Dive into a world of mining adventures and build your own city!

— Become the mayor: upgrade buildings, trade with villagers, and grow your city
— Explore islands: discover new lands and mine valuable resources
— Upgrade your character: improve speed, strength, and luck
— Fight and defend: protect the city from monster raids and battle in the arena
— Earn wealth: sell resources and become the most successful player

Are you ready to become the best mayor and hero of this world?
