## How to play

- WASD / Arrow keys = move
- Walk onto blue/green floor pads = unlock stations or upgrade (stand to deposit cash).
- Walk onto the wash pad with a hose = spray soap or water on dirty cars.
- Collect cash piles = earn money
- Follow the on-screen task list for the tutorial.
