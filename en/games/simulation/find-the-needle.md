Use the on-screen controls
Joystick — Movement

## About

Finding a tiny needle in a giant haystack is no easy job, and it's all yours. Dig through the hay layer by layer, shrink the stack, and get closer to finally finding that needle.

As you progress, you unlock new ways to collect hay, more powerful tools, and upgrades that let you clear it faster and more efficiently. The resources you earn can be spent in shops on further upgrades and progression.

🌾 A huge pile of hay to clear
🛠️ Different tools and ways to collect hay
💎 Upgrades and progression
🛒 Shops with new possibilities
🔍 And one tiny needle hidden somewhere inside

A simple goal that turns out to be not so simple — find the needle in the haystack.
