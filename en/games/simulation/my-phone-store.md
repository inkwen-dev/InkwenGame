My Phone Store is a fun and casual game about creating and selling tech devices!
Build smartphones, laptops, and tablets, improve your production, and sell devices to customers. Grow your shop, earn money, and turn a small store into a successful electronics business.
Features:
Simple and addictive gameplay
Create phones, laptops, and tablets
Sell devices and earn money
Upgrade your store and expand your business
Play, earn, and build the best gadget store!
