"Veggie Harvest" - fast farming clicker! Grow veggies, harvest with drag mechanic, upgrade crops and discover new ones! Grow your perfect idle farm!

## How to play

- Harvesting: Hold left mouse button (or finger on mobile) and drag across ripe vegetables on the grid. Faster harvesting = more bonuses!

- Buying upgrades: Click buttons in the right panel - sale price, growth speed, planting frequency

- Switching tabs: Use bottom buttons to switch between crop upgrades, passive bonuses, and crop properties
