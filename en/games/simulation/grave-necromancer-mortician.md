## How to play

Touch the screen and guide the necromancer with the joystick that appears beneath your finger.

Walk onto marked zones to construct buildings, take orders and complete tasks. For a funeral, dig a grave, bring the coffin, cover the grave and place a gravestone. Carry an urn to the crypt and seal the niche, or bring candles to the altar, perform a ritual and trap the spirit in a jar.

Collect coins at the counters, souls above completed graves and crystals from rituals. Spend your resources on new structures, necromancer upgrades and skeleton helpers. Your assistants can take orders, dig graves, carry supplies and carve gravestones on their own.

Raise the graveyard level to unlock new locations. There is no way to lose, so grow your business at your own pace.

## About

You're a young necromancer in a huge purple hat, running the night shift at a very unusual business: a cozy graveyard. Take orders from a very unusual crowd, grow your idle tycoon, and let skeletons handle the heavy lifting.

How to play

⚰ Take orders from ghosts, a zombie, a widow, a vampire, a headless knight and a witch. Customers pay up front, wait patiently and eventually fall asleep in line.

🪦 Complete funerals from the first shovel of dirt to the gravestone, place urns in the crypt, and perform altar rituals. Finished jobs bring coins and souls; rituals also award a crystal.

⚒ Build one step at a time: walk onto a zone, invest resources and watch 14 structures rise from the ground in dust and sparks. Play vertically with one finger—the joystick appears wherever you touch.

☠ Hire skeletons with souls. A gravedigger, carrier, stonemason and registrar take over the routine while you manage the cemetery and collect glowing souls.

Features

🦴 Skeletons work on their own: digging, carrying, carving gravestones and taking orders. Upgrade their speed and carrying capacity in the barracks.

⚙ Improve the coffin workshop, stonemason's shed, registration gate, crypt and altar. Each building has three upgrades and changes its appearance as your operation grows. At the necromancer's shop, improve speed, carrying capacity and profit.

🗺 Grow your business across five cemeteries, from a village burial ground to a sky necropolis on floating islands. Reach level 8 in one to unlock the next; each keeps its own progress, and you can return anytime.

⚡ Activate boosts for necromancer speed, carrying capacity, generous payments or hardworking helpers using ads or crystals.

🎟 Earn level rewards through the Undertaker Pass, with one free and two paid reward tracks.

☽ Collect up to two hours of offline income while you're away, and claim a free shop gift every 15 minutes. The game is free to play with ads and in-app purchases; ads can be removed with a purchase.

♨ Relax without shift timers or failure. This simulator lets you expand at your own pace, and every purchase raises the cemetery level and brings a reward.

🎃 Enjoy Halloween flavor in a cozy idle tycoon filled with moonlight, lanterns, mist and friendly ghosts instead of real scares.

This hand-drawn graveyard simulator has its own music and sounds, glowing souls and sleepy ghosts. The necromancer keeps the business moving while the cemetery stays charmingly spooky—more playful Halloween tale than horror.

Build your graveyard, grow the idle tycoon and put your bony crew to work!
