Imagine yourself as a kindergarten teacher. Change diapers, feed and put the little ones to bed. Only you can create an atmosphere of warmth, care and love…

## How to play

⁃ Move with the joystick;
⁃ Come to the diapers to take them;
⁃ Come to the baby to change the diaper;
⁃ Take a bottle and go to the baby to feed;
⁃ Take the baby and go to the crib to put him to bed.

## About

Imagine yourself as a kindergarten teacher. Change diapers, feed and put the little ones to bed.
Only you can create an atmosphere of warmth, care and love that will make the kindergarten an ideal place for the growth and development of children.
Are you ready to become a teacher?
