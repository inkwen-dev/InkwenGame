## About

Witch's Shop: Potion Brewing is a cozy simulator where you play as a witch running her own magical potion shop. Gather rare ingredients, brew powerful mixtures, and fulfill unusual requests from your customers.
Every visitor has a different problem. Can you create the right potion and turn your small shop into the most famous magical store around?

- Collect rare ingredients
- Brew different magical potions
- Serve customers with unique requests
- Upgrade and expand your shop
