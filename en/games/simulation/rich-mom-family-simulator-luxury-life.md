## About

Experience the glamorous lifestyle of a billionaire mother in Rich Mom Family Simulator Luxury Life! Play as a successful businesswoman, manage your family life, complete exciting missions, and enjoy the luxuries of being part of a wealthy family.

Start your day with a workout at a luxury gym, dress up in stylish outfits, and drive your sports car to the office. Manage employees, meet business tycoons, and grow your success as a powerful businesswoman.

Enjoy your wealth by decorating a luxury mansion, buying expensive cars, jewelry, gadgets, and even a yacht. Throw grand parties, spend time with your husband and family, and experience different aspects of a millionaire lifestyle.
