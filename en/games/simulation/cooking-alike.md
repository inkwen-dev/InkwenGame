## About

Cooking Alike is an engaging puzzle matching game with a kitchen theme, where you find and connect similar items to score points. Quickly observe and accurately connect pairs of images such as vegetables, cooking utensils, and ingredients to clear the board. The gameplay is simple, addictive, and gradually increases in difficulty, helping to improve memory and reflexes. Try your best now to conquer high scores in Cooking Alike.
