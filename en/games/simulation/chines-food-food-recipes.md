Do you like to eat Chinese food! Well then download this game and start cooking. Ever wanted to have a taste of Chinese kitchen food or wanted to become super chef in Chinese restaurant cooking game? Super chef, get ready to cook delicious Chinese recipes and learn to cook Chinese food in restaurant cooking game.
