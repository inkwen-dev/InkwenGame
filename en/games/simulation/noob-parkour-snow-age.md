## About

This time, the ice age welcomes the noob, who is trying to reach his home in the Minecraft universe. Collaborating with Squirrel Scrat, he joins the new adventure of completing all the levels and reaching his home. Your goal is to complete more than 10 levels by displaying your parkour skills. Be careful, more dynamic obstacles are waiting for you in this adventure.
