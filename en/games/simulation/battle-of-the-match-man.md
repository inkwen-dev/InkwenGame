## About

You can command the army of the Stick Kingdom and manage their resources and workload to effectively defeat the enemy. Mining, construction, equipping, upgrading, attacking, and defending until your people are liberated. First train miners to collect gold, and then use your income for units such as shields, archers, barbarians, and even mages! Build a defense tower using stones collected by miners. Dont forget to upgrade every unit and building you have to reach the full limit of your strength.
