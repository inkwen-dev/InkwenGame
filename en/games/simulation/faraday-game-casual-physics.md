## About

Faraday Game is an interactive experience inspired by Faradays Law, letting players explore electromagnetic induction. Move a magnet to alter the magnetic flux in a coil, generating current to light a bulb. Test your skills, maximize light intensity, and learn about electromagnetism in a fun way! Features: Two Modes: Angular (rotate the magnet) or Linear (move it vertically). Scoring: Earn medals&mdash; 24 V, 60 V, 110 V! Languages: English and Portuguese.
