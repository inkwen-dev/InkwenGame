## About

Survival Island is an exciting survival simulator set on a tropical island!
Explore the island, gather resources, collect wood and stone, build shelters, and upgrade your character. Your survival depends entirely on your skills!

Craft tools, unlock new opportunities, expand your territory, and turn the wild island into your own base. But be careful — resources are limited, and you must constantly improve to survive.

Game Features:

* Resource gathering
* Building and crafting
* Character upgrades
* Island exploration
* Survival adventure atmosphere
* Simple and fun controls

Can you survive on the island and build your own paradise?
