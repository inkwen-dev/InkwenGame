## About

Heaven &amp; Hell Idle is a fun 3D idle arcade management game where you build and expand two very different worlds. Welcome visitors, earn money, unlock new attractions, upgrade your facilities, and hire helpers to automate your growing empire. Explore heavenly cloud attractions and fiery hell areas, improve your character and businesses, increase your income, and keep expanding. Manage both sides efficiently and turn Heaven and Hell into the ultimate idle entertainment empire!
