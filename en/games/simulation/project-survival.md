## About

PROJECT: SURVIVAL

Welcome to Project: Survival — a fast-paced zombie survival shooter where every shot, every second, and every decision counts.

Drop into Ghost Map City, where chaos echoes through empty streets, or brave the twisted halls of Zombie Hospital, where every corner hides something waiting to strike. Scavenge what’s left of the world, complete deadly missions, and climb the ranks as you fight through relentless waves of the undead.

From crawlers that lurk beneath wreckage to spitters that melt through your cover, every encounter pushes your instincts and aim to the limit. Survive long enough, and you’ll earn rewards to expand your loadout, unlock brutal new weapons, and forge the arsenal that keeps you alive when the horde closes in.

Your progress is your power — and your only lifeline. Collect ammo and medkits from fallen enemies to stay in the fight. Each mission brings a new test of endurance and precision, taking you one step closer to becoming the ultimate survivor.

Die? Maybe.
Give up? Never.
Use rewarded revives to get back up, skip impossible missions, or unlock exclusive weapons to turn the tide. There’s always another chance — if you’re bold enough to take it.

Two maps. Endless zombies. No excuses.
This is your fight, your survival.

Survive the outbreak. Complete deadly missions. Build the ultimate arsenal.
Welcome to Project: Survival.
