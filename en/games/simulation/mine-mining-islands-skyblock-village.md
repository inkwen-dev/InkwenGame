## How to play

PC controls:
W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → — move.
The space bar is a jump.
Mouse movement is camera control.
Left mouse button — destruction of blocks

Management for mobile devices:
The joystick on the left is moving.
The button on the left with the image of the tool is the destruction of blocks.
The button on the right is jump.
Swipes on the right side of the screen — camera control.
