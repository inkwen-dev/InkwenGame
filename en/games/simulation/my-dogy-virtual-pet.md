## How to play

Use the mouse or touch the screen to interact with Dogy and the objects. Follow the instructions on the screen.
You can see the needs progression of the Dogy in the icons at the top of the screen. Try to keep the bars full attending the needs in each room.

## About

Meet Dogy, your adorable virtual pet who needs your love and care! My Dogy Virtual Pet is a fun and engaging pet simulation game where you take care of an adorable puppy. Feed him when he's hungry, give him a bath to keep him clean, and play fun games together to keep him happy. When he's tired, tuck him in for a good rest. Dogy will react to how well you take care of him, so make sure he always feels loved! Whether you're a pet lover or just looking for a cute and relaxing game, My Dogy Virtual Pet is the perfect companion for you. Explore the joy of having a virtual pet without any mess!
