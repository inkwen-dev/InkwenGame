## About

Take control of a powerful industrial magnet and clean up the yard! Collect metal scrap, carry it to the sell zone and turn every load into cash. Upgrade your magnets power, range, capacity and value to lift heavier objects and earn bigger rewards. Unlock new areas, discover valuable scrap and keep improving your machine until nothing is too heavy to collect!
