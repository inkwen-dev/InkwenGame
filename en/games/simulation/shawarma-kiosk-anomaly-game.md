## About

Welcome to the scariest fast-food job in the city.
In Night Shift Zombie Shawarma, you’re not just a cook — you’re a survivor running a small kiosk in the dead of night. When the clock strikes midnight, your shop turns into a hotspot for zombies, and every order could be your last.

Game Features:

Serve the Undead
Run your night-shift kiosk and prepare Burgers, Shawarmas, and Drinks for impatient zombie customers. Serve fast — zombies don’t like to wait, and mistakes can be deadly.

Smart Inventory Management
Handle food deliveries, organize racks, and keep your kiosk running smoothly. A messy kitchen during the night shift can cost you your life.
