## How to play

Accept a delivery mission from your phone, travel to the pickup point, collect the package, and place it in the scooter’s storage box. Follow the route to the destination, remove the package from storage, and deliver it to the customer before time runs out. Complete deliveries quickly to earn more coins, unlock scooters, and purchase upgrades.
Desktop controls: Use W to accelerate or move forward, S to reverse, A/D to steer or move left and right, Space to brake, and the mouse to control the camera and select interaction buttons. Use the on-screen buttons to enter or exit the scooter, interact with packages, open storage, and select missions.
Mobile controls: Use the on-screen movement, steering, brake, interaction, phone, and scooter buttons.
