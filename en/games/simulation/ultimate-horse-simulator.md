## How to play

Desktop:
Sprint = Shift Key
Attack = E 
Special Attack 1 = V
Special Attack 2 = X
Action = F
Skip Level = N 
Pause =P 
Setting =Ctrl 
Info = I 

Mobile:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.

## About

Experience the exciting life of a wild horse in Ultimate Horse Simulator! Explore a beautiful savanna jungle, build your horse family, protect your babies, and survive against dangerous wild animals in this thrilling horse survival adventure.

Play as a powerful Arabian horse, find your mate, and raise your family in the wilderness. Search for food and water, feed your baby horses, and protect your family from lions, tigers, wolves, bears, and other predators.

Complete challenging survival missions, fight wild animals using special attack powers, and earn coins to unlock new horses and upgrades. Explore the forest, discover safe places for your family, and become the strongest horse in the jungle.

 Features
- Play as a wild Arabian horse
- Build and protect your horse family
- Raise and feed baby horses
- Explore a beautiful savanna jungle
- Fight lions, tigers, wolves, and other predators
- Use special powers and energy ball attacks
- Complete exciting survival and fighting missions
- Collect coins and unlock new horses
- Find food and water to maintain energy
- Detailed 3D environments and animal sounds

Explore, survive, fight predators, and raise your horse family in Ultimate Horse Simulator!
