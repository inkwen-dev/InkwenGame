## How to play

Move the mouse or swipe left and right to aim. Press and hold to prepare the cast, then release to cast the line. Wait for a bite and press the screen or mouse button to hook the fish. During the fishing mini-game, keep the indicator inside the yellow zone. Successful critical catches can bring heavier and rarer fish. Sell your catch, upgrade your tackle and unlock new locations.
