## How to play

The game features intuitive controls tailored for every device: 

On PC:   

    Use the arrow keys or WASD to move your character around the island.  
    The left mouse button handles casting, hooking, and reeling in fish—just click at the right moment!
     

On mobile devices:   

    Move using the virtual joystick on the left side of the screen.  
    Tap the “Cast” button on the right to throw your line, then tap again to hook and reel in your catch.
     

Sell your fish to earn coins and invest them in better rods and floats. Upgraded gear speeds up fishing and boosts your chances of catching rare species!
