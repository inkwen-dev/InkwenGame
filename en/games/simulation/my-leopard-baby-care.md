## About

Do you like animals? Do you know that although a leopard is a kind of fierce animal, a leopard baby is very cute? Do you want to care for the leopard baby? In this game, you can feed the leopard baby delicious foods, bathe her with rainbow bath salt, and dress her up in smart outfits. If you want to relax, you can click to open magic eggs with butterflies in them and the little leopard baby loves playing with butterflies too. Leopard baby gets tired after so many fun activities, put her to bed and count stars to let her fall asleep...If you like this game, please recommend it to your friends. Enjoy!
