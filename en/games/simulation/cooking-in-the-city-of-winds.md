## How to play

The Windy City is famous for its exotic cuisine. There are many complex dishes made with unusual ingredients. But despite the difficulties, let's try to repeat a few recipes from the cookbook of the Windy City. The result can be saved as a PNG image. Depending on the gaming device, a computer mouse click or a simple touch on touchscreens is used to control it.

## About

Welcome to the City of Winds. The culinary festival starts here today! Participants came from all over the magical land to prepare their favorite dishes and delight the citizens. Try your hand at making smooth pudding, tapioca bubble tea and the famous Hocus-Pocus marmalade dessert. This is simply heaven for those with a sweet tooth. Don’t forget that the dish should not only be tasty, but also beautiful. Use your cooking skills to decorate the dish as elegantly as possible. And victory will be yours!
