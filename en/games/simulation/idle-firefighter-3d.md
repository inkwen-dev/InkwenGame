## About

Idle Firefighter 3d is a firefighter and fire truck simulator game. You will play the role of a firefighter and your duty is to save the survivors of the fire. Here, new emergencies keep happening and you need to be on standby and must put out the fire as soon as possible! The fire extinguisher is your friend, if the props run out, you will not be able to put out the fire.
