## How to play

Desktop: move with WASD or the arrow keys. Mobile: drag anywhere in the garden to move with the floating joystick. Your blower works automatically as you move. Push leaves onto the blue tarps, collect the bags and bring them to the sell point. Stand on the upgrade and helper pads to improve your business. Clear an area to unlock the next location. Open Settings to toggle sound, music or optional mouse movement. Optional videos provide extra bonuses; all areas remain playable without ads or purchases.

## About

Turn messy autumn gardens into a thriving leaf-cleaning business. Blow colourful leaves onto collection tarps, carry bags to the truck and reinvest your earnings in stronger blowers, bigger bags and helpful workers. Clean seven different areas, complete quests, unlock new equipment and start a fresh season with permanent bonuses. Play at your own pace with satisfying 3D leaf physics and colourful cartoon scenery.
