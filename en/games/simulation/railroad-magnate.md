## How to play

Tap available upgrades to improve your stations, trains, and routes. Collect income, unlock new buildings, increase train speed, and expand the capacity of your railroad. Keep growing your profits and turn a small station into a large transport empire.

## About

Become the owner of a growing railroad network and build it from the ground up. Run trains, unlock new stations, earn money from passengers, and upgrade every part of your route. The bigger your railroad becomes, the more platforms, buildings, and trains appear on the map. Simple controls, colorful low-poly visuals, and constant progression make the game easy to start and satisfying to keep playing.
