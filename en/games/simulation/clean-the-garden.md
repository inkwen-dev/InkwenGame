## How to play

- Keyboard: Use WASD or the arrow keys to move, Space to jump, E to interact, B to open upgrades, H to highlight remaining leaves, 1–4 to select a tool, Q to cycle tools, Tab to pause, and Escape to close an open menu or resume.
- Mouse: Move or drag to look around, hold the left mouse button to use the current tool, and use the mouse wheel to cycle tools.
- Touch: Use the left joystick to move, swipe the right side to look, and use the on-screen buttons to collect leaves or interact, highlight remaining leaves, jump, switch tools, open upgrades, and pause.

## About

Clear a leaf-covered property one patch at a time. Gather leaves, carry them to compost bins, turn them into coins, and spend your earnings on faster movement, larger carrying capacity, and stronger tools.

Start with your bare hands, then unlock a rake, pitchfork, and leaf blower as new gates open from the Front Garden to the Backyard, Orchard, and Park Section. Follow objectives, improve each tool at its station, clean every area, and replay with the option to keep or reset your upgrades.
