## About

Coffee is one of the favorite foods of many people and one of the most effective drinks for refreshing. Taylor also likes coffee very much. She decided to try to run a coffee shop. This is not an easy task. She finds her friends To help her with this work, first, Taylor needs to check the customer's orders, then start to prepare the coffee and desserts the customers need, and finally, when the customers check out and leave the cafe, don't forget to clean the tables and chairs. Does Taylor's work satisfy customers? Come and experience a day when Taylor runs a cafe!
