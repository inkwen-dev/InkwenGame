## About

Billionaire Lumber Tycoon is a unique hybrid tycoon game that combines idle production mechanics with real investment and trading systems. Manage automatic tractors to harvest wood, upgrade speed and capacity, and build a strong passive income system. Use the 15-second investment panel to analyze market charts and predict price movements for quick profits. Trade multiple coins affected by dynamic news events and apply smart buy-sell strategies. Convert diamonds at fluctuating rates to maximize earnings. Explore 4 different worlds with multiple maps, start from scratch on each one, and grow your empire again.
