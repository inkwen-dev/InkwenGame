## How to play

Drag on the machine with the mouse or your finger to move the claw, or click/tap the arrows for fine moves. A soft marker shows where the claw will land, and the eye button swings the camera to the side to judge depth. Click or tap GRAB! to drop the claw and watch it carry your plushie to the chute. Every miss fills the luck meter: when it is full, the golden Lucky Claw is guaranteed to grab the plushie under it. Rewarded videos are always optional (3 extra tries, double coins, a free special claw or free tries in Free Play). Esc closes windows and goes back.

## About

Grab the cutest plushies in the arcade! Cuddle Crane is a cozy 3D claw machine game. Steer the claw over a pile of pastel plushies, drop it and carry your favorite to the chute. Mochi, the round pink bear who runs the arcade, shows you the ropes and cheers every catch. Six themed machines (Cozy Classics, Ocean Pop, Dino Park, Sweet Treats, Space Pals and Spooky Cuties), 48 original plushies from common to legendary plus sparkly shiny variants, 60 challenges with stars and a giant Mega Plushie at the end of every machine, and Free Play to fill your album at your own pace. Four special claws help with tricky prizes: the strong Quad Claw, the Hook for keychains, the Magnet for bell collars and the Suction Cup for capsules and gift boxes. The claw is fair: clear rules, honest grabs and a luck meter that guarantees a catch after a few misses. Complete album pages to unlock new decor and fill your Plushie Room with your favorites. No energy and no lives: play as long as you like.
