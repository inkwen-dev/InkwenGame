## How to play

Catch and put brainrots on the base, improve the collection and storage of wood to go further.

WASD Management
Space - Jump
Pcm - Rotate the camera
E - Install/take a brainroot
F - Throw it away

## About

Collect wood!  

Build a bridge to reach the brainrots! 🌉

Get to new islands and collect more cool brainrots!
