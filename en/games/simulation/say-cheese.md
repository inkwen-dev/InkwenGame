## How to play

Arrange the whole family on the glowing spots before the timer runs out. Drag relatives with the mouse or your finger: tall folks at the back, the couple on the gold stars, kids in front. Click or tap anyone who looks away or blinks. Press Space or the Say cheese! button in the last seconds so everyone smiles for the photo. Everyone in frame, eyes open and looking at the camera earns 3 stars.

## About

You're the family photographer, and nobody will hold still. Say Cheese! is a funny 3D arranging puzzle against the clock: drag relatives onto their spots, tap the ones who wander off, blink or look away, and shout "Say cheese!" before the timer runs out. Shoot a wedding, a 4th of July BBQ, picture day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, prom and graduation. Nail each client's request, keep the happy couple centered, and earn 3-star prints for the family album. Rename your family, collect films and frames in the photo studio, and play a new Daily family every day.
