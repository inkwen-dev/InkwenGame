## How to play

Tap anywhere on the screen to speed up your money production and boost your earnings instantly. Use mouse clicks to upgrade your rows, unlock higher-value bills, and expand your income system. Merge money by combining identical bills to create more valuable currency, increasing your overall profit. Keep upgrading strategically to grow faster and maximize your idle earnings even when you’re not actively tapping.

## About

Money Maker Idle is a satisfying idle game where you grow your cash flow by merging money, unlocking higher bills, and expanding your earnings row by row. Start small, combine bills, and watch your money maker evolve into a fast-moving wealth machine. Every merge brings stronger value, every upgrade pushes your income further, and every tap helps speed up your path from basic cash to massive fortune.
