## How to play

Arrows keys or WASD keys to drive the vehicle Left mouse button to shoot C key to change tank view camera F key to get on off vehicle K key to teleport in tank Tab to toggle pause

## About

Tired of driving a car or a motorbike? Now you get the chance to drive a tank through the crowded city roads, or in the desert. You have two game maps with realistic graphics, you can drive whatever car you want by simply pressing “F”. You can also shoot.
