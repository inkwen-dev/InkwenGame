## About

Hey, animal lovers! Enter the enchanting world of Panda Lus Tree House, a super fun casual simulator that will warm your heart. In this game, you play as the caretaker of the adorable panda Lu. Your adventure begins by building a magnificent tree house, a cozy and vibrant home for your furry friend. First, you need to make sure Panda Lu is well taken care of. Give him all kinds of different entertainments on the tree house to keep him happy. You can also expand the tree house and add new rooms and areas for Panda Lu to explore. As you progress, take part in fun activities with Panda Lu.
