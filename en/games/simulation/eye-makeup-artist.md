## About

Eye Makeup Artist is a fun-filled simulation game. You are a brilliant makeup artist and you are getting ready to compete in the annual eye makeup competition. In this highly competitive competition, the most stunning and creative eye makeup will win first place. You are free to use your creativity and boldly use various color combinations. In your hands, even the most ordinary colors can look stunning. You can incorporate cute elements like popcorn and bees into your eye makeup design, and use bold colors. You are the best makeup artist. Can you win this game?
