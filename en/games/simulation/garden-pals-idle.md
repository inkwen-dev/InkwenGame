## How to play

• Tap plants in your garden to collect coins instantly.
• Buy seeds in the Seeds tab — rarer seeds grow more valuable plants.
• Seeds grow in the Greenhouse on a timer, then appear in your garden.
• Drag two identical plants together to merge them into a stronger one.
• Spend coins in the Shop on permanent income upgrades.
• Reach new levels to unlock more garden plots (up to 12).
• Prestige to earn Fame Stars and unlock new themed gardens with fresh plants.
• Collect every plant in a garden to earn a permanent Artifact bonus.
Your plants keep earning while you're offline — come back for a reward!
