## How to play

Drive through each dig site and push into buried terrain to drill automatically. The highlighted outline shows the exact next block and its remaining durability. Collect treasure until your cargo fills, then return to the golden unloading ring to bank it. Spend credits on vehicle and camp upgrades, recover artifacts, reconstruct discoveries, and complete all five regions. Finishing the final expedition unlocks a stronger veteran run with permanent bonuses and a remixed world.

Controls:
- Move: WASD, arrow keys, or the virtual joystick
- Dig: Drive into excavatable terrain
- Bomb: 1
- Scanner: 2
- Magnet: 3
- Mega Drill: 4 once unlocked
- Pause/settings: pause or gear buttons
- Reconstruction: drag pieces onto matching silhouettes, or tap a piece followed by its silhouette
- Every gameplay action is available through touch-screen controls

## About

Relic Rush 3D is a colorful top-down excavation adventure where players drive an upgradeable drilling vehicle, uncover treasure and artifacts, rebuild colossal discoveries, and master five distinct regions. Organic dig sites, dynamic biome weather, secret chambers, expedition upgrades, and stronger veteran rebirths make every run visibly progress.
