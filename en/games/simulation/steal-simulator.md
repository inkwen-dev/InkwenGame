## About

Steal Simulator is a fun casual game where you control a thief participating in a competition to collect items on the map. Move skillfully, choose targets wisely, and bring back as many loot as possible to surpass your opponents. Each level presents new challenges that require speed and smart tactics. The gameplay is simple but addictive, providing an entertaining, humorous, and engaging experience!
