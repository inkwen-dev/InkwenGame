## About

Experience the busy life of a mother and police officer in Police Mom Simulator! Manage your family responsibilities while protecting the city, solving criminal cases, chasing gangsters, and completing exciting police missions.

Start your day by preparing breakfast and dropping the kids at school before heading to the police station. Attend meetings with the police chief, investigate criminals, chase suspects, make arrests, and drive police cars through the city.

After completing your police duties, spend time with your virtual family, go shopping, enjoy outings with your husband and kids, and complete different household activities.

Features
* Play as a police officer and virtual mom
* Complete family and police missions
* Solve criminal cases and investigations
* Chase and arrest gangsters
* Drive police cars around the city
* Attend meetings at the police station
* Take care of your virtual family
* Go shopping and enjoy family outings
* Explore a detailed 3D city
* Smooth and easy controls

Balance your family life with your police career and keep the city safe in Police Mom Simulator!
