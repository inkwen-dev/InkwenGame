## How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys (joystick on mobile) and drag to look around. Press R or the Ride button to get on your pet, Space to jump or fly. Follow the green arrows to an egg and touch it to pick it up, then ride back to your ranch before the timer runs out. Eggs hatch in the nests: press E or tap Hatch. Place pets on your ranch to earn cash and spend it on food, radars, upgrades and rebirths.

## About

Master of Riding a Pet is a bright 3D pet-riding adventure. Explore a big open world on the back of your pet, find eggs hidden in meadows, forests, deserts, snowy peaks, a waterfall cave and a volcano, rush them home before they break and hatch 30 rideable pets, from a tiny snail to a mighty dragon. Faster pets reach rarer eggs. Grow your ranch, feed and age your pets, survive lightning storms that mutate them, buy radars and upgrades, and rebirth for bigger rewards.
