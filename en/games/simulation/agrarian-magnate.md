## How to play

The tutorial is built into the game and adapts to your progress.

Controls:
PC: left-click to select. On the farm, mission selection, and factory, hold left-click and drag to view the field.

Mobile: tap to select. On the farm, level selection, and factory, drag the field with your finger.

## About

Agrarian Magnate is a farming business simulator. The farm, processing, and store are all in your hands. Build your business empire!
Complete levels, complete tasks and earn money. Manage your farm, factory and store. Hire salespeople, upgrade your equipment, and take your business to the next level.Buy and earn daily bonuses. Bonuses are given for one round.
