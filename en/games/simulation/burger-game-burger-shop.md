## How to play

In Burger Shop IDLE, you'll take on the role of an aspiring burger entrepreneur with big dreams and an even bigger appetite for success. Your mission? To create a burger empire that will leave customers hungry for more!  

Start by opening your own small shop, serving customers and making them feel at home. But don't get too comfortable, because the burger business is a fast-paced and highly competitive business. As you progress, you'll face many challenges that will test your management skills. Expand your empire by opening new branches in different locations, each with its own unique set of customers and demands. Upgrade your kitchen equipment, discover secret recipes, hire more staff, test your management skills, and decorate your burger shop with eye-catching decorations to attract even more hungry customers.
