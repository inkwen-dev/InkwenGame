## About

Haunted House Idle is a fun 3D idle arcade management game where you build and expand your own haunted attraction. Welcome visitors, guide them through spooky experiences, earn cash, and unlock new haunted areas. Expand your attraction with creepy shadows, chasing clowns, a rotating bridge, dangerous lasers, slime monsters, photo areas, mask shops, and more. Upgrade your character, increase speed and capacity, hire helpers, automate attractions, complete tasks, and grow your earnings. Keep expanding your haunted house and turn it into the ultimate spooky entertainment empire!
