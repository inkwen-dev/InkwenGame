## How to play

For computer:
- Move: W, A, S, D
- Jump: Spacebar
- Break blocks: Hold down LMB
- Place blocks: RMB / F
- Inventory: E
- Pause: Tab
- Help: J
- Drop: Q (Shift+Q Drop full slot)

For mobile devices:
- Move: Left Joystick
- Jump: Arrow icon button
- Break blocks: Hold down the sword icon button.
- Stack blocks: Hold down the cross icon button.
- Inventory: Orange button in the middle of the screen.
- Pause: Purple button in the upper right corner of the screen

## About

Embark on a unique survival adventure on floating islands in a 3D world, where each island offers valuable resources. Explore vibrant landscapes, battle monsters to grow stronger, and trade with villagers for useful items. Build plantations and breed animals to ensure a steady supply of food and resources. The skies are full of islands, each one holding secrets and challenges. Survive, craft, battle, and thrive in this exciting world!
