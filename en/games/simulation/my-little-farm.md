## How to play

- Move using the on-screen joystick;
- Approach a tree to cut it down;
- Approach water to extract water;
- Accumulate resources to construct a building.

## About

- A casual arcade game with simple and intuitive controls;
- Gather resources to build a farm;
- Chop trees to get wood;
- Chop rocks to get stone;
- Dig a well to get water;
- Breed animals to collect fertilizer for your plants;
- Build a mill;
- Harvest wheat and process it into flour;
- Bake bread and hire helpers.
