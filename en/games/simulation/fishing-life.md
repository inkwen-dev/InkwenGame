## About

Fishing Life&mdash;a relaxing clicker game with 2D cartoon visuals. Choose your target fish, click the water to reel it in, and manage its energy. Prioritize upgrading Strength first. Skins enhance tap strength, outfits boost damage, and Keepnet nets extra prices. Assistants increase Income, Strength, Damage, and Price. Open chests and claim cash from charged drift bottles. Luck might grant you treasure chests&mdash;open them for valuable assistants!
