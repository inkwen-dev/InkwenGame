## How to play

Move with WASD or the joystick and look around with the mouse or by swiping. Check cameras, close doors, manage power and use found items. Survive until morning each night and find notes to unlock the endings.

## About

5 Nights at Tung Tung Hotel is a 3D survival horror. Work night shifts in a creepy hotel, watch the cameras, collect notes and items, uncover the hotel's secrets and survive five nights to reach one of the endings.
