## About

The Hustler is a fun Simulator game where you start with nothing and work your way to the very top!

You start poor and build your wealth. 

As you get faster, you can start your own cool businesses. You will have to rush around to stack supplies, keep long lines of happy customers moving, and collect big piles of cash.

The more you work, the bigger your empire grows! You can use your money to move faster, carry more items, and unlock awesome new shops.

Manage your shops, lead your workers and make the most profit. 

It is a non-stop race to see how much you can build. Do you have what it takes to be the best hustler in town?
