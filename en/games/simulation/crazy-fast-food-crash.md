## About

Test your speed and reflexes! In this arcade game, 6 different fast food items randomly fly upward from the bottom of the screen. The foods cannot be controlled until they reach the plate level. Once they do, you have only 1 second to drag and throw the correct food onto the matching plate. Match correctly, earn money! Each successful match rewards you with money. Use your earnings in the Upgrade section to increase your income, boost starting lives, and control game speed. Simple but challenging! As the game progresses, the pace increases and reflex demands rise. With short, fast-paced rounds and colorful visuals.
