## About

Little kids can sometimes be careless when it comes to personal hygiene. Baby Panda’s Bath Time is a free educational app designed to make young children, especially toddlers and pre-k kids, learn basic bathing and hygiene practices in a fun way. In Baby Panda’s Bath Time, Momo, our beloved baby bunny, and Kiki, the cute baby panda, are waiting for help. Let's make thick bath foam, fight germs, and wash it out in the bathtub!
