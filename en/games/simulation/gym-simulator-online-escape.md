## How to play

On the computer:

Space bar - jump
WASD - move the character
Left mouse button - swing
Right mouse button - rotate the camera

On a smartphone:
Left joystick - move the character
Tapping on the right side of the screen will rotate the camera
A separate button for jumping
Tapping on the screen anywhere - swing

Ready to be the one who breaks through the castle walls and enters the legends? Start your transformation right now!

Controls:
Desktop: WASD / arrows, space - jump, click to select option;  
Mobile: virtual joystick, virtual jump button, tap to select option  

Upgrades / Progression / Economy:
• Power Points: Earned through training. Can be spent on skins. The amount of power points affects the damage dealt with each hit when battling bosses.  
• Trophies: Earned through boss fights. Can be spent on pets. Required to advance to the next level.  

Core Gameplay & Story  
Q: What is the goal of the game?  
A: You find yourself trapped in a world where the most important thing is to enhance your strength through various training. Defeat all the bosses to gain the opportunity to move to the next level.  

Q: What grabs attention in the first few minutes?  
A: Simple controls, clear achievements, and the ability to buy different pets. The tutorial is presented in a gentle mentoring style, and there are no strict requirements on how to develop your character: build your own strategy by combining different types of training.  

Mechanics & Progression  
Q: What unlocks as you play?  
A: New difficulty levels, training loads, and pets unlock as you progress, allowing for faster advancement.  

Q: How can you level up faster?  
A: Choose combinations of training and pay close attention to the Wheel of Fortune, which allows you to quickly earn a large number of trophies or power points.  

Economy & Customization  
Q: What can you buy/improve?  
A: You can buy:  
- pets: regular or more exclusive  
- skins for character customization  
- VIP status, granting access to more skins and faster progress accumulation.  

Q: Are there skins/customization options?  
A: You can change your character's clothing and hairstyle, as well as add wings.  

Retention & Engagement  
Q: Are there daily tasks/wheel of fortune/achievements/tasks?  
A: The Wheel of Fortune: provides the opportunity to quickly earn a lot of power points or trophies.  

Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What does it include?  
A: Onboarding introduces the player to the types of training, guides them through the scenario of their first victories, and helps them quickly get oriented in the game.
