## How to play

Kingdom building screen.
- Click on the scaffolding to see how many more resources you need to collect to build a new building;
- when there are enough resources, a hammer icon will appear above the building. Click on it to build a new building and move on to building the next building.

Resource Gathering Screen.
- 3 stacks of hexes appear at the bottom, which you can pick up and place on the field;
- take a stack and place it on the field so that the top tiles overlap. The tiles will then flow over each other;
- when you collect a stack of 10 or more hexes of the same type, the stack disappears and you collect that resource;
- at the top of the game screen you can see the counter of hexes that you need to collect to finish the level.

## About

You are the 3rd son of the great king of a magical land. Your parents send you to a faraway land to establish a new kingdom and prove that you are the most capable of their children!

In the game you collect resource hexes. There are 6 resources in total: food, water, stone, wood, diamonds and metal. When you collect enough resources, you can build a new building. Unlock all the buildings, create a beautiful magical kingdom!
