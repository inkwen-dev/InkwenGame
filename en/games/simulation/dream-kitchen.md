## About

Dream Kitchen is a fun and fast-paced restaurant simulation game inspired by shawarma-style cooking gameplay. Take customer orders, cook the right food, and serve every dish on time to keep customers happy. Complete levels with 3-star ratings, earn coins, upgrade your kitchen equipment, and unlock new recipes. Explore multiple themed chapters, including a Pancake Shop, Street Stall, Fast Food Restaurant, and Western Restaurant, each with unique dishes and challenges!
