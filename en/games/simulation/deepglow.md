## How to play

Click or tap a resource to harvest it before the timer runs out. Between phases, spend your resources on the upgrade tree to get stronger and unlock new biomes. Keep hunger and thirst up: hungry = weak hits, thirsty = half the materials.
Desktop: mouse, or Arrows/WASD + Space to move and harvest; P or Esc to pause.
Mobile: tap resources and upgrades; II button to pause.

## About

Deepglow is a cozy pixel-art clicker in a bioluminescent night world. Each phase is a race against the timer: tap glowing trees, fruit bushes, springs, rocks and crystals to harvest them, then spend what you gathered on a branching upgrade tree with sharper tools, bigger yields and longer days. Watch your hunger and thirst, travel through six biomes and reach the heart of the Deepglow. No game over: every phase moves you forward.
