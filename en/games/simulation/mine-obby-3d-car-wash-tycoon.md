💻 PC:
WASD - character movement 🏃
Right mouse button - rotate camera 🔄
Left mouse button - interact ⛏️
Space - jump 🦘

📱 Mobile devices:
Joystick - character movement 🏃
Touch screen - rotate camera 🔄
Action button - interact ⛏️
Jump button - jump 🦘

## About

🚗 “Mine Obby 3D: Car Wash Tycoon” lets you build a small car wash and turn it into a huge automotive complex!

💦 Develop your car wash
Unlock foam, brushes, rinsing, drying, polishing, and other services.

💰 Earn money
Serve cars, wait for them to reach the cash register, and collect your earnings.

🏗️ Expand your business
Build a gas station, café, and repair shop. Upgrade every facility to increase your income.

🚦 Manage the traffic
Add more cars, improve their speed, and unlock gold, emerald, and diamond roads.

🐾 Collect pets
Equip pets to increase your character’s running speed.

⭐ Unlock every upgrade and become the ultimate car wash tycoon!
