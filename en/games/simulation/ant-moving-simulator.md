## How to play

Click or tap a front numbered box to move it into an empty slot. Your ants will automatically collect reachable blocks of the same color.

Plan ahead and keep room for your next move! Linked boxes move together, so you need enough empty slots for the whole group.

Clear every block to beat the level. Stuck? Try a power-up! Use the on-screen buttons to activate power-ups or pause the game.
