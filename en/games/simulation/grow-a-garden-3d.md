## How to play

Desktop Controls:
[ WASD ] - Move
Hold Right/Left Mouse Button and Rotate the Mouse to look Arround
[ LMB (Left Mouse Button) ] - Plant Seed 
[ Space ] - Jump
[ E ] - Intreact [Buy/Sell/Collect]
[ Tab ] - Inventory

Mobile Controls:
JoyStick to Move around and Drag On the Right side to look arround
Buttons for Picking up item and Planting also for Other such as Jumping

Thre game has an In-game Tutorial which Guide the player.

## About

Welcome to Grow a Garden, a fun and peaceful game where you plant seeds and grow magical trees! 🌳
Explore different seasons, customize your character, and watch your garden grow bigger every day.
Every seed is special and every garden is unique!

Game Features 🌟
🌱 25+ Seeds – Plant over 25 different seeds, and each one grows into its own unique tree
🌳 Giant Trees – Some trees have a rare chance to grow into huge GIANT trees 😲
🎨 Player Skins – Choose and change your player skin to match your style
🌍 Easy & Relaxing Gameplay – Simple controls that are fun and calm for all players
✨ Growing Rewards – Earn rewards as your garden grows bigger and better
🪴 Creative Gardening – Design and grow your garden the way you like

Game Seasons 🍂
❄️ Winter – Snow covers your garden and gives trees a cold, magical look
🌪️ Sand Storm – Strong winds and sand make the world feel wild and exciting
🌧️ Rainy Day – Rain helps plants grow faster and makes everything fresh
🌙 Night – The garden becomes calm and peaceful under the moonlight
