## About

You are invited to join Hole And Fill Collect Master. Here, you will play as a magical &ldquo;hole&rdquo;, by devouring and collecting all kinds of food to fill yourselves and become more and more powerful. The goal is to collect as much food in the scene as possible, such as cakes, meat, donuts, etc. The bigger the item you eat, the faster you upgrade.
