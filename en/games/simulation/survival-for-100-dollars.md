## About

Congratulations, you're the owner of a fortune of 100 dollars! Unfortunately, that's all you have. Welcome to a survival simulator set in harsh reality.

In this game, you'll:
Masterfully manage your budget: is "Doshirak" a delicacy or an everyday staple?

Look for work in the urban-type settlement: from unloading train cars to carrying out dubious errands.

Monitor your hunger, health, and mood (it's hard to be happy when your pocket is empty).

Prove to everyone that you're an economic genius, capable of surviving where others give up. Your empire starts with a hundred-ruble note!
