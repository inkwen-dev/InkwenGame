## How to play

Stay on the log and knock your rival into the water. Keyboard: W steps away from you and S steps toward you (the arrow keys work too); 1, 2 and 3 fire the skills on your hotbar; Esc or P pauses. Touch: hold the up and down buttons to step, tap a skill on the hotbar to use it. Step the same way the log is rolling to keep your footing, step against it to slow it and turn it over - but shoving costs balance. Empty your rival's balance meter before they empty yours, then pick a skill and take on the next opponent.

## About

Log Splash is a fast top-down log rolling duel. Two lumberjacks share one floating log, and the first to lose their balance goes in the water. Your footwork spins the log: step with it to keep up, step against it to shove your rival off. Win a duel, draft one of three skills, and face a harder opponent on a new river - ten hand-made rivals with their own tricks, then an endless river that keeps getting meaner. Low-poly forests, farm shores, cheering crowds and very big splashes.
