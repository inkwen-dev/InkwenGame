## About

Enjoy an exciting school life as an anime girl in Anime Girl High School Simulator! Attend classes, meet friends, complete school missions, participate in sports activities, and explore a beautiful Sakura-themed high school environment.
Customize your schoolgirl with different outfits, hairstyles, and hair colors. Attend classes, take surprise quizzes, visit the cafeteria and library, and complete challenging school activities.

Enjoy fun anime love-story missions with your classmates, develop a crush, and experience memorable moments with your friends. You can also drive a sports car and explore areas outside the school.
