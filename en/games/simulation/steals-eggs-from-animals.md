## How to play

Run into an animal's zone, steal its egg, and escape back to the safe zone.

💻 PC Controls:

WASD – Move
Hold Right Mouse Button – Rotate camera
Space – Jump
Hold E – Interact

📱 Mobile Controls:

Joystick – Move
Swipe on the right side of the screen – Rotate camera
On-screen buttons – Additional actions
