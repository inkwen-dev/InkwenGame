## About

Sheep Saga is a heart-warming farm simulation game where players care for a flock of adorable sheep, ensuring they are well-fed and happy. Tend to your sheep diligently to harvest valuable products like milk and wool, which can be sold at the market for profit. As you expand, hire assistants to automate tasks, letting you focus on customization and growth. With charming graphics and relaxing gameplay, Sheep Saga offers a serene, engaging experience in building and managing your dream sheep farm.
