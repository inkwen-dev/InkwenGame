## How to play

Goal:
Collect cars scattered across the floor, identify their category, and place them on the correct shelves. Earn money and purchase upgrades to clean up the store even faster!

PC Controls:
• WASD — Character movement
• Mouse — Look around / Camera control
• Left Mouse Button (LMB) — Pick up or place car
• Right Mouse Button (RMB) — Drop car
• Shift — Sprint
• Ctrl — Crouch
• Space — Jump
• P — Pause

Mobile & Tablet Controls:
• Left joystick — Move
• Right side swipe — Rotate camera
• On-screen buttons — Pick up / Place car

## About

Chaos in the toy store: thousands of toy cars are scattered across the floor! Sports cars, muscle cars, fire trucks, and heavy machinery are all mixed up. Your goal is to bring back order and place every car on its proper shelf.

No timers, no rush, no penalties - just a relaxing, satisfying organizing experience!

Key Features:
• Thousands of toy cars: discover sport cars, classics, and rare collectibles.
• Store transformation: watch empty shelves turn into neatly organized displays.
• Earn rewards: get coins for every correctly sorted car.
• Upgrades: carry more cars at once, move faster, and highlight matching shelves.
• Complete collections: fill every display 100%!
• Cozy anti-stress gameplay with fun physics.
