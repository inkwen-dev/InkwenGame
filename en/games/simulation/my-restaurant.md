## About

Build, design, and manage your dream restaurant in My Restaurant! Start with a small dining space and transform it into a thriving restaurant by purchasing furniture, cooking stoves, and decorations to customise your restaurant. Prepare and serve delicious food, keep customers happy, and earn cash to unlock upgrades and new menu items. Expand and improve your restaurant as you progress through exciting levels and create the ultimate dining experience!
