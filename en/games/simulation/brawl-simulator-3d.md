## How to play

Mobile devices:
- To move players, use the left blue joystick
- To aim, use the right red joystick
- To shoot, release the right red joystick

Desktop devices:
- To move players, use the arrow keys or WASD
- To aim, move the mouse
- To shoot, press the left mouse button
