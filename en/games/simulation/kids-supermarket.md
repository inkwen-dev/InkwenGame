## About

Welcome to Kids Supermarket, a casual supermarket simulation game for kids. Have you ever gone shopping alone? Feel confused about finding goods among a large number of products? Join Kids Supermarket to elevate your observation and categorization skills. Our diverse and clearly aligned products will make you feel like shopping in a real-life supermarket. Pick up your shopping basket and start your journey in the supermarket.
