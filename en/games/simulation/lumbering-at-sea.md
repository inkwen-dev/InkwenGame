## How to play

Your main goal is to run forward along the path, time axe swings to chop trees and automatically collect wood (guided by on-screen indicators or proximity), gather coins for upgrades, and reach the end gate or next island before falling off or losing progress. Build your raft with collected lumber to advance—survive longer runs for high scores by upgrading speed, cutting efficiency, and critical hits between attempts!

PC Controls:
Mouse: Click/tap (or hold/repeatedly click) to swing the axe when near trees or indicators appear.

Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap the screen to swing the axe and chop trees when close or prompted (follow indicators for timing).

## About

Set sail on an endless ocean adventure in Lumbering At Sea, a captivating 3D stickman runner with lumberjack action! Control a nimble stickman lumberjack dashing across glowing paths on tropical islands, chopping trees to harvest wood planks while racing toward distant checkpoints. The core idea is resource timing and raft-building progression—perfectly time your axe swings to collect enough lumber to construct a raft that propels you to the next island before falling into the sea or running out of momentum.
