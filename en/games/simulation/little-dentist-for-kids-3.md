## About

Six kids are sitting in the chair and their teeth are a disaster. Grab the right tool and get to work: drill out the black cavities, flick off the green gunk, buff the yellow tartar with little circles, scrub the orange stains left and right. Pick the wrong tool and the poor kid jumps. Then rinse, dry, next patient. Fix all six and Endless mode opens up, where the clock never stops and only fast hands keep you going.
