## How to play

Move: WASD or arrow keys. On touch screens (or with the mouse), drag anywhere to use the on-screen joystick.

Carry: walk up to a pallet or machine to pick items up, and walk up to the next machine or shelf to drop them off. No buttons needed.

Build: stand on a price pad to spend cash on it. You can pay a bit at a time, and the pad keeps what you've paid.

Serve: stand at the register to serve customers, then collect the cash they leave.

Wardrobe: tap Wardrobe on the home screen or in the pause menu, then tap a piece to put it on your chef.

Pause: Esc or P.

Goal: follow the arrow through the short tutorial, then keep unlocking machines, workers and new rooms until the whole restaurant runs on its own. Then fly to the next country on the World Tour.

## About

Busy Bites Tycoon is a cozy restaurant tycoon game. You start with one empty counter and a line of hungry customers. Carry ingredients, cook, serve, collect the cash, then spend it on machines, conveyor belts and staff until the whole restaurant runs itself.

WORLD TOUR: FOUR RESTAURANTS, FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

USA, Pizza Parlor: bake pizzas, then open a Back Kitchen for hot wings, a Dessert Lounge for milkshakes and a Delivery Dock with scooter drivers.
Mexico, Taquería: grill carne asada and press tortillas for tacos. Serve elote and horchata, open the Paletería, and fill the Patio with churros and hot chocolate.
Türkiye, the square: bake simit at the Simit Büfe, pour çay for guests in the tea garden and clear their glasses, roll döner dürüm and sell baklava in the Çarşı.
India, Highway Dhaba: brew chai six cups at a time, fry samosas and serve thalis in the dining shed. Buses stop for a meal break: serve every rider before the horn for a FULL BUS bonus.

Finish a restaurant to fly to the next one. Every restaurant stays yours forever, and each finished one earns a passport stamp that raises your prices everywhere.

FEATURES
