## How to play

Computer:
Hold Left Click on the water to aim, release to cast.
Click or Space when the float really goes under, then hold to reel in.
In a fight, keep the tension out of the red and hold A/D to lean the rod away from the fish.
Row with W/S and A/D (or the arrow keys), or Right Click the water to row there.
E opens the dock shop at the dock. H shows all the other keys.

Phone:
Left stick: push to row and turn.
Right stick: hold to aim, let go to cast.
Tap the right stick when the float goes under, then hold it to reel in. Ease off when the line turns red.

## About

Row out onto a dark lake with a lantern, a rod and the feeling that something below is watching.

Night Lake is a calm, eerie fishing game. Cast your float, watch it bob in the lantern light and strike when it really goes under. Every fish fights back: keep the line out of the red, lean the rod against the pull and bring it to the boat.

Sell your catch at the dock, buy better baits and tackle, finish the night's quests and row on to new waters: a pine lake, a jungle bayou, a frozen lake, a flooded quarry, a ruined mill pond, a misty marsh, a great basin fed by every lake, each with its own fish, sounds and night.

But the lake is not empty. The lantern stutters, the crickets fall silent and a shadow longer than your boat glides underneath. Monsters need their own bait and strong gear, and they can tow your boat across the water. Every lake hides a legend that only rises for the right bait after midnight.

- Float fishing, spinning lures and feeder fishing
- Trophy and Unique sizes to chase in your Field Guide
- Lake monsters and a legend in every lake
- Co-op: fish the same lake with a friend, or join a public lake
- Leaderboard for the biggest catch
- A second rod, a pond that earns while you are away, daily gifts
- In English, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, German or French

Your progress is saved automatically.

Made by a solo developer; art and sounds were created with the help of AI tools and edited by hand.
