## How to play

1. Tap an item on a workbench to repair it and earn coins. Tap quickly to fill the rush meter for double income.
2. Press Upgrade to raise a bench's level and earnings. At levels 25, 50, 100, 200 and beyond the bench works twice as fast.
3. Hire a helper so the bench works on its own, including while you are offline.
4. Open new benches for more valuable items, and buy Tools for permanent multipliers.
5. Complete Goals for bonus coins. Use Buy x1 / x10 / MAX to upgrade in bulk.
6. Open the Map to move to a new district when you have earned enough: income is multiplied and you keep a permanent perk.
7. When a collector arrives, repair the item by hand: pick a tool and drag over the object to wash, scrape, glue and polish.

Controls: mouse or touch. Progress saves automatically. Language: English or Russian in Settings.

## About

Restore Lab is a cozy idle repair tycoon. Start with one workbench and a pair of dirty sneakers, then grow a whole restoration business! Tap items to repair them faster, upgrade your benches and hire helpers who keep the shop running even while you are away. Unlock eight kinds of treasures: sneakers, mugs, teddy bears, pocket watches, vintage cameras, toy trains, music boxes and lanterns. Reach level milestones to double a bench's speed, buy one-time tools for big income boosts, complete goals for bonus coins and fill the rush meter for double income. When business is booming, open a branch in a new district: Market Square, Old Town, the Harbor and the Antique Quarter. Each one multiplies your income for good and adds a permanent perk. Visiting collectors bring special orders you can repair by hand, washing, scraping, gluing and polishing for a big reward. Offline earnings, a daily gift and a calm music-box soundtrack. Plays on phone and desktop in English and Russian.
