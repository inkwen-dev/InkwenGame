## How to play

Build a scene, play with physics and complete challenges to earn stars.

- Place items: pick a toy on the shelf (Avocado, Crate, Rock, Plank, Bomb, Barrel, Anvil, Balloon, Trampoline, Spikes, Fan, Glass and more), then tap or click the field.
- Grab: press on an object, drag and release to throw it. A quick tap on an avocado opens its moves.
- Moves: pick Moves and tap an avocado to make it run, dance, backflip, jump, flex, dab, floss or kick, or tap "Everyone dance".
- Wand: pick a spell in the row above the shelf (Grow, Shrink, Float, Bubble, Revive, Disco), then tap an avocado or a toy. Tap a bubble to pop it.
- Blast: tap the field to set off an explosion. Pistol: press, drag toward the target and release. Zap: tap to call down lightning.
- Freeze: tap an object to lock it in place, tap again to release it. Delete: tap an object to remove it.
- Nail and Rope: tap an object, then another object or the background.
- Challenges and Daily: tap the star panel at the top left for goals, the daily gift, quests and skins.
- Modes: pick a map, play missions (limited moves, up to three stars each) or Rampage (60 seconds of damage for the leaderboard).
- Premium toys unlock with stars, pits or a short optional ad. Exclusive toys unlock forever after one optional ad.
- Top bar: pause, slow motion, gravity, clear, reset, Modes, sound, language, how to play.
- Keyboard: Space pause, S slow motion, G gravity, C clear, R reset, T Rampage, D delete, M avocado, Esc grab.

## About

Avocado Yard: Ragdoll Sandbox is a free ragdoll physics game where floppy avocados are your toys. Drop them into the yard, make them dance, run, jump and backflip, stack crates and planks, nail things together, tie ropes, set up trampolines, fans, spikes, glass panes, a spinning saw and a cannon, then grab, throw, zap with lightning and blow everything up. Every hit is real-time physics: ragdolls wobble and go flying, red barrels set off chain reactions, balloons float away and a black hole swallows the whole scene.

New: the Magic Wand with six spells. Grow an avocado into a giant king or shrink it to pocket size, make it float, trap it in a soap bubble, bring a knocked-out avocado back to life, or drop a disco ball and watch everyone dance.

Take on 42 challenges, each worth a star; stars unlock premium toys like the homing rocket, the black hole, the mega avocado and the mega bomb. Six maps change the rules: the Yard, night Rooftops, a Construction site with a wrecking ball, a Volcano with a lava floor, a slippery Frozen lake and the low-gravity Moon. Ten hand-made missions give you a clear goal and a handful of moves, and Rampage is a 60-second score attack with a global leaderboard.

Come back every day for a daily gift that grows over a 7-day streak and three fresh daily quests. Spend the pits you earn on skins from Ninja and Zombie to Robot and King, or on new maps and toys. Exclusive toys such as the jetpack, car, turret, Tesla coil, tornado, minigun and portals unlock forever with one short optional ad. Your yard is saved automatically.

No download needed: play in the browser on PC, tablet and phone, in English, Spanish, German or Russian, with touch controls, keyboard shortcuts and a short guided tutorial.
