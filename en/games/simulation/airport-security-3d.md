## About

Airport Security 3D is an airport security check simulation game with 3D cartoon models. Your mission is to arrest criminals with fake IDs and weapons while approving civilians. On the counter, you need to check the passengers name and corresponding ID card. At the security checkpoint, you are required to check their baggage and find the criminals who take weapons. Finally, you can question the criminals.
