## How to play

As the cashier, your job involves scanning items, processing payments, and ensuring accurate transactions. Whether it's cash or credit, each customer brings a unique challenge to your checkout line. Pay close attention as you scan - mistakes like charging for the wrong item or giving incorrect change can lead to unhappy customers and affect your earnings.
