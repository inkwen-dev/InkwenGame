## About

Welcome to Brainrot Cleaning, the weirdly satisfying cleaning simulation that turns chaos into calm. In this fun and quirky casual game, your job is to scrub away the mess caused by the wild world of brainrot characters and their hilarious antics! In the Cleaning Italian Brainrot or the Underwater mode, you will finish various cleaning tasks.
