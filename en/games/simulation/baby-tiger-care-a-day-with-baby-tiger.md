## About

In people's impression, tigers are very ferocious animals, but in the Baby Tiger Care game, you will have the opportunity to take care of a very cute young tiger. Your task is to spend a nice day with the little tiger Lily. Including getting up with her, dressing Lily, feeding her, you can even make handicrafts with Lily, because the tiger is very smart and docile. Finally, don't forget to bathe Lily. Are you ready?
