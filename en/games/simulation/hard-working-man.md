## How to play

Click on different resources to gather materials and use them to build houses, craft tools and create useful parts. Every action is based on clicking, so collect resources, upgrade buildings and progress step by step to grow your settlement

## About

Hard Working Man is an offline farming and survival simulator. Start with nothing, rebuild your farm, gather resources, and craft tools to create your dream homestead. Harvest berries and mushrooms, grow vegetables and fruit trees, go fishing, cook food, and expand your farm. Sell crops at the bazaar, buy seeds and tools, and build a workshop, forge, and even your own car from junk. Enjoy realistic survival mechanics, crafting, building, and resource management. Discover secrets, upgrade your farm, and prove you can build an empire from nothing! Perfect for fans of farming, crafting, and survival games.
