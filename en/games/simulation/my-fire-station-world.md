## About

Have you ever wondered how the firefighter rescues and helps others in emergencies? How do they deal with dangerous fires? This role-play takes you to explore the world of firefighters and learn about their daily work. Our game has 6 scenarios about the life and work of firefighters for you to choose from. You can drag and drop to place the characters as you like, place props, and change the firefighting costumes.
