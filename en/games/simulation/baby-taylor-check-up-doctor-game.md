## About

Taylor will go to check up! Kids are growing up every day. They need regular physical examination to make sure they are healthy. Go to the doctor’s office with Baby Taylor and solve fun mini-games that teach kids all about the human body. Check her height and weight, test her version, play with cool doctor tools such a stethoscope, and lots more! You can learn about doctor visits in a fun way! Have a good day with Baby Taylor!
