## About

Ice Cream Vendor is a fast-paced browser game that puts you behind the counter of a busy ice cream stand. Pick the right cone, layer on the flavors, and add chocolates and toppings to match each customer&rsquo;s order. Every level raises the stakes with new combinations and quicker customers, so only the sharpest vendor can keep up. How many orders can you get right before the rush melts you down?
