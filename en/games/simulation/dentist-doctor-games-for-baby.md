## About

This is a dentist simulation game for all ages. In the hospital, animals are queuing up for dental treatment. You need to help the animals to treat their teeth. You need to use a hook to take out the leftover food from the sharp teeth spines and then brush the teeth with a toothbrush and toothpaste.
