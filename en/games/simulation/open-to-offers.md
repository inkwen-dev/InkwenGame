## How to play

1. Open a box in the “Cases” tab — the roulette shows what's inside.
2. Put your find up for sale: “Storage” → item → price → “List”. A price near the market sells faster, above the market pays more, but buyers will haggle.
3. Answer buyers in “Chats” with the buttons below: accept, name your price or decline. Sometimes saying no makes a buyer raise the offer!
4. Never follow links from “buyers” — they are scammers. Block them and get a vigilance bonus.
5. Invest your revenue in the “Upgrades” tab and complete collections for price bonuses.

Short on money? Tap the laptop on the home screen. Controls: mouse or touch.

## About

Become the best flipper in town! Buy attic boxes, lost parcels and port containers — anything can be inside, from an unpaired sock to an imperial egg.

Put your finds up for sale and haggle with buyers in chats. Everyone has their own character: a granny on a pension, a kid with a piggy bank, an IT guy, a tourist, a fashionista, a gardener — 20 types in total. Some are generous, some are stingy, some are in a hurry. Name your price, push the deal, deliver it yourself — and don't fall for scammers with a “payment link”!

• 8 boxes and 48 items of different rarity — from junk to mythic
• Lively negotiations: counteroffers, “final offer” bluffs, “knock off a bit for a taxi”
• Collections with price bonuses
• Upgrade your storage, listings, charisma and auto-reply
• Side hustle on the laptop, daily bonuses and a leaderboard
