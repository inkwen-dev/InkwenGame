## About

Build a Keycap Pyramid is a satisfying 3D building game where every keycap counts. Scoop up keycaps by the hundreds, carry a wobbling tower of them on your head and watch your pyramid rise layer by layer. Every finished pyramid is saved to your plot with your name on top, and the next one is even bigger! Chain combos and hunt for gold (x10) and rainbow (x50) keycaps. Get stronger on benches and treadmills: type the letter that pops up for a perfect rep. Hatch 15 Keycap Pets that boost your keys, cash and training. Defend your pyramid from Glitch raiders by throwing keycaps, and type the word to break the shield of their boss. Make it yours with 5 pyramid shapes, 10 color palettes and your name in keycaps on top. Share a live world with up to 3 other builders and cheer with emotes. Builder bots keep earning while you are away, and there are daily challenges and a 7-day reward calendar.
