## About

Little Hippo Care is an educational game for children. Its very interactive and you wont be able to put it down. In this game, you will help the mother hippo take care of her baby hippopotamus. For example, help the little hippo bathe, take it to the park for a picnic, feed it, coax it to sleep, make accessories for it, etc. In addition, we also provide you with many carefully designed small toys, so that both you and the little hippo can experience the fun of childlike innocence.
