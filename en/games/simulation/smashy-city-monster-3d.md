## About

A casual adventure game, where you play as a crazy, angry, and huge monster similar to a Godzilla in the city. Your goal is to cause destruction, chaos, demolish, ram, smash all interactions, and also annoy citizens. Gain points and experience, you get more rewards and you get faster speed. The town environment contains many obstacles, such as explosive cars, pixel fences, buckets, block lawns, street lights, street fire hydrants, cube garbage cans, and many others.
