## How to play

Controls are simple and easy to pick up in seconds.

Walk up to a tree and hold the action button — your character will start chopping. Once the tree falls, chop the trunk into logs and carry them to the warehouse. Each delivery earns you coins.

Spend those coins on upgrades: increase how many logs you can carry, chop faster, and expand warehouse capacity. Purchase a tablet to unlock the orders system — buyers will send time-limited deals offering coins in exchange for log deliveries.

Hire lumberjacks and collectors to automate your operation. Lumberjacks chop trees on their own while collectors pick up loose logs — but each lumberjack needs a house first. Build one through the buildings menu and place it anywhere on the map.

Keep an eye on your orders tablet and sell logs before deals expire. Unlock new trade partners for bigger and more profitable contracts. Develop your base, grow your team and see how far your lumber empire can go.

## About

Lumberjack Simulator 3D is a relaxing economic game where you build a logging operation from the ground up. Start with nothing but an axe and grow your humble camp into a thriving wooden empire.

Chop trees, collect logs and haul them to the warehouse. Spend your coins on upgrades, unlock a tablet to manage incoming orders, and hire helpers — lumberjacks who chop for you and collectors who gather logs automatically.

Build houses for your workers and expand your base by placing buildings wherever you like. Accept deals from buyers, negotiate with business partners, and keep your warehouse stocked — the more logs you deliver, the more you earn.

A dynamic day-night cycle, a living world and full control over your progress make every session feel fresh. Adjust music, controls and language to your liking and get to work.
