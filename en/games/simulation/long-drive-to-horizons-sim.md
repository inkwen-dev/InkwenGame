## About

Long Drive to Horizons Sim throws you into an epic desert survival adventure! This open-world simulator blends the freedom of a sandbox with high-octane racing and intense shooter action. Strap in as you pilot a lone vehicle across endless, unforgiving dunes.

🚗 Build & Customize
Gather parts and resources to assemble your dream ride. Mix and match bodies, engines, and wheels to craft a unique off-roader. Deep repair mechanics mean every bolt and circuit counts.

🔧 Maintenance & Upgrades
Keep your car battle-ready: refuel, swap out worn components, and unlock performance boosts. Hone your mechanic skills to tackle each rugged challenge.

🔍 Scavenge & Loot
Roam abandoned camps, crashed caravans, and derelict garages for vital supplies. Scavenge weapon parts, ammunition, and crafting materials in a richly detailed sandbox.

🏁 Dune Racing
Race across dozens of kilometers of treacherous desert tracks. Balance fuel, speed, and durability as you outpace rivals in blistering competitions.
