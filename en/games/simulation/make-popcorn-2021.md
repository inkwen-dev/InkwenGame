## About

Don’t be left holding the Popcorn when it Pops! Popcorn Poppin’ is a fast and fun game where players must pass the popcorn bucket before the popcorn pops. While the pop-up timer counts down, players follow the instructions from the dice and pass the popcorn as quickly as they can, trying not to be the one holding the bucket when the popcorn pops.
