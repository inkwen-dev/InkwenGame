## How to play

Left mouse button: select buildings, heroes, equipment, and menu options.
Click and drag in the village or character previews: rotate the view.
Mouse wheel in the village or character previews: zoom in or out.
Before battle: drag hero portraits onto the 3x3 grid, or click a portrait and then a square.
During battle: use the on-screen buttons to pause, resume, or change battle speed.
Esc: close menus or return from a building to the village.

Battles are automatic. You prepare and position your heroes before combat; you do not move or attack manually.

## About

The Last Company is a 3D fantasy strategy RPG where you recruit heroes, build your party, and see how long your company can survive. Prepare in the village, choose your next battle, and watch your decisions unfold in automatic combat. Gold is scarce, enemies grow more dangerous, and fallen heroes are gone for good.
