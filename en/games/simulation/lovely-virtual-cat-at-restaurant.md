## About

Lovely Virtual Cat At Restaurant is a very unique cooking game. Lovely cat opened a restaurant and it's very popular. Many animals are here to try some of the dishes in his restaurant. Come and help lovely cat manage his restaurant. Picking up the orders and preparing dishes for the animals are your basic works. You will also find some very interesting things in his restaurant. For an example, you can feed the lobster and watch it grow bigger. You can even change the decorations on the tables according to the vibe. You can let the animals make cute sounds by touching them. There are many other funs in this game. Come and enjoy it!
