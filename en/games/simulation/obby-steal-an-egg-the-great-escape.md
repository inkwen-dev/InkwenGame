## How to play

Steal, hatch, upgrade.

Steal eggs: approach NPC pets and other players, press the steal button. The higher your speed, the more stealthy your theft.
Hatch pets: bring an egg to your base and activate the incubator. The pet will start earning coins automatically.
Train on the treadmill: run on it to gain speed. Speed helps you steal faster and escape from thieves.
Upgrade your base and treadmill: spend coins on upgrades — they speed up training and increase pet income.

Controls:
WASD — movement,
mouse — camera control,
E — steal egg, activate incubator.
On mobile — joystick and on-screen buttons.

Tips:
Rare eggs glow or look unusual — they give the most valuable pets.
Watch other players: if you see a slow one with an egg — take it.
Invest in the treadmill first — speed pays off fastest.
