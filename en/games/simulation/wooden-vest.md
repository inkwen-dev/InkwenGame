## How to play

Move with WASD (on a phone, the on-screen joystick), run with Shift and jump with Space. Turn the camera by dragging with the mouse or a finger, or with the arrow keys; the mouse wheel zooms, and ' or Q locks the mouse to the camera. Walk up to a station and press E (or tap its button) to use it. Spin the roulette, buy an axe, put it on a stump, pick up the wood from the crate, throw it into the chipper and step on the green pad to collect your money. Keys 1-9 pick an item from the hotbar, Tab opens the backpack, M mutes the sound and Esc opens the options.

## About

Wooden Vest is a colorful blocky lumber tycoon. Spin the Axe Roulette for rare axes, place them on stumps and watch them chop wood on their own. Carry the wood to the chipper, turn it into money at the sawmill and reinvest in faster axes, better stumps, new floors in the sky and a helper who works for you. Open wood chests, combine stumps, fuse four axes into a rarer one and enchant them with golden, frozen, toxic or void power. Your island keeps earning even while you are away.
