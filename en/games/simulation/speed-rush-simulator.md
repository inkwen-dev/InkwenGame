## How to play

PC:
• WASD or arrow keys — move.
• Space — jump.
• E — interact with a training station or open an egg.
• R — open three eggs by watching a rewarded ad.
• Hold the mouse button and drag — rotate the camera.
• Mouse wheel — zoom.
• Shift — capture or release the mouse cursor.
• Esc — release the cursor.

During races, running is automatic. Use A/D or the left/right arrows to steer, and Space to jump.

Mobile:
• Play in landscape mode.
• Use the left joystick to move.
• Tap the right jump button while holding the joystick to run and jump together.
• Swipe on the game world to rotate the camera.
• Tap the on-screen buttons to interact and open menus.

Use the fullscreen button to enter fullscreen mode.

## About

Train your speed, collect trophies, and hatch pets in a colorful 3D running adventure! Race through forests, Pumpkin Valley, and castle-themed areas. Jump over obstacles and reach checkpoints to earn rewards.

Build your pet team to boost your training, complete rebirth challenges, and unlock stronger training stations. Customize your clothes and hairstyle, and collect Halloween pets: a Vampire, a Zombie, and a Pumpkin!
