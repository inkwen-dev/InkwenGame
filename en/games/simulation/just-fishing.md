## About

Just Fishing is a arcade game. Just shoot to fish and get more money to buy good boat and fork. You can fish for anything if you have a white sail, wind and a spear. Old man's reckless voyage to the end of the ocean. With personal combination of fish ability and spearing skills, catch the fish together with the old man!
