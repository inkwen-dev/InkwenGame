## About

Food Conveyor is an exciting restaurant management simulation game where you become a chef and operate a professional food service line. Prepare dishes, arrange food on the conveyor belt, and serve customers quickly to earn profits. Upgrade your restaurant, unlock new dishes, and optimize the process to develop your own culinary empire. The gameplay is fun, addictive, and extremely relaxing!
