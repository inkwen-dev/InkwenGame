## How to play

Desktop
WASD = Player Movement
Mouse = Camera Move
Mouse Left Click = Action
Left Shift = Sprint
Ctrl = Settings
N = Skip Level
P = Pause
I = Info

Mobile
Joystick to move player
Drag screen area to rotate camera

## About

Experience the journey of motherhood and family life in Pregnant Mom Simulator Family Life! Play as a virtual mom and manage daily household activities, family responsibilities, pregnancy tasks, and baby care missions. Complete exciting levels and experience different stages of family life through realistic and engaging gameplay.
Choose from three unique modes: Pregnancy, Baby Care, and Life Without Him. In Pregnancy Mode, experience the daily routine of a pregnant mom, prepare meals, manage household tasks, attend hospital checkups, and complete pregnancy-related missions. In Baby Care Mode, take care of your newborn, complete baby care activities, manage daily routines, and keep your little one happy and comfortable. In Life Without Him Mode, take on new challenges and experience the responsibilities of managing family life independently.
Features

3 exciting modes: Pregnancy, Baby Care, and Life Without Him.
