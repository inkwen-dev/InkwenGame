## How to play

Move the mouse to guide your equipped tool around the garden. Press Tab or click the Shop button to open the shop. Use the mouse to purchase upgrades and select tools. Click the X button to close the shop.

## About

Mowmentum is a relaxing idle lawn-mowing game. Guide your tool across an overgrown garden, cut grass and plants, earn money, and purchase upgrades. Unlock stronger hand tools, automated mowers, new zones, seeds, beetles, and more as you transform the garden and grow your collection.
