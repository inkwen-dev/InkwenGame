## How to play

W / Arrow Up → Move Front
S / Arrow Down → Move Back 
A / Arrow Left → Move Left
D / Arrow Right → Move Right

- To click any button use mouse

## About

Navigate busy streets, pick up passengers and drop them off safely at their destinations. Every ride is a new challenge that will test your driving skills and city navigation abilities. Get powerful taxis and complete exciting missions and earn rewards for exceptional service.
