Use mouse or touch on the screen

## About

You in the game Cooking Place will help the chef to serve the customers. In front of you on the screen will be visible to the rack behind which your hero will be. Customers will come to her and place orders. They will be displayed next to customers as pictures. You will have to study it carefully and then start cooking. Certain foodstuffs will be at your disposal. You, following the instructions on the screen, will have to prepare a given dish and then give it to the customer. If everything suits him, then the client will be satisfied and pay for the prepared dish.
