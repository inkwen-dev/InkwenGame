## How to play

Mouse: move the mouse to steer the nozzle – no clicking needed. Mouse wheel / drag to zoom and pan the upgrade tree.
Touch: slide your finger at the bottom of the screen to steer the nozzle like a joystick, or touch the floor to point the nozzle there. Pinch to zoom the upgrade tree.

## About

Suck It All is a cozy, chaotic cleaning game. Your vacuum sits in the middle of the room – steer its hose and nozzle onto the junk to break it and suck it up for coins. Rounds are short, so every second counts!

Spend your coins on a big upgrade tree: a longer hose, a wider nozzle, stronger suction, a helpful cat, a mop saber, roomba raids and much more. Pop plastic bags for chain explosions, burst popcorn buckets, grab batteries to overload your nozzle and smash giant boss sofas for a jackpot.

The mess grows tougher every round, so keep upgrading! Unlock five rooms – Bedroom, Kitchen, Bathroom, Garage and the Cosmic Attic – each with its own twist, then do a Deep Clean to start over with permanent bonuses.
