Choose a video theme, adjust the sliders based on what each topic needs, and release your video to start gaining views, money, and subscribers over time. Keep creating more videos, upgrade your equipment and skills from the right-side panel, follow trending topics for better results, unlock new features, and become the top YouTuber!

## About

Step into the shoes of a rising digital star in this comprehensive content creator simulation. Your core gameplay loop revolves around planning, recording, and editing videos to maximize your views and subscriber count. Start by selecting trending themes like gaming, memes, or tech, then fine-tune your recording style by balancing visuals versus conversation and choosing a tone that resonates with your audience. Once the footage is captured, head into the editing suite to arrange clips, add transitions, and apply text effects to boost your video's quality and viral potential.
