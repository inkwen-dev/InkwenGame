## About

Welcome to our game and your first super cool day as a boss! Sometimes, work life is boring. But in our game, it is not! Because here, you are the only boss. Go to the office and enjoy life! There are multiple levels you can experience. Hire/Fire an employee, throw papers to your colleagues to wake them up, or investigate The company your father built is going bankrupt. As a boss, you have a chance to go back in time to save your company. Manage your office with an iron fist or let your employees slack off without consequence. How you manage your office will determine your companys success.
