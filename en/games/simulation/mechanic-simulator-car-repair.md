## How to play

Control of the game:

• X - sit down
• LKM - To deliver the item
• E - Raise the item
• Q - Drop the item
• WASD Management
• Space bar - Handbrake
• R - Turn the car over
• Esc Pause
• Right shift - Nitro (In the car)
