## How to play

1. Click the oven and other generators to get flour, dough, and ingredients.
2. Drag two matching items together to create a higher-level treat.
3. Collect the required items and complete a customer’s order to earn coins and fame.
4. Open the city map, earn neighborhood trust, take on timed deliveries, and beat the clock.
5. Build new branches, upgrade your vans, and expand your bag to keep your kitchen running faster.

## About

Open a family bakery and turn it into the city’s favorite spot. In the kitchen, merge matching ingredients and treats to create higher-level dishes. Serve customers, complete orders, and grow your reputation.
Unlock the city map: earn the trust of different neighborhoods, send out delivery vans, and open new branches. Decorate your bakery, collect recipes, complete daily goals, and compete in the weekly bakers’ leaderboard.
