## About

Burger Life is a fun simulation game where you have to manage your own burger restaurant. Start by buying the essential equipment, serve your clients, hire employees and grow your business. Complete all tasks and make the best decisions to achieve your highest goals. Expand your burger restaurant to the max and have fun while doing it!
