## How to play

Steal an egg from a guardian and escape before they catch you! Hatch the eggs you collect and place your best ones in the pen to generate money every second. Spend your money on the treadmill to increase your speed and challenge faster guardians. There are 6 guardian levels, each requiring more speed to overcome. Equip trails to gain additional speed multipliers and progress even faster. On PC, move using WASD or the Arrow Keys and press E to interact. On mobile, use the on-screen touch controls to move and interact.
