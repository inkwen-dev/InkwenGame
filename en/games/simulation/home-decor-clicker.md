## About

Build your dream home one click at a time!
In this relaxing clicker game, you earn money by tapping on your house. Every click brings you closer to upgrading your home, earning gold, and unlocking beautiful decorations. As your income grows, you can customize your house with furniture, decorations, and upgrades that reflect your progress.
The more you play, the more efficient your earnings become. Invest your gold wisely, decorate your home, and watch it transform from a simple house into a stunning living space.
Perfect for fans of idle and clicker games, this game offers a satisfying sense of progress, customization, and relaxation.
