## About

K Games Challenge is used to replay the games in the famous film called Squid Game. Under a 3D game engine, you are able to play Green Light and Red Light, Honeycomb Toffee, Tug of war, Marbles, Stepping Stones, and SP Game with AI opponents. With arcade play rules and controls, it will be simple for you to win all games in a limited time. Like Marbles, we need you to throw the marble into the hole in six chances. You can only complete the level in green areas. Good luck!
