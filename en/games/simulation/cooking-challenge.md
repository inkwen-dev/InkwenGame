## About

A hungry customer is an impatient customer. Which needs to be served as soon as possible. This is exactly what will happen in the game Cooking Challenge. Your restaurant is open. Quickly put cutlets on the pan, throw potato straws into the boiling oil and turn on the beverage dispenser. All this will be needed soon and let the dishes be ready in advance so that hungry customers do not wait and are not nervous. Serve quickly, get paid a solid tip, complete the tasks of the levels. And there will be fifty of them. Buy equipment, upgrade it and all this for the fastest service in Cooking Challenge.
