## How to play

Use WASD or arrow keys on PC, or the floating joystick on mobile, to move around the shop. Pick up sweets, deliver them to customers and counters, collect money, and spend it on upgrades, new stations, and shop expansion. Keep serving customers to grow your SweetStreet Shop

## About

Sweet Street Shop is a charming shop management simulation where you run your own candy and pastry store. Serve customers, collect money, unlock new products, upgrade your character, hire helpers, and expand the shop into a successful sweet business
