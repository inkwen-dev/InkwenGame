## About

Hyena Simulator 3D is an animal simulation game where a hyena will complete different tasks in 10 different missions where difficulty increases gradually. Instruction will be given for the next mission from a hyena which simulates to perform the tasks. Get ready to attack your enemies and defeat them to earn more points.
