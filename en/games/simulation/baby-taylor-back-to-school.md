## About

Baby Taylor Back To School is an educational game. The long vacation is coming to an end. TodayTaylor is going to pack her school things. Lets help Taylor pack her things! Taylor is still asleep, wake her up quickly, and help her brush her teeth and wash her face. The most important thing for school is the school bag. Help her organize the books and pencils she needs to bring to school and put them in her school bag. And the most important thing is the school uniform, dont forget it. Finally, help Taylor review the knowledge she has learned so that her knowledge will be more solid.
