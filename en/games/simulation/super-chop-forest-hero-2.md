## About

Pull the Hero Axe out of the old stump and become the mightiest lumberjack in the forest! Chop trees, fill your backpack with logs and fly them to the truck to sell for coins. Buy stronger tools, from Twin Axes and Power Gauntlets to the Hero Saw, Thunder Axe and Plasma Cutter, and upgrade your strength, backpack, speed and Hero Flight. Unlock six forest zones, from Oak Woods to the Redwood Giants, where every tree is worth more. Battle the giant Elder Trees, smash the rot rigs of Baron Blight, clear monster dens for rare materials and relics, rescue forest animals and rebuild the village to earn a beaver helper, a frost pup and a double jump. Plant rare seeds in your own grove, dig up buried treasure and collect new hero gloves. Play solo or online with up to 8 heroes!
