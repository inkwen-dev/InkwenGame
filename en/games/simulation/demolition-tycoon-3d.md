## How to play

Mouse or touch hold the demolition button or building to attack Hold Space to strike Escape opens settings Click or tap menus to buy upgrades select machines and explore districts

## About

Start with a mini loader and build a demolition fleet. Hold to strike, break buildings apart and earn coins as your power grows with every hit. Upgrade power, speed, profit and critical hits, then save for bigger machines. Complete 80 contracts across seven districts, from a backyard to a harbor and alpine heights. Unlock seven machines, including the Titan crusher, and continue into tougher contracts after the campaign. Wood, brick, concrete, metal and glass have different impact effects. Progress is saved locally. Supports ten languages and mouse, keyboard and touch controls.
