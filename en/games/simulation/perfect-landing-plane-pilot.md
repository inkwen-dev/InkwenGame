## About

Perfect Landing, Plane Pilot is a 3D flying game where the sky is the only road you need. Take off across bright island landscapes, thread your plane between spinning wind turbines, and keep the nose steady all the way down to a clean touchdown. Every flight is a run for distance and coins. Steer left and right to dodge obstacles, fly as far as you can, and cash in what you collect. Coins go straight into your hangar: upgrade the plane you fly, unlock faster aircraft, and push each new route further than the last. The early trips are short and forgiving, with a simple propeller plane.
