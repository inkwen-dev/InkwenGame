## About

In the world there are many different, important and necessary professions. One of such wonderful profession is a doctor. In this entertaining game, your clinic and you, like a real doctor, will take wild animals that are sick and who need treatment and emergency medical care. Your clinic is always ready to accept new patients. You have to use all knowledge and skills, efforts and diligence, in order to cure these wild animals from cold, bruises or any other wound. Let’s get started!
