## How to play

Controls:
Tab - Pause
WASD - Drive
Space - Handbrake
Shift - Nitro
N - Next car
R - Repair car
C - Change camera
V - Slow motion

On mobile devices, use the on-screen controls.

Drive around and smash cars however you like.
