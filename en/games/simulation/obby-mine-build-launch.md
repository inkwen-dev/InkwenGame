📱 Mobile Version (Joystick & Touch):
Movement — joystick
Mining/Harvesting — tap the right side of the screen

## About

Dive into a fast, satisfying browser obby where the real goal is simple: mine resources, upgrade everything, and grow your digging empire. Grab your tool, break blocks, collect loot, and turn every run into progress. Improve your base, boost your power, and watch your income climb.

Mine more — upgrade faster:
• Upgrade tools to mine quicker and carry more.
• Upgrade your base to process resources and unlock stronger boosts.
• Hire workers to speed up routine farming while you push for bigger upgrades.

Build your style and your squad:
• Buy cute pets that help you mine and accelerate progress.
• Collect skins to stand out and customize your character.
• Unlock new tools and upgrades to keep your grind smooth and rewarding.
