## About

It is a game based on creating popcorn, with simple mechanics, easy controls, shop, levels. The game is easy, control the bar to pop the corn and turn it into popcorn to fill the bag. Earn points to unlock new skins for small corn, and earn levels to unlock new bars. Unlocked skins give new bonuses, new bars have special powers. By getting golden coins, you can also buy new bags of popcorn.
