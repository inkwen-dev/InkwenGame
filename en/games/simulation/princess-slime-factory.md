## About

Eliza opens a slime factory! Let's find out how to quickly and easily prepare a beautiful slime at home with the princess. We will need simple ingredients such as liquid soap, shampoo, toothpaste, shaving foam, baking soda, glue, food coloring and glitter. There are several ways to make a slime. Try each one of it. Now that our slime is ready, create a stylish package for it. The design of the slime bottle is an important part of the factory. Making slimes is so much fun!
