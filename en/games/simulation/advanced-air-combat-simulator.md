## About

Advanced Air Combat Simulator, where players must obliterate enemy jet fighters to emerge victorious. Take control of your aircraft using the WASD keys for navigation, while employing the mouse to aim, look around, and unleash devastating firepower with a left-click. Navigate through intense dogfights, strategically outmaneuvering foes in high-stakes aerial battles.
