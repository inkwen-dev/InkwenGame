## About

Trash Baron: Turn trash into treasure!

Have you ever thought that trash is not waste, but a resource? In Trash Baron, you will create a waste recycling empire. Start with a small landfill and turn it into a high-tech enterprise!

Gameplay:
♻️ Collect and sort: Tons of waste - plastic, glass, metal, and rubber. Sort it all properly.
🏭 Recycle into goods: start production lines! We turn plastic bottles into new bottles and so on
💰 Sell and grow: the higher the quality of your recycled products, the higher the profit. Earn money to buy new equipment, expand your workshops, and hire employees.
📈 Become a baron: from a small waste collection point to the largest waste recycling plant in the city. The future of the environment and business is in your hands!
