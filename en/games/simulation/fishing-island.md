## How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to walk (or use WASD / arrow keys). Stand on the fish circle at the end of a pier to fish, put the catch on a stall (green arrow zone), and pick up goods from the blue arrow zones. Collect coins from the tables and stand on a price zone to build stations, hire workers and raise new land. Visit the gear shop to upgrade your basket, boots and rod. Automate the whole island to sail to the next one. The Menu button shows island stats and settings.

## About

Turn a tiny pier into a giant automated fishing port! Fish with your rod from the end of the pier, carry the catch to the market stalls and watch customers line up. Open new land that rises from the sea, hire fishers, cutters and porters, run conveyors, smokehouses and sushi bars, and grow a beach grill into a cozy cafe with a cook. Fill ship orders, collect 16 fish species and golden fish, and sail across four islands - from a warm bay to an icy harbor. Cozy 3D idle tycoon: one finger to play, pure satisfying growth.
