## About

My Virtual Dog Care is a cute pet care game. This game is designed by Yiv.com for kids who love pets. The game has a lively and cute painting style and smooth animations, which can make you feel very comfortable. The gameplay of this game is also very simple and addictive. This game has a total of 4 parts: putting the puppy to sleep, feeding the puppy food, helping the puppy bathe, and playing with the puppy. This is the same as taking care of a puppy in real life. But the difference is that taking care of the puppy in this game is effortless. You only need to tap your fingers to make this Virtual Dog happy and healthy.
