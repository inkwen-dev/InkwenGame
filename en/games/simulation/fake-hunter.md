## How to play

Each customer brings one Blobby. The official model is shown on the card in the corner. Compare the figure on the counter with the official one, turn it around to check the back label, and decide before the customer runs out of patience: REAL to buy it, FAKE to reject it. Right calls earn coins and stars, mistakes cost money. Spend your coins on upgrades between days.

Mouse / touch: tap REAL or FAKE (or drag the figure right / left). Tap the tools to turn the figure, use the UV lamp or the scale. Press and hold on the figure for the magnifying glass.

Keyboard: Right arrow or D = REAL. Left arrow or A = FAKE. Up arrow, W or Space = turn the figure. Z (hold) = magnifying glass. U = UV lamp. P or Esc = pause.

## About

REAL... OR FAKE? You have seconds to decide!

Customers pour into your collectibles shop with adorable Blobbies to sell. Some are the real deal. Others are shameless knock-offs with a third eye, the wrong ears or a label that smells fishy. Spin it, zoom in, hit it with the UV lamp... and SLAM your verdict!

Buy the real ones and watch the coins rain. Bust the fakes and become the legend of the neighbourhood. Get fooled... and the scammers laugh all the way to the bank.

- 7 dream shops to unlock: neon arcade, tropical, luxury, festival and more
- 9 extra modes: daily challenge, endless, time attack, auctions and friend challenges by code
- Minigames between days, a sticker album to complete, upgrades and staff to hire
