## How to play

Everything is played with one finger (or mouse), no keyboard needed. Drag to
move your lure through the water. Flick upward through a fish's mouth to
catch it, sweep through several at once for a COMBO that multiplies your
coins. Swipe up to the surface to bank your catch. Tap a mutant fish to
scare it away; never flick into its mouth or it steals your lure. Fill a
zone's Fishdex, beat its Zone Challenge, then buy the next lure in the shop
to unlock deeper zones.

## About

Deep Freeze Fishing is a flick-and-catch arcade fishing game set beneath a
frozen lake. Snag fish by flicking your lure straight through their mouths,
chain multi-fish combos off the ice ceiling, and fill your Fishdex across
five ever-deeper zones. Complete each zone's Fishdex to unlock its Zone
Challenge, schools of fish you'll need sharp aim to clear.
Watch out for mutant fish: hook one by accident and it swims off with your
lure. One-finger controls, quick sessions, 60 species to collect.
