## About

Aqua Fish Rush is an energetic endless 2D underwater runner game in which the player is required to navigate through the water with their fish while dodging other fishes that are not friendly. As the game goes on, the speed raises, making it harder to defeat the enemies. The game offers good controls, nice graphics depicting ocean environments, and easy gameplay that is quite easy to learn but takes time to master. Attempt to survive for as long as possible and try to defeat your highest record in this exciting aquatic adventure.
