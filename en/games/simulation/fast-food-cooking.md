## About

Welcome to Fast Food Cooking, an exciting restaurant tycoon game! Become the ultimate chef and manager as you turn a small food stall into a successful restaurant empire. Cook and serve delicious burgers, pizzas, desserts, and more to satisfy your hungry customers. Start small, upgrade your kitchen, and unlock new food items and restaurant types. Manage your time wisely, improve your service speed, and grow your business step by step. With fun gameplay and rewarding progress, Fast Food Cooking is perfect for anyone who loves cooking and management games!
