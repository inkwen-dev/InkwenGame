## How to play

Clean every dirty surface to complete each level.
Desktop: move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, hold left click to spray. Press V to get a bonus tool (rewarded video). Press B to open the shop.
Mobile: use the left side of the screen to move, drag to aim, and hold the spray button to clean.
Earn coins, buy upgrades and unlock new tools to clean faster!

## About

Grab your pressure washer and bring the neighborhood back to life! Wash Master is a relaxing 3D cleaning game in first-person view. Aim, spray, and watch thick mud disappear to reveal bright, clean colors. Clean houses and more, earn coins, upgrade your gear and unlock powerful new tools. Simple to play, deeply satisfying.
