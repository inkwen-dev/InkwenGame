## How to play

Controls: mouse, touchscreenControls: mouse, touchscreen

Click (or tap) buttons and cards — this opens cases, selects items, and switches sections via the bottom panel.

Case: press the open button — the roulette spins on its own, then choose "Keep" or "Sell."

Selling: set a price — drag the slider or just click the desired spot on the bar, then press "Put up for sale."

Buyers write to you in "Chats": reply by choosing from ready-made lines — say hello, ask about the item, agree, or haggle.

The "Work" section is a clicker: tap the computer to earn money and level up your income.

At the top — balance, gifts, and leaderboards; at the bottom — section navigation.

Collect all items and earn more money than other players to become a king of resale!

## About

Think getting rich on reselling is easy? Think getting rich on reselling is easy? Then welcome to "My Resale. Cases and sales" — a simulator where you can turn dollars into a fortune 🤑

Open cases and catch your luck: anything can drop — from a ballpoint pen to a cool cars. Put your loot up for sale, haggle with buyers, and learn to sense prices. The buyers here are all different: chatterboxes, the impatient, thrifty tricksters, and even trolls who are a pleasure to teach a lesson.

Short on cash? Go "to work" — click the computer and level up your income. Claim daily gifts and build a collection of a hundred items!

Climb the leaderboards among collectors, tycoons, and top sellers. Can you become the king of resale? Let's find out!
