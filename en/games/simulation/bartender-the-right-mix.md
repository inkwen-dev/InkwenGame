## About

Help Miguel the bartender mix cocktails for himself in the right ratios! Choose from a shelf full of different drinks and mix them together to make the ultimate cocktail for a good night out. Add ice or lemon to give it that extra kick but be careful, mixing in the wrong amounts or simply mixing the wrong drinks could end in the worst of ways! No bartending school required!
