## About

Welcome to Toca Avatar My House, a casual simulation game where you arrange your own house in the gaming world. If you are a Toca Boca enthusiast, try this life simulator game. You will find more pleasure living in a lovely house designed and decorated by yourself! Click on any of the items and drag to move them to any place you want. Put things in order, categorize cooking utensils, fill the cats bowl with food...
