## About

Kids Happy Kitchen is a very addictive cooking game. In this game, you are an owner of a tiny but popular restaurant. 3 customers are here to taste your cooking. They want to try your specialty, curry rice, fired potato shreds and fried dumplings. As a sophisticated chef, come and show your skills. Have a good time!
