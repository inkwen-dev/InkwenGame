## About

Donut Cooking Game is a very fun cooking game.It's a very good game for kids who love desserts. Because you can make the cutest and the moset delicious donuts in the world. Firstly you knead the dough. And then you use molds to cut shapes in to the dough. You can make different shapes of donuts at once. After you fried the dough, add some jams, sugar and chocolates to the donuts. After the donuts are done, choose you favorite box and decorate it pack the donuts. You also can eat them in this game.
