## How to play

Driving/Walking/Flying - WASD
Right mouse button - Rotate camera
Space - Handbrake
C - Change camera
B - Look back
H - Horn
J - Hazard lights
Z - Left turn signal
X - Right turn signal

For mobile devices, use the in-game interface.

## About

Dive into the exhilarating world of car crashes with the Beam Drive Car Crash Test Simulator. Perfect for those who love realistic car destruction and high-speed racing, this free online game is accessible on both mobile and PC. Prepare for an entertaining experience filled with destruction physics!

Controls:
Desktop: WASD — movement, right mouse button to click buttons, ZX — left and right turn signals. Mouse wheel — zoom in/out camera, left mouse button — rotate camera.
Mobile: Tap the buttons. Left/right buttons, accelerate forward, reverse.

Key Features:
• Realistic controls: The game features highly accurate controls, along with vehicle destructibility. Watch as cars crumple, tear apart, and explode into pieces!
• Create your own levels: If the basic maps aren't enough for you, create your own — you decide what will be on your map and how it will look!
• Choose your car: Pick the car you like and hit the road! Wonder how long your journey will be.

Game Modes:
• Classic mode: A classic mode where you can play on 8 ready-made maps and test the cars!
• Map Editor: A separate large world where you can create your own maps as your heart desires. Come up with your own challenges and obstacles or just create something unique.

Vehicles / Cars:
• Caro: A powerful muscle car. It will impress you with its speed and power. See how it withstands obstacles in its path!
• Flash: The futuristic electric car! Powerful and fast enough. Watch how it takes a hit!
• Syper: A sports car designed specifically for speed. Street racing is its forte, but how will it perform on the map? Find out!

A: Test the cars' durability by completing challenges, or create your own! It's your sandbox where you can play as you wish! Choose a car and go racing on the map! You can complete challenges or immediately destroy your car on the first obstacle! Watch as cars turn into heaps of metal, or try to keep them intact. The choice is yours!

Q: How do you win / what counts as losing?
