## How to play

Desktop:
AWSD: Player Movement
Mouse = Camera Movement
P = Pause
Ctrl = Setting
I = Info
E = Attack
Shift = Sprint
F = Actions
U: Click to fly
J: Click for walk
E: Move up while flying
X: Move Down while flying

Mobile:
Joystick to Move Player
Drag Screen To Move Camera

## About

Experience the exciting life of a wild duck in Duck Family Simulator Animal Games! Explore beautiful islands, peaceful lakes, and lush green forests while raising your duck family and surviving against dangerous predators. Hunt for food, protect your ducklings, complete thrilling missions, and discover the adventures of life in the wild.
Fly freely across the island, explore hidden locations, collect coins, and unlock new adventures as you progress through the game. Customize your duck, improve your survival skills, and experience realistic wildlife gameplay in a vibrant 3D open world.

Features
-Raise and protect your own duck family.
-Explore beautiful islands, forests, lakes, and open-world environments.
-Hunt for fish, insects, and other food to survive.
-Battle dangerous predators including crocodiles, snakes, sharks, giant lizards, and wild dogs.
-Fly through the skies, collect coins, and discover hidden areas.
-Build a safe home and care for your ducklings.
-Complete exciting survival missions and wildlife challenges.
-Smooth controls, realistic animations, and immersive 3D gameplay.

Become the leader of your duck family and experience a fun wildlife adventure filled with exploration, survival, family-building, and action in Duck Family Simulator Animal Games. Explore the wilderness, protect your loved ones, and prove your survival skills in this exciting animal simulator.
