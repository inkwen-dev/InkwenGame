## How to play

Open the character-and-blocks button, choose a category and an item, then click or tap an empty spot in the world to place it. Build structures, add characters and equipment, and use the crossed-swords button to switch bot battles on or off. Open the hammer menu to craft equipment when you have the required resources.

Desktop: use the mouse to place and drag objects. Drag empty space to move the camera; use the mouse wheel or + / - to zoom. Press the character-control button and select a character: A / D or Left / Right to move, W to jump, Space or right mouse button to attack, and G to drop the held item.

Mobile: tap the menus and world to place items, drag to interact, and use the on-screen movement, jump and action controls when controlling a character. Use + / - to zoom.

This is an open-ended sandbox: build, experiment and create your own challenges. The current in-game text is Russian.

## About

Build a pixel world, fill it with ragdoll characters, and see what happens! Toybox: Pixel World Sandbox is a 2D physics sandbox where you create your own fun. Place blocks, spawn cats, rabbits, pigs and other characters, experiment with weapons and objects, and set up red-versus-green team battles. Take control of a character or watch your creations react to physics. Gather wood and crystals to craft new equipment, then rebuild and try a different idea. No fixed missions or single way to win: your next experiment is the goal.
