## How to play

Drag anywhere to walk (floating joystick for touch and mouse; WASD or arrow keys on a keyboard). Everything else happens when you walk over it: grab popcorn at the machines, serve the counter and car windows, collect cash, tidy seats, and stand on a pad to spend your earnings on seats, machines, upgrades and helpers. Finish the cinema to open the Drive-In. M mutes, Esc closes panels.

## About

Run your own tiny movie theatre!

Grab fresh popcorn from the machine, stock the snack counter and ring up happy moviegoers. Every guest pays twice: once for snacks and once for a ticket when they take their seat. Messy seats slow the queue, so tidy up between films.

Stand on a pad to spend your earnings: more seats, a soda fountain, a second popcorn machine, a manager's office with upgrades, and helpers who clean, serve and restock for you. Open Hall 2 with a bigger screen and pricier tickets.

Finish the cinema and open the Drive-In, a whole new venue. Cars roll in, park in front of the giant screen and order snacks, and you carry popcorn, sodas and hot dogs right to the car window. Hire carhops and an attendant, widen the lot, and turn it into the best drive-in in town. Your fame grows with every finished venue and boosts your income everywhere.

- One-finger controls: drag anywhere to walk; everything else happens when you walk over it
- Two full venues with their own twists, and more coming
- Hire staff and upgrade carry size, speed and machines
- Earn while you are away, and collect a daily bonus
- Cozy low-poly art and original music
