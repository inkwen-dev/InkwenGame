A cosy cooking game about running a little hotdog van in the park. Grill or boil sausages, drag buns into the boat and squeeze ketchup, mustard and toppings on…

## How to play

Drag a bun into the paper boat, then drag a sausage onto the grill (tap it to flip when the underside turns golden) or into the boiling pot. Drag the cooked sausage onto the bun, then hold a sauce bottle and draw along the hotdog, and drop or sprinkle toppings. Swipe the hotdog up (or tap the green arrow) to serve the customer at the window. Make what they ask for, or surprise them with extras: they react to their own tastes. Earn coins and stars each shift, then buy ingredients, upgrades and van decor in the shop. Works with mouse or touch.

## About

A cosy cooking game about running a little hotdog van in the park. Grill or boil sausages, drag buns into the boat and squeeze ketchup, mustard and toppings on with real physics. Every customer has secret tastes: improvise, learn what each regular loves and hates, invent your own recipes, and decorate your van as your stand grows.
