## How to play

Move with WASD on desktop, or touch anywhere on screen for a joystick that follows your finger. Tap cars to repair them, walk up to workers to collect their earnings, and open the phone menu to hire staff and buy upgrades that grow your garage, supermarket, and gas station.

## About

Garage Boss is an idle tycoon where you grow a single repair bay into a full garage, supermarket, and gas station in one connected world. Hire workers, upgrade everything, and watch your mini empire run itself while you tap, walk, and expand for more.
