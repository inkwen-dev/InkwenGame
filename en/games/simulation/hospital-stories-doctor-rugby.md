## About

In Hospital Stories: Doctor Rugby, you'll take on the role of a comedic physician tasked with treating the wild and wacky injuries of a ragtag rugby team. Using your skills and quick thinking, you'll perform a variety of medical procedures, from mending wounds to removing foreign objects, all while navigating a series of mini-games filled with absurdity and humor. Will you have what it takes to make it through every level and become the ultimate rugby team doctor?
