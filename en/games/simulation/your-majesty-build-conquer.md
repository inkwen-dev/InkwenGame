"Your Majesty - Build & Conquer"  is an exciting strategy game that transports you to a world of kingdoms, where your task is to restore the greatness of your empire. Take on the role of a monarch who must revive a ruined settlement, turning it into a thriving capital, and expand your territories by conquering new lands.
