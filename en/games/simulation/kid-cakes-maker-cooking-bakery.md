## About

Welcome to Kid Cakes Maker Cooking Bakery, a casual cooking simulation game for kids who love food and DIY games. Bake a delicious and beautiful cake with cute animals! Learn to use different cooking tools and master recipes for the best rainbow cake! You need to shop for ingredients and cooking tools in the store. Mix ingredients in the bowl and put the mixture in the moulds to take shape. Heat the cake in the oven.
