## About

Real Flight Simulator plays like an airplane driving simulation game with 3D models. In 6 levels, you can learn how to drive an airplane including starting, steering, and landing. All of the complicated instrument panels are simplified for players to learn. The more expensive the airplane you drive, the better acceleration of it you can enjoy including the Power, Speed, Solution, and Brake. Good luck in the sky!
