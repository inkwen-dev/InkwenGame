## About

Get ready for a wonderful pixel battle in Pixel Island War! This strategy game brings you to a wonderful pixel world. Here your task is to conquer islands and expand your empire. Build bases, gather resources and prepare for fierce battles on different islands. For example, choose an island to place humans on. Humans will build towns and multiply. Choose sheep to produce wool and meat.
