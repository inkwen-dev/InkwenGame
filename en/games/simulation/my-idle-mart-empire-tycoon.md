## About

Want to become the next retail tycoon in My Idle Mart Empire Tycoon? This addictive idle simulation game lets you manage your store empire. It started as a small store and gradually became a huge shopping center. Customize your store and upgrade your items. Watch as your profits grow even while youre offline. Unlock the checkout to greet customers. Move the goods quickly and put them into the belt to produce goods.
