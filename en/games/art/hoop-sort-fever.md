## About

Ditch the ordinary water droplets, Hoop Sort Fever injects a splash of excitement into the color sorting genre! This addictive ring stacking game will keep you entertained for hours, challenging your brain with vibrant hoops and a fresh twist on a classic formula. How to Play: - Tap or drag a hoop to move it to another stack. - Simple rule: Only stack hoops on the same color or an empty pole. - Stuck on a tricky level? Use the undo, add pole, or skip level options to keep the fun rolling. - Sort all the hoops by color to complete the level - its as satisfying as it sounds!
