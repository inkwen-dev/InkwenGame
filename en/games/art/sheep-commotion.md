## How to play

Draw a path from a sheep to the matching pen. White sheep go to the white pen, black sheep go to the black pen. Avoid wolves, rocks and puddles, keep greedy sheep away from apples, and finish levels faster to earn more stars.

## About

Sheep Commotion is a cheerful path-drawing puzzle game about guiding every sheep to the right pen. Draw safe routes, collect stars, avoid wolves, rocks and puddles, and solve increasingly tricky levels with different sheep behaviors.
