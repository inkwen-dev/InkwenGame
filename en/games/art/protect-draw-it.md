## About

Protect Draw It is a free casual cartoon online game for boys played on Puzzlegame. In each level, there are wolves who want to eat sheep. You need to draw the fences reasonably to separate the sheep from the wolves. Remember the number of fences is limited. Come and experience the simple and interesting puzzle challenges. Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop are playable.
