## About

Art Puzzle Fun is a very fun casual puzzle game, a free online puzzle game. You just have to put together a complete figure based on the given squares, which you need to complete in a countdown timer. This game is a great exercise for children's intelligence. Do you want to have superb thinking skills? There are plenty of levels to challenge you.
