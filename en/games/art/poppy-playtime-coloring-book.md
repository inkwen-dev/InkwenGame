Use mouse or tap on the screen

## About

Welcome to the game Poppy Playtime Coloring Book where you will find 18 different pictures that need to be colored. They need to be colored and for this you are provided with a set of fifteen felt-tip pens of different bright colors. On the left you will see a set of circles of different diameters. You can also save the colored image. Have fun!
