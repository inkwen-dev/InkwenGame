## How to play

The goal of the game is to design unique rooms using meditative coloring by numbers. Focus on color schemes by tapping on the picture.

Control - touch / left mouse button.

## About

Immerse yourself in a game where the process of coloring becomes truly meditative. Create and decorate houses as a designer. Just touch the screen and the colors will match the numbers in the image. Forget about boring routine - now you are thinking about color combinations. For even more relaxation, a music player has been added.

Key Features:

- Play to calm music and enjoy relaxation.
- Become a designer by creating rooms from scratch.
- Intuitive interface for ease of use.
- The illustrations represent parts of the rooms you create.
- Use your creative vision to bring your dream rooms to life.

Explore a world where coloring combined with music creates the perfect relaxation experience. Expand your creativity, create amazing rooms and find peace in this pixel game.
