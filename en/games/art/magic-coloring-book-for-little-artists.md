## About

a calm and creative interactive game for kids that combines outline drawing and classic coloring. Instead of receiving a ready-made picture, children create it step by step. First, a magical marker automatically draws the outline while the player holds the screen. Then the finished drawing can be colored using bright and friendly colors. The game helps develop fine motor skills, focus, patience, and color perception in a fun and relaxing way.
