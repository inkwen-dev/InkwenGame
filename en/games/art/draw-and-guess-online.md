## How to play

1. Join a Room: Enter a public room or create a private one to play with friends. Minimum 3 players are required to start.

2. Take Turns Drawing: When it’s your turn, you will receive a word to draw. Use the drawing tools to illustrate it clearly.

3. Guess the Word: While another player is drawing, type your guesses in the chat. The faster you guess correctly, the more points you earn!

4. Score Points: Points are awarded for correct guesses and for how many players successfully guess your drawing.

5. Rounds & Winner: The game continues for several rounds. At the end, the player with the highest score wins.

6. Use Hints (Optional): Some versions have hints or drawing aids to make guessing easier.

## About

Draw and Guess is a fun and fast-paced online multiplayer game where creativity meets quick thinking! Players take turns drawing a given word while others try to guess what it is in real-time. The faster you guess correctly, the more points you earn. Compete with friends or players from around the world and show off your drawing skills!
