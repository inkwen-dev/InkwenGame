## How to play

Touch and drag to draw a protective line.
Keep the dog safe for at least 10 seconds.
The shorter your line, the more stars you earn.
Join the fun and save the dog today!

## About

Watch out! The cute dog is in danger.

The evil bees are here to sting him, and it’s your job to protect the dog! Draw a line to block the bees and keep him safe. This is not just any puzzle game—it’s the ultimate save dog rescue game, where your creativity and quick thinking will be put to the test.

But the challenge doesn’t stop at the bees. The dog must also avoid lava, water, spikes, and bombs. Can you step up and master the art of save the doge? Whether it’s outsmarting swarms or battling obstacles, this is your chance to shine in the clash of doge vs bee!
