## About

Draw a Perfect Circle is a minimalist and challenging game that tests your precision, focus, and control. The idea is simple: draw a perfect circle. But be careful, every attempt shows how hard perfection is to achieve. Have fun, challenge yourself, and invite your friends to see who can get closest to the perfect circle!
