## How to play

PAINTING: Bring your ideas to life with a palette of vibrant colors. From soft pastels to electric neons, paint your way to a phone case that screams 'you'.
ACRYLIC ART: Delve into the trendy world of acrylic art. Swirl, mix, and pour your way to stunning abstract designs that stand out in any crowd.
STICKERS: Add personality and flair with an array of whimsical and edgy stickers. From quotes to quirky characters, the perfect sticker is waiting to be placed.
POP IT: Embrace the satisfying trend of pop it and fidget toys by incorporating them into your designs. Why not have a phone case that's as fun to play with as it is to look at?
KEYCHAINS: Accessorize your custom case with adorable keychains that dangle and dance with every move. Choose from a variety of designs to add that perfect finishing touch.
