## About

Online game "Coloring Barbie" invites girls to show off their talents as an artist, coloring a collection of pictures with your favorite heroine. The game is dedicated to the cartoon about Barbie "Dreamtopia". Knowing how girls love to draw, the doll decided to hold a contest for them. For him, she picked up a large collection of colorings, which depict various subjects of the cartoons "Barbie and the magic Dolphins", "Dreamtopia". Start the game, choose a drawing to your liking and start creating. In total, there are 29 colorings in the album. Pick up a brush and paint different characters. The image can be painted with stirs or filled with a single click from the bucket.
