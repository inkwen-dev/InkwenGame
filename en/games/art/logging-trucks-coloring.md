## About

Logging Trucks Coloring is a Coloring Book game with Logging Trucks.There is a eight images with logging trucks and equipment. Your job is to choose your favorite image and to color it as you wish. You can change colors, erase colors and save or print the image. Use your mouse to color or your finger if you want to play in some mobile devices.
