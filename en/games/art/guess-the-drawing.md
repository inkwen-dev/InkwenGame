## About

This is a casual puzzle game where you draw and I guess. Maybe youve played a game like that before, where someone draws on your back and you draw on the front, and then you see if you agree. If you like that kind of game, then this game is for you. In this game, you need to memorize what the person behind you draws.You can use the coins you get to buy the brushes you need. Come and experience this new and interesting game world.
