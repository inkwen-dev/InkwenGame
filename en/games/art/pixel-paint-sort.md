## How to play

Tap a tile to select its connected group of the same color.
 Move the selected tiles onto matching free pegs.
 Use the tray to temporarily store tiles and plan your next moves.
 Complete every color, and reveal the full pixel picture.
 Finish the level of this sort puzzle by placing every tile on its matching peg.

## About

Pixel Paint Sort is a captivating puzzle game that blends pixel coloring and tile sorting in one relaxing, satisfying experience. Watch the picture come to life with every move, turning a jumbled-up board into a stunning masterpiece!
