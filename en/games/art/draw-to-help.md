## About

What would you draw to help a hungry dog or a cat caught in the rain? Draw to Help is a fun puzzle game where every picture tells a little story. Figure out whats missing, draw the solution with your finger, and watch the characters react! No drawing skills required. Just think, draw, and have fun!
