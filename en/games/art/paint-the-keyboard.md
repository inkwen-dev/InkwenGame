## How to play

A game about painting keys: press buttons, earn money, hire workers and expand the keyboard.
On the computer:
WASD — move.
Hold down the right mouse button and move the mouse — rotate the camera.
Mouse wheel — zoom in and out of the camera.
On a mobile device:
The joystick is moving.
Swipe on the screen — rotate the camera.
Move two fingers together or apart to indicate the camera's proximity and distance.
To fill the roller with paint, go to the paint tank.

## About

"Paint your keyboard" is a vivid game about developing your own keyboard field. Run on the keys, paint them with a roller and get paid for each press. Buy new paints, boost your character's speed, increase the width of the roller and the paint supply. Open new areas of the keyboard and hire workers who will press the keys with you. Start a new round of development with constant additions and discover artists for self-painting. Turn a small field into a huge bright keyboard workshop!
