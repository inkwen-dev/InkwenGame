Use mouse or tap the screen

## About

Today, in the game Ambulance Trucks Coloring Pages, we will go to a drawing lesson in elementary school. The teacher will give you a coloring book on the pages of which you will see black and white images of ambulances. You will need to click on one of them and open this image in front of you. After that, a special drawing panel will appear. With it, you will choose a color and apply it to a specific area of the drawing. So gradually you will make the drawing completely colored.
