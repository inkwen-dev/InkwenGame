## About

Diy Keyboard is a very unique art game. Have you ever thought about design your own style keyboards? Here is the game that can make you satisfied. Here you can experience 5 different manufacture methods: Resin, Acrylic, Pop it, Stencil and Decor. We also carefully prepared lots of beautiful ornamental for you. In our game, you have every opportunity to use your design and drawing talents. You can draw your keyboards to white, blue or red... You can also make your keyboard transparent if you like. Come and try this game! We are sure you will love it!
