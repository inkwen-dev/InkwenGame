## About

In Draw And Guess, players take turns drawing while others guess the word or phrase being depicted. Its a fun and interactive game that tests creativity and guessing skills. Players often use quick sketches or doodles to convey the idea within a time limit, adding an element of urgency and excitement. The game fosters communication and teamwork as players collaborate to decipher the drawings and advance through rounds.
