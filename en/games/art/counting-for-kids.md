## About

Counting Game for Kids is a set of unique educational kids counting games aimed at helping children learn counting numbers from one to nine. This game has 10 levels with 10 different themes: animals, planet, fruits, vegetables, flowers, learning tools, etc&hellip; In particular, with each level, the kids will learn how to write and pronounce the words of each picture that the kids counts. Teaching numbers in a way that engages fun and creativity, the kids will simply love playing the counting games.
