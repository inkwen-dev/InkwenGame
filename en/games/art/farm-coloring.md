## About

Surely you have been to your grandmother in the village before and know how different animals should look like. Why, it's a village, you probably watch TV, and it's full of animals and different villages. In this game, you will need to paint the village, so remember what the cows and houses in the villages look like, and start painting the image. Good luck.
