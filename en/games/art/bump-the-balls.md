## About

Test your logic and understanding of physics in an engaging puzzle game. Your objective is to help two balls meet by drawing a suitable line. Once the drawing is complete, the balls begin moving under the force of gravity. Consider the position of obstacles, dangerous objects, and moving mechanisms. Sometimes a simple platform is enough, while other levels require a more elaborate route. The game includes 100 unique levels. The difficulty gradually increases as new obstacles appear and solutions require greater precision. If a level seems too difficult, you can use a hint.
