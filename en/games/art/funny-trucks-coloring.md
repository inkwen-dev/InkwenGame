## About

Funny Trucks Coloring is coloring kids game with cute trucks. Here you can play with twelve images. Choose image that you want to color and go to game mode. From the right side on the screen you can choose the color that you want to use. On left side on the screen there is brush size. So choose brush size and color and start to play. If you made same mistake you can erase with rubber. At he end you can always save your beautiful truck image.
