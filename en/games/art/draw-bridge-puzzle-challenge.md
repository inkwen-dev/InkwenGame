Draw Bridge: Puzzle Challenge - solve puzzles, draw bridges, connect paths and test your logic with stickman in a fun game with creativity!

## How to play

- Swipe your finger across the screen to create a path and guide your motorcycle to your destination;
- To draw a bridge, you need to draw a line across the open areas and your vehicle will drive over them;
- You can only draw the line once;
- Absolutely, you have to touch the flag to win;
- Draw strong and balanced bridges.

🌈Show your creativity and pass all levels!
