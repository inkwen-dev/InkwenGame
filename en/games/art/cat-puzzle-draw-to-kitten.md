## How to play

1. Drag your finger over the cat and start drawing a line towards the kittens;
2. Be careful, avoid enemies and obstacles on the way;
3. Make sure all cats take their kittens home safely!

## About

«Cat Puzzle: Draw to Kitten» is an addictive casual puzzle game where you need to help cute pets reunite by drawing a safe line for cats to their kittens to get home!

If the cats collide, they will become dizzy and you will lose. Use your skills by playing this game and immerse yourself in the atmosphere now! 😻
