## How to play

Find Your Calm, One Color at a Time! Step into a peaceful world of art and mindfulness with Mandala Meadow, the relaxing mandala coloring game. Pick a beautiful design, fill each section with soft, soothing colors, and watch intricate patterns bloom to life. From simple flowers to elaborate lotus, peacock, and animal mandalas, there's a calming coloring puzzle for every mood — no timers, no pressure, just you and your art. Perfect for relaxation, stress relief, and creative fun!

Desktop Controls:

Click a section: Select the area you want to color.
Click & drag: Paint freely within the lines.
Color palette: Click a swatch to pick your color.
Brush / Fill toggle: Switch between hand-painting and instant fill.
Zoom slider: Zoom in for fine detail.
Undo / Reset: Fix mistakes or start a mandala fresh.
Mobile Controls:

Tap a section: Select where to paint.
Drag to paint: Color with your finger, neatly inside the lines.
Tap a swatch: Choose your color.
Pinch to zoom: Get up close for intricate areas.
Undo / Reset buttons: Adjust or restart anytime.
