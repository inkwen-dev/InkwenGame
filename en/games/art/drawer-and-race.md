## About

In Drawer And Race, participants compete to accurately depict a given word or phrase through quick drawings. Its a fast-paced game where players race against the clock to convey their chosen word, often employing simple and intuitive sketches. The emphasis is on speed and clarity, challenging players to think on their feet and express ideas concisely. Website Developer <a href='https://www.game-plays.com/'>https://www.game-plays.com/</a>
