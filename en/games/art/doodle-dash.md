## About

In this playful physics-based puzzle game, players draw lines to create paths for a bouncy ball to travel. The goal is to help the ball go as far as possible by using clever lines and strategic timing. The farther the ball rolls, the more ink you earn &mdash; and more ink means more possibilities! Use your ink to upgrade your ball, unlock new types of balls, and reach even greater distances. With a fun doodle-style art direction and quirky mechanics, this game blends creativity, planning, and light-hearted challenge into one endlessly replayable experience.
