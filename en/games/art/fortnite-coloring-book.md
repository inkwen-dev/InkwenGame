Use mouse or tap to screen to play this game

## About

Fortnite Coloring Book includes 12 different images of Fortnite Characters from which you can choose and color them as you wish. You have 24 different colors and 9 pencil sizes that they can use. After they are done with painting, they can use the print button to save the colored image and show it to their friends!
