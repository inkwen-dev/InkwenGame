## How to play

Choose a room. Drag to orbit the camera, then tap or click objects and paint them from the palette (brush, eraser, fill). Color the whole room to finish it and score points. Save favorite looks in slots. Watch a rewarded ad only if you want to reset a room. Desktop: hold left mouse to rotate, click to paint. Mobile: one-finger drag to rotate, tap to paint.

## About

Color the Room is a cozy 3D coloring game. Pick a fantasy room — castle, witch hut, space station, underwater, garden, or graffiti loft — and bring every object back to life with brushes, fills, and textures. Save your looks, earn points, and climb the leaderboards.
