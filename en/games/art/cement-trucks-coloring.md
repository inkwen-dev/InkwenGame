## About

Cement Trucks Coloring is a coloring page game with cement trucks. At this game you can play with eight images of trucks. Color the truck as you think it best looks like a cement truck. With the eraser you can erase the mistakes in the painting or change the color. Save your color image or print it. Use mouse to color or tap on the screen. First choose the color from the pens from right side and color the truck parts.
