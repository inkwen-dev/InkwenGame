## How to play

It's time to show your creativity to draw the fastest way to help the hero get to the goal:
1. Click on the character to start drawing a line;
2. Take the hero home, but avoid obstacles and enemies.
3. Determine the fastest and safest route to your destination.

## About

«Draw path to home» is a casual puzzle game in which you need to help your characters get to the house by drawing lines! Gather a collection of heroes (Nubik, Huggy Wuggy, Rainbow Friends, Cat Juan and others).
