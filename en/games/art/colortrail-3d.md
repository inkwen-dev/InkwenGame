## About

Welcome to Color Trail 3D — a fun and satisfying hyper-casual arcade game where every move paints the path with color!

Control your cube, move through exciting 3D tracks, and fill every path while avoiding dangerous obstacles and traps. Test your timing, reflexes, and precision as the levels become faster and more challenging.
