## How to play

Draw or guide the snake through the correct path on the puzzle board.
Collect apples to complete the level objective.
Break ice blocks and clear obstacles by moving carefully.
Think before every move because the snake can get stuck.
Find the best route and complete the puzzle.
Play more levels to unlock harder and more interesting challenges.

## About

Draw The Snake is a fun and colorful puzzle game where players guide snakes through grid-based levels by choosing the correct path. Your goal is to move the snake smartly, collect apples, clear obstacles, and complete each puzzle without getting stuck.

Each level becomes more challenging with tricky paths, ice blocks, and clever puzzle layouts. The game is easy to understand but needs good planning and strategy to master. With simple controls, bright visuals, and relaxing puzzle gameplay, Draw The Snake is perfect for players who enjoy brain-training games.
