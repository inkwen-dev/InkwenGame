## About

CHROMASPIN &mdash; an arcade experience where color matching meets speed and reflexes! Control the 4-colored spinning ring in the center of the screen. Meet the colorful balls attacking from all directions with the right colored section of the ring. A wrong match costs you a life &mdash; lose all 3 lives and the game is over. Chain combos to multiply your score and reach the high score. With Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels, its the perfect challenge for both beginners and speed enthusiasts!
