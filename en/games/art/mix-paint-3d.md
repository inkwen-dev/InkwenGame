## How to play

Recreate the target color and paint the 3D object. Reach at least 60% accuracy to pass the level, and aim for 90% or higher for a perfect result. Click or tap a pigment to load the brush, then hold and drag across the palette to mix colors. During painting, hold and drag to move the brush or spray across the object. Use Clear, Hint, and Done by clicking or tapping their buttons. The game uses a mouse or touchscreen; no keyboard keys are required.

## About

Mix & Paint 3D is a relaxing color-mixing and 3D painting puzzle. Choose pigments, blend the target shade on your palette, then paint each object and see how closely your result matches. Every level combines hands-on mixing, satisfying painting, and an accuracy challenge.
