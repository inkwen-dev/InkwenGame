## How to play

1. Pick sparkling diamonds and sort them by color.
2. Move each diamond into the matching cell on the board.
3. If the right spot is temporarily unavailable, use the storage area.
4. Plan your move order to avoid cluttering the board.
5. Use hints and boosters whenever you need a quick breakthrough.
6. Fill all cells correctly — and watch the picture shine in full beauty!

## About

Welcome to Jewel Coloring: Crystal Sorting — a relaxing puzzle game where colorful crystals gradually transform into beautiful sparkling pictures.
Sort the gems by color and place them into the matching cells. With every correct move, the image becomes brighter and more complete. Simple controls, satisfying gameplay, and a calm atmosphere make each level enjoyable and easy to play.

- Select a crystal and identify its color.
- Move it to a matching cell on the board.
- Use the storage area when the required cell is blocked.
- Plan your moves to avoid filling the storage slots.
- Use hints or boosters when you need help.
- Place all crystals correctly to complete the picture!
