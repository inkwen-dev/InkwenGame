## About

Welcome to the game Halloween Sprunki Coloring Book where you will find 12 different pictures to color. They need to be colored and for this you are provided with a set of fifteen felt-tip pens of different bright colors. On the left you will see a set of circles of different diameters. You can also save the colored image. Have fun playing this Sprunki coloring book game!
