## About

Tip Master Wine is a fun casual skill game where you need to skillfully pour drinks into a glass without overflowing. Tilt the device, control the pouring amount, and stop at the right moment to complete the challenge. Each level increases in difficulty, requiring focus and precision. The gameplay is simple but addictive, providing a relaxing and enjoyable entertainment experience!
