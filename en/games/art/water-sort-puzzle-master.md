## About

Water Sort Puzzle Master is a relaxing yet challenging logic puzzle where you organize colorful liquids into the correct tubes. Each level introduces more tubes and colors, increasing the difficulty and testing your strategy skills. Tap to pour water between tubes and sort each color correctly to complete the level. With smooth animations, satisfying liquid physics, and hundreds of levels to solve, this addictive puzzle game is perfect for players who enjoy brain-teasing challenges and relaxing gameplay.
