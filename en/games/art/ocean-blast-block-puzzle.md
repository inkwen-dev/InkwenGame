## About

Ocean Blast is a premium block puzzle game set in a stunning underwater world! Drag and drop ocean-themed blocks onto the grid, clear full rows and columns to score big, and chain combos for massive points. Features Classic endless mode and Adventure mode with 100 levels across 5 ocean worlds &mdash; coral reefs, kelp forests, sunken ships, and boss battles! Earn coins, use boosters, and beat your best score. Easy, Medium &amp; Hard with full progress saving!
