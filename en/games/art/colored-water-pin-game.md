## About

Colored Water Pin is a free online puzzle game played on PuzzleGame for boys. You need to complete the challenge in different order of pulling the pin. The water of different colors must get into the corresponding flasks, and you can enter the next level when they are all filled. The time to show off your casual game skills has come! Play it on Mobile, Tablet, or Desktop!
