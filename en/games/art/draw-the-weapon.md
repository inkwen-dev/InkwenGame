## About

Draw The Weapon is a free online action and drawing game for boys played on PuzzleGame. The game is divided into two parts. First, you need to draw your weapons with limited ink. Next, enter the battlefield, and then shoot the enemy down the cliff with weapons to win the battle. In addition to a variety of weapons, we also provide wonderful skins for you, Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop are playable!
