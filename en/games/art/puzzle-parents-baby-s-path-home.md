1. Click on the character to start drawing lines;
2. Draw a line to the kid, but avoid obstacles
3. Make sure all Sims get to their babies safely and back home with them.

## About

«Puzzle Parents: Baby's Path Home» is a fun puzzle game with unique characters (nubik, haggy waggie, kisi misi, rainbow friends) who will help you win. Draw smart to find the fastest way home with your little one!

At each level, players will face a unique puzzle filled with winding paths, obstacles and rewards. The main goal is to ensure that the child returns home safely using logic, problem-solving skills and a little creativity. However, it is not as easy as it seems!
