## About

This coloring book game has two modes. One is coloring mode in which you have lots of pre-drawn pictures that you have to color as you like. And the other mode is painting mode which gives you a blank paper to draw whatever you want using various tools! These tools include color pencils and markers, color buckets, stickers and etc. Whenever your work is done, you can save the picture or even share it with others via social media
