## About

Drawing For Kids is an amazing animal coloring game, you need to draw an animal following the guide. Once you have finished your job, every animal will magically revive, butterflies start to flutter and laugh, hedgehogs roll into a ball, and rockets fly into space. Have a good time in Drawing For Kids with these animals!
