## About

In Draw To Home, players use drawing mechanics to guide a character to their house. By sketching paths and obstacles, players navigate the character through challenges towards the destination. Its a creative blend of drawing and puzzle-solving, where each stroke determines the characters journey. As players progress, levels may become more complex, requiring strategic planning and precise drawing to overcome obstacles and safely reach home.
