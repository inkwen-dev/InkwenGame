Use MOUSE and LEFT-CLICK

## About

Smart Ball Colors is a fun and anti-stress puzzle game. You use your ball shooter to paint the shapes you come across. When sending the balls, watch out for the spinning wheel! The puzzle will not be completed if the balls hit it. You can use the coins you collect for extra life.
