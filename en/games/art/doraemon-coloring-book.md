## About

Doraemon Coloring Book is an online game that allows children to engage in a creative and interactive activity while expressing their love for the popular Japanese manga and anime series. The game features various coloring pages that depict scenes and characters from the show, including the iconic blue robot cat, Doraemon, and his friends Nobita, Shizuka, Gian, and Suneo.
