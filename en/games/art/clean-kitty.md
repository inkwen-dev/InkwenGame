## How to play

Look at where the tap is and where the tub is.
Hold and drag to draw a line. Lines become solid objects that the water flows along.
When you let go, the tap turns on. Movable objects (rubber ducks, soap bars, blue pipes and the tub) also start to fall. Plan your line with that in mind.
You can keep drawing while the water flows, as long as you have ink left.

How to win
Fill the tub until the gauge beside it is full, and the level is complete.
The more ink you have left, the more stars you get (up to 3).
You lose if you run out of ink before drawing a line, or if not enough water reaches the tub.

Watch out for
Glowing red plates: water that touches them turns to steam.
Levels with two taps: catch water from both.

Controls
Mouse: Hold the left mouse button and drag to draw. Release to finish the line.
Touch: Put your finger down and drag to draw. Lift your finger to finish.
Use the Hint button to see a suggested line when you're stuck.

## About

Clean Kitty is a cute physics puzzle game about drawing. Your kitty needs a bath, but the water from the tap won't reach the tub by itself. Draw lines with your finger or mouse to make ramps, walls and funnels that guide the water into the tub.

Every level is a small puzzle. Your ink is limited, so plan each line. Rubber ducks, soap bars and pipes drop the moment you finish drawing. Hot red plates turn water into steam, and some levels have two taps to catch at once. Fill the tub with as little ink as possible to earn three stars and a "Purr-fect!"

100 hand-made levels that start easy and get tricky
Many different ways to solve each puzzle
Relaxing, cozy look, short levels and hints when you're stuck
Available in 8 languages
