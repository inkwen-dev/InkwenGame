Choose a picture you like and turn a blank field into a bright pixel artwork. Pick the required colors and carefully fill the cells by following their numbers.

## How to play

Goal: completely color the selected picture. Find the number inside a cell, choose the color with the same number, and fill every matching cell. The picture is complete when every section has the correct color.

Desktop:
- Left mouse button — select a picture or color.
- Left mouse button on a cell — fill it with the selected color.

Phone and tablet:
- Tap a picture or color to select it.
- Tap the required cell to fill it.
