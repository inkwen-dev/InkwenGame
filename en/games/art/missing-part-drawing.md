## About

Puzzle game has never been so fun and exciting. Each level will be a new challenge for you, you will become an artist, a creator of ideas or even feel like a genius! With DOP Draw one part game to pass each level, you have to draw the missing part of the picture with a single line to complete the picture or to solve the problems that the characters in the picture are facing. A table with only 3 legs? What you need to do is draw another leg to help the table stand upright. A boy is about to propose to a girl but forgot to bring a ring? Help him achieve his goal right away by drawing a diamond ring. Has an apple been bitten by someone? Return the finished apple to its original shape with just a single line. And many other situations await you to overcome.
