## About

Beat Bugs Coloring is a puzzle coloring game with popular characters of the cartoon Beat Bugs. You can find eight different images with beat bugs. Choose your favorite image and start to color. You can color as the character look or you can add something else in the character. Try to color with different colors and give the character new look. On your right side on the game screen is the colors. Choose color and put on the image. You can save colored image and to print it later if you want. Start to color and have fun at this kids coloring game.
