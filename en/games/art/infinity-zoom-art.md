## About

Infinity Zoom Art is a puzzle game at yiv.com. This game provides lots of fun pictures and hidden objects for you to discover. As you can see from the name, some pictures are shrunk to very small buttons. You just need to click the buttons, and you can zoom in on the pictures to find objects. If you dont have any clue about finding objects, you can watch a reward ad and get 5 hints. The gameplay is very simple, but some objects are hard to find. This game tests your patience and observation ability.
