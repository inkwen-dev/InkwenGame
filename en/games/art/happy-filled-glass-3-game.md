## About

Happy Filled Glass 3 is a fun online puzzle game for kids for free. In this game, how to make the water fill in the cup below the pipe is a permanent question. Because there are many obstacles between the pipe and the cup. You can use the pencil to draw lines to overcome the effects of the obstacles. There are many levels in this game. Hopefully, you won't fail in level one. Who knows? Good luck!
