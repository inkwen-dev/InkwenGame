## About

In this game you will find various categories of coloring pages and a mode for drawing! In addition to an endless palette of colors and a variety of brushes in Fun Colors there is a ability to upload your own images and photos! So that your eyes dont get tired of being in front of the screen for a long time, you can switch the interface to night mode! Finished your masterpiece? - send it to print or save to your device without leaving the game!
