## How to play

Draw the picture the game suggests. Look at what other players are drawing if you're having trouble. If you can't draw it, click "Skip."

## About

A fast, funny drawing party game for up to 4 players. 
Get a word, draw it with your finger, and let the game guess what it is. The faster it guesses, the more points you earn. 
Play with friends or bots, complete 5 drawing challenges in 3 minutes, climb the podium, and unlock cute new animal avatars. 
Simple controls, hand-drawn style visuals, quick matchmaking, smooth touch drawing, cheerful sounds, and no losing state: you always gain points and experience, but only one player takes first place.
