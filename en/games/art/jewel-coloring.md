## How to play

Place gems in cells of the matching color to complete a diamond picture. Select a gem, then select where you want to move it. Use the buffer below the board for temporary storage.
Once all gems of one color are in place, extra buffer slots unlock. Watch an ad to get even more space. The wand, broom, and magnet can help you out of tricky situations.
On mobile, tap to select and move gems. Drag with one finger to move the board, and pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out.
On a computer, use the left mouse button to select and move gems. Hold the left mouse button and drag to move the board. Use the mouse wheel to zoom in or out.

## About

Jewel Coloring is a colorful puzzle game where precious gems turn into vibrant mosaics.

Simple controls, colorful gems, and satisfying animations make Jewel Coloring a relaxing way to unwind and exercise your logic.
