## How to play

## About

All kids love to draw and paint! Today we will find ourselves in the amazing underwater world, where many interesting inhabitants. Do you know what color the octopus is? It turns out that he can change his color. But the crocodile is always green! Test your knowledge by coloring the sea creatures. Use the movable panel on the right to select the desired color. Drag the palette panel with the left mouse button. Enjoy this new game "Sea creatures - coloring book"!
