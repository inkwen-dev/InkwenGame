## About

This is a type of Spot the Difference puzzle in which players must find 5 differences between two similar images in each level. The difference found will be marked with a yellow circle so that you don't go back to it. Do not mindlessly click on the pictures, in this case the game will be stopped for a few seconds. In this game, the differences are small, barely discernible, you will have to sweat to find them.
