## About

Friday Night Funkin games are a real musical battle, surrounded by a fascinating romantic plot. The user needs to hold out and win competitions, surpass a talented opponent, play melodies better than him. This can be done within the framework of the "story", consistently passing the proposed tasks, or at the levels of free play. Today you will have to paint all the heroes of this wonderful story!
