## How to play

🎨 Draw Your Way to Victory
Your creativity is the heart of the game! Use simple drawing mechanics to sketch the perfect path. Draw a ramp, a bridge, or a loop—every line you draw instantly transforms into a real, drivable road. But be careful: the realistic physics engine demands precision! A shaky curve or a steep climb could cause your vehicle to crash.

## About

Draw The Road: The Ultimate Physics Puzzle Game
Get ready for a unique blend of creative drawing and challenging physics puzzles! In Draw The Road, your finger acts as architect, engineer, and driver. Your mission is simple: draw the road, set up the layout, and get your vehicle safely to the finish line.
