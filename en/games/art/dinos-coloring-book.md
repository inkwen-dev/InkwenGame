## About

Coloring Dinosaurs Book is a painting, drawing and coloring game for kids! Hello my little friend, have fun with Dinosaurs in this fantastic Coloring App designed especially for you! It will be fantastic to watch your baby coloring, interacting, having fun and improving his technique thanks to this fantastic musical and educational application. The game is a classic color the dinosaurs but you can choose to color them freehand or you can choose the version where you click on the images to color them.
