## About

Welcome to the game where you will find 12 different pictures that need to be colored. They need to be colored and for this you are provided with a set of fifteen felt-tip pens of different bright colors. On the left you will see a set of circles of different diameters. You can also save the colored image. Have fun playing this Italian Brainrot Coloring Pages game!
