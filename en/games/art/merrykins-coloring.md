## About

Merrykins Coloring pages is a warm and cozy winter coloring game for kids and adults alike! Immerse yourself in New Year magic with cheerful festive music. Pick favorite colors, paint charming seasonal illustrations, or try the fun random fill mode. Use a wide range of tools: pencil, fill, eyedropper, eraser, multiple brushes, copy &amp; paste, and undo. Discover 30 delightful winter scenes: childrens, snowmen, kittens, puppies and bear cubs, Christmas tree, Santa Claus and the Snow Maiden, snowy adventures, and more. Each picture tells a small story you can make truly your own. Save your pictures as JPG and share them with friends! Have fun!
