## How to play

✏️ Draw lines or shapes anywhere on the screen.
⚽ Use your drawings to guide and push the two balls.
🧲 Take advantage of gravity and physics to control their movement.
🚧 Avoid obstacles and prevent the balls from falling away.
🤝 Your goal is to make both balls touch each other.
💡 Plan your drawing carefully and use as few lines as possible!
🎯 Complete the level when the two balls successfully meet.
