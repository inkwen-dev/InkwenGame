## About

Muscle Cars Coloring is a coloring kids game with eight levels. Choose from offered cars and start painting. Choose the color you want from the offers. Paint car parts in different colors or the whole car in one color. You can choose the thickness of the brush and reduce or control it if you want from what you will do. Use the eraser to remove any paint or mistakes if you have. Save the colored image.
