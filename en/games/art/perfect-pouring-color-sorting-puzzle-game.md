## About

Perfect Pouring - Color Sorting Puzzle Game is a fun and addictive puzzle game! Try to sort it and pour colored water in the bottles until all colors are in the same bottle. A relaxing and challenging game to train your brain HOW TO PLAY - Tap any glass bottle to pour water to another bottle. - The rule is that you can only pour the water to another bottle if it is linked to the same color and there's enough space on the glass bottle. - Try your best and don’t get stuck - but don’t worry, you can always restart the level at any time.
