## About

Snake: MLG Edition is a cool multiplayer snake game where you have to collect snacks, drinks and pizza to grow. Keep growing until you reach the top of the leaderboard. You can use the boost to move quickly in front of other snakes. Once they run into you, they will leave a trail of money that you can eat to get even bigger! The game has a lot of references to popular memes, both in audio and text, and in the form of quick GIFs. There are many different cool snakes to unlock.
