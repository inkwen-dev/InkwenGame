## About

GoTet.io is a tile matching growing game. You start with a 2x2 square and you move around the map, bumping into tiles and gluing them. The objective is to fill your square until the time is up. You have spells that help you and you can collect in-game coins to buy and equip charms that improve your game play. Can you get to the highest level?
