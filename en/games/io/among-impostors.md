Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

In Among Impostors, you have to expand your team by eating the foods found around. In order to be stronger against your enemies, you can get stronger by taking the bonuses on the surface. If the number of your enemies is more than you, your enemies will become stronger by eating you. You can use the protective shield, speed shoes and impostor group bonus to collect the most Impostor. With the points you will earn at the end of the game, you can have new impostors or strengthen your existing character. You can play other impostor games for free at meyagames.com.
