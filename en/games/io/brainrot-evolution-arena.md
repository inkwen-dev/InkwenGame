## About

Play an addictive evolution game where you control an Italian Brainrot creature in a fast-paced arena. Walk around, eat food to gain experience, and grow bigger with every level. As you evolve, your creature becomes stronger, faster, and more chaotic. Eat weaker Brainrot creatures instantly, avoid stronger enemies, and survive as long as possible. There are no complex attacks &mdash; victory depends on movement, positioning, and level advantage. Short sessions, simple mechanics, and constant action make this eat-and-grow Brainrot game highly replayable and perfect for quick fun.
