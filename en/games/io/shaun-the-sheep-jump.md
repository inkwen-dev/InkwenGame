## About

Help Shaun the Sheep run as far as you can, but be careful! He's holding an egg in the spoon! Collect as many stars as you can by throwing the egg into the air. The stars will reward you points. If the egg, or chicken, drops you will lose a life, if you lose all three lives you have to start over.
