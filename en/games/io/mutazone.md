## About

In this game, you will find yourself in the midst of a wave of attacks composed of zombies, mutants, and other creatures. You need to control your survivor, customizing them with various clothes, hairstyles, pets, and weapons. Your gun will fire automatically, so you need to move around the area and eliminate as many monsters as possible while avoiding their attacks.
