## About

CREATED ESPECIALLY FOR KIDS Feed the Monster uses an exciting and proven play-to-learn technique. Kids enjoy playing the different levels, collecting and growing their pet monsters, while learning the reading and writing fundamentals of Arabic. All Arabic content in the game is based on years of experience in research and content development for and by native Arabic speaking experts. It relates to all essential skills involved in reading such as Phonological Awareness, Phonics, Vocabulary and Comprehension. Fun and engaging letters, syllables and words puzzles Vocabulary memory games Arabic Reading and Writing lessons Challenging “Sound only” levels Easy to use interface Parental Progress report
