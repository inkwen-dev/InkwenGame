## About

Knife Ring IO is a fast-paced arcade game where players control spinning rings of knives and battle opponents in an arena. Collect knives, grow your ring, and strategically attack rivals while avoiding their blades. With simple controls and competitive gameplay, the goal is to outlast opponents and dominate the leaderboard.
