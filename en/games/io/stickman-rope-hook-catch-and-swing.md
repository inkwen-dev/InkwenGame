## About

Discover Stickman Rope Hook, the new game. Tap to hook and make a stunts, With your hero avoid all obstacles in your way and finish all levels. Can you execute all these acrobatic tricks in a row like a boss? In this game, embody the stickman Boy. Finish all the levels with the same agility as a Stickman. For that, you just have to: - Press your screen to hook your stickman with your grapnel and release to let go - multiple stickman character - Swing from hook to hook to pass all the levels Thanks to the bumpers and your grapnel, execute acrobatic tricks and show your friends who is the boss! You think you can do better than a stickman? Prove it! The faster you go, the more amazing your tricks will be.
