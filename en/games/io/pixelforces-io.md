## How to play

WASD or arrow keys to move Space bar to jump Right mouse button to aim Left mouse button to shoot L for toggle flashlight in night mode Esc or tab to toggle pause

## About

Pixel Forces is a challenging 3D shooting online game. You can choose from five different multiplayer modes and maps and customize your character. The environment is structured like a vertical maze with trampolines. You can create a room to play, or enter in an already created room. Features: • Five multiplayer game modes: RPG, Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Explore, and Battle Royale • 5 amazing and unique maps with trampoline platforms • Room options • 12 character skins
