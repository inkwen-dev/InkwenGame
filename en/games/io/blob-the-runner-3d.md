## About

Are you ready for a super fun run race? Blob will be your favorite runner of all times! Your goal is easy - Watch out for the obstacles, push them off and collect all jellies to become a GIANT blob! If you can’t escape from the deadly obstacles and lose a limb, or a few, don’t worry! Our friendly jelly can continue the running for you, no rush, just clash all jelly particles and sculpt your Blob from scratch! Remember, the price pool at the end will be determined by your ability to get your Blob bigger. Get bigger, stronger and super jelly to collect special rewards! We may also have some surprises as you slide on the roof :) Wait! Did we mention you can also make over your Blob with super cool hats and plenty of color matching skins?! Join this epic race and show your unique style by customizing your Blob Runner :)
