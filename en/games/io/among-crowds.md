Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Players start as small crewmates in a crowded space station. The goal is to collect items scattered across the map to grow in size and strength. As your crewmates grow, you can consume smaller crewmates and absorb their crewmates, becoming even larger and more powerful. Strategic growth and hiding are key&mdash;avoid larger crewmates while planning to eat weaker ones.
