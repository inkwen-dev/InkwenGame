## About

Take the fastest route possible, dodging trains and buses about to arrive. Keep an eye out for the rolling timber in the forest! Run by changing direction on zigzag using intuitive controls. Help your beloved, lovely Squid character to escape from the clutches of the authorities! After double tapping, use the skateboard to feel for the one-of-a-kind board in the subway. Players worldwide compete for the highest score in the rank, and scores are shared in a friend list. Increase your score multiplier by unlocking the next level. Complete tasks or boxes to earn experience points that will allow you to advance to the next level. The more the number of score multipliers you have, the higher the score you receive.
