## About

Party.io 2 is much more fun now with its renewed backgrounds! You can choose one of the fun characters and start the game. All you have to do in the game is to touch the players and throw them out of the arena. You can see the kill count on the left of the screen. Be the last person in the arena and be the winner of the game! Have fun!
