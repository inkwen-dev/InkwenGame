## About

Fast Jump 3D is a fun action game. Our goal in this game will be to jump from blocks quickly. That's why you have to be pretty fast. Otherwise, the blocks will disappear after a few seconds. Collect diamonds for more points. Good luck to everyone in this game, which is the HTML5 version of the "Fast Jump 3D" android game.
