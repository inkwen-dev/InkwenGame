## About

Swing one circle around the other, plant a leg to switch which ones spinning, and use momentum as your only weapon. Collect glowing pickups to fill your meters, smash through spike balls and rival spinners with a charged leg, and take down a boss bumper to unlock home decoration. Then head into the shared multiplayer world &mdash; paint your territory, duel other players, and chase a set of achievements as you go. Simple to pick up, with a genuinely different feel than anything else in the genre.
