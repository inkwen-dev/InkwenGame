## About

Do you have what it takes to become the MASTER OF CUPS? The idea is simple: move the balls from one cup to another until they end up in the goal cup. It’s easier said than done! This game contains endless levels with tricky set up: sometimes there’s one ball, sometimes two balls. Sometimes there are obstacles, or slides to help you. The trickiness never ends!
