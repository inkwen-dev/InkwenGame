## How to play

You can choose between many different pets sheep rams cats horses dogs and even ducks Unlock new pets and upgrade the behaviour of each pet you choose You can increase their speed of movement

## About

This is a simple game, easy to learn, but difficult to master. You will become more and more addicted to this game. Eat all kinds of garden crops around the farm, but make sure not to be trapped by farmers or other dangers in the farm The more you move around and eat crops with the flock, the more their numbers will increase.
