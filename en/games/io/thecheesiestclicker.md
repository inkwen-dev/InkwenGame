## About

Welcome to the cheesiest clicker on the internet! Tap the giant cheese wheel to earn cheese, then reinvest it into 33 upgrades &mdash; from a Tiny Mouse all the way to The Final Cheese itself. Hire farmers, build factories, mine ancient cheese, and eventually bend reality across the galaxy. Keep the clicks coming to build up a combo multiplier and trigger explosive critical hits. Watch for the golden cheese that floats across the screen &mdash; click it fast for a huge bonus! When youve earned enough, prestige to reset your run for permanent Moldy Cheese, which powers up everything forever and unlocks an exclusive shop of permanent upgrades!
