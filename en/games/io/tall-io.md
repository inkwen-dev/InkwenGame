## How to play

Understanding the &controls& in Tall.io is crucial for success. Players use the arrow keys or WASD keys to navigate their &stickman& throughout the arena. The goal is to collect &cubes&, &coins&, and &bricks& scattered around the arena to increase &height&. The spacebar is used to attack or &smash& opponents, making strategic use of acquired &swords& and &armor&.

## About

Tall.io is an engaging and addictive browser-based HTML5 game that combines elements of growth strategy and battle-arena action. Players control a &stickman& character in a dynamic arena where they must &grow taller& by collecting &bricks& and &rainbow orbs&, ultimately enhancing their size and strength. This &multiplayer& game invites players to compete on various islands, using their &stickman& avatars to fight for dominance. The game stands out for its vibrant graphics, seamless &online gameplay experience&, and thrilling competition.
