## About

grab.io is a fast land-grab arcade game in the paper.io style, set on a round arena packed with hungry bots. Sneak out of your territory, draw a loop and slip back home to claim everything inside. Steal land from rivals, snatch stars for bonus points and dodge every trail &mdash; one touch is fatal. Outgrow the bots, top the board and wear the crown.
