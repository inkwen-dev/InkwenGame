## About

Leafino is a 2D platform game where you play as a leaf who needs to collect orbs while avoiding the fire ball enemies at any cost. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. Controls: Use WASD or Arrow keys for movement Press "W" or "Up Arrow" key twice for double jump.
