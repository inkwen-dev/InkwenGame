## About

Ice Dash is a fun and frosty adventure where you draw icy lines to guide Santa through exciting levels. Help him slide across your custom paths, avoid icy traps, ride moving platforms, and collect yummy cookies along the way! Watch out for obstacles and keep your balance &ndash; the challenge gets harder as you go. Can you get every cookie and make Santa dash to victory?
