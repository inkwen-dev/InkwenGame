## About

Tailoff.io: Snake Dragon Arena is a fast-paced survival game set in a dangerous dungeon arena. Guide your snake, devour creatures, collect scales and grow from a small hatchling into a powerful hunter. Outsmart rival snakes, defeat bosses, unlock new skins and discover elemental dragon transformations with special attacks. Avoid larger enemies until you are strong enough to challenge them, use power-ups wisely and climb the arena ranking. Play with mouse controls on desktop or touch controls on mobile.
