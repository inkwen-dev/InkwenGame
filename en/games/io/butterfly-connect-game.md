## About

Butterfly connect The simplest matching pair is when same-species butterflies are located next to each other. This can occur on the edge of the board or somewhere in the middle. Because they are easy to spot, I usually look for these simple pairs first, to help clear out the center of the board. An alternative strategy might be to save some of these obvious matches for later, when you might need some quick matches to pump up the timer.
