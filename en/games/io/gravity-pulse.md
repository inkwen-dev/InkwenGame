## About

Gravity Pulse is a minimalist arcade survival game where the player navigates a small arena, balancing risk and reward. The game is built around Attractor Fields &mdash; invisible forces that guide player behavior, creating flow, tension, and emergent mastery. Core Fun: Discover invariants in a chaotic system while being subtly guided by invisible attractor fields. Player Goal: Survive as long as possible and collect points.
