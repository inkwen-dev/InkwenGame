## About

PenguinBattle.io is fun addictive hyper casual io game. you control a little penguin with which you are fighting on the iceberg. Your task will be to push the other penguins out of this iceberg. If you stay as the last, you win. It is such an ice battle royale where the last living penguin wins. Parts of the iceberg fall off every few seconds.
