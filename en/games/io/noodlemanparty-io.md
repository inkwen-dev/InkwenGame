## About

In the 3D multiplayer game Party.io, all players will fight on the same platform. Once thrown out of the platform, they will fail. If you are attacked, you will faint. If you faint, you will be thrown out. So attack other players and don't make them hit you. React faster to win the war, have fun in the Party.io!
