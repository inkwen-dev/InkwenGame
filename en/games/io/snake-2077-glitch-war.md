## How to play

Objective: Slither through the Cyberpunk arena, collecting neon food to grow longer.
How to Win: Cut off other snakes to destroy them and eat their remains for massive points. Climb to the #1 spot on the leaderboard!
Controls:
Desktop: Move Mouse to steer. Hold Left Click to Boost (consume mass to speed up).
Mobile: Use the on-screen Joystick to steer. Tap the Lightning Button to Boost.

## About

Welcome to Snake 2077: Glitch War, a futuristic evolution of the classic snake IO genre.
Dive into a chaotic, neon-lit digital arena where the only rule is survival. Collect energy pellets to grow your cyber-snake, use tactical speed boosts to cut off opponents, and scavenge coins from their remains. Featuring stunning glitch art aesthetics, smart AI bots with unique personalities, and a massive collection of unlockable skins, every match is a fight for dominance.
Can you survive the glitch and top the global leaderboard?
