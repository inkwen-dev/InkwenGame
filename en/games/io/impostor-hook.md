## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

In Impostor Hook game, you must use the hook chain to pull the enemies on the opposite side to our side. After guiding your character, it will automatically throw the chain and if the chain falls on the Impostor, you will score. If the other side imprison you with their chain, you will be punished for 2 seconds. You can unlock 15 different Impostor characters with the points you earn in the game. You can play all impostor games for free at meyagames.com.
