## About

A powerful tornado can destroy everything! Especially the water tornado formed from the sea. In Super Tornado.io this multiplayer online game, you will become the master of the world. You can control the city by the sea at will, blow away all the buildings and cars, and compete with other players. Tornadoes fight, swallow them and absorb them as part of your own power. When you have enough power, don't be afraid of any other players, use tornadoes to rule the world until you are satisfied.
