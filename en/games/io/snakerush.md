## About

SnakeRush is a fast-paced arcade snake game where your goal is to grow as big as possible while surviving against other snakes. Collect delicious snacks, unlock unique skins, and beat your highest score. Use speed boosts strategically to outplay opponents and dominate the leaderboard. Features: - Smooth and responsive controls - Fast-paced arcade gameplay - Unlock multiple snake skins - Collect snacks to grow bigger - Compete for the highest score - Play instantly in your browser - Optimized for desktop and mobile devices
