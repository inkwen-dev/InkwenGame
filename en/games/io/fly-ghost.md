## About

Fly Ghost - It's a fun game in a simple but challenging way, a ghost slowly flies by the man dodging pipes and without touching the ground. Gameplay: You need to constantly control the frequency of clicks on the screen to adjust the flight height and landing speed of the bird, so that the bird can smoothly pass through the tube space on the right side of the screen. If the ghost accidentally cleans and touches the tube, you lose.
