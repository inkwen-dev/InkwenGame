## About

Are you fed up with normal running race? You don't hear it wrong. Collect the bricks, wooden planks, or decks and stack them into piles. Then take a shortcut from the main race by building the bridge over the sea. By this way, you can run faster than other competitors and win the game. But don't be too confident, if the pile of brick is not enough to connect 2 points of the racing road, you will fall! Remember to stack enough decks to finish the map. Among us, only one takes the champion. Is it you?
