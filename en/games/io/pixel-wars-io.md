## About

Collect pixels and draw beauty with them! The game has elements of hyper casual clicker and pixel art. Here you can not only collect pixels but also draw 2D images on the drawing arena with their help. This is a game for all ages and experience levels: clicker lovers, pixel art lovers and those who just want to draw will love it. Want to have more drawing pixels? Collect power-ups: slow down the movement of obstacles, doubling the won pixels, a magnet to attract bonuses and an extra life.
