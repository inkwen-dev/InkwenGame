## About

Castle Wars: Legacy is a tactical multiplayer card game where you build your deck, defend your castle, and break through enemy walls. Stack resources, summon armies, cast spells, and outsmart your opponent in fast-paced strategic duels. Battle friends online, climb daily tournaments, conquer the campaign, or fight AI in quick matches. Collect dozens of unique cards, build custom decks, and unlock new castle skins. Easy to learn, deep to master.
