## How to play

Space to attack or do an action use mouse to solve tasks Vampire R to become invisible T to transform

## About

Full Multiplayer game like Among Us Instead of the impostor be a mighty Vampire. Villagers defend your village! The vampire is one among you. Don't let the vampire win. The game is tricky nobody knows who the vampire is in the beginning. But he becomes stronger over time. Villagers can make weapons and other equipment by solving tasks. Go to remote places like the mines or the forest to get valuable resources, but always be cautious because the vampire can become invisible. For more info read the tutorial in the game
