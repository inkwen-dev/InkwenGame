## About

Idle Fall Balls is the new addicting simulation game. Watch the different balls (football, tennis, golf, pool, volleyball, rugby) fall down through the various obstacles. Click near the balls to control them and direct them in the right direction. Upgrade your balls to generate more and more coins, improve your skills and traits.
