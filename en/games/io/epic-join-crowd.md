## About

In Epic Join Crowd, a fun-filled hypercasual game, you have to save the prison warriors and destroy the boss monster. Recruit warriors to your team by avoiding deadly obstacles, approaching prisons and rescuing warriors. In order to destroy the deadly boss at the end of the level, your team must consist of strong warriors. Try Epic Join Crowd for a smooth game experience with 50 different levels and beautiful graphics!
