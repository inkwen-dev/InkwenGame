## About

Help out this rushed cheerleader to get ready for her date in this makeover game. Not only that you will transform her, but you will be her personal guidance in fashion matters too. Treat her face with special masks and clean the skin for the following makeup. Choose wisely, match the manicure and the hairstyle with the final look. Either you will dress her up in a specific outfit or in a dress, make sure she attends at her date.
