## How to play

Move across the falling hex tiles and stay above the void for as long as possible. Every step breaks the floor, so plan your moves carefully. Avoid falling, survive longer than everyone else, and become the last player standing.
PC controls: Use Arrow keys or WASD to move
Mobile controls: Use the on-screen joystick to move
