## About

Prepare for war with different barrels in Barrel Wars. Take a small hit so that the barrel does not break and you must swing your weapon accurately to destroy the enemy. Action-packed arena battles await you in the multiplayer Barrel Wars game. Thanks to daily and hourly bonuses, you will be able to earn money quickly and strengthen your character. If you want to gain an advantage over other players, you have to fight and win many battles. Upgrade your warrior with 16 different weapons with unique mechanics and 7 different barrel characters! Note that sometimes character power-ups can fail. Grab your gun now and join the barrel wars!
