## About

Limax.io is a funny io game where you need to grow fatter and fatter. Kill others and eat them to grow into the fattest limax ever! You start as a small slug, and you must eat food orbs that appear around you. The more you eat, the bigger you get and the faster you dash. To kill another player, you must speed up and make them fall into the trail you leave behind. Your ultimate goal is to reach the top of the leaderboard.
