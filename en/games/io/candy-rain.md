## About

A fun and addictive casual game where your goal is to catch falling candies using a bucket and score as many points as possible. Stay focused and react quickly &mdash; not everything falling from the sky is safe! Avoid dangerous bombs while collecting sweet candies to keep your game going. With colorful visuals, smooth gameplay, and increasing difficulty, every level challenges your speed and reflexes. How long can you survive and reach the highest score?
