## How to play

Race to the finish in this frantic multiplayer platformer! Navigate through obstacle-filled stages alongside up to 9 other players in real time. Dodge hazards, ride moving platforms, and unleash your dash to surge ahead of the pack!

Desktop Controls:
← / → (Arrow Keys or A/D): Move left and right.
↑ (Up Arrow) / W / Space: Jump — double jump supported!
Shift: Dash — burst forward for a speed boost.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Drag to move left and right.
J Button: Tap to jump.
D Button: Tap to dash forward.

## About

DashMash.io is a chaotic multiplayer obstacle race where up to 10 players battle it out in real time! Jump, dash, and dodge your way through stages packed with moving platforms, swinging hammers, crumbling blocks, and deadly spikes. Each round cycles to a brand-new map, forcing you to adapt on the fly. Reach the finish line first to claim the top spot on the leaderboard — if the chaos doesn't get you first!
