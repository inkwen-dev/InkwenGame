## About

Missile Escape is a simple, fast paced and addictive 2D game where your mission is to dodge all homing missiles that comes towards you with one single objective: shoot you down! Use the simple control to fly your plane and avoid the missiles. Make them collide with each other and collect stars to increase final score.
