## About

Collect resources, woods, crystals and ancient bones of dinosaurs in this unique Idle game. Let your lumberjacks chop the woods, or go yourself under the ground to dig & mine valuable resources Go underground and carve the way to your character to collect resources, but do not touch the explosive dynamites and bombs. The mining is endlessly generated.
