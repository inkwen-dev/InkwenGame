## About

Enter a chaotic block-eating adventure where size is everything! Control a hungry black hole, move around the map, and swallow blocks, objects, and structures to grow larger. Start small and consume everything you can find to increase your size and power. As you grow, bigger blocks become available and you can clear the environment faster. Compete against the clock, collect as much as possible, and become the biggest hole on the map. How large can you grow?
