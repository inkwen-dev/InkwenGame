## About

Imposter Duck : Online is an online multiplayer social deduction game where every match is different.You and the other ducks are trapped inside a high-tech space station. Most players are innocent crew members working together to complete missions and keep the station operational. However, one or more players secretly become the Imposter.The Imposter blends in with the crew while sabotaging systems, creating chaos, and eliminating players one by one. Crew members must stay alert, cooperate, and identify suspicious behavior before its too late.
