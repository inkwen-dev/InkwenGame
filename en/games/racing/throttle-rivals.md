## How to play

Hold and drag to accelerate and steer. On a keyboard, hold Space or Enter and steer with A/D or arrow keys. Dodge traffic, collect boosters and finish first. Spend earned coins on cars and upgrades.

## About

Race through traffic, grab boosters and outpace three rivals. Unlock and upgrade 20 cars across 50 highway races, then keep racing in endless mode. Climb the leaderboard with your total coins earned.
