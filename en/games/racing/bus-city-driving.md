## About

City Bus Driving Simulator game is especially designed to test your driving and gaming skills. In this game you will drive through different cities having multiples levels which are to be completed. User can select bus of its own choice, city environment and then have to accomplish various challenging tasks to get reward. User will have to move the passengers around the city to drop them to their desired destination by overcoming the real traffic environment and challenges.
