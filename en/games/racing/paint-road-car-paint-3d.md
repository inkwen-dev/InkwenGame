## About

Paint roads with roller cars! move your car in a maze city puzzle! Advance to the next level of the puzzle by painting (coloring) all of the roads in a maze puzzle with bright colors with your car. Complete each level by painting all the paths. Tap to make each car race. Be careful, don't let the cars collide! Try to pass over all tricky obstacles in this fun line-coloring and brain training 3D game. Tap to make your car slide along the path – watch your cars surfing to the finish line! Make sure there is no empty or unpainted (uncolored) road in the Paint Road - Car Paint 3D maze puzzle! Why you’ll love Paint Road - Car Paint 3D: • Dozens of challenging levels • Simple & intuitive controls • Beautiful minimalistic graphics Think logically and complete the Levels, Advance all levels and become the best player! Good luck on your travels!
