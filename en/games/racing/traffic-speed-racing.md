## About

In the game Traffic Speed Racing, we together with a young guy will take part in races with obstacles. These can be mines, pits and other hazards. You sat behind the wheel of the car will have to fly on the highway at the maximum possible speed and meet in a strictly allotted time. All obstacles you will have to pass by at speed. Also on the road can be bonus items.
