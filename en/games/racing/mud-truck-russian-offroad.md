## About

Mud Truck Russian Offroad is a driving simulation game adventure in which you need to drive extreme 4x4 off-road Mud Truck to climb hills by overcoming the cliff obstacles and dirty roads. For the uphill driver, the sky-high tracks are made up of narrow hills and big dirty mountains. So get ready for some real offroad driving adventure fun by performing impossible truck stunts over dangerous and curvy dirty off-road tracks.
