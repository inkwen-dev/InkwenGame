## How to play

On PC: 
WASD - control the car 
Hold RMB and move the mouse - control the camera 
Space - jump 
TAB - pause 
R - back to the start

On mobile: 
on-screen controls

## About

Every new car increases your speed, helping you conquer even tougher levels. Earn rewards, collect powerful boosts, unlock rare supercars, and keep improving your performance. Rebirth to gain permanent bonuses and become even faster with every run.

Enjoy simple controls, colorful graphics, a wide variety of vehicles, and endless progression. Challenge yourself, master every obstacle, and prove you have what it takes to become the ultimate keyboard parkour champion!
