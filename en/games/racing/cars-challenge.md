## How to play

Play different modes, complete missions, buy new cars and tuning them!

Controls:
TAB - Pause
Spacebar - Jump
Double Space - Airplane mode
Move - WASD
C - Camera Change

For mobile devices use the game interface!

## About

Welcome to the game about cars, in which you can play in different modes compete with bots, perform tasks and tuning your new cars!

Modes in the game:

- Arena:
Clash with other cars and survive in the arena!

- Spinning Wheel:
The obstacle wheel is spinning, your task is to get to the finish line faster than other cars and not fall into the abyss!


- Hexagons:
Hexagons fall down as soon as a car passes over them.
Show your dexterity in steering and jumping to stay last in the arena and win.

- Insane Track:
On the crazy track you will have to go through a bunch of challenges and obstacles to get to the end of the crazy track faster than the other cars and win!

And much more!
