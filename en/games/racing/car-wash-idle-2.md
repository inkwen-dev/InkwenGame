## About

Car Wash Idle is a fun 3D idle arcade management game where you build and grow your own car wash business! Clean dirty cars, collect cash, unlock new washing stations, upgrade your equipment, and increase your earnings. Expand your car wash with better machines and new areas, improve washing speed and capacity, and hire helpers to automate your business. Keep upgrading, serve more cars, earn more money, and turn your small car wash into the ultimate car cleaning empire!
