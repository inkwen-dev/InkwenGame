## About

Crazy Lemmings 2 is a fast-paced and addictive arcade game where your mission is to save the reckless lemmings as they leap off the cliffs! Take control of the rescue craft, move quickly, and bounce the lemmings to safety before they hit the ground. The more lemmings you save, the higher your score. Test your reflexes, challenge your skills, and see how many crazy lemmings you can rescue!
