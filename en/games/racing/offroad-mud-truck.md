## About

Offroad Mud Truck is an exciting 3D truck driving game where you take powerful trucks through muddy roads, rocky trails, steep hills, and challenging off-road environments. Transport heavy cargo, overcome difficult terrain, and complete challenging missions without getting stuck. Test your driving skills as you carefully control the truck through deep mud, sharp turns, and rough paths. Earn rewards, complete levels, and unlock new trucks as you progress. With realistic physics, smooth controls, and immersive 3D environments, Offroad Mud Truck delivers a thrilling off-road driving experience.
