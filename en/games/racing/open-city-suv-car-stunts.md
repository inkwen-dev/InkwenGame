## How to play

W / Up Arrow accelerate
S / Down Arrow brake
A / Left Arrow steer left
D / Right Arrow steer right
Space handbrake
ESC - pause / options

## About

Get Behind the Wheel and Enjoy an Amazing Realistic SUV Simulator!

Jump off ramps, perform crazy stunts, overcome obstacles, and explore a huge open city — all without worrying about the rules of the road!

In Free Ride Mode you can enjoy complete freedom with plenty of ramps, jumps, obstacles, and places to perform incredible car stunts.

Want to enjoy the scenery? Take your SUV and explore the city, discover hidden locations, and find new places to jump, crash, and show off your driving skills!

But there’s more than just stunts.

Your main mission is to deliver illegal packages** throughout the city while staying one step ahead of the police. Complete risky deliveries, avoid police chases, hide from the cops, and escape before they catch you!

Choose from detailed SUVs with **visual and performance tuning**. Customize your vehicle and build the perfect off-road machine for both stunts and dangerous deliveries.

Enjoy complete freedom, explore the open city, perform insane SUV stunts, deliver illegal packages, and escape the cops in Open City SUV!
