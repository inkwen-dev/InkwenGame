## How to play

Controls:
WASD - drive
Space - handbrake
Shift - boost
C - change camera
R - reset car
N - new vehicle
K - repair
B - slow motion
Tab - pause

Mobile users: tap on-screen buttons.

Objective: wreck, tune, and explore to your heart's content!
