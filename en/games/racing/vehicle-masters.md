## About

Vehicle Masters is a minimalist arcade parking challenge with over 40 increasingly difficult levels. Maneuver your car through tight spaces, avoid obstacles, and park perfectly in the marked spot. Each level introduces new layouts &mdash; narrow alleys, moving barriers, sharp corners, and crowded lots. What makes it unique? Simple controls with realistic physics: steering plus manual gear shifting (forward and reverse). On desktop you control gears manually with keys; on mobile you only steer &mdash; gears switch automatically for smooth gameplay.
