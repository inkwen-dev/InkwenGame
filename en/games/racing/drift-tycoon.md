Drift Tycoon is a casual idle game about cars, racing and building your own vehicle collection.

## How to play

Buy cars from the car panel. Each car generates income while driving around the track.

Spend your coins on new cars and level upgrades. Upgrades increase your income and help you grow your garage faster.

Complete tasks to receive extra rewards. The achievements panel shows your goals and progress.

When you have enough progress, use prestige. It resets your current progress but gives prestige points and unlocks new perks.

Perks increase income, reduce upgrade costs and provide other useful bonuses. Compare your results with other players on the leaderboard.
