## About

Cosmic Sprint is an exhilarating space-themed action game where you guide your UFO through a galaxy filled with obstacles and dangers. Navigate through an asteroid field while avoiding space mines and meteor showers, all while collecting cosmic energy to unlock new spacecrafts. The vibrant cosmic environment and thrilling gameplay make it a fast-paced, addictive experience that will test your reflexes and keep you on the edge of your seat!
