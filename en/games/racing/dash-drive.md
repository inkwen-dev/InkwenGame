## How to play

Arrow Keys - To drive the spaceship Z - Shoot missiles X - Drop mines behind the space ship

## About

Dash Drive is an endless survival game where you play as the captain of the space ship. The space ship is under attack by lot of missiles and mines from all directions. Your goal is to evade them or shoot them down with your own missiles and mines and staying alive as long as possible. Note : You cannot shoot another missile till your first missile is in the world. You got to move around to stay alive.
