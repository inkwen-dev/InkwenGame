## About

Build A Truck is a very fun game. In this game, you can assemble the coolest truck by yourself. We've prepared different kinds of truck frames, tires, fancy stickers, beautiful colors,and cool accessories for you. You can try every item one by one to find the perfect match. Hope you can assemble the truck that you really like. After assembling the truck, come and take your car for a run around the track. 3 different tracks full of surprises are waiting for you to conquer. Invite your friends to play this game with you and have fun!
