## About

Car Jam Escape is a puzzle game where players clear a crowded parking lot by moving cars in the correct order. Each vehicle can only move forward in the direction it faces, so players must study the layout and plan carefully. The goal is to guide every car out without collisions or blockages. As levels progress, the puzzles become more complex, requiring logical thinking and strategy. With simple rules and challenging traffic jams, the game offers a fun and brain-teasing experience for puzzle lovers.
