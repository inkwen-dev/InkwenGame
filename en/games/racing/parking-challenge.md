Use arrow keys or on screen buttons to drive

## About

Your task in the game Parking Challenge is to find the place intended for you and install the car there. Maneuvering between cars and trucks, try not to touch them with the bumper. Also, do not run into concrete curbs. One collision and you have to start the level all over again.
