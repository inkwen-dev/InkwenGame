## How to play

You can move the car with the W A S D or Arrow keys Can use nitro with F Can turn on lamps with L Can use handbrake with Space You can switch cameras with C

## About

Get ready for action and fun! Take action-packed jumps from the ramps above the traffic flowing in the city in Meya City Stunt game. Complete missions in 15 different levels and earn money. You can also find meyacoins in hidden places on the map. Do the task if you want, or navigate the city and the ramps in free mode. With the MeyaCoins you earn, you can buy new and faster cars. Meya City Car Stunt game with 5 different sports cars is waiting for you! Have fun.
