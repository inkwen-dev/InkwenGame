## About

Bike Stunt Game is a thrilling 2.5D arcade racer where you perform insane tricks on a motorbike. Choose from three modes &ndash; Time Trial (beat the clock), Trick Challenge (earn style points), or Survival (avoid crashes at all costs). With a unique side-scrolling perspective that blends 2D gameplay with 3D depth, youll navigate ramps, loops, and obstacles across a handful of hand-crafted levels. What makes it unique? The 2.5D view gives you depth perception for landing perfect jumps while keeping simple side-scroller controls. Unlock new bikes as you master each level. Works flawlessly on desktop and mobile browsers.
