## How to play

🖥 PC Controls
	•	W, A, S, D — Move character
	•	Space — Jump
	•	Right mouse button + mouse movement — Camera control

📱 Mobile Controls
	•	Left joystick — Move character
	•	On-screen button — Jump
	•	Right side of the screen — Camera control
