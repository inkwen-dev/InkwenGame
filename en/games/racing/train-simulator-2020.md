## How to play

- Drag the Accelerator and Brake to control the movement of the train - Change camera angle clicking Camera icon

## About

Drive your train in some beautiful landscape in this Train Simulator 3D game. Drag the brake and accelerator to start moving the train and reach the destination station in each level. However you have to look for other trains and signals, to reach your Exit point safely. Have fun!
