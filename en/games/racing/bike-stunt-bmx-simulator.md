## About

Ride your BMX on big ramps and get crazy air, or get technical with street skating. Do huge flips and stunts, or chain together awesome combos with manuals, grinds and wallrides. The choice is yours with 7 different skate parks made with inspiration from all around the world. You can even create your very own skate parks to ride! Both your rider and your BMX bike are fully customizable, with tons of options to make yours absolutely unique!
