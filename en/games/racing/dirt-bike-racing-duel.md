## About

What about riding your Dirt Bike over seven continents in lots of different countries, lands, and places? The places where you'll ride your bike is prepared for the dirt bike duel races. Your goal is to get your bike to the finish line without crashing. You can play Dirt Bike in single or in two player modes. You can duel against a friend in 2 player game mode. There are lots of levels and exciting surprises inside these levels that will be waiting for you! Check the shop sometimes and don't forget to buy new bikes or upgrade your current bike!
