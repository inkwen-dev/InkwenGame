## About

Untangle a colorful toy-town traffic jam in BEEP OUT! Tap each car to send it in the direction of its roof arrow, clear blocked lanes, and pick the right order to free the board. Across 30 levels, match vehicles with waiting passengers and manage limited parking spaces. Undo a move or use a hint when you get stuck.
