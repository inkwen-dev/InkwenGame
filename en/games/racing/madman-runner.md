## About

Madman Runner is a classic runner game in which you help the character survive on a dangerous road. He had urgent business for which he was racing, not sparing his legs. Help him quickly respond to an emerging obstacle: a car, tires, bombs and other objects. At the same time, you definitely need to collect coins and all kinds of food so as not to become exhausted from endless running.
