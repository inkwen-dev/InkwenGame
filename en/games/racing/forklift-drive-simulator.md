## About

Even dream how to drive, transport crates & pallet in a forklifter and park a forklift. In this driving & parking mania simulation game, you’ll be able to perform transportation duty across the airport, seaport & city. Your job is to pick heavy crates and keep it accurately in airplanes and grand cargo trucks. You get to drive a forklift, move a pallet around and do crazy stunts. Drive your fork lift and carefully pick the crates and then move them to a special drop off zone.
