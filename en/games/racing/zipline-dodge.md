## About

Zipline Dodge challenges players to navigate a perilous course while suspended on a zipline. Dodge obstacles and pitfalls as you soar through lush jungles, towering mountains, and futuristic cityscapes. With intuitive controls and adrenaline-pumping action, players must exhibit lightning-fast reflexes to conquer each level. Collect power-ups, unlock new characters, and race against friends in this thrilling adventure that combines speed, skill, and strategy.
