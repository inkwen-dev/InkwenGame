## About

Master truck drivers are ready to prove their skills on challenging snowy roads! Check out the Missions section or compete in Races to unlock new and more powerful trucks. There are 12 OffRoad Trucks in total in the Truck Driver: Snowy Roads and they all have different styles, power and driving. If you want, you can also try your luck in the Free Drive section. Maybe there will be random missions where you can get coins! Choose the music you want from the radio and start the adventure!
