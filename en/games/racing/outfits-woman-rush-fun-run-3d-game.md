## About

Outfits Woman Rush is a captivating casual game. There is probably a pursuit of beauty in every girl. As the designs and colors of clothes grow more and more dazzlingly delicate these days, women of average income cannot afford to buy all of the newest clothes. However, this game could be an alternative to that. You can pick up as many clothes as you can when you walk your way. You should dodge the obstacles or your clothes will be stripped off!
