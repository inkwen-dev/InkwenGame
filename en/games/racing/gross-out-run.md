## About

Gross Out Run is a parkour game. Compete against two other AI runners and the first player to reach the finish line wins! Be careful, you have to avoid the sledgehammers on the road, which will block your progress and stain your clothes simultaneously. Its time to make a change! Can you get to the finish line first and keep yourself tidy?
