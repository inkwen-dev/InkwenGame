## About

Time to show your skills by making a puppet walk in the Balanced Walking game! This walk will be a little hard at first. You should decide how long you need to hold the button very carefully. If you hold too much the character will make a long step and this may cause fail. Try to make your steps balanced and get the character to the finish! You can play this challenge in 2 player mode and have much more fun with your friend! Lets get it begun!
