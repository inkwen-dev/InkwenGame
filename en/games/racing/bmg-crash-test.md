## How to play

Destroy various wheelbarrows on large maps with lots of hammers, crushers, ramps, presses, and obstacles!

Controls:
TAB - Pause
B - Slow down time
N - next car


Move - WASD
Spacebar - Handbrake
C - Camera Change

For mobile devices use the game interface!
