## About

Car Evolution Driving is not merely a collecting running arcade game but also an automobile parts management game with 3D stickman characters and cartoon vehicles. In collecting parts, you can collect items to make your car more and more advanced. When you collect enough parts of a target car, you can drive it to the show platform automatically.
