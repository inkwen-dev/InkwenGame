## About

K Game Glass Bridge Survival is recommended as a third-person jumping arcade game with 3D Squid Game roles. In order to survive and complete the current level, you need to jump and overpass the glass bridge at each level. You will be provided with the correct track to the finish platform. As long as you memorize them and jump with following orders. The squares will increase after you overpass each level. Good luck and have a good time!
