## How to play

Use ARROW keys to drive a car

## About

Let's drive super strong unstoppable cars and smash everything in your way. Everything your car intersects is crashing, smashing and will be destroyed. The more things you will smash the more cars and locations you will open. Features: - 5 cars; - 3 locations: big city, canyon and countryside; - smash glass, destroy trees and rocks, walls and fences and even bus stops; - do not destroy road signs - this reduces points.
