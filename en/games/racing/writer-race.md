## About

Introducing Writer Race &ndash; a thrilling wordplay adventure! In this game, youll step into the shoes of a swift scribe, dashing through literary landscapes. Your mission? Rewrite the narrative! With only 80 words at your disposal, craft a tale that captivates, amuses, or astounds. But beware, the clock is ticking! Challenge friends or AI opponents, and see who can weave the most compelling story in record time. Writer Race is your ticket to literary glory.
