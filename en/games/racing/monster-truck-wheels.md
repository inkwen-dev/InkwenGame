Use arrow keys to drive or touch buttons on your devices

## About

You in the game Monster Truck Wheels will be the driver who will manage them. At the beginning of the game you sit behind the wheel of the monster truck and bring it to the starting line. On a signal, you are going to speed ahead. On your way you will see obstacles of different levels of difficulty. You are driving a car will need to either overcome them at speed, or slowed down to hold the car carefully so as not to let it turn over.
