## About

Reboun.io is a speedy ball dodging game. Hone your skill and grab your reaction time, as you need them both in this fast-paced race to the end. Move right to proceed through the levels and avoid the enemies at all costs. Eat food to gain experience, level up to increase your speed. In Duo and Co-op, touching your feinted green teammates will revive them, but be careful, you only have 10 seconds to do so!
