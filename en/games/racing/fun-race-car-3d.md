## About

Here is a car stunt racing arcade game waiting for you to challenge. Your goal is to arrive at the finish line first of the three drivers, and you must be the championship. You will receive three yellow stars if you complete the level without any crashing. When you crash the obstacles, you will respawn from the start of the current obstacle. Good luck and have fun!
