## About

Get ready for realistic drift simulation! Create chaos in the city with Drift City. Burn tires in the city and earn drift points in the action-packed drift game. Strive to be the drift king by buying new cars with the points you earn. Gain fame in the city by unlocking 30 different achievements. Some drift bonuses will appear in the city for certain periods of time. You can increase your drift score by collecting bonuses. You can also look at the simulation from a different perspective with 4 different cameras. You can drift even more fun by purchasing 6 different cars. Get behind the wheel and start burning the tires to be a nightmare for the vehicles in the traffic flowing in the city!
