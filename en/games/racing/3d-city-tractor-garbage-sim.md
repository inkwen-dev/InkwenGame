## About

3D city tractor garbage sim is a realistic Trash Dumping simulation game. You take the role of the dump tractor driver and must drive the tractor to carry the city sites dump trash across the site to where it has to be. Drive over the whole city and try not to drop the dump trash. Carry heavy Garbage across the site safely and park your tractor to complete the game.
