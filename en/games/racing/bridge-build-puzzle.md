## About

Bridge Build Puzzle is an online game for free with bridge-building as its main gameplay. In the game, you need to constantly exert your intelligence to build bridges and make sure that the length of the bridges will not be too short or too long. Although the operation of the whole game is simple, it is fun to play. Can you let the car pass safely?
