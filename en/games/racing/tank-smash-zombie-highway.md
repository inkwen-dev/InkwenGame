## About

In the video game Zombie Smash Racer, you take control of a tank and race against waves of zombies while destroying anything in your path and destroying any zombies that get in your way. This game provides you with two objectives: the first is to eliminate as many zombies as possible, and the second is to keep going for as long as you can without dying. Because you will suffer damage if you encounter counter barriers, you will either need to avoid them or eliminate them to stay alive. You may challenge your friends when you use the services offered by Google Play!
