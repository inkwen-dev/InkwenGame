## About

Crazy Moto is a fast-paced motorcycle stunt racing game where physics and balance decide everything. Twist the throttle, fly off ramps and blast through 22 hand-crafted levels packed with loops, explosive barrels, spikes, collapsing platforms and deadly drops. Beat the clock to earn up to 3 stars on every level and chain insane mid-air tricks &mdash; Front Flip, Back Flip, Double Flip and Wheelie &mdash; to rack up a higher score and a faster best time. Spend your stars to unlock and ride powerful new bikes, including the Desert Racer, the Martian and the Firehead, each with its own look and feel.
