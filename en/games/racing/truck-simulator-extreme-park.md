## About

Get ready to test your precision and strategy in Truck Simulator Extreme Park! In this unique truck parking challenge, you dont drive the truck &mdash; you draw the path! Sketch the perfect route from your truck to the finish line and watch as it follows your lead. But be careful &mdash; sharp turns, tight spaces, and tricky obstacles stand in your way! Features: Draw-to-drive gameplay &ndash; intuitive and fun Challenging levels that test your logic and planning Realistic environments and detailed truck models Increasing difficulty with new twists every level
