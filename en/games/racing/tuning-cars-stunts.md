## How to play

WASD or Arrows - Drive T - Time Slow R ESC - Restart Scene Tab - Next Car Mouse Wheel - Distance Camera Enter - Reset Car F - Nitro Car Add Force C - Camera Car I - Info Panel Q - Music on off

## About

You can choose from 5 cars for fun. Tuning cars: bumpers, hood, pipes, rims, spoiler. Upgrade of the car in 3 stages, acceleration depends on it. Change color: body, glass, smoke, neon. Change the effect of the camera graphics: 4 presets or off. Change the graphics of the game: low, medium, high, ultra.
