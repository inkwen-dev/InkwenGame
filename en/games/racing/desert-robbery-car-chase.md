## About

Desert Robbery is a fun car game where we aim high score by carefully avoiding police cars in the desert with the car we robbed. There's a robber in the game. This robber is running from the cops in a car. We make sharp maneuvers while fleeing the cops so that the police cars collide with each other. You get points along with bumper police cars. At the same time without accidental long stay in the game we get points. We maintain our high score target with the scores we have achieved. Desert Robbery is an entertaining car game prepared for us who love adventure games. This game is with you with the difference hitgames.org. Have Fun!
