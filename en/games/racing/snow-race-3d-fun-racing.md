## About

Snow Race 3D: Fun Racing is an addictive arcade game. Do you want to participate in a snow race? This is probably the most fascinating and addictive game you have ever played! Collect the snow around you to make huge snowballs, then let them build ladders and climb to higher levels. Your opponents will have the same talent and you will have to race against time to race against them! Be the first to reach the finish line to win! Come join us and become a snowball master!
