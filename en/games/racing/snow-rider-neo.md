## How to play

Desktop Use Left Right Arrow keys or A D to steer Press Space Up Arrow or W to jump Press Esc or P to pause resume Mobile Hold the on-screen left right buttons to steer and tap JUMP to jump Tap the pause button to pause

## About

Carve through snowy forests and mountain slopes in Snow Rider Neo, a fast-paced 3D endless sledding game. Dodge trees, rocks, snowmen and rolling snowballs, time your jumps over logs and gaps, and ride ramps as the downhill run gets tougher. Clear skies can give way to snowfall and blizzards. Choose from three sleds with different handling, collect gifts to unlock new paint finishes, and beat your best distance. Play with keyboard controls on desktop or on-screen buttons on mobile.
