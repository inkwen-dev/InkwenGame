## How to play

WASD - move LKM - shoot RKM - grenade Space - jump Shift - time slow

## About

The fight has begun. Only a few elite fighters went to the streets. Are you one of them? Almost no one gets out of the gouse. Gangs dominated the city and the only way to save humanity is to get rid of them. The game has several levels, and in each you will have to accomplish your goal without dying. Shoot 100 enemies, destroy their cars.
