## About

Super Race 3D is a fun and free leisure online game for boys. You need to let the crowd reach their destination through escalators and roads. In the game, you need to choose a reasonable time to guide the crowd through the danger zone and finally reach the cars. Try to avoid all kinds of vehicles coming and going, avoid being hit by vehicles and losing people. Come join us and take the challenge!
