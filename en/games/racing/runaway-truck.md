## About

Here is a car avoiding arcade game with a 3D cartoon car and a plane. With provided and limited fuel, you need to run as farther as you can by means of rushing from the ramp and avoiding incoming obstacles. There are other different planets with different kinds of tracks waiting for you to unlock and experience. Good luck!
