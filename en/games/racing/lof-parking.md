## How to play

On Touch-Screen devices you can use buttons to drive the car On all devices with keyboards you can use arrow keys to drive the car

## About

Drive the car towards asked parking slot. Avoid any collision with other cars or walls. You will have 5 chances to complete a level. Each time you collide with wall or any other car, you lose 1 chance. Park fast and safely to get more score. There are 10 challenging levels in this game. Complete all to be a winner.
