## About

Do you want to take fast rides and flights on the long streets of the city? Ultimate Flying Car game invites you to realistic flying car simulator experience. Explore the sky with stylish flying cars, or unlock new cars by participating in races. You can play all of these game modes either 1 player or 2 player. Player 1: Move: "W,A,S,D" NOS: "SHIFT " Look back: "T" Fix car's position: "R" Handbrake: "SPACE" Player 2: Move: "ARROW KEYS" NOS: "M" Look back: "L" Fix car's position: "U" Handbrake: "K"
