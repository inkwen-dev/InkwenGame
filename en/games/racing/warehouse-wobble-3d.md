## About

Drive a forklift, balance wobbly loads and deliver them in one piece! In Warehouse Wobble 3D you pick up towering stacks of boxes, fragile TVs, rolling barrels, a very heavy safe - and yes, a piano - and drive them across busy warehouses to the green delivery zone. Brake too hard and the stack topples. Take a corner too fast and your cargo goes flying. - 20 levels across 4 warehouses: Loading Dock, Cold Storage, Night Shift and the Mega Depot - Real physics: every load sways, leans and can tip over - Dodge patrol robots, time the roller doors and slide across icy floors - Earn up to 3 stars per level and collect coins - Upgrade your forklift in the garage: stronger engine, grippier tyres, stabilizer and better brakes - Progress is saved automatically - Plays on desktop and mobile (touch controls)
