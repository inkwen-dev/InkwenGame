## About

“Drift Challenge” is a racing game where you have to score points by drifting through the racing circuit. Try to slide on the track as much as you can to accumulate a lot of points and reach the target score. You must cross the finish line before the target time, beating the target score to unlock new levels. A super fun and challenging racing game! Have great fun!
