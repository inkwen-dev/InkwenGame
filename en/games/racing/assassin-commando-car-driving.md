## About

Assassin Commando Car Driving is a vertical-version car battle arcade game with 3D cartoon vehicles. Your car is equipped with a mechanic arm, a machine gun, and a baffle. You can crash enemy vehicles, punch them at the side, and shoot them when the machine gun skill is ready. I strongly suggest you upgrade the baffle first. Crashing the backside of the target is easier than attacking at the side.
