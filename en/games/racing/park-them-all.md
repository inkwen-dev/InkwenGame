## About

In this fun and addictive game, your main task is to sort out the parking spots so that all the cars can find their place and settle down comfortably. Its like being the air traffic controller of a bustling car parking lot! Whether you call it Park Away, Seat Cars Away, or Master of Parking, the goal remains the same &ndash; clear the car jam by arranging the parking spots smartly.
