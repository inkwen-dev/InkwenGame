Obby Cart Rush is a fast, colorful 3D cart-coaster obby! Jump into your wooden minecart and race down wild roller-coaster tracks across 5 themed worlds - Mountain, House, Forest, City and Zoo. Hold to speed up, brake before the signs, survive loops and steep drops, and reach the flag as fast as you can to earn Bronze, Silver and Gold medals.
