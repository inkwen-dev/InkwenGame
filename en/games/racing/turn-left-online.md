Tap and hold to turn left

## About

Turn left is recommended as a normal traffic command simulation game. With 3D cartoon vehicles on the street, you have a chance to help some vehicles turn left at the correct timing while avoiding the other vehicles going straight. You need to observe the interspace and judge it is able to overpass your car or not. I strongly suggest you drive to the ultimate position and wait there first. It will be easier for you to find the interspaces and have a good time!
