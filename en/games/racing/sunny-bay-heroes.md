## How to play

PC
- W A S D / arrow keys (Z Q S D on AZERTY) — walk, drive
- Shift — run (in a car: turbo)
- Mouse — look around
- Space — jump; helicopter up; handbrake
- Shift or Ctrl — helicopter down
- E or Enter — talk, get in and out, pull drivers out, use shops
- Left mouse — water blaster
- F or right mouse — foam bat
- C — call your ride
- G — SHOP
- H — horn / siren
- M or Tab — city map
- P — pause
- X or Backspace — skip a cutscene

Touch: floating joystick to move, drag to look; GAS / BRAKE and ◀ ▶ while driving; context button (TALK, DRIVE,
FLY…), jump, blaster, bat and RIDE buttons; MAP and PAUSE at the top.

## About

Welcome to Sunny Bay, hero! Baron Rust and his Rust Crew have pinched every wheel, engine and propeller in town to
stop the Grand Race. Mayor Marigold needs a hero — and Captain Skye has just flown one in: you.

Explore a whole seaside city with nothing off-limits. Drive jeeps, sports cars, taxis and buses, zip around on a
scooter, fly a helicopter and sail a boat across the bay. Pull the goons out of their getaway vans, race them round
the ring road and soak them with your water blaster. Nobody gets hurt in Sunny Bay — the baddies just get wet, dizzy
and very embarrassed.

21 story missions take you from your first ride to the Grand Race finale, and there is always something to do in
between: coin trails, seven stunt ramps with slow-motion jumps, golden stars, Hero Tasks, taxi, police, ice-cream and
bus shifts, and a daily gift that hands you a brand-new car on the right days. Spend your coins in the SHOP on new
rides, outfits, the turbo and the coin magnet.

Features
- A full open city: downtown, beach, harbor, park, hills and Baron Rust's scrapyard
- Cars, scooters, a helicopter and a boat — call your favourite ride any time
- 21 story missions, races and repeatable side jobs
- Stunt ramps, close calls and big air for bonus coins
- SHOP with 11 rides, outfits and upgrades; daily gifts and Hero Tasks
- Kid-friendly action: water blaster and foam bat, zero violence
- 10 languages, progress saved automatically
- Plays on PC (keyboard and mouse) and on phones and tablets (touch)
