## About

Steer a bunch of cars on a highway without rules and try not to crash them, at least not all of them. Fill the road with your hoard of cars and complete the fun levels. You'll be dodging rocks, pipes, holes in the road, trucks and many more, that will send chunks of your hoard of cars flying and explode.
