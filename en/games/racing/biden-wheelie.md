## About

Biden Wheelie is a simple 2D game with 10 levels that a player has to overcome by keeping a Jeep on the rear wheels while driving. To make it more difficult, there are various obstacles in each level - some you can ride over, some you must avoid. Collected dollars increase total score. Have fun.
