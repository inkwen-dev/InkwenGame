## How to play

Tap / Left Click / Space to jump.
Hold while airborne to rotate backward and perform backflips.
Land the X-bike smoothly to keep your speed and build your score.
Collect Golden Gears to fill your Repair Meter and recover lost hearts.
Watch the environment carefully—trees, ramps, bridges, signs, rocks, and even rewards may troll you.
You have 3 Hearts. Crashing costs one heart.
After a troll defeats you, you quickly retry the same challenge. Beat it to earn REVENGE.
Survive longer, discover new trolls, master their tricks, and ride as far downhill as possible.

## About

Troll Rider is an endless downhill X-bike adventure where the road cannot be trusted.
Ride deeper and deeper down the mountain, jump across gaps, perform backflips, collect Golden Gears, and survive a world full of unexpected troll traps. Trees may fall, ramps can disappear, bridges can break, signs may lie, and even a harmless-looking Gear trail might lead to trouble.
Crash? You still have a chance. Use your hearts, retry the same troll instantly, learn its trick, and come back for REVENGE.
The farther you ride, the stranger the mountain becomes—with new biomes, new traps, helpful surprises, massive set pieces, and trolls that start changing the rules you thought you already understood.
How far can you ride before the mountain finally gets you?
