## How to play

Accelerate - W/Arrow up
Shoot gun - Spacebar
Use nitro - Left Shift/N
Tilt forward/backward - Left/Right arrow or A/D

## About

Zombie Derby is a wild mix of racing and zombie mayhem from Brinemedia. Jump behind the wheel and plow through hordes of the undead standing in your way. But be careful - your fuel is limited, and once you stop, you’ll become easy prey. Time your nitro boosts wisely, as they might be the only thing that gets you over a hill or across a deadly gap.
