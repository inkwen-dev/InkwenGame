Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Fighting Vehicles Arena is not merely a vehicle matching puzzle and a fighting game with 2D cartoon blocks. Before the battle begins, you can add more body parts, wheels, and weapons to your vehicle. You can control the battle by yourself or select the auto fight. Both the defense parts and the offense parts are necessary. If you lose all weapon parts before your opponent, you will lose the battle. Good luck!
