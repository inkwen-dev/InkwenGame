## About

Drive Car is a fun skill game where you control a car to navigate challenging roads, collect stars, and reach the finish line safely. Drive skillfully, avoid obstacles, and choose the right direction to prevent the car from flipping over. Each level features different terrains, requiring quick reflexes and precise control, creating an engaging and challenging entertainment experience.
