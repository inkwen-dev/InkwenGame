## How to play

Use the WASD or arrows keys to steer the car
Use the space as the handbrake
Use C to change the camera position

## About

Drive Quest is an open-world driving adventure where the road is yours to explore. Cruise between cities, uncover hidden areas, and take on exciting challenges like drifting, stunts, and speed tests. With 35 customizable vehicles and a richly detailed map filled with ports, highways, and secret zones, this game delivers endless driving thrills and style-driven gameplay.
