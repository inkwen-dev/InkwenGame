## About

Welcome to IMPOSSIBLE MONSTER TRUCK 3D STUNT on the extreme ramps. Players offered impossible tracks and need to steer and hit the pedal in order to reach the finish. Players can buy a variety of trucks with the money they earn each level. Every truck has its own stats and performance. Players will experience addicting gameplay where their trucks will roll mid-air, smashed bumper first, flip mid-air, flip upside down, barrel roll while flying and many more impossible stunts. High replayability is guaranteed.
