## How to play

Your goal is to drive along the highway as far as possible.
If the car fell apart during the descent, click on "Over" at the top right. There you can start this route all over again.

How to play:

On the computer: arrows or WASD — go.

On the phone: buttons on the screen.

## About

"Break The Car Completely" is a unique crash test simulator that challenges your extreme driving skills. The main task is simple: to reach the finish line, overcoming dangerous obstacles.

However, the key difficulty lies in the fact that the entire track is literally littered with various obstacles. Any collision, even the smallest one, will instantly destroy your car, so the only survival strategy is extremely careful and calculated movement. This is a test for those who value precision and control over the situation.

This driving simulator was created especially for fans of exciting BeamNG walkthroughs, because the game contains tracks styled after similar well-known routes. Now you have the opportunity to go through them on your phone or computer.

The developers have implemented destructible cars and realistic motion physics simulation, which makes every ride unpredictable and exciting. Despite the sophisticated physics and high-quality graphics, the game is not demanding on the resources of your device and gives you an authentic experience of real driving, even on weak or old gadgets.
