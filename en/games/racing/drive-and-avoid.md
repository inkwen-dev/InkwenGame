## About

As you drive your way level by level and escape the evil orb from destroying your health with your blue car! Dodge as many car obstacles as you can and prepare for levels raising difficulty! Be alert at all times when driving in this game because youll need to restart your journey if you ever run out of health! Challenge your reflexes, and make sure to put your thinking caps on when dodging with all your might. Introducing 30 levels, where 1st level is the easiest, and the last level can be pretty challenging depending on your skill level! Your character The Blue Car must dodge and avoid other cars, and the evil orbs fired spheres!
