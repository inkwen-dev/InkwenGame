## How to play

Controls:

  📱 Mobile
  Use the on-screen buttons to control your car.

  🖥️ Desktop

  Player 1:
  W / S — Accelerate / Brake
  A / D — Steer
  Space — Handbrake / Drift

  Player 2 (Local Co-op):
  ↑ / ↓ — Accelerate / Brake
  ← / → — Steer
  Right Shift — Handbrake / Drift

  Controls can be customized in the game settings.
