## About

Car City Stunts is a free 3d car game based on a free drive with various cars on various types of surface mostly represented by ice. Players goal is to collect coins in a map usable for buying other cars. All fans of offline derby tricks and numerous smoke tires on the asphalt. Perform many serious tricks. To perform complex tricks, accelerate to the maximum and forward to the springboard or super twisted pipe.
