## About

This is a real-world automobile smashing and demolition derby auto racing game called Demolition Derby Destruction 2021. It was inspired by one of the most popular events that is presented at county fairs and festivals in the United States. This "car racing game" is available all over the world, and it gives players the opportunity to experience the utmost excitement that can be had by destroying things and beating their opponents by crashing their vehicles and cars into one another as quickly as they can. If you can keep going for a longer period of time, you will have a better opportunity to win.
