## About

Drive Mad is a car game where you drive on a track filled with obstacles. Your aim is to reach the finish line in one piece. You have to balance your speed so your car doesnt flip over. This is harder than it sounds, as there are many thrilling and creative stunts and obstacles for you to enjoy. Do you have what it takes to finish every level in Drive Mad?
