## About

Shifted Realms is an exciting shape-shifting adventure game where you transform into different forms to overcome obstacles and reach the finish line. Become a human to climb stairs, a car to race across roads, or an airplane to soar over wide gaps. Switch forms at the perfect moment, outpace your rivals, and conquer every challenge. With fast-paced gameplay and creative levels, Shifted Realms delivers a fun and addictive adventure.
