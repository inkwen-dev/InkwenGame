## About

Real Simulator: Monster Truck is a 3D car driving game that lets you drive monster trucks. There are 10 monster trucks and 4 maps of free driving. This game has a vehicle purchase and development system. There is also "Racing" mode to compete with Artificial Intelligence and "Stunt" mode to drive monster trucks on amazing roads. "Racing" and "Stunt" consist of 12 levels.A wide variety of racing cars to choose from Wide open-world with 4 maps available: Forest, Desert, Hill, and Moon
