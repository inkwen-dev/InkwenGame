## About

Do you like truck-driving simulation games? Grand Truck Simulator offers you a chance to transport animals as a truck driver. Your mission is to reach the destination within a limited time to receive special rewards in time. As for the acceleration of the truck, it steers slower than normal cars because it always carries a huge animal.&nbsp;When you are lost, follow the green arrows on the street to guide the correct directions. Please always obey traffic rules and drive safely!
