## How to play

Use A/D or Left/Right arrow keys to move the car, Space to jump, and Mouse/Touch to interact with game UI.

## About

Car Battle is an adrenaline-fueled driving brawler where your war vehicle doubles as both weapon and armor. Take on foes in solo campaigns or challenge friends in intense two-player duels, with each fight spanning five action-packed stages. Upgrade your car, refine your tactics, and unlock unique avatars to personalize your fighter as the battles grow more challenging.
