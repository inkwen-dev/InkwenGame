## About

Machine Crash Fighters is a fast-paced action game where you build your ultimate machine to fight against the CPU or challenge all your friends. Choose your favorite fighter, wheels, and weapons and conquest the arena to be the best player. Use more than 30 vehicle parts to build your fighting robot vehicle and unleash the power of your machine! Earn coins and unlock more special parts such as Drills, Saws, Missiles, Turbo, or Machineguns, and don’t lose the fight.
