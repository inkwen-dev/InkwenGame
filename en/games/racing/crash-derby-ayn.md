## About

Get ready for an action-packed battlefield. A total of 8 racers showcase their skills on the specially designed battlefield. There are 3 special abilities: "Damage X5", "HP +25", "SHIELD". By collecting these skills, you can beat your opponents. The last man standing wins. This mode is a mode in which vehicles try to crash and explode. The vehicle explodes when its HP drops to 0. The opponent with 0 HP will withdraw from the battle. Your HP + 20 increases when you detonate another. There are ranking rewards at the end of the battle. You must be in the top 3 to receive these ranking rewards. Let's get on the battlefield and roar like a lion.
