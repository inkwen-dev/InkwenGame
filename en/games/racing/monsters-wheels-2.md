## About

Monsters Wheels 2 is a monster truck game where every event asks something different. Race a packed grid to the finish line, flatten a row of parked cars, land the biggest backflip you can or beat the clock &mdash; 24 levels in total, spread across jungles, a beach, an amusement park and the Arizona desert. Three monster trucks are available, each with upgrades for engine, suspension, tyres and nitro. Prize money from every event goes straight into the garage.
