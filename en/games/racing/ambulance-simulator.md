## About

Ambulance Simulator is a 3D game, where you are an ambulance driver and you have to save human lives. Your mission is to get the paramedics to the accident location, pick up the patients and take them to the hospital, you have a mini map to guide yourself on the streets. In difficult situations you have to drive a helicopter to the location. Do your job and save the people lives.
