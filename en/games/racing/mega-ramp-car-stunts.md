## About

Mega Ramp Car Stunts plays like a hard-core stunt car-driving simulation game. With 3D obstacles on the track, you have a chance to challenge 10 levels in the Stunt Mode and 16 levels in the Giant Ramp mode. Whether you meet checkpoints or not, you need to drive the car to the finish line safely. Dont forget to buy better vehicles in the garage when you have enough green diamonds.
