## About

Magical driving is a unique and fun game in which your vehicle transforms from a truck to a boat to an airplane. Your goal is to avoid other vehicles and play as long as you can. Just click or tap anywhere on the screen to jump up or down depending on what type of vehicle you are in. Collectible stars give you points while other power-ups transforms your vehicles while the hearts give you more health
