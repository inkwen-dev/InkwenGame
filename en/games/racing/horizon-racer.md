## How to play

## About

HORIZON RACER

It's right behind you!

You don't escape a black hole, you just delay the inevitable.

This isn't a straight line with obstacles bolted on. The track corkscrews through full barrel rolls, wraps up walls and across ceilings, and throws hills at you at the speed of light. 

Runs straight in your browser. Global leaderboard included, so when you beat your friend's score, they'll know.
