## About

"Cool Tuning: Paint the Car" is an exciting game for all fans of auto tuning and creativity! Our game resembles such hits as "Car Mechanic Simulator" and "Need for Speed", but offers a unique experience of creating and selling unique cars. Perfect for those who love customization and creativity.

Game features include: buying cars, spray painting, gluing films and stickers, and selling your masterpieces at auction. You can discover new colors, films and stickers in the store for even more variety.
