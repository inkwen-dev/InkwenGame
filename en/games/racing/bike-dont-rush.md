## About

Bike Dont Rush is a bike game where you try to move forward without hitting obstacles in the sea. This bike riding game is a hyper casual game where you try to progress without hitting obstacles. You need good reflexes to move forward without hitting these constantly moving obstacles on this track full of traps. How far will you be able to ride on this track full of obstacles leading to infinity?
