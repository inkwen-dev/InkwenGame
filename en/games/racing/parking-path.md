## About

In the game Parking Path you should try to park the cars to the designated places. The topic of today's lesson is car parking. In front of you on the screen you will see a playing field on which several cars will be located. Each of them will have a different color. At a certain distance, you will see special parking spaces that are also color-coded. Your task is to place the cars in the places corresponding to their color. To do this, using the mouse, you will have to draw driving routes for each vehicle. Keep in mind that there may be various obstacles on the way of vehicles. Also, they should not collide with each other.
