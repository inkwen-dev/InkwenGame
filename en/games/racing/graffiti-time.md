## About

In Graffiti Time, a stealth-based platform game, you'll be spreading your artistic talents throughout the city by sneaking around and leaving your mark on various surfaces. You'll have a trusty can of spray paint at your disposal, as well as a variety of colors and stickers to customize your art. As you paint the city, you'll need to watch out for the law enforcement and security guards who will try to catch you. The game features dozens of levels filled with creative puzzles and challenging obstacles. Do you have the skills and cunning to complete the game and become a graffiti master?
