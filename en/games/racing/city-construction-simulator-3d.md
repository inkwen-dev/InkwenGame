## About

City Construction Simulator 3D is a realistic builder game, in which one can be a construction contractor. Your driving skills for different construction vehicles is needed for development of new and already existing busy roads. These repairs are needed because heavy traffic on busy city roads damages their structure. Along with this, a big city needs new routes for improving the flow of traffic by improved infrastructure.
