## How to play

Arrow keys or WASD keys to drive Space bar to handbrake C key to change view camera Shift key to boost bike R key to reset scene G key to reset bike E key to turn on police lights X to hide instructions T

## About

Do you like motorbikes? If so, you’re in the right place. Motorbike Simulator is a realistic simulation for the motorbike and stunts lovers. You can choose from three different bikes and four maps. In every environment you have a multitude of platform specially created for the most craziest stunts you can think of. Features • 3 cool bikes: Racer, Police and Cross • 4 different maps • the most craziest platforms for stunts
