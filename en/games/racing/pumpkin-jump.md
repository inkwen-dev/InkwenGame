## About

Pumpkin Jump is a single-touch jumping reaction game, has everything you need to make your next app store hit! Use your skills and agility to survive by jumping over obstacles and avoid getting hit. Just tap the screen and makeup to three jumps before touching the ground again. Avoid the dangerous obstacles or your Pumpkin will explode and the game will be over!
