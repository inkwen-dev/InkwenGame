## About

Try to reach the shortest times in more than 40 levels in this mad stunts game, you have to be as fast as you can in the bike race. Try crazy stunts and collect stars in this dirt bike game! Upgrade your motocross bike with earned in-game gold and restart levels for new records. Physics based riding Extreme racing. Feel the stunts and obstacles, remember there is no Trial frontier! To buy new bikes you have to do somersaults as much as possible in the levels, for this you need mad skills. Buy new Motocross, BMX bikes! Update! Break the record! Stay tuned for 50+ episodes and make sure you get the highest score on all levels until then.
