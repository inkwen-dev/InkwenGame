## How to play

The goal of the race is to get as far along the highway as possible.
Try not to crash into cars, so you can drive on.
Also complete missions on the open map in parking mode and earn money. 
Spend the money you earn on improving your car and buying new cars.

PC control:
WASD/arrows - machine control
Z - emergency
QE- turn signals
C - camera change

Phone controls:
On-screen buttons / on-screen steering wheel. The mode is switched in the game settings.
