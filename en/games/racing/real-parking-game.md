## How to play

Steering: Arrow keys or WASD (←/→ or A/D)
Accelerate: W or ↑
Brake / Reverse: S or ↓

Steering: on-screen joystick
Accelerate: on-screen gas button
Brake/Reverse: on-screen brake button

## About

Real Parking Game puts your driving precision to the test. Start in a garage full of cars and progress through dozens of hand-crafted levels packed with obstacles, narrow lanes, and tricky parking spots. Your goal is simple but challenging: navigate the course, avoid collisions, and reach the designated parking place to complete the level.

Features:

Large garage with many cars to unlock and collect.
Multiple levels.
Realistic driving physics and responsive controls for precision parking.
Progressive difficulty — beginner-friendly tutorials up to expert parking puzzles.
Optimized performance for smooth gameplay on mobile devices.
Whether you enjoy relaxed precision driving or intense obstacle runs, Real Parking Game delivers addictive, approachable gameplay that rewards skill and patience. Download now, pick your favorite ride, master the obstacles and park like a pro!
