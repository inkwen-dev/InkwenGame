## How to play

The W key or the up arrow is gas The S key or the down arrow is the brake Using the A D keys or the right left arrows on the keyboard you can rotate the motorcycle and perform a somersault

## About

Test your motorcycle racer skills on tracks with different terrain, environments and moving elements in the Moto Stuntman game. You have to race through unique landscapes and overcome natural obstacles to reach the finish line. Driving powerful motorcycles, perform stunts to win extra seconds and set the best time. Enjoy a dizzying adventure on the surface of the earth and descending into its depths, but do not forget about caution on the way to victory!
