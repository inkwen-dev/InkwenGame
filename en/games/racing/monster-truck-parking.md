## About

Brain for Monster Truck Parking is a unique and imaginative driving game in which you must control a monster truck and aim to make deliveries. You must control both the movement of the truck and also create a path for it to move along from point to point. You must also collect all the gold stars on a level and ensure that your cargo does not fall off of the truck. Use your mouse to draw lines between the platforms – once you have created a track, you can then use the arrow keys to move your truck forwards or backward. Think creatively and try to figure out the optimum route for each level.
