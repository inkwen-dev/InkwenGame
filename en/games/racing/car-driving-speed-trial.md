## About

Are you ready to compete in the amazing Stunts Speed Of the car Trial? You may choose your vehicle, paint it, and then drive it quickly across the globe to discover the thing you need to gather to win. The object might be a key or money, and the game provides a minimap that tells you where the object key is located.
