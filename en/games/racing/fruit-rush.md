## About

Fruit Rush is a collecting running arcade game with 3D watermelon blocks and platforms. As a pineapple man, you need to collect watermelon blocks to overpass obstacles on the track to the finish line. The more you collect, the more coins you could win. Have a good time and complete all levels!
