## How to play

Use a mouse to control the cars or use A and D keys on the keyboard

## About

Mini Road is a game where you need to control two cars at the same time. You need to collect the objects with the same color as the car and to avoid objects with different colors, or colors that the opponent car has. It is interesting to play and hard in some funny way. Try to pass more circles as you can and to drive the cars as long as possible.
