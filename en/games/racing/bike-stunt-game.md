## How to play

Complete each level by reaching the finish line. Your bike moves forward automatically — just control balance and tricks.

Desktop – Up/down arrows to lean forward/backward. Spacebar to jump. Hold down during jumps to perform tricks (backflip, Superman, etc.).

Mobile – Tap left/right side of screen to lean. Swipe up to jump. Tap and hold mid-air for tricks.

Both – Land smoothly to avoid crashing. Level ends if you wipe out.

Three modes: Time Trial (beat best time), Trick Challenge (hit score target), Survival (one crash = game over). Complete all levels in any mode to win. Have fun stunting!
