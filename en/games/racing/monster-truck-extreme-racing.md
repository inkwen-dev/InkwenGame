## About

Powerful Monster Trucks are ready to race... Time to prove who is the lord of the deserts! Race and modify your trucks in Monster Truck Extreme Racing! You have to finish the races in top 3 places to win prizes. You can also win prizes by performing various stunt shows on the huge "Free Drive" map. To win the next races, be sure to visit the "Garage" section and buy new Monster Trucks as well. Game has 1 Player and 2 Player options.
