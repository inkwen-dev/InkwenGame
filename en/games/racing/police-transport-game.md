## About

Police Transport Game puts you in charge of transporting police vehicles safely across the city. Drive a powerful car carrier truck, load police cars onto the trailer, and deliver them to their destinations while completing exciting transport missions. Navigate busy roads, avoid traffic, and handle every delivery with precision and care. Unlock new trucks, transport more vehicles, and take on increasingly challenging levels. With realistic driving physics, detailed 3D graphics, and smooth gameplay, Police Transport Game offers an exciting experience for fans of truck driving and transport simulation.
