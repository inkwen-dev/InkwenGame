## About

City Constructor Driver 3D is a freight simulation game. You can use 3D trucks to move bricks. You need to transport bricks to build more buildings in the city. There is no time limit, you can experience driving with two additional tracks. Keep tapping while parking the truck will increase the construction speed! What are you waiting for, there are so many buildings waiting for you to build!
