## About

Swipe To Park The Cars is a captivating 2D mobile game that puts your parking skills to the test. In this addictive title, players must skillfully guide a fleet of colorful cars into their designated parking spots using intuitive swipe controls. The challenge lies in the increasing complexity of parking scenarios, from crowded lots to tight spaces, requiring precision and quick thinking.
