## How to play

W / up arrow key = accelerate
S / down arrow key = move backward
A / left arrow key = turn left
D / right arrow key = turn right
R = restore position
Space = handbrake (drift)
Left Shift = nitro

## About

Battle Arena Race to Win is a high-octane arena game where you join the Red or Blue team and engage in fierce car battles. Choose from a variety of cars, collect and upgrade them to dominate the arena. Balance speed, durability, and acceleration to outmaneuver opponents. Equip your car with strategic items, upgrade it, and climb the leaderboard to lead your team to victory!
