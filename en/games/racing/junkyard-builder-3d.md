## About

Build a custom offroad truck and take it from a dusty junkyard to 10 driving worlds. Earn coins on each run and upgrade 10 part categories, including engines, wheels, suspension and turbo. Personalize your truck with paint and flags. The new driver career adds XP, levels and short contracts with coin rewards. Earn XP by driving, collecting items and landing jumps. Unlock cosmetic flags, claim a free reward-wheel spin every ten minutes and use Play Again to chase your next record. Career progress, coins, rewards and wheel cooldown are saved automatically. A single-player 3D driving and vehicle-building game with desktop and mobile controls.
