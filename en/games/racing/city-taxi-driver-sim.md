## About

City Taxi Driver Sim is a marvelous city taxi driving game available for smart phone. Multiple cars and realistic scenes combine to the whole game. Keep the brain occupied and obey traffic rules. Drunk driving is a very serious crime in the US. The police will arrest you. A friendly passenger may call your taxi. Earn enough money for new taxi. The taxi is given to you in perpetuity. Are you ready to start a new adventure? I can foresee you will be a professional city taxi driver!
