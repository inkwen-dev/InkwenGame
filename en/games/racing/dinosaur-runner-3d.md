## About

How do you like dinosaurs? Dinosaur Runner 3D offers you a chance to gather more dinosaurs to make a dinosaur army and defeat your enemies. Collect the dinosaurs and pass the blue numbered walls to increase the number of your army. Dodge all kinds of trap obstacles and red-numbered walls to avoid unnecessary downsizing. Use the ramps to jump over the obstacles you cant walk through directly. Buy more units to win the final battle!
