## About

How long will you wash your car after driving? Car Wash Garage Service Workshop is actually not a car washing simulation game but a car parking simulation game under a 3D game engine. The objective here for you is to take the passengers to their destinations on time. As long as you avoid the dirty mud roads while driving, you could just transport passengers and earn the cash. Don't worry if your car is dirty, drive to the garage service and wash the car for 600 dollars!
