## How to play

Use arrow keys to control the car or tap on the icons to play on mobile devices

## About

In this game you have to drive on the highway. You will drive fast and will have to avoid other cars moving on the road. The road has three lanes so it will not be difficult for you to change lanes on time and avoid a collision with another vehicle. But sometimes there will be vehicles on every lane so you will have to pass them at the right time. The longer you stay in the game, the faster the car will increase, so the game will become harder and more interesting.
