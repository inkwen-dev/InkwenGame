## About

Twisted Auto Metal is an explosive vehicular combat game set in a post-apocalyptic wasteland where chaos reigns and only the deadliest drivers survive. Inspired by classic titles like Twisted Metal, this high-octane shooter puts players behind the wheel of brutalized, weaponized cars, including the iconic Nissan Skyline, now reborn as a steel-clad death machine. In this world, there are no rules&mdash;only carnage, speed, and the desperate hunger to outlast and obliterate the competition.
