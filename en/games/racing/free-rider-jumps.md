## About

Endless Bike Race is another game from the popular creator of Free Rider HD! Here you will be sitting behind the wheel of a mountain bike and traverse difficult terrain. The bike itself rides forward and you will be trying to stay on track. Drive forward and jump, try to do back and front flips, but be very careful not to fall on your back. If you do, the game will end for you and you will have to start the whole level from the beginning.
