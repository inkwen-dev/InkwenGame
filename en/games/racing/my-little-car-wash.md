## About

Wash and shine with Car Washing Game, a 2D cartoon game that lets you clean different vehicles. You don&rsquo;t need any experience, just follow the hints and use the tools to make the vehicles look new. You will have 22 vehicles to choose from, each with its dirt and stains. You will also have the drivers watching you as you work, so be quick and efficient. You will earn yellow stars for each vehicle you wash, and you will need them to unlock more vehicles. Car Washing Game is a game that will teach you some washing skills and make you have fun!
