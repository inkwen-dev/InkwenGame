## About

Neon Dash: Survive the Dash is a high-octane, rhythm-based platformer where precision is everything. Navigate a gritty, dark-pop world filled with lethal spikes, rotating sawblades, and shifting gravity. Use jump pads and mid-air orbs to defy death as the speed intensifies across multiple neon-drenched levels. Experience cinematic visuals with a pulse-pounding aesthetic designed for those with lightning-fast reflexes. Can you survive the dash?
