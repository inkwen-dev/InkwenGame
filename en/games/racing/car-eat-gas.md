## About

In this game you need to control the car who want to eat gas. This car is thirsty. But is it not so easy as you think. Race against rolling blades who want to destroy the car. You need to avoid blades and to eat all gas tanks to pass the level. But there is a trick. You can eat the only car between gas tanks and then you can pass trough blades. Your car have 3 lives, so use them well.
