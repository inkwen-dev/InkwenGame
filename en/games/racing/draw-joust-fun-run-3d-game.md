## About

Draw Joust is a battle relaxation game. The players will be driving their own cars in a very open field and will take all kinds of wonderful weapons to battle each other. You can change the shape of your vehicle at will but you must complete this move before the battle begins. Weapons are allocated at random. For starters, axes, javelins, or iron sheets can all be used in Draw Joust, have fun!
