Controls for PC (desktop):

A/D - left/right
Space - stop

Controls for mobile:
controls by corresponding screen buttons

## About

Drive a realistic car, drive along tracks with obstacles. Lots of different tracks for car stunts.
You have to drive the car along the tracks in the sky, overcome interesting obstacles and try not to fall off the track into the abyss!
The game has many different levels and realistic car physics.
