## About

As a professional truck driver, the zoo asks you to transport different animals to the marked locations at all ten levels in a 3D truck driving simulation game called Wild Animal Transport Truck. In a limited time, which is definitely enough for you to drive, you need to drive stably on the track to the marked parking lot. As long as your truck falls into the lake, the game will lose directly. Good luck!
