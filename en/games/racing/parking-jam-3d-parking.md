## About

Slam the brakes! You’re in a Parking Jam! It is time to leave the parking lot, but why are everyone else’s cars in the way? You need to move them … but hold on! It needs to be done in the right order, because these tight parking lots have tons of obstacles! Can you solve this tricky parking puzzle and get all the cars on the road? In this fun and colorful game, you challenge your logic skills, critical thinking, and timing precision. There are tons of beautiful car colors and patterns to unlock, to keep you on your toes. It’s super satisfying to flip the cars on the road (or into each other, if you pick them in the wrong order!)
