## About

Are you ready to burn tires in desert races? To become a legend of drag racing, you have to win all the races. You will earn money in every race you win. With the money you earn, you can buy new cars or strengthen your car. To be the strongest, you have to take a quick start, check the tachometer and shift gears. Have fun!
