## About

Noob vs Pro: Drift Tag &mdash; use your speed, drifting skills, and reflexes to pass the bomb to your opponents! The car holding the bomb must pass it to another player before the timer runs out. If you are caught with the bomb, your car explodes and you are eliminated. Drift through tight spaces, use ramps and obstacles, outsmart your rivals, and become the last driver standing! Race with different cars, compete with your friends, and rise from Noob to Pro!
