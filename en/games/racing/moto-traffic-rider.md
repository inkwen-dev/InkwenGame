## How to play

Controls in Moto Traffic Rider are simple and intuitive. Hold the throttle button to accelerate and release it to slow down. Lean the motorcycle left or right to steer, avoid traffic, and take corners smoothly. Use the brakes to reduce speed in heavy traffic or before dangerous turns. Activate nitro for sudden bursts of speed and overtake vehicles closely to earn extra points and money. Keep a balance between speed and control — crashes will end your ride.

## About

Moto Traffic Rider puts you behind the handlebars of a powerful motorcycle and delivers a whole new level of realism while keeping the old-school spirit and simple gameplay.

Jump into the saddle, switch to the first-person view, and race through busy highways, weaving between streams of cars. The game features a full career mode, enhanced graphics, detailed environments, and realistic motorcycle sounds recorded from real bikes. Complete missions, earn money, and unlock new motorcycles to become a true highway legend.

Moto Traffic Rider combines fast-paced arcade racing with the atmosphere of a real biker adventure. Ride day and night on highways and city streets, pushing your skills to the limit.

Features:
• First-person camera view for full immersion
• 13 unique motorcycles with realistic engine sounds
• Detailed roads and dynamic day/night cycle
• Full career mode with no fuel or time limits
• Motorcycle upgrade and purchase system
