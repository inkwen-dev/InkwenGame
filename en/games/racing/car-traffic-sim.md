## About

Car Traffic Sim is a realistic driving game, you have a busy street with many cars and you have to take your fuel or driving under the pressure of time, if you want to be relaxed you have endless road and complete freedom to drive. Be careful and avoid to collide with the other cars. Features • 3 game modes • Multiple levels to acomplish • Good graphics • Realistic gameplay
