## About

Cat Evolution is a fun parkour game. You will play as a cat, as a carnivore, collecting pieces of meat for you to evolve faster, light vegetables will degrade you. Also, dont forget to collect diamonds, which can be used for upgrades! Evolve from a cat to a tiger or even farther to the finish line to conquer the animals!
