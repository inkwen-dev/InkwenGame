## How to play

Press the screen to start the race Your character moves along the track automatically Collect colored pieces along the way Hold the mouse button or keep your finger on the screen to spend collected pieces and build a ladder Release to stop building

## About

Race against a rival on colorful tracks and try to reach the finish line first. Collect pieces scattered along the route and use them to build ladders while running. A smart shortcut can give you the lead, but your supply is limited. Avoid traps, collect speed boosts, and keep an eye on your opponent. Explore 30 levels featuring different tracks, obstacles, and climbs. Simple controls make it easy to start, while every race challenges you to choose the right moment to build.
