## About

Supercars Garage is an addictive merge car game where you collect, combine, and upgrade powerful supercars. Buy cars, place them on the track, and earn money as they race. Merge two cars of the same level to unlock faster and more valuable vehicles. Upgrade your garage, increase your income, discover new supercars, and build the ultimate car collection in this fun idle merge racing game!
