## About

Crash Tower 3D is a physics destruction arcade game. Launch a powerful vehicle, time your boost in the gold zone and smash through 30 increasingly tough walls, towers and houses. Earn coins from each impact, unlock three vehicles and upgrade each one separately. Repair damaged vehicles and try again when a structure survives. Explore a destruction yard that changes from daylight to sunset and a starry night. Play with mouse, keyboard or touch, with 20 languages, saved progress and separate music and sound settings.
