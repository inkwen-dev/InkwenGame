## About

City Minibus Driver game invites you to be a driver in a crowded city. In career mode, you must take the passengers to the bus stops on the routes, on time. You will earn money after each successful mission. It is also possible to earn money in the minibus racing tournament. With the money you earn, you can buy faster and luxury minibuses from the garage.
