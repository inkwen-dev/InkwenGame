## About

Sandwich Runner is a fun game. Collect ingredients on the way to the goal, how high will it reach? The diner is very hungry and he cant wait to eat. Collect steaks, chicken thighs, fried eggs, and sushi. Be careful to avoid bad food like mold and rot or it will make the diner sick! Red peppers will make the diner spitfire! Can you keep the diner happy and pass the level? Good luck!
