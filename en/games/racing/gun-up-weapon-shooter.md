## About

Gun Up Weapon Shooter is a wonderful parkour game. Pick from a variety of cool and powerful weapons to start your adventure. Collect rewards, upgrade your weapons, and become more destructive. You can choose to increase fire frequency, increase income, or upgrade the year to help you successfully pass. But beware of moving knives, they can stop you from moving forward. Complete all challenges, you can travel longer distances and earn more income.
