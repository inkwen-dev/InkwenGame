## About

Extreme Drift Racing is a 3D racing game. Players can drive many different vehicles that can be customized in the garage. Each car has unique characteristics, so each driver will be special. The game is suitable for all ages. Fasten your seat belt and get ready for the craziest drifts and races. These cars go fast and you need to be a good driver to handle them at every turn at high speed. Features of the game Six unique tracks for racing and drifting. There are 10 cars in the garage, you need to unlock them. Up to seven bots to compete with. Statistics are available for you to find out your best score. Set your nickname. Settings is where you can customize your game - set graphics quality, turn on / off sound.
