Controls WASD or arrow keys to move Space bar to use handbrake C to change camera view

## About

Uphill Bus Simulator is a classic 3D driving simulator. You can choose from multiple bus models and three different game modes. You need to drive the bus in order to pick up the passengers. The map in upper left side of the screen shows you the road to the closest bus station from where you need to pick up the passengers. This is a realistic simulation game where you can test your driving skills. Features: • Different bus models • Multiple levels to play • Three game modes
