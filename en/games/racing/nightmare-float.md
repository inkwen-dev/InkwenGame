## About

Nightmare Float is an exciting, spooky arcade game where players guide a creepy balloon character through a haunted forest. With chilling visuals and atmospheric sound effects, the objective is to float upwards while avoiding various haunting obstacles like shurikens, spikes, and eerie clouds. Collect potions along the way to unlock new characters and stay afloat as long as possible in this spooky adventure. The more you float, the higher your score, but beware&mdash;the forest gets more dangerous the higher you go!
