## About

Zombie Derby: Blocky Roads is an action-packed game blending driving and zombie slaughter. In a post-apocalyptic world overrun with the undead, only the bravest and most skilled drivers and survivors will survive. Use your car to mow down zombies and clear obstacles in your path. Don't forget to keep an eye on your fuel or your vehicle will fail mid-level. Earn money to upgrade your ride with new cars, weapons, and optimizations. Can you conquer all stages with three stars? Give it a try!
