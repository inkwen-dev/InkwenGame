## About

Nitro Rally Evolution is a fun classic 2D arcade racing game similar with the classic racing games of the 80's. Play 20 different circuits to race in a car that runs automatically; unlock two or more powerful cards that are hidden and discover other features while playing. Use the turbo when you need an extra speed boost and set the best lap time. The game shows colorful and detailed graphics and a nice sound effects you will enjoy.
