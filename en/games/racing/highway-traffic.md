## How to play

W or up arrow to accelerate A D or left right arrow to steer car Space bar to use handbrake

## About

Are you ready to drive fast on the highway? It’s time to show your driving skills on high speed among the multitude of cars on the highway. All you have to do is to avoid colliding with other cars and collecting as many points as possible. You have at your disposal many car models, four game modes as a single player, 3 weather options and smooth car controls. Have fun! Features: • Multiple car models • Three weather options • Four game options
