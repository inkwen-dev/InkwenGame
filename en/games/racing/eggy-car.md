Desktop:
Use arrow keys to accelerate and apply brake.
On Mobile:
Tap on paddles to accelerate and apply brake.

## About

Eggy Car is a simple yet challenging physics-based casual driving game where the goal is to drive as far as possible without letting the egg in your car fall off and crack! Controls are minimal, but finding the right balance between braking and accelerating is crucial, especially when navigating hills. Buckle up and get ready for the (hopefully not) bumpy ride.
