## About

Welcome to Car Flip – a game where you have to perform crazy stunts and jumps from trampolines in a car – all in order to park your car in the most original and unusual way! Can you do a triple flip and land parked with your wheels up? Well done! Make a crazy jump through rings of fire? Fantastic, this will net you a lot of points! The main thing is to land in the parking lot via the most epic and insane methods!
