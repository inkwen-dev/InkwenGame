## How to play

Ride your motorcycle through busy craft-style city traffic.

Move left and right to dodge cars, trucks, and other bikes. Collect coins on the road and try to survive as long as possible.

Use your coins to upgrade your motorcycle, improve speed, handling, and power. The farther you ride, the higher your score.

Avoid crashing, react fast, and beat your best distance!
