## About

Are you prepared to play an awesome simulator? Taxi Driver Las Vegas is an online game set in an open environment with objectives to help you improve your cab driving abilities. Pick up the passengers and drive them to their destination, following the directions and exploring all of the maps to locate the streets and addresses. This game includes many game modes to keep you entertained; you can accomplish the stages in taxi or open mode and freely explore the whole landscape.
