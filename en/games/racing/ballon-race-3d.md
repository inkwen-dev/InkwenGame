## About

In Balloon Race 3D, race against other players in a whimsical world where collecting balloons makes you fly! Start on the ground and gather as many balloons as you can to lift off and speed towards the finish line. The more balloons you collect, the higher and faster you soar. Aim to outmaneuver your rivals and be the first to cross the finish line in this thrilling airborne adventure! Collect coins to unclock new skins. Enjoy!
