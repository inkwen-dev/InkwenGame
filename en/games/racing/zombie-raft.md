## About

Zombie Raft is a survival game. The zombie apocalypse is coming and its time for a raft survival game! Protect yourself and other survivors from the looming zombie tsunami, and push them all back, its your responsibility to survive on the raft! Squeeze the vehicles forward and push them if necessary! Keep going, escape the zombie tsunami, and conquer the barren land! If stranded survivors are found, you need to rescue them! Join us and experience the best zombie game!
