## How to play

Use the arrows ⬆️⬇️ or "WS" to move and ⬅️➡️ or "AD" to spin.
Sometimes you’ll need to slow down, and other times you’ll need to speed up to win. Try different tactics.
If your bike gets stuck, press ⬅️➡️ or "AD" to hop!

## About

Join the exciting world of Moto Obby, where your motorbike skills face intense challenges. Overcome tricky hazards like swinging hammers and sharp spikes in a quest for victory. Test your reflexes across various levels in this thrilling online game, available on phones and computers.
