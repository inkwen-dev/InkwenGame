## About

The apocalypse engulfed the whole world. Everywhere there are crowds of running zombies that kill everyone in their path. Your main goal is survival at any cost. The only thing that will help you with this is your car. Earn money by killing zombies and performing epic stunts. Equip your vehicle with a machine gun to shoot back from these walking dead. Fill up a full gas tank to get away from the chase as far as possible, pump the engine to go faster and stay alive. Don't let the main hero die. Carmageddon is already here, let's go!
