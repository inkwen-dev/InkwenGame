## How to play

Tap to start, then drag left or right to steer (mouse drag, arrow keys or A and D on desktop; P pauses). 
The car accelerates by itself. 
Pass close to traffic for near misses: they add time and fill the Breeze meter, and a full meter starts a Boost that knocks cars aside. 
Reach each checkpoint before the timer ends and pick a side at every fork. 
Reach a goal to collect its cover. 
Spend Miles on upgrades, cars and new maps.

## About

City Pop Drive is a one-thumb arcade drive through pastel 80s city pop scenery. 
Pass traffic close to fill the Breeze meter and boost, reach each checkpoint before the timer runs out, and choose the scenic or the express road at every fork. 
Five maps (Seaside, Midnight Expressway, Crossing City, Maple Pass, Snow Resort), each with its own twist and adaptive music that changes with your route, and 25 record-sleeve covers to collect.
