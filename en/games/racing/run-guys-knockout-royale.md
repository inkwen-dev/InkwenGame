## About

Enjoy chaos, laughter, and action in Run Guys: Knockout Royale. A running and multiplayer royale game. In seesaw mode, run over one seesaw after another, keep balance and rush to the finish line. Racing at breakneck speeds through spinning wooden columns, trap floors and trap obstacles. Each level will knock out the slowest players. In mountain climbing, be the first to reach the summit and jump onto the crown.
