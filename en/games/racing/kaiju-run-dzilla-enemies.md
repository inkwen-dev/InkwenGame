## About

Kaiju Run - Dzilla Enemies plays like a normal running arcade game with 3D vertical-version play rules. It is managed for you to collect green items and defeat the final dinosaur boss at all levels. The stronger you grow, the more scores you can win by punching the monster farther. The diamonds you win are used to unlock more skins in the Shop. There are also 6 challenges in the DIY map. You can try different animals!
