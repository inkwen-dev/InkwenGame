## How to play

TAB - pause menu (or Escape)
WASD - moving.
Spacebar - handbrake.
Shift - nitro.
C - change camera.
R - reset car.
K - restore car.
N - switch car.
For mobile devices, use the game interface.

## About

All cars can be broken, dented and destroyed! Realistic car damage and deformation system.
Arrange police chases - hit a police car and it will start hunting for you.
Crash test of cars - watch their destruction. All the destruction of cars has a realistic simulation.
Different maps and cars.
A lot of crashing props - cannons, catapults, hummers, presses etc.
