## About

Labo Brick Train is a game for kids that can stimulate their imagination and creativity. It is an amazing train building and driving app for children. It provides a virtual sandbox where children can freely build and play with brick trains. In Labo Brick Train, children build unique trains by putting together pieces of colorful bricks like a puzzle. They can select from 60+ classical locomotives templates, which range from old steam trains to powerful diesel locomotives to modern high-speed trains.
