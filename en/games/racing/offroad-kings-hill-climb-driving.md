## About

A new time has come for the driving season. A battle for the real off-road drivers to see who can last the longest and can reach the finish line on a difficult course. There will be hills, rivers you need to cross and also go over bridges. Do you have what it takes to drive a real professional? Let's find out! On your way you will also get to earn some money so don't worry about using the same car all the time. Good luck and have fun!
