## How to play

A W D - Control the car R - Switch gear C - Change camera H - Horn Space - Handbrake Esc - Pause game

## About

Car Parking School is an epic car parking game with load of levels. Park your car within the allocated parking place in each level. Feature: - Create driver license. - Car customization: paint, spoiler and rims. - Car upgrade: Engine, brake and speed. - Five game mode: Classic, Modern, Mission, Timer and Snow.
