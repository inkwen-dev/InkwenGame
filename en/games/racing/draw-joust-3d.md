## About

Here is both a drawing puzzle and car battle arcade game with 3D stickman game arts. You can control a stickman in a simple car. To defeat a red stickman, you need to draw different parts to make sure you could use your wheels to smash his vehicles at all levels. Use your brain and try to control gas and back for victory!
