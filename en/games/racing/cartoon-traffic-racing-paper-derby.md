## About

Race through paper cartoon chaos!

Immerse yourself in dynamic traffic racing in a unique world of paper and cardboard, where tiny cardboard cars compete on busy roads. Maneuver between cars, avoid collisions, overcome obstacles, and utilize the environment!
Every race is a battle for first place. Huge stationery items, ramps, obstacles, cars, and other interactive elements transform each map into a veritable playground where you can find new ways to overtake, collide, and defeat opponents.
Unlock new cars with unique designs and powerful performance, customize them to suit your playstyle, and navigate increasingly challenging maps with intense traffic. Race through colorful arenas, weave through dense traffic, cause massive collisions, and prove that your cardboard car is the fastest on the road!

Key Features
Dynamic traffic racing – maneuver between cars, avoid collisions, and find the fastest way to victory.
Unique paper and cardboard world – a vibrant universe with oversized stationery and unusual locations.
Interactive environment – ​​use ramps, obstacles, and various objects to gain an advantage.
Modern, colorful graphics – stylish visual design with a unique papercraft atmosphere.

New cars and bonuses – win races, earn rewards, and unlock new content.
Destruction and chaos – crash into obstacles, cause accidents, and turn every race into a true road rage.
