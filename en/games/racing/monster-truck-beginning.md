## About

Monster Truck: Beginning is a wonderful game in which you test your reflexes and skills. Take an endless ride with your car. Choose the difficulty and do not stop. You can ride on a road where the traffic is one way or on a two way road. You can choose from several maps which you unlock for the money. For the money, you can also improve your car for example type of wheels , you can improve controlling, speed or you can change the color of your car. Take a perfect ride on your monster and show what is in you. Have fun.
