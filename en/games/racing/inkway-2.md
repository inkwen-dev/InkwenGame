## About

In this fun and challenging puzzle game, your goal is to draw a road that safely guides the car to the winning point. Use your creativity and planning skills to create the perfect path while avoiding obstacles and dangerous areas along the way. Simply draw a line on the screen to build a road for the car. Once your path is ready, the car will follow it toward the destination. Be careful with your route, as a poorly designed road can cause the car to crash or get stuck. Each level brings a new challenge, requiring you to think carefully about the best route. You can play at CrazyCubix.com
