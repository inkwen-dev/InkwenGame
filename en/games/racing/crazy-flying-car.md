## About

Control a car with wings, make dangerous flights over the abyss and overtake your rivals!
You need to get to the finish line first. To do this, you can simply drive along the track, pushing your opponents, or take a risk and fly off the track, going into flight in the hope of shortening the path by air. To take a risk or to rely on maneuvers - it's up to you!
Play and fly right now!
