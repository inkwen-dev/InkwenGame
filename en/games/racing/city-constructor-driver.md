## About

Here is a relaxation truck driving transport simulation game with 3D cartoon trucks and bricks. As a truck driver, you are required to transport the necessary bricks in this town. Absolutely, just waiting for building process automatically is very boring thing that will perhaps waste your time. So you are allowed to keep tapping the button to speed up the whole building process at each level. Keep calm and complete the transport and building missions!
