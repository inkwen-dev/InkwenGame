Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Are you good at challenging the balanced platforms and ramps? Mad Truck provides you with a driving simulation game. In the Level mode, you need to keep the car stable at the side. In the challenge Level mode, you can drive the car to overpass different obstacles at the normal view. All levels require players to reach the finish line as soon as possible. If you can complete the level at the marked time, you can win all 3 yellow stars.
