## About

Police Car Line Driving is a cool and addictive 3D car-avoiding arcade game. You have to chase unauthorized vehicles and drive as far as you can for higher score records. Each vehicle will give you an extra 100 scores right away. You have to dodge the bombs, other vehicles will do the same. If you hit them, you will lose the game immediately. Your current score and best score will be shown on the settlement page. If you want to improve your current score, you can restart and try again.
