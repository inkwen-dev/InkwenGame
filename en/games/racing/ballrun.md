## How to play

Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Choose the correct route to make the turn. The game will randomly generate routes, each route corresponds to a color, by controlling the character to choose the same color with the character of the route to turn, enter the new route after the characters color will randomly change. Each time you choose the correct route you will get one point, so try and see how many points you can get.
