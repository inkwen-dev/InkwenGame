## About

Flip Runner 3D is an original toy dedicated to parkour. If this discipline is close to you, then you will perfectly understand what and how to do in order to achieve victory. Everyone else is invited to carefully go through the first level in order to study all the rules and understand what actions need to be done, and which ones are better to refuse. Difficult tests await you ahead, but if you prepare well, you will not only be able to pass them, but also become the owner of the best gaming account!
