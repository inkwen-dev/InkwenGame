## About

Jake Black Cat 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a black cat whose mission is to collect the bowls containing cat food while avoiding the enemy cats, flying robot cats, spikes, and saws and reach your cat house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Jake Black Cat" series.
