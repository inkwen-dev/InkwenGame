## About

Dice Pixel Stealer 3D is both a strategy and physics launch arcade game with 3D dices and stickman models on a platform. There is always a pixel pattern sitting in the middle of the platform. You are allowed to launch dice and summon the number of stickman members from throwing each time. The dice will destroy enemy stickman members before it stopped. Good luck and collect over half of the pixel pattern!
