## About

This is a game about eating competition. The game is decompressing and the rules are simple and understandable. Click on the screen to drag the characters to pick up the hamburger vegetables on the ground, and then feed them to sumo wrestlers. The more you eat, the stronger you are. If you push the enemy into the river, you will win
