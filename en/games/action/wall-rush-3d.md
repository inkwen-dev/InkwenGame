Run as fast as wind and race against all best runner in the world. Leap from buildings and vault over wall. Only the 1st winner can reach to the top of victory.

## How to play

- Swipe left and right to control the runner.
- Avoid obstacles and dangerous gaps.
- Stay on the course. You can jump onto rooftops and run along wall edges, but don’t fall.
- Collect coins while running.
- Beat your opponents by reaching the finish line first.
