## About

This is an interesting leisure magical swallowing game. It continues the classic greedy snake playing method, controls a greedy snake to constantly adventure and break through the pass, swallows various items in the scene, so that your body can continue to grow, and pay attention to avoiding bigger greedy snakes, so that you can finally live to get high scores. The greedy snake swallowing world also provides personalized greedy snake skin that can be unlocked, The whole process requires high hand speed and responsiveness of players.
