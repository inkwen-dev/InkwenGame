## How to play

🎮 Controls (PC):
W / A / S / D — move character
Space — jump
LMB — club attack
Camera — RMB + mouse movement

📱 Controls (Mobile):
Joystick — move
Jump button — jump
Attack button — club attack

All actions are performed using easy on-screen buttons.

🚀 Progress & Power-Ups
Climb the mountain, knock down rivals, and earn experience during matches.
The more you play, the stronger and more stable your character becomes.
