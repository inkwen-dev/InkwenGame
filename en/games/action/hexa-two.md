## About

Hexa Two is a fun 3D running and jumping game for 1 Player and 2 Players! Play this game online by yourself against lots of other characters or challenge one of your best friends to play on the same computer in split screen mode! Your goal is to keep running on a ground formed by hexagons, that will just fall down once somebody touches them.
