## About

Here is a collecting arcade game called Coffee Stack. On the conveyor belt platform track, it is managed for you to collect as many coffee materials as you can including dixie cups, cup sets, and green coffee liquid. If possible, you need to avoid losing cups from traps and free hands. Good luck!
