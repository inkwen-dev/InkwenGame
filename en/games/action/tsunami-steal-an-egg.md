## How to play

🏃 Move with the direction keys or the on-screen stick
🥚 Hold the action key to steal an egg from a guard
🪺 Tap to place an egg or a pet and to open a nest
💰 Sell your haul and stack your cash
🏃 Train speed on the treadmill to reach new lands
💎 Spend gems in the shop on boosts and looks
🏆 Climb the global ranks
⚠️ Stole an egg? Run to your base before the guards catch you!
🌊 Watch out for the giant wave!
🥚 The rarer the egg, the stronger the pet!
