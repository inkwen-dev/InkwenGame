## About

Bounce, Collect, and Conquer! Jump Pumpkin Jump is a thrilling platformer game where you take control of a mischievous pumpkin on an endless adventure. Your mission is to jump, leap, and dodge your way through a series of challenging levels, collecting coins and avoiding pesky enemies along the way. How to Play: Navigate the Levels: Guide your pumpkin through a colorful world filled with platforms, obstacles, and hidden treasures. Jump and Collect: Time your jumps perfectly to reach new heights, collect valuable coins, and unlock exciting power-ups.
