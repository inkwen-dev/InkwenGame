## How to play

🕹️ Move: Use your mouse or touch controls to guide your mosquito around the arena.
🔵 Collect: Fly through colorful dots and energy to increase your number and become stronger.
⚔️ Battle: Challenge other mosquitoes and use smart movement to make them crash or lose their advantage.
🚀 Boost: Use your speed boost at the right moment to escape danger, chase enemies, or grab valuable energy.
💥 Survive: Avoid dangerous collisions and stronger opponents while looking for the safest path to grow.
🏆 Climb the Leaderboard: The higher your number and score, the higher you rank. Keep growing, dominate the arena, and fight your way to #1! 🦟👑
