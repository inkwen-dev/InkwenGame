## About

Adventure begins in Bomb Battle Arena game. Use your bombs to counter the enemies and collect items in 85 different levels. You can increase your bombs and speed ability with the points you collect between levels. You can fight with Pirate King or Tomato Boy character. After destroying the enemies, you can move to the next level by finding the castle icon.
