## About

Game Objective The goal in Rooftop Runner is simple: run as far as you can without falling. As you move across rooftops, you must: Jump over gaps between buildings Avoid obstacles and hazards Collect coins scattered across rooftops Stay ahead of danger and survive as long as possible The longer you run, the higher your score becomes. Jump &ndash; Tap or press the jump key to leap across rooftop gaps. Change Direction / Dodge &ndash; Move left or right to avoid obstacles. Collect Coins &ndash; Run through coins to increase your score and rewards. Timing your jumps correctly is important. If you jump too early or too late, you may fall between buildings.
