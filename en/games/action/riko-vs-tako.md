## About

Riko vs Tako is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect chocolate ball candies while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots and their bullets, spikes, giant blades and reach the loop hole in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
