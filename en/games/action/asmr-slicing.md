## About

ASMR Slicing is a simple app game. Do you like the Fruit Ninja game? You could continue to experience slicing fruit, but the rule is different. There are some big fruits on the platform, you have to break them into pieces. Those pieces will drop off under gravity. Eventually, they will drop into a blender, you can get a tasty juice!
