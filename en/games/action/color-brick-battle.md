## About

Color the Brick Battle is a fast and addictive brick breaker game where color is the key to victory. Control the paddle, bounce the ball and destroy bricks by matching their colors. The ball starts white, but it changes color when it touches the colored side of the paddle. Only bricks with the same color can be destroyed, so precision and positioning are essential. Test your reflexes and strategy in a classic arcade experience with a colorful twist.
