## About

Introducing Time Bomb Rush, the newest parkour game! Dont miss out on the action. In this thrilling game, your mission is to prevent your explosives from detonating until you reach the finish line. Navigate through obstacles, gather fuses, and compete against the ticking clock before the fuses burn out. Remember, success is achieved by holding onto every single fuse until the very end. Will you emerge victorious?
