## About

Atanu Boy 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect money while avoiding the enemy gangsters, flying drones and their bullets, saws, spikes and reach the pink flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 level to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Atanu Boy" series.
