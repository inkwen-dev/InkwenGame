If playing in single player mode Move W A S D or ARROW KEYS or MOUSE and LEFT-CLICK If playing in two player mode Player 1 W A S D Player 2 ARROW KEYS

## About

The Dash Party arena fight is beginning! It is time to slide over the platform ground and divide your opponents into pieces. Various characters and various game arenas are waiting for you. The more you dash, the more knife options will appear in your character's hands. Play with your friend and make the arena fight more exciting and fun! Let's go!
