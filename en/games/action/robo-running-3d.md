## About

Run with an ink man who does parkour. He holds a magical pen that can turn your drawings into real objects, such as boats, weapons, or even swim rings. But you must collect ink because without it, the pen will lose its magical power. Your ultimate goal is to overcome all obstacles and win the final Boss battle.
