## About

This is a platform game with cube. You need to move the cube on the platform. Each side of the cube is a with different color. And the platform is painted with different colors that are on the cube. The object of the game is to move through the platform with the cube but to match the colors of the cube with the colors of the platform. So you have to rotate the cube while jumping on the platforms.
