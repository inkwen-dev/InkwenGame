## About

Choco Benno 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you have to collect all the glasses of the chocolate milkshake while avoiding the chocolate eater monsters, flying monsters and their bullets, spikes, giant blades and reach the Chocolate exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Choco Benno" series.
