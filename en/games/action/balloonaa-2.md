## About

Balloonaa 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as balloon boy who have to collect all of the helium gas containers while avoiding the enemy balloons, spikes, pins, and saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Balloonaa" series.
