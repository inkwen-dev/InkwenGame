## About

Venom's Adventures is an adventure game story about this cute venom. You need to control the venom to get to the end of the road. Your venom makes sure you can jump over the obstacle. Bypass obstacles and find hidden paths to get diamonds. You can also spend diamonds on more difficult levels. Or unlimited levels waiting for you to try!
