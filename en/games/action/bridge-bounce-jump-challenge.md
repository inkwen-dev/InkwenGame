## About

Bridge Bounce: Jump Challenge is a fast-paced arcade runner where every jump counts. Bounce across a high bridge, flip through gates, dodge barriers, and leap over dangerous gaps above the water. Collect coins, keep your momentum, and see how far you can go without falling.
Easy to play, challenging to master — one mistake can end your run!
