## How to play

Click or tap to start Hold and drag left or right to steer on touch devices On desktop use A D or the Left Right Arrow keys Recruit runners avoid traps keep the leader alive and reach the finish Choose NEXT to continue or RESTART after failing

## About

Lead a fearless crowd through chaotic city roads in Crazy Bus Escape Runner. Swipe or use the keyboard to dodge dangerous obstacles, recruit new runners, survive brutal traps, and reach the finish together. Complete five increasingly challenging stages, protect your team, and prove you can escape every frantic course alive.
