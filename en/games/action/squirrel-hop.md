## About

"Squirrel Hop" is a really fun platform game. You have to tap left and right buttons to jump on platforms. Collect walnut to get some seconds. Tap and jump fast to make a high score. To make this game more interesting for kids, we have added cute graphics in it. With funny sounds, it would be fun playing with squirrel characters. Theme and characters get change as you make a nice score while playing the game.
