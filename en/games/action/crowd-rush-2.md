## About

Lead a crowd of adorable blue runners down the track! Drag left or right to pick your gate: &times;2 or +5? The right call depends on how many youve got &mdash; choose smart and your crowd snowballs into a stampede of hundreds! Red enemy mobs (and giant brutes worth 10!) block the road: crash into them and both sides melt away one-for-one in a wild brawl &mdash; bring more, win big. Made a bad pick? Rescue gates give you a comeback. Perfect runs feel glorious, a few mistakes still squeak through, careless runs get crushed. Reach the finish to win!
