## About

Towra 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect the keys while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures, spikes and saws and reach the tower in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Towra" series.
