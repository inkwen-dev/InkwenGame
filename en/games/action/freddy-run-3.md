## About

Freddy founds himself trapped once again inside a terrifying nightmare so he needs your help in order to survive inside the medieval haunted castle he dreams of. Be aware as once you hit the Play button, there is no turning back and you must complete all sort of dangerous challenges while wondering the castle hallways, rooms and dungeons fighting monsters and escaping deadly traps. If you manage to make it through, in the end you shall face the Skeleton King aka final Boss into a one vs one battle.
