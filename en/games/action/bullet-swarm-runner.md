## About

Bullet Swarm Runner is a fast-paced 3D endless runner where you dodge deadly obstacles and take down a massive boss with your bullet swarm. Steer left and right to avoid spinning swords, swinging hammers, sweeping lasers, and rotating spike pillars. Use your coins to upgrade your starting bullet count and revenue multiplier, then unlock new bullet skins in the shop. Survive the run, reach the boss, and fire everything youve got to win.
