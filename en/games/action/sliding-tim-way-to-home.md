## How to play

Press the spacebar to make the player start running and release when needed to stop the player from running You can also use the LMB instead of the space bar

## About

Adventure game based on completing a difficult path. You play as the hero Tim. He was far from home and he needed help to run back. But the path ahead is not easy, along the way you have to overcome many different obstacles, you will need to go through mud, desert and even red-hot lava. It is important to keep the pace, to slip somewhere, and to slow down in time. At first glance, a simple task can turn into a failure, because with each new step, unpredictable routes await you.
