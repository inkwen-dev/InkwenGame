## About

Crazy Runner &ndash; is a classic and cool is never ending runner game where you have to run on the beautiful road, jumping over cars, bombs, tires, and collect cool coins, and food on the road! Be careful not to get under the car, and do not jump on the bomb! The main goal of the game to go as far as possible at the same time gaining the greatest number of points!
