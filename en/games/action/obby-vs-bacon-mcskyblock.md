## About

Are you ready for Obby and Bacons adventure? They need to survive in the block world. In their first adventure, they wont face a very tough life. Obby and Bacon need to catch all the animals and collect all the diamonds. Gather all the diamonds and reach the finish line. Obby and Bacon have a great friendship; they are perfect friends. Help them reach the finish line together. Be careful of the cliffs on the way. And dont forget to watch out for obstacles on the road, or you wont be able to reach the end of the level.
