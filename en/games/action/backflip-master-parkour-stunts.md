## About

Test your nerves, timing, and stunt skills in the ultimate physics-driven backflipping challenge!
In Backflip Master 3D, step onto dizzying rooftop ledges, towering cliffs, and urban platforms to pull off breathtaking backflips. Control your momentum with intuitive one-touch mechanics: crouch to build jump power, tuck tightly into a ball to accelerate your spin, and release at the perfect split-second to stick a clean, two-footed landing!
Land flat on the bullseye for maximum points and star ratings, but be careful—miscalculate your rotation and you’ll take a hilarious ragdoll crash into the ground!
Can you complete every level, unlock all stunt skins, and claim the title of Backflip Master?
🌟 Key Features
Satisfying Physics: Experience realistic body mechanics combined with entertaining ragdoll fail physics.
Intuitive One-Button Control: Easy to pick up, intensely rewarding to master.
Challenging Environments: Jump from gym mats, construction sites, cliff edges, and skyscraper rooftops.
Landing Accuracy & Scores: Hit the center of the target zone to earn 3-star perfect scores.
Interactive Tutorial: Step-by-step guidance for beginners with mid-air slow motion to help you nail your first flip!
Fun Unlockables: Earn coins from great stunts to unlock fresh characters, outfits, and accessories.
