## About

Throw Sword is an engaging stickman action game where you control a stickman throwing swords to defeat opponents. Aim carefully, throw with precise force, and strike at the right moment to eliminate enemies before being defeated. With simple gameplay, fast-paced action, and eye-catching combat effects, Throw Sword offers an exciting entertainment experience. Test your reflexes, conquer high scores, and become a sword-throwing master!
