## How to play

CONTROLS:
WASD or arrow keys - Move
Mouse - Look around and aim
Hold the left mouse button - Shoot your web and swing
Release the left mouse button - Let go of the web
Space - Jump, or leap off while swinging

## About

Master web swinging in this 3D obby parkour game! Shoot your web, build momentum, and release at the right moment to cross gaps and reach the next platform.

Earn rewards, improve your character, and unlock new costumes as you progress. Use rebirth to gain permanent bonuses and push further with every run.

Each obstacle tests your aim, timing, and control. Find your rhythm, perfect your swings, and see how far you can go!
