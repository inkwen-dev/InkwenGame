## About

**Color Battle Filler** is an exciting singleplayer strategy puzzle game where your goal is to conquer the entire board by spreading your color across every tile. Starting from one corner, you must carefully choose colors to expand your territory while minimizing the number of moves. Each decision shapes the board, creating satisfying waves of vibrant color.
