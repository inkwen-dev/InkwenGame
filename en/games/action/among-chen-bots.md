## About

Among Chen Bots is a 2D Sci-Fi themed platformer where you have to collect the Blue Energy boxes while avoiding the enemy bots, spikes, giant blades, and flying bots and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
