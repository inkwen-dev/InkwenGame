## About

The zombies and variant creatures are coming over you in waves. It is time to defend yourself in this game! The heroes are armed with weapons but they need to improve them by earning some gold from the zombie destruction. Upgrade your armories, weapons, and earnings on each round and make your defense stronger. Play with your friend and defend your lands against the zombies together by 2 player game mode. Let the action begin!
