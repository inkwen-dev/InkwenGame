## About

Ninja Versus Ninja is a non-violent simple yet fun battle arena fighting game. Proudly play as the Blue Ninja to fight your evil arch-nemesis Red Ninja. You'll unlock 30 action-filled arenas to battle, Ninja to Ninja. Fight for your glory. Become the fastest untouchable ninja in the universe. Your enemy is no newbie. Use your sword or throw deadly throwing stars. Who will win and who will become defeated? Have fun with the Ninja Versus Ninja game. Features Fighting game without violence Two fighting strategies, close combat vs throwing stars 30 levels, every arena looks different
