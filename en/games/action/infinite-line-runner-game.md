## About

Infinite Line Runner Game is a fast-paced and addictive runner game that challenges your reflexes, focus, and timing. In this game, players control a character running endlessly along a line-based path filled with obstacles, turns, and increasing speed. The goal is simple: keep running as long as possible, avoid obstacles, and achieve the highest score.
