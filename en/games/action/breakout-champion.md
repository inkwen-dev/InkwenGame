## About

Breakout Champion is a classic breakout game. Lines of bricks are coming down toward you. Your goal is to destroy them all by repeatedly bouncing a ball off a paddle into them before a line of bricks touches your bounds. Eliminate all the bricks to complete a level. Complete each level within 5 minutes. Handle the paddle with the help of a mouse or fingertip.
