## About

Time to push some chairs of office workers at various offices! These office people have some weapons and tools in their hands. Their goal is to push each other outside of the arena by using their weapons. There are lots of weapon types and chair types that will be waiting. You can join this office battle in 1 player and 2 player game modes. Call a friend and start this office chair-pushing battle now!
