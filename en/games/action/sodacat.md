## About

Beat Cat is a one-button rhythm runner starring sodacat, a chubby music-loving kitten. Dash through a rainy neon alley and jump to the beat &mdash; tap to leap, tap again for a double jump. Dodge neon equalizer pillars, speaker towers and walls, leap through gaps, and collect glowing music notes to build big combos. Every obstacle arrives on the beat, so find the rhythm, chain your jumps, and chase a new high score!
