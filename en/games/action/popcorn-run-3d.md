## About

There is naked corn on the cob who wants to gather all the corn kernels that have all ran away overnight, how insane. Your mission is to assist it in collecting these corn kernels and reaching the conclusion, however! Stay alert for potential hazards, such as roadside stoves and barbecuing stations! Its fire will transform the corn into popcorn or anything else. Butter is a fantastic thing; convert it into a skateboard to help you speed up. There are several game levels with numerous variants ready for you to tackle!
