## About

Tim Adventures 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the cookies while avoiding the enemies, and their bullets, spikes and ground saws and reach the purple flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Tim Adventures" series.
