## About

Bomber Battle Arena is an exciting 3D collecting game that lets you play as a bomb-wielding character. Your goal is to strategically detonate bombs to clear various green plants and tree stumps, collect a golden key, and reach the treasure chest to advance to the next level. Be careful of the bomb&rsquo;s blast radius and avoid contact with other monsters along the way. Get ready for an explosive adventure through 10 challenging levels full of explosive action!
