Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Balloonaa is a 2D little platformer where you play as a balloon boy and you have to collect the containers of helium gas while avoiding the enemy balloons, pins, spikes, and ground saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
