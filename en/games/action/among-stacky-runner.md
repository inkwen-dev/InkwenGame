## About

Among Stacky Runner is an adventure game where you have to collect yellow blocks in order to overcome the stairs and various obstacles. You can build a ladder from the blocks you collected using the left click button. In Among Stacky Runner, it is necessary to use the blocks you have sparingly in order to move on to the next level. Play the free Among Stacky Runner game to get the highest score by reaching the top.
