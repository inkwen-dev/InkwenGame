## How to play

Players can enter the game screen by clicking the button on the right of the selection interface The left and right legs of the player are displayed on the left and lower sides of the interface respectively and the player needs to control the movement

## About

It's a funny walking game. Players play different roles in the game, to meet a variety of walking challenges, and the gameplay is similar to a jump, according to the click time to walk, the step must be calculated accurately, or will fail. In the game, the player needs to have a good balance in order to successfully reach the end.
