## How to play

Controls
PC:
- WASD/Arrow keys - Move
- Hold down the right mouse button and drag - Pan the camera
- Spacebar - Brake

Mobile:
- Joystick on the left - Move
- Hold and drag across the screen - Pan the camera
- Button on the right - Brake
