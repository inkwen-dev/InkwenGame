## About

You are a predatory black hole in a city! You have to devour surrounding objects, including chairs, trees, and even buildings to grow up and become bigger than opponents. Keep moving, find the best spots full of objects that you can eat if you want to score maximum points and win. Take care and stay away from big black holes — otherwise your game will soon be over...
