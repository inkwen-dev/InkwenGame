## About

The fun Drunk stickman games continue with Slap Wars. The aim of the game is to beat the opponents with balanced slaps. Try to stop the slap power bar in the green area. In this way, your slaps will be effective. The opponents will get harder each round and the bar will speed up! Drunken Slap Wars game can be played by 1 or 2 people. Let the fun matchups begin!
