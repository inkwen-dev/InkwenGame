## About

Stickman Kombat 2D is a fast-paced fighting game where you battle stickman warriors in intense 1v1 arena combat. Choose your fighter and master punch, kick, and special attack combos to knock out your opponent. Fight through multiple levels and game modes against tougher enemies, dodge incoming blows, and chain devastating combos to win the match. Simple mouse click or tap controls make it easy to jump into action, while timing and strategy keep the combat challenging. Test your skills across a variety of stickman characters and arenas, and become the ultimate champion!
