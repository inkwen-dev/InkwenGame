## About

Digital Circus Town Builder is a captivating simulation game that combines the charm of a circus with the excitement of urban planning. In this immersive virtual world, players take on the role of a town mayor tasked with creating a one-of-a-kind circus-themed town. Build and customize your towns infrastructure, from colorful big tops to thrilling amusement rides, all while managing resources and keeping your citizens entertained.
