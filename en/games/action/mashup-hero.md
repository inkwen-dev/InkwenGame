## About

Mashup Hero is a fast-paced mecha parkour game where you assemble a robot while sprinting to the finish line. Swipe to avoid traps, then unleash a massive shockwave to crush your opponents in the final arena. Earn cores to upgrade speed, armor, and strength, unlock new skins, and complete increasingly tricky courses. Simple one-finger control, bite-sized stage, massive hits.
