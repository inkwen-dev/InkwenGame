## About

+1 Speed Superhero Obby is a 3D obby where every step adds speed. Run the colorful towers, train on treadmills, smash speed gates and climb through five worlds. Every speed milestone unlocks a new superhero power that changes how you play the same course: wall run, water run, gravity flip, wrecking ball and a sonic boom. Hatch sidekick pets, suit up in hero costumes, race the ghosts of other players and join live servers with other heroes for shared server boosts and goals. Daily rewards, a daily tower and rebirths keep the speed climbing.
