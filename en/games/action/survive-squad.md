## About

Welcome to Survive Squad, a hot survival game that combines Strategy and Role-Playing. In a world full of crises, you will be the commander of the squad. Lead your Heroes against endless enemies! Use your wits and strategy to survive the battlefield. Collect and lay out your weapons in your Backpack System. Merge weapons of the same type for upgrades. Flexible match equipment to create the strongest survival warrior.
