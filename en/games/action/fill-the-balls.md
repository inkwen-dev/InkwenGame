## About

In Fill The Balls, you have to help the Santa who exploded the bomb to fill the balls into the bowl. You can adjust the handles by clicking or tapping anywhere on the screen to fill the balls into the bowl. After adjusting the arms, you have to press the Ready button and send the balls into the bowl. Try to solve the whole puzzle with 100+ Levels.
