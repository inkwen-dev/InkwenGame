## About

Coin Craze is an adventure RPG game in which you need to control a boy who is collecting coin. The boy is standing on a moving platform you need to tap/click when it is over another platform to make him jump on it. If you miss the next platform the spike below the platform will end the journey of the boy. As game progresses you will find different fantastic items which help you to collect more and more coins.
