## About

Mini Arrows is a retro-style platformer where you guide a blob character to his goal; turning on and off arrow blocks on the level you change the direction and actions on the character, use the arrows to move wisely the character. The game has 27 levels, a speedrun mode,retro-style graphics, and addictive music.
