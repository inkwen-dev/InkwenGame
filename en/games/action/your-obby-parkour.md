## About

Enter a thrilling world of non-stop challenges! Run, jump, dodge, and conquer in the ultimate parkour platformer! 
Survive crazy levels — from burning lava and icy slides to water traps and mysterious portals. Watch out for trampolines, spinning traps, and tons of hidden paths!

Play with friends and travel through fun regions — from simple to brutally hard! Discover the hidden little dragon, collect trophies, and unlock secret shortcuts and bonus zones!

Be careful of:
Deadly skeletons
Lava-filled volcanoes
Slippery ice paths
Horror mazes where it's easy to get lost!

Ready to be a true parkour master? Find the fastest paths, conquer extreme stages, and show the world your skills!

Perfect for fans of obby-style maps, parkour games, secrets, adventures, and multiplayer fun!
