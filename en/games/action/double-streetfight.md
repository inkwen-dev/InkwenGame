## How to play

ARROW - MOVE Z - PUNCH PRESS 2 SECONDS POWER PUNCH X - KICK ESCAPE GRAB C - GUARD V - GRAB ARROW LEFT AND RIGHT - PUSH TO 2 TO RUN THEN CAN PUNCH OR KICK SPACE - JUMP

## About

Play Double StreetFight multiplayer campaign and also single player campaign, this game can play 2 people in campaign and at 8 people in free for all, you will fight with enemy bots around the all 3 mission and levels, collect coins when knock down enemy and buy costume. Play with your friend and finish all levels and win the match or you can fight 1vs1 in free for all. Have fun and join to our discord for new update and also to report bugs.
