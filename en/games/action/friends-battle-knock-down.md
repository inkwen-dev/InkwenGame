## How to play

Press the S key or the Down Arrow key to push or hit The player with the most points in 120 seconds wins Use the WASD keys or the Arrow keys to move

## About

In this adventure, two fighting friends need to push each other off a cliff. In this fun game, push your friend off the cliff. To throw them off, just push them. Be careful as TNT falls from the sky, and if it explodes, it can knock you down too. Avoid the TNT to stay safe and win the game by being the one who knocks the most enemies down.
