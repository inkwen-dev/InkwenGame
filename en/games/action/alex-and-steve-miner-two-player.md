## How to play

Use the WASD keys and the Arrow keys to move You need to move in sequence to pass each level Use the E key to grab the box Hold down the L key to open the valve

## About

Alex and Steve accidentally enter a cave, and you need to help them find their way out. Team up with a friend and work together to escape from the cave. To get out, youll have to overcome the obstacles and challenges inside the cave. The cave is full of difficulties, so you must be very careful. Challenges are present everywhere in the cave. Alex and Steve need to work as a team.
