## About

Have you ever made a stickman with ropes and sticks? Rope Man Run 3D is a 3D vertical-version running arcade game that is made from the Unity game engine. Your objective is to help a colorful rope-man arrive at the finish platform safely. It will change the corresponding color after overpassing a colorful wall. I hope you could avoid obstacles and complete racing!
