## About

Compete against other players to get a real parkour experience. Jump from wall to wall, climb ropes, swipe to go faster, double click to jump higher, grab to swing, use bars to not fall. Never stop running! There dozens of cards and all of them have different skills. Increase your ranking by beating your opponents. You can also customize your character. Skin, clothing, dance and much more!
