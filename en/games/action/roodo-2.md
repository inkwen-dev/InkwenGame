## About

Roodo 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect the Golden keys while avoiding the yellow and green enemy robots, flying bots, spikes and ground saws and reach the Exit door. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original Roodo game.
