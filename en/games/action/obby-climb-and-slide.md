## How to play

Your goal is to reach the top of the tower. Earn coins as you climb up. Buy new floaties for your character to climb faster and new pets to earn even more.

🎮 On PC
● WASD – Move
● Mouse – Camera control
● Space – Jump

📱 On Mobile
● Use the on-screen controls
● Drag anywhere on the screen – Camera control

## About

🏔️ Ready to conquer the tallest slides? In this game, you can! 🎢

Climb higher and higher up the ladders, then rush down the thrilling slides! 💨 The farther you slide, the more coins you collect! 💰

And with cute pets by your side, you’ll earn even more — they’ll help you gather coins! 🐱🐢🐰

What awaits you:
• Long ladders and exciting slides for speedy rides 🪜➡️🎢
• Funny pet companions 🐦
• Bright levels and new heights ⛰️
• Fun gameplay and colorful graphics 🎨

Climb up, slide down, and become the ultimate slide champion! 🎮✨


Controls:
Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys - move around the map, Space - jump, click - confirm actions.  
Mobile: virtual joystick - move around the map, tap - confirm actions.

Tips & Tricks:
• Auto climb: allows you to release control of the character for a while.  
• Cup: Don't forget to separately go for the cup and collect it; without this action, climbing will only earn you coins for descending.

Upgrades / Progression / Economy:
• Pets: allow you to earn more coins, depending on the multiplier.  
• Boats: affect the speed of ascent and the number of coins earned during descent.

Levels / Maps / Worlds:
• 5 worlds  
• Each world features a slope, a boat shop, and three pet sales stations. The more expensive the unlock for a new world, the more challenging the ascent to the top will be, but the rewards will be greater (more coins, and at the top, you can earn not just 1 cup, but 5, 10, and so on).

Core Gameplay & Story  
Q: What is the goal of the game?  
A: Reach the top of the slope, collect the cup, and enjoy sliding down to repeat the feat. Earn coins, acquire new gear, and enjoy the process.

Q: What grabs you in the first few minutes?  
A: Simple, understandable gameplay, an easy task, and soothing graphics draw you in from the very first moments of the game.

Mechanics & Progression  
Q: What unlocks as you play?  
A: As you progress, new difficulty levels, pet collections, and watercraft become available.

Q: Does the difficulty change?  
A: Each new tower becomes harder to overcome and requires the character to be more upgraded.

Q: How can you level up faster?  
A: You can start with short ascents to upgrade your boat, allowing you to ascend the entire height of the slope more quickly later on.

Economy & Customization  
Q: What can you buy/upgrade?  
A: You can purchase pets (three power levels depending on price) and boats (which affect the character's ascent speed on the ladder).

Retention & Engagement  
Q: Is there onboarding in the game? What does it include?  
A: The onboarding guides the player through all available options on the basic path.
