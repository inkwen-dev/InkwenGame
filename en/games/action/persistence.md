## About

In Persistence, you will control the last survivor of the Nenju tribe. Nenju has been consumed by monsters, and your mission is to guide this last survivor to Eiju! But don't think it's going to be easy, you'll need a lot of "Persistence" to complete this quest. Persistence breaks the fourth wall, pay attention to the character's lines, they can help you... Or not! Use the arrow keys to switch between game options, and return to select! Good game, go, save the last survivor of the Nenju tribe.
