## About

The ultimate kicking simulator! Train your legs, increase your kick power, and smash through powerful barriers to unlock new worlds and challenges. Every tap makes you stronger and helps you progress faster.

Collect wins to unlock stronger kicks, discover unique pets that give massive boosts, and equip powerful auras to multiply your strength even more. Level up your character, rebirth for permanent bonuses, and become unstoppable.

Compete against other players, climb the leaderboards, and prove that you have the strongest kick in the game. Even while offline, your power keeps growing, so you can always come back stronger.

Can you break every obstacle and become the ultimate kick master?
