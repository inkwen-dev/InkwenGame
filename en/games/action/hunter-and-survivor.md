## About

Hunter and Survivor is an intense hide-and-seek game where you play as either the Hunter or the Survivor with completely opposite objectives. If you become the Hunter, you must track down and catch all the Survivors before they collect enough keys on the map. Every second that passes is either an opportunity or a failure. When playing as a Survivor, you need to move skillfully, hide intelligently, and quickly gather all the keys to open a safe escape route before being caught by the Hunter.
