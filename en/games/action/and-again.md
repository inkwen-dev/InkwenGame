Mouse click or tap to play

## About

Together with the main character of the new online game And Again, you will go on a journey through the painted world in search of treasures and adventures. The location in which your hero will be will be visible on the screen in front of you. By controlling his actions you will have to move around the area. Overcoming various obstacles and traps, you will have to help the hero find special magic pins. With their help, he will be able to activate the portal leading to the next level.
