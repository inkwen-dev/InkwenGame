## How to play

The red player moves using the WASD keys The blue player moves using the arrow keys Press the Bottom Arrow key and the S key to fire and buy cannons

## About

Are you ready to battle your friend? Throw cannonballs at your buddy and destroy their castle. Are you up for the red versus blue cannonball fight? Load up the cannon and fire away. Purchase a cannonball and launch it. The first castle to crumble wins. Battle your friend and emerge victorious.
