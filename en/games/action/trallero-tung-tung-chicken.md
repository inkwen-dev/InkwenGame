## About

Have you entered the world of farmers? You need to catch the chickens. Catch all the chickens and win the game. The first to collect 10 chickens wins. Choose one of the farmer characters, Trallero or Tung Sahur, and race against your friend to win. You can break wooden crates by jumping on them. Jump three times to break the crate and grab the chicken. Collect all the chickens before your friend. Bring the chickens to the coop as fast as you can.
