## How to play

Grab bottles and throw them on special platforms, completing challenges and gaining points.

At each level there is a zone marked as "Win", when a bottle hits it, the level will be passed and access to a new one will open.

Management:

For the computer:

   Arrows - hand control
 
   Space bar - hold down the space bar and the character grabs the bottle, throws it when released.

For a smartphone

    Arrow buttons - hand control
 
    A button with a hand - when pressed, the character grabs the bottle, when released, throws it

## About

Can you flip bottle and put it flat on the bottom? And on the neck? And if you need to throw it on a tiny platform?

Test your skills, show precision and dexterity to toss the bottle and gently land it on the platform. 
Every movement matters: you have to control your hands, balance and calculate the trajectory so that the bottle fits perfectly on its target. 

Collect points for successful landings and unlock new levels full of exciting obstacles and unexpected turns. 

Luck and skill go hand in hand in this exciting precision game, where each level brings a new challenge and the opportunity to become a real bottle master!
