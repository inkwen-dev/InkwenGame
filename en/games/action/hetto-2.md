## About

Hetto 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the bottles of magic potion while avoiding the enemies, flying robots, fire, spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Hetto" series.
