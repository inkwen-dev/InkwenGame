Tap the screen to hit your enemy.
For each enemy you kill, you get currency that you can spend on upgrading your character.

## About

Boys - Battle for the District is an addictive idle clicker game where you tap to battle and conquer urban turf against rival gangs. Grow your power through relentless tapping as you defeat foes and expand your district empire.

Core Gameplay

Tap the screen furiously to smash opponents in fast-paced idle battles - no complex controls needed. Watch enemies crumble with each click.

Key Features

1) Idle Tapping: Simple one-tap combat to defeat gangs and claim districts.

2) Upgrades & Prestige: Boost damage and restart stronger each run.

3)Endless Growth: Climb leaderboards through incremental power spikes.
