## About

In this adventure, a noob and their closest friend, the Pro, are trying to reach and open a chest in a dangerous forest. The forest is so perilous that it could lead to their deaths, which they must avoid. Only the noob can assemble the key to the chest, so the noob needs to find all the pieces of the key. Meanwhile, with their powerful sword, the Pro can defeat all the monsters in the forest, ensuring a safer journey to the chest.
