## About

Heroes Inc. is a cool superhero platform action game in which you have to create and train your own little stickman hero. In this free online game on Silvergames.com you will start by selecting two elements to combine them and make the perfect superhero, like a rock and super strength. Once you have created your character, send him out to fight off evil training robots using your unique powers, that can go from fire and ice to radioactivity and random elements like scissors. What would your perfect superhero look like? Have fun playing this free online game Heroes Inc.!
