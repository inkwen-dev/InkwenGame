Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Among Yetto Bots is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect all of the golden balls while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots and their bullets, spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
