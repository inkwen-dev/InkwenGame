## About

Tom is ready to run anywhere to fill his pockets with gold coins. Go on an adventure with Tom. Be careful, the roads leading to gold are dangerous. They are filled with obstacles like cars, buses, blocks, etc. You must run along the streets and help Tom not to run into obstacles and not to lose. Collected coins can be spent in the store on a vehicle that will help you move even faster. Have fun with this addictive Runner!
