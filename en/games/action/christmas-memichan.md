Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Christmas Memichan is a 2D Christmas themed platformer where you play as a Santa Cat called Memichan who have to collect all of the chocolate bars while avoiding the enemy demon cats, spikes, saws and reach your cat house door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
