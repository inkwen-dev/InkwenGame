## About

Step into a fast-paced 2D action world where you are the lone hero standing against waves of enemies! Gameplay Highlights: Enemies appear randomly from both left and right sides of the screen. React quickly and click to attack with precision&mdash;time your moves to survive! Watch out as the pace increases and the enemies come faster! Clean and vibrant 2D visuals make the action feel smooth and satisfying.
