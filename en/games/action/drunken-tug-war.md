## How to play

Player 1 W or Touch the screen Player 2 UP ARROW or Touch the screen

## About

The Drunken game series continues with Tug War in its new episode. The aim of the game is to pull the rope as fast as possible and pull your opponent to your side. The green radioactive area in the middle eliminates players. Each time you touch this area, your health bar will drop. Press the keys as fast as you can and pull the opponent.
