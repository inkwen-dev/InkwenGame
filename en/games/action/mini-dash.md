## How to play

Desktop -X Left Click C or Right Click to Jump -X or Left Click in the air to Dash Left -C or Right Click in the air to Dash Right Mobile -Touch Left or Right to Jump -Touch Left in the air to Dash Left -Touch Right in the air to Dash Right

## About

Mini Dash is a retro-style platform game where you command a small blob character; use his air-dashing skills to evade obstacles and collect the level coins! Also, you have a speedrun mode to test your skills.
