Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Christmas Chuni Bot is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have collect all of the battery cells while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots and their bullet, spikes, ground saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
