## About

"Brave Soldier - Invasion Of Cyborgs" is a pixel art platform/side scrolling game where the player must fulfill his mission by defeating all the Cyborgs that invaded Planet Earth. The game contains fifteen levels and a BOSS with unique and very creative mechanics. With a pixel art drawing style, the game gives you a feeling of nostalgia, along with lots of fun! Face the Cyborg Skull boss and save the world! Have fun!
