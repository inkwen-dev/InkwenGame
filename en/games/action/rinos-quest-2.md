Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Rinos Quest 2 is a simple platformer where you have to collect silver keys while avoiding spike monsters, flying enemies, and other traps like saws. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the sequel of Rinos Quest original series. The second installment has more new levels, enemies and traps.
