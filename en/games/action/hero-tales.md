## How to play

Your character runs automatically You only have to give him directions to collect items and regain life Each level ends when you reach the finish line

## About

A fantasy world, filed with monsters and magnificent views are waiting for you. Incarnate a charismatic and valiant character. He is all in to save his life and get rid of his enemies. But be careful, each enemy you kill will drain your life. It’s up to you to manage the balance between you life and your attacks. You can regain life thanks to bananas! Hearts and little apples you will find along the way.
