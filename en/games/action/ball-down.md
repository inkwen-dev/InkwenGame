## How to play

Tap on the left or right side of the game screen to control the ball

## About

Ball Down is a funny arcade game where you need to control the ball that falls from the top of the game to the bottom. Your job is not to allow you to fall the ball to the bottom. There is a different types of platforms, and the ball needs to slide on them to slow the falling. But be careful, if you keep the ball on one platform you can lose the ball too if the ball hit on the top of the game screen. Some of the platforms will be sloped and the ball will fall faster.
