## About

Stick Archers Battle is a fun 1-2 player bow challenge game. Try to shoot accurate arrows at your opponent by adapting to the changing environments and physics. Before releasing your arrow, guess the right angles and distance so your arrows can go to your friend. You can play the game in "1 Player", "2 Player" and "Death-match" modes. The one who reaches 5 score first, wins the game.
