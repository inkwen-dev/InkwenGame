Use WASD or arrow keys for movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Lovei is a 2D platform game where you need to collect heart potions while avoiding the spikes, saws and enemies and instill the feeling of love in the heart of the man again who lost it due to the recent breakup. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
