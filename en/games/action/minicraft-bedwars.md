## How to play

The red player moves using the WASD keys The blue player moves using the arrow keys Press the Bottom Arrow key and the S key to fire and buy cannons

## About

How about protecting your bed and destroying your friend&rsquo;s bed in Bedwars mode? Choose either the red or blue team. Collect gold as quickly as possible and use the gold you&rsquo;ve gathered to buy cannonballs for your cannon. Fire cannonballs from your cannon to hit your friend&rsquo;s bed. You can collect both your own gold and your friend&rsquo;s gold. Gather all the gold and purchase cannonballs. Remember, if you miss your shot, you&rsquo;ll run out of cannonballs and lose the game. You must be very careful.
