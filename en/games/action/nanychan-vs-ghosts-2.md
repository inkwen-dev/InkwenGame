## About

Nanychan vs Ghosts is a 2D Halloween themed platformer where you have to collect red balls while avoiding the Pumpkins, ghosts, monsters, spikes, fire, flying pumpkins and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Nanychan vs Ghosts" series.
