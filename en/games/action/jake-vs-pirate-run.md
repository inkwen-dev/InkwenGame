## About

Jake vs Pirate Run is an exciting running game where you have to help a young sailor complete a challenging obstacle course while being pursuit by an evil pirate that wants to steal his treasure. While on the run dealing with all sort of deadly obstacles and dangerous enemies don't forget to look back and avoid being shoot by the pirate. Have fun and enjoy a truly impressive game while struggling to beat all 5 levels and complete the mission by helping Jake reach his ship and sail away from any threats.
