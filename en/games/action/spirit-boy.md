## About

In this clever puzzle adventure, you guide a boy through tricky levels filled with deadly traps and clever challenges. Spirit Boy transforms into a ghostly soul when faced with danger, letting him float past spikes and reach hidden areas. But to finish the level, he must return to his human form and find the exit door all while guarding his three precious lives.
