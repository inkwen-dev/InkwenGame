## About

War Of Tanks: Paper Note is a competitive game that you can play with your friend and alone with "1 PLAYER" and "2 PLAYER" options. In the "1 PLAYER" option, there are 3 different levels that will appear randomly. There are 5 targets you have to destroy in each level. Each time you destroy a target, the lasers will be turned on so that you can move on to the next target. If you touch the laser without destroying the target, your tank will explode and you have to start over. You can see the destinations and the end point on the map. In the "2 PLAYER" option, there are 20 levels in total. You can choose the number of games and fight against your friend in random levels that you encounter. Increase the fun even more by using the bonuses found in all levels. Have fun!
