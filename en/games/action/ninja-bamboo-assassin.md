## How to play

Ninja: Bamboo Assassin is an action-packed arcade game where players take on the role of a skilled ninja on a stealth mission through treacherous bamboo forests. As the Bamboo Assassin, you must navigate through challenging levels filled with enemies, obstacles, and traps. Employ your stealth, agility, and precision to outmaneuver adversaries and eliminate targets silently. The game emphasizes fast-paced combat, strategic planning, and quick reflexes, offering an immersive experience that tests both your skills and timing. With its sleek design and captivating gameplay, Ninja: Bamboo Assassin delivers a thrilling adventure for fans of ninja-themed games.
