Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Ice Cube Man is a 2D cute little platformer where you have to collect all of the bags containing ice cubes while avoiding the fire cubes, flying thunder cubes and their bullets, giant blades, fire and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
