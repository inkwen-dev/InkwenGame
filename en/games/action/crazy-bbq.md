## About

This is a very interesting casual parkour game. The game is very simple and easy to operate. You will control the character to go through the pass. During the pass, you will collect diamonds and kebabs on the way. You can pass the pass by feeding the collected kebabs to the dogs at the end. There are many interesting levels in the game. Come and experience them
