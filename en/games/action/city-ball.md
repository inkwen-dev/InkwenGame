City Ball is a rolling ball game set in the streets of a city. To be successful, you must overcome the obstacles that come your way.

## How to play

Avoid obstacles by moving left and right jumping over obstacles and smaller ball under them As the rolling time increases the rolling speed will also increase You have the right to hit 3 obstacles in total
