## About

Steal An Egg takes the classic Steal Brainrot loop and flips it into an egg-hatching economy race. Play in 1 Player or 2 Player mode, buy mystery eggs from the center conveyor, carry them back to your base, and wait for them to hatch into random Brainrot characters! The better your lineup, the faster you generate coins, and the sooner you can afford the rarest eggs.
