## About

All you have to do is get as far as you can, but watch out for the crates and explosive TNTs! Penguins cant fly, so use your jumps wisely! Game gets faster and harder the further you go! - HOW TO PLAY - Jump from one platform to another, avoiding obstacles. Collect gems to increase your score. Collect 5 diamond gems to restore health by one heart. Use gems to buy power ups. The 3 in total to choose from: - Running Shoes: Your score increases at a quicker rate. - Shield: Take one free hit without losing health - Bandana: You have the ability to triple jump!
