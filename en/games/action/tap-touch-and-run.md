## About

Tap Touch and Run is a tapping puzzle game where you need to control different animals on the stage. At this game you can play in 18 levels. Pass them all to win this game. The stage is interrupted and you have to jump in those places. There are spikes along the stage and you should skip them too. To make the game more fun, on some levels they rotate or move left and right, making the game even harder to play. You can play with six characters individually or select all of them so that they will rotate through the tracks.
