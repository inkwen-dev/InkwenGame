## About

**Rise Zombies** is an action-packed zombie survival game set in a dangerous post-apocalyptic city. Fight endless waves of zombies, collect gold from defeated enemies, and upgrade your combat skills, speed, and defense. As the undead grow stronger and more numerous, use smart tactics and quick reflexes to survive. Explore the ruined city, defeat every zombie, become stronger, and fight for survival!
