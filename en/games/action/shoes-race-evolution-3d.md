## About

A fantastic running game that not only captivates the player but also brings them joy when they are engaged in the game. Your goal should be to amass the most comprehensive collection of footwear that can be discovered in the city. Observe how your shoes' shape transforms into something utterly unique before your eyes. It is recommended that any necessary adjustments be made to them. Caution is strongly urged because you will be required to conquer the obstacles.
