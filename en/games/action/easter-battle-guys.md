## About

Hello, are you up for the Easter egg hunt competition? Choose one of the Jelly Bon characters and start collecting the eggs. You should compete with your friend. Be careful. Collect all the eggs without falling off the platform. Be cautious to outsmart your friend and collect all the eggs. Watch out for sharp rotating knives coming from the sides and above, they can kill you and your friend. If you make a mistake, you lose, and your friend wins. Be fast and collect all the eggs. The one who collects the most eggs wins.
