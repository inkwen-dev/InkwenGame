Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Inoi is a 2D platformer where you need to collect water containers while avoiding the cactus enemies in the desert area. Collect all of the water containers and reach the red flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty is moderate.
