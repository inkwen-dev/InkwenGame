## How to play

Use WASD or Arrow keys for player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Inoi 2 is a 2D simple platformer where you need to avoid green, yellow and red cactus of different sizes and reach the red flag. You have to collect all of the water containers before reaching the red flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the sequel of the original Inoi game.
