## How to play

Desktop -C Up Arrow or Left Click to Jump -X Space or Right Click to Turn On Off Elements Mobile -Touch Right to Jump -Touch Left to Turn On Off Elements

## About

You control a small blob character with the power to turn on or off elements on the level. Use that power wisely to reach the goal to the next stage! You can also test your skills in the speedrun mode. Mini Blocks was made in a retro-style fashion, inspired in games from the 8-bit era.
