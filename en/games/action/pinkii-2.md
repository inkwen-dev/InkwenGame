## About

Pinkii 2 is a simple 2D Platformer where you need to collect yellow flowers while avoiding enemies including flying monsters and reach the pink flag in order to go to the next level. This is the sequel of the original Pinkii series with more new enemies. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
