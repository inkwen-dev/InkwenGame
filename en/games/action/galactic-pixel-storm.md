## About

Embark on an exciting cosmic adventure in Galactic Pixel Storm! Immerse yourself in a world of captivating pixel art graphics and mesmerizing sound effects. Pilot your spacecraft through a realm of pixel art targets, smashing them with rockets in a symphony of explosive brilliance. Your mission is to meticulously obliterate each pixel art piece, shattering them into a mesmerizing dance of one or two pixels. As a player, youll enjoy the thrill of upgrading your spacecraft, selecting from three unique ship types, and harnessing a variety of game-changing power-ups.
