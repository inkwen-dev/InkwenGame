Tap to jump and avoid falling into gaps or bumping into obstacles. The levels are designed to be progressively more difficult, requiring the player to improve their timing and coordination. With its minimalist design and addictive gameplay, Stickman Jump offers an immersive experience for fans of skill jumpers. Do you think you can master every level? Don't give up and good luck with Stickman Jump.

## About

Stickman Jump is a fast-paced platformer in which you have to jump and run across platforms with a little stickman. You control a small character who sprints at full speed and needs precise timing to jump over obstacles and complete each level. The further you get, the greater the challenges that will test your reflexes and concentration.
