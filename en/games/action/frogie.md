## How to play

Use LEFT-CLICK or SPACE key to jump

## About

An old school arcade game comes for retro game lovers! Try to reach to highest score by jumping from one platform to another and take your place at the top of the leaderboard. If you hold your finger on the screen, you can stay on a platform if you catch it but don't hold too much because if you hold too much, your character goes back and if it disappears, you fail. Tap the screen at right time and jump by bouncing one to another. You can share your score with your friends by the share option. Have fun!
