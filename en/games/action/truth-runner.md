## About

As a young girl, which way do you like to pass in real life. Truth Runner is a running arcade game on a 3D platform that is full of different career items. It is managed for you to choose a confirmed role and keep your outfit at each level. You will some famous uniforms and play the role of Harley Quinn, Wonder Woman, and Lolita. Glad you will always choose your favorite routes to the finish area!
