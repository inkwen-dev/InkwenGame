## How to play

player remains on one location 
use q,w,e,r to trigger skills thats unlocked
use a,d  to move the CAMERA around 
and use your mouse to focus the location player auto default attacks 
use 1 , 2  for potions 

esc opens the menu to save or change settings

and navigate and interact with npc's using the mouse left click 

access inventory with key I or clicking on the button

## About

Looter, endless wave defender.

Defend the outpost against hordes of waves , gather gear, merge gear, enhance gear and choose your starting class
Player skill upgrades works like a roguelike , every new game , you start at level 1 , build up your character  skills.
Participate in the monthly active leaderboard to see how far you can get.

Top 3 monthly leaderboard holders receives permanent exclusive cosmetics via the inbox
