## About

Enter a dangerous Halloween world and survive against endless waves of enemies. Your swords orbit around your character and automatically attack nearby enemies. Collect more swords to increase your power and make your attacks stronger. During the fight, collect useful items such as shields and other upgrades to improve your chances of survival. As you progress, stronger and more dangerous enemies will appear. Fight your way through every wave, defeat powerful bosses, collect upgrades, and see how long you can survive. Can you become the ultimate Halloween Survivor?
