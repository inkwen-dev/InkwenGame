## About

Utoo 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect blue cubes while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures, saws and spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original Utoo series.
