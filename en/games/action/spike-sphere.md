## About

SpikeSphere is a fast-paced action game where you control a spiked ball and swing through dangerous obstacles using a sticky rope. Aim carefully, attach to walls, and master your timing to cross deadly gaps while a massive fire chases you from behind. Test your reflexes, precision, and swinging skills across challenging levels. Keep moving, escape the flames, and see how far you can survive!
