## How to play

🕹️To control the hook, move your finger across the phone screen or the mouse with LMB held down on your PC.
🎯Your goal is to destroy abandoned buildings. To do this, you need to wrap the buildings as best as possible using a hook and rope, and then press on the gas as hard as you can to demolish the structure.
💡You can also improve your winch and buy new cars. Calculate your strength correctly to demolish the entire building and earn more points. Happy destruction!
