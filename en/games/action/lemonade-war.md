## How to play

Press WASD keys to move the red player Press the Arrow keys to move the blue player Collect lemons from the tree and take them to the lemon stand to score points

## About

Would you like to pick lemons and win the race? Collect lemons to defeat your friend. You must help the blue and red players win the lemon race. Grab the lemons from the tree in the middle of the map and take them to the lemon stand. The person who collects the most lemons wins the race. Gather lemons and win the game. The water is slowly rising, and your only chance is to collect lemons as quickly as possible.
