## About

Mani Mouse 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you play as a mouse who have to collect all of the slices of cheese while avoiding the enemy cats, flying cat robots, mouse traps, pins, and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Mani Mouse" series.
