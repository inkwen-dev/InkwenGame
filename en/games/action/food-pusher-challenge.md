## About

Collect and stack as many food as possible and close to your partner to win the game. The wrestling match is about to begin. This is a four-person, two-group match. Collect food to help your partner increase strength and defeat opponents. Avoid green foods and choose high-calorie foods to make your partner stronger and stronger.
