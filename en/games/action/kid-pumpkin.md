## About

Interesting platform game for kids, where you need to collect coins and to pass all 12 levels. But there is some obstacles on the way. You will find these enemies. The most common enemy is the red one. It can be killed by jumping on their head or tackling. The yellow ones can also be killed jumping on their head or tackling. But be careful because these enemies will also jump every few seconds! The black ones are the most dangerous and in fact, invincible enemies and can’t be killed at all. So you just have to jump over them and avoid hitting them in any way. Use the up arrow key of keyboard or up arrow on the right side of the screen to jump and use the down arrow key of keyboard or down arrow on the left side of the screen to slide.
