## About

Raktoo 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you have to collect all of the black grapes while avoiding the enemy monsters, flying monsters, their bullets, spikes, and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Raktoo" series.
