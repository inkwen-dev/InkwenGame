## About

Bomb Evolution is an exhilarating cannon shooting arcade game that boasts vibrant 3D cartoon game art animation. On the mysterious and mist-shrouded island, you have the opportunity to strategically establish not one, but three main bases, bolstered by an additional rocket. Engage in an enthralling battle against a formidable opponent, both of you operating under identical circumstances. Your ultimate objective: obliterating all the enemys main bases positioned on the neighboring fog-laden island. May victory in this war be your prized accomplishment!
