## About

Boxed Platformer is a kid's platform game. Your job as a player is to control the little player to collect all stars. Jump from platform to platform and collect stars. The obstacles will be shown suddenly on the game screen, you need to avoid them because they can hurt you. You have three lives to play. Pass the levels and have fun.
