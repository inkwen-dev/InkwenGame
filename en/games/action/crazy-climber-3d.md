## About

Crazy Climber 3D is a 3D hyper casual game where you try to reach the finish line by avoiding obstacles. The gameplay of the game is very simple. Avoid obstacles by going left and right and collect pieces of steps on the floor. You need to build pieces of steps that you collect to overcome big obstacles. You should also try to collect yellow diamonds as you move in order to buy new characters and continue your progress. This game, which contains an infinite number of levels, can also be played on mobile devices.
