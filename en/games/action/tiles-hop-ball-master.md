## About

Tiles Hop Ball Master is a ball jumping skillful arcade game. With 3D platforms and a white ball, you are supposed to control the real-time position of the ball to arrive at the top of the platforms at each level. While the ball is jumping on the platforms automatically, you could also collect some gold coins for more scores. Good luck and have a good time!
