## About

Nekoman vs Gangster 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a cat who have to collect all of the silver keys while avoiding the Gangsters, and their bullets, saws and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Nekoman vs Gangster" series.
