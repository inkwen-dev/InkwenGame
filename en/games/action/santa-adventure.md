## About

Santa Claus is on new adventure. He need to collect gifts for next Christmas. He need your help to do that. Control the Santa through the stages. He is Santa but he have only three lives at this game. So you need to avoid all obstacles on Santa's way. Jump over them or go under them. When you lose live you will be returned to the beginning of the level. Use up or down arrows to control the Santa, or just tap on the icons on the screen. Pass all levels and have fun.
