## About

"Shadoworld Adventure" is a very fun platform / retro game! The character, a brave creature of the shadows, must cross the dark valley and defeat the enemies. Jump on enemies to eliminate them and watch out for the angry obstacles that fall on your head. Dodge the spikes to avoid losing the game! Find the key to open the portal at the end of each level. The game has 50 levels! Have a good time!
