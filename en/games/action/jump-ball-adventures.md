## How to play

Tap on screen to jump or use arrow keys to play this game

## About

In this game your task is to control the ball through different platforms. Jump on them and collect the stars. Avoid spikes. Skip them or you will have to start the level over. This game has 40 levels. Each level is a new adventure. Go through all the levels and have great fun with this game. You can change the ball in different designs. Choose your favorite ball and start the game.
