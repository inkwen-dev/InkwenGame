## How to play

The goal of the game is to defeat all the enemies on the level.

The gameplay is very simple. All you need is to swipe your finger on the phone screen, or the mouse cursor on the computer screen to cut the enemy into halves.

Enemies can hit you or throw weapons at you, be careful. Slow down time and chop flying bullets. Cut barrels of explosives in half and beat off giant shurikens from your enemies! Do you have enough agility and reaction to repel attacks and cut all enemies into pieces?
