## About

Have you ever dreamed of your favorite career? Career Rush plays like both a collecting arcade game and an avoiding vertical-version running game that is made from 3D cartoon game art animations. You need to help the girl to collect green money bags and choose a career while avoiding the red money bags at each level. Keep calm and have a good time!
