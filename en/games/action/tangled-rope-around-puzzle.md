## About

If you have a rope with unlimited length, how will you use it to pass all pillars and set it at the pointed position without touching other colorful ropes and pillars? Tangled Rope Around Puzzle requires you to solve rope puzzles on a 3D platform. With arcade game rules, you could control one side of the rope to pass around the pillars at all fifty levels. Keep calm and complete them all!
