## About

Christmas Memichan 2 is a 2D Christmas themed platformer where you play as a Santa Cat called Memichan who have to collect all of the chocolate bars while avoiding the enemy demon cats, flying cat robots and their bullets, spikes, saws and reach your cat house door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Christmas Memichan" series.
