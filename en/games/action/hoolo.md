## About

Hoolo is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the potato chips packets while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures and their bullets, ground saws,spikes and reach the green flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
