Use WASD or Arrow keys for the players movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Jezaa 2 is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as an anime girl who have to collect purple cubes while avoiding the monsters, flying creatures, their bullets, ground saws, lava, and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play with increasing difficulty. This is the second installment of the original Jezaa series.
