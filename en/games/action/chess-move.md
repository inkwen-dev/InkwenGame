## How to play

Click on one of your blue pieces to see where it can move Then click one of the highlighted locations to move it there

## About

Capture the Red King on each level in this fun chess-like puzzle game. The pieces move like chess pieces but you don't need to know how to play chess to complete each of the 24 levels. If you get stuck just click on the Walkthrough button at the bottom of the screen on each level.
