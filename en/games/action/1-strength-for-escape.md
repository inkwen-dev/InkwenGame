## How to play

🎮 How to Play

💻 PC

🏃 WASD — move character
🖱️ Left mouse button — interact with objects and buttons
🖱️ Hold right mouse button — rotate camera
      Space — jump
💪 Train button — perform exercises and gain Strength
🧱 Approach walls — if you have enough strength, break them and move forward

📱 Mobile Devices

🕹️ Virtual joystick — move character
👆 Swipe screen — rotate camera
⬆️ Jump button — make character jump
💪 Train button — perform exercises and gain Strength
🧱 Approach walls — use accumulated strength to break obstacles

## About

💪 Become the strongest and break out of dangerous zones!

In +1 Strength to Escape, you must train, build up your strength, and smash through the obstacles standing between you and freedom. The more you train, the stronger you get—allowing you to break down tougher walls and unlock new zones.

🏋️ Train — perform exercises to gain Strength.
💪 Boost your strength — use heavier weights and improve your training efficiency.
🧱 Smash walls — use your accumulated strength to break through.
🌎 Unlock new zones — each area gets tougher but offers greater rewards.
🏆 Earn wins — use them to further your progress.
🔄 Rebirth — reset your current progress for permanent bonuses and grow stronger with every new cycle.

Can you become strong enough to shatter every wall and pull off the ultimate escape? Start training and prove that you are the strongest!
