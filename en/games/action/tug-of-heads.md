## About

Tug of Heads is a fun wrestling game that can be played for 1 and 2 Player. There are more than 50 hand-designed levels in the game that will surprise you. The aim of the players is to protect the heads of the characters from danger in wrestling. Game conditions will change at every level! You can play this fun wrestling against your friend or the CPU. Choose how many sets to play and start the wrestling!
