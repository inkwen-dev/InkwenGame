## How to play

Move around the island, survive the enemy waves, and defeat the required number of enemies to complete each level. Punch enemies directly or launch nearby objects into the crowd—throwing enemies into the ocean is also a perfectly respectable strategy! Complete levels to unlock new islands and boxing weapons.
Desktop Controls:
WASD — Move
Mouse — Look and aim
Left Mouse Button — Punch
E — Open or close weapon selection
R — Open or close settings  
Mobile Controls:
Left joystick — Move
Drag the right side — Look and aim
Glove button — Punch
On-screen menu buttons — Weapons and settings

## About

Summer Punch Party is a fast-paced first-person action game where you punch waves of enemies across sunny tropical islands. Unlock ridiculous gloves, send opponents flying with hilarious ragdoll physics, and prove that sometimes the best summer activity is punching trouble directly into the ocean!
