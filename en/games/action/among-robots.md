Use WASD or Arrow keys for player s movement Use W or Up Arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Among Robots is a 2D platformer where you have to collect key cards in order to unlock the door and to go to the next level. You have to avoid enemy robots, spikes and saws. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
