## How to play

🕹️Controls:

- 💻 On PC:
Move the mouse while holding the left button to move the character.
- 📱 On mobile devices:
Use the virtual joystick - just move your finger across the screen to move.

🎯Game Objective:
Push all the ragdoll opponents off the arena and be the last fighter standing in this fun battle royale!

🔥 Tip:
Stay away from the edges and always keep an eye on your opponent to avoid being thrown into the abyss.
