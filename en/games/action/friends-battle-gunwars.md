## About

In the new game of the Friends Battle series, weapons are drawn and magazines are loaded. Fire your weapon and hit your friend. The fastest and most skilled shooter wins. Shoot the flying drops in the game and change your weapon. Remember, the red team starts with a shotgun and the blue team with an assault rifle, but you can change these weapons. The one who takes 20 bullets loses the game. To win, you must dodge bullets, collect drops, and defeat your enemy.
