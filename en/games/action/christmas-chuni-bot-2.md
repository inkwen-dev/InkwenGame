## About

Christmas Chuni Bot 2 is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have collect all of the battery cells while avoiding the enemy bots and their bullets, flying bots and their bullet, spikes, ground saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Christmas Chuni Bot" series.
