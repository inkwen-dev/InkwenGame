## About

Blacko Ball 2 is a 2D simple platformer where you have to collect the small red balls while avoiding Red and Orange enemies, spikes and ground saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Blacko Ball" series.
