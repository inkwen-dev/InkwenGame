## How to play

😊 Let's get to know the game:

🎮 Game Modes:
Single-player mode - practice your controls by completing obstacle courses.
Multiplayer mode - compete or team up with other players to progress through the game together.

✏️ Player Tools:
Pencil - draw new elements on the level.
Eraser - erase previously drawn elements.

🎮 Controls on a Computer:
Move your character - press W/A/S/D.
Jump - press Space.
Draw or erase lines - move the mouse with the left button held down.

📱 Controls on a Smartphone:
Move your character - use the virtual joystick.
Jump - press the virtual button.
Draw or erase lines - swipe your finger across the screen.

Tap "Play" and win the game! 🎉
