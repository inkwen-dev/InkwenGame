## About

Ichikas Adventure 2 is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as Anime girl called Ichika who have to collect all of the necklaces while avoiding the enemies and their bullets, flying creatures, spikes, and saws and reach the door of your house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Ichikas Adventure" series.
