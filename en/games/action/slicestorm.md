## About

SliceStorm &ndash; Fruit Slicing Action Game Slice, dice, and survive in this fast-paced fruit-slicing arcade game! Swipe across your screen to slice juicy fruits while avoiding deadly bombs. Each slice earns points and builds your combo multiplier for massive scores. Features &middot; 7 unique fruits &ndash; Watermelon, orange, lemon, apple, pineapple, strawberry, kiwi, and banana (each with special juice effects) &middot; Combo system &ndash; Chain slices to multiply your points &middot; 3 lives &ndash; One mistake could end your run &middot; Increasing difficulty &ndash; Fruits spawn faster as your score rises &middot; Rich 3D-style graphics &ndash; Each fruit features detailed cuts and halves &middot; Immersive audio &ndash; Satisfying slice sounds and juicy splats How to Play Swipe your finger (or mouse) across fruits to slice them. Miss too many or hit a bomb and you lose a life. Survive as long as possible and climb the leaderboard! Perfect for quick gaming sessions &ndash; easy to learn, hard to master. Can you beat your best score?
