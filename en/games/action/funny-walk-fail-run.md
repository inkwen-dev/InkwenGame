## About

Funny Walk Fail Run is a fun-filled arcade action game with funny and magical gameplay! You will control a villain in the game to do challenges. But dont expect him to walk steadily, because he always falls and bumps in all sorts of funny positions! Your job is to help him navigate each step of the shaky road.
