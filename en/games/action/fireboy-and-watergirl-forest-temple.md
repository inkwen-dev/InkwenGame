## How to play

- AWD to move Watergirl - Arrow keys to move Fireboy - R to restart level

## About

Fireboy and Watergirl is a cool adventure game, here at the Forest Temple, collect all the diamonds and get each character to the exit door! Since fire and water don't mix, don't let Fireboy go in the water lakes and don't let Watergirl go in the fire lakes... And keep them both out of the green lakes.
