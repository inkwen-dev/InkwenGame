## About

Command your own pirate ship, set sail, and embark on a bold voyage! Have you ever dreamed of becoming a pirate and conquering the ocean? Now you have a chance to achieve it! Starting from a small boat equipped with cannons, fight against your enemies and seize their treasures. Upgrade your spaceship with the money earned along the way. You can also customize your flag and recruit bandits to join your team. Who is ready to become the ultimate ruler of the ocean?
