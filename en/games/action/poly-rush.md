## About

PolyRush is a fast-paced flight game set in a stunning low-poly universe. Pilot your aircraft through challenging obstacles, master the art of the charged jump, and unleash powerful dashes to break through barriers. Collect coins to unlock unique planes and custom trails in the Hangar. Featuring customizable pilots, daily missions, and a global leaderboard. How far can you go?
