## About

Are you ready to battle with your friend? Arrows are coming from all directions, so be careful. If you want to defeat your friend, you must avoid the arrows. If you can go on without getting hit by arrows for 10 seconds, you win the game. Every time an arrow hits you, the duration will increase by 1 second, so you must be very careful. You need to defeat your friend. Beat your friend and be the winner. You have 10 seconds to win. Be very careful, arrows are everywhere.
