## About

This is game where you need to tap on the screen to bounce the ball on the moving levels of boxes that can eat the ball. So you need to be careful and jump at the right moment to avoid the boxes that can destroy the ball. The ball and boxes move left and right across the platform, making the game more difficult to play.
