## About

Welcome to the Beer Clicker Game! In this addictive and fun-filled game, youll embark on a journey through the world of brewing, one click at a time. As you start, youll be greeted with a simple screen displaying a tempting glass of frothy beer. Your mission? Click, click, and click some more! Each click earns you points, and with those points, you can unlock exciting upgrades to enhance your brewing empire.
