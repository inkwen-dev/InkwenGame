## About

Amoung run is an interactive game of choosing time in which you have to get as far as possible with the hero in the game, that is, to cover as much distance as possible. You have to avoid the traps on the road and not fall off the platform. The game requires skill and speed of movement. You have to pay attention to all the obstacles and opponents that appear on the route. If you lose your life, you have to restart the game from the beginning. After you accumulate points you can exchange Among Run from the store.
