## About

Otaho Bird 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a bird who have to collect all of the slices of bread while avoiding the enemy birds, flying birds and their bullets, rotating blades, and fire and reach the nest in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Otaho Bird" series.
