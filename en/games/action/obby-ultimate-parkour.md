## How to play

Goal: climb through all 50 stages and reach the finish on top of the Sky Tower. Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to run, Space to jump, hold a mouse button and drag to turn the camera, mouse wheel to zoom, P or Esc to pause. Mobile: the left side of the screen is the joystick, swipe on the right side to turn the camera, tap the button to jump. Lava, water, hammers and lasers send you back to your last checkpoint flag. On the Color Floor, stand on the color shown at the top of the screen before the other tiles fall.

## About

Run a giant obby course above the ocean! Five worlds (Rainbow Meadow, Lava Volcano, Candy Land, Ice Peaks and the Sky Tower) and 50 stages of jumps, lava, spinners, trampolines, conveyors, vanishing blocks and moving platforms. Every stage ends with a checkpoint flag, so a fall sends you back only to the last one. The big challenge is the Color Floor: a color lights up over the field, and a few seconds later every tile of another color drops into the water. Swinging hammers, blinking lasers, falling platforms, boost pads and wind fans keep every zone different, and secret coin chests hide off the path. Bot runners race next to you: they fall, cheer at checkpoints and chat. Collect coins and unlock mythic brainrot skins, the 67 skin, hats, wings, pets and trails. Claim a daily gift and spin the fortune wheel every day.
