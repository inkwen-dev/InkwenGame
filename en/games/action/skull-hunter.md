## About

A flappy horror game themed about of a flying skull ghost in a cave. Destroy all enemy skulls. Hit the enemy's skull or make them collide with each other. Use enemies to help destroy stone obstacles. Don't hit the cave wall or fall. Hit 10 enemies and get "fire power" to destroy anything. Complete each difficulty level.
