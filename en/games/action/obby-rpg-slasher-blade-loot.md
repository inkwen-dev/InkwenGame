## How to play

PC:
• W / A / S / D — movement
• Mouse — camera control
• LMB — attack
• 1 — use ability
• E — interact

Mobile devices:
• Joystick — movement
• Screen swipe — camera control
• Attack button — attack
• Ability button — use ability
• Interact button — interact with objects

## About

A dynamic action RPG where dangerous levels, enemies, and constant character progression await you. Fight enemies in close combat, use powerful abilities, and clear challenging obby stages filled with trials.

Collect a variety of weapons and armor, compare stats, upgrade your gear, and strengthen your hero. Each item affects attack, defense, and health, while upgrades make your character even stronger. Choose the best equipment and shape your own playstyle.

Progress through levels, unlock new areas, grow more powerful, and defeat increasingly dangerous enemies. The game combines easy-to-learn mechanics with deep RPG progression that fans of loot, upgrades, and action will enjoy.
