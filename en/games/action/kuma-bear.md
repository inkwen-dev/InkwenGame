## About

Kuma Bear is a 2D little platformer where you play as a bear who have to collect all of the chocolate bars while while avoiding enemy dogs, flying birds, spikes and pins and reach your own house in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
