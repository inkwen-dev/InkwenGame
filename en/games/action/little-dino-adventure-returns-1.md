## About

HTML5 Platform adventure game, where the little Dinosaur must cross the valley of fear, where he encounters many challenges. Collect all the golden eggs to reach three stars in each stage. Play through the arrow keys or even the touch buttons on your phone’s screen. Little Dino Adventure Returns is a super fun and challenging 2D side-scroller game in the same style as blockbuster games like Super Mario, Donkey Kong and Sonic. The game features 06 levels that will challenge you to beat it. The designs are very colorful and beautiful!
