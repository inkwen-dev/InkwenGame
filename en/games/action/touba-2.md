## About

Touba 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect the bowls of grains while avoiding the enemy birds, spikes and reach the nest in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Touba" series.
