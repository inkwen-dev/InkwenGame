## How to play

Embark on a Legendary Adventure!
Step into the world of Knight Legend, where courage and skill define your fate. Battle vicious monsters, upgrade your gear, and unlock new levels as you journey through an epic quest. Earn gold to buy powerful armour and loyal pets that will fight by your side!

Desktop Controls:
↑↓←→ : Move your knight.
W A S D / Z Q S D : Alternative movement controls.
ENTER: Accept / submit.
ESC: Pause or close menus.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
Accept/Submit Button: Tap to confirm actions.

## About

Knight Legend invites you to dive into an epic quest where you fight monsters, upgrade your gear, and unlock new levels. As you progress, youll earn money to buy powerful armors and loyal pets to aid you in your journey.
