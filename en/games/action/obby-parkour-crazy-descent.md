## How to play

💻 PC Controls:

WASD or Arrow Keys — move character.

Hold Right Mouse Button (RMB) and move mouse — rotate camera and look around.

📱 Phone & Tablet Controls:

Virtual Joystick — drag the joystick in the desired direction to move character.

Swipe across screen — rotate camera and look around.

## About

👋 Welcome to "Obby Parkour: Crazy Descent" — a fast-paced 3D parkour testing your speed and reactions!

Your main goal is to descend through generated platforms as low as possible while dodging moving obstacles. One wrong move and you fall straight into the abyss! The lower you fall, the faster you go: with every platform passed, your speed constantly increases, turning the parkour into a wild rush.

✨ Game Features:
🗺️ New levels: cover the distance and unlock new exciting stages!
🚀 Dynamic acceleration: reach crazy speeds and test your reflexes.
🤑 Coin collection: pick up gold coins right on the platforms as you drop.
🛍️ Cool shop: buy pets, viral memes (Six-Seven, Ballerina Capuchina, Tralalero, and more), and stylish accessories — from glasses and capes to the legendary Dominus!

🏆 Ready to become the ultimate fall champion? Jump in and show what you've got!
