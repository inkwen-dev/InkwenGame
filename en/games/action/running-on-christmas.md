## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

On your journey through the winter forest you need to avoid or destroy all the evil creatures and placed traps. Some of the traps are fixed, some move, as you will attack the trolls and goblins. If you do not have time to jump over it, you will die. Remember that the number of lives is limited so be careful and help the panda in time to get to Santa Claus House.
