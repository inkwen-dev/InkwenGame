Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Aisa Bot 2 is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you play as a bot who have to collect all of the cubes while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots, their bullets, spikes and ground saws and reach the loop hole in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Aisa Bot" series.
