## About

Shell Strikers is a turn-based artillery combat game where strategy meets explosive action. Lead your team of soldiers in intense battles across diverse battlefields. Each turn, position your soldier, aim carefully, and choose the right weapon from your arsenal. Account for wind conditions that affect every shot! Modes: - Campaign: 9 missions with increasing difficulty (First Steps &rarr; FINALE: Armageddon) - Quick Play: instant random battle Weapons: Rocket (unlimited), Grenade, Water Gun, Airstrike, Dynamite - each with unique tactical uses.
