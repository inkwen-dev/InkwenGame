## About

Hordes of stickman, wave by wave trying to wear you down, waiting for your first mistake. You as the chosen stick guy should mindlessly beat them up with your kung fu. Nobody knows why you are the chosen, nor anyone knows why these idiots run towards you, just to get punch in their sensitive parts. Tap on the white circle to kill enemies, earn souls and buy new weapons.
