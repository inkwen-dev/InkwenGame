## About

Curve City Stickman is a 3D stickman running game and your mission is to reach the finish line without getting hit by obstacles. You move the stickman character to the right and left to move forward. This stickman game has an amazing environment design and also has very dangerous obstacles. A dangerous run awaits you with blades, hammers and guillotines.
