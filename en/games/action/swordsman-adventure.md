## About

Swordsman Adventure is a thrilling adventure game where you wield your sword freely on the battlefield to complete a series of missions. In this role-playing game, you control your soldier to attack enemies of different hit points. You get rewards at the end of each mission completed, which you can use to upgrade your attack points or to equip with a new weapon.
