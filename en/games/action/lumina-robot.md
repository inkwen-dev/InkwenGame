## About

Embark on an adventurous journey with Lumina Robot, a captivating platformer puzzle game. Navigate your robot through a series of dark and enigmatic levels, using the light on its head to illuminate the way. Collect card keys to unlock doors and progress to new and increasingly complex challenges. Each stage tests your logic and reflexes as you strive to guide your robot to victory. But be cautious, as hitting the torches will cause you to restart the level. Can you reach the trophy in Lumina Robot?
