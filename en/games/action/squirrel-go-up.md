## About

Either use your mouse or press enter to start the game. Move your cute squirrel using your keyboard and go up avoiding obstacles and jumping on the platforms. Do not let the rocks hit you, collect hazelnuts as you go up for making more points. Have fun in this easy-to-learn addicting game Squirrel Go Up.
