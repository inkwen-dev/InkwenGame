Use W A S D or Arrow Keys To Move Use Space to Jump Use Ctrl To Fire Use Alt to use JetPack

## About

Savage Jon is an action-adventure platform game, where Jon looks for adventure and flight monsters, and uses Jet Pack to survive the deadly forest and return home. The mission is to guide Jon through ten levels, collecting trophies in the hideout of his enemy. Are you ready for the action-packed adventure?
