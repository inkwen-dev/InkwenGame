## How to play

## About

Master the art of archery in this fast-paced stickman battle! Take down waves of calculated enemies using legendary bows and mystical items from your arsenal. Battle through challenging levels, earn gold, and visit the Mystic Arsenal to buy powerful upgrades like the Flame Bow, Diamond Arrow, and Force Shields. featuring precise physics-based aiming and epic magical effects, can you become the ultimate archer?
