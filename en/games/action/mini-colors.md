Desktop -X Space or Left Click to switch gravity Mobile -Touch to Switch Gravity

## About

Mini Colors is a puzzle game where you have to turn on/off color platforms in order to reach your loved one; is inspired in some retro games and holds a minimalistic style. The game has 36 levels with different puzzles and a SpeedRun mode.
