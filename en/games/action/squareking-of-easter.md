## About

The square king needs to escape from the castle. Help the square king and leave the kingdom. During the Easter holiday, monsters attacked the castle. They surrounded it from all sides. To escape the castle, you must collect all the Easter eggs. Be careful &mdash; the castle is filled with dangerous traps and sharp blades. And of course, flying and ground-running monsters are waiting for you around the castle. Reach the chest as quickly as possible and escape the castle!
