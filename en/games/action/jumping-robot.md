## About

Jumping Robot is an exciting online game where you are in control of a nimble and agile robot that loves to jump! Your mission is to navigate through a series of platforms and collect batteries to increase your score. The gameplay is straightforward yet challenging - you must time your jumps perfectly to land on each platform and avoid falling into the depths below. To make things even more interesting, some platforms will be constantly moving left and right, adding an element of unpredictability to the game. This requires quick reflexes and precise control to stay on track. The ultimate goal is to collect as many batteries as possible.
