## About

Maxoo 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect silver keys while avoiding the enemy balls, flying creatures, pins, and lasers and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Maxoo" game with new enemies and traps.
