## About

PixelParkour is a pixel-style action game where you take on the role of a professional thief in a black outfit with exceptional parkour skills.Your mission: overcome obstacles, avoid dangerous traps, collect gold, and find your way to the finish line safely. Each level is full of surprises, requiring you to be quick and precise with every jump!
