## About

Murder is a fun assassination game. Creep up behind the king and take him out quickly and quietly. Be careful – if he catches you, it’s off to the dungeon with you! Play Murder and experience what life is like for the typical scheming vizier. If you succeed, you’ll be the new king – but be careful, your adviser is after your crown! Experience Murder online right from your browser and test your observation and reaction skills. You can play Murder for free, but a wrong move will cost you your life.
