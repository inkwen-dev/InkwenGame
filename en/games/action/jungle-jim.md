## About

Have fun with this entertaining new 2D platformer game. Jungle Jim will need all your jump and run skills to reach the treasure chest at the end of each level. Along the way, break blocks, pick up fruits and collect coins to increase your score while avoiding enemies, spikes, and exploding blocks. Note that big mushrooms can serve as trampolines. There are 7 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
