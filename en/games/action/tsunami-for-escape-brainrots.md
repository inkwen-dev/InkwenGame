## About

A tsunami is coming, so be very careful! The entire world will be flooded. You and your friend must work together to rise high into the sky. Be careful not to fall down! Escape the tsunami alongside the Italian Brainrot characters and enjoy the endless journey upward through the sky. As you climb higher, watch out&mdash;many obstacles are waiting for you, and you must avoid them. Use the jump ramp to boost yourself upward and collect all the coins. Dont leave any coins behind!
