## About

Angle Fight 3D is a very fun action fighting game. In the game, the player will control a matchmaker to fight and break through. The player needs to select weapons for the matchmaker, adjust the action of your matchmaker when he fights according to the enemy's offensive action, and successfully destroy the enemy by relying on the advantages of weapons and actions. Try the new matchmaker adventure challenge.
