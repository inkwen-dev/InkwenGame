## How to play

The red player moves using the WASD keys The blue player moves using the arrow keys Press the Bottom Arrow key and the S key to fire and buy cannons

## About

Are you ready for the noobs battle? You need to defend your bed. Quickly collect coins and buy cannonballs. Fire the cannonballs from the cannon to break the bed. The first one to break the bed wins. Fire the cannonball to explode the bed. Fight with your friend and win this battle.
