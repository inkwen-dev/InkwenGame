## About

Magic Tunnel Rush is an endless tunnel game consist of amazing gameplay and soundtrack. Players must avoid the gaps to keep the ball in the tunnel. The balls will speed up and control will be more difficult. The color of the tunnel changes at every stage. Control the ball by tapping on left or right to move the ball to the according position. If you miss a platform, you fail. Game has 1 Player and 2 Player modes!
