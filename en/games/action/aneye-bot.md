## About

Aneye Bot is a 2D Sci-Fi themed platformer where you play as a Robot with an eye and your mission is to collect all of the ice creams while avoiding three-eyed enemy robots, spikes, and saws and reach your own house. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
