## About

Gear up for an intense firefighting experience in Firefight Upgrader! Take on the role of a courageous firefighter, battling towering flames and saving lives. Customize your firefighter with an array of powerful upgrades, from advanced hoses to high-tech gear. Strategize your approach, prioritize missions, and rescue civilians in perilous situations. With realistic fire physics and dynamic environments, every blaze is a new challenge. Can you rise through the ranks, upgrading your skills and gear to become the ultimate hero?
