## About

Control the pixel boy rush to go through a challenging obstacle course. Pixel Boy loses Pixels when he hits the obstacle! Sprint to ensure his survival and reach the finish line in a piece to get a perfect run! You can collect pixels and stars through various obstacles to recovering lost body parts of the Pixel Boy. But be careful! If you hit too many obstacles, you'll lose all your pixels and have to start over!
