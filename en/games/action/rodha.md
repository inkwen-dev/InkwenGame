## About

Get ready for a thrilling adventure in Rodha, the action-platformer that delivers fast-paced, heart-pumping gameplay. In this exciting ball game, youll take on the role of one of 9 unique characters, each with their own distinct personality, as you make your way through 60 challenging levels filled with exciting platforming action. As you explore the minimalist world of Rodha, youll encounter various obstacles and challenges, from cannons and moving saws to other contraptions designed to stop you in your tracks.
