## About

Aventuras da Capivara Medieval is an exciting endless runner, where you become the powerful and courageous capybara that explores the dark lands of the Dark Ages. In this hostile and dangerous world, you will need to use your cunning and special skills to get past enemies, overcome challenging obstacles and fight for your survival.
