## About

Welcome to the great kingdom craft! prepare yourself brave mickey, get ready to run, jump, dash and rush through the kingdom. In this craft game, you will be a runner who has to run and collect coins in an endless runner game mine. You need to be very careful as there will be many mouse in your way. You will need to use your craft Mickey and reflexes to avoid them and keep running. There will also be power-ups that you can collect to help you in the craft game.
