## About

Twilight Trek is a mesmerizing star-collection adventure where you control a bouncing ball on a magical journey through the night sky. Using a floating platform, you must time your jumps carefully to soar higher and collect as many shimmering stars as possible. With each successful jump, the challenge intensifies&mdash;gravity shifts, platforms move, and dazzling cosmic obstacles stand in your way. Precision and skill are key as you navigate the endless twilight, chasing the stars that light up your path. How many stars can you gather before the night fades? Embark on your Twilight Trek and find out!
