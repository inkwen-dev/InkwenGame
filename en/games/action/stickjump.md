- Use W up arrow to change direction - Hold S down arrow to dash

## About

StickJump is a high-speed reflex game where you control a stick figure running around an endless circle. But dont be complacent&mdash;obstacles appear continuously and completely unexpectedly, always trying to block your path! Your task is very simple: jump at the right moment, dodge accurately, and try to run for as long as possible. The longer you survive, the faster the speed increases and the challenges become harder, creating a thrilling experience in every moment.
