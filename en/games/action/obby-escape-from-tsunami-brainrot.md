A dynamic parkour game where you have to overcome obstacles and escape from the oncoming tsunami. Reaction, speed and accuracy decide everything!

## How to play

Control (computer):
● W, A, S, D - Movement
● SPACE BAR - Jump
● Mouse Movement - Camera rotation
● E key - take and put the Brainrot

Management (phone):
● Joystick on the left and buttons on the screen - Movement
● Pinch and move on the right side of the screen - Camera rotation
