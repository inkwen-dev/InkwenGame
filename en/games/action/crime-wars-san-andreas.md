## About

You will need to play as a hero who fights urban crime. There will be many different vehicles in the city to move around. You will be able to find and shoot different weapons, such as a pistol, a submachine gun, a shotgun, a sniper rifle and a bazooka. On the mini map of the city you can find first-aid kits and armor for your hero. You have to eliminate various criminal gangs. You can shoot them or hit them with a car, the choice is yours. The game has 6 levels. At the sixth level, you can fight the big boss.
