## How to play

1. Goal
Guide the characters along the track, collect food, and increase your team's strength. Avoid traps, keep your collected supplies, and reach the finish with the largest possible reserve. Defeat your rivals in the final clash and complete the level.

2. Controls
On desktop, move the characters with WASD or the arrow keys. You can also hold the left mouse button and move the pointer in the desired direction.
On a phone or tablet, swipe left and right to guide the characters toward food and away from obstacles.
