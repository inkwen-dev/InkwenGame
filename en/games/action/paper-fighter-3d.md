## About

In a retro environment created from paper layers; martial masters are ready to showcase their skills. Paper Fighter 3D game includes 1 or 2 player short fight matches. Each fight match consists of 3 rounds and the one who can take 2 rounds wins the match. Use your punches and kicks at the right time, the special strike will be ready when your power bar is full.
