## About

Roof Rush 3D is a high-octane urban runner where you navigate a pole-vaulting master across a dangerous city skyline. Collect pieces to grow your pole and use it to slide across parallel rails, but stay centered&mdash;lose your balance, and you fall! Dodge saws and obstacles that shrink your pole while progressing through vibrant, color-shifting levels. Unlock cool skins like the Ninja or Zombie, dominate the Endless mode, and reach the finish line for a cinematic victory celebration.
