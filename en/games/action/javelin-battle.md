## About

Javelin Battle is a spear-throwing physics puzzle game with 2D stickman models. We need you to eliminate all incoming enemies at each level in the Normal Mode. When you destroy enemy infantrys shields, they will run to you quickly. I strongly suggest you destroy the enemy spearmen on the platforms first. Dont forget to choose skills to increase your abilities and power. Keep calm and aim carefully.
