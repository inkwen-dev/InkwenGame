## About

Beat the robbers with one punch! You are a police officer. A police officer should always be ready for a fight! Keep your fist strong! You may encounter robbers in an unexpected place and time. Besides all the fighting techniques you know like boxing, kickboxing, karate or wrestling, the most important thing that will help you win is your fist! Look around, eliminate the robbers with the right move. Make sure you knock out every single one of the robbers! Robber Vs police Officer: Fighting offers offline funny and entertaining fighting game enjoyment with its 3D graphics, animations, effects and unique game simulation. Press Play button and show them how strong a police officer you are!
