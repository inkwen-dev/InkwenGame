## How to play

🖥️ PC Controls:
W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → — Move
Spacebar — Jump
Move the mouse — Control the camera

📱 Mobile Controls:
Use the joystick on the left — Move
Tap the button on the right — Jump
Swipe on the right side of the screen — Control the camera
