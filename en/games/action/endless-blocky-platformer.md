## About

In this one-of-a-kind action role-playing game, you'll go on adventures filled with magic while battling a never-ending horde of hostile foes and powerful monsters in the wonderful realm of Blocky World. There are many different situations, each with its own set of antagonists, such as fearsome witches, orcs, and face thieves. Determine the class you want to play as, acquire rides and pets, and plot out your journey. Daily objectives and challenges, playable characters that may be edited, riddles, hidden areas, and more are all included. The game is now in the testing phase, and it will get frequent updates in the future. Leave your thoughts and suggestions below, and together we can help make Blocky World even better.
