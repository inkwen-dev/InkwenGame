## About

Mine Keeper is a simulation game where you play as a dwarf lord and create one of those great kingdoms of yours. Build your own town, and take care of your nation. Collect fruits and sell them to provide food for your people. Provide better houses for a living and build new facilities or direct your money to create your military forces from an army of dwarves. Mine into deep mines to get valuable stones and gems. Dont forget to take your troops with you because there are monsters in the mine!
