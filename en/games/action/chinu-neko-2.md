## About

Chinu Neko 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you play as a neko(cat) who have to collect cat food bowls while avoiding enemy cats and their bullets, ground saws, spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Chinu Neko" series.
