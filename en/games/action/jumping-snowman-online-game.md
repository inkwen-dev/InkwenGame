## About

The Jumping Snowman game is a simple but fun game that will get you excited for the winter season. Players take on the role of one who wants to go into the frozen world and stop the jumpstart from going off-planet. In order to do so, they must complete various tasks included learning about freeze effects, working with our understanding of nebulae, and much more! The game is set in an ice age where cold temperatures keep all life in check.
