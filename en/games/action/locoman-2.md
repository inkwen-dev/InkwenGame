## About

Locoman 2 is a simple 2D platformer where you have to collect silver keys while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures, spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original Locoman series.
