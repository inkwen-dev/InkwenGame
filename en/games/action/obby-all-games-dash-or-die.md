## How to play

Choose an obby map or mini-game and try to reach the finish line. Jump across platforms, avoid traps, and do not fall into the gaps. In the lava challenge, move quickly before the rising lava catches you. During bike parkour, control your bike carefully and keep your balance on difficult obstacles.

Desktop controls:

W, A, S, D — move your character or control the bike.
Space — jump.
Mouse — move the camera and look around.

Mobile controls:

Use the virtual joystick to move.
Tap the jump button to overcome obstacles.
Swipe across the screen to rotate the camera.

## About
