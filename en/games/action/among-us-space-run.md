## About

Among Us Space Run is fun arcade game suitable for all ages. You, as a Among Us character need to stay on the screen as long time as possible and collect as many space sheep as possible to find the way back home and save all your crewmates. Stay away from the top or bottom of the stage. Have fun playing.
