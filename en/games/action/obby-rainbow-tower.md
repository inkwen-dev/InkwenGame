## How to play

Player 1: Move W A S D, Sprint Shift, Jump Space, Double Jump Space+Space 
Player 2: Move Arrows, Sprint K, Jump L, Double Jump L+L 
Mobile: On-screen buttons.

## About

Obby Rainbow Tower is a fast, winter-themed tower race where every second and every jump matters. Each floor is packed with moving platforms, icy tiles, spinning lava bars, surprise snowstorms and snowball portals that try to knock you into the void. You can replay the tower as many times as you like, trying to beat your own best time or racing a friend side by side in 2 player mode.
