## About

King Rathor 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as a King who have to rescue the queen from the enemy soldiers and you have to collect the ornaments while avoiding the enemy soldiers, flying enemy birds, spikes and saws and rescue the queen in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original King Rathor series.
