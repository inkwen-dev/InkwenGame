## How to play

Use the WASD and Arrow keys to move Watch out for the snowballs coming from the left and right and push each other into the water The first player to fall into the water 10 times loses the game Playable on both mobile and PC

## About

Powerful snowballs are being fired at you from two towers on the left and right &mdash; be careful! By the way, you can push your friend and knock them into the icy water. The player who falls the most loses the game. To win this cold battle with your friend, you must manage to stay on the ice. The first player to fall into the water 10 times loses. Be very careful &mdash; staying on the ice is extremely difficult!
