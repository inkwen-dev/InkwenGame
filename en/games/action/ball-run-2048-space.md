Roll, merge, and race through space! Guide your ball along the track, collect matching numbered balls to grow your value, and avoid spikes and obstacles. Choose safe lanes and reach the finish with the biggest number possible.

Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Move left and right to steer the ball.

Mobile Controls:
Touch & Drag: Steer left and right.
