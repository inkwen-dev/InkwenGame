## About

Mega Crowd is a fast-paced 3D crowd runner where you grow your team, dodge traps, choose the best math gates, and race toward the finish. Multiply your crowd, avoid dangerous obstacles, battle rival groups, defeat powerful bosses, and build the tallest human staircase at the end of each run. The bigger your crowd, the higher you climb and the more coins you earn. Unlock new cosmetic skins, improve your score, master increasingly difficult levels, and see how far your crowd can go!
