## About

Meera Quest is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as a cute anime girl called Meera who have to collect keys in order to escape the Monster world while avoiding the monsters, lava, fire and spikes and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
