## About

2048 Run 3D is a brand-new free digital online game for boys. In the game, control your ball to merge in different numbers! You can merge balls with the same number and color until 2048 is merged. At the same time, there are a lot of obstacles on the road. The final score depends on the number you merge. The bigger the number, the higher the score and the richer the reward. Have fun with it!
