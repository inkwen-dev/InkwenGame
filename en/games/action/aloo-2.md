## About

Aloo 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as Aloo, the potato man, and you have to reach the finish flag while collecting the energy drink bottles and avoiding the ball monsters, giant blades, saws and spikes. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Aloo" series.
