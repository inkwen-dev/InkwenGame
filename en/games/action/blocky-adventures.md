## About

Blocky Adventures is a fast platformer where every jump counts. Guide your little block hero through 50 levels across five distinct worlds, from icy caverns to blazing volcanoes, plus a secret mega-level for the boldest players. Collect the energy orbs, dodge patrolling and flying enemies, and race the clock in Speedrun mode. Can you conquer all 50 levels and uncover the secret world?
