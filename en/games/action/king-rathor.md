Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

King Rathor is a 2D platformer where you play as a King who have to save the queen who was held captive by the enemy soldiers. You have to collect the ornaments while avoiding the enemy soldiers, saws, spikes and rescue the queen from the danger. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
