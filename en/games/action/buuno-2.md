Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Buuno 2 is a 2D platformer where you play as an office worker who have to collect the important documents while avoiding the Evil coworkers, drones, their bullets, spikes, rotating saws, and reach the purple flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Buuno" series.
