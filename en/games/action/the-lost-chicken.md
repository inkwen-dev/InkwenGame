## About

The Lost Chicken is a 3d arcade game where players smash, bump and bounce through revolving helix platforms to reach the end. Sound easy? You wish!! Your chicken smashes like a brick through colorful trees that block its descent, but if you hit a tree, it’s all over! Your chicken shatters to pieces and you have to start your fall all over again.
