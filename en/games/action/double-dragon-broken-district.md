## About

Double Dragon: Broken District is a martial arts story through four missions. Choose Mina or Ken and fight beside an AI sibling to find a missing trainer after your dojo burns. Chain punches and kicks, dodge enemy windups, throw foes, carry staffs and unleash Twin Spirit attacks. Web Dev <a href='https://www.friv.org.es/'>https://www.friv.org.es/</a> Three encounters per chapter, distinct bosses, checkpoints, touch controls and an original arcade-inspired soundtrack bring the district to life.
