## About

Huggy needs to survive. Help Huggy and ensure he reaches the finish line. Do everything you can to get him into the black portal at the finish line. The area is full of obstacles: spinning gears descending from above and traps on the ground. Reach the portal safely without dying. Remember, the time is slowly running out, so you must hurry and reach the portal without hitting any obstacles before the time runs out.
