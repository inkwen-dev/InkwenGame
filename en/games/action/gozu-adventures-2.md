## About

Gozu Adventures 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the cup cakes while avoiding the various kinds of enemies, flying creatures, spikes, rotating saws and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Gozu Adventures" series.
