## About

Tom was walking down the street when suddenly a bad dog started running towards him. Help Tom escape from the dog. He has to overcome all obstacles otherwise the dog will catch up. Tom should jump through obstacles or bends of birds and spiders that appear. Swipe up and down on the screen to jump and bend.
