## How to play

Click or tap the left half of the screen to strike an enemy approaching from the left Click or tap the right half to attack an enemy approaching from the right Do not strike too early wait until the enemy is within attack range

## About

A fast-paced arcade game about a fighter caught in the middle of a street showdown. Enemies approach from the left and right, and your task is to choose the correct direction and strike at the right moment. Every defeated gangster increases your score, while the pace keeps rising and a single mistake can end the run. Earned points remain available between games. Spend them to unlock new fighters and change your hero&rsquo;s appearance. Simple controls let you jump straight into the action, while the increasing difficulty tests your reactions, focus, and sense of rhythm. How many enemies can you defeat in one attempt?
