## About

Do you dare to bet your life on an exciting roulette wheel showdown? Buckshot Roulette is a gun simulator that combines strategy, shooting and survival. Instead of a pistol, a powerful shotgun was used. Every pull of the trigger is a choice of fate. The winner lives, the loser dies. You will begin a Turn-Based shooting roulette game. Each round will have a fixed number of bullets and blanks.
