## About

Cocktail Run is a 2D pixel platformer built for quick arcade fun. Run, jump, and dodge dangerous spikes or shooting plants to reach the finish line safely. Collect coins along the way to unlock unique character skins in the shop, and grab your daily rewards. Fully optimized for both PC and mobile devices.
