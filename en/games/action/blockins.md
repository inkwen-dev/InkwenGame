## How to play

Move - WASD or Arrow Keys Switch - X or Space bar Back - B or ESC Restart - R

## About

In Blockins, use your puzzle-solving skills to stack Tetris-style blocks and reach the portal. With blocks of different shapes, reaching the portal with even just one block wins the level. Maneuver blocks, jump between platforms, stack and rotate to overcome obstacles. Discover a variety of creative and satisfying puzzles, but don't hesitate to use hints if stuck.
