## About

Chicken Yeet is a fast, funny arcade launcher about one brave chicken, one very enthusiastic farmer, and a completely unreasonable journey to space. Time the power meter for a PERFECT hit, steer through glowing boost rings, collect coins and golden feathers, and dodge everything from birds and balloons to meteors and space debris. Spend your rewards on unlimited upgrades and unlock an absurd arsenal of launchers, including a frying pan, squeaky hammer, turbo shovel, barnstorm guitar, leaf blower, and rocket racket.
