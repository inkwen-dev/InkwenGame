## About

Aloo 3 is a 2D platformer where you play as a Potato Man called Aloo and you have to collect energy drinks while avoiding the various kinds of blades, saws, rotating blades, and 3 different kinds of enemy monsters. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the third installment of Aloo series.
