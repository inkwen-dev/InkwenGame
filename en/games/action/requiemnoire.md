## About

**RequiemNoire** is a fast-paced reflex game where speed and accuracy are the keys to survival. Press the correct arrow keys before time runs out to restore your timer and escape a deadly blade. One wrong move or slow reaction can bring you closer to defeat. Stay focused, react quickly, master each sequence, and test how long you can survive in this intense reaction and timing challenge!
