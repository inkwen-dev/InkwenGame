## How to play

Control (computer):
● W, A, S, D - Movement
● SPACE BAR - Jump
● Mouse Movement - Camera rotation
● E key - take and put the Brainrot

Management (phone):
● Joystick on the left and buttons on the screen - Movement
● Pinch and move on the right side of the screen - Camera rotation

## About

An exciting arcade game in the genre of parkour. 
Run, avoid the sleepers and escape from the huge brainroth that is inexorably approaching. 
Test your reflexes, speed and agility by completing challenging levels and saving Brainroth from danger!
