## About

Start an adventure with the fearless and equipped stickman! Defeat the enemies that come stronger in each level and Upgrade the Stickman by collecting the falling items and golds! At the end of the level, go to the Upgrade section from the game menu and increase the strength of the character and activate special attacks. Get ready for the Bosses that will come in the next chapters. Let the adventure begin!
