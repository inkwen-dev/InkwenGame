## About

Do you have confidence in your balance skills while you are jumping from a platform to a platform? Flip Jump Race 3D is recommended as a skillful jumping arcade game that is made from 3D stickman and circular platforms. The objective is to jump over all circular platforms and reach the final square platform. Sometimes, you don't need to follow the provided track and jump one by one. As long as you can control the fallen point on the next platform, you are able to jump farther than one platform!
