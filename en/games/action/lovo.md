## How to play

Touch Controls Mouse Swipe NORTH NORTH-EAST NORTH-WEST to jump drag and hold left right to walk Tap Click on eggs to break them and get Power-Ups

## About

LOVO is on a mission to reach new heights by hopping from nest to nest. Along the way, youll encounter enemy animals trying to impede your progress. While jumping higher and higher, dont forget to tap to unlock power-ups hidden inside eggs on the nests, giving you temporary boosts and abilities to overcome obstacles. With simple controls and addictive gameplay, LOVO offers a fun and challenging experience as you strive to set new altitude records.
