## About

Emerald and Amber is a platform game where you have to get stuck to solid surfaces to reach your loved one. It was inspired by some classics of the NES era with retro-style graphics and mechanics, but with a flexible difficulty aiming to be an enjoyable and relaxing experience. The game has 27 levels and a Speedrun mode.
