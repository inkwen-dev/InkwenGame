## About

You must take your place in the adventure of the men in red and yellow costumes, as an action-packed journey awaits you. The red and yellow players need to collect purple potion boxes. If you gather all the potion boxes, the yellow player will claim them and can activate the portal with the potion. The red player can see and eliminate invisible enemies. You must be very careful&mdash;monsters are everywhere, and they want to eat you!
