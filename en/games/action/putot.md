Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Putot is a 2D platformer where you have to collect cubes containing treasures while avoiding the enemy aliens, flying creatures and their bullets, spikes, rotating lasers and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
