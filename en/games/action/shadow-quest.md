## About

In the Shadow Quest three little witches were lived happily together but after sometime one Monster came and wanted to take their power .If They refused to do so,the Monster taken away one of those witches with him.Hence One little witch went to get their friend. The main object in this game is to complete the level.You will have to find the door to go to the next level(which is set at the end of the level).During the crossover you have to face many obstacles such as spikes , automatic canon, and others creature.
