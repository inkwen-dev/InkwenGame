## How to play

For PC 🖥️: Use the WASD keys to control the character and the right mouse button to rotate the view. You can zoom the camera in and out using the mouse wheel.

On mobile devices 📱: a joystick will be available for movement, jump button and you can rotate the camera with your finger.
