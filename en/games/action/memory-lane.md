## About

Your goal in each level is to reach the Goal Platform without falling or touching enemies. Levels are played in two phases. A. Reveal Phase: Duration: Short preview at the start of each level &bull; All platforms become visible. &bull; You cannot move. &bull; Study the layout, paths, traps, and pickups. Your task: Remember what you see. ________________________________________ B. Action Phase: &bull; All memory phase platforms become invisible unless they are marked to stay visible. &bull; Touching platforms reveals them permanently. Your task: Reach the goal using your memory and reflexes.
