## How to play

Choose a weapon, then drag back and release to aim. Press GO to launch your attack!

Use JUMP and drag to set an optional jump before firing. Some weapons require you to select a spot on the map instead of aiming.

Knock opponents off the islands while keeping yourself out of the lava. Watch the terrain crumble and the lava rise—the last fighter standing wins!

## About

Blast, bounce, and bonk your way to victory! Bonk Islands is a colorful physics battle game where four quirky fighters clash on floating islands above rising lava. Fire rockets, tear through destructible terrain, and send your rivals flying with unusual weapons. Stay on solid ground, dodge danger, and become the last fighter standing. Earn coins after each battle and customize your character with funny hats, outfits, and accessories.
