## About

I guess all of you have seen plugs in real life. Plug Head 3D is a vertical version adventure game with 3D arcade game rules. You are supposed to run to the finish line while collecting electric energy items. Except for the clean outlet bridges, all obstacles will consume your electric energy in different quantities and colors. Glad you will complete all levels and have fun!
