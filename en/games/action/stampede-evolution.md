## How to play

Desktop: WASD or the arrow keys (or drag with the left mouse button) to move, Space or right-click to jump, P to pause. Mobile: left thumb is a joystick, tap JUMP or the right side of the screen to jump. Your herd throws food at predators automatically. Steer into green gates to grow your herd and avoid red ones, steer into blue boards to pick an upgrade, and reach the finish arch with as many animals as you can. Walk around camp to level up, then evolve into bigger animals. In Stampede you run forward on your own: dodge walls and water and go as far as you can.

## About

A 3D nature platformer where you lead a growing herd of animals. Walk your camp to level up and evolve from a squirrel to a lion, then run and jump through hand-built biomes (flower meadows, autumn woods, rainy marshes, frozen rivers, night forests) while your herd throws food at the predators that hunt it. Steer into the good gates to grow the herd, pick upgrades, beat bosses and earn three stars on every level. Or try Stampede: an endless auto-run that keeps getting faster and wilder.
