## About

Fyer Bot 2 is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer where you have to collect fire cubes while avoiding the enemy Fyer bots, flying bots, bullets, turrets and their bullets, spikes, giant blades and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Fyer Bot" series.
