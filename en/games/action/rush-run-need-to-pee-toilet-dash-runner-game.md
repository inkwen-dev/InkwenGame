## About

Rush Run: Need to Pee is not a typical pee-and-poop game. It’s a fast-paced, addictive runner + drawing puzzle game where timing, creativity, and quick decisions decide everything. 🚀

Your mission is simple—but urgent: help a desperate little dude rush through crazy pee & poop maps and reach the toilet in time. Draw the perfect path, avoid messy obstacles, collect coins, and make it to toilet time before it’s too late!

Toilet Time = Victory
