## About

Tile Multiplier Flier is a fast-paced game where players strategically place letter tiles to form words and earn points. Each tile has a multiplier value, enhancing scores for longer or more complex words. Players compete against each other or the clock to achieve the highest score within a set time limit. With its blend of vocabulary skills and tactical thinking, Tile Multiplier Flier offers a thrilling challenge for word enthusiasts of all ages. Ready to soar to the top of the leaderboard?
