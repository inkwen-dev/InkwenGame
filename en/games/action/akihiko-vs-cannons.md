## About

Akihiko vs Cannons is a 2D platformer where you play as a guy called Akihiko who have to collect Gold Bars while avoiding the Cannon bots, bullets, spikes, flying Cannon Bots, and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. You have 6 lives to start with.
