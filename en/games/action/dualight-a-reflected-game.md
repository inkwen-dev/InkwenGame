## About

DuaLight is a challenging 2D pixel art hyper-casual platformer. The true path lies in the reflection. Navigate levels where the real world tricks your eyes, and only those who watch the reflection below can move forward. Minimalist and short. In DuaLight &ndash; A Reflected Game, nothing is as it seems&hellip; or rather, everything only appears in the reflection. You control a character in a world where invisible platforms can&rsquo;t be seen in the real world, but only in the reflection below the screen. You&rsquo;ll encounter spikes and invisible platforms until you look at the reflection and realize the path was there all along.
