## About

Gorbo the Orc is a bold, funny platform adventure. The Minotaur Warlord stole the golden crown of the Tusk Clan, and Gorbo is going to get it back, one monster at a time. New to platformers? The first levels teach you to run, jump, swing your cleaver, chain a 3-hit combo and throw your axe, one step at a time. 48 levels across 6 worlds, 6 boss fights and 144 hidden gems. From the Whispering Forest to the Volcano Fortress, every world brings new monsters and tricks: archers and wizards that shoot at you, shield bearers, charging bears, ghosts, storm clouds, icicles, fire jets, swinging blades, slippery ice, bounce mushrooms and locked gates. Fight your way. Knock arrows out of the air with your cleaver, stomp monsters and throw your axe like a boomerang. Drink potions when your hearts run low and spend your coins at the merchant. Your progress is saved, so you can come back and finish the quest anytime.
