## About

Mizu Quest is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as a magical anime girl who have to collect all the bottles of the magical potion while avoiding the depressed monsters having tridents, spikes, lava, and fire and heal the heart of the guys, who are going through heartbreak because of rejection, in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
