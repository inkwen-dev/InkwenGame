## About

In this adventure, Steve mounts his horse and wants to go to the villagers. The villagers are waiting for him to show him the chest they found. Steve must collect all the gold, defeat all the monsters in the forest, and reach the chest. When he opens the chest, he will become the king of the entire world. The chest contains a very valuable item. Steve must be very careful, as the forest is filled with monsters and obstacles.
