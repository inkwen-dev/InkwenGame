## How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys on desktop, or drag anywhere on the screen to use the joystick on mobile. Your hero attacks automatically when an enemy is within reach. Defeat every enemy to clear the level; a boss waits on every fifth level. Step out of the orange danger circles, take out healers first and hit shieldbearers from the side. Spend coins on upgrades and new swords between levels.

## About

ASMR Obby Slicer is a colorful 3D sword arena. Slice blocky pinatas and robots to pieces: they are filled with paint, so every hit splashes the arena with bright colors and no blood at all. Clear five-level chapters, beat a unique boss at the end of each one, open chests, upgrade your hero and unlock swords with special abilities, skins, hats, paints and blade trails.
