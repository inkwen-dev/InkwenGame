## About

Get ready to help and take control of brave cats, and fight against their archenemy, the Rat King! Its causing problems and needs to be stopped; we dont know how a rat could grow this big. These cats are upset about the evil plans of the Rat King! With the use of a slingshot, they plan to reach the evil king and scratch its face! Your objective in this game is to complete each level by launching the cats toward the enemy to kill it.
