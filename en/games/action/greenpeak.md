## How to play

🎮 PC Controls
• Move — WASD or arrow keys ⌨️
• Jump — standard jump key
• Sprint — Shift ⚡

📱 Mobile Controls
• Move — virtual joystick on the left
• Jump and sprint — buttons on the right

⚡ Stamina
Sprinting uses stamina. If you run for too long, stamina will run out. Slow down or stop to recover it.

🎯 Game Goal
Climb to the top of the green mountain ⛰️, explore the world 🌿, and collect all meme collections 😄 along the way.

🔥 Checkpoints
Checkpoints are areas surrounded by torches 🔥.
Stand inside a checkpoint to save your progress. After falling or dying, you will respawn there.

🤝 Online Play
Explore the mountain together with friends, walk, jump, and enjoy the journey at your own pace.

## About

🎮 GreenPEAK is an atmospheric online parkour game set on a lush green mountain! 🌿⛰️ Explore scenic paths, streams, forest hideaways, and caves, enjoy the environment, and make your way to the peak! 🧗‍♂️✨

Dive into a calm yet engaging world where it’s not just about jumping 🦘, but also discovering beautiful nature 🌲, meeting friends, and sharing the journey together. Collect rare memes 😄🖼️ along the way to the top!

💬 The game features an online mode — meet, chat, and explore the mountain together 🌄 with no complex combat or survival mechanics. No damage, poison, fire, or cold — just parkour, nature, and a fun adventure. 🎈

🌟 If you enjoy obby games, peak, only up, or parkour — this game is for you!

• 🤝 Online play with friends
• 🏃 Parkour and jumping

• 🎧 Relaxing, meditative gameplay
