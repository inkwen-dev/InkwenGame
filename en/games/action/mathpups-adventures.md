## How to play

Use the WASD or Arrow keys to move On touch devices use the on-screen arrow keys to move

## About

MathPup is through with math for the day but is still hungry for dog bones. Help MathPup get all of the dog bones in the level. After that MathPup will need to get the trophy to complete each level. 32 levels for MathPup to complete. Get the trophy on each level to complete it. MathPup will have to get all of the dog bones on a level before the trophy appears. If you get stuck on a level just click on the Video button on the Main Menu to see the walkthrough video.
