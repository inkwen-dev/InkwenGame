## How to play

Hit the bomb at the right moment and send it flying back toward your opponent.

Desktop Controls:

A / Left Mouse Button: Hit the bomb.
In local two-player mode, Player 1 uses A and Player 2 uses L.

Mobile Controls:

Tap the screen: Hit the bomb.
The game is played in landscape orientation.
