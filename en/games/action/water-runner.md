## How to play

At the right time you must press the left click of the mouse and slide it to the right and left if you touch the fires your water will decrease If you don t have enough water you will lose

## About

Our character, who is running for his flowers, has 2 difficult roads and at the end of those roads he waters his flowers, which he loves more than anything, but for this he needs to collect water on the roads, the more water he collects without touching the flames on the roads, the more he waters his flowers, but if he cannot collect enough water on one of the difficult roads, the other difficult He can't cross the road and his poor flowers are left without water.
