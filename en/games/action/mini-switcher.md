## About

Switch the gravity to guide a small blob character to the goal, traveling around 30 levels and facing a variety of colorful enemies and obstacles with different behaviors. You can also test your ability on the speed run mode. Mini Switcher was made in a retro-style fashion and was aiming to be a short relaxing experience.
