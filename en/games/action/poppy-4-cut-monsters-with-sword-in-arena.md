## How to play

🎯 Your goal is to defeat all the monsters in the arena using your sword! Cut down your enemies and stop the monster invasion. The further you go, the harder it gets! ⚔️

🕹️ Controls:
On PC 💻 — hold down the left mouse button and move the cursor to attack.
On phone 📱 — swipe your finger across the screen to strike with your sword.

🔥 Tips:
✅ Watch your enemies' movements and strike first!
✅ Combine attacks to deal more damage.
✅ Don't forget to upgrade your sword as you progress through the game!
