## How to play

MOVE LEFT-RIGHT SWIPE MOUSE

## About

Your mission in this game; It is to find the answer to the math question you have been told in a short time and to pass the correct answer on the gate that will appear in front of you as you move forward. You will see the math operation at the bottom of the screen. To get through the gate in front of you, you have to go through the gate with the correct answer. If you pass through the gate with the wrong answer, the game will be over somehow. You succeed when you pass through the gate with the correct answer.
