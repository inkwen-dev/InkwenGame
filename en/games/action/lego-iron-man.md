## About

Iron Man Mod - in this game you are given armor and high-tech gadgets with the abilities of a popular superhero. When you equip a hero with armor, it gives you super-technological power. Create your own armor; you will have a large selection of different colors and abilities. By putting on the super armor of the iron man, you will become invulnerable. You can craft and use various rockets and TNTs to blow up enemies or objects that stand in your way. Also in the bonus mode, you will find the infinite gauntlet of Iron Man, the same from the marvel universe. It has all six infinity stones, and there is a separate function for each stone. With a time stone, you can control time. The reality stone will teleport you between dimensions. Space will take you to different places. Power stone can cause thunderstorms and natural disasters. Also in the application you will find a bonus mod and bonus skins! - Iron Man - Iron spiderman - Iron captain america - Сolor superheroes
