## About

Angelos Adventure: Searching for Elizabeth is a platform game in which you will search for your girlfriend Elizabeth after she has been abducted by aliens. You will have to go to outerspace to find her and she will be on one of six planets. She will appear on a different planet every game. Jump on enemies to destroy them. Jump on vases to obtain their contents. In the first 5 levels on Earth youll need to smash at least 10 vases to go to the next level. On the 6 space levels youll need to walk over the Earth planet to go back to Earth.
