WASD or Arrow keys for player movement Press W or Upper arrow keys twice to double jump

## About

Super Pig on Xmas is a 2D Platformer where the player needs to collect candies and give it to the piglet who is eagerly waiting to receive the Christmas candy. Your mission is to collect the candies while avoiding traps and enemies. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
