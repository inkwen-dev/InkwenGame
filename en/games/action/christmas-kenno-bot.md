## About

Christmas Kenno Bot is a 2D Christmas themed platformer where you play as a bot who have to collect green gift boxes while avoiding the enemy bots, flying bots, their bullets, saws, spikes and reach the Eskimo's door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
