## About

Christmas Deno Bot is a 2D Sci-fi themed platformer on a Christmas setting in which you play as a robot called Deno, who have to collect the fuel containers while avoiding the enemy bots, flying robots, giant blades, saws and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
