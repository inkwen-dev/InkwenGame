## About

Choco Benno is a 2D cute little platformer where you have to collect all the glasses of the chocolate milkshake while avoiding the chocolate eater monsters, spikes, giant blades and reach the Chocolate exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
