## About

Take back the waterfront in Final Fight: Harbor Justice, an arcade-style 2D brawler with two playable fighters. Choose powerful Nessa or quick-footed Ivo, explore the docks and city blocks, and face patrols with readable attack warnings. Punch, jump, block, grab and throw enemies, pick up pipes, and smash crates for supplies. Fight through the scrolling campaign, demolition bonus rounds and boss encounters while local checkpoints keep your progress safe. Detailed character artwork, recorded impacts, waterfront ambience and an original arcade groove bring the uprising to life. Play with keyboard and mouse or on-screen touch controls.
