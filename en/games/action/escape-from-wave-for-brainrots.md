## How to play

For the computer:  

- Press the WASD keys or the ⬅️⬆️⬇️➡️ arrows. 
- Rotate the camera by moving the mouse with the right mouse button held down 
- Interaction with animals by pressing the E key 
- Jump to the Space 

 For the phone:  

- Move using the joystick on the left side of the screen 
- Rotate the camera using the right side of the screen 
- Interact with animals using a pop-up window above the animal 
- Jump on the button on the right side of the screen

## About

Welcome to the game "Escape from Wave for Brainrots"! 🌊🧠

A giant tsunami is already coming — and only you can save the Brainrots! Grab them, run away from the wave and get them to the base before it's too late. The faster you act, the more chances you have to save the rarest and most valuable brainrots.

Boost your speed to take the brainrots further and faster.
🧠 Find rare Brainrots — from ordinary to truly legendary.
🏠 Deliver them to the base, expand the collection and become a real savior.

Each new brainroot is a step towards progress, new opportunities and even more action. Test your reaction, strategy and speed — because the tsunami is not waiting!

Can you save all the brainrots and become the main hero of the base?
Run, rescue and collect the rarest ones before the wave catches up with you! 🌊🔥
