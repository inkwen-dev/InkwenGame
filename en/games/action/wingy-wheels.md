## How to play

Hold the mouse button, tap and hold the screen, or hold Space to dive down a hill. Release to fly off the hilltop and glide.
Land on downward slopes to keep your speed. Several perfect slides in a row start Fever mode.
Avoid landing on steep uphill slopes and keep enough speed to climb, or the run ends.
Reach the next island before sunset to get extra time.

## About

Wingy Wheels is a cozy hill-sliding game with winged cars. Dive down the slopes to build speed, launch off the hilltops and glide through the sky. Chain perfect slides to enter Fever mode and collect coins along the way.

Spend your coins on 5 upgrades (Longer Day, Glide Wings, Engine Power, Coin Magnet, Lucky Coins) and unlock 6 colorful cars. Complete 3 missions to level up and raise your score multiplier. Every run has a new track, and the Daily Challenge gives everyone the same track each day. Race the sunset: reach new islands to earn extra daylight before night falls.
