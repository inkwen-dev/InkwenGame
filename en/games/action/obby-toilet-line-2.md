## About

Obby Toilet Line starts in the most chaotic place imaginable: a gas station restroom line. Around 30 different characters are already waiting, and the queue stretches all the way past the store entrance. Your goal is simple, but the situation is not: earn money while you wait, move up the line, and reach the mysterious toilet at the end. This is not just a &ldquo;stand still&rdquo; queue. The game uses an idle-style progression system: the position you&rsquo;re standing in determines how much you earn.
