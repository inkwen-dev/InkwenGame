## About

Jelly Phil 2 is a 2D cute little platformer where you have to collect pink jellies while avoiding the jelly enemies, saws and reach the exit door in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Jelly Phil" series.
