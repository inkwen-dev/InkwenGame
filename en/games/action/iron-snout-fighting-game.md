## About

Iron Snout is an online fighting game where you play as a pig, and have to fight the wolves. Punch, kick and flip your way through waves of wolves and other bad guys. Dodge axes and other thrown projectiles away with the arrow keys. Play Iron Snout to show those wolves not to mess with pigs. You and a friend can play Iron Snout for free in the 2-player Wolfieball mode, or you can take on the wolves solo in Classic and Sudden Death modes.
