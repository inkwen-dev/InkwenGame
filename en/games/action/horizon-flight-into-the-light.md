## How to play

Desktop: arrow keys or A/D to steer, Space, Up arrow or W to jump.
Mobile: hold the left or right half of the screen to steer, swipe up to jump.
The sun slowly sets. Blue crystals raise it back up, building shadows make it set faster. Green crystals give you jumps. The flight ends when the light is gone or you crash. Spend collected triangles in the Hangar on permanent upgrades.

## About

Glide toward the setting sun: weave between white towers, collect triangles and catch blue crystals that lift the sun back up and throw your glider forward in a rush. Every 3 km a new world begins with new obstacles — ramps, rolling balls, gates, grinders, windmills, pendulums and presses. How far can you fly before the light is gone?
