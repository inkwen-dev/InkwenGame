## About

In our adventure, theres a school monster that constantly chases you. Sometimes its a green monster, and other times its a black monster. You need to escape from the school monsters. The school monsters want to eat you. Escape the school monsters with Noob and Pro. While running from the monster, never, ever look back. Run from the monster, collect all the diamonds you find on the way, and reach the portal at the end of the level.
