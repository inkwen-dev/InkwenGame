## About

In Squid Gaming, you must survive. Compete with your friend &mdash; first, make it through the Green Light, Red Light game alive, and then beat your friend in the tug-of-war game. Remember, you have to reach the end of the game before time runs out. After that, challenge your friend again in the next game, where the best tug-of-war player wins before time runs out. A short but fun competitive game you can play with your friend. Play in two different modes with your friend and celebrate the winner.
