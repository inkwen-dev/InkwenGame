## About

Experience high-octane ninja action in Assassin Ninja Rush! Take control of an assassin ninja, sprinting and dodging through enemies. Strategically eliminate foes to reach new levels. Unlock characters and weapons by completing levels or using gold coins in the in-game store. Dodge bullets, smash crates for health, and hone your skills to become the ultimate ninja assassin! Assassin Ninja Rush delivers an immersive, arcade-style 3D adventure that will put your ninja abilities to the test.
