## How to play

Click on monsters to deal damage. Hire heroes - they will fight for you automatically. Earn gold and level up your heroes to deal more damage. Use skills for powerful bonuses. Defeat bosses every 5 zones to push further. Unlock artifacts for permanent upgrades. The game continues even while you're offline!

## About

Heroes vs Monsters: Idle RPG is a clicker where you assemble a team of heroes and send them into an endless battle against monsters. Level up your heroes, unlock artifacts, use powerful skills and push through hundreds of zones. Progress continues even while you're away - come back and collect your rewards!
