## About

Join Numbers is a popular puzzle game. How about your math skills? This game needs you to pass the different number's gates. You will start the game with a number, move the number to control it through the number's gates on the road ahead, the number you control must be greater than the number gate to successfully reach the end. Try your best to unlock more levels now!
