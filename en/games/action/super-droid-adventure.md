## About

Super Droid Adventure is an arcade game where you help the little Robot to defeat a powerful boss that has destroyed his castle. During your journey, you'll encounter different traps and dangerous enemies that will try to stop you from reaching your goal. Break blocks to find power-ups, coins and other surprises. Use firepower to make the job easier and good luck!
