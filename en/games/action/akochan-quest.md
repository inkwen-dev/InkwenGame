## About

Akochan Quest is a 2D anime themed platformer where you play as Akochan, a girl, who have to collect all of the necklaces while avoiding the zombies, the heart monsters, saws, rotating blades and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you continue.
