## About

Heart Star is a puzzle game created by Adventure Islands. Change the world around you! Heart Star lets you control two characters who must reach the end zone in each level by manipulating the game objects associated with their appropriate colors. Switch between the girl and boy to move carefully and change the room. Use teamwork to master every level!
