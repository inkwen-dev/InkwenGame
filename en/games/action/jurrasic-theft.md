## About

Jurassic Theft is a cool platform game by playfreeonline.games in which you try to steal some precious eggs from dinosaurs! The mad professor has travelled back in time to the Jurassic period to steal dinosaur eggs so he can start his own Jurassic theme park. You need to collect all the eggs that you can find while keeping the professor alive in this dangerous dinosaur filled environment. Watch out for the patrolling Velociraptors on the ground, Mosasaurus in the water and the Pterodactyls in the sky and the giant T-rex running around!
