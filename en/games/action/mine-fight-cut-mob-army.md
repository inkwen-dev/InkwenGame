🎯 Your goal is to cut all the mobs with a sword and complete all the levels! Fight hordes of zombies, skeletons and creepers, become stronger and prove that you are a real warrior! ⚔️
🕹️ Controls:
On the computer 💻 – move the mouse and press the button to attack.
On the phone 📱 – swipe the screen to swing the sword.
🔥 Tips:
✅ Cut mobs in a row to get more points!
✅ Use the environment – ​​push enemies into traps and explode obstacles!
✅ Upgrade your sword and become even stronger!
