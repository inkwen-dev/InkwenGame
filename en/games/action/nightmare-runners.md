If playing single Move W A S D or ARROW KEYS Jump SPACE If playing two player Player 1 Move W A S D Jump G Player 2 Move ARROW KEYS Jump L

## About

Nightmare Runners is the ultimate knockout game with both fun and surprising traps! Runners race at night and unknown traps and monsters can get in your way. The aim of the game is to advance in each race without being eliminated. Of course, this is not as easy as it seems; because there are many obstacles, traps and rivals... You can play the Nightmare Runners game against 1 Player online / bot opponents, or against your friend in 2 Player mode. You can unlock new skins and characters with the coins you get with game achievements.
