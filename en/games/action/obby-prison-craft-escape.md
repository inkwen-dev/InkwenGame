## About

Obby Prison: Craft Escape is an exciting survival adventure in which you need to escape from a prison full of dangers. Once behind bars, you find yourself surrounded by vigilant police, other prisoners, and countless traps in the world. 

Use your skills to navigate through the complex layout of the prison. Avoid police guards, disable security systems to plan your ultimate escape.

The block environment provides endless opportunities for research and strategy development, offering new challenges at every turn. Will you be able to outsmart the police and survive, or will you remain a prisoner forever?

Fight your way to freedom in Obby Prison: Craft Escape, the ultimate test of your survival instincts and creativity. Will you be able to escape from the indestructible prison?
