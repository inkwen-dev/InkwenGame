## About

Clash of Orcs is the next instalment of this fantastic series. In this title you take control of an Orc Warlord and have your very own orc clan! You must fight against the other clans and vie for power as the top orc boss. This is a tower strategy game and you must send wave after wave of orc troops to attack your enemies.
