## About

Kaitochan vs Ghosts 2 is a 2D Halloween themed platformer where you have to collect yellow balls while avoiding the Pumpkin enemies, zombies, fire, spikes, and flying monsters and reach the Exit door in order to go to the next level. The Exit door will only be unlocked when all of the yellow balls are collected in each level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Kaitochan vs Ghosts" series.
