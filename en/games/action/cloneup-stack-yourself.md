## About

CloneUp &ndash; Stack Yourself is a 2D pixel-art platformer where your greatest tool is&hellip; yourself! Create clones, stack them, and use them as platforms to dodge spikes, reach new heights, and make your way to the exit door. In CloneUp &ndash; Stack Yourself, you&rsquo;ll use copies of the main character to reach platforms and avoid deadly obstacles. For some unknown reason, you have the power to create clones of yourself and even stack them to form makeshift platforms. Use them to cross dangerous gaps, escape spikes, and climb higher toward the goal. Your mission: reach the exit door on each level, using your reflexes, logic, and clever clone placement.
