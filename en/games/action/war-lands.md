## About

War Lands is a good action RPG with random elements and maps. The characters and environments are hand drawn giving a very creative feeling when playing. You are a warrior specialized in any discipline, you can carry from the classic sword and shield, two-handed sword or a magic scepter. You must enter a portal that will take you to a world full of monsters. Defeat hordes of the undead, trolls, skeletons, sorcerers, archers and defeat the bosses you will find at the end of your journey. As a mercenary, we recommend that you open all the boxes and destroy all the barrels you find in your path to collect as much money as you can and level up. Fill your inventory with different weapons, which you can spell to improve their effectiveness. Every time you die don't worry, there is no evil that doesn't come, you will reappear in a village where you can improve your skills, collect your rewards for completed missions or cast spells on your weapons. These are some of the features of War Lands Tons of items, upgrades and powerups. Randomly generated dungeons, elements, enemies and bosses You'll never play again. Stylized hand-drawn characters and environments.
