## About

Labubu and his friend need to escape the island during summer! Be very careful as you adventure together and collect all the golden clams along the way. Of course, collect those golden clams first &mdash; then reach the giant gift box! Both adorable Labubu friends must make it to the gift box, because inside are their favorite things. But watch out &mdash; the path is full of dangers and obstacles!
