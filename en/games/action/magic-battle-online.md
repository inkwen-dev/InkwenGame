## About

Magic Battle Online is an epic online battle with different elements and abilities! Choose an element: fire, water, wind, earth, ice, lightning, light, darkness, crystals, void, and time. Each has its own unique spells and combat abilities! Up to 10 real players can fight in the arena simultaneously. The map changes every five minutes! Level up your elemental mastery from Apprentice to Alpha, increase your spell ranks, forge runes, and fuse them into more powerful ones! Challenge your friends to a duel and participate in events! Can you become the most powerful mage in this cubic world?
