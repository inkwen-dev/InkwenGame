## About

Bulldozers Jigsaw is a game with bulldozers images. This game offer to you twelve images with bulldozers. Your job is to solve the pieces to get the image with bulldozers. If you want to play all levels you need to unlock them first. For that purpose you will start with first available level to play. Choose the game mode that you want to play. You can play in 3 modes as easy with 25 pieces, medium with 49 pieces and hard mode with 100 pieces. Put the pieces in the right place to get the image with bulldozers. This is only way to go to the next level. Play this addictive game and have fun.
