## About

Fruity Cubes Island is a delicious cube blasting puzzle game . Everyone loves a tropical island with lots of fresh fruit! Match your fruit blocks to complete a complete line and earn points in this tricky puzzle game. Each level in Fruity Cubes Island has a goal for you to achieve, and it gets increasingly difficult with smaller game boards and more complex fruit cubes
