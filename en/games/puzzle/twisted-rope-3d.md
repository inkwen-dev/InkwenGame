## About

Rope Tangle Puzzle is a captivating mobile game for Android that challenges players to untangle ropes arranged in intricate configurations. This free-to-play puzzle game requires strategic thinking as players manipulate ropes without creating new knots. Using simple touch controls, players can select and move ropes to solve increasingly complex puzzles, all set against vibrant visuals and a user-friendly interface.

Each level offers a fresh challenge, demanding critical thinking and quick problem-solving to untie the knots. Success relies on careful planning and precise rope arrangement, making the game a delightful mix of fun and mental exercise. Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts, Rope Tangle Puzzle delivers an engaging experience that puts your skills to the test.
