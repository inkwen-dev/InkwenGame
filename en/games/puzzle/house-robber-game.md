## About

House Robber is a casual puzzle game in which thieves burglarize homes. You play the role of a thief, take advantage of the darkness of the night to start your theft journey! Everything must be careful, not be discovered by the security guards, quick battle, get the things and quickly get into the car to escape. The theft journey is very exciting, are you ready to join? Have fun!
