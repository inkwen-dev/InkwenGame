## About

As the name says, Tricky Trees is an easy to learn but hard to master Match-3- game in the best tradition of the genre. You not only need to match the right items but also beat the time what can indeed become very tricky but also pretty addictive. Your task is to match three or more fruits in a row and collect the numbers of fruits depending on the goal each level is giving to you! Good luck and lots of fun!
