## About

**Twist and Tug** is a creative rope puzzle game where you drag, connect, and arrange ropes to match the target shape. Position each node carefully, prevent ropes from overlapping, and recreate the correct pattern to complete each level. Puzzles become more complex as you progress, testing your logic, observation, and problem-solving skills. Master every rope challenge and enjoy satisfying brain-teasing gameplay!
