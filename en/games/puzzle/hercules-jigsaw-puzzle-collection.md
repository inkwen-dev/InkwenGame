## About

The new puzzle is a free game. Hercules is trying to prevent the minions of Hades, and only you are able to help him cope with the situation. And to do this, take the mouse in your hands and collect the picture from the pieces. Together, they will show a color image. The correct location is determined by the fact that the fragment will no longer move from its place. And until that happens, the next one won't appear. Only when everything is installed in place, the flash game will agree with the result and present a pleasant gift. All the action is accompanied by exciting music, so you will have to forget about sleeping and yawning!
