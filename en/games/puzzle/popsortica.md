## How to play

Select a cube from a column to pick it up, then click on another column to place it but only if the colors match. 
Continue sorting the cubes until all columns are empty.

## About

PopSortica is a new chapter in Poppy’s adventures! 

Join the underwater SORTING voyage! 

Challenging yet relaxing levels await as you sort things out.

The rules are simple, but the difficulty increases with each new level.
Special obstacles and conditions will challenge your skills, while boosters will help you achieve the best results faster.

Dive deep with Mermaid Poppy and challenge yourself in this exciting sea sorting adventure!
