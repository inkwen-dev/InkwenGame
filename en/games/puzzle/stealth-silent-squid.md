## How to play

Goal: guide the tentacle through guarded rooms and free the squid from the cage. Collect tentacle parts to increase your growth range. Grab soldiers and furniture, throw them, break barriers, and solve puzzles that open the way forward.

Desktop controls:
W, A, S, and D — move the tentacle.
Left mouse button — move the tentacle toward the pointer.
Mobile controls:
Swipe across the screen to guide the tentacle.

## About

A captive squid waits in a cage at the top of a guarded tower. Guide the tentacle through rooms, collect lost parts, and grow longer.

Soldiers block the way, but everything around you can become a weapon. Grab enemies, throw furniture, break barriers, and solve puzzles to reach the cage.

- Grow by collecting lost parts
- Physics-based fights with soldiers
- Throw enemies and objects
- Puzzles and dangerous obstacles
- Bright 3D levels
