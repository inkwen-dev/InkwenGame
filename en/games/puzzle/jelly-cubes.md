Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Pick and drop sets of jelly cubes on the board. You can rotate the block sets by clicking on it. Put three of the same color together to make points and remove jellies. You're not limited by time, only by empty space on the board so make your moves carefully. Make bigger groups of matches to get more points.
