## About

Blocky Ball 3D is a game where you try to get the ball to the exit by moving wooden disc blocks. You must create the correct route within the given time and find the escape by collecting the stars. The routes with the stars will give you some clues. Marble discs in some sections cannot be moved. Train your brain by playing over 200+ levels!
