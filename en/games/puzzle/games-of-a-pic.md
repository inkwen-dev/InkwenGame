## About

‘Games of a Pic’ is an unusual but interesting game in which you start game with a photo. At the first step you repair the photo. On second level, you play a hidden object game. Then to test your observation skill there is a quiz game. The fourth game of this photo is a spot the difference game. On fifth and sixth position, you will find some more interesting games. Just explore the series of mini games made on a single photo.
