## About

Snake Blocks is a 2D puzzle game where you control a snake of blocks. The goal of the game is to reach the end of each level by maneuvering your snake around obstacles and collecting power-ups. The game features a variety of levels, each with its own unique challenges. Some levels require you to navigate tight spaces, while others require you to use power-ups to solve puzzles.
