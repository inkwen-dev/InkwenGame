## About

Number Order is a fun and brain-boosting puzzle game that challenges your logic and speed! Arrange numbers in the correct sequence by sliding, tapping, or swapping tiles across different grids and difficulty levels. It starts easy but quickly tests your sharpness with increasingly tricky patterns and time limits. Perfect for players of all ages, Number Order is a simple yet addictive way to sharpen your mind, improve focus, and have fun. Unlock new themes, beat your high scores, and master the art of perfect sequencing. Get ready to think fast and stay sharp with the exciting world of Number Order!
