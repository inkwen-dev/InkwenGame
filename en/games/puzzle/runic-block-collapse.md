## About

Welcome to Runic Block Collapse! Tap any block to clear connected blocks in a chain reaction. Removing one block costs 250 points. Removing 2 or more blocks earns points, with larger groups earning more per block. Reach the target score to advance. If you fail, start over. Enjoy endless fun!
