## About

Test your brain with DOP Fun: Delete One Part! This interactive puzzle game will challenge your thinking and keep you hooked with its clever scenarios. Erase just the proper part to solve each puzzle. Look at the picture and figure out the hidden problem. Find the wrong part. Touch the screen and drag your finger to erase part of the drawing and see what lies behind it. Not all solutions are obvious! Youll need logic, creativity, and sometimes even a sense of humor to win.
