## About

Help Sprunki escape from the dark forest prison! Sprunki Drop is a thrilling physics-based puzzle game featuring 60 exciting levels of fun and challenges. Your mission is to remove all the boxes except the grass where the prison is standing. Simply tap or click on the boxes or blocks to destroy them. Use the set blocks cleverly and strategically to guide Sprunki safely onto the grass. Be careful and precise to solve each level! Help Sprunki on this adventurous escape and enjoy hours of entertaining gameplay in this delightful physics puzzle!
