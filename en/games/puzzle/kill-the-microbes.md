## About

In the game Kill The Microbes we will fight with you exactly with harmful microbes. We will see the playing field on the screen. On it in different places chaotic will be located microbes. You need to destroy them all. When the microbe burst, then some parts of it will fly in different directions. If these particles hit other microbes, those in turn will also burst. So carefully study their location and select such an object, clicking on which you can kill all the germs.
