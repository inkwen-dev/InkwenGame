## About

In Find 6 Differences, your mission is to spot small differences between two pictures before time runs out. Look carefully and tap on the differences you find, but be careful&mdash;random taps will cost you time. If you get stuck, you can use one of three helps. Find all six differences to move to the next level, but if time runs out or you tap wrong spots too often, you lose. Its a fun way to test and improve your attention to detail!
