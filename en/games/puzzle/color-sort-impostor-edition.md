## About

Color Sort Impostor Edition is a fun and relaxing puzzle game that challenges your logic and focus. Sort the colorful characters into the correct tubes so that each tube contains only one color. The rules are simple, but the puzzles quickly become more challenging as new colors and obstacles appear. Plan your moves carefully, avoid mistakes, and enjoy a smooth, satisfying sorting experience designed for players of all ages.
