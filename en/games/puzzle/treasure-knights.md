## About

Treasure Knights is an addicting logic game in which your mission is to help a hero get a lost treasure. To do so, you'll have to figure out how to make the treasure fall through the castle's rooms to your hero. If you're a fan of puzzle games and are looking for a new game, check out this Hero Rescue and enjoy a fresh take on a classic puzzle. Although Treasure Knights has straightforward gameplay, it gets harder and harder to find solve the puzzles as you beat levels. The game begins in a castle, which is divided into sections with pins. Each time you pull out a pin, everything in that section will fall. To win the game, pull out the right pins to make all the castle's hidden treasures fall toward your hero.
