## About

Color Loom Puzzle is a vibrant and engaging puzzle game where colorful threads descend through multiple columns, waiting to be completed. Match each thread with the correct spool of the same color to fill it and clear objectives. With limited spool slots available, every move matters. Plan ahead, manage your space wisely, and complete all required threads to overcome challenging levels. Test your strategy, organization, and puzzle-solving skills as you weave your way through increasingly complex challenges.
