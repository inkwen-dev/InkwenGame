## About

Balls Catch Game is a fun and addictive casual arcade game that challenges your reflexes and timing. Colorful balls fall from the top of the screen, and your goal is to catch them using the correct bucket. Stay focused, move quickly, and avoid missing the falling balls as the speed increases with each level. Earn points, complete exciting challenges, and improve your high score in this simple yet entertaining game. With colorful graphics and easy-to-learn gameplay, Balls Catch Game is perfect for players of all ages.
