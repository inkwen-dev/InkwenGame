## About

Harvest Cut Master game is a fun puzzle game consisting of 55 Levels in total. In each level you have to harvest the crops in the field. To be able to harvest different crops, you need to upgrade your vehicle. You can also achieve this by purchasing new agricultural vehicles. Do not forget to visit the "UPGRADE" section with the money you earn after the harvest.
