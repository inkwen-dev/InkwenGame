## About

Welcome to a world of juicy fruits! In this game, tiles with fruit images fall from top to bottom in multiple rows. Your task is to catch them before they disappear and organize them into special slots at the bottom of the screen. Collect three identical fruits to clear the board and score points. It is a great game to train your reaction and logic, suitable for players of all ages.
