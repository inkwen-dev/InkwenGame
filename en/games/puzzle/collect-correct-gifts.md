## About

Christmas is on the doorstep and that is why we need to prepare gifts for our loved ones. In this game you have to collect gifts in right boxes. Yellow gifts in yellow boxes and red gifts in red boxes. Click on the gifts to change the direction and to go to the right box. You can do three errors after the game is over.
