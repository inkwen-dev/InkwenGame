## About

Rabbit Carrot is a cute pixel puzzle game where you help a little rabbit collect carrots and reach the goal. Tap to switch ice walls on or off, create a safe path, and avoid spikes, moving enemies, and tricky obstacles. Each level becomes more challenging, testing your logic, timing, and reflexes. With simple controls, charming pixel graphics, and addictive puzzle gameplay, Rabbit Carrot offers a fun brain-training adventure!
