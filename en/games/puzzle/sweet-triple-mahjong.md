## How to play

Tap tiles that are free on the left or right and match three identical tiles to remove them from the board Plan your moves carefully to avoid getting stuck and clear all tiles to finish the level and move to the next one

## About

Sweet Triple Mahjong is a relaxing yet challenging tile-matching puzzle game with a delicious candy theme. Match three identical tiles that are free on the left or right to collect them and clear the board. Each level introduces new tile layouts that test your observation and strategy skills. Easy to learn but hard to master, Sweet Triple Mahjong offers smooth gameplay, colorful visuals, and satisfying progression across multiple levels. Perfect for quick play sessions or long puzzle-solving fun.
