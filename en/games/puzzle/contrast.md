## About

Contrast is a clean and engaging puzzle game that challenges your observation skills across 20 thoughtfully designed levels. In each level, you are presented with 4&ndash;5 similar-looking objects. At first glance, they may appear identical&mdash;but one is different. Your goal is to carefully analyze the details and tap the odd object out before moving on. As you progress, the differences become more subtle, testing your focus, attention, and visual perception. With a minimalistic design and distraction-free gameplay, Contrast keeps your mind sharp while offering a relaxing puzzle experience.
