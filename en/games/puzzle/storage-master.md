## About

Storage Master is a very unique and popular storage game. If you are a neat person, this game is perfect for you! You can gain lots of pleasure while you are organizing the messy things into a well-ordered look. In this game, you need to put things to their right places. If you find it is a little bit difficult for you to find the right places, just click the hint to know the answer. Have fun!
