## About

Sprunki Satellite Catcher: Soar Through Space And Catch Satellites. Fun and free to play online game designed especially for kids who enjoy simple and exciting space-themed adventures. This colorful game puts players in control of a cheerful Sprunki character as it floats through outer space collecting satellites. Your goal is to grab as many satellites as you can without touching the top or bottom edges of the screen. With easy to learn controls and endless replay value, this game is perfect for casual gamers and young players looking for a playful arcade experience.
