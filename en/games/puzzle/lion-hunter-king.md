## How to play

Please use right mouse button to shot and left mouse button for zoom and arrow buttons for move the hunter

## About

There are lions and tigers in this lion hunting game that is looking for prey. You are a lion hunter sniper who shoots and kills lions and tigers. You have large collection of guns including x-ray gun that will scan the body of lion or tiger. You can hit the head, heart or in lungs in this lion hunting sniper game. If your first fire is lost, the lion will approach you and will kill you. So you have to manage the time also.
