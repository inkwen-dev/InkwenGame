## About

Collect the delicious food in this html5 game. Press any food to start. Now move the 'mouse or finger tip' on identical adjacent food (horizontally, vertically or diagonally). Select at least 3 food. Release mouse button to make the match. Each 6th food will give a bonus. More than 7 food will give a time bonus. Collect the request food within the giving time, or you will lose the game.
