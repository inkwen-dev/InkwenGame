## About

Bubble Touch is a game where bubbles come from the bottom up and you have to shoot them before they cross the finish line. The bottom box gives you one point, the middle box gives you two points and the upper box gives you three points. Some bubbles are faster than others, and sometimes they come more at once. Be careful and earn more points while playing this game.
