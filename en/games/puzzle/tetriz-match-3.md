## How to play

Use buttons with mouse or touch or keyboard Arrow keys to play this game

## About

Shuffle and shift the falling group of candies so that they can form a horizontal or a vertical or a diagonal line of similar candies anywhere in the board. Use left, right keys for shifting and up key for shuffling them. Don’t let the board filled till upper row.
