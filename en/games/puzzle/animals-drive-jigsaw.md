## About

Animals Drive Jigsaw is a jigsaw puzzle games with cute animals. You can play with twelve images of animals jigsaw. Start with first image solve it and unlock next one. Try to solve all 12 images. When you choose which mode you wanna play the image will shuffle in 25,49 or 100 pieces. Drag the pieces in right position to get the image with Animals who drive.
