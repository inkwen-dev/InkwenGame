## About

Welcome to the game "Among As Slide"! Here you will find a collection of slide-puzzles with images of the funniest characters "Among Us". These guys regularly fight for survival and power on the ship. And in space conditions, this is quite difficult. Especially when you consider that you can only be in the role of a peaceful crew member or a traitor. Crew members need to perform all sorts of tasks, prevent disasters on the ship and look for traitors. But the imposters will commit outrages and sabotage the ship, as well as kill the crew.
