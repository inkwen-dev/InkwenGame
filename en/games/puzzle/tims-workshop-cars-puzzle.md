## About

Tim's Workshop it's cars game for toddlers. By dragging and dropping car parts (body, doors, wheels, headlights, etc.) kids assemble a cool cars. There is totally 17 cars here such as an ambulance car, a fire engine, concrete truck mixer, an excavator and a bulldozer, passenger and sports cars, minivans, etc. Kids can ride each car they made - there is a mini testdrive for every solved puzzle with cute passengers and drivers. Guess who will drive the new assembled car - little kitten, elephant, hedgehog or maybe lion cub?
