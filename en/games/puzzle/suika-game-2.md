## About

Suika Game 2 is an engaging and meditative puzzle game where your goal is to merge as many identical fruits as possible to create bigger ones. Starting with small cherries, you progress toward the giant watermelon! Choose the perfect moment to drop each fruit to prevent the game field from overflowing above the Danger Limit. Test your strategy and reaction speed as you compete for the highest score. This is the classic fruit merge mechanic powered by a dynamic physics engine, fully adapted for mobile devices and PC. Challenge yourself and beat your own highscore!
