## About

This time in the game Sprunki Incredibox Memory you will need to improve your memory. Cards with the image of Sprunki will appear on the playing field. Each character has a double. Remember the location of the heroes. After a few seconds, the cards will turn towards you with the side that will be the same for everyone. You must click on the cards to find paired Sprunks. Please note that you have a limited number of steps, you will find their limit at the top in Sprunki Incredibox Memory.
