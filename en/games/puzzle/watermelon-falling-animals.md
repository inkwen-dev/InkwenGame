## How to play

Tap the screen to drop animals into the container. Try to make identical animals land on top of each other - when they match, they merge into a new, larger animal and give you points.

Plan each drop carefully so the animals combine efficiently and don’t pile up too high. If the container becomes overfilled, the game will end.

Use powerful bonuses to avoid losing, create bigger merges, and score even more points.
