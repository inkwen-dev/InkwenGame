## About

Summer Match Party is a classic and free online leisure game for boys. The game is highly interactive. You need to pay attention to the patterns on the sign, choose the blocks that are the same as those on the sign, and then quickly occupy them and push other competitors away. Each level is set for 3 rounds, only by surviving completely can you win and get more rewards. Have fun!
