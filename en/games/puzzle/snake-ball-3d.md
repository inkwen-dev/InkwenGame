## About

At Snake Ball 3D its time to test your reflex, as you collect hundreds of balls that will give you extra life and length to easily navigate through an environment full of blocks and obstacles. Are you ready for a unique adventure? In this snake game, you have to collect extra ball to add to your length and either dodge obstacles or face them head on! But remember that when you decide to hit the obstacles, you will lose a number of your balls equal to the number written on that obstacle. So, you always have to try to collect extra balls (which are somehow your extra lives! ) and if necessary, hit the obstacle with the lowest number.
