## How to play

Removes all octopuses of the color with which you swap them

## About

If you suddenly get stuck at one of the stages, boosters will come to your aid. For example "rainbow" - removes all octopuses of the color with which you swap them. And "Bomb" - will remove all the octopuses that are next to it.
