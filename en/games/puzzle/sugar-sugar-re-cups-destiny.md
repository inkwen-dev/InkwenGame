## About

Attract with your finger on the screen to route enough sugar in all the cups in each level. New puzzle components are introduced as you progress: color filters, gravitation switches, teleports, etc. The practical physics lead to hundreds of sugar fragments dripping down the screen. This is the official 'sugar, sugar' game, a physics puzzle game for you! In almost every update, new levels are included! 12 degrees and also counting! In the extra sandbox mode, you can attract anything you desire! Sugar, Sugar is that pleasant puzzle game you know from the internet browser yet totally redesigned for mobile with new graphics and lots of new degrees.
