## About

Welcome to Set The BOX, a fun and addictive physics-based puzzle game where you challenge your stacking skills and precision. The goal is simple: stack the boxes as high as you can without toppling them over. But beware, the higher you stack, the more unstable it gets! Features: Challenging Gameplay: Each level requires careful planning and precise control to stack the boxes perfectly. The gameplay is easy to pick up but hard to master. Intuitive Controls: Simply tap and hold to drop the boxes. The longer you hold, the more accurately you can position the boxes.
