## About

Bounce Ball Jump is a puzzle game with Ball. Control the ball that moves and jumps on the stairs. There are spikes and other obstacles on the stairs. You need to avoid them. But there are also items that you need to collect that will help you in the game further. You can assemble a shield, which will protect you for a while from the spikes. There is also a stick that will push all the obstacles in front of you.
