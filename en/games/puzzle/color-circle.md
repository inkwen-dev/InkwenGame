## About

Color Circle is a fun skill game that tests your reflexes and concentration. In the game, you control a multi-colored rotating circle and watch the clock hand spin continuously. Your task is to click at the right moment when the hand points to the matching color. Just a slight delay or a wrong color, and youll have to start over. With increasing speed and constantly changing colors, Color Circle offers a simple yet highly addictive experience!
