## How to play

Blast the Bubbles Away! Step into the sleek dark arena as a bubble-popping master and aim your shooter using dual next-bubble previews (16 shots left) to match 3+ colors—reds, yellows, blues—for explosive chain reactions that clear encroaching walls, hit trophy score goals like 1100, collect gems and stars (1/3), and activate boosters to conquer addictive high-stakes levels!

Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Drag to aim (follow the trajectory line), click or release to shoot.

Mobile Controls:
Touch: Drag to aim, tap or release to shoot.

## About

Bubble Shooter Mega is an exhilarating bubble-popping arcade puzzle game featuring vibrant clusters of red, yellow, and blue bubbles in a sleek dark interface, where players aim and shoot from dual next-bubble previews (16 shots remaining) to match 3+ colors for explosive chain reactions, clearing encroaching walls to meet trophy score goals (1100), collect gems and stars (1/3), and unleash powerful boosters in addictive, high-stakes levels.
