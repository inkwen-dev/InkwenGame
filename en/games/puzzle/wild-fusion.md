## About

Wild Fusion is a relaxing and addictive idle merge game where you combine animals to discover new and powerful creatures. Merge two matching animals to unlock higher-level species, expand your collection, and fill the grid with colorful wildlife. Buy more animals from the shop to speed up your progress and create new combinations. Enjoy simple gameplay, vibrant graphics, and satisfying merge mechanics as your wild world grows!
