## About

Save My Pet is a fun and addictive puzzle game where you must protect your adorable pet from a swarm of angry bees! Using your creativity, draw a protective barrier around your pet to keep it safe from danger. But be careful, your lines must hold for a few seconds while the bees try to break through! With increasing difficulty, unique challenges, this game will test your problem-solving skills and quick thinking. Can you keep your pet safe from harm? Play Save My Pet now and find out!
