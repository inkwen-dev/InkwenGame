## About

Put your reflexes to the test in Dot Dodge, an exciting arcade game that challenges your timing, precision, and focus. Control a rotating shield and defend the center from incoming dots. Rotate at the perfect moment to block the approaching dots and keep your score climbing. The gameplay is simple to learn but difficult to master. As you progress, the speed increases and the challenge becomes more intense, requiring quick reactions and sharp concentration. Stay alert, dodge danger, and see how long you can survive in this addictive endless challenge!
