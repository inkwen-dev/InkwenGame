## How to play

Drag left or right to aim, then release to drop a celestial body into the jar. Two identical bodies that touch merge into the next, bigger tier and give points. Chain merges for combos. Don't let the stack stay above the red danger line for 2 seconds. Use Shake, Pop and Swap boosters when the jar gets crowded.

## About

Drop, merge, and build a universe! In Cosmo Merge you drop adorable celestial bodies into a glass jar. When two identical ones touch, they merge into something bigger: stardust becomes a comet, a moon, planets, a blazing Sun and finally a Galaxy. Chain merges for combos, use boosters when the jar fills up, and play endless mode or star-rated missions. Unlock 8 skins and complete your cosmic album!
