## About

Shark Boy is a colorful underwater puzzle game where you will accompany the boy and his friendly whale on a quest to find precious golden shells. Skillfully utilize anchors to pull, swing, or create momentum to move the two main characters precisely to each position. Each level presents a new challenge with unique terrain, different obstacle arrangements, and numerous dangers lurking in the deep water. Just one miscalculation can cause you to fall into a trap or collide with dangerous creatures. Observe carefully, plan your path logically, and collect all the golden shells to complete the mission.
