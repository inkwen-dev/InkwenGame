## About

Bubble Shots is a classic arcade skill game where the objective is to shoot and make disappear as many bubbles as possible. Aim and shoot bubbles, and create groups of more than 3 of the same color to burst them! Use amazing power-ups such as special bubbles and bombs to clear the stage faster. And remember: time is running!
