## How to play

To complete the level, you need to move the ball using swaps across the screen (when playing from a computer, you can use the arrows on the keyboard), coloring the entire maze.
Make sure the puzzle is completely filled in! If you don't fill in at least one square, you'll be stuck in the color maze forever!

## About

The game will appeal to fans of puzzles. There are 100 unique levels in the game. The difficulty of the levels gradually increases. If the level is too difficult for you, you can skip it by watching a video advertisement. For each level you complete, you are given crystals that can be spent on unlocking new items. Collect the entire collection!
You will definitely like:
- Smooth ball movement
- Lots of beautiful and vibrant paint colors
- Simple one touch control
- Great way to kill time
