## How to play

Hold anywhere (or press SPACE) to run the engine; let go and the rocket falls, hold again to catch it. Slide your finger (or use the arrow keys) to steer into coins, rings, balloons and fuel cans. When the ring turns green at a stage separation, tap again for a PERFECT boost. Upgrade in the shop, merge rockets in the hangar and fly higher every time.

## About

Hold the screen and your little rocket roars off the launch pad! It has real weight: let go and it slows, stops and falls, hold again to catch it and climb. Catch coins on the way up and watch them add up into one big number, fly through rings, pop balloons and grab fuel cans. Catch ten coins in a row for AFTERBURN and triple coins. Nail PERFECT stage separations and break the sound barrier past the clouds, the aurora and the space station, then drift back down under your parachute. Spend your coins on a stronger engine, a bigger fuel tank, longer fins, more valuable coins and a tractor beam. Get a second rocket and MERGE them: the rocket blooms into a brand-new, stronger one, with 12 rockets to discover. Your goal: SPACE at 100,000 m. Then keep climbing all the way to the Moon!
