## About

Christmas Cars Find the Bells is a beautiful online game with hidden bells. Find out the hidden bells in the specified images. Each level has 10 bells. There are 6 levels in total. The time is limited so be fast and find all hidden bells before time runs out. Clicking in the wrong place several times reduces the time by an additional 5 seconds. So, if you are ready start the game and have fun!
