## About

FIFA World Cup Jigsaw Puzzle is a game where you are presented with a total of twelve different, but always interesting pictures with famous football players from the World Cup, each of which has four sets of fragments. The choice is free. You can choose any picture and any set of fragments according to your level and experience in assembling puzzles. In addition, during the construction you can add or disable the rotation option and the background display. Enjoy the game and get ready.
