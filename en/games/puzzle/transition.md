## About

Find the right color of being and help him to find his purpose in life. Little creature is sad, because he does not know the meaning of his life. But one day, the magic come. You must help him to get to the platform in the same color. But you must pay attention to the time and the black platforms, because the game will end. ----- The game was created on Bratislava Game Jam 2016 and game won in category Attendee Choice. The theme of this event was the transition same as name of game.
