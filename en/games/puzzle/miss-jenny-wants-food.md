Use mouse to play this game

## About

Miss Jenny Wants Food and she is always hungry. Help her to eat all food, but avoid other subjects. She will eat all, so it will be easy for you to play. You need to react fast and to catch all food. When you drop eight pieces of food the game will over, or if you want to eat something that is not food the game will over too.
