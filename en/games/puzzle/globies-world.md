## About

It is a strategic and enjoyable game where you can train your brain. In your interplanetary route, you have to capture both neutral planets and enemy planets with creatures living on your planet. If you don't use the right strategy, hostile planet creatures will take over your planet and you will lose the game! You will not realize how the time passes while playing this game where the excitement will be at its peak. Have fun.
