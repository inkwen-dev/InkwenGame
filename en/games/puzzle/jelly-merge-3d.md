## About

Jelly merge 3D is a colorful and addictive puzzle game! It is easy to learn, but difficult to master! Your task is to move the cubes, connecting them by color and bypassing obstacles, until there is only one cube of each color left on the field. The game gets harder with each level, more different colors and cubes appear. There are 70+ unique levels in the game!
