## About

Do you want to be a champion? Try this game Bridge Race that combines collecting and racing! You need to collect your colored blocks to build the stairs to the next floor, not only to collect them quickly but also to prevent your opponents from encroach on your stairs. You can't win until you get to the last level. Come and compete together!
