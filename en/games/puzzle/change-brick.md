## About

Change Brick is an engaging puzzle game where you need to move and arrange colorful blocks to their designated positions. Each level presents a strategic challenge, requiring you to calculate the best path, avoid getting stuck, and utilize space intelligently. The difficulty increases progressively with many new and unique layouts, offering a thrilling sense of accomplishment. Change Brick is suitable for training your logic skills and providing lighthearted entertainment every day.
