## About

"Match Objects 2D" is a simple but addicting puzzle game. You need to drag paired items into the hatch, but don't forget to keep an eye on the timer! You must meet the allotted time to move to the next level. The difficulty increases with each level. You can also use the hint if you find it difficult to find a pair of objects.
