## About

Untangled 3d is a very interesting 3D game to untie the rope. In this game, players can see all kinds of objects tied with the rope, such as animals, dolls, hearts of peaches, phones, etc., which are hoisted after being tied. You need to untie the rope according to the binding direction of the rope, reverse the touch screen, and follow the route of the rope. Have fun!
