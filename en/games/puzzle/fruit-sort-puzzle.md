## About

Fruit Sort Puzzle is a casual game that is extremely easy to pick up and is also a free online matching game. The game incorporates hawthorn and fruit, with various fruits of the same color falling on top of the fork to form a hawthorn, if the fork doesn't fit the game is over, try to see how many hawthorns you can get.
