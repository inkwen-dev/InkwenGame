Underwater Blast is a colorful and addictive match3 puzzle game set in a lively underwater world. With each level, new obstacles like seaweed traps, drifting bubbles, and hidden treasures keep the gameplay fresh and strategic. Powerups and special boosters help you unleash massive chain reactions, rewarding you with dazzling animations and high scores. Dive in and experience a fun, relaxing, yet highly engaging puzzle adventure beneath the waves!
