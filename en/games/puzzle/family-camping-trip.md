## About

Family Camping Trip is a fun and creative 2D cartoon puzzle game for kids and girls. You have to help the Millers find and match items for their trip. You can also design new outfits for them as you like. You will earn badges when you complete any missions. You also have to wash the car with the tools and the hints. There are more exciting links for you to unlock and play for the Millers. You will love this game and its mini puzzles!
