Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Stop the candy troops to come forward by shooting a similar candy towards them. There are 2 modes. In beginner mode a group of two candies can be eliminated while in expert mode you need at least 3 candies. Collect all the stars to win this game. Fall down of a candy will take one out of four lives.
