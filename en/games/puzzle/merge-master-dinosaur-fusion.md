## About

How can you win a natural battle if you have a dinosaur army? In this 3D puzzle arcade game, you have a chance to decorate the formation for dinosaurs before each battle. Both you and your AI opponent have the same type of dinosaurs, but it could only attack you with limited formation. Try your best to beat him with the fewest costs!
