## About

"Paint Island" is a hypercasual game focused on painting capsules by passing over them with a paintbrush. In the game, players are asked to paint all of the capsules. The objective of the game is to paint all of the capsules. This game can be played by all age groups and can be played on web platforms. The game aims to create a lasting impact on players with attractive graphics and fun mechanics.
