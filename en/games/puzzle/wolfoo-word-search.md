## How to play

Swipe or click-drag across letters in any direction mdash horizontal vertical or diagonal mdash to select a hidden word Match all words in the list to clear the level Tap the Hint button if you need help

## About

Wolfoo Word Search is a friendly word-hunting puzzle for kids! Join Wolfoo on a treasure-map adventure across 30 levels and find every hidden word in the letter grid. Each level shows a picture word list &mdash; spot words like CAT, SOFA and LAMP by swiping across letters in any direction. A gentle hint helps when youre stuck, and finding a word plays its sound to build early reading and vocabulary skills. With bright cartoon visuals, simple swipe controls, and levels that grow with the player, its perfect for preschoolers and early readers practicing spelling and letter recognition. Can you clear all 30 levels and find every word?
