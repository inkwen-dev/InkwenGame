## How to play

Move the rail blocks left, right, up, or down.
Arrange the pieces to create a continuous railway track.
Make sure all rail sections connect correctly.
Complete the track from the starting point to the destination.
Once the track is ready, the train will automatically cross it.
Successfully guide the train to complete the level.

## About

Build the perfect railway and guide the train safely to its destination! Move and arrange the rail blocks in the correct direction to create a complete track. Think ahead, solve each puzzle, and make sure every rail piece connects properly. Once the track is complete, the train will automatically travel across it and complete the level.

With simple controls and increasingly challenging puzzles, Crossy Rails is a fun and relaxing logic game that tests your planning and problem-solving skills.
