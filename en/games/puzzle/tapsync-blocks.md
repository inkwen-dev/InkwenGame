## About

Get ready to test your timing and reflexes in TapSync Blocks! In this vibrant arcade challenge, your goal is to tap at just the right moment to match the moving color with the central block. Each correct match earns you points, but a single wrong move costs you a life. With only 3 lives per round, precision is key! As the game speeds up, can you keep your cool and maintain perfect sync?
