## About

Get on a fun puzzle-solving adventure to save the sushi! The Sushi Brothers are undertaking the ancient rite of passage that all little Sushi must complete. Before they can safely land on the plates of happy customers at the hibachi, they must practice landing safely under the most challenging circumstances. Are you ready to help them navigate through obstacles and ensure their safe arrival? Put your puzzle-solving skills to the test and guide the sushi to its destination in Sushi Bros!
