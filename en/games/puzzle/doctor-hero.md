## About

Doctor Hero is a surgery game where you start as a medical intern at a hospital, and rank up to become a doctor or surgeon. Diagnose your patients, examine their limbs, pinpoint their afflictions and diseases, operate on them and eventually be the most successful surgeon in the world! Are you ready to cure the world, Doctor Hero?
