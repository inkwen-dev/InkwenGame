## About

Space Rocket is game where you need to go from one planet to another planet. After finding analyzes of alien life on other planets the team began to be chased by a strange black bubble, its purpose is to escape passing from one planet to another not to lose the collected samples. Tap on screen to play. When you are in the same direction as the other planet, tap the screen to switch to the other planet.
