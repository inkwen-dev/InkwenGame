## About

Assemble a wide variety of brick buildings and scenes in this classic constructor game. Start as a baby builder and level your way up to increasingly complex construction sets. This relaxing and satisfying game is sure to bring you back to the imagination and fun of your childhood! Find the right piece and simply tap to add it to your build. Get hands-on fun without the clutter or accidentally stepping on toys.
