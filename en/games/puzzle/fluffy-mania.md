## About

Fluffy Mania is a casual arcade game created by Bekho Team. In this game, your objective is to form chains on fluffy animals on the screen. The longer you can maintain the chain, the more points you will earn. Putting a cute twist on the traditional matching genre, Fluffy Mania is the best game to relax and kill time with.
