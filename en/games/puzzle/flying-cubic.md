## About

Flying Cubic is a puzzle game and the object of this game is to collect the little rings with the help of a cube that moves up and down the screen. Tap the screen to control the direction of movement of the cube. Avoid the rotating stars, if you touch them the game will end. There are also spikes on the top and bottom of the game that you should also not touch.
