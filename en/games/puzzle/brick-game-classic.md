## About

Brick Game Classic is a classic Tetris online game. If you are a fan of classic games, then this game that has been popular for many years is just right for you. When any row or column is filled, it is eliminated. You can change the shape of the cubes before they fall to eliminate them quickly. You can also make them fall faster if you are satisfied with their shape. Now join the world of fun games.
