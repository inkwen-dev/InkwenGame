## About

Control and stack different monster trucks at this matching truck game. The goal of this game is to stack the same type of monster trucks in a row, horizontally or vertically to destroy them and to earn points. Try not to touch the top of the game screen with trucks, otherwise, the game will be over. Match three or more same monster trucks to play this game. It is better for you to match them vertically.
