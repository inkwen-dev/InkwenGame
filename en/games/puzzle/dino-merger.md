## About

Dino-Merger is an addictive puzzle strategy game where players combine various dinosaur species to create powerful new hybrids. Navigate vibrant prehistoric landscapes, solve challenging merging puzzles, and unlock unique dinos with special abilities. Strategically merge to evolve your dinosaur collection, defend your territory from rivals, and embark on exciting quests. With stunning graphics, intuitive gameplay, and endless combinations, Dino-Merger offers endless fun for dinosaur enthusiasts and puzzle lovers alike.
