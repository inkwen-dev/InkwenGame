## About

trezeBlocks 2 is a tetris-style fun matching game. You need need to make perfect rows and columns of blocks, dragging the white blocks (pieces) to the desired places. Coins can be colected and used to replace pieces! The game is over when there are no more spaces on the grid and there are not enough coins to replace them.
