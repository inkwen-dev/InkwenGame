## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

You should help kara to climb the mountain and reach its peak. But there are many places that you can fall down, so you should be very careful. All you have to do is to jump on the stones (platforms) and avoid falling by jumping over holes. You have normal and double jump and you should use them wisely to progress. There is also a time limit and you will lose the game if you run out of time.
