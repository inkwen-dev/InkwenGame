## About

Winter is a beautiful time of year and so are the jigsaw games. You can play Jigsaw Game whenever you want. This jigsaw game is with winter pictures. You can play with 12 different pictures. You must first compile the first image to go to the next one. Choose between three modes to play the game. You can play in easy mode with 25 pieces, medium mode with 49 pieces, or hard mode with 100 pieces. Select whatever you want to play and start the game. Use mouse or your finger on the screen to drag the pieces into right position to get the image with Winter Background.
