## About

Today we present to you the puzzle game Candy Connection. In it we have to solve a rather interesting puzzle. Before us there will be a field divided into the same number of squares. In some of them there will be sweets. You need to find the same objects and build them in a line horizontally, vertically or obliquely at least three items. Then they will disappear from the screen, and you will be given points.
