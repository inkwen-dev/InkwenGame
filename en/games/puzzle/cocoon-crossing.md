## About

Cocoon Crossing 3D is a cozy 3D routing puzzle. Every caterpillar wants to reach the cocoon of its own colour &mdash; drag its head or tail and it crawls, tile by tile, across a sunlit garden board. The twist is over-and-under: two caterpillars cant share a tile on the same level, but at a crossing one climbs over a bridge while the other slips under a tunnel. Untangle the paths, work around rocks, mud, thorns, ice and one-way gates, and bring every worm home to hatch into a butterfly. 140 handcrafted levels, an endless Zen mode, a daily puzzle, and collectible butterflies.
