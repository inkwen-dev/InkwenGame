## About

Toy block is a simple and challenging square game. Match the same color cubes and destroy the box to qualify. There is no time limit. You are only limited by the number of moves. Go ahead and hit these cartoon cubes! All you need to do is take the matching cubes and let the new cube fall off the top. Match or more adjacent cubes to complete missions, reach high scores, and clear target.
