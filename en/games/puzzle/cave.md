## About

Cave is puzzle game where you need to navigate cave man to collect flying ghosts. Controlling the player is difficult, and the best option is to be slow as you move through the cave. Be careful not to hit the walls of the cave, because that way you lose part of your life. Tap on screen if you play on your mobile device, or use space bar, A i D keys if you play this game in your PC.
