## About

New Year Puzzles is a cozy jigsaw game with festive winter artwork. Choose a picture, select the difficulty, and assemble the image piece by piece. Drag fragments onto the board, rotate them when needed, and connect the details until the scene is complete. Use the hint option if you get stuck, or challenge yourself to finish faster with fewer moves. Simple controls, relaxing gameplay, and holiday phere make it perfect for quick breaks for both kids and adults.
