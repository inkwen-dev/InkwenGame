## About

Bouncy Woods is a puzzle game in which you must destroy all the bricks and recruit as many ducks as possible in the progress. There are a total of 3 unique worlds and 40 levels! Make sure to practice your aiming because the levels get progressively harder! How far can you advance in this responsive & bouncy adventure?
