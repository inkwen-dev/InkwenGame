## About

Pong The Ball is a puzzle hypercasual game. Your job is to pong the balls with same color. From the top and bottom of the game will be balls with different colors. Between them will be a ball who will go up and down. So you need to match that ball with ball from the line of the top or bottom that is same color as vertical ball. The color of ball who move vertical will change randomly so you need to be focused and to change the other balls quickly.
