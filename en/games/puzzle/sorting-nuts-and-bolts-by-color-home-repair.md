## How to play

Select a Nut: Click on the bolt you want to take the nut from.

Move it: Click on another bolt to move the selected nut there.

Sort by Color: Match nuts of the same color on one bolt. Make sure there's enough room on the bolt!

Use Tools: Use bonuses if you need to add a bolt, cancel a move, or restart the level.

The goal is to clear the board by sorting all the nuts by color.
