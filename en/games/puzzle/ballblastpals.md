## About

BallBlastPals is a bright and exciting bubble-shooter adventure where fun meets fast thinking! Plan your shots carefully, create satisfying chain reactions, and clear the board to win each level. With smooth controls, cheerful visuals, and rewarding gameplay, BallBlastPals is perfect for quick play sessions and endless fun.
