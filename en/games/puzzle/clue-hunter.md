## About

Clue Hunter is an interesting crime-solving puzzle game. If you also like detective games, and logic to solve puzzles, you absolutely can not miss it! There are five levels in this game, Help Mom, Save the Girl, Laundry Master, Loss Escape, and Strange Neighbors. As simple as it may seem, passing the levels is difficult! Dig deep into the details and use your analytical skills to find hidden objects and clues. One after another difficult cases are waiting for you to solve! Wish you success!
