## How to play

Place It Right! Organize every object into its perfect spot in this relaxing puzzle game. Arrange frames, tools, kitchen items, books, keys, buttons, pencils, and many other everyday objects until everything looks neat and satisfying. Observe each level carefully, find the hidden pattern, and place every item in the right position. Some puzzles are based on color, size, shape, symmetry, or object category.

Desktop Controls:
Mouse Drag: Pick up and move an object.
Mouse Release: Drop the object into place.
Left Click: Select or interact with an object.

Mobile Controls:
Tap and Drag: Pick up and move an object.
Release Finger: Drop the object into place.

## About

Place It Right is a cozy organization puzzle game about putting everyday objects in their perfect spots. Sort, arrange, and align items such as frames, tools, kitchen objects, books, and keys until every scene feels neat and satisfying. Use simple drag-and-drop controls, observe the pattern, and enjoy the calm feeling of making everything fit just right.
