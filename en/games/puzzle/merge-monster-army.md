## About

Merge Monster Army is a composite game for boys. Arrange troops on the map to gain an advantage and gather the strongest army of the most dangerous monsters! You can improve your monsters by connecting two of the same and getting a new one that will be stronger and more powerful. Keep in mind, as soon as your squad comes out against the enemy, you can not do anything. Come and join the fight!
