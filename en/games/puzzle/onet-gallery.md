## About

"Onet Gallery" is an incredibly addicting game in which you can have a great free time and get a lot of positive impressions by collecting figures from colorful cubes! Despite the rather simple game mechanics, which reminds everyone of the well-known "Mahjong", the game will improve your attentiveness and concentration. Thanks to high-quality 3D graphics and pleasant soundtrack, you can relax and get rid of all the accumulated stress.
