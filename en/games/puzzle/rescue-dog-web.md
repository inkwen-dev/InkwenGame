## About

Rescue Dog Web is an online puzzle game designed for kids for free. The dog is trapped in a maze. If you drag it and release your hand, it will bounce in the opposite direction. You need to make the dog bounce at the walls and use the reaction force to let it bounce out of the door. In each level, the number of hits against the wall.
