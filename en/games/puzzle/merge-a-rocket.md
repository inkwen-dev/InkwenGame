## How to play

Walk up to a rocket to pick it up.
Carry it to another rocket of the same level to merge them into a higher-level rocket.
Press Launch to fire your rocket at the enemy base, or Drop to put it down.
Collect in-game coins and open Upgrades to get better rockets and carry more of them. Activate the Shield when the enemy attacks.
Use Rebirth to reset your progress for an in-game bonus. You can also watch an optional ad to boost your rocket's damage or double your in-game coins.

Controls:

PC: WASD or arrow keys to move, mouse to look around, Space to jump, Shift to sprint.
Mobile: on-screen joystick to move, swipe the screen to look around, Jump button to jump.
Use the on-screen buttons for Launch, Drop, Shield, Upgrades, Shop, Rebirth and the sound toggle.

## About

Merge rockets and defend your city! Pick up rockets, combine two of the same level to make a stronger one, then launch them at the enemy base before it destroys your town. Spend in-game coins on upgrades, raise your shield for protection, and use Rebirth to start over with bigger in-game bonuses.
