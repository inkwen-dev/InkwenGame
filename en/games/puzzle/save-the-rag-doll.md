## About

Control the ragdoll and save this doll from bombs, blades, spikes that are on the both sides of the game area and all other obstacles. Click on the shield and move the shield with the ragdoll. The shield will protect the doll from everything except from the bombs.So avoid bombs and other obstacles and try to keep the doll safe.
