## About

Turquoise Blocks is a game in wich you must drag the pieces to the grid to fill rows or columns to score. You’ll always have three pieces to play. You won’t lose if you run out of space to fit the pieces, your only limitation it will the time (golden bar). You can collect and use coins to power-ups (invert and rotate grid, drag and drop fixed blocks, arrange down the grid and delete pieces).
