## About

Sokoban Master is a challenging and addictive puzzle game inspired by the classic Sokoban experience. Your mission is simple: push every crate onto its target position. But be careful&mdash;crates can only be pushed, never pulled, so every move matters! Progress through multiple difficulty levels, from beginner-friendly puzzles to more challenging stages. Earn stars by completing levels efficiently, track your moves and progress, unlock achievements, and even create your own puzzles with the built-in Level Editor.
