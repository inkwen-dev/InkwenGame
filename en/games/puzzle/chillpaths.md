## About

ChillPaths is a relaxing yet brain-teasing puzzle adventure set in a cool, icy world. Your mission is simple: slide frozen bars and clear the way for a little boat to reach its destination. But don&rsquo;t be fooled&mdash;each level brings clever layouts that challenge your logic, planning, and problem-solving skills. Navigate through frosty grids, move blocks in the right order, and unlock the perfect path. With smooth controls, calming visuals, and satisfying &ldquo;aha!&rdquo; moments, ChillPaths is designed to be easy to pick up but rewarding to master.
