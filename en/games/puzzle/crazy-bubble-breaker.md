## About

Crazy Bubble Breaker is a fun and challenging 2D bubble shooter physics puzzle game. You have to destroy all bubbles with falling balls. You can aim the bubble with a subline and release the ball. You can also bounce the ball off the walls to hit more bubbles with one ball. Can you eliminate them? Dozens of levels are waiting for you to challenge! Use your brain and try to finish all levels with the fewest balls!
