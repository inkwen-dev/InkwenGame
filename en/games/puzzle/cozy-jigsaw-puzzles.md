## How to play

Drag and drop puzzle pieces from the tray onto the board to assemble the picture. Pieces snap into place automatically when positioned correctly. On harder difficulties, pieces may need to be rotated — tap the rotation buttons to turn them 90 degrees. Use the hint button to show a semi-transparent reference image over the board. Pinch to zoom or use the zoom button to focus on a specific section of the puzzle. If you're stuck, use the magnet power-up to auto-place a piece, or watch a short video to earn a free one. Connected pieces merge into groups that move together. Complete the entire picture to win and earn coin rewards.                                                                             
                                                                                                                                                                                     
Controls: mouse or touch to drag pieces; zoom in/out with pinch gesture or the zoom button; rotate pieces with on-screen buttons.
