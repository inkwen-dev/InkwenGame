## How to play

Tap on the screen anywhere to spawn a ball drag to aim and release to shoot Bring the cursor back close to the ball to cancel the shot and tap anywhere again

## About

Break Ice is a physics based puzzle game which is calm, fun and a little challenging game (if you want to collect all the gold). Players have to break all the ice bars in a single shot and collect all the gold while they are at it. Some levels are designed to be played multiple coins to collect all the gold, so don't be upset if you can't get them all in 1 shot. Game consists of 100 levels and is very refreshing to play.
