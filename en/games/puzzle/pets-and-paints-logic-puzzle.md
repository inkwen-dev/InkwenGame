## How to play

Drag a clue block onto the board with your mouse or finger. To add an individual animal or color, select its tool and click or tap the target cell.

Combine clues to fill the board. Each animal-color pair must appear only once. Use the arrows to browse clues, undo to reverse a move, and the cross tool to clear a cell.

Try different placements until all the clues fit. If you get stuck, you can choose to watch an ad for an extra hint.
