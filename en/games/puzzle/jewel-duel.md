## About

Not even the oldest ones remember, when their beautiful country, Kartucia, was taken over by two fiery wizards by means of black magic. It is a really long time that Kartucia is immersed in the darkness and every-day suffering inflicted by the servants of the fiery wizards. Legend has it that a hero will come and restore peace in the country. Time is getting short, however! The fiery wizards are about to open the abyss of horror, bringing thousands of demons. Everything would be really lost then. Everybody believes that you are the hero. Do not disappoint them!
