## About

Santa Dream is game with happy little Santa who want help from you. You need to navigate Santa on his journey to collect presents for all kids. Avoid all obstacles on Santa's way and collect more gifts. Avoid especially monstrous eyes. If you touch them the game will end. Everything is left to you. The holidays will depend on you and your Santa.
