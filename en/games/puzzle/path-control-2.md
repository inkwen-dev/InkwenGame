## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Aim of the game is to hit the basket with standing ball. By clicking on objects with the mouse you will force them to move left or right and change their location. This way you can make the ball move in the direction you want. When the ball enters the basket, you receive points and proceed to the next level. Have fun.
