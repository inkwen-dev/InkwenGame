## About

Hangman Winter challenges you and you need to win, and to do this, guess the words, targets or letters and do not let the gallows appear. You have to do it gradually letter by letter and reach the solution. In the lower right part you have a button that can help you with an unknown letter. At the top you will see the topic in which the word is given, this will greatly facilitate your task.
