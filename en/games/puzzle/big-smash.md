## How to play

Computer - Click and hold to pull back the cannon aim by dragging and release to fire Phone or tablet - Tap and drag to pull back and aim then lift your finger to fire

## About

Aim your tennis balls at a tower of cubes on the table and knock the whole lot down. Stone, wood, ice, jelly... every cube falls differently! Objective Bring down the tower. Each level drops you in front of a stack of cubes built from different materials &mdash; stone, wood, ice, jelly, cardboard, puzzle pieces &mdash; and your job is to topple the whole thing using your cannon. You dont win by blasting everything; you win by figuring out which cube is critical to the structure and knocking it out from under the rest. Clear all 69 towers to finish the game, and keep an eye out for TNT blocks!
