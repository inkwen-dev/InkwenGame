## About

Hexa is a puzzle game, it is addictive and funny to play. The goal of this game is to connect the lines to get the shapes that is on the help image. At the beginning of the game click on the ? or help to see how the hexa puzzle will look for that level. Some levels are more harder than other, so you do not give up. Tap on the blocks and move them to change the positions.
