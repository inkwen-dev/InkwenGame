## About

The Last Light of Lyra is a cozy, story-driven cosmic puzzle adventure set in a universe on the edge of collapse. As the last spark of hope, you guide gentle mining ships across a starmap of mysterious, fading planets&mdash;transferring resources and solving intricate logic-based challenges. Beneath its dreamy visuals lies a hidden structure of clever math-inspired puzzles designed to quietly stretch your brain as you restore balance to the stars. Use addition and subtraction to solve the puzzles.
