## About

Thanksgiving Rush is a puzzle match 3 game with colorful and beautiful graphics, collect three or more of the same icons. The aim of the game is to reach a given number of points in each level to advance to the next. The more icons of the same type matched in a row (vertically or horizontally) - or in a group - the more points you receive! Time is of the essence, so hurry and play Thanksgiving Rush today!
