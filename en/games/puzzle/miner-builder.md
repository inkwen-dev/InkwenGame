# Miner Builder

<a href="https://inkwengame.com/games/miner-builder" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Play on InkwenGame</a>

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/miner-builder/cover.jpg)

You'll become a skyscraper builder! Operate construction equipment and build the tallest skyscraper in the world. Collect blocks using a bulldozer.

## How to play

⁃ Move using the on-screen stick/WASD/Arrows;
- To board a bulldozer, approach it. Wait for the gauge to fill;
- To exit the bulldozer, press the button;
- Board the bulldozer and collect cubes;
- To board a crane, approach it. Wait for the gauge to fill;
- To exit the crane, press the button;
- Board the crane, build a house from cubes, and earn coins;
- To upgrade construction equipment, go to the upgrade menu and press the button;
- Upgrade your character and unlock new types of cubes;
- To unlock a new conveyor belt, accumulate coins and purchase a conveyor belt;
- To hire assistants, accumulate coins and purchase an assistant.
