## About

Fruit Goals Match is a fun and strategic fruit-matching puzzle game where you collect specific fruits to complete each mission within a limited number of moves. Every level introduces new goals that reward smart planning and careful decisions. New missions unlock continuously, keeping the gameplay engaging and endless. Optional reward ads let you earn extra moves and see how far you can progress.
