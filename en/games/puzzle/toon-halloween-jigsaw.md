## About

Toon Halloween Jigsaw is a free online game from genre of puzzle and jigsaw games. In this game you have a total of 12 pictures. You need to start from the first one and to unlock the next image. You have three modes for each picture: Easy with 25 pieces, Medium with 49 pieces and Hard with 100 pieces. Have fun and enjoy!
