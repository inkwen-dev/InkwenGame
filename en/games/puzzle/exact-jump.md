## About

Аs the title of the game says in this game you have to be precise. You need to put the ball exact in the target. Tap on the screen to move the jump to the target. The target will change the position every time when you put the ball inside the target. Try to exact target more time as you can before game end. You can make three mistakes and after that the game will end.
