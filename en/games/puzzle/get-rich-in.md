## About

Get Rich in: is a fun and addictive online game inspired by viral YouTube memes! Drop a coin and watch it bounce its way down through obstacles. Your goal is simple&mdash;time your drop perfectly and see how many bounces it takes for the coin to land between the two squares. Sounds easy? Think again! With satisfying physics, unpredictable bounces, and exciting moments, every drop feels unique. Test your timing, improve your strategy, and chase the perfect drop to become a true coin master.
