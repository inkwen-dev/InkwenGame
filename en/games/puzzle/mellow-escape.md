## How to play

Move the blocks by dragging or swiping them across the board. Clear a path for the blue character and guide it to the exit to complete the level.

Try to solve each puzzle using fewer moves to earn more stars. Progress through the campaign, collect coins, and unlock new themes and character skins.

On desktop, use the mouse to drag the blocks. On mobile devices, simply touch and swipe.

## About

Mellow Escape is a cozy sliding puzzle game where you move blocks, clear the path, and help a curious little character reach the exit. Solve handcrafted puzzles, earn stars and coins, unlock new themes and character skins, and progress through increasingly challenging levels — all at your own pace, with no timers or pressure.
