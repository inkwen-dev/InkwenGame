## About

Brainrot Rescue is a festive puzzle game where players help Brainrot Claus retrieve his stolen gifts from mischievous snowmen. The gameplay revolves around strategically pulling pins to guide Santa safely through various obstacles, such as hot lava and icy traps, to collect presents and defeat enemies. With 30 challenging levels, players must use problem-solving skills to determine the correct sequence of pin pulls, ensuring Brainrot safe passage and the successful recovery of all gifts.
