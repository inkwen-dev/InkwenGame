## About

This game has a simple gameplay. The clock hand is rotating and all you have to do is to touch or click on the screen when it reaches the designated number or hour. Also if you click on the right time, then that number will be pronounced, so this game can be also amazing for teaching kids how to read the clock and its numbers. But don’t think that this game is just for kids.
