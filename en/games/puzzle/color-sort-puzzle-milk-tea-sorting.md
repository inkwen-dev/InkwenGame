## How to play

Tap or click a bottle to pick it up, then select another bottle to pour the colored water.

You can only pour water into a bottle if:

* The top color matches the target bottle’s top color
* Or the target bottle is empty
* And there is enough space in the bottle

Sort all the colors into separate bottles to complete the level. Plan your moves carefully and use logic to solve increasingly challenging puzzles!
