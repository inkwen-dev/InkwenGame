## About

Ink Streak is a fast-paced arcade game where timing and reflexes are the keys to victory. Tap the screen to control multiple characters as they run and leave colorful trails behind. Time every move carefully to prevent collisions while speed and character count increase. Stay focused, master the rhythm, and keep everyone moving safely in this colorful and addictive reflex challenge!
