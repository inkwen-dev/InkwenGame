Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

It is a classical bubble shooter game with 36 challenging levels. In each level you will find a different challenge. To complete a level you must eliminate all the fruits from the screen. The fruits will be eliminated when the fruit shot by you combines with a group of 3 or more than 3 fruits of same type. You must complete a level within allowed time. You will get better score if you complete the level early.
