## About

In Goods Sorting Shopping Master, you organize messy goods by moving and matching them into neat groups. It&rsquo;s a fun puzzle game that combines smart thinking with relaxing gameplay. In the game, you&rsquo;ll face shelves filled with mixed goods such as drinks, snacks, and daily items. Match and sort three identical items to clear the space and proceed, based on the customers needs. Manage space carefully to keep sorting.
