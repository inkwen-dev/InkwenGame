## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

During the clashes with their opponents, many transformers received serious damage. Now you in the game Robotex will need to help them. You will play for the mechanic, who will have serious work. In front of you, robots will appear on the screen. They have a lot of breakdowns, which will be displayed as black and white drawings on the screen. On the right you will see a panel on which the nodes and units needed for repair will be located. You will have to take them one at a time and drag them to the playing field. There simply put in the place where this node should stand. So gradually you completely restore the robot.
