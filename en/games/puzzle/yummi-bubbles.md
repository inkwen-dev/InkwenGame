## About

Embark on a delightful journey of popping bubbles with Yummi Bubbles &ndash; a fun-filled HTML5 bubble click game that will immerse you in a world of colorful orbs and addictive gameplay. In Yummi Bubbles, your objective is simple yet captivating: pop as many bubbles as you can within the given time limit.
