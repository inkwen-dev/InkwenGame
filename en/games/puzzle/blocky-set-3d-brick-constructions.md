## How to play

Mouse to drag and drop blockys, and interacting with UI buttons
A, W, S, D moves camera
R rotates blockys

## About

A 3D emulator of real-life brick-building games, where you are presented with a complete model and must reproduce it by placing the pieces in the correct location.

Enjoy!
