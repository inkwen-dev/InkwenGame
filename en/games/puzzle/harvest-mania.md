## About

Harvesting Mania is a highly addictive Block removal games. In it, you will have a remover block which you can move to left or to right by tapping or clicking on the screen. If the remover block sits on its identical block, it will remove the whole row. Keep removing rows as they are moving fast to the end point. If the block touches the upper corner of screen, the game will over.
