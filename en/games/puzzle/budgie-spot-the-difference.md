## About

Get ready for a fun bird puzzle adventure with Budgie Spot the Difference! Look carefully at two colorful budgie pictures and try to find everything that has changed. Some differences are easy to see, while others are cleverly hidden! Test your observation skills, find every difference, and complete each level. With cute budgies, colorful scenes, and relaxing gameplay, this is a fun game for kids and bird lovers of all ages.
