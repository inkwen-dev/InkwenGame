## About

The Disk Destroyer game is simple in essence and not at all so simple in execution. The task is to use the yellow disc to knock down all the pink discs on the field. You need to concentrate and carefully watch the rotation of the arrow around the disk that you have to throw. As soon as the arrow points to one of the targets, click on the disk and it will fly in the right direction. The difficulty lies in the fact that the arrow runs fast and it is not so easy to stop it at the right time. Try to complete the maximum levels in Disk Destroyer.
