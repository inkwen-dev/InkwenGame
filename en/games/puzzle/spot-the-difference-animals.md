Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

In the game Spot the Difference - Animals you will find a full search for differences on ten fascinating levels with animals. Do not click unnecessarily, if you click wrong three times the game will end. The difference found will be marked with a circle so that you do not return to it. The game will make you focus and become more attentive, the differences are small, barely discernible, you have to sweat to find them.
