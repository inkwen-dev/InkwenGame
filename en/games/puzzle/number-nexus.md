## About

Enter the Number Nexus, a world where logic and quick thinking collide! Numbered boxes fall from above, and it&rsquo;s your job to place them wisely. When two blocks of the same value meet, they merge into a single higher number, freeing up space and boosting your score. Every move matters: stack carefully, chain your merges, and keep the board clear while chasing ever-larger numbers. The more you combine, the higher your score climbs&mdash;and the closer you get to mastering the nexus of numbers. With endless possibilities and addictive gameplay, Number Nexus is the perfect test of strategy, foresight, and mathematical mastery. Make your mark!
