## How to play

Swap adjacent character tiles to create matches of three or more, earn XP, trigger cascades, and reach each level’s XP target before running out of moves. Winning completes the level and unlocks the next endless challenge. Use mouse click-and-drag on desktop, or touch and swipe controls on mobile and tablet.

## About
