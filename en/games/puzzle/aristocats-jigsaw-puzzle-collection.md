## About

Go to the party with the aristocratic cats! There will be everything: a lot of people, delicious treats, great music and fun. However, one evil, the journalist decided to spoil the holiday. He snuck into the party and took pictures of its visitors. As a result, the party turned into a colorless picture, disintegrated into small pieces. Help the cats to restore the brightness and integrity of their evening and continue to have fun at the party! Arrange the details of the image in their places. There is no time limit for completing this task. So you can take your time and calmly restore the image. To assemble the image, use the mouse. Click on the red button on the screen to see the next item. Then drag it to the desired location on the screen. If everything is correct, the part is fixed.
