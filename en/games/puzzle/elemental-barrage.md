## About

Elemental Barrage is a casual volley shooter. Monsters march down the board row by row &mdash; drag back, aim and release a barrage of elemental balls that bounce through them. Grab +1 pickups: when your ball tray overflows, twin balls merge into mightier elementals with burn, freeze, chain lightning and more. Every 10 rows a boss enrages; beat it to set a checkpoint. Buy the glowing upgrade, collect launchers and ball skins, and chase the Daily Barrage.
