## About

Pony Jigsaw is a kids jigsaw game with cute pony. In this game there is twelve images with little pony. But you need to solve first available image to unlock the next one. So , can you solve all 12 images? When you start with the game you can choose one of three modes to play. You can play at easy mode with 25 pieces, medium with 49 pieces or hard with 100 pieces. It is up to you to put all the pieces together in the right place to get the picture with Pony.
