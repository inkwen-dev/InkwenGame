## About

In this relaxing game that will push the boundaries of your mind, fit all the blocks in the center and create a square. Click on a single block or a group of blocks to scroll to its center. The game gets harder as you progress, but the key to solving this puzzle is to choose a group of blocks, not a single block to scroll.
