## About

Rotating Triangles is a puzzle game where you need to control the rotating triangle. The rotating triangle is made up of three triangles of different colors. At the top of the screen come slats colored like triangles. The color of the triangle should match the color of the lath to make the lath disappear. So you need to rotate the triangle to set the triangle the same color as the slat.
