## About

Dragon Merge is a casual game where you can merge and discover new dragons. Discover new and stronger dragons by merging the identical dragons. With your dragon collections, beat the monsters in the battle arena. Your goal is to get the last dragon! This is an addictive, easy-to-go, and neverending play!
