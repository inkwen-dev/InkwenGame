## About

Ninjatris is a merge numbers game. You have to merge two same numbers. The numbers are from 1 to 9. When you reach number 9, the whole row is released. The next suggested ninja number can be seen at the bottom of the screen. The game is played by tapping the screen. Enjoy and have fun!!!
