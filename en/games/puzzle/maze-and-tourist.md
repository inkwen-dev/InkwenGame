## About

Explore continent of Africa as a tourist and become familiar with its different countries and tourism attractions. During your long travel, you visit 15 different countries in Africa. Each country is considered as a level and in it, you will become familiar with that country’s name, flag, coat of arms, capital, official language and one of its most famous tourism attractions.
