## About

Ancient Calculate is an engaging math puzzle game where you calculate the total score to unlock magical doors. Each door requires an exact value, forcing you to choose and add or subtract logically to achieve the correct result. The gameplay is simple yet challenging, helping to develop logical thinking and quick reflexes. Explore ancient mysteries and conquer every door!
