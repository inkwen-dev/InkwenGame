## About

Halloween Connection is puzzle Match 3 game with a scary Halloween theme. In this game you have to connect 3 or more identical heads of scary faces. You have 30 levels to complete the task assigned to you at the bottom of the game. For a certain number of moves you need to connect a certain number of scary heads.
