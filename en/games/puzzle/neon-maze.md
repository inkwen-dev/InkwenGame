## How to play

In Neon maze, your goal is to clear the board by removing every arrow in the right order. Tap an arrow only when the path in front of it is free. A correct move removes that arrow and opens more space on the grid. Because each level gives you a limited number of moves, planning the sequence is the key to success. Use hints when needed, think a few moves ahead, and untangle the full neon maze to win.
