## About

Catch Insect is a fun skill-based puzzle game where you must use your thinking and reflexes to catch cunning insects. Each level presents a different challenge, requiring you to observe the terrain, calculate your path, and choose the precise moment to strike. Just one wrong move, and the prey can escape. With its simple yet strategic gameplay, Catch Insect offers a relaxing yet stimulating experience, suitable for all ages.
