## About

Welcome to Falling Gem, the ultimate gem-matching puzzle game that will captivate you with its vibrant visuals and engaging gameplay. In this gem-themed game, your mission is to swap and align glittering gems like diamonds, emeralds, rubies, and sapphires to create rows or columns of three or more identical gems. Every successful match causes the gems to vanish, making way for new ones to fall into place, creating opportunities for more combos and higher scores.
