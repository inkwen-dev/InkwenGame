## About

Two watermelons, two apples, two eggs; spot the pair and drag it into the hoop before the clock hits zero. Simple idea, but when the timer is ticking and the multiplier starts climbing you wont believe how fast your hands move. Match fast, stack up to x20 points and beat your own score. The more you chain, the bigger the reward. One rule: be quick. Great for a 2-minute break that turns into an hour.
