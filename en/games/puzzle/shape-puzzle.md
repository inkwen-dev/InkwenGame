## About

Shape Puzzle is a simple yet captivating puzzle game where you adjust pieces of different shapes and place them in the correct positions on the board. Each level presents a new layout, requiring you to observe carefully, rotate, and arrange each piece precisely to complete the picture. As you progress, the difficulty increases with more complex shapes, forcing you to think carefully and arrange them skillfully. With intuitive gameplay, sharp graphics, and a highly satisfying sense of completion, Shape Puzzle offers a relaxing yet challenging experience for all players.
