## How to play

Click a piece to select it
Move the mouse to position the selected piece
Left Click on the trunk grid to place it
Click an empty buffer slot to store a selected piece
Click a placed piece to pick it up again
Rotation

W / Up Arrow: Tilt forward
S / Down Arrow: Tilt backward
A / Left Arrow: Rotate left
D / Right Arrow: Rotate right

## About

Your summer vacation is finally here. The car is ready, the trunk is open, and your wife has decided that “packing light” means launching an endless parade of suitcases, beach bags, souvenirs, and suspiciously box-shaped travel essentials straight at you.

In Vacation Overload, your job is simple: fit everything into the trunk before the luggage pile turns into a total vacation disaster.

Rotate, stack, shuffle, and squeeze each piece into place across a 3D grid. Plan ahead, keep the queue under control, and prove that yes, somehow, all of this absolutely necessary vacation stuff can fit.

Can you survive the packing chaos and still make it to summer break?
