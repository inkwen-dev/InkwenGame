## How to play

Tap to send a cannon onto the conveyor The cannon fires at pixels of its matching color After a full cycle it moves into a spare slot Slots are limited mdash watch the order Shoot in the right sequence to clear the board

## About

A fast-paced game about reaction and the right order of actions. Cannons fire at pixel blocks of their matching color, breaking them and clearing the board piece by piece. To beat a level, you&rsquo;ll need to manage the flow of shots, avoid overloading the conveyor, and build combos. Simple to learn, but highly addictive: tap &rarr; flow &rarr; pixels fly.
