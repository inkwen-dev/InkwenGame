## About

Spaceship Jigsaw is a fun online puzzle game. Drag the pieces into right position using mouse. Solving puzzles is relaxing, rewarding, and keeps your brain sharp. You need to spend $1000 to be able to purchase one of the following pictures. You have three modes for each picture from which the hardest mode brings more money. You have a total of 10 pictures.
