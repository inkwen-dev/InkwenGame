## About

Goods Master is a relaxing sorting puzzle where your job is to tidy up the shelves. Jars, bottles and boxes are scattered across the store &mdash; drag them around until every shelf holds one matching set of goods. Complete a shelf and it clears away, making room for the next batch. You only get a limited number of moves per level, so think before you tap. Use boosters when you get stuck: shuffle the items, transform a good into another one, or merge them with the hammer tool. Over 20 hand-crafted levels with growing difficulty, colorful cartoon art and satisfying combo effects.
