## How to play

- Tap and drag a shape to move it into empty cells.
- Shapes cannot pass through each other.
- Complete the whole picture to clear the level.
- Play through endless levels.

## About

«Slide the Block: Build the Pictures!» —  an exciting logic puzzle that will test your spatial thinking and ingenuity!

Move the shapes across the game board to assemble a complete picture. But be careful: blocks cannot pass through each other! Plan each move, find the correct sequence of movements, and unlock new images with cute animals and other characters.

Perfect for brain training, developing logic, and simply a pleasant way to pass the time.
