## About

Players begin their journey as small snakes and eat colorful dots to grow.

However, the bigger you get, the more challenges you face.
You will have to avoid colliding with other snakes while you try to eat them. Your strategy and snake control will be key factors in your survival. Be careful, because if your head touches another snake, you will explode and the game will end.

But don't worry, you can always start over and try to beat your previous record. Slither Snake 2048 is a game that tests your skills, reactions and strategic thinking. Are you ready to become the biggest snake? Try it now!
