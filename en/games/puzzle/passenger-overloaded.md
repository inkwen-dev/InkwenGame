## About

Passengers Overload - City Bus Simulator Game is a unique game of its kind. Press and hold to load the passengers into the bus, release to stop. Load the passengers precisely, not more, not less to avoid explosion. Hurry up! the crowd is getting late to get to their work. Help them catch their bus on time and in limit so that they don't explode. You have a maximum of 3 buses to fit the crowd into, use your turns wisely to avoid getting failed. Fit the crowd into the bus as max as you can and in less number of buses possible in order to advance into next level.
