Tile Match Guard Baby is a fun and relaxing puzzle game where you match colorful tiles to protect a sleeping baby from cute, mischievous monsters!

## How to play

1 Tap tiles to move them into the tray 2 Match 3 identical tiles to clear them 3 Clear all tiles before the tray fills up to win and guard the babys sleep
