## About

Going Rolling Ball is an exciting and fast-paced arcade game where players control a rolling ball through challenging paths and obstacles. The goal is to keep the ball balanced while navigating through narrow tracks, sharp turns, and moving barriers. As the game progresses, the levels become more difficult, requiring quick reflexes and precise control. Collect points, avoid falling off the track, and try to reach the highest score. With smooth controls and engaging gameplay, Going Rolling Ball offers a fun and addictive experience for players of all ages.
