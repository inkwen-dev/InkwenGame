## About

Among Us - Find Us is a free online hidden object game. A certain area will appear on the screen in front of you. You will have to examine it all very carefully. Find all the Crewmates and Impostors that have faded in the pictures themselves. As soon as you find one of them, just click on it with the mouse. There are a total of 10 Crewmates and Impostors in each level, there are a total of 6 levels.
