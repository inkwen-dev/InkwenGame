Zap those numbers by pitting them against one another! Let's see how much you score in this race against time!

## How to play

1. A numbered tile appears at the bottom of the grid.
2. Tap a column to place the tile upward.
3. When two identical numbers meet, they merge into double the value.
4. Keep merging while preventing the grid from overflowing.
