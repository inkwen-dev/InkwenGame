## About

Welcome to the World of Sweety Shapes! Sweet Candies are Falling from the sky and your mission is to pop 2 or more Shapes of the same color to complete your level. Some pets are trapped between the sweety shapes and your mission is to bring them to bottom to help them. Explore some challenging levels and try to complete them all. Have Fun
