## About

Doll Doll is a fun fighting game combined with collection elements, where you roam the map to gather adorable Doll rabbits. Each time you collect 3 rabbits of the same color, you score points and create interesting combo chains. The map is full of challenges with many unexpected situations, requiring you to move skillfully and choose the right path to collect all the rabbits. Simple and easy to play but equally engaging, Doll Doll offers a light and colorful entertainment experience!
