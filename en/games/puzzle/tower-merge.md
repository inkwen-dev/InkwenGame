## About

Tower Merge is a game about merging blocks and increasing income. You start with one tower but are then able to get more towers. Special towers give you special bonuses. Try to make the tallest tower and reach the highest numbers. There are also many prestige upgrades. You get golden blocks when you have enough money, spend them to speed up your progress!
