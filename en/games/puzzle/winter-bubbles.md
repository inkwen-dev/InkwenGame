## About

Winter Bubbles is a Classical bubble shooter game with winter theme. Aim and release the candle in order to combine it with other similar candles. Your goal is to collect the Golden Bell with the allowed time. Don’t let the candles reach the border. There are several power-ups which can be activated by shooting candle on them.
