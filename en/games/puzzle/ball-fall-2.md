## About

Ball Fall is a free and addictive online game for boys played on PuzzleGame. Choose the right direction and throw your colored balls from a height and make them drop into the point. The point will change its position randomly, so try to throw more balls at once as you can. Mobile, Tablet, and Desktop are playable. Some levels are super hard! Can you pass them all?
