## About

Halloween Hidden Numbers is a beautiful online game with hidden numbers. Find out the hidden numbers in the specified images. Each level has 10 numbers from 1 to 10. There are 6 levels in total. The time is limited so be fast and find all hidden numbers before time runs out. Clicking in the wrong place several times reduces the time by an additional 5 seconds. So, if you are ready start the game and have fun!
