## About

Balloon Slicer is a very addictive online puzzle game for free. It is designed for boys. There are lots of ballons surrounded by obstacles. Click the blade and drag. Release the mouse and the blade would fly along the opposite direction. Make sure the blade fly into the suitable area. And it will bounce while touching obstacles to slice all the ballons and you will pass the level. Enjoy!
