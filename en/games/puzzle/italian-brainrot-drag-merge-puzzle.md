## About

A Revolutionary Jigsaw Experience. Each level, one image is randomly selected and split into 3&times;3, 4&times;4, 5&times;5, or 6&times;6 pieces, then completely shuffled. Your goal is simple: restore the picture by placing every piece in its correct position. But this is no ordinary jigsaw. When two neighboring pieces are placed correctly next to each other, they merge into a single movable cluster. Instead of slow, one-by-one placement, you can drag any piece or merged group onto another to instantly swap positions.
