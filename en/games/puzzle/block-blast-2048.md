## How to play

- Tap adjacent blocks with the same numbers to merge them into larger values.

- Each successful merge increases your score and frees up space on the board.

- Plan your moves — space management is the key to long runs.

- Enjoy the magic of transformation as small numbers turn into impressive combinations.

- Play at your own pace, enjoy the journey, and aim for new heights — records will come naturally!
