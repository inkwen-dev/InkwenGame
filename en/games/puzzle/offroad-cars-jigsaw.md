## About

Off-road Cars is a game where you can play with twelve different pictures of off-road vehicles. Click on the image and choose how you want to play the puzzle. You can play it easily with 25 pieces to put together. In the medium way with 49 pieces and in the hard way with 100 pieces of the picture with an off-road vehicle. You have unlimited playing time, so start with the game and try to have fun and play all 12 levels. Just put the pieces in right places and have fun.
