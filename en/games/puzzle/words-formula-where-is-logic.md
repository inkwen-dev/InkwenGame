## About

The formula of words is an intellectual game of logic. The game consists of 2 modes: Mode #1 (Associations): You are offered two pictures and need to understand what they are associated with. For example: lion + throne = king Mode #2 (What they have in common): You are offered three pictures and you need to understand what they have in common. For example: hair cutting + lawn cutting + bush cutting = haircut.
