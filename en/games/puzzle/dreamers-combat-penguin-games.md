## About

Dreamers Combat Penguin games are being offered by Dreamers Academy to all gamers in which you must save your house from your adversary penguins. To save your house you will be given a machine gun using which you can kill all your opponents. In this game you have to save your house, if your opponents penguins break your house then you will have to play that level of the game again. If you build your house with others, then you reach the next level.
