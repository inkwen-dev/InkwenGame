## How to play

Use arrow keys or touch the screen

## About

In the far wonderful world live snake breeds that eat only vegetable. You in the game Vegetable Snake will fall into it and will help in the development of one small snake. She will have to crawl in various locations and look out for vegetables that lie everywhere on the ground. Your snake will have to absorb them and thus grow in size and gain strength. To do this, using the control arrows, you will have to direct its movement and bring it to the vegetable.
