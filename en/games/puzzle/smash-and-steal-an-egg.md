## How to play

Break eggs with your pickaxe, carry the hatched animal back to your base before the guard catches you, and place it in your pen to earn money. Spend money on stronger pickaxes, trails and pen upgrades, and train on the treadmill to run fast enough for the next zone.

On PC:
WASD - move
LMB - swing
RMB - look around
Space - jump
E - interact (steal, open, upgrade)
F - inventory
1-7 - choose the pickaxe, bat or animals

On smartphone:
Joystick - move
Swipe - look around
Tap - swing the pickaxe
Bottom right - jump and action buttons

## About

"Smash and Steal an Egg!" - smash eggs with your pickaxe, steal the animals that hatch and outrun the guards to build the richest farm!

- Smash eggs in 9 zones: from the Forest to the Galaxy.
- Collect 72 animals: common, rare and legendary.
- Outrun the boss guards and carry your loot home.
- Train your speed on the treadmill and buy trails.
- Upgrade your pickaxe to crack the toughest eggs.
- Earn in-game currency even while you're offline.

Start with a simple chicken from the Forest and unlock every zone step by step. Merge identical animals into new eggs, complete the Index and catch the Titanic Egg that appears every half hour. The faster you run, the farther the zones and the more valuable the animals!
