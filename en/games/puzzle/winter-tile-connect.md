## About

Winter Tile Connect is a fun and relaxing puzzle game where you match two identical winter tiles. Draw a line to connect them using three or fewer straight segments. The path may turn no more than two times and cannot cross other tiles. Clear all tiles to finish the level. The game has many levels and each one becomes a little harder. Play, relax, and enjoy the calm winter mood while you train your brain.
