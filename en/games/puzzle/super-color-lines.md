## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Play the game Super Color Lines and test your intelligence. In the game, in front of your screen, the playing field will be visible divided into an equal number of cells. In some of the cells square chips of different colors will be visible. You have to carefully place one line in five objects from the chips of the same color. Moving of the objects is by clicking on the selected item and move to the location you specified. As soon as the line is set with 5 objects will disappear from the screen and points will be awarded to you.Heave fun!
