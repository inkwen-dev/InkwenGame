## How to play

1. The entire game takes place in a doorway-shaped play area.
2. A flower appears at the top, which moves with your finger or mouse cursor.
3. Release the mouse button or your finger to make the flower fly down.
4. Make the identical flowers collide and then they will join together to become another flower.
5. When the letter icon appears at the top of the screen, it means you have received an order for a new bouquet.

## About

Combine flowers in the relaxing atmosphere of a summer garden and earn coins! Sell your bouquets to customers and clear your garden of unwanted flowers with your pruning shears! Earn more coins than other players to take a place on the leaderboard!
