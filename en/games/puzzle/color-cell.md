## About

Color Cell is a puzzle game with colored blocks. The goal of this game is to create 4 blocks with matching colors to clear them. The blocks which you will see on the screen you need to place them on the board and try to match the colors of the blocks. You can move the blocks in all directions. To break and arrange them right. Play this game while there is place on the board. Achieve the best result and enjoy. Use mouse to play this game or use your finger if you play in the mobile devices.
