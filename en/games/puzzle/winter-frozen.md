## About

The glittering crystals of ice of different shapes resemble shining diamonds. They will become your main elements in the game Frozen Winter. To complete the level, it is necessary to collect a specified number of blocks. Form the rows or columns of three or more identical ice figures to take them off the field. Have fun!
