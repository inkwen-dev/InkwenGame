## About

Gravity Box is an exciting online physics-based game where you control a box using gravity to navigate through challenging levels. Your goal is to make the box move upstairs by flipping gravity at the right moments. Avoid obstacles, time your moves perfectly, and reach the top without falling! With simple controls but tricky mechanics, Gravity Box will test your precision and timing skills. Can you master gravity and guide the box to victory?
