## About

This is an interesting game where you have to release the little ball and it becomes a bigger ball with the number 5. That means you have to hit that bigger colored ball five times with the little ball to earn a point. Fill the screen with balls and try to destroy as much as you can. Watch out for the little ball because it can fall from the bottom of the frame and then the game is over.
