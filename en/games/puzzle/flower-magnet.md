## About

Flower Magnet is a relaxing and enchanting puzzle game where you guide a magical magnet to collect beautiful blossoms. Swipe and slide through dreamy gardens, attracting flowers of different colors while avoiding obstacles and tricky paths. Use strategy and timing to gather every bloom and complete each level with grace. With soothing music, elegant visuals, and increasingly clever puzzles, Flower Magnet is perfect for players seeking calm yet challenging gameplay. Unlock new garden themes, magnet styles, and special floral effects as you progress. Let nature guide your moves&mdash;step into a blooming world of logic and beauty with Flower Magnet!
