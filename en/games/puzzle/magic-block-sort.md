## How to play

Sorting these magic blocks is easy to learn but hard to master! Here is how you play:
🎯 Tap to Move: Tap any tube to lift the top block, then tap another tube to drop the block into it.

📏 Sorting Rule: You can only place a block on top of another block if they have the same color/symbol, or if the destination tube is completely empty.

📦 Fill the Tube: Keep sorting until each tube contains only one matching type of magic block.

🧠 Use Special Tools: Stuck on a hard level? Use the Undo button to take back your last move, or Restart the level to try a fresh strategy!

⏳ No Time Pressure: Play at your own pace! There are no timers, so you can relax and find your calm through sorting.

## About

Magic Block Sort is a fun, addictive, and brain-teasing puzzle game that will keep you entertained for hours! If you love classic sorting games but want a fresh, magical twist with 3D blocks, this is the perfect game for you.

Your mission is simple but challenging: sort all the magic blocks with matching colors and symbols into their own tubes. Strategy and forward thinking are your best tools. As you progress, the levels become more complex with more blocks, hidden mystery symbols, and extra tubes to test your IQ! Can you master the ultimate sorting puzzle?
