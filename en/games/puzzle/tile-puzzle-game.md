## About

Tile Puzzle Game is an engaging and visually appealing tile-based puzzle game that tests your spatial reasoning and problem-solving skills. The game starts with a shuffled grid of numbered tiles and an empty space. Your objective is to rearrange the tiles by sliding them into the empty space to achieve the correct numerical order. With a built-in timer and move counter, you&rsquo;ll need to think quickly and strategically to solve the puzzle before time runs out.
