## How to play

– Tap any block with an open face to pick it up — buried blocks can't be taken yet.
– If the block's icon matches one of the goal chips at the top, it flies straight to that goal.
– Any other block goes to the storage bar at the bottom. If the bar overflows, you lose — so don't hoard!
– Complete a goal chip and the next category slides in; stored blocks of that category fly to it automatically.
– Clear all the categories before the timer runs out to win the level.
– Stuck? Use boosters: Hint finds a matching block, Magic Wand collects one instantly, Shuffle rearranges icons, and Undo returns your last block.

## About

Block Match 3D is a relaxing yet brain-teasing 3D puzzle where you take apart a giant cube of blocks, piece by piece. Every block carries a cute icon — donuts, animals, emoji, music and more.

Match blocks to the active category goals, dig deeper into the structure, and clear the whole cube before the timer runs out. Simple to pick up, tricky to master: plan your taps wisely, because the storage bar at the bottom fills up fast.

Dozens of levels, colorful icon collections, and handy boosters like Hint, Shuffle, Magic Wand and Undo will keep you matching for hours!
