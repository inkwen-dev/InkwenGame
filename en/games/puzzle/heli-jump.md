## About

Heli Jump is a game where you have to control the helicopter that needs to take off and land safely at the next place. You need to be fast and precise. You are entitled to three errors before the game ends. You can change the color of the helicopter. Check out your lucky color and try to get as far as you can in this flying game. By holding you give the helicopter strength to fly. If it is too strong it will collide, but even if it is too slow it will fall into the abyss. Find the right moment to fly safely.
