## How to play

Your goal is to correctly mark every orange hexagon as either blue or black.

A number inside a dark clue cell shows how many adjacent cells are blue.
Use the clues together to identify the correct state of each orange cell.
Mark all required blue cells and eliminate the remaining cells as black.
Complete the puzzle when no orange cells remain.
Controls

Desktop

Left-click: Mark a cell blue
Right-click: Mark a cell black
Click the same cell again: Undo that mark
U or Ctrl + Z: Undo
R: Restart
H: Hint
Esc: Pause

Mobile

Tap: Mark a cell blue
Long-press: Mark a cell black

Some versions may also include separate Blue and Black selection buttons.

Clue Examples
0: None of the neighboring cells are blue.
6: All six neighboring cells are blue.
3: Exactly three neighboring cells are blue.
Special clue symbols may indicate that blue cells must be consecutive, separated, or located along a specific direction.

Use deduction, compare overlapping clues, and solve every board without guessing.

## About

Test your logic in a clean and relaxing hexagonal puzzle experience.

Each orange hexagon hides one of two states: blue or black. Use the numbered clues to determine the correct state of every cell. Each number tells you how many connected neighboring cells must be blue.

Start with simple tutorial puzzles, then progress through 30 increasingly challenging levels featuring overlapping clues, consecutive groups, directional clues, and advanced deduction patterns.

Every puzzle is designed to be solved through logic alone—no guessing required.
