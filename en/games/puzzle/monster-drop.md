## About

Monster Drop is just the coolest physics-based puzzle game available. Built from the ground up with a team of artists and programmers to give you the best gaming experience possible. Main Goal: Drop the monster onto a metal block by destroying all other blocks. Once all the blocks are gone and the monster is on a metal block, press the ZAPPER button to fry the monster! But be careful, hit the ground and it's all over and the Monster will eat you. Features: - 90 well crafted levels. - A Monster Zapper to destroy monsters. - 3 words with all new monsters. - Smooth 60 frame one second Gameplay. Enjoy!
