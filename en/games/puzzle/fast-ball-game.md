## About

Fast Ball is a game where you need to hit the ball and control it with the beam. It is a kind of brick break game. Here you need to try the ball not to fall from the beam and hit the big white ball. That is the goal of this game, to destroy more white balls as you can without falling your ball from the beam. When you hit the white ball, she will disappear, and the new one will be shown in a new place.
