## About

This is a special bubble shooter game in which you will find a rotating wheel of GEMS. To complete the game you need to collect all the GEMS on the wheel. Shoot the GEM towards the wheel to make the group of at least three GEMS of the same color. Your points are reducing with the time, So finish the game early to save more points.
