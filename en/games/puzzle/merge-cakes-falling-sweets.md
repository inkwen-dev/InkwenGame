## About

Merge cakes and complete stage missions to achieve victory with Pig Baker across snowy fields, scorching deserts, and lush meadows! As you progress, the environment will challenge your cakes with unique effects&mdash;so stay alert! Strategically use items to overcome obstacles and keep the merging magic alive. Clear stages faster than anyone else and rack up high scores! Aim for the top spot and become a top player. You can check your rank anytime by clicking the Candle Button in the top-right corner.
