## How to play

Drag a beast onto another beast of the same type to merge them into a stronger creature. Keep combining matching beasts to reach the level goal, and leave room on the board for your next moves. On desktop, click and hold with the left mouse button, drag, then release. On mobile, touch and drag with one finger. Use the on-screen buttons to pause, toggle sound, and access available bonuses.

## About

Merge Beasts is a colorful merge puzzle game about combining matching creatures and discovering stronger beasts. Plan your moves, manage the space on the board, and work toward each level goal. Simple drag-and-drop controls make it easy to pick up on desktop or mobile, while each merge brings you closer to your next discovery.
