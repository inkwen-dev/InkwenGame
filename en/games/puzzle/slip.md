## About

Slip is a puzzle game where you need to control the ball which moves on the lane left and right. Blue and orange cubes will come to the blue ball. You should avoid the orange cubes but collect the blue ones. Make the best result and collect as many cubes as you can. If you touch the orange cube the game is over. Your cubes are blue so collect them.
