## About

Become the master of modern warfare in Tank Arena! In this action game, you control your own tank and fight with other opponents. Although your opponents are AI, they will be relentless in pursuing you. You need to hide behind structures and keep moving if you want to survive. You can also pick power-ups that can help destroy your enemies quicker.
