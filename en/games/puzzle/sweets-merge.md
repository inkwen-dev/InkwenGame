## How to play

Click and drag a sweet to move it on the board Release it over another identical sweet to merge them into a higher-level dessert

## About

Sweets Merge is a delightful and addictive puzzle game where your goal is to combine identical candies and desserts to create even tastier treats! Start with simple sweets and merge them step by step to unlock cakes, chocolates, donuts, and more. The gameplay is easy to learn but becomes more challenging as your board fills up, requiring smart thinking and careful planning. Each move counts, so you must decide wisely how to place and merge your sweets to avoid running out of space. Enjoy colorful graphics, smooth animations, and satisfying merging effects that make every upgrade feel rewarding.
