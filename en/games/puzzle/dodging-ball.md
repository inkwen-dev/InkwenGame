## About

Dodging Ball is a complicated game with ball. You need to balance ball on the beam and to avoid flying boxes. It is not easy for play. you need to be focused on the ball but on the same time you need to watch on flying boxes. Try to play longer as you can without fall the ball from the beam or without collide with some boxes.
