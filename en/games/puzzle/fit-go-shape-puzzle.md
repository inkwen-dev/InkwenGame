## About

Get ready for a fun and immersive shape journey. Try to pass the shapes through the boxes with Maskedgames. You have to be quick for this. On the Zig Zag map, replace the player with the same shape in whatever way the obstacles are on the endless path. Try to reach the highest score by overcoming obstacles quickly and agile. Visit maskedgames.com for more shape games and games in different categories! Have fun.
