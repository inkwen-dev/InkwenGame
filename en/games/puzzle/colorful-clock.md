## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

The clock is moving very fast and in a very weird way! All you have to do is to match the color of the clock’s hand with the circle around it by clicking on the screen on the right time. It may seems easy, but the clock will move faster and faster as you progress and it changes color and direction after every successful match. You will lose if you don’t click on the right time or you match the wrong colors. So focus and try to earn as much score as you can!
