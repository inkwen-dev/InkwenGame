## About

This is simple Pop It HTML5 game with some modification. In this game, you need to fill lines with colors. When the line is full, it is automatically destroyed and the player is given points. The more you destroy lines at one time, the more points you get. The first bubble is always of a random color. You can pick any spot on the line with this color if you haven't burst there yet. You cannot pop colors similar to the first until you complete the task. You can burst the following colors only near the last bubble that you burst, at a distance of one cell horizontally and one vertically. Good luck and have fun:)
