## About

A small orange ball has abilities like change of gravity. In the game Gravity Ball the ball will go on a journey, and you will help him by demonstrating his skills. By clicking on the character, you will make him change position relative to the path, avoiding collisions with sharp spikes or blocks. In this case, red crystals can not be ignored - these are your points scored. The ball will be swept with a solid speed and your reaction should be just as lightning fast. How far the traveler will be able to pass is your merit.Heave Fun!
