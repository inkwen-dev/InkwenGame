## How to play

Tap a Tile
Select a tile to move it to the collection tray.
Match Three Identical Tiles
Collect 3 matching tiles to remove them.
Clear the Board
Match all tiles to complete the level.
Think Ahead
The tray has limited space, so choose tiles carefully.
Complete Levels
Solve puzzles and unlock new challenges.

## About

Tile Match Camp is a relaxing and addictive triple-match puzzle game set in a beautiful camping world. Explore nature, match colorful items, and clear challenging levels as you journey through forests, campsites, and outdoor adventures.

Match three identical tiles to remove them from the board and uncover new challenges. Plan your moves carefully, keep your tray from filling up, and complete each level to advance further into the adventure.

With charming visuals, smooth gameplay, and hundreds of satisfying puzzles, Camp Match Adventure is perfect for players who enjoy casual brain-training games and relaxing puzzle experiences.

Can you master every level and become the ultimate camping puzzle explorer?
