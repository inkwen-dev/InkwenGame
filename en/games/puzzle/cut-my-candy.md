## About

Cut My Candy is a dark fantasy physics puzzle game where your goal is to feed a hungry demon! Swipe to cut ropes and watch the candy swing, fall, and bounce based on real-world physics. Navigate through 25 challenging, hand-crafted puzzle chambers filled with dangerous spikes, floating bubbles, and tricky momentum swings. Plan your cuts carefully to collect all 3 stars in each level before guiding the candy into the demons mouth. Can you master the physics and conquer all 25 levels?
