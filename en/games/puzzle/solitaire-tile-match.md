## How to play

Tap tiles on the board to send them to your dock, and collect three matching tiles to clear them. Tiles are layered, so clearing the ones on top reveals new tiles hidden underneath. Your dock only has a few slots, though — if it fills up before you can make a match, the level is over. When you're in a tight spot, use a power-up to save yourself: Undo a pick, Shuffle the board, grab a Hint, or add an Extra Slot. Clear levels to earn coins and gems, unlock new chapters, and check back daily for login rewards.
