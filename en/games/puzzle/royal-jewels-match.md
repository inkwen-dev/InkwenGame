## How to play

The goal of the game is to match three or more jewels of the same type to complete levels and earn coins. Use your coins to purchase unique decorations, and for every purchase, you'll earn crowns that help you unlock the next exciting adventure!

## About

Matching jewels has never been so much fun! Unlock unique levels, participate in special events and take on surprising challenges. Collect coins with your matches and show off your decorating skills. Earn crowns as you progress and become a true master of the game!
