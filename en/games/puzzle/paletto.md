## About

Paletto is a colorful puzzle game that challenges your logic, memory, and pattern-matching skills! Each level presents a target color pattern, and your goal is to recreate it by using the available coloring mechanisms in the correct order. Think carefully, plan your moves, and transform the puzzle until it perfectly matches the given design. As you progress, the patterns become more complex, testing your observation and problem-solving abilities. With intuitive gameplay, vibrant visuals, and satisfying puzzles, Paletto offers a fun and relaxing experience for players of all ages.
