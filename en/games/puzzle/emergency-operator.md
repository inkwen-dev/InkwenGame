## About

In Emergency Operator, you take charge of urgent 911 calls. Understand the callers situation and decide whether to send the fire crew, police, medical team &mdash; or if no help is needed at all. Make the right calls to save lives, avoid false alarms, and keep the city safe. Prove your skills with accurate decisions and get promoted to higher ranks. Every decision counts!
