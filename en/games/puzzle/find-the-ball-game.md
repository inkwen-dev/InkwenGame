## About

Find the Ball is an engaging and fun puzzle game designed to test your observation and memory skills. In this game, three animated cups with expressive faces shuffle quickly, hiding a red ball beneath one of them. Your goal is to follow the movement of the cups carefully and guess under which cup the ball is hidden after the shuffle stops. With cheerful graphics, dynamic animations, and intuitive controls, Find the Ball provides endless entertainment for players of all ages. It&rsquo;s a perfect blend of challenge and fun, encouraging focus and improving your reflexes. Can you keep track of the ball and win? Test your skills now!
