## About

MealMerge is a fun and creative puzzle adventure set in a vibrant jungle where colorful food blocks form wiggly worm-like chains! Your objective is to connect the food blocks by matching them with the correct screw positions and build the complete chain. Each level presents a new challenge that requires careful planning and logical thinking. Rotate, place, and connect the food blocks in the right order to complete the puzzle. As you advance, the layouts become more intricate, offering increasingly satisfying brain-teasing gameplay. With charming visuals and intuitive controls, MealMerge is an enjoyable puzzle experience :)
