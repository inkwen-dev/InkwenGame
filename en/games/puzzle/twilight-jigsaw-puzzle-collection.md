## About

Your task in the game is to collect portraits of your favorite characters of "Twilight", the vampire Edward and his girlfriend Bella. The game takes you to the world of vampires and werewolves. Try to solve all the puzzles, put together the mixed pieces of portraits of a young man and a girl. According to the game conditions, you can change the position of the tiles by clicking on the pieces. This game is not easy, you need to include logical thinking, build the necessary drawings from the pieces. You can enjoy this game. Young people will be happy to see their new images. And if you want to see the history of the heroes in pictures-collect puzzles.
