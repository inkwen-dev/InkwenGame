## About

Aim, match and smash all the dinosaur eggs in this relaxing color-matching adventure. To break the eggs, you must match three or more of the same color eggs together, then fire the egg that you are given into the pile of magic eggs stacked in the play area. Try to break all of the dinosaur eggs with least amount of shots. 24 levels to play. Enjoy!
