## How to play

Push each colored box onto a square marked with a star of the same color. Success means matching all stars with their respective boxes
Desktop: Click on a box to push it one square in the direction indicated by an arrow on the box.
Touch devices: Tap a box to move it in the arrow’s direction
Arrow indicators: Boxes display arrows pointing the direction they'll move.
Color-changing tiles: Passing over a colored dot (paint tile) changes a box's color (except black boxes)
Black boxes: These remain their original color and cannot change; they cannot complete a star color requirement
