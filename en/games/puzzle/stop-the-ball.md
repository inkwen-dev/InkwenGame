## How to play

Use mouse or tap on the screen

## About

Run the path to win, avoiding crashing into obstacles. The colored ball will roll down the chute, which stretches for an infinite distance. It is quite difficult to get them out of it, but it is possible if you pass the obstacles incorrectly. There will be a lot of them on the way of the ball: straight, curved, cruciform, jagged and round. They move in different planes and rotate in different directions. Approaching the next obstacle, you can stop the ball and choose the right moment to pass. The goal is to get as far as possible. Take your time, speed in this case is not the main thing.
