Sort colorful gems in this relaxing puzzle. Match diamonds, clear the board, and reveal beautiful artwork. Easy to learn, hard to put down!

## How to play

Tap a group of misplaced gems to select it, then tap an empty matching-color area on the board or an available buffer slot to move it. Correctly placed gems stay fixed. Sort every active gem into its matching color and empty the buffer to complete the level and reveal artwork. Use touch on mobile or the mouse on desktop.
