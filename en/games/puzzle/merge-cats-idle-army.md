## How to play

🖱️ PC: click and drag📱 Mobile: tap and drag
Recruit cats: tap the spawn button to buy a new cat with Soul Fragments. New recruits also drop in for free over time.
Merge cats: drag a cat onto an identical cat (same level) to merge them into a stronger one. 🐱 + 🐱 = 😼
Battle: drag cats onto the battle tiles to send them into the fight. They attack automatically.
Collect souls: enemies drop Soul Fragments with every hit. Use them to recruit and upgrade cats.
Beat bosses: defeat a boss to advance to stronger enemies and bigger rewards.
Boost up: activate x2 Damage, Auto-Merge or Time Jump, and grab the free boosts that fly across the screen. 🎁
Upgrade and Ascend: upgrade spawn level and speed, and Ascend when progress slows to come back permanently stronger.
Come back daily: claim offline loot, daily rewards and quest prizes. 📆

## About

Welcome to Merge Cats Idle Army, the purr-fect idle merge game! 🐱✨ Drag two identical cats together and watch them merge into a stronger, cooler, cuter cat. There are 100 cat levels to discover, from tiny fluffballs to legendary feline warriors.

Send your army onto the battlefield and let them battle wave after wave of rival cats and huge bosses. Every hit drops Soul Fragments 💎, which you spend to recruit even more powerful cats. Your army keeps fighting while you're away, too: come back to up to 12 hours of offline loot 💤💰.

Stack boosts like x2 Damage, Auto-Merge and Time Jumps, and Ascend to restart stronger with permanent bonuses. Spin the Ascend Wheel, complete your Cat Book collection, and take a break with the Tile Puzzle mini-game. In Merge Cats Idle Army the merging is satisfying, the progress never stops, and the cuteness is maxed out. 😻🔥
