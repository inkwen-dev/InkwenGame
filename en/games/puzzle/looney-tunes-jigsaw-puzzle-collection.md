## About

The game "Looney Tunes Puzzles" is a real treasure for fans of this famous cartoon. You will find a large number of puzzles with characters from Looney Tunes. Even for those who have never seen this cartoon, our game will bring a lot of fun. To assemble the puzzle, you need to drag the parts from the lower block to the puzzle field. But all this needs to be done before the timer runs out of time. Otherwise, you'll have to start over. On the first level, you will meet three characters from the cartoon-Bugs Bunny, Lola Bunny and Marvin. Bugs Bunny is a symbol of Looney Tunes. This is a cheerful rabbit-prankster. He always finds a way out of any mess. Lola Bunny is a charming pink bunny. She works as a pizza delivery guy at a local cafe and is friends with Bugs. And Marvin is a very unusual creature. He's an alien from the planet Mars. He is always dressed in the clothes of the Roman Empire. And with the rest, you will get acquainted only when you cope with the rest of the puzzles. Have a nice game!
