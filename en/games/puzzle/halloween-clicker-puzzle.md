Play our newest casual Halloween puzzle game Halloween Clicker Puzzle. The game has 10 levels with Halloween pumpkins, batt, and witches images. Each level is locked and it will be unlocked when you solve the previous level. Build the correct image by rotating the pieces of the puzzle before time runs out. Tap or click on a piece to rotate.
