## About

Enter a fun and strategic puzzle challenge in Tiles Layer Game! Guide your path using the mouse and create tiles step by step as you move toward your destination. Every move builds your trail, so plan carefully. Once you reach the end point, drop the first tile to trigger a chain reaction&mdash;if all tiles fall perfectly till the end, you win! But if any tile is left behind, the level fails.
