## About

Butterflies jigsaw is a game where you can choose an image with Butterfly from twelve images. After that choose in which mode you want to play the game. You can play in easy, medium or hard. And you need to put the pieces from the image in the right position to get the image with butterflies. Use a mouse to drag the pieces.
