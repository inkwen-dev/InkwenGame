## About

CrossPath is an HTML5 Puzzle Game. In this puzzle game, you should drag the diamonds to draw a line in available spaces on the puzzle board. Each diamond has a number, you can draw lines from that diamond as many times as the number is. There are some obstacles and you can&rsquo;t drag the lines on them. The puzzle board gets harder when you pass the levels. If you draw the lines in the wrong way, you can undo your moves.
