## About

Puzzle Time – Sea Creatures is a game designed specifically for kids. The objective of this game is to put the pieces of puzzles together. Once all pieces are placed correctly, it not only shows the picture of a Sea Creature but also shows a fun fact about them allowing kids to broaden their knowledge and learn something new as they play.
