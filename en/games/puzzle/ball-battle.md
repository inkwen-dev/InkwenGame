## How to play

Mouse to aim and click to shoot

## About

Ball battle is a game in which you play as the blue and compete against the red. Hit the white balls with your main ball to change their color. When all the white balls are transformed, if there are more blue balls than red ones, you win. Otherwise, you lose. Good luck ;)
