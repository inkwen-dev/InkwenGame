## About

The game The Best Memory will quickly lift your mood and at the same time, improve your memory. In general, there are only advantages, so do not miss it, immerse yourself in the cheerful atmosphere. Your task is to open identical tiles and find pairs with funny pictures. Each found pair will be eliminated from the field and you will make sure that the space is empty. Time is not limited, but the better the time, the better the result.
