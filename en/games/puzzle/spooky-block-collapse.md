Use touchscreen or mouse to play this game

## About

Collect all the spooky blocks by clicking or touching them in groups. If you collect a group of 2 or more you will get score. If you collect more than 7 blocks in a single turn you will get a power-up (Bomb or Arrow or Magic spooky block). If you tap a single block, 200 points will be deducted from your score. Use the information in the projection box to see how much each collection is worth. Reach or pass each level's target amount and clear all the blocks in the board to go on to the next level. That's okay, if you don't reach the target amount as you can always retry the level.
