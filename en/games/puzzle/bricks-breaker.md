## About

Relax your brain and play the best bricks breaker game this year. Touch and shoot balls to break all bricks. You must clear all bricks to finish each level. Find the best aim angle to break a maximum of bricks. The bricks are destroyed when their score reach 0. Break the bricks before they hit the bottom of the screen. There are over 3000 levels to play !!!
