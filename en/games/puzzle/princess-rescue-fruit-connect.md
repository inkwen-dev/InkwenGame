## About

Begin a fruity puzzle adventure. Princess Rescue Fruit Connect is a delightful match puzzle game where your mission is to rescue a beautiful princess by matching fruits and solving tricky levels. Connect two identical fruits to eliminate them. But notice clearing the board before time runs out. All while progressing to rescue the princess.
