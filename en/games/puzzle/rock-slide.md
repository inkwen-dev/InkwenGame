## About

Rock Slide is an engaging puzzle game where you need to move and place rocks onto the correct switches to open the exit door. Each level requires careful observation of the terrain, strategic planning of your movements, and avoiding getting stuck. The gameplay is simple yet challenging, increasingly addictive, and provides a satisfying feeling when you find the correct solution.
