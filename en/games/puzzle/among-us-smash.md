## About

Among Us Smash is a game with Among Us balloons. There is different colors on among us balloons and you need to smash it. Smash all balloons except the blue balloons. You have the right to smash the blue balloons three times. After that the game will end. Be careful because sometimes the balloons come in a group of several balloons and there is a blue balloon hidden between them, so when you smash the others, the blue one will smash as well. Smash as many balloons as you can and get the best result.
