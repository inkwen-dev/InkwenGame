## About

Its time to put your sorting and coordination skills to the test! In this game, youll be faced with a series of color chains that need to be sorted by color. To do this, youll need to use your physics skills to manipulate the chains. You can pull them, push them, and spin them to get them into the correct order. The game gets more challenging as you progress, with more chains and more colors to sort. But with a little practice, youll be able to master the game and get the highest score. So what are you waiting for? Start sorting the color chains today!
