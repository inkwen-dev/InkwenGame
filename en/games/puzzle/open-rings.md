## How to play

Drag around a ring to turn it Double-tap a ring to put it back Hold still for 1 second to put every ring back Tap Hammer Hint or Firework in the bottom bar to use a booster Use the and - buttons to zoom

## About

Open Rings is a relaxing ring puzzle about freeing gems. Every level is a tangle of faceted rings, sliding bars and little locks. Turn a ring, pull a bar and slip each piece off the board without letting anything collide. Features: - 384 levels, from gentle warm-ups to tricky tangles. - Rings, sliding bars, locks that open when other pieces are gone, and bombs that blast everything around them. - Collect diamonds to earn gems, then spend them on boosters: Hammer, Hint and Firework. - A free daily gift: 5 of every booster, every day. - Plays in 12 languages, on phone and desktop.
