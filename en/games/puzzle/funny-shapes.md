## About

Funny Shapes is fun online game for the little ones. Love these different activities to help little ones learn shapes! Funny Shapes is a matching game. This game is very suitable for children who are learning shapes. If you have little kids, properly you can teach them to recognize shapes by this game. It is really an educational game. Just match the shapes into the proper frame, it is easy to play. Have fun playing.
