## About

Enter Flower Jam is a vibrant casual match game with colorful flowers and exciting puzzles. You aim to complete the level by swapping flowers and eliminating them by getting six petals of the same color! Place petals of the same color together to make six petals to eliminate. Get props to help you eliminate tons of flowers.
