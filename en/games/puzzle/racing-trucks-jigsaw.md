## About

This is a jigsaw game with racing trucks. Here you can play in five different images with racing trucks in them. Click on the image that you want to play and choose how many pieces you want to play. You can play in easy mode with 25 pieces, medium or with 49 pieces, or hard with 100 pieces. The image will be opened in a new layer and the pieces will shuffle. Put the pieces in the right position to get the image with racing trucks. Do that without pressure and without a time limit.
