## About

Cat Strapped is an off-the-wall word puzzler that will keep you coming back for more. You start the game with 8 lovable Cats strapped with C4!!! As you play the game you guess letters. For each letter, you guess wrong a poor little kitty will explode! With every correct puzzle, you will get 2 cats back. The game is over when you run out of cats.
