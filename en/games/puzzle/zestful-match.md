## How to play

Tap or click on any fruit tile to move it into your tray at the bottom.
Match 3 identical tiles in the tray to remove them and earn points.
The tray has a maximum size of 7 tiles. If it fills up without any matches, you lose!
Clear all the tiles from the game board to win the level.
Use Undo to revert your move, Hint to find a match, or Shuffle to rearrange the board if you are stuck.
Climb the Global Leaderboard by clearing levels and achieving high scores!
