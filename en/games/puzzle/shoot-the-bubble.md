## How to play

The goal is simple: pop the bubbles by matching three or more bubbles of the same color. Aim carefully, use bank shots off the side walls, and clear the board.

On Desktop

* Aim: move the mouse to adjust the aiming line, which starts from the launcher at the bottom and shows you the bubble’s trajectory.
* Shoot: left-click to launch the bubble in the indicated direction.
* Bank shots: the bubble bounces off the side walls. Use the aiming line to hit groups of bubbles that are difficult to reach with a direct shot.

On Mobile

* Aim: place your finger on the screen and drag it to adjust the direction. The aiming line is always visible and helps you aim precisely.
* Shoot: lift your finger (or tap the screen) to launch the bubble toward the target.
* Bank shots: just like on desktop, use the side walls to angle your shots.

In Challenge Mode: the wall of bubbles continuously moves downward. Create combos to push it back, and don’t let the bubbles cross the danger line.

## About

Dive into the classic puzzle game that has won over millions of players! Aim, shoot, and match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them and clear the board. Every shot counts, and the most spectacular combos can earn you record-breaking scores.

Adventure Mode
Take on hundreds of levels: plan bank shots off the walls to strategically destroy bubbles, unlock special bubbles, and face increasingly difficult challenges.

Challenge Mode
Put your nerves to the test in endless mode! The wall of bubbles moves toward you: create combos to push it back and never let it cross the danger line. How long can you survive?

Special Bubbles
Unleash chaos with special bubbles: the Lightning Bubble makes a certain number of bubbles matching the color it hits explode, the Bomb Bubble destroys everything around its landing point, while the Fire Bubble and Mystery Bubble add strategy and surprises.
