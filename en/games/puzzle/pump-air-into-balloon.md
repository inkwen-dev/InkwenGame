## About

Pump Air into Balloon is funny game where you need to pump air with the pump and within 60 seconds the balloon should go up with the gift. Then extra time is added and you need to pump another balloon. Try to pump as many balloons as you can before time runs out. Move the pump lever up and down without interruption to pump the balloon.
