## About

Bubble Truck is a great arcade puzzle game. The trucks will come from the left side of the game screen, and they will park below the unloading area. From there will throw out bubbles with different colors. The goal of this game is to load the colored truck with bubbles that have the same color as the truck. To do that sometimes you need to open and close different bars to find the right path to the truck. Tap at the bars with the mouse to play this game.
