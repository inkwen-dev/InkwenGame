## About

Play our newest casual puzzle game called Mr. Bean Rotate. The game has 10 levels with exciting images of Mr. Bean's life. Each level is locked and it will be unlocked when you solve the previous level. Build the correct image by clicking to rotate the puzzle pieces before time runs out. Enjoy and have fun!
