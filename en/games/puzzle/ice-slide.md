## How to play

HOW TO PLAY:
- Swipe in any direction (Up, Down, Left, Right) to slide.
- The block slides in a straight line until it hits a wall or obstacle.
- Cover every tile on the grid to solve the puzzle.
- Reach the target tile to win and unlock the next level.

## About
