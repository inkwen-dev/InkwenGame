## How to play

To play click on a bubble to select it and then click on a tube to drop it You can also tap the screen or use the left mouse click to select and move the bubble

## About

In Fun Water Sorting, your goal is to sort liquids by color by mixing them together in the correct tubes. To start, move the fluids into the appropriate tubes until each one contains only one color. Use logic and problem-solving skills to find the right solution. If you need help or make a mistake, you can use hints or undo your last move. Challenge your friends to see who can complete the game the fastest and share your progress with them.
