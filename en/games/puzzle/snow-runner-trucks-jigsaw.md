## About

This is a jigsaw game with Snow Trucks images. There is twelve images with snow trucks. Start to play with first available image. Choose which mode you want to play. You can play easy mode with 25 pieces, medium mode with 49 pieces or hard mode with 100 pieces of image. If you solve the puzzle you will unlock next level. So put the pieces on right place to get the image with snow truck and try to unlock all images. Use mouse or just drag the pieces to play this game. You can play this game on your computer on your mobile devices.
