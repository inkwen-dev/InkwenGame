## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Tray to help Santos Dumont stay as long as possible on the plane and cross Paris. With just a few simple touches on the screen, you can control the plane. You should be careful not to hit the tree trunks that constantly appear on the screen. Make the most points passing through the tree trunks and be careful not to blow the plane crashing into the trunks.
