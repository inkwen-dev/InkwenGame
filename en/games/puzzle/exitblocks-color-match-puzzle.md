## How to play

Objective: Clear every colored block from the board before the timer reaches zero.

How to play:
- On desktop, click and drag a block with the mouse. On mobile, touch and drag it with one finger.
- Move each block to a gate of the same color. A correctly matched block leaves the board.
- Plan your moves so blocks do not trap or block one another.
- Use available boosters when you need help with a difficult layout.
- Clear all blocks to win the level and unlock the next puzzle.

Primary controls: Left mouse button and drag on desktop; touch and drag on mobile.
