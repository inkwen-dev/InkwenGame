## About

Fruit Drop is an addictive fruit merge puzzle game! Drop kawaii fruits into the jar, merge identical ones to grow bigger fruits and score higher. Inspired by Suika Watermelon Game, Fruit Drop adds unique twists: use CHANGE to swap your next fruit and SHAKE to rattle the jar for surprise merges! Physics-based gameplay, dreamy color transitions and a cute kawaii style make every session relaxing yet challenging. Can you fill the jar without overflowing? Play free online, no download needed. Mobile &amp; desktop friendly HTML5 game by MAPI GAMES.
