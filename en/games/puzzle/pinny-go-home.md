## About

Pinny Go Home is a fun 2D physics puzzle adventure where you help a cute penguin trapped inside a giant water bubble return to the icy land. Shoot small bubbles to create force, control Pinny&rsquo;s direction, avoid dangerous obstacles, and use underwater currents to your advantage. Master the perfect angle and timing to overcome challenging levels in this creative, addictive physics-based puzzle game!
