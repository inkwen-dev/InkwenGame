## About

Crack n Pop is a fast-paced, fun-filled arcade game where your reflexes and aim are put to the test! Eggs are falling from above, and it&rsquo;s your job to blast them out of the sky before they hit your cannon. Take control of your trusty cannon, aim carefully, and shoot at the right moment to crack the eggs and rack up points. Miss too many&hellip; and it&rsquo;s game over! As you progress, the speed increases and patterns get trickier, keeping every round exciting and challenging. Game Features: Simple tap-to-shoot controls Fast, addictive gameplay Colorful eggs with fun cracking effects Increasing difficulty for endless challenge
