## About

Bubble Shooter Winter Pack is a classical bubble shooter game with winter theme. Aim and release the bubble in order to group it with other similar bubbles. Your goal is to collect all the bubbles within the allowed time. Don’t let the bubbles reach the border. Complete all the 48 levels to win this game.
