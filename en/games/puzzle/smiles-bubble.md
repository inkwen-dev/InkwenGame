## About

If you love emoji and like games, this super fun emoji game is for you ! The goal of the game is super simple : you just need to find similar emoji and connect them in less than 3 straight lines. If you are able to link and connect all emojis icons during the limited time, then you win and advance to the next level with new emoticons and smileys to connect. Every level is getting a bit more difficult but it's always super fun to play EMOJI LINK.
