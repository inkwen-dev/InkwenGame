## About

Trace, learn, and create in this fun and colorful educational adventure! Follow guided lines to complete A-Z uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers 0-9, words, and shapes while hearing clear pronunciation and enjoying bright effects. Choose from vibrant colored pencils, use helpful hints when needed, and improve your rating with every perfect trace. Designed especially for kids, this interactive experience makes learning feel like play. Smooth controls, rewarding feedback, and endless practice opportunities keep young learners engaged and motivated. How many letters and shapes can you master?
