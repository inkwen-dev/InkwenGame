## About

Christmas Math is mathematics game for kids. Even though it's a Christmas holiday you can have fun with math games. Practice math in this Christmas game. In 60 seconds try to answer as many tasks as you can correctly. You need to add, subtract, divide and multiply. Click on the math mark that you think will match the result and test your knowledge.
