## About

Spectral Seek is a mysterious object-finding game set inside a haunted mansion filled with secrets, shadows, and whispers from the past. Explore eerie rooms and forgotten halls as you search for hidden objects scattered throughout the mansion. Each level challenges your observation skills, blending spooky atmosphere with clever hiding spots. The deeper you venture, the trickier the finds become, testing your focus and attention to detail. Every discovered object brings you closer to uncovering the mansion&rsquo;s secrets.
