## About

Single Stroke Draw Line is a puzzle game where you must draw in one line and connect all the dots without any route repetition. Give your brain an exercise and improve your IQ. This is a mind-challenging game with very simple rules. It starts with very easy levels and increases in difficulty as you progress in the game. Youve got two game modes to choose from: Classic Mode for chill vibes and Timer Mode for those who love a good challenge. Take your pick and dive in!
