## How to play

Tap or click an available product to send it to the tray. Match three identical products in the tray to clear them. Remove every product before the timer expires, and avoid filling all tray slots. Use boosters when a level gets difficult. Controls: left mouse button on desktop; tap on touch devices.

## About

Fresh Drop: Triple Match is a colorful 3D puzzle game where players collect matching fresh products, build sets of three, and clear every item before time runs out. Handcrafted levels introduce new obstacles, boosters, and satisfying effects while keeping each session quick and easy to learn. Progress is saved automatically, so players can continue on desktop or mobile.
