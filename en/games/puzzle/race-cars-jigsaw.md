## About

This is jigsaw game with racing cars. You can play in twelve levels. When you start the game in first level choose in which mode you want to play. There is three modes easy with 25 pieces, medium with 49 pieces or hard with 100 pieces. Finish the first level to go to the next one. Can you solve all 12 levels? Use mouse to drag and drop pieces in the right places. There is no time limit during play this game.
