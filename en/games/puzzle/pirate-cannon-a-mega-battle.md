## How to play

Sink Enemy Pirate Ships! Command the Pirate Cannon A Mega Battle, the thrilling bubble shooter puzzle where you launch numbered cannonballs from your mega cannon at the bottom to match and burst clusters of hex-shaped enemy pirate boats with identical numbers floating above the water. Clear all ships before your balls run out to claim victory and dominate epic sea levels!

Desktop Controls:
Mouse: Drag to aim the cannon, release to fire the numbered cannonball.

Mobile Controls:
Touch: Drag to aim the cannon, release to fire the numbered cannonball.

## About

Pirate Cannon A Mega Battle is a thrilling pirate-themed bubble shooter puzzle game where players fire numbered cannonballs from a mega cannon at the bottom to match and sink clusters of enemy hex-shaped pirate boats bearing the same numbers floating above the water, conquering challenging levels in an epic sea battle.
