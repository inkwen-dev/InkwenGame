## About

Scientist and Robot is an exciting puzzle adventure where you play as a scientist searching for the missing parts of a broken robot. Explore laboratory rooms, solve clever puzzles, unlock hidden areas, and collect useful tools to progress. Find every robot part and assemble them to bring your creation back to life. Test your logic, observation, and problem-solving skills in this fun science-themed puzzle game!
