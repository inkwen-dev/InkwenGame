## About

Ocean is a matching puzzle game developed by ANV Games. Sharks in the ocean take over village after village, and the precious marine life cannot defend themselves without your clear instructions. Ocean offers a refreshing take on the classic match-three genre by enabling chains, combos, upgrades, and unlockable sea creatures! Are you ready to go through the oceans?
