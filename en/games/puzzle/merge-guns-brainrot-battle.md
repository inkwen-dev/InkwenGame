## How to play

Tap / Click the Buy button — purchase a box
Tap / Click a box — open it (may require multiple taps)
Drag & Drop an item — move weapons between slots
Drag an item onto an identical one — merge and upgrade
Drag a weapon into a slot around the boss — it starts attacking automatically
Tap boost buttons (Damage x2, Auto Open) — activate temporary power-ups

## About

Open. Merge. Destroy Bosses.
Dive into a fast-paced merge arcade where your goal is simple: assemble a deadly arsenal and take down bosses one after another.

Buy mystery boxes, open them, and merge identical weapons to upgrade their power and unlock devastating stack combos. Strategically place guns, bombs, and knives into combat slots around the boss — they’ll attack automatically while you focus on optimizing your setup and tactics.
Face off against bizarre and powerful bosses like Tralalero Tralala, Tung Tung Sahur, and Cappuccino Assassino—each with unique behavior and escalating difficulty that will push your build to the limit.
Watch out for rage phases, grab limited-time gift events, boost your damage, and activate auto-opening to speed up your progression. Every decision matters as difficulty scales endlessly.
Defeat bosses, earn rewards, and see how far your build can go in an infinite challenge of power and strategy.
Complete the tutorial, master the coin economy, and prove that your loadout can destroy any titan.
