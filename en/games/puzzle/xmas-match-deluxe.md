## How to play

Use mouse or touch to play this game

## About

Swap adjacent gifts in order to make a horizontal or vertical row of 3 identical gifts. To complete a level you need to collect required number of gifts in limited turns. Collect more than 5 gifts to get a spell (power-up) which will show up on the right side of the screen. Cast spells by dragging and dropping it on the target. The "hint" and "double" spells are cast by clicking on them. Click next to know about spells.
