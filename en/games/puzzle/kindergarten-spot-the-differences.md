## About

Ready to play KinderGarten Spot the Differences? Grab your mouse and get ready to hunt differences in a challenging, fun, and FREE puzzle game! Your goal is simple: look at the pictures, find the differences, then click or tap each one you locate. Every level will present you with two beautiful photos that look almost the same. There are several small differences between them, however. It's your job to spot them as quickly as you can. It's easy to play casually, but tough to master!
