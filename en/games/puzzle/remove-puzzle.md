## About

Remove Puzzle is a very unique online puzzle game designed for kids. You can play it online for free. Remove all the squares on the screen and you will pass a level. You are only allowed to remove squares along the directions where nothing blocks them. At each level, the squares are placed in different combinations. And each level is harder than the former level. Good luck!
