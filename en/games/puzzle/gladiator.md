Played with mouse

## About

It is an exciting game where you fight to the death with your opponent and the competition is at its peak. After detecting your opponent's weak point, you must attack by matching the buttons that are your opponent's weak points. If you do not attack in 4 moves, your opponent's turn passes and he attacks you. Different character choices and in-game rewards are waiting for you in the game. A game where learning will be short and fun will be long awaits you!
