## How to play

Desktop: Left-click anywhere above the jar to drop a vegetable. Mobile: Tap anywhere on the screen to drop a vegetable.

Goal: Drop and merge identical vegetables to evolve them into larger ones. Keep merging to score points, but be careful — if the vegetables overflow the top of the jar, it's Game Over! Spend your earned coins in the shop for upgrades, and use crystals to open boxes.
