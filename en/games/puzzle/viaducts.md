## How to play

Desktop:
- Click the blocks to rotate them
- Use Scroll Wheel to zoom in and out
- Click + Drag to pan camera around

Phone/Tablet:
- Tap the blocks to rotate them
- Pinch to zoom in and out
- Swipe to pan camera around
