## About

Rooster Ranch is a relaxing egg-matching puzzle on a sunny farm. Tap an egg to send it along the open path into your nest tray. Collect three eggs of the same color to hatch a cute chick. An egg can only leave the board when its path to the tray is clear, so plan every move. If the tray fills up, the level is lost. Clear crates and blocked paths across 1000 levels. Use boosters when you get stuck: Magnet pulls a matching egg, Undo sends the last egg back, and Hammer opens a crate. Complete daily quests, spin the lucky wheel and collect the hourly reward chest.
