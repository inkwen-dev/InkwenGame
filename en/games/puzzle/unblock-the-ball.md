Mouse or tap to play

## About

Unblock The Ball is a simple and addictive puzzle game. There are many pipes made of different materials in the game. Players can only move the wooden pipes so that all the pipes are connected together smoothly so that the ball can be pushed to the end. There are stars to collect in the process of reaching the destination. The more stars, the better. Come to join us!
