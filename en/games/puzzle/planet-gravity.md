## About

"Planet Gravity" is a physical base game. We simulate asteroids flying over space. And create the solar system step by step. On each level, we will have some missions for you to finished. The goal of this game is increase your interesting on universe and helping you to the pass the time. Overall it is a Sandbox universe base, object gravity simulation game. And if you want to create the universe on your own freely, we have two games suggested to you. Find "myDream Universe" or "Sandbox Planet" on Appstore.
