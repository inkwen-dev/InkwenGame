## How to play

Use your mouse or finger (on touch devices) to move and place the blocks on the board. Arrange them to form complete lines horizontally or vertically. When a line is completed, it will disappear, giving you more space to keep playing. Try to clear as many lines as possible without running out of space. The more lines you remove, the higher your score!
