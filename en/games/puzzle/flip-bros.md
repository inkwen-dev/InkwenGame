## About

Jump, flip and and shoot yourself at the right time! Flip Bros is a funny platform game where you take down your enemies by launching yourself at them! All you need to do is to jump, rotate, and shoot yourself at the right time. The key to success is to maintain your momentum and seize the perfect moment! Seems simple, right? Be careful with all kinds of obstacles that could hurt you or stop you. Let us flip together!
