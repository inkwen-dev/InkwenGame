## About

M2 Blocks is an addictive and strategic puzzle game that challenges your logic, planning skills, and reflexes. Your goal is simple but engaging: merge matching numbered blocks to create higher values and score as many points as possible. Each move matters &mdash; one wrong decision can quickly fill the board, so think ahead and play smart. With clean visuals, smooth controls, and fast gameplay, M2 Blocks is perfect for quick sessions or long puzzle-solving marathons. Whether you are a casual player or a puzzle expert, the game offers a satisfying challenge that keeps you coming back for more.
