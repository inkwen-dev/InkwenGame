## About

Welcome to Number Merge 2048. This is an amazing puzzle game. Connect the numbers and don’t run out of moves! Make lines by draging your finger of the same numbers. The higher the line, the more points you get. As you connect the same numbers, they add up to multiples of 2. Have fun connecting numbers. get a high score and challenge your friends!
