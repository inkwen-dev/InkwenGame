## About

“Neon Shot” is a very fun casual game in NEON Style that will test your skills and reasoning. With a “Drag and Drop” system you can pull the elastic to make the ball bounce and knock down the targets. Try to finish each level with as few balls as possible to get more points. You can pop boxes of TNT explosives to break more than one target at the same time and save balls. The designs are beautiful and colorful.
