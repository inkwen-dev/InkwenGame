Clear the road by dragging ice blocks around using your left mouse button or finger

## About

Lost Yeti is a 2D puzzle game created by Neutronized. In this game, you are tasked to help our lost yeti return home by moving blocks of ice. Start with the retro puzzles in Snowy Hills where you can slide frozen rows and columns to clear the path, and move on to more colorful worlds. You must find the quickest way home while eating all the snacks in your way. Explore all 3 worlds, 60 levels, and various types of captivating enemies and puzzles. Are you ready for this Yeti?
