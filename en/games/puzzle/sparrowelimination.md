## About

An innovative mahjong themed sliding elimination game, the core gameplay is for players to find two identical mahjong tiles, slide them to the same horizontal or vertical row, and click on match elimination. Kuai Ten is a puzzle elimination gameplay that combines mahjong elements with numerical operations. The core rule is to add up the number tiles of the same color to 10 to eliminate them. Characters without numbers (such as whiteboard, Dongfeng, etc.) need to be paired with two identical tiles to eliminate them.
