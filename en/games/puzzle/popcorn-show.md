## About

Popcorn Shop is a Hypercausal puzzle game!Keep the barrel full of popcorn. Don't discard anything and get the highest score! The popcorn cup is sad because it's empty. Your job is to draw a line, fill the popcorn cup with popcorn, and smile again! Thousands of unique levels can be played with ease and fun using simple rules, but can be challenging.
