## About

Math Game 3D is a fun and educational HTML5 puzzle game that challenges your math skills. Drag the numbered cubes onto the correct math expressions to solve each equation. Every correct answer removes a gray tile and gradually reveals the hidden picture underneath. Enjoy colorful graphics, smooth gameplay, relaxing music, and responsive controls in this engaging learning adventure for all ages.
