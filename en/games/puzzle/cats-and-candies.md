## About

Cats And Candies is a bright and exciting match-3 puzzle game. Match three or more candies in a row, create powerful combos, and complete levels while helping adorable cats collect sweet treats. The game features 60 levels divided into 3 boxes: Wooden, Stone, and Fire (20 levels each). Every new box introduces fresh goals, obstacles, bonuses, and increasing difficulty. Colorful graphics, a cozy atmosphere, and a fully Russian interface make the game perfect for relaxing and training your logic skills. Complete all the boxes from Wood to Fire and help the cats gather every candy!
