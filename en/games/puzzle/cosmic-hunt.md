## About

Cosmic Hunt blasts off into a galaxy of hidden words! Explore 8 space-themed categories - From distant Planets and blazing Stars to legendary Astronauts and cutting-edge Space Tech. Drag across the letter grid to find words hidden in every direction. Race against the clock in Hard Mode or take your time in Normal Mode. Use hints when the cosmos gets tough, climb the leaderboard and beat your own record. Simple to learn, impossible to put down. One small tap for you, one giant hunt for mankind!
