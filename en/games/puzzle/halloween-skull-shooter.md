## About

You in the game Halloween Skull Shooter will have to destroy bubbles all and not let them touch the ground. You will see in front of you on the screen bubbles falling to the ground. They will have different colors. You will have to throw single charges at them. They will also have color. You will need to fall into the exact same cluster of objects with the given charge. Then these objects will explode, and you will get points.
