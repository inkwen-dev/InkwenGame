## About

Starweave is a space-themed puzzle game offers a relaxing yet challenging journey through the cosmos.

The universe has gone dark and it's your goal to reconnect hidden constellations to light up the night sky.

Each level has a unique hidden constellation that you light up by linking stars together.

Space is not empty you have to be careful from stellar, orbits and cosmic friction.

Click and drag to weave a glowing trail between the stars.

As you glide through space, you will need to crush floating obstacles and navigate clever pathways to complete each celestial pattern.

Disconnect from stress of the day, connect stars and reveal ancient constellations in space sky.
