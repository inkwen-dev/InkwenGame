Tap on the screen to play this game

## About

As the title of the game says - Dot Tap is a touching puzzle game where your job is to tap on the screen when the blue ball should match the purple ball. The position of the purple ball will be changed every time after successful matched balls. Sometimes will be very close to the blue ball and sometimes will be far away. So you need to follow the screen all time and to watch on the position of the purple ball.
