## How to play

🌟 How to Play

👉 Tap any stack of hexagon tiles to pick it up.

🎯 Choose the best spot to drop the stack.

🎨 Match colors—tiles of the same color automatically merge!

🧠 Think ahead to avoid blocking your moves.

⚡ Use boosters when stuck to shuffle or clear tiles.
