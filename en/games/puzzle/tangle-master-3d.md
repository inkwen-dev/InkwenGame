## About

Tangle Master 3D is a very interesting decryption game. The player untie all the wires entangled by moving the plug. The basic gameplay is that you need to move the plug on the right to the left. Some of the levels in the first half are very simple, but the levels in the second half are very difficult. You will have a wonderful time in Tangle Master 3D! Come and use your wisdom to complete all the levels!
