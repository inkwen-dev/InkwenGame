Drag to aim, then release to launch your bubble into the board. Land it next to matching bubbles to merge and pop them — clear each level's goals before you run out of moves!

## About

Bubble Smash is a relaxing yet addictive bubble-merge puzzle game. Aim and launch bubbles into the playfield, merge matching ones to pop them in satisfying chains, and clear each level's goals before you run out of moves. Chain pops together to trigger explosive combos, unleash power-ups when you're stuck, and work your way across a colorful level map. Easy to pick up, tricky to master — one more pop is never enough.
