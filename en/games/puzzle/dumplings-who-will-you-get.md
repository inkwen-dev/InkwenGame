## How to play

1. Tap the steamer to get a box, then tap the box to open it.
2. Drag one dumpling onto another of the same level to merge them into a new character.
3. Tap unlocked dumplings to earn coins.
4. Spend coins on more boxes and kitchen upgrades, complete orders and grow your collection.
5. Every 7th box is rare. Keep merging until you unlock all 12 levels.

Use the mouse on desktop or tap and drag with your finger on mobile.
