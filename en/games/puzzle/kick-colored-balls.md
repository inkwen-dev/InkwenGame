## About

The goal at this game is to kick colored balls. On both sides of the game area will be balls. On the left side will be the yellow ball and on the right the blue one. Tap on left or right side and the balls will hit. In the middle of the screen will move balls in line in yellow and blue color only. With the balls that are on the side you have to destroy the coming balls. But the yellow ball will destroy the yellow ball only and the blue ball the blue one. If you mix the blows you will lose.
