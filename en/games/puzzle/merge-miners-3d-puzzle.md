## About

Classic merge game with a twist. Easy to pick up and play! Become rich by buying tools, upgrading them, and letting physics do the rest. Start your mining empire today! Merge and Miner is an addictive merging game where you begin with a single miner tool and must dig deep by breaking pixel blocks to make a mining empire. In each level, you can purchase a new miner tool and merge it with your existing mining tool to upgrade it into a more powerful and advanced miner tool.
