## About

Aliens in Chains is a new casual game on desktop and mobile you can play at Superkidgames.com for free, this game is different and unique adventure. Cut chains and throw combos to progress in your mission and to stop aliens getting away with it. Go across the galaxy through more than 700 levels. Get ready to search for aliens and pop connections they make with your sharp clicks. The goal of the game is to collect enough aliens of certain type and complete objectives. Your moves are limited, so don’t get addicted while collecting other aliens. Try to spend less moves and get more points.
