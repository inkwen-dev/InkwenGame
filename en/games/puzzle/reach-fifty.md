## About

Reach fifty is a game about puzzle and reflexion in which you must regroup numbers to create sums of fifty. You have the possibility to slide the different forms and numbers, horizontally and vertically, and add numbers to each other. If you reach the fifty number, this form freezes and cannot be used anymore. Regroup all forms to reach the end of the level.
