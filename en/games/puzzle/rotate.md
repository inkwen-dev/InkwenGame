## About

In this game you can choose to play with a circle, triangle, square or hexagon. They all have spikes on the inside and a small ball that you must control to avoid colliding with the spikes. Choose a shape you want to play with and rotate left or right to avoid spikes but also to collect diamonds and other achievements that will appear, such as ball protection, extra life and more.
