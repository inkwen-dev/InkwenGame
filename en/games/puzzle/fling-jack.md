## About

Fling Jack is an exciting Halloween-themed game where players launch Jack, a mischievous jack-o-lantern, from platform to platform, collecting coins along the way. Set in spooky environments like haunted forests and eerie graveyards, the goal is to navigate through challenges and avoid spooky obstacles, all while gathering as many coins as possible. The game&rsquo;s simple yet engaging mechanics make it a fun and thrilling adventure, offering endless entertainment for players of all ages, especially during the Halloween season.
