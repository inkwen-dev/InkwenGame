## About

Another online game in the form of a puzzle, in which the player should collect all the elements in one picture. To assemble a full-fledged image with a drawn Remy from the animated film "Ratatouille", take each piece and attach it to the picture, relying on the outlines of the pieces in the picture. A suitable game for children from the youngest age, allows you to improve attention, intelligence and speed. The game has a bright interface and several levels.
