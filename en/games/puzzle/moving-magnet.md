## About

Moving Magnet is an engaging logic puzzle game where you control and push magnetic blocks to their designated positions. Utilize the attraction and repulsion forces of magnets, calculate the optimal path to overcome obstacles, and complete each level. Every level presents a new challenge with increasing difficulty, helping to develop thinking and problem-solving skills. The gameplay is simple but incredibly captivating!
