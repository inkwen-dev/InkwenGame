## About

A short pattern of connected dots appears on the screen. Watch it carefully, remember the shape, then recreate it after the lines disappear. Each level becomes more challenging: more lines appear, the grid changes size, diagonals are introduced, and longer connections become possible. After the grid expands, the difficulty eases slightly so the new layout feels fair, then gradually rises again. The game trains visual memory, focus, and precision. The rules are simple: look, remember, and repeat the pattern as accurately as possible.
