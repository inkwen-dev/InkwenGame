## About

The rules are very simple - do not let the constantly moving ball hit the ball that does not match its color. The task is even easier - to score maximum points. Be extremely careful and move the top or bottom rows of balls in time to prevent the game from being stopped. Your best result will remain recorded so you can see your growth dynamics.
