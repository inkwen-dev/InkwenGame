## About

Draw Surfer is a free casual online drawing game for boys. You need to move your fingers to complete the route drawing so that the character can keep surfing. There will be a lot of obstacles in the course, you need to have a certain level of ingenuity and quick reaction so that the stickman can easily avoid obstacles to reach his destination. Be careful, don't let him disappear from your sight!
