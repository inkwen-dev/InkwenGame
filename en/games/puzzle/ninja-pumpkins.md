## About

Ninja Pumpkin is interesting platform game! Use the up arrow key of keyboard or up arrow on the right side of the screen to jump and use the down arrow key of keyboard or down arrow on the left side of the screen to slide. Every 30 coins will give you one extra life. This game has 10 levels in total. Your final score is based on the remaining time that you have at the level’s end.
