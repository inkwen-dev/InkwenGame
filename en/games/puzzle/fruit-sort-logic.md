## About

Fruit Sort Logic is a fun and relaxing puzzle game where you sort colorful fruit juices on shelves. Move the juices around and match three of the same kind to clear a shelf. Space is limited, so each move matters. Clear all shelves to finish the level and unlock new ones. With smooth controls, bright visuals, and endless levels, the game is easy to play and satisfying to master. Perfect for a calm but clever puzzle experience.
