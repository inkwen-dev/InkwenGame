## How to play

Tap or drag to drop and merge fruits Match the same fruits to clear them Tap a wall with a Spirit Stone to break it and use Clear Shuffle and Fire Spell to get past tricky boards

## About

Fruit Immortal is a relaxing fruit-merge puzzle with a cultivation twist. Drop and match fruits to clear the board, collect rare golden and spirit fruits, and break through 36 cultivation realms &mdash; from Qi Refining all the way to Wuji. With 60+ levels, satisfying combos, golden-fruit blasts, and special items like Clear, Shuffle and Fire Spell, its easy to pick up but hard to master. One-finger controls, endless fun. Can you ascend to the very top?
