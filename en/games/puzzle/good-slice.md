## About

Come to start a juicy cut fruit journey at Good Slice! This is a casual puzzle game that will test your cutting skills. Your task is to swipe the screen to cut the fruit into perfect pieces and let them fall smoothly into the juicer. The more precise the cut, the more juice youll get!
