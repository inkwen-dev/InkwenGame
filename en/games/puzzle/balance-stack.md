## About

The player needs to stack the Shapes One by One without making them fall. After all Shapes are stacked, a short timer starts to make sure the stacked structure is stable. If one of the pieces falls, the game is over and the player can retry the level. Consists Of Several Shapes, Cube, Triangle, Cylinder, Sphere, and Rectangle. you will play it randomly so it will be more challenging and fun at a higher level up to Level 50.
