## About

This is a Valentine’s Day special Bubble Shooter game in which you will find a rotating wheel of heart shape balls. To complete the game you need to collect all the balls on the wheel. Shoot a balls towards the wheel to make the group of at least three balls of the same color. Your points are reducing with the time, so finish the game early to save more points.
