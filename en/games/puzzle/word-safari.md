## About

Word Safari takes you on a wild hunt through letter jungles! Spot hidden words across a colorful grid - forwards, backwards, diagonal, any direction. Six exciting categories to explore: Animals, Food, Space, Ocean, Cartoons, Jobs and more. Six languages supported so the whole family can play. Each word you find triggers a burst of confetti and particles - because every discovery deserves a celebration! Simple to pick up, impossible to put down. Whether youve got 2 minutes or 2 hours, Word Safari keeps your brain sharp and your eyes sharper. Ready to hunt? The words are hiding - go find them!
