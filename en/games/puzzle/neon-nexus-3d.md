## About

Neon Circuit is a relaxing, glowing puzzle game where your goal is to connect all the neon wires to power the circuits. Rotate the nodes to create a continuous flow of energy from the power source to all the targets. With an infinite number of procedurally generated levels, the difficulty gradually increases.
