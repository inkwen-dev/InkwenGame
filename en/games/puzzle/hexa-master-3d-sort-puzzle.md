## How to play

The game offers an exciting twist on the classic sorting puzzles. Get ready to dive into a colorful world of hexagon tiles! Your task is to match and merge these tiles to achieve your goals. Experience the thrill of shuffling, matching and organizing the hexagons. Enjoy the calming gameplay and the satisfaction of stacking tiles perfectly.
