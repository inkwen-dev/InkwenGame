## About

Mahjong Pyramids is a quick-fire mahjong puzzle game in which you need to match tiles in pairs as quickly as you can to complete each level. Each tile has an object etched onto its face, find the two identical tiles to match them together and remove them from the puzzle. Once you have no tiles left you have completed the level.
