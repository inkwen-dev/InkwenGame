## About

MergeTrainLastRail is a relaxing merge puzzle game set aboard a mysterious train. Drag and drop items to merge them into higher-tier objects, fulfill passenger orders, and unlock new chains as you progress. Discover dozens of merge chains, complete story chapters, collect rewards, and expand your board. Features: drag-to-merge gameplay, order fulfillment, chapter-based progression, daily events and bonuses, energy system, and satisfying merge effects. Easy to learn, hard to put down &mdash; perfect for quick sessions or long relaxing play.
