## About

Explore the world of treasure hunting adventure with a smooth gameplay with Prince Arthur who went for the treasure hunting. who was desperately follow his father&rsquo;s wish to brought their treasure.There are several colours of Treasure available in the game.For each colour of treasure ,the same colour bowl will appear to collect.Player has to pull off the right pin to release the treasure in the right bowl.Be careful while pulling the pin if pin pulling goes wrong the treasure goes to the wrong bowl and then the game will restart. Main object of this game is Treasure .Pull the pin for the same colour treasure matching with bowl to get reward
