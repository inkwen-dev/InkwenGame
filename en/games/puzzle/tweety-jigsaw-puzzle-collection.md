## About

This cool puzzle game will captivate even the most restless players who do not like to give up when faced with difficulties. The task is very simple - to collect a puzzle with a small Twitty. In front of you will be a set of elements and the main background on which you need to install everything. Use the mouse to drag the parts one by one and, if necessary, turn them over.
