## How to play

Aim, pull back, and launch dice across the board like a slingshot. When two matching dice touch, they merge into the next number and fly off to find their own match, triggering chain-reaction cascades. Each level gives you a limited number of throws and a target score to beat — plan your shots carefully to set off the biggest possible cascade and clear the board.

Mobile Controls:
Drag Back: Pull the die back to aim, like a slingshot.
Release: Let go to launch the die in the aimed direction.

## About

When two matching dice touch, they merge into the next number — and that one flies off to find its own twin, kicking off a chain reaction. A single well-aimed shot can set off half the table in a cascade of merges. You won't be able to look away. 😌
