## About

Brik Brok is an enjoyable puzzle game where you are a paddle Destroy all the blocks using your happy paddle to hit your square-face-ball, like in a weird tennis match! Hit and destroy all the blocks as soon as you can. After any bounce the ball gains speed, making it harder and harder to intercept it!
