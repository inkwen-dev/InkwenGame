## About

Time Control is a puzzle game where you need to control the red ball. She will move vertically from one point to other. When the ball hits the point you will earn a score. But the squares will rotate around the ball path, so you need to avoid them. For that, you need to control the speed of the ball. Click or tap on the screen to slow the movement of the ball and realize to move normally. Only in that way you can avoid collision with squares. If you hit at the squares the game will be over. So try to do more points as you can and control the ball successfully.
