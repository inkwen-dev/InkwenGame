## About

Seaweed Aqua is a relaxing puzzle game where you untangle seashells stuck in rubber bands under the ocean. Observe carefully and untangle them in the correct order to avoid further entanglement and free all the shells. Each level has a different layout, increasing difficulty, requiring logical thinking and patience. The gameplay is simple, addictive, and the ocean scenery is vibrant.
