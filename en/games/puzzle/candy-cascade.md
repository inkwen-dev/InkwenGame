## How to play

Tap a candy and drag it towards a neighbour - the two swap places. The move only counts if it forms a line of three or more identical sweets: they disappear, the candies above fall down and may form new lines. The goal and the moves left are shown at the top; finish the goal before the moves run out to complete the level. Four in a row make a rocket, a cross-shaped match makes a bomb, five in a row make a rainbow candy; swap two special candies to fire them together. Ice holds a candy in place but still counts in a match, crates never join a match, locks open when you finish the goal for their colour. Boosters at the bottom: hammer, rocket, shuffle - restock them with gold or by watching an ad. Mouse or touch only, no keyboard required.

## About

Candy Cascade is a classic match-3 puzzle about sweets. Swap neighbouring candies, line up three or more and complete the level goal within the moves you are given. 100 levels with different tasks: collect candies of one colour, break the ice, smash the crates. Longer lines give special candies: four in a row make a rocket, a cross-shaped match makes a bomb, five in a row make a rainbow candy that removes every sweet of one colour. When moves run short, the hammer, rocket and shuffle boosters help. Daily quests, a leaderboard and three languages.
