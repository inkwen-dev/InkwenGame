## How to play

Click on a group of 3 or more of the same items to remove them Collect as many flowers as indicated to let the Bee pass and try to reach the Bear Drop the spade down to remove the trees

## About

Let your bee deliver the honey to the hungry Bear. Click on a connected group of 3 or more of the same flowers to remove that exact flower just in front of the Bee. If you have collected enough flowers, the Bee will move forward to the next flower. The honey needs to be delivered by the Bear before you run out of moves.
