## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Together with a team of adventure seekers you will land on an island where, according to legend, pirates have buried treasure chests. You in the game Find the Treasure will need to find them all. You will see the playing field divided into square cells. Somewhere under one of them is a treasure chest. With the help of special arrows you will need to explore all the cells and find the desired item. Remember that there may be various traps in the ground that you will need to avoid. Heave fun!
