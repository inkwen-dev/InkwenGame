## About

In Swap Pins, dive into a challenge of strategy and colors! Match pins with matching bases by skillfully moving them around the board. You can only move pins according to their matching colors. Be careful not to move a piece and run out of moves, this will result in Game Over. Test your mental agility as you progress through 15 increasingly challenging levels. With vibrant graphics and engaging gameplay, get ready for addictive fun and sharp thinking! Are you ready?
