## About

A logic puzzle where every move changes the whole situation on the board. Arrows are scattered across a grid. Tap an arrow and it flies off the board in the direction it points. But theres a catch: if another arrow blocks its path to the edge, the move fails and costs you an attempt. So first you have to work out the right order. Simple mechanics, short rounds, and levels where it feels good to find the solution step by step. No timer, no rush: just logic, observation, and picking the right next move.
