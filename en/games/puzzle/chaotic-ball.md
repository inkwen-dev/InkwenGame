## About

Chaotic Ball is a game where you need to collect the stars with small orange ball. But the ball is all chaotic. She make chaotic movies. So you need to control the ball and to go to the stars. There will be big balls and you need to avoid them too. If you hit with small ball into big blue ball the game will over. You can crash three times before game will over. Collect more stars as you can and get the best score.
