## About

Dinosaurs are ancient and powerful creatures. They lived on Earth long before the advent of mankind. These powerful titans were of enormous size and very diverse species: triceratops, Tyrannosaurs, allosaurs, iguanodons, diplodocs, and others. Some of them were herbivores, but some of them fed on the flesh. In the game "Puzzle Dinosaurs", you can slightly expand your knowledge of dinosaurs, adding puzzles with different types of them. You need to drag the puzzle elements onto the field. When the picture is folded, you can go to the next level, where you will be waiting for a new dinosaur. This game will open for you the wild world of ancient reptiles. Have a nice game!
