## About

Container Trucks Jigsaw is jigsaw puzzle game with container trucks. You can play in 12 levels in this game. Select image that is free to play and chose mode to play. Put the pieces in right position to get the image with container truck. Drag pieces on the screen to play this game. Unlock all images and have fun in this jigsaw game.
