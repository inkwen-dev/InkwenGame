## About

Pull The Trigger :collect Bubble - challenge yourself, your brain and your friends in this game filled with satisfying graphics and innovative levels! You are against physics and difficult levels - WHO WILL WIN? All the balls must go into the basket ... Can you remove and perform the nails in the correct order? It should be simple: gravity pulls the balls down into the basket .. But then the nails are all right! Can you help the balls and turn the pins over and take the balls where they should be? But wait: there is another cheat level! Sometimes some balls are colorless: they have to touch a ball of color before the nails enter, so the color also spreads to them. It's simple but very difficult!
