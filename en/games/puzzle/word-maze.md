## About

Word Maze is a brain-boosting puzzle game where words and mazes collide! Sharpen your vocabulary and problem-solving skills as you navigate twisting paths of letters to form words and unlock each level. How to play: Swipe through the letter maze to connect and create valid words. Solve the puzzle by finding all hidden words. Use hints if you get stuck, and keep your mind sharp as levels get harder! With a clean interface, relaxing music, and thousands of challenging levels, Word Maze offers endless fun for word lovers of all ages.
