## About

Put The Candy is an engaging puzzle game where you arrange colorful candies into the correct test tubes. Observe carefully, choose the right order of moves to complete each level without getting stuck. The difficulty gradually increases with new challenges, helping to develop logical thinking and sorting skills. The gameplay is simple and relaxing but highly addictive, suitable for all ages!
