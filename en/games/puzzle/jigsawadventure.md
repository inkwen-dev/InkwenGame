## About

Jigsaw Adventure is a jigsaw puzzle game full of retro adventure atmosphere. Players will piece together various mysterious patterns in a scene like yellowed parchment, from castle silhouettes to fantasy treasures. Each successful is like unlocking an adventure anecdote, and while training their observation skills, they start a different jigsaw puzzle adventure journey.
