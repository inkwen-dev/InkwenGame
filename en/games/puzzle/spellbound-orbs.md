## How to play

Aim Your Wand/Cannon: Drag your mouse or finger across the screen to target the descending clusters of magical orbs.

Match 3 or More: Launch your charged orb into matching color clusters to burst them and clear the magical board.

Bank Wall Shots: Ricochet orbs off side boundaries to reach tucked-away bubbles and clear difficult angles.

Trigger Spells & Boosters: Clear large clusters or chain consecutive pops to activate explosive fireball runes, color bombs, and lightning spells.

Prevent Board Overflow: Keep bursting orbs before the descending ceiling breaches the bottom boundary line!
