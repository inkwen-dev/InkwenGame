## About

Animal Merge is an incredibly fun and addictive casual puzzle game that will keep you entertained for hours! Drop cute and colorful animals into the container, match identical ones together, and watch them magically merge and evolve into bigger, more powerful creatures. The gameplay is simple to learn but surprisingly deep and challenging to master &mdash; making it perfect for players of all ages!
