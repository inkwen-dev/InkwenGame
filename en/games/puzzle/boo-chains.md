## About

Link cute Halloween monsters with the same number or one apart and watch them blow up! Long chains create pumpkin bombs, lightning and a wild ghost that explode together for huge combos. Travel across a spooky map with 5 zones, free monsters trapped in webs, chains, tombstones and armor, and beat a giant boss every 10 stages. Unlock 5 monster heroes with special powers, buy upgrades, complete daily missions and grab your nightly gift. Can you survive the whole night?
