Use mouse or tap the screen

## About

The main characters from the cartoon "Super Wings" invite you to solve new exciting puzzles with them. Your attention will be offered puzzles, in the form of a slider, which you need to collect so that the result is a complete picture. You are given a picture of mixed pieces vertically or horizontally. You just need to move them to get a beautiful whole image. Nothing complicated, but not as simple as it seems at first glance. In total, you will have access to more than ten images that need to be folded. Have fun and collect a whole collection of pictures with your favorite Super Wings heroes. Have a nice game!
