Touch in the ropes to make new arrangements

## About

Rope draw is a puzzle game in wich you will push ropes to make new arrangements with them. Your objective is to copy the arrangement that will appear above. If needed, there is a hint button that will show you the first moves. There are 120 different levels and the 'Fun' mode (unlocked after beating level 40), in which you can insert new ropes or even push them as you wish.
