## About

Logical Journey is puzzle game where you need to pass levels with obstacles and enemies. Collect all apples before go to the next levels. You can move boxes to make your way, to smash them and to jump over levels. It is super funny game with lot of levels and you need to activate your brain to pass all levels. Make your strategy and win in this awesome game.
