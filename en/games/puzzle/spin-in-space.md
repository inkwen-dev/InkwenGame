## About

The smaller the planet the more points you get and points are also awarded for distance travelled. Keep an eye on you fuel but don’t worry we have coded it so it drops between 2 planets so its always accessible. Planets will start to disappear making it harder the longer you survive. It's a never ending game made harder by increasing speed and fewer planets.
