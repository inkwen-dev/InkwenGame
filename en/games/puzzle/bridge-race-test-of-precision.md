## About

Bridge Race is an exciting online game that challenges your focus, timing, and quick reflexes. The main goal is to guide your bridge safely across different platforms by passing it through various shaped gaps &mdash; without crashing! As the bridge moves forward, players must quickly adjust its height or width to match the upcoming obstacles. Each level becomes more challenging, with faster speeds and trickier shapes that test your reaction time and hand-eye coordination. One wrong move, and your bridge will crash!
