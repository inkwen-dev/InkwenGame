## About

Welcome to Big Vowels Fun &ndash; The Ultimate Vowel Match-3 Challenge! Step into a bright and playful world where vowels take center stage! In Big Vowels Fun, your mission is to match A, E, I, O, and U tiles to rack up points, clear the board, and race against the clock across multiple exciting levels. How to Play: Match 3 or more of the same vowel tiles to score points. Beat the timer by making quick, smart matches. Advance through multiple levels, each with increasing speed and tricky patterns. Chain combos and power-ups to boost your score and keep the game flowing.
