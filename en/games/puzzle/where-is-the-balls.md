Mouse and touch

## About

In this game, initially the ball is placed under one of the cups (you can choose between 3 and 6 cups), which will alternate positions between them, randomly. At the end of the movements, the player must indicate which cup contains the ball. As the player guesses correctly, the exchanges happen more quickly.
