## About

Aim of the game Milk For Cat is to feed various cats with milk. There is a sitting cat and a bag of milk hang on a rope above the cat at a certain height. Milk can swing from side to side like a pendulum. You will have to guess the best moment and cut the rope while the bag of milk is over the cat. If the cat is full, you will receive points for it.Have fun.
