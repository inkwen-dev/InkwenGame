## About

Rabid Rabbit is funny kids game where you need to control the rabbit in the laboratory. Click on the screen to move rabbit up or down and you need to collect all the carrots. Avoid toxins and experiments that are in the laboratory. If you touch them the rabbit will go crazy and the game will be over
