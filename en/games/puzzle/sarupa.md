## About

This game is a 3-match puzzle game which you line up 3 monkeys of the same color to make them disappear. You will erase more and more monkeys falling down the tree. As the level goes up, the types of falling monkeys increase and the difficulty also increases. In the play mode Difficult, two monkeys fall together, but you can enjoy the chain more.
