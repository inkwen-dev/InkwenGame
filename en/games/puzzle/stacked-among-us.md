## About

Stack Among Us is matching game with Among Us imposters and crewmates. They will all be together now and you need to stack them one on top of the other or next to each other. Stack three identical among characters to make them disappear. Earn the most points during the game. As time goes on the game will accelerate and will be harder to play.
