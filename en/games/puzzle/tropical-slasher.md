Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

In this game Tropical Slasher your task is to cut all the fruits and not to let you escape. The knife must be hung on the side trees. You have to avoid the bombs, because if you hit the bomb the game ends. You are limited in time and collect as many points as possible. Good luck.
