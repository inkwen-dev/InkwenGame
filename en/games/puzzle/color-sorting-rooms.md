## How to play

Tap on a flask to pick up the top color, then tap another flask to pour the liquid. You can only pour if the colors match or if the target flask is empty.

The goal is to sort all colors so that each flask contains only one color. Plan your moves carefully, as space is limited and mistakes can make levels harder to solve.

As you progress, more colors and complex layouts will appear, requiring better strategy and thinking ahead.

Complete quests by collecting specific colors across multiple levels. Finishing quests rewards you with points that can be used to unlock room decorations.

Use helpful bonuses if you get stuck, such as adding an extra flask, undoing a move, or removing a color.

Relax, think, and enjoy the satisfying process of organizing colors!
