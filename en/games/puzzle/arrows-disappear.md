## How to play

Your main goal is to quickly tap and remove all blocks that show upward arrows (↑) while avoiding blocks with other directions (↓ ← →). Clear the blocks as fast as possible before the tower grows too tall and collapses. The faster and more accurately you tap, the higher your score!

PC Controls:
Mouse: Click directly on the blocks with upward arrows (↑) to make them disappear.

Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap on the blocks showing upward arrows (↑) to remove them.

## About

You are faced with a growing stack of 3D blocks, each showing different arrow directions. The core idea is quick decision-making — tap the blocks with upward arrows to make them disappear before the tower collapses. What makes it unique? Clean 3D visuals with satisfying physics, increasing speed and complexity, one-tap simple controls, and the tense “just one more level” feeling as the stack gets taller and faster!
