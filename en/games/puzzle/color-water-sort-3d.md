## How to play

Become a master of color sorting! Your main objective is to sort the colored liquid in the tubes until each tube holds only one color. You win the game when all liquids of the same color are grouped together and every tube is correctly sorted. Use your logic and strategy to untangle this colorful mess!

PC Controls:
Left Mouse Click: Click on a tube to select it, then click on another tube to pour the water. 

Mobile Controls:
Single-Finger Tap: Tap on a tube to select it, then tap on another tube to complete the pouring action.

## About

Color Water Sort 3D is a fun and challenging 3D casual puzzle game. The core concept revolves around sorting chaotic colors into perfect order, offering players an incredibly satisfying and stress-relieving experience. Set in a vibrant 3D environment, your task is to sort the colored water in the tubes until each tube contains only one single color. With levels ranging from simple to brain-teasing, it perfectly balances strategic thinking with a relaxing atmosphere, making it the ultimate brain trainer and time killer. What makes it unique is its realistic 3D visuals, smooth pouring physics, and the deeply addictive sense of accomplishment with every puzzle solved.
