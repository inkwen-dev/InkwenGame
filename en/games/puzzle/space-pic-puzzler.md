## About

'Space pic puzzler' is a interesting photo puzzle game. Playing this game is very simple. Using the touch swipe or mouse swipe, swap the pieces of a picture which are horizontally or vertically adjacent. Keep swapping the pieces until you get success in forming the whole picture. The target picture is shown in the left panel. Each second is reducing your score, so complete it as soon as possible.
