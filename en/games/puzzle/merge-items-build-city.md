A game about building a city. Make houses and streets simple for the residents of the city and make the lives of the citizens happy.

## How to play

- Control with the mouse on the computer screen or by pressing your finger on the smartphone screen;
- To start construction, click on the building;
- Select a task - click with the mouse or finger on the screen;
- To connect objects, you need to drag and connect the same ones.
- Connect stones to get bricks;
- Connect the tree to get the boards;
- A game about building a city, connect objects to build;
- Build a hospital and a kindergarten;
- Open shops and banks;
- Each building generates income. Increase profits and get rich;
- Complete tasks and complete quests to increase your income;
- To build a building, you need to complete all the tasks;
- Create stairs, windows and walls.
