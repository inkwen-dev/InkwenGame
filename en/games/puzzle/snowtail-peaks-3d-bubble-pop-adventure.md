## How to play

How to Play
Drag to aim — The glowing line shows where your bubble will go, including bounces off the lane rails.

Release to shoot — Match 3 or more same-color bubbles to pop them.

Use bank shots — Bounce off the side rails to reach bubbles behind obstacles or in hard-to-hit corners.

Clear falling bubbles — Bubbles that aren’t connected to the top fall away after a match. Clear the whole board to win the stage.

Watch your shots — You only have a limited number of shots. Run out before the board is clear and you lose.

Swap bubbles — Tap the launcher area to swap your current bubble with the next one.

Power-ups (unlock as you progress):

Bomb — Arms an explosive next shot that blasts nearby bubbles.
Rainbow — Arms a shot that clears one color from the board.
Lift — Pushes the bubble stack away from you, buying you more time.
