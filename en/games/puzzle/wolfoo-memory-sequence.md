## About

Wolfoo Memory Sequence is a colorful memory game for kids starring Wolfoo and friends! Watch carefully as the characters light up one by one, then tap them back in the exact same order to win. Each round adds a new step, so your memory has to keep up. Play across 15 levels of increasing challenge &mdash; from Easy warm-ups to tricky Expert rounds. Clear rounds to earn up to 3 stars, build combos for bonus points, and keep your 3 hearts alive. Ran out of lives? Watch a short video to Revive and keep playing. Featuring lovable Wolfoo characters, cheerful music, and gentle kid-safe fun. Great for training focus, concentration and short-term memory.
