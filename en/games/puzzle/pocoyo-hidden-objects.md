## About

The Pocoyo Hidden Objects game is an exciting game that challenges you to find hidden objects in 8 different levels. Your aim is to locate ten objects in each level before your time runs out. With each level, the objects become harder to find, and the game becomes more challenging. You must use your observation skills to spot objects cleverly hidden in the images. The scenes are full of bright, vivid colors that make the game even more enjoyable. Play the game now and see how many hidden objects you can find!
