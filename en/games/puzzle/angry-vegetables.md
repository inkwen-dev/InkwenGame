Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Start by pressing a vegetable and then move the pointer on the similar vegetables which are adjacent to the previous. Make a chain of 3 or more to remove them. If you make a chain of 7, you will get 2x power vegetable and If you make a chain of 10, you will get 1 minute bonus. Try to get maximum score in this time bound game.
