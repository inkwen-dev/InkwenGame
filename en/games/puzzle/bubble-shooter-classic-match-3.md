## How to play

On the computer - Use the pointer and left mouse button to shoot the bubble at the right place On a mobile device - Use your fingers to tap on the screen

## About

Shoot the bubbles to create a combination of three or more bubbles of the same color. The more bubbles in a combination, the more points you earn. If the combination does not come together when firing, then you get a strike. After several strikes, a new row of bubbles appears. The game will end if the bubbles reach the bottom of the screen.
