## About

Seat Jam 3D is surely an entertaining puzzle game in which you place the passengers in the correct seats. You simply need to click on the passenger and then click on the correct seat. The first fewlevels are very simple, but before long, youll have to think long and hard before you can put all the passengers in the right seats. Solve the bus rush. They can move up and down, left and right, but not diagonally. Train your brain with engaging puzzles in Seat Jam 3D. Come and join the game!
