## About

How high can you go, and can you hit the right color when you get there? The object of Color Valley is to make a ball jump high enough to hit a ring circling above it. But you can’t hit just anywhere, you have to hit the portion of the ring that is the same color as your ball.. Are you up for it? Then go. Be careful not to pass through the wrong color, or you’ll have to start again. Like other play game you can jump ball with correct color and switch the block to get more score pass monument valley or jump ball use touch mode
