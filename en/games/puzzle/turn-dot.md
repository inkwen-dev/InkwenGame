## About

Turn Dot is a puzzle game with white dot or ball. The ball need to move on the yellow path. Tap on the screen to change the way on moving the ball. Move only on the selected path if you go outside the path or hit on the corners the game will over. So tap when you need to pass the cornet and have fun with this game.
