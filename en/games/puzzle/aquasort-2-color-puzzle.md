## About

AquaSort 2: Color Puzzle is an engaging and relaxing water-sorting puzzle game designed to challenge your mind and provide hours of satisfying gameplay. Your goal is simple: sort all the colored liquids into their respective bottles until each bottle contains only one color. With hundreds of levels, the complexity increases as you advance, introducing more colors and bottles to manage. The game features adjustable difficulty settings (Easy, Normal, Hard) that dynamically change the level generation to offer a smoother or much tougher challenge, including an increased number of empty bottles on the Hard setting.
