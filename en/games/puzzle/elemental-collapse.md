## How to play

Use a mouse or touchpad to select groups of 2 or more matching blocks and collapse them Clear larger groups to score more points and earn powerful boosters Selecting a single block will incur a 250-point score penalty

## About

Unleash the power of the elements in Elemental Collapse, an exciting block-matching puzzle game! Tap groups of 2 or more connected blocks of the same element to clear them, trigger satisfying chain reactions, and reach the target score to complete each level. The larger the group you collapse, the higher your score. Plan your moves carefully, create massive combos, and overcome increasingly challenging puzzles. Powerful Boosters Arrow &ndash; Clears an entire row and column. Magnet &ndash; Clears all blocks of the selected element. Bomb &ndash; Explodes to clear the 8 surrounding blocks.
