## About

See Match is a challenging match-3 puzzle game. Connect 3 or more of the same fishes horizontally or diagonally. The more fishes you match, the higher your score! Keep an eye on the time. You will fail to pass the level if you do not gather sufficient grids in time. The faster you complete the task, the more stars you earn! If you find yourself stuck, you can always use the special bonuses to help you.
