## About

Jewel Miner is a simple and fun clicker game. Select 2 or more jewels with the same color and matching them. The more jewels you matching in a single tap – the more points you earn for every jewel. Instructions: - Tap or click on two or more jewels of the same kind to eliminate them. - Reach the target points of each level to move on
