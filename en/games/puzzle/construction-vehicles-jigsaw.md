## About

In this Construction Vehicles Jigsaw game you have 6 images of construction vehicle in three modes to play. Choose one of the mode for the game that you previous choose and start to play. Drag and drop the pieces to solve the puzzle and create an image of wheel loader, fork lift, dump truck, excavator, mixer truck, mega dump truck. Enjoy, and have fun!
