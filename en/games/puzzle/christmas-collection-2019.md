## About

Collect christmas gifts so that Santa can send them off to the good little boys and girls. Press any gift to start. Now move the ‘mouse or finger tip’ on identical adjacent christmas gifts (horizontally, vertically or diagonally). Select at least 3 gifts. Release mouse button to make the match. Each 6th gifts will give a bonus. More than 7 gifts will give a time bonus. Collect the request gifts within the giving time, or you will lose the game.
