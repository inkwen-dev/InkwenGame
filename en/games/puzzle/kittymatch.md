## About

Welcome to the fluffiest cleanup mission ever! Tap the stacked items to pick them up and drop them into your slots. When three identical items gather together&mdash;poof!&mdash;they merge into a satisfying match and disappear (as if your kitty just approved it). Keep matching smartly to clear the pile, and once all items are gone, you&rsquo;ve completed the level! But hey&mdash;this isn&rsquo;t just tidying up. Keep your eyes sharp: there are hidden Easter eggs tucked between the stacks, and plenty of adorable cat skins waiting to be unlocked. Match, clear, and collect&mdash;because every great kitty deserves a cute new look!
