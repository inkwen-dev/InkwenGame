## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Move the fruits to the blank spaces in order to create a horizontal or vertical row of 3 identical fruits. Use as less moves as possible for more score. To make a fruit move, first tap on it and then tap on any blank tile. If there is any open path between the fruit and its destination, it would travel to the new place. Complete a level before your score reaches to 0. You can use special powers like Bomb and extra score for matching fruits faster.
