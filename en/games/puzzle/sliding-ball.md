## About

Test your reaction and natural reflexes in Sliding Ball game. You will control the ball, which is on a straight line, moving from left to right and vice versa in a horizontal plane. The ball cannot get away from this line, it has a limited field of activity. This is the difficulty of the task, because various figures will soon begin to fall from above, trying to touch your ball.
