## About

Today we offer you to put together a very beautiful and unusual puzzle, which will depict pets. All of them are very beautiful, so you will put the puzzle together with joy and a smile on your face. All the animals will be sitting on the lawn, basking in the sun. But to see all the pets you need to put the picture with small pieces. This can be done very simply, because you just need to move the pieces of the puzzle to the right place and then the picture will gradually add up and you will be able to see all the pets that are depicted on it. If you find it difficult and do not know how to put this puzzle, do not worry, because you will have a hint that will show you on the screen already folded picture, so you can see what you do next. Good luck!
