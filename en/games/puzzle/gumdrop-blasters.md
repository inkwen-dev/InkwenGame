## About

Blast your way through a world of square-shaped candy madness in Gumdrop Blaster! Match and shoot colorful gumdrop bubbles to clear the board in this addictive candy-themed bubble shooter. With each level, the challenge grows as new patterns of sweet square candies descend faster and trickier. Use precise aim and power-ups to create explosive candy combos and clear the screen before it overflows! Colorful gumdrops, satisfying pops, and endless blasting fun await!
