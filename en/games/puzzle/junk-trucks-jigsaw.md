## How to play

Use mouse to drag the pieces or tap and slide on screen on mobile devices

## About

This is a jigsaw game with Junk Trucks. There is 12 interesting images with cartoonized junk trucks. Choose your way of playing this game. You can play in easy mode, medium or hard. It will depend on the game mode how many pieces you will need to assemble the picture with. If you are a beginner in these games, start with easy mode and try to assemble the image of a junk truck with 25 pieces. For medium you need 49 pieces and for Hard 100 pieces. You need to solve the first image to proceed in the next level in the game.
