## How to play

The goal of the game is to bring the child home to the arms of a loving family!
1. Click on mom or dad to start drawing lines.
2. Draw a line from the parent to the baby of the same color.
3. Be careful when drawing a line - avoid walls, dogs, cars and thieves.
4. Don't let the parents run into each other.
5. Make sure all the children get home safely.

Save the kids right now!

## About

Save as many children as possible by solving all the drawing puzzles! The game is suitable for those who want to relax!
Bring the crying poor kids back to their loving families. Help mom and dad find their child and get home. The controls are very simple - draw a line from the parent to the baby. Careful avoidance of obstacles and choosing the optimal route will allow you to get to the baby and save him.
