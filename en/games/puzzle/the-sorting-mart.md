## About

The Sorting Mart is a store sorting puzzle game that lets you experience the fun of shopping in a store. Rearrange items between shelves to match three identical ones on a shelf, clearing it and letting the upper shelves drop down. Clear entire stacks to shift adjacent shelves and keep matching before time runs out. If you get stuck, use the shuffle button to mix up the shelves and find matches more easily. Challenge your sorting skills as levels become more difficult!
