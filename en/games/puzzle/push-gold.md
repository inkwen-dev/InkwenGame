## About

PushGold is a puzzle game where you take on the role of a clever miner tasked with pushing gold blocks to their designated positions. Every move requires careful calculation because the narrow terrain can cause you to get stuck. As you progress through levels, the difficulty gradually increases with more obstacles and tight tunnels, forcing you to observe, plan, and choose the correct pushing direction. Conquer the mine and collect gold in the most skillful way!
