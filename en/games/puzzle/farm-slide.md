## About

Sliding puzzles, or simply put-tag, are interesting and entertaining. They make you think and calculate the steps ahead. We offer you a game Farm Slide Puzzle by <a href=https://4gameground.com>4GameGround.com</a>, the theme of which is a cartoon farm. Painted cows, sheep, chickens, donkeys, piglets and other farm animals. They are placed on the pictures, which will spoil as soon as you select one of them. The mixed fragments are numbered to make it easier for you to arrange them in order and thus restore the image. Move the squares around the field using one free cell.
