Use mouse or tap on the screen to play this game

## About

This is jigsaw game with tractors. You can play with twelve images with tractors. Each level you can play in EASY, MEDIUM or HARD mode. At the start of the game you will have only one image available for play. Other eleven is locked. Start with first image, solve the image and unlock the next one. Can you unlock all images in this jigsaw game. Use mouse to move the pieces in right position.
