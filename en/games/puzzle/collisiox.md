## About

CollisioX 3D is a colorful and addictive 3D puzzle game where you swipe to move all balls and merge matching colors. Plan each move carefully as every ball slides in the same direction until hitting an obstacle or another ball. Solve creative levels, overcome tricky layouts, and master increasingly challenging puzzles. With vibrant graphics, satisfying physics, and strategic gameplay, every move tests your logic and spatial skills!
