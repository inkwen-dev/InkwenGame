## How to play

Swipe a tile, or tap one tile and then a neighbour (mouse or touch). Tap a power-up to fire it, and swap two power-ups to combine them. Boosters: tap the button, then the board. Keys: M mutes, Esc opens pause or closes a panel, Enter plays from the harbor.

## About

Tidewell is a cozy match-3 puzzle about rebuilding a storm-battered harbor. Swap sea treasures to make matches, craft flares, buoys, gulls and tide pearls, and combine them for huge chain reactions. Clear crates, sand, nets and message bottles across 100 handcrafted levels, then spend your stars to restore the pier, lighthouse, market, boatyard and café. Bring a Harbor Pal into every level: the crab, puffin, octopus and turtle each have a special power that charges as you play.
