## About

This is a special bubble shooter game in which you will find a rotating wheel of smiley toon faces. To complete the game you need to collect all the toons from the wheel. Shoot the smiley toon towards the wheel to make the group of at least three of same of the same color. Your points are reducing with the time, So finish the game early to save more points.
