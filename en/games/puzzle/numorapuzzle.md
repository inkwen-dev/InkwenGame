## How to play

Objective
Create the numbered tiles shown in the GOALS panel before you run out of moves. Complete every goal to win the level.

Controls
- Mouse: left-click any group of two or more connected tiles with the same number.
- Touchscreen: tap any group of two or more connected tiles with the same number.

Matching tiles merge into one tile with a higher value, and new tiles fall into the open spaces. Plan each move to build larger groups and reach the target numbers. Use the hammer or +3-moves booster when you need help. In Endless mode, keep merging for the highest score you can reach.

## About

Numora Puzzle is a colorful number-merging puzzle game that blends quick, satisfying matches with thoughtful board strategy. Tap groups of matching tiles to combine them into higher numbers, complete each level's goals, and keep your chain going all the way to 13. Handcrafted challenges, an endless mode, bright space-themed artwork, collectible rewards, and helpful boosters make every short session feel fresh.
