## About

Today, Bear decided to put his house in order, and he began with the shelves in the sideboard, on which he found an old album with photos. At this point, the cleaning of the bear stopped, because he began to look through all the photos and remember the old days. That's just with some photos there was trouble, they broke, which was very upset with the bear. In the game, your task will be to help the Bear, and put the puzzle together so that the result is a whole picture. To do this, you will need to drag the puzzle pieces on the black and white picture to put it together. Try to do everything as best as possible, so as not to upset the old Bear.
