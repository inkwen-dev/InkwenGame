## About

Arrow Rope Maze is a relaxing yet clever escape puzzle where your goal is to guide each arrow along its rope without getting tangled. Every new level tightens the paths, adds traps, and challenges your logic. Enjoy soft visuals, smooth animations, and gentle ASMR-inspired sound effects as you slide arrows through each maze. Plan your moves, avoid overlaps, and find the perfect sequence to reach the exit. With simple drag-to-move controls and handcrafted levels, the game offers the ideal mix of calm and challenge. No timers, no stress&mdash;just pure puzzle satisfaction. Perfect for fans of logic games, rope puzzles, and relaxing brain challenges
