Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

You will operate with purple diamonds with five faces. The task is to shoot crystals, filling special niches on the playing field. This does not happen with one shot. In this case, it is undesirable for the stones to hit the red columns. Each blow is a loss of life, and you have only thirty of them. It seems a lot, but treat them with care, otherwise, literally waste at the initial levels.
