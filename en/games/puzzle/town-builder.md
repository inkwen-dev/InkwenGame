## About

Town Builder is a simple and fun game featuring colorful isometric graphics. You just need to tap the screen to drop the building block. If the dropped block is near the last one, you will earn higher points. Each stage has a different points requirement to complete and unlock the next stage. The early stages have fewer points requirements and are easy to win, while the higher stages require more precise block dropping to achieve higher points.
