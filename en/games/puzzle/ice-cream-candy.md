## About

The ocean is full of cute candies and other garden creatures! In this classic Match 3 casual game, 60 challenging levels with different goals are waiting for you! Match at least 3 candy of the same color to remove them from the field. Make combinations of 4 or more fish to create powerful tiles which explode areas on the board. Try to clear all levels with 3 stars and earn as many points as you can!
