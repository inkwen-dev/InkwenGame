## How to play

Goal: Match all snacks of the same type to clear the vending machine
Controls: Tap any snack to move it to an empty slot at the bottom
Strategy: You have 5 sorting slots - use them wisely! Plan your moves carefully to avoid getting stuck
Progression: Complete each level to unlock the next challenge. Levels get progressively trickier with more snack varieties and complex arrangements
