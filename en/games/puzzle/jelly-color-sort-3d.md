## How to play

Tap a jelly to select and move it.
Place jellies into the correct sections.
Match jellies of the same color in the same section.
Sort all the jellies before the timer reaches zero.
Plan your moves carefully to avoid getting stuck.
Use Freeze Time to temporarily stop the countdown.
Use Destroy Jelly to remove a difficult jelly.
Use Auto Shuffle to rearrange the jellies and create new possibilities.
Watch a rewarded ad to activate a power-up when needed.
Complete the puzzle before time runs out to advance to the next level.

## About

Put your sorting skills to the test in Jelly Sort 3D: Color Puzzle! Move colorful jelly pieces and place jellies of the same color into the correct sections before time runs out.

Think carefully, plan your moves, and complete each puzzle as quickly as possible. When you get stuck or need extra help, use useful power-ups by watching a short rewarded ad. Freeze Time can pause the countdown, Destroy Jelly can remove a troublesome jelly, and Auto Shuffle can rearrange the puzzle to give you a fresh chance.

With simple tap controls, colorful 3D jelly pieces, challenging levels, and helpful power-ups, this color sorting puzzle game is easy to learn but challenging to master.
