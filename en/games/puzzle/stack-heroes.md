## About

This is a game with flying heroes. Stack the heroes in a line or in a row of three to destroy. This is a slightly different matching 3 game. Arrange the next hero in the right place to make a series of three identical heroes. Be careful not to reach the top of the game, then your game is over. Tap on the screen to put the heroes on that place. Can you reach the best score?
