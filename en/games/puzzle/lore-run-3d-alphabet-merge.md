## About

Initially, you are the ‘Alphabet A’ and you have to run on a track. After the running stage, you will confront your enemy. Merge, Fight and Challenge yourself to become the king of Lore Run 3d - Alphabet Merge. HOW TO PLAY: -Gather your squad on the way. -Unlock all Characters and merge as quick as possible to beat all the enemies. -Merge the same alphabets i.e “A” with “A”, “B” with “B” etc… Are you ready to take part in? Try to know more about the Alphabet in this game!
