## About

Find The Difference is an interesting observation game where you need to find the differences between two seemingly identical images. Each level is a visual challenge that requires focus and keen eyesight. The difficulty increases as you progress, with more intricate details. The simple yet captivating gameplay allows you to both entertain and improve your observation skills.
