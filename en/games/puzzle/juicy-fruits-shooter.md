## About

Juicy Fruits Shooter is a delightful bubble shooter game that puts a fruity twist on the classic genre. Armed with a colorful fruit cannon, players embark on a juicy adventure to shoot and match fruit bubbles. The objective is to clear the screen by strategically aiming and launching fruits to create combinations of three or more identical fruits. Dont let fruits touch the bottom line and get a high score. Enjoy!
