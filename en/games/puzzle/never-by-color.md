## How to play

1. Tap the pile — a handful of parts lands on your tray (or just wait: the tray fills up on its own).
2. Tap parts of the same shape to select them. You can select several identical ones at once.
3. Tap the box for that shape — the parts fly into place. Sort them in a row without mistakes to build a combo!
4. Remember the main rule: sort by shape, not by color. Put NOTHING in the red box.
5. Clear the whole pile — and as a reward, a new model gets built for your collection.

## About

The Japanese room has drowned in a mountain of colorful parts. Bring back its calm!

Scoop up a handful of parts onto your tray and sort them into boxes. There's one rule, and it's ironclad: sort by SHAPE — never by color! Cubes with cubes, plates with plates, and let the red trap box stay empty.

No timers, no losing. Just you, a mountain of parts, and the pursuit of perfect order. The most meditative tidying of your life.
