## About

When decorating your aquariums for your lovely fish, solve challenging and interesting puzzles. Do you enjoy rescuing Fish? Are you able to master the art of solving the one-of-a-kind pin puzzle? If you’re looking for a true Save The Fish or pin fish rescue game, you’ve come to the right place! In this fun game, guide your fish to safety by avoiding lava while allowing water to flow into the fish tank.
