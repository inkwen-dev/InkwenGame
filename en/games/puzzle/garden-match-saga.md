## About

Garden Match Saga is a match 3 puzzle game where you have to match multiple types of fruits to progress. What you have to do is to position at least 3 or more similar fruits on the same line to clear them and succeed in reaching the objectives set for each level. You have 3 objectives per level. The game has 400 very interesting levels. Good luck with the game.
