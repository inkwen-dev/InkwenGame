## About

Abyss Sweeper reinvents the classic Minesweeper formula into an intense, infinite descent into the unknown. You are the pilot of a specialized drilling craft, tasked with navigating a treacherous, never-ending grid of rock and volatile energy. Unlike traditional puzzle games, you cannot simply avoid the mines&mdash;you must hunt them. Your fuel is constantly draining, and the only way to replenish it is to HARVEST the very mines that threaten to destroy you. How to Play: DIG (Tap): Clear safe tiles to reveal numerical clues. HARVEST (Long Press / Button): Identify a mine location and harvest it to regain critical Fuel. SURVIVE: Watch your Hull
