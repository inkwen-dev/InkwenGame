## About

Tap Game is a matching puzzle game where you need to click on empty blocks that is corner of two same blocks. Than the blocks will disappear. The empty blocks will fill up quickly and you have to find the corners as fast as you can because the whole board will fill up quickly. Play longer as you can before the board is full with blocks.
