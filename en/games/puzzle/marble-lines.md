## How to play

Left Mouse Button Click on a marble to move and click on a new position BackSpace or Ctrl Z Undo last move F7 Sound on off

## About

Try to remove lines of marbles before the board is full! Every move 3 new marbles will appear and you can move 1 to a new position. Make lines of 5 or more marbles with the same color to remove them. Lines can be horizontal, vertical or diagonal.
