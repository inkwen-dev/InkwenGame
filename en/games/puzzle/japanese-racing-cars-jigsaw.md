## About

This is jigsaw game with images of Japanese Racing Cars. These cars are known around the world for their speed and their drifting. We selected beautiful and interesting pictures with Japanese cars. Choose how many pieces you want to divide the image. It can be in 24, 48 or 100 pieces. Then assemble the image to get a picture of a Japanese race car. Solve first image to unlock next one. This game have 12 images. Play all levels and have fun.
