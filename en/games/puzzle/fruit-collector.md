## How to play

Tap on the left and right sides of the screen to play this game or use the arrow keys

## About

Collect the fruits with the basket. The basket can move on the left and right sides and the fruits will fall from the top of the game screen. Try to collect more fruits as you can. Between fruits sometimes the bombs will fall and you need to avoid them. You can lose three fruits or be hit by bomb three times before the game end.
