## About

Embark on a colorful journey through the African savannah! In this engaging puzzle game, you will find 6 beautiful levels featuring the most iconic wild animals: the majestic lion, the gentle elephant, the funny zebra, the graceful giraffe, the cute leopard, and the mighty hippo. The game is perfect for both kids and adults thanks to its adjustable difficulty settings. You can choose the number of pieces for each puzzle: from easy (16 pieces) to expert (100 pieces). Assemble the pictures, enjoy the stunning artwork, and improve your concentration!
