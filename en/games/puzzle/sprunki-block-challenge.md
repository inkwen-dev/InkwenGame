## About

Sprunki Block Challenge is a super fun and free to play online game that kids will absolutely love! Join the adorable Sprunki on a block breaking adventure full of surprises. Your mission is simple but exciting: click on the right blocks to help Sprunki fall safely to the ground. But watch out: tricky obstacles and floating platforms make each level a clever puzzle to solve.
