## About

Looking for an exciting and engaging game to play during Easter? Look no further than Easter Hidden Stars! This fun and free online game is perfect for kids, and challenges them to find 10 hidden stars on each of the game's 8 levels. Simply use your mouse to click on a star when you spot one, and keep an eye on the timer located in the lower right corner of the screen. You'll have a set amount of time to find all 10 stars in each picture, so be quick and focused! Ready to start the game and have some fun? Let's go!
