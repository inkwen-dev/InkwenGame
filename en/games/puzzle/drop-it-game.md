## About

Drop It is a gem elimination puzzle game. It moves the gem horizontally, fills the gem with a line, and eliminates the high score. Eliminate one line and score. If you eliminate multiple lines, you get a high score. If the player's gem reaches the top, the game ends. The game is both fun and strategic. It requires the player to observe, judge, move and finally reach a line, or lines.
