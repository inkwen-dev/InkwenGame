## About

In this game you have to locate the differences between the two pictures in the limited amount of time given to play each time! To play, use your mouse as control. Make sure that you don&rsquo;t make a mistake more than three times because that will make you fail. One minute is the total time you will play through the 20 levels in this game! In each level you have seven differences. Good luck!
