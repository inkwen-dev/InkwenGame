## How to play

Pop balloons by clicking on them. Fill the basket with balloons to complete the level. Roll balls through the multiplier windows to increase their amount!

## About

Pop balloons into smaller ones and guide them through the matching holes. Make sure every balloon fits through and lands in the basket below. Think ahead and choose which balloons to pop to solve each level. Fill the basket to complete the puzzle!
