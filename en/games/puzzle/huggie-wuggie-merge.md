## About

Welcome to our newest merge game Huggie Wuggie Merge. You have to merge two Huggie Wuggie blocks with the same numbers. The numbers are from 1 to 9. When you reach number 9, the whole row is released. The next suggested Huggie Wuggie block number can be seen at the bottom of the screen. The game is played by tapping on the screen with a mouse or with a touch. Enjoy and have fun!!!
