## About

Welcome to the game Sprunki Solve and Play where 16 famous Sprunki characters are presented. You need to assemble the pictures by swapping to place the pieces. To change drag parts to another position. Finish a puzzle, go to the next one. On each puzzle you have a limited time to complete it. After you have completed all the puzzles, go to the last scene where all the characters are shown. Click on them to start singing.
