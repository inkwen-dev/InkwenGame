## How to play

Dive into a delightful puzzle adventure where colorful cakes fall from above — and it's your job to line them up! React fast, create matches, clear the board, and keep the glass from overflowing. Unlock new flavors and take on increasingly tasty challenges as you progress!

Desktop Controls:
← (Left Arrow): Move cake left
↓ (Down Arrow): Drop cake faster
→ (Right Arrow): Move cake right

Mobile Controls:
Left Button: Move cake left
Down Button: Drop cake faster
Right Button: Move cake right

## About

Match delicious cakes of the same color, clear the board, and earn points! Unlock new flavors and increase the challenge as you progress. A simple yet addictive puzzle game with bright, tasty desserts.
