## About

Arrower is a minimalist and relaxing puzzle game. The goal is to move all yellow arrows to the yellow box. You do it by touching the arrows, they will move to the direction where they are pointing (up, left, right or down). In addition, you'll find different special boxes to help the arrows to find the right path. Enjoy!
