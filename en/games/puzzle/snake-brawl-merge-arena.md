## How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your snake. Eat orbs with the same number as your segments to merge them and grow stronger. Bite snakes whose numbers are smaller than your head and avoid bigger ones (green ring = safe, red ring = danger). Defeat the target number of snakes or the boss before the timer ends, then tap fast in Power Smash for bonus coins.

## About

Slither into the arena and grow the mightiest merge snake! Every segment has a number - eat matching orbs to merge them 2048-style and power up. Bite through smaller snakes to steal their segments, dodge the bigger ones, and topple crowned bosses before time runs out. Then tap like crazy in the Power Smash bonus to break multiplier walls for coins. Upgrade power, speed and magnet, and unlock 8 snake skins!
