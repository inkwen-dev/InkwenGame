## About

You are hungry and have to eat all the food that is thrown your way! Just open your mouth and keep eating the foods! You are very very hungry, so don’t miss any food! Also be wary of the bombs that are thrown your way and keep your mouth shut when you see them! You can also use the gathered food for unlocking new characters in shop.
