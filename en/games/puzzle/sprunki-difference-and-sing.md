## About

Sprunki: Difference and Sing is a fun and free online game designed especially for kids! Your goal is simple: find 5 differences in each level to win. Get some info about Sprunkies and what sound they make. With its playful Sprunki theme, the game offers endless enjoyment. Stuck on a tricky level? Use hints to reveal a difference and keep going. Dive in and have a blast!
