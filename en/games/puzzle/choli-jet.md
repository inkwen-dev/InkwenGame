Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

You are a cute but weird little creature that wants to travel across the land and you are using a jet pack to do this! But because of your low budget (you are a chicken after all), your jet pack has a very low quality and can only be used in very short bursts. In other words, you have to flap through obstacles and progress! Do your best to dodge obstacles and go as far as you can.
