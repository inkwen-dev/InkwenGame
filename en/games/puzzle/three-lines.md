## How to play

Tap on the targets to play this game

## About

At this game there is three lines. A Blue Line, Orange Line and Yellow Line. There is one target on each line. Balls with different speeds move through the lines from top to bottom. You need to match the target with the ball. Do not let more balls fall or the game will over. Try to target the balls on all lines. An interesting and fun game in which you will find fun for you.
