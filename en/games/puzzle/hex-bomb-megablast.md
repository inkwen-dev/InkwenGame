## About

HEX BOMB - MEGABLAST is a game in between a BUBBLE SHOOTER and a BRICKS BREAKER. Hexagonals blocks are falling from the top of the screen and you need to shoot at them and blast them before they reach the bottom of the screen. When you press the screen, the ball shooter will slow down to let you adjust your fire. When you release your finger, it fires automatically. Try to collect as many bonus and upgrades as you can to master the game like a pro and advance in the game.
