## How to play

Move the fruit left and right with your mouse or finger, then click or tap to drop it. When two identical fruits touch, they merge into a larger fruit and give you points. Keep combining fruits to create bigger ones and increase your score. The game ends if the fruits pile up too high and reach the top of the container.

## About

Fruit Drop 3D is a colorful physics-based fruit merging puzzle. Drop fruits into the container, combine matching ones to create bigger and more valuable fruits, and build the largest fruit possible. Plan every drop carefully, use the physics to your advantage, and try to beat your high score without letting the container overflow.
