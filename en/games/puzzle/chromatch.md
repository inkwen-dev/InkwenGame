## About

Chromatch &ndash; Test Your Reflexes, Match Your Colors! Get ready for Chromatch, the fast-paced color-matching challenge that keeps you on your toes! A spinning wheel divided into four vibrant color sections awaits your precision. Your task is simple: throw the arrow of a given color and hit the matching section. But be careful&mdash;land on the wrong color, and you&rsquo;ll lose one of your three lives. As the wheel speeds up, your reflexes, timing, and focus will be put to the ultimate test. Easy to pick up but hard to master, Chromatch delivers endless fun in short bursts, making it perfect for casual play or high-score chasers.
