## About

Food Blocks Puzzle or also called 1010 is a simple HTML5 puzzle game. Drag Food blocks into grid board until board grid vertical or horizontal line are full filled, then blocks that get filled will be removed, and make sure there is a possible moves for available blocks. Try to get the highest score!
