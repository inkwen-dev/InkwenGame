## About

**Dive into Sea Merge Drop!** Merge adorable sea animals by dropping matching pairs. Watch them evolve into even cuter creatures! Tap to release animals strategically - create chains of merges for higher scores. Dont let the ocean fill up or its game over! Each merge earns points and unlocks new aquatic friends. Reach level targets to progress through the game. How many merges can you create? How high can you score? Can swim all the way through 10 levels? Simple one-tap gameplay with charming visuals. Perfect for quick play sessions or diving deep into merging madness! Ready to make a splash?
