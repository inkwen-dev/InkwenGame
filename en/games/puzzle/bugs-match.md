Use mouse to move the insects

## About

This is a match-3 game where you have to matching 3 identical insects to destroy them. In this game you can move the insects as you like. It is important that they are 3 identical in a row horizontally or vertically. The goal is to keep the insects from reaching the top of the screen. Then the game is over. As you play the game in a certain period will accelerate the movement of insects. Then you need to be faster and destroy more insects.
