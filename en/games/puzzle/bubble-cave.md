## About

Bubble Cave is a unique 3 match game that anyone can enjoy! Different bubbles are falling down from the top. You have to match 3 bubbles of same color by rotating the cave center. Watch for special bubbles which can make the difference during gameplay. Destroy bubbles before they reach cave border.
