## About

Neon Crush is a match-3 game. Match-3 is a popular type of casual puzzle game. The objective of the game is to make combinations of pieces of the same type to burst the pieces and earn points. With various types of unique power-ups you can burst pieces of the same type, burst all the pieces on the screen or even burst a “bomb”, to clear a piece region and accumulate more points. Play with your friends and get the highest score possible!
