Drag a card from the field onto the deck that matches its category Park a card you cant place yet on the shelf Clear every card to finish the level

## About

Tidydeck is a calm, no-fail sorting puzzle. Drag each object card onto its matching category deck and clear the board. Park tricky cards on the shelf; a deck closes when its full. No timers, no losing &mdash; just the quiet satisfaction of putting everything in its place.
