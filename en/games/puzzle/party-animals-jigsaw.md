## About

Party Animals Jigsaw is a game with funny images of party animals. This game allows you to play with 12 pictures with happy animals. Select the image you want to play and then select on how many pieces to split the image. Easy level is if you play with 25 pieces, medium level with 49 and hard if you play with 100 pieces. Drag the pieces and assemble the image from them. Choose the weight you want to play this game with and enjoy. There is no time limit while playing this game.
