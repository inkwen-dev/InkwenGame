## About

Combine missiles to stack your ammo and unleash explosive chaos in vibrant 3D pixel art arenas. Merge identical missiles to create larger, more powerful warheads with increased blast radius and damage. The more you combine, the bigger the explosion. Strategize your merges, manage your space, and prepare for satisfying chain reactions that wipe out everything in sight. Bright voxel-style visuals and dynamic destruction make every detonation feel impactful and rewarding. Build your arsenal and dominate the battlefield.
