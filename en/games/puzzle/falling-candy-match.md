## About

Falling Candy Match is a fast-paced puzzle game where players must match colorful candies as they descend from the top of the screen. With intuitive swipe controls, strategically align candies to create matches and earn points. Watch out for special candies with unique abilities that can help clear the board and boost your score. With vibrant graphics and addictive gameplay, Falling Candy Match offers endless fun for players of all ages.
