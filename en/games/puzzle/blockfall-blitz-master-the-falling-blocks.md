## About

Blockfall Blitz is a fast-paced, addictive puzzle game inspired by classic falling block titles. Strategically place descending tetrominoes to complete and clear horizontal lines. Race against the clock as the blocks fall faster with every line you clear. Plan ahead using the Next Piece preview and outsmart the grid by utilizing the new Hold Piece feature! How many lines can you clear and how high can you score before the blocks reach the top?
