## About

Angry Bubble is a fun action puzzle game where you control a character to eliminate all monsters appearing on the arena. Move skillfully, choose the right moment to attack, and utilize the terrain to defeat enemies. Each level presents a new challenge with increasing numbers and strength of monsters. The gameplay is simple, fast-paced, and highly engaging, perfect for fans of casual, action, and puzzle games!
