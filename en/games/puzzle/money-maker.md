## How to play

How to play Money Maker
- drag pins onto the board to increase profits.
- drag one pin onto another to merge them into a higher-tier pin.
- upgrade the Money Machine to raise the base banknote value and your earnings.

## About

Money Maker is a merge game about running a banknote machine. Drop money, place power-up chips in the right spots, and let every bounce boost your income. Unlock upgrades and new chip slots to multiply profits and keep the cash flowing. How far can you scale your money machine?
