## About

Fruit Merge Arena is a fun and relaxing game where you drop fruits and merge the same ones to create bigger fruits. Keep merging step by step and try to reach the ultimate fruit &mdash; the watermelon! But be careful, space is limited and the board can fill up quickly. Plan your moves wisely and keep merging to stay in the game.Feeling stuck or running out of space? Don&rsquo;t worry! You can watch a reward ad to remove all instances of a selected fruit and make room for new moves.How far can you go? Can you reach the watermelon and win the arena?
