## About

Get ready to experience the juicy fun of Fruit Catcher, a simple yet addictive game that will test your reflexes, timing, and hand-eye coordination! Step into the colorful world of fruits, where your only goal is to catch as many falling fruits as possible before they hit the ground. Sounds easy? Think again &mdash; with every level, the speed increases, and the challenge gets even more exciting! Use your mouse (or finger on mobile) to move the basket. Catch fresh fruits like apples, oranges, bananas, and more. Avoid bad fruits or bombs that can ruin your streak.
