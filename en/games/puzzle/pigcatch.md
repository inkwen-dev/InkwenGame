## About

Tap to pick items from the physical stack and place them into your slots. Match three of the same item to clear them away and free up space&mdash;simple, satisfying, and sneakily tricky. Plan your taps carefully: one bad pick can clog your slots, but a smart combo can collapse the whole pile in your favor. Clear every last item to beat the level and move on. Along the way, keep an eye out for hidden Easter eggs, and unlock adorable skins to make your sorting journey even cuter.
