## How to play

Tap or click an object in the pile to send it to your tray. Three of the same kind match and clear. Collect the goal objects shown for the level to win. If your tray fills up with no match, you lose. Boosters let you undo a move, pull a matching set with a magnet, add a tray slot, or shuffle the pile. Everything is played with taps or mouse clicks, no keyboard needed.
