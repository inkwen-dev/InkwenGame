## About

Sugar Candy Saga is fun addictive hypercasual match3 game.Match 3 or more required candies to complete the level. The objective of Sugar Candy Saga is to swap one candy with another candy on a horizontal or vertical chain of three or more candy of the same color.Sugar Candy Saga game has 2 modes. One is level mode in this mode you have to collect the given number of candies to win that level and the second mode is Time Rush .In this mode you have to collect as much candies as you can before the times out.
