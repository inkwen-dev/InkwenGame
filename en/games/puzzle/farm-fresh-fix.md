## About

Farm Fresh Fix is a fun and educational puzzle game where players organize scattered pieces to complete delightful vegetable pictures. Each level presents a different farm-fresh veggie &mdash; from crunchy carrots to cheerful tomatoes &mdash; waiting to be put back together. Drag, place, and fix the pieces correctly to reveal the full image and move to the next challenge. With 12 engaging levels, the game gradually introduces new vegetables and slightly trickier layouts, making it perfect for young players and casual puzzlers alike. A special learning screen before each puzzle helps players discover the vegetable they&rsquo;re about to assemble.
