## How to play

Click or tap a good to move it to another shelf. Group 3 identical goods on the same shelf to match and clear them. Clear all goods from the shelves before the timer runs out to win the level.

Stuck or short on time? Use boosters: the hammer removes an item, freeze stops the clock, shuffle rearranges the board, and the magic staff clears a full set instantly. Chain matches quickly to build combos and earn more stars.

Everything is played with the mouse or a single tap — no keyboard needed.
