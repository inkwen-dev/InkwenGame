## How to play

You’ll need to collect colorful threads from various knitted objects and place them into matching colored boxes. Think carefully before placing your threads into temporary slots, and use your strategic mind to avoid filling up the available space. As you progress, you’ll unlock new levels, each more complex than the last.

Q: How do you win / what counts as losing?  
A: If you manage to distribute all the threads onto the spools without using the spare slots for them, you win. However, if you run out of free slots and the toys are not fully unraveled, unfortunately, you'll have to restart the level.
