## About

Pick and Patch is a fun matching puzzle game where pieces of a picture are missing, and it&rsquo;s your job to fix it! Each level shows a character or object with blank spots. You have to pick the correct pieces from the options and place them in the right place. Its simple, fun, and great for improving attention and visual memory.
