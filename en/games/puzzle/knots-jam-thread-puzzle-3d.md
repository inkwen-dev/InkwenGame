## How to play

Your task is to sort these threads into groups of three to fill designated areas on a virtual fabric canvas. - As you align each thread correctly, not only do you solve the puzzle, but you also bring a beautiful embroidered image to life. - The game requires careful planning and a strategic approach to place each spool of thread in the perfect order to complete the embroidery and clear the board.
