## About

This is puzzle matching game with garbage trucks. Connect 3 or more same garbage trucks and earn points and time. When you connect more trucks the more time for playing will be added in the game. So if you want to play more times and to play in the higher levels try to connect five or more trucks. As you progress through the levels, your playing time will be shorter. So you need to connect more trucks.
