## About

Matching Trucks is in the genre of matching games. You need to connect three or more identical trucks to disappear and collect coins. In the upper right corner you can see how many more trucks you need to connect to play the next level. If you matching more trucks at once you will get more playing time and more points, so you will reach the next level faster. As you play through the levels, in the upper levels the time accelerates and you have to play faster and matching more trucks in one move.
