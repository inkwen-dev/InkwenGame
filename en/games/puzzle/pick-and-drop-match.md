Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Collect gifts by making a row of 3 similar gifts. You can make these rows by dragging gifts from the fronts of a conveyor belts and dropping it to another belt. Keep doing this before the gifts reach the end of the conveyor belt. Try to get as many matches as possible to beat your record.
