## About

Spooky Tile Master is a Halloween-themed game where you clear the board by grouping spooky hex tiles in sets of three. Tap to move tiles to a matching or empty spot. But beware! If no tiles fit or space runs out, the level is over. Race against the clock to master each eerie puzzle!
