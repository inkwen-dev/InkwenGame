## How to play

1. Tap a vehicle to drive it in the direction of its roof arrow.
2. If the path is clear, it leaves the lot and heads to the open garage of the same color.
3. Each garage fills in order: big vehicles first, then cars, then bikes. Check the empty spaces in the garage.
4. Vehicles that can't park yet wait in the parking slots and drive in automatically when their garage opens.
5. Don't let the waiting slots fill up! When all slots are full, the level ends.
6. Watch the NEXT list to see which garages come next, and plan your moves.
7. Stuck? Use boosters: Undo takes back a move, Hint shows a good move, +Slot adds a waiting slot, and Scan locks vehicles that can't park right now.

## About

Park Sort is a colorful parking puzzle where every move is clear and every choice matters.

Every vehicle has an arrow on its roof. Tap it and it drives straight that way. If the road is clear, it leaves the lot and heads for the garage of its color. If something is in the way, it bumps and comes back.

Garages arrive one after another, and each one fills in order: big vehicles first, then cars, then bikes. A car that can leave right now isn't always one that should. Cars with nowhere to go wait in the parking slots and drive into their garage as soon as it opens. But if the waiting slots fill up, it's game over!
