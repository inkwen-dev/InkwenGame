## About

TrashTide is a fun and relaxing ocean cleanup adventure where every catch counts! Dive beneath the waves and help restore the sea by collecting floating trash while carefully avoiding fish and other marine life. Every piece of waste you collect makes the ocean cleaner, but grabbing the wrong thing will cost you valuable points. With simple controls and satisfying gameplay, TrashTide is easy to play yet challenging to master. Stay focused, react quickly, and see how clean you can make the ocean!
