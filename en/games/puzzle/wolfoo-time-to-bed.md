## How to play

Tap an empty hexagon tile to place a blocker Trap the character so it cannot reach the edge of the board Tap the Hint or Undo buttons for help On mobile tap the tiles on desktop use the mouse

## About

Wolfoo wont go to bed &mdash; help catch him before he sneaks away! Wolfoo - Time To Bed is a cute hexagonal trap puzzle for kids. Each turn you place a blocker on the honeycomb board; Wolfoo (and later his friend Kat) tries to run to the edge. Wall off every path to trap him and win the level! Play 20 levels of growing challenge, from Easy to Expert. Stuck? Use up to 3 free Hints a day to see the best tile to block, or Undo your last move to fix a mistake. From level 10, use the Speaker to lull characters to sleep for a turn.
