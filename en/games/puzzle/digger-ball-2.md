Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

In the second part of the game Digger Ball 2, you will continue to work on a special digger. Before you on the screen you will see a ball of a certain color lying on the surface of the earth. At a certain depth underground, a special well will be visible. You will have to make the ball fall into the well. To do this, use your mouse to dig a tunnel underground. A ball rolling along it will fall into the well and you will receive a certain amount of points for this.
