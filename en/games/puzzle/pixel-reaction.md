Touch the screen to create a pixel which will absorb the other pixels when they come in contact

## About

Multicolored pixels are going crazy. Your goal is to create a chain reaction and catch as many pixels as possible. Click at any location to build a large pixel that captures all the others. Pixel Reaction is a game that tests your logic and capacity for anticipation. Its simple and precise gameplay will appeal to those who have little time to play. Immerse yourself in an original and addictive universe.
