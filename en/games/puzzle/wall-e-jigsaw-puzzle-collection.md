## About

A very interesting and absolutely simple game that will definitely appeal to fans of the cleaning robot Valley from the cartoon of the same name. The essence of the game is to collect a frame from a cartoon, with your favorite character. The image is "cut" into small parts of different shapes. The items appear in turn. You need to install one, and then click on the red button, which is located in the lower left corner of the screen. And only then will the next piece of the image appear. Control is carried out with the mouse.
