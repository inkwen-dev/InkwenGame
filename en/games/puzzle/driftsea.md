## About

DriftSea is a calm yet challenging sea-themed glide puzzle game where strategy meets smooth movement. In each level, you slide floating bars across the ocean grid, carefully planning every move to reach specific goals. The mechanics are simple to learn, but mastering the puzzles requires logical thinking, foresight, and precision. As you progress, levels introduce new layouts and increasing complexity, encouraging you to think ahead and optimize each glide. The soothing ocean atmosphere and clean visuals create a relaxing experience, while the puzzles keep your mind engaged.
