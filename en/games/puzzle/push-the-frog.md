## About

Control your little frog to eat all the mosquitoes. Master the length of the frogs tongue and make the frog reach the appropriate position to eat the mosquitoes. Think carefully about planning the route. The game has a simple and interesting style, and the gameplay is educational and novel. It is very suitable for playing when killing time.
