## How to play

🕹️ Use W A S D or Arrow keys to move

💣 Press Spacebar to place a bomb

⏲️ The bomb explodes after 3 seconds. Bombs placed side by side trigger each other 🔥

⏳ You have 99 seconds for each level. Collect clocks from boxes to extend this time ⏰

🔧 You can increase the number and range of bombs with various power-ups

👾 You need to hit the boss 3 times with bombs to defeat it
