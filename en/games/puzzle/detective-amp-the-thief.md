## About

Caught the thief and punish him, collect bank treasure Some bad thieves plan to rob a bank. So detectives are assigned against that case. As thieves hide behind walls, Player has to help to find the perfect path towards the Thieves. Draw lines to find a path and the detective will punish them. Be careful! There were many traps in the paths so be wise and use your brain to help the Detective. Don&rsquo;t merge their paths either Game will be over, follow the colour code of the bag and detective.
