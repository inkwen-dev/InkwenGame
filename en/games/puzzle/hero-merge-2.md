## About

Hero Merge is an exciting tower defense game where strategy and firepower combine! Merge your heroes to create stronger units, strategically position them on grid, and defend your wall against waves of different enemies. Unlimited waves, plenty of perks and many different units! Will your heroes stand strong, or will the enemies break through?
