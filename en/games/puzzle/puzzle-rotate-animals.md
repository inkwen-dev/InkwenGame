## About

Welcome to our newest casual puzzle game Puzzle Rotate - Animals. The game has 10 levels with different animals. Each level is locked and it will be unlocked when you solve the previous level. Build the correct image by rotating the pieces of the puzzle before time runs out. Tap or click on a piece to rotate. Enjoy and have fun!
