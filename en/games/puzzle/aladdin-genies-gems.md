## About

Join the Aladdin Genies & Gems and help Aladdin and his darling Princess Shiraz liberate the Pomegranate Palace from the villainous wizard Jafar. Start your magic genie Aladdin match quest and experience a thrilling fairytale world. Aladdin Genies & Gems transports you to the mysterious world of ‘One Thousand and One Nights’, also known as ‘Arabian Nights’, where you will relive tales of adventure, magic, and genies! Aladdin and his beloved princess are trapped, and they need the power of the genies and magic lamps to liberate their palace. Can you complete all of the levels and help them to destroy the evil forces?
