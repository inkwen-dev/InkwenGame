## About

Get ready for a fun and addictive bird adventure! Control a flying bird in this exciting 2D game and help it survive as long as possible. Tap the screen to make the bird fly upward and avoid dangerous hurdles coming from the front. Be careful &mdash; if the bird touches the ground or hits obstacles, you will lose a life. You have 3 lives to keep the journey going. Test your reflexes, improve your timing, and see how far you can fly while dodging every challenge in your path!
