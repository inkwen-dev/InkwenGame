## How to play

Hexa Tower: drag hexagon stacks onto the board. Neighboring tiles of the same color merge, and a tall enough stack clears. Fill the goal bar before the board runs out of space. Hexa Number: drag across neighboring number tiles so their sum equals the goal. Longer chains score more. Use power-ups (Bomb, Shuffle, God Hand) when stuck. On the village map, tap to build and upgrade, and drag to explore. Mouse or touch.

## About

Restore a lost village hidden in the fog! Hexa Village combines two relaxing puzzle games: sort colorful hexagon stacks in Hexa Tower and connect number tiles to hit the goal in Hexa Number. Earn stars to clear the fog, chop the forest, rebuild houses, the forge and bridges, and uncover a hand-painted fairy-tale world.
