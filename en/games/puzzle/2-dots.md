## About

This is a game with 2 dots in different colors located horizontally at the top of the screen and at the bottom. They move from left to right. There is a smaller dot in the middle that moves up and down and is hit by the larger dots. The small dot should only collide with the larger dot that has the same color. You need to control the horizontal row to match the color of the dots.
