## How to play

A monster is shown on the playing field above, which must fall. 

If the keyboard is available, you can use the left/right arrows to move the next monster
and the down arrow or space bar to reset.

Your task is to drop the monster down the playing field, while trying to get it to hit the same monster. When two identical monsters come into contact, a new, larger one appears instead.
If the entire playing field fills up and the monsters go beyond the top line, then the game session will be lost.

Coins are given for connecting monsters. They can be used to unlock new monsters in the store.

It is possible to change the monster for watching video ads!

## About

Collide the same monsters with each other, but do not cross the top lane - otherwise you will lose! Unlock new monsters and have fun on Halloween!

Coins are given for connecting monsters. For coins you can unlock new monsters in the store.
More points are given for new monsters when connecting, so there is a chance to improve your record!

The main goal is to unlock all the monsters and take the first place in the leaderboard. 

Demonstrate your skill and dexterity in connecting monsters!
