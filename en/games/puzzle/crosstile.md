## How to play

Read the theme at the top of the level.
Drag letter fragments around the board.
Place matching fragments next to each other.
Correct connections snap together automatically.
Connected pieces move as one group.
Use word intersections to discover the full crossword.
Connect every fragment to complete the level.
Use a hint if you get stuck.

## About

CrossTile is a relaxing word puzzle where crossword solving meets jigsaw-style gameplay.

Each level gives you a theme and a set of scattered letter fragments. Drag the pieces around the board and connect fragments that belong together. Correct pieces snap into place and become one larger group. Keep linking words and intersections until the entire crossword is complete.

With simple controls, clean visuals, and no timers or lives, CrossTile is easy to learn and satisfying to master.
