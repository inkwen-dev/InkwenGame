## How to play

Tap a slime on a touchscreen, or click it with the left mouse button, to send it onto the loop. The slime shoots pixels of its own color as it moves; the number on it shows its remaining shots. Choose the order of your slimes carefully and send parked slimes back onto the loop when their color is needed. Keep parking spaces available and clear every pixel to complete the level. Use boosters to help with difficult puzzles. No keyboard controls are required.

## About

Slime Loop is a colorful puzzle game where cute slimes travel around a loop and clear matching pixels. Choose which slime to send next, manage its shots, and keep your limited parking spaces from filling up. Clear pixel-art pictures across 450 levels, discover new puzzle mechanics, and use helpful boosters when a tricky board needs a fresh approach. Simple one-tap controls make it easy to start, while careful planning turns every cleared picture into a satisfying win.
