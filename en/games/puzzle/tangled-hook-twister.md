## About

Tangled Hook Twister Twist, Turn, and Let Gravity Do the Rest! Challenge your spatial logic and patience in Tangled Hook Twister, the ultimate physics-based puzzle game where precision is everything. In this minimalist 3D experience, your goal is simple but the journey is winding: guide a set of colorful rings through a complex, intertwined wire structure. There are no engines or thrusters here&mdash;just the raw power of gravity and your ability to rotate the world.
