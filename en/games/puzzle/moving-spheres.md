## About

Moving Spheres is a puzzle game with balls. The goal of this game is to catch the ring with the sphere. The ring must not collide with the sides of the game and fall to the ground. So you have to control it with the sphere or the big circle. Move the sphere to cushion the impact. Try to make a good result. You can drop the ring 3 times, after that the game is over.
