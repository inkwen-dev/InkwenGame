## About

This is jigsaw game with Easter cute bunnies and Easter eggs. This game has twelve images with cute bunnies. Choose first image that is available and start to play. You can play easy level with 25 pieces, medium with 49 pieces or hard with 100 pieces. Play without time limit and enjoy this festive game. Tap on pieces and move to the position on the board to get the full Easter image.
