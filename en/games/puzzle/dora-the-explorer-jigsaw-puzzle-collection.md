## About

In today's game, you will find 12 puzzles that you need to collect to experience the adventures of Dora the Explorer. As you already know, Dora is very fond of traveling. This time, she decided to keep her memories not only in memory, but also in the photo. And for this, Dora came up with a puzzle in the form of a puzzle that you can put together to collect cool pictures. Let's get started! At first, you will only have one puzzle available, which you must put together to open the next puzzles. Also choose the level of difficulty, it can be easy, medium or heavy. Collect picture after picture to put together a whole collection of puzzles with the adventures of Dora the Explorer and her friends. Have a nice game!
