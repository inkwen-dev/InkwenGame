## About

Rescue Puzzle 3D is a lightweight 3D fun puzzle game. You need to click on the correct objects in the small three-dimensional scene to trigger mechanisms, remove obstacles or form a chain reaction to meet the level conditions and rescue the injured character. The levels are short but full of ingenuity: turn off the power switch, move rocks, release water to extinguish fire, synthesize props... If you make a wrong move, everyone will lose, but if you make a mistake, everyone will cheer. It supports mouse and touch, and you can fix it and go even if you have fragmented time.
