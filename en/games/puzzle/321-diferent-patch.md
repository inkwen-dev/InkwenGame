## About

321 Diferent Patch is a casual, quick-play game in which you have to discover the different figure among several that look the same. You have three seconds to find the different figure. Look at every detail, that can make the difference. The difficulty increases with every step. And be careful, the figure that is wrong once, may be the right one the next time. Do you want to play quietly? Relax mode is for you. Just like Normal mode, but with plenty of time to examine the figures. And if youre ready, try the Extreme mode.
