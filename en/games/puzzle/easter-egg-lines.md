## How to play

Use mouse or touch-screen to play this game

## About

Move the Eggs to the blank spaces in order to create a horizontal or vertical row of 5 identical items. To move just tap the Egg and then tap the location you want it to move. If there is any open path between the Egg and its destination, it would travel to the new place. Each time you move a Egg and if no match happens, 3 new Eggs will be added to the board.
