## About

Here is the new innovative candy match-3 game! Swap candies to make sweet lines of 3 or more to create candy blasts! Match 3 of the same colored candies to earn points. If you match 4 candies, they become a special candy! Use the power of special candies for special advantages. Clear stage missions with a given number of moves. A variety of missions and obstacles provide exciting new challenges.
