## How to play

Tap/click on 3 same tiles to choose them and release the underneath tiles. Clear the whole board to complete a level

## About

Join travelling through PoppyTile village with challenges in both match-3 and mahjong genres!
Meet the charming girl Poppy who will be by your side during the throughout your journey!

Match 3 identical tiles to gradually clear the board. Progress further across the map with increasingly difficult levels.

Use boosters to complete levels as quickly and comfortably as possible.

Classic boosters:
- Add an extra slot, 
- Cancel the last action, 
- Mix tiles on the board
- Use an idea!

How far can you go?

See you in PoppyTile!
