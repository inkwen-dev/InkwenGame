## About

In the distant prehistoric times, there was a small town of Badrock, where the Flintstone family lived. This family has always caused only positive emotions and a smile. The Flintstones are ready to do it again and again. In this game you have to help a cute family. There is a picture that depicts a happy moment in the life of the Flintstones. Sort the tiles and make this picture whole and bright.
