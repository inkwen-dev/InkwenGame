## How to play

Collect all gems. 

Desktop controls:
Move Up: Up Arrow / W 
Move Down: Down Arrow / S
Move Left: Left Arrow / A 
Move Right: Right Arrow / D 
Restart: R
Undo: Z 

Mobile controls: 
Move Up: Swipe Up
Move Down: Swipe Down
Move Left: Swipe Left
Move Right: Swipe Right

## About

Bump Bandit is a clever top-down puzzle game wrapped in a retro jazzy atmosphere. You play as a quirky little thief determined to snatch every diamond in the city—and by the time the heist is over, he might just steal your heart too. Follow this charming rogue to the end of his adventure and uncover the unexpected twist in his story.
