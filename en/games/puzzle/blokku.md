## About

BLOKKU is an addictive block puzzle game! Drag and drop pieces onto the 11x11 grid, fill complete rows and columns to clear them and score points. Plan your moves carefully &mdash; once the board fills up, the game is over! Enjoy satisfying combo clears, explosive particle effects and relaxing music. Switch between day and night mode. Easy to learn, impossible to put down. How high can you score?
