## About

Point to Merge is an engaging puzzle game where player must strategically connect numbered blocks to reach the highest value possible. Starting with two blocks numbered 2, the player must point arrows to direct each block towards its match, merging numbers in a sequence (2, 4, 8, 16, etc.). Precision is key&mdash;one wrong move or pointing outside the level restarts the puzzle! With 50 progressively challenging levels, Point to Merge combines sharp thinking and skillful direction to master the art of merging. Can you reach the final number?
