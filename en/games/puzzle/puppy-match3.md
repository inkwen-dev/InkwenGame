## About

An exciting logic game in which you will play as a puppy. You need to help him get all the lost pictures and this will require your ingenuity. To do this, you do not need to move the chips among themselves, it is enough to find a group of identical blocks of 3 or more tiles and click on them. Then the colorful tiles will burst and clear the playing field.
