## About

Create cute cats in the colorful animal app Cat Safari. Mysterious crates seem to appear regularly on a large abandoned field – and inside, each one holds an adorable kitten! Combining two of the same kind of cat yields something completely new – step by step, you'll be able to breed unique new breeds of cats. Experience the unique cat game and initiate a cat evolution of your very own! Feline fanatics aren't the only ones who'll be scrambling to get more and more cats.
