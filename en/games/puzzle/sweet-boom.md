Sweet Boom is a puzzle game with beautiful graphics. Pop sweet candies to complete levels. But the number of clicks is limited, so count. There are 4 types of candies, when touched or hit by candies – they grow and burst. There are additional levels with additional objects – ice, firecrackers and a bomb. They help in destroying all sweet things.
