## How to play

Lucky Clash is a fast-paced game where your main objective is to defeat enemies, survive each round, and collect rewards to become stronger. Use strategy, timing, and a bit of luck to overcome each challenge and progress through the game. To win, you must defeat your opponents before they defeat you and make the best choices during each clash.

Controls:
Use the mouse or touchscreen to interact with buttons, choose actions, and control the game.

## About

Lucky Clash is a fast-paced 3D tactical Tower Defense game where players summon powerful monsters, merge units, and defend their kingdom against relentless enemy waves. The game combines quick, easy-to-learn battles with strategic depth, letting players create strong teams, discover powerful relics, and use clever combinations to survive each challenge. Its unique mix of monster merging, elemental synergies, and dynamic battles makes every run feel different.
