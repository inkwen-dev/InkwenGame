## About

Halloween Bubble Shooter is a puzzle game. When you bring balloons of the same color together, the balloons will disappear (3 or more balloons). There are balloons at the bottom of the screen that you can shoot upwards, you can match the balloons above and pop them. The more balloons you pop, the more points you earn. If you don't pop a bubble every time you shoot, more balloons will come from above and your job will be difficult. If the balloons from above touch down, the game is over. Have fun.
