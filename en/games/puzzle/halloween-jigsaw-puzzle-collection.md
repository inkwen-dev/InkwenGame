## About

Halloween Jigsaw Puzzles is a free online puzzle game for kids. In this game you will find 12 beautiful pictures dedicated to Halloween. You need to start with the first image and unlock the next one as you progress. You have three modes for each image: easy for 25 fragments, medium for 49 fragments, and hard for 100 fragments. Have fun and enjoy!
