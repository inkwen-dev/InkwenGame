## About

It is a classic bubble shooter game where you eliminate bubbles by shooting similar bubble on them. Your goal is to eliminate all the bubble appearing in the screen. You can shoot 1 out of 3 bubbles. To select the bubble you want to shoot, just tap on any one bubble, appearing left side of the arrow. Complete all 48 levels to win this game. If you shot a bubble and it does not make a match of 3 similar bubbles, 200 points will be deducted from your score.
