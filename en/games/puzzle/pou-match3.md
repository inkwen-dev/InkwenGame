## About

Pou games are funny adventures of a small and completely helpless alien who accidentally came to our Earth and decided to stay here forever. Such games are perfect for the youngest gamers, as they are characterized by simple missions, bright and interesting graphics and fun music. Connect the same images with the mouse (in the amount of at least three pieces) and get maximum points!
