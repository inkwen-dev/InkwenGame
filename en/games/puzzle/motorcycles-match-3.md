## About

Motorcycles Matching 3 is a game where you have to match 3 or more identical motorcycles. Motorcycles can be matched horizontally or vertically. This will earn you points and add seconds to your playing time. The more points you make the more time you will have to play. So be careful and fast and play for as long as you can.
