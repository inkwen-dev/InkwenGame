## How to play

In this game, you'll sort 3 pencil-shaped pieces with the same color to complete beautiful pictures. It's a mix of puzzle and art that brings every image to life as you play.

## About

🖍️ Dive into Pencil Jam for a fun and colorful sorting challenge!

What makes Pencil Jam special:
- Simple Puzzles: Just tap the hexa and watch them merge to fill a full picture.
- Bright, Bold Art: Each puzzle you solve turns into a vibrant picture.
- Relaxing Play: No timers or stress, just enjoy the game at your own pace.
- Creative Fun: More than just matching, you’re creating beautiful art as you play.
