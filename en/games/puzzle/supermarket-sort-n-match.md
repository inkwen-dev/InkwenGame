## About

In Supermarket Sort n Match, rearrange items between shelves to match 3 identical items on one shelf, clearing it and allowing upper shelves to slide down. Clear entire stacks to shift adjacent shelves. Collect all items before time runs out as difficulty increases with each level. Enjoy the ultimate item-sorting and matching challenge!
