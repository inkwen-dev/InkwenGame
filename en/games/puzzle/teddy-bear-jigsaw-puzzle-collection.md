## About

charming Teddy bears have become the favorite toys of many girls. This educational online game will offer kids to play with bears in interesting children's puzzles. For the puzzles, the game developers have selected good pictures with bears from cartoons or teddy bears. Thanks to the easy difficulty modes, the puzzle collection will be perfect for toddlers and preschool children. The first two images of Teddy are opened at once. The rest are unlocked after you pass the previous ones. Solve puzzles and develop your thinking and intelligence with Teddy bears.
