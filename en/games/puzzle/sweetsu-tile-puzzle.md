## About

Sweet Tile Puzzle is a relaxing yet strategic game. Match 3 identical Japanese-themed tiles by drawing them into a limited stack. Clear the board by collecting all tiles while managing space wisely. With each level, challenges grow as the stack shrinks and more tiles appear. Can you master the art of tile matching?
