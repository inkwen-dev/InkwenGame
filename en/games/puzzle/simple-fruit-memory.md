## About

Simple Memory Game is a fun and engaging memory-matching puzzle where players test their concentration and visual recall across 10 increasingly challenging levels. Each level presents a grid of cards hiding colorful fruit emojis. Players must flip the cards two at a time to uncover matching fruit pairs. The objective is simple: match all the fruit pairs in each level to progress to the next. As levels advance, the number of cards increases, demanding sharper focus and stronger memory skills. Quick recognition and strategic flipping are key to achieving the best possible score.
