## About

Collect Honey Puzzle is a popular and favorite merge game. You are now in charge of collecting honey, but you need to slowly upgrade to collect it. You can start by collecting sticks and upgrading to bricks. Upgrade from one brick to three and then to the honey jar and finally collect the honey successfully. You can also challenge your highest record anytime, and anywhere. If you are interested in this game, join us!
