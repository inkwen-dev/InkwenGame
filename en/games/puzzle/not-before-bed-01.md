## How to play

Click or tap an available monster to collect matching blocks. Collect 3 to free its slot. Clear all blocks to win.

## About

“I can stop after one level.” Sure you can.

Choose an available little monster. It automatically collects blocks of its color. Fill it with three and it leaves, freeing a collection slot. Clear every block to finish the board.

Not Before Bed 01 is the first game in the series. Different little games. The same thought: one more round.
