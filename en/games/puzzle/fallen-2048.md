## How to play

Click left button on mouse and swipe left or right

## About

The player uses the mouse to move falling tiles left or right. Only same numbers can allign! The goal is to align tiles with the same number so they merge when they touch, creating higher numbers. The game ends if the screen fills up, so players must think quickly and strategically to keep merging tiles and reach higher scores.
