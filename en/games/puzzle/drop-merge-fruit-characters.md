## About

Magical puzzle game where each fruit is a unique character with its own story! Dive into an amazing world where you can match identical fruits to create new ones. Turn small blueberries into juicy strawberries and strawberries into ripe cherries! The game features 26 incredible characters, each with their own personality and catchphrases. Spin the wheel of fortune, get daily rewards, and buy upgrades in the store. Realistic physics, beautiful animations, and pleasant music create a cozy atmosphere.
