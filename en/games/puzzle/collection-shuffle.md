## How to play

1. Select a card and insert it into the top of a column.

2. The new card pushes every card in that column downward.

3. The bottom card is pushed out and returns to the position of the card you selected.

4. Use this circular movement to rearrange the board and group matching items together.

5. Complete every required collection before you run out of moves.

Plan each shuffle carefully. One move changes several positions at once!

Collection Shuffle

Collection Shuffle is a colourful casual puzzle game where every move changes the board. Insert an item into a column, push the other items forward, and carefully organise matching summer objects together.

Plan your moves wisely, because each level gives you only a limited number of chances. Create complete collections of fruits, beach equipment, tropical drinks, sea creatures, and other vibrant summer items to clear the puzzle.
