## About

Touch and Leap is a game where you have to jump from pole to pole without falling between the poles and finish your game. Some poles are close and some far. So you need to set the jumping force to be correct to jump on the pole. If the strength of the jump is too strong or too weak, you will miss the pole and the game will be over. Try to jump on as many poles as you can without falling.
