## About

Merge the Presidents is a fun and addictive merge game where you combine the heads of famous U.S. presidents to create bigger and more powerful presidential characters! Start with smaller presidential heads, merge matching ones together, and watch as your collection evolves through history until you reach the ultimate giant presidential head. Start merging, combine every presidential character you can, and see how far you can go! Can you master the board and reach the ultimate big presidential head?
