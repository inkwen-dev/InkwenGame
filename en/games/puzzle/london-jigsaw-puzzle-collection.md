## About

Many people traveling around the world and visiting various cities bring themselves various souvenirs. Imagine that your friend brought you a memory puzzle with views of such a famous English city as London. Now you will need to put all the puzzles together and get the pictures. In front of you on the playing field, you will see the pieces of the puzzle mixed together. Try to pull them apart so that you can see everything at once. Now try to imagine the image of course and start the game. You need to drag the elements to the desired location and connect them together. When you are done, you will get a picture of one of the sights of London.
