## About

Go Monkey &amp; Banana is a fun adventure skill game where you control a monkey climbing and jumping over obstacles to collect as many bananas as possible. Jump accurately, cling to ropes skillfully, and avoid dangers to continue the journey. Each level presents a new challenge with increasing speed and difficulty. The simple yet addictive gameplay is suitable for all ages who love adventure and item collection games!
