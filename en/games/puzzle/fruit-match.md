## About

Fruits Match is a match-three game where you collect three matching tiles to clear them. Collect and clear three or more fruit tiles to solve the stage. You may use one additional deck, shuffle, or undo chance per stage. These powerups allow you to add an additional deck slot, shuffle the cards or undo your previous action. Get started today and show the world your mastery of fruit-matching!
