## About

We present a new puzzle game Buses Differences. With its help, you can test your attentiveness and intelligence. A playing field will appear on the screen, conditionally divided into two parts. In each of them you will see a picture that depicts a bus. At first glance, you will think that both images are the same. But there are all such differences between them, which you will have to find. Examine both images carefully. As soon as you notice an element that is not in one of the pictures, select it with a mouse click. Thus, you will designate this element and get points for it.
