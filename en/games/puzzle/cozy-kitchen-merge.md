## About

Cozy Kitchen Merge is a cute cooking puzzle where you place dishes on the tray, watch the queue, and merge 3 or more of the same dish&mdash;sides or corners both count. Each merge makes the next recipe and bumps your score. Every 100 points gives you an Eat Token. Tap the fork button to munch one dish and make space when things get tight. Keep at least one empty spot; no room for the next dish means game over. Climb the recipe ladder (1&rarr;10), unlock sweet achievements, and chase your best score. Soft art, cozy sounds, quick runs&mdash;perfect for a relaxing break. And hey, please dont wreck the game menu. Things may fall.
