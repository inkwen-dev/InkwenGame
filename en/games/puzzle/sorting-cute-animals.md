## How to play

The gameplay is very simple:

1. Find the same animals
2. Place them on the same shelf
3. Completely clear the field of all animals
4. Congratulations! The level is passed!

## About

A new sorting game with cute animals!

A relaxing game in which you need to arrange the animals in the cells. You have to place them next to each other to connect three identical ones.

"Sorting Cute Animals" is a simple and relaxing game for all ages and any gender!
