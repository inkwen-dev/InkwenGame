## About

Coronavirus Crush is a puzzle match 3 game. Collect three or more of the same virus and destroy. The aim of the game is to reach a given number of points in each level to advance to the next. The more viruses of the same type matched in a row (vertically or horizontally) - or in a group - the more points you receive! Time is of the essence, so hurry and play!
