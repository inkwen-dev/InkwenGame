## About

Tatu Bounce is a fun game where you drop a turtle to break the rocks below. Carefully calculate the falling angle, create a chain of continuous destruction, and score as many points as possible. Each bounce of the turtle gives an opportunity to break more rocks, but you only have one drop &ndash; so think carefully before acting!
