## About

In this game your task is to pop the balloons before they disappear from the screen. With every balloon that does not popped and the balloon escapes, will be taken away from your playing time. There are also balloons with x on them. You should not popped them as they will take away your playing time. Try to popped as many balloons as you can while playing this game. But the balloons move from left to right and it is not easy to shoot them. Some of them are hard to reach.
