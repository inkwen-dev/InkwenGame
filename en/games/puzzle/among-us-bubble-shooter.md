## About

Aim, match and smash all the bubbles in this relaxing color-matching adventure. To pop the bubbles, you must match three or more of the same color bubbles together, then fire the bubble that you are given into the pile of bubbles stacked in the play area. Try to pop all of the bubbles with least amount of shots. 24 levels to play. Enjoy!
