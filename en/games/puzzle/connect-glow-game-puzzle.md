## About

Connect Glow is one of the simple and addictive line puzzle game. Play Calming & Relaxing games today and have fun connecting spots. Funny connecting games with Anti-stress and Anxiety relief games. The game presents Numberlink puzzles: each puzzle has a grid of squares with colored dots occupying some of the squares. The objective is to connect dots of the same color by drawing 'pipes' between them such that the entire grid is occupied by pipes. However, pipes may not intersect. Difficulty is determined by the size of the grid, ranging from 5x5 to 14x14 squares. The game also contains a time trial mode. Free play through hundreds of levels, or race against the clock in Time Trial mode.
