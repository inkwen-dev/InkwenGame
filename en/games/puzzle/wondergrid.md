## About

WonderGrid is a captivating jigsaw puzzle journey through the Seven Wonders of the World. Piece together breathtaking landmarks from across history &mdash; from ancient architectural marvels to iconic global treasures &mdash; as each puzzle reveals the beauty and grandeur of human achievement. Designed for both casual players and puzzle lovers, WonderGrid offers relaxing gameplay with satisfying challenges. Arrange scattered pieces on the grid, find the perfect fit, and watch each wonder come to life as you complete the image. With increasing complexity and beautifully detailed artwork, every level feels like a rewarding discovery.
