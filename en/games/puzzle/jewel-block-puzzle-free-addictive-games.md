## About

Jewel Block Puzzle - Free Addictive Games is a simple but very addictive puzzle game in which you will operate with colorful crystal blocks. In this case, you must make solid lines of shapes that intersect the field vertically or horizontally. The pieces appear in threes and you have to put everything out, only then a new batch will appear.
