## About

Save The Monster is a kind of puzzle game where you need to move the monster on the board to collect coins and to avoid rockets who want to destroy you.The monster can move from field to field , so you need to find the best way to collect coin. When you collect ten coins the monster will drop the bomb who can destroy some rockets. Play longer as you can, avoid rockets and collect coins.
