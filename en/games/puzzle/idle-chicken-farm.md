## How to play

Build Your Ultimate Egg Farm!
Start with a single chicken and grow it into a thriving egg production empire. Collect and sell eggs, merge chickens to unlock stronger breeds, upgrade your coop and production line, and expand your farm with new facilities. Take a risk by incubating eggs for a chance to hatch new chickens and earn valuable rewards!

Desktop Controls
Left Mouse Click: Interact with all game elements.
Click & Hold + Drag: Collect and sell multiple eggs by dragging the cursor across them.
Mobile Controls
Tap: Interact with all game elements.
Tap & Drag: Collect and sell multiple eggs by dragging your finger across the eggs.

## About

Idle Chicken Farm is an idle/merge farming game where players start with a single chicken and build a thriving egg production business. Earn money by selling eggs, merge chickens to unlock stronger breeds, and upgrade your coop, production line, and delivery system. The game's unique hatchery mechanic lets players choose between selling eggs for guaranteed profit or incubating them for a chance to hatch new chickens and earn bonus rewards. With simple controls, strategic decision-making, and satisfying progression, Idle Chicken Farm offers a fun and rewarding gaming experience.
