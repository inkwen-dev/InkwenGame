## About

Dump Trucks Match 3 is a matching game with dump trucks. Move trucks to be 3 or more same trucks in rows to destroy them and to earn points and more time on the time meter.  You need to be fast and destroy more trucks to get more points and more time to play and to go to the next levels. When you play in higher levels the game will be more difficult. So start to play and enjoy the game.
