## About

Rotate the tiles to connect the power source to all terminals. Each tap spins a pipe segment 90&deg; &mdash; align them to build a continuous glowing circuit before you run out of patience! 40 handcrafted levels across 8 zones of increasing complexity: from simple 4&times;4 grids to massive 8&times;8 networks with up to 5 terminals to power simultaneously. Earn 3 stars on every level by solving it in fewer moves than par. Can you light up all 40 circuits?
