## How to play

Tap on screen to drag and move the pieces to play this game

## About

V8 Trucks jigsaw is a game where you can choose to play from one of twelve images with V8 trucks. Click on the image that you want to play and choose mode. You can play in easy mode with 25 pieces, medium with 49 pieces, or hard with 100 pieces. You have unlimited time to play just try to put the pieces in right position to get the image that you want.
