## How to play

• Aim by dragging your finger across the screen.
• Release to shoot the bubble.
• Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.
• Rescue cute animals and clear every level.
• Bounce shots off the walls to reach difficult spots.
• Create big combos for higher scores.
• Complete level objectives to unlock new magical worlds.

Plan every shot carefully, use boosters wisely, and become the ultimate Bubble Shooter master!
