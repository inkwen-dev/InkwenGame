## About

Join Wolfoo and Lucy in Wolfoo 2048 &mdash; a fun drop-and-merge number puzzle for the whole family! Drop numbered tiles into a 5-column board. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into a bigger one: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8&hellip; all the way up to 2048 and beyond. Plan each drop, build big chains, and beat your high score in this relaxing endless mode. Need a hand? Use three helpful boosters &mdash; Undo (take back a move), Swap (change the current number) and Hammer (remove any tile). Each booster gives one free use per game; watch a short video to refill it.
