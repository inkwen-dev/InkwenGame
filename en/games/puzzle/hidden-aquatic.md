## About

Hidden Aquatic is an engaging puzzle game where you arrange sea creatures in their correct positions. Observe carefully, drag and drop accurately, and complete the layout to unlock the next level. Each level increases in difficulty with more fish and unique ocean creatures. The gameplay is simple, relaxing, yet challenging, helping to train your thinking and observation skills.
