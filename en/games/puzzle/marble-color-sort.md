## How to play

🔴 Tap to Drop: Release marbles from the top trays and watch them tumble with realistic physics!
🟡 Ride the Conveyor: Marbles land on the ever-moving circular conveyor belt. Plan your moves carefully!
🟢 Match & Pack: Guide the marbles into bottom boxes with 3 slots of the same color.
🔵 Don't Run Out of Space: If the conveyor is full of marbles and there are no matching boxes available, you lose! Think ahead and drop wisely.
