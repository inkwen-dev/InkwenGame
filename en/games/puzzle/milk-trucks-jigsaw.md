## About

Milk Trucks Jigsaw is a game where you can choose to play with one of the twelve pictures with Milk Trucks. Select an image and then select a game mode. You can play in easy mode with 25 pieces to put together a jigsaw puzzle, medium way with 49 pieces or hard way, where you will have to put together a picture of 100 pieces.
