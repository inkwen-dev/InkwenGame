## About

An interesting and exciting puzzle awaits you in the new online game Ultimate Block Puzzle. Today you will create various objects. In front of you on the screen you will see a playing field in the upper part of which an object of a certain shape will be drawn. Below it, blocks of different shapes will appear on the panel in turn. You can use the mouse to move them up and place them inside the object. By making moves in this way, your task is to completely fill the internal field of the object. As soon as you do this, you will be given points in the Ultimate Block Puzzle game.
