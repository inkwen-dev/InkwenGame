## About

Tank Craft is a fast-paced merge and defense game where smart upgrades decide the battle. Combine matching tanks to create stronger war machines, arrange your armored force, and fight waves of quirky zombies and monsters. Earn coins, collect diamonds, unlock powerful weapons, and unleash special skills such as nuclear bombs and lightning strikes. Upgrade your rank, expand your arsenal, claim valuable rewards, and build an unstoppable tank army!
