## How to play

The topmost row and leftmost column determines the amount of filled blocks in a column row Click tap a cell to fill it

## About

If you enjoy playing Sudoku, then you will definitely love this Nonogram puzzle game too! Your goal is to determine which cells need to be painted over and which ones should be left empty. The numbers in the rows and columns will give you a clue, but be careful: you only have three lives!
