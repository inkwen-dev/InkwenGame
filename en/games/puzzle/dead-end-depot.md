## About

Push, but never pull. Dead-End Depot is a warehouse logic puzzle where every parcel must be delivered to the gate with the matching color and symbol. Plan each move carefully. Moving the wrong parcel first can block the entire warehouse. Features: &bull; 60 handcrafted puzzle stages &bull; Multiple parcel sizes &bull; Star-based level progression &bull; Conveyors, one-way floors, teleporters and fragile tiles &bull; Keyboard arrows/WASD, swipe, or on-screen controls &bull; English and Korean language support
