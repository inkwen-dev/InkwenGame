## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Main character in this game is an orange who is stuck on top of some boxes and platforms! He is afraid of height and so you must help him reach the ground by removing these boxes and platforms. But be careful because as you progress, some dangerous objects appear such as bombs and cactuses and if you touch them, you will lose one life! You have 5 lives at the start and if you lose them all, the game is over. As you progress, you may earn some extra lives based on your score.
