## About

Wolfoo Spot The Difference is a gentle find-the-difference puzzle for kids! Look at two almost-identical Wolfoo pictures and tap every little difference you can spot. With colorful cartoon scenes full of Wolfoo and friends, lots of levels that grow from easy to tricky, and a friendly star rating, its perfect for building focus, attention to detail and visual memory. Simple one-tap play makes it easy for preschoolers and early learners, while the trickier scenes keep older kids searching. Take your time, look closely, and see how many differences you can find. Can you clear every level?
