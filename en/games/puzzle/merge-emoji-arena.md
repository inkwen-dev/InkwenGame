## How to play

Your main objective is to defend against endless waves of increasingly difficult enemy emojis and challenging bosses. Defeat enemies to earn gold, which you use to constantly expand and upgrade your army!

Buy Units: Click the "Buy Unit" button to spawn a basic emoji onto your merge board.

Merge & Evolve: Drag and drop two identical emojis onto each other to merge them into a higher-level, more powerful unit with greater damage.

Deploy to Battle: Drag your strongest emojis from the merge board into one of the three battle lanes to start attacking the enemies.

Controls: The game is played entirely with simple Left-Click (or tap/drag on mobile) controls. Use your mouse or finger to buy, drag, and merge units!

## About

Merge Emoji Arena is an addictive casual idle-merge game where your favorite emojis come to life to battle! Build your ultimate army by purchasing basic emojis, merging identical ones to evolve them into stronger fighters, and deploying them to the front lines. What makes this game unique is its seamless blend of satisfying puzzle-merging with hands-free idle combat—your deployed emojis will continuously fight and farm gold for you even while you have the game running in the background!
