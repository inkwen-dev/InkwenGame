## How to play

Goal: craft the secret item from the ingredients in your inventory.

Controls:
Mouse or finger - drag an ingredient from the inventory into a crafting table slot
Tap a slot - put the ingredient back
Clear button - empty the whole crafting table at once

If a craft attempt fails, the ingredient backgrounds light up to help you:

Green - the ingredient is correct and in the right place
Blue - the ingredient is in the recipe, but sits somewhere else
Black or grey - the ingredient is not in the recipe at all

Not every item in the inventory is useful, some never appear in any recipe, so you will need some wit.

Recipe Quiz has no crafting table: the recipe is already laid out, just tap the item it produces.

The tutorial is always available in the settings, the gear button in the corner of the screen.

## About

A crafting table and an item you have built a hundred times. Now do it from memory.

A crafting puzzle: you lay out the ingredients, and after every attempt the game hints how close you are. The rest is on you.

In Classic Mode the target is shown and you only have to recall its recipe. Guess the Recipe hides it, so you build blind. Daily Craft gives one puzzle a day, the same for everyone. Recipe Quiz flips it around: the recipe is right there, and you name what it makes. One mistake ends the streak.

Crafts you open are collected in the Craft-o-pedia, records go to the leaderboards.
