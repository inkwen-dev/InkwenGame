## How to play

Press and hold your finger or mouse button within the playing area to select the monster's fall trajectory. Release your finger or mouse button and the monster will fall onto the playing field!  It will be easy to connect monsters at first, but with each new one the playing field will fill up. Drop identical monsters on top of each other. When the highest-ranking monster connects, it will disappear and you will get more points.
