## About

A spooky-cute arcade puzzler where you control a quirky little steampunk cannon. Shoot and match creepy eyeballs or skull orbs before they slither their way to the drain! Chain combos, clear the track, and survive as the creepy snake keeps crawling closer. Simple to play, tricky to master and just the right amount of creepy.
