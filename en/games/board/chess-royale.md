## How to play

There are three types of magical abilities:
- create new pawn;
- create new obstacle;
- change one piece to another.

To use the ability, drag the card onto an empty cell (if it is a new pawn or a new obstacle) or onto a piece (if it is a change of piece).

## About

A non-standard chess game in which kings have magical powers. The player needs to cut down the enemy king, building a victorious tactics by combining the queue of moves and magical abilities.
