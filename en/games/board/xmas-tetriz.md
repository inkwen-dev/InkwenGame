Use mouse or touch screen to play this game

## About

This is Block Tetriz game with Christmas theme. Use left, right and down keys to move the blocks. Use up key to rotate the blocks. Alternatively you can tap or click buttons to do the same. Your target is to fit the blocks to make a row of blocks There will be 4 interesting power-ups to get your excitation to highest level.
