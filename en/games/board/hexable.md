## How to play

Click and drag a piece to place it on the board Fill up a full line in any direction to clear it Keep clearing lines and try not to run out of playable moves Clear two lines in a row to earn currency and unlock a new theme

## About

In the Hexable game your goal is to fill a board resembling a hexagon with straight rows or columns using differently shaped objects. Filling a row or column will make space for other objects and will give you points. Try to play for the best possible score!
