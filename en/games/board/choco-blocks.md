## About

Enter the delightful world of Choco Blocks, a tantalizing puzzle adventure where strategy meets sweetness! In this engaging game, your task is to pick and drop sets of blocks to complete rows. But there&rsquo;s a sweet twist: special chocolate blocks are hidden within the rows, waiting to be collected. Your mission is to strategically arrange the blocks and collect all the specific choco blocks from the board before time runs out.
