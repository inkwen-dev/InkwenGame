Use mouse or touch-screen to play this game

## About

Santa Gifts Rush is a Christmas based puzzle game. In this game you’ll need to help Santa collect all the gifts and then land safely to Earth to distribute the gifts. To have Santa collect a gift, click on the gift and Santa will move to it and collect it. After all gifts are collected, click on Earth to have Santa land. Collect gifts and return to Earth in such a way that Santa avoids all obstacles.
