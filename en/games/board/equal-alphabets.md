## About

A fun way to practice picture recognition and the alphabet. Make matches of pictures that contain objects whose names start with the same letter. Make matches by clicking/touching an image and dragging it to the matching image. Complete a level within 2 minutes to get a bonus. Get 500 points for correct matches or a 100 point penalty for wrong matches. Complete all 12 levels to win the game.
