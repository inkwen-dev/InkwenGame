## How to play

Drag and Drop the blocks on the board

## About

1010 Christmas is a unique puzzle game in which you need to collect all the Stars from the board. In order to collect them, you need to fill the row or the column which is containing the Star(s). For filling the row or column, pick and drop the available block sets from the left panel. You can drop the block sets until there is accommodation available, after that the game will over. Keep dropping and feeling rows and columns until all the Stars are collected from the board.
