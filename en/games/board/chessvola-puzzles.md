## How to play

Solve each chess puzzle by finding the best move or sequence of moves from the given position. Click or tap a piece, then click or tap the destination square to make your move. If your move is correct, the puzzle continues automatically; if it is wrong, try again or use a hint.

Complete the 5 Daily Puzzles to build your streak and earn rewards, or play from the full puzzle library to improve your training rating and leaderboard position.

Controls:

Mouse: click a piece, then click the target square
Touchscreen: tap a piece, then tap the target square
Hint button: shows the next correct move
Reset button: restarts the current puzzle
Skip button: moves past the current puzzle when available

## About

CHESSVOLA is a chess puzzle training game built for quick daily practice and long-term improvement. Solve tactical positions from a database of 30,000 unique puzzles, complete daily challenges, improve your training rating, build streaks, earn rewards, and compete on leaderboards. With multiple difficulty levels and themed puzzle categories, every session gives you a clear goal and a new way to sharpen your chess skills.
