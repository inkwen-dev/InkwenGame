## About

Dice and Flag is a fun and strategic game where you control a character to advance into the opponents territory to capture the flag. Each move is determined by dice, creating unexpected and exciting situations. Plan your moves, seize opportunities, and outsmart your opponents to win. The gameplay is simple yet full of strategy, providing an engaging and enjoyable experience.
