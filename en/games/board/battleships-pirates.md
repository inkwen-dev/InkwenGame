## About

Battleships Pirates is a classic guessing turn based game, where each team has a grid on which they secretly mark out a number of 'ships'. Place your ships according your logic, how will be hardest hitting, or choose the cube to replace it random. Try to be the first player to sink all of the opposing players' ships wins the game. Brilliant game for encouraging logical thinking!
