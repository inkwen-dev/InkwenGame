## About

Chinese Checkers is a traditional board game, some people call it "Chinese chequers" or "Hop Ching Checker Game". This game named Chinese Checkers Master, because we have develop a powerful and intelligent AI player. You can play with it or other friends. This game is very flexible, you can config 0 to 6 human players to play the game. Why 0? You can set AI players only, it will demo to you how to play the game! For more detail of the game rule, you can find it on: Wikipedia : https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_checkers FEATURES: - Flexible to place the balls - Powerful computer players - Up to 6 players - 3D game board
