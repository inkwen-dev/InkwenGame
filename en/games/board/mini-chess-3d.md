## How to play

Objective: Solve each tactical puzzle by capturing target pieces or delivering checkmate within the allowed number of moves.
Controls:
Click / Tap on a chess piece to select it and reveal its legal moves.
Click / Tap on a highlighted target square or enemy piece to move and capture.
Use the Hint and Undo buttons if you need assistance solving tricky puzzles!

## About

Pocket Chess 3D is an addictive 3D tactical puzzle game that transforms classic chess into bite-sized, brain-teasing challenges! Analyze unique board setups, predict enemy moves, and find the winning checkmate in just a few smart moves. With sleek 3D visuals, smooth animations, and hundreds of clever levels, it’s the ultimate chess puzzle experience for both beginners and grandmasters!
