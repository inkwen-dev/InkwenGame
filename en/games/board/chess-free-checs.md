## About

Take no prisoners and protect your King at all costs! This 3D Chess game is the best way to play the classic Chess board game on Android! Chess is a two-player strategy board game played on a checkered board with 64 squares arranged in an 8×8 grid. Each player begins with 16 pieces: one king, one queen, two rooks, two knights, two bishops, and eight pawns. The objective is to checkmate the opponent's king by placing it under an inescapable threat of capture.
