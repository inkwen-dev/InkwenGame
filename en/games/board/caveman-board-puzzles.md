## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

It is a time bound game where one needs to find 1 difference between two identical looking boards, filled with cavemen. Once you find a difference between the boards, both boards will refresh to generate a new difference. Find maximum differences within allowed time to get more points.
