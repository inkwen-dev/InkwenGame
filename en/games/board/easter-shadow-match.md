## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

It is an educational as well as fun game for kids. Just look the photo displayed in the left box and click correct shadow of the photo in right panels. Each time you select correct shadow, 200 points will be added to your score. On clicking the wrong shadow 40 points will be deducted from your score.
