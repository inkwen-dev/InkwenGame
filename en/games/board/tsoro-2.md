## About

Tsoro revitalizes an ancient board game with strategic depth and modern flair. Drop colored balls into a wooden board to earn points, aiming to land in your "bank" for bonuses. Unlock star balls by mastering ball combinations, offering unique effects that spice up each challenging turn.
