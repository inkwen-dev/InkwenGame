## About

Rotate to Escape is a captivating puzzle adventure where you must navigate 60 treacherous dungeon levels to find the key and break free. But danger lurks at every turn deadly traps, falling crates, and razor-sharp thorns stand in your way. The twist? You can rotate the entire dungeon to alter gravity, turning walls into walkways and pitfalls into paths. Think fast, plan your moves, and outsmart the dungeon&rsquo;s tricks to claim your freedom&hellip; if you dare! Can you escape, or will the maze claim another victim?
