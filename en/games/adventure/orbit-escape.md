## About

In the game Orbit Escape you have to tap the screen at the right time to make the spaceship move in a straight line towards the next planet. If you don&rsquo;t do this at the right time, and consequently don&rsquo;t reach the planet, it results in game over. You can also collect coins to buy spaceship skins.
