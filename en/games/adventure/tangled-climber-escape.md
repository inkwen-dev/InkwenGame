## How to play

Click and drag a climber to an available anchor slot Rearrange climbers to remove all rope crossings Use the Cut Rope power-up to remove one rope Use the Freeze Time power-up to temporarily stop the timer Untangle all ropes to complete the level

## About

Tangled Climber Escape is a challenging puzzle game where stranded climbers have become tangled together on a mountainside. Your goal is to move climbers between anchor points and untangle every rope before time runs out. Each level introduces more climbers, tighter spaces, and increasingly complex knots that require both strategy and speed. Use power-ups wisely, clear every tangle, and guide the climbers to safety.
