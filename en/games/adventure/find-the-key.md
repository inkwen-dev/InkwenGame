## About

Find The Key is a challenging maze puzzle game where you explore tricky paths, collect stars, and find the key to unlock the exit. Plan every move carefully to reach hidden stars without getting trapped. Once you collect enough stars, grab the key and open the path to the goal. With increasingly complex mazes and strategic gameplay, FindTheKey will test your logic, patience, and problem-solving skills!
