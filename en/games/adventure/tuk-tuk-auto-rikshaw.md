## About

Tuk Tuk Auto Rikshaw is a fun and realistic driving simulator where you become a professional tuk tuk driver. Pick up passengers from different locations, drive through busy city streets, and drop them off safely at their destinations. Follow traffic rules, avoid obstacles, and complete exciting transport missions to earn rewards. Unlock new auto rickshaws and explore detailed city environments as you improve your driving skills. With smooth controls, realistic physics, and engaging gameplay, Tuk Tuk Auto Rikshaw offers an enjoyable driving experience for players of all ages.
