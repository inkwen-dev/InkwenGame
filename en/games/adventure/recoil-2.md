## About

You play as a little character and using various weapons you will need to kill the nasty pink viruses ! You cannot move around though, your only way to move is by using the recoil of your weapon to propel yourself ! Watch out for the spikes, they will eliminate you instantly ! Interact with many differents mechanics and make your way through each level !
