## About

Bedroom Defense is a thrilling horror survival strategy game where you must protect your bedroom from terrifying waves of monsters. Find a safe room, reinforce doors, build defenses, equip powerful weapons, and upgrade your bed to survive each night. Manage resources wisely as enemies become stronger and more dangerous. With strategic defense, tense horror gameplay, and challenging survival mechanics, can you survive until morning?
