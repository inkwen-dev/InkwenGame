## About

Maze Escape is an exciting 2D game where you play as a brave knight lost in a mysterious maze. Your mission is to find the Holy Sword, the only key to escape from the dangerous maze. However, the challenge doesnt end there! To activate the power of the Holy Sword, you need to collect enough lost crowns scattered throughout the maze. Use your intelligence, courage, and exploration skills to conquer Maze Escape!
