## How to play

PC Controls:
Move — W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys;
Jump — Space;
Run — Shift;
Crouch — Ctrl;
Interact — E;
Open Menu — Tab;
Open Inventory — I;
Zoom Camera — Right Mouse Button (RMB);
Bonus Speed from Ads — Z;
Bonus Jump Power from Ads — X.

Mobile Controls:
Move — Joystick;
Jump — Jump Button;
Run — Run Button;
Crouch — Crouch Button;
Rotate Camera — Right side of the screen;
Open Inventory — Button in the top-left corner;
Interact — Button with the "E" icon;
Open Menu — Button in the top-right corner.

## About

🎮 Welcome to the exciting world of "Sprunki Adventure 3"! 🏞️

Embark on an exciting adventure with Sprunki! Explore colorful locations, meet unique characters, and complete fun quests. Each character can give you a special task, and completing it will earn rewards and bring you closer to uncovering the mystery.

🧩 Complete character quests

Your main mission is to help Orange Sprunki open a mysterious chest. What is hidden inside? Treasure, an ancient secret, or something unexpected? Only the most curious players will discover the truth!
