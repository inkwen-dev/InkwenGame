## About

Skull vs Zombies is an exciting physics-based game that promises hours of fun. Each level is a unique challenge that demands a mix of logic, skills, and strength. Your task is to launch skulls at the invading zombies, taking advantage of the games realistic physics. Aim your shots carefully to knock down structures and eliminate the zombies. As you progress, the levels become increasingly complex, requiring creative solutions to destroy all the zombies. With its intuitive controls and captivating gameplay, its a must-try for gamers looking for a fresh challenge.
