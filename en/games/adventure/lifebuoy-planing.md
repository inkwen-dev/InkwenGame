## About

Lifebuoy Planing is an arcade-style action game where you become a flying lifebuoy. You slide through fast, twisting slides, build speed, and launch into open water at the end. Momentum, angles, and timing decide how far and how well you fly across the waves. Perform daring launches, avoid obstacles, and master each slide&rsquo;s layout. Simple controls meet skill-based physics, creating satisfying runs that reward precision, speed, and perfect launches every time. Push limits, chase distance records, and refine every run.
