## How to play

WASD - movement control, spacebar - dive/surface, shift - accelerate, F - turn on/off the flashlight, Q - use a stun gun. To rotate the camera use the left mouse button.

## About

In this immersive underwater survival simulator, you find themselves stranded on an alien ocean planet, faced with the daunting task of escaping its treacherous depths. If you like Subnautica, you will like this game too. As they embark on this gripping adventure game, they must don their scuba gear, hone their fishing skills, and gather vital resources to evade the lurking underwater monsters and secure their passage to freedom. With each encounter, you must strategize, adapt, and overcome the challenges presented by this unforgiving aquatic world in order to emerge victorious and ultimately conquer the game.

Explore the ocean-covered underwater world, but keep an eye on your oxygen levels and beware of threats lurking in the darkness. As you delve deeper into the depths of this enigmatic ocean planet, your survival skills will be put to the ultimate test. Armed with your trusty scuba gear, you must navigate through the underwater labyrinth, gather resources, and outmaneuver the lurking monsters to secure your chance of escape
