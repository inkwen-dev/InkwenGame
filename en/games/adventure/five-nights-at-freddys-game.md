## About

Five Nights at Freddy’s is an extremely popular horror video game. There’s something strange happening at the famous pizza place. Animatronic animals which entertain children during the day go into a completely different state at night. Their programming makes them watch out for intruders, and they become angry if they see someone, who is not supposed to be at the restaurant after hours. You're responsible for security who has just started working at the pizzeria Freddy which is visited by little bears even at night. Your task is to survive without getting attacked. Look at what the cameras installed in the restaurant show you. In case of danger, close all the doors. Do not do that too often or you may not have enough electricity.
