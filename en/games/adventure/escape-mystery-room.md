## About

You are now stuck in a house like alcatraz, but you have to escape the place and have an outside mission that needs to be done stealthy or you will be occupied by robots! You don't have a mobike, even you have nothing! At such an important moment, you can’t be stupid or a innocent bigfoot. you have to brainstorm to seek for the blackbox to solve the challenges you encountered in every room. Your JUEGOS in this game amounts to acting as a detective, you can call yourself Ganes or Ros. You're clever enough to handle tricky, complex and sometimes horrible puzzles.
