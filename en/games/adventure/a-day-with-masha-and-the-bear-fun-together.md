## About

Good morning, Masha! It’s time to wake up... today is gonna be an exciting day full of games and activities to test your abilities and knowledge. Now, with A DAY WITH MASHA AND THE BEAR you can play and have fun with the most complete and varied app, the best option for fans of Masha and the Bear.
