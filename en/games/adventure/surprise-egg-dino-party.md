## About

A few years ago dinosaurs were living in our world. Unfortunately they are extinct. Many of us know them from photographs and books. In this game we wanted to introduce you to dinosaurs! We are sure that they will attract your attention with their colorful skins and different colors. It's very simple what you have to do. Click on the egg to break it and finally reach the dinosaur toys! If you want to see all dinosaurs, you can go to Dinosaur Room by clicking the "Toys" button. Come on, meet them! Have fun!
