## About

Three Cups is a mind-bending puzzle game that challenges players to test their focus, memory, and strategic thinking. In each level, three cups are shuffled rapidly, hiding a small object underneath one of them. Players must track the cup concealing the object and choose wisely, as the cups move quicker and the stakes rise higher with each round. Play the Three Cups game at Game Monetize now and have fun.
