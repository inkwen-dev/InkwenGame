## How to play

🎯Your main task is to get out of the school and not let the evil teacher catch you. You can interact with some items(hide, pick up, activate, etc).

🕹️Control character:
- On the desktop. Press the WASD keys or move the mouse with the left button pressed.
- On mobile. Move your finger across the screen.
