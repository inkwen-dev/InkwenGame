## About

Examine your patients carefully, find the source of their pain, and start treating and curing it. In order to carry out this task and this mission, in your clinic there are all kinds of high-tech equipment, as well as the latest devices and discoveries in medicine. The Ear Doctor is a great fun doctor simulation game where you can play at being the doctor, taking care of their patients, looking right inside their ears with the proper equipment and then using their expert doctor skills to clean up those poor ears and put a smile on everyones' face!
