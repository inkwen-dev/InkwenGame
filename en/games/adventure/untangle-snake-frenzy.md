## About

Untangle Snake Frenzy is a fun and challenging puzzle game where a tangled mess of snakes blocks every path. Your goal is to carefully select and remove the free snakes those not blocked by others to gradually clear the board. Each move matters, as one wrong choice can cost you a life. Plan ahead, think smart, and untangle the chaos to set every snake free.
