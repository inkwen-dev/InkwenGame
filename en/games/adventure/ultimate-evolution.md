## How to play

PC
WASD or arrow keys = movement
Space = jump
Right mouse button = move the camera
Tab = toggle cursor
Mouse-only controls are fully supported



Mobile
Left joystick = movement
Right joystick = move the camera

## About

Fight. Evolve. Dominate the arena.

Battle other players in fast-paced PvP arenas.
Jump straight into the action!
Explore 20 unique worlds, from icy lands to underwater realms.
Defeat bosses, unlock pets, and discover new challenges.

Attack, speed, crit chance, and crit damage

Movement and mobility
