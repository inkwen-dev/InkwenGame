## About

Embark on an exciting journey with Circle of Heroes, a fast-paced arcade game that tests your reflexes and strategy. In this game, you control a rotating circle of heroic characters, each with their unique abilities. The objective is simple yet challenging: match the falling heroes with the corresponding hero on the circle to earn points and progress through the levels.
