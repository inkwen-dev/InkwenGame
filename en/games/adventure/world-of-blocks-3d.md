## About

Do you like building games? World of Blocks 3D invites you into a completely open and huge craft world. In this world, you can use dozens of items / mines to design most of the things you can imagine and take actions in a world of your own. For example, first design, then fly over your unique design using the flying mode! This wonderful simulation game may become addictive for you. It is possible to design a completely different world in every game.
