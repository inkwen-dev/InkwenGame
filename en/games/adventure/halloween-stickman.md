## How to play

Use a computer mouse to control the game.
If you have a mobile device, use a stylus or a finger.
It is necessary to watch the story animation and choose a way to punish the Stickman. You need to click on the buttons to see all the punishments of the Stickman.

## About

Halloween Stickman is an exciting adventure game where you can exact revenge on your tricky friend by rewinding time. Set in a spooky atmosphere, experience a captivating story with your choices influencing the outcome. Play for free on any device, and immerse yourself in this thrilling 2D horror journey.
