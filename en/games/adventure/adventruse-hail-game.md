## About

Get ready for a fun and challenging color-matching adventure! In Adventurous Hail, colorful hailstones fall from above, and your mission is to place the correct color converter beneath each one. Match the colors perfectly to transform hail into beautiful snowflakes and keep the game going. Stay focused and react quickly one wrong color match will end the game! Test your reflexes, accuracy, and speed as the falling hail gets faster and more challenging. How long can you survive and achieve the highest score?
