Customize skins and bases while stealing birds from other players in the 3D world. Join friends in this free multiplayer game with friends and become the king!

## How to play

Movement
W A S D : Move around
↑ ← ↓ → : Move around (arrow keys)
Space : Jump
U : Swing
P : Toggle first/third person camera
M : Teleport to your base

Shop & Birds
H : Buy nearest bird
X : Sell nearby bird

Menus & Panels
T : Open chat
E : Open emotes panel
G : Open leaderboard
I : Open skin shop
O : Open settings
R : Open rebirth panel
J : Open daily spin / rewards
V : Invite friends
K : Open Discord
L : Leave lobby
Esc : Unlock mouse
Click empty space : Close open menus
Close button : Close open menus
