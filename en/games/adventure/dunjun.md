## About

Dunjun is a fast, slide-to-the-wall dungeon crawler across 60 hand-built rooms. Race the clock, dodge traps and enemies, and recover the 12 Relics of Power scattered through a cursed dungeon. 

Only the determined make it through.
Some never return.
