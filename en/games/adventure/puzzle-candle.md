## How to play

Light up the candles by solving each puzzle.

Tap or click the candles and puzzle objects to interact with them.
Use the available clues and arrange the candles correctly to complete each stage.
Clear the level by lighting all required candles.

Goal: Solve the puzzle and light every candle.

## About

Your goal is simple: Reach the end of each level -- but getting there won't be easy.
Solve tricky puzzles and master precise platforming to clear the stages. Use a variety of helpful items to turn the challenge in your favor and find clever ways to progress.
Explore a total of 50 levels set in different environments, each offering new obstacles and surprises.
