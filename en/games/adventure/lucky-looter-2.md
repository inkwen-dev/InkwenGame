## About

Lucky Looter is a new addictive 3D game, featuring colorfull environments and hours of gameplay! Be quick and sneaky and grab tons of loots! Watch out for the patrols and become the king of thieves! How to play: - Drag your finger on the screen to move your Thief! - Release your finger to hide in the box! - Don't get caught by the Police! Features: - Sweet 3D low poly environments - Tons of collectables: buy new cars, new clothes, and much more! - Easy to pick up
