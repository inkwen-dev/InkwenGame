## About

You were hired as a diver to go and collect 7 items from the ocean and also to find the skeleton key to open the treasure chest that is on the ocean floor. Once you find all 8 items, and also collected the treasure, then you'll need to find the boat. Be careful because some of the ocean inhabitants are dangerous and don't run out of oxygen. Good luck.
