## About

Save My Pet Game is a fun and exciting puzzle game where your mission is to protect adorable pets from dangerous obstacles and tricky situations. Use your logic, timing, and problem-solving skills to find the safest way to save your pet and complete each level. Every level brings a new challenge that requires careful thinking and quick decisions. Draw paths, avoid hazards, overcome obstacles, and create smart solutions to keep your pet safe. As you progress, the challenges become more interesting and test your creativity.
