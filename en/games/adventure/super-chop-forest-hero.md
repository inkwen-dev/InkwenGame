## How to play

Hold the chop button on a tree to cut it down, then walk to the truck in the meadow to sell your logs. Spend coins in the Hero Shop on stronger tools, a bigger backpack, speed and flight, and deliver logs to the village to unlock new buildings. Open the gates to new forests, clear monster dens (red warnings show where an attack will land, so step out of them) and defeat each Elder Tree boss. Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, hold left click to chop, Shift to run, Space to jump, E to use, Q for Hero Smash, B for the shop, H to fly to the truck, O to play online, C for quick chat. Mobile: left side of the screen to move, right side to look, hold CHOP to chop, plus JUMP and HERO SMASH buttons.

## About

Super Chop: Forest Hero is a first-person 3D chopping adventure. Pull the Hero Axe from the stone, chop your way through six forests from Oak Woods to the Redwood Giants, and sell your logs at the truck for coins. Upgrade from axes to the roaring Hero Saw, the Thunder Axe and the Cosmic Cleaver, deliver logs to rebuild the village, and take on monster dens where enemies telegraph their attacks so you can dodge and strike back. Wake the giant Elder Trees for boss fights, find relics, plant a grove, befriend forest animals and play online with up to 8 heroes per server, sharing trees, dens and bosses. No typing and no free chat: online players use quick-chat phrases and generated hero names.
