## About

A post-apocalyptic survival clicker with idle and RPG elements. You awaken inside the underground Jericho Complex as Object 73 &mdash; a clone who keeps returning to life after every death. Each new cycle is a chance to uncover the truth behind the Jericho Project. Explore ruined laboratories, collect resources and fight dangerous enemies including mutants, rogue synthetics and combat drones. Defeated enemies drop materials that can be used to craft weapons, upgrade equipment and survive longer. Discover multiple locations across the complex such as laboratories, armories, archives and contaminated sectors.
