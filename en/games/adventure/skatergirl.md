## About

Play with keyboard (arrow keys and mouse or enter) or play with XboxOne controller. Haven't tested with Playstation controller but give it a try, Speed increases as you collect more orbs. Also pick up the green shield icon to safely pass through objects for a limited amount of time. Also pick up the pink clocks as they will slow down time for a limited amount of time. This game is fun and addictive and I loved playing it and making it.
