## How to play

Complete the given objectives and reach the end line to complete the level.

Desktop : 
Use "Left" and "Right Arrow" keys to move around
Use "Up Arrow" and "Space" key  to jump
Use "A" to attack
Use "W" to toggle bullet time
Use "Q" to select between the boys

Mobile:
Tap on gui buttons on screens to perform actions
