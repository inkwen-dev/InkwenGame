## How to play

WASD – Movement
Spacebar – Take off into the sky
Shift – Boost while flying
Left Mouse Button – Hit, interact with the world
E – Pick up / drop item
F – Mem

Mobile Controls:

Joystick – Movement
Right Button – Take off into the sky
Drag Joystick Up – Boost while flying
Right Button – Hit
Touch Screen – Interact with the world
Right Button – Pick up / drop item
Right Button – Mem

## About

Become a Bird! Step into the wings of a fearless city bird and unleash pure chaos across an open world. Fly freely, pull off crazy tricks, steal food, use wild and unexpected weapons, explore rooftops and streets, complete missions, and become a true urban legend. In this sandbox adventure, every flight is full of laughter, surprises, and total freedom.
