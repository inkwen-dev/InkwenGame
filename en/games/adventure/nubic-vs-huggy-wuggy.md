## About

Huggy Wuggy broke into the world of Minecraft! Help Nubic defeat them all! Pick up various weapons and go into battle! Beware of the strong paws of the Huggy Wuggy, the destructive attacks of the huge Huggy and the Ender-Huggy blocks flying at you! Is it difficult to cope with a large number of enemies? Throw a grenade into the crowd and slow them all down! But be careful, after every 10 levels passed by Nubic, the boss of Killy Willy comes to the surface to avenge all Huggy Wuggies! Can you beat him too?
