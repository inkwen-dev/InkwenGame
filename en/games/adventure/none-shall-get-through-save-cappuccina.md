"None Shall Get Through: Save Cappuccina!" - physics puzzle with a gravity gun. Move blocks, open doors, and save the ballerina from the evil AI!

## How to play

PC:

Q - hide/show controls

Z - show/hide mouse

Left Mouse Button - use guns

Left Shift - hold to walk

WASD - run, 4 directions

Space - jump, climb boxes

Tab - menu

E - use, stop using

1 - use and jump, no gun

2 - gravity gun

3 - rifle

Mobile:

? - hide/show controls

Left Joystick - run, 4 directions

Jump - jump, climb boxes

Top Left button - menu

Hand - use, stop using

Pull - use gravity gun

Bullet - shoot

1 - use and jump, no gun

2 - gravity gun

3 - rifle

Unstuck - takes the player to the beginning of the level.

Restart - starts the current level from the beginning, resetting the puzzles.
