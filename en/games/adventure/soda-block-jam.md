## How to play

Swipe a soda crate to move it in any direction The crate collects bottles of the same color as it moves Other colors and crates block the way Once full the crate exits through its matching color gate

## About

Soda Block Jam is a refreshing twist on classic puzzle games. Navigate through vibrant levels by swiping soda crates in continuous motions, collecting soda bottles, and delivering them to their designated exits.
