## How to play

PC Controls:
Move — W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys;
Jump — Space;
Run — Shift;
Crouch — Ctrl;
Interact — E;
Open Menu — Tab;
Open Inventory — I;
Zoom Camera — Right Mouse Button (RMB);
Bonus Speed from Ads — Z;
Bonus Jump Power from Ads — X.

Mobile Controls:
Move — Joystick;
Jump — Jump Button;
Run — Run Button;
Crouch — Crouch Button;
Rotate Camera — Right side of the screen;
Open Inventory — Button in the top-left corner;
Interact — Button with the "E" icon;
Open Menu — Button in the top-right corner.

## About

🎮 Welcome to the frozen world of "Sprunki Adventure 4"! ❄️🏔️

A real disaster has struck the world of Sprunki — the huge Central Heater suddenly broke down, and a harsh winter quickly covered all the lands!

🧩 Begin a new adventure with Sprunki!
🔧 Search for lost parts to repair the Heater
👥 Help characters and complete winter quests

Your main goal is to collect all the necessary parts and help repair the Great Heater before the world completely freezes.

Can you bring warmth back to the world of Sprunki? 🔥
