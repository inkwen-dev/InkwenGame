## How to play

WASD to move Right mouse button to aim Left mouse button to spawn fort part or shoot E to cancel building or to pick-up a weapon 1 2 3 4 to switch a weapon 5 6 or 7 to build a structure Space bar to jump

## About

Fort Builder is a great simulation game where you can build forts and train yourself with different weapons. You have at your disposal three structure options, various weapons and projectiles to use, you have complete freedom to use your imagination and build the most amazing fort.
