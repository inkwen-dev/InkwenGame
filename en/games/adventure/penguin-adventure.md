## About

Penguin Adventure is a puzzle game where you need to control the penguin to collect all diamonds and to unfreeze his girlfriend.There are nine levels in this game and every level is much harder than before. So you need to tap on penguin and drag for power. Watch from spikes because they will hurt the penguin. There are also fire balls and other creatures who can hurt the penguin. Try to pass all levels and to enjoy this puzzle game. You can continue from last time if you play this game again.
