## How to play

A keyboard and mouse are required to play on PC:
WASD — Move
E — Read notes
Tab — Return to the main menu

For mobile devices:
On-screen joystick — Move
“Main Menu” button — Return to the main menu
“Read Note” button — Read notes

## About

An atmospheric first-person horror game where you must explore an abandoned factory and uncover its dark secrets. Solve puzzles, search for useful items, and carefully investigate the dark rooms—danger can appear at any moment.
The factory is filled with intense chases, unexpected jump scares, and encounters with Plush and BOTY. Hide, run, and use your surroundings to survive. Every corridor hides a new mystery, while strange notes and traces of the past gradually reveal what happened at the factory and why its toys came to life.
