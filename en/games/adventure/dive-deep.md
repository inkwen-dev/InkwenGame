## How to play

Desktop Controls:
W  or  ↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward.
A  or  ← (Left Arrow): Move left.
S  or  ↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward.
D  or  → (Right Arrow): Move right.
Left Click : Shoot Balloon Gun
Right Click (Hold) : Grab & Pull
Right Click (Tap) : Attach Tow Rope
Right Click again while roped : Release Roe
P  or  Esc : Pause / Resume

## About

Dive Deep is an underwater treasure-hunting adventure! Explore a sunken world full of hidden treasure, dodge patrolling sharks and spiky pufferfish, and use your trusty balloon gun to lift heavy loot up to the surface. Shoot balloons onto treasure chests to make them float switch to your hook gun to grab treasure directly and reel it in — hold to pull it toward you, or tap to lock a tow rope and drag it wherever you need.
