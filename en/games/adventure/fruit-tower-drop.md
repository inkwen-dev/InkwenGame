## About

Fruit Tower Drop is a fast-paced arcade game where you guide a bouncing ball down a tower of rotating fruit slices. Navigate through gaps in the slices to descend while avoiding dangerous zones. The deeper you go, the higher your score. Stay focused, time your drops, and keep the momentum going without hitting the wrong spot.
