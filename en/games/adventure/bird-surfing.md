## About

Bird Surfing is a 3D online game, where you’ll be flying with a bird over beautiful landscapes. Your only objective will be to fly through the rings and to get as far as possible. At the beginning, this might seem very simple, but it will get progressively harder and harder with each level, since your reaction time will have to get slower and slower. Also dodge trees and other obstacles that can take one life away. Try to get the best score you can and overcome your previous high score. You can pick new skins in the menu, so come on, let’s try the game out
