## About

Two ducks which are lovely from each other, are embarking on an adventure to collect golds in the jungle. You should cooperate your friend and collect all golds and take these golds to the treasure box. Around of the golds there are some enemies and traps. You should avoid enemy attacks and should overcome the traps by the help of your friend. Ducks are moving automatically inside of the game, you only should direct them to the correct way.
