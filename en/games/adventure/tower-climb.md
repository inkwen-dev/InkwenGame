## About

"Climb the Tower" is an exciting mini-game for speed and reaction, where you have to play the role of a real ninja who needs to climb to the top of an incredibly high tower! Help the main character to stay alive and successfully overcome many dangerous obstacles on the way! Get up as fast as you can, because below you will be a lake of boiling lava, following you on your heels! Be extremely careful not to fall into one of the many insidious traps that lie in wait for you ahead!
