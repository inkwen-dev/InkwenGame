## About

Jump Up is a fun and challenging 2D platformer game where you control a player and move through exciting levels filled with enemies and dangerous obstacles. Use the WASD keys to move and press Space to jump over hurdles, avoid traps, and survive enemy attacks. Reach the next checkpoint and complete levels by using quick reflexes and perfect timing. Test your jumping skills and see how far you can go in this fast-paced adventure!
