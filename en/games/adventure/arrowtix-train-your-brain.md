## About

Arrowtix is a satisfying, brain-teasing puzzle game where your goal is to clear the board by sliding tangled arrows out of the grid. But theres a catch: each arrow can only move in the direction its pointing, and its escape path must be completely empty! With 60 tricky levels spread across Normal, Medium, and Hard difficulties, Arrowtix will test your spatial logic and strategic thinking. Can you untangle the mess and clear the board?
