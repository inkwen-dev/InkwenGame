## About

The intergalactic warriors are in the middle of a fierce battle against aliens. The extra-terrestrials have invaded our planet and started to destroy everything. These guardians (that's what we call the earth warriors) are preparing to clean up our planet and protect it from harm; to do this, they need your help. But watch out: Their boss, the Fatboy, will not let you achieve your goal. Attack on Fatboy is a game that will test your speed and your fighting abilities.
