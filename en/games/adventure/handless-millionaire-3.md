## About

lick (or tap) your mouse on the hand on the right side of the screen. This is the safe zone for your hand. When you are ready to grab money (when the timing is right! Watch for patterns) drag the hand through to the money and quickly back into the safe zone. You need a little time to grab the money so do not pull it back too fast or you get nothing. You will know if you were too slow&hellip; How much money can you acquire in one round? Submit your name to our Highscore Board to show us. Or try to pass all Rounds and become a champion.
