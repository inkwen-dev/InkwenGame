## About

Use your brain to rescue the Animals from the deadly Bees. Deadly Bees are waiting to attack the Animals, you have to use your brain and draw safety zones for those needy Animals. Be careful! While drawing the lines as the wrong zone means the game will be over, use low lines to collect 3 stars.
