## About

Explore the busy city with a brave ninja, help him to reach his ninja town Game story starts with Sarogi the Brave Ninja, but one day he went to the nearest city. He explores the City, but trouble comes when he knows that no Ninja is allowed in that city. Now the player has to play the role of Ninja Sarogi and help him to escape that city. Be careful! When crossing the Rails and the Rivers, avoid survival drones. If you like genres such as adventure, rpg, platform, or just want to have a good time then this game is for you.
