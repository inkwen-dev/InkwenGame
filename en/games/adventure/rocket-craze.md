## About

Build your own rocket and launch it into space. Fly as high as you can and up to the Moon. Compete with your friends for the best time in the journey to the Moon. Help your country become the dominant power on the Moon by landing your rocket several times on its surface. Use your abilities to manoeuvre between the obstacles and your strategic skills while upgrading rocket's components
