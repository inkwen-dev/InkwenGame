## How to play

Mobile: Use a joystick to navigate and 2 buttons to control your abilities.
Desktop: Use the arrow keys to move, use A to attract and S to repel

## About

ℹ️About
You’re an Orb-shaped drone with the ability to repel and attract objects, awakened in a strange cave-like structure in a post-apocalypse scenario. Discover strange materials with unusual properties and use them to navigate in this dangerous and mysterious place.
