## About

In Pixel Wizard, you step into the robes of a lone 2D wizard, battling relentless waves of enemies across multiple days of survival. Inspired by Brotato, this fast-paced, rogue-lite arena shooter challenges you to harness arcane magic, collect powerful (and sometimes cursed) items, and adapt your build to endure the escalating chaos. How long can you last?
