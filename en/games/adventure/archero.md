## About

Archero an interesting Archery game with a cute painting style. You will set foot on a fantastic adventure. There are many dangerous creatures on your way, as a brave hero you have to beat them till you kill the evil dragon. You can upgrade your equipment with the scrolls, you will be stronger. There are many rare types of equipment in the treasure chest, go to get them!
