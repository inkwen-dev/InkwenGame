Each level contains colored points on floating pillars Connect matching colors by drawing a path between them Paths cannot cross or overlap Use all available space to connect every pair Complete all connections to finish the level

## About

Cloudline Connect is a relaxing yet challenging puzzle game set among floating pillars in the sky. Connect matching colors by drawing paths between them, but plan carefully paths cannot overlap. As levels progress, more colors and tighter spaces will test your logic and strategy. Think ahead, use space wisely, and complete every connection to solve the puzzle.
