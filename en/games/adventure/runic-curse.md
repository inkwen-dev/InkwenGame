## About

Runic Curse is an action-adventure game where you step into the boots of Adrian, a shipwreck survivor stranded on a mysterious island. 
As you explore ancient ruins and cursed lands, uncover the secrets behind the Rune Curse and search for your lost crew. 
Face danger, solve mysteries, and embrace your fate in a world shrouded in shadows. 
Features Dynamic combat system with endurance management and rolls that give invulnerability frames. 
RPG elements: Level system with selective improvement of characteristics, equipment, and abilities for previously inaccessible areas. 
Many options for combining cold weapons and magic runes.
10 vast locations with various enemies and bosses. 
Crafting consumable runes and improving runes for weapons. 
More than 55 types of spells. 
New Game + in unlimited quantities. Boss Rush mode. Customization of the button layout.
