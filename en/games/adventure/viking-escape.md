## About

Viking escape – is a game in which you control a Viking flying on a dragon, the Viking must go its way to the end to get out of a dark forest filled with goblins and other mutants. Viking can throw different types of weapons, and the dragon breathe a fiery flame. Help the Viking survive to get out of the woods and collect as many points and ammunition as possible!
