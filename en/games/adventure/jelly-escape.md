## About

This is a very casual game. Players need to operate cute jellies and keep rushing forward on the runway to collect small pieces of jellies. The volume will become visible to the naked eye until the end of the race. The game is not very difficult, but it needs to test the players' hand speed and avoid various obstacles on the runway. Come and have a try.
