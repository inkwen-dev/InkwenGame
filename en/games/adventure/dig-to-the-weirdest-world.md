## About

Dig to the Weirdest World is a colorful 3D mining adventure. Steer your drill underground, collect valuable discoveries, and sell your cargo to upgrade power, energy, and storage. Unlock eight drilling rigs and use shockwave, turbo, and scanner abilities to push deeper. Explore 29 underground worlds, from crystal caves and giant mushrooms to lava landscapes and mysterious lost places. Complete 25 quests, collect 261 discoveries, and work toward a depth of 170,000 meters. This single-player expedition supports keyboard, mouse, and touch controls, with saved progress and English and Russian localization.
