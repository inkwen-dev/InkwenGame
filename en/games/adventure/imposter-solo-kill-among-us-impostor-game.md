## About

Among Us is a multiplayer social deduction game developed by the American company InnerSloth. It was released on June 15, 2018. The "Among Us" game has a space theme. In it, players are randomly divided into two teams: crew members and traitors. Imposter solo kill is a fun and engaging puzzle game. Your mission is simple: Execute all living crewmates in the spaceship. Let no one survive. The rule is simple: Hunt all the living crewmates, and escape through the vent to become the best imposter ever. Be careful though, if you get caught under enemy’s vision, you have to start over again. Do you think you can catch the impostor in the space crew fast? Don’t kid yourself! Your spaceship is now invaded by 150+ kinds of traitors! Discover them all if you want to survive. Drag an impostor into another identical one to discover a new kind of traitors! Keep merging and collecting evolutions of impostors until you catch the last one! Among Us mini game.
