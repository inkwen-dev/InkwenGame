## About

Match, merge, and complete delicious cakes in Cake Slice Match! Place colorful cake slices next to matching pieces to combine them onto a single plate. As more matching slices connect together, full cakes are created and turned into sweet rewards. Each completed cake earns money that can be used to progress further and unlock new challenges. With different slice colors, satisfying merging mechanics, and increasingly tricky layouts, every move requires smart planning and careful placement. Easy to learn but hard to master, Cake Slice Match delivers a relaxing yet rewarding puzzle experience. How many perfect cakes can you create?
