## About

The addictive adventure game Letter Ball is about fun simple control, mind-blowing puzzles, and a fun brain workout! You control the ball and hit the letters to make words. Your task is to collect the words in the right order, Each level is more difficult than the previous one because you need to make an effort to get the necessary items. Jump, roll and avoid obstacles. Become the best Letter Ball player!
