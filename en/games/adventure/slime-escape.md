## About

Slime Escape is a puzzle-adventure game where you control a flexible Slime sliding through challenging mazes to find the escape key. Skillfully navigate the Slime through turns, narrow corridors, and unexpected paths; each maze offers a different layout that requires you to observe carefully and calculate the most efficient route. Only by collecting the key can you open the gate and escape!
