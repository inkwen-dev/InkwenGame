## How to play

Fight your way through caverns, defeating enemies and collecting weapons and power-ups as you travel deeper. Clear all the stages and the bosses to discover the century-old secret that they are hiding.

WASD to move, SHIFT to dash, J/K to attack, R to switch weapon, L to use power-up

## About

Cavern of Gears is a 2D action-adventure set in a steampunk underworld. Fight through gear-driven caverns with a growing arsenal of weapons, uncover the fate of the workers who vanished below, and face down the powers that took their place.
