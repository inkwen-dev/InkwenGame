## About

Schoolboy Escape: Runaway is a stealth-based adventure where you help a determined kid outsmart strict parents and break free from his locked home. Navigate shadowy halls, avoid detection, and use clever tricks to carry out your great escape. You’ve made a promise—now it’s up to you to keep it and reach your friends outside.FeaturesStealth mode: Hide, sneak, and avoid meeting your parents.Horror atmosphere: Beware of every unexpected sound, creak and rustle, peer into the dark corridors of the old house.Interactive house: Explore every corner of the house, and become a real detective.Realistic sounds: Listen to footsteps and rustles to predict the movements of parents.
