## About

Help Imposter Escape is an adventure escape casual game. The operation is simple and easy to learn, suitable for all players to try. The imposter is trapped in a strange space and your task is to help it find the exit. Beware of obstacles on the path, spikes will split you into two sections. Collect gold coins to unlock more skins. Don't give up, fifty levels await your challenge, good luck!
