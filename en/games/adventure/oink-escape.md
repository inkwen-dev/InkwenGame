## About

OinkEscape is a fun 3D survival game where you control a cute pig and dodge dangerous traps on a limited arena. Move quickly as obstacles appear from different directions or suddenly fall from the sky. Stay alert, react fast, and survive as long as possible to achieve your best score. With simple controls, fast-paced gameplay, and unpredictable challenges, OinkEscape is perfect for fans of casual survival and reflex games!
