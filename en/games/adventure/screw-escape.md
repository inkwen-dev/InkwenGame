## About

Screw Escape is a challenging screw puzzle adventure game. Dive into this twisted world of twisted iron where you can unlock nuts and bolts, click on products to unlock screw holes that cause metal plates to fall. This is more than just a challenging game; this is a real bolt game that will rock your brain. Are you ready to start the challenge? Join us and become a screw master!
