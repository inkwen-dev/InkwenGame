## About

Are you ready for the struggle for survival in natural life? With the Cube Craft Survival game, you must meet all your needs by yourself. Go around the land and collect supplies for your basic needs, such as hunting tools that you see on the list on the left, food clothing. Remember, the land is full of wild animals. Gather the necessary weapons materials for the hunt and start hunting. Pack vegetables for eating. You will not understand how the minutes pass in the 3D game. Start playing the most realistic of craft games. Have fun!
