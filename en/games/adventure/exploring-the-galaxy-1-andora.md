## How to play

PC - WASD + mouse, phone - joystick + touch
1. Move around Andora and gather resources: chop trees, break rocks, collect ore.
2. Take your haul to processing and workshops to get materials for upgrades and crafting.
3. Upgrade your tools to gather faster and unlock tougher resources.
4. Spend resources to expand the planet: unlock new land tiles and unexplored areas.
5. Explore different biomes, find new materials, and complete quests for rewards.
6. Carefully avoid or shoo away bothersome creatures so you can keep building in peace.
7. Grow your production: build and speed up facilities to progress faster.

Tip: upgrade tools first, then expand territory — progress will be steadier. Welcome to Andora!
