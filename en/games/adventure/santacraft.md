## About

SantaCraft is a 3D is a third-person shooter in which you must survive the zombies and try to reach the portal. Enjoy an intense shootout against the zombies in a vast blocky world. You are the only person alive, and your mission is to build the portal as soon as you can and use different types of weapons to shoot all the approaching zombies. Destroy zombies in the world of Minecraft! You will have to be attentive and prepare your weapon to defend yourself from the evil zombies.
