## About

Help your cute little dinosaur to get as far as possible. In this infinite jump'n run you can always set new high scores. A permanently updated score and highscore display and the possibility to increase the speed of your dinosaur with collected coins promote the fun of the game. The Dino can be controlled very easily with the help of touch-control. Use the left or half-left quarter of the display to move the dinosaur. Use the right half of the screen to jump.
