## How to play

In front of you is a field full of cute sheep, but there’s a problem — they’re all tangled up! Help the sheep escape the pen and reach the barn. Tap the sheep that have a clear path in a straight line, remove the ones blocking the way, and clear the pen until the field is completely free!

Good luck and have fun!

## About

Sheep Escape: Farm Sorting Challenge is an exciting casual puzzle game about a farm full of animals! Help the sheep find their way out and make a great escape from the farm.

On the field you will see sheep that can move only in straight lines. Think ahead, plan your moves, and guide the animals along the correct path to clear the field and complete the level.

Each level is a new challenge: more sheep, more obstacles, and trickier combinations. Use logic and strategy to solve farm puzzle tasks and help every animal escape.

Game features:
• Simple and intuitive sorting mechanics
• Fun and challenging puzzle levels
• Cute sheep and a cozy farm atmosphere
• Relaxing casual gameplay
• Interesting logic challenges
