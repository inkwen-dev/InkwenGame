## How to play

PC Controls:
Move — W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys;
Jump — Space;
Run — Shift;
Crouch — C;
Interact — E;
Open Menu — Tab;
Open Inventory — I;
Zoom Camera — Right Mouse Button (RMB);
Bonus Speed from Ads — Z;
Bonus Jump Power from Ads — X.

Mobile Controls:
Move — Joystick;
Jump — Jump Button;
Run — Run Button;
Crouch — Crouch Button;
Rotate Camera — Right side of the screen;
Open Inventory — Button in the top-left corner;
Interact — Button with the "E" icon;
Open Menu — Button in the top-right corner.

## About

🎮 Welcome to the scorching world of "Sprunki Adventure 5"! ☀️🌵

A new disaster has struck the world of Sprunki — extreme global warming has turned the once peaceful lands into a blazing desert! The rivers have dried up, the lakes have disappeared, and everyone is desperately searching for water.

💧 Begin a brand-new adventure with Sprunki!
🥤 Collect 15 bottles of water hidden across the world
👥 Help characters and complete exciting quests

Your main goal is to find all 15 bottles of water and fill the giant bathtub before the heat becomes unbearable.
