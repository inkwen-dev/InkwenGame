## About

Welcome to block's world — a huge 3D sandbox where anything is possible!
Want to build? Grab blocks and create houses, castles and whole cities. Love adventure? Explore caves, forests and villages. Not afraid of danger? Switch to survival mode — zombies and monsters come out at night, and you'll have to defend yourself!
🧱 What awaits you:

— Creative Mode: unlimited blocks, build anything

— Survival Mode: gather resources, craft tools, fight off enemies

— Dozens of block and item types

— Dynamite to blow everything up

— Friendly graphics and easy controls for kids
The cube world is waiting for its hero. Build, craft, survive — play your way!
