## About

Paper Ball Adventure is a hand-drawn single-player puzzle-platform adventure. Guide a brave paper ball through 12 creative levels where the rules are never quite what they seem. Jump across sketchbook platforms, interact with words and objects, uncover hidden paths, and solve playful logic challenges involving gravity, colors, doors, shadows, and more. Each level introduces a different brain-teasing idea, combining simple platform controls with surprising puzzle solutions.
