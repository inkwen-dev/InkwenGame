## How to play

Survive hundreds of enemies while leveling up and upgrading your skills.

↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward.
↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward.
← (Left Arrow): Move left.
→ (Right Arrow): Move right.
🖱️(Left Click): Move in all directions by dragging your mouse while pressing left click.


Mobile Controls:
Move in all directions by touching the screen.

## About

In the land of ancient Japan, a mysterious portal has opened, unleashing hordes of monstrous creatures into the world. As the last line of defense, a group of brave samurai warriors must stand against the onslaught to protect their homeland. Each warrior possesses unique skills and abilities, handed down through generations, to combat the dark forces. Together, they must fight to close the portal and restore peace to the land.
