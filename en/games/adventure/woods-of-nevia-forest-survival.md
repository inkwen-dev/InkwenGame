## About

Woods of Nevia: Forest Survival is a one-tap action adventure where you battle and survive in the wild. Wield your axe to fend off wolves, chop wood, and build shelter as you explore for resources and hidden chests. Level up, choose upgrades, and outlast growing dangers while managing hunger and health in this engaging forest challenge.
