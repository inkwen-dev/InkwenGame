Trapped in a haunted house during Ramadan nights, collect instant noodles to escape the eerie, noodle-loving Tung Tung. This horror-comedy survival game challenges you to navigate dark corridors, dodge a cheery yet creepy drumming figure, and find freedom before dawn breaks. Enjoy the quirky chaos and tension-filled atmosphere!
