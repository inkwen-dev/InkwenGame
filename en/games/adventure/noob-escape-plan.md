## About

The noob was caught and imprisoned in an underground prison. To make an escape, you need an exact plan that you have to come up with. Make your way through underground chambers and catacombs so that the Noob bypasses dangerous traps and insidious enemies, finding a way out of this prison. On the way to freedom, the Noob will find 25 simple and 25 hardcore levels where skeletons, zombies, endermen, traps, lasers and more will interfere. The path to freedom will not be easy.
