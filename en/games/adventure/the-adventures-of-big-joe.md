## How to play

Use WASD, the arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick to move.

Move the map using WASD, the arrow keys, or the cursor.


Game Rules:
Stop in one place, and Joe will start digging nearby.

Dig up all the ground to complete the level.

## About

Join the pirate Big Joe on an adventure!

Sandy beaches, an old shovel, and treasure chests await you!

Dig! Dig!! Dig!!!

Deep beneath the sand, someone has buried untold treasures and incredible artifacts. Retrieve them all in this humorous adventure!
