## About

Worm Path is a challenging logic puzzle game where you guide a clever worm through tricky mazes to reach the exit. Move across tiles, push blocks, avoid obstacles, and plan every step carefully to prevent getting stuck. Each level introduces new layouts and increasingly difficult puzzles that test your strategy and problem-solving skills. Find the perfect path, overcome every obstacle, and help the worm escape!
