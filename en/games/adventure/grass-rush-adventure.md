## How to play

WASD or Arrow Keys - Move Space or hold Left Mouse Button - Swing your tool Cutting also starts automatically near grass B - Open or close your backpack leave Daily Garden when inside S - Sell all items while your backpack is open Esc - Open Setting

## About

Cut grass, discover rare treasures, and grow stronger with every swing! Grass Rush Adventure is a colorful incremental game where you explore six worlds and uncover more than 300 collectible treasures. Sell your finds to buy stronger tools, expand your backpack, and upgrade your cutting range, speed, luck, and pickup reach. Equip magical auras and rebirth for permanent Strength and Gold bonuses that help you push further. Complete Magic Plant quests, explore the treasure-filled Daily Garden, and hunt for your next big discovery. Start with a simple sickle and work your way toward powerful fantasy weapons!
