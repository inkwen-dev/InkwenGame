Roll diagonally and test your timing in Roll or Fall! Control your dice as it moves across tricky platforms filled with dangerous obstacles like lava, freezing blocks, and hollow tiles that can send you falling. Tap at the right moment to change direction, stay on track, and keep moving forward. Collect coins along the way to unlock new dice characters, each adding a fresh look to your run.
