## How to play

🖥️ PC Controls:
W, A, S, D or ↑, ←, ↓, → — Move
Spacebar — Jump
Move the mouse — Control the camera

📱 Mobile Controls:
Joystick on the left — Move
Button on the right — Jump
Swipe on the right side of the screen — Control the camera

## About

🔓 Escape from Prison is an exciting platforming adventure where you must overcome dangerous challenges and find your way to freedom!

Explore the prison, complete challenging levels, avoid traps, and outsmart the guards who will do anything to stop your escape.

🎮 Game features:
— 🚨 Alert guards patrolling the prison
— ⚠️ Laser traps, surveillance cameras, and dangerous obstacles
— 🗺️ Many challenging levels on the road to freedom
— 🔑 Hidden paths and unexpected challenges
— 🌟 Famous blogger inmates you’ll meet along the way
— 🏃 Dynamic platforming and an exciting escape adventure

Will you be able to overcome every obstacle and escape from prison? Embark on an unforgettable adventure with your favorite characters and prove that nothing can stand between you and freedom!
