## About

Tung Tung Sahur: GTA Miami drops you into the chaotic, neon-soaked streets of Miami during the holy month of Ramadan. As the city sleeps, you ride through the night on a wild, action-packed mission to wake up the neighborhoods for sahur &mdash; the pre-dawn meal. Armed with your trusty tung tung (drum), a motorbike that somehow defies physics, and a playlist of hyped-up beats, your job is to finish your mission &mdash; and maybe cause a little mayhem along the way. But watch out: rival sahur crews, annoyed citizens, and the Miami PD aren&rsquo;t going to make it easy.
