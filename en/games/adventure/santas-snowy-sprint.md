## About

Santas Snowy Sprint is a festive adventure packed with holiday cheer and nonstop action! Help Santa dash across snowy rooftops, leap over chimneys, dodge icy obstacles, and collect gifts scattered along the way. Unlock festive outfits, beat your high scores, and experience the magic of the holidays. Get ready for a jolly race like no other in Santas Snowy Sprint!
