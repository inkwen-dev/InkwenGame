## About

Wolfoos Town Dream City is a bright, friendly pretend-play world for young children! Roam a cheerful town with Wolfoo and pals, then tap the places you love to play inside them. Visit THE MALL to shop, the SCHOOL to learn and create, the HOSPITAL to care for patients, the THEATER for a show, and the amusement park for happy rides. Every building opens a new little activity &mdash; decorate rooms, try outfits, play mini games and explore at your own pace. Activities: town-map exploration, shopping mall, school, hospital care, theater, playground and amusement park.
