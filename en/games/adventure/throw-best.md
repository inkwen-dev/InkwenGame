## About

Challenge your accuracy and timing in Best Throw Game, a fun and addictive skill-based throwing adventure! Control the ball and launch it toward moving targets using simple drag-and-release mechanics. The direction and power of each throw depend on your swipe, so every shot requires precision and strategy. To earn points, guide the ball through the correct colored guard and into the target. As the game progresses, targets become more challenging, testing your reflexes and throwing skills. Master perfect shots, improve your accuracy, and set new high scores in this exciting arcade experience!
