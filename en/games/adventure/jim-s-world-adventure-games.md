## About

Jim's World gives you the chance to step back in time to your childhood with the legendary challenge in the adventure game: Princess Rescue. The World of this Game - a totally new old-school running game, contains well-designed levels, various enemies, super bosses, simple gameplay, excellent graphics, and soothing music and sounds.
Jim's World has been nothing and empty since the Princess got kidnapped into the jungle. But then, the adventure begins!
Your task is to help Jim run through the mysterious jungle, jump over the obstacles and super evil monsters, and save the beautiful Princess at the adventure's final destination
