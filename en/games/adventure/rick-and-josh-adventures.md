## About

Each of the heroes must activate their portal by picking up a special stone. Everyone has their own abilities, for example, Huggy can jump high using a double jump. Steve can crawl into narrow spaces and cling to walls. The game is similar to "Fire and Water" You can play together on the same device. There are 25 levels in the game, new mechanisms and traps appear as you progress, the game becomes more complicated and confusing.
