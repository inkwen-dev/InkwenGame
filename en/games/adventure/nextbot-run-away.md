## About

Get ready for the craziest race of your life in NextBots: Run Away! This is something completely different from anything you have seen before. In this addictive arcade game, meme pictures known as Nextbots come after you! The player will have to run as long as possible, dodging persistent Nextbots that become more and more strange as they go. Or you can play in sandbox mode. Collect power-ups, use upgrades and show off your reflexes in this crazy meme escape game!
