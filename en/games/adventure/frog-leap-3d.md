## About

Get ready for an exciting adventure in Frog Leap 3D: Parkour Runner! You play as a nimble frog trying to navigate through challenging floating islands. Show off your parkour skills by leaping across tricky platforms, avoiding deadly traps, and collecting shiny coins along the way. Features: - Character: Play as a fun, agile frog with precise jumping mechanics. - Levels &amp; Worlds: Conquer 15 challenging levels spread across 3 unique worlds (The Grove, The Deep Forest, and The Volcano). - Mode: Single-player 3D Obby (Obstacle Course) platformer. - Gameplay: Timing is everything! Calculate your jumps, find checkpoints, collect coins, and reach the final platform without falling into the void. Can you beat all the worlds and prove you are the ultimate jumping master?
