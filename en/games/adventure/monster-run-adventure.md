## About

In our magical world lives a little monster - Riki. He like to travel in his world searching adventure. One day traveling through the mountains fell into a deep mine. Now our hero Riki with your help need to get to the surface. You are in the game Monster Run Adventure and you need to help him to find his back home. Our hero is able to slide up the wall. He will do it at an ever-increasing rate. Obstacles and mechanical traps will arise in the path of his movement. You must force your character to jump from one wall to another. Heave Fun.
