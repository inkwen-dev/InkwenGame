## How to play

Control the character using the WASD buttons or the on-screen joystick. The character attacks nearby enemies himself.

## About

In this exciting fantasy RPG, you play as a mighty wizard tasked with defending your magic castle from terrifying monsters. The world is full of mystical creatures, dangerous dungeons, and hidden treasures, and only the strongest will survive. Unleash your full magical potential and gather your team of magical helpers to assist you in the battle for survival.

Game Features:
* Epic Wizard Battles
* Monster Hunting and Slaying
* Magical Upgrades and Progression
* Exploration and Quests
* Build and Defend Your Magic Castle
* Boss Fights and Challenges
