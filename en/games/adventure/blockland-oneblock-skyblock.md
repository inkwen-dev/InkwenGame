## How to play

Move using the WASD keys Use Shift to prevent yourself from falling Press Space to jump Use the mouse to break or place blocks Mobile touch controls are also available

## About

Are you ready for an adventure that starts with a small island and a single block in the sky? In OneBlock Skyblock, use your resources wisely to expand your island, discover new resources, and build your own world. As you break the single block, different resources and surprises will appear. Expand your island, build structures, manage your resources, and become stronger as you progress. Start with a single block, build a huge island in the sky, and try to complete all the levels!
