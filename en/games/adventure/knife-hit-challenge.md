Click or touch to throw the knife

## About

Throw the blades, collect the diamonds, and then go up the level ladder. Remember that you only have a limited number of cutting weapons to complete each level. Don't play other swords or people and obstacles! Don't waste your knife in vain! Collect diamonds and buy new knives in the store.
