## How to play

PC Controls
WASD or Arrows = movement
Space = broom leap / rush
Z = choose to take the first spell
X = choose to take the second spell
C = choose to take the third spell
Mobile Controls
Joystick = movement
Rush Button = leap/ rush

## About

Broomcraft Mystic Evasion is an action game where you take to the skies on a broom, engaging in battles against hordes of enemies! Collect experience points from defeated foes – experience that will help you level up and grow stronger. Explore new spells and evolve the ones you've unlocked to defeat enemies even faster. Face a variety of different enemy types and challenging bosses attempting to halt your progress. Accumulate coins to unlock new, faster, and more agile brooms. Don't forget to upgrade your brooms to achieve maximum power. Combine different magical spells, each associated with its own element, and find your winning strategy! Two distinct game modes are at your disposal: a leveled game mode where you can train, explore new enemies, and, most importantly, conquer bosses; and an endless mode for those seeking to challenge themselves against wave after wave of monsters. Either way, good luck in battle – you'll need it!
