## How to play

Each night runs from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
As the nights progress, the creatures become stronger and the game becomes more difficult.
Check your windows regularly.
Use your flashlight to see what is outside.
Turn off the lights when something is nearby.
Check under your bed for anything hiding there.
Stay alert until 7:00 AM.
Survive all 8 nights to complete the game.


Controls:

Desktop:

SpaceBar: To Under the bed/Return to Bed
Shift: Checks on window
 Z: Flashlight 
 X: Turn off lights

## About

You are alone in your house. Something is outside. A strange virus has spread through animals, turning them into terrifying creatures. Now you must survive 8 nights, from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM, while keeping watch over your home. 

Use your flashlight to check the windows and look under your bed. If something is outside, turn off the lights so it cannot see you. Stay alert.
