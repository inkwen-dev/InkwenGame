## About

Forced to a new world, Pleia must cultivate the landscape and gather magic to restore her companion, Briony who's spirit is stuck in a lantern. Enchant seeds and avoid critters to protect the delicate ecology, and they will donate magic when your home gets close. Use your gathered magic to purchase upgrades and restore your companion to health.
