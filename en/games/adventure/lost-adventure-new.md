## About

Survivors of a mid-air crash find themselves in a mysterious land. The pilot, amidst crafting, mining, and solving puzzles, works tirelessly to save passengers stranded on separate islands in the wild grass. Can they conquer the challenges, reunite, and escape the enigmatic realm before the unknown dangers lurking in the wilderness engulf them all? Embark on a thrilling adventure after a plane crash strands you in a mysterious, wild land. As the pilot, you must gather resources from wreckage, craft tools, and navigate through the wild grass to rescue scattered passengers on small islands.
