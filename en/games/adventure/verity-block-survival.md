## About

Enter a mysterious block-based world where survival is your only goal! Explore a dark and dangerous environment, gather resources, craft useful items, and build a safe place to survive. But be careful&mdash;strange creatures and unexpected dangers may appear when you least expect them. Explore abandoned areas, discover hidden secrets, and find a way to survive the night. Every decision matters as you search for resources and uncover the terrifying mystery surrounding this strange world. Are you brave enough to survive?
