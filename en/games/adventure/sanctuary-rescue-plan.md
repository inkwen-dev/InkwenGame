## About

This is a secret room rescue game with simple gameplay and multiple levels. In the game, the player needs to help Stickman to rescue him from a closed secret room. There will be some random traps in the secret room. You have to be flexible and use your hand to cut the rope so that Stickman falls to the ground and escape from the secret room.
