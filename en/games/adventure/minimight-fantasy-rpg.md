## How to play

Choose your party members and enter the adventure. During battles, select a character and use attacks or skills to defeat enemies. After each fight, collect gold and items, upgrade your equipment, buy new skills and hire stronger fighters. Defeat bosses to unlock new floors and continue your journey.
