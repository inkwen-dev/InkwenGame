## About

Wizard Adventure is a survival adventures masterpiece.This Wizard Adventure game will challenge your capability to defend the enormous amount of attack throughout the whole game. In the gameplay of Wizard Adventure there is a magical gem in a dark cave protected by some monster bats. One day a Wizard came to this cave by mistake and saw the magical gem and wanted to take it with him.After taking the gem the monster bat woke up and attacked the Wizard. Main object of this game is to survive and save himself from those Monster Bats. Features of this game: High Quality graphics Game size is minimal Super simple controls Interesting quests.
