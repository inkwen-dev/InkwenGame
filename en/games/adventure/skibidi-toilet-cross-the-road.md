## About

Embark on a wild adventure with Skibidi Toilet Cross the Road! The Skibidi Toilet is stranded in the middle of a mysterious forest, and it needs to get to the other side of the road. But beware! The path is full of tricky rivers, fallen logs, and wild creatures lurking in the trees. Jump over rushing streams, dodge rocks, and avoid getting caught by mischievous forest critters. Each step takes you closer to the goal, but the forest is full of surprises! Can the Skibidi Toilet conquer the wilderness and make it to the other side? The Skibidi beat never stops, so keep dancing and watch out for the next challenge!
