## About

Cute Bunny Escape is a point and click escape game developed by 8BGames. Imagine that you bought a cute bunny to your relative house as a present. Later your relative went away for shopping. Unfortunately your bunny got trapped inside a room in your relative’s house. Find some hidden object to solve some interesting clues to escape the Cute Bunny. Good luck…Have fun!
