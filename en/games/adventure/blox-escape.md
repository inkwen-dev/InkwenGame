## About

This is logical puzzle game where you need to escape from the frame. It is a kind of sliding game . You need to slide the blocks to do a path for block with star on him. You have unlimited time to do this. Just slide the boxes and find the best way to escape with block with star.You can play in 25 levels, they are all different and some of them are real hard.
