## How to play

💥 Your goal? Blow up buildings with dynamite, bombs, and rockets! Tear down everything in your path and turn any structure into rubble.

🕹️ Game Controls:
- On desktop: Use your mouse to rotate around objects by holding down the left button. Set your explosives in the perfect spots for maximum impact.
- On mobile: Swipe your finger across the screen to choose where to place the explosives. Rotate the object and drop bombs wherever you like.

🚀 Pro Tip: Upgrade your weapons to cause even more destruction and boost your efficiency!
