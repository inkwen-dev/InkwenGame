## About

Vampire Survivors Dark is an online action game where you choose your beloved hero among four characters and defeat the darkness minions you encounter. You will fight with them on the battlefield. Eliminate them with strategic movements, and get the diamonds dropped to the ground to fulfill the level bar at the top. Resist these predatory creatures as long as you can in this RPG game.
