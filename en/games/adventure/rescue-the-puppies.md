## About

Rescue the puppies is a point and click game developed by 8B Games/Games2mad. Imagine that you had been to a farm house there you found two puppies got trapped inside the cage by the forester. Mother dog is in search of her little puppies, Finally she came to know the place where her fawn was kept and now looking for help. Here is the interesting puzzles and hidden objects to find and get escape from the cage. Good luck.. Have fun!
