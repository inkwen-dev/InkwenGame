## About

Escape Maze Rush is a fast-paced and addictive HTML5 puzzle-action game! Help a brave bunny navigate through tricky mazes filled with fruits, enemies, spikes, and hidden surprises. Your mission: collect all the fruits, grab the key, defeat enemies, and reach the glowing portal to escape each level! Featuring 20 challenging stages and 10+ unlockable character skins available from the start, every run is packed with action and excitement. Can you beat all the mazes and become the ultimate escape master?
