## About

Survival War is an exciting survival adventure game where you explore a mysterious island, gather resources, and expand your territory. Chop trees, collect and sell materials, unlock chests, buy tools, and build useful workbenches. Manage your energy, solve puzzles, and fight enemies to stay alive. Explore new areas, upgrade your equipment, build your base, and create your own survival world!
