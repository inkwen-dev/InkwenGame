## About

House of Danger is a fun skill game where you race to complete various tasks in an apartment where your opponents are watching you in real time and setting traps to defeat you. Avoid obstacles like falling lamps and swinging cabinets to complete all your objectives and win the round. Similarly, keep your opponents from completing their tasks by activating your own traps at the right moment. Check out the wheel of fortune at the end of each round to find out the rules for the next round. Turn on the "multiplayer" mode and play with a friend.
