## About

Sift Heads World: Ultimatum is the final game in the Sift Heads World series of point and click action games. You play as a trio of friends that are now wanted by both the police and an Italian Mafia, but the time for running has come to an end. It's now the time for action, and you'll be going for the head of the Mafia himself, Alonzo. Play the thrilling finale of this awesome series and find out what comes next for Vinnie and the gang.
