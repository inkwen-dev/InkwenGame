## About

Bead Route Challenge is a colorful timing puzzle where streams of beads travel along winding conveyor routes. Your objective is to place the correct colored buckets to collect matching beads before they pass by. With only a limited number of bucket slots available at any given time, every placement matters. As levels progress, conveyor paths become more complex, demanding sharper timing and smarter decisions to collect every bead.
