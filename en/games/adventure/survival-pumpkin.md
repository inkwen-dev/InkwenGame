## About

Survival Pumpkin is a fun and addictive Halloween-themed endless survival game where you control a cheerful pumpkin as it jumps to escape spooky enemies and obstacles! Tap the screen to make your pumpkin jump and avoid creepy bats, skulls, zombie hands, and magical cauldrons. Stay alive for as long as you can to score the highest points and challenge your reflexes in this spooky yet thrilling gameplay. Perfect for players of all ages, Survival Pumpkin offers simple one-tap controls and visually vibrant Halloween graphics. Enjoy endless hours of fun as you test your skills, timing, and patience to survive in the haunted night!
