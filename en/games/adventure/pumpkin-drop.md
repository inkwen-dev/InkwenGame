## About

Pumpkin Drop is the coolest physics-based puzzle game. Drop the Pumpkin down onto a metal block by destroying all the other blocks. Once all the blocks are gone and the zombie is on a metal block press the ZAPPER button to fry the Pumpkin! Explore all three worlds and clear all the puzzles.
