## About

Untangle Yarn is a puzzle game in which you use your trusty kittens to unravel the balls of yarn. Just select the yarn and drag it to free it from the tangled mess. Pay attention to the color of the connecting threads. Red means it's tangled, pink means it's untangling, and yellow means you've successfully untangled it.
