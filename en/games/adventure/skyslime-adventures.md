## About

Skyslime Adventures is a fun puzzle adventure where you guide a clever slime through floating platforms to unlock hidden treasure. Move the key toward the treasure chest, use physics and terrain to your advantage, and overcome traps, obstacles, and environmental puzzles. Each level challenges your logic and timing with creative mechanics. Explore the colorful world, solve every puzzle, and help the slime claim the treasure!
