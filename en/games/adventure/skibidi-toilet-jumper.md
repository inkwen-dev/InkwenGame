## About

Skibidi Toilet Jump is a fast-paced, challenging arcade game that will test your reflexes and coordination. In this game, you control a toilet that moves up and down. Your goal is to reach the top of the tower without getting caught in the rising electric current. You will also need to shoot and destroy the enemy robots patrolling the tower. The game is easy to learn but difficult to master. The further you go, the more challenging it will become. Developer: odgameinfo@gmail.com
