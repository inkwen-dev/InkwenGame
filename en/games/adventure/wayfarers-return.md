## About

Wayfarers Return is a challenging puzzle adventure where you guide a young traveler on his journey back home. Explore dangerous paths, avoid deadly traps, outsmart mysterious creatures, and solve clever obstacles along the way. Every level tests your logic, timing, and reflexes with new challenges. Plan each move carefully, discover the safest route, and help the wayfarer overcome every danger to finally return home!
