## About

Choco Benno delivers a charming 2D platform adventure with increasing challenges. Maneuver an endearing robot through perilous environments, mastering double jumps to outwit chocolate-eating monsters and evade traps. Journey through eight intricate levels, savoring the thrill of collecting chocolate milkshakes while dodging crafty obstacles.
