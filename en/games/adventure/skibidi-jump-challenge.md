## About

Skibidi Jump Challenge is a fun yet challenging game to play. Here, the player has to make a stack of dustbins and jump at the right time to place the skibidi toilet on top of the stack. Focus on the timing; do not let the toilet fall, or the game will be over. Website developer <a href='https://naptechgames.com/'>https://naptechgames.com/</a>
