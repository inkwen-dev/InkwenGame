## About

Stickman Sky 3D is a fast-paced action game where you control a stickman running through floating sky platforms, dodging obstacles, jumping between gaps, and collecting rewards. The game challenges your reflexes with increasing speed, dynamic traps, and thrilling sky-high environments designed to test precision, timing, and quick decision-making.
