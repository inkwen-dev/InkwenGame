## About

Explore three industrial districts in a third-person stealth game. Avoid patrols, complete objectives and reach extraction across nine operations. Use crouching, physical cover and decoys to stay undetected. Earn credits for equipment upgrades, choose from three difficulty settings, and learn the controls in a separate practice yard. A three-district campaign links operations with checkpoints between missions.
