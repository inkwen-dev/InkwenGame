## About

Twist And Roll is an engaging skill puzzle game where you rotate the terrain and control balls to roll to the correct destination. Calculate the rotation direction, time your moves precisely, and overcome obstacles to complete each level. Every level presents a new challenge with increasing difficulty, requiring logical thinking and quick reflexes. The gameplay is simple, relaxing, but highly addictive!
