## About

As a Primal Druid, your mission is to capture, train, and command an army of creatures — from fierce battle cats and hybrid monsters to legendary mythical dragons. Strengthen your squad, complete thrilling quests, and survive in a dangerous open world filled with challenges.
 
Gameplay Highlights: 
• Tame Creatures: Battle wild animals and beasts to turn them into loyal minions. Summon powerful allies like an alpha wolf or build an unstoppable army of pocket-sized critters.
• Level Up Your Squad: Collect rare materials and craft powerful artifacts to upgrade your creatures’ abilities and dominate the battlefield.
• Explore & Survive: Forage for resources, face aquatic monsters, and uncover hidden secrets in the untamed wilderness.
• Quests & Challenges: Complete exciting missions, unlock rewards, and test your skills against the dangers of the wild.
