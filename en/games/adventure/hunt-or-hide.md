## About

Hunt Or Hide is a very fun online puzzle game for boys for free. In this game, you can choose to be a hunter or a hider. Enjoy the intensity of chasing the hiders or hiding from the hunter! For both the hunter and the hiders, the duration of each level is the 60s. If you win as a hider, you will get new skins for props. If you win as a hunter, you will get coins for you to unlock skins! Enjoy!
