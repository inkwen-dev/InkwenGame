Escape a flooding tomb by catapulting creatures upward in Sling Tomb 2D. With a unique elasticity mechanic, navigate traps and collect coins, aiming high while dodging hazards. Strategic slinging is essential as water rises and dangers loom above, challenging your reflexes and precision in this thrilling arcade adventure.
