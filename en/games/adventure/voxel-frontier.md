## About

Voxel Frontier is a Minecraft parody that is VoxelWork Games very first project. There are mobs and other tools in the game you can use to survive. You can join your friends by using a websocket server running on your computer. Dimensions: The Fungi Dimension - It can be retrieved by going to a village and entering a fungi portal Mobs: - Orc (basically a zombie) - Spider - Chicken
