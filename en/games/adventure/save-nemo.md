## How to play

Your goal in Save Nemo is to help the fish escape dangerous situations and save the Fish Princess.

On desktop, use the left mouse button. On a phone or tablet, tap and drag on the screen.

- Choice puzzles: click or tap one of the available options to choose an action.
- Pin puzzles: click or tap the right pin, or hold and drag it to move it. Think about the order before releasing water or opening a path.
- Pipe puzzles: click or tap pipe pieces to turn them and connect a continuous route.

Avoid traps and the shark, solve each puzzle to continue the adventure, and use decorations to make your island your own.

## About

Save Nemo is an underwater puzzle adventure where every choice can save the day! Help a brave little fish escape dangerous traps, outsmart a hungry shark and rescue the Fish Princess. Choose the right actions, pull pins in the correct order and connect pipes to solve a variety of brain-teasing mini-games. Explore the underwater story and decorate your island with charming items along the way.
