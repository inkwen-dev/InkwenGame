## How to play

Welcome to Golf Invaders, the ultimate arcade golf shooter! Your golf course has been invaded by punks, bums, and other troublemakers. Your goal? Aim, shoot, and eliminate all invaders using precision golf shots!

🎯 Aim & Shoot – Drag to aim your shot and release to fire the golf ball. Be strategic—each level has limited golf balls, so make every shot count!

💥 Use Power Shots – Unlock explosive golf balls to deal massive damage and clear multiple invaders at once!

🌦 Adapt to Weather – Wind impact ball physics, making each shot more challenging!

👾 Defeat Unique Invaders – Face off against different enemies, each with their own behaviors.

Play Golf Invaders now and experience the most action-packed golf battle ever! ⛳🔥
