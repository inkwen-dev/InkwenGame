## About

Head basketball sports which are a simplified of basketball game. Before each game, you can choose your character and then customize its appearance and other features of the game like playground, weather, AI difficulty, match duration and etc. The rest is simple! You have four buttons to control your character and earn scores against your opponent. Two keys are for moving, one is for jumping and the other one is for kicking the ball with your foot or head.
