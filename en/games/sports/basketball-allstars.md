## About

Basketball Stars is a cool 2 player basketball game by MadPuffers. Choose your team and enter a challenging tournament. Control your player to perform awesome dunks and 3 pointers to win the game. Feel free to block and slap your opponent to knock him out and get the ball, and pay attention to the supershot bar on the top of the screen, once it’s fully loaded, you can use the hot special dunk from wherever you are standing, it never fails. Be the best player on the field. Enjoy Basketball Stars!
