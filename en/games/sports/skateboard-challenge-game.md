## About

“Skateboard Challenge” is a very fun extreme Sport Game! Your objective is to collect all the letters that form the word “S-K-A-T-E” and also the letters that form the word “C-O-M-B-O”, as well as reaching a target score contained in all levels. Beat all the levels performing different extreme maneuvers, use the rail to make combinations of maneuvers and multiply your score! Collect coins to unlock characters. A super radical .
