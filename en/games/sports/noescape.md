## About

NoEsca[e is a game where there are 3 sided-walls in a rectangular space and 4th side is defended by pair of gloves which do the keeping. There is an oval-football contained inside which must not escape from the 4th side while moving. Your job is to defend it with given gloves. Have fun :)
