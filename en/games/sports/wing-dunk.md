## How to play

Tap the screen, click, or press Space / Up arrow to flap your wings and fly up. Gravity pulls the ball down. Drop into each hoop from the top to score. Go through without touching the rim for a SWISH: chain them for bigger combos and more points. Don't miss a hoop, fall off the bottom, or fly off the top! Complete challenges to unlock new balls and wings, and pass the players ahead of you in the ranking.

## About

Flap your winged basketball and dunk it through the hoops! Tap to fly, dive into each hoop from above, and chain perfect SWISH shots to build huge combos and set the ball on fire. Unlock 31 skins by completing challenges, climb 4 leaderboards and beat the other players one by one. Simple to learn, hard to master!
