## About

Ultra Shot is a fast-paced basketball shooting challenge where precision and timing are everything. Step onto the court, read the defender, and place perfect shots into the hoop. Master four distinct shot types: normal shot, fadeaway, power dunk, and floater. Build streaks to earn combo bonuses, improve your abilities in the Transfer Market, and take on three legendary boss defenders: The Wall, Lightning Hands, and the unpredictable Oracle. With three difficulty levels and increasingly intense gameplay, every shot counts. The question is simple: can you score under pressure?
