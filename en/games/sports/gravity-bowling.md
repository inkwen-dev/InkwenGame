Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

In the game Gravity Bowling, you will continue to participate in a rather original bowling competition. In front of you on the screen you will see a playing field on which pins will stand on a small platform. There will be a bowling ball at a certain distance from them. It will be suspended from a rope. The ball itself, thanks to the rope, will swing like a pendulum. You will need to guess the moment and cut the ball with the mouse. Then the ball, having flown the distance you need, will knock down the pins. If you knock down all the objects with one hit, you will be given points and you will go to the next level of the Gravity Bowling game.
