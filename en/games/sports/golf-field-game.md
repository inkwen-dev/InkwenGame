## About

Golf Field is a free online ball game for boys. You need to drill golf balls into holes in various game levels. On the way, you will encounter thorns laser and blocked routes that need to be opened. On some levels, the height of the golf course will also change. You will be able to train your brain and change the way you think. Have fun with it!
