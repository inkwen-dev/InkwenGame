## About

Football Crash 2 is a fast and free American football runner you can play online, with no download. Grab the ball, sprint down the field and dodge waves of defenders to score touchdowns and beat your high score. Hold the left or right side of the screen, or drag your finger, to swerve through the defensive line. Pass close to a tackler for a PERFECT DODGE, chain them together for a x5 combo, collect coins and grab lightning bolts to trigger CRASH MODE and smash through everyone. Pick up a shield to survive one tackle. Unlock all 24 teams, each with its own perk: magnet, kevlar, fast feet, long crash, double touchdowns and more.
