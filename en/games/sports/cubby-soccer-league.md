## How to play

For computer:

- Use arrow keys to move left and right

- use "Spacebar" to move forward

- Use "Spacebar" to shoot and take penalties

- Use the arrow keys to control the goalkeeper.

For mobile:

- Use the on-screen Direction keys to move left and right

- use "ball icon" to move forward

- Tap the "ball icon" or the "screen" to shoot and take penalties

- Tap the colors on the goal to control the goalkeeper
