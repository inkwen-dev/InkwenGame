## About

Extreme Balancer 3 is an adventurous game in which you have to balance the ball and reach to the boat by escaping the traps. The whole surrounding is full of water and you have to balance the ball on the wooden bridges by avoiding the obstacle and you have to reach the boat without falling in water. Extreme Balancer 3 comes with improved controls than its earlier part so you can balance the 3D ball much accurately. Enjoy!
