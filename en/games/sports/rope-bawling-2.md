## About

Rope Bawling is a new kind of object understanding puzzle casual level game. Rope Bawling combines the gameplay of bowling with the gameplay of physical line-drawing puzzles. Unlike traditional bowling games, Rope Bawling requires you to cut the Rope and knock down all the bottles as the ball falls. As the game goes on, the level of difficulty will be more and more difficult, each time the challenge must be the accurate grasp of the time to cut the rope!
