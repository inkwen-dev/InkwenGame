## About

Dodgeball is a water sport game in which you will be able to play various entertaining game modes inside water. You can participate in a match, you can participate in wrestling inside the water against your opponent. You can also participate in a career mode. In wrestling mode, hit your opponent as fast as you can and drop your opponent over the other player. In a soccer game, should score five before your opponent. Join various game modes, customize your player, and have a great time!
