## How to play

Choose a mode. Press on the pitch, drag in the direction you want to move, and release to push your player. A longer drag makes a stronger push. On a keyboard, hold a direction key (WASD or an arrow), then release it. Score more goals than the other team.

## About

Every shot can turn into a wild tumble. Play soccer with floppy ragdoll players: practice alone, face bots in 1v1 or 2v2, or find an online opponent. Aim your pushes, score goals, watch replays, and collect cards to change your player's kit.
