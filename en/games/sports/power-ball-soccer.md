## How to play

Jump onto the field, use awesome powers, and score goals to beat your rivals! Try Challenge Mode for tougher matches and unlock cool new characters as you play.

Desktop Controls:

W: Move upward.
A: Move downward.
S: Move left.
D: Move right.

E: Special ability.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
Special ability: Tap to activate.
