## About

Floppy Football is a fun and addictive arcade-style game that is enjoyed by people of all ages. The objective of the game is to keep a floppy ball in the air as long as possible by hitting it with a player’s head. The longer the ball stays in the air, the higher the score. The game is simple to play and can be enjoyed by both casual and hardcore gamers. To play the game, simply use the arrow keys to move the player and press the spacebar to hit the ball. The ball will start to slow down as it gets higher, making it more difficult to keep it in the air. In conclusion, Floppy Football is a simple yet entertaining game that provides hours of fun. Whether playing alone or with friends, the game offers a challenge to players of all skill levels. Try it out today and see how high you can score!
