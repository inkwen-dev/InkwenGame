## About

GoalKing is an exciting penalty football game where you participate in intense shooting battles to score goals and win. Aim carefully, control the power, and choose the right moment to beat the opponents goalkeeper. At the same time, you must react quickly when standing in front of the goal to block dangerous shots. The gameplay is simple yet thrilling, providing a realistic and highly engaging penalty experience!
