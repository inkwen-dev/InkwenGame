## About

Step into the sandy arena of Crab Penalty, where you take on the challenge of scoring goals with an unlikely yet charming player&mdash; a crab! Set on a picturesque beach, this fun and engaging game combines the thrill of penalty shooting with the unique twist of controlling a crab. Aim carefully and use your skills to outsmart the goalie, ensuring your crab kicks the ball into the net. Crab Penalty offers endless entertainment and a delightful seaside adventure.
