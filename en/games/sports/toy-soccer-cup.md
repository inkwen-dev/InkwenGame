## How to play

Drag any of your toy players to aim, then release to flick them and strike the ball — score more goals than your rival before the timer runs out. On mobile, touch and drag; on desktop, click and drag with the mouse (no keyboard needed). Win matches to earn coins, unlock new teams and gear, climb the leagues, and take on other players in real-time online duels.
