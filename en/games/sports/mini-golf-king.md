## About

It's not just golf. It's an action-packed mini-golf course, full of adventures and thrills you've never experienced before! Play beautiful courses against golfers from around the world in real-time online tournaments! Earn and collect trophies to unlock the best stadiums. Discover and upgrade golf clubs, balls and gloves to customize your game. Compete in the weekly leaderboard and tournament to win awesome prizes and experience glory. Perform incredible trickshots in Hole in One Challenge and Tour Challenge. You can also play with your Facebook friends! Playing your shot is pretty easy. Just slide and release to hit the ball like you're playing pool! Aim and shoot carefully to collect the most gems while taking the fastest path to the hole. You can even hit your ball against your rival's to take away their gems! Hit your ball through wonders. Golfers' wildest dreams have finally come true! Let's ride slide tubes, swing on drawbridges, jump over pyramids, explore mazes with bomb traps, and go deeper and fly higher with accelerators and springboards! Taking advantage of all the tips while honing your skills is the key to victory!
