## About

Soccer Rebound is football played on a pinball table. You control the two flippers in front of your own goal, and your toy teammates do the running: they chase the ball, pass it and shoot on their own. Keep the ball out of your net, bank it off the side rails to get round the keeper, flip it up to your playmaker for a first-time finish, and from stage 4 use the Bend Shot to curl your next flip round a defender.
