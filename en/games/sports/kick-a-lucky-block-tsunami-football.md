## About

Kick a Lucky Block: Tsunami Football is a fun and fast-paced game where your goal is simple &mdash; kick the lucky block as far as possible! Every kick gives you a chance to roll a unique brainrot and test your luck. Once you land, you turn into a brainrot and must escape a massive tsunami that you triggered yourself! The farther you kick, the bigger the rewards &mdash; but also the greater the danger. Collect brainrots and place them on your plot to generate income. Buy weights and train your legs to kick farther and stronger! The game blends randomness, progression, and action into an exciting gameplay loop perfect for quick sessions.
