## About

Hey, would you like to play an infinite golf game? Yes, exactly! I mean, after one hole comes another right away, and you dont have to wait long lines or swap clubs to play. All you have to do is enter the direction and carefully measure the force of your shot! Remember that when you hit a shot, you can occasionally collect dollars that youll need to unlock all the available balls! Like the coral one, the green one, the yellow one, and so on! Come on, what are you waiting for? Play infinite golf now!
