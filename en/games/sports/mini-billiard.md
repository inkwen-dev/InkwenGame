## About

The game is a simple reinterpretation of the game of billiards but without rules and technicalities ... your child will be really happy to play it for hours and hours! Just to understand, you have to get all the billiard balls into the hole as quickly as possible trying to make the fewest mistakes! This game is ideal for all children and teenagers who do not want a challenging and technical pool but rather prefer a fun, simple, not boring and colorful pastime with which to spend time.
