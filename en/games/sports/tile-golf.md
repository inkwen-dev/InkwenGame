## About

Tile golf is a new golf game developed in javascript and HTML5 . The objective of the game is to collect first the coin and then to put the ball on the whole. To score higher you need to do these using a single shoot. Try to complete all the 21 levels. Also, you can submit your score to the leaderboard.
