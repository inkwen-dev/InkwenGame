## About

Pro Football Eleven 2027 is a 3D football game by BREYMANTECH, played at full eleven-a-side inside the packed, floodlit Breymantech Arena. Pick from 10 European and American clubs and play with assisted controls: pass, through ball, cross and shoot with a power gauge. The match has offside, fouls, cards, set pieces, goal replays, ragdoll tackles and smart team AI. Choose your mode: Exhibition: a quick match against the CPU. BREYMANTECH Cup: an 8-club knockout tournament. Draws go straight to penalties.
