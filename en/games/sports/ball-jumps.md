## About

In this interactive game, players click the mouse to propel the ball into the air, aiming to achieve higher scores and unlock new achievements. With each click, the ball jumps, accumulating points as it ascends. Players strive to surpass their previous high scores, while also pursuing various achievements to enhance their gaming experience.
