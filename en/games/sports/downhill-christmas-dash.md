## About

The snow is falling and Santa Claus is hitting the slopes! He has strapped on pair of skis and is racing down the hill collecting gift boxes along the way. Race to the bottom and collect as many gifts as you can, but look out for those evil Snowmen! They are out to stop Santa from his Christmas mission. If you bump into a snowman you will fall and the game will end. There are special power-ups along the way that will help you get a higher score. The winged boots increase your speed and raise the gifts scores. The golden star make you invincible for 8 seconds.
