## About

The chameleon snake is a very interesting alternative game. In the game, the color of the stars will change every time the greedy snake controlled by the player eats the stars. Then we can only hit the obstacles with the same color as ourselves. Then we can eat the stars all the time and get longer. There are also interested friends come to experience it.
