## How to play

Desktop:
- WASD or arrow keys: move
- Or hold the left mouse button: walk towards the cursor
- Walk onto pads to unlock them, walk through cash to collect it
- Space or left click: Power Shot during matches

Mobile:
- Drag anywhere on the screen: move (a joystick appears under your finger)
- Tap: Power Shot during matches

## About

Build the greatest youth football academy in town! Start with a single ball crate on a sunny Sunday Park pitch and grow it into a full Training Ground with a gym, rondo ring, free-kick wall, agility course and your own 7-a-side pitch.
Sign young talents off the bus, carry balls and water bottles to keep the drills running, and collect training fees as your players improve. Every trainee has their own stats, position and rarity, from Common to the rare Wonderkid.
When a player graduates, it's your call: sell them to a big club for a fat transfer fee, or promote them to your academy squad. Hop on the team bus for away matches, nail the Power Shot to score, and climb from the bottom division to the top of the league.
Hire ball boys, water carriers, a receptionist, coaches and an accountant to automate your academy, then upgrade everything in the Manager's Office. Come back every day for daily rewards, quests, scouting trips and the Daily Cup. Your staff keep earning while you're away!

Features:
- Relaxing idle-arcade academy building with two areas to unlock
- Collect trainees of four rarities and fill your Hall of Fame
- Sell or promote graduates and manage your squad
- Quick away matches with a timing-based Power Shot
- League with 5 divisions and promotions
- Daily rewards, quests, scouting missions and a Daily Cup
- Plays on desktop and mobile, portrait and landscape
