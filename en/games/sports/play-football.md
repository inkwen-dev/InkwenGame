Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

This is a casual game in which there is a boy soccer player that You should find the exact angle of the shot, and just click to shoot the ball between the player's legs, in the start screen. You need to be careful that the ball passes only from the player's legs. Gradually, the speed of the game is increased. This game is very interesting.
