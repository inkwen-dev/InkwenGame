## How to play

Drag your finger (or mouse) to draw the shot path
Release to kick the ball
Use curves and angles to outsmart the goalkeeper

## About

Penalty Shooter is an immersive football penalty game that perfectly captures the most thrilling moments of a football match. You’ll take turns playing both the role of the taker and the goalkeeper – as the taker, you’ll need to outwit the goalkeeper with precise aim and perfect timing to send the ball into the back of the net; as the goalkeeper, you’ll need to read your opponent’s intentions, make crucial saves and become the hero of the team. Easy to pick up, hard to master. Are you ready to become the ultimate penalty taker?
