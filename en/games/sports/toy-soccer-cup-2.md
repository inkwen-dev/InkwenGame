## About

Toy Soccer Cup is a fast, tabletop-style soccer game where you flick your little players to pass, shoot and score. Simple to pick up, tricky to master. Game modes: &bull; Quick Match &ndash; jump straight into a game against the CPU. &bull; Tournament &ndash; beat rival teams one by one and lift the cup. &bull; 2 Players &ndash; play against a friend on the same device. &bull; Online Multiplayer &ndash; challenge another player in real time. Customize your team in the shop: unlock kits, balls and pitches. Score three goals or lead when the clock runs out to win. Colorful 3D stadiums, a cheering crowd and satisfying goal celebrations bring every match to life.
