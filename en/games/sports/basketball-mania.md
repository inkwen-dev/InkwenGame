## About

Basketball Mania is a free online sports simulation game for kids. It helps you to improve your throwing skills. You just need to orient and slide up to throw the ball to the basket. Each level has a certain number of goals for you to achieve. In each level, you are allowed to fail only 3 throws. Reach a certain number of goals and you will move on to the next level.
