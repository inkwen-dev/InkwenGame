## About

Prepare to play a fun game of golf and pool. Golf and billiards at the same time! You have to make calculations on the snooker board to get the ball into the golf notch. Drop the ball in the direction the ball will go with the mouse. Get the ball into the hole with an accurate shot and move on to the next level. To make the best score, you have to make successful shots in a row. Keep the fun always with 30+ different levels and pool tables! Have fun.
