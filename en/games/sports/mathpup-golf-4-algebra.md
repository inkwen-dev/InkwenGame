## How to play

Swipe using touch or the left mouse button to set the angle and power of each shot Release to have MathPup hit the ball

## About

MathPup loves golf and algebra. This educational golf game combines both of those. 30 holes to play without any time limit but you will have to answer basic algebra questions to play each hole and if you hit the ball in the water or sandtrap you'll have to answer an algebra problem correctly to remove the penalty. If you need help completing a hole or just want to see how to play, click on the Video button on the main menu to see the overview video along with how to complete each hole.
