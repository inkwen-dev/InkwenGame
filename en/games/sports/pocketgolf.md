## About

A minimalist golf challenge drawn in a hand-made paper style. Flick the tiny paper ball across doodle fairways, bounce off pencils and erasers, and sink the hole in as few strokes as possible. Easy to pick up, tricky to master &mdash; unlock new balls and beat your own best score in bite-sized sessions.
