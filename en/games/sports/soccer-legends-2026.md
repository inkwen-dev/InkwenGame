## How to play

Score more goals than your opponent before time runs out. Move, jump, and strike the ball with perfect timing while using your supershot to surprise your opponent and gain the advantage. 
Player Controls
Move: A / D or Left / Right Arrow Keys
Jump: W or Up Arrow Key
Slide: S or Down Arrow Key
Shot: X or L
Supershot: Z or K
