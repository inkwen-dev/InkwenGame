## About

Calling all blockheads! In Blocks - Move &amp; HIT youre a hero on a mission, but the path is ever-changing. Navigate a maze of immovable stone blocks that bounce you off course with every bump. Will you hit a 45-degree angle and zip diagonally, or take a full 90-degree turn? Master the rebounds, solve the shifting puzzle, and guide your block to its destined end!
