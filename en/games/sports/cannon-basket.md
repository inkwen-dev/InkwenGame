## How to play

Tap or click to activate the cannon and launch a ball.Control the platforms by tapping or dragging to adjust their position.Use the platforms to guide the ball’s trajectory toward the basket.Try to get all the balls into the basket to complete the level.

## About

Cannon Basket is a fun and challenging physics-based puzzle game where your goal is to shoot colorful balls into the basket by adjusting moving platforms. Use your brain and quick reflexes to set the right trajectory and get every ball in! With simple one-touch controls and 45 unique levels, the game offers the perfect mix of logic, precision, and arcade-style fun. As the difficulty increases, you’ll need sharp timing and smart strategy to succeed. Can you complete every stage and become the master of ball cannons?
