## About

Drunk Basket is a hilarious 1-2 player active-ragdoll basketball game. Take control of wobbly, physics-driven players and time your shots to sink baskets before your rival play solo against the AI or go head-to-head with a friend on the same device. Every round throws in a random twist, from low gravity to a giant ball, keeping every match unpredictable and fun.
