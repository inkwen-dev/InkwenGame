## How to play

Use left and right arrow keys to move left right respectively

## About

Kick is a single player game in which gamer assumes the character of a soccer player keen on sharpening his skills in one of the most unconventional locations(street alleys). Awareness, speed and shot accuracy will come handy as you kick the ball around. Points are subtracted when ball smashes a window but are added when the ball consequently hits a wall. The aim is to keep the points total above zero for as long as possible
