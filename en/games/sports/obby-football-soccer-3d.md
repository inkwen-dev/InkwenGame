## How to play

🎮 Controls (PC):
Shift — acceleration (sprint)
Q — ball protection.
E — sliding tackle and ball tackling.
LMB — kick the ball (the force of the kick depends on the duration of pressing).

📱 Controls (phone):
All actions are performed via convenient buttons on the screen.

🚀 Player upgrade
Play matches, earn experience and improve your character's parameters in the upgrade window:

⚡ Kick force — kick harder and more accurately.
🏃 Speed ​​— move faster across the field.
💪 Endurance — play longer at maximum.

🎨 Extras
- Unlock and buy unique skins to stand out from other players.

- Combine skills and playing style to become a real football star! ✨
