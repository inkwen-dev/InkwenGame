## About

Curve Free Kick is a 3D free kick game with realistic ball physics. Move your player behind the ball and choose your run-up: the wider the angle, the more the ball curves, and the longer the run-up, the harder the shot. Bend the ball around the wall, use the wind and beat the goalkeeper. The wind blows from the left or right on every level and pushes the ball in the air. Curve with the wind for a huge swerve, or against it to cancel it out. The keeper can be fooled by both wind and spin.
