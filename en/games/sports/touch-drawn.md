## About

With Touch Drawn, which is a fun soccer game, you have to show the route the footballer will go and get the player to the goal line. You have to heck out the rival football players in your way. You can also develop tactics by moving your teammates correctly. Hypercasual fun awaits you with 100 different levels and 16 different football players!
