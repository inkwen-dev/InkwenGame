## How to play

• Fly — drag on the screen (WASD or arrows on PC): move in any direction in zero-g.
• Dash-Strike — tap the screen (Space or J on PC): a quick dash that kicks the ball and knocks the rival away.
• Brake & Trap — hold (Shift or K on PC): stop fast and catch the ball.
• Special — when the meter is full, unleash your hero's signature power strike.

## About

Ragdoll Football is wild arcade soccer with ZERO gravity! Your goofy yet lovable ragdoll player floats, tumbles and flies across the arena to smash the ball into the opponent's goal.

Every match lasts just 60 seconds and bursts with silly physics, flashy special strikes and surprise modifiers — giant ball, bouncy ball, huge goals, currents and more. No boring rounds, ever!

Win cups in Tournament mode, climb the ranked ladder in 1v1 Duels, unlock dozens of characters, balls and arenas, complete daily quests and storm the leaderboards. Easy controls, instant fun and endless replayable chaos — drop in for a minute and stay for hours.
