# Kick Off

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/kick-off/cover.jpg)

A dynamic sport game based on physics. Bounce the ball and try to make it into the goal. You loose a chance when the ball drops below the edge of the screen.

## How to play

Drag to move the player character Moving the player character into the ball will make the ball bounce Do not let the ball fall below the edge of the screen You score when the ball bounces into the goal on the top of the screen
