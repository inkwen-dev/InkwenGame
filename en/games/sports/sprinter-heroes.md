## How to play

Tap the LEFT RIGHT ARROWS and A D by turns to run

## About

The Sprinter Heroes game invites you to a Worldwide running tournament. The heroes of the run can be you and your friend. Run on seven different continents and try to be the champion with high scores! You have to race both with your friend and with other runners. Every next level you are unlocking is getting harder and harder.... The last race is really tough!
