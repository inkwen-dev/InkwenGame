Use your mouse or finger to interact with the patient or use any of the medical tools

## About

Step into the wacky world of Hospital Stories: Doctor Soccer, where you take on the role of a humorous doctor working at a soccer team's infirmary. With a variety of mini-games, you will have to tackle some truly absurd medical conditions of the team and their loved ones. From patching wounds, extracting foreign objects, treating zits to even hitting scars with sausages(?), this game will keep you entertained. Are you ready to complete all levels? This game is sure to test your medical skills in a way you never thought possible.
