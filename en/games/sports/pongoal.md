## How to play

Mobile/Touch Devices:

Tap the UP button to move your paddle upward
Tap the DOWN button to move your paddle downward
Hold buttons for continuous movement

Desktop/Keyboard:

Press UP ARROW or W to move paddle up
Press DOWN ARROW or S to move paddle down

## About

PONGOAL is an exciting fusion of classic Pong and high-stakes football action! Experience the timeless paddle gameplay you love, reimagined with the energy and intensity of a penalty shootout. Defend your goal, outsmart your opponent, and score spectacular goals in this fast-paced arcade sports challenge.

Battle against intelligent AI opponents across three difficulty levels, or challenge a friend in local multiplayer mode. Every match is a test of reflexes, strategy, and precision as you race to be the first to reach the winning score. With dynamic physics, responsive touch controls, and thrilling goal celebrations, PONGOAL delivers non-stop competitive fun!
