## About

With 3D stickman basketball models and physics ball-launching arcade game rules, Jump Dunk 3D requires you to shoot three goals first at each level. I strongly suggest you focus on the releasing position of the ball instead of the jumping stickman model. Don't forget to use the glass walls to make a clean shot!
