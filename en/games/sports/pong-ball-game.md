## About

"Arcade Soccer Pong" mode added. Now you can play ping-pong with obstacles - namely, rocks - on the field! Game becomes unpredictable and challenging. Or you can collect coins, to get best score. Or you can mix it. Anyhow, this definitely changes your pong-ball experience to better and will add more fun to the game. Go to "Options" and visit "Game Mode" tab: use scrollbar to pick either original pong-ball mode or arcade taste. By default, "classic" ping pong soccer mode is selected. When selecting "arcade", you will get an option to also adjust flavor - if to have rocks on the field (and how many of them) and if to show coins, available for collection. To pick coins, make sure the ball hits it.
