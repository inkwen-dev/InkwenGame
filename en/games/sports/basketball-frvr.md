## About

Do you love basketball? Do you want to test your skills and have fun at the same time? Then you should try Basketball Shoot, a 3D simulation game that challenges you to score as many baskets as you can. You can use your mouse to adjust the power and angle of your shot and choose the right moment to release the ball. The more baskets you make, the higher your score will be. But be careful, if you miss a shot, you will have to start over. Try Basketball Shoot today! Have fun!
