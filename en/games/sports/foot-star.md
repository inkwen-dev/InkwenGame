## About

Foot star is a shooting soccer game, in this game u must collect all 3 stars making a good kick to achieve a goal. Hot to play: Set your aim and strength using mouse so that the ball lands safely in the goal in each level. Collect all the 3 stars on each level to have opportunities to unlock all game achievements. Foot star has 30 levels which increase in complexity on every stage more obstacles comes in front of you but sure u will deal with. Have fun.
