## About

"Street Cricket" is a casual game with cute graphics and sounds. In each round, you get a target, ball and wickets to start with. As soon the ball reaches you just tap to hit it. With the power bar, you can take the shots higher. More high power bar means more runs. Just start tapping and hit the shots. It's Cricket time. So, grab your bat and start hitting sixes.
