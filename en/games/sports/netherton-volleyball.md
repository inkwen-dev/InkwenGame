## About

Tap on green, spike over the block, win the cup! Netherton Volleyball is a quick pixel-art volleyball game where you call the shots for a whole 6v6 team. Time your taps to serve, pass, dig and block, then pick your hitter: each rings color shows the chance to score. Your players level up after every match and learn new moves, from quick sets and pipes to jump serves and diving saves. Beat 18 rival teams, each with its own style, and lift the cup in a best-of-five final. Short matches, real volleyball rules, and the first match teaches you as you play.
