## About

Choose your favorite runner from the 8 available and beat everyone else in a breathtaking race! Clear the hurdles perfectly to avoid being overtaken by your opponents. Climb onto the winners podium to participate in the next race and win all 12 stages to be crowned the sole champion! Use the space bar to jump at the right moment. Perform a perfect jump to earn more points.
