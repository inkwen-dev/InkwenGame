## About

Hit &amp; Knock Down is a fun casual game where you aim to throw baseballs. Shoot as many targets as possible. Test your precision, reaction speed, and throwing skills. You aim to hit the right targets in each level. The goals at the level are very varied, such as wood, stone, etc. Each level presents different targets. You should hit with a baseball.
