## About

Flip Dunk is a fun shooting game with you. In Flip Dunk, you have to shoot baskets with baskets. You have to use the launcher for this. You must prevent the ball from escaping from the launcher. Otherwise, the game will fail. There are 2 different modes in Flip Dunk game. If you want, you can play different +100 levels in the mission mode, or you can play the ball until it falls with the endless mode. Have fun.
