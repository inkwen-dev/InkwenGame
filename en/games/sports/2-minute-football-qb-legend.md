## How to play

Your goal is to complete passes and score Touchdowns! (getting the ball in the end zone)  You must have more points than your opponent when the game ends in order to win the games. 

Select a play by tapping it. When the players start moving, look for the player that is the least guarded by the defense. Tap near the player you'd like to throw the ball to. Running is automatic, but can be controlled by clicking and dragging the mouse to the left of right, or dragging your finger on touch devices.
