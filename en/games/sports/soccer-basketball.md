## About

Tap and slide the soccer ball, control its angle and power, then shoot towards the hoop. The perfect effect and physic effect will help you see more clearly. The game is an endless arcade game, than shoot towards the hoop to win the levels; as the difficulty could increases, there are four different random movements for the hoop. You have only 3 shoots to complete each level! Are you able to try to sign the best record in the local leaderboard? HOW TO PLAY: 1: Touch the screen! 2: Drag the ball to change the power and angle! 3: Release to shoot!
