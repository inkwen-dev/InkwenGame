## About

So let's get down to shots on goal. In principle, everyone is ready, the goalkeeper stands in his place, the additional player interfering with the goal has taken his position and the ball is waiting only for your kick. To kick, select the part of the goal into which you will direct the ball, and then simply drag your mouse in the direction of the kick and hope that the ball will go right into the net without hitting the goalkeeper or a running player.
