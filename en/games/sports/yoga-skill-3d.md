## About

Yoga Skill 3D is a laid-back game where you guide a girl through yoga poses to pass a diamond wall. Tap red points on her body to adjust her stance, while a top screen drawing indicates if she fits through the gap. Tap a button to confirm the pose, aiming to navigate her through unharmed. A model assists in perfecting leg, arm, and body alignment. Website Developer <a href='https://www.crazygamesx.com/'>https://www.crazygamesx.com/</a>
