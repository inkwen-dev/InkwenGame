## How to play

Swipe up from the ball to shoot: a faster swipe kicks it higher and a curved swipe bends it around the wall. Score to fill each stage, keep your streak alive for bonus points and don't run out of balls. Keyboard: arrow keys to aim, A/D to add curve, Space or Enter to shoot. Mouse: drag from the ball and release to shoot.

## About

Football Free Kick is a 3D arcade soccer game. Swipe to shoot, curl the ball over the defensive wall and beat a diving goalkeeper in the top corner. Tour 7 stadiums from the park to the moon, with wind, jumping walls and sharper keepers at every stage. Build goal streaks, pick perks between stages, finish missions, open reward chests and unlock new balls and trails.
