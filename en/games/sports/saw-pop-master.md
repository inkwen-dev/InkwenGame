## About

Saw Pop Master is an intense arcade skill game where precision and timing are everything. Pop targets by launching them through spinning saws, moving traps, and deadly obstacles without getting shredded. Each level ramps up the challenge with faster blades, tighter gaps, and clever patterns that test your reflexes. Master perfect throws, earn high scores, and unlock new visuals as you survive the chaos. Easy to pick up but brutally addictive, Saw Pop Master keeps your nerves sharp and your timing sharper.
