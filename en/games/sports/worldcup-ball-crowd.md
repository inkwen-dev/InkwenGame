## About

Lead your growing crowd of soccer balls down the pitch in this fast-paced football runner! Race through colorful gates &mdash; some multiply your team, others shrink it, so steer wisely to keep your crowd as big as possible. Dodge red-and-white traffic cones blocking your path, or youll lose balls on impact. The more balls you bring to the finish line, the bigger the victory tower you build and the higher your score! Race through 3 action-packed levels filled with gates, obstacles, and surprises.
