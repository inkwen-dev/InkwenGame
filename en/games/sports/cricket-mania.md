## How to play

The goal is simple — score as many runs as possible before losing your wickets!

Gameplay Flow:

1 🏃 Bowler Runs In: Watch the bowler’s motion to predict the delivery.
2 ⚾ Ball Delivery: Visual cues reveal the type — Straight, Slow, or Fast.
3 🕒 Timing Window: Press Left or Right when the ball reaches the sweet spot.
4 💥 Shot Outcome: Perfect timing = clean boundaries; late = you’re bowled!
