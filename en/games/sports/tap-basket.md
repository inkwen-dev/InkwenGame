## About

Tap Basket is an addictive basketball tap game! Bounce the ball by tapping the screen and aim for the hoop before the timer runs out. Every tap sends the ball upward; time your shots perfectly to score! Land clean dunks for bonus points and build a fire streak for massive scores. The hoop switches sides after every basket, keeping you on your toes. As you progress, the hoop starts moving and the timer gets shorter. How many baskets can you sink?
