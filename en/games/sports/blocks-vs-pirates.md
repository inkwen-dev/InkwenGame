## About

Blocks Vs Pirates is an exciting sports game to play for all ages. Dive into this technical game that tests your aiming ability to make a goal. The challenge is simple yet thrilling: how long can you stay in the game? Your endurance is determined by how well you can aim. Calculate the right angle and degree of force and launch the Ninja through into the basket! In Blocks Vs Pirates, you need to master the art of precision. Each level presents new obstacles and challenges, ensuring that the game remains engaging and stimulating.
