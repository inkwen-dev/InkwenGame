## About

AIM BALL is a 3D basketball shooter played with one finger. Swipe up to shoot: the longer the swipe, the more power. Tilt it to aim left or right. You get 60 seconds. Baskets are worth 2 points, 3 from behind the arc, 6 from the far spots that appear now and then. Score three in a row and you go ON FIRE for double points. When the clock hits zero you get one last shot from half court, worth 12. Unlock 29 balls in the Skins menu: lava, lightning, nebula, hologram, neon grid, plus four with a glowing aura and a spinning ring. 20 quests pay you points to spend. Beat your record and share a clip or a score card.
