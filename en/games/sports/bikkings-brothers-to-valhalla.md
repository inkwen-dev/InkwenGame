## About

Vikings gave up on longships; hovercrafts get you to Valhalla faster. You and a crew of raiders on an island world with two ways to earn your seat at the table. Race to collect eggs and haul them through the Valhalla gate before the others get there, or settle it on the pitch in hovercraft football, first to five goals. Either way, expect company: the competition will ram you, shoot at you, and throw up a shield the second you think youve got them.
