## How to play

Objective: Keep the ball airborne and pass through every single hoop. Missing a hoop costs 1 Life ❤️ (3 Lives in Endless mode, strict 1-fail in Challenges). Score consecutive clean swishes without touching rims for bonus multiplier stars and Fire Mode!

Controls:

Mobile / Tablet: Tap anywhere on the screen to hop upward.
Desktop / Laptop: Press Spacebar, Up Arrow (↑), or click the Left Mouse Button to hop.
Pause / Menu: Click the Pause ⏸️ icon or press Escape (Esc) / P.
