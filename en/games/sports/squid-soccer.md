## About

Squid Soccer is a fun great soccer game! Stop an onslaught of incoming soccer balls; this free kicks challenge puts you in control of a star Squid Goalkeeper. The objective is to save every shot on goal; You can dive in any direction at any height; stop the last goal to enter the bonus round! Try to win all medals!
