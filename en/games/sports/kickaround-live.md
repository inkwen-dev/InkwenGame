KickAround Live is a skill-based multiplayer football game. Dribble, pass, head or loft the ball to score a goal. There can be from 1 to 8 people per match!

## How to play

Move WASD or arrow keys Dash spacebar ctrl shift Pass the ball Head Tackle left mouse button Loft ball Switch Player right mouse button Tackle or Jump middle mouse button Zoom mouse wheel Toggle Chat Tab
