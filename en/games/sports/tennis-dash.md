## About

Tennis Dash is a fast and fun game where a cute cat plays tennis like a pro! The tennis ball comes from different directions, and your job is to move the cat left and right to hit it. Be quick&mdash;if you miss the ball three times, the game is over! It&rsquo;s easy to play but really hard to master. Try to beat your own high score and keep the rally going!
