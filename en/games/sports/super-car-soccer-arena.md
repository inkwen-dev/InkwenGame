## How to play

Experience full control of your supercar in the ultimate soccer arena!
Use W, A, S, D or the Arrow Keys to move your car—drive forward, reverse, and steer left or right with precision.
Press Shift to activate Nitrous Boost for explosive speed during attacks or defense.
Hit Space to apply the Brake and make sharp stops or controlled drifts.
Use the Mouse to freely look around, giving you better awareness of the arena and ball position.
Tap C to change the camera view, switching between dynamic perspectives for better gameplay.
If your car flips or gets stuck, press R to reset your car position instantly.
