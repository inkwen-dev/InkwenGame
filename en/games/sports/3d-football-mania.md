## How to play

Score more goals than your opponent before the 2 minute timer runs out. Use Arrow Keys to move, V to pass, SPACE to shoot, SHIFT to sprint/tackle and F to switch to the teammate closest to the ball. On mobile, use the Joystick and PASS, SHOOT, SPRINT and CHANGE buttons.
