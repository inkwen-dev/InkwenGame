## About

A single-player billiards game with levels, different tables, and simple controls. Your goal on each level is to pot the required balls while staying within the available number of attempts. Choose a cue, aim carefully, set the shot power, and complete challenges one by one. The game is made for quick sessions and calm skill practice. There is no multiplayer: your progress depends only on your decisions, precision, and ability to choose the right shot.
