## About

Hoop Pair is a clean, skill-based basketball puzzler where you guide two balls at once into a single hoop. Instead of flicking or shooting, you draw a path&mdash;a simple guiding line&mdash;that both balls follow as they drop toward the basket. Each level challenges your timing, accuracy, and planning as obstacles, curves, and moving platforms come into play. Draw the right curve, angle, or bounce pattern to make sure both balls reach the hoop together. One perfect line can solve the entire puzzle&hellip; or send everything off course.
