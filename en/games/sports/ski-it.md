## About

Get into Ski It, a charming cartoon-style skiing game where you control one of five quirky skiers. Glide down snowy slopes, dodging obstacles like stones, trees, and other hazards in a vibrant retro world. Your goal is to avoid these dangers and stay on your skis for as long as possible. The longer you last, the better your score. With its delightful graphics and challenging gameplay, Ski It brings the classic arcade spirit to life. How long can you navigate the slopes and achieve the best result?
