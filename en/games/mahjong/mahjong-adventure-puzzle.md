## About

Carefully choose your moves, sharpen your observation skills, and plan ahead as the puzzles become more challenging. Enjoy beautiful traditional tile designs, smooth gameplay, and relaxing challenges that are easy to learn but fun to master. Can you clear every tile and complete all the levels?
