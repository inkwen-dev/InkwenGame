## About

Mahjong Puzzle Game is a relaxing and challenging tile-matching game inspired by the classic Mahjong experience. Match identical tiles, clear the board, and carefully plan your moves to complete each level. Test your observation, memory, and strategy as you search for matching pairs and remove them from the puzzle. Each level brings a new challenge, making the game easy to learn but fun to master. Enjoy beautiful tile designs, smooth gameplay, and a relaxing puzzle experience suitable for players of all ages.
