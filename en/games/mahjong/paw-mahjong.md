## About

In Paw Mahjong you have to match the same animal icons and try to beat all the levels. Select a tile with an animal icon and click again to move it to another tile. If two of the same animals touch each other, you earn points and destroy the adjacent crates. in many levels there are special tiles that allow you to move animals either in a straight line or in a circle.
