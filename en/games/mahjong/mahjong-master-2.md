## About

The classic solitaire game of matching Chinese tiles in your pocket, thanks to Inlogic. Carefully consider every move and clear the field full of tiles piled in thoughtful pyramids. Look for similar free tiles to match and remove them. Matching is possible only those which are free from at least one side. Quick playing gives you bonus points. Can you earn all the golden stars in all game modes?
