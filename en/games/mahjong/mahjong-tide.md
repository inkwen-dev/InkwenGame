## How to play

Select two identical tiles that are free to remove them. A tile is free when no tile lies on top of it and its left or right side is open. Any two flowers match each other, and so do any two seasons. Clear every tile to win the board. Matching pairs quickly builds a chain. Hints and undo are there when you are stuck. Play with a mouse on desktop or by touch on a phone or tablet.
