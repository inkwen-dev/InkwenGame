## How to play

Mobile: Touch to select tiles, drag to rotate the cubes
PC: Click to select tiles, drag with the mouse to rotate the cubes

## About

Match, tap, and clear your way to victory in Matching Tiles 3D.
Train your brain and sharpen your memory in this fun and relaxing 3D puzzle game. Your goal is simple: find and select two tiles with the same image to clear them from the board. Keep matching until all tiles are gone and claim your win.
With beautifully designed 3D objects and satisfying gameplay, Matching Tiles 3D offers the perfect balance between challenge and relaxation. Whether you want to unwind or test your focus, this game is made for you.
