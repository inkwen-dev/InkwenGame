## How to play

Clear the Tropical Tile Board! Dive into Tile Matcher – Triple Fun, a relaxing triple tile-matching puzzle inspired by Mahjong Solitaire. Tap to find and match three identical fruit-themed tiles—like peaches, blueberries, and bananas—to clear the vibrant, stacked board and conquer increasingly challenging levels against a serene beach backdrop with a surfer and palm islands.

**Desktop Controls:**  
Mouse: Click to select and match tiles.  
Hint Button: Click the lightbulb icon for a helpful suggestion.  
Shuffle Button: Click to rearrange tiles on the board.  

**Mobile Controls:**  
Tap: Select tiles to match three identical ones and clear them.  
Hint Button: Tap the lightbulb icon for a suggestion.  
Shuffle Button: Tap to reshuffle tiles.

## About

Tile Matcher Triple Fun is a relaxing triple tile-matching puzzle game inspired by Mahjong Solitaire, where players tap to find and match three identical fruit-themed tiles (like peaches, blueberries, and bananas) to clear the vibrant, stacked board against a tropical beach backdrop. Its core concept revolves around strategic triple matching to conquer increasingly challenging levels, with unique vibrant fruit designs, a serene seaside theme featuring a surfer and palm islands, and helpful tools like hints and shuffles for endless addictive fun.
