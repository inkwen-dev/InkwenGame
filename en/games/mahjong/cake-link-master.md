## About

Cake Link Master is a fun puzzle game where you tap two matching cakes to connect them with a line. The line can turn up to two times but cannot go over other tiles. The game has 120 challenging levels, and the difficulty grows as you play. Clear the cakes, solve the puzzles, and enjoy hours of sweet, relaxing fun!
