## How to play

Tap or click two free tiles showing the same gem to remove them A tile is free when nothing covers it Cleared tiles go to the slot tray at the bottom - if it fills up the level ends Use the power-ups below the tray when you get stuck

## About

GEM MAHJONG is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle with a sparkling twist. Tap any two free tiles that show the same gem to clear them from the board. Work your way down through the stack before the timer runs out - but watch your slots: fill all of them without making a match and the level ends. FEATURES - Classic mahjong-solitaire matching, rebuilt for quick mobile sessions - 15 hand-crafted gem symbols, easy to tell apart at a glance - Four power-ups: Magnet, Sweep, Shuffle and Flip - Two extra slots you can unlock when things get tight - Portrait layout, works with both touch and mouse Simple to pick up, satisfying to master. How deep can you dig?
