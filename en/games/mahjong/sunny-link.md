## About

Sunny Link is an easy and fun game where players match tiles with summer icons. The goal is to connect two identical tiles. Tiles can be linked if you can join them with three or fewer straight lines. The game has many levels to enjoy. As you play, you&rsquo;ll hear sounds of the sea. It&rsquo;s perfect for relaxing and having fun in the sun!
