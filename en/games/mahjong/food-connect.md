## How to play

Use mouse to play this mahjong connect game

## About

You can test your pattern detection skills in this relaxed mahjong &amp; connect puzzle game with sweet food theme. You can eliminate pair of same tiles. The line connecting the two clicked tiles cant have more than 2 corners. Can you clear the board before time runs out? Enjoying matching sweet food with 30 puzzle stages in this game. Be careful, the tiles will be collapsed in some stages.
