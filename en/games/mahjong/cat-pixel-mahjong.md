## How to play

Pair the same tiles and clear all of them from the board

## About

Pixel Cat Mahjong is an enigmatic mahjong game. However, instead of the usual tiles with hieroglyphics, very funny and cute cats (or scary) are waiting for you. You should look for identical images, but also a symbolic line between them. You need to quickly search for such images because you have little time.
