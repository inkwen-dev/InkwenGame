## About

You can test your pattern detection skills in this relaxed mahjong &amp; connect puzzle game with summer theme. You can eliminate pair of same tiles. The line connecting the two clicked tiles cant have more than 2 corners. Some levels the tiles can auto slide( to left, to right, to up, to bottom). Can you clear the board before time runs out? Enjoying matching summer tiles with 30 puzzle stages in this game.
