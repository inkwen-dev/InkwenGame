## About

Connect 2 same halloween items with a path that no more than two 90 degree angles. Clear the board by removing all pairs of identical halloween items. Becareful, in some levels the item tiles can float ( to bottom, to uper, to left, to right, to center or split). There are 27 challenging levels in this game. Complete a level before time bounds to get extra bonus.
