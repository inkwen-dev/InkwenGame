## About

The wonderful world of the Magic Islands is in desparate need of magic to help restore the waning natural beauty of their ancient cities with a new wild energy. Mahjong: Tile Match features classic mahjong solitaire in a uniquely original visual format. Collecting components for magic spells adds a fun layer to the gameplay, and meeting cute magical creatures to help out gives the varied levels of the game more significant depth.
