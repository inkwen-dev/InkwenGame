## How to play

Clear the game field by clicking on pairs of identical tiles Tiles can be removed only if between them you can draw not more than 3 lines You can rotate the tiles by clicking on the boxes with rotation sign and move the tiles by pressing on the arrows

## About

Advent Mahjong is a connect Mahjong in Cristmas setting. By passing a level you get one day closer to Cristmas and the closer you get the more challenging the levels are.
