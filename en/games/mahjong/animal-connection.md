## About

This game is a mahjong match game. Mahjong match games allow you to connect and remove tiles which: directly touch. touch via a single direct line. touch via a line which has 1 or 2 right angle turns in it. the pictures near the bottom of this article show a couple legal moves. highlighted in white line segments with rounded circles at the ends.
