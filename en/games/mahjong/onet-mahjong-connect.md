## About

Onet Mahjong Connect is an easy and fun puzzle game where players match identical tiles. The goal is to connect two matching tiles. Tiles can be linked if they can be joined using three or fewer straight lines. With many levels to enjoy, the game offers a relaxing and engaging experience &mdash; perfect for taking a break and sharpening your mind!
