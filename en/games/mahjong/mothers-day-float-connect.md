## How to play

Use mouse to play this html5 mahjong connect game

## About

Connect 2 same mothers day items with a path that no more than two 90 degree angles. Clear the board by removing all pairs of identical mothers day items. Becareful, in some levels the item tiles can float ( to bottom, to uper, to left, to right, to center or split). There are 27 challenging levels in this game. Complete a level before time bounds to get extra bonus.
