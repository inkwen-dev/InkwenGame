## How to play

Tap any open tile on the board.
The selected tile will move to the tile bar at the bottom.
Match three same tiles to remove them from the bar.
Keep matching tiles until the full board is cleared.
Plan your moves carefully because the bottom bar has limited space.
Do not fill the tile bar completely, or the level may fail.
Use boosters or shuffle options when you are stuck.
Complete each level to unlock new puzzles and continue your tile matching journey.

## About

Tile Match Explorer is a blend of traditional tile match games and innovative puzzle mechanics, offering a fresh twist on the beloved match puzzle genre. Here, matching tiles becomes more than a game it's a journey. As you navigate through the myriad puzzles, Tile Explorer evolves, presenting new challenges that require you to think differently about your strategy. The thrill of making a triple tile match and clearing the board is unmatched, offering both satisfaction and the eagerness to tackle the next puzzle challenge.
