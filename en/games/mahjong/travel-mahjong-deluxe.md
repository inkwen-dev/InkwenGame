## About

Travel Mahjong Deluxe is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle where you match identical tiles and strategically clear the board. Find pairs of beautiful travel-themed tiles that are free on at least one side and remove them to complete each level. As you progress, new layouts and challenges will keep the game fresh and engaging. Use the Hint feature whenever you get stuck, or watch a reward video to gain unlimited time and continue your puzzle-solving adventure.
