## About

Jungle themed game based on the popular classic Chinese game. To play remove paired free blocks. The block is considered free when at least two adjacent neighboring sides of it are free, i.e. they have no neighbors on at least two adjacent sides. Combine matching blocks with similar blocks. Use the hints to help you clear the board. Clear the board of all tiles to complete a level
