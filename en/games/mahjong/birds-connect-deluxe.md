## About

Bird Connect Deluxe is a classic puzzle game. In this you need to eliminate all the tiles appearing on the board. You can connect identical tiles to each other to eliminate both in which each connection can have no more than 2 turns. There are 15 challenging levels in this game. You'll get time bonus if you finish a level before time.
