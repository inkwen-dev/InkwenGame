## How to play

Tap on matching groups of candies (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to blast them away! 🍫🍬💥

Earn points with every blast, but watch out: some candies hide bombs that cost you 1 life! 💣❤️

If your lives drop to zero, it’s game over — so think strategically! 🧠⚡

Use the upgrade menu to boost your tap rewards, increase your starting lives, or purchase powerful candy bombs! 🍫⬆️

When you unleash a candy bomb, it clears all candies on the screen, gives you their points, and reduces the number of candies required to win the level! 🍭🔥

If you still haven’t cleared enough candies, new ones will drop, and the challenge continues until you claim victory! ⏳🍬

Beat all 125 levels and push your limits as the difficulty rises! 🚀🎯
