## About

Mahjong Solitaire Game is a classic tile-matching puzzle game that takes inspiration from traditional Mahjong games. It features a unique gameplay where players need to connect and eliminate matching tiles on a board made up of stacked Mahjong tiles. By following specific game rules, players must clear all the tiles on the board by matching and eliminating pairs of identical tiles. Test your skills and strategize your moves to clear each level in the Mahjong Solitaire Game.
