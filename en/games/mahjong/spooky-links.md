## How to play

Tap or click on a spooky tile then find and select its matching tile If the path between them has two or fewer turns they will be cleared Clear all tiles to complete the level Use your mouse or touchpad to play

## About

Spooky Link is a relaxing Halloween-themed game where you connect matching spooky tiles. Tap a tile, then tap its match. If the path between them has two or fewer turns, the tiles are cleared. Each cleared tile creates more space to connect others. Enjoy many levels of fun with Halloween surprises!
