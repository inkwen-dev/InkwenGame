## How to play

Choose one of the three game modes:
-"Puzzles" - solving spatial puzzles with a limited number of tiles;
- "Board Clearing" - clearing the board from mahjong tiles in limited time;
- "Arcade" - is an endless mode with increasing difficulty, where you need to combine mahjong tiles on the board for as long as possible, without allowing them to be too much.

COMPUTER INPUTS:
- LEFT MOUSE BUTTON - drop the tile on the board;
- "R" - restart current level;
- "H" - show hint for current puzzle;
- "ESC" - open and close the pause menu.

MOBILE INPUTS:
- All controls are performed by TAPPING and/or MOVING YOUR FINGER across the screen.
