## About

Blossom Link is a captivating flower-themed puzzle game where you match and connect flower tiles. Simply tap a tile, then find its matching pair&mdash;if the path has two or fewer turns, you collect the tiles. As you clear tiles, you create space for more matches, helping you complete the board.
