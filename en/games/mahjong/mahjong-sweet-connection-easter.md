## How to play

Your main goal is to clear all the sweet tiles from the board by connecting three or more identical candies, eggs, or chocolates in a straight line (horizontally or vertically). Match as many as possible to create combos and clear the level faster. Complete each level by removing all tiles before running out of moves!

PC Controls:
Mouse: Click on the candy tiles to select and connect matching sweets in a line.

Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap on the candy tiles to connect three or more identical ones in a straight line.
