## How to play

Use a mouse or touchpad to select two matching free Mahjong tiles A tile is free if it has no tile on top and at least one side is open Match all pairs to clear the board and complete the level

## About

Mahjong Bird Tiles is a relaxing Mahjong solitaire game where your objective is to clear every layered board by matching pairs of identical free tiles. A tile is considered free when it has no tile covering it and at least one side is open, so choosing the right match is the key to success.
