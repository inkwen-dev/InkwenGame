## How to play

- Connect 2 identical characters, with no more than 3 straight lines allowed.
- Each level has a time limit and a limited number of image replacements.
- The game will end when the time runs out or when the image replacements are exhausted.
- Hints and image replacements can be used at your discretion on each level.
- Additional hints and image replacements can be obtained on each level.
