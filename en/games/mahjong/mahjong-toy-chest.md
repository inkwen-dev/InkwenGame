## How to play

Solve the puzzle by matching pairs of tiles together Only tiles that are uncovered and have at least one free side are allowed to be matched Click with the mouse or use touch to select tiles Tip matching the guitars will shuffle the tiles

## About

Race the clock and clean up your playroom by matching pairs of identical toys: little trains, balls, stuffed animals, books... Mahjong Toy Chest is an HTML5 mahjongg game that offers several nice surprises, such as different animations and sounds for each object, trophies to collect by performing specific actions and the ability to switch between 2D and 3D view. This uniquely playful iteration of one of our favorite games will keep you occupied for hours!
