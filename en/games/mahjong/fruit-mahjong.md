## How to play

Controls

Select a tile — click or tap it. Your pick is shown at the top of the screen.
Rotate the board — drag with the mouse or one finger. It spins a full 360°.
Zoom — mouse wheel, or pinch with two fingers.

## About

Fruit Mahjong is mahjong solitaire with a twist you can see from every angle. Juicy fruit tiles are stacked into layered arrangements in front of an ancient jungle temple — and the match you're hunting for might be sitting on the far side. Drag to orbit the board, spot the pair, clear the stack.

One six-minute clock runs across all 50 levels. Every board you clear buys you ten more seconds, so the deeper you go, the tighter it gets. Match fast to build a combo: each pair in a chain scores more than the last, and the match chime climbs in pitch as your streak grows — break the chain and the multiplier resets.

Boxed in? Burn a hint to clear a pair for you, or shuffle the remaining tiles into a fresh layout. Run out and you can watch a short video to earn another. Your best score is saved, so every run is a chance to beat it.

Rules

Clear the board by matching two identical fruit tiles.
A tile can only be taken if it has a clear side. Tiles hemmed in on both left and right, or both front and back, are locked until you clear around them.
Pick a tile, then pick its match. A wrong pair makes both tiles wobble and stay put.
Chain your matches. Land the next pair within about two seconds to keep the combo going — pair one scores 100, the next 200, then 300, and so on.
Watch the clock. You start with 6:00 for the whole run, and each cleared level adds 10 seconds. Hit 0:00 and the run ends.
Hint reveals and clears a matching pair. Shuffle rearranges the tiles still on the board. You get one of each; a short video earns another.
Lost a run? Play Again drops you back on the level you lost, not at the start.
