Shisen-sho is a mahjong puzzle game to remove all mahjong tiles according to rules. It is a simple game but addictive!

## How to play

2 pieces with same picture can be removed at a time 2 pieces can be removed only if they can be connected with a maximum of three lines Click the Hint button to show a pair of tiles which can be removed Click the Undo button to undo a last action
