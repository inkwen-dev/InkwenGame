## About

This is a classic puzzle game, inspired by popular Mahjong block removal game. In this, you need to eliminate all the tiles appearing on the board. You can connect identical tiles to each other to eliminate both in which each connection can have no more than 2 turns. There are 24 challenging levels in this game. Complete a level before time bounds to get extra bonus.
