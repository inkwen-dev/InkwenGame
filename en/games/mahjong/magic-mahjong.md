## About

Dive into the world of &ldquo;Magic Mahjong,&rdquo; where classic mechanics meet the mystique of runes. Each level brings unique layouts and increasing difficulty. The core mahjong rule: to select a pair, a rune must be open &mdash; it must have no neighbor on its right or left. Find rune pairs, clear the board, and earn points for streaks of matches.
