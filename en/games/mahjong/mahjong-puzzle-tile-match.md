## How to play

Click on horizontally or vertically adjacent tiles to make a match.
Match tiles in the same row or column, even if there is empty space between them.
Move matching tiles to the same row or column.
Clear the board to win!

## About

Mahjong Puzzle: Tile Match is a soothing yet brain-teasing tile-matching game where you clear the board by linking identical tiles in smart rows and columns.
