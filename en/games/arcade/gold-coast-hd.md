## About

Gold Coast is a great game with very beautiful graphics and music. The goal of the game is to collect the required number of points for passing to the next level of the mine, you need to collect precious stones, gold, silver and bags with bonuses, also beware of explosive barrels, you can improve your achievements by shopping in the store. This game has 20 levels with a gradually increasing degree of complexity, and the game is ideally suited for mobile devices.
