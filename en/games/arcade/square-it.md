## About

Welcome to the hyper-casual game of the square seal that flies like a bird! Build snow towers and show off your ability to get past the barriers! Watch out for enemies because bears, penguins, flying birds, and more can appear... even dragons! You can land on the snow 3 times in a row and activate the SPECIAL POWER to knock down the obstacles!
