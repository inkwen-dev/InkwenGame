## About

Sweet Screw Factory is a physics-based puzzle game where you unscrew colorful candy bolts to drop and guide candy blocks into matching collection jars! Remove screws in the right order to control how platforms fall, pivot, and slide. 120 levels across 6 candy factory worlds with locked screws, chain reactions, trampolines, portals, and boss puzzles. Easy, Medium &amp; Hard difficulty with full progress saving!
