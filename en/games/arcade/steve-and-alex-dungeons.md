## About

Steve and Alex are trapped in a dungeon in the dark, and you must help them escape. You need to rescue both of them one by one. The dungeon is very dangerous and filled with obstacles. Be careful of jumping over obstacles and watch out for spinning wheels. Collect the diamonds you need and escape from the dungeon. You must reach the door at the end of the dungeon.
