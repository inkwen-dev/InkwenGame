## About

Game Overview Players can choose to play against the computer (P1 vs CPU) or against another player (P1 vs P2). The game revolves around capturing each others pieces. However, not all pieces can be captured&mdash;some must not be taken. Additionally, certain pieces have special roles or ranks. The victory conditions are as follows: Force your opponent to capture all of your forbidden-to-capture pieces. Capture a set number of the opponent&rsquo;s valid targets. Successfully guide a specific number of your own pieces to the designated escape points.
