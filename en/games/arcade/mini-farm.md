## About

Build your farm as your heart desires. Many cute animals are waiting for you to grow them, which will bring you more interesting farming. Players play as farmers, start growing various crops, manage their own farms, restore the real planting process. You can sow crops, water and harvest them. You can also find ripe fruit trees to collect fruits!
