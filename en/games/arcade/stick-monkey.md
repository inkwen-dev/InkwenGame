## About

Smart & strong monkey takes part in a game called Stick Monkey to reach his village. The rule of the game is to get high score with crossing the poles. Poles are separated from each other, you have to connect them with the bamboo sticl. Hold your finger to stretch out the stick. You will lose the game whether the stick is a little shorter or longer than the length between the poles. Play as the chief of the monkey to help him get high score. Good luck and enjoy the game of Stick Monkey
