Use the mouse or Touch to move and play

## About

In Catch From the Air, your mission is to collect and survive as long as possible during the matches. Catch's gameplay system is based on a fun time for the player, containing different music in 8-bits for each different skin that is added in future updates. Every person that the player loses, letting it pass down, will lose lives, in which, when it reaches 0, it is restarted and the player can have fun again for a longer time in a Global Ranking. Compete for rankings, and show your skills to everyone.
