## About

Stickboy Hook is a fast, addictive one-tap swing game where you launch a brave stickman across colorful obstacle courses using a magical rope hook. Tap and hold to throw your grapple, release to fly through the air, then grab the next anchor point to keep your momentum going. Time your swings perfectly to clear gaps, dodge spikes, slide down ramps and rocket through finish lines. Modes: - Classic: 200+ handcrafted levels of increasing difficulty - Hook Mode: pure swinging challenge against the clock - Leaderboard: compete for the best level times
