Blockivo is a simple block puzzle game. Place colorful shapes on an 8×8 board, complete full rows and columns, and try to score as many points as possible.…

## How to play

Drag one of the available shapes onto an empty space on the board. A full row or column disappears and gives you points. After you place all three shapes, a new set appears.
If the board gets too crowded, use Line Breaker to remove two selected rows or columns. Undo lets you take back your last move. The game ends when none of the available shapes can be placed. Try to beat your best score and move higher in the leaderboard.
