## About

Duo Apple Monsters is an exciting adventure game where you collect apples and conquer obstacles. The main characters are Red Apple and Green Apple. They live in Appleton, a magical world. One day, the evil sorcerer Morula appears, threatening to plunge Appleton into darkness. Red and Green Apple join forces to save their world. Through 13 levels, they gather apples and overcome challenges, ultimately defeating Morula and restoring peace to Appleton.
