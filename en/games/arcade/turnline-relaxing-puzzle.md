## How to play

Tap a tile to rotate it.

Your goal is to connect the lines and complete the entire network.

Tap a tile to rotate it.
Check how its lines connect to nearby tiles.
Keep rotating the pieces until the paths match.
Connect every required section to complete the level.

Correctly connected paths are highlighted as you progress.

If you get stuck, you can use a hint.

Use the mouse on desktop or simply tap the tiles on a phone or tablet.

## About

Turnline: Relaxing Puzzle is a calm minimalist puzzle about connecting lines.

Tap the tiles to rotate them and find the correct position for every part of the network. Once all required paths are connected, the puzzle is complete.

The first levels are simple and introduce the rules naturally. Later puzzles become larger and more challenging, with branches, multiple paths and increasingly intricate layouts.

There is no timer, so you can think and solve each puzzle at your own pace.

• Thousands of levels
• Gradually increasing difficulty
• Clean and relaxing visuals
• Hints for difficult puzzles
• Progress saving
• Great for both quick and longer sessions
