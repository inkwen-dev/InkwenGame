## About

City Takeover is an arcade game that is both entertaining and addictive. It's a three-dimensional game. In this game, you can take over as many cities as you like. It's time to find out if you're a capable army commander! You can take the city when the number of your soldiers equals or exceeds the number of people in the city to be captured. You must also keep an eye out for the enemy creeping into your city! The team with the most brains wins! Will you be victorious in the end? Come give it a shot! Best of luck!
