## How to play

Movement:

W, A, S, D — move forward, left, backward, right.

The space bar is a jump.

Shift — running / acceleration.

Interaction and attack:

The left mouse button is the main fire (shooting).

Hold down the right mouse button to rotate the camera.

Other: pop-up buttons

## About

A mad cow has escaped from the farm! Feel the taste of freedom! Destroy the city, fly a helicopter and fight back against the cops in this absurd action game! Forget about green pastures and peaceful mooing! Dasha is not an ordinary Burenka. Tired of milking and hay, she runs away from the farm to assert her rights and live freely as a human being. And what do free people do? That's right — they walk around the city, go to cafes, drive cars and.. sometimes they destroy everything in their path.

In this crazy game you will find:

Hardcore gameplay: Run through the streets, enter buildings and control a variety of vehicles, from cars to a combat helicopter!

Total Destruction: You are a walking natural disaster! Shoot, smash and explode everything you see. Buildings, lampposts, police cars — nothing can resist your fury.

Resistance to the system: Local law enforcement officers are not ready for such a threat! Give a decisive rebuff to the police, who will try to return you to the stall. Your goal is not just to survive, but to wreak havoc and prove that cows can be free too!
