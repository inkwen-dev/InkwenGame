## About

Pipe Connect is a logical game where your job is to connect the pipes. On the board will be just circles, and you need to connect two same circles to get the pipe. There will be different types of pipes, and you need to connect them all to pass the level. But the pipes cant cross each other or hit the pipes. You need to find the path for each pipe.
