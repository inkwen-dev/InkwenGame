## How to play

Tap Mouse Left Button

## About

Steps to Play: Tap to Ascend: To make the balloon rise, tap the left mouse button. Each tap will give your balloon a gentle boost upward. Descending: The balloon will automatically start descending when youre not tapping the left mouse button. Use this to control your descent carefully. Avoid Obstacles: In the game, you will encounter mountain-like obstacles. Maneuver your balloon carefully to avoid crashing into them, or its game over. Collect Red Flags: On each mountain, there will be a red flag, collecting which will earn you points. Each flag will give you 10 points. All the best! Enjoy the thrill of flying.
