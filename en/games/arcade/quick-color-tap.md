## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

You see a screen with 16 colored squares (4 colors of red, blue, green and yellow) and by tapping once on the screen, the game will start. As soon as the game starts, the background color will blink very quickly with one of those 4 colors and you have to choose a square with the same color to remove it. If you choose a square with a different color, you will lose. This will continue until every square is removed and you will win when you have successfully removed all squares. You have to do your best to do this as fast as possible so that your final record time is lower!
