## About

Ziply is a highly addictive, minimalist logic puzzle game. Your goal is simple: connect numbers in sequential order (1, 2, 3...) to draw colorful pipes and fill every single empty tile on the board. With 1,500 handcrafted levels spread across three grid sizes (Classic 5x5, Advanced 6x6, and Master 8x8), Ziply will push your brain to the limit. Want an extra challenge? Try the fast-paced Time Rush mode to see how many puzzles you can solve before the clock runs out. Earn stars, collect daily login rewards, and unlock beautiful custom themes like Wood, Glass, and Paper!
