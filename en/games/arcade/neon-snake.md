## How to play

Guide your glowing snake across a dark grid, eating orbs to grow and rack up points. Chain quick eats to build combo multipliers up to ×8 — but one wrong move and it's over. Grab speed boosts for high-risk high-reward runs, slow orbs for breathing room, and rare bonus stars for massive point spikes. Compete on global leaderboards across all-time and weekly rankings.
