## About

Quick Dice is fun board game to play. In front of your screen, a playing field will appear with various visible colored dots. Your opponents in this game are few. The moves in the game are done in order of priority. First you need to roll special dice and certain numbers will fall on them. Number mean how many moves you will need to make. Then, you will pass along the game map and light a specific point. Your goal is to fill most of the playing field with dots of your color.So heave fun!
