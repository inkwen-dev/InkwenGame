## About

Retro Jack is a pixel-style adventure game where players guide a lively pumpkin character named Jack through eerie forests and spooky landscapes. The main objective is to collect sparkling jewels scattered across each level while skillfully avoiding rolling stones that appear suddenly and threaten to crush Jack. With increasing levels of difficulty, intuitive controls, and nostalgic pixel graphics, Retro Jack combines classic arcade gameplay with a Halloween twist, offering an engaging experience as players help Jack complete his thrilling jewel-collecting quest.
