## How to play

Tap, click or press SPACE to leap to the other wall. Tap again in the air to flip. Leap through enemies to slash them and chain combos. Collect coins to unlock 10 ninjas with their own powers. Beat each boss by leaping into it when it is open. Keep climbing: the mist below rises faster the higher you go. Pause with the pause button (Esc on desktop).
