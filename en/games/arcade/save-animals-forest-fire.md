## About

Save animals from forest fires. Find the right way to escape from the burning forest. Fragile bridges will carry a cat, a hare, a turtle and many others over the dangerous waters of rivers, swamps, an ocean with sharks and an abyss. Rescue animals in different regions from Africa to the North Pole. All rescued animals can be settled on your farm. Go through the levels and there will be more and more pets.
