## About

Color Switch Dash is a fast-paced hyper-casual arcade game that challenges your reflexes and timing. Control a constantly moving shape and switch its color to match incoming obstacles. Only the correct color lets you pass, and one mistake ends the run. The longer you survive, the faster and more intense the game becomes. With smooth controls, bright neon visuals, and endless gameplay, Color Switch Dash is easy to learn but hard to master. Compete against your own best score, react quickly, and test your focus in this addictive color-matching challenge designed for quick and satisfying play sessions.
