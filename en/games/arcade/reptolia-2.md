## About

Reptolia 2 is a 2D platformer where you have to collect all of the giant insects(bugs) while avoiding the enemies, flying creatures, saws, spikes and fire and reach the cave in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed. This is the second installment of the original "Reptolia" series.
