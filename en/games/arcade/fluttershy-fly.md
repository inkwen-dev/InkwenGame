## About

Fluttershy Fly is a side-scroller game where the player controls the Fluttershy. Fly the Fluttershy as far as you can without hitting any of the obstacles and enemies. You have 3 health. Collect coins and health to keep you going. Press "SPACEBAR" or MOUSE LEFT CLICK to fly. Press "F12" to switch to full screen mode.
