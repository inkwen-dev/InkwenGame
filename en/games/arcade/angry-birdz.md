## About

Angry Birdz is a captivating physics-based mobile game that challenges players to launch a variety of quirky, feathered creatures at intricate structures built by mischievous pigs. Utilize unique bird abilities, each with its own strategic advantage, to topple the pigs defenses and retrieve stolen eggs. With engaging levels, and clever puzzles, Angry Birdz offers a fresh take on the classic slingshot gameplay, providing hours of addictive fun for players of all ages.
