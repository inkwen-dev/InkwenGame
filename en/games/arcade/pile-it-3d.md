## About

Here is a ball-collecting arcade game, which is made from 3D cartoon models in pipe mazes. Your mission is to release the balls to fill the marked positions up at each level. Normally, you could just hold the green button to release balls at an even speed and win the game easily. Keep calm and have a good time on all levels!
