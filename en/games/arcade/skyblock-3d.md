## About

Are you ready to survive on a small island floating high in the sky? Start your adventure with limited resources, collect blocks, create new areas, and gradually expand your island. Use your resources wisely, discover different materials, and build your own world. As you progress, unlock new opportunities, improve your island, and transform it into a massive floating home. An exciting adventure combining survival, exploration, and building awaits you!
