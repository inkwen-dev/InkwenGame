## About

Every launch is a compact physics challenge where timing, aim and momentum matter. Guide your square through shifting openings while gates approach from the sides, above, below and the corners. Survive as long as possible in Endless mode as the pressure steadily rises, or take on carefully designed levels with fixed, memorable challenges and new gate types introduced as you progress. Collect coins during runs, unlock colorful skins and equip playful cosmetic accessories. Random color themes and animated backgrounds keep every session fresh, while precise mouse and touch controls make the game easy to learn and rewarding to master.
