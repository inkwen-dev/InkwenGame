## About

Downhill Rush game is an endless arcade game. You have to go down the endless mountain and complete the levels. You have to watch out for obstacles in Downhill Rush game. These obstacles put you in a deadlock and make you lose the game. You should collect the coins on the map as much as possible. With these coins, you can open new levels or change your character.
