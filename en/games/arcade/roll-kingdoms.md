## How to play

Press or tap ROLL to throw two dice and move around the board. Land on coin, crown, mystery, upgrade, and mini-game tiles to earn rewards. Spend coins to upgrade each building from level 0 to level 4. On desktop, use the mouse and follow the keyboard instructions displayed during mini-games. On mobile, use touch controls and follow the on-screen prompts. Complete every building and objective in Sunny Keep, Pirate Cove, and Ember Kingdom, then finish the Royal Festival to complete the campaign.

## About

Roll Kingdoms is a colorful 3D dice-and-kingdom adventure. Roll the dice, explore three floating kingdoms, collect coins and crowns, upgrade 18 buildings from ruins to royal landmarks, unlock characters and dice, complete missions, and master six interactive mini-games. Build Sunny Keep, Pirate Cove, and Ember Kingdom, then complete the Royal Festival.
