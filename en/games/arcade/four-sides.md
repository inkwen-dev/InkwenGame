## About

This is a game with four balls in the middle of the game who is with different colors. They can rotate when you tap on the screen. From the top of the screen, left, right and from bottom will came smaller balls with same colors as the balls in the middle. So you need to rotate the four balls to match with smaller balls with same color. Be prepare to react fast or you will loose the game at the beginning.
