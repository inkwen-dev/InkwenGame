## How to play

Pick an ink (wood, stone or jelly), then draw one line. Release it and it falls and obeys physics. Protect Mochi until the timer ends, get Mochi home, or deliver the strawberry to win. Use less ink to earn more stars. Stuck? A hint shows the solution. Controls: touch or mouse to draw. Keys: 1-3 pick an ink, R restarts, P pauses, H shows a hint, Esc closes dialogs.

## About

A cute physics drawing puzzle where your line becomes real. Mochi is in trouble, and you can draw the way out: sketch a single line with your finger or mouse, and when you let go it turns into a real object. Light wood floats and burns, heavy stone sinks and survives lava, and bouncy jelly springs anything back up. Shield Mochi from falling rocks and cannonballs, bridge lava and rising water, and roll the strawberry to a hungry friend. 30 handcrafted puzzles across three worlds (Sunny Meadow, Ember Quarry and Lagoon Isles), and using less ink earns more stars.
