## About

This is a catching game. Test your speed and reflex. Catch the red boat before he reaches the end line. The red boats will be coming in different sizes and with different speeds. Sometimes the boats will be smaller and faster will fall, and sometimes will be bigger and slower. The slower boats you can catch easily. Anyway, you can make three mistakes before a game ends.
