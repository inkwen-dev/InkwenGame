Slide to rotate the maze

## About

Most people have played maze games when they are young, Multi Maze 3D is actually a puzzle game with 3D arcade game rules. Your mission is to pour over the marked number of balls into the vessel between two boards under the maze. As long as you rotate the maze, the balls will move with gravity. Keep calm and use bigger numbers!
