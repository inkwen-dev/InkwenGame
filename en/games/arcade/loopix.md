## How to play

Draw a closed loop around creatures with your comet trail to capture everything inside. Grow bigger, beat the Guardian of each world and jump to the next world through the black hole. Never touch another comet's trail.

PC:
Mouse - steer your comet
Hold left click or Space - boost (you lose a little size)
Arrow keys / A-D - steer with the keyboard

Mobile:
Drag your finger - steer
Double tap and hold - boost

## About

Loop around cute creatures with your glowing comet trail to capture them all!

Loopix is a fast and colorful .io arcade game. Steer your comet, draw a loop around groups of creatures and close it to catch everything inside. The bigger the loop, the bigger the combo! Grow from a tiny spark into a giant supernova.

Watch out: other comets are hunting too. Touch their trail and you're out, but trap them inside your loop and their points are yours!

Features:
- Easy to learn, hard to master: just draw loops!
- Boost to escape or to close a loop at the last second
- Defeat a different Guardian boss in every world and travel to the next one through a black hole
- Hatch cute companions that follow you and give bonuses
- Unlock skins, hats and permanent upgrades
- Daily gifts, missions, lucky wheel and a monthly Cosmic Pass
- Plays great on PC, tablet and phone
