## How to play

Press anywhere, pull back and let go: your sponge flies the opposite way (like a slingshot). The longer you pull, the farther you fly. While you still have water, you can pull once more in mid-air to jump again. Land on blue OASIS islands to refill water automatically. Use trampolines, cannons and water slides to cross the big gaps, smash walls and fly through rings for bonus drops. If you fall or run out of water, spend your drops on upgrades and try again. Reach the GOAL to unlock the next world. Keyboard: hold Space to charge, arrow keys to turn, release to fly.

## About

Soak & Squeeze is a bouncy 3D physics slingshot game starring a cute, squishy sponge. Soak up water, then squeeze it out to blast yourself across floating islands over a bottomless gap! Every world is completely different: a toy kitchen, a bubble bath with giant soap bars, a toy box with ABC blocks and conveyor belts, a windy garden, and a rainbow sky of clouds. Ride water slides, get fired from cannons, bounce on trampolines, smash foam walls and fly through boost rings. Fall or run dry and you are back at the start, but every bit of progress earns drops: spend them to upgrade your sponge with a bigger tank, more power and more income, plus wings, a rain cloud and rescue bubbles. Each world gives you a brand-new sponge to grow again. Can you reach the far-away goal?
