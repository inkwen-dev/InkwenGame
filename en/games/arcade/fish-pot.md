## About

Fish Pot is a delightful and addictive game where your goal is to catch and collect colorful fish into the perfect pot! Tap, aim, and guide the fish through tricky paths, avoiding obstacles and making sure each fish lands safely. With vibrant underwater environments, relaxing music, and simple one-touch controls, Fish Pot offers a calming yet challenging experience for players of all ages. Unlock new types of fish, upgrade your pots, and discover beautiful aquatic worlds as you progress. Ready to test your skills and fill your pot with the ocean&rsquo;s finest?
