## About

Wolfoo Escape is a kid-friendly trap-dodging platformer. Join Wolfoo, Piggy, and Pando as they tiptoe through witch Kats mysterious rooms, each one packed with hidden traps. Move carefully, time your jumps, grab the stars, and dash to the glowing door to clear all 12 levels. Simple controls make it easy for little players to pick up, while trickier trap layouts keep older kids challenged. Choose your favorite character, earn three stars on every stage, and see if you can help everyone escape safely. Bright cartoon visuals, gentle challenge, and quick levels make it perfect for short play sessions on desktop and mobile
