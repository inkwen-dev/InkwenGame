## How to play

Your main goal is to blow up the city with a nuclear bomb. How exactly you will do this is up to you.

On a PC, use WASD for movement, LMB for shooting, F to get into a vehicle.

It's even easier on touch devices: all the necessary buttons are displayed on the screen, so you definitely won't miss anything!

## About

In the game world, you can do everything: ride cars, blow up buildings, fight enemies and complete exciting missions. Use a variety of weapons, build objects with a tulgan and experiment with destruction.

The main goal of the game is to destroy the city using a nuclear bomb. How will you achieve this? It's up to you to decide. Move towards your goal through story missions or immerse yourself in a limitless sandbox of possibilities.
