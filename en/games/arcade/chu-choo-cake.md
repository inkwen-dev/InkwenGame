## About

If you love cake, then you will want to help Chef Piggo to save his creations from the hungry mice! These mice are greedy, and they will stop at nothing to try and stop the chef. This game combines elements of both Pacman and Donkey Kong and has retro graphics too. Move the chef around the level and try to drop the cakes to the bottom by standing on them. You must save each cake before you can complete the level - make sure you avoid the greedy mice too though!
