![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/evade/cover.jpg)

A high-speed chase game where you deliver packages across a city while the police hunt you down. Lose them, make the drop, and move on to the next job.

## How to play

Use the arrow keys or WASD to drive, Space to handbrake, and Shift to boost. Drive to each drop point, but shake the cops first — you can't deliver while they're on your tail.
