## About

Our game talks about a jungle bee, who had difficulty reaching the hive, but was unable to do that then she called for the help. Are you sure that you are able to deliver it to the hive ? What you have to do is get away from all obstacles, which are represented by branches, trees, leaves, spiders or other things. Make sure that it is not healthy enough, so tap the screen to fly the bee in the right direction and follow the appropriate path to reach the target.
