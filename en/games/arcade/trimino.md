Trimino is a triangular-domino tabletop game for one player versus bots.

## How to play

1. Goal: reach 400 match points before your opponents.
2. Place a tile so equal numbers meet on shared edges.
3. No move: draw from the boneyard (−5) or pass (−10).
4. Score the sum of the tile’s numbers plus bonuses for opening, bridges, hexes, and emptying your hand.
5. A round ends when someone plays their last tile or the game is blocked.
6. Use the menu for the tutorial, skin shop, quests, and the coin wallet.

## About

Trimino is a triangular-domino tabletop game for one player versus bots.

Place tiles so matching numbers meet on shared edges. Close hexes, build bridges, and stack bonuses. The match goal is to reach 400 points first.

Includes a tutorial, daily rewards, quests, cosmetic skins, and light monetization: optional ads between rounds and coin packs.
