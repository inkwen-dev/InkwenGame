## About

Route Digger 2 is interesting puzzle game. On the screen you will see a ball of a certain color on the surface of the earth. Underground is a special well in different colors. You will have to make the ball fall into the well with same color. To do this, use your mouse to dig a tunnel underground. A ball rolling along it will fall into the well and you will receive a certain amount of points for this. Have fun!
