## About

Cups Tower Builder is a puzzle game where you need to create a tower from cups. The cups will move horizontally on the screen and when you tap on the screen the cup will fall to the ground. Do that will all cups and try to fall them one above the other. When some cup falls from the building the game will be over. Play longer as you can and create the longest cup building.
