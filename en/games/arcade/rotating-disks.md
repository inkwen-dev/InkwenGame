## About

Rotating Disks is a puzzle controlled game where the goal of the game is to control two rotating disks. The yellow disks will move both at same time. From the center of the game will coming balls with purple and yellow color. You need to avoid purple balls and to catch yellow balls. Catch more yellow balls as you can during playing this game. If you catch purple ball the game will over. It is not very hard to play but it is interesting and addictive.
