## About

As a collecting drawing puzzle arcade game, Castle Raid 3D requires you to group a team to invade the castle at each level. The enemies will decrease your quantity of army on the track. You need to find the correct area and overpass them to group more members of your team by means of drawing a line. Good luck and have a good time!
