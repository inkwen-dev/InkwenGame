## About

In the adventure of Noob and Pro, there are very dangerous desert monsters. You must kill all the monsters and animals. Although Noob is clumsy and unintelligent, only he can activate the portal. Noob needs to collect iron and flint to activate the portal. Of course, Pro needs to help Noob. Pro must kill all the monsters and animals and collect all the coins. Together, Noob and Pro must reach the portal.
