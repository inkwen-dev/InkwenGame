## About

Cut everything into equal slices. You can cut it into two slices, three slices, or four slices. The closer the area of the tiles, the more stars youll get. The real challenge is completing perfectly equal slices! Try to see how many slices you can cut perfectly. Cut it All is a division arcade game with 2D cartoon animal and fruit models. Some of them are axisymmetric figures, others are not. Only when you cut them with 53% and 47% do you complete the current level. Glad you will always cut them perfectly!
