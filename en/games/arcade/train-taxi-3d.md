## About

Your main goal in this game will be to complete each level, collecting all the passengers along the way and get to the final station. The more people you collect, the longer your train will become. Many interesting levels await you, as you progress through which you will encounter various obstacles. Skillfully overcome all the traps and get to the finish line. For completing levels, you will receive gold coins that you can spend on unlocking new train models. To earn the required amount of money faster, go through the bonus levels in which you need to collect as many coins as possible. Unlock all 9 unique taxi trains and hit the road. And smooth and simple controls will allow you to get the most out of the game. Good luck!
