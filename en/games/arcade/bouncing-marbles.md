## About

Bouncing Marbles is an exciting endless jumper game in which you touch and hold screen to control the colorful Marble jumping around, try not to run out of the path and touch the obstacles, collect coins to unlock new ball. Bounce on as many platforms to make high-scores. Challenge your friends and have fun.
