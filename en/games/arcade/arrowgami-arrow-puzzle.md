## About

ArrowGami is a puzzle game where your goal is to clear the board by untangling colorful winding arrows. Tap an arrow to make it slide off the screen in the direction it points, ensuring its path is clear of obstacles. Carefully plan your sequence of moves to avoid collisions and complete the stage. Finishing a level reveals a completed pixel art silhouette, such as a hot-air balloon. You can earn coins to unlock different visual themes or use hints if you get stuck on a difficult pattern.
