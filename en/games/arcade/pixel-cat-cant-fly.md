## About

Pixel Cat Can’t Fly is a game of reflexes in which you have to keep your little pixel cat in the air as long as possible. When you touch the screen, your cat will jump. You need to make it go through holes in walls. If you’re not accurate enough and your cat hits a surface, you lose.
