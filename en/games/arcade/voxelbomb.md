## How to play

Desktop: WASD to move, mouse to look, left click to place dynamite or use a tool, E to detonate, right click to retrieve a charge, number keys or mouse wheel to switch tools, Space to jump, Shift to sprint, C to switch camera, R to rebuild. Mobile: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, tap a wall to place dynamite, and tap BOOM to detonate. Switch to WALK for joystick movement.

## About

Stick dynamite on voxel buildings, trigger spectacular chain reactions, and bring every structure below the red line. Earn stars by using fewer charges, unlock and upgrade ten demolition tools, and keep smashing in sandbox mode after completing each level.
