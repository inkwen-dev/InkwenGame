## About

This game puts a crazy twist on the classic Whack-a-Mole experience by letting you smack zombie animals. Use your hammer to hit different types of zombie cows across the head and earn points for it. Be careful not to hit the dog that deducts your points. There are 7 levels that get gradually more difficult, so prepare to test your reflexes. Don't forget to share this game with your friends and compare your high scores!
