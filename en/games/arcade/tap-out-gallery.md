## About

Dive into a clever arrow-block puzzle where every tile points the way. Click blocks to remove them, but plan ahead: each removal changes the route through the stack, opening new options or trapping you in dead ends. Web Dev <a href='https://www.kizi10.org/'>https://www.kizi10.org/</a> Clear the board by creating smooth directional chains, using green starters and orange movers to guide your progress.
