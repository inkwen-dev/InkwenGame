## About

Cute Pony Hidden Stars is a delightful hidden object adventure that puts your observation skills to the test! Explore enchanting scenes filled with adorable ponies and search carefully for the hidden stars cleverly scattered throughout each picture. Every level challenges you to find all the stars before moving on to the next magical adventure. Stay focused, look closely, and enjoy the colorful artwork as you uncover every hidden star. Perfect for players of all ages, this relaxing and fun game offers a charming way to sharpen your attention to detail while exploring a world full of cute ponies!
