## About

Frozen Escape is a thrilling 2D platformer set in a dangerous arctic world. You play as a brave penguin trying to escape the freezing depths of a mysterious ice cave. Navigate through 100 challenging levels filled with deadly ice spikes, freezing water, and powerful gusts of wind. Game Features: 100 Unique Levels: Progress through increasingly difficult frozen stages. Diverse Enemies: Face off against furry Yetis, heavy Ice Golems, and fast-flying Frost Bats. Character Shop: Collect golden gems to unlock cool skins like the Ice Penguin and Royal Penguin.
