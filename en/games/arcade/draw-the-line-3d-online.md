## About

For the ball to reach the destination, you need to have the proper level of ingenuity and quick reaction, without which you will have a hard time. Watch the ball carefully, do not let it disappear from sight, otherwise you will not be able to direct it, and most likely it will have a tragic accident. You have the opportunity to send your ball into space and admire the beauty of bright stars, but remember that the birds are waiting for their liberator and are ready to accompany you on the way to the finish line. Prove your skills and save the birds from their cages. Good luck!
