## How to play

A or D or Left or Right arrow to move the character.
W or Up arrow to jump.
Hold F to throw the sword to hang and swing.
Press G to attack or hold G to throw the sword forward.

When it is hanging:
Use the A or D keys, or the Left or Right arrow keys to swing.
W or Up arrow to go up.
Use S or the Down arrow to go down.

## About

The Spartan fruits must overcome obstacles and find chests containing coins to unlock rewards and advance to the next stages. They must also collect gems to unlock new types of fruit.
