## About

Color Dash is a fast-paced endless runner that tests your reflexes and color matching skill! Pilot a speeding rocket and match its color to smash through colored gates. Dodge the wrong colors and react to frantic color changes to keep your run alive. Grab powerful shield and nitro power-ups to blast further and beat your personal best score in this addictive arcade adventure. How high can you score?
