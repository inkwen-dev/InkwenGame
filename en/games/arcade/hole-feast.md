## How to play

Drag anywhere on the screen (or use WASD / arrow keys) to move your hole. Objects smaller than the hole fall in and make it grow. Swallow smaller rival holes and avoid bigger ones. Finish first in the 90-second battle or clear 100% of the map to win coins, then upgrade your start size, speed and time.

## About

Hole Feast is a bite-sized .io-style munch-fest! Drag to glide your glowing hole across colorful low-poly picnics, cities and farms. Snacks, cars, cows and whole buildings tumble in as you grow: SIZE UP! Chain gulps into combos, outgrow three hungry rival holes in 90-second battles or clear 100% of the map. Earn coins, upgrade size, speed and time, and unlock 8 dazzling rim skins.
