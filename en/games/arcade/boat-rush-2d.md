## About

Are you ready to take your boat and dive into adventure? be careful, although the boat is strong and fast, there are cases where it is weak and it can sink at any time. To avoid sinking, you must never, ever hit the obstacles until the finish line. obstacles can explode you and sink your boat. Collect the gold with the boat, buy yourself a more beautiful boat, get the most expensive one.
