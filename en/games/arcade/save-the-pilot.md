Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

This is an action game in which you must shoot down the enemy planes and eliminate them to get points. You can earn points by collecting items on the page. You must also escape from rockets, otherwise you’ll be loser. Gradually, the speed of the game is increased and make you confused. Airplanes are also upgraded. This game is very interesting.
