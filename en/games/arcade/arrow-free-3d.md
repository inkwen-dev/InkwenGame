## How to play

Rotate the Cube: Spin the 3D cube to inspect all six sides.
Plan Your Moves: Analyze the arrow paths and anticipate chain reactions.
Tap in Sequence: Tap the arrows in the correct order to remove them without getting stuck.
Clear the Stage: Remove every single arrow to complete the challenge!
