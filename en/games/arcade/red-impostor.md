## About

Imposter solo kill is a free new 3D imposter killing game. There are many imposters in different color in a spaceship. Your mission is simple play with all imposter in a spaceship. Imposter in red color is a hunter in spaceship. Their target is to kill all the hunter spaceship. Now there are many tricks how red imposter kill their crewmates in spaceship. If red imposter become fails in killing their crewmate game will ended. So, you need to make best strategy to kill cremates.
