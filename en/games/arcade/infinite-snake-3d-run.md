## About

Blocky Snake is an exciting endless one-touch game in which you touch the screen to move the snake right or left. Try to avoid the obstacles standing in your way, break the blocks to get score, collects the bodies to increase the length of the snake, collect coins to unlocks new body types and new environment types.
