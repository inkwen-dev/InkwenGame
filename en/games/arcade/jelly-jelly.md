## About

Collect all the plankton generated by the funny Jellyfish, be careful don't touch them or you'll lose a life! The main goal of the game is collect as much plankton as possible and sign your personal record in the scoreboard. Occasionally you will see a red balloons, touch them and earn a bonus of +100 points. Enjoy with this great nice game. Key Features: - Very easy for all Kids. - 3D Graphics. - 3D animations. - Sound Effects fantastic. - Fun music. - Colorful and fun.
