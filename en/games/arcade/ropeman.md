## About

Ropeman is an arcade-style old-school game in which you try to climb the ropes which are coming from the sky with a man. The game has two game modes as slide and tap. You can have a consistent climbing option with a slide option and you can have a health bar with a tap gaming option. Pick one and challenge a friend and let the fun begin!
