## About

Ball Throw Goalie is a fast, skill-based arcade game where precision and timing decide the score. Aim, adjust your power, and throw the ball past an active goalie who reacts to every move. Each level increases the challenge with smarter goalkeepers, moving targets, and tighter angles. Web Dev <a href='https://www.crazygamesx.com/'>https://www.crazygamesx.com/</a> Master curved throws, fake shots, and perfect timing to outplay the goalie, rack up points, and prove you have the accuracy of a true goal scorer.
