## About

Flappy Ring - jump or lose. One click - one jump. Set records! Compete with your friends! You are a brave goose trying to get home, flying through a scary forest. Dont run into obstacles. Collect coins and compete with other players! In the game you will find a generated infinite level. The ability to continue the game after death. Become the best goose in the high score table and tell your friends!
