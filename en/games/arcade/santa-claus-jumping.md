## About

Santa Claus Jumping is a Christmas winter game in which you have to jump between two objects. If Santa Claus jumps too high, he will touch the sharp ice and he will burst. Try to stand up on the platform to earn points. Try not to touch the sharp ice. Enjoy and have fun!
