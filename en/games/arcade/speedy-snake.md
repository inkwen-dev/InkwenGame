## About

Can you be the fastest growing snake in an arena full of colorful snakes? Fast Snake, this fun action packed game, is designed for snake game enthusiasts. In the arena we have round balls for you in different sections. You know the rules, the big snake swallows the little snake. Eat, eat, eat more to become a great snake. Be so big that your opponents are afraid to approach you. You have to be careful, you're not the only snake in the arena! When you touch other snakes, you lose the game. If the other snakes touch you, collect the balls that come out when they disappear and grow your snake. Have fun!
