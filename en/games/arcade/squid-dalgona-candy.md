## About

Are you ready to play a popular and entertaining puzzle game? You must use a needle to remove the desired form from the candy molds in the Squid Dalgona Candy game. You should gently remove the candy mold since it is hard and brittle. It's game over if you shatter the candy! To extract the candy without breaking it, you must click the button on a regular basis. The sweets will shatter if you click a lot and put the "Crack Meter" to the red level. The game is also finished if you don't remove the candy inside the time limit.
