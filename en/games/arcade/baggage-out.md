Baggage Out! is a fun interactive game where players organize and load baggage onto a bus by completing simple tasks and challenges.

## How to play

Become a Bus Hero! Help get all the baggage ready before the bus leaves. Drag and place the baggage into the correct spaces and complete the task.

Controls:

Mouse / Touch: Drag the baggage and place it in the correct position.
Tap: Select and interact with objects.
Goal: Arrange all the baggage correctly to complete the level.
