## About

Flappy Minecraft is a fast-paced arcade game that combines classic Flappy Bird gameplay with a familiar Minecraft-inspired visual style. Players control a flying character and must stay airborne while passing through obstacles with precise timing and quick reflexes. Unlike traditional Flappy-style games, the green block obstacles appear suddenly and randomly, forcing players to react instantly instead of memorizing patterns. This unexpected obstacle system makes every run unique and significantly more challenging. Simple controls, increasing difficulty, and unpredictable block placement create a highly addictive experience focused on reflexes
