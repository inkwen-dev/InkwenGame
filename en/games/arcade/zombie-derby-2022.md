## About

Zombie Derby is a racing game combined with a zombie killing fest, created by Brinemedia. Drive your car to kill the zombies that get on your path. Make sure to use your fuel wisely as you run out of it. When you are standing still you will be an easy pray to the zombies coming your way. Also don't forget to use your nitro-boosts as it can just help you to get over that hill or gap.
