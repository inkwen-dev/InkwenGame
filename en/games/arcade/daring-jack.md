## About

Jack, an adventurer, was flying over the sea in his small aircraft. When he reached a jungle island, his aircrafts engine failed and he lost the control. He managed to escape before the aircraft crashed and landed safely on the island. Now, he must find a way to escape the island, and his only option is to find a boat. He knows of the old boat house on the island but is uncertain how to reach it. Guide him through the jungle to the boat house. Help him gather essential items for survival, focusing on securing food and shelter while navigating the challenging terrain.
