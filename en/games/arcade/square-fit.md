## About

Square Fit is easy to pick up and play, yet offers a rewarding challenge. Your objective is to tap and hold to grow a square, then release it at just the right moment to fit into the designated target area. Sounds simple, right? Well, the puzzles become progressively trickier, requiring a strategic approach to conquer each level.
