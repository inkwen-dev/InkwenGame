## About

In the new game Jumping Yellow Ball, you will travel to an amazing neon world. Your character is an ordinary ball. Tiles of different sizes will be visible in different places, separated by a certain distance. By clicking on the ball you will call a special line. With its help, you can calculate the trajectory and strength of the jump.
