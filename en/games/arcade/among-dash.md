## About

Are you ready for a new Among Us adventure? In this new game the imposter who is running for his life is starting a stubborn race. While running the dangerous obstacles show up and imposter has to avoid most deadly traps. You have to keep jumping for pass through the obstacles so this way you can get rid of astronauts that are coming for you or you may even save your own life.
