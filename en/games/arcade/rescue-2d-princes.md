## About

Embark on a thrilling rescue adventure in Rescue 2D Princes, an engaging touch-only 2D game designed for mobile and PC platforms. Take on the role of a brave hero in a world of danger and enchantment, tasked with saving the princess. Navigate through 100 challenging levels, each packed with unique obstacles and enemies. Every swipe or tap is a step closer to rescuing the princess. Your wit, agility, and quick reflexes are your best weapons in this captivating quest. Can you conquer all levels and be the hero the princess needs? The realm is counting on you. Join Rescue2D Princes and start your adventure today!
