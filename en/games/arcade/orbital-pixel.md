## About

You are a little pixel and you must survive as long as possible! Go from orbit to orbit to avoid enemies but be careful! If you go too far of your orbit you will lose. Orbital Pixel is an hypnotic game that will test your reflexes, attentions and speed. Avoid the enemies, stay in your orbit and get as much collectibles as possible, how much time will you survive?
