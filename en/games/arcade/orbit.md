## About

Orbit is a simple, fun hyper casual game, you control the satellite around The Earth. The satellite is designed to run on its own in the orbit, and you can just tap and hold to slow it down. Too easy? watch out for rocks and asteroids in other orbit, speed wisely to avoid crash.
