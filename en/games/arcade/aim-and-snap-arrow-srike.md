## About

Aim &amp;Snap is an addictive spear-throwing arcade game that tests your precision, timing, and reflexes! Tap to aim, drag to adjust your angle, and release to launch your spear at enemies and targets with realistic physics-based impact. Every level throws new challenges your way &mdash; moving enemies, tricky obstacles, and wall-bounce mechanics that demand smart strategy and perfect timing. Feel the satisfaction of a clean hit as your spear snaps, sticks, and smashes through targets in vivid 3D environments. As you progress, difficulty ramps up with faster enemies, tighter angles, and more complex stage layouts, keeping the action fresh and endlessl
