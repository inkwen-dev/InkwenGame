## How to play

Open safe tiles.
Numbers show nearby hidden monkeys.
Use numbers to find monkeys.
Avoid monkey tiles.
Reveal all safe tiles to win.

## About

Welcome to MonkeySweeper, a fun puzzle game where you need to use numbers and your thinking to find the hidden monkeys.

The game is simple to play. Look at the numbers on the board and try to understand what they are telling you. Open the tiles you think are safe and keep going until you find all the monkeys. But be careful, one wrong move can change everything.

Some puzzles will be easy and quick to solve. Other puzzles can make you think for a while before you know what to do next. There is no need to rush. Take your time, look at the board and find the correct move.

If you like Minesweeper, you may already know how the number clues work. If you enjoy Sudoku, you will also like using logic and looking for small patterns to solve the board.

MonkeySweeper is made for people who enjoy quiet puzzle games and want something they can play anytime. You can play a quick round when you have some extra time or stay longer and try to solve harder boards.

Every game is a little different and sometimes the answer is right in front of you. You just need to look a little closer.

Find the safe tiles, follow the clues and discover where the monkeys are hiding. Make a mistake? Thats okay, try again and see if you can do better next time.

So, are you ready to find the monkeys?

Start playing MonkeySweeper and see how many puzzles you can solve.
