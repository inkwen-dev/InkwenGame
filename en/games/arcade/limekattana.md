## About

KattanaLime is a fruit cutting game in which players use a virtual katana to slice different types of fruits that appear on the screen. The mechanics of the game are simple: the player must tap and slide his finger on the screen to cut the fruits into smaller pieces while avoiding touching bombs or dangerous objects that appear in the middle of the fruits.In KattanaLime, the player must beware of the bombs that appear on the screen along with the fruits. If the player touches a bomb, it will explode and the game will end immediately.
