## How to play

Use the blind monkey to find the bomb, the deaf monkey to read the manual, and the mute monkey to handle the bomb. Switch between all three, follow the instructions, and solve every bomb puzzle to win.
Desktop Controls:
- WASD / Arrow keys: Move.
- Mouse: Look around after clicking the game.
- Left click / E: Interact; hold when required.
- 1 / 2 / 3: Switch to Blind / Deaf / Mute.
- M: Open or close the manual.
- A / D or Left / Right arrows: Turn manual pages.
- Z: Zoom.
- P: Pause; click RESUME to continue.
Mobile Controls:
- Joystick: Drag to move and turn.
- Monkey portraits: Tap to switch characters.
- USE: Tap or hold to interact, or tap the bomb’s controls directly.
- MANUAL: Open the instructions; tap the arrows to turn pages.
- ZOOM: Take a closer look.
- PAUSE: Pause the game.
