## About

Falling Ball, aka Drop Zone, is a fun and engaging online game that tests your precision and timing skills. The objective is simple yet challenging: guide the balls to fall perfectly into the designated target areas. With each level, the targets become trickier, requiring sharper focus and quicker reactions. Adjust your strategy to navigate obstacles and moving targets. As the game plays, levels get progressively harder, keeping the challenge alive. Aim for the highest score by landing multiple balls in the targets with perfect accuracy.
