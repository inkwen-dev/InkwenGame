## About

Animal Turtle Saver is a thrilling mobile game where players embark on a heroic adventure to rescue endangered sea turtles from perilous situations. Navigate through challenging obstacles, including pollution and predators, as you race against the clock to save as many turtles as possible. With stunning graphics and intuitive controls, immerse yourself in this conservation-themed journey to protect these majestic creatures.
