## About

Level Once Again is a story-driven puzzle platformer with 100 rooms, 4 worlds and 7 bosses. Kaal has kidnapped Tara &mdash; guide Veer through 100 trapped rooms to rescue her. Same room, same door, but a brand new trick every time: floors drop, spikes pop up, doors run away. Only the first 3 rooms are easy &mdash; after that, every room is a trap. Features: - 100 handcrafted rooms across 4 worlds (Dungeon, Workshop, Tower, The Rift) - 7 boss battles with unique patterns - Story scenes (skippable) &mdash; English and Hindi - Hint system: stuck?
