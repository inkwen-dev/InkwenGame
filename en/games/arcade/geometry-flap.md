## About

Enter a world of bold outlines, sharp corners, and pure reflex. In Geometry Flap, you control a shapeshifting arrow darting through a black-and-white maze of geometric chaos. Tap to flap, weave through deadly gates, and dodge precision traps in this high-speed gauntlet of skill. Features: One mistake = instant crash. Simple to play, brutally hard to master. Unlock dozens of slick arrow skins. Will you survive the rhythm of geometry? Challenge your reflex. Flap to glory.
