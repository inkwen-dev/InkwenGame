## About

Merge identical Halloween blocks by moving them in any direction with the help of arrow keys or swiping your figure on the screen or swiping mouse pointer. Each time you merge two blocks you will get a new block. Just give a try to see can you reach to maximum level block? You can shuffle the board or regroup the items for limited number of times.
