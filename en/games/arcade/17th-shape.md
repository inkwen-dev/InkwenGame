## About

Merge identical shapes by moving them in any direction with the help of arrow keys or swiping your figure on the screen or swiping mouse pointer. Each time you merge two shapes you will get a new shape . Just give a try to see can you reach to 17th shape? You can shuffle the board or regroup the items for limited number of times.
