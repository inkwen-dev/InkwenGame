## How to play

Use arrow keys to move or press the buttons on the screen

## About

Our hero decided to break into the castle and save them all. You in the game Stickman Adventures will help him in this adventure. Your hero will have to overcome many locations on the way to the castle. They will be located various mechanical traps and other hazards. Your hero will run as fast as he can on the road. As soon as he gets closer to these dangerous places you will have to make him jump. Also help him collect first-aid kits. With their help, your hero will be able to replenish his health.
