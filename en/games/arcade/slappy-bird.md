## How to play

Mobile: Tap anywhere on the screen to smash the pipes.
Desktop: Click the left mouse button or press Spacebar to smash the pipes.

## About

Instead of controlling the bird, you control the green pipes. Time your smashes perfectly to crush birds as they fly through the gap and collect coins for every successful hit.

But don't let too many escape—missing three birds ends the run. Spend your coins on permanent upgrades to increase coin rewards, reduce smash cooldown, and improve the chance of rare golden birds worth bonus coins. Test your reflexes, beat your high score, and see how long you can keep the skies clear!

Key Features:
Smash birds to earn coins
Endless high-score challenge
Permanent upgrades
Rare golden birds with bonus rewards
Simple controls, fast-paced action
