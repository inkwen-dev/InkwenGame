## About

Word Learner is a brain puzzle game for toddlers and kids. You can play by category or random. Select one of the given categories such are: the category with animals, fruits, countries, birds, and flowers. When you select the category the word will show on the top of the screen. Watch the word he will disappear for short time. The alphabet will show on the screen and you need to click on the alphabet to get the correct word. Learn new words, names, and letters. Have fun and play without mistakes.
