## About

It is a classic breakout game with different types of bricks and challenging levels. 2x Brick needs to be hit 2 times where as ‘Shoot Brick’ can be eliminated by shooting only and if ball hits ‘Shoot Brick’, the ball will be destroyed. ‘Extra Ball Brick’ gives you an extra ball. Eliminate all the bricks to complete a level. Complete each level within 5 minutes. Handle the paddle with the help of mouse or finger tip.
