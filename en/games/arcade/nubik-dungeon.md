## About

Immerse yourself in the exciting world of dungeons, where incredible adventures and dangers await you. In this game you have to go through a variety of dungeons full of traps and riddles. Your task is to survive and reach the exit, overcoming all obstacles on the way. Each dungeon will be unique, with its own set of traps and puzzles. You will have to use your wits and reflexes to overcome all obstacles.
