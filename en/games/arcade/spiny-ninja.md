## About

In Spiny Ninja, embark on a thrilling island adventure, mastering stealth and strategy to evade enemy patrols, avoid view cones, and knock foes out from behind. Hide in shadows, collect coins, and find safe paths to save points. Use coins to buy shields for protection in intense levels. If defeated, revive your ninja with coins or rewards. Reach the destination to conquer levels and unlock tougher challenges!
