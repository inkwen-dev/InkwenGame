## About

Extremely addictive and super fun. Grow your stick to the next level to get more point at the end of the line. Try to avoid obstacles by sliding left and right. Can you make it to final stage? It's a free, fun and satisfying game. The longer the stick, the easier it is to capture the stage. It's running by its own, the only thing you need to do is to slide your finger left and right.
