## About

Knife Train Test is a thrilling game where players master their timing and precision aboard a high-speed train. Navigate through each level by dodging obstacles and throwing knives at targets to clear your path. With escalating difficulty, the game challenges your reflexes and aiming skills under time pressure. Unlock various knives and train designs to customize your gameplay experience.
