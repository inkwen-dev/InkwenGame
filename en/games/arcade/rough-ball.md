## How to play

Don’t Fall Down!
Step into the world of Rough Ball, an addictive and challenging physics-based platformer. Control a cheeky smiley ball through dangerous rocky caves. Your main goal is to roll, bounce, and slide carefully to reach the red flag without falling into deadly pits. Master slippery slopes and tricky jumps to win each level in this fun “do not fall” adventure!
Desktop Controls:
← (Left Arrow): Roll left
→ (Right Arrow): Roll right
Spacebar: Jump
Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Drag to roll the ball left or right
Tap Jump Button: Tap to make the ball jump

## About

Rough Ball is an addictive and challenging physics-based platformer where you control a cheeky smiley ball on a dangerous journey through rocky caves. Your goal is simple but tricky: roll, bounce, and slide carefully to reach the red flag without falling into the deadly pits. With slippery slopes, tricky jumps, and cute skull warnings, every level tests your precision and timing in this fun "do not fall" adventure!
