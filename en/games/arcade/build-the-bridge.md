## How to play

Objective:
Guide the explorer safely across each gap by adjusting both sides of the bridge. Build a stable path and reach the finish flag to complete the level.

Controls (Keyboard):

A – Extend the left side of the bridge.
S – Retract the left side of the bridge.
K – Retract the right side of the bridge.
L – Extend the right side of the bridge.
M - Mute, unmute
Enter – Start the game / Continue.
Space – Continue after completing a level.

Goal:
Create the perfect bridge, avoid mistakes, and complete every level with precise timing and careful planning.

## About

Build the Bridge is a fun and challenging physics puzzle game where you control both sides of a bridge to help the explorer safely cross dangerous gaps. Use precise timing and smart movements to create the perfect path, overcome obstacles, and complete increasingly difficult levels. Easy to learn but hard to master!
