## About

A classic avoid and collect game, where you are a flying cup of coffee. Collect all the sugar you can, avoiding obstacles and other bad things... Do not pour anything else in your coffee except sugar and water! If your cup gets dirty, try to wash it away with some water! Collect sugar and spend your points in the shop to change the skin of your cup. Customize your character changing its color.
