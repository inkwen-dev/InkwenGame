## About

Ball Hit Domino is a fast-paced puzzle game where players strategically place dominos to create a chain reaction when hit by a ball. With vibrant graphics and intuitive controls, the goal is to set up the dominos in such a way that when the ball is launched, it triggers a cascading effect, knocking down as many dominos as possible.
