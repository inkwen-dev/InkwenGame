## How to play

Tap or click the big button to earn cash - the spacebar works too. Spend that cash on the upgrade rows beside the stage: raise your income per tap, then hire hands, which tap for you on their own and keep going while the game is closed. Tap quickly to build the combo meter for critical hits. Use the arrows under the button to move between buttons you have unlocked, merge three matching hands into a stronger tier from the roster, and tap fast to beat the boss guardians that appear as you level up. Watch for golden buttons drifting across the screen - catch one for a burst of cash.

## About

One big red button, and an empire that grows every time you hit it. Cash from each tap buys upgrades that make the next tap worth more, then hired hands that tap alongside you and keep earning while you are away. Behind them wait eleven collectable buttons, six tiers of hands to merge, combo chains that pay out crits, golden buttons that rain money, and the guardians standing between you and the next button. Easy to start in one tap, and it keeps opening up for an hour.
