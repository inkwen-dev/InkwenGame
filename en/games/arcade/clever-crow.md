## About

Clever Crow is a physics puzzle inspired by the classic fable. A thirsty crow must raise the water in a long-necked jar high enough to drink by tossing objects in to displace it. Aim and fling pebbles through the narrow neck: water rises with real displacement physics. But choose wisely big stones are efficient yet wont fit a narrow neck, ice floats and melts, cork barely helps, and a sponge soaks up your water. Battle a crosswind on later stages, beat par for 3 stars, and watch the crow fly over and drink when you win. 8 hand-crafted levels, mouse/touch controls, and no two solutions alike.
