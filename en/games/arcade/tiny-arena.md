## How to play

Hold anywhere to move with the on-screen joystick. Stop moving and your hero auto-fires at the nearest enemy — dodging and shooting are one and the same. Clear every monster in a room to open the door, then pick one of three upgrades and push on. Beat the boss to finish a world and carry your build into the next. In Endless mode, survive as many rounds as you can for the leaderboard. Watch an ad to revive after a deadly hit.

## About

Tiny Arena is a fast, one-thumb dungeon crawler. Battle through monster-filled rooms, grab a fresh upgrade at every door, and push as deep as you can. Fight across five themed worlds, spend coins on permanent upgrades in the meta-shop, and take on an Endless mode with a global leaderboard. Move to dodge, stand still to auto-fire — easy to pick up, tough to master
