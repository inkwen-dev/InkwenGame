## About

Pilot the little green creatures called "Gnammy" and collect all the plankton generated by the sea, be careful don't touch the red monsters or you'll lose one life! The main goal of the game is to collect as much plankton as possible and sign your personal record. Occasionally you will see a red balloon, touch them and earn a bonus.
