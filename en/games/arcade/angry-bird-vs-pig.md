## About

Descending from the skies, Angry Bird engages in a mortal battle against Heel. This is an exciting adventure in which you have to skillfully knock down buildings so that the bird can fall right on the heel and destroy it with its menacing chase. The game requires high skill and strategic thinking to succeed. The angry bird can use its abilities to destroy various buildings and move to new objects. Each level is a unique puzzle that requires quick solutions and good accuracy.
