## About

In this adventure, Noob is now very strong; Noob is saving Pro. Pro got injured in the forest and needs Noob to get home. If Noob can find the Superpower Totem, he can rescue both of them from the forest. With the Superpower Totem, he can jump very far or run very fast. Use all the superpowers to reach the portal and get through the doors to reach the portal.
