## About

Are you ready for the 2nd adventure of noob steve, who is funny and so cowardly? In this adventure, axes are coming and you have to watch out for them, they can kill. Dodge or jump from them to avoid dying! You need to collect all the green eyes, collect them all and the portal will open. If the portal opens, you must go to the next level.
