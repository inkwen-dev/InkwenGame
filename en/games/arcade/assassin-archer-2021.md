## About

Assassin Archer 2021 is a fun and challenging game. In this game, you are controlling a bowman which is rivaled by another AI-controlled archer. You need to carefully choose an angle for your shot, and then apply enough power to it, in order to hit the opponent with your arrows. The AI opponent also does the same thing in his turn. You need to finish your opponent off as fast as possible, before he does the same to you!
