## About

To move through the thickets of the tropical jungle during the next trip to South America, Help the baby fly as far as possible to get to the deep and inaccessible parts of the rainforest. Tap on the Floor to make it fly up rapidly. Maintain the flight at a normal altitude so that the baby does not catch the pipes.
