## About

All aboard for a brain-teasing adventure! In Train Puzzle, your goal is to become the ultimate railway engineer. Slide, rotate, and connect the tracks to create a perfect path for your train to travel safely across the grid. With charming 3D graphics and intuitive controls, you must use logic to avoid dead ends and guide your wagons to the finish line.
