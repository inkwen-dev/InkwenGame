## About

Easy Bratz Coloring where you color your favorite Bratz character! This fun educational game aids children train their coordination skills and helps recognize color, lines and shapes. Select one of the beautiful hand-drawn Bratz images and let your creativity fly! The brushes can be used to color free-hand and train motoric abilities, whereas by using the bucket, bigger areas or details can be filled out quickly. Have fun coloring!
