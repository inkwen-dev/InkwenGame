## How to play

Merge identical dates by moving them in any direction with the help of arrow keys or swiping your figure on the screen or swiping mouse pointer

## About

Try and reach December 25th by merging identical dates by swiping or using the arrow keys. Dates merge to create a new date closer to Christmas. Use the limited amounts of Shuffle and Regroup to rearrange the board when you get stuck.
