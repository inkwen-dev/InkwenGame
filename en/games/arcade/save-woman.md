## About

Save Woman - is an exciting game where you have to play the role of a hero rescuing a girl from various dangerous situations. There are many levels waiting for you, each of which represents a unique challenge. In each scenario you are given two options for action, and only one of them will lead to a successful rescue. You will have to think carefully about your decisions, because the wrong choice can lead to failure.
