## About

Princess Castle Hidden Object is fairytale story game. Enjoy cute medieval scenes. From level to level you explore how the princess spend the time. Look into living room, bedroom, garden at the castle, big castle spaces and secret room. You will find crown, rich clothes, attire, jewel, ornament, coat of arms and other things of the princess in the castle. You goal is to find series of hidden objects on each level. Use Hint and Zoom buttons to find items quickly. You can play any level in the game. Completed levels will be unlocked and you can replay them with different hidden objects set. When you complete the game press More Games button to play more hidden objects games.
