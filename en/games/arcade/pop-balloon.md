## About

Pop Balloon is an exhilarating and fast-paced arcade game that combines precision with rapid reflexes. In this game, players are challenged to pop as many balloons as possible using a set of bouncing spikes. The objective is simple yet addictive: aim and launch the spikes in such a way that they hit and burst the balloons scattered across the screen.
