## How to play

Move and rotate falling blocks to complete horizontal lines. Use arrows or WASD on desktop, swipe/tap controls on mobile, Space for fast drop, and Hold to save a piece. In Bot Duel, your field is on the right on desktop and the bot field is shown on the left; on mobile the bot appears as a small non-blocking window. Survive longer than the bot to win bonus coins.

## About

Pixel Blocks is a compact arcade puzzle about clean lines, fast restarts, and readable pixel effects. Play classic score attack, try physical falling cells, build sand bridges, escape the rising hazard, defuse hot bombs, unlock visual skins, and challenge a bot in Bot Duel. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard and earn 15, 30, or 45 bonus coins when the bot tops out first.
