## How to play

Arrow keys or swipe to jump or duck. Avoid obstacles as you attempt to run as far as possible. Multiple modes: Endless, Levels and challenges.

## About

Dino Dash is a fast, fun, and action-packed endless runner game that sends players racing through a colorful prehistoric world. Take control of a brave little dinosaur and dash across ancient landscapes filled with danger, surprises, and exciting challenges. Your goal is simple: run as far as possible, avoid every obstacle in your path, and keep your adventure going for as long as you can.

As the speed increases, your reflexes will be put to the test. Jump over hazards, dodge tricky barriers, and react quickly as the world rushes past you. Every run feels fresh as you try to beat your best distance, collect more coins, and master the timing needed to survive longer. The easy-to-learn controls make Dino Dash perfect for quick casual play, while the increasing difficulty keeps the game exciting for players who love chasing high scores.
