## About

Let the elevator go down without touching the obstacles! Elevator fall-Break The Lift is a 3D hypercasual arcade game where gamers save the elevator from numerous obstacles. The goal is to safely reach the ground and rescue the falling character. How to Play - Hold your finger to brake, release to fall - Don't touch the obstacles - Help your elevator to reach the bottom of the skyscraper. Features - One tap control - Lots of stunning levels - Unlock multiple funny skins: Auto ,cars, Bus, Pizzmotorcycle, Plane, etc - Difficult obstacles: rotator, rod, stack ball, flipper, etc. Don't try to touch them otherwise you bounce and fail - Great time killer hypercasual game. - Nice graphics and animation - Stack your rewards and become the best lift rescuer ever - Addictive gameplay
