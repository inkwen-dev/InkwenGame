## About

House Renovation Master is a casual idle game of building houses. Want to become a real estate tycoon? Buy old houses, renovate them into new ones and sell them for money. Fix the damaged furniture and floors and give the walls a new paint job. This is not a small project, upgrade your character to be more efficient! May you become a real estate tycoon soon!
