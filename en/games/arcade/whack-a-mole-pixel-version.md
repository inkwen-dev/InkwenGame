## About

-&gt; Whack a Mole &ndash; Pixel Version is a fast-paced arcade game in a retro pixel style where your goal is to hit as many moles as you can before they disappear. Test your reaction speed, focus, and tapping skills! -&gt; Gameplay: Moles appear randomly on the screen &mdash; your task is to tap them before they hide again. Each successful hit earns you points, while bombs take away them! -&gt; Goal: Get as many points as possible in a limited time and beat your own record!
