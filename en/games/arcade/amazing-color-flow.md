## About

Amazing Color Flow is a fun arcade game in which you must fill an empty tank to make it fill. You must help the water to flow in the right direction. To do this you can remove the sticks. There is more than enough water to fill the tank with the correct color but be careful don't collapse the colors. There are 20 challenging levels to complete. Good luck!
