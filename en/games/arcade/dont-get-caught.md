## About

Cheating on a test is an art! Drop your head, open your phone, and tap the answers. The most important thing &ndash; dont let the teacher catch you! His anger bar fills up when: You take too long to answer You answer incorrectly The teacher looks at you while youre staring at your phone If the anger bar fills completely &ndash; you get an F and eternal shame! There are brainrots sitting in the classroom &ndash; theyll try to distract you.
