## How to play

Your tavern has 4 counters Animals progress along these counters to receive their ice-cream Do not throw ice-cream on an empty counter

## About

A flood of cute animals are invading your tavern! They do not want to use your toilets but eat all your ice-cream stock! Quench their thirst before they get impatient. Send them the ice-cream and retrieve the glass when they finish. Beware; some animals are thirstier than others. Cute Forest Tavern is a game of skill, speed and reflex in which you must distribute ice-cream. With these multiple counters, send the ice at the right animal.
