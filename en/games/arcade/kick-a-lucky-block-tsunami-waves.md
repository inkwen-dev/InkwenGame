## About

Kick a Block: Lucky Football is a fun and fast-paced game where your goal is simple — kick the lucky block as far as possible! 😎🍀
Every kick gives you a chance to roll a unique brainrot and test your luck.

Once you land, you turn into a brainrot and must escape a massive tsunami that you triggered yourself! 🌊
The farther you kick, the bigger the rewards — but also the greater the danger.

Collect brainrots and place them on your plot to generate income. 👷
Buy weights and train your legs to kick farther and stronger! 💪

The game blends randomness, progression, and action into an exciting gameplay loop perfect for quick sessions.
