## About

In Impostor Stealthy Ninja, you take on the role of a cunning ninja infiltrating enemy bases. Your mission: eliminate targets and complete objectives without getting caught. Blend into the shadows, disguise yourself, and use an array of ninja skills to outsmart guards and other ninjas. Each level presents new challenges, requiring strategy and quick reflexes. With its engaging stealth mechanics and intense action, Impostor Stealthy Ninja will test your wit and agility. Can you become the ultimate shadow warrior?
