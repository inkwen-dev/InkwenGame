## How to play

Hold the Left Mouse Button or press the Spacebar on PC to turn right. Let go to turn left. On Mobile, just tap and hold anywhere on the screen to drift right, and release your finger to drift left. Time your turns perfectly!

## About

Short Description: Get ready to burn rubber! Drift Master is an addictive arcade drifting game where timing is everything. Tap to slide around tricky corners, avoid obstacles, and survive as long as you can.

Long Description: Do you have what it takes to tame the asphalt? Drift Master puts you behind the wheel of explosive cars in an endless arcade drifting experience! The rules are simple: hold to drift right, release to drift left. But mastering the tight turns and narrowing roads will test your reflexes to the limit.

Collect coins, dodge dangerous obstacles, and chain together massive combos to unlock a garage full of legendary vehicles—from the classic street racer "Blaze" to the furious "Phoenix." How far can you drift before you crash?

Key Features:
