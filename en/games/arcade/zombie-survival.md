Zombie Survival is fun html5 game suitable for all zombie lovers. The aim of the game is simple. You need to get our cute little Zombie to the platform area to clear the level and enter a new one. It is not an easy task! You have to calculate with the bouncing. Click or tap on the objects to remove them of the stage. Do not fall out off the screen!
