## About

Get a rush from this super addictive level-based Coin Rush game. Roll your coin along the Coin Rush twisty road track and get a perfect hit - make sure it lands right smack into the hole. Avoid tricky obstacles like deadly spikes, secret doors and spinning gates - getting a perfect hit into the hole can be dangerous! Beware of the twisty road speed ramp! It'll make your coin roll so fast, you'll need to be a pro to control it and get it into the hole.
