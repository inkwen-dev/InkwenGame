## How to play

Desktop: Use A/D or Left/Right arrow keys to rotate the gears
Mobile: Use the buttons to Rotate Left/Right

## About

Rolling In Gears is an engaging puzzle physics platformer game where you control a ball navigating through a series of mechanical challenges. The game’s main mechanic revolves around rotating gears and moving platforms to guide the ball towards its target destination. Players need to carefully maneuver through the levels, using precision and timing to overcome obstacles and reach the end of each stage.
