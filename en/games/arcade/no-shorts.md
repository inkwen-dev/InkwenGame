## How to play

🎯 Your task is to cause as much damage to stickmen and the environment as possible by shooting from a cannon. You can quickly switch between levels, like and create your own ragdoll stories.

🕹️ Controls
- Click in the right place to shoot.
- Quickly switch between levels by swiping up and down or using a virtual button.

💡Tip:
You can create your own ragdoll stories in the level designer and like others.
