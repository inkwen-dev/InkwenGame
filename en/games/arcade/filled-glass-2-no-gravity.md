## About

Filled Glass 2: No Gravity is a fun online puzzle game. Click on the field outlined by a red rectangle and colored balls will sprinkle from there. You must fill the glass that is glued upside down to the top of the screen. The task is to fill the container to the level of the dotted line. When it turns green, stop backfilling. If at least one ball falls outside the dotted line, the level will have to be replayed. There will be many different obstacles on the way to filling, so that you do not get bored.
