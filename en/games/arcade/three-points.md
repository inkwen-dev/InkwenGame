## About

Three Points is a fast-paced neon reflex arcade game that challenges your precision and timing. Rotate the glowing triangle to match the color of each falling ball before it hits the ring. One mistake ends the game, so stay focused and react fast. How to Play: Tap or click to rotate the triangle by 120 degrees. Match the top color with the falling ball to score points. Collect special bonus orbs with letters inside for temporary power-ups like time extension, shield, or score multiplier.
