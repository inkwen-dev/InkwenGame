## How to play

Arrange battles in the sandbox and unlock new items by passing challenge in a variety of genres. Go through mini-games to unlock new characters for the sandbox and make the battle of your dreams come true.

Use the WASD buttons on the keyboard or touchscreen to move the character.
Use the left and right mouse buttons or the touchscreen to interact with sandbox objects and the interface.
