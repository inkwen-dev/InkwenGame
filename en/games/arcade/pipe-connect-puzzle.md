## About

Pipe Connect: Games Puzzle is a brilliant puzzle solver with an acute attention to detail and a sharp mind. Their function is to join dispersed pipe segments on a grid to provide a continuous water flow path from beginning to end. They must rotate and position pipes of different shapes, such as straight lines, elbows, and T-junctions, within a set amount of time or space as the puzzles get harder. Obstacles such as various sources, restricted routes, or challenging layouts are specific to each level. The character uses reasoning to see the finished process before acting, and he lives on strategy and planning.
