## About

Ben 10 Universe Color Fall - Pin Pull is a captivating puzzle game in which everything revolves around colors. The purpose of this game is very simple - Ben 10 Universe all you need to do is to Pin Pull the right to make the colors go to right container. Only challenge is to pull them in the right order. It’s going to be extremely engaging and fun. The more stars you get, more rewards you earn. There is no time limit but each level has target points to proceed to the next level. Unlock high-powered pins to make your play more exciting and effective.
