## About

Halloween crosswords is a game with words and clues. Read the clue first and click on the number block you like to write. For across, click once on the block number, and for down, twice. Watch the green arrow position beside the clue to ensure you are not wrong. The game consists of three levels 2 about Halloween and the last about movie thrillers. The game works on mobile and tablets, but you must use the arrows to navigate the crossword blocks.
