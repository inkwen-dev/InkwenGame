## How to play

Master the Colors! Bounce your way through an endless whirlwind of vibrant obstacles in this addictive color-matching challenge. Guide your colorful ball upward, timing your jumps perfectly to pass through rotating barriers and collect stars to switch colors on the fly!
In this fast-paced arcade game, you'll tap to make the ball bounce higher and higher, changing its color by collecting stars to match obstacle sections. Pass safely through matching colors (or slip through gaps between mismatched ones) while avoiding deadly crashes to climb as high as possible and rack up an unbeatable high score!
Desktop Controls:
Mouse Click or Spacebar → Bounce the ball higher.
Mobile Controls:
Tap the Screen → Bounce the ball higher.
