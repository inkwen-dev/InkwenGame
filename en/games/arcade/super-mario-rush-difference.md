## About

Super Mario Rush Difference is a fun and challenging online game that is free to play on mobile and all modern browsers. The objective of the game is to find the differences between two seemingly identical images within a given time frame. Players are presented with two images side-by-side, and they must spot the differences between them. The differences can be anything from a change in color, shape, or position of an object. Some of the differences can be quite subtle, so players need to pay close attention to detail to spot them all. Super Mario Rush Difference is a great game for fans of the Super Mario franchise and anyone who enjoys challenging puzzle games. It can be played online for free and is accessible on most devices, including computers, tablets, and smartphones.
