## About

In this adventure, the troll-faced stick must remain calm and humorous as he needs to survive in this sky parkour challenge. The troll-faced character must be fast and collect all the gold before time runs out. He needs to gather the gold, run, and reach the black portal. Surviving in this perilous world full of parkours is truly difficult. To survive, you must run very quickly. You need to collect the gold safely and pass each parkour without falling down. Navigate through the obstacles on the way and reach the portal.
