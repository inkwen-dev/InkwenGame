## About

Talking Angela Coloring Book is a virtual pet created by Slovenian studio Outfit7. Talking AngelaColoring Book feature pictures from the famous game about cat and kitty, which all children love and enjoyed painting and coloring. With the help of the coloring books, kids continue to get acquainted with the images of their favorite protagonists named Talking Angela. Kids love these colouring pages with Talking Angela. It is good for of all ages and are a great educational tool that helps children develop fine motor skills, creativity and color recognition.
