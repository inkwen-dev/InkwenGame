## How to play

Rush hour belongs to the dead — and you are the truck. Your rig drives itself down an endless highway. Smash zombies for score and combos, weave past cars, and hop onto the sidewalk when the lanes get ugly. Cars nick your HP for a few points; trucks hurt more and pay more. Near-misses reward clean driving. Grab fuel cans before the tank runs dry, and snag the orange Rampage pickup for a smash frenzy. Survive as long as you can, beat your best score, and climb the leaderboard. There is no finish line — the wreck is the end, the high score is the win.

Desktop Controls: ← (Left Arrow) or A: Change lane left / hop onto the left sidewalk. → (Right Arrow) or D: Change lane right / hop onto the right sidewalk. Spacebar: Retry after a wreck.

Mobile Controls: Swipe left: Change lane left / hop onto the left sidewalk. Swipe right: Change lane right / hop onto the right sidewalk.
