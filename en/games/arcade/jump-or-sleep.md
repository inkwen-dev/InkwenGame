## About

"Hop or Sleep" is an endless platform game in which you touch and hold to compress the bird and then release it to let it hop from one level to another while keeping the bird alive. – Preserve your funds while meeting a diverse range of individuals along the way! – It is essential that you pay attention to the spikes because if you allow the bird to go this far, it will die. Coins may be accumulated to unlock new characters.
