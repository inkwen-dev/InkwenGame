## About

There isn’t much time! Are you ready to fight evil and save the world in Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Battle for New York? In this fast-paced action game, work together with Mikey, Donnie, Leonardo, and Raphael to save New York City from the Kraang! It’s a race against the clock as you complete various hectic minigames before the clock runs out. Work as a team, taking advantage of each turtles’ strengths and weaknesses to take down the Kraang before they turn NY—no, the whole WORLD into mutant Kraang hybrids! Are you ready to get your ninja on? Every time you completed the mini-game and go around again it gets harder and harder.
