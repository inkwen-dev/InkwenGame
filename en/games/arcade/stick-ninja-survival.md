## About

Stick Ninja Survival is a rogue-like arcade game with 2D stickman characters and monsters. When the Red Sun comes, the sky is covered by the Red Moon, and the world becomes extremely terrifying. People fled in fear from the monsters mouth. You only need to dodge enemies and their bullets because of automatic attack. When you earn some gem resources, you can use them to upgrade your character, Skills, and Talents.
