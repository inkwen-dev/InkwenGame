## About

Dark Sprint is a fast-paced, adrenaline-pumping runner set in a shadowy, mysterious world. Dash through eerie landscapes filled with traps, crumbling paths, and lurking dangers as you guide your character through the darkness. With precise timing and quick reflexes, avoid deadly obstacles and unlock new areas cloaked in secrets. Collect glowing orbs, power-ups, and hidden relics to boost your speed and score. The deeper you run, the darker and more thrilling it becomes. With stylish visuals, intense gameplay, and haunting soundscapes, Dark Sprint offers an unforgettable running experience. Can you outrun the darkness and survive the night?
