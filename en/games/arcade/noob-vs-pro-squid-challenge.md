## About

Squid challenge is waiting for you with a lot of stage friends. The best way to spend time is to be a friend noob and a friend a pro and play two player games. In the first game, you have to move forward without turning his back. In the next game you have to be careful in pulling the rope be the fastest puller and you win. In the next game, reach the finish line with your friend and be the winner of all games and your friend.
