## About

Dive into this addictive 2D puzzle game where you control the gem itself! Your goal is to launch the stone directly into the crate. But the path isnt easy: treacherous walls, traps, and complex cave obstacles block your way. You will need sharp aim and logic to succeed. Calculate your trajectory, use ricochets, and master the physics to navigate around barriers. With every level, the shots become harder to land. Can you make the perfect throw into the box and conquer the dangerous caves?
