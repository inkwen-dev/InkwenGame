## About

In Cover Orange: Wild West, a puzzle-platform game, you must use different objects to create a shelter for our orange protagonists and protect them from acid rain. This time, our citrusy heroes find themselves in the wild west. Drag the available objects and drop them to form a protective cover over the oranges as the storm cloud passes over. Each level also features a hidden star to collect. Have fun solving these entertaining puzzles with family and friends by sharing the game with them!
