## How to play

Desktop / Web: Click and hold the left mouse button, drag to aim and adjust your jump power, then release to leap! 
Mobile Web: Tap and hold anywhere on the screen, swipe to aim and adjust your jump power, then release to leap!

## About

Help the little unicorn flip and jump through exciting levels to reach the destination! Time your moves carefully as you avoid obstacles and tricky challenges along the way. On the other side, another unicorn is waiting to be reached. With simple controls and fun gameplay.
Little Unicorn: Magic Leaps is an enjoyable adventure for players of all ages. Can you master every leap and safely guide your unicorn to the finish?
