## How to play

Tap at the game screen when you think it is the right time to jump

## About

In this game, your job is to control the diver and jump in the pool with water from the jumping place. If you think this is easy, it is not at all because the pool will start to move to the left and the right side, and also he will become smaller and smaller and the jumper will jump from the high. So, try to jump always in the pool, if you miss the pool the diver will be smashed, and you will lose a life. When you lose three lives the game will be over.
