## About

Logic game, the purpose of which is to feed the ever-hungry cat. Your task is to find a way to get to the treat, that is, cut the rope in such a way as to collect all the stars on the level and, of course, feed the "yummy" to the pet. The game has a lot of levels, try to pass them all with three stars.
