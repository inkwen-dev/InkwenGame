## About

Ring on Tube is waiting for you, collecting the pieces placed on the tube with the ring ring. Start the game now and clean the tube with rings at 100+ different levels. You will have to enlarge and shrink the ring according to the obstacles on the tube. To do this, simply click the mouse or touch the screen with your finger. Collect as many points as possible and move on to the next level. Have fun.
