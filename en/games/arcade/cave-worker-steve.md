## How to play

Click on the screen to earn money Use the signs to go to the village or the enchanted table Click on it if you can afford to buy something

## About

Steve, who works in the cave to protect and develop his village, collects money by breaking the blocks in the cave. By saving this money, he can increase his pickaxe to stone, iron, gold, diamond and emerald levels. He can cast a spell on his pickaxe from the enchantment table and turn his pickaxe into its most powerful state.
