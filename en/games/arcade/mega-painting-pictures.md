## About

IN MEGA PAINTING PICTURES YOU WILL FIND 48 BEAUTIFUL DRAWINGS THAT YOU CAN PAINT WITH THE MOUSE OR TOUCH, YOU WILL DISCOVER 6 DIFFERENT CATEGORIES IN WHICH YOU CAN WIN POINTS AND BONUSES. YOU HAVE MANY COLORS AND DIFFERENT PENCIL STROKES, YOU CAN ALSO ERASE TO CORRECT OR CHANGE THE COLOR YOU HAVE CHOSEN. YOU CAN TAKE PHOTOS AT ANY TIME TO PRINT THEM LATER AND DECORATE YOUR HOME WITH BEAUTIFUL AND COLORFUL PAINTINGS.
