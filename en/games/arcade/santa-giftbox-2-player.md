## How to play

Use the WASD keys or the Arrow Keys to move Hold the gift box for 2 minutes to win the game When time runs out the player holding the gift box wins

## About

Be very careful, yes, the New Year has arrived, and Santa Claus and the Green Monster need to race. You need to be either the Green Monster or Santa Claus and hold the gift box in your hands. The one who holds the most gift boxes by the time the timer runs out wins. Take the gift box and avoid your friend, dont let them take it from you. To win the game, hold onto the gift box until the time runs out.
