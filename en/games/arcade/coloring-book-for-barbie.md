## About

Coloring Book for Barbie and drawing and that’s why coloring books were always popular among kids. And in recent years, coloring book games have also became very popular. Our coloring book includes 16 different images of characters, vehicles and etc. that kids can choose from and color it as they like. There are 8 colors and 9 pencil sizes that they can use. There is also an eraser than can be used for erasing the colors and correcting mistakes.
