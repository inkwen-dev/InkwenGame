## How to play

1. Press Start at the booth — balloons soar, the squirrel clings to a wall.
2. Tap (or press Space) to leap to the opposite wall.
3. Catch balloons in flight; gold ones are worth more.
4. Dodge ledges and pigeons.
5. After a fall, check height and balloons, beat today's record, and try again.

Goal: maximize height per run. Height feeds the leaderboard; balloons fuel achievements.

## About

A fairground squirrel sells balloons — until a gust steals them skyward. There’s only one thing left to do: climb after them.

Go Balloons! is an endless pixel wall-runner. Cling to pavilion facades, tap once to leap to the opposite wall, and snatch balloons in mid-air. The higher you go, the faster the pace and the denser the hazards: sticking signs and pigeons eager to knock you off course.

Missing a balloon is safe — only collisions end the run. After each attempt you see height and haul, and your best daily height enters the champions board. Unlock achievement seals and come back for a new record.

Controls stay simple: tap or Space to jump. No combos — just timing, nerve, and another climb. How high can you rise today?
