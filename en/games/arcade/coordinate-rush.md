## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Guide the hero to the location given in the Search Coordinate box by clicking on the corresponding location on the coordinate grid. Getting the hero to the correct location will award 500 points while an incorrect location will deduct 200 points from your score. Get as many points as you can before the 3 minute timer runs out.
