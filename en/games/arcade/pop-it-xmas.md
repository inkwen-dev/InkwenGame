## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

Pop all of the bubbles on the fidget popper to complete a level. You can pop only that bubble which sparks gift. Early you pop the target bubble, more score points will get. If you can’t pop that bubble while gift is sparking then your score will reduce by 50 points. Popping a wrong bubble will subtract your score by 100 points.
