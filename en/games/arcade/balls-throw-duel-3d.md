## About

Are you able to throw a ball into a ring? Balls Throw Duel 3D is a skillful physics ball launching arcade game with 3D balls and rings. In order to win, you need to throw the balls into as many rings as you can. You will get points immediately as long as the ball stay at the middle of each ring. I hope you will always win the AI opponents and have fun!
