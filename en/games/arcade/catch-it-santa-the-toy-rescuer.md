## How to play

Controls are simple: Choose the position where to move the basket and press the corresponding key — Q, A, E, D (or Q, A, P, L) on a computer, or tap the arrow buttons on your phone or tablet. Catch the toys before they fall. The more toys you catch, the more points you earn.

## About

Casual game about catching items rolling in from four directions.
Help Santa save the toys from falling! Toys are rolling in from four directions, move the basket quickly to catch each one before it falls. 
Miss three toys, and the game is over.
The more toys you catch, the more points you earn.
