## About

Happy Orange Glass is a fun and challenging puzzle game where your goal is to draw a line and guide the juice safely into the glass. Plan your path carefully, avoid obstacles, and find the best way to complete each level. Draw the Perfect Path Use your creativity and logic to draw a path from the juice source to the glass. Think carefully before drawing, because the juice will follow the line you create.
