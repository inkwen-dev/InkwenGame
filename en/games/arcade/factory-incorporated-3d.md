## About

Many people know that factories and plants have existed for a long time and produce all sorts of products from mobile phones to simple household appliances. In this game you will have a chance to see how a factory for the destruction of similar products works, where alarm clocks, burgers and other goods are produced. The Factory Incorporated game takes you to a factory that has an event. A large batch of products, and it must be destroyed immediately. Since there is a lot of marriage, you will need to destroy it all very quickly! The press will do all the work for you, and you just need not to overload it or crush certain objects so as not to break it. A large glowing circle will help you do your job more accurately, good luck!
