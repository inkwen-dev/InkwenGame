## About

Two Cubes 3D game is a vertical endless retro tunnel game. Try to control the cube falling down the tunnel and keep it away from the blocks. The game can be played by both 1 PLAYER or 2 PLAYER . Try to go the longest distance in the game or prove that you are better than your friend! Follow the positions of the blocks from below... The cubes will speed up and control will be more difficult.
