## About

Colored Wall is a puzzle game with a ball. Your job is to control the ball to pass through colored walls. The ball will change the colors each time when you need to pass the wall. You can pass on the part of the wall who have same color as the ball. So you need to react fast and to control the wall to the wall with same color.
