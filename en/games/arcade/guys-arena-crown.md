## About

In the final game of the Guys series, you must collect all the crowns. Catch the crowns in the flying helicopter and bring them to the finish line. The one who collects the crowns the fastest wins. Be careful not to fall off the platform; if you do, you lose. Compete with your friend and may the best one win. Be careful, gather all the crowns as quickly as possible. Collect all the crowns to win.
