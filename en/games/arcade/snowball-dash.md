## How to play

Control a bouncy red ball as it rolls down a snowy mountain! Dodge trees, rocks, and other obstacles while trying to survive for as long as possible. The faster you go, the harder it gets!

🎮 Controls:
PC (Keyboard):
Press A or Left Arrow to move left.
Press D or Right Arrow to move right.

Mobile (Touchscreen):
Touch the Left side of the screen to move left.
Touch the Right side of the screen to move right.

## About

Control a lively red ball as it bounces its way down a snowy mountain! Avoid trees, rocks, and tricky obstacles to stay in the game as long as possible. The deeper you go, the faster it gets — and the tougher the ride! Grab glowing crystals along the path to boost your score and test your reflexes. How long can you survive the snowy descent?
