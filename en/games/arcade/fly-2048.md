## About

Fly 2048 is a unique and challenging fusion of two legendary arcade mechanics: the mathematical logic of 2048 and the intense, skill-based navigation of Flappy Bird. In this game, you dont just slide tiles on a grid; you control a flying tile that must navigate through narrow gaps between block barriers. Each successful pass through a gate increases your score and transforms your tile into the next power of two. As your score climbs, the games speed increases, demanding faster reflexes and tighter control. Watch your number grow from 2 to 2048 and beyond while trying to beat your personal best score.
