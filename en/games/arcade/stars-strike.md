## About

Stars Striking is a addictive Block removal puzzle game. In this game player will have a master block that can be moved left or right by tapping or clicking on the screen. Move your master block and position on to its identical block to remove the entire row. Keep striking star troops as they are moving fast to the end point. If the block touches the upper corner of screen, the game will over.
