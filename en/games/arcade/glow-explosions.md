## About

The goal of this game is super simple: you must destroy all moving glow dots in the screen. Just tap somewhere on the screen to create an explosion. All the other dots that will touch the explosion area will also explode, thus creating a chain reaction. You must remove all the glow dots in the screen to win. Are you ready to blow all glow dots?
