## About

Welcome to Screw, Nuts and Bolts Puzzle, a fun and relaxing wood puzzle game where your goal is to solve puzzles and free birds trapped in wooden structures. Each level challenges you to remove nuts, bolts, and screws in the correct order to break apart the wood. As you solve each puzzle, watch the wooden blocks fall and the birds fly away, giving you a sense of achievement. This wooden puzzle game is suitable for players of all ages, with puzzles that start off easy and get harder as you go. Use your brain to figure out the right order to remove the screws, all while enjoying the calming visuals of the game.
