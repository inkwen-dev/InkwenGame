## About

Elastic Man is a funny game where you get to pinch and drag a man with a very elastic face. The man is like a balloon that will stretch when pulled. There is no goal in this game, just have fun pulling and dragging the elastic face of the man. Medium quality is preferred as you won't see the wrinkles in the face of Elastic Man that makes him creepy. The medium quality mode also ensures a great stable performance of the game.
