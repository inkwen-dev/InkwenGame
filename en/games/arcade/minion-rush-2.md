## About

Minions Run is an all-ages endless running game where you can run alongside minions to complete a series of secret missions for Gru and his team. Sprints past your neighbor's house, dodges traps in a lab, charges into the villain's fortress, and introduces new adventures and challenges. You might meet El Maggio, play games with Margalo and the girls, and learn to be the greatest minion secret agent ever. Run wild with the minions
