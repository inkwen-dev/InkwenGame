Tap on the screen to control the ball

## About

Math Up is a brain game for kids and toddlers. You need to know the math to play this game. In the circle will be given the math tasks, and you need to know the answer and pass with the ball at the place in the circle where is the correct number. Do that with all tasks to make a greater score. The circle will be rotating all the time. So you need to be sure of the correct answer and to wait the right time to pass in the circle at the right number.
