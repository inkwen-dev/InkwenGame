## About

The objective of the game is to climb as far as you can without colliding with any obstacles. You control a character who must jump and dodge in a vertical world filled with danger. The further you go, the harder it gets, with faster movement and trickier traps. Along the way, you can unlock various fun and colorful characters, each bringing a unique style to your run. With simple controls and endless challenge, every climb is a chance to beat your best score.
