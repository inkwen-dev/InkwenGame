## About

Shape of Water is fun and challenging game suitable for all ages. You need to make a road for the water to the shape and guess the shape to enter a new level. Use geometry shapes to make a road and make good water flow. Avoid dripping to much water out of shape or you need to restart the level. Nine levels of fun, so what are you waiting for?
