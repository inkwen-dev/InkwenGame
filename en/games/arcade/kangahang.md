## About

Step into the quirky world of KangaHang, where your word-guessing skills are put to the ultimate test! Meet Kanga, the snarky hero at the center of the action. Kanga is full of irony, ready with witty remarks every time you make a mistake. Click/tap letters to uncover hidden words or phrases across a variety of categories including Olympic sports, Cartoon Characters, Animals, Plants and more. Solve 10 puzzles to rescue Kanga from a sticky situation.
