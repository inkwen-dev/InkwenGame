## About

Hand Or Money is a fast reaction and timing game about pure risk and reward. Wait for the right moment, then click to stretch your hand out and grab the cash hiding behind a dangerous guillotine. Mistime it and you lose your hand &mdash; time it perfectly to snatch the money and build up big combos for extra rewards. Between rounds, choose one of three random buffs to power up your run, stack crazy effects, and see how far your luck and reflexes can take you.
