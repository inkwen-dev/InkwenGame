## About

‘Colors of Holi’ is a theme game based on Indian Festival named Holi. People find it very funny and celebrate it with water, colors and funny songs with funny dances. How to play: Select a color-pot and then a blank tile to carry it to that tile. Move the color-pots in order to make a straight row of 5 or more similar color-pots. Use power-ups to clear the overcrowded board. You can play this game until you have room in the board for new color-pots.
