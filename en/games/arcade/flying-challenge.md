## About

Flying Challenge is a Flappy Bird game with a twist. In this game, you control a bird that is trying to fly through a series of obstacles, including bow, and other objects. The goal is to fly as far as you can without hitting anything. The game is simple to play but difficult to master. You can control the bird by tapping and holding on the screen. This will make the bird flap its wings, which gives it lift. You need to time your taps carefully in order to avoid the obstacles.
