## How to play

1. Study the picture and find beads that do not match the color of their current area.
2. Tap the misplaced beads to move them into the bead tray.
3. Select a bead from the tray, then tap a matching empty space to place it correctly.
4. Plan each move carefully, as the tray has limited space.
5. Use Fix Area, Clear Tray, or Magnet when you need assistance.
6. Finish the picture before time runs out to earn stars and coins.
7. View completed artwork, save favorites, and replay the finishing animation in the Gallery.

## About

BeadHouse is a relaxing color puzzle game that turns every completed challenge into a delightful piece of bead art. Observe the picture, spot the beads placed in the wrong areas, and rearrange each color to restore the original design.
Discover 54 handcrafted levels, collect stars and coins, and keep your finished creations in the Gallery. Simple tap controls, satisfying effects, and useful boosters make BeadHouse easy to enjoy while still rewarding careful observation and smart planning.
