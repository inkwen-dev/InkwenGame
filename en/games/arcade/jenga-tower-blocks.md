## About

In addition to strengthening physical and mental skills in youngsters, tower blocks are a physics-based game that can be played from an early age. This challenging and entertaining game is ideal for families and children aged six and up. The original Jenga game is the old wooden block game families have enjoyed for decades. There's no need to be concerned if you don't have any friends nearby. Practice your stacking abilities by playing Jenga alone, building your tower and attempting not to let it come crashing down. If you're playing with coloured wood blocks, you win by seeming to be the last person to remove a block without causing your stack to collapse!
