## How to play

Tap on groups of matching fruits (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to pop them! 🍇💥

Earn points with every pop, but watch out: some fruits hide bombs that cost you 1 life! 💣❤️

If your lives drop to zero, it’s game over—so think strategically! 🧠⚡

Use the upgrade menu to boost your tap earnings, increase starting lives, or buy powerful pomegranates! 🍎⬆️

When used, a pomegranate clears all fruits on screen, gives you their points, and reduces the required fruit count to win the level! 🍊🔥

If you still haven’t cleared enough fruits, new ones will drop, and the challenge continues until victory! ⏳🍓

Conquer all 125 levels and push your limits as the difficulty rises! 🚀🎯
