## About

Heavy flight over skyscrapers in which you try to avoid the missiles that move to you. Try to collect as much money as possible so you can buy one of the aircrafts that are given to you in the store menu. Collect power from the green coins so that you can use the shield and destroy the missiles. Have fun!
