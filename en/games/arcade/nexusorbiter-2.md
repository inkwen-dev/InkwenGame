## About

Single-finger orbital-defense arcade. Your drone orbits a fragile space station and auto-fires at incoming hazards &mdash; tap to flip the orbit direction, thats your only control. Each stage layers in new threats: glue bombs you must shoot at range, homing suicide drones you cant miss, EMP drones that silence your auto-fire on contact, healing orbiters that protect bosses. Six upgrade tracks shape your build. 30+ stages, multi-boss waves, plus a fresh daily challenge for bonus scrap every day. The game features infinite stages and dailyChallenges. The upgrade system is locked to some max level upgrades, but that only to prevent abuse of mechanics.
