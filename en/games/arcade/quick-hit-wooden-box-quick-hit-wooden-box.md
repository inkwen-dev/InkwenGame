## About

Skywire is an action platform game created by Nitrome. In this game, you help carry your passengers to the station while navigating the vessel on the rail. Avoid dangerous robotic animals such as ostriches, parrots, rhinos, and camels, as they will cause your passengers to fall off. There is even a game mode for two-player racing! Go ahead and jump into this rail riding classic!
