## About

Enter a mysterious block-based survival world filled with adventure, danger, and unexpected encounters! Gather resources, craft useful tools, build your shelter, and explore a vast environment as you fight to survive. But this world is not as peaceful as it seems. Strange creatures and terrifying events can appear at any moment. Explore dark areas, discover hidden secrets, and stay alert as you progress. Collect materials, improve your equipment, expand your base, and do everything you can to survive in this challenging adventure!
