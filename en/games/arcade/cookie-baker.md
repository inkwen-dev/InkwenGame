## About

How to Play: A puzzle game with simple rules - clear out panels by touching cookies that measure up to more than 3 and more for a combination. Touch a cookie and the match will register. Vertically or horizontally, line up more than three same cookies to sort them. Sort more than target number, and you beat the stage. Don't miss out on this epic craze! Have Fun!
