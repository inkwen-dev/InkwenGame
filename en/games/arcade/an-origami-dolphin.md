## About

An Origami Dolphin is a relaxing skill game inspired by the art of origami. Guide a folded paper dolphin through textured blue origami tubes, collecting points as you pass through. Bubbles float gently by. With simple controls (tap or click to jump), minimalist graphics, and smooth visual effects, it offers accessible, stress-free fun. Ideal for all ages. Beat your high score in style!
