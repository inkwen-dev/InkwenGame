## About

Tap Pumpkin is an addictive arcade game that will test your reactions and accuracy. In this simple but addictive game, your goal is to tap the screen to make the pumpkin jump and avoid moving obstacles. The game may seem simple at first glance, but progress is far from easy. With each level, the difficulty increases and you will need instant reaction and accuracy.
