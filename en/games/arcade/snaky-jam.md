## How to play

Click or tap a container to select it, then click or tap an available slot to place it. Match the container colors to the snake, clear its colored layers, and clear every snake to win the level. Keep space available and protect the rabbit: finish before a snake reaches the end of the path or the timer runs out. Use extra slots, freezing, hammers, keys, and cooling boosters when you need help. On desktop, use the left mouse button; on mobile, tap the screen. No keyboard controls are required. Open the gear menu to adjust music and sound.

## About

Snaky Jam is a colorful 3D sorting puzzle where every move makes room for the next. Select containers, place them in the available slots, and match their colors to the winding snake. Plan ahead as the boards grow trickier, work around frozen, locked, and burning containers, and use clever boosters to clear the jam. Simple tap controls and playful animations make each level easy to pick up and satisfying to solve.
