## About

Use your wits and find as much candy as you can in Find The Candy: Winter. In this fun game you have to get around all the pushable buttons, movable shelves and other mechanisms in order to discover all the hidden candies and grab them into your bag. Watch carefully because a lot of candies are hidden among the snow. Another thing you should take care of are the stars that are also hidden but aren't as easy to find as the candies. Each level has 3 hidden stars so try finding them all before you advance to the next level.
