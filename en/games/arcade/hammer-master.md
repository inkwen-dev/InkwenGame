## About

Are you want to be a hammer master who could fix anything? To be that, you need a good nail skill. Let us start exercise! Slide to move the hammer. Drive all nails which have the same color. When the energy is full, you will boost a magic ability. Do not knock the rock, you will die! In the end, let us count how many nails you have driven.
