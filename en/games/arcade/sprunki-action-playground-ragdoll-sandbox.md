## How to play

Dive into a world of complete madness and freedom - an explosive 2D action game where chaos, destruction and endless possibilities reign! 🌀🔥

Here you will find control over funny sprunki with realistic physics, complete freedom of creativity, many weapons, costumes and objects for experiments! 🎭💣

🚀 What can you do in the game? 🚀

💥 Destruction without borders - blow up, crush, destroy everything in your path, creating the most insane scenes!

⚔️ Battles with sprunki - arrange epic fights using cold and firearms! 🗡️🔫

🛠️ Construction - create your own arenas, traps and unique structures from many elements! 🏗️

👕 Clothing and customization – dress up your sprunki in different looks, combine styles and create your own heroes! 🎨

🎮 Complete freedom of action – build, destroy, fight, create chaos without restrictions and rules!


Ready to create your own world of chaos and destruction?🔥🎮
