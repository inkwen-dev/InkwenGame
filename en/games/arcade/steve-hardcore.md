## About

Are you ready for Steves exciting adventure? There are green monsters everywhere, and you need to collect all the obsidian pieces. Gather all the pieces and reach the opened black hole. Go through the black hole and reach the portal in the cave. You must reach the portal safely. Remember, youre in hardcore mode. You have three chances to die. If you die more than three times, all levels will be deleted, and youll start from scratch.
