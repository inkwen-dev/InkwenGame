## About

Connect Dots is a classic and challenging puzzle game. Your goal is to connect all the dots using horizontal and vertical connections close together and without overlapping. It is not necessary to follow a specific order or correct path to solve the puzzle, just that all the points are connected. The difficulty increases with each level. Challenge yourself and find a correct path through the points, call your friends to see who goes the furthest!
