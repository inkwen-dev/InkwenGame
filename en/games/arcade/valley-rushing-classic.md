## How to play

As a runner, cross the field past the white line the required number of times without getting tagged. As the chaser, tag every runner with your knife. Caught runners go down and become chasers unless a teammate revives them in time. PC: WASD to move, hold the right mouse button and drag to turn the camera, Space + A/D for a side dash, Shift for a hard stop, left mouse button or E to strike, hold E to revive. Mobile: joystick on the left to move, drag to look around, DASH and HIT buttons on the right, hold the button to revive.

## About

Valley Rushing Classic is a 3D tag game in a blocky valley. Runners sprint across a green studded field between two white lines, grab gems and dodge the chaser's knife. Every caught runner turns into a chaser, so each round gets tougher until the last runner stands or falls. Revive downed teammates, buy abilities with gems, unlock skins, knives, trails and mythic brainrot outfits, spin the wheel and claim daily gifts. Pick the bot difficulty: hard bots dodge better and pay more coins and XP.
