## How to play

Control the hole and move around the level to eat objects. Start by eating smaller objects, grow bigger, and then eat larger objects that become available as your size increases. Your goal is to collect enough objects and complete each level before the timer runs out.

Desktop Controls

WASD: Move the hole.
Arrow Keys: Move the hole.

Mobile Controls

On-Screen Joystick: Drag the joystick to move the hole in any direction.

Complete the level by eating the required objects before time runs out. Use boosters and upgrades to help you complete difficult levels.
