## About

In this game you are a window cleaner. Cleaning windows in tall buildings is a dangerous job. But if you are with a colleague, it can be dangerous but also fun. You will compete who will reach the goal faster. To do this, you need to avoid obstacles, you can collect the flags that you will use as parachutes and you will go down very quickly. But you can also take the water sponge and shoot it at your colleague. Very interesting game where you will try to be the first.
