## How to play

MOVE LEFT-RIGHT SWIPE MOUSE

## About

Collect new snake parts as you progress along the track and try to become the giant worm in the arena. Each track has a number of gift boxes to collect. If you reach the finish line before collecting these gift boxes, you will fail.
