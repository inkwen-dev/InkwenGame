## About

Super Penguins is a block-matching puzzle game with 2D cartoon cute penguin blocks in different mazes. As the commander of the Penguin Rescue Squad, you need to plan a safe track to rescue the trapped penguins from different shapes of ice blocks at each maze level. Keep calm and have a good time in this game!
