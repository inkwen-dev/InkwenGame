## About

There are legends of a pixel that lives in the rainbow. And it's true! He jumps from color to color. You have to guide his steps and pass it from platform to platform. It should not jump into the void. One fall and the game is over. Rainbow Tile is a game that tests your speed and anticipation. Move the pixel as quickly as possible and never make mistakes. The pastel atmosphere of the game will please any player. What is your best score?
