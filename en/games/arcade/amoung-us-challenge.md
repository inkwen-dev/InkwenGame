## How to play

Red player should use D for right A for left S for down and W for up jump The game consists of 10 chapters In the next chapters some diamonds are hidden in boxes Can you find them

## About

Are you ready to have fun with your friend? The door will be unlocked when our characters collect all the diamonds on the screen. When both players collect the diamonds and reach the door, the next chapter will be unlocked. Blue player should use arrow keys.
