## How to play

Controls are super simple — hold the screen and move your finger up or down to guide your Fluffy 🧊. Avoid fire 🔥, ice ❄️, lasers ⚡, and spinning saws. Don’t crash and don’t lift your finger!

Collect coins 💰 while flying — use them to buy chests 🎁. Chests may contain eggs 🥚 that hatch into new Fluffies! Each one is a fluffy cube with its own style and charm. Some are rarer than others — build your full collection!

You can also upgrade the incubator 🔧 — higher levels hatch Fluffies faster, helping you unlock new heroes even quicker.

Your drone companion 🚁 floats in the main menu, protects the incubator, and can be recolored 🎨 to match your style.

Let’s fly! 🐾✨
