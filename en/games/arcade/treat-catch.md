## About

Welcome to **Treat Catch** &mdash; a colorful arcade game starring an adorable Labrador puppy who loves treats! Move the bowl and catch tasty goodies falling from the sky: bones, steaks, cheese, chicken and more. But watch out &mdash; socks, bombs and other bad items are falling too! Build combos, score points and earn **Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Diamond medals**. The better you play, the faster and harder the game becomes. Can you reach Diamond? With cute graphics, simple controls and exciting endless gameplay, **Treat Catch** is easy to learn and fun to master. Catch the treats, avoid the trouble and beat your high score!
