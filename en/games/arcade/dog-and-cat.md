## About

In this adventure where the friendship between the dog and the cat exists, collect all the fish and bones. The dog and the cat siblings urgently need to find 3 boxes. There are very secret things in the box and they need to find and throw the boxes into the air. Collect all the boxes with your friend and complete the levels. You must be very careful. Sharp rotating knives are coming towards you.
