## About

Rainbow Monster Survival is a thrilling horror survival game where you must escape from terrifying Rainbow Monsters inside a creepy playground. Trapped alone, your mission is to search for hidden blocks while avoiding deadly monsters roaming the map. Each level challenges your stealth, timing, and survival skills. Hide in boxes, stay quiet, and listen carefully to monster footsteps to survive. Simple controls, intense gameplay, and scary encounters make this rainbow horror game exciting and fun. Can you find all the blocks and survive the nightmare?
