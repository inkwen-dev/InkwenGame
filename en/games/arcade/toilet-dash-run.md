## How to play

Objective: Get every character safely to the restroom with the matching symbol.

Press or touch a character, then drag to draw a route to the correct door. Release on the destination and plan the remaining routes. Avoid walls, obstacles, enemies, and paths that would make the characters collide. You win when every character reaches the correct restroom safely.

Controls:
- Mouse: Hold the left mouse button and drag.
- Touchscreen: Press and drag with one finger.
- Restart: Select the circular-arrow button to redraw the level.
- Hint or skip: Select the video button and complete the rewarded video when available.

## About

Toilet Dash Run is a lighthearted path-drawing puzzle game. Guide each character to the matching restroom by drawing safe routes around fences, hazards, and moving enemies. Plan every line before the rush begins: once the routes are ready, everyone moves at the same time. Handcrafted levels, unlockable character looks, and quick mouse-or-touch play make every escape a fresh challenge.
