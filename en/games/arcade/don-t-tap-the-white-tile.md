## About

Don’t Tap The White Tile is the most addictive and frustrating rhythm style game on the Internet! You'll have to try to tap only on the black tiles of a piano but avoid the white tiles. If you tap even one white tile or miss one black tile, it is game over. Train your fingers to tap faster and faster each time !
