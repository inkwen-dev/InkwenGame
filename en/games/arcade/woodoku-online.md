## About

Woodoku is a casual game. Your goal is to put the blocks into the box and fill them and then eliminate them after filling them horizontally or vertically, testing your patience and carefulness. Consider whether the rest of the material can be filled later, and there is enough space for the next round of material. There are three modes to choose from. Have a good time.
