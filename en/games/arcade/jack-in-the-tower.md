## About

Jack in The Tower takes the Endless runner (i guess endless flyer) genre! Help our friend "Jack" the pumpkin to get as high as he can, avoiding the obstacles such as rolling mines, spikes, bats on the way and collecting as many coins as possible. Don't hit any enemies or it's game over for Jack! Challenge your friends scores on the local leader boards to see who comes out top in this addictive, challenging and entertaining arcade game.
