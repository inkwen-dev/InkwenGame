## About

You are trapped in a nextbot world. Survive for a certain amount of time and escape. Nextbot, like many other killers, will simply chase you. He or they moves at a quick pace. Upon making close contact with a you, Nextbot will knock you away, instantly killing you. He ( they ) does not suffer from a cooldown and can wipe out multiple humans at once.
