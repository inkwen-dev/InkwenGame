## About

Burger Fold Puzzle challenges players to become master chefs by solving culinary conundrums. Craft perfect burger combinations by strategically folding ingredients to create delectable masterpieces. With each level offering a new burger to assemble, precision and speed are key. Navigate through a variety of ingredients, obstacles, and challenges while aiming for the ultimate burger perfection.
