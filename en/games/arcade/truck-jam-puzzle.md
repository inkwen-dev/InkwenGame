## How to play

Click with the left mouse button or tap a truck with a clear route, then click or tap an empty loading slot to park it. Match truck colors to the waiting cargo. Fully loaded trucks leave, freeing space for your next move. Clear the trucks before the timer runs out, and avoid filling every slot with trucks that cannot collect cargo. Use the on-screen booster buttons when you need help. Complete levels quickly to earn more stars and unlock the next puzzle. All menus use mouse clicks or touch; no keyboard keys are required.

## About

Untangle a colorful truck traffic jam in this 3D sorting puzzle! Send trucks out in the right order, match them with cargo of the same color, and keep your limited loading slots clear. Discover locked, frozen, hidden, and overheating trucks as the puzzles grow more challenging. Use helpful boosters and aim for three stars on every level.
