## About

Step into a world where the only light is your own aura. Guide a brave boy in a dark hoodie through candle-lit caves, sleeping monsters and hidden passages. Collect every glowing gem, avoid bombs, pits and traps, and unlock the exit gate of each level. A cozy dark platform adventure designed for short relaxing sessions at home or on the go.
