## How to play

Tap a kitty at the bottom to send it onto the conveyor. As it rides around the tray it eats the first candy of its color in each row and column. A kitty that isn't full after a lap waits on a cushion — tap it to send it again. Eat every candy to clear the tray. Don't let all cushions fill up!

## About

A tray of glossy jelly candies forms a cute pixel picture — a strawberry, a puppy, a rainbow. And here come the chubby, hungry kitties! They hop onto a conveyor belt that rolls around the tray, and every kitty munches only the candies of its own color. Pick who rides next, keep the waiting cushions free, and gobble up the whole picture bite by bite!

Easy to learn and oh so satisfying — but the order matters. Clear tricky trays with ice-covered candies, chocolate walls and mystery kitties, and earn three stars by planning ahead.
