## About

Orbit Tap Rush is a fast-paced hyper casual arcade game where timing and reflexes decide survival. Two planets rotate around a shared orbit while dangerous obstacles fall from the sky. The player must tap the screen to instantly reverse the orbit direction and avoid collisions. The challenge increases as the game progresses, testing the players reaction speed and focus. Every 30 seconds, a Star Power appears. When collected, it grants a temporary shield that protects the planet from obstacle collisions for a short time.
