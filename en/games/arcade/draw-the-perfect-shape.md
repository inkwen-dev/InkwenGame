A relaxing yet challenging hypercasual game where you test your precision. Choose a geometric shape circle, square, or triangle and try to draw it perfectly…

## How to play

The objective is to draw the requested shape as accurately as possible to achieve a 100% score.
Controls: Click and drag your mouse (on PC) or touch and swipe your finger (on mobile) to draw.
You must complete the shape in one continuous line without lifting your finger or releasing the mouse button. Once you release, the game calculates your precision score.

## About

A relaxing yet challenging hypercasual game where you test your precision. Choose a geometric shape circle, square, or triangle and try to draw it perfectly with a single continuous stroke. The game's smart algorithm analyzes your stroke in real-time and scores your accuracy from 0% to 100%. Simple to learn, but hard to master!
