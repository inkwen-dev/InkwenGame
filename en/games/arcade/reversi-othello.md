## About

Reversi Othello is an addictive and strategic online game that puts players tactical skills to the test. Inspired by the classic board game Othello, this digital version offers an engaging and challenging experience for players of all ages. In Reversi Othello, players take turns placing their colored discs on a grid board, aiming to surround their opponents pieces and flip them to their own color. The objective is to dominate the board with as many of your discs as possible by the end of the game. The rules are easy to grasp, making them accessible to newcomers, but mastering the game requires foresight, planning, and cunning moves.
