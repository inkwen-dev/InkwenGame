## How to play

Mouse & Touch Controls:
• Left Click / Tap: Select and place items from the palette
• Drag & Drop: Move placed objects freely across the arena
• Widget (↺ / ↻ buttons): Rotate active object to adjust angles
• Trash Button (🗑️ DEL): Remove selected item
• Right Click (PC): Quick delete object under cursor
• Mouse Wheel / Swipe: Pan and scroll camera up/down

Keyboard Shortcuts (PC):
• Q / E or A / D: Rotate object left / right
• 1 – 9: Quick select items from the palette
• Spacebar: Start / Resume physics simulation
• Delete / Backspace: Delete active item

## About

Ragdoll Rescue: Physics Puzzle is a hilarious, satisfying physics puzzle game where your mission is to save an unlucky ragdoll hero pushed off towering cliffs by a mischievous villain!
Strategically place mattresses, trampolines, booster springs, fans, ramps, and portals to build the ultimate rescue contraption. Direct the hero's bounce trajectory away from deadly spikes and dangerous hazards to land safely with maximum health!
