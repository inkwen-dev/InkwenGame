## How to play

DESKTOP

Move - W A S D (read by physical key position, so it also works on AZERTY)
Cast - hold SPACE at the water's edge, release to throw. Hold longer to cast further.
Strike, reel and shoot - F or the left mouse button
Aim the bow - drag the mouse while SPACE is held
Interact with shops, cars and jetskis - E
Fishdex - Q
Switch tool (rod, sword, bow) - 1 2 3
Pause - P or Esc

MOBILE

Move - the on-screen joystick at the bottom left
Cast, reel, strike - the big round button at the bottom right
Switch tool - the three buttons just above it
Interact - tap the prompt that appears on screen
Menus - the icons in the top corners

To land a fish, cast into the water, wait for the bite, then hold the action button to keep the line tight and release when the tension meter fills. Bigger fish pull harder and pay more coins. Coins buy better rods, lures, swords and bows, plus cars, jetskis, accessories and skins, all from the shops on the beach.

## About

Reel & Slash II: Beach Legends is a 3D beach fishing game where every cast turns into a fight.

Cast from the shoreline, wait for the bite, then reel hard and finish the fish with your sword. The bigger the catch, the harder it pulls, and the more coins it pays.

- 100 real fish species across 10 levels, from a 1 cm pygmy goby to a 26 metre humpback whale
- Rods, lures, swords and bows to buy, each one stronger than the last
- Accessories, skins, cars and jetskis to unlock
- A Fishdex to fill, and a beach full of anglers with their own gear and names

Drive a car along the sand and smash through the floating coins, or take a jetski out to sea and hunt the golden multiplier rings that multiply everything you collect.

Can you land the whale?
