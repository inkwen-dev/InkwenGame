## About

Velocity Breaker is a high-speed reflex game where survival depends on precision and timing. Control a fast-moving energy shard as it races through a constantly scrolling neon tunnel filled with deadly obstacles. The game moves forward automatically &mdash; your job is to dodge, dash, and react in milliseconds. Choose between Easy, Medium, and Hard modes, each increasing speed and challenge. Master the dash mechanic, survive intense obstacle patterns, and push your reflexes to the limit while chasing the highest distance-based score.
