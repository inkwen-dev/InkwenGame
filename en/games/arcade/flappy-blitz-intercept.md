## About

Get ready for an adrenaline-pumping twist on the classic flapping frenzy! In Flappy Blitz: Intercept, youre not just avoiding obstacles; youre a nimble, pixel-art jet tasked with a critical mission: Intercept the enemy missiles! Navigate a relentless barrage of incoming pipes and guided enemy rockets. Tap to flap your wings and maintain altitude. Time your taps perfectly to slip through the gaps, but keep a keen eye out for the fast-moving missiles&mdash;theyll track you! Score points by passing through the pipes and by strategically leading the enemy missiles into them for a satisfying Blitz bonus!
