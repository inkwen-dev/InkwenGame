## About

The Fat or Thin game is a fun, relaxing runner where you can help a cute furry bear lose extra weight after winter hibernation! There are more than 30 fascinating levels ahead of you, each of which you must pass the obstacle course and finish, not forgetting to watch your diet. Are you ready to take your will in your fist and completely give up burgers, pizza, and doughnuts, replacing them with healthier food?
