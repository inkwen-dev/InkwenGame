For PC:

For phone:

Left side of the screen - move
Right side of the screen - look around
Tap the screen - gain speed

## About

My Fast Brainrot is a crazy clicker where memes come to life!

You control a real Italian Brainrot - a funny little animal with a thirst for speed. Your task is simple: click, upgrade, and accelerate your hero to incredible speeds to leave your rivals far behind.

Every race is a challenge! Compete against other Brainrots, win, and prove that your meme is the fastest. But speed is just the beginning.

Victories earn you pet eggs, which you can upgrade, crossbreed, and collect to create a whole collection of rare creatures. The stronger your pets, the faster and more powerful your Brainrot becomes!
