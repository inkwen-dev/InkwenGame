## About

Click to Control Chaos is a fast-paced casual game where disorder is your greatest enemy. Colorful objects move wildly across the screen, and every click you make temporarily restores balance&mdash;at a cost. While calming one area, chaos spreads elsewhere, forcing you to think fast and react even faster. With dynamic visuals, smooth animations, and constantly escalating intensity, the game delivers a unique challenge where control is never permanent. Simple to play but hard to survive, Click to Control Chaos tests your reflexes, focus, and ability to manage growing disorder. How long can you keep chaos under control?
