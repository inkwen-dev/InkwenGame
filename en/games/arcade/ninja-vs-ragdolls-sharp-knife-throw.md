## How to play

🎯 Game Objective
Throw knives at ragdoll enemies and hit the target accurately! To pass a level, defeat all enemies using skill and precision. The more accurate your throw, the faster you win!

💻 On PC:
Aim with the cursor and click the left mouse button to throw a knife in the chosen direction.

📱 On Mobile Devices:
Tap the screen at the desired spot—the knife will fly where you point your finger.

🔥 Tip
Watch the enemies' movements and choose the right moment to throw. Use different types of knives and become the sharpest ninja in the arena!
