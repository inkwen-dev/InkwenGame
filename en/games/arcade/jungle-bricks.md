## About

Jungle Bricks is a highly addictive game that comes with a Jungle theme in which a jungle boy collects lots of fruits(such as apples, grapes, cherries) in his pocket by breaking bricks. Aim the target and shoot balls to break maximum bricks in one go to help the Jungle boy to get more fruits. You need to break all the bricks on the screen to complete the stage. A number displays on every brick that indicates how many times you need to hit the brick with the ball to break. But, make sure that any of the bricks never touch the bottom. As you progress in the game the levels would get difficult to complete. Enjoy the endless fun-loving game and clear as many stages as possible to make more & more scores.
