## How to play

Press WASD keys to move Double jump active Mobile control available Take the key open the door

## About

You need to survive in the world of Pixelpool, help him find the key and collect the red life-giving diamonds while searching for the key, don't forget, if you die, you will lose your last life. Find the key and go to the door before you die! If you don't get the key, the door won't open.
