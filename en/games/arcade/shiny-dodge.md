## About

Shiny Dodge is a fast, addictive arcade dodging game where you control a glowing energy orb and survive as long as possible! Move left and right to avoid falling red spikes while collecting coins and rare golden stars to increase your score. Pick up powerful abilities like Shield, Magnet, and Slow Motion to stay alive longer and push your high score even higher. The game starts simple but quickly becomes challenging as falling objects speed up over time. Each run is unique thanks to randomized spawns and smooth controls. Play with keyboard arrows or use simple touch controls on mobile.
