## How to play

Use your mouse or touch screen to drag a snake’s head through empty cells. Fill the board and use all of each snake’s remaining moves to solve the level. Plan around obstacles and special tiles as new mechanics appear. Use Undo to take back a move, Restart to try again, and Hint for help. The pause menu contains sound, language, and visual settings.

## About

Garden Snakes is a relaxing logic puzzle game set in a colorful garden. Drag cute snakes through every empty tile, plan around obstacles, and discover new mechanics across 115 handcrafted levels. Switch between 2D and soft clay 3D styles, undo your moves, and use optional hints when you need a nudge.
