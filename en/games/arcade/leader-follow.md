## About

Embark on an epic adventure in our immersive game, where the dynamic Leader Follow script takes center stage. As the leader, youll strategize and navigate perilous challenges, while your AI-driven companions follow your every move, adapting to your decisions. With the power of Leader Follow, teamwork and camaraderie are essential for victory. Are you ready to lead and conquer?
