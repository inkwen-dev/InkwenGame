## About

Slice Brawl Stars Clicker is a fast-paced slicing game where you cut through waves of Brawl Stars-style characters. Use your blade to slice enemies before they reach you, earning points. The more precise your cuts, the higher your score. This arcade-style clicker combines reflex-based gameplay with a fun, chaotic challenge. Can you survive the slicing frenzy?
