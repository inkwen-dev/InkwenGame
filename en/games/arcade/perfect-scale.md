## About

Prefect Scale Game is a fun, challenging, and rewarding game. Fruit weight style game with simple rules. The goal of the game is to get as many fruit on your scale as possible without picking up any fruit (hence the name). It's a free-to-play casual game that's easy to learn but hard to master.
