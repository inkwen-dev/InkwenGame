## How to play

Desktop Controls:
↑ (Up Arrow) / W: Accelerate.
↓ (Down Arrow) / S: Reverse.
← (Left Arrow) / A: Steer left.
→ (Right Arrow) / D: Steer right.
Spacebar: Jump.
Shift: Brake.
Mouse: Move the camera.
Mobile Controls:
Left / Right Buttons: Steer the car.
Gas Button: Accelerate.
Brake Button: Brake; hold while stopped to reverse.
Jump Button: Jump.
Swipe: Move the camera.

## About
