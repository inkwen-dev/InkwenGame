## About

Halloween Link is a classic block elimination game. In this game you can eliminate 2 similar blocks at a time. The rule is that the both block must be connected with a line with maximum 2 turns. To complete a level, you need to eliminate all the cards on the Board. Complete all 21 levels to win this game.
