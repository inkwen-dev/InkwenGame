## About

Carrot Climber is a cute and fun game where a hungry rabbit climbs walls to collect carrots! Each level has hooks placed on the wall, and you need to help the rabbit jump from one hook to another. Aim carefully&mdash;if you miss, the rabbit will fall! Collect carrots and coins along the way to unlock the next level. It&rsquo;s a sweet mix of challenge and fun for all ages.
