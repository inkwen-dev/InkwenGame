## About

Jump, bait, and trap the rabbits in this fun timing-based puzzle game! Click and hold to lower the carrot and tempt the rabbits toward it. As soon as they jump to grab the carrot, release at the perfect moment to catch them in the hole. Every level comes with a different mission to complete, and the challenge gets harder as you progress. Unlock new levels, meet smarter rabbits, and watch out for clever and tricky ones that use different behaviors to catch the carrot. Master your timing, learn each rabbit&rsquo;s movement, and see how many levels you can complete!
