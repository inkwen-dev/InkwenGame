Fruits vs Zombies catapults you into a fruity 2D arcade adventure where you use fruits as your weapon against the undead. Harness the unique powers of each fruit, aim strategically, and topple zombie fortresses with precision throws. Beware, timing and physics are your allies in this deliciously chaotic battle!
