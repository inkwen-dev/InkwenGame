## About

Numblocks Hunter is an Arcade-Action Math game. In this game you need to keep the blocks away from the base by eliminating the rows of blocks. At a time you can remove on row. To eliminate a row you need to tap one block in the frontline which is showing the answer of a math question given on the base block.
