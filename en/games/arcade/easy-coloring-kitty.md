## About

Easy Coloring Kitty is a super fun and relaxing coloring game made just for kids. Get ready to bring cute kittens to life with bright colors and silly designs. Pick a picture, grab your favorite shades, and start painting. There is no right or wrong way to play: just have fun, be creative, and let your imagination run wild. Whether you like rainbow cats or classic fluffy friends, this game is all about smiles and color. Perfect for young artists who love animals and fun art time. Coloring has never been this easy or this cute!
