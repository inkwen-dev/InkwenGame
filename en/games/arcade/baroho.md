## How to play

Move the platform-manipulator with your keyboard or on-screen joystick (touchscreen only).

Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys.

With a gamepad connected, use both analog sticks — each controls one side of the platform.

Tilt the manipulator to roll the ball left or right across the board.

Aim for the holes with yellow rims and glowing arrows.

Watch your ball count — you'll need every last one to win.

Collect coins (just don't drop them in the hole!) to buy new ball skins.

And when fans show up, get ready for an extra physics challenge!

An arcade game of skill and composure in full 3D.
