## About

Wall Crusher Hero is a fun relaxing game in which you have to launch a stickman into pixel mosaics to crush them! Collect gold coins and upgrade your hero to destroy walls more efficiently and effectively! Remember that you only have 15 stickmans per try, so be smart!
