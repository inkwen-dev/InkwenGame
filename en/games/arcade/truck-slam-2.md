## About

Truck Slam is a dynamic arcade game about arena truck battles featuring physics and destruction. Jump into the fight, accelerate, ram your opponent, and use a slam or swing to knock enemies off the platform. Complete levels and upgrade your stats &mdash; durability, power, jump, and speed &mdash; to conquer more difficult arenas! Features: Physics-based collisions and spectacular crashes Levels with different arenas and obstacles Stat upgrades after victories 1-player and 2-player modes on a single device Playable on both PC and mobile
