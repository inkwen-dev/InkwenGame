## How to play

Swipe to aim and release to shoot the balls. Break all the bricks before they reach the bottom. Each brick shows the number of hits needed to destroy it. Use power-ups to clear tough levels faster. Bounce shots off walls to hit more bricks and create combos. The level ends when all bricks are cleared or reach the bottom. Keep aiming smart and breaking bricks to achieve high scores!
