## About

Fill The Holes is a fast-paced arcade game that tests your reflexes and color-matching skills. Tap on portals to gather colors, filling corresponding holes while keeping up with the escalating speed. Experience the thrill of rising up the global leaderboard in this addictive and visually vibrant challenge.
