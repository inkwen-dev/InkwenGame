## How to play

Goal: grab the snack and carry it out through the pet flap or the door without being seen. Stay out of the colored vision cones; hide under tables or behind curtains when someone looks your way. Big snacks are slow and only fit through the door. 
Desktop: arrow keys or WASD to move, Shift to tiptoe, Space to grab, drop or use things. 
Mobile: drag anywhere to move (inside the dashed ring you tiptoe, beyond it you run) and tap the big button to act. Earn medals for finishing unseen, grabbing the bonus snack and beating the par time.

## About

Crumb Bandit is a cozy 3D stealth heist game starring a hungry raccoon. Sneak into a home kitchen, a bakery, a mini market, a movie theater and a grand manor to steal donuts, wedding cakes, popcorn and golden treats, all without getting caught. Dodge flashlights, security cameras, lasers, robot vacuums and sleeping guard dogs, hide under tables and behind curtains, and slip out through the pet flap. 44 heists, four medals per job, unlockable districts and a raccoon den you can decorate.
