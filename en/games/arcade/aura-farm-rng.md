## How to play

Press ROLL to receive a random aura and coins. Open Auras, choose My auras, and select a find to equip. Spend coins on luck and roll speed. Gather materials in the Wilds and craft items. Turn on AUTO after 25 rolls. Your goal is to grow your collection and unlock deeper regions.

Desktop: R or Enter: roll; WASD or arrow keys: move; Space: jump. F or a short left click on the world: attack the nearest enemy in range. Mouse drag or Q/E: rotate the camera. Mouse wheel: zoom. Escape: close a panel. Roll shortcuts apply outside open panels and text fields.

Phone: move with the left joystick, swipe on the right to rotate the camera, and pinch outside the joystick area to zoom. Use the on-screen roll, jump and attack buttons.

## About

Aura Farm: RNG is a 3D browser RNG game about collecting rare auras. Press ROLL to get random auras and coins, equip your favorites, and spend coins on luck and roll-speed upgrades.

Build a collection of 65 auras with fire, lightning, wings and cosmic effects. Four special auras come from bosses or crafting.

Explore the Wilds, defeat monsters and find chests. Gather materials to craft potions and luck gloves. Complete three daily quests and weekly challenges, and unlock deeper regions as your collection grows.

Some auras are easier to obtain at their matching time of day. World events boost luck and make event auras available. AUTO unlocks after 25 rolls. After unlocking it, receive capped offline rewards when you return.
