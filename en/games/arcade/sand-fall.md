## How to play

Move the falling block left and right and choose the best position before it reaches the bottom. Once it lands, the block turns into sand and begins to fall naturally.

Create a connected path of the same color from the left side of the board to the right side to clear that sand and earn points. Keep the sand below the danger line — if the pile reaches too high, the run ends.

Controls:
• A / D or Left / Right Arrow — move the block
• Space — fast drop
• P — pause
• R — restart
• On mobile — drag or tap to move the falling block

In Level Mode, complete the required number of bridges to finish the level and earn up to 3 stars.

## About

Sand Fall is a fast-paced puzzle arcade game inspired by classic falling-block games with a unique twist. Place colorful blocks before they hit the bottom, where they break apart and transform into flowing sand. Create connected paths of the same color from one side of the board to the other to clear the sand, earn points, complete levels, unlock bonus modes, and chase a new high score.
