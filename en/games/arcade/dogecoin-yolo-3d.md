## About

How do you like surfing on a coin? Dogecoin Yolo 3D is a skillful avoiding arcade game with 3D coins, green and red numbers. There is always a lowest marked score number. You need to collect green numbers to get more scores to complete the corresponding level. It doesn't matter if you crash red numbers. Even you lose all scores, you could still get numbers as long as you have a few distances to the finish line.
