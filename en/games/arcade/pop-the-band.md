## How to play

Pop every rubber band off the snack to clear the level!

Look at the tangle. Every band is wrapped over or under the others — only a band with nothing crossing over it can be popped.
Tap the top band to snap it off. It cuts right where you tap and flops away!
Wrong band? It wobbles and you lose a life. You get 3 mistakes per level — lose all three and the puzzle resets.
Drag to spin the snack and check every side — sneaky bands love to hide around the back.
Stuck? Use a powerup:
⚡ Zap — cut any band, even a buried one
🧲 Magnet — yanks the top band off for you
🔍 Finder — highlights the band that's safe to pop
🛡️ Shield — forgives your next mistake
Clear the level to earn coins and stars, push your Gold Quest forward, and race the bees to the finish!

## About

Pop the Band is a satisfying 3D puzzle game where colorful rubber bands are wrapped tight around delicious snacks — eggs, donuts, burgers, watermelons and 40+ more! Your job: pop them all off, one by one.

But there's a catch — a band can only snap free when nothing is crossing over it. Study the tangle, spin the snack around, and pop the bands in the right order. Tap wrong three times and the puzzle resets, so look before you snap!
