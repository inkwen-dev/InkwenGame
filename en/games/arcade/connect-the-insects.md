## About

This is a classic puzzle game, inspired by popular Chinese tile removal game, Mahjong. In this you need to eliminate all the tiles appearing on the board. You can connect identical tiles to each other to eliminate both in which each connection can't have more than 2 turns. There are 21 challenging levels in this game. Complete a level before time bounds to get extra bonus.
