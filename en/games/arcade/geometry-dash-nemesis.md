## About

Are you tired of jumping? It's time to take revenge on our little hero square man. Instead of jumping over obstacles in this new kind of geometry game, the square man destroys the obstacles with his weapon. but it is not very easy. Square man has to improve his gun. Every obstacle you hit with a gun gives you gold. square man develops his weapon with this money and destroys all obstacles.
