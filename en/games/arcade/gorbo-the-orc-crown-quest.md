## How to play

Reach the door at the end of each level. The last level of each world is a boss: beat it to open the door. Find the 3 hidden gems in every level, collect coins and spend them at the merchant. Lose all your hearts and you get back up at the last banner.

Keyboard: arrows or A D to run, Space, W or up to jump (hold to jump higher), X or J to swing the cleaver (tap fast for a 3-hit combo), C or L to throw the axe, E to drink a potion, Esc or P to pause.
Touch: arrow buttons to run, the jump, sword and axe buttons on the right, tap the potion to drink.

## About

Gorbo the Orc is a bold, funny platform adventure.
The Minotaur Warlord stole the golden crown of the Tusk Clan, and Gorbo is going to get it back, one monster at a time.

New to platformers?
The first levels teach you to run, jump, swing your cleaver, chain a 3-hit combo and throw your axe, one step at a time. Then the Whispering Forest gives way to the Crystal Mines, the Dark Castle, the Frozen Peaks, the Haunted Swamp and the Volcano Fortress.

48 levels across 6 worlds, 6 boss fights and 144 hidden gems.
Every world brings new monsters and new tricks: archers and wizards that shoot at you, shield bearers, charging bears and boars, ghosts that fly through walls, storm clouds, falling icicles, fire jets, swinging blades, slippery ice, bounce mushrooms, and locked gates to open with a key.

Fight your way. Swing a cleaver combo that also knocks arrows and spells out of the air, stomp monsters, and throw your axe, which flies back to you like a boomerang. Drink a potion when your hearts run low, and spend your coins at the merchant on potions and extra hearts.

Your progress, gems and coins are saved, so you can come back and finish the quest anytime.
