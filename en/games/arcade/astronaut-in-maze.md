## How to play

Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

This is a simple but challenging puzzle game. You'll need to get the astronaut's ship safely to his station. Finding a way home for him can be tricky as there are obstacles to avoid and plenty of ways to get lost. Getting his ship to the station with the least amount of moves will give you a better score. Be careful because if the ship flies off the screen he'll be lost forever and you'll have to restart the level. See if you can complete all 24 levels to win the game.
