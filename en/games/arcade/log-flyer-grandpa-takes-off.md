## How to play

Press Let's Fly to launch. On desktop, hold W or Up Arrow to raise the nose, S or Down Arrow to lower it, and Space to run the installed engine. Release Space to glide and save fuel. On mobile, hold the round up and down buttons on the left to steer, and hold the engine button on the right for thrust. Earn coins after each flight, buy and equip upgrades in the workshop, and fly farther. Reach the village at 6 km, then continue toward a new distance record. An optional video reward in the workshop grants 300 coins only after a completed ad. Open Settings for sound, language, and control instructions.

## About

Build a flying log and send Grandpa soaring across a snowy mountain valley! Launch from a ramp, balance your flight, and earn coins to upgrade the nose cone, wings, engine, fuel tank, and ramp. Complete permanent missions, unlock colorful paint jobs and pilot titles, meet a startled eagle, and reach the village to earn a cheerful piglet copilot. The village is not the finish line: keep flying and chase your best single-flight distance on the SCORE leaderboard. Includes English and Russian text and voice acting.
