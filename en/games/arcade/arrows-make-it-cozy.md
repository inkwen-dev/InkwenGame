## How to play

Tap an arrow — it will slide straight in the direction it points. If the path is clear, the arrow escapes. If another arrow is in the way — they collide and you lose a life. You have 3 lives ❤️ per level and limited time ⏱️. Free all the arrows before you run out!

On PC: click on an arrow with the mouse.
On mobile: tap the arrow with your finger.

Boosters:
1. 💡 Hint — highlights the path of one arrow that can safely leave the field.
2. 🗺️ Compass — reveals all arrow paths on the level until it's completed.

Chain combos! 🔥 Send arrows off in a row without collisions and within 10 seconds between moves — combo points go to the leaderboard.
