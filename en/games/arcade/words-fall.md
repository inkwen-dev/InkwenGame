## About

Words Fall is an exhilarating word puzzle game that challenges your vocabulary skills! As letters rain from the sky, swiftly create words to clear them before they stack up. With each level, the pace quickens, and your word-building abilities are put to the test. Unlock power-ups and bonuses to gain an edge, and compete with friends for the highest score on the global leaderboard. Immerse yourself in this addictive wordplay experience, combining speed and strategy. Words Fall offers endless word-building fun for players of all ages. Can you rise to the challenge and conquer the downpour of letters?
