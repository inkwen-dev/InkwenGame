## How to play

Enemies march toward your castle wall. Stop them before it falls.

Press on an enemy, drag to lift it, then flick and let go to throw it. Harder throws hit harder when they land. Quickly tap an enemy to swat it, even heavy ones. Flick a body sideways to bowl over a whole line, or lift one high and flick down for a ground slam. Swipe fast across empty ground to sweep enemies off their feet.

Tap a weapon button, then tap the field to fire. Tap traps marked "TAP!" and grab dropped power-ups. Throw enemies into the moat, lava or quicksand for bonus gold.
Between days, spend gold in the Keep on upgrades, defenders and abilities.

Works with mouse or touch.

## About

The horde is coming, and the only thing standing between them and your castle is your hand.

Siege Clash is a physics castle defense game where you grab invaders and fling them into 
the sky, into each other, into the moat or into traps. Bowl them down like pins, slam them into the ground, fire your wall's Ballista and Mangonel, and spring trapdoors, spiked rollers and tesla coils at the right moment.
Battle across 50 days and five worlds, beat five bosses, and finish the job by hurling the smug Baron Grimmholt into a volcano. Between fights, upgrade your castle, recruit defenders and unlock abilities.

Easy to pick up, tough to master. The horde is at the gate. Show them who holds the wall.
