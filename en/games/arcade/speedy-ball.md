## About

It's time to compete against both obstacles and time! Your reflexes must be ready at any time in the Speedy Ball game. Control the ball in the going direction and stay away from the gaps! There are also roads with certain colors, green speeds your ball up, red slows it down, and yellow makes it jump! Platforms are getting harder at every level! Come on, move the ball now and complete all levels!
