## About

It’s endless type game, you need move the board to hit ball, breaking bricks. Do not let the bricks touch ground and board. Every time speed increase, keep your mind. How to play: Use left and right arrow keys to move the board. Press space to shoot the ball or Click mouse left button to play the game.
