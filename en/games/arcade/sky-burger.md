## About

Sky Burger is fun addictive hyper casual game.In this game, we have lots of ingredients that are falling from sky. In each level, there is certain number of missions that you need to pass to advance to the next level. In each level, you are required to collect a certain amount of each ingredient . You can move your base object left and right to collect falling ingredients.
