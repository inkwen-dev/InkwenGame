## About

Strategize your taps and unleash chain reactions in Microbes! The play area is swarming with microbes&mdash;some can shoot, others can&rsquo;t. Your goal is to touch the right microbes in the right order, triggering shots that wipe out all the non-shooters. Plan your moves carefully, because one wrong touch could break the chain! Think smart, tap wisely, and cleanse the petri dish in this infectious puzzle challenge!
