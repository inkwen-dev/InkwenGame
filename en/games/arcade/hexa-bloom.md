## How to play

Drag a stack from the tray at the bottom onto any empty hexagon (or tap a stack, then tap a cell). When the top colors of neighbouring stacks match, the tiles slide together automatically, which can trigger whole chain reactions. Collect 10 tiles of one color on a stack to make them bloom and clear. Reach the level goal shown at the top before the board fills up. Use boosters when stuck: Hammer smashes a stack, Shuffle gives new tray stacks, Undo takes back your last move. Works with mouse and touch.

## About

Hexa Bloom is a relaxing, oddly satisfying hexagon stack-sorting puzzle. Drop colorful tile stacks onto a honeycomb board and watch matching colors flip across in glossy, chiming cascades. Gather ten of a color and the stack bursts into a bloom of petals! Travel through 60 handcrafted levels across three dreamy gardens (Cherry Blossom, Ocean Lagoon and Desert Dusk), each adding new twists like stones, locked cells and single-color goals. Plan chain reactions, earn up to three stars per level, unlock helpful boosters (Hammer, Shuffle, Undo), collect beautiful tile styles, claim daily gifts, keep your Daily Bloom streak alive and chase high scores in Endless mode.
