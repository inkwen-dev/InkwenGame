## About

You must catch animals. Don’t hit the bombs and fire. Bonus system. Collect diamond star to buy skins. You will be immersed in the incredible atmosphere of the cosines. There are 5 types of animals to catch. Game has a shop, available 7 skins for box: standard, violet, black, lama, bear, panda, unicorn . Every time increasing speed. Enjoy advance, keep your mind!
