## About

Slice your veggies to make a bowl of soup! In Perfect Slices Master, you will manipulate a knife to cut your veggies into small pieces. Careful as you chop along your way for chopping boards and other sharp boards will appear to numb your knife! Earn coins while slicing! Visit the shop for more kinds of knives to use! Enjoy the satisfying shopping experience and watch your chopped veggies turn into a nice bowl of soup!
