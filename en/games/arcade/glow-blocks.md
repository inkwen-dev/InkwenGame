## About

Glow Blocks is a vibrant neon puzzle game inspired by the timeless classic Tetris. Stack glowing blocks in a futuristic grid, clear full lines, and climb through endless levels as the speed increases. With its colorful neon visuals, cyberpunk atmosphere, and smooth gameplay, Glow Blocks is both nostalgic and fresh. Challenge yourself to beat your high score, enjoy the glowing effects, and test your reflexes in this addictive endless puzzle experience!
