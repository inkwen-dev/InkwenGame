## About

Hello! An exciting adventure awaits you where you need to escape sharp spikes rising from the ground. The spikes are coming from below, and you must constantly climb higher without stopping. Reach the highest point either alone or with your friend. Be careful of the spikes coming from below. Collect all the coins on the way and buy yourself different costumes.
