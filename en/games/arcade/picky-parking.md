## How to play

Drag a car to a reachable square, or click/tap a car and then its destination. Each square traveled uses one fuel. To solve a puzzle, use all the cars' fuel, leave exactly one car in every row and column, and keep all cars apart, including diagonally. Cars marked with a lock cannot move.

On desktop, use number keys to select cars and arrow keys or WASD to move. Z undoes a move, R resets the puzzle, H requests a hint, and E opens Settings. On touch devices, use the on-screen controls. Returning a car to its start also rewinds all moves made after that car's first trip. After the free hints are used, watching a video for another hint is optional; you can always keep playing without it.

## About

Picky Parking is a relaxing logic puzzle where every move uses precious fuel. Guide colorful toy cars into a perfect parking arrangement: use all their fuel, place one car in each row and column, and keep cars from touching—even at the corners. Learn the rules through five quick interactive lessons, then tackle Easy, Normal, and Hard puzzles. Undo moves, return a car to its starting point, or use a hint when you need a little help.
