## About

Are you good at shooting targets with a sniper rifle? Sniper Champion 3D is a first-person shooting skillful arcade game. The objective is to win over 1000 scores in sixty seconds at all levels. The more scores you won, the more stars you are able to win finally. The targets will arise randomly. So you need to aim fast and shoot the yellow areas for the most 500 scores. Don't worry about ammo and shooting speed, keep calm and concentrate on your quick aiming!
