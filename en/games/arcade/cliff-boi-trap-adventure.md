## About

Cliff Boi: Fail = Try Again! is a hilarious and addictive 2D cartoon side-scrolling platformer and trap adventure game! Guide a cute chibi boy with round glasses across 25 action-packed stages filled with unexpected surprises, collapsing bridges, and funny traps. Run forward across towering canyon cliffs, leap over wide chasms, and grab onto cliff ledges to climb your way to safety! Just when you think the path to the GOAL sign is clear, watch out for sudden pop-up floor spikes, spring-loaded boxing gloves, swinging spiked wrecking balls, 100-ton falling sky anvils, speeding soccer balls, sneaky rock beetles, and lightning storm clouds!
