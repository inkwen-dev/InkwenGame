## About

Jump Hero is a fast-paced reaction game where timing is everything! Take control of a fearless ninja hero as he leaps between platforms in a futuristic dojo. The objective is to jump as far as possible without falling the farther you go, the higher your score. Modes: - Endless Run Mode (score-based) - Speed Increases with Progress Characters: - Main Character: Shadow Ninja with customizable jump animation Levels: - Procedural level design &ndash; every run is unique - Dynamic background transitions based on distance
