## About

This online emoji-matching game can help improve your mood by providing a fun and easy way to connect with others. The game is simple - just match the emoji with the corresponding emotion. If you're feeling sad, try matching the sad emoji with a happy one. Or, if you're feeling happy, try matching the happy emoji with a sad one.
