## About

Catch The Cat is a game where you need to punch the cats that are showing from the wholes. But it's all cats that pop up from the holes and do that only with the cats. If you miss some cat to punch, you will lose your life. The cats will show at different places, and sometimes at more holes at once. Be fast and hit more cats as you can before your life is finished.
