## How to play

Control a fragile egg and guide it through challenging platforms by balancing, tilting, and jumping your way upward.

Use movement and momentum carefully to overcome obstacles and avoid falling or breaking your shell. Precision and timing are key to mastering each level.

Controls:

• W / A / S / D — Move the egg forward, backward, left, and right
• Space — Jump
• P — Pause the game

Use tilt and inertia to find the perfect angle for jumps, maintain balance on difficult surfaces, and carefully control your movement to reach the top.

## About

A fragile egg begins a dangerous journey toward the sky. The world around you is unstable — steep slopes, sharp edges, and tricky obstacles that can crack your shell at any moment.

Master the physics of movement as you balance, tilt, and jump your way upward. Each level introduces new challenges, from simple inclines to complex platforms that require precision, timing, and steady control.

Unlock a variety of fun and epic egg skins as you progress and turn your climb into a unique adventure. With realistic physics, challenging gameplay, and a colorful visual style, every move matters.

Can you keep your balance, climb higher and higher, and reach the stars without cracking?
