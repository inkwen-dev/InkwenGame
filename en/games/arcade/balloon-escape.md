## About

Balloon Escape is a fun endless arcade adventure where you control a hot air balloon and fly higher through the sky while avoiding dangerous obstacles. Collect coins and gems to unlock new balloon skins, beat your high score, and challenge yourself to survive as long as possible. The higher you fly, the more difficult the journey becomes. Enjoy smooth controls, colorful visuals, and endless gameplay designed for both desktop and mobile devices.
