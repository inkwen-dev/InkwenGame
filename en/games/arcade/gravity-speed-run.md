## About

Gravity Speed Run is a fast-paced platformer where you control a mini character, switching gravity to navigate through obstacle-filled levels. Tap to run on the top or bottom of the track, avoiding deadly spikes and traps—one hit, and you restart. Each level gets harder with more obstacles and increased speed. Earn coins by completing levels and unlock new skins for your robot avatar. Test your reflexes and conquer the challenge!
