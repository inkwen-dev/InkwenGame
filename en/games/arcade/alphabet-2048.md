Use mouse keyboard or touch to play this game

## About

Merge identical letter blocks by moving them in any direction with the help of arrow keys or swiping your finger on the screen or swiping mouse pointer. Each time you merge two blocks you will get a next letter. Just give a try to see what level you can reach? You can shuffle the board or regroup the blocks for limited number of times or with rewarded videos.
