## About

Wind Traveler is an immersive adventure game set in a vibrant, fantastical world where players harness the power of the elements to traverse landscapes and solve puzzles. With stunning visuals and intuitive controls, embark on a journey as the titular Wind Traveler, mastering the art of wind manipulation to overcome obstacles and discover hidden secrets. Unravel the mysteries of ancient ruins, encounter diverse creatures, and uncover the truth behind the fading winds. Your destiny awaits in this captivating tale of exploration and magic.
