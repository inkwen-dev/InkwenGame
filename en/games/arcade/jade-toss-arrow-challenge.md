## How to play

Hit the Jade Pot! Step into a quiet temple courtyard and play pitch-pot, the ancient East Asian game of tossing arrows into a jar. Each hit scores points and keeps your lives; each miss costs one of your 5 lives. Chain 3 hits in a row to enter the zone and double your points, land in the centre of the mouth for a Perfect bonus, and keep going as the pot moves farther away and the wind picks up. Beat your best score, clear the challenges and take on the daily challenge!

Desktop Controls:
Left Mouse Button (hold on the arrow): Grab the arrow.
Drag Down: Set the throw power. A longer pull throws farther.
Drag Left / Right: Aim to the side to fight the wind.
Release Left Mouse Button: Throw the arrow.
Click: Start the next throw.
Speaker Icon (top bar): Turn sound on or off.
Menu Icon (top bar): Pause, challenges and settings.

Mobile Controls:
Touch the Arrow: Grab the arrow.
Drag Down: Set the throw power. A longer pull throws farther.
Drag Left / Right: Aim to the side to fight the wind.
Lift Your Finger: Throw the arrow.
Tap: Start the next throw.

## About

Jade Toss: Arrow Challenge is a relaxing 3D skill game based on pitch-pot, the ancient East Asian game of tossing arrows into a jar.

Pull back the arrow, aim and let it fly into the jade pot. As you score, the pot moves farther away, the wind picks up and a small bonus pot appears. Chain hits to enter the zone and double your points.

Simple one-touch controls, calm courtyards from Vietnamese, Chinese, Korean and Japanese tradition, and new arrows, pots and scenes to unlock. Take the daily challenge and beat your best score!
