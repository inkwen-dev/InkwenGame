## How to play

Tilt the deck to roll your core into the glowing exit gate. Collect coins and activate beacons to set checkpoints. Watch each tile's pattern: pink tiles disappear, glass breaks after you leave, ice reduces grip, and crosswinds push sideways. Clear bays to bank collected coins plus a completion bonus, then spend your coins on new cores in the Core locker.

Desktop: use WASD or the arrow keys to steer. Press P or Escape to pause and resume, or R to restart during play. You can also drag the on-screen stick with a mouse.

Mobile: hold and drag the on-screen stick. Use its corner switch to swap to directional arrows. Open Controls to change pad side or choose Drag or Motion steering. Motion controls require device support and permission. Play in landscape orientation.
