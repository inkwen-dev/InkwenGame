## How to play

Controls
Mouse: Hold the left mouse button and drag backward to aim and set the launch power. Release to send the sponge forward. Click again when the sponge slows down to stop it and prepare the next launch.

Mobile: Touch and drag backward to aim, then release to launch the sponge. Tap it when it slows down to stop and aim again.

## About

Sponge Strike: Clean & Smash is a satisfying physics puzzle game where every launch brings a hidden picture to life. Pull back, aim, and send your sponge sliding across the board to remove the dark coating underneath it. Bounce off walls, smash colorful block towers, spin up moving obstacles, trigger bombs and buttons, and collect coins along the way. Clear every objective, reveal the full picture, and use your rewards to unlock new sponge skins.
