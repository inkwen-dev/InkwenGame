## About

In this adventure, you need to collect the red and yellow keys to find out if its heaven or hell. You must be very careful as there are obstacles everywhere. Both the obby and noob must be extremely cautious, or you might die. Collect the yellow and red keys without dying and get back to the door before time runs out. You need to be quick. Dont forget, you must go everywhere together. If you get separated, youll get lost on the camera.
