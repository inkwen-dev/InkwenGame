## About

In Tangled Knots, players are immersed in a captivating puzzle adventure where they must untangle complex rope configurations within limited moves to free the ropes ends. With mesmerizing visuals, intuitive mechanics, and brain-teasing challenges, the game offers a satisfying quest for players to showcase their strategic thinking and become the master untanglers. The goal of the game is to untangle the knotted ropes and free the ropes ends by manipulating and rearranging the knots with precise moves. Players must strategically plan their actions to achieve this within a set number of moves.
