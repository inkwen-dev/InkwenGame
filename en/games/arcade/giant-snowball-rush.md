Tap and hold to move

## About

Push a snowball for a racing, and finally reach the finish line with larger and larger snowballs. In the process, players need to flexibly avoid various obstacles and collect gold coins and props as much as possible. When you keep pushing the snowball, the snowball will roll bigger and bigger. The disadvantage of the big snowball is that it is more difficult to control.
