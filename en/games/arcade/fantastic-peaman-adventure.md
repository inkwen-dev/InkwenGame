## About

Get ready for a brand new adventure with Peaman. With renewed enemies, different characters, new sounds and new chapters, Fantastic Peaman Adventure is with you. Collect peanuts on maps to fight enemies and destroy them by throwing peanuts at enemies. Complete 30 different missions in 3 different worlds and complete the adventure. With the gold you earn, you can buy new characters from the market or buy potions for new life. Visit meyagames.com for this legendary game and more.
