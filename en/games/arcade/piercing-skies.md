## About

Dodge, jump, and survive under a sky of danger in Piercing Skies! Arrows rain down from above as you leap between three narrow platforms, timing each jump to avoid getting struck. Quick reflexes and sharp focus are your only defense in this fast-paced survival challenge. Every second you last adds to your score&mdash;but one mistake, and its game over. Can you outlast the storm and master the art of survival beneath the piercing skies?
