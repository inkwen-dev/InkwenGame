## About

Go Up is a simple but challenging hyper casual arcade game where timing and focus are the key to victory. Control a small box and guide it upward toward the winning line. Each tap changes the direction of the box. The first tap moves the box to the left, the next tap moves it to the right, creating a zigzag movement pattern. The path is filled with obstacles on both sides, so the player must carefully time each tap to avoid collisions. Reach the winning line to complete the level and move on to the next challenge.
