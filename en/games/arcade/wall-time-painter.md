## About

Prepare to embark on an artistic journey like no other with Wall Time Painter. This captivating mobile game combines creativity and strategy in a unique way. As a time-traveling artist, your mission is to restore historical murals by accurately recreating them on the digital canvas. With a wide range of eras to explore and intricate details to replicate, Wall Time Painter offers a delightful blend of history and artistry.
