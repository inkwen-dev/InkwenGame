## About

Ball Sort Puzzle is a fun and addictive puzzle game! Try to sort the colored balls in the tubes until all balls with the same color stay in the same tube. A challenging yet relaxing game to exercise your brain! ★ HOW TO PLAY: • Tap any tube to move the ball lie on top in the tube to another tube. • The rule is that you can only move a ball on top of another ball if both of them have the same color and the tube you want to move into has enough space. • Try not to get stuck - but don’t worry, you can always restart the level at any time. ★ FEATURES: • One finger control. • FREE & EASY TO PLAY. • NO penalty & time limit; you can enjoy Ball Sort Puzzle at your own pace!
