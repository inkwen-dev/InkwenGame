## How to play

Movement & Attack:

- PC: A / D, ← / →, or on-screen virtual left/right buttons.

- Mobile: on-screen virtual left/right buttons.

Skills: tap the corresponding on-screen skill buttons (cooldowns and energy depend on the hero/perks).

Progression: collect coins, complete quests, upgrade skills, and unlock new heroes.

## About

Only Jump RPG is a fast-paced action runner with RPG progression: one road, one jump, thousands of decisions. Jump left/right, strike, use skills, and dodge traps and enemy attacks. Unlock heroes, collect items, and power up your skills.

Key Features:

- Minimal buttons — maximum action: a jump = attack + movement.

- RPG progression: upgrade unique hero perks.

- Dangerous patterns: spikes, chasms, ghosts, and tight timing.

- Quick runs: short sessions with a growing skill ceiling and depth.

- Hero collection: unlock characters with distinct abilities.
