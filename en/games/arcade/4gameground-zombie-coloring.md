## About

Coloring zombies and skeletons is a free online game from the genre of memory games and monsters. Flip the tiles and try to match them in pairs. Connect all the tiles in pairs to win. Try to complete the game in as few moves as possible! There are 4 levels. Use the mouse to click or tap the screen on the squares. Concentrate and start playing. Enjoy it!
