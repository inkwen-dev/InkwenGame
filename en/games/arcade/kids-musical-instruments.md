## About

This engaging game is designed to spark creativity and musical interest in young minds. With a variety of 9 different musical instruments, children can explore and enjoy the unique sounds of each instrument, making learning both fun and educational. Children can tap on the instruments to hear their notes, fostering an interactive and immersive learning experience. This hands-on approach helps kids to better understand and appreciate music. The game includes a Piano, Cat Piano, Pan Flute, Harp, Xylophone, Flute, Guitar, Saxophone, and Platillo, each with its distinct sound. This variety ensures that children can explore different musical genres and styles.
