## About

Find 5 Differences is an online game that challenges players to spot five differences between two seemingly identical images. The game is designed to test and improve visual perception, concentration, and attention to detail. This game features various images of different rooms in a house, such as the living room, kitchen, and bedroom. Each image has been carefully crafted to make it difficult to spot the differences, ensuring that players are fully engaged and challenged. To play the game, players simply need to examine the two images side-by-side and click on the areas where they spot a difference. The game keeps track of the number of differences found and the time taken to complete the task. Have fun playing.
