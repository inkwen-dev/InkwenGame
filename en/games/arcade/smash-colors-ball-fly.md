## About

Fascinating arcade game Smash Colors. A game with gravity. To prevent the ball from falling down, it is necessary to tap and support its flight. You need to overcome the walls. The wall can only be passed in the sector where the color matches the color of the ball. After passing each wall, the color of the ball changes.
