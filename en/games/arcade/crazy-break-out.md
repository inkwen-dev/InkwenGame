## About

Everybody knows about the classic break-out game. And now it is time for Crazy Break-Out. In Crazy Break-Out, instead of breaking bricks using the call, the player breaks the balls free. And the more free balls means the more craziness. You can use all the balls while they are bouncing around. Can you use having many balls to your advantage? Good luck ;)
