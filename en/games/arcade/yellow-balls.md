## How to play

Use mouse or tap on the screen

## About

The simple yet dynamic Yellow Balls game will test your ability to respond quickly to changing circumstances. At the bottom of the screen is a yellow dot, if you click on it, shooting will begin. The balls will pop up and move up in a straight line to reach the target located at the top. In the upper left corner you will see a number. After you make a mistake three times the game starts again.
