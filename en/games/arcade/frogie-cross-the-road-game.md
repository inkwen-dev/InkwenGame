## About

“Frogie Cross the Road” is a fun game where you have to cross roads, rivers and countryside. Help the frog “Frogie” to reach the end of each level and beware of cars, wild boars and rivers. You must dodge the cars and wild boars so you don’t get hit and you must also jump over the pieces of wood on top of the river so you don’t fall into it. Try to reach the end of each level before the time runs out. Have a good time!
