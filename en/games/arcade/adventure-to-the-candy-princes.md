## About

In this adventure, we have taken the kings crown and must rescue the Sugar Princess. The Sugar Princess is held captive in a dungeon in the Ice Kingdom. To rescue her, collect all the candies in the Candy Forest and free the Sugar Princess. Help the Sugar Princess on her way to the Ice Kingdom. Activate the portals by collecting all the candies. Beware, there are enemies in the Ice Kingdom and they are quite strong. It is very difficult to defeat them, so avoid and escape from them.
