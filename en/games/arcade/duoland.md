## About

Two siblings are on their way to becoming little pirates, and you should join the adventure too. The little pirates are searching for chests and collecting treasures in my name. Each sibling is looking for different gems and chests on the island, so they must sequentially find the chests that belong to them. The first rule of being a pirate is to collect everything on the island, including all the coins. After collecting everything, a hidden pirate ship will suddenly appear in the top right corner of the island. Get on that ship and escape from the island.
