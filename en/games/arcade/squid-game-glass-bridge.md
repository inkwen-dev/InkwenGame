## About

Your goal in Squid Game Glass Brigde game developed by meyagames.com team is to reach across by choosing the right glasses. Since one of the two glasses on the platform is produced using thin glass, it cannot bear the weight. This is why you will fall. You have to look carefully to find the difference between the 2 glasses. If you want, you can change your ability to understand the difference between the two glasses by selecting the difficulty level from the main menu. In addition, if you do not reach the other side within the specified time, the game is over. For more games, please visit meyagames.com.
