## About

Jelly Shift up and down to change its form. Make sure to shape the jelly correctly so it can fit through the obstacles. Swipe up and down to change shape reach destination with jelly. You must slide your finger up and down to change the shape of the object and match it to the shape of the bridge. However, if you touch one of the obstacles, you will die.
