## How to play

On a smartphone - control the hero move the joystick on the left side of the screen - attack move the joystick to the right side of the screen On computer - W move up - A move left - S move down - D move to the right - Attack L

## About

It's a wild pixel action about a bunch of dudes against a bunch of zombies. No quests on multiple levels, no unnecessary storylines - just slash and bang, guns and crowds of undead. Overpower enemies, collect coins and unlock one of 9 heroes: pyromaniac, knife master, dude with a AK, miner, dude with a stick, dude with a saw and more.
