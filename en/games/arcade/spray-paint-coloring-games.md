## How to play

🖌️ Choose a painting tool — spray paint, paint roller, or spray gun.
🎨 Use the selected tool to paint the highlighted area.
🔄 Complete each layer one by one to gradually reveal the hidden picture.
🎯 Stay inside the target area and try to cover it completely.
🧩 Choose the right tool for each layer to paint efficiently.
✨ Finish all layers to reveal the final artwork and complete the level!
