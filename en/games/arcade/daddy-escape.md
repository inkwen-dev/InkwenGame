## How to play

Your goal is to safely navigate Daddy through each level to the exit door. 
But beware, there are pins blocking his way!
Tap and pull the pins in the right order to clear a path for Daddy.
Use your cunning to avoid traps, outsmart enemies, and collect valuable treasures along the way.

Get ready for an Epic Puzzle adventure like no other! Daddy Escape - Pull the Pin is here to challenge your wits, strategic thinking, and problem-solving skills. Help Daddy make his daring escape by pulling the right pins and overcoming a series of mind-bending obstacles.
