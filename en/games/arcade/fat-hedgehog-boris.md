## How to play

Move left and right using the arrow keys or the on-screen controls. Press Space to jump. Press Space again while in the air to perform a downward attack. Defeat enemies from above, break certain blocks, collect coins, avoid traps, and reach the end of each level. Taking damage makes Boris fatter and harder to control.
