## How to play

Slide the Bricks

Swipe or click-and-drag any brick horizontally to the left or right.

Bricks fall automatically if there is empty space beneath them.

Fill the Gaps

Position falling blocks into empty slots to complete a solid, unbroken horizontal row.

Clear Lines & Score

A full row instantly clears, awarding points and lowering the board.

Clear multiple rows with a single move to trigger combos for bonus points.

Watch the Rising Board

Every time you make a move that doesn't clear a line, a new row of bricks pushes up from the bottom.

Avoid the Top Edge

The game ends immediately if the brick stack reaches the very top row. Keep the board low to stay alive.

## About

Brick Slide is an engaging block falling puzzle game that tests your spatial logic. Slide colored brick pieces horizontally to fill empty gaps and clear entire horizontal lines.
