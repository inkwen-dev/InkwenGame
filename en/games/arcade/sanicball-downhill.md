## About

It's time to go fast... Really fast!! Today you will experience what it's like to be the one and only Sanic. In this endless rolling game you will put your skills to the test by going all the way down in a city filled skyscrapers where only the masters of players can survive. But be aware of the platforms as they get harder after each level. Do you think you can make it? Also don't forget to pick up boost pads to make the jumps and avoid obstacles along the course at all costs. Collect many rings to buy cool new characters too! Good luck Sanic, gotta go fast!
