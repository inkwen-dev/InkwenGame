## About

Snow Ball Run is a fresh and an original game that makes you experience the best adventure run till the very end. This is a brand-new game in the play store with tons of engaging levels and challenging opponents. Your goal is simple – compete against other opponents by running as fast as you can and collect the snowballs on your run to increase the size and grow bigger than your opponent. Shoot the roadblocks on your way to overcome the obstacles and use the weapons to freeze your opponent to stay ahead in the race. Boost your powers by winning as many levels as possible.
