## About

Obstacube is an exhilarating endless runner that challenges your reflexes and strategy as you navigate through an ever-changing world of obstacles. Your primary goal is to survive for as long as possible while dodging a variety of hazards that increase in difficulty the further you progress. Collect coins strategically placed along your path to buy skins or trails in the shop!
