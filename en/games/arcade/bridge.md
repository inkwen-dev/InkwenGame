## How to play

Goal: Build a bridge just long enough to reach the next pillar. If the bridge is too short or too long, it will tumble into the abyss! Aim for the red center dot for bonus shards and combo multipliers.

Controls:

Mobile & Tablet: Tap and hold anywhere on the screen to extend the bridge 
release to drop it.
Desktop / PC: Click and hold the Left Mouse Button (LMB) or Spacebar to grow the bridge 
release to lower it.
Pro Tips:

Landing in the exact red center awards double points and bonus crystals.
On crumbling towers, don't hesitate — the platform will collapse if you wait too long!
Use your Second Chance (Revive) once per run to save a record-breaking streak.

## About

BRIDGE is a stylish, minimalist physics platformer and timing runner where precision and composure are everything.

Build bridges of exact lengths to leap across towering obsidian monoliths suspended over a bottomless chasm. Time your releases perfectly to hit the ruby sweet spots in the center of platforms, trigger satisfying Perfect-combos, and rack up high scores!

Key Features:

Clutch Edge-Grab Mechanic: Made a near miss? Catch the crumbling ledge by your fingertips and pull yourself up in the nick of time.
Dynamic Obstacles: Face moving monoliths, varying elevations, and crumbling towers that collapse into the void.
2 Game Modes: Endless High-Score Runner and a handcrafted 15-stage Campaign across 3 unique worlds.
Metagame & Wardrobe: Collect crystal shards during runs to unlock and equip unique cube skins (Shinobi, Ronin, Cyber, Oni).
Organic Soundscape: Pure, tactile procedural acoustic audio with resonant marimba notes and crisp wood transients.
