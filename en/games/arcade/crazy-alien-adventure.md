## About

Crazy Alien Adventure is an addictive and exciting game. You will play as a brave character who runs, jumps, and dashes through various worlds, collecting stars and power-ups along the way. Your goal is to rescue the animals that are trapped in cages by smashing the wooden stakes with your rush power. The more you run, the more points you get. You can also use the stars to buy items and outfits that will enhance your gameplay. Discover different kinds of animals in the game and try to save them all. How far can you go in this amazing endless jumping game?
