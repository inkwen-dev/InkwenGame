## About

In the course of this fun and exciting game, you, and your little ones, will be transferred to the wonderful world of summer holidays and real trips. So get in your car and go on a hilarious picnic in nature. The whole game consists of various mini-games in which you have to perform various exciting tasks. At the very beginning you and your kids will need to collect all the things that are most necessary for the campaign, to immerse them and to take off on the car. On the road, you will have many tests and a real car accident, after which you will need to repair the car, repair the wheels and engine. When you arrive in the tent city - the most interesting will begin. You will find a dismantled tent, which you will need to collect correctly and a picnic with many sweets, the fastest boat on which you will race along the river, avoiding various obstacles and fishing, kites, hide-and-seek with friends and much more.
