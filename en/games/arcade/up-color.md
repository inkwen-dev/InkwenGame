## About

A this kids arcade game your job is to control the player and to pass through bricks with different colors. There is three different colored bricks. First when you pass the brick the player with change the color and at the next wall with bricks you need to pass only at the brick with same color as the player. It is interesting and funny to play. Control the player with mouse or slide on the screen to play.
