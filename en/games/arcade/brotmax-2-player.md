## How to play

The red player moves using the WASD keys The blue player moves using the arrow keys Retrieve the red and blue keys and reach the finish line

## About

In the second game of the series, the adventure is more challenging, and the cube monster wants to eat you and your friend. The cube monster now has allies. You and your friend need to escape. Grab the key with your friend and head to your friends waiting at the finish line. Your friends are waiting for you at the finish line. Be careful of obstacles. There are obstacles and dangerous traps everywhere. Try not to die with your friend.
