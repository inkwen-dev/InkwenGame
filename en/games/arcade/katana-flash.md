## How to play

The aim line spins on its own. Tap the screen, click or press Space when the line crosses enemies: your ninja dashes along it and cuts everyone in the way. Dash into arrows to send them back at your enemies. Clear every wave to finish a stage; beat the boss every 10th stage. P or the pause button pauses. On phones and tablets, hold the device sideways.

## About

Katana Flash is a one-button ninja action game drawn in ink on a sunset sky. Your aim line turns by itself; you only choose when to tap. Tap at the right moment and your ninja dashes along the line, cutting every enemy in the way. Road to the Shogun: 100 stages across 10 regions and 10 bosses. A new enemy in every region: archers whose arrows you knock back, shield bearers you cut from behind, bombers, ghosts, chained twins and more. Rebuild your ninja village (5 buildings, 10 levels each), forge 10 katanas that change your dash (wall bounce, double dash, slow time, ink trail), unlock 8 costumes, earn up to 3 stars per stage, play the daily challenge and endless waves in the Dojo.
