## About

n this game you will find 12 different images to have fun with Masha and the Bear. Two identical images will appear in each game, but they are not identical because there are 5 differences that are almost imperceptible. Pay close attention and find the 5 differences! Will you be able to find them all? In this game, children should pay attention to identify the differences on the images. It can be a change in color, in size, objects that are added to the pictures... You have to have a good eye to find all the differences!
