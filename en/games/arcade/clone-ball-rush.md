## About

Clone Ball Rush is both a skillful avoiding and a collecting ball arcade game with 3D models and platform tracks. Green walls will enlarge or increase your current balls. Red walls will reduce or decrease your balls. Try to complete the level by means of helping at least one ball pass the finish flag. Good luck and have fun!
