## How to play

The hero runs automatically and turns around at edges.

Desktop Controls:

↑ Up Arrow / Spacebar → Jump

Mobile Controls:

Tap screen → Jump

Objective: Collect all the bags in the level. Once collected, the door opens.

Reach the door to complete the rescue mission and advance to the next level.

Avoid enemies and traps—touching them ends your run!

## About

Hop To Rescue is an addictive platformer adventure by NapTech Games where every jump counts! Guide your hero across tricky platforms, dodge enemies, and collect all the bags to unlock the exit door. Each level brings new challenges that test your timing, reflexes, and rescue skills. Play anywhere, anytime, and enjoy smooth platform gameplay designed for endless fun.
