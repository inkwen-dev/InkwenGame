## About

Rotating Bones 3D is a 3D puzzle platform game where you control Mr Bones, a rolling skull trapped in a floating ancient world of ruins, traps and magical structures. Instead of jumping, you rotate the environment to guide the skull through increasingly complex levels. Collect golden stars, solve physics-based puzzles and avoid spikes and hazards. Each rotation changes gravity and opens new paths. A fun, colorful and cinematic experience with a unique rotation-based gameplay mechanic.
