## About

Stairway Sprint is an exhilarating mobile game that puts players in control of a bouncy ball on a mission to ascend a series of challenging staircases. As you guide the ball upwards, youll need to deftly navigate a maze of obstacles while collecting valuable items along the way. With intuitive tilt controls and engaging gameplay, Stairway Sprint offers an addictive experience that tests your reflexes and precision. Can you conquer the stairs, avoid pitfalls, and become the ultimate sprinter in this thrilling journey to the top?
