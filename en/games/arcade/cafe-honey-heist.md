## About

Fly through a warm, bustling caf&eacute;, sip delicious nectar from coffee cups and treats resting on tables, and carry your loot to the hive outside. But stay on your toes&mdash;the giant caf&eacute; owner is always listening! Buzzing too loud will alert him, sending him on a hunt to swat you right out of the sky. Land on table surfaces to rest, lower your noise level, and plan your next move. Collect enough nectar, unlock powerful abilities, and complete 5 hive deposits to win!
