## About

This is a wonderful coloring book for young children. It is worth paying attention to the easel, it already has a canvas on which Dora and the Slipper are depicted. Unfortunately, the artist did not have time to finish this picture, so we will help him with this. Using a brush and a palette of colors, we can paint over all the white areas of the drawing in any colors that our imagination can come up with. Using a brush, you need to grab the desired color from the palette and move it to the desired area of the drawing. For a few minutes, the drawing will come to life and fill with bright colors, and all this thanks to your help.
