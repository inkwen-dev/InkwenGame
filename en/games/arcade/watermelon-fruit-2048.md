## About

The game is a very simple but addictive game where you have to touch 2 identical objects together to make them explode and go to the next level. Click or drag and drop fruits anywhere on the screen to drop them on top of the fruit of the same type and earn points. 2 fruits of the same type merge and turn into a bigger fruit in the position where they collide, so you can blast the watermelon, the biggest fruit, and earn 1000 points
