## About

Neon Collider brings the legendary brick breaker gameplay into a stunning futuristic universe of light and energy. Test your skill across 100 levels of increasing difficulty, featuring dazzling neon colors, pulsing synthwave music, and explosive visual effects. Use your paddle to bounce the energy ball, destroy every glowing brick, and collect power-ups that change the course of the game &mdash; from multiball and slow motion to the ultimate Fire Mode, where you can shoot and obliterate blocks with pure plasma energy! Progress through levels one by one, unlock new stages, and resume your game anytime thanks to automatic save.
