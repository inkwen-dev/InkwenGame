## About

In this game, we do not have to save the dog but to free it from imprisonment. Calculate the trajectory and send the cute dog to freedom. Hit the wall a certain number of times to reduce the number to 0 and unlock the door, and then escape. Challenge yourself and train your brain to find the best way to pass.
