## How to play

Tap the screen or click the mouse button to rotate the trunk. Watch the branches ahead of the character: if the path is blocked, turn in time and keep moving.

Try to survive as long as possible. Your score increases during the run, and your best result is saved. Collect coins along the way and unlock new characters in the selection menu.

## About

This is a single-player arcade game about reaction and focus. The character runs forward along a rotating trunk, and your task is to turn the path at the right moment to avoid obstacles. The longer you survive, the higher your score becomes.

You can collect coins during runs and use them to unlock new characters. The game is built for short sessions: the rules are simple, but the speed and direction changes quickly make every run tense.
