## About

Charlotte Crossing is a simple and addictive casual game where precision and timing are everything. You control Charlotte, a cute little cat who walks forward automatically across platforms. When she reaches the edge, she stops and waits for your action. Hold the screen to make a stick grow and release to create a bridge. The goal is to make the bridge exactly the right length to reach the next platform.
