## About

Are you ready for an adventure in Redmax game? Your adventure will be escaping from the giant cube. The cube monster is coming, so run away from it. While running away from the red cube monster, collect the keys and grab all the coins. You must get the key; your friends are waiting for you at the end of the level. Grab the key and safely reach the end of the level. Hold your friends hands at the end of the level and move on to the next level.
