## About

Here is an opportunity for you to dress up and decorate a doll in a 3D arcade game called Doll Designer. With collecting and avoiding play rules, you need to collect the clothes and decorations following the pointed performance. Only when you collect over 75% of correct items, then you could complete the current level. Have a good time!
