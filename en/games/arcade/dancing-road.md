## How to play

Hold the left mouse button and drag left or right on desktop, or touch and drag on mobile. Guide your ball into balls of the same color and avoid balls of other colors. Color gates change your ball, so look ahead and move to the matching lane. Reach the end of the track, collect rewards, and improve your score to unlock more balls, roads, and songs.

Primary controls: left mouse button and horizontal mouse drag on desktop; touch and horizontal drag on mobile. No keyboard controls are required.

## About

Dancing Road is a fast-paced rhythm runner where you guide a glowing ball along a neon track and match balls of the same color to the beat. Dodge mismatched colors, react to color-changing gates, collect rewards, and master multiple music tracks. Simple one-touch controls, vivid cosmic roads, and music-synced challenges make every run easy to start and hard to perfect.
