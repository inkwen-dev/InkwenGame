## About

Blocky Champions is a fast-paced HTML5 arcade platform runner game designed primarily for desktop users. Players control a rolling football that moves forward automatically and must jump across platforms, avoid traps, and survive as long as possible. The game is best experienced on a keyboard, where precise timing and quick reactions are required to dodge obstacles and reach longer distances. With its colorful blocky environment and simple yet challenging gameplay, Blocky Champions delivers a fun arcade experience for PC players.
