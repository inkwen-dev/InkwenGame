## About

In the game Flappy Talking Tom, the usually talkative Tom cat will behave in silence. He is not up to talking when the hero is preparing to set a record for the longest flight. Help him overcome incredible obstacles. The game is very similar to similar toys with clumsily fluttering chicks. Cats can't fly at all, so the task will be more difficult. Try to keep the hero in the air and slip into empty spaces between obstacles. If your device has a touch screen, hold the character with a touch of your finger.
