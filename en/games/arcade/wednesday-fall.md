## About

Wednesday Fall is an arcade fall down game with beautiful graphics and one of the most compelling forms of addiction. Simply bounce the ball to navigate an amazing maze and level. You have to play Wednesday Fall in this game down a succession of intriguing circular courses that are supported by a pole. And as you descend, you must avoid all the hazards!
