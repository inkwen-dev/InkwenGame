A quality physics arcade game where little jelly substances keep drowning over and over again. Only you can protect them!

## How to play

Click the mouse into an empty space or touch the screen to make a jump.
Choose the right time to make the jump without drowning.
Collect jelly bonuses to spend on starting boosts or unlocking new skins.
