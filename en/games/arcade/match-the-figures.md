## About

In Match the Figures, players race against the clock to fit various shapes and figures into their correct spots. Each level presents a set of unique figures and outlines, challenging players to drag and match each piece before time runs out. As the levels progress, the shapes get more intricate, and the timer shortens, making for a fast-paced and engaging puzzle experience. With intuitive controls, bright graphics, and increasingly complex levels, Match the Figures keeps players on their toes, sharpening spatial awareness and reflexes. Can you beat the clock and match them all?
