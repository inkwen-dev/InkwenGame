## About

Rope Bawling 3 – This is a game in which you need to cut the rope to help the bowling balls destroy the pins, on your way there will be lasers, thorns and chains that need to be destroyed by the laser. There will also be obstacles cubes that can be destroyed only by a special fireball and pipes from which a powerful flow of air. To pass the levels you need to skillfully control gravity.
