## How to play

Find every hidden Puffie in the picture to complete the level. The counter in the top-left corner shows how many you have found.

On a computer: click a Puffie to find it, hold and drag to move around the scene, and use the mouse wheel to zoom.
On a phone or tablet: tap a Puffie, drag to explore, and pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Tap carefully: a wrong tap costs a heart. Use the lightbulb button when you need a hint. Complete all three scenes for a character to add its portrait to your album. Open the top-right menu to adjust sound, music and language, or return to the main menu.

## About

Tiny Puffies. Big hiding places.

Explore 30 cozy, illustrated scenes in this hidden-object game, from leafy gardens to star-filled workshops. Look closely: playful little Puffies are tucked among books, plants and everyday treasures. Tap each one to reveal its color and watch your collection grow.

Meet 10 lovable characters, complete their scenes and collect their portraits in your album. Zoom in, move around the picture and enjoy the search without a countdown. Stuck on one sneaky Puffie? A hint can help you spot it.

A warm, charming seek-and-find adventure for a quick break or one more cozy level. Play with touch or mouse, in portrait or landscape. Available in English and Russian.
