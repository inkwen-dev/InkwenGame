## About

Welcome to the ultimate Cyberpunk evolution of the classic Snake! Hack the system, collect energy barrels and data crystals, and survive the neon grid. Beware of deadly security walls and AI Hunter Drones. Use your DASH ability to smash through enemies, and spend your hacked Credits in the Black Market to buy permanent upgrades like Auto-Shields and Magnetic Fields. Features pseudo-3D graphics, adaptive synthwave audio, and a Hardcore mode!
