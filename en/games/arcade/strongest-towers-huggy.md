## About

Strongest Towers Huggy pits players against a relentless onslaught of quirky monsters. Assemble an arsenal of cuddly towers, each boasting unique abilities, to defend against waves of foes across vibrant landscapes. Strategize tower placement and upgrade paths wisely to fend off ever-evolving enemy waves. Engage in challenging levels, unlock powerful upgrades, and harness the strengths of each tower to triumph in this delightful tower defense adventure. Ready to hug it out while defending against the adorable yet formidable invaders?
