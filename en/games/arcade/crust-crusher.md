## How to play

Goal: crack every planet's core. Tap or click the planet to throw your rock; each hit carves a crater, pays gems and fills the crack meter. When the meter is full the planet cracks and the next one flies in. Spend gems in the dock on Power, Rate, Lucky strike, the Auto-loader (then hold to keep throwing), bigger rocks and moonlets that smash on their own. Tap the golden comet for a bonus. After cracking the Lava planet, a Supernova trades your galaxy for stardust and permanent upgrades. Mouse or touch only; no keyboard needed. The Menu button holds settings, the planet album, stats and help.

## About

Smash voxel planets layer by layer, crack their molten cores, and go Supernova. Every throw carves a real crater into a world made entirely of voxels: rubble flies, gems spill into your ring, and the crack meter climbs. Spend gems on harder hits, faster throws and bigger rocks (a boulder, a meteor, a bouncing wrecking ball, a splitting ice comet, a drill bomb), buy moonlets that keep smashing on their own, and crack twelve planets from the Meadow to the Star-forged. Then trade your galaxy for stardust in a Supernova, keep the black hole and the moon for good, and start again in a recoloured galaxy with its own twist. A daily planet pays a streak bonus every day.
