## About

Galaxy Zero is an exciting, fast-paced arcade game where players catch falling apples using a basket, with the goal of achieving the highest score possible. The game features two thrilling modes: Speed Rounds, where apples fall at an ever-increasing pace, testing your quick reflexes and reaction time, and Survival, where you must keep catching apples without missing too many, as each mistake brings you closer to game over. Whether youre competing to beat your personal best or aiming for the top of the leaderboard, each session is a race against time and precision. Can you survive the chaos and become the ultimate apple-catching champion?
