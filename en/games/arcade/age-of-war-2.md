## About

The massively popular web game "Age of War" has been remastered for the best mobile experience. Take control of 16 different units and 15 different turrets to defend your base and destroy your enemy. In this game, you start at the caveman age, then evolve! There are a total of 5 ages, each with its own unique units and turrets. Can you advance faster than your enemy?
