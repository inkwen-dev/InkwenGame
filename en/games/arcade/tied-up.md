## About

Tied Up is an action survival game with a unique twist! Control a small ball connected to a larger ball with a spring joint, and swing around to dodge and attack. Enemies come in challenging waves, each wave bringing new dangers trying to collide and take you down. You have 3 lives per round&mdash;survive as many waves as possible and push your high score higher each run. Easy to play, hard to master&mdash;can you stay alive while being Tied Up?
