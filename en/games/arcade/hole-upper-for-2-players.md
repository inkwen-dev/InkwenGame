## About

Double the holes, double the chaos! Hole Upper For 2 Players is a fast-paced, physics-based, local split-screen arcade game where size matters. Take control of a gravity-defying, hungry hole and compete head-to-head on the same screen. Strategize your movement, swallow an endless sea of colorful geometric blocks, grow larger by the second, and out-eat your opponent before the timer hits zero. Grab a friend, pick your color, and clear the board in the ultimate feeding frenzy!
