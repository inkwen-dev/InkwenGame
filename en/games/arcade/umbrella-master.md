## About

Umbrella Master is a fun game. Just tap the screen to open the umbrella and start flying in the sky. Tap the screen to close the umbrella and start falling towards ground. Your task is to perfectly land on top of the vehicles running on the road. If you miss the vehicle and land on the ground you will lose a life. Try to collect the rewards coming randomly to score points. Be careful from birds flying in the sky. Score more and beat the other players in Hall of Fame.
