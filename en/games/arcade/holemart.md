## How to play

Move the Hole
Drag your finger to move the hole around the market.

Consume Objects
Absorb smaller objects to increase your size.

Grow Bigger
The more items you swallow, the larger your hole becomes.

Unlock Larger Items
As you grow, you can consume bigger shelves, carts, and objects.

Clear the Market
Keep swallowing until the market is completely cleared.

## About

HoleMart is a fun and addictive casual game where you control a growing hole inside a busy market.
Move around the area, swallow objects, and expand your size as you consume more items.

Start small by absorbing tiny goods, shelves, and carts. As your hole grows bigger, you’ll be able to swallow larger objects and clear the market faster. The more you consume, the stronger and bigger your hole becomes.

Simple controls, satisfying growth, and smooth gameplay make HoleMart perfect for quick play sessions and endless fun.
