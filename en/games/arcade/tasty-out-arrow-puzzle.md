## How to play

Left-Click / Tap: Select and tap any arrow pointing outward to launch it off the board.  
• Mouse Drag / Scroll: Navigate menus and inspect the Elite Badge showcase.  
• Tip: Arrows will bounce back if their path is blocked by another piece. Plan your launch sequence carefully to save Steps!

## About

Tasty Out! is a satisfying 2D logic puzzle game where your goal is to untangle delicious food-shaped arrow boards before your moves run out! Tap each directional arrow to clear the path, think ahead, and clear the screen step by step.  

KEY FEATURES:
* Deceptively Smart Puzzles: Over 150 handcrafted micro-levels shaped like sushi, burgers, and sweets.
* Move-Efficiency Bonus: Master every layout with minimum steps to earn bonus Gold and Trophies.
* Weekly Competitive League: Climb from Wood to Diamond against simulated player rankings.
* Exclusive Elite Vault: Spend your hard-earned Gold to tackle VIP stages and collect rare Food Badges.
* Endless Replayability: Unlock the infinite League mode once you conquer level 150.
