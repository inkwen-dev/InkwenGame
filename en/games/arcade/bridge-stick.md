## About

Bridge Stick is an arcade game, and the goal of this game is that you need to pass the warrior between pillars. Tap on the screen and the bar will be shown. The bar will grow as you tap the screen. You need to make the bar have sufficient length to connect two pillars and to create a bridge, where the player can walk. If you make the stick shorter or longer the player will fall down and the game will be over. Try to pass more pillars as you can.
