Use WASD or Arrow keys for the player s movement Use W or Up arrow key twice for double jump

## About

Reco Ball is a 2D cute little platformer where you play as a ball who have to collect all of the copper coins while avoiding the enemy balls, rotating blades, spikes and reach the yellow flag in order to go to the next level. There are 8 levels to play and the difficulty increases as you proceed.
