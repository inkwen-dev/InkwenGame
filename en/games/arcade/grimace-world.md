## About

Do you love Grimace Shake? Well! you are in the right place to play a Grimace game. Tap or click to jump to collect the purple Shake to unlock the house. Once you collect all shakes, a door will appear with the yellow clown waiting for you. Go to that door to unlock the next level and be careful with fire traps. Have fun and explore tons of amazing and challenging levels.
