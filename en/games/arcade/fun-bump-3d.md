## About

With all kinds of colorful 3D blocks, Fun Bump 3D needs you to help the stickman and push the ball to the finish line at each level. You are only allowed to bump and push the blocks that have the same colors as yours. Or else, you will lose the game directly. Try to use the special blocks with large sizes and higher width, good luck!
