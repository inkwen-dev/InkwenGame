## How to play

Use mouse or mobile touch to redirect character to patch

## About

In order to avoid being seen by other players, the squid gamer could only go back and forth on a flat surface. You may touch a block to alter its walking environment, which will aid him in his quest to obtain the candy. A touch on the zombie's body can also shift the zombie's travelling direction. However, keep in mind that if the candy or even the squid figure falls off the screen, you will lose the game immediately.
