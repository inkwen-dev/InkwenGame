## About

Smashy Bird is a fun and addictive arcade-style game where you take control of two powerful green pipes to crush a tiny, yellow birds as it tries to fly through the gaps. Your goal is to time your smashes perfectly, avoiding letting the bird escape. Each successful smash earns you points, and the difficulty increases as the birds speed ramps up! Easy to pick up but hard to master, Smashy Bird is the ultimate test of timing, precision, and reflexes. How many birds can you smash before they fly away?
