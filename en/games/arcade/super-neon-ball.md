## About

Keep a neon ball in the air in the Super Neon Ball skill game. Your neon ball falls toward the bottom of the screen, knocking out blocks in the platform that prevents it from plummeting into the abyss. Keep the ball in the air, bouncing off the platforms that slide by overhead when they come in handy and collect money icons as they float past.
