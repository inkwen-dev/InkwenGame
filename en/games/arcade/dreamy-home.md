## How to play

The game has simple controls.

If you are playing on a computer:
- Click on an item with the mouse;
- Hold down the mouse key and drag the item to the desired location.

If you are playing on a smartphone:
- Tap the item with your finger;
- Hold your finger on the touchscreen and drag the item to the desired location.

There are also 3 boosters available to you:
- Hint (light bulb icon). Sets the item to its correct location;
- Freeze. Freezes the timer for 60 seconds;
- Compass. Will show the location of any 4 items.

## About

It's not just a game, but a warm and peaceful adventure that helps you feel the magic of ordinary moments. 🌸

Opening box after box, you will find personal items and lovingly find the perfect place for them. Room by room, step by step, you will assemble mosaics of memories and emotional stories hidden in simple objects.

There's no need to rush - enjoy the process: create cosiness, decorate, arrange and let the space tell its story without words. It's a calming activity where there is no rush, only calm and contentment. 🍃

From small little things to treasured heirlooms, every object carries a piece of life. As you immerse yourself in the game, you'll reminisce, dream and smile as you unpack an entire life step by step.

Immerse yourself in a cosy world where soft visual details, relaxing sounds and thoughtful gameplay create an atmosphere of warmth and nostalgia. ✨
