## About

The glass bridge game, one of the Squid Game games, is now on the web! One of the two glasses on the platform is made of fake glass. You must find the right glass and jump over it to reach the next glass and reach the final. If you choose the wrong glass, the glass will break and you will fall down. You must also cross over to the other side within the specified time. By choosing the difficulty level of the game on the main menu, you can gain an advantage in understanding the difference between the glasses. For more games, visit maskedgames.com.
