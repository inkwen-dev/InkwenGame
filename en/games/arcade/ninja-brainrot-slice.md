## About

Ninja Brainrot Slice is an endless arcade game that puts your ninja reflexes to the ultimate test! Instead of slicing fruit, you&rsquo;ll slash through the crazy heads of your favorite Brainrot characters &mdash; Tung Tung, Sahur, Crocodilo, and Tralala. Swipe fast, build combos, and climb the leaderboards as the heads fly across the screen. But be careful &mdash; one wrong slice into a bomb and your run is over! Fast-paced, funny, and addictive &mdash; Ninja Brainrot Slice turns chaos into a slicing challenge like no other.
