## About

Disk Area is a kids puzzle game. At this game you need to put disk at the selected area. Tap on screen to get the disk power to move to the area. If you give him more power he will go away from the area, or if you give him less power the disk will be closer to the area. So you need to give the right power to go the disk to the area. After every success the area will change the position.
