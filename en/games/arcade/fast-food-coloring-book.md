## About

Fast Food Coloring Book is a fun coloring puzzle game featuring the different images of the fast food bites. Pick any of the given four food images and start coloring it using the colors on the bottom. Increase or decrease the size of the tip to make it easier for you to apply colors. Have fun with your creations and share it with your friends and family! Enjoy playing this coloring game online for free!
