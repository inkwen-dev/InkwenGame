## About

The game is a compilation of 3 popular arcade games. 1. game in the style of &ldquo;watermelon games&rdquo; where you need to combine fruits to improve them. You can also use different boosters. 2. a game where you have to throw a spear at balls that move in a pattern. The longer you play, the harder it is to hit the balls. 3. a game where you can control a cube to hit randomly generated holes in the wall. Also in the game there is a store with skins, which can be obtained for coins or advertising Play now!
