## About

Rope Swing is a cool game where you have to use your rope to swing through the levels! There are a lot of great levels that feature many obstacles along the way. Make sure to not fall into spikes or gaps and try not to hit one of the many obstacles that you'll face along the way. Swing and bounce your way through the level, and make sure to collect coins
