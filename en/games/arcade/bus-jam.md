## How to play

- Tap (or click) a vehicle in the car park to drive it out along its arrow.
- It can only leave if nothing is parked between it and the way out; otherwise it
  bumps back into place and you lose nothing but the move.
- A vehicle that gets out pulls into a free bay and boards passengers of its own
  colour from the front of the queue.
- A full bay drives away and frees itself up again. If every bay is full and the
  passenger at the front has nowhere to go, the level is over.
- Clear the whole queue to win the level, and earn coins and stars.
- Boosters: VIP lifts any vehicle straight out, Arrange sorts the queue by colour,
  Jumble reshuffles it. Coins buy more, or watch a short ad.
- Locked bays can be opened with coins or a rewarded ad at any time.

Controls: tap / click only. No keyboard needed. Works on phone, tablet and desktop.

## About

Bus Jam is two puzzles stacked on one screen. At the bottom is the jam: a car
park crammed with cars, vans and buses, each painted a colour and pointing
somewhere. Tap one and it drives out along its arrow - if anything is parked in
the way, it only bumps and honks. Above it a queue of passengers winds through
the park in colour blocks, and every vehicle you free pulls into a bay and takes
the passengers of its own colour with it.

That is the whole squeeze: a bay only leaves when it is full, you only have a few
of them, and pulling out the wrong vehicle fills one with passengers nobody
needs. Clear the queue before the bays jam up and the level is yours.

Levels are generated, so they never run out, and the lot is laid out in ten
different packings - rows, rings, herringbone, spiral, sunburst - across thirteen
worlds that change every five levels, from City Park to Snow Town. Coins buy
extra bays and three boosters: VIP pulls any vehicle straight out, Arrange
re-sorts the queue, Jumble reshuffles it. There is a seven-day login reward, a
weekly win event with four prizes, a daily chest, a VIP pass for double coins,
and a profile full of lifetime awards to finish off.
