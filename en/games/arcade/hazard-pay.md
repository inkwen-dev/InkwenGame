## About

Hazard Pay is a troll platformer set in the worst factory on earth: floors vanish, doors run away and spikes pop up right where you land. Pick your worker: Cinder the salamander wall-jumps, Pip the crash-test dummy double-jumps and Rivet the robot hovers and scans for hidden traps. Clear 60 hand-built shifts and spend your hazard pay on hats and trails. Whatever looks safest usually is not.
