## About

The rabbit Dorote, owner of an orchard, was preparing for an event in her village. When inspecting his oranges, he noticed that some of them were hiding explosive surprises, left by someone. However, she was in a hurry and couldnt handle it now, as she needed to prepare the juice for the event. Help her cut the oranges carefully, avoid cutting the wrong things!
