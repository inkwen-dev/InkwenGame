## About

Are you ready for the adventure of Fire and Water? In this journey, you need to help them. The stick figures must be quick and reach the portal as fast as possible. You need to be fast to survive in the block world. The block world is full of dangers. This is just the beginning of the adventure, but reach the portal and start the journey. Watch out for spikes and spinning wheels; they are everywhere. Find the keys in the houses and collect them. Without keys, the portal wont open.
