## About

In a game filled with adventures, the noob has to protect the sheep. You must be very careful to protect the sheep. The sheep isnt very smart and cant distinguish friend from foe. As a noob, you must help the sheep. Take your sword and attack all enemies. Kill the enemies to clear the path for the sheep. Help the sheep reach the barn safely. The sheep must reach the barn unharmed. You need to clear the path for the sheep. There should be no enemies or obstacles in its way.
