## How to play

How to Play

Goal: Collect as much cash as you can, keep moving and don't get surrounded.

Desktop

W / ↑: accelerate
S / ↓: brake and reverse
A D / ← →: steer
Space: handbrake (drift)
Esc: pause

Mobile/Tablet: Move the joystick up to accelerate, down to brake/reverse, and left/right to steer. Move diagonally to drive and steer together.
Drag the right side of the screen to look around while driving. The camera smoothly returns behind your car after release.

Spend collected cash in the Garage to unlock new vehicles.

Tips

Drive over the green cash stacks. Gold $100 bundles are worth more but sit in riskier spots.
Watch for the SURROUNDED warning. Keep moving and look for an open gap, or ram through a weak patrol car.
Breaking out after the capture meter passes 70% gives you an Escape Bonus.
When you're busted, you can watch an ad once per run to break out and keep going.
Spend your cash in the garage on faster or heavier cars.

## About

Heist Escape: City Lockdown

You've pulled off the heist. Now get out of the city alive.

Race your getaway car through busy streets, grab stacks of cash off the road and stay one move ahead of a police force that works as a team. Patrol cars chase you, interceptors cut you off at the next junction, SUVs ram you and vans set up roadblocks, while a helicopter follows you from above with its searchlight.

The police don't just chase you, they try to box you in. If they fully surround you, you're BUSTED. But if there's a gap your car can fit through, you can still get away. Push weak patrol cars aside, reverse out of a trap, squeeze through tight gaps or make the police crash into each other. Break free when the capture meter is nearly full to earn a big Escape Bonus.

The longer you survive, the harder the chase gets, across 6 pursuit levels. Spend your stolen cash in the garage to unlock 6 getaway cars, from the Starter Compact to a heavy Armored SUV that shoves police aside and the fastest Supercar, and pick a paint color for each.

Physics-based crashes: cars, traffic, cones and barriers all react to hits
Smart police that flank, intercept, ram and surround you
A police helicopter chasing you from the start of every run
A large city built from blocks, with parks, parking lots and construction sites
Works on desktop and mobile
