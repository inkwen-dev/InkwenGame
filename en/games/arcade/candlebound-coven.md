## How to play

Goal: survive the five-minute dream. Your rites attack on their own; you only move Mina and choose upgrades.
Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move; mouse drag also moves. Click a rite card to pick it at each level-up.
Mobile: drag anywhere on the screen to move with a floating stick; tap a rite card to pick it.
Pick up glowing candle motes to level up. Survive bosses and reach the morning to clear a dream and open the next one. Spend Candles on the Moon Tree and in the Coven Shop between runs.

## About

Candlebound Coven is a five-minute survivor-like roguelite set in the dreams of Sister Mina, a hungry little nun cat. She carries one candle into the dark while cute dessert monsters close in from every side. You never press attack: your rites cast themselves. You steer Mina, gather the candle motes the fallen leave behind, and every level pick one of three midnight rites — orbiting candles, homing charms, a supper chime, frost, meteors and more — so every run is a new build. Bosses wake every 45 seconds and the dream breaks at five minutes. Between runs, spend Candles in the Coven Shop and on the 177-star Moon Tree, clear daily and weekly tasks, collect trophies, fill the bestiary and open five dreams, from the Midnight Kitchen to the Witch's Attic.
