## About

Snake Way is a fun and challenging puzzle game where you guide colorful snakes through clever paths and help them escape the board. Plan your moves carefully, avoid obstacles, and clear each level with precision. As the levels progress, the puzzles become more challenging and rewarding. Can you master every path and help all the snakes escape? Features: - Simple and relaxing gameplay - Challenging puzzle levels - Easy-to-learn controls - Colorful visuals and smooth animations - Great for casual and puzzle-game fans - Playable on desktop and mobile browsers
