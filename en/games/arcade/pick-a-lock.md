## How to play

Tap anywhere on the screen everytime the indicator inside the lock is on the yellow spot When the number in the middle reaches 0 the lock opens Open as many locks as you can

## About

Get ready for popping locks in this simple, yet exciting game! Challenge your senses in this fast-paced madness! Each lock is harder to unlock than the previous one. If you make one mistake you have to start all over again, so be careful! Kill the boredom now and pop some locks!
