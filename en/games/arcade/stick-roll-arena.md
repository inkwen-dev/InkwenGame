## How to play

- Tap / Click anywhere on the screen to roll the dice

- Your stickmen will automatically push the enemy team

- Time your rolls correctly to summon more warriors

- Push all red opponents off the arena to win!



Simple one-tap controls. Easy to learn, fun to play!
