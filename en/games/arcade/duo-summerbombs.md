## About

Are you ready for some fun? The player holding the bomb when the timer runs out loses! In this exciting two-player game, get rid of the bomb before time is up. Compete against your friend and prove who is the better player. To become the best, pass the bomb away as quickly as possible and avoid it until the countdown reaches zero. Stay alert, react fast, and do everything you can to keep the bomb out of your hands!
