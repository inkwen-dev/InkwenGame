## About

Cake Stack is a fun and addictive hyper-casual game! Guide your cute yellow character as it runs along the track and stacks delicious layers of cake. Tap or click to play: dodge obstacles, collect golden coins and build your cake tower higher and higher. The game features many exciting levels, unlockable characters and skins, and a shop where you can spend your coins. Run out of moves? Watch a video to revive and keep playing! Simple one-touch controls make it perfect for players of all ages. Easy to learn, hard to master &mdash; can you build the tallest cake and set a new high score? Play Cake Stack now!
