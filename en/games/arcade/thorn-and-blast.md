## About

Thorn and Blast is a thrilling action-puzzle game where quick thinking and precision are your best allies! Your mission is simple yet addictive &ndash; blast away colorful thorns by aiming and launching powerful balls. Clear all the thorns from each level with limited shots to achieve victory. With every stage, the challenge grows, testing your timing, strategy, and aiming skills. Are you ready to master the art of the perfect blast? Dive into the world of Thorn and Blast and experience endless fun, fast-paced challenges, and satisfying victories!
