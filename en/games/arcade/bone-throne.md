## How to play

Desktop: Left-click near an enemy to attack it, or near a fallen corpse to raise it as a skeleton minion. If both are in range, attacking takes priority.

Mobile/Touch: Tap works exactly the same as clicking - attack or raise with a single tap.

No keyboard needed - the whole game is played with one input.

## About

You are the villain. An army of paladins, rangers, and knights is marching on your throne to end your reign of darkness - so raise their fallen as skeletons and make them fight for you instead.

Bone Throne is a horde survival roguelite where every click does double duty: blast the heroes storming your crypt, then click their corpses to raise skeleton minions that fight at your side. The more heroes fall, the bigger your undead army grows - until the screen is a chaotic swarm of bone and magic.

Survive wave after wave of soldiers and glowing energy orbs, defeat a rotating cast of RPG hero bosses, and pick a roguelite card after every boss kill to build your undead war machine. Unlock new playable warlocks between runs and see how many waves you can survive before your throne finally falls.

Features:

One-click combat - attack enemies and raise your undead horde with the same click
A rotating roster of hero bosses, each with a unique attack pattern
Roguelite card choices after every boss fight - a new army build every run
Unlockable warlocks with unique starting powers
Wave modifiers that twist the run every few waves
No install - instant browser play on desktop and mobile
