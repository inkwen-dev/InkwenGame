## How to play

Complete horizontal lines and keep the stack from reaching the top of the board. On PC: A/D move, S soft drops, Space rotates, W hard drops, C holds a piece and Esc pauses. On mobile: swipe left/right to move, tap to rotate, drag down to soft drop, flick straight down to hard drop, and use HOLD to store a piece. Plan with the next three pieces; T-Spins, difficult-clear chains and Perfect Clears award extra score and build FLOW.

## About

An endless Tetris score attack that reacts to how you play. Clean clears and strong chains make the music, background and effects grow from CALM to FLOW, HYPE and OVERDRIVE. Plan around the next three pieces, use HOLD, perform T-Spins, keep difficult-clear chains and chase Perfect Clears. The speed rises as you progress, runs award BITS, and your best score can be submitted to the leaderboard. Progress and an unfinished run can be restored.
