## About

Ninja vs Slime is a casual game where you need to destroy slime who coming to you. Throw ninja stars at your opponents. The stars will bounce off the slimes and make ricochets so you can destroy more slimes at once. But slimes have more lives. some have 20, 30 or more. So you need to be precise in throwing and destroy the slimes that are closer to you so that they do not destroy you.
