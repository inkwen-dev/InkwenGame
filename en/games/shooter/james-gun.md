## About

James Gun is a funny skill game of action and shooting. You're busted and you need to jump out of your lover’s window, now! Try to land on your sports car without falling to your certain death. Hold onto the ledges to slow down your fall, grab onto another ledge and let go when it’s safe to do so. You’ll soon find out that your relationship with gravity is far more dangerous than the one that’s making you sneak out of this apartment.
