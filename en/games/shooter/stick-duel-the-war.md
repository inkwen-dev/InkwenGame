## About

Stick Duel series continues with The War episode. Brand new physics, weapons and levels are ready in the new game. Whether you play against the CPU or against your friend, the goal is to win the game by taking 5 rounds. Play by considering the advantages and disadvantages of weapons and the designs of 20 different maps. Stay away from fire pools and traps!
