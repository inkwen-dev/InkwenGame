## About

Survive against waves of bizarre monsters trying to invade your farm. Collect temporary power-ups like rapid fire, explosive ammo, health packs, and electric fences. At the end of each stage, pick from random upgrades, unlock new weapons, deploy crop duster airstrikes, slow enemies with traps, or grab a magnet to pull power-ups. Progress further to unlock new playable heroes with unique styles.
