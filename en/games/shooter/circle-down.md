## About

Circle Down is a funny arcade shooter game. The goal of this game is to shoot the rings to become a balls before hit the beam. The rings will fall to the beam. Aim and shoot them with the balls. If rings hit the beam three times the game will over. Try to transform more as you can rings to balls and gave fun.
