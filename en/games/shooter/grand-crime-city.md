## About

Step into the gritty streets of Grand Crime City, where danger lurks around every corner and only the toughest survive. In this action-packed shooter game, youll take on the role of a fearless vigilante completing intense missions to bring order&mdash;or chaos&mdash;to the city. With 20 thrilling missions, each more challenging than the last, youll be chasing down enemies, taking out rival gangs, and fighting to survive in an urban warzone. Explore the open city, accept tasks, and prove your dominance in the crime underworld.
