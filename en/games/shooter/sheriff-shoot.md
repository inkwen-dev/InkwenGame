## About

The sheriff in the game Sheriff Shoot got bored and decided to have fun shooting bottles. But the day before he had drunk a pint of strong beer and his movements became somewhat unsteady. In this state, he is unlikely to hit the targets, so you must help the hero so that he does not embarrass himself in front of his friends in Sheriff Shoot. Control the sheriff's hands to point the gun at the target.
