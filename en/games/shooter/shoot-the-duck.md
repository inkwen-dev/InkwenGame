## About

Your hunting dog whines in impatience, because the duck hunting season is coming. He can not wait to bring you a booty, which you cleverly shoot. To complete the round in Shoot the Duck, you must hit the maximum number of flying targets. Their number is located on the bottom horizontal panel.
