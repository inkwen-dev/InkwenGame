## About

During the fall season this year lots there have been more spiders in the area so you were hired to go out and exterminate them. What they didn't tell you was that not only were there more spiders, but they are also a whole lot bigger than usual. Try to survive as long as possible, and if you can, try to find the 2 emergence holes and destroy them to stop the spider apocalypse.
