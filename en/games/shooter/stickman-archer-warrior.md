## About

The archers have taken their places! An exciting fight begins with Stickman Archer Warriors. Many enemies are waiting for you on the steps with arrows in their hands. Focus on the target and make your shot. You have to hit it right, don't miss it! There are 4 different special powers in the game. When you face your opponent, examine it and choose one of these powers. As you progress through the chapters, you can buy new arrows with the gold you earn. Have fun!
