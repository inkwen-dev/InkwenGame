## How to play

Movement:
- Mouse to Look around
- WASD keys to Walk
- Left shift (hold) to Run
- Left Ctrl to Crouch
- Space to Jump

Weapon:
- Left click to shoot
- Right click to aim
- R key to reload
- T key to inspect weapon
- Q / scrollwheel key to cycle equipped weapons

Shop:
- G to open Gun Shop, and G again to close Gun Shop
- H to open Hunter's Supply, and H again to close Hunter's Supply
- J to open Purchases

Mobile controls: touch.
