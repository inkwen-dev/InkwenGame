## About

Challenge your friends and family members to 2-player arcade tank battle! Mini Tanks is a super-powered addicting shooting action game for everyone where two players engage in huge explosives! With a perfect overhead view of the battle field, players must aim carefully in the enclosed zone and basically try to eliminate their opponent as quickly as possible! You can also play with computer.
