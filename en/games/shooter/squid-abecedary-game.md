## About

Squid Abecedary Game dives into an underwater world where players aid Squidy, a lovable squid, in learning the ABCs. Navigate through immersive aquatic environments, solving puzzles and challenges tailored to each letter of the alphabet. Engage in interactive activities that reinforce letter recognition and phonics skills while uncovering hidden treasures. With engaging gameplay and delightful visuals, embark on an educational adventure that makes learning the alphabet a joyous underwater escapade.
