## How to play

Press Tap to Skip Story WASD to move Q to Aim Up and Down while holding Sniper to zoom out and in E to pick up items R to reload F to drop item Space to jump T to Enter Car X while ride Car to walk backward Space while riding Car to Brake

## About

Red and White Poly is an FPS Shooter Game-themed at a time when Indonesia is still struggling to defend its independence. You are a freedom fighter against allied forces who are trying to steal Indonesian independence, you will be placed in a historic battle that is expected to make you understand a little about the history of Indonesian independence.
