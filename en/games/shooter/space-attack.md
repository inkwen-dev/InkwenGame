## About

In space war your job is to protect it the Earth from asteroids and small planets as long as possible! Use your mouse to move and shooting. In each level you have of ammo which increases the number of hits, get the extra score and ammo at the end of the level. The game has a bonus: three types of your spaceship, three types of laser, protection spaceship life improvements.
