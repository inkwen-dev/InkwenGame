## How to play

How to Play
- Move: WASD
- Look: Mouse
- Fire: Left Mouse Button
- Use doors, switches, and terminals: E
- Switch weapons: 1–5 or mouse wheel
- Open automap: M
- Pause: Esc
- Fullscreen: F
On mobile, use the left stick to move, drag the right side of the screen to look, and tap the on-screen controls to fire, interact, switch weapons, open the map, or pause.
Look for keycards and terminals to unlock new areas. Explosive barrels can damage enemies but will also hurt you at close range. If your health or ammo becomes critical, you may use a Reward Ad once per attempt for an Emergency Supply. After death, one Reward Ad can restore you with a Revive.

## About

Hellgate 1993 is a fast, retro-inspired first-person shooter set inside a dying horror-sci-fi facility. Fight through eight hostile worlds, uncover secret routes, unlock sealed sectors, collect stronger weapons, and face a final boss at the end of every world.
Every world contains three stages: two combat missions followed by a boss encounter. Manage health, armor, ammo, keys, and your arsenal as new enemy types appear throughout the campaign. Find hidden supplies, activate terminals, survive ambushes, and bring the light back to the last surviving human signal.
