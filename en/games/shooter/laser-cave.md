## About

Embark on a thrilling adventure with the Laser Cave game! Perfect for fans of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, this exciting shooting game will test your skills and speed. Explore the dark depths of a mining tunnel and take on the fast-paced Kraang robots. Defend yourself from their powerful weapons by destroying the attacking drones. You'll be randomly assigned one of the turtles from the TMNT series, with the same controls for each character. Use the Up and Down Arrows or your mouse to aim, then press the Space Bar to fire your weapon. Don't forget to keep an eye on the energy bar and wait for your weapon to recharge before your next shot. Ready to take on this challenge and emerge victorious?
