## About

Zombie Graveyard is a fast-paced arcade shooter set in a haunted cemetery. Fight the undead across two modes: Campaign &ndash; progress through increasingly difficult levels, or Survival &ndash; face endless waves until you fall. Armed with a pistol, shotgun, and power-ups, you must clear each level by killing every zombie. What makes it unique? The graveyard evolves as you play &mdash; tougher zombies rise from graves, and tombs break apart. With tight controls, eerie visuals, and two distinct ways to play, every match tests your reflexes. Compatible with desktop and mobile browsers.
