## About

MISSION Command the M4 Sherman in WWII. Destroy German Panzer barracks while defending your base from an armored onslaught. STRATEGY Push sandbags into position to create cover and block enemy shells. Use the environment to build your own fortifications. DEADLOCK WARNING Watch your path! If you get trapped by sandbags, you will be rendered immobile. This is a tactical failure. In this state, the mission is over; you must refresh the page (F5) to redeploy your M4. WIN / LOSS WIN: Destroy enemy barracks. LOSS: M4 destroyed, base leveled, or trapped by sandbags.
