## About

Survev.io Battle Royale is a thrilling multiplayer game where you fight to be the last character standing on a shrinking island. Start with nothing but your fists, scavenge for weapons and supplies, and stay within the safe zone. Choose between solo, duo, or squad modes, and use strategy and skills to outlast up to 50 opponents.
