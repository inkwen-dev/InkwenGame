## About

Super Lule vs Zombies is a fun aim and shoot game featuring our beloved hero Lule. This time the brave ex military guy has to defeat an entire army of zombies that took over the beautiful land where Princess Lili lives. Keep in mind that you have a limited amount of only 7 bullets that you can use inside each of the 18 levels so use your ammo wisely and try to kill more than one zombie by using the same bullet.
