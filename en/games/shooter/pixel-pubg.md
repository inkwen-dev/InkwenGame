## How to play

WASD or arrow keys to move Left mouse button to shoot Right mouse button to aim F to interact or pick a weapon or parachuting Space bar to jump Left shift to run R to reload C to crouch Tab to open inventory

## About

Pixel PUBG is a 3D shooting game where you have to choose from five different characters and two maps to play. You will find a lot of weapons inside the game, pick them up and start shooting. Prove your skills in this challenging game and kill all your opponents. Have fun! Features: • 5 characters to choose • 2 maps: Battle Royale and Sandbox • Weapons all over the map • Inventory
