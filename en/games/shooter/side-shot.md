## About

This is shooter puzzle game for kids. The goal of the game is to shoot and destroy colored rings. You will shoot from the side, or tap blue wall to shoot with blue bullets and to destroy only blue rings, or tap red wall to shoot on red rings. Bullets and rings must be matched with same color to destroy them. You can make three mistakes before game is over.
