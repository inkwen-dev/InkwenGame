## How to play

Use mouse to aim and shoot the pig into base or use finger and slide on the screen if you play at mobile devices

## About

This is very interesting kids puzzle game. It is one kind of shooter game. Your job as a player is to shoot the pig and to catch the stars with the pig. Depend of the level and of the obstacles there will be few base where pig can land. So you need to jump from base to base, to avoid spikes and obstacles and to collect stars. When you collect star the hole will be open for the pig to escape from the level. There is only way to pass the level and to go to the next one.There is 25 levels and try to pass them all.
