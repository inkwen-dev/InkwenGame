## About

Slip into the role of the new town marshall. At the every month payment day all the citizens comes to the bank to deposit his money. In the same hour the bandits starts a bank heist. You have to shoot down all the bandits but not the people who deposit his money. Look for all 12 doors and get money deposited behind every door to complete the level. Features: -Cute Graphics -Easy to learn gameplay -Very addictive -Great for your family too
