## About

Wild Bull Shooter game is designed to equip you with the latest of sniper gun to head the angry bull one by one bullfight. This endless Wild Bull Shooter game is one of the most addictive game play. It is designed by keeping the real bull attack hunters or bullfights in mind, hence multiplied the experience of hunting. It exhibits the combo of both sniper shooting game and hunting game to give the user double treat to let them stick to the end. The user is provided with map facility to locate the pray to hit them before they hunt you down.
