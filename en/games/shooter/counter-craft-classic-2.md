## About

Counter Craft Classic 2 is a thrilling first-person shooter that combines the best elements of Counter-Strike weapons with Minecraft-style environments. Step into blocky battlefields filled with dangerous mobs, intense firefights, and fast-paced action. Armed with iconic weapons like the AWP, AK-47, Desert Eagle, and more, your mission is simple yet challenging: eliminate enemies and find the exit on each level to progress. Each map offers unique layouts, strategic hiding spots, and waves of pixelated foes determined to stop you.
