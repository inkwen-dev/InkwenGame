## How to play

WASD to move T to chat R to reload P to pause C to crouch Space to Jump Left mouse click to fire Mouse scroll to change weapons

## About

If you enjoy CS:GO or CoD you’re in the right place. Masked Forces is a shooting action game which put at stake all your strategy and survival skills. You mission is to kill all your enemies, survive in a battlefield environment and win the battle. Features: • Multiplayer and singleplayer missions • Armory and weapons shop • Good graphics
