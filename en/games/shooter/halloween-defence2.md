## About

It’s Halloween time and the pumpkin heads are attacking again! If you want to survive, you have to shoot down these pumpkin heads before they reach you. Each pumpkin head that is destroyed is considered as one score point. You have two lives and whenever a pumpkin head reaches you, you lose one life. When you lose your two lives, you will also lose the game.
