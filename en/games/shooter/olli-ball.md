## About

Get ready to make Olli fly in this addictive game. The lovable round elephant known from European books and toys needs to fly as far as he can to catch his rubber ducks. Tap twice at the right moment to launch and keep Olli as long as possible flying, bouncing and floating to gain the biggest distance and unlock new levels.
