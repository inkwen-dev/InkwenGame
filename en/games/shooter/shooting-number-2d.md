## About

Shooting and merging the numbers is a really entertaining game for the whole family. Touch on the column you wish to fire at the bottom of the screen to start the game. Make an effort to match blocks equal in size and have the same number. The identical pieces will merge and form a new block with multiple numbers when combined.
