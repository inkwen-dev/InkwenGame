## About

Play a fun arcade game in Pretty Sheep Run. You guide an adorable little sheep dashing across endless platforms. Keep your sheep running while cleverly avoiding dangerous traps. Control your sheep running to shoot different numbered doors. Change the numbers into positive numbers. Thus increasing the guns DMG, ATK SPD, etc.
