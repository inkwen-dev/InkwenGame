## About

Sky Glide is a visually captivating sky adventure where players guide a paper plane through a serene, cloud-filled world. The goal is to soar through the bright skies, collecting floating paper planes and avoiding dangerous obstacles like spinning gears and dark void planes. With each level, the challenge increases as the sky becomes more crowded with hazards, testing your precision and reflexes. The peaceful, picturesque setting with fluffy clouds and a soft blue sky creates a relaxing and immersive experience that will keep you engaged as you aim to glide further and achieve a high score in the endless sky.
