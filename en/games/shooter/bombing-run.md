## How to play

Click tap at any part of the screen to call an airstrike in the path of your enemies Click the plus button towards the lower left of the screen to open upgrade menu

## About

Your enemies want to take your land. But they don't know you own the skies! Defend the area by calling on airstrikes against invading forces in this fast paced, action packed game and see how long your forces can hold the line with your air cover. Rain fire from the skies, upgrade your ground forces, unlock new units, do whatever you must, but do NOT let the enemies pass into your territory!
