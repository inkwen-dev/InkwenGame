## About

Rookie, join the space forces! Build you career as a fearless spy capable of destroying enemies with one-shot from the shadows. Take the role of a huge Terminator, armed with a big mass destruction system and trample your enemies. Climb into the shoes of a special forces soldier with a Tesla-type lightning gun, or take control of a remote strike bot with a photon weapon. Everything is possible in the space squad. Explore all the nooks and crannies of the abandoned lunar base, enjoy the views of deep space and defeat your rivals. Command has high hopes on you, soldier!
