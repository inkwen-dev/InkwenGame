## How to play

Tap on screen to change the direction of the plane

## About

This is a game where you have to avoid the bombs from the rotating cannon with the plane. You can collect stars for more points. Be careful which side the cannon moves on and be on the opposite side of it. It's the best way to protect yourself. Sometimes the cannon will turn upside down unexpectedly, so be careful and show yourself as a good pilot.
