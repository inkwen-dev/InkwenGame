## About

Astro Shooter is the high-octane arcade shooter where danger comes from every angle! Command your advanced starfighter against endless waves of enemies attacking from all directions. Collect power-ups mid-run for temporary, powerful builds, and take down massive 3D boss ships. Spend your hard-earned coins in the permanent garage to unlock new ships and max out your core stats, ensuring you get further in every run. Your run ends, but your power doesn't!
