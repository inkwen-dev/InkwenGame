## How to play

WASD to move F to pick-up medkit Space to jump Left mouse click to shoot Left Shift to run

## About

Zombies are everywhere, they are waiting in the dark for the next victim, their hunger is bigger than your courage so you have to use all your arsenal to defeat them and save yourself. Find the glowing card to finish the level, otherwise you will not be able to survive. Are you ready to be a zombie hunter?
