## About

In Ninja Clash Heroes you take command of a squad of professional heroes. Might it be a sneaky ninja Scout, a full armored samurai, a wise Buddhist monk or a deadly sniper Geisha, you'll find a character to you liking. The action takes place in a beautiful oriental garden, but you'll have little time to witness the beauty of surroundings as the enemy is thriving to steal the Fortune Cat you side sworn to protect. Unlock the deadly powers of your class and collect stars to open crates with awesome weapons and boosters. Upgrade your character, earn medals and enjoy Ninja Clash Heroes to the fullest!
