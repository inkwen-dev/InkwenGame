## About

Shot is a shooter game. The goal of this game is to shoot from the rotating arrow to the little circle. The circle is small so it will be harder for you to hit it. When you hit the circle, the position of both the arrow and the circle will move. With every hit you will earn points. You have the right to three misses. Then the game will over.
