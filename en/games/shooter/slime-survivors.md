## About

Slime Survivors is a simplified roguelite. Survive against endless waves of opponents. Defeat them and increase the level and your characteristics. Complete achievements and unlock artifacts to become even stronger! The game has bosses for defeating which you will receive powerful artifacts! The enemies are: slugs, skeletons, goblins, golems, and so on.
