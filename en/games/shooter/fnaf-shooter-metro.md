## About

FNAF Shooter: Metro is a fast-paced horror FPS game set inside a dark, abandoned subway station. Battle waves of terrifying animatronic enemies, aim carefully, collect rewards, and buy stronger weapons as the danger increases. Explore grimy platforms, shadowy tunnels, and damaged metro trains while fighting to survive each new wave. With intense first-person shooting, creepy atmosphere, and nonstop arcade action, FNAF Shooter: Metro delivers a thrilling survival challenge for fans of horror games, animatronics, and online shooter games.
