## About

Space Shooter is a classic arcade-style game where the player pilots a spaceship and faces endless waves of enemies. The main objective is to destroy as many enemy ships as possible, avoid incoming projectiles, and survive for as long as you can. The spaceship can be controlled with the keyboard or mouse, and every laser shot counts in the battle for survival. As the game progresses, difficulty increases with faster and more aggressive enemies, keeping the adrenaline high and the challenge engaging.
