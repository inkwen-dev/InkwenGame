## About

FRAGBYTE is a fast-paced 3D first-person wave survival shooter that runs right in your browser. No downloads, 100% free. MODE: Offline single-player. Survive endless waves of enemies inside a hand-drawn ink arena. There is no final level &mdash; each wave hits harder than the last. How many waves can you survive? ENEMIES: Stickman soldiers, shield brutes, blade runners, laser snipers, and a brutal arena boss at the end of every run. ARSENAL: Assault rifle, pump shotgun, marksman sniper, katana, ink grenades, and a grapple hook. MOVEMENT: Wall-jumps, crouch-slides, and grapple-hook swings. Chain headshots to climb the score.
