## About

Game is with Christmas Special Level. Wendigo: The Evil That Devours is a chilling and intriguing first-person shooter game in which you must try and eliminate the evil horror that is Wendigo. Wedigo is a truly horrible entity and its power and evil are spreading throughout this small mining town - the once profitable town has now been turned into a place of horror and it is your job to face the terrors and unlock Wendigo's secrets. Wendigos were once human, but after being forced to eat human flesh to survive, they become supernatural monsters that retain little of the human features they once possessed.
