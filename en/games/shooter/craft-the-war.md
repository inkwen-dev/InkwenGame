## How to play

Objective
Join or create a multiplayer room, defeat opponents, and help your team reach the match score or objective before the other side. In free-for-all modes, earn the highest score before time runs out.

Controls
WASD — Move
Mouse — Look and aim
Left Mouse Button — Fire
Right Mouse Button — Aim
R — Reload
Space — Jump
Shift — Sprint
Ctrl or C — Crouch
Number keys / Mouse wheel — Switch weapons
Tab — Scoreboard
Esc — Pause menu

Mobile devices use the on-screen movement, aim, fire, jump, reload, and weapon controls.

## About

Craft Theft War is a fast-paced online first-person shooter set across compact industrial battlefields. Join or create multiplayer rooms, choose your loadout, and outmaneuver rival soldiers in team and free-for-all combat. Responsive gunplay, multiple weapon types, vehicles, character selection, and varied match modes make every battle play differently.
