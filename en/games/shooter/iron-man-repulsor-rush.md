## About

Suit up as IRon Man and take to the skies in this fast, colorful arcade flyer! Hold to fly up, release to dive, and let your auto-firing repulsors blast the incoming swarm. Some drones fire back with quick energy bolts, so stay sharp and keep moving, camping at the bottom is not an option. Grab power-ups for an extra heart, a protective shield or triple shot, and chase your best distance as the action ramps up. Easy one-touch controls, built for desktop and mobile. How far can you push the rush?
