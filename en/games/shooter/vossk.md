## About

VOSSK is two games feeding each other. Fly out and you are taking ground: the swarm pours out of Vossk hive-nodes, and silencing one pushes back their next attack. Your haul is only cargo until you reach the extraction point, so die with a full hold and most of it stays out there. Come home and its a factory. Every Core you banked becomes a generator, a refinery, a turret on an isometric grid that starves if you overbuild. Then the Cartel comes for the platform: pilot the defense, or let the turrets handle it and eat the losses. 8 weapons, 5 drones, 10 buildings, 7 zones, named bosses and a daily challenge.
