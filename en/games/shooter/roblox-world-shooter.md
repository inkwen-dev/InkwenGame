## About

Manage your little character and see him through the never-ending conflict in Mini Obby War Game, an action-packed 3D game set in a Roblox environment. With over ten unique and challenging levels, this amazing game is within your reach. Keep sprinting to avoid being caught by the waves of enemy troops who will shoot you if they see you, and aim and fire at all adversaries. As you defeat enemies, youll find coins that may be used to unlock additional characters with unique abilities.
