## About

Digit Shooter! plays like a vertical-version shooting running arcade game. With 3D green numbers, you can launch green numbers to shoot green numbers and numbered walls. You can shoot them and collect them to make your number bigger. Dont waste bullets and time on red walls while avoiding them. When you reach the finish line, try to focus on one line to shoot, then you will collect more rewards
