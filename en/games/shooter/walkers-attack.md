## About

Walkers Attack is an action-adventure game. The end of the world is coming, make your way through a world full of danger and survive! Experience non-stop action with auto-fire and strategic movement. Gather resources, defeat hordes of zombies, and complete missions to unlock new themed levels ranging from sci-fi to medieval and urban. As the levels increase, so does the challenge. Are you ready to embark on a survival adventure? Can you be humanitys last hope? Good luck!
