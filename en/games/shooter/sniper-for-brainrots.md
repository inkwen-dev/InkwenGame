## How to play

Player 1

Move: WASD
Jump: Space
Sprint: Shift
Scope: F or Right Mouse Button
Zoom: Mouse Wheel or Shift while scoped
Shoot: Left Mouse Button or Space while scoped
Interact: E
Upgrade Brainrot: Q
Open Rebirth Menu: T

Player 2

Move: Arrow Keys
Jump: L
Sprint: K
Scope: Right Shift
Zoom: K while scoped
Shoot: L while scoped
Interact: O
Upgrade Brainrot: U

Mobile: Touch controls are automatically enabled on mobile devices. Mobile supports Single Player mode only.

## About

Sniper for Brainrots is an action-packed collection and survival game where every successful shot unlocks a brand-new Brainrot. Use your sniper rifle to hit the balloon above a Brainrot, instantly transform into it, and race to the Safe Zone before a massive tsunami catches you. The farther you shoot, the rarer the Brainrot you can collect but the faster the wave becomes.

Build your collection, expand your Base, unlock powerful sniper rifles, customize your character, and compete with friends in this addictive Brainrot adventure featuring 87 unique collectibles!
