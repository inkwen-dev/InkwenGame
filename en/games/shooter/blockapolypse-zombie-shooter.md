## About

Blockapolypse: Zombie Shooter is a defense shooting simulation game with 3D pixel undead creatures. To defend this position, you need to use weapons to defend incoming zombies from 3 directions. After you kill all zombies, you still need to defeat a giant undead creature. As long as you lose all of your HP, you will lose the game. When you earn enough gold coins, you can buy better weapons in the gun shop. Good luck and survive!
