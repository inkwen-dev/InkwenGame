## About

Drunken Duel is an entertaining western game with physics-based one-button control that can be played as two people and one person. In the game, two drunk rag dolls duel. The game rules are very simple, you must first choose which mode you will play. If you want to play the game alone, you should choose 1 player mode. When you select this mode, the computer will match you with a bot playing at random difficulty, each game consists of five matches, to score points in each match, you must lower your opponent's energy level and maintain your own energy level. whose energy is reset first loses and his opponent scores and wins the game with a total of five points.
