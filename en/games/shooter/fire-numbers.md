## About

Fire Number is a shooting puzzle game. At this game, your job is to control the shooter and to shoot on the blocks with a number in them. The number that is on the blocks means that you need to shoot that many times to destroy the block. At the beginning find the blocks with smaller numbers. Create a path to move with the shooter and collect points. You can collect some extras and get more power, new bullets, and other achievements. So, destroy more blocks as you can and get more points to get the best score.
