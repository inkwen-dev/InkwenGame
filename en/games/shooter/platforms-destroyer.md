## About

This is interesting Platforms Destroyer kids game where special cannon shoots colorful balls. Cannon is a pipe from which balls fall out at your command and bombard the platform that is below. But platform moves up so your task is to break the line before she got to the cannon. At the same time, you have limited number of shots, and if the ball hits the spikes, you will lose one of your three lives. Have fun.
