## How to play

- WASD — Move
- Mouse — Look around
- Left mouse button — Shoot
- Right mouse button / E — Aim
- Space — Jump
- Shift while moving — Slide
- C — Crouch
- R — Reload
- 1–4 / Mouse wheel — Switch weapons
- Q — Previous weapon
- Hold F — Plant or defuse the bomb
- Tab — Scoreboard
- P — Pause

## About
