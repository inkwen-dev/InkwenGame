## How to play

PC: Use WASD keys to move your ship.
Mobile devices: Use swipes to control movement.

Game Objective:
Destroy enemy ships by collecting modules, upgrading your ship, and surviving longer than anyone else.

## About

Explore deep space,
piloting a cubic ship that can be infinitely expanded with modules.
Collect new decks, melee and ranged weapons, boosters, and rare upgrades on the map, transforming your ship into a true battle station. Enemies also grow, making every encounter a dynamic battle for survival.
Complete levels, upgrade your stats, unlock new skins, and experiment with builds. Vibrant 3D graphics, rich effects, and constant progression make space battles exciting and unpredictable.
