## About

Bug Blaster is a game where you work as an insect exterminator. You need to destroy the spiders that are hanging and attacking you with cobwebs. You are armed with a special pump that sprays poison. Hit the spiders and destroy them before they kill you. But you should also beware of the snake that appears in some levels. Do not get too close to the snake as it will grab you and kill you. Monitor your health on the left side of the screen. Go through all the right levels and exterminate all the insects and bugs.
