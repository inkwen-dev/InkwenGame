## How to play

Use your mouse to play the game or tap on the screen

## About

Our hero is small archer trying to win kings tournament. He intends to participate and win, but you need to train a lot. We built a special road along which round targets stand, you need to move and shoot. If you hit exactly the bull's eye, get an additional arrow as a gift.If you miss 5 times the bull's eye you lose the game and start from beginning. Have fun!
