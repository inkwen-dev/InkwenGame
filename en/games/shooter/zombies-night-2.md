## About

Zombies Night is a first person shooting game. This is a second part of this shooting game. In the dark night zombies come out and attack city. Your mission is to protect the city and kill all zombies. Use mouse to shoot and aim, R for reload, G for grenade and 1-6 number to change weapons. Enjoy!
