## About

Are you ready for the fight of the tanks? Fight and win against your friend or computer in Tank Mayhem game! Take advantage of the bonuses that support you. One of the strongest bonuses is Drone Mini Tank! It attacks the enemy fast! If the Drone Tank cannot hit the enemy, it will attack kamikaze. Other bonuses will also increase your firing power.
