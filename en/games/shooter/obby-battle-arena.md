## How to play

In the game, you can choose the game mode and maps on which you will play. There are many skins and additional accessories for the character.

Controls in the game on PC:

WASD - movement
1, 2, 3, 4 - change weapons
Shift - fast run (weapon is hidden)
R - reload
F - hide weapon (increases movement speed)

LMB - shoot
RMB - aim

Esc - game menu
Tab - leaderboard
