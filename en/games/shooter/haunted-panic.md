## About

Guard twelve haunted doors in Haunted Panic. Tap monsters before they strike, spare friendly ghosts and hammer bonus chests for gold. Travel through six Halloween worlds across 24 nights, unleash pumpkin blasts and face the Night Lords. Earn stars, unlock relics and improve your Arcade powers, or try Classic mode with one life.
