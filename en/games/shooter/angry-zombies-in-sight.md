Hold the skull pull the slingshot back and release to shoot The farther you pull the stronger the launch Hit enemies directly or break the structures around them Clear the level by defeating every enemy before you run out of attempts

## About

A single-player physics arcade game where you launch skulls from a slingshot and knock out the undead on each level. Every stage is about choosing the right angle, pull strength, and timing. Destructible structures, obstacles, and limited attempts turn each level into a compact challenge of aim and planning.
