## How to play

Played with arrow keys and spacebar

## About

In Mads Shark, you can think of yourself as a shark swimming in an ocean, but this shark has special powers. When you start the game, you can think that the shark has 50 bullets or 50 bombs. By directing your shark, you can hit the bombs that come to you with its special power or escape from them. The further you go the harder it gets and this sea is very dangerous. You can eat fish that are smaller than you and increase your score after eating the fish. While eating the fish, you should be careful with the ships and bombs. You can save the game with one wrong move. Have fun.
