## How to play

In Game modes that include Paddles simply move your mouse to move the paddle, in modes that allow it you can also click to shoot missiles. (Watch your ammo!)
When eliminated, simply select a sabotage and then where to deploy it with the mouse.

## About

ATTENTION, COMMANDER.

Eight cities surround the arena. The Core is firing meteors in every direction. Your job is simple: make sure they hit somebody else.

LIVE ROUND is an eight-player arcade game about deflecting incoming meteors away from your city and into your opponents. Fire blasts to change their course, move your deflector into position, and try to survive as the arena gets increasingly chaotic.

Between rounds, choose upgrades that change how you play. If your city gets wiped out, you’re not finished: You can stick around and sabotage the players still alive.

Play online, against bots, or in private rooms across five different game modes, including Defence, Paddle, Vault, Invaders and Belt.

Protect your city.
Send the meteors elsewhere.
Be the last player standing.
