## About

Narrow.One is a 3D multiplayer archery game developed by Pelican Party Studios. Take part in a fast-paced archery action where you and your teammates try to capture the enemy flag in a beautifully designed castle arena. You’re only allowed to use a bow and some arrows, so make sure your aim is on point and your reflexes are fast. Invite your friends to play with you and enjoy the thrill of facing your opponents together. Narrow.One is a ton of fun!
