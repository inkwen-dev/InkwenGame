## About

A satisfying physics puzzle shooter packed with 100 handcrafted stages. Take control of a powerful cannon, line up the perfect shot, and launch heavy cannonballs into towers, stacks, and tricky block formations. Your goal is simple: knock every block off the golden base plate before your shots run out.

Each stage challenges you to think carefully about angle, force, timing, and momentum. Some levels can be cleared with brute force, while others require smart ricochets, chain reactions, and precise hits on weak points. As the stages progress, the layouts become more complex, with tougher structures, awkward angles, and fewer chances to make mistakes.

With easy-to-learn controls, satisfying destruction, and clever physics-based puzzles, every shot matters. Aim well, make the blocks tumble, and clear all 100 stages to prove you are the ultimate cannon master.
