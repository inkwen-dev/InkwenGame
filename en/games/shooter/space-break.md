## How to play

Choose the type of your ship, pump it up and start playing!
The goal of the game is to pass the level passing through waves of enemies, at the end of each level you are waiting for a battle with the boss. Playing continuously you gain points, the further you pass the higher your record will be.

On your phone, use your finger to move from side to side to control the ship.
On your PC, use your mouse by holding down the left mouse button to control the ship.
