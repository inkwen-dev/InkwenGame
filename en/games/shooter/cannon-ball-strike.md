## About

Click or tap the screen to throw balls into the container below. Time your moves and try not to waste any balls. Your goal is to fill the bucket below with the colored balls in your arsenal. Aim wisely, shoot the ball and fill all the buckets with colors. Balls that you cannot get into the bucket will deduct points from your final score.
