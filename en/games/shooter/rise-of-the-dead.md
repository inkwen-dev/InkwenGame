## How to play

Keyboard Controls:  
    Movement:  Arrow keys (W A S D)
    Menu: Tab  
    Shoot:  left click 
    Reload:  R  
    Punch:  E
    Change weapon: Q
    Use prop 1:  G
    Use prop 2: F
    Zoom: Scrollbar
    

Mobile Controls:  
    Movement:  Virtual Joystick [Joystick Image]  
    Actions:  Tap corresponding buttons

## About

Rise of the Dead is a shooting horror game where you must fight off waves of zombies at every angle while completing task-driven quests. As the city falls into chaos, your mission is to uncover the cause of the apocalypse and fight to survive. Upgrade your weapons, buy new guns, and improve your skills to increase your chances. Play through different modes like sniper missions, survival challenges, and resource exploration. Dive into the intense horror of this post-apocalyptic world and battle to stay alive!
