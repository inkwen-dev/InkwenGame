## How to play

Goal: get the most kills before the three-minute timer ends. First to 15 kills wins Deathmatch; the first team to 30 wins Team Deathmatch.

DESKTOP
WASD: move. Mouse: look. Left click: shoot. Right click: aim down sights. R: reload. Q or mouse wheel: switch weapon. Shift: sprint. Ctrl: crouch. Space: jump. Tab or Esc: PDA menu and scoreboard.

MOBILE (landscape)
Left stick: move. Drag on the right side: look. Fire button: shoot. Aim button: aim down sights. On-screen buttons: reload, jump, crouch and switch weapon. PDA button: menu and scoreboard.

Tip: aim down sights for accurate shots at range.

## About

SWAT OPS: Body Camera is a first-person tactical shooter seen through an operator's body cam: lens distortion, camera shake and a gritty retro look. Drop into three-minute Deathmatch or 4v4 Team Deathmatch against bots on four maps, from a derelict foundry to a frontier village. Earn coins in every match to open cases, unlock 100+ weapon skins and charms, and climb the season battle pass. Plays in the browser on desktop and mobile.
