## How to play

WASD - Move Space - Jump C - Crouch LMB - Shoot RMB - Aim R - Reload P - Pause menu 1 2 3 - Select weapon

## About

Ultimate Strike features big arsenal of weapons which consists of twenty seven different guns and intense multiplayer battles across nine highly detailed maps, you spawn with random weapons loadout and your mission is to shoot all rival players in order to win.
