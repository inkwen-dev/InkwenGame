## About

Do you want to battle with the retro air fighters in different game modes? Your only goal in this game is to destroy the enemy air fighters and survive in both single player and two player game modes. In the "Campaign" game mode, your goal is to complete 12 levels by destroying enemy air fighters and by collecting medals. If you want, it is possible for you to fight against your friend in "Battle vs "Player" game mode. You can challenge your opponents by strengthening your defense by collecting weapons and bonuses inside of the game.
