## About

Super Zombie Shooter 2 throws you into a dangerous research facility packed with zombie hordes. Explore dark labs, abandoned corridors, and hidden rooms while fighting to survive. Each level challenges you to find the exit and move deeper into the complex. Unlock new weapons ranging from pistols to powerful rifles and experimental guns. Search for ammo and supplies, defeat tougher enemies, and uncover the mystery behind the outbreak as you fight your way to safety.
