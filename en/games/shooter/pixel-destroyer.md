## About

Pixel Blast is an exciting physics-based arcade puzzle game where your goal is to destroy colorful pixel shapes using bouncing balls. Dive into a world of pixel destruction! In this game, you&rsquo;ll need to use your wits and precision. Each level features a unique shape made of pixel blocks. Your objective is to destroy them all using a limited number of shots. The balls follow the laws of physics, bouncing off walls and obstacles, allowing you to reach even the most hard-to-access blocks.
