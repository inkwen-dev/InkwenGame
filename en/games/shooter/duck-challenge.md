## About

Almost at every fair, various interesting attractions are opened so that people can relax and have fun. One such entertainment is target shooting at the shooting range. Today we will go to the Duck Challenge shooting gallery to try our hand at shooting. Before you on the screen will appear targets in the form of moving ducks. Goals will move at a certain speed. You will need to direct a special sight at them and click the mouse. Thus you make a shot, and hitting the target will earn a certain number of points.
