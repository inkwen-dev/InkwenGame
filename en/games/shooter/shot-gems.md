## How to play

Tap on screen to shoot with gems

## About

The goal at this game is to fill the empty spots with gems. Every spot you need to fill with 10 gems. At some levels will be a obstacles so you need to avoid him or to use them to fill the spots that is hidden from you. Pass more levels as you can and fill more spots as you can with 1000 gems.
