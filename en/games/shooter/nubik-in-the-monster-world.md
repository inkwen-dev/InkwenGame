## How to play

In the settings menu, you can enable the auto-shoot feature, which is perfect for mobile gaming. You can also adjust mouse sensitivity for PC and swipe sensitivity for mobile directly from the main menu. Use the radar to easily find your way around the level!
Input Layout:
For PC:

    Mouse – Rotate camera

    Tab or Esc – Pause

    WASD – Move

    Left Mouse Button (LMB) – Shoot

    F or E – Interact

For Mobile:

    Swipe on screen – Rotate camera

    Joystick – Move

    Tap on screen – Shoot

## About

Nubik saved the kittens and defeated all the monsters! But just when you thought it was over, the monsters returned!

The evil Huggy has kidnapped all the kittens yet again! But this time, Nubik has armed himself with a whole new arsenal of weapons! In this game, you'll get your hands on an AK-47 assault rifle, an M16 rifle, a grenade launcher, a machine gun, and two incredibly powerful shotguns!

Help Nubik find the kittens to claim a reward of one million coins! Search for and open loot chests, upgrade your character, and blow up TNT blocks! What's more, Nubik's friend keeps getting caught by the monsters and thrown behind bars—help Nubik break him out of prison!
