## About

When we say try zombies, you dream of monsters that will try to eat right away, you live and make you one of them. Yet not all of them are like the others. George is a pretty zombie. Actually, he's a cowboy zombie. All he wants is to drive the pig and not harm all the people around him. Help the cowboy zombie use pigs for as long as possible by answering each math question correctly. This might be the coolest math game you will ever play! Things get harder as you progress, so test your math skills in this totally crazy game! Have fun.
