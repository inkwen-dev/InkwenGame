## About

A city of cubes and 2 heroes lost in the city are ready for the mission. Different creatures, zombies want to destroy you in different parts of the cube city. The only thing that will help you in this survival fight is powerful special weapons. In Cube Battle Royale you can fight one player or 2 Player! Decode devices in areas and leave the area within 20 seconds! After completing missions in all areas, a helicopter will come to your rescue.
