## About

Welcome to Token Strike &ndash; Fire Away and Smash the Crypto Targets! Lock, load, and aim your crypto cannon in this explosive arcade-style shooter where precision meets power! In Token Strike, your goal is simple: blast through waves of digital coins and crypto tokens with perfect aim and timing to rack up massive scores. How to Play: Aim your cannon at falling or floating crypto tokens. Tap to fire and smash tokens into bits to score points. Hit combos, chain explosions, and keep the coins from piling up.
