## About

Connect Hero is a fun puzzle game where you control a cannon to shoot bullets and push superhero symbol gems back to their correct positions. Each level is an interesting challenge that requires you to aim carefully, calculate the force, and cleverly utilize the terrain. Just one wrong shot can cause the gems to deviate from their trajectory. With simple yet challenging gameplay, Connect Hero offers a light and highly engaging entertainment experience!
