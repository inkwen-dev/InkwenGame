MineWar Soldiers vs Zombies is a game based on the world of minecraft but now a war between the living and the zombies begins, your duty is to position your different soldiers to face the zombies! You can choose between different soldiers with different skills they can shoot or fight with enemies. Make sure you collect all the coins and diamonds that the zombies leave when they die and to be able to call many more soldiers.
