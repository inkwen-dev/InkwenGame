## About

Yellow Ball is a puzzle game where you need to hit the yellow ball with small white ball that is on the center of the circle. Yellow ball can change the positions in eight points. The white ball will rotate around the axis, so you need to find right angle to shoot and to hit yellow ball. Try it is funny to play, you can make three mistakes, after that the game will over.
