## About

Fallen Figures is a game where you need to shoot in a figures who fall to you. Shoot and destroy figures before they fall down. You have ten lives, or you can miss 10 figures before game over. You can earn bonuses and achievements, so sometimes the figures will slow and you can destroy more figures at once. Shoot linear or from left to right to catch all fallen figures.
