## How to play

Defeat every enemy piece to clear the chamber and advance. Move your king by clicking or tapping a highlighted square, or use WASD / Arrow Keys. Select an enemy to aim, then click it again or press Space to shoot. Press R to reload and Shift to use Dash. Every move, shot and reload spends a turn, so watch enemy attack patterns and plan carefully. Clear chambers to earn upgrades, unlock weapons and survive all floors.

## About

Chess Tactics is a turn-based tactical roguelike where chess meets a shooter. Control a lone king armed with powerful weapons, move across the board, predict enemy attacks and eliminate hostile chess pieces. Clear increasingly difficult chambers, unlock new weapons, choose upgrades and build a different strategy each run.
