## About

Here is an online game about a fearless adventurer and treasure seeker, a brave warrior and a martial artist. There are five locations ahead of you, each of which consists of 50 levels. On the levels you have to fight a lot, using throwing weapons, as well as magical abilities. You can upgrade your hero and his equipment, insert runes and other improving items into it.
