## How to play

Click/Tap anywhere, to turn orbiting direction, satellite only fires at closeby asteroids.
Click on rocket button to use consumable rockets.
Click on upgrade buttons to upgrade your satellite.

## About

Single-finger orbital-defense arcade. Your drone orbits a fragile space station and auto-fires at incoming hazards — tap
to flip the orbit direction, that's your only control. Each stage layers in new threats: glue bombs you must shoot at
range, homing suicide drones you can't miss, EMP drones that silence your auto-fire on contact, healing orbiters that
protect bosses. Six upgrade tracks shape your build. 30+ stages, multi-boss waves, plus a fresh daily challenge for
bonus scrap every day.
