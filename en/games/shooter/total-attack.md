## About

Total Attack is a kids puzzle game. The goal of this game is to destroy the cubic which come from the top of the game. You can do this with the ball on the bottom. The cubes are different colors, they are blue and red. There are blue and red walls on the side of the game. The ball with which you shoot should be the same color as the cube to destroy the cube. Therefore, you will shoot the ball in the side walls and it will get the color of the wall. So a blue cube is destroyed with a blue ball, and a red cube with a red ball.
