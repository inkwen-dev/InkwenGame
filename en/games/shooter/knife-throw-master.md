## About

Knife Throw Master is a fun game of reflexes and skills in which you must show off your knife throwing skills! During each challenge you must throw all of your knives into a rotating fruit board and unlock special knives. You cannot hit any other knives attached to the spinning board. collect candies to earn more lives or watch ads to collect hearts. Enjoy!
