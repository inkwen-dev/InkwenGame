## How to play

Control your character, look around, and fight enemies on the map. Use weapons to defeat opponents, collect useful items and resources, then craft or upgrade your gear.
Stay aware of your surroundings: enemies can attack from different directions, so it is important to move, aim, and shoot at the right moment. The more accurately you play, the faster you can complete each level and move forward.
Your main goal is to survive, defeat all enemies, and complete the mission. Upgrade your weapons, unlock new options, and use the best arsenal for every battle.

## About

«Gun Craft and Fight» is a dynamic first-person shooter where you fight enemies, collect resources, and craft new weapons to complete combat missions. Upgrade your arsenal, choose the right weapon for each situation, and clear levels where reaction, accuracy, and smart tactics matter.
The game combines classic FPS gameplay with crafting and progression elements. Explore locations, defeat enemies, unlock new opportunities, and become stronger with every battle. The further you progress, the more dangerous the enemies become — so it is time to craft more powerful weapons and use them to the fullest.
«Gun Craft and Fight» is perfect for fans of shooters, action games, weapons, survival, and upgrade-based gameplay.
