## About

A fun action game where you are the Viking and you direct your dragon through swarms of enemies to reach the end of each level. There is one big bonus loop within each level that contains a plethora of enemies, but there is only time for one before the bonus ends! The game ends when all of your life and energy runs out. Each weapon gives a different bonus to the Vikings' score so be sure to try them all on each level!
