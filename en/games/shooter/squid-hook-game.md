Use mouse or touch pad to play this game

## About

In Squid Hook Game, pull the Squid Game character on the opposite side to your side with the rope. Take advantage by sending the rope accurately to the other side. You can buy 5 different Squid game characters by accumulating the points you will earn at the end of each game. You can increase the action by purchasing dolls, players and workers. Grab your hook and fight against other players! Have fun.
