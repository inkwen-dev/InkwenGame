## About

Zombie Mission 8 continues the adventure from where it left off. After the space station, Zombies invade the Mars Colony this time. Scientists and workers on Mars have been taken prisoner by Zombies. You must use the necessary weapons and vehicles to save them. Also, do not forget to collect the objects in the levels. A warning will come before meteor streams. After the warning, you must immediately go to the shield cabin.
