Use Mouse Left Click or Use Left or Right Arrow Keys

## About

Control the cannon to shoot the enemy, be careful, because it includes civilians. If you shoot civilians then the game ends. If you let the enemy enter the base, the game ends. Military Defense is simple to play, tap the right for shoot the right line, the left screen shoot the left line. Simple yet challenging gameplay that count on your quick reaction.
