## How to play

The world is fully destructible with realistic physics: any block that loses its connection to the ground will fall and break apart.

Game Modes:
• Sandbox: Experiment with building and destruction without limits.
• Capture the Flag: Seize the enemy's flag and bring it back to your base.
• Team Deathmatch: Eliminate more opponents than the enemy team.

Controls (Keyboard & Mouse):
• TAB – Pause menu
• WASD – Move
• LMB (Left Mouse Button) – Shoot / Build
• RMB (Right Mouse Button) – Aim down sights / Dig
• 1-5 – Select item
• SPACE – Jump
• C – Crouch
• R – Reload
• J – Toggle minimap size
• M – Open map
• T – Open chat

## About

Red & Blue: Online. Build! Destroy! Fight! — is a fast-paced voxel multiplayer shooter with fully destructible, physics-driven maps — buildings collapse and debris change the battlefield. Build bases and fortifications, carve tactical paths, and turn the environment into a weapon. Core modes: Sandbox (creative freedom), Capture the Flag (team tactics), and Team Deathmatch (intense PvP). Quick matchmaking, custom rooms, localized UI and optimization for web/mobile platforms. Join the fights — build, destroy, win!
