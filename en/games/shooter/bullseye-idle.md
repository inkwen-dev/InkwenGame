## How to play

Tap or click a target to shoot it, and press M to mute. Your guns fire on their own, so put every coin back into more guns and more power. Keep tapping to fill the FEVER meter for double damage, push deeper for tougher targets and bosses, and win timed trials for permanent trophy upgrades.
