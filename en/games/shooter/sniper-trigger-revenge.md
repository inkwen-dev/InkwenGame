## About

Stickman: Snipers Revenge is a small puzzle game. Your task is to kill all opponents with limited ammunition in stock and collect all the stars. The difficulty is that it is not always easy to reach the enemy, you will have to use the ricochet and surrounding objects to achieve the main goal. Also, your hero will receive money for completing levels, which will allow you to buy weapons and skins.
