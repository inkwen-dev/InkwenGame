## About

RGB Shooter is a fast-paced 2D action shooting game where color-matching meets arcade chaos! Switch between red, green, and blue bullets to destroy enemies of the same color. Use your reflexes to survive endless waves of colorful attackers. As you level up, the game becomes faster and more challenging. Activate your Ulti ability to fire random-colored bullets and turn the tide of battle when things get intense. Simple controls, vibrant visuals, and addictive gameplay make RGB Shooter a perfect experience for action and arcade fans!
