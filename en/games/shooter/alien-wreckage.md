## About

A lonely explorer travels across the outer space His goal is to gather the most ancient artifacts in the universe He is ready to face any kind of alien menace in order to do it He will explore abandoned alien wreckage, looking for precious treasures Not a single emotion, no empathy for the creatures living on those wrecks He just wants one thing: become the most famous explorer of the universe Alien Wreckage is a fast-paced 2D multidirectional shooter with a retro pixel-art style, where exploration, combat, and risk-taking go hand in hand.
