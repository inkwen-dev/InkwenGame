## About

As you enter the 2021 New Year, you must protect your gifts. The enemy characters who come to the gift castle will attack to steal your gifts. With the arrow in your hand, you must hunt them and protect your gifts. If 3 gifts are stolen, the game will fail and you will restart. Defend the Gift Stronghold in New Year 2021!
