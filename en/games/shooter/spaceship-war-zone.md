## About

pace Shuttle War is an immersive and exciting space shuttle game. While playing this fun game, you&rsquo;ll have to tap and swipe the screen to take control of the space shuttle and destroy your enemies with laser fire. As an endless one-touch game, it offers a game experience that gets progressively harder and harder. In the game, you will face many enemies as you move through the depths of space with your space shuttle. Enemy ships, space stations and obstacles will test your skills and reflexes. By making quick and accurate decisions, neutralize the enemies and enjoy the visual feast full of explosive effects.
