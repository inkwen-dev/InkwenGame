## About

Hunt Owl in the magical forest by shooting sling Game story begins with the land of Willowvale, where the king Alden goes for a night hunt with his trusted sling. He wants to hunt Owls. Player has to shoot the Owls, where they hide in blocks and barriers. By shooting the balls (limited qty.) with sling you have to break all of those barriers and hunt Owl. Be Careful! Balls are in limited quantities so try to use minimum and hunt. If you like genres such as adventure, action, rpg, sling shooting, quests then this game is for you. Features of this game: High Quality graphics Game size is minimal Super simple controls Interesting quests
