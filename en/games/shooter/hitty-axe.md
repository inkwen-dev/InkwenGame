## About

Hitty Axe is a simple click & smash casual game. Tap the screen to throw your axe , trying to hit the target you aim at. Sometimes your target was at fixed place, sometimes they will moving, beware at the bomb target, if you hit them, you will lose the game! Use your limited number of axes carefully and win the game!
