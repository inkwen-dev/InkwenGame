## About

Fruit Shoot Boom is a funny archery game in which you have to collect as much fruit as possible by skewering as many apples, pears, peaches, and other pieces of fruit with a single arrow. Keep firing arrows to stack up the bonus points. Can you defeat the giant fruit boss at the end of every level by bombarding each fruit king with arrows?
