## About

Starship Ducks is a low-gravity lunar shooter where your own gun is your worst enemy &mdash; every shot shoves your fat duck paratrooper backward. Pick one of four jetpacks, drop by pod onto endless moon terrain, and survive escalating waves of drones, gunships, tanks and bug swarms. Between waves grab a 2-pick-1 systems upgrade; kills spill SMGs, shotguns, health packs and shields. How many waves can you survive?
