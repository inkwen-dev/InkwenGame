## How to play

Use WASD or mouse to shoot or touch the screen

## About

Prepare your battle weapons for battle and make your way through the barrier of militants so carefully that the enemies will not notice you. Walk through the corridors of the base and look for hostages in the rooms. Remember that if they find you, you will have to fight not for life, but for death. Break the walls to pass obstacles and destroy everything that does not give you a turn. Use accuracy in shooting, if you find an enemy warrior.
