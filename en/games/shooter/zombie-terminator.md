## How to play

Your mission is to eliminate all zombies and survive until the end of each level. Move carefully to avoid being surrounded while aiming and shooting enemies. Defeated zombies drop coins and power-ups that can improve your weapons and survival abilities. As the game progresses, stronger enemies will appear, so stay alert and become the ultimate zombie terminator!

PC Controls:
↑ (Up Arrow): Move up
↓ (Down Arrow): Move down
← (Left Arrow): Move left
→ (Right Arrow): Move right
Space: Shoot / Fire
Shift: Speed boost

Mobile Controls:
Joystick: Drag to move in any direction
Shoot Button: Tap to attack zombies
Boost Button: Tap for a temporary speed boost

## About

Zombie Terminator is an intense zombie shooting survival game set in a post-apocalyptic city overrun by the undead.
