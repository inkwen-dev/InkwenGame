## About

Hellforge: Demon Protocol is a 2D side-scrolling run-and-gun campaign with 20 industrial missions and progressive difficulty. Fight patrol soldiers, shield troops, gatling brutes, mech hounds and berserkers. Survive 16 trap types, discover secret pickups, collect six weapons, armor, grenades and shields, and defeat ten animated bosses that return as elite enemies in later levels. Checkpoints, three lives, dodging and two weapon slots reward skillful play. Web Dev <a href='https://www.poki.com.es/'>https://www.poki.com.es/</a>
