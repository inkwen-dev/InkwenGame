## About

Tanks for the Nation is a fast-paced 2D tank battle game set in the era of World War II. Choose from dozens of legendary war machines &mdash; from light Pz. I and T-26 tanks to mighty Tigers, Panthers and IS-3 heavies. Upgrade your garage, buy new models, collect coins and fight across four different battlefields. Command 4 vehicle classes &mdash; heavy, medium and light tanks plus tank destroyers &mdash; each with unique armor, speed, fire rate and penetration. Realistic combat: shells ricochet, armor has weak spots (the rear is the most vulnerable!), and wrecked tanks block your line of fire. Every battle is a mix of strategy and adrenaline.
