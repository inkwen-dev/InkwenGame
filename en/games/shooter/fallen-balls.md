## About

Touch the screen to shoot with the white ball. When the white ball is over yellow ball you need to shoot or to touch the screen again to destroy yellow ball. When two balls are matched you need to tap the screen to destroy the ball. Try to destroy more yellow balls as you can before you miss the target. Get best score and have fun.
