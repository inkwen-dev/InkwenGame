## About

Forest Fortune is a cozy 3D leaf-collecting simulator set in a charming hand-painted cartoon woodland. Walk through a lush forest filled with oak trees, wooden fences, vegetable beds, a rustic cabin, and a cozy picnic area. Aim your crosshair at leaves and fruits scattered across the ground, click to pick them up, and carry them to the compost pen to sell for coins. Spend your coins on upgrades &mdash; a bigger basket, faster hands, the ability to grab multiple leaves at once, or premium compost for higher sell prices.
