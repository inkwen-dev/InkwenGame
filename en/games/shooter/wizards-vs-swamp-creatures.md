## About

You’re still expecting your admission letter to the school of wizards? While you’re waiting, you may want to try and give yourself a foretaste of your soon-to-be great life as a magnificent sorcerer. Between hunting dragons and racing on your broom, you’ll also have to… hunt creatures of flabby jelly in the swamps around your tower. It’s finally time to put both of your feet in the mud and to launch fireballs on those little vermin.
