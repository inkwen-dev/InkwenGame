## About

Aircraft Shooter, you will face five different enemies, each with their own shooting abilities. Some enemies will fire rockets, some will shoot bullets, and some will use lasers to attack. In addition, some levels have powerful bosses that require special strategies and skills to defeat them. You will need to use your weapons and avoid enemy attacks to complete each level and defeat the bosses where they appear. The game has a shop to improve the plane, weapons and increase the number of lives.
