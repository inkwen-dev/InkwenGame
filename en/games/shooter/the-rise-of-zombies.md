## About

This is a thrilling real-time survival strategy game where you control a growing horde of zombies against humans. The world is alive with day-night cycles: during the day, humans shoot with higher accuracy, while at night they are weaker and less aggressive. Your goal is to infect humans, expand your army, and destroy survivors before they wipe you out. Meteors occasionally fall&mdash;collect them to unlock mutations that boost zombie speed and strength. Every match is dynamic: humans roam, panic, and fight back intelligently. Zombies infect survivors on close contact, instantly turning them into allies.
