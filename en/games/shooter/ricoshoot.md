## About

In this game, you need to aim the ball into the pipe. Easy, right? Not at all. You will have to find a perfect angle to make your ball ricochet into the pipe. But be careful, your ball has limited hitpoints, and each time the ball ricochets of the green walls, your ball loses 1 hitpoints. Can you find the perfect shot for all 30 levels?
