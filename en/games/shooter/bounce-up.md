## How to play

Launch balls onto the board and earn coins by hitting pegs and landing in scoring slots. Use coins to buy upgrades between runs, improve your ball setup, and create stronger combos. Your goal is to earn as many points and coins as possible in a single run.
Controls: move the mouse and left-click or press Space to launch a ball. On mobile, tap the main game area to launch. Press Esc to pause the game.
