## How to play

Goal: descend as deep as you can, reach the exit of each level and survive the bosses.

Phone: left stick — move and jump, right stick — aim and fire (auto-aim is on by default). Buttons on the right: grenade, cutter, weapon swap.
PC: A/D — move, W — jump, Space — jetpack, mouse — aim and fire, right mouse button — grenade, 1–4 — weapons, F — cutter, Esc — pause.

The level goal and the “rift hungers” counter are in the top-right corner; the arrow at the screen edge points to the goal. Spend gold in the Arsenal (pause → Arsenal, or after a level). The cutter slices any rock but barely hurts enemies.

## About

The colony of Sorrow-IV has gone silent. The Ash Legion sends its last executor to the very bottom, where something ancient and hungry has awoken.

ASH OF THE DEPTHS is a dark sci-fi shooter where every pixel of the world obeys physics. Rock collapses, sand pours, water flows, magma cools into stone and gas explodes from a single spark.

WHAT IS INSIDE
• 100 levels and a 6-chapter story campaign with two endings.
• 7 biomes: flooded mines, frozen cisterns, a meat manufactory, toxic marshes, crystal veins, the forges of the abyss and the bottom.
• 15 enemy types and bosses: the infected, gunners, snipers, shield bearers, borer worms, the Flesh Smith and the Womb Mother.
• 5 weapons for different situations and a plasma cutter to carve your own path.
• 10 pieces of ancient armor that turn the hero into an armored slayer.
• The Arsenal: gear and upgrades for gold — a helper drone, a fireproof suit, an endless rebreather and more.
• Daily Descent, ambushes, hidden caches, an endless mode and a matter sandbox.

Fully translated into 15 languages. Progress is saved automatically; sign in to keep it in the cloud.
