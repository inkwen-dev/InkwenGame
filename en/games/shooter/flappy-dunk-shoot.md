## About

Flappy Dunk Shoot is amazing game with classic and customise Balls. Tap the screen to shoot rings. No more dunk, only swishes! Pass all hoops and try new challenges. Try to dunk hoops to make the challenge completed. This is the remix of these types of hopping ball shooting and flappy shoot with dunking fun. One shot, one dunk Shoot hoops and dunk in this bouncy flappy ball shoot game. To be in the ranking, don't forget to share your score!
