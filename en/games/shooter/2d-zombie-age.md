## About

Safeguard your residence against the encroaching 2D Zombie Age. These creatures are dangerous and cunning, so exercise caution, aim with precision, and prevent them from nearing you. Each stage presents three or more onslaughts teeming with zombies, including more oversized, swifter variants that require rapid elimination. Check your inventory for tools that can simplify your task. Employ grenades, rockets, poison, and snowflakes to decelerate and obliterate the hordes.
