## About

Become the archer hero by saving the beautiful and sweet princess who is in trouble. You will face several enemies and warriors along the way, try to hit as levers precisely to free it. There are some balloons scattered throughout the scenarios, each balloon has an important function for the game, but be careful, as some of them can be dangerous! Have a good time.
