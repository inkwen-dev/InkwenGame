## How to play

Desktop: Press SPACE to jump and X to shoot. 
Mobile: Tap the right side of the screen to shoot, and the left side to jump.

## About

Mad Day Special throws you straight into an all-out alien invasion of Earth, where only speed, firepower, and sheer madness stand between you and total destruction. Race across hills, smash through walls, and blast alien enemies as you fight to rescue your beloved pet octopus, Fluffy. Part racing game, part shooter, this is an action racing experience that won’t let you down.

This is a streamlined web version of the popular Mad Day game. With quick loading, simple controls, and addictive progression, it’s built for both short sessions and long, high-score-chasing runs.

With its blend of racing and shooting, Mad Day Special delivers chaotic fun and that irresistible “one more run” feeling. Drive hard, shoot fast, and don’t let the aliens take Fluffy.
