## About

Mr. Toni is in Miami City, and he is ready to take down some enemies so that he can conquer the city. Shoot all of the people in each level to complete your mission. The bullets ricochet off of walls, but your ammo is limited so aim and shoot carefully. Finish all 20 levels to become the biggest gangster in Miami in this fun online physics based shooter game.
