## About

Help medic Tess keep Omar and the convoy alive across 23 stages in five campaign routes. Fight through city streets, carnival grounds, bayou paths, a storm and the final bridge. Aim your rifle, reload between threats, shove infected away and use medical supplies wisely. Rescue survivors and secure fuel while checkpoints preserve the journey. Web Dev <a href='https://www.snakegame.com.es/'>https://www.snakegame.com.es/</a> Illustrated environments, varied infected and an original tense soundtrack support this 2D survival action game on desktop and mobile.
