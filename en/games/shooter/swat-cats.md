## How to play

Controls
W = move forward
A = move left
S = move backward
D = move right
Lctrl = fire
R = recharge
Right mouse = aim mode
Left mouse = fire
1 = switch to general gun
2 = switch to pistol
3 = switch knife
4 = switch to bomb
5 = switch to heal
T = chat
C = sit down
Space = jump
M = maps
Tab = pause
F = pick up weapon

## About

SWAT Cats is an online shooter set in a world where every hero is a battle cat. Ready for purr-fect battle with wild action? Jump in, grab your helmet, and join the fight.

You can pick from 4 cat operatives for now. We have 4 battle cats, but we can add more!

Choose Your Operative:
- Private Nine-Lives
- Corporal Strongpaw
- Sergeant Silent-Purr
- Captain Scratch

You start as a Private. In later online matches, you can switch to other fighters!

SWAT Cats features several comfy, fast-paced maps that are great for action and shooting.

The best part of online games is playing with friends. We made this shooter multiplayer, so team up and hop into matches together.

Want to chat with other players? The game has an in-game chat.

SWAT Cats shooter is multiplatform! So you can enjoy the game both on mobile and PC!

Pick your cat, grab your gun, and dive into the fight!

See you on the battlefield, meow. Combat "meow"!
