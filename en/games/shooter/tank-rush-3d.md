## About

Tank Rush 3D is actually not a tank driving but a collecting arcade game under a 3D game engine. Your mission is to help your tank overpass the obstacles and arrive at the finish area on the platform track at each level. If possible, I strongly suggest you to collect over 10 coins before collecting bullets, you could get stable earnings and enough ammo at the same time!
