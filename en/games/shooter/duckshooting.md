## About

Duck Shooting is an exciting and adventurous shooting game based on the amusement parks. When you enter the colorful shooting range to shoot there is floating a duck with a crown, and there is a pouty red and offended bird. But there are ducks with a cross! Your task is to hit the ducks and monsters. But be careful to hit the wrong duck because when you do it means that you cannot shoot at them because the points are reduced! Goodluck!
