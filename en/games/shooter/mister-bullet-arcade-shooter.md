## How to play

To win - you need to eliminate all opponents on the level.
Controls:
PC - use the mouse to control the camera. Shooting is automatic when the sight is aimed at the enemy.
Mobile - use your finger to control the camera. Shooting is automatic when the sight is aimed at the enemy.

## About

ou are a cool special agent Mr. Bullet. You are sent on the most dangerous missions in the world. Only Mr. Bullet knows how to use all types of weapons perfectly. Only Mr. Bullet can sneak into the enemy base unnoticed.
