## How to play

The goal of the game is to survive and protect your transport from hordes of zombies attacking from all sides and wanting to deprive you of a chance to escape by killing you or destroying your last hope of salvation.
WASD - movement
Left mouse button - shooting
Right mouse button - aiming
1-6 (depending on the number of weapons available) and the mouse wheel - weapon selection
R - reloading
G - grenade
Tab - menu / pause
Shift - running
Space - jump / explosive jump
E - push away opponents

After each level, you can buy new or improve existing weapons, increase the strength of the machine, buy grenades, as well as buy super-powers: Repelling opponents and explosive jump.
