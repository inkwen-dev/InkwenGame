## About

In this game, you need to throw Easter eggs in the balloon basket. Try to put more eggs as you can in the basket. Take a position, aim, and shoot to the basket. But the rabbit that is in the balloon basket is a bad pilot and the balloon will not be stable at all. He will go up and down and left and right. For that reason will be harder to throw the eggs inside the basket. But that is the interest in this game. And after all, if you make a mistake and drop the eggs on the floor the new life will be born. The little chicks will hatch from the eggs.
