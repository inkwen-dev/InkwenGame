## About

Jump into the war zone and get ready to complete different tasks to lead your side to victory. Shoot down enemies while you search for three hidden keys and hidden weapons within each level. After securing all of the items try to reach the exit before the timer expires. Try to beat all action packed levels in this fun platform game. How to play: Use touch controls or Arrow keys to move, Spacebar to shoot
