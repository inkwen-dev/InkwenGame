## About

EpicShooter3D is built with one simple goal: to be fast, smooth, simple, and fun &mdash; no installs, no logins, no waiting. Just open the game and start shooting. This game will always be free. No downloads. No accounts. Just pure browser FPS action. Even though I created this game, its not just my game anymore. A lot of the features you see today came directly from player feedback, and I genuinely value every suggestion. I promise to keep listening and improving the game with the community in mind. At its heart, EpicShooter3D is meant to be a stress-buster &mdash; but also a place where friends and total strangers can compete, climb the leaderboard.
