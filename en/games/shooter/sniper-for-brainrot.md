## About

Use your sniper rifle to hit the balloon above a Brainrot, instantly transform into it, and race to the Safe Zone before a massive tsunami catches you. The farther you shoot, the rarer the Brainrot you can collect but the faster the wave becomes. Build your collection, expand your Base, unlock powerful sniper rifles, customize your character, and compete with friends in this addictive Brainrot adventure featuring 87 unique collectibles!
