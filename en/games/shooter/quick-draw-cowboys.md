## How to play

Become the fastest cowboy in the duel. Watch the screen carefully, wait for the draw cue, then choose the correct direction before your opponent does. Win duels to earn coins, unlock stronger guns and new cowboys, and survive tougher enemies. You can also enter Online PvP to match with another player in a real-time quick draw battle.

Desktop Controls:
W: Shoot upward.
S: Shoot downward.
A: Shoot left.
D: Shoot right.

Local PvP Controls:
Player 1 uses W/A/S/D.
Player 2 uses the Arrow Keys.
Enter / Spacebar: Confirm or continue on some screens.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Direction Buttons: Tap the matching arrow direction when the duel cue appears.
Menu Buttons: Tap to choose Campaign, Local PvP, Online PvP, Shop, and upgrades.
