Use w s a d to move Use space to collect weapon Use g to drop weapon Use p to pause the game Left mouse button - shoot melee attack Right mouse button - special melee attack Mouse wheel

## About

Try to survive on the surface of an unfriendly planet until rescuers arrive. A formidable world full of predators will meet you. Prove that you are the deadliest of them all! The game features the level generator, so no 2 play-throughs are ever the same!
