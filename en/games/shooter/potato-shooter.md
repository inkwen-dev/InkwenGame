Roguelike survivor where a cursed potato faces relentless enemies. Upgrade your arsenal, dodge projectiles, find artifacts that break the game. Die. Try again.

## How to play

MOVEMENT
• Use Arrow Keys or WASD to move around
• Press SPACE to dash—quickly dodge enemy attacks
• Avoid enemy projectiles and contact damage

COMBAT
• Your equipped weapons fire automatically at nearby enemies
• Defeat all enemies in a room to unlock the exit door
• Collect coins dropped by defeated enemies

UPGRADES & ITEMS
• Visit PEDESTALS to buy new weapons and artifacts
• Use ANVILS to upgrade your weapons (increases damage & attack speed)
• Collect ARTIFACTS to gain special passive abilities
• Pick up HEARTS to restore health

NAVIGATION
• Move between rooms through DOORS
• Rooms get progressively harder as you go deeper
• You have one life—defeat enemies and survive!

QUICK TIPS
• Use DASH to quickly escape tight situations
• Focus on killing enemies efficiently to earn coins faster
• Prioritize upgrading weapons over buying new ones
• Artifacts stack their effects—build synergies
• Keep moving to avoid enemy attacks
• The deeper you go, the better the rewards

GOAL
Survive as long as you can and dig as deep as possible into the endless dungeons!
