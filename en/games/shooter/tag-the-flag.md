## About

Tag the Flag is a non-violent action shooter game. Proudly play as the Blue Soldier while shooting and defending against the Red Soldiers. Find the Red Flag in enemy territory before it's too late! Fight for Blue glory. Become a stealthy soldier, because the enemy will non-stop tag your flag. Who will win and who will be defeated? Winning is fun in Tag the Flag. Enjoy the game! Features Shooting game without violence Two action strategies, offensive shooting vs defensive shielding Large arena
