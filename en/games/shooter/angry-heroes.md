## About

Are those here who miss crazy angry birds? The adventure with Angry Heroes is back. Try to knock down your enemies from the tower where they are hiding by throwing angry birds. In the game, which consists of a total of 20 different chapters, each chapter will be more challenging than the previous one. You have to do calculations like solving puzzles. How many monster enemies can you shoot with one bird? Place your angry bird on the slingshot. Focus on your goal by pulling it backwards. When you are sure that the enemy is aiming at you, let go and let the angry bird fly. Here's the exact hit! Time will stand still in the fun and much-loved game. We are sure that you will quickly pass the sections. Your goal is to have fun. Let's click to play a game with these colorful birds and let the fun begin!
