## About

Its time to fire your weapon, Steve. Fire your gun and start jumping. You can jump by shooting and go wherever you want. Draw your bow and shoot the arrow. You must collect all the boxes. Collect the boxes and kill all the invisible monsters. You cant see them until you collect the boxes. So, collect the boxes first and then hunt down all the monsters!
