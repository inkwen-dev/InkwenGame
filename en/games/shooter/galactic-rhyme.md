## About

Galactic Rhyme is a simple fun vocabulary game for kids to practice words and phrases that rhyme. Help build their skills as they try to match the words that rhyme. Dodge, avoid and shoot the words that don't rhyme. Watch out for those explosive bombs and barrels! Hurry before the time runs out! More updates are coming soon so stay tuned.
