## How to play

WASD or the arrow keys to move left, right, forward, and backward.
Mouse to aim, click or press Space to throw mail, and press Shift to dash while moving.

## About

Welcome to Dispatch Panic, where every delivery counts! Race through a chaotic office, aim your mail at waiting desks, and dodge obstacles before your boss loses patience.
Chain successful deliveries to build combos, handle urgent requests, and grab coffee and power-ups to keep your shift going. Missed deliveries and collisions raise the boss’s anger—how long can you survive?
