## How to play

PC - use "SPACE" to FIRE
Mobile - Tap screen to FIRE

You exist in an arena with other guns!  shoot them down to win, last man standing takes the win. Each shot taken add recoil to your gun which allows you to move around the space. Get ready, take aim and enjoy

## About

Fast-paced arcade shooter where every tap fires your weapon and flips it through the air, turning recoil into movement. Master ricochets, chain precise shots, and stay in control as you bounce around the screen in chaotic, skill-based action.

Switch between different weapons like SMGs and shotguns, each with unique feel and strategy, and adapt on the fly to survive longer. Grab powerful upgrades such as rockets for explosive shots and shields for protection, adding depth to every run.

Simple to learn but hard to master, the game delivers nonstop action, satisfying mechanics, and endless replayability.
