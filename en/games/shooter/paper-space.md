## How to play

Tap on screen to control the space ship or use spacebar to shoot and arrow keys to control the ship

## About

Paper Space is a space game where you have to dodge lasers from your opponents and shoot other spaceships and rockets. Be careful not to collide with any of your opponents or your ship will be destroyed and the game will end. Collect stars and destroy as many enemy ships as you can.
