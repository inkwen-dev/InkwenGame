## How to play

Desktop Controls:
W = speed up
S = slow down
A = left turn
D = right turn
Q = sharp left turn
E = sharp right turn
J = fire
K = sub-weapon
Space = pull up
Shift = roll
R = reload

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Move in all directions by dragging the joystick.
Fire Button: Tap to shoot.
Pitch Button: Pull up.
Roll Button: Let the airplane roll.

## About

Dogfight is a thrilling air combat game where you control a battle plane in intense aerial battles. Perform maneuvers like looping, rolling, and turning as you engage in dynamic dogfights. Experience the rush of real air battles and showcase your piloting skills.
