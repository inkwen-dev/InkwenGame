## About

Get ready for action in Full Space Shooter! Take control of your combat spaceship and face endless waves of aliens and enemy ships in this fast-paced classic space arcade. Dodge enemy fire, grab power-ups in the heat of battle, and collect diamonds to make your ship increasingly powerful. As you progress, the enemies will get faster and the bosses more challenging. Manage your energy wisely, visit the shop to upgrade your arsenal, and jump into the Ranked mode to put your reflexes to the test. Survive as long as possible to achieve the highest score and dominate the global leaderboard!
