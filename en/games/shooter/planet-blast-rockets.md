## How to play

Goal. Tune your rocket, choose an impact point, and destroy cities, continents, and planets. Balance fuel, mass, and blast power, upgrade modules, and unlock new targets across the galaxy.

Controls. On desktop, press and hold the left mouse button to adjust rocket parameters, choose a target, and launch. On mobile, perform the same actions with taps and finger gestures. After each launch, review the result, improve weaker parameters, and prepare the next rocket.
