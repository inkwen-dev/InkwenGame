## About

Treasure Hunt is an action shooting game. The army of gem blocks is coming to chase your base. To stop them, you need to eliminate the rows of blocks one by one. See how long you can stop them! You can eliminate a row of block by shooting your block on its identical block. Longer you fight, more points you will gain.
