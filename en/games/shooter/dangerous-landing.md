## About

Dangerous Landing is a new version of the classic Blitz game, your plane's fuel is running out, the only hope of salvation is to create a landing strip, luckily the buildings below are empty and ready to be destroyed, tap on the screen or press the space key on your keyboard to drop your bombs, more and more buildings will be added to each level, how long can you resist?
