## About

Do you feel ready to play Desert Shooter? This great game has the feature of being a multiplayer game. So you can share your trump card with your friends in the desert arena. Each of the 3 different game characters has different weapons. Choose the game hero that is close to you and start the game. Shoot with your gun, use your bombs at the appropriate moments and try to score the most points. You have to move a lot in the arena and use your weapon perfectly.
