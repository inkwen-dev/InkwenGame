## About

Monster Haul is a fast pixel-art monster-survival brawler. Move, aim, and blast endless waves of slimes, bats, ghosts, goblins, spiders, bombers and more. Survive and draft from 60+ wild weapons &mdash; blasters, boomerangs and chain lightning to flamethrowers, saw blades, meteor strikes and the rare Black Hole. Explore 3 randomly chosen worlds (Verdant Wilds, Ashen Crater, Frostbite Tundra) and face deadly elites and bosses that unlock as you push deeper. Level up, stack powerful upgrades, beat your high score and share it. Easy to pick up, hard to put down &mdash; great on desktop and mobile.
