Use A S D and W to move and the mouse to aim and shoot Press P to pause and restart the game

## About

Horde Zombie is a shooting game, in which you will have to kill the zombies that will appear. Each kill will give you points, which you can use to buy new weapons and new bullets. Don't let the zombies touch you or you will die; And be careful, because as time progresses, the zombies become stronger and faster.
