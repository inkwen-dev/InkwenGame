## How to play

Mouse/Touch: Aim through scope
Left Click/Tap: Fire
Right Click/Hold: Zoom
Keyboard: WASD to pan view, R to reload, G for grenades, N for nukes, ESC to exit to lobby
Gamepad: Full controller support - S Sound enable . disable

Mobile/Tablet touch to fire (auto aims) , touch for the weapons

## About

Retro Sniper is a fast-paced 2D sniper shooter with retro arcade aesthetics. Take on precision elimination missions across 42 levels spanning urban streets, tropical jungles, frozen tundras, deep ocean, outer space, and underwater enemy bases.

What makes it unique:

Diverse environments – Fight on moving trains, infiltrate neon cyber cities, storm maximum-security prisons, and dive into underwater combat against sharks and giant squids
Protect civilians – Mermaids, hostages, and innocent bystanders must be spared or face heavy penalties
Escalating chaos – Start with a sniper rifle, unlock machine guns, grenades, and tactical nukes as enemies bring helicopters, jets, tanks, and UFOs
