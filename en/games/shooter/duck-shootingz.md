## About

Duck Shooting is a fun shooting puzzle game where you aim carefully and use bouncing bullets to hit every duck. Calculate the perfect angle, ricochet shots off walls, and use the environment to complete each level. As you progress, explosive barrels, switches, and tricky obstacles add new challenges. With simple controls, physics-based puzzles, and strategic shooting gameplay, Duck Shooting offers a fun and addictive experience!
