## How to play

The controls in King of Fishing are simple and easy to learn. Just tap or click on the fish to shoot your net and try to catch them. Every successful catch rewards you with coins that can be used to upgrade your fishing power and improve your chances of capturing bigger and rarer sea creatures.

As you earn more coins and catch higher-value fish, you will climb the ranks and unlock new ocean areas filled with exciting challenges. Keep tapping, keep upgrading, and compete to become the King of Fishing!

## About

Dive into the thrilling underwater world of King of Fishing!
