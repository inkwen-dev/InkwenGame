## About

Combat Penguin, yes you heard right. In this game, the penguin is in combat with different types of enemies. He will shoot at snowmen, robots, and warriors with vehicles. But there will be different shields that will protect the warriors. The penguin needs to protect the house. After each wave of attack, you can go to the shop to improve your weapon, buy new shields, and buy protection for the house. Аs the game is in progress at every next level you need to fight with more enemies. So every next level is harder to play. You need to earn and use some of the offered help that can help you in the battle with the enemies.
