## About

Crazy Counter Attack is an online game that combines thrilling gameplay with strategic challenges. Take the opportunity to shoot and attack the enemy by turning somersaults to avoid the enemies pursuit. The accumulated gold rewards can be used to buy different offensive guns in the store. Added turntable and check-in functions, come and unlock different game props!
