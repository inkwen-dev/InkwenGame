## About

"Zombie Shooter" ("Lemmy vs Zombies") - is a pixeled 2D bloodbath of zombie apocalypse, inspired by pixel graphics of retro games. The old world drowned in chaos and the cities crowded with living deads... A leak of secret military virus or deserved punishment of the humanity?...You haven't time to ask questions, your only target is to survive in that bloody hell as longer as you can! The only advice: always keep moving and kill zombies faster, not allowing them to come closer. By the way, this funny but really difficult survival shooter can become a serious challenge even for an experienced gamers! Download and Play for free right now! ( Created as a tribute to Lemmy Kilmister. )
