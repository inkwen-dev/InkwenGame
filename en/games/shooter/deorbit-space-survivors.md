## How to play

PC
Move: WASD or Arrow keys
Aim & shoot: Automatic
Open a chest: Press F when standing next to it (or click the OPEN button)
Pause: On-screen pause button
Mobile
Move: Drag the on-screen joystick (it appears wherever you touch)
Aim & shoot: Automatic
Open a chest: Tap the OPEN button when you're next to it
Play in landscape orientation

## About

Deorbit is a fast-paced top-down space survivor shooter. You command a lone starship stranded in hostile sectors, and wave after wave of enemy swarms close in from every direction. Your weapons fire automatically — your job is to fly, dodge, and stay alive as the horde grows.
Vacuum up glowing XP orbs to level up mid-run, then choose from a hand of upgrade cards to shape your build on the fly — more firepower, drones, shields, and devastating new abilities. Crack open chests that drop into the battlefield for extra loot, push through 10 escalating phases per sector, and brace for massive boss encounters that can turn the tide.
Every run earns gold. Spend it back at your hangar to permanently upgrade your ship, unlock new vessels, and gear up before diving back in. Claim daily rewards, chase new records, and see how deep into the sectors you can survive. No two runs play the same.
Think you can beat your best score? Suit up, Commander.
Tips
Grab XP orbs to level up, then pick the upgrade cards that fit your build.
Open chests for bonus drones, cells, and items during a run.
Spend gold in the hangar between runs to make your ship permanently stronger.
Survive to face the sector boss — beating it pushes you deeper.
