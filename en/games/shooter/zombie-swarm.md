## About

Zombie swarm is a fast-paced top-down survival shooter where you fight off endless waves of the undead. Pick your hero, blast through hordes of zombies, and stay alive as the swarm grows with every wave. Collect resources, craft powerful weapons and items, upgrade your heros skills, and unlock special perks between battles. Take on PVE waves solo or team up in co-op (Solo/Duo) to survive together. In Defense mode, build your card deck and protect the drill from incoming zombies. Explore different worlds, open chests, find rare keys, and master new abilities. With many zombie types, each with unique behavior, every run is a fresh challenge.
