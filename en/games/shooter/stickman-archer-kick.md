Use the mouse or touch controls to play.

Aiming: Press and hold the screen or mouse button, then move to adjust the angle and power of your shot.

Shooting: Release the press to fire the arrow.

Objective: Hit the ropes or environmental objects to free the trapped Stickmen and complete the level.

Progression: Successfully complete all levels and collect stars to unlock new characters and features.
