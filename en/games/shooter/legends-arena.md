## How to play

Navigate using the WASD keys Switch weapons by scrolling the mouse wheel or pressing the 1 2 3 4 keys Press Left Shift to sprint Reload your weapon with the R key Crouch by pressing the C key Jump using the Spacebar

## About

Welcome to the ultimate online combat platform! Here, only the most legendary warriors clash. Legends Arena is an unrestricted shooter game where you team up to face off against your adversaries. At the minimum, pair up with a teammate to take on another duo, consisting of your rival and their partner. For those seeking more action, Legends Arena also provides options for 3v3 and 5v5 team deathmatches. The choice of battle mode is yours! Gear up and step into the arena!
