## About

You can play Micro Tank Wars game against AI or your friends on 2 Player mode. Try to destroy enemy tanks on 15 uniquely designed platforms through precise shots. Surprise your enemies by using advanced tactics on moving walls and labyrinth levels! Focus on destroying your enemies by receiving minimal damage, because total score of 15 levels will decide the winner of the game.
