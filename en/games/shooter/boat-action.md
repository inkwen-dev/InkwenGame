## About

Today in the game Boat Action we will go with you to the mountains. There is a world-famous race on inflatable boats and you will take part in it directly. On the screen you will see a river and your boat is located on it. Your task is gaining speed to swim across the water surface as far as possible. On your way there will be golden coins. You need to try to collect them as much as possible. For this you will be credited with gaming points. Also collect other items that can give you bonuses. On the track there will also be found stones and various traps, which you certainly need to avoid. If you get into them, your boat will explode and you lose the round.
