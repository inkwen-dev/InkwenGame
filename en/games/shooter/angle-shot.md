## About

In this game you have to shoot with the small ball towards the big ball. But it is not simple because the little ball shoots at an angle and not in a straight line. You need to shoot at a time when the big ball is far away from you. You can miss five times before the game is over. Try to have a good result. Find the shooting angle and score points.
