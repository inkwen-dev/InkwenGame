## How to play

W A S D or ARROW KEYS to move around Mouse to aim Left mouse click to fire Right mouse click to fire 2 click E to get out of the tank

## About

Armored Wolf - 2D tank shooter with a very addictive gameplay, made in the best traditions of classic 16-bit games. The player alone will have to break the enemy tank armada. A variety of guns and upgrades for them will help you with this, which can be installed in between battles for coins earned in battles.
