## About

Destroy the virus that is all around you. In this game you have the opportunity to do that, but if you miss three times the game will end and the virus will win. The virus will move around you and you only have one syringe that rotates. You need to inject medicine at the right time to hit the virus. Tap on screen to inject medicine.
