## About

Fight your way to victory! Play and fight against other warriors who will fight for your soul and steal them and become more Powerful! Be careful of your surroundings because there can be multiple enemies at a time to take you on! Collect souls to become more powerful and defeat them! Use your awesome boosts to help you in troublesome situations! Buy new and more weapons as you fight your way through the arenas. What side will you take? Can you defeat them all?
