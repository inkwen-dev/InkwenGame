## How to play

Swipe through meme cards to slice them and score points. Slice in a row to build a combo. Collect coins and power-ups, grab hearts for an extra life, and avoid bombs. Don’t let memes pass by!

Slice: drag with your mouse or swipe with your finger.
Use buttons: click or tap.
Pause: click or tap the pause button, or press P or Esc.
