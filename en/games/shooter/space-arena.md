## About

Blast off into a high-stakes galactic conflict in Space Arena! As a pilot in the deep reaches of space, you must navigate through treacherous enemy formations and survive increasingly difficult waves of scouts, fighters, and heavy transporters. Protect your ship while engaging in intense dogfights across multiple challenging levels. Collect powerful upgrades to boost your fire rate, add multi-shot capabilities, and activate defensive shields. Featuring a premium futuristic design with Orbitron typography, smooth particle effects, and high-octane gameplay, Space Arena is fully optimized for a seamless experience on both desktop and mobile.
