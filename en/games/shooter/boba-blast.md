## How to play

Pop pearls off your mega-ball faster than your rival. Win by clearing your cluster first, or by popping more pearls if you both run out of shots.

Controls
Press anywhere on the play area to start aiming.
Drag to shape your shot:
Up → higher arc (lob)
Down → flatter, line-drive shot
Left / right → steer aim
Release to fire along the glowing trajectory arc.
Tap Swap balls to switch your active shot with the preview ball beside it.

## About

Fast, head-to-head pearl-duel game. You and a rival each face a spinning mega-ball of colorful boba pearls. Shoot matching pearls to pop them in satisfying chain reactions, chip away at the cluster, and be the first to clear your ball.

Matches feel like a competitive arcade duel: aim carefully, manage limited shots, and race the clock while your opponent does the same. Wrong-color hits ricochet instead of popping, so every throw matters.
