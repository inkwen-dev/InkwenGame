## About

Gun Shot – is a game in which you need to shoot at the target with a variety of weapons and score as many points as possible. You practice shooting to choose one of the 4 types of weapons: gun, colt, automatic gun and machine gun. If you miss 3 times the whole target game is over. Have fun.
