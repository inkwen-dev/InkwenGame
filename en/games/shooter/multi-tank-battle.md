## About

Do you love multiplayer games? This game is a very contentious tank game. In Multiplayer Tank Battle, each team has 5 tanks and the team that develops the best strategy wins the match. All you have to do is scatter your teammates on the battlefield and wait for enemy tanks. Wait for the attack and shoot. The team that destroys 15 tanks wins the first match. The team that gets two matches is the winner of the battle. Are you ready for war? Let's start.
