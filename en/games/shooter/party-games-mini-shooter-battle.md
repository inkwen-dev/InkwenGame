## How to play

Become the ultimate sharpshooter of the party! In this chaotic showdown, your main goal is to maneuver skillfully and shoot accurately to eliminate all opponents while staying safe. The player who survives until the end or secures the highest kill count within the time limit wins. Keep an eye out for special weapon spawns—they are your key to turning the tide of battle!

PC Controls:
W/A/S/D or Arrow Keys: Move your character in all directions.
Left Mouse Click / Spacebar: Fire your weapon.
Shift: Dash or boost speed.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: Drag the left joystick to move.
Fire Button: Tap the shoot icon on the right to attack.
Boost Button: Tap to gain a temporary speed burst.
