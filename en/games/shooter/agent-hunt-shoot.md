## About

You play the role of an elite agent and are locked in a nervous battle. Enter the Agent Hunt Shoot, grab a powerful weapon in your hand and destroy your enemies with precision shooting! Hide behind the crates to cover yourself and hunt down the enemies. Winning selects higher damage guns, bombs, helmets, etc.
