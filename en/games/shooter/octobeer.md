## About

Welcome to Octobeer! Youre the festivals best server, and the sky is full of thirsty guests. Hold to charge, slide to aim and release to throw a beer - every guest you serve pays you coins. Each Run lasts five waves. Earn enough coins to pay the rent before its due, or the festival is over. Pick Run skills, unlock new shot styles, collect rare mugs and climb all 20 Runs of the festival. Watch your beer: every throw uses one, and when it runs dry the Run ends. Features: - Easy one-finger aiming with satisfying arcs and combos - 20 Runs with rising challenge and boss guests - Run skills, shot styles and a mug collection to complete - Hall
