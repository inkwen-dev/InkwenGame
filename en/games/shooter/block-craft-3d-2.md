## About

Block Craft 3D is a first-person RPG game with 3D pixel Minecraft models. Starting from a block base, you can attack the monsters outside to claim diamonds with weapons. I strongly suggest you build bombs to destroy terrains or build blocks to make yourself safe. When you earn enough diamonds, you can unlock and use 4 Melee weapons, 11 guns, and 8 bombs in the store. Glad you will have a good time in this block world.
