## About

Zombie Shooter is a game where you have to destroy all the zombies. But there are many obstacles and the destruction of zombies will not be easy, but it will be fun and addictive. Use the obstacles to help ricochet the bullets and get good results. Your ammo is limited, and if you spend it before destroying all the zombies, you will replay the level. This game has 30 levels.
