## How to play

In the game Pineapple Pen 2 you can control the game on a computer using a mouse and on a mobile device using your finger Your objective is to throw a pen at the fruit and score as many points as possible

## About

In this game, your aim is to knock gold out of rotating fruits by precisely calculating each throw to reach metal locks, and open the treasure chest. Careful though, if your ball hits your pen, you will lose. Get ready for a challenging and fun gameplay experience! Good luck!
