## About

In the game Shooting Target we have four types of weapons in our arsenal: colt, pistol, assault rifle and machine gun. Their images are located at the bottom in the lower left corner. In fact, you can choose whichever you want from the start. In the upper left corner, the scoring and your misses will be kept. If their limit is reached, the game will end.
