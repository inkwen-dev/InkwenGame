## About

Wild Hunting is the dream of every sniper hunter. Wolf Hunter 2020 lets you hunt the biggest and most dangerous animals in the world. You will rely on your marksman shooting sniper skills to kill and trap animals including Zebras, Cheetahs, Elephants, Lions and even dinosaurs. Moreover, chasing your target animals on a moving truck makes this offline game even more challenging. Do you have what it takes to become a sniper hunting legend?
