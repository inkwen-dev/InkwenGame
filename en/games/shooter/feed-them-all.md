## How to play

🍓 Feed Them All! Take control of a fearless fighter and feed hungry monsters with juicy fruit shots! Run around the arena, dodge incoming attacks, and aim at enemies to take them down. Face wild bosses, stay on the move, and blast your way through exciting levels!

Desktop Controls:
- WASD / Arrow Keys: Move your character.
- Mouse: Control movement using the on-screen joystick.

Mobile Controls:
- Touch and Drag: Move your character using the on-screen joystick.

Objective:
Shoot fruits to feed hungry monsters, defeat all enemies, and survive challenging boss fights to complete each level!
