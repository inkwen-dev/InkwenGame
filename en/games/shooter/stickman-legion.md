## About

Lead a growing army of stickmen into battle! Fight off waves of enemies and turn them into your own soldiers. Level up, pick upgrades and evolve your gun into a minigun, railgun or rocket launcher. Call in drones, turrets and airstrikes, promote your troops, and take down huge bosses like tanks and fortresses. 10 stages, 2 islands, and permanent upgrades to unlock between runs.
