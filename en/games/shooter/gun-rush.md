## About

Begin an action-packed gun battle runner. Gun Rush is an electrifying arcade game that combines gun merging with platform running. Run forward, avoid obstacles, collect weapons, and blast your way to the finish line with ultimate firepower. Control the direction of your gun, shooting power, ATK speed, and Range to increase its value. Increase your damage by using high-value doors.
