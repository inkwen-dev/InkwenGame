## About

Hero Masters is a single-player game in which you will have a real fight with the city villains! Dive into the fantasy world, unlock and develop special skills, and defeat monsters to save the world! This is not just a first-person shooter, but a full-fledged strategy in which you have to properly manage resources, unlock new characters and superpowers in order to clear the world from monsters.
