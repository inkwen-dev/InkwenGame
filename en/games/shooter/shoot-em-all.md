## About

What will you do if you know the bullet tracks before a battle started in advance? Shoot Em All is more like a puzzle game than a skillful arcade game. You have to make sure your blue character is alive at every shoot. It is permitted for you to control all the guns together at each round. Could you eliminate all red enemies at all levels?
