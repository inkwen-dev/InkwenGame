## How to play

Use arrow keys to move X to shoot

## About

Far in the sea lives a shark named Johnny. One day, our hero decided to visit his distant relatives living many miles away. When he sailed the sea, he accidentally got into a war zone and now you will have to help him survive and get out of this zone in the game Crazy Shark. With the help of control keys you will have to force your shark to pick up speed and change its location in space. Quite often on her way will come across obstacles or submarines leading through her fire. You will need to do so that your character would avoid colliding with these dangerous things.
