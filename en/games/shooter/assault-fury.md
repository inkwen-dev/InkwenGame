## How to play

Press right mouse click to aim Press left mouse click to shoot Press R to reload Press Tab to pause

## About

Soldier, you’re under attack! The enemy troops have defeated the majority of our soldiers and surrounded you. Use your weapons and try to escape. You can use different objects that surrounds you (oil cans) as a weapon against the enemy or to hide. Fight! Features • Multiple weapons • 40 levels • Multiple npc types • Upgrade system
