## About

As a person who saves the world from evil forces and robots, you expect to have a good training base. But what if that training base is surrounded by these robots? Well that must be a good time to run and gun to destroy them. Use your skills in this classic FPS game to aim and shoot and also pick up new guns around the map. Can you survive? Of course you can. Do your best and have fun killing the enemies!
