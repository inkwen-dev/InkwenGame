## About

&zwnj;Flowers vs Zombies&zwnj; is a strategic plant-placement game where players defend against zombie attacks by placing flowers that automatically shoot bullets. Two flowers can be merged into a more advanced one, with higher-tier flowers having greater attack power. Killing zombies earns points, and the higher the score, the better the ranking.
