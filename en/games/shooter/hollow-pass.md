## About

HOLLOW PASS is a desktop first-person aim trainer that fights like a live firefight: ninety seconds, one P-12 pistol, and up to four bots that move, take cover and shoot back. Clear them all and a new life starts instantly; die and you respawn with the pistol while your rifle stays where you dropped it. Rifles are picked up off the floor &mdash; the KR-76, the suppressed C-5 CARBINE, the scoped B-50 BOLT &mdash; or end a bot outright with the knife from behind.
