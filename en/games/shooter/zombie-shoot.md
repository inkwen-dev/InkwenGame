## About

We are with you in the game Zombie Shoot. On the screen in different places we will see standing zombies. In the hands of our hero will be a bazooka. You need to shoot it so that the shells fall near the enemies. In time they will explode and destroy the enemy. In doing so, when shooting, you need to consider the trajectory of the flight of shells and how they can bounce off objects. After all, some of the opponents are in very difficult places.
