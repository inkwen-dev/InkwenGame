## About

Rescue Kitty Puzzle is an online puzzle game. The little kitty is trapped in a maze. If you drag it and release your hand, it will bounce in the opposite direction. You need to make the kitty bounce at the walls and use the reaction force to let it bounce out of the laser door. In each level, the number of hits against the wall is different. Have Fun!
