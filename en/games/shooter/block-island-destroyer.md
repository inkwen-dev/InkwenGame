## How to play

🎯 Goal: destroy all blocks on the island.

🔫 Shoot: hold the left mouse button or hold your finger on the screen
🎥 Camera: WASD or arrow keys, Ctrl + mouse wheel to zoom
📱 Mobile camera: two fingers to rotate, pinch to zoom

🔁 Game loop:
• Shoot blocks and collect coins
• Buy and upgrade guns in the shop
• Go back to battle and break more

💡 Broken blocks stay broken between battles.
