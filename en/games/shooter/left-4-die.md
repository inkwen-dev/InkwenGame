## How to play

WASD - move Left Mouse button - attack R - reload SPACE - jump 1 - 4 - select weapons. Approach the weapons dealer to buy weapons, the armor dealer - to buy armor, the barber - to change your appearance, the commander - to receive a new mission. Complete missions and kill zombies to earn money and experience. The higher your level, the more powerful equipment and higher rewards for missions you can obtain, but you will also face more numerous hordes of zombies chasing you.
