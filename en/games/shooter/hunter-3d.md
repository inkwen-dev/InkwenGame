## About

Today, we present you an interesting 3D game where you’ll be playing as a hunter. The game is divided in 2 parts and in each of them, you will be competing with an opponent. You will be tasked with shooting more targets than your competitors. The closer to the middle you hit, the more points you’ll get. Your objective is to win, so be sure to be as accurate as possible.
