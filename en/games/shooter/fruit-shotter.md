## About

Fruit Shooter is a fun casual game where you control a cannon to shoot and destroy various fruits. The more accurate your shots, the higher your score! But be careful, if you hit obstacles, you will fail. Observe the trajectory, aim precisely, and choose the right moment to eliminate all the fruits in each level.
