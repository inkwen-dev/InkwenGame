## About

Snake Train Zone is an exciting shooting combat game. If you are a big fan of the Snake series of games, then this game is for you! Your task is simple, enter the arena, collect colored balls and when the number bar is full you can add a soldier that follows you. Here you can not only experience the joy of shooting but also the fun of breaking through and upgrading! Are you ready for this combat?
