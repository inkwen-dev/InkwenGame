## About

Viking Hunter is a game where you need to control the viking and he must destroy all the enemies that come to him. He will throw knives at the enemy and will defend himself with a shield from the enemies. So you need to collect knives and shields to have enough weapons to fight the enemy. Click on the knife and shield icons to play. Move the warrior across the track. If you do not have weapons you can avoid the enemy attack by changing lanes.
