## About

Tap &amp; React Plus is a fast-paced reaction game where every tap counts. Keep the red square alive by tapping it quickly before it shrinks away. Hit yellow squares for bonus points, and avoid the blue ones&mdash;they end your run instantly! Stay focused, react fast, and climb to the top score in this addictive reflex challenge!
