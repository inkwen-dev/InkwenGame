## How to play

In Strike Bullet, every shot counts. Fire and ricochet your bullets to eliminate all enemies. Use glass panels, explosive barrels, and portals to set up the perfect path.
Desktop Controls:

Mouse: Aim by moving the cursor.
Left Click: Fire your bullet.
R: Restart the current level.

Mobile Controls:

Touch & Drag: Aim by dragging on the screen.
Release / Tap: Fire your bullet.
Restart Button: Restart the current level from the in-game UI.
