## How to play

Survive the waves of bugs. Your frog attacks on its own: click or tap a bug to lick it right away. When the belly is full, press CROAK (or Space) for a big blast. After each wave, pick one of three mutations (click or keys 1-3). Collect 4 of the right ones to unlock a fusion. Beat the boss at wave 30 to purify the pond, then go deeper. P pauses.
