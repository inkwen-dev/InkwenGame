## About

Cannon Surfer is an interesting arcade game. You will push a ball cannon to arrive at the end. Stay away from the blocks. Remember to integrate the tank, with the tank improving, you could beat the boss easily. If you can not handle it, there is a rainbow shell on your right that will help you.
