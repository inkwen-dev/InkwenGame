## About

**Robotu Rebellion** is an action-packed puzzle escape game where you control a robot trapped inside a dangerous factory. Avoid deadly lasers, mechanical traps, and hostile security robots while searching for a way out. Discover hidden paths, solve security puzzles, and hack machines to overcome obstacles. Use stealth, strategy, and quick reflexes to survive the mechanical maze and escape the robot rebellion!
