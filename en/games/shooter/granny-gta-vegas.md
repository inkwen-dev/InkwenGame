Granny GTA Vegas drops the infamous horror legend into the heart of sin city! Armed and unhinged, Granny escapes her haunted house and brings chaos to the neon streets of Las Vegas. Run, shoot, and wreak havoc in this action-packed open-world mayhem, blending GTA-style gameplay with classic action vibes. Can you survive the heat and conquer the city, or will Granny become a permanent ghost of Vegas?
