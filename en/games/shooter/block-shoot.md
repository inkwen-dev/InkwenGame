## About

Block Shoot is a online puzzle shooting game. Puzzles with figures will appear on the screen. Your job is to destroy all the objects and clear the screen of the figures. You have limited shots that are fixed and therefore you have to shoot in a certain order to destroy all the pieces. At some levels, figures will appear with a sign on them, and you must not hit them because they will take your life. It is interesting and logical puzzle game. Start to play and try to pass all levels. There is 21 levels at this game. Tap on the bullets that you want to shoot to play.
