## About

Paint or Seek is a original hide and seek action game where you can paint your character, blend into the environment, and avoid being discovered by Seekers. As a Hider, your goal is to avoid being found. Paint your character to match nearby walls, objects, patterns, and decorations, then choose a good hiding spot and stay hidden until the timer ends. The better your camouflage, the harder it will be for the Seeker to notice you. As a Seeker, your goal is to find every hidden player before time runs out. Search the entire map, look carefully for characters blending into the environment, and catch all Hiders before they survive the round.
