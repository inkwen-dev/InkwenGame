## About

The Zombie Mission 10 Prison Breakout episode isn't over yet! A new call came to the heroes from the secret part of the prison... In the More Mayhem episode, the adventure continues from where it left off. Different zombies and bosses threaten humanity with traps and weapons. You must immediately start the mission and fight in 20 different levels. With the new feature coming to the game, you can change the skin option just below the selected character. The strongest boss, of course, is waiting for you in the final level! Don't forget to buy weapons and upgrade with the coins you earn as you pass the levels!
