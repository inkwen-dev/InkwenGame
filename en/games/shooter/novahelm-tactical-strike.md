## How to play

Desktop: W A S D to move, mouse to look and aim, left click to fire, right click to aim down sights. SHIFT toggles sprint, CTRL toggles crouch, SPACE jumps and vaults over low cover, R reloads, G throws a grenade. Press 1 to 6 to pick a loadout slot and ENTER to deploy. Press ESC to release the mouse. Use the sound button in the bottom right to mute.

Your team of four fights a four-soldier enemy squad to a score limit. Score a point for every enemy you eliminate. Play matches to unlock the sniper rifle, machine gun and grenade launcher. The SMG and rifle unlock by watching a rewarded ad from the loadout screen. Open the RANKING button on the loadout screen to see the shared lifetime-kills table.

## About

Novahelm is a fast 4v4 team-elimination shooter played from a third-person view inside a maze of shipping containers and concrete loading bays. Pick your weapon, push the enemy base, and hold the site with your squad. Six weapons with distinct recoil, aim-down-sights, grenades, sprint, crouch, vault, and a killstreak system that rewards aggression. Play matches to unlock the sniper, machine gun and grenade launcher, or watch a short ad to open the SMG and rifle right away.
