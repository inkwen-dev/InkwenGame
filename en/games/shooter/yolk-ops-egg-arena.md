## How to play

## About

Yolk Ops is a first-person arena shooter where egg fighters battle across eight unique 3D maps. Play Free for All, Team Scramble, or King of the Yolk against AI bots. Choose from seven primary weapons and a sidearm, use grenades and arena pickups, and compete for the most eliminations. Bot counts vary between matches. Supports desktop, gamepad, and touch controls. Single-player only; online multiplayer is not supported.
