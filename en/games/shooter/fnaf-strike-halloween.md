## About

FNAF Strike Halloween is an intense first-person shooter set in a spooky Halloween-themed world inspired by the legendary Five Nights at Freddy&rsquo;s universe. Your mission is simple but terrifying &mdash; survive each level by blasting through waves of deadly FNAF animatronics and find your way to the exit before they catch you. Every level is packed with chilling decorations, haunted corridors, flickering lights, and eerie sounds that create the ultimate horror-shooter experience. As you advance, new challenges, hidden traps, and stronger animatronic enemies appear, testing your reflexes and accuracy.
