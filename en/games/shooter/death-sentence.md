## About

Death Sentence is an intense tactical action game where you assist assassins in completing missions to eliminate targets accurately and secretly. Observe the terrain, choose the right moment to strike, and avoid detection to complete the level. Each mission presents a new challenge with increasing difficulty, requiring quick reflexes and smart strategies. The gameplay is engaging, tense, and extremely exciting!
