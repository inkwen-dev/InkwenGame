## About

Last Quiver is a fast-paced survival defense game where skill, timing, and precision decide how long you live. You play as a lone archer standing your ground against relentless waves of enemies and zombies. With only your bow, limited arrows, and quick reflexes, you must fend off incoming hordes before they overwhelm you. Every shot matters&mdash;miss too often, and you may find yourself facing the end with an empty quiver. As waves progress, enemies grow faster, tougher, and more unpredictable. Aim carefully, prioritize targets, and make split-second decisions to survive longer and score higher. Power-ups, combo hits, and more!
