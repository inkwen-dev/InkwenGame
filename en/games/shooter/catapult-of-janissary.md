## About

The Janissary Series continues with the Catapult of Janissary. In this game, you aim to land a hit on your opponent by adjusting the angle and speed of the catapults. During the combat, catapult bonuses such as "Smart", "Ghost", "Big" "Bomb" may increase your chances in the game. You score two points if you can hit your opponent in your first try or by hitting them with the powershot. The player, who scores 5 points first wins.
