## About

Garden War is a fun combat game set in a colorful garden, where you must move skillfully and face mischievous insects. Each level offers lively battles that require you to dodge, counterattack, and use skills wisely to defeat enemies. With fast-paced gameplay, vivid effects, and simple yet engaging mechanics, Garden War provides moments of lighthearted entertainment filled with excitement right in the familiar garden!
