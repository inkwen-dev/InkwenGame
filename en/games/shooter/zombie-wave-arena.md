## How to play

Objective: Survive endless waves of zombies in the arena. Kill zombies to earn coins, then spend them between waves on weapon upgrades, new guns, armor, and grenades. Every 5th wave summons a powerful boss with double the HP of the last — defeat it to unlock new zombie threats and bigger rewards. The arena changes every 10 waves. There is no final level: you "win" by beating your best wave and high score — reach Wave 50 to conquer the Death King and every arena.

Desktop controls: WASD or Arrow Keys — move · Mouse — aim · Left Click — shoot · Right Click — throw grenade · G — switch grenade · R — reload · 1–6 or Q — switch weapons · P / Esc — pause.

Mobile controls: Left thumb — virtual joystick to move · FIRE button — shoot with auto-aim · on-screen buttons for reload, weapon swap, and grenades.
