## About

Zebra Hunter is a relaxing first person perspective zebra hunter game. Users can look around the map by swiping the screen and moving anywhere there they want. The game has 16 hunting levels so far. You hunt in valley surrounded by mountain and sometimes near the river. You have 2 semi automatic sniper guns in the game. We hope you like our 3D zebra hunting game.
