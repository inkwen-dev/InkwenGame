## About

Air Battles is an exciting aerial combat game that puts players in control of powerful fighter jets in intense sky warfare. Players must navigate through dynamic environments, dodge enemy fire, and engage in fast-paced dogfights against skilled opponents. The game features multiple missions, each with increasing difficulty, where strategy, precision, and quick reflexes are essential to survive and dominate the battlefield. With a variety of aircraft to choose from, each equipped with unique weapons and abilities, players can customize their combat style and upgrade their arsenal.
