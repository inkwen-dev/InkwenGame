## About

Gun Fest legend is here with a brand new Squid Game theme. Duplicate their weapons by doing the math and destroy the Squid Game employees. As the boss character, the Squid Game baby character will sometimes appear and you will need as many weapons as possible to destroy him. When it reaches the final line, stop and destroy the truck trying to escape from the island. If you want to have 6 different weapons, you must accumulate the points you earn at the end of each level. Have fun!
