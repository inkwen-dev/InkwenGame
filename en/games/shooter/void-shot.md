## How to play

Goal: survive as many waves as you can. Destroy every ring in a wave to advance; beat the boss on every fifth wave. Your best wave goes to the leaderboard.
Controls — mobile: touch and drag anywhere to move the turret. Desktop: move the mouse, or use Arrow keys / A and D; Esc or P pauses the game. Firing is automatic — just aim.
Catch what falls from destroyed rings: coins, hearts (extra life) and temporary power-ups. Lost all lives? Watch an ad to revive once per run. Spend coins in Upgrades to boost damage, fire rate, coin gain, and unlock the twin shot.

## About

Void Shot is a one-hand neon arcade shooter set in the glowing depths of space. Slide your turret along the bottom of the screen — it fires on its own — and pop endless waves of pulsing rings before they reach you. Every fifth wave ends in a boss fight, and each boss plays differently: some teleport, some split, some spawn minions. Random wave modifiers — crosswind, low gravity, turbo drift, extra bounce — reshuffle the rules so no two runs feel the same. Catch power-ups (frost, double damage, rapid fire, shield), bank coins and permanently upgrade your turret between runs. How deep into the void can you go?
