## About

In Halloween Defender game you need to defend the pumpkins from the Halloween creatures. Shoot creatures to destroy them. They will come in large numbers and will want to destroy the pumpkins. Save your pumpkins, stay focused and destroy as many creatures as you can, collect points and play longer as you can.
