## How to play

Gameplay - Hold and drag your sniper scope to aim release to shoot - When aiming at moving target time your trigger for the best outcome - Shoot fuel tank for extra fun

## About

Trigger you are the hitman like johnny trigger and you have to aim the target like we do in western sniper.Traditionally such popular games force us to run after the target and hide around the corner to stay safe. Aim through your sniper scope and make your best shot! For sure bad guys still could shoot you, but not as easy as it used to be in the early days. Now you a highly experienced assassin who knows the most suitable location for an excellent shot. When ever you want to gun down all you have to do is just open the app and play it. You have the felling in this game that you are playing snipers vs thieves. Stay frosty! Leverage your wits and expertise in sniping to bring them down.
