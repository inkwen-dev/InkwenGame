## About

Neon Assault is a fast-paced arcade shooter where you pilot a neon spaceship against relentless waves of alien enemies. Dodge incoming fire, collect power-ups, and take down massive bosses every 5 waves. Earn coins to unlock new ship skins, build combos for bonus score, and see how far you can survive as the waves get faster and harder.
