## About

Cube Battle Royale is actually a third-person shooting simulation game with 3D pixel game art animation. You are allowed to collect many useful objects on the map. You need to activate the radio machine so we can call for help at each level. Just start the game alone or call one of your friends in 2 players mode in Cube Battle Royale!
