## About

This is a shooting game, where you need to shoot the balloons. Shoot the balloons with the arrow. Do not let them escape your sight because you will be deprived of points. Do not pop X balloons. You can move the bow and arrow up and down so you can shoot the balloons more easily. Hit as many balloons as you can and earn points before the game is over.
