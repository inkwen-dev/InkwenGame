## About

Party Games: Mini Shooter Battle is a battle-royale shoot-em-up game with 2D cartoon models. In 4 modes, you can shoot other players to earn scores for coins and gems. Then you can buy more roles and guns in the Shop. Your character can aim at the closest target automatically so that you only need to fire and dodge bullets from enemies. More kills and less death will make you claim more coin and gem rewards. Good luck!
