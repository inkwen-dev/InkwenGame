## About

In the game Air Force Attack you will need to take part in large air battles on the side of one of the countries that is fighting against the neighbor of the aggressor. Destroy enemy aircraft, gather supplies, first-aid kits and fuel in an endless battle mode! In this game, the degree of difficulty gradually increases, and the game is ideal for mobile devices.
