## How to play

Arrow keys or on screen arrows to move On some levels you can also go back the way you came Find 9 Christmas gems and destroy the mage to win the game Left mouse click or tap on screen to shoot Jump on snowmen to destroy them

## About

You took your pet reindeer out in the forest on a winter's day. You had heard there were diamonds in the forest so you'll be looking for those while you are out there. Unfortunately, there is an evil mage in the forest who casts a spell turning the forest elves and even the snowmen into dangerous creatures. He also takes your reindeer. To break the spell you must find 9 Christmas gems. Once you do this you'll also find your reindeer.
