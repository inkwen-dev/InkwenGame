## About

Mech-Core: Survivor is an intense, fast-paced top-down roguelite survival game. Take control of a heavily armored Mech and fight against endless waves of relentless alien swarms in a dark metallic arena. Collect Nanites from defeated enemies to level up and unlock devastating weapon upgrades like Rapid Lasers, Homing Missiles, and EMP Shockwaves. Combine different abilities to create the ultimate build. Can you survive the invasion and defeat the swarm bosses? Features: Fast-paced top-down shooter combat. Unique level-up system with stacking upgrades. Multiple enemy types and challenging boss fights.
