## How to play

Use mouse to aim mouse left button to fire

## About

Zombie Target Shoot is a fun carnival arcade style shooting game. The goal is to try and shoot the different zombie targets and accumulate as many points as possible with only 50 shots of ammo. If you shoot the zombies directly in the eye then your points will be higher. To make the shooting even more fun just hit multiple targets with one shot and your score doubles! The green cans of zombie juice if hit will score you 75 points. Have fun shooting and see what high score you can get in only 80 shots!
