## About

Swing, jump, flip and slide your stickman body from one neon color shape to another to make them glow and light-it up. Collect stars as you swing, jump and flip to raise your score, in the funnest of all jumping games. Just when you start to feel like a stickman boss and are loving that color glow, things get complicated (or “sticky,” if you will) as you jump, flip and pass levels. Color shapes start moving, rotating and even exploding, and lethal spikes become a constant threat to your poor little stickman life. If you miss a jump, if your swing’s a dud, if your flip isn’t on point, or if you hit a spike… you fall into oblivion!
