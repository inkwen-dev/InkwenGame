## About

Shoot'n'Shout Turbo is a action platform game where you put your aim to the test by shooting monsters. Strange alien creatures have invaded Earth and you are the only hero who can stop them! Pick up your gun and bazooka to eliminate all the enemies and save the world in this physics-based shooting game. Aim carefully and shoot all the beasts, but be careful not to miss or waste your bullets - otherwise we're all doomed!
