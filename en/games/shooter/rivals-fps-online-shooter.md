## How to play

To start a game, create a map or join other players.

The controls are the same as in many top shooters.

On PC:
- Movement - WASD
- Camera rotation - Using the mouse
- Jump - Space
- Crouch - C
- Interact with objects - E

On mobile:
- Movement - Virtual joystick on the left
- Camera rotation - Swipes on the right side of the screen
- Jump, reload using virtual buttons
