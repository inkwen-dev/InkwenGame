## How to play

Fight through each operation and complete its objective to reach extraction. Move, jump, shoot, throw grenades, rescue captives and destroy enemy forces. Some missions require you to defend positions, collect intelligence, destroy supplies or defeat a boss. Checkpoints save your progress during longer operations.
Keyboard: WASD or Arrow Keys — move and aim; X or J — fire; Z, K or Space — jump; C or L — grenade; E — tactical module; Esc or P — pause.
Mobile: use the on-screen joystick or directional buttons to move and aim. Use the on-screen buttons to fire, jump, throw grenades and activate your tactical module. Touch controls work in both portrait and landscape orientation.

## About

A fast-paced retro run-and-gun action game inspired by classic arcade shooters. Fight through a long campaign packed with enemy squads, vehicles, dangerous environments and massive bosses. Rescue captives, destroy enemy supplies, complete special objectives, unlock weapons, upgrade your soldier and master tactical modules. Explore changing battlefields, take on daily challenges and boss hunts, and build your perfect combat loadout.
