## About

Embark on a thrilling journey in Stickman Hunter, where your mission is to protect your tribe from hidden enemies. Strategically use your ammunition to eliminate foes and rise as the tribes chieftain. With each of the 10 challenging levels, master the art of precision and cunning to uncover and defeat concealed threats. Do you have what it takes to lead your tribe to victory? How to Play: 1- Aim and shoot your weapon to hit hidden enemies. 2- Conserve ammunition and plan your shots carefully. 3- Progress through 10 increasingly difficult levels.
