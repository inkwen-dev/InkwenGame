## How to play

Swipe left and right to move your character.
Collect stickmen and gun parts to build a bigger weapon.
Pick up cash bundles to earn more coins.
Avoid obstacles that reduce your weapon's size or power.
Reach the finish line with the strongest gun possible.
Defeat the final enemy to complete the level and earn rewards.
Upgrade your damage, fire rate, and income to become unstoppable.
