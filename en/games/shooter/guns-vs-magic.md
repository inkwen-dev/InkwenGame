## About

Step into the shoes of Silvius, a courageous student of magic, as you fight to save the world from the dark powers unleashed by your former mentor, Lucius. Once a wise and benevolent wizard, Lucius has succumbed to the sinister influence of a cursed crystal, turning him into an evil sorcerer intent on dominating the world. Now, its up to you to confront Lucius and his army of enchanted creatures in a battle of wits, power, and courage.
