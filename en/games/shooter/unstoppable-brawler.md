## How to play

PC Controls (Keyboard & Mouse):
• [W, A, S, D] or [Arrow Keys]: Move your fighter around the arena.
• [Left Mouse Button (LMB)] or [B]: Punch attack (Spam for rapid combo).
• [Right Mouse Button (Hold RMB)] or [N]: Shield Guard (Reduces incoming damage by 80%).
• [Spacebar] or [Left Shift]: Instant Dash Dodge to evade enemy strikes and traps.
Mobile / Touch Controls:
• Virtual Joystick: Drag to move your fighter.
• Punch Button: Tap to punch enemies.
• Guard Button: Hold to block enemy attacks.
• Dash Button: Tap to quickly dash and reposition.
Pro Tips for Victory:
1. Never stop moving: Keep circling around groups of enemies to avoid getting surrounded.
2. Collect Potions Immediately:
   - Red Potions: Instantly restore your Health points.
   - Blue Potions: Instantly refill your Dash Energy meter.
3. Guard Wisely: When heavy enemies charge their attacks, hold Guard to withstand the blow, then counter-attack!
4. Choose Upgrades Strategically: If you're low on health, grab the Full Heal or Health Boost. If you prefer high mobility, prioritize Movement Speed and Energy Regen!
5. Use Reroll (Ad): Don't like the 3 upgrade cards? Tap "Reroll Cards" to get a brand-new selection!

## About

UNSTOPPABLE BRAWLER is an action-packed 3D casual arcade fighting game where you play as an unbreakable, heroic bean warrior taking on waves of comical and fierce arena opponents!
Train hard in the gym, step into the neon combat arena, and show the world what real power looks like. Dodge enemy attacks, unleash rapid-fire punch combinations, and maintain an ironclad guard to outlast every wave. Collect glowing health and energy vials dropped during combat to stay alive, and choose game-changing upgrades after every cleared wave—such as bonus attack damage, enhanced movement speed, faster energy regeneration, or an instant full heal.
Key Features:
• Fast-Paced 3D Combat: Smooth, responsive brawler controls optimized for both PC (Keyboard & Mouse) and Mobile (Touch).
• Dynamic Wave System: Fight through increasingly difficult waves of quirky enemies, bombers, and bruisers!
• Deep Skill Upgrades: Pick strategic upgrades after each victory to customize your fighter's build.
• Reroll & Revive System: Out of luck? Reroll upgrade cards or revive right in the heat of battle!
• Vibrant Cartoon Visuals: Colorful stylized 3D graphics, expressive ragdoll physics, and satisfying comic-style punch VFX.
• Cross-Platform Cloud Save: Your highest wave records are securely saved so you can jump right back into the fight anytime, anywhere.
