## How to play

Scan the environment carefully to find camouflaged targets hidden in the scene. Use your sniper scope to zoom in, aim precisely at the hidden target, and take the shot. Complete each level by successfully finding and shooting the required targets.

Desktop Controls:
Look Around: Hold the Left Mouse Button and move the mouse to look around.
Scope: Move the cursor to the scope button and hold the Left Mouse Button to open the sniper scope.
Aim While Scoped: While the scope is open, move the mouse to look around and aim.
Fire: Release the Left Mouse Button to fire.

Mobile Controls:
Move: Swipe the screen to look around and aim.
Scope: Press and hold the Scope Button to open the sniper scope.
Aim While Scoped: While the scope is open, swipe the screen to look around and aim.
Fire: Release the Scope Button to fire.
