## About

Top Shooter io is a pixel shooting game in which you enter in a room full with other players, you have to show everyone that you are great soldier and you can survive until no enemy is on his feet, you can use the various power-ups in battle, customize your character’s appearance and you can play with bots in 2 different maps. Try 2 different game modes, win awesome perks by defeating your enemies and be the stronger soldier ever.
