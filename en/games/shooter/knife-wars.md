## About

If you like knife throwing and spaceship games, you will love this game. The game includes 2 different types of games but played for the same purpose. In the first episodes, throw a knife at randomly rotating wood and planetary objects in the scene and try to break it. But beware, you cannot throw another knife on top of a knife you throw. In the second part, you fight against enemy spaceships. Beat them and collect points!
