## About

**Repel the Monster** is an action-packed monster shooting game where you fight endless enemies and destroy their summoning portals. Defeat monsters to earn points and collect dropped weapons and ammo to strengthen your firepower. Destroy every portal to stop the invasion and complete each level. With fast-paced combat, simple controls, and addictive survival gameplay, fight for the highest score and become the ultimate monster hunter!
