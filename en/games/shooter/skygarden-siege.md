## About

Skygarden Siege is a casual arcade shmup where nature fights back against the Iron Fleet. Take control of a Skygarden guardian ship, dodge enemy attacks and defend the floating gardens from mechanical invaders. Collect Light Blossoms, unlock upgrades and push deeper into the conflict between nature and machine.
