## How to play

Defeat all enemies in each state to unlock the next. Clear all 50 states to win. Boss fights every 10 states.

Desktop – WASD or arrow keys to move. Mouse to aim. Shoot will be auto.

Mobile – Left joystick to move. Tap enemies to aim/shoot. Tap special button for power-up.

Both – Collect stars from defeated enemies for health and ammo.

Eight enemy types — each has unique behavior: fast, armored, ranged, explosive, etc. No friendly fire. Each state has 3 rounds. Complete all 50 levels and defend America. Good luck, Guardian!

## About

Combat Guardian: Under Attack is a patriotic arcade shooter where you defend America across all 50 states — that means 50 unique levels. Battle through 8 different enemy types: street thugs, rogue agents, traitor soldiers, biker gangs, smugglers, corrupt cops, mercenaries, and powerful bosses. Each state features its own terrain, color palette, and enemy combinations. Start in Alabama and fight your way to Wyoming, liberating the nation one level at a time. What makes it unique? Enemies grow smarter and more aggressive as you progress. Unlock special attack power-ups and star-spangled finishers. With fast-paced combat, tight controls, and massive replayability, this game delivers classic arcade action. Works perfectly on desktop and mobile browsers. Answer the call — become the Combat Guardian!
