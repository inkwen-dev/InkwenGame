## About

The jungle is a perilous place, full of unknown viruses and undiscovered species. Many animals living in the wilderness are sick because of the humid environment. Come and help these poor animals treat their injured parts, for example, Elephant's noses and fox ears, carefully remove the parasitic viruses, and clean their injured parts, are you ready to be a qualified jungle doctor? Honey, don't sit idly by when the animals really need help. You are the savior they are longing for with kindness and excellent medical skills. Jungle Doctor, please buckle up and venture into the forest!
