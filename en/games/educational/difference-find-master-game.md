## About

Put your observation skills to the ultimate test with Difference Find Master, a fun and challenging puzzle game where you must carefully compare two similar images and find the hidden differences. Each level presents you with two images that look almost identical, but several small details have been changed. Your goal is to carefully inspect both pictures, spot every difference, and complete the level before time runs out. The differences can be hidden anywhere, so stay focused and examine every corner of the images.
