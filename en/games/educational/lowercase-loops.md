## About

Lowercase Loops is a fun and interactive educational game where you practice drawing lowercase letters using your mouse! Improve your handwriting skills as you trace smooth and precise letter shapes, following guided paths. Perfect for learners of all ages, this game helps develop muscle memory, letter recognition, and fine motor skills in an engaging way. With a variety of letters to trace and a simple, user-friendly design, Lowercase Loops makes learning to write both enjoyable and rewarding. Start tracing today and master the art of lowercase letters!
