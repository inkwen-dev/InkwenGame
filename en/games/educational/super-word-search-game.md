## About

“Super Word Search” is a casual game that has the function of stimulating people’s memory. Also, according to studies, it can rejuvenate the brain of elderly people. In addition to these benefits, it is a game that can be generally beneficial for people of all ages. In this game, there are three modes (easy, medium and hard), where “hard” is the most challenging, with a system of time and points multiplied, making you end up with many more points than in the other game modes. Find the words hidden in the letters of each level. Good luck and have fun!
