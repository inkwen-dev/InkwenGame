## About

This is simple educational puzzle game. In this game you need to continuously touch or click 2 photos which show opposite photos. For completing a level you must find 4 pairs. Complete level before 2 minute to get bonus. Get +500 score for correct and -100 for wrong match. Complete all 14 levels to win the game.
