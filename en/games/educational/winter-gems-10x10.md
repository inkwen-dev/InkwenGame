## About

10x10 Winter Gems is a pure matching puzzle game with different 2D gems on a 10x10 platform. As an endless game, if you want more scores, you need to match as many three patterns of gems as you can in each group of gems. Only when you collect ten gems in a straight line, you can match them and claim scores. I strongly suggest you forget about the colors and patterns of each group of gems, just regard them as normal blocks and get as many scores as you can!
