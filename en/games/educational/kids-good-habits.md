## About

Kids Good Habits is a lovely educational game. This game has beautiful graphics and animations, as well as very detailed interactive design. This game is divided into four parts, namely, helping the panda brush its teeth, helping the panda take a bath, feeding the puppy, and judging whether the lamb and giraffes behavior is correct. It helps you form good habits in your daily life by letting you play small games. Have a good time!
