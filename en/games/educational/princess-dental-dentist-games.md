## How to play

👸 Choose a princess patient and start the dental check-up.
🔍 Examine her teeth to find cavities, dirt, and other problems.
🦷 Select the correct dentist tool for each treatment.
🪥 Clean and repair the teeth step by step.

😁 Make sure all dental problems are treated and give the princess a beautiful smile!
⭐ Complete treatments to meet more princess patients and take on new dental challenges.
