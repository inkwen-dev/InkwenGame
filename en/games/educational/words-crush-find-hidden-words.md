## About

The aim is to simply find all the hidden words. The words on each level are related to each other. With this game you can easily improve your vocabulary, concentration and spelling skills! HOW TO PLAY • Words can be vertical, horizontal, diagonal and even backwards. • Swipe letters to find, select and collect words. • Find all the words to advance to the next level! • The faster you find the words, the higher your score and ranking!
