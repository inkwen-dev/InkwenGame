## About

Farm of Words is a fun and relaxing word game in a wordcross style format made for the smartest brains! This game is a cross between all the aspects of word games you love to make you totally addicted and entertained! Swipe the letters to find and guess the hidden words, build up your vocabulary, and have fun! Farm of Words starts as an easy word game and gets harder as you level up! Can you beat the game? Besides, you can use the gems you get to use the system&rsquo;s hint feature to easily pass difficult levels.
