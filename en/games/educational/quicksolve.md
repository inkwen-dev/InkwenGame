## About

QuickSolve is a fast-paced timed math challenge designed to sharpen your mind under pressure. Each round presents a new math question&mdash;simple, clear, and quick to read&mdash;but you only get a few seconds to pick the correct answer before the timer runs out. As you progress, the pace increases and the questions become trickier, testing your ability to think fast and stay calm. One mistake costs you a life, and running out means starting over. With clean visuals, instant feedback, and smooth gameplay, QuickSolve turns mental math into an exciting reflex-based puzzle.
