## About

"Who Is Who?" is a fun logic game that offers you over a hundred scenarios and you need to think outside the box to solve them. There are choice based scenarios such as "Who is the impostor?" but there are also riddles where you need to find a hidden object, and much more. Each level brings something new so you will never get bored. You can use the hint if you get stuck or skip a level. Tricky puzzles are waiting for you!
