## About

Get ready to shape up with Geo Champs! Designed for young learners aged 5 to 8, this fun and educational game helps kids recognize and identify basic geometric shapes in an interactive way. From circles and squares to triangles and more, children will boost their visual learning and shape recognition skills while playing through colorful levels. With cheerful visuals and simple gameplay, every young player can become a true geometry champion!
