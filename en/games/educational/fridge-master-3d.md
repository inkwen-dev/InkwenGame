## About

Are you a master of space organization? There's a whole lot of food and drink in here, but even more stuff will fit if you put it in the right order. Open the shelves in each fridge, organize the space efficiently, and drag items into the compartments one by one until you fill the entire fridge. The process is fun and relaxing.
