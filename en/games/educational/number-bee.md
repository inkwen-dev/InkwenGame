## About

NumberBee is a colorful 3-level kids math adventure where little learners help Buzzy the Bee fly through the Honey Meadow, Count Forest, and Number Hive! Learn counting, addition, and number recognition through fun multiple-choice questions, drag-and-drop honey drops, and matching challenges. Features animated bee character, 3 lives, level progress saving, cheerful music, sound effects, and sweet honey rewards for every correct answer!
