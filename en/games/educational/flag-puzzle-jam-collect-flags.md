## How to play

🧩 Welcome to Flag Puzzle Jam 🌍

Immerse yourself in Flag Jam, a unique blend of puzzle-solving, flags collecting, and visual ASMR effects! Tap to match banner pieces, watch them glide smoothly into place, and clear the board as you complete each flag. With easy-to-learn mechanics and stunning visual effects, Flag Jam is the perfect game to help you unwind.

🎮 How to Play:

🏁 Simple & Addictive Gameplay: Just tap on a banner piece, and it will automatically move to its correct spot. Complete each flag with the right pieces!
🧠 Strategic Puzzle Solving: Plan each tap carefully – if you fill up all slots with the wrong pieces, you’ll lose the level. Focus on matching pieces to clear the board!
🗺️ Complete Flags to Progress: Each level challenges you to complete flags by finding and matching all the correct pieces.
🌐 Collect & Display Flags: Unlock national symbols from various countries around the world as you complete levels. Build your own banner collection in the Avenue of Flags!
