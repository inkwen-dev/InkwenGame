## About

Alphabetic train is a game that is educational and fun. In this game the turtle on the train has a board with a letter on it. You'll want to collect the pictures that start with that letter. To collect a picture, tap the screen when the picture is over the turtle such that the letter hits the picture. Hitting an incorrect picture will reduce your health. Collect bonus cards to get more fuel for the train or to increase your health. Collect green cards to increase your score but don't hit the red cards as it will reduce your score. Reach the selected target of 25 or 100 alphabetic cards before the train runs out of fuel or you health reaches zero.
