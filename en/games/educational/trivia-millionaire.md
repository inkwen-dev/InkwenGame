## How to play

Answer questions by selecting the one correct option out of four choices. Each correct answer earns rewards and moves you further through the difficulty curve. If a question feels too challenging, you can use hints to improve your chances. The farther you progress, the harder the questions become, and each mistake matters more. Successful runs earn in-game currency and leaderboard points. Score as high as you can, climb the rankings, and compete for a spot among the top trivia players.

## About

Trivia Millionaire is a trivia game where players answer questions by choosing the correct option from four possible answers. As they progress, they move through increasingly difficult levels that test their knowledge, focus, and ability to think quickly. The game covers a wide range of topics, offering a broad and engaging quiz experience. In addition to solo play, it also features a competitive mode where players can face each other in head-to-head trivia matches. At its core, the gameplay is built around rising difficulty, score progression, and constant knowledge challenges across different categories.
