## About

Cities game is a timeless game that puts your knowledge and vocabulary to the test. Your challenge is simple: name cities that start with the last letter of the previously named city. The winner is the last player standing who can name a valid city when others cant. Dont miss your chance to test your knowledge and compete against others! Cities game will immerse you in a world of learning and fun, helping you discover fascinating cities around the globe.
