## About

Dive into a world where color meets chemistry in FlowTint &mdash; a vibrant puzzle game that challenges both your logic and your learning! Your mission: fill the entire grid by choosing from a set of colors &mdash; but with a twist. Each color represents a chemical element, helping you learn while you play! Strategize each move carefully &mdash; you have only 22 moves to make the colors flow and cover the entire space. As you progress, the puzzles get trickier, colors multiply, and your mastery of both patterns and periodic elements grows stronger. It&rsquo;s a perfect blend of art, logic, and science &mdash; wrapped in one relaxing yet challenging experience.
