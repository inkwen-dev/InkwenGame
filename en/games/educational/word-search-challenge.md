## How to play

Use mouse or touch the screen

## About

Animals ask you to find their names on the game board on the right. There are scattered in the chaotic order of the letters, but if you look closely, you will find hidden words that beasts expect from you. When the word is found, under the corresponding animal letters change color. The game has five levels and you can visit not only on land, but also under water.
