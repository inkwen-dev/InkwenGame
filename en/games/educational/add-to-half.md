## About

Sharpen your fraction skills in Add to Half, a fast-paced and brain-teasing math puzzle! In this game, your goal is simple&mdash;but tricky: find two fractions that add up to exactly one-half. Each round presents you with a mix of fraction tiles floating or flipping on the screen. Tap or click the correct pair before time runs out! As you progress, the fractions get more challenging &mdash; from simple ones like &frac14; and &frac14; to trickier combinations like and &sup1;&frasl;. Speed, accuracy, and quick thinking are your keys to climbing the leaderboard.
