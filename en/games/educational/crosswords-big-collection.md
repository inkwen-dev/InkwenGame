## How to play

1. Pick a level from the list or start with the daily crossword.
2. Read the clue — it corresponds to one word in the grid.
3. Type the answer letter by letter into the empty cells. Words cross each other, so every solved word helps with the rest.
4. If you're stuck, use a hint to reveal one letter.
5. Return to unfinished levels anytime — progress saves automatically.
6. Every solved word earns you points that climb the leaderboard and rank you against other players.
7. Unlock collection items and complete quests for bonus rewards.

## About

Crosswords: Big Collection is a classic crossword game for anyone who loves to think. You get over 800 levels with more than 30,000 unique clues, a daily crossword that refreshes every day, and a collectible items system with quests and rewards.

The game is translated into six languages: Russian, English, German, Spanish, French, and Italian. Choose your theme — soft green or dark brown — to match your mood. The interface adapts to any screen: play on PC or smartphone with the same comfort and clarity.

Progress saves automatically. Every solved word earns you points that push you up the leaderboard. Come back to unfinished puzzles, unlock collection items, and compete with players worldwide.
