## About

Kids camping is an educational game. In this game, you will help the little bear family to complete the camping successfully. The little bear family is going to go out for a spring outing. Before the spring outing, they need to prepare some necessities. Help them prepare these items at home. On the way to the spring outing, they will encounter various dangerous obstacles. Slide the screen to help them avoid obstacles. After arriving at the campsite, you can help the little bears set up tents, make barbecues, feed them desserts, and help them make kites.
