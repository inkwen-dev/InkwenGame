## About

Alphabet Soup for Kids is a fun and educational game for all ages. Learning with fun gives more attention and interest to kids. So let us learn from this game. In this game we see the soup where the snacks are filled with alphabets, all you have to do is to collect the alphabets in order from A - Z which can be capitals and small letters. Complete all letters as soon as possible and complete the game.
