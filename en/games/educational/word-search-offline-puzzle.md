## How to play

1. You're given a grid of letter tiles. Every letter is part of a hidden word — your goal is to find all the words and fill the whole board.

2. Press and drag your finger (or the mouse) across connected letters to trace a word. Unlike a straight-line word search, in Fillwords the path can bend around corners.

3. Release to submit. If the word is correct, its tiles lock in with color. If not, just try a different path — nothing is lost.

4. Find every word to clear the level. There's no timer and no pressure — take your time.

5. As you complete levels, you climb an evolution ladder — from atom and molecule all the way to mind.

6. Stuck on a word? Use a Hint to reveal a letter.

7. Tap any solved word to see what it means and grow your vocabulary.

Tip: the game works fully offline, so you can play anywhere.
