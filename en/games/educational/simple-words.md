## How to play

Play at your own pace in this relaxing word game that’s as beautiful as it is brainy.

Rules are simple -
• Tap/Click any letters to make a word
• Used letters go dark
• Clear a row when all its letters are used

Simple rules — satisfying strategy.

Try different modes:
🌞 Daily Challenge - Can you reach to the top?
🔁 Round Mode - Play at your own pace, as many rounds as you like.
🔢 Moves Mode - See how far you can go in just a few moves.
🤖 VS AI - Challenge a clever computer opponent!
