Use a mouse or touchpad to select and swap words between rows then group 4 related words in the same row to complete a set

## About

Find groups of 4 related words and place them in the same row to complete a set. Swap words between rows to arrange them correctly. Some levels contain special sets that, when completed, reveal new words for you to group. Complete all the sets before the time runs out! If you get stuck, use a hint to highlight a group of words.
