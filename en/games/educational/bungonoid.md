## About

Bungonoid is a simple one touch arcade game that is a little like break out and a little like pinball. There are three medals to be achieved based on score and there are three new paddles that can be unlocked also based on score. There are seven backgrounds will be picked each time you play. Enjoy with Bungonoid!
