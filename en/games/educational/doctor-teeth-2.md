## About

Doctor Teeth 2 is a medical-themed simulation game. Use a variety of different tools to clean up and tend to your patient's teeth. You can use the stethoscope icon if you're stuck and need a hint. You can learn more about dental hygiene while watching your patient's fun reactions while playing this game. Have you ever wanted to be a dentist? This is your chance to experience a day in a dentist's life!
