## About

My Virtual Pet Shop is a pet simulator game. In this pet shop game suitable for children of all ages, you can choose your pets and take care of cute animals, including playing with them, feeding and serving the pets. Cleaning, dressing up kittens and puppies, your purpose is to maximize the happiness of pets and develop your pet business. It is worth noting that you must complete the task within the specified time. Some pets also need deep cleaning, are you ready?
