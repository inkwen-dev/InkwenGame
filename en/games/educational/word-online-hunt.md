## About

Word: Online Hunt Word: Online Hunt is a fun and addictive word search puzzle game where your goal is to find hidden words by dragging your mouse across the letters. Test your vocabulary, improve your concentration, and see how quickly you can discover every word hidden in the puzzle! Search carefully through the grid of letters and look for the words shown on the screen. When you find a word, simply click and drag your mouse over the letters in the correct order to form and select it. Words can be hidden horizontally, vertically, diagonally, and in different directions, so keep your eyes open and think carefully! As you progress, the puzzles become more challenging, putting your word-finding skills and observation to the test. Can you find all the hidden words before time runs out?
