Bus n Ball Jam is a fun and engaging color puzzle game where every bus, every ball, and every drop counts!

## How to play

🚌 Arrange the buses: Tap and move the buses at the bottom to clear the path. Each bus has its own direction, so choose carefully!

🔴 Drop the balls: As buses leave the track, colored balls drop onto the conveyor belt. Watch each drop and plan your next move.

🟡 Move along the belt: Balls roll along the moving conveyor belt. Don't let the belt get overcrowded!

🟢 Match colors: Fill all the slots to solve the color puzzle.

🔵 Avoid overload: If too many balls pile up on the belt without a matching color slot, you lose. Think ahead, arrange smartly, and manage every drop!
