## How to play

The goal of the game is to spot the differences!

1. Read the task at the top of the screen carefully.

2. Look for the one shape that is different from the others in its form, size, emotion, sound, or other feature.

3. Click on it with your mouse or tap it with your finger to proceed to the next level.
