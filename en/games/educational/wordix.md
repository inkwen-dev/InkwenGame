## How to play

Input Type letters or tap keys on the virtual keyboard Submit Press Enter to submit your guess Delete Use Backspace to erase a letter Hints Green correct letter and position Yellow correct letter wrong position Gray letter not in the word

## About

Wordix is an engaging logic game where your task is to guess the word within a limited number of attempts. It&rsquo;s the Russian version of the popular game Wordle, but with one important difference: the words are chosen from a pre-prepared list, and you can play as much as you want without waiting for a new word each day. The game develops your thinking skills, expands your vocabulary, and provides an entertaining way to spend time. It&rsquo;s suitable for both children and adults. Simple rules make the game accessible to everyone, and color-coded hints help you find the correct answer faster.
