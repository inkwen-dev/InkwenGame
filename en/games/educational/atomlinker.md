## About

AtomLinker is a fast-paced, chemistry-themed chain-matching puzzle game where your goal is to link three or more identical chemical elements to trigger powerful reactions. Slide, connect, and strategize as you form longer chains to unlock combo boosts, element power-ups, and explosive molecular effects. Each level challenges you with unique objectives&mdash;collect specific atoms, break frozen tiles, trigger chain reactions, or race against time. As you progress, you&rsquo;ll encounter elements from across the periodic table, each bringing new gameplay twists and special abilities that keep every round exciting.
