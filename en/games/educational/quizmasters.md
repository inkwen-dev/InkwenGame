## How to play

Read each question carefully and choose the correct answer from four possible options. Each correct answer moves you higher on the prize ladder and brings you closer to the grand reward. The questions become more difficult as you progress, so stay focused and think quickly.

Earn cash, crystals, and collectible reward stickers as you play. Use your rewards to unlock new collections and keep improving your progress. You can also challenge other players in head-to-head trivia battles and prove who knows more.
