## How to play

Each level shows a country flag
Choose the Correct Answer from four options below the flag.
If you’re unsure, tap the Hint button.
Keep answering correctly to unlock new levels and test your world flag skills!

## About

Fun With Flags – Quiz Game is the ultimate challenge for players who love geography, trivia, and brain-teasing fun! Test your knowledge of world flags as you progress through levels filled with colorful flags from across the globe.

Each level displays one flag along with four answer options—only one is correct! Choose wisely and unlock new levels as you master flags from every continent.

Stuck on a tricky flag? No worries! Use the Hint button to get help by removing wrong choices or receiving clues that guide you to the correct answer.

Perfect for kids, students, and adults—whether you're learning or sharpening your global IQ, this game makes world geography fun, engaging, and rewarding!
