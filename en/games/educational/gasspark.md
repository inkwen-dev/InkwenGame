## About

Welcome to GasSpark, the high-pressure arcade reaction game where every barrel is a ticking time-bomb of volatile gas! Your mission is simple &mdash; tap every barrel before it overheats and explodes. But be warned: the sparks fly faster than you think. Barrels ignite, pressure rises, flames flare, and one missed tap can start a chain reaction that engulfs the whole board. Only the quickest players can tame the spark!
