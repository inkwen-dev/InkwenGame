## About

Candies, candies, kids love candies. However, children never think of the pain of toothache when they eat sweets. Come to Mad Dentist to experience the process of dental diagnosis and treatment. In this dentist game, you will encounter various dental problems of toothache patients. Clean teeth and solve these dental problems for children, let them feel the pain of a toothache, remember this lesson so that children will be restrained when eating sweets in the future. How angry the dentist is that the children are not taking good care of their teeth!
