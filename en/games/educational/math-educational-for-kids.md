## About

with the help of this game you can learn math but also you can test your knowledge. This game is for beginners in mathematics but also for players who have more knowledge of mathematics. You can play with adding, subtracting, multiplying or dividing. Then you will choose whether you want to count only in units, tens, hundreds or thousands. If you choose hundreds or thousands, the math will be difficult and interesting, but you will also have more time to solve the task. Choose a task according to your knowledge and play math.
