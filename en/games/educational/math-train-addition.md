## About

Math Train Addition is an educational and fun game. In this game you will see a math addition-problem on the balloon and 4 possible options will come from the right. To select correct answer from the options, tap the screen when the correct answer is over the balloon such that the balloon hits the correct answer. Hitting an incorrect number will reduce your health. Collect stars to get more points. Keep playing until the time runs out or your health reaches zero.
