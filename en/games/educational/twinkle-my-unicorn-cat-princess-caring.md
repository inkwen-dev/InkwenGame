## About

Help baby cat Twinkle hatch from her magical egg! Show the kitty around the royal palace and take good care of her! Make sure to feed kitty Twinkle yummy food in the royal kitchen! Sing the unicorn kitten to sleep when she's tired! Bathe messy kitty Twinkle!
