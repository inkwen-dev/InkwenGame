## About

Kids Dentist Games is a cute dentist game. In the game, you play as an experienced dentist. You need to rescue the characters who come to need help. Among them, there are cute cats, beautiful princesses, righteous policemen, and kind old grannies. Pick a person you think needs the most help and start working on his or her teeth.
