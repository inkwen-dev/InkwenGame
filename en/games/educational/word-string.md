## How to play

🔡 How to Play? 🔡
✔️ Start with the first word.
✔️ The next word must either:
🔹 Form a compound word with the previous word (e.g., “Sun” → “Flower” → “Pot”)
🔹 Complete a well-known phrase (e.g., Orange → Juice ("Orange Juice")
✔️ Keep linking words until you complete the full chain!
