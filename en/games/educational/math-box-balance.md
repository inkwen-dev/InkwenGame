## About

Math Box Balance is a puzzle game with numbers. Early levels have 2 boxes, and as you progress you can reach up to 8 boxes. Each box contains 4 number blocks, and the sum of these numbers is shown inside the box. Your goal is to make the sum of all boxes equal. You can swap number blocks between boxes to achieve the equal sum. It is an endless game where the difficulty increases over time, your score goes up, and the fun grows with it.
