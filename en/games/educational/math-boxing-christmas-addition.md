## About

Santa needs to get in shape for his long night of delivering gifts. Have Santa throw punches and kicks at the Bah Humbug punching bag by answering the addition problems. Answer correctly before time runs out so that the Grinch doesn't throw a punch and steal one of Santa's boxing gloves. Game ends when Santa runs out of gloves.
