## About

“Alphabet Writing For Kids” is one of the best educational app for toddlers, preschoolers and kindergarten kids to learn how to trace uppercase and lowercase alphabets from A to Z. By this app, kids can also listen to the phonetics of words improving theirs speaking skills. Each letter and related word will be read out loud and clearly so that kids will understand them easily. Kids will learn how to write and read with fun and for free. Key Features: - ABC Tracing games. - Over 60 funny cartoon sticker. - Lot of funny sounds and musics. - Kids learn english alphabet. - Letters, Number and Animals. - Uppercase and Lowercase letters. - Super simple game interface.
