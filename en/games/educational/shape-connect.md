## About

Shape Connect is a cute and fun game where kids help two teddy bears meet each other! There&rsquo;s a broken road between them, and you need to fill the gaps with the right shapes from the bottom of the screen. Place the shapes correctly to complete the path so the teddy bears can cross and be together. It&rsquo;s a perfect game to learn about shapes and problem-solving.
