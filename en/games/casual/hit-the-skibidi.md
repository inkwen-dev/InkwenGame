## About

Hit The Skibidi is a fun game where you need to throw a diamond sword to Steve by tapping the screen at the right time. When you reach a score of 10, Steve transforms into Herobrine, and when you reach a score of 20, Herobrine turns into Skibidi Toilet, playing the magnificent song of Skibidi Toilet! Focus while throwing the swords and try to send them to empty spaces. Enjoy the game!
