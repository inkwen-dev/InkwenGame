## About

A shipment of ducks has fallen into the sea. The ducks are drifting surrounded by hungry octopuses. Manage the duck rescue boat, day and night, and collect as many ducks as you can. Watch out for the octopuses, there are a limited number of hits. Collect the life floats to increase the number of hits.
