![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/dino-piler/cover.jpg)

Play as a cute dinosaur that lays eggs. Build the biggest egg tower and compete with other players. Your task is to lay eggs of different colors in a row

## How to play

In order to place an egg you need to tap on the right side of the screen or press the arrow to the right on the keyboard In order to remove an egg you need to tap on the left side of the screen or press the left arrow on the keyboard
