## About

The magical holiday of Christmas is just around the corner! But real magic is what we do with our own hands. Join the cute princesses and make this holiday unforgettable. First, we will make fake snow from cornmeal and shaving foam. Then, using snow, we'll decorate the jar, turning it into a wonderful Christmas lamp. Christmas should not only be beautiful, but also delicious! Therefore, the next step is to make wonderful icing Christmas gingerbread. And finally, we will give new life to old things. Using ordinary socks, the princesses will show you how to make a cute Christmas gnome. DIY a magical Christmas!
