## How to play

PLAYER 1:

A,D: Left/Right Movements
W or SPACE: Jump

PLAYER 2:

RIGHT / LEFT ARROWS: Left / Right Movements
UP ARROW: Jump
On Mobile Devices:

Only 1 Player mode is enabled on mobile devices. Tap left and right on the screen to move left and right. Tap with two fingers to jump.

## About

Snow Rush 3D is a fast-paced winter action game where your reflexes and timing are put to the test on icy tracks. Ride your sled through endless snowy paths, dodge trees, snowmen, and barriers, and try to travel as far as possible without crashing. Snow Rush 3D offers both single-player and two-player modes, letting you compete with a friend on the same device. Each run becomes more challenging as your speed increases, making every second a true test of focus and control.

Collect gifts along the way to unlock new sleds and add variety to your runs. With smooth controls, clean 3D visuals, and an addictive gameplay loop, Snow Rush 3D delivers quick and exciting sessions that are easy to start but hard to master.
