## About

Unleash your fury in BREAK IT, a fast-paced action game where an angry fuzzy creature breaks through walls with sheer foot power! Take control of a mischievous Labubu-style hero on a vertical descent through layers of obstacles, spikes, and breakable bricks. Your mission: smash everything in your path &mdash; but be careful not to hit the deadly spikes or unbreakable blocks!
