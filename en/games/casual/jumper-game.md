## About

Welcome to our newest Jumper game. The goal of the game is to jump between two objects. If the black creature jumps too high, it will touch the upper spiky ceiling and it will explode. Try to stand up on the platform to earn points. Try not to touch the upper spiky ceiling. Enjoy and have fun!
