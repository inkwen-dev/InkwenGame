## About

The Earth: Evolution is a relaxing placement game. What does your fantasy planet look like? You can use beautiful objects to decorate and build your unique planet in different areas of the universe. Collect sunlight by placing buildings and when you have enough sunlight, you get more resources needed to build your planet. Escape from the stressful daily life for a few minutes or even longer!
