## About

ReefRescue is a fun underwater adventure where every piece of trash you collect helps protect the ocean! Take control of a brave little seahorse and swim through colorful reefs, collecting floating waste while carefully avoiding fish and other sea creatures. Stay alert, react quickly, and keep the ocean clean without disturbing marine life. With simple controls and engaging gameplay, ReefRescue is easy to pick up and enjoyable for players of all ages. Challenge your reflexes, improve your timing, and become the hero of the reef!
