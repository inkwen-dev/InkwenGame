## About

Stack Ball Helix is a 3d arcade game where players smash, bump and bounce through revolving helix platforms to reach the end. Your ball smashes like a brick through colorful platforms that block its descent, but if you hit a black one, it's all over! if you pick up speed, then even dark stacks will not interfere, and the ball will be able to overcome them.
