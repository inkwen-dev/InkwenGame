## About

Box Brothers ek fast-paced reaction game hai jisme tumhe boxes par dikh rahe arrows ke according correct arrow key press karni hoti hai. Har sahi move se tumhara score badhta hai, lekin galat arrow press karne par tum damage lete ho. Isliye dhyaan se aur sahi timing ke saath khelo. Agar tum lagataar sahi arrows press karte ho, to tumhara combo streak banega jo tumhe extra points deta hai. Jitni fast tum react karoge, utna zyada score milega. Lekin yaad rahe &mdash; agar tum zyada der idle rahe, to tum points lose kar doge. Toh ready ho jao, focus karo aur speed ke saath khelo&hellip; How high can your score go?
