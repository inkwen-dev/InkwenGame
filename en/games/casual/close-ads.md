# Close Ads

<a href="https://inkwengame.com/games/close-ads" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Play on InkwenGame</a>

![cover](https://img.inkwengame.com/games/close-ads/cover.jpg)

A fast chaos arcade game where your only goal is simple: close ads. But every few levels, the ads become more annoying.

## How to play

PC

Left Mouse Button — click close buttons
Mouse Move — aim and chase ads
Double Left Click — required on some levels
UI Button: Restart From Level 1 — restart progress

Mobile / Tablet

Tap — press close buttons
Double Tap — required on some levels
Drag finger / move finger — follow moving ads visually
UI Button: Restart From Level 1 — restart progress
