## About

Perfect Snapshot is a casual stress relief game. Buddy is a puppet whose limbs can be retracted at will. Your goal is to secure Buddys limbs so that he can successfully take the photo. When Buddys limbs are stretched too long, he will show an uncomfortable expression, but when taking pictures, he will turn into a smiling face!
