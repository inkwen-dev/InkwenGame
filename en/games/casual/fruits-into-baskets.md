## How to play

🍊 Tap a fruit on the screen and it will fall.
🍊 If a fruit is stuck and can't fall — remove the ones blocking it from below first.
🍊 Collect 3 fruits in a basket and a new basket will open.
🍊 If there's no matching basket for a fruit — no worries, it will wait on a temporary plate.
Clear all the fruits from the field — level complete. You're the best!

## About

Just tap on the fruits, watch them fall into place, and enjoy the satisfaction of everything going where it belongs.
Your goal is to clear the whole field and sort all the fruits into the right baskets. No timer, no pressure — just you, the fruits, and that satisfying feeling of order after each level.
Perfect for short breaks when you need to unwind and breathe. Grab your phone, get comfortable, and sort fruits to your heart's content! 🍊🍋🍇
