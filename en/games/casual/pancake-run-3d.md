## About

Pancake Run is a fun casual game. You need to make delicious pancakes and deliver them to discerning customers! You can add delicious fruits on top of the pancakes, such as strawberries, bananas, and blueberries! Try to stack your pancakes as high as possible! Then be careful of the traps and gluttons on the road!
