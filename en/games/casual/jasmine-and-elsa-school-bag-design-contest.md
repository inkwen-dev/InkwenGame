## About

It's time to go back to school! Young princesses Jasmine and Elsa are known for their art talents. They decided to surprise everyone with their new school backpacks. Each of the girls came up with their own unique design and created a cool school backpack, unlike everyone else. Who will be the best designer, Elsa or Jasmine? Use a palette of colors and textures, as well as many decorations, stickers and decorative elements. Participate in this contest with the princesses and help defeat your favorite!
