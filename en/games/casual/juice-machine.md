## About

Juice Machine is a relaxing casual game where you operate a colorful blender. Fruits continuously appear, and your task is to wait for the blades to blend them into delicious juice. You can click directly on the fruits to speed up the blending process and earn extra resources. Use the rewards to upgrade the blender to the maximum level, increasing speed and efficiency. Simple, fun, and extremely addictive!
