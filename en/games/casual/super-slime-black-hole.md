## About

Super Slime: Black Hole invites you to become a powerful slime predator in a world full of objects waiting to be swallowed! In this exciting black hole game, your slime acts like a living black hole that can absorb anything smaller than itself. This relaxing eat-and-grow game experience combines simple controls with satisfying growth mechanics, similar to the fun style of Hole.io. With smooth gameplay, simple actions, and endless growth opportunities, this casual arcade game adventure is perfect for those who enjoy a relaxing simulation game. Keep eating, keep growing, and become the strongest slime in this exciting level up game!
