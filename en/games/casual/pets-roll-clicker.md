## About

Pets Roll is a colorful idle gacha game where you roll eggs, hatch adorable cube-style pets, and build the ultimate collection. With 187 unique pets across 11 rarity grades &mdash; from Common kittens to Legendary golden dragons &mdash; every roll is a surprise! How to play? Click or tap the Roll button to hatch a pet. Use the shop to buy pets and eggs, manage your nests, complete daily quests, and collect buff multipliers to boost your luck. Features - 187 unique pets with cube-style art - 11 rarity grades from Common to Legendary - 26 themed collections - 17 egg tiers with incubation nests - Rebirth prestige system - Daily quests, bonuses &amp; shop
