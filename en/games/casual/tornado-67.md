## How to play

Control a tornado and absorb everything around you to grow bigger and stronger! Start with small objects such as trash, trees, and road signs, then move on to cars and even entire buildings.

On PC, use WASD to move the tornado. Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse to rotate the camera and look around the environment. Use the mouse wheel to zoom the camera in and out.

On mobile devices, control the tornado using the on-screen virtual joystick.

## About

Become the hungriest tornado in the world!

Start your journey as a small whirlwind capable of absorbing only tiny objects, and gradually grow into a massive destructive force of nature.

Explore 3 unique locations: a sunny beach, a bustling city, and a suburban neighborhood. Absorb everything in your path — from flip-flops and beach umbrellas to cars and entire buildings.

The more objects you collect, the stronger your tornado becomes, allowing you to absorb even larger and heavier objects.

Destroy, grow, and unleash a true tornado apocalypse!
