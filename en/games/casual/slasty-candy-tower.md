## How to play

Each game level consists of several stages which gradually become more difficult.
To complete the stage you need to feed all of the chewbrick-monsters with a candy from your cannon.
Feed a monster simply by tapping on the screen or pressing the space bar at the right time to make a candy shot - and you'll feed the chewbrick.
