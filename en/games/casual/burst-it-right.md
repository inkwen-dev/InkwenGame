## About

Get ready for a colorful popping challenge in Burst It Right! Your task is to guide drifting balloons into position and pop them using matching-colored pins. Accuracy is key &mdash; only a correct color match will burst the balloon, while mismatches count as failures. With limited chances and increasing speed, your timing and strategy will be put to the test. Can you keep up the popping streak and master the art of precision bursting?
