## About

The Black entices puzzle enthusiasts with its sleek simplicity, challenging players to flip and strategize across expanding grids. Each move affects neighboring tiles, demanding clever tactics to darken the entire board. From 3x3 to 6x6, this captivating game offers escalating complexity and endless enjoyment for strategic minds.
