## About

Fisquarium is an idle clicker game that combines the joy of clicking with the soothing atmosphere of an aquarium. In this game, players click to earn fish, unlock diverse species, enhance production through upgrades, discover new fish types, collect trophies, and immerse themselves in the relaxing world of aquatic wonders. Whether youre a casual player or an avid clicker fan, Fisquarium offers a laid-back and enjoyable experience!
