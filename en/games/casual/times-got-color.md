## About

Match the Colors, Earn the Points! Times Got Color is a fast-paced and addictive color matching game that puts your reflexes to the test. In this game, youll face a colorful clock with four different colors on its circumference. As the clock hands rotate, your goal is to tap the screen at the exact moment the clock hand aligns with the matching color.
