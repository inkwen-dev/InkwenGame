## About

Roof Rails Online is undoubtedly one of the most interesting new arcade games this year, with addictive gameplay and 3D art style. If you are a fan of such games, don't miss this game. Your mission is to be on the roof. Use a wooden pole to maintain balance. Collect wooden blocks to increase the length of the pole. At the same time, avoid gears to reduce the length of the pole. When you reach the end, don't forget to take all the gems away. Are you ready?
