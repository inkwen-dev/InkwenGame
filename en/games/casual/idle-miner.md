## About

Idle Miner is an idle game about a cat who loves mining and getting different resources. There are 25 different resources; you can sell them or use them for crafting better pickaxes. Craft better and better pickaxes and mine into the depths of Earth. Also, there are 9 upgrades that will help you get better materials and craft better pickaxes. After level 50 you can prestige to make your cat stronger!
