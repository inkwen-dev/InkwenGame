## How to play

Stop the Snake Before It's Too Late! Take control of a powerful drill and defend the surface from massive segmented snakes crawling up from underground. In this intense arcade defense game, you'll time your strikes to shatter the snake's body piece by piece. Swing your drill, aim carefully, and destroy every segment before the snake's head reaches the top!

Desktop Controls:
Tap to launch drill attack.
Spacebar to launch drill attack.

Mobile Controls:
Tap to launch drill attack.

## About

Snake Drill is a casual arcade defense game where you protect the surface from giant segmented snakes crawling up from underground tunnels. A giant segmented snake is crawling up through winding tunnels toward the surface, and your only weapon is a swinging pendulum drill. Time your strikes to shatter the snake's body segment by segment before its head breaks through. Upgrade your drill, use a roguelike card system that changes every run.
 
Swing your drill like a pendulum, time your attack, and shatter the snake's body segment by segment before its head reaches the surface. Simple to pick up, hard to master.

One button. One drill. One chance to stop what's coming.
