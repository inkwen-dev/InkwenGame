## How to play

Objective: Destroy all blocks
• How to Win: Aim/Reverse direction of lasers to eliminate all blocks. 
Destroyed blocks spawns buffs: +1 Count / +1 Damage / +1 Speed etc..
Gather buffs - to became stronger and finish level faster

Control:

on PC: 
 - Click: Left Mouse Button (or Space) - to Reverse direction of turrets
 - Hold: Left Mouse button (or Space)  - to Stop movement

on Mobile:
 - Tap and Release - to Reverse direction of turrets
 - Tap and Hold: Left Mouse button or Space  - to Stop movement
