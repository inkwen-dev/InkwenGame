## How to play

Welcome to Unboxing Brainrots - a vibrant and wildly addictive game where every click brings you closer to new discoveries! 🎁🐾

A whole world of surprises awaits you here:

✨ Open mysterious cases and find rare Italian animals with unique designs.
⚔️ Upgrade and upgrade your weapons to be ready for any challenge.
🐒 Collect and breed pets, create incredible combinations, and unlock new creatures.
🏰 Develop your own base: build, mine resources, and become a true unboxing master.

Every case holds a surprise, and every new discovery can be decisive! Will you be able to collect the entire collection of funny animals, unite them into a team, and create your own invincible empire?

🌈 Colorful graphics, frantic action, and a sea of ​​humor await you in this game!
🎮 Become part of the crazy world of Brainrots and prove that you're the best collector!
