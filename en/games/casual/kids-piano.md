## How to play

Click on the animal to activate its tool Click on the animal icon on the piano panel to make the piano play with the sounds of that animal Click on the keys on the piano to make it play

## About

Forest animals gathered in the forest together to play music together. You have a pinano that can play normal piano sounds, but apart from that it can play with animal voices, and animals have their own instruments that they can use, just click on the beast to activate them.
