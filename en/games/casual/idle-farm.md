## About

Idle Farm is a casual idle farming game. Leave the chaotic city life behind and start building your dream farm. Unlock various types of plants, vegetables, fruits, and animals and make money with their help even when you're offline. Sell your crops and organic products to buy new types of seeds, even magical ones. This is your first step to becoming the world's most successful farmer. You know what they say: When life gives you lemons, plant their seeds and watch 'em grow!
