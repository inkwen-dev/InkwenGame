## How to play

WASD = move the player
Move mouse on X-axis (click and drag on screen) = turn the player
On-screen joystick (bottom left) = move the player
Eat button = eat animals and objects (eating also happens automatically when close)

## About

Play as a hungry dinosaur on a wild mission! In each level, you get a task to complete eat animals, smash environment props, and devour everything in your path. But beware: a giant dinosaur roams the map, hunting for you. If it spots you, it will eat you and the level fails! Stay hidden, move smart, and finish your task to unlock the next level. As you progress, more giant dinosaurs appear, making every level harder and more thrilling. Can you survive, eat, and destroy your way to the top of the food chain?
Features:
Play as a dinosaur and complete eating tasks in every level
Destroy environment props and eat animals around the map
Avoid the giant enemy dinosaurs patrolling each level
Levels get harder with more enemy dinosaurs as you progress
Simple controls with keyboard, mouse, and on-screen joystick support
