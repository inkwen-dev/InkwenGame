## About

This is an easy and relaxing game in which you are the owner of a pet store! Positive and beautiful graphics, meditative gameplay. Grow food, feed animals, build new enclosures, sell animals to buyers. Your goal is to earn as much money as possible, for this try to grow and sell as many animals as possible. Have a good game!
