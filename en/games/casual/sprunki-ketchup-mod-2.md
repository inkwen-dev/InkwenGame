Transform ordinary Sprunki into ketchup versions in this physics-based game. Precision and strategy are key as you calculate perfect trajectories across 30 challenging levels. Use your slingshot skills to aim, launch, and bounce ketchup accurately, mastering the art of splattering for a messy, fun-filled adventure!
