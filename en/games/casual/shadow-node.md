## How to play

"SHADOW NODE" is a cyberpunk idle hacker game. Click "EXECUTE" to mine Data, then spend it in the Market on Bots and power-ups that boost your hash rate automatically. Complete Quests and Syndicate Ops in the Network to earn Intel and Dark Matter. Watch your Trace Level. If it hits 100% you get locked out. Reach higher levels and Prestige to reset for permanent multipliers. Offline earnings keep your network growing while you're away.
