## About

SkyAtoms is a light, educational, and fast-climbing arcade game where science meets the sky. Each cloud drifting upward carries an atomic symbol from the periodic table&mdash;H, O, Na, Cl, and many more. Your mission is to jump from one cloud to the next, climbing higher and higher while recognizing and reacting to the elements that appear. Timing is everything. Tap to leap, land safely, and choose the right cloud before it floats away. Some clouds move, some fade, and some drop quickly, turning each jump into a race against gravity.
