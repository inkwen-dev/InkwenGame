Choose the requested items from the shelf, collect them on a tray, and carefully pack each night order.

## How to play

During the selection stage, tap the items shown in the order. The items you choose are placed on the tray.

During the packing stage, drag items from the tray into the box. If an item does not fit, choose another spot or rotate it with the rotate button. You can return an unsuitable item to the tray or restart the whole layout.

Use a hint when you are not sure what to do next. The more compactly you fill the box, the more stars you earn.
