## How to play

Rotate the maze to get the Capyballras out of the maze and into the pipe to complete the levels! Once in a while you will have a bonus level where you can collect even more Capyballras to score more points. Earn coins to spend them on unlocking newer Capyballra designs.


Desktop Controls:

Use your mouse to drag around the maze to rotate it clock wise or anti-clockwise

Mobile Controls:

Use your finger to drag around the maze to rotate it clock wise or anti-clockwise
