## About

Eat eat is a fun and addictive game where you have to put balls in the mouth of a spinning head. The eaten balls will reappear in the game. The more balls one eats, the higher the score will be. You can buy balls by paying for them with a bill, but the price of the ball increases. Try to complete all 40 levels and get the highest score!
