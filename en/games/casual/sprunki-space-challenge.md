## About

In this exciting online game, kids can blast off into space and take to the skies to save the day. By hitting a speeding space car or falling rocket, players can jump into the air and start taking out enemy spacecraft. With special abilities at their disposal, they can rack up points and show off their skills. The game is free to play and offers a fun and challenging experience for kids.
