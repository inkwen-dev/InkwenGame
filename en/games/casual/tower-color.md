## About

Tower Color is a new one-hand addictive 3D Game with an infinity of levels to complete & mesmerizing colors. Level after level, the difficulty increases and the tower gets higher and higher! How to play? - Start each level by choosing the right angle to hit the tower, swipe right or left. - Throw the Ball on Blocks of the matching color to destroy it! - Make the Tower collapse!
