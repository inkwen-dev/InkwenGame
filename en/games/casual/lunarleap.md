## About

LunarLeap takes you on an exciting space adventure where every leap counts! Guide your brave astronaut through mysterious cosmic landscapes, collect valuable spaceships, and avoid dangerous zones that stand in your way. Time your movements carefully as the challenge grows with every level. Stay alert, dodge hazards, and gather as many spaceships as possible to achieve the highest score. With simple controls, colorful visuals, and engaging gameplay, LunarLeap is perfect for players of all ages who enjoy fun arcade adventures in outer space.
