## About

Gun Bounce Idle throws you into a wild chain reaction of bullets, bounces, and nonstop destruction. Drop your weapons into the arena and watch them ricochet across the field, spraying fire in every direction while stacking up cash with every hit.

Build your ultimate damage machine by upgrading power, attack speed, crit chance, and special modifiers. Unlock new guns with unique behaviors and turn simple shots into explosive chaos. The more you optimize, the more the screen fills with satisfying destruction.

Level up to unlock stronger targets, complete achievements, and push your build to its limits. When progress slows, activate Prestige to reset and return stronger than ever — multiplying your power and accelerating your climb to insane damage numbers.
