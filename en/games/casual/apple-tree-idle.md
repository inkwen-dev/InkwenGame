## About

Apple Tree Idle is a clicker-idle game about cats who like apples so much! Manage them, help them collect apples from the tree, and sell the apples for profit. Also you can make magical apples, eat them, and increase your income. What about apple juice? Yes, you can make it too! Manage your cats and try to make it as fast as it possible!
