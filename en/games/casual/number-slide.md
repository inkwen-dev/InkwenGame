## About

Number Slide is the ultimate number sorting puzzle Put your logic and problem-solving skills to the test in Number Slide, a fun and addictive number puzzle game designed to challenge your mind and sharpen your thinking speed. In Number Slide, you are presented with a grid filled with randomly arranged number tiles. Your objective is simple yet highly engaging &mdash; rearrange the numbers by sliding tiles until they are perfectly ordered either in ascending or descending sequence, depending on the challenge.
