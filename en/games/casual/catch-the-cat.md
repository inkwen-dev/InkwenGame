## How to play

The goal of the game is to draw a line using the mouse or touch so the character reaches the kitten and brings it safely back home, dodging all obstacles. The player earns coins, which can be used to customize the kitten and improve its mood.

## About

Catch the cat is a cozy line-drawing puzzle. Draw a path from the kid to the same-color kitten, dodge cars, dogs, holes and fences, grab the food when the kitten is hungry, and bring it safely home. +50 hand-tuned levels, collectible coins, a pet home where you feed and play with your cat, and a wardrobe to dress it up. Simple one-finger controls. Playable in English, Portuguese and Spanish.
