## About

Control your plane by using your mouse / touch and avoid the obstacles on your way to reaching the higher score. Be careful, the more distance you travel, the faster your plane gets. On certain parts, the environment around you changes and so do your obstacles. Can you avoid obstacles and reach the gold medal?
