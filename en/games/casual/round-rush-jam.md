## How to play

Tap or left-click a colored piece to send it along the direction shown by its white arrows. A piece leaves the board only when its path is clear, so move blocking pieces in the right order and use boosters when a puzzle gets tricky. Remove every colored piece before the move counter reaches zero to win the level.

Controls: left mouse button on desktop; tap on mobile and tablet. No keyboard controls are required.

## About

Round Rush Jam is a bright casual puzzle game where every piece can move only in the direction of its arrows. New obstacles, linked pieces, rotating paths, and useful boosters keep every level fresh and satisfying.
