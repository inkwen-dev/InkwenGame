## About

Monkey Farm is an idle/management game made by TinyDobbins. You control a cute monkey character that just created their supermarket. Plant fruits, move around from station to station to fill the stands with bananas or corn, or more. Clients will pick them up and wait for you at the cashier desk. You can upgrade your character, unlock new work stations, or even recruit other workers to help you manage the store !
