## About

Timer Cannon

Every second on the screen is coming to kill you — and the only way to buy life is to destroy time itself.

🕹️ About the Game

How to Play: Aim, time your blast, and fire. A single precision tap triggers the heavy cannon, launching explosive rounds across the field to obliterate the nearest approaching clock before its hand completes a full rotation. Every clock you destroy clears vital breathing room and adds precious seconds back into your run's master timer. Miss your window, and the ticking swarm closes the gap.
