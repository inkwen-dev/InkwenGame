## How to play

The goal of the game is to turn a desert island into a prosperous paradise by completing tasks, constructing buildings, collecting resources and discovering new territories. The player develops the infrastructure, explores the secrets of the island and achieves a high level of survival and prosperity.

Controls - Swipe/Left mouse button
