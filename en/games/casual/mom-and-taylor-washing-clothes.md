## About

This is a family game for doing housework. First, you must help Taylor brush her teeth and wash her face. Then after Taylor, Mum, and the puppy have breakfast, Mum will take them to the park to play. You have the opportunity to go to a fascinating grocery shop, you can choose laundry supplies, pay for them, and learn to count money.
