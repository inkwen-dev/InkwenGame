## About

GermAway is a fast, simple, and highly reactive survival game where your only mission is crystal clear: stay away from every germ on the screen. You control a tiny character moving through an environment filled with roaming, bouncing, and unpredictable contagious germs. They slide, swirl, chase, or drift across the arena&mdash;one touch is all it takes for the infection to spread and the game to end.
