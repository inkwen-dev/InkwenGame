## How to play

1. Control the mirrors. Your main tool is clicking on the mirrored elements located on the playing field.
2. Charge all targets. There are light bulbs on the field that need to be charged.
3. You will see how a bright stream of light spreads smoothly and precisely along the route you have created, consistently charging all the cells.
