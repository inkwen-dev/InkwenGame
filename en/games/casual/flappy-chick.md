## About

Flap through obstacles without hitting them and go as far as you can! You are a little (but fat!) cute flightless chicken that wants to travel across the land! And you can’t fly, you are using a jet pack to do this. Do your best to dodge obstacles and go as far as you can. How to Play? Play with Left Mouse Button or Space Key.
