## How to play

Move — WASD or Arrow keys
Move (mouse) — hold the left button and drag in a direction, then release
Move (touch) — swipe in a direction

Move one step at a time. Choose up, down, left, or right to reach the goal.

## About

A rolling-cube puzzle where color is everything. Roll a painted cube to the exit, landing only on tiles that match the face touching the ground. Solve 60 levels of color-matching paths — with platforms that rotate, move, and teleport — in a satisfying test of spatial thinking.
Fans of classics like Bloxorz will feel right at home.
