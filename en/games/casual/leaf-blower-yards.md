## How to play

Mouse or touch the blower drives toward your pointer or switch to the joystick and drag anywhere Keyboard arrow keys or WASD Click the cards at the bottom to buy upgrades

## About

Drive a ride-on leaf blower through yards buried in autumn leaves. Your air stream gathers the leaves into a pile in front of the nozzle. Drive the pile into the bag zone: every 50 leaves fill a bag and pay coins. When a yard is clear, the lawn shows a picture.
