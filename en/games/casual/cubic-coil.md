## How to play

PC: Rotate cube with WASD. Steer snake with Arrow Keys.
Mobile: Swipe on the left side to rotate the cube; swipe on the right side to steer the snake.

## About

Guide a green-black voxel snake inside a rotatable cube. Spin both snake and cube to line up moves, collect voxel fruit squares and rare big fruits for bonus points. Classic Snake rules, reimagined in 3D with a crisp green backdrop.
