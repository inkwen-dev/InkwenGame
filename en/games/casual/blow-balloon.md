## About

Blow balloon is an easy and fun game. Inflate the air into balloons of different colors and shapes. Choose the balloon blowing hero, inflate air using the mouth. Hold the finger on-screen and then blow the balloon blow like the bubble gum. When balloon blown enough, release your finger. If a Balloon size is too big, it will explode and if it is small then Balloon Hero cannot sell it to customers. Blow the balloons perfectly so the customers can buy from balloon heroes. Blow balloon of perfect size. Blow into perfect shapes to get coins. Get the combo when blow consecutive bubble gum balloons of perfect size.
