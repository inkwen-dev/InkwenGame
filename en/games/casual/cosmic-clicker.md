## About

Cosmic Clicker is a highly engaging and monetizable idle clicker game set in a vibrant, stylized cosmos. The core loop is simple and addictive: click celestial objects to earn points, spend them on strategic upgrades, and watch your score soar into the trillions. With deep progression systems including missions, achievements, permanent upgrades, and a prestige Rebirth mechanic, the game is built for long-term player retention.
