## About

*Knock the Fruit* is a casual mini-game belonging to the match-and-eliminate genre. The gameplay mechanics are as follows: set within a 9x9 grid matrix, the core objective is to eliminate adjacent fruits of the same color to meet the target score required for each level. Players can eliminate adjacent fruits&mdash;either vertically and horizontally (in Normal Mode) or in all eight directions (in Hammer Mode)&mdash;simply by tapping on a fruit. Points are awarded for eliminating two or more fruits in a single move, with the score calculation dependent on both the quantity of fruits eliminated and the current level number.
