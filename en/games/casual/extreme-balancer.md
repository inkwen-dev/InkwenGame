## How to play

Roll the ball forward carefully on the wooden bridges and narrow tracks
When you see moving platforms / spinning parts, pause and watch the pattern first.
Your goal is to roll the Balancer Ball across wooden bridges, dangerous paths, rotating blocks, and moving platforms.
Time your movement, then cross quickly but controlled.
If the ball falls, the level restarts—try again and improve your timing.

## About

Extreme Balancer is an exciting and challenging game where you have to balance a ball on narrow wooden bridges and guide it safely to the end of each level. Be careful, if the ball falls off, you will have to start over! The game has beautiful 3D graphics and realistic physics that make it feel like you are really balancing the ball.
