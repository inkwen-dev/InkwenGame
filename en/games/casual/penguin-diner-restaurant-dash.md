## About

Penguin Diner is undoubtedly the most interesting business simulation game this year, with addictive gameplay and 2D art style. Penny got lost in the snowstorm. He decided to open a fun restaurant and provide customers with a variety of food and drinks, but Penny couldn't support the operation of the restaurant alone, she needs your help. Your task is to prepare food for the guests, complete the order and serve the food as soon as possible. The income from the operation can be used to decorate the restaurant or upgrade the food level! Are you ready?
