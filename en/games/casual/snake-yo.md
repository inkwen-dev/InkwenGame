## About

“Snake YO” is a top down style game where you are a snake and you need to eat magic food to grow and become strong. Stay as long as possible and destroy as many enemy snakes as possible to accumulate points and save your best score. Use dash to do more difficult and risky maneuvers. A very fun game to play anytime. Have a good time!
