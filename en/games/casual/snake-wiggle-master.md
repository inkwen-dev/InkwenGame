## About

Looking for a fun snake maze game that’s easy to start but keeps you thinking?

In Snake Wiggle Master!, you guide a snake through clever mazes where one smart move can open the whole path.

Move your snake through tight corridors, plan the right order, and race against the clock. ⏰ The challenge comes from thinking fast but smart. Some paths look safe at first, but one wrong move can block the maze completely. Choose carefully, move efficiently, and clear the level before time runs out. ✨

With simple controls and clean visuals, Snake Wiggle Master! is easy to play and exciting to master.
