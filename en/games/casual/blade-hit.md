## How to play

Left mouse button/tap on screen - throw knife.
The player's task is to throw all the knives without hitting the previous ones.

## About

Throw knives at logs to break them, collect coins, and unlock new knives. Collect up to 96 weapons (swords, staffs, knives, tridents, shurikens, and more), set records, and become a knife master in Blade Hit!
