## About

Hi friends, what is your level of interest in pirates? This is a lovely game from Jhurr called Cursed Pirate Treasure. As everyone knows, the treasure is typically hidden by someone. Pirates will do whatever it takes to find treasure. One of the pirates set out to claim the enormous riches that he had discovered. However, the loot is cursed, and these pirates become skeletons as a result of an unsolvable curse they acquired through one means or another. In order to reach the highest treasures and gather as much treasure as you can, you must assist the small, cursed pirate in this game. Avoid getting struck by the swords, spears, and mallets
