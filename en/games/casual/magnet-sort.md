## About

Magnet Sort is a fast-paced industrial arcade game where you control a powerful magnetic crane above a moving factory conveyor. Time each drop carefully, collect valuable components, build combos, and avoid dangerous batteries and explosive canisters. Take on 40 increasingly challenging contracts in Career mode. Contracts are grouped into series and feature different combinations of target components, strict time limits, and valuable rewards. Lives carry over between contracts, so every mistake matters. Complete shifts to earn credits and purchase permanent upgrades for cable speed, magnet range, protective armor, and credit income. In Endless mode, there is no final objective. Survive the accelerating conveyor, collect rare components, build massive combos, and compete for the highest score on the leaderboard. Master the magnet, upgrade your equipment, complete every contract, and become the factory&rsquo;s best scrap sorter!
