## How to play

How to play

Drag / swipe (or use the stick) to roll your ball.
Bump into objects smaller than you — they stick and you grow.
Reach the level goal before time runs out.
Bigger ball = bigger things you can pick up.
Use power-ups when you’re stuck, and watch a quick ad to continue if the clock hits zero.

## About

You steer a sticky ball through colorful voxel worlds — city streets, picnic spreads, deserts, pastry kitchens, and outer space. Roll into toys, food, furniture, and whatever else is smaller than you; everything you touch sticks and makes you bigger. Hit your size goals, chase stars, and keep rolling through 50 stages (then endless mode). Use power-ups like magnets, freezes, and size boosts when you need a push — and don’t let the timer catch you.
