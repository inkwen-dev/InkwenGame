## About

Geo Drop is an exciting 2D arcade game where precision and quick reflexes are your keys to victory. Dive into a world of geometric challenges as you strategically launch white balls from the top of the screen to hit and destroy yellow geometric shapes before they reach the top. With a sleek black background and striking red walls, the vibrant colors make every level visually engaging. Master the art of the perfect shot and watch as shapes shatter before you. Can you handle the increasing speed and complexity? Test your skills and aim for the highest score in Geo Drop!
