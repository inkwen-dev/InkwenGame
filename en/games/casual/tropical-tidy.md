## About

Welcome to Tropical Tidy &ndash; The Juiciest Sorting Puzzle! Escape to a vibrant tropical paradise where fruits are everywhere, and only the sharpest minds can bring order to the juicy chaos! In Tropical Tidy, your mission is to sort, match, and organize colorful fruits into their correct places before the board overflows. How to Play: Tap, drag, and place fruits into their matching containers. Strategize your moves to create perfect sorted stacks. Watch out &mdash; the puzzle gets trickier as more fruits appear! Clear levels, unlock new tropical backgrounds, and aim for high scores.
