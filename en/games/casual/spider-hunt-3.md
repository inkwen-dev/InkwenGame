## How to play

Place bomb using mouse

## About

At the start, you'll place a bomb in the bug's path, and the bomb explodes, killing the bug. Then you put bombs again and get more gifts to increase the effectiveness of destroying them. The game also has a time limit. When you kill all the bugs in the allotted time, you win and go to the next screen. Otherwise, you will lose and keep going through it until you succeed.
