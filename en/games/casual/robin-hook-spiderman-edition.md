## About

Use your ropes to swing a destructible rag doll from one hook to the other. You have full control over your ropes, touch a hook or object to throw your rope into it, touch again to release it and fly through the sky with stunts. complete all levels with the same agility as a spider stunt. To do this, all you need to do is: - Tap to hang and do amazing jumps - Tap your screen to hook your stick man with your grappling hook and release to let go - Avoid all the obstacles that stand in your way - Swing from hook to hook to pass all levels with different stunts Using bumpers and your grappling hook, perform acrobatic figures and show your friends who's the boss! Think you can do better than a stick hero? Prove it! The faster you go, the more amazing your stuff will be.
