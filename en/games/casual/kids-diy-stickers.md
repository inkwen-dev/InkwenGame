## About

Kids DIY Stickers is a casual game. Imagine how fun it would be to DIY a special card with cute stickers. You have a transparent card and a variety of cute stickers, such as cute puppies, bunnies, calves, beautiful flowers, etc. You can choose your favorite stickers from these stickers and stick them on this transparent card. Finally, you can save and collect the cards you have made.
