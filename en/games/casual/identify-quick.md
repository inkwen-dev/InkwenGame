## About

Test Your Color Perception and Speed! Identify Quick! is a fast-paced arcade game that challenges your color vision and reflexes. In this game, youll be presented with a colorful background that changes color rapidly. Amidst this vibrant backdrop, a hidden box will appear, matching the color of the background. Your task is to spot the box before it disappears.
