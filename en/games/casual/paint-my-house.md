## About

White houses are boring and no joy. Bring the colours in and paint everything in seconds! To complete every level, paint all-white spaces. Swipe left, right, up and down and fill all the empty spots! Every level is 100% possible to solve. Yet not everyone can do it! Are you up for the puzzle?
