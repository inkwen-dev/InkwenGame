## About

ChickZ Stack is a fun and addictive arcade game where flying chickens take to the sky! stack your chickens to overcome obstacles and glide through colorful, challenging levels. The taller your chicken stack, the easier it is to pass walls, traps, and enemies. Unlock adorable flying chickens, explore new worlds, and compete for high scores. With simple one-tap controls and fast-paced gameplay, ChickZ Stack is easy to play but hard to master. Stack, fly, and flap your way to victory!
