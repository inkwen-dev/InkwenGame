## About

Play as the naughtiest cat in town in Bad Cat Prankster - Mom's Return! Step into the paws of a mischievous feline and turn an ordinary house into your personal playground. Scratch furniture, knock objects off shelves, climb onto rooftops, and create hilarious chaos before Mom catches you.
Explore every corner of the house, complete funny prank missions, and use your cat instincts to outsmart humans. But be careful Mom is always watching, and you'll need to stay sneaky to avoid getting caught. Hide, escape, and continue your playful adventures while causing maximum mischief.
From making a mess in the living room to performing tricky stunts around the house, every level offers new challenges and exciting opportunities for feline fun. Can you become the ultimate prankster cat and survive Mom's return?
