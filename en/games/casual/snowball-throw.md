## How to play

Tap to throw the snowball

## About

In this game the player starts with 6 snowballs to throw. For each throw the distance is calculated, and the total score is added. Power-ups can help the player during the game, like Additional lives (+3), Silver star (adds score in 1000) and Golden Star (adds score in 5000).
