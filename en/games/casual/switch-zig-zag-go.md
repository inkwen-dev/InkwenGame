## About

In fact, nothing could be further from the truth. Lasting more than 30 seconds in a ZigZag game is a challenge that you'll have only your mousetip to get you through: tap the screen once to make the black ball rotate one way, tap twice and it will change direction. It's that simple and yet so complicated at the same time.
