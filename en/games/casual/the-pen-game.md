## About

The Pen Game is a fun yet challenging skill based game inspired by the popular pencil game, where you have to masterfully get the pencil on the table without hitting any obstacles to rack up as many points as you can. Multiple different modes of the game are available ranging from endless casual to general classic, with three different modes each. Challenge yourself by trying out all the modes, and become the best at the Pen Game.
