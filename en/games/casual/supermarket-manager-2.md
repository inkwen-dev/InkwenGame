## About

Supermarket Manager is a very fun game. Do you often go to the supermarket? Have you ever imagined what's like to be a supermarket manager? In this game, you can experience a day of being a manager in a cute supermarket. You need to clean up the floor at the beginning of the day. And organize the fruits on the shelf... Finally you can start serving customers. Have a good time!
