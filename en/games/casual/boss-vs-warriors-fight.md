## About

You can choose one of the colorful characters in order to participate in the game Boss vs Warriors. Your character will race down the road, and catch up with ferocious monsters. At a certain distance, you can shoot and hit your enemies. But if the distance is lost, your only chance is to jump over the monster. You can also collect coins and extra lives on your way.
