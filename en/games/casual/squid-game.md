## How to play

The game has 7 levels, in each level you will be explained the rules of the game.

Control in the game:

WASD-move.
Space — jump (not used everywhere).
Left Shift — freeze (only for the game "Red Light - Green Light").
Mouse — action.
Escape-pause.

## About

In this game you will find the fun of all the games. We have collected the best and combined them into one whole. You will have to play all the popular games from the "Squid Game". However, remember: there should only be one winner left in the end.

All games are carefully recreated based on the original games. You will fully immerse yourself in the atmosphere of each of them, enjoying all the game modes.
