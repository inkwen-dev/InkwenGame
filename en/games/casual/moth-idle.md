## About

Moth Idle is a simple idle game where you have a field of lamps and some moths. The moths like light and absorb it, and each absorption makes them stronger. There are 10 different colors of lamps, each color gives a bonus to this color multiplier. Also, there are 8 different upgrades, e.g. make moths faster or gain more light. There is a 2-layer prestige system where you can prestige or rebirth to make moths stronger.
