## How to play

Swipe left / right → move the ball left or right.
Drag forward → roll the ball forward (you can go faster or slower depending on how you drag).
When traps come, slow down and align the ball straight before crossing.
Keep balancing until you reach the end.
Reach the boat to finish the level.

## About

Rolling Balance Ball 3D is a simple but challenging balance game where your main goal is to guide a rolling ball across narrow wooden bridges surrounded by water. You must keep the ball steady, avoid traps, and safely reach the boat at the end of each level. The gameplay feels physics-based, so small moves matter one wrong swipe and the ball can fall into the water.
