## About

Prepare delicious food and take it to a picnic, pick a friend to join in, let everyone have a full meal, and gather around the campfire to spend a pleasant evening together. Help them bring some food to the forest: only the mother cat knows what her family likes to eat, and chooses the food she wants according to her instructions. Choose your favorite cartoon characters and invite them to participate. You can choose different characters for each picnic. On the way to the forest, remove all obstacles.
