## About

Hidden pokemon is a hidden object html5 game for all devices. Point and click to the right pokemon to get scored. For every pokemon, you discover you get 100 points. Clicking the wrong pokemon you get 25 points penalty for every mistake you do. You have 15 seconds to discover all of them. To pass the level you have to discover all hidden pokemon.
