## About

Once the stickman woke up and realized that everything had changed around. Outwardly, everything remained the same, but the very essence of the city became different, tougher, and even deadly dangerous. To fully understand the situation, take the hero to the streets of Killer City and you will immediately understand everything. It became extremely dangerous to walk, if the hero was not hit by transport, a real killer would lie in wait in the gateway or around the corner. Your task is to collect maximum money. Green packs are lying right on the pavement or in houses. Stock up with a bat to fight off the attack and take care of yourself when crossing the road.
