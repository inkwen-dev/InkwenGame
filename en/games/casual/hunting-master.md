## How to play

Your main goal is to aim and shoot your bow at the animals on different floating islands. Pull back to adjust power and angle, then release to fire accurate arrows. Defeat as many animals as possible to earn points and stars. Use powerful abilities like Meteorite and Thunder to clear multiple targets at once!

Mobile Controls:
Touchscreen: Tap and drag on the screen to aim and pull the bowstring, then release to shoot.

## About

Become the ultimate hunter in Hunting Master, a fun and strategic archery shooting game set in a beautiful desert world! Jump between floating islands and use your bow to hunt wild animals like boars and wolves with precise shots. The core idea is aim, power, and chain reactions — shoot arrows to defeat animals and collect points while upgrading your bow with powerful abilities. What makes it unique? Stunning floating island visuals, satisfying bow-shooting mechanics with glowing effects, multiple power-ups (Meteorite, Thunder, etc.), and addictive progression as you become a stronger hunter in this desert hunting adventure!
