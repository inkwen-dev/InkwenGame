## About

This is a clicker game about hesitation, doubt, and the eternal “well… maybe”.

In this world, birds never give clear answers.
To any question you’ll hear:
“I doubt it”, “50/50”, “unlikely”, “partially”, “hard to say” — and dozens of other evasive replies.

Click, collect feathers, and upgrade your flock of doubtful birds.
Unlock new ones and complete the full collection of the most uncertain creatures in the world.

🔹 Feathers — used for regular upgrades and faster progression
🌾 Grain — a rare currency for unique improvements and special bonuses
📈 Many upgrades with unexpected effects
🏆 Leaderboards — compete to become the most doubtful keeper of grain
💬 Chat — talk with other players who are just as uncertain

You don’t need to be right here.
You just need to doubt.

Become a legend of hesitation.
Or… maybe not.
