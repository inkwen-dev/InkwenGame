## How to play

Your goal is simple: get the red ball safely to the green target.

Each level is a small physics puzzle made up of balls, planks, platforms, and obstacles. The red ball moves with gravity, but its path is often blocked by other balls.

Tap the removable balls in the correct order to make them disappear. Removing one can change the balance of the level, allowing the red ball to roll, slide, bounce, or fall onto the planks below.

Think before you tap. Removing the wrong ball too early could send the red ball in the wrong direction or make it miss the target completely.

Plan your moves, clear the right obstacles, and use the planks to guide the ball all the way to the finish.

Simple to understand, but each new level introduces a different puzzle to solve.

## About

Planko is a strategy game where you need to make the ball travel from one point to another. By tapping on the targets you can make the planks move by making them fall. Be sure to make sure you make them fall at the right time to ensure you don't miss your chance.
