## About

Become a dentist and help our friends to have a healthy mouth. Brian, Katie, Frank and Peter have come to the dental clinic for you to help them clean their teeth, put fillings or fix broken teeth. Your children may perform dental treatments and play as experts dentists. Help your kid become a dentist, it's educational and fun. Enjoy our amazing dentists game. Choose one of the characters and invite them to sit in the dental chair. An educational game where children will have a lot of tools and accessories to be the best dentists and learn that oral health is very important.
