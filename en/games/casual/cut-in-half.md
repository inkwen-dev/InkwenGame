## How to play

**Become a Slicing Master!** Step into the shoes of a precision slicer and cut in half every object, obstacle, and element that crosses your path. In this thrilling casual slicing action game, you’ll use your knife or sword to clear paths and advance through stages in a vibrant low-poly 3D cartoon world. Navigate the grassy campsites and nature scenery, tap to start each stage, collect coins to boost your attack power, and guide the cute smiling yellow emoji ball to show them who’s the master slicer!

**Desktop Controls:**  
↑ (Up Arrow): Move upward.  
↓ (Down Arrow): Move downward.  
← (Left Arrow): Move left.  
→ (Right Arrow): Move right.  
Spacebar: Slice with the knife or sword.  
Shift: Activate boost.

**Mobile Controls:**  
On-Screen Joystick: Move the smiling yellow emoji ball in all directions by dragging the joystick.  
Slice Button: Tap to cut objects, obstacles, and elements in half.  
Upgrade Button: Tap to boost your attack power with collected coins.

## About

Cut In Half is a casual mobile slicing action game where the core concept revolves around precisely cutting objects, obstacles, and elements in half using a knife or sword to clear paths and advance. In its vibrant low-poly 3D cartoon world of grassy campsites and nature scenery, players simply tap to start each stage, collect coins for upgrades, and boost attack power (shown at 100% Lv. 1 in the screenshot) while navigating cute characters like the smiling yellow emoji ball. What makes it unique is the satisfying physics-based cutting mechanic combined with effortless one-tap controls, an intuitive upgrade system, and rewarding progression that turns simple slices into addictive, visually charming challenges.
