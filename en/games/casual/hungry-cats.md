## How to play

Guide a group of adorable cats as they wander through tiny worlds, searching for food before they curl up for another cozy nap. 😴 But be careful—you control all the cats at the same time! Plan your moves wisely and rotate the level to help every kitty reach their snack safely.

Desktop Controls:
W: Move forward
A: Move left
S: Move backward
D: Move right
Q: Rotate platform left
E: Rotate platform right

Mobile Controls:
Left Button: Move left
Right Button: Move right
Rotate Left Icon: Rotate the platform left
Rotate Right Icon: Rotate the platform right
Up Icon: Jump
Down Icon: Interact with items

## About

Hungry Cats is a casual puzzle game where you guide several cats at the same time to find their food. By rotating the entire level, you must think carefully about how every cat moves together. This unique mechanic turns simple movement into a fun and playful puzzle challenge. 🐾
