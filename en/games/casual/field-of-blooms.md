## How to play

Mouse: move to guide your tool, nothing to click or hold. Move to the edge of the screen to scroll the garden.
Touch: hold and drag your finger. Drag near the edge to scroll.
Space, Esc or Tab pauses.
1, 2, 3 or the buttons at the bottom switch tools.

## About

Your garden dried out. But you can save it!
Sweep the watering can across the field and dry sprouts open in a line behind your cursor. Then the fertilizer turns them into fat golden heads, then the basket collects the lot. Cash in what you gathered, buy a wider spray and a longer timer, and go back for more. Harvest the whole map and the season starts over, permanently richer!
