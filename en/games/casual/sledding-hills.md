## How to play

Desktop Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: For push sled on start.
A or On-Screen Joystick: Move left.
D or On-Screen Joystick: Move right.

Mobile Controls:
On-Screen Joystick: For push sled on start.
On-Screen Joystick: For turns.
