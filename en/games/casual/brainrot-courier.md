## How to play

PC Controls

WASD = move
Space = jump
E = interact
Mobile Controls

Swipe the left side of the screen = move with the joystick
Tap on the right side button = jump
Swipe the screen = move camera view
Tap on the in-game UI = interact

## About

Brainrot Courier is an exciting skateboard adventure in which your goal is to collect the most legendary Brainrots! Ride on colorful platforms, jump over dangerous chasms, perform risky maneuvers to reach rare characters scattered around the world.



The further you go, the more unique and valuable Brainrots you can find. From popular characters to ultra—rare legends, each find brings you closer to becoming the best Brainroot collector.



Features



* Dynamic platform gameplay on a skateboard

* Collect dozens of unique and fun Brainrots

* Jump over chasms and overcome difficult obstacles

* Smooth and responsive skateboard controls

* Unlock rare and legendary Brainrots

* Collect the greatest collection of Brainrots

* Fast and addictive gaming sessions

* Suitable for players of all ages
