## How to play

Controls on computer: mouse — building and camera (right button — rotate, wheel — zoom), WASD/arrows — move camera, R — rotate item, Delete — sell, Esc — exit walk mode.

Controls on phone: tap — build and select, two fingers — rotate and zoom camera, joystick — walking in walk mode.

## About

A cozy 3D builder where you design and furnish your very own house from scratch. Raise walls and floors, paint them any color, hang wallpaper, lay flooring, arrange furniture, and decorate the yard: from flower beds and a campfire to a pool and a fountain.

Finished a room? Step inside! First-person walk mode lets you stroll through your house, climb the stairs, and see the result with your own eyes, by day and by night.

Want more than a sandbox? Fill client orders: build to the brief, get graded on your work, earn experience, and level up. A special challenge order is available every day. As you level up, new plots unlock — so you can work on several houses at once.
