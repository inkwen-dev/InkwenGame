## About

Raccoon Clicker is a cute and relaxing idle game where you build your own raccoon world, one tap at a time! Start by clicking your little raccoon to earn your first coins, then unlock helpers like mama raccoons, parks, trash planes, and even space upgrades. The more you tap, the faster your raccoon family grows. Collect adorable raccoon characters, upgrade your world, and follow funny news headlines about strange raccoon activity in the human world. Each upgrade makes your raccoon empire bigger, from simple trash collecter friends to cosmic raccoon explorers. Build, tap, upgrade, and discover what the raccoons are really planning!
