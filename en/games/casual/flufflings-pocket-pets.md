## How to play

Attract Stray Cats:
Leave food outside to attract cats.
More food and toys will bring more cats to your yard.

Earn Coins:
The happier the cats are when they leave, the more coins they will leave behind.

Adopt Cats:
Put a collar on a cat to adopt it.
Adopted cats will never leave!

Interact with the Cats:
Hold a cat to see its information.
Fill bowls with food or water by dropping items into them.
Drag and release the ball to play with the cats.
