Navigate intricate mazes in Pac-Chef as an ambitious cook, collecting coins and culinary treasures while dodging hostile chefs. Unlock special abilities from gathered ingredients to fend off foes. With retro pixel art and strategic skill-based gameplay, every level presents a unique recipe challenge. Can you perfect the culinary quest?
