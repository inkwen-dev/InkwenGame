## About

A fun and exciting decompression game that can release stress and exercise your reaction to test your reaction. In the game, press and hold the screen to generate stairs to control the characters ascent and descent, avoid obstacles, rescue companions, and finally reach the top of the tower. Kill the flying dragon on the top of the tower!
