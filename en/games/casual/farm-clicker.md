## About

A small farm has been waiting for its hero for a long time and for sure you are the one who will be able to develop it so that it flourishes and grows. Managing a farm is not easy, and it's not even about the size of the farm, but about the constant care of everyone who lives on the farm, including the animals. Your task is to click on all the animals that appear in front of you.
