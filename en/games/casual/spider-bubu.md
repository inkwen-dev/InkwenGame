## How to play

*To move from wall to wall, tap and release on mobile, or click and release with the mouse on PC. *Collect all three pumpkins and safely reach the visible portal. *Playable on both mobile and PC.

## About

Spider-Bubu swings from wall to wall across the city. Halloween has arrived, and you need to collect all the Halloween candies along with Spider-Bubu. Don’t forget to grab the giant pumpkin too! You must be very careful in the city — sharp spinning blades and pointed spikes are everywhere. Collect all the pumpkins and safely reach the portal that appears in the city.
