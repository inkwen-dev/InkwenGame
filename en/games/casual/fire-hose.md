## How to play

On desktop use the mouse to aim and click to release the water. Press Q to toggle mouse lock

On mobile user the virtual joystick and the button to trigger the water from the hose.
You win by extinguishing as many fires fires on the screen without running out of water

## About

Think you can handle the pressure?

Use your mouse or the left virtual joystick on mobile to move and aim, then click or hit the right trigger to spray and put out every fire before your water runs out.

Each level gets harder with more flames, trickier layouts, and less room for mistakes. Waste too much water and you will fail. Stay sharp, move fast, and clear them all.

Upgrade your water deposits to carry more water, spray longer, and survive tougher stages.

Move with the left joystick
Spray with the right trigger
Put out every fire
Upgrade your water deposits
Beat fast and challenging levels

Every drop counts. Start spraying now!
