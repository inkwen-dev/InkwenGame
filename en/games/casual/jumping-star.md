## About

Jumping Star is an addictive casual jumping game. Its time to stack your own zombie towers and help the lively boy climb the delicious bouncy tofu trampoline tower. Beware of obstacles that appear from both sides and try to stand in the middle for a smoother and safer ride. Try to challenge every move and unleash the boys acrobatic skills! Go, Robots!
