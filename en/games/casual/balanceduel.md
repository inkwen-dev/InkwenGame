## How to play

- Use A or the left arrow to go back - Use D or the right arrow to move forward

## About

Balance Duel is a fun fighting game based on the element of balance, where you wield weapons and directly confront opponents in laughter-filled battles. Your task is not only to attack but also to maintain your balance, timing your strikes wisely to knock down your opponent before you lose your own footing. Every collision is unpredictable, creating matches that are both tense and humorous. The player who maintains better balance will be the winner!
