## How to play

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys

Open backpack: B

Confirm / reroll: Space

Pause: Escape

Attacks auto-cast. Point-and-click to navigate menus; right-click or drag to equip items in the backpack.

## About

Scribble your way through waves of classroom doodles in this notebook-themed survivor RPG. Spells auto-cast. You just dodge and pick upgrades.
16 stages from elementary to university. 64 unique bosses. Spells with crazy combos. Many gear pieces to loot, upgrade, and stack.
