## About

Stickman Army Pusher is a fun and addictive game where you play as a blue stickman commander. Your goal is to strategically place blue stickmen to push the red stickmen off the edge. Use your army wisely to outnumber and push the opponents away, winning each level by making all red stickmen fall. Enjoy smooth gameplay and simple controls, perfect for quick and exciting battles.
