## How to play

Right Click on Mouse

## About

It’s Halloween time and the pumpkin heads are attacking again! If you want to survive, you have to shoot down these pumpkin heads before they reach you. Each pumpkin head that is destroyed is considered as one score point. You have two lives and whenever a pumpkin head reaches you, you lose one life. When you lose your two lives, you will also lose the game. But you are given a second chance! Choose one of the 4 pumpkin heads shown to you and if you are lucky enough, you will find an extra lives in it and can continue playing. So do your best and shoot down the evil pumpkin heads!
