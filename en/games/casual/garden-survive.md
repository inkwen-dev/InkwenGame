## About

You should survive with your cube characters inside of a garden made with cubes. You should be careful with the razors come to you and the walls which fall down on you and the spiked woods. If you can catch the heath which appears sometimes in the garden, you can extra life. On each time the level increases, you will have right to unlock new character.
