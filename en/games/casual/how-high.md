## About

Bounce. Climb. Survive. Repeat. How High is a fast-paced, addictive arcade adventure that challenges your reflexes, timing, and focus. You control a lively bouncing ball as it climbs higher and higher through a never-ending tower of planks. Every bounce weakens the plank beneath you...stay too long and it disappears. But the higher you climb, the tougher it gets.
