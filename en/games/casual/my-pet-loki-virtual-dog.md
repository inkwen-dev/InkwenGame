## About

My Pet Loki Virtual Dog is a cute and delicate Grooming game. In this game, you can experience a day of taking care of a dog. There are 5 parts in this game. Pick a random section to start your experience. Puppies are also afraid of cold and need to wear clothes in winter, so you can go into the dressing section and pick a personalized outfit for your dog. You can also enter the feeding section and use the coins to buy tasty food for your puppy dog. It doesnt matter if you run out of coins, you can still get coins by playing mini-games. The virtual puppy dog also needs your company, pick toys to play with in the toy section.
