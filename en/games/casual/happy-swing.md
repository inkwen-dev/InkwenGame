## How to play

🕹️ Game Controls & Concept 🕹️

💡 Game Concept:
In this game, the player embarks on a journey to find a lost soul. Timing is everything—jump and swing at the perfect moment to navigate through the world and reach your goal. Every swing brings you closer, but one wrong move can set you back! Can you master the rhythm and claim the soul?

🎮 Controls:

🖥️ Mouse (PC):
Jump & Swing – Click the Mouse Button to jump and grab onto points for swinging.
Perfect Timing – Release at the right moment to maintain momentum and progress smoothly.

📱 Mobile:
Jump & Swing – Tap the screen to leap and grab onto swing points.
Precision Matters – Tap and release with perfect timing to avoid falling!
