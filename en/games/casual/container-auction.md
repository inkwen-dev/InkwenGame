## How to play

1. Choose a location — the higher the level, the more valuable the containers
2. Inspect 3 containers and select one for auction
3. Peek inside through the gap or watch an ad for a full preview
4. Negotiate with bots — raise your bid or pass
5. Win the auction, open the container and discover what's inside!
6. Sell items for profit, restore worn-out pieces

## About

"Container Auction" — an exciting auction simulator! Bid on mysterious containers, competing against rivals. Peek inside through the door gap, assess the contents, and place your bid!

Open containers and discover items — from old books to gold watches and diamond brooches. Every item has its own rarity: common, uncommon, rare, epic, or legendary!
