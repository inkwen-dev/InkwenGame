## About

Funny creatures want to organize their own musical group. In the new exciting online game Sprunki Incredibox you have an extended orchestra of nine singers, to whom you will help everyone choose their own unique image. In front of you on the screen you will see a playing field where there are black and white silhouettes of Sprunki. A panel with icons will be visible at the bottom of the playing field. By dragging them over the silhouettes you can perform certain actions on a particular character. Thus, gradually in the game Sprunki Incredibox you will create for each Sprunki its own unique image.
