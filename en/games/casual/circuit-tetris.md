## About

Energize your brain with Circuit Tetris! In this innovative puzzle arcade game, blocks arent just shapes&mdash;they are vital components of a massive circuit board. As pieces descend, you must rotate and strategically place them to create a continuous path of electricity. How to Play: Connect: Build a path from the glowing power strip on the left to the terminal on the right. Power Up: Pieces automatically glow with neon green light when they are successfully connected to the power source.
