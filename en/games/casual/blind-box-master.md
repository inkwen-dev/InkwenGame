## How to play

Tap or click to play. Pick a box on the desk, buy and open it to discover cute collectible items of different rarities and conditions. Sell duplicates for coins, complete daily tasks and achievements, upgrade your luck and idle income, synthesize items, and collect all 100 collectibles. Controlled entirely with mouse clicks or taps.

## About

Blind Box Master is a cozy, hand-drawn blind box collection game. Open mystery boxes and discover 100 adorable collectibles across 6 rarities and 5 conditions (Broken to Mint).

Features:
• 4 box tiers, Lucky Charm upgrades & Legendary chases
• 20 cute themed series to collect
• Daily & weekly tasks, achievements, card-flip mini game
• Sell, haggle, synthesize 3 items into higher rarity
• Idle income & showcase your best finds

Tap to play. Desktop & mobile.
