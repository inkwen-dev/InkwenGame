## About

Do you like simple construction and development games? Welcome to Idle Craft 3D, this idle building game! The purpose of the placement building game is to stack blocks, get involved in the construction industry, and build the tallest skyscraper in the world! Build the foundation at the beginning of the game, accumulate it and make it a great landmark! You can learn how to build beautiful houses, and we will guide you step by step through the entire process from foundation to completion of the house. Don't forget to find resources and upgrade your tools. You can make special furniture and buildings in the game, enjoy cool 3D graphics, and become the richest idle construction tycoon!
