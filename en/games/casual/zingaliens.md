## About

ZingAliens &ndash; Action-Packed Alien Blasting Fun! Get ready for an exciting space adventure in ZingAliens, a fast-paced arcade game where waves of mischievous aliens are invading! Your mission is simple: hit the incoming aliens the exact number of times required to destroy them before they escape. Each alien has a number showing how many hits it needs. Some are easy to defeat, while others require quick thinking and rapid tapping. Stay focused, aim carefully, and smash the aliens before they take over!
