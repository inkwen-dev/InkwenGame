## About

Arrow JamEscape is a game that requires high concentration as you click arrows moving in the correct direction without colliding with any arrows on the board, continuing until all arrows are cleared. There is also a special golden arrow &mdash; if you are sharp and quick-witted enough to help it escape the board, you win. But it&rsquo;s not that simple &mdash; there is also time pressure. You only have 1 minute to complete the challenge. One wrong move costs you one heart, and each level gives you only 3 hearts to use.
