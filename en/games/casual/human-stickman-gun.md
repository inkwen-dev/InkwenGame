## About

In Human Stickman Gun game, you start as a small, simple gun. As you move forward, you collect stickmen who join your gun and help it grow stronger. Each stickman takes a spot to build a bigger and better gun. Shoot barrels to earn money, dodge obstacles, and keep upgrading your gun. There are lots of cool and tricky levels to explore. Have fun and good luck!
