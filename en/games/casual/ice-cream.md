## About

Do you like to indulge? Well we have a treat for you. The most delicious Ice Cream game is now available on your mobile phone. You fingers won’t be able to resist it. As a proud owner of an Ice Cream Stand, your job is to prepare the most intricate ice cream cones in a record time. Disappointing customer is no option for the future of your business.
