## About

SapotaGo is a fast-paced reflex arcade game built around a playful twist: a bouncing object trapped inside the curved world of a giant sapota! Your goal is simple&mdash;keep the object in motion and prevent it from escaping. The sapota&rsquo;s circular edges send the object ricocheting forward from one side to the other. At the circumference, a movable paddle awaits&mdash;your only defense. Slide it in any direction to catch, reflect, and control the direction of the bouncing object. Miss once, and the run ends. As the pace increases, every rebound becomes a test of timing, precision, and quick decision-making.
