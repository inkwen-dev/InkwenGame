## About

Giant Race is a casual and entertaining game that combines parkour and fighting. Collect characters of the same color to make yourself stronger and watch out for obstacles like spikes and rolling gears that will make you less in number. Also, don't forget to collect diamonds, which can unlock more interesting accessories in the store. Finally, defeat the big boss to move on to the next level.
