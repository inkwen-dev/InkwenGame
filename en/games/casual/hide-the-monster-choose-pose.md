## How to play

🎯 Click on the monster figure to change its pose;
🧩 Drag the shape to the cover shape to find the correct monster pose;
🏆 After matching the pose, your monster is saved!
⏰ Time warns how long it will take for the monster to become dangerous.

## About

Help the cute monsters hide from the gaze of an invisible enemy! Each monster has its own unique hide-and-seek pose. Choose the most appropriate pose for your monster to quietly blend into the crowd and avoid danger!

Each time the level will become more difficult, and the time to solve the level will decrease! Get creative in this exciting challenge and help all the monsters hide!
