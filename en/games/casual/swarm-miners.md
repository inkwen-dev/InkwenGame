## How to play

Move with WASD or the arrow keys (touch: drag the on-screen joystick). Walk up to a rock and your miner swings automatically — fill your backpack, then sell ore to the merchant for coins. Spend coins on stronger tools, bigger backpacks, new worlds, and pets. Press E (or tap) to talk to vendors and open menus; press 1-9 to switch hotbar items and place dynamite with E inside the mine. Unlock every world and build the strongest swarm!

## About

SWARM MINERS is a cozy mining-idle adventure. Swing your pickaxe to smash gem-rich rocks, hatch a swarm of collectible pets that mine and fight alongside you, and unlock 40 vibrant worlds from sunny meadows to cosmic voids. Upgrade tools, chase treasure rocks, spin the Wheel of Fortune, blast ore with dynamite, and battle bosses. Easy to pick up, endlessly rewarding.
