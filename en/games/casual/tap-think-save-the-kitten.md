## How to play

Tap on arrow boxes in the right order. It will make a weapon of the same color and will shoot at the snake so you could save the kitten!

## About

Danger slithers closer! 

A colorful snake is stalking a helpless kitten, and only YOU can stop it! Tap away at the directional arrow-boxes in the right sequence to solve the puzzle and reload your colored gun. Match the colors, fire the bullets, and watch the beast shrink! Every second counts in this thrilling idle tap challenge. 

Can you outsmart the serpent and save the kitten?
