## About

Sweety Mania is a super addictive Match-3-Game. Collect all the ingredients listed at the top of the screen by clearing lines of 3 or more of the same ingredients in a row. But be careful: You need to finish the level before the 2-minute timer runs out. Can you beat the clock and become the Sweety Mania champion?
