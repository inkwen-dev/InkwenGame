## About

Baby Panda Dream Job is a very interactive and educational game. If you want to have fun while learning about the various professions, then this is the game for you. In this game, you can experience three jobs, bakery clerk, courier, and construction worker. You will learn how to choose the bread that customers like, how to build a complete house out of bricks, and how to send a delivery from the delivery station to the customer. This game has very fine interactive features to keep you engaged. It also has a thoughtful voice feature.
