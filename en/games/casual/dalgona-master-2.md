## About

Game Features: - An array of Dalgona and honeycomb treats, each demanding a unique approach to cutting. - Master the candy challenge across multiple levels. - A diverse set of cookie-carving tools to achieve the perfect cut. - Enjoy beautifully designed dalgona candy that makes each session visually appetizing. - A perfect game for those who love precision, challenges, and, of course, sweets.
