## About

Stellar Pulse is a fast-paced arcade game set in deep space. Pilot a small bioluminescent fish through glowing neon gates: cyan barriers above and magenta ones below, with only a narrow gap between them. Gravity pulls you down while each tap or key press gives you a short thrust upward. The gap slowly shrinks and gravity ramps up as your score rises, so timing and precision matter. Dodge every ring, beat your high score, and try not to clip the edges&mdash;one touch of the barriers or the floor and your flight is over.
