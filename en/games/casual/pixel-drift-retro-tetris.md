## About

Pixel Drift is a retro-styled Tetris game with a CRT phosphor aesthetic, chiptune music, and 6 unlockable maps. Stack falling tetrominoes, clear lines, and climb the leaderboard. Unlock neon, fire, ice and void worlds &mdash; each with unique rules, speed, and atmosphere. Earn coins, collect achievements, and master the combo system to dominate the high score board.
