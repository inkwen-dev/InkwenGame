Smash Master is a fun 3D physics-based puzzle game where players use a cannon to smash and knock down stacked objects.

## How to play

Aim the cannon at the stacked objects.
Find the weakest or most effective target point.
Tap or release to fire the cannonball.
Use physics and chain reactions to knock down multiple objects.
Complete the required objective before running out of shots.
Clear the level and move on to the next challenging structure.

## About

Smash Master is a fun 3D physics-based puzzle game where players use a cannon to smash and knock down stacked objects.

Carefully aim at weak points, fire the cannonball, and create satisfying chain reactions as crates, barrels, jars, fruits, and other objects crash to the ground. Each level introduces a new structure and challenge, while the limited number of shots encourages players to think before firing.
