## About

Santa Claus Finder is a traditional guessing puzzle game with a 2D Santa Claus and Christmas scenes. As an endless game, you need to locate the position of the Santa Claus in three cups at each round. The more times you find Santa Claus's position in three cups, the more scores you will get finally. The moving speed of the cups will increase round by round. To tell the truth, as long as you can locate the key cup all the time, it is not hard to find where he is! Good luck!
