“Tap Tower” is a game in which you can test your attentiveness by building the highest tower out of moving platforms. Score the most points and win first place!

## How to play

In the Tap Tower you need to tap the screen in time so that the moving platform is exactly above the previous one The more platforms you place the higher your tower will become The higher you build the tower the more points you earn
