## How to play

Become a Kitty Guardian!  
Step into the shoes of a loving cat caretaker and nurture adorable kawaii cats in a festive snowy winter wonderland. In this charming casual idle simulation game, you’ll collect cat food as the core idle resource (with passive production like +50/s) to level up your felines, raise their Sweetness stat, and unlock new features via daily check-ins and challenges. Manage cozy habitats, complete tasks, and show them who’s the best cat parent by building the ultimate heartwarming feline paradise!

Desktop Controls:  
Left Mouse Button: Collect cat food and interact with cats or resource icons.  
Click on CheckIn icon: Claim daily rewards.  
Click on Challenges icon: Complete tasks and unlock features.  
Click on bottom icons: Access Toy Room, food bowl, Kitty Wiki, or Recycle.

Mobile Controls:  
On-Screen Tap: Collect cat food and interact with all elements by tapping the screen.  
Tap Button: Tap CheckIn or Challenges for daily rewards and tasks.  
Tap Bottom Icons: Tap to open Toy Room, feed cats, view Kitty Wiki, or use Recycle.

## About

Kitty Kitty is a charming casual idle simulation game where players nurture and level up adorable kawaii cats in a festive snowy winter wonderland, collecting cat food as the core idle resource (with passive production like +50/s) to raise stats such as Sweetness and unlock new features via daily check-ins and challenges. Its unique appeal lies in the heartwarming blend of cozy cat-collection mechanics, customizable habitats (Toy Room, food bowls, Kitty Wiki, and recycling), and seasonal Christmas charm with Santa-hatted felines, decorated trees, and snowmen—all presented in vibrant, playful 2D art that emphasizes relaxed, feel-good feline care over competitive gameplay.
