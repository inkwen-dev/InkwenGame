## About

Join the exciting adventure of Fauna Protectors, a fun and engaging card-based memory game that tests your skills while celebrating the wonders of the animal kingdom! In Fauna Protectors, you take on the role of a guardian, tasked with pairing up matching animal cards to save these adorable creatures. Sharpen your memory and reflexes as you flip over cards, spot identical animal pairs, and clear the board before time runs out. With each level, the challenge grows as more cards and rare animals appear.
