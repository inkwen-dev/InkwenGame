## About

Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey: A Feline Hero’s Adventure Begins!

Join a brave cat on an epic quest in Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey, a fresh twist on the classic balls-and-cups game. This isn't just about tossing balls into cups—it's a full-blown adventure packed with clever puzzles, physics-based challenges, and one courageous kitty determined to save the day.

Your Cat, the Hero
Meet your furry champion—a quick-thinking, nimble cat with a heart of gold. As danger looms and traps lie in wait, it’s up to this whiskered warrior to take on wild levels filled with spinning platforms, explosives, and tricky obstacles.

Precision Meets Puzzle
Each level is a test of aim and timing. Launch the ball just right, bounce it off the environment, and land it perfectly in the cup. It’s not just about power—success requires strategy, finesse, and a little creative thinking.

Become a Cup Champion

Cup Quest – Hero’s Journey isn’t just a puzzle game—it’s a heartwarming, skill-testing adventure that’ll keep you coming back for more. Grab your paws, line up your shot, and get ready to become a legend.
