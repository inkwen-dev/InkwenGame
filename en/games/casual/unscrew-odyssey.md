## How to play

> 🔩 Tap a coloured screw to send it into a free box of the same colour. Drag across the model to rotate the puzzle and reveal hidden screws. Keep enough free box slots, use a booster when the board gets tricky, and remove every screw to finish the level. On desktop use the mouse; on mobile tap and drag with one finger. ✈️
