## About

Monster Rush Cat is a fast-paced arcade game where you launch a cute monster-cat to catch spinning candies in the sky. Time your jumps perfectly, collect coins, and avoid falling to your doom! As you progress, the challenge increases with moving targets and tricky timing. Unlock new monster skins and aim for the highest score in this colorful, one-tap skill game!
