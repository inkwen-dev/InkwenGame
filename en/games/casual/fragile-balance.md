## About

Can you maintain the fragile balance? In Fragile Balance, you must carefully drop brick blocks onto a platform, trying not to tip the scale. One wrong move &mdash; and it all comes crashing down! Test your precision, patience, and steady hand in this minimalist yet addictive game. Fragile Balance is a physics-based arcade game where the player drops brick blocks onto a narrow platform. The goal is to build the highest and most stable tower possible without causing it to collapse. Every drop is a test of accuracy and balance.
