## How to play

🦷 Check the patient's teeth and identify the problem.
🔧 Choose the correct dental tool for each treatment.
🪥 Clean, repair, and treat damaged or dirty teeth.
🎯 Carefully use each tool to complete the procedure.
😁 Treat all the problem teeth to finish the level.
⭐ Complete treatments successfully to unlock new patients and challenges!

## About

🦷 Become a Super Dentist! Step into your very own dental clinic and help patients get their perfect smile back! 😁 Examine dirty and damaged teeth, remove cavities, clean plaque, and fix dental problems using a variety of fun dentist tools. 🪥 Each patient brings a new challenge, so use the right tool at the right time and carefully treat every tooth. Complete your dental treatment and leave every patient with a bright, healthy smile! ✨
