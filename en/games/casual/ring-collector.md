A D or Left-Right Arrow or Mouse or Touch

## About

You should collect as many rings as possible with Ring Collector, which is a fun casual game. You should carefully pass the rings through various obstacles. In order to move on to the next level, you must insert the ring in the hole as much as the specified amount of rings. A game where you can spend time without getting bored with 75+ different levels.
