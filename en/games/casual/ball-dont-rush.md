## About

Ball Dont Rush tells the story of a ball trying to avoid obstacles in the sky. This ball speeding game is an arcade game where you try to move forward without hitting obstacles. You must have good reflexes to advance without hitting these constantly moving obstacles in this battle arena full of traps. How far can you go on this track full of obstacles leading to infinity?
