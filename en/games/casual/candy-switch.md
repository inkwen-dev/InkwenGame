## About

A great high score game of agility called "Candy Switch". Here you have to control a small candy ball that has a certain color and can change it under circumstances. The main goal of the game is to hold the ball as high as possible, passing through obstacles of different shapes. Tap to start jumping and go through the same color as your candy ball. good luck!
