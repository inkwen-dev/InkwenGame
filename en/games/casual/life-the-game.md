## About

Life The Game is the best way to experience an entire journey, from birth to death, in game form! From study dates to actual dates, play Life The Game and see if your life is delightful or disastrous. This Life game features different minigames for each stage of your life. There are four new fun mini games: Burger Madness, Study Session, Travel Puzzles & Rainbow Melody. You can play Life The Game for free, but your bad decisions might cost you your in-game survival! Join thousands of Life The Game online players and cycle through tons of fun minigames!
