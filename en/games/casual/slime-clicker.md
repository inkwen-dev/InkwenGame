## About

Get ready to click your way to ultimate slime glory in Slime Clicker, the most relaxing and fun idle clicker on the web! ✨

Tap, click, and chill as you rack up points by booping your adorable slime. Every click brings you closer to new levels, shiny upgrades, and delightfully cute skins — from Bat Slime to Rabbit Slime, each evolution is lovelier and more rewarding than the last.

Simple, satisfying, and absurdly cute, Slime Clicker is the perfect game to unwind, laugh, and click your heart out. Whether you play for five minutes or five hours, there’s always another slime waiting to make your day brighter.
