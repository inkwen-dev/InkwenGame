## How to play

Tap / swipe / drag to move and control your sheep through the stage.
Use physics to your advantage:
- Bounce to cross gaps
- Slide / stretch to fit through tight paths
Avoid traps and tricky obstacles, and keep your sheep on the safe path.
Reach the end point to complete the puzzle.
Earn rewards to unlock new sheep and custom items.
