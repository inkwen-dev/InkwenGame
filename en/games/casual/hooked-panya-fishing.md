## How to play

Reel In the Big One!
Cast your fishing line into Bamboo River and test your timing against 13 fish, each with its own fight style. Keep the indicator in the moving green zone to land the catch before it gets away. Chain catches into Endless Mode for rarer fish and rising difficulty, and hunt the Night Fish.

Mobile Controls:
- Tap Cast: Cast your line.
- Tap Reel in Rhythm: Tap when the indicator aligns with the green zone to reel in the fish.
- Tap Steady Hands: Your next cast starts with a fuller bar and one grace tap. Free to try early on.
- Tap Fish Scanner: Peek at the next fish’s rarity before you cast. Free early on, costs coins later.
- Tap Lucky Bait: Guarantees your next catch is a species you haven’t seen yet. First use free, then costs coins.

Desktop Controls:
- Space: Cast and reel in rhythm.
- Click: Use Steady Hands, Fish Scanner, Lucky Bait and Pause.

## About

A tap-timing fishing game: time your reel and land the catch before it escapes.
- 13 fish species, each with its own fighting style: calm, brisk, aggressive, heavy, erratic.
- Panya’s Journal: discover every species and fill in all the silhouettes, including 3 Night Fish with a coin bonus when you catch them all.
- Endless Mode: deeper water, faster fish, shrinking green zone, rarer catches.
- Build HOT, FIRE and LEGEND streaks, and watch out for junk on your line.
- Play on desktop or phone: a casual fishing game with seasonal events and limited-time themes.
