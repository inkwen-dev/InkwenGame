## About

Step into the exciting world of crypto-powered Plinko! Plinko Ledger combines the classic thrill of Plinko with a modern cryptocurrency theme. Choose your favorite digital currency, customize the board, and watch as your coin drops through a maze of pegs on its way to exciting rewards. Want more control over the challenge? Adjust the number of holes on the board to create different risk and reward strategies. More slots mean more possibilities, while fewer slots can lead to bigger decisions and faster action. Every drop is a test of luck, strategy, and excitement!
