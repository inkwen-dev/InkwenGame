## About

Ready to become a piano master? In Magic Piano Music, tap the magic tiles to follow the beats and play your favorite music. Follow the rhythm of the music and click with precision, dont miss any notes. The game will end after three misses. Theres tons of music here like classic piano music, pop music and more! Every song will take you on an immersive arcade rhythm adventure.
