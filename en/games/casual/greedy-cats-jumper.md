## About

Greedy Cats Jumper is fun, top free jump game in which you jump from one platform to another by avoiding obstacles (crabs, birds etc.). Lots of obstacles to avoid (Crabs, Birds, Spiders, Spiky Platforms) Global leaderboards, fun achievements! Beat other jumpers' scores! Many fantastical worlds to play in- (ICE Age, Underground, Ghostly etc..)
