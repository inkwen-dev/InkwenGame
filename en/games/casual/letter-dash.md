## How to play

Tap on the letters on the keyboard or press the key on your keyboard if you play on a PC

## About

In Letter Dash, players are presented with falling letters, and they must quickly type the corresponding letters on their keyboard to destroy them before they reach the bottom. The objective of the game is to type the letters accurately and swiftly, preventing them from piling up and reaching the bottom. As the game progresses, the falling letters may increase in speed or become more challenging, increasing the difficulty level.
