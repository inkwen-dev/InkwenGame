## How to play

Look carefully at the colored orbs on the screen.
Choose the correct arrow direction.
Tap to shoot the arrow.
Make the arrow bounce and hit the matching colored orbs.
Pop the orbs in the correct way to clear the level.
Plan your move before shooting because every arrow matters.
Complete all level goals and move to the next challenge.
